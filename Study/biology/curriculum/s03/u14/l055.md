A chicken lays its egg outside after internal fertilisation. Why does one label, internal or external, not fully describe reproduction?

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**两种配子共同贡献遗传信息。** 在本课有性生殖情境中，精子与卵细胞融合形成受精卵。精子不是已经缩小成形的动物，卵细胞也不是只提供一个空容器。受精卵结合两方遗传物质，之后细胞分裂并逐步分化，形成胚胎的结构与功能。

**把地点分成两列。** 代表池蛙在水中完成体外受精，早期发育也在体外水中；鸡是体内受精，胚胎可在产出的卵中继续发育，卵黄提供储存的养分。人类通常在输卵管发生受精，早期胚胎向子宫移动并在适合条件下着床，继续在体内发育。不是所有青蛙或动物都完全遵循此代表模式。

**人类发育还需要持续支持。** 早期胚胎逐步形成器官基础，随后胎儿阶段继续生长和功能成熟。胎盘参与氧、养分与废物的交换，并有其他支持作用；母体和胎儿通常保有各自血液循环，物质跨交换界面转移，不能画成两套血液全部直接混合。胎盘也不是保证阻挡所有物质的绝对过滤器。

**从过程推理，而不是只记名词。** “鸡卵在体外，所以受精一定在体外”忽略了事件顺序。应分别寻找配子融合与胚胎发育的证据。成功融合不保证每个胚胎都存活；本模型的成功分支额外假定活性与后续支持条件适宜。

### English

**Two gametes contribute inherited information.** In this sexual-reproduction case, sperm and egg fuse to form a zygote. A sperm is not a miniature preformed animal, and an egg is not an empty container. The zygote combines inherited material, then its descendants divide and progressively differentiate.

**Use separate site columns.** The representative pond frog has external fertilisation and early development in water. Chickens have internal fertilisation, with development continuing inside laid eggs supported by yolk. Human fertilisation usually occurs in an oviduct; the early embryo moves toward the uterus and, under suitable conditions, implants and continues developing. These are specified cases rather than rules for every animal.

**Human development needs continuing support.** Early embryonic development establishes organ foundations; fetal growth and functional maturation follow. The placenta supports oxygen, nutrient and waste exchange and other functions. Maternal and fetal circulations usually remain separate while materials cross an interface. The placenta is not an absolute filter blocking every substance.

**Reason from processes.** “A chicken egg is outside, therefore fertilisation was external” overlooks the order of events. Seek separate evidence for fusion and development. Fusion does not guarantee survival; the model’s successful branch additionally assumes viability and suitable later support.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

误区：“精子是小人，卵只是食物”或“胎儿直接吃下母体吃的食物”。两种配子都贡献遗传信息；消化与运输之后，适宜的小分子等经交换界面到达胎儿，完整食物不沿一根管子直接进入胎儿胃里。

### English

Misconceptions treat sperm as a tiny person, egg as only food, or a fetus as directly eating the pregnant parent’s meals. Both gametes contribute inherited information. Digestion and transport precede exchange of suitable materials; whole meals do not pass down a tube into the fetal stomach.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

配子通常各含一套染色体，融合恢复该物种典型的成对染色体状态；以后会学习减数分裂如何形成配子。早期分裂增加细胞数，不等于每轮都让胚胎总大小翻倍；细胞分化也不等于每种细胞都获得完全不同的一套基因。动物界另有无性生殖等方式，本课模型只对应给定有性路线。

### English

Gametes usually contain one chromosome set; fusion restores the species’ typical paired state. Later lessons explain meiosis. Early divisions increase cell number without necessarily doubling overall embryo size each time. Differentiation also does not mean each cell receives a wholly different gene set. Other animal reproductive modes exist; the model covers only the specified sexual route.
