# U14 · 两种植物与一种动物的生命路线 / Life routes of two plants and one animal

## 中文

用课程中豌豆、草莓匍匐茎和代表蝴蝶三例制作比较，可文字作图，也可完全在屏幕内完成。

1. 三条有顺序的路线：豌豆种子→幼苗→花→果实/新种子；草莓母株→匍匐茎→节上根→子株；蝴蝶卵→幼虫→蛹→成虫→下一代卵。
2. 分别标出同一个体发育的连接、产生下一代的连接和有无配子融合。草莓示例只展示营养繁殖支路，不代表草莓不会结种子。
3. 选一个新情境：有花蜜无幼虫食物的虚拟花园。说明能支持什么、还不能确认什么，提出一种额外证据。

提交三栏：**主张**写三种路线的共性与差异；**证据**引用花粉管/胚珠、节上根和幼虫/蛹的线索；**推理**解释代际连接与资源需要，补一句英文和一个模型局限。生长或填写完整不自动证明解释正确。

无需采集、饲养、培养、比较个人青春期或提供身体数据。

## English

Compare lesson pea, strawberry-runner and representative-butterfly routes in text, on paper or entirely on screen.

1. Order three routes: pea seed → seedling → flower → fruit/new seeds; strawberry parent → runner → node roots → daughter plant; butterfly egg → larva → pupa → adult → next-generation eggs.
2. Mark individual-development links, next-generation links and whether gamete fusion occurs. The strawberry case shows a vegetative branch, not a claim that strawberries cannot make seeds.
3. Analyse a fictional garden with nectar but no appropriate larval food. State what is supported, what remains unconfirmed and one additional evidence need.

In the three fields, give a **claim** comparing routes, **evidence** from pollen tubes/ovules, node roots and larva/pupa structures, and **reasoning** connecting generations and resources. Add an English sentence and one model limit. A complete submission is not automatic proof that its explanation is correct.

No collecting, rearing, culturing, personal puberty comparisons or body data are required.
