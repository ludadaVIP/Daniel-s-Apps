## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

豌豆花收到花粉却不一定结出种子；草莓的长茎却能连到一株小苗。为什么新生命不只走一条路线？

### English

A pea flower can receive pollen without necessarily producing seeds, while a strawberry’s long stem can connect to a new plant. Why is there more than one reproductive route?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先预测传粉与受精是不是同一事件；再猜草莓匍匐茎形成子株时，是否一定先有卵和精细胞融合。

### English

Predict whether pollination and fertilisation are one event, and whether runner-derived strawberry offspring necessarily begin with sperm–egg fusion.

### 中文

花图显示花粉留在柱头附近、花粉管延伸到含卵细胞的胚珠。另一图显示母株、匍匐茎、节上生根和子株。两幅都为简图，不提供真实长度与时间。

### English

The flower shows pollen near the stigma and a tube extending toward an ovule containing an egg. The other diagram shows parent, runner, node roots and daughter plant. Neither supplies real distances or times.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

在豌豆路线中切换相容/不相容花粉，逐步观察传粉、管生长、融合与种子发育；再切换草莓路线，追踪节上生根与子株。按钮不表示真实植物可被随意改变。

### English

Switch compatible/incompatible pollen in the pea route and step through transfer, tube growth, fusion and seed development. Then trace rooting and a daughter plant on the runner route. Controls do not imply arbitrary changes to real plants.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**先后发生的事件要分开。** 传粉是花粉从花药到合适柱头等接收结构的转移。花粉粒不是一粒微小种子。相容且有活性的花粉在合适条件下萌发花粉管，管参与向胚珠递送精细胞；精细胞与卵细胞融合才是这里聚焦的受精。花粉粒通常不整粒沿管钻进卵细胞。

**种子和果实的来源不同。** 成功受精并继续正常发育时，受精卵发育为胚，胚珠形成种子相关结构，子房通常发育成果实。因而果实可能包着多粒种子；有几个种子并不由花瓣数直接决定。传粉本身不能保证融合、每个胚珠成功或后续存活。

**植物也能走营养繁殖支路。** 草莓匍匐茎伸出，合适节位能生根并形成子株。此支路没有配子融合，常产生与母株遗传上很相近的个体，但仍可能发生突变，环境也会影响表现。草莓同时能开花结种子；不是一种植物只能选一种繁殖方式。

**解释一个反例。** 两朵花都收到花粉，一朵后来有种子，另一朵没有。相同的第一步观察不证明后续相同；可以继续查相容性、配子活性、是否融合与生长条件。不要把“没种子”直接改写成“从未传粉”。

### English

**Separate linked events.** Pollination transfers pollen from anthers to receiving structures such as a suitable stigma. A pollen grain is not a tiny seed. Under suitable conditions, viable compatible pollen germinates a tube that helps deliver sperm cells toward the ovule. Sperm–egg fusion is the fertilisation event emphasised here; the whole pollen grain does not travel down the tube into the egg.

**Seeds and fruits have different origins.** Following successful fertilisation and development, a zygote forms an embryo, an ovule forms seed structures and the ovary usually develops into fruit. A fruit may contain several seeds. Pollen arrival does not guarantee fusion, success of every ovule or later survival.

**Vegetative reproduction provides another branch.** A strawberry runner can root at a suitable node and form a daughter plant without gamete fusion. Offspring are often genetically very similar to the parent, although mutations can occur and environment affects expression. Strawberries can also produce flowers and seeds.

**Explain a counterexample.** If two flowers receive pollen but only one later produces seeds, equal first-step observations do not establish equal later events. Compatibility, gamete viability, fusion and growth conditions need checking. Seed absence does not prove that pollination never happened.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

误区：“花粉到达就是受精”或“果实就是长大的种子”。前者把转移与融合混同，后者把子房与胚珠混同。模型的两个相容性分支是可直接比较的反例。

### English

Misconceptions equate pollen arrival with fertilisation or fruit with an enlarged seed. These confuse transfer with fusion and ovary with ovule. The compatibility branches provide a comparison.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

被子植物有双受精：一个精细胞与卵细胞融合，另一个与中央细胞融合，启动胚乳发育，参与支持胚。主图只追踪第一条，不能因没有画出就说第二条不存在。营养繁殖中的遗传相近也不是“每个后代所有表现永远相同”；遗传、突变与环境要分开。

### English

Flowering plants have double fertilisation: one sperm fuses with the egg, while another fuses with the central cell to initiate endosperm development supporting the embryo. The main diagram follows only the first route. Genetic similarity in vegetative offspring also does not imply identical expression forever; inheritance, mutation and environment remain distinct.
