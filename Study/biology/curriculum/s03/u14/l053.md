## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

毛虫与成蝶外形、食物都可能不同，为什么仍可属于同一物种？一株豌豆结出种子，原来的植株真的变回去了？

### English

A caterpillar and adult butterfly may look different and eat different foods. How can they still belong to the same species? Does a pea plant actually turn back into a seed?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先猜：所有动物都有蛹期吗？循环图最后回到开头，是个体倒退，还是下一代出现？

### English

Predict whether all animals have pupae, and whether a cycle returning to the beginning means individual reversal or a new generation.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

四位置图比较代表蝴蝶、代表青蛙、豌豆和草莓匍匐茎。蓝箭头追踪顺序，虚线提醒代际或繁殖联系；图形不按比例，也不表示各阶段时间相等。

### English

The four-position diagram compares a representative butterfly, frog, pea and strawberry runner. Arrows show sequence and a dashed connection prompts generational reasoning. Sizes and stage durations are not proportional.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

逐步选择四个阶段，再切换生物比较。找出蝴蝶独有的蛹期、青蛙的蝌蚪阶段、豌豆种子里的下一代与草莓的营养繁殖支路；不要把四个图的位置当作完全相同的生物学阶段。

### English

Step through four positions and switch organisms. Identify the butterfly pupa, frog tadpole, next-generation pea embryo and strawberry vegetative branch. The same diagram position need not represent equivalent biological stages.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**生命的变化不只是放大。** 生长可增加大小，而发育还会改变结构与功能。代表蝴蝶经历卵、幼虫、蛹与成虫；幼虫常取食适宜叶片，成虫的取食和活动方式可不同。蛹看似不动，内部仍有复杂活跃的发育，不是死亡再复活。

**相似的词，不一定是相同的过程。** 代表青蛙经历受精卵、蝌蚪、幼蛙和成蛙等阶段，身体结构与生活方式也变化，但没有蝴蝶式蛹期。真实青蛙生活史有多样性，所以本图说“代表情境”，不把一个物种的顺序推广给全部两栖动物。

**循环图需要代际标签。** 豌豆种子内的胚萌发成幼苗，发育成开花植株；通过生殖形成的新种子含下一代的胚。原植株不是退回同一颗种子。蝴蝶的成虫产生下一代卵，也不是成虫自己的身体倒流。草莓匍匐茎则提供另一种繁殖支路，子株不必经过精卵融合。

**把资源需求加进周期。** 只给花园增加成蝶能用的花，不证明幼虫也有合适食物。观察到成蝶还可能反映迁入。支持完整生命周期，需要检查不同阶段的资源和实际繁殖证据，不能只数一时飞过的成体。

### English

**Life changes are more than enlargement.** Growth can increase size, while development also changes structure and function. A representative butterfly passes through egg, larva, pupa and adult stages. Larvae often need suitable leaves, while adult feeding and movement can differ. A quiet-looking pupa still undergoes active development; it is not death followed by revival.

**Similar words can hide different processes.** The representative frog changes from fertilised egg through tadpole and froglet to adult without a butterfly-like pupa. Frog life histories vary, so this is a representative case rather than a rule for every amphibian.

**Label generations on a cycle.** A pea embryo grows from its seed into a flowering plant. New seeds contain embryos of the next generation; the parent does not reverse into its original seed. Adult butterflies likewise produce next-generation eggs. A strawberry runner provides a different reproductive branch without gamete fusion.

**Add resource requirements.** Flowers used by adult butterflies do not establish that suitable larval food exists. A visiting adult may have arrived from elsewhere. Supporting a complete cycle requires checking stage-specific resources and evidence of reproduction, not merely counting passing adults.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

误区：“蛹不动，所以不活跃”或“外形变了就是另一物种”。不动不代表没有细胞过程；同一个体的不同发育阶段可非常不同。反过来，外形相似也不单独证明同种。

### English

Misconceptions: an immobile pupa is inactive, or a changed shape means a different species. Lack of movement does not mean lack of cellular activity. Different stages can look very different, while similarity alone also does not prove species identity.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

比较周期时要先问“图中的箭头是什么关系”。有的是同一个体的时间顺序，有的是亲代到子代的关系，有的表示物质流动；三者不能混用。高阶段会用生活史研究繁殖机会、阶段存活和种群结构，但本图没有存活率或每个阶段的持续时间数据。

### English

First ask what each arrow represents: temporal change within an individual, parent–offspring relationship or material flow. These are different relations. Later life-history studies examine reproductive opportunities and stage survival, but this diagram supplies neither survival rates nor durations.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

一块虚拟花园只有花蜜资源，没有特定幼虫食物。提出一个不能由现有观察解决的问题，并说明需要哪些阶段记录。无需捕捉或饲养昆虫。

### English

A fictional garden has nectar but lacks a particular larval food. Pose a question the current observations cannot resolve and identify stage records that would help. No capturing or rearing insects is needed.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

试说：A life cycle connects development and reproduction. Metamorphosis is not simply getting bigger. The new embryo belongs to the next generation.

### English

Practise: “A life cycle connects development and reproduction.” “Metamorphosis is not simply getting bigger.” “The new embryo belongs to the next generation.”
