## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

两个同龄人身高不同，一个先经历明显变化。能用“更高”证明所有系统都更成熟吗？

### English

Two people of the same age differ in height, and one changes visibly earlier. Does being taller establish greater maturity in every system?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

预测生长、发育与青春期是不是同一件事；再猜同一激素信号到达不同细胞时，是否必然触发相同反应。

### English

Predict whether growth, development and puberty are identical, and whether one hormone signal necessarily triggers the same response in every cell it reaches.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

图用信号、匹配受体、靶细胞和特定反应表示一般化学通信，不是人体激素浓度图。下方证据卡分别涉及大小变化、功能成熟与证据不足的比较。

### English

The diagram shows a signal, matching receptor, target and specific response as general chemical communication, not human hormone concentrations. Evidence cards distinguish size change, functional maturation and unsupported comparison.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

比较有/无匹配受体与有/无信号，逐步看特定反应是否出现；再把三张证据卡归为生长、发育或推断不足。没有某个特定反应，不等于细胞没有其他活动。

### English

Compare matching/non-matching receptors and signal availability, tracing the specific response. Classify cards as growth, development or unsupported inference. Lacking this response does not eliminate all other cell activity.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**生长与发育相关，但重点不同。** 骨长度、细胞数量或体积的增加可以体现生长；结构和功能逐步成熟属于发育。青春期包含生殖系统逐步成熟及其他身体变化，并不是一夜完成的开关，也不等于身高曲线上的一个点。人的思考、情绪和社会能力也不能只靠身体外形判断。

**变化需要协调，而不是身体“突然知道”。** 脑与内分泌系统参与协调，多种激素经循环到达不同组织，具有相应受体和细胞内机制的靶细胞作出反应。模型把一般信号简化为一个配对，真实青春期有多条调节路径与反馈，并不只由一种激素或一个器官控制。

**具体结构与功能有科学名称。** 睾丸的发育与精子产生能力建立有关；卵巢、子宫等结构的成熟可伴随月经周期逐步建立。月经涉及子宫内膜的周期变化与脱落，不是受伤，也不表示每次周期都已发生受精。声音、体毛、皮肤、身高等变化节奏可不同，不能据其中一项判定所有生殖功能。

**用尊重与证据处理差异。** 遗传、营养与多种条件会影响生长发育。两个同龄人的身高差只证明所测身高不同，不能单独诊断激素水平、健康或发育阶段，更不能给人排名。本课不收集身体信息；对自己变化的困惑可向可信任成人或医护人员询问。

### English

**Growth and development emphasise different things.** Increased bone length, cell number or cell volume can show growth. Development includes progressively formed and mature structures and functions. Puberty involves reproductive maturation and other bodily changes; it is neither an overnight switch nor one point on a height curve. Thinking, emotions and social abilities cannot be judged from appearance alone.

**Changes require coordination.** Brain and endocrine pathways help coordinate tissues. Circulating hormones influence targets with relevant receptors and internal mechanisms. Our single signal–receptor pair is a general schematic; real puberty involves several pathways and feedback loops, not one hormone or organ alone.

**Use scientific names.** Testicular development relates to the establishment of sperm production. Maturation involving ovaries and the uterus can include the establishment of menstrual cycles. Menstruation involves cyclical changes and shedding of the uterine lining, not an injury or evidence of fertilisation in every cycle. Voice, body hair, skin and height changes can have different timing; no one feature establishes all reproductive functions.

**Respect differences and evidence limits.** Inheritance, nutrition and other conditions influence development. A height difference establishes that measurement alone, not hormone levels, health, puberty stage or personal worth. No body information is collected here. Questions about one’s own changes can be discussed with a trusted adult or health professional.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

误区：“同龄人必须同步，更高者一定更成熟。”反例：生长和不同功能变化的节奏并不完全同步，单一外观指标信息不足。另一个误区是“激素一到就使所有细胞相同改变”；靶细胞的受体与内部状态都重要。

### English

Misconceptions demand synchrony among peers or treat greater height as complete maturity. Different changes need not occur together, and a single external measure is insufficient. Hormone arrival likewise does not make every cell respond identically; receptors and internal state matter.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

一般激素路径可写成“产生信号→运输→受体识别→细胞内改变→组织反应”。反馈还会反过来影响上游活动，为U15–U16做准备。一个模型中关掉信号只表示未提供被画的特定信号，不表示真实细胞内所有激素或生命过程归零。不要把示意图的帧数当成发育天数。

### English

A general hormonal path is production → transport → receptor recognition → intracellular change → tissue response. Feedback can influence upstream activity, preparing for U15–U16. Switching off the illustrated signal withholds that signal only; it does not remove every hormone or living process. Frame numbers are not developmental days.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

面对“我们班最高的人一定最成熟”的说法，写一条能确认的观察和两条不能据此得出的结论。只用虚构语句，不测量、记录或讨论具体同学的身体。

### English

Respond to “the tallest person in class must be most mature” with one supported observation and two unsupported conclusions. Use fictional statements without measuring, recording or discussing specific classmates’ bodies.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

练习：Growth is not identical to development. Cells respond to relevant signals through matching receptors. Developmental timing varies among individuals. 用varies描述差异，不给人贴排名标签。

### English

Practise: “Growth is not identical to development.” “Cells respond to relevant signals through matching receptors.” “Developmental timing varies among individuals.” Varies describes differences without ranking people.

## Check / 挑战自己

### 中文

四题检查生长/发育、受体机制、身高比较的证据边界与puberty。若凭一个尺寸推断健康，回看证据卡如何区分观察和解释。

### English

Four questions check growth/development, receptor mechanisms, height-evidence limits and puberty. If a size measure was used to infer health, revisit the observation–inference distinction.
