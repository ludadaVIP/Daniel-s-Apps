# 假如我是一名生态研究员 / If I were an ecological researcher

## 研究问题 / Research question

同一块草地，为什么路边与随机样方会产生不同的全场数量估计？哪些是观察证据，哪些是仍需检验的解释？

Why can roadside and random quadrats give different abundance estimates for one meadow? Which statements describe observations, and which explanations still need testing?

## 数据与方法 / Data and method

使用原创虚拟本地草地，36个1m²样方，计数表示同一种虚构植物在同一时期的可区分个体。数据不是真实新西兰调查。采用6或12格可重复随机方案，或明确标记的手选方案；随机位置在看计数前生成、不放回。另提供固定路边6格的对照。可揭示96株教学普查，但真实调查往往不知道总数。

Use an original virtual local meadow with 36 quadrats of 1 m². Counts describe distinct individuals of one fictional plant species at one time, not measured New Zealand field data. Use reproducible random samples of 6 or 12 cells, or an explicitly chosen sample. Random positions are generated before counts are viewed and drawn without replacement. Compare the fixed six roadside cells. The teaching census of 96 can be revealed, although real surveys often lack a known total.

## 学生作品 / Student output

1. 列出样方编号、逐格计数、抽样面积、样本密度和全场估计。密度分母是抽样面积，估计为密度×36m²。用整数估计时允许四舍五入。
2. 引用自己的样本和路边方案至少两条数字证据，解释代表性、选择偏差和随机误差。相同位置的重复不是独立空间样本。
3. 写主张、机制或替代解释、还需收集的具体变量/重复/对照，以及中文与英文摘要。不要把空间关联直接当作光照或降雨的因果证据。
4. 完成五道跨单元理解题，可回看和订正。

1. Record quadrat IDs, counts, sampled area, sample density and estimated field abundance. Divide by sampled area, then multiply by 36 m²; rounding to whole plants is accepted.
2. Cite numeric evidence from both samples and discuss representation, selection bias and sampling variation. Repeating identical positions is not an independent spatial sample.
3. Write a claim, mechanism or alternative explanation, specific further data, and Chinese and English summaries. Spatial association alone does not establish light or rainfall as a cause.
4. Complete five revisable cross-unit questions with feedback and lesson links.

## 保存与评价 / Saving and evaluation

草稿自动保存。修改样本、计算或文字会撤销作品保存状态，需要再次保存。系统只检查结构与估计计算，不自动判定自由文字、科学推理和英语正确。可下载包含全部数据与文字的纯文本双语报告。

Drafts autosave. Changing samples, calculation or writing invalidates the saved-project status. Structural and arithmetic checks do not automatically grade free writing, scientific reasoning or English. A plain-text bilingual report preserves all data and writing.

本阶段完成还需L049–L072的24课、U13–U18的6任务、有效保存作品，以及五题都答且至少4题正确；其他阶段的课程、任务或作品不代替本阶段要求。课程保持家庭试学状态，儿童实际试用和外部科学审阅另行记录。

Stage completion also requires all 24 lessons L049–L072, six missions U13–U18, a valid saved project, and all five questions answered with at least four correct. Earlier stages do not substitute for these requirements. Content remains a family pilot; real child trials and external scientific review are recorded separately.
