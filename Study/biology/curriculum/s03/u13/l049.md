## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

一滴水里有小点，面包酵母也像小点。看起来相似，是不是同一种生命？先别急着给它们贴“细菌”标签。

### English

A tiny dot in water and a baking-yeast cell may look alike. Does that make them the same kind of life? Resist labelling every small dot a bacterium.

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

预测：单细胞一定等于细菌吗？没有细胞核就没有遗传物质吗？保存最初想法，再用结构证据修正。

### English

Predict whether every single-celled organism is bacterial, and whether lacking a nucleus means lacking genetic material. Save an initial idea and revise it using structural evidence.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

比较预先标定的细菌、面包酵母与霉菌菌丝图。细菌的DNA画在细胞内，酵母与菌丝标出细胞核；图的大小不是实际比例。

### English

Compare pre-labelled bacterial, baking-yeast and mould-hypha diagrams. Bacterial DNA lies inside the cell; nuclei are marked in yeast and the hypha. Drawing sizes are not real proportions.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

选择一个代表样本，先隐藏结构证据，再显示。根据当前提供的证据选“代表细菌/代表真菌/线索不足”。未知照片不能只靠这三幅图断定身份；本题的分类范围只限这些已标定样本。

### English

Choose a representative sample. Hide and reveal structural evidence, then select bacterium, fungus or insufficient current clues. This comparison covers the pre-labelled samples; an unknown photograph needs further identification evidence.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**“微生物”说的是观察尺度，不是一张家谱。** 许多微小生命需要放大才看得清，包括细菌、微小真菌和某些原生生物。蘑菇整个子实体能被肉眼看见；它仍是真菌。病毒常与微生物一起研究，但没有细胞结构，不能把它画成更小的细菌。

**细胞核与DNA不能混为一谈。** 代表细菌有膜、细胞质、核糖体和DNA，但DNA没有被细胞核包住。酵母有细胞核，是单细胞真菌；霉菌常由丝状菌丝组成。菌丝不是植物的根，真菌也不靠叶绿体制糖，而是从环境取得有机物。

**比较结构不等于鉴定未知。** 有细胞核能支持“真核细胞”，却不能独自证明“真菌”，因为动物、植物和许多原生生物也有细胞核。图中没看到核，也可能是观察条件不足。真正辨认需可靠样本信息、尺度、结构、培养或分子证据等；本课不要求自己培养。

**试着解释新情况。** 一位同学说“酵母只有一个细胞，所以一定是细菌”。反例是酵母既单细胞又有真核结构。应分别问个体由多少细胞组成、细胞是否有核，而不是把两个问题合并。

### English

**“Microorganism” describes an observation scale, not a single family.** Bacteria, microscopic fungi and some protists are small organisms. A visible mushroom is still fungal. Viruses are often studied alongside microbes, but are not cellular organisms or miniature bacteria.

**DNA and a nucleus are different things.** The representative bacterium has a membrane, cytoplasm, ribosomes and DNA, but no nucleus enclosing that DNA. Yeast is a unicellular fungus with a nucleus. Moulds commonly grow as hyphae; hyphae are not plant roots. Fungi obtain organic materials rather than making sugars in chloroplasts.

**Comparison is not unknown-sample identification.** A nucleus supports eukaryotic cell organisation, not a fungal identity by itself: animals, plants and many protists are also eukaryotic. A nucleus missing from an image might simply be unresolved. Identification requires additional reliable information; home culturing is unnecessary here.

**Apply the distinction.** “Yeast has one cell, so it must be bacterial” joins two different questions. Yeast supplies a counterexample. Ask separately how many cells form the organism and whether its cells have membrane-bound nuclei.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

误区：“细菌没有核，所以没有DNA。”反例：细菌仍能生长、复制遗传信息和繁殖。缺的是包围DNA的细胞核，不是DNA本身。另一个误区是按颜色判断有益或有害；结构身份也不自动决定作用。

### English

Misconception: no bacterial nucleus means no DNA. Bacteria still copy genetic information and reproduce; what is absent is a nucleus around the DNA. Colour and structural identity also do not independently establish helpfulness or harm.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

原核生物还包括古菌；本课仅用细菌作代表，不能把“无细胞核”直接译成“只有细菌”。真菌菌丝可以有隔膜，也可能在较长连续区域中含多个核。图只保留比较所需线索，没有声称每一段都是一格相同细胞。科学模型要说明省略了什么。

### English

Prokaryotes also include Archaea, so “no nucleus” does not mean “Bacteria only”. Fungal hyphae may be partitioned by septa or contain multiple nuclei in longer continuous regions. The schematic retains comparison clues without asserting identical compartments. A scientific model should state what it leaves out.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

同学递来一张无比例尺的手机照片，问“这是不是致病细菌”。列出两条还需要的信息，并解释为何只放大像素不能补回真实结构。

### English

A classmate presents a phone image with no scale and asks whether it shows a pathogenic bacterium. Name two missing kinds of information and explain why enlarging pixels does not recover unresolved structures.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

用这句表达证据：Yeast is unicellular, but it is a fungus. Bacteria have DNA without a membrane-bound nucleus. 将but用于反例，将without用于结构比较。

### English

Practise: “Yeast is unicellular, but it is a fungus.” “Bacteria have DNA without a membrane-bound nucleus.” Use but for a counterexample and without for a structural contrast.

## Check / 挑战自己

### English

The four questions examine DNA/nuclei, yeast/mould, limits of unknown-image evidence and hypha. For identification mistakes, revisit the difference between pre-labelled and unknown samples.
