## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

如果身体并非无菌，我们为什么不会在每次接触微生物后都感染？不能封起来的呼吸道又怎样保护表面？

### English

If the body is not sterile, why does each microbial contact not cause infection? How can open airways protect their surfaces?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

预测皮肤、黏液/纤毛和胃酸是不是以同一种方式发挥作用。哪一种主要依靠连续结构，哪一种推动颗粒，哪一种改变化学环境？

### English

Predict whether skin, mucus/cilia and stomach acid work in the same way. Which uses a continuous structure, which moves particles and which changes chemical conditions?

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

皮肤用相邻表层块表示连续结构；呼吸道图的蓝层是黏液，小线是纤毛；胃图中的点表示酸性环境线索，不是酸分子的真实数量。

### English

Skin tiles represent a continuous outer layer. Blue airway material is mucus and small lines are cilia. Stomach dots indicate acidic conditions, not counted acid molecules.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**防线首先改变进入机会。** 皮肤表层连续，能限制许多外来因子进入内部。皮肤上仍可有常居微生物，所以“有屏障”与“表面完全无菌”不是一回事。破口可能改变进入机会，但不等于已经发生感染。

**呼吸道用捕获加运输来清理。** 黏液可黏附某些颗粒，纤毛摆动推动黏液，使被捕获的物质更容易离开相关表面。不要把纤毛当成会主动辨认所有敌人的小手，也不要把黏住等同于已经杀死。捕获、移动与杀灭是不同机制。

**胃提供化学条件。** 酸性环境能抑制许多病原体，但不同因子耐受能力不同，不能说“经过胃就全部死亡”。身体还有多种先天防御以及后续免疫反应，多层机制一起工作，而不是一面绝对可靠的墙。

**迁移到新解释。** 若图中显示纤毛运动减弱，合理推断是黏液清除过程可能受影响；不能直接由动画算出病原体数量或诊断某人。要先指出机制，再保留未测结果的边界。

**检验一条解释。** “胃酸把黏液推出呼吸道”把两个部位的机制拼错了。纠正时应同时指出部位、结构或条件和作用：呼吸道纤毛推动黏液，胃的酸性条件抑制许多病原体。只背“保护身体”还不能解释它们怎样工作。

### English

**A barrier first changes entry opportunities.** The continuous outer skin limits many agents from reaching internal tissues. Resident microbes can still occur on the surface: a barrier is not a sterile exterior. A break can alter access without proving infection.

**Airways combine trapping and transport.** Mucus can trap particles, and ciliary movement transports mucus away from relevant surfaces. Cilia are not little hands identifying every enemy. Trapping, movement and killing are separate processes.

**The stomach supplies chemical conditions.** Acidity inhibits many pathogens, but tolerance varies. Passage through the stomach does not mean every agent is destroyed. Other innate defences and later immune responses provide additional layers rather than one perfectly reliable wall.

**Explain a new case.** Weaker ciliary movement could affect mucus clearance. An animation alone cannot establish pathogen counts or diagnose a person. Identify the mechanism first, then state which outcomes were not measured.

**Test an explanation.** “Stomach acid pushes mucus out of the airway” combines mechanisms from different sites. Correct it by naming the site, structure or condition and action: airway cilia move mucus, whereas acidic stomach conditions inhibit many pathogens. “Protects the body” alone does not explain how.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

误区：“第一道防线一旦失效就必然患病。”反例：身体还有其他防护层；进入机会、感染建立与症状是不同证据层次。反过来，存在防线也不保证任何情境都安全。

### English

Misconception: a first-barrier failure guarantees illness. Further defence layers exist, and entry opportunity, established infection and symptoms require different evidence. Having a barrier likewise guarantees no universal safety outcome.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

“先天”不表示只在出生时工作，也不表示每种反应完全没有区别。这个学段先认识快速、较广泛的防御；后续再比较特异性受体、免疫记忆与疫苗。此处避免把皮肤、炎症、抗体都称为同一种屏障。

### English

Innate does not mean active only at birth or identical in every detail. This stage introduces relatively rapid, broad defence; later lessons compare specific recognition, immune memory and vaccination. Skin, inflammation and antibodies should not all be called the same barrier.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

把课内三张屏障图各配一句“因为……所以可能……”。不需检查、拍摄或比较任何人的皮肤、体液或身体状态。

### English

Match each lesson barrier diagram to a “because … therefore it can …” explanation. No inspection, photographs or comparison of anyone’s skin, fluids or body condition are required.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

### English

Practise: “Mucus traps particles.” “Cilia move mucus.” “Stomach acid inhibits many pathogens.” Many is not all; helps is not guarantees.
