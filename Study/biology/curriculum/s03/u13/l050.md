## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

面包里的气孔和森林里的落叶减少，都可能与微生物有关。为什么它们不是一群只会制造麻烦的小东西？

### English

Bread pores and disappearing forest litter can both involve microbes. What do these observations reveal beyond a story of tiny troublemakers?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先猜面团气体来自糖本身、酵母细胞里的过程，还是新制造的空气；再预测热灭活酵母对照与活酵母有何差别。

### English

Predict whether dough gas comes from sugar alone, a cellular yeast process or newly created air. Compare predictions for live and heat-inactivated yeast.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

图中糖进入酵母利用的路线，另一侧标出CO₂和乙醇。糖、气体和酵母的图形都不按比例；箭头不是反应式的精确物质数量。

### English

The diagram connects available sugar to yeast activity and labels CO₂ and ethanol products. Shapes and arrows are not scaled sizes or exact reaction amounts.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

保持水、适温与本例选定的低氧条件不变，分别改变糖可用性和酵母是否可存活，逐步看产物。随后比较面团、落叶、已知致病与未知斑点四张卡，根据作用证据分类。

### English

Keep water, suitable temperature and the low-oxygen conditions selected for this example fixed. Change sugar availability or yeast viability separately, then inspect products. Classify dough, litter, established disease and unknown-spot cards by effect evidence.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**角色来自作用，不来自标签。** 酵母是真菌，不是细菌。已知面包酵母能在适当条件下利用糖；本模型选择低氧发酵情境，示意CO₂与乙醇产生，气体可以使面团膨起。发酵本身不需要氧参与；真实酵母也可进行需氧呼吸，某些条件下有氧时也可发酵。本活动固定一种发酵情境，不计算途径选择，不能把产气一概等同于发酵。发酵支持细胞取得可用能量，和植物用光能制造糖不是同一过程。模型固定了水与适温等条件，不表示“随便加糖就一定成功”。

**用对照追问原因。** 先只移除可用糖，其他模型条件相同；再恢复糖并换热灭活酵母。两种缺条件情境都不画新的发酵产物。它们帮助说明底物与细胞活动的重要性，不能给出真实气体量、反应速率或面包质量。真实实验还需重复、测量和明确对照。

**分解者把物质接回生态系统。** 真菌和某些细菌可分解死有机物，从中取得物质和能量，矿质元素因而有机会重新被植物利用。能量不会循环成新阳光，而会在过程中逐步散为热。细菌也参与食品加工与其他生态过程，不能把“微生物”与“病原体”画等号。

**解释一个新情境。** 面包师使用已知酵母，不代表桌上的未知霉斑也能食用。角色与安全判断需要身份、作用、条件及可靠记录；信息不足时保留未知，是更有证据的答案。

### English

**A role follows effects and context.** Yeast is fungal, not bacterial. Known baking yeast can use sugars under appropriate conditions. This model selects low-oxygen fermentation, showing CO₂ and ethanol; the gas can expand dough. Fermentation itself does not require oxygen. Real yeast can also respire aerobically and can ferment in oxygenated conditions under some circumstances. This activity fixes one fermentation case rather than calculating pathway choice; gas release alone does not identify fermentation. Fermentation supports cellular energy use and is different from photosynthetic sugar production. Water and suitable temperature are assumed, so adding sugar does not guarantee success.

**Use controls to ask why.** First remove available sugar while retaining other model conditions. Restore sugar and choose heat-inactivated yeast. Neither missing-condition case produces new fermentation products here. The comparison highlights substrate and cell activity, without predicting gas volume, rate or bread quality. A real experiment also needs measurements and repetition.

**Connect decomposers to ecosystems.** Fungi and some bacteria use dead organic materials, helping mineral elements become available again. Energy is gradually dissipated as heat rather than recycled into sunlight. Microbes therefore have varied roles rather than all being pathogens.

**Transfer the reasoning.** A baker’s use of known yeast does not establish that an unknown mould spot is edible. Identity, effects, conditions and reliable records matter. Keeping an unknown classification can be the best-supported judgement.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

误区：“有益菌永远有益，真菌都能吃。”同一生物的影响与所在位置和条件有关；已确认用途的物种也不能为所有未知样本背书。不要用培养、闻或品尝未知斑点来完成本课。

### English

Misconception: a helpful microbe is always helpful, or every fungus is edible. Effects depend on conditions and location; a documented use does not establish safety for unknown samples. No culturing, smelling or tasting unknown spots is needed.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

将“菌种身份”“反应条件”“产物证据”“人类用途”分成四层。例如观察气泡只支持产气，不能直接证明气体全是CO₂，更不能证明食品安全。更高阶段会学习酶、细胞代谢和定量对照；此处先练习让结论不超出证据。

### English

Separate organism identity, process conditions, product evidence and human use. Bubbles establish gas release, not automatically pure CO₂ or food safety. Later lessons add enzymes, metabolism and quantitative controls; here the key habit is keeping conclusions within evidence.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

阅读已有食品包装或课程卡，不需制作食品。找出一个微生物用途，再说明包装不能告诉你的两件事，例如细胞结构或实时反应速率。

### English

Use an existing package or lesson card without preparing food. Identify one microbial use and two things the label cannot establish, such as cell structure or the current reaction rate.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

试说：The role depends on the evidence and the context. Live yeast can ferment sugar under suitable conditions. 用can避免把有条件能力说成每次必然发生。

### English

Practise: “The role depends on the evidence and the context.” “Live yeast can ferment sugar under suitable conditions.” Can expresses a conditional capacity rather than an inevitable result.

## Check / 挑战自己

### English

Check CO₂’s role, materials versus energy, missing evidence on the unknown card, and decomposer. When correcting, explain why a tempting generalisation went beyond the evidence.
