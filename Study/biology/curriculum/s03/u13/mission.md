# U13 · 微生物角色证据展 / Microbial role evidence exhibit

## 中文

只使用课程四张虚拟证据卡：已知面包酵母、落叶分解真菌、已确认致病作用的细菌、身份未知的彩色斑点。将它们分别归入“给定情境有帮助 / 给定情境有危害 / 证据不足”，每张引用一条作用证据。未知不是错误答案，不以颜色或大小代替身份与功能证据。

在三栏手帐中提交：

1. **主张**：列出四个角色判断，写出“不是所有微生物都会致病”的准确版本。
2. **证据**：引用卡片记录，再加活酵母/热灭活或有糖/无糖的一组模型对照；明确固定了水、适温与发酵条件。
3. **推理**：解释条件如何支持观察结果、为什么未知斑点不能据此食用，并连接身体的一种屏障。补一句英文和一个模型不能回答的问题。

可完全在屏幕中完成；不培养、闻嗅或品尝未知样本，不记录他人健康信息。

## English

Use only the four fictional lesson cards: known baking yeast, leaf-litter decomposer fungus, a bacterium with established disease evidence, and an unidentified coloured spot. Classify each as helpful in this context, harmful in this context, or insufficient evidence, citing one effect statement each. Unknown is a valid conclusion; colour and size do not establish identity or function.

Submit in the three notebook fields:

1. **Claim**: list the four role judgements and accurately correct “all microbes cause disease”.
2. **Evidence**: cite card records and one yeast-viability or sugar-availability comparison; state the fixed water, temperature and fermentation assumptions.
3. **Reasoning**: connect conditions to the model result, explain why the unknown spot is not established as edible, and link one body barrier. Add an English sentence and a question the model cannot answer.

The screen-only version is complete. No culturing, smelling or tasting unknown samples, or recording other people’s health information.
