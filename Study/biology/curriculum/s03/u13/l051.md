## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

虚构来源连到表面，再连到手与身体入口。只要箭头接通，就能说某个人已经生病了吗？

### English

A fictional source connects to a surface, a hand and an entry site. Does a complete arrow chain establish that someone is ill?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先预测移除表面→手连接会改变哪些路径。共同空气途径会随之自动消失吗？

### English

Predict which routes change when the surface-to-hand link is removed. Would a shared-air route automatically disappear too?

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

点与线代表来源、环境环节和入口，不代表真实同学或确诊者。接触链与共同空气链各自列出；本图没有人数、剂量或概率轴。

### English

Nodes and links represent sources, environmental steps and entry, not actual classmates or diagnoses. Contact and shared-air routes are separate; no dose, probability or case-count scale is shown.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

在两种路线间切换。恢复或移除接触链的一条连接，逐步追踪能够到达的节点。用“这条路径”而不是“所有风险”描述结果；理想化移除不等于真实卫生措施达到100%清除。

### English

Switch routes, restoring or removing one contact link. Step through reachable nodes. Describe this path rather than all risks; idealised link removal does not imply perfect real-world hygiene.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**先把三个问题分开。** 病原体描述相关感染因子在宿主中造成疾病的能力；传播描述它通过某条路线转移；感染涉及进入宿主并建立活动。一次接触不等于感染，感染也不一定立即产生明显症状。许多微生物并不致病，不能看到“微生物”就给人贴上患病标签。

**从来源追到入口。** 物体表面可成为某些感染因子的中间环节；手再接触适合的入口可能形成转移机会。另一些因子可通过共同空气传播。不是每种病原体都适用所有路径，具体情况需要可靠资料，不能从一张连线图猜出是哪种病。

**改变一个连接，只证明相应范围内的改变。** 在理想化接触模型中移除表面→手连接，后续触摸链不再接通；切换到共同空气，另一条路线仍在。这个对照既不证明“风险已经归零”，也不证明“干预完全没用”。真实效果还有剂量、时间、环境、病原体与宿主条件。

**把推理用在新情况。** 如果报告只写“清洁后表面指标降低”，可以说测到的表面指标变了，却不能直接推断每个人的感染结果。要回答后者，需要与问题相配的研究证据，而不是更粗的箭头。

### English

**Separate three questions.** A pathogen has disease-causing potential in a relevant host. Transmission concerns movement through a route. Infection involves entry and establishment in the host. Contact is not infection, and infection need not immediately produce obvious symptoms. Microbial presence alone does not justify labelling a person ill.

**Trace source to entry.** A surface can be an intermediate step for some agents, followed by a hand and a suitable entry site. Shared air provides a different possible route for other agents. Not every pathogen uses every route, and a line diagram cannot identify a disease.

**Keep the intervention’s scope.** Removing the surface-to-hand link interrupts this idealised contact chain. The shared-air path remains when selected. That establishes neither zero risk nor a useless intervention. Real outcomes also depend on agent, dose, timing, environment and host conditions.

**Apply the reasoning.** A report of a lower surface indicator supports a change in that measured indicator. It does not directly establish every person’s infection outcome. That question needs appropriately designed evidence, not merely a more dramatic arrow.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

误区：“传得过去就一定感染。”反例：路径图没有给出感染剂量、宿主状态或成功建立感染的证据。另一误区是把同一套路线套给全部疾病；不同感染因子的途径可不同。

### English

Misconception: a possible transfer guarantees infection. The diagram supplies neither an infectious dose nor host or establishment evidence. Different infectious agents also need not share the same routes.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

网络模型的节点和边是研究者的选择。删掉一个边只在给定模型中改变可达性；现实里还可能存在未画出的路线、重复接触和不同时序。读公共资料时，留意它研究的是表面指标、接触次数、感染还是症状，避免把不同指标偷换。

### English

A network’s nodes and edges are modelling choices. Removing an edge changes reachability within the chosen model; omitted routes, repeated contacts and timing may matter in reality. When reading reports, distinguish surface indicators, contacts, infections and symptoms rather than substituting one outcome for another.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

用课内虚构图即可，不调查或公布同学健康信息。写一句针对某一路径的解释，再写一句“本图不能告诉我……”。

### English

Use the fictional diagram without collecting or sharing classmates’ health information. Write one route-specific explanation and one sentence beginning “This diagram cannot tell me …”.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

练习区分：Transmission describes a route. Infection is not the same as contact. A connected path is an opportunity, not a diagnosis.

### English

Practise the distinctions: “Transmission describes a route.” “Infection is not the same as contact.” “A connected path is an opportunity, not a diagnosis.”

## Check / 挑战自己

### 中文

四题检查路径含义、病原体与微生物、单一干预的结论范围和transmission。推理题要同时说明哪条链变了、哪条没变。

### English

Four questions check path meaning, pathogens versus microbes, intervention scope and transmission. For the reasoning item, state both which chain changed and which did not.

### English
