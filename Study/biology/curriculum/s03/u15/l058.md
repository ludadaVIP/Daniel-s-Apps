## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

手碰到书本，然后决定把它拿起来。知道“碰到了”与让肌肉“动起来”，会不会走同一条单行路线？

### English

Your hand touches a book and you decide to lift it. Do incoming touch information and outgoing movement instructions follow the same one-way route?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选一个解释，不评分。学习后再比较你的初始想法：知道碰到东西就代表肌肉指令已递送；感觉输入与运动输出有方向不同的路线；所有信息只在肌肉里处理。

### English

Choose an initial explanation without a score, then revisit it after learning: Receiving touch information guarantees motor delivery; Sensory input and motor output have different directions; All processing occurs inside muscles.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**输入与输出要分开追踪。** 感受器把刺激转换为信号，感觉神经元把信息传向脑或脊髓等中枢。中枢整合感觉、目标和其他信息，运动神经元再把相应指令传向肌肉。肌肉是执行动作的效应器，而不是所有信息的总指挥。脑和脊髓都参与整合，具体路线取决于活动。

**神经元不是中空的电线。** 信号传播依赖细胞膜上的电位变化，许多神经元间的突触通过释放化学信使影响下一细胞。动作电位并不是一个小物体在管道内跑。真实路线包含很多细胞和突触，本图每站只代替一个功能环节，没有计算传导时间。

**同一观察可能需要两条证据链。** 虚构模型中关闭运动连接时，感觉信息仍可到达中枢，但图中没有肌肉指令递送。因此“有输入”和“有输出”不能互相替代。真实个体有其他通路、肌肉状态和多种调节，不能把这个简图当作诊断某种损伤或预测恢复的依据。

### English

**Trace input and output separately.** Receptors transduce stimuli, and sensory neurons carry information towards the brain or spinal cord. The CNS integrates sensory input, goals and other information. Motor neurons then carry relevant commands towards muscle. Muscle is an effector that performs a response, not the controller of every message. Both brain and spinal cord integrate information; the detailed pathway depends on the activity.

**A neuron is not a hollow electrical wire.** Propagation depends on electrical changes across the cell membrane. At many synapses, released chemical messengers affect the next cell. An action potential is not a little object moving through a pipe. Real pathways contain many cells and junctions; each diagram station represents a functional role, without calculating conduction time.

**Input and output require different evidence.** Switching off the fictional motor link still lets information reach the CNS, but the diagram shows no command delivered to muscle. Evidence of input cannot substitute for evidence of output. Real bodies have other pathways, muscle conditions and regulatory processes. This schematic cannot diagnose an injury or predict recovery.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：感觉神经元和运动神经元只是同一条线上随意换的名称。反例：名称强调相对中枢的传递方向；把运动连接关闭，图中输入仍能到中枢。

### English

Misconception: sensory and motor neurons are interchangeable labels on one wire. Counterexample: the names describe direction relative to the CNS; switching off the motor link leaves the model input able to reach the CNS.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

神经元的树突、细胞体、轴突适合不同的信息处理与传递功能，但真实结构有多样性。兴奋与抑制输入会共同影响是否产生动作电位；有髓鞘的轴突可采用跳跃式传导。不能用图上“线更短”就算出真实反应速度，也不能把所有突触说成化学突触，因为还存在电突触。

### English

Dendrites, cell bodies and axons support different processing and transmission roles, with real structural diversity. Excitatory and inhibitory inputs influence action-potential generation; myelinated axons can use saltatory conduction. Shorter drawn lines do not calculate real reaction speeds. Not every synapse is chemical: electrical synapses also exist.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **神经元 / neuron**：接收、处理和递送信息的神经细胞。
- **感觉神经元 / sensory neuron**：将信息传向中枢神经系统的神经元。
- **运动神经元 / motor neuron**：将信号传向肌肉等效应器的神经元。
- **中枢神经系统 / central nervous system**：由脑和脊髓构成，参与信息整合。
- **突触 / synapse**：神经细胞与另一细胞递送信息的连接部位。
- **效应器 / effector**：产生响应的肌肉或腺体等结构。

### English

- **neuron / 神经元**: A nerve cell that receives, processes and transmits information.
- **sensory neuron / 感觉神经元**: A neuron carrying information towards the central nervous system.
- **motor neuron / 运动神经元**: A neuron carrying signals towards effectors such as muscles.
- **central nervous system / 中枢神经系统**: The brain and spinal cord, involved in information integration.
- **synapse / 突触**: A junction where a neuron communicates with another cell.
- **effector / 效应器**: A structure such as muscle or gland that produces a response.
