## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

血液会经过许多细胞。为什么同一种激素能改变某些细胞的活动，却不会让所有细胞做完全相同的事？

### English

Blood passes many cells. Why can one hormone change some cells without making every cell do exactly the same thing?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选一个解释，不评分。学习后再比较你的初始想法：血流经过的所有细胞都同样响应；合适受体与细胞内过程共同决定响应；激素是细胞活动的主要食物。

### English

Choose an initial explanation without a score, then revisit it after learning: Every cell contacted by blood responds identically; Suitable receptors and intracellular processes determine response; Hormones are the main food for cell activities.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

左侧分泌细胞、中间血液、右侧细胞受体构成信息路线。A/B只是虚构标签，不对应具体药物、身体状况或剂量。

### English

Secretory cells, blood and cell receptors form an information route. A/B are fictional labels, not specific medicines, conditions or doses.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

先选信使A，分别配A受体与B受体，逐步比较血液到达和细胞响应。再选同时有A/B受体的细胞，测试两种信使；控制其他设置不变。

### English

Choose A, compare cells with A versus B receptors and step through arrival and response. Then try a cell with both receptors and test both messengers, holding other settings fixed.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**血液负责递送，受体帮助识别。** 内分泌细胞释放激素，激素经内环境进入血液后可经过很多组织。具有相应受体的细胞才具备响应这种信号的条件。信使“到过某处”与“该处发生了某种响应”是两件事，本模型用A/B配对把它们分开。

**激素发信息，细胞完成工作。** 激素与受体结合后影响细胞内通路，改变已有蛋白质的活动或基因表达等。细胞仍需物质、能量和合适条件；激素不是替代葡萄糖的食物，也不是把器官像玩具一样直接推动。一个细胞可以有多种受体，同一激素在不同细胞也可产生不同作用。

**比较递送方式比背“快慢”更有用。** 神经信号沿特定神经网络递送，许多突触局部释放递质；内分泌信使经血液较广泛分布，但依靠受体选择靶细胞。神经响应常很快，许多激素作用较持久，不过某些激素也能迅速改变活动，不能规定任何激素永远慢。若两组虚构细胞都接触A，只有A受体组响应，结果支持受体在本条件中的作用，而非证明真实细胞只有一种决定因素。

### English

**Blood delivers; receptors help recognise.** Endocrine cells release hormones that enter blood through internal fluids and can pass many tissues. Suitable receptors give cells the capacity to respond to a particular signal. A messenger arriving somewhere and a particular response occurring there are different claims; fictional A/B matching separates them.

**Hormones convey information; cells do the work.** Receptor binding affects intracellular pathways, changing existing protein activity or gene expression, among other effects. Cells still need matter, energy and suitable conditions. A hormone is not food replacing glucose, nor does it directly push an organ like a toy. One cell can carry several receptor types, and a single hormone can have different actions in different cells.

**Compare routes rather than memorising an absolute speed rule.** Neural communication uses particular networks and often local neurotransmitter release at synapses. Endocrine messengers circulate more widely but target through receptors. Neural responses are often rapid and many hormonal effects longer-lasting, yet some hormones act rapidly. If both model cell groups encounter A but only the A-receptor group responds, this supports a receptor role under those conditions, not a claim that real responses have only one determining factor.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：激素随血液到处走，所以每个细胞都受同样影响。反例：A信使经过A受体与B受体细胞，本图只有匹配受体显示所选响应；同时有两种受体的细胞又可响应两者。

### English

Misconception: a circulating hormone affects every cell identically. Counterexample: A passes cells with A and B receptors, but only the matching set shows the selected response; a cell with both receptor types can respond to either.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

受体可位于细胞表面，也可在细胞内，取决于激素等分子的性质。配对常被比作“钥匙与锁”，但真实结合是动态分子相互作用，受体数量、信使浓度、细胞内通路与反馈都会影响敏感性和作用。因此本图的开/关响应只是入门比较，不是药物效应模型。

### English

Receptors can lie at the cell surface or inside cells, depending on properties of the hormone and receptor system. A lock-and-key analogy is useful but real binding is dynamic; receptor abundance, messenger concentration, intracellular pathways and feedback affect sensitivity. The model’s on/off response is an introductory comparison, not a drug-effect model.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

两个虚构细胞都接触信使A，甲响应而乙不响应。提出一个受体相关解释，并写出还可能需要检查的细胞内条件。

### English

Two fictional cells both encounter A; one responds and the other does not. Suggest a receptor-related explanation and an intracellular condition still worth checking.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **激素 / hormone**：由细胞释放、在较远靶细胞等处传递调节信息的化学信使。
- **内分泌 / endocrine signalling**：化学信使经内环境和血液到达较远靶细胞的信号方式。
- **靶细胞 / target cell**：具有适合受体并可产生相应响应的细胞。
- **受体 / receptor**：能够识别特定信号的细胞分子结构。
- **特异性 / specificity**：信号与受体等之间有选择的匹配关系。
- **化学信使 / chemical messenger**：以分子形式在细胞间传递信息的物质。

### English

- **hormone / 激素**: A chemical messenger released by cells to regulate targets, often at a distance.
- **endocrine signalling / 内分泌**: Signalling in which messengers travel through internal fluids and blood to distant targets.
- **target cell / 靶细胞**: A cell with suitable receptors and the capacity for a corresponding response.
- **receptor / 受体**: A cellular molecular structure able to recognise a particular signal.
- **specificity / 特异性**: Selective matching between signals and receptors or other structures.
- **chemical messenger / 化学信使**: A molecule conveying information between cells.
