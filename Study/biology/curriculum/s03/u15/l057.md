## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

看见一只鸟、听见它鸣叫、感觉羽毛轻触皮肤，三条消息怎样变成大脑可以处理的信息？神经里会不会真的跑着一幅鸟的照片？

### English

Seeing a bird, hearing its song and feeling a feather touch skin begin in different ways. How do they become information the brain can process? Does a tiny bird photograph travel through a nerve?

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**外界的物理变化不是神经里的内容本身。** 光是电磁辐射，声音是介质中的振动，轻压会使皮肤中的结构形变。视网膜的感光细胞、内耳毛细胞及皮肤内的机械感受器分别具有适合这些作用的结构。刺激引发细胞状态改变，形成可影响神经活动的信号，这个过程叫感觉转换。

**传到脑的是神经信息，不是原物体。** 神经元膜上的电活动及神经元之间的信号递送，使信息沿特定路线进入中枢。脑结合路线、活动方式及已有经验进行解释。因此“检测到了刺激”与“知道那是什么”不是同一个步骤。人也有身体位置、平衡等感觉，并非只有常说的五种。

**用新情境检验解释。** 在昏暗房间里，你可能先听见鸟叫却看不清鸟。可用解释是声音通道获得了可用信息，而视觉线索不足；不能据此说鸟没有颜色或眼睛完全没工作。要判断具体情况，还需要光照、遮挡和多个感官线索。模型中的配对只验证典型通道，不能测量真实阈值或排除器官的其他感受器。

### English

**Physical changes are not the contents of nerves.** Light is electromagnetic radiation, sound is vibration in a medium, and gentle pressure deforms structures in skin. Retinal photoreceptors, inner-ear hair cells and skin mechanoreceptors have structures suited to these effects. A stimulus changes a receptor cell, generating a signal that can influence neural activity: sensory transduction.

**Neural information reaches the brain, not the original object.** Electrical activity in neuronal membranes and communication between cells carry information along particular pathways. The brain interprets that activity using its source, pattern and experience. Detecting a stimulus and recognising its cause are separate steps. Humans also sense balance and body position; the familiar five senses are not the complete list.

**Test the explanation in a new setting.** In a dim room you might hear a bird without seeing it clearly. Auditory information may be usable while visual evidence is insufficient. This does not show that the bird lacks colour or that the eyes have stopped working. Light, obstruction and other sensory evidence would matter. Model matching illustrates representative channels; it does not measure thresholds or exclude every other receptor in an organ.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：神经像电线一样运送声音本身。反例：耳蜗中的机械作用先改变感受细胞，再影响神经活动；声音振动并未原样沿听神经传播。

### English

Misconception: a nerve carries sound itself like a wire. Counterexample: mechanical effects in the cochlea first change receptor cells and then neural activity; the original vibration does not travel unchanged along the auditory nerve.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

同一种刺激的强弱不一定靠“每个神经信号变大”编码。许多神经元的动作电位具有近似全或无特性，刺激强度可与放电频率、募集的细胞数及时间模式有关。感受器还可能适应持续刺激。模型没有这些机制，所以“未显示”不能解释为真实反应必定为零。

### English

Stimulus strength is not necessarily encoded by making each nerve impulse larger. Many neurons have approximately all-or-none action potentials; strength can be represented by firing frequency, recruited cells and timing patterns. Receptors may also adapt to sustained stimuli. These processes are outside the model, so an absent displayed response does not prove zero real activity.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### English

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **刺激 / stimulus**：能引起生物响应的内外条件变化。
- **感受器 / receptor**：能够检测特定类型刺激的细胞、结构或神经末梢。
- **感觉转换 / sensory transduction**：把刺激的作用转换为神经系统可使用的信号。
- **神经信号 / neural signal**：神经细胞产生和传递的信息形式。
- **知觉 / perception**：脑对感觉信息进行整合和解释形成的体验。
- **机械感受器 / mechanoreceptor**：对形变、压力或振动等机械作用敏感的感受器。

### English

- **stimulus / 刺激**: A change in internal or external conditions that can elicit a response.
- **receptor / 感受器**: A cell, structure or nerve ending able to detect particular kinds of stimuli.
- **sensory transduction / 感觉转换**: Conversion of a stimulus into a signal usable by the nervous system.
- **neural signal / 神经信号**: Information generated and transmitted by nerve cells.
- **perception / 知觉**: Experience arising from brain integration and interpretation of sensory information.
- **mechanoreceptor / 机械感受器**: A receptor sensitive to mechanical effects such as deformation, pressure or vibration.
