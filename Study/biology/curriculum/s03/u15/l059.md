## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

故事中的人物还没来得及认真想“该缩手”，手已开始避开有害刺激。脊髓在做什么，脑是否完全被排除？只操作模型，不做疼痛或热物实验。

### English

A story character starts withdrawing a hand before consciously deciding what to do. What is the spinal cord doing, and is the brain excluded? Use the model only; no pain or hot-object experiment.

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选一个解释，不评分。学习后再比较你的初始想法：任何反射都完全不让脑知道；脊髓可组织缩手，脑也会收到信息；运动神经元先检测皮肤刺激。

### English

Choose an initial explanation without a score, then revisit it after learning: No reflex ever informs the brain; Spinal circuits can organise withdrawal while the brain also receives information; Motor neurons detect skin stimuli first.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

数字1–5为感受器、感觉神经元、脊髓中间神经元、运动神经元、肌肉。金色虚线是通向脑的信息分支，不是“缩手后才开始”的时间表。

### English

Numbers 1–5 represent receptor, sensory neuron, spinal interneuron, motor neuron and muscle. The gold dashed brain branch is informational, not a timetable that starts only after withdrawal.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**快速的关键是有合适的局部回路。** 在本例缩手反射中，皮肤内能检测有害刺激的感受器影响感觉神经元，信息进入脊髓，经过中间神经元影响运动神经元，再使相应肌肉收缩。真实缩手还需要协调多块肌肉，本图只保留一条主要兴奋路线，省略对拮抗肌的抑制等连接。

**不先等待有意识决定，不等于脑不知道。** 脊髓回路可以组织动作，相关信息也沿上行路线到脑形成知觉；脑还可调节脊髓活动。图上各站是功能顺序，不是同样长的时间片，因此不能根据格子数计算毫秒，也不能断言脑信息必须在全部动作结束后才传递。

**不要把一个反射弧当成所有反射的模板。** 缩手反射常含中间神经元及多个突触；某些牵张反射包含感觉神经元对运动神经元的直接连接。共同点是有神经回路和效应器，具体回路并不相同。新情境中若故事只说“动作很快”，仍不足以单凭速度判断它一定是哪一种反射。

### English

**A suitable local circuit enables a prompt response.** In this withdrawal example, receptors detecting a harmful skin stimulus influence sensory neurons. Information enters the spinal cord, passes through interneurons to motor neurons and activates relevant muscle contraction. Real withdrawal coordinates several muscles; this diagram retains one main excitatory route and omits connections such as inhibition of antagonists.

**No prior conscious decision does not mean an uninformed brain.** Spinal circuits can organise a response while related information ascends to the brain for perception. The brain can also modulate spinal activity. Stations show functional order, not equal time slices, so their number cannot calculate milliseconds. Nor does the diagram imply that information starts reaching the brain only after the whole movement ends.

**One reflex arc is not a template for every reflex.** Withdrawal commonly involves interneurons and several synapses; some stretch-reflex pathways include a direct sensory-to-motor connection. Neural circuits and effectors are shared principles, but detailed wiring varies. In a new story, “the movement was fast” alone is insufficient to identify a particular reflex.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：反射一定不经过任何中枢神经结构。反例：本例在脊髓整合，而脊髓本身就是中枢神经系统的一部分；通向脑的分支也保留。

### English

Misconception: reflexes never involve the CNS. Counterexample: this response is integrated in the spinal cord, itself part of the CNS, with information also reaching the brain.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

反射强度不是固定不变的按钮结果。突触数量、神经纤维特性、同时到来的抑制或兴奋输入、脑的调节等都会影响真实响应。反射可以保护身体，也参与姿势与其他调节；“自动”不意味着毫无调节。本课不设计个人反应速度测验。

### English

Reflex strength is not a fixed button outcome. Synapse number, fibre properties, concurrent inhibition or excitation, and descending brain modulation affect real responses. Reflexes contribute to protection, posture and other regulation; automatic does not mean unregulated. This lesson does not provide a personal reaction-time test.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

比较故事A“手在有害刺激后自动回撤”和故事B“想好把书放哪里再移动手”。各写一条可能的整合路线，再指出仅靠文字故事仍不能确定哪些细节。

### English

Compare story A, an automatic withdrawal after a harmful stimulus, with story B, deciding where to put a book before moving. Suggest an integration route for each and state details the stories alone cannot establish.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **反射 / reflex**：由神经回路组织的较自动响应，不需先作有意识决定。
- **反射弧 / reflex arc**：从感受器到神经整合和效应器的功能路线。
- **脊髓 / spinal cord**：中枢神经系统的一部分，可参与反射整合与上下行递送。
- **中间神经元 / interneuron**：连接和整合神经元信息的神经细胞。
- **缩手反射 / withdrawal reflex**：对有害刺激作出回撤动作的反射，本例含多个突触。
- **有意识的决定 / conscious decision**：需要有意识处理和选择的行动决定。

### English

- **reflex / 反射**: A relatively automatic neural response not requiring a prior conscious decision.
- **reflex arc / 反射弧**: A functional route from receptor through neural integration to effector.
- **spinal cord / 脊髓**: Part of the CNS supporting reflex integration and ascending/descending communication.
- **interneuron / 中间神经元**: A neuron linking and integrating information between neurons.
- **withdrawal reflex / 缩手反射**: A withdrawal response to a harmful stimulus; this example is polysynaptic.
- **conscious decision / 有意识的决定**: An action choice involving conscious processing.
