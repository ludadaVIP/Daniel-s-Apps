## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

一条真正稳定的生命曲线，会不会完全没有起伏？让一个虚构内部条件先偏高、再偏低，看看同一个负反馈原则能不能解释两个方向。

### English

Must a stable living system produce a perfectly flat line? Disturb one fictional internal condition upwards and downwards, then ask whether the same negative-feedback principle explains both responses.

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选一个解释，不评分；学习后回看：稳态意味着数值永远精确不变；负反馈只能把数值向下调；负反馈抵消偏离，方向可向上或向下。

### English

Choose an unscored initial explanation and revisit it later: Homeostasis means a value never changes at all; Negative feedback can only lower a value; Negative feedback opposes deviation, upwards or downwards.

### 中文

选择向上扰动，比较反馈连接与关闭时的记录，再用向下扰动重复比较。逐步指出哪点是扰动、哪段是响应；不要把“负”理解为曲线必须向下。

### English

Choose an upward disturbance and compare the feedback-connected and off records, then repeat with a downward disturbance. Identify the disturbance and response; negative does not require a downward curve.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**生命不是把条件冻住。** 细胞反应需要适宜的温度、含水状况和物质供应，外界变化与内部活动又不断造成扰动。稳态依靠持续检测与调节，使条件在适宜范围附近变化，而不是要求每一瞬间都保持完全相同。不同条件有不同范围与调节机制。

**反馈要看响应与最初变化的关系。** 一个条件偏高，响应使它趋向降低；条件偏低，响应使它趋向升高。两者都在抵消最初偏离，所以都叫负反馈。“负”不是不好，也不是一定减少数值。感受、整合和执行可以由不同结构协作，有些细胞还把检测和释放信号结合在一起。

**用记录而不是印象判断。** 模型向上扰动由50到80，连接反馈后依次为69、61、55、53；向下扰动则由50到20，再到31、39、45、47。比较离50的距离，可看到两种响应都减小偏差，且不是立即精确回到50。关闭只代表本图选定调节器缺席，真实身体可能还有其他调节，不能据此推断生命系统必定保持同一偏差。

### English

**Living systems do not freeze conditions.** Cellular reactions need suitable temperature, water conditions and supplies, while external change and internal activity continually disturb them. Homeostasis uses ongoing detection and regulation to keep conditions near suitable ranges, rather than identical at every instant. Different variables have different ranges and mechanisms.

**Judge feedback by its relation to the original change.** When a condition is too high, a response tending to lower it opposes the deviation. When it is too low, a response raising it also opposes the deviation. Both are negative feedback. Negative does not mean harmful or always decreasing. Detection, integration and response can be distributed among structures; some cells combine sensing and signal release.

**Use records rather than impressions.** After the arbitrary model rises from 50 to 80, connected feedback gives 69, 61, 55 and 53. A downward disturbance goes from 50 to 20, then 31, 39, 45 and 47. Distances from 50 decrease in both cases, without instantaneous perfect restoration. Switching off refers only to this selected controller. Real systems may have other regulators, so the off comparison does not predict an entire body.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：负反馈就是把数值降低。反例：偏低模型从20向47回升，响应抵消原来的下降，仍是负反馈；判断关键是相对于扰动的方向。

### English

Misconception: negative feedback means lowering a value. Counterexample: the low model rises from 20 towards 47, opposing the original fall. Its direction relative to the disturbance makes it negative feedback.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

真实调节会有延迟、灵敏度、多个反馈环和可变参考状态，有时出现小幅波动或过冲。正反馈则放大最初变化，需要其他停止条件；它可参与生理过程，却不等于让所有内部条件稳定。本模型不模拟正反馈、复杂振荡或临床范围，只训练从证据辨认响应关系。

### English

Real regulation includes delays, sensitivities, interacting loops and changing reference states, sometimes producing fluctuations or overshoot. Positive feedback amplifies an initial change and requires other stopping conditions. It can serve physiological processes but does not generally stabilise every internal variable. This model excludes positive feedback, complex oscillations and clinical ranges; it trains evidence-based identification of response relationships.

### English

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **稳态 / homeostasis**：通过调节把内部条件保持在适宜范围的动态过程。
- **负反馈 / negative feedback**：响应抵消引起它的最初偏离。
- **偏离 / deviation**：某条件相对参考状态或范围的改变。
- **调节中心 / control centre**：整合信息并协调响应的功能环节。
- **效应器 / effector**：执行调节响应的结构，如肌肉或腺体。
- **参考范围 / reference range**：本模型用来比较偏离与恢复的约定范围，不是个人健康阈值。

### English

- **homeostasis / 稳态**: Dynamic regulation maintaining internal conditions within suitable ranges.
- **negative feedback / 负反馈**: A response opposes the original deviation that triggered it.
- **deviation / 偏离**: A departure from a reference condition or range.
- **control centre / 调节中心**: A functional component integrating information and coordinating a response.
- **effector / 效应器**: A structure performing a regulatory response, such as muscle or gland.
- **reference range / 参考范围**: An agreed model range for comparing deviations and recovery, not a personal health threshold.

### English
