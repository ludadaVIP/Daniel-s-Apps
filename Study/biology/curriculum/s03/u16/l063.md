## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

同一套调节系统面对“相对偏高”和“相对偏低”两种情况，为什么需要不同信号？胰岛素是不是把糖直接变成能量的工具？

### English

Why does one regulatory system use different signals for relatively high and low glucose? Is insulin itself a tool that directly turns sugar into energy?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选一个解释，不评分；学习后回看：任何血糖变化都由同一种激素向下调；偏高与偏低可用不同信号协调相反方向；胰岛素本身就是细胞呼吸释放的能量。

### English

Choose an unscored initial explanation and revisit it later: One hormone lowers glucose after every change; Different signals coordinate opposing responses to high and low states; Insulin itself is energy released by cellular respiration.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**葡萄糖是物质，激素是调节信息。** 血液把葡萄糖送到组织，细胞可将其用于代谢，也可在合适条件下储存。葡萄糖来自吸收及体内储备等过程。激素协调这些过程，不是能量本身，也不直接充当把葡萄糖“剪开”的消化酶。

**相对偏高时，β细胞释放胰岛素。** 胰岛素促进一些组织的葡萄糖摄取，并影响肝、肌肉等的储存与代谢，使血糖趋向回落。并非所有细胞的葡萄糖摄取都用同一种胰岛素依赖机制，更不是要求把血液中的葡萄糖全清空。糖原是可供调用的储备，而不是激素本身。

**相对偏低时，α细胞释放胰高血糖素。** 它主要通过肝等靶组织的代谢作用，促进糖原分解及产糖过程，使葡萄糖释放到血液，趋向抵消下降。本图突出肝糖原路线，假设有可用储备。两条回调都抵消偏离，符合负反馈；但真实水平还受多种激素、组织活动和条件影响。只凭模型方向不能诊断个体，也不能制定饮食、运动或用药方案。

### English

**Glucose is matter; hormones carry regulatory information.** Blood supplies glucose to tissues, which can use it in metabolism or store it under suitable conditions. Glucose comes from absorption and internal stores, among other processes. Hormones coordinate those processes; they are neither energy itself nor digestive enzymes directly cutting glucose apart.

**Relatively high glucose stimulates beta-cell insulin release.** Insulin promotes glucose uptake in some tissues and affects storage and metabolism in liver, muscle and other targets, tending to lower blood glucose. Not every cell uses the same insulin-dependent uptake mechanism, and the aim is not to remove all circulating glucose. Glycogen is a usable material store, not a hormone.

**Relatively low glucose stimulates alpha-cell glucagon release.** Through targets including the liver, glucagon promotes glycogen breakdown and glucose-producing processes, increasing release to blood and opposing the fall. This diagram highlights glycogen and assumes usable reserves. Both routes oppose deviations and fit negative feedback, but real levels reflect several hormones, tissue activities and conditions. Model directions cannot diagnose an individual or determine diet, exercise or medication.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：胰高血糖素就是“把胰岛素倒着用”。反例：它来自不同胰岛细胞，识别受体并影响肝的代谢；二者不是同一分子向相反方向流动。

### English

Misconception: glucagon is simply insulin running backwards. Counterexample: it comes from a different islet cell type and uses receptors to influence liver metabolism; they are not one molecule flowing in opposite directions.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

胰腺内分泌调节与细胞代谢、膜运输和基因表达相连。肝不仅分解糖原，也可通过糖异生形成葡萄糖；这些过程需要原料和能量。模型只保留两个代表信号，不包含肾上腺素等其他调节，也不把真实血糖看成只由一条固定比例公式决定。

### English

Pancreatic endocrine regulation connects metabolism, membrane transport and gene expression. The liver can form glucose through gluconeogenesis as well as breaking down glycogen, requiring substrates and energy. The model retains two representative signals but excludes other regulators such as adrenaline and does not reduce real glucose control to a fixed-ratio formula.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

### English

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **血糖 / blood glucose**：血液中的葡萄糖，是细胞可利用的一种燃料物质。
- **胰岛 / pancreatic islet**：胰腺中包含多种内分泌细胞的细胞群。
- **胰岛素 / insulin**：由β细胞释放、促进特定组织摄取与储存等的激素。
- **胰高血糖素 / glucagon**：由α细胞释放、促进肝增加葡萄糖释放等的激素。
- **糖原 / glycogen**：由葡萄糖单元组成的储存多糖。
- **负反馈 / negative feedback**：调节响应抵消最初偏离的关系。

### English

- **blood glucose / 血糖**: Glucose in blood, a fuel substance available to cells.
- **pancreatic islet / 胰岛**: A pancreatic cluster containing several endocrine cell types.
- **insulin / 胰岛素**: A beta-cell hormone promoting uptake and storage in particular tissues, among other actions.
- **glucagon / 胰高血糖素**: An alpha-cell hormone promoting increased liver glucose release, among other actions.
- **glycogen / 糖原**: A storage polysaccharide built from glucose units.
- **negative feedback / 负反馈**: Regulation in which a response opposes an initial deviation.
