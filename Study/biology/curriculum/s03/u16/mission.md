# U16 · 有证据的反馈图 / Feedback maps with evidence

## 中文

**驱动问题：为什么“让数值升高”的响应也可能属于负反馈？**

1. 在L061连接反馈，记录向上、向下扰动后第2、3、6点，用与50的距离比较偏差。明确45–55只是一条任意模型范围，不是健康读数。
2. 任选体温、血糖或水分回路，搭建：偏离→检测与整合→信号→效应器→抵消偏离。圈出反馈箭头，说明它返回的是条件改变的影响，不是原激素倒流。
3. 补一组条件对照：如偏热时较干/较湿空气，或较浓/较稀内环境对ADH—重吸收—尿量方向的影响。列出改变和保持的条件。
4. 用三栏手帐提交主张、具体模型记录和推理；加一句英文、一个模型局限、一条还需要的证据。区分模型记录、真实观察与机制解释。

**自检：**是否先判断偏离，再判断响应是否抵消它？是否把保热和产热、信号和葡萄糖、重吸收和尿液分别标清？图是否允许适度波动，而非宣称绝对恒定？无需测个人身体数据、改变饮水、冷热暴露或运动。

## English

**Driving question: why can a response raising a value still be negative feedback?**

1. Connect feedback in L061 and record snapshots 2, 3 and 6 for both disturbance directions. Compare distances from 50; 45–55 is an arbitrary model range, not a health reading.
2. Choose a thermal, glucose or water loop: deviation → detection/integration → signal → effector → opposing deviation. Circle the feedback relation; the changed condition feeds back, not the original hormone flowing backwards.
3. Add a controlled comparison, such as drier/more humid air when warm or concentrated/dilute internal conditions affecting ADH → reabsorption → urine. List changed and held conditions.
4. Submit a claim, specific records and reasoning in the notebook, with an English sentence, model limitation and missing evidence. Separate model records, direct observation and mechanism.

**Self-check:** identify the deviation before judging opposition. Distinguish heat conservation from generation, signals from glucose, and reabsorption from urine. Allow regulated fluctuation rather than perfect constancy. No personal body measurements, changed drinking, heat/cold exposure or exercise are required.
