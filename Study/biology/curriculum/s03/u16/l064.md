## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

肾把更多水重吸收回身体，为什么形成的尿量反而可能减少？保住水与补进水，是不是同一件事？

### English

Why can greater kidney water reabsorption produce less urine? Are conserving water and adding water to the body the same process?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选一个解释，不评分；学习后回看：重吸收越多，尿量一定越多；ADH让肾制造新水；更多水回到血液时，留在尿中的水相对更少。

### English

Choose an unscored initial explanation and revisit it later: More reabsorption necessarily means more urine; ADH makes the kidneys create new water; More water returning to blood leaves relatively less water in urine.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

左侧下丘脑与垂体后叶是产生/释放信号的位置，右侧集合管到血管的蓝箭头是水重吸收。管内向下箭头与相对尿量有关，不能把两条水路混淆。

### English

Left-side hypothalamus and posterior pituitary locate signal production/release. Blue arrows from collecting duct towards a blood vessel show water reabsorption. The downward tubular arrow concerns relative urine output; these water routes differ.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

选择内环境溶质相对较浓或较稀，逐步追踪信号到肾与水返回血液。比较两种状态都存在重吸收，但相对程度不同；其他条件固定，不计算体重、喝水量或实际尿量。

### English

Choose relatively concentrated/dilute internal solutes and step through signalling and water returning towards blood. Both states retain reabsorption at different relative levels. Other conditions are fixed; body mass, drinking volume and actual urine are not calculated.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**水影响内环境浓度，肾并非只做过滤。** 血液经肾小球过滤后，管液中许多有用物质和水会被重吸收，返回内环境与血液；留下的一部分水与溶质形成尿液。把“更多重吸收”理解为“更多尿”，是把返回路线和排出路线弄反了。

**相对较浓时，ADH促进保水路线。** 固定溶质负荷等条件，水相对不足可使内环境渗透浓度升高。下丘脑的相关细胞检测变化；ADH由下丘脑产生，经神经元运到垂体后叶储存和释放，随血液到肾。它提高集合管等部位的水通透性，在合适渗透梯度下使更多水重吸收，尿量相对少、浓度相对高。ADH减少时，该部位水通透性相对低，较多水留在尿中，但基础重吸收并未消失。

**保住水不等于补进水。** 口渴可作为相关支路促使摄入，肾通过减少水丢失帮助调节，却不会制造新的水；代谢产水等也属于完整水账本。真实调节还涉及血容量、盐、其他激素与环境。本模型只比较两种条件下的相对趋势，不能据此决定个人喝多少水或用尿液外观判断健康。

### English

**Water affects internal concentration, and kidneys do more than filter.** After glomerular filtration, much useful material and water in tubular fluid are reabsorbed towards internal fluids and blood. Some remaining water and solutes form urine. Interpreting more reabsorption as more urine confuses the return route with the exit route.

**Relatively concentrated conditions promote an ADH conservation route.** With factors such as solute load fixed, relatively less water can increase internal osmotic concentration. Relevant hypothalamic cells detect changes. ADH is made in the hypothalamus, transported along neurons to the posterior pituitary for storage and release, and carried in blood to the kidneys. It increases water permeability in regions including collecting ducts, allowing more reabsorption under a suitable osmotic gradient and relatively less, more concentrated urine. With less ADH, those regions have lower permeability and more water remains in urine; baseline reabsorption has not vanished.

**Conserving water is not adding water.** Thirst can form an associated intake-promoting branch. Kidneys help by reducing water loss rather than creating water; a complete balance also includes metabolic water and other routes. Real regulation involves blood volume, salts, other hormones and environment. This model compares relative trends only; it cannot determine a person’s drinking needs or assess health from urine appearance.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：ADH由垂体后叶制造，水重吸收越多尿也越多。反例：ADH主要在下丘脑产生、由垂体后叶释放；水从管液回到血液后，留在尿中的水相对减少。

### English

Misconception: the posterior pituitary makes ADH, and more reabsorption means more urine. Counterexample: ADH is produced in the hypothalamus and released from the posterior pituitary; water returned from tubular fluid leaves relatively less in urine.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

ADH可影响集合管细胞水通道蛋白的分布，让膜的水通透性改变；水的净移动仍需要渗透梯度，并非ADH把水“拖过去”。肾髓质梯度、滤过、盐转运和血容量信号相互联系，本课没有完整建模。相对浓度不是只看水总量，还必须说明溶质量是否改变。

### English

ADH can alter aquaporin distribution in collecting-duct cells, changing membrane permeability. Net water movement still requires an osmotic gradient; ADH does not physically drag water across. Medullary gradients, filtration, salt transport and blood-volume signals interact and are not fully modelled here. Relative concentration depends on solute as well as total water, so solute conditions must be specified.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

在固定溶质负荷的虚构模型中，水重吸收增加、尿量减少。有人说“肾已经补回身体失去的全部水”。用保水与摄入的区别反驳，并列出模型未给的一条水收支信息。

### English

With solute load fixed, a fictional model shows more reabsorption and less urine. Someone claims the kidneys have replaced all lost water. Use conservation versus intake to explain the error and name a missing water-budget quantity.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **水分平衡 / water balance**：水的摄入、产生和丢失等过程共同影响的内部状态。
- **抗利尿激素 / antidiuretic hormone (ADH)**：由下丘脑产生、经垂体后叶释放，调节肾保水等的激素。
- **重吸收 / reabsorption**：物质从肾管液返回内环境和血液的过程。
- **集合管 / collecting duct**：肾中水通透性可受ADH调节的管道部位。
- **渗透 / osmosis**：水通过选择透过性膜的净移动。
- **溶质浓度 / solute concentration**：一定量溶液中的溶质含量，此处用于相对比较。

### English

- **water balance / 水分平衡**: An internal balance influenced by water intake, metabolic production and losses.
- **antidiuretic hormone (ADH) / 抗利尿激素**: A hypothalamic hormone released from the posterior pituitary, regulating kidney water conservation among other actions.
- **reabsorption / 重吸收**: Movement of substances from kidney tubular fluid back towards internal fluids and blood.
- **collecting duct / 集合管**: A kidney duct whose water permeability can be regulated by ADH.
- **osmosis / 渗透**: Net water movement across a selectively permeable membrane.
- **solute concentration / 溶质浓度**: Solute content relative to solution quantity, used here for relative comparison.

## Mission / 放到生活里

### 中文

为较浓/较稀两种模型状态各记录ADH、集合管通透性、重吸收、尿量的相对方向。另画口渴与摄入支路，说明肾保水不会制造水；不改变饮水，不收集个人尿液数据。

### English

Record relative ADH, collecting-duct permeability, reabsorption and urine-volume trends for concentrated/dilute model states. Add a thirst/intake branch and explain why conservation creates no water. Do not change drinking or collect personal urine data.
