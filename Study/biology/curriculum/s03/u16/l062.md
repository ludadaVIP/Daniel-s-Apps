## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

两个虚构场景中空气都比皮肤凉，一个较干、一个较湿。为什么流汗同样明显，冷却效果仍可能不同？寒冷时“少散热”和“多产热”又有什么区别？

### English

In two fictional scenes air is cooler than skin, but one is drier and one more humid. Why might equally visible sweat have different cooling effects? In cold conditions, how do conserving and generating heat differ?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选一个解释，不评分；学习后回看：汗只要出现在皮肤上就有同样的冷却；蒸发条件与皮肤血流都影响散热；寒冷时皮肤血管扩张是主要保热办法。

### English

Choose an unscored initial explanation and revisit it later: Visible sweat always gives equal cooling; Evaporation conditions and skin blood flow affect heat loss; Wider skin vessels are the main way to conserve heat when cold.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**体温取决于热量收支，不只是“觉得热”。** 细胞代谢持续产生热量，身体又与环境交换热量。内部条件偏热时，调节可增加热量向表面运输和散失；偏冷时则可减少散失并增加产热。皮肤感受与内部温度调节有关但不是同一个测量，本课只讨论给定的内部偏离情境。

**较热时，汗需要蒸发才带走对应的热。** 皮肤血管扩张增加表面血流，可把热从内部带到皮肤；在本图空气较凉的条件下有利于散热。汗中的水由液体变成蒸气需要能量，可从皮肤等处带走热。若空气更湿，皮肤与空气之间的水蒸气条件差较小，蒸发散热可能受限，汗滴多少不能单独量出冷却量。

**较冷时，两条策略不能混为一谈。** 皮肤血管收缩减少部分热量流失，是保热；颤抖的肌肉活动增加代谢产热，是产热。两者方向上有助于抵消偏冷，但不会改变天气，也不保证任何极端环境下都能维持状态。对照湿度时应保持风、衣物等因素不变；图中箭头不提供真实速率或安全暴露时间。

### English

**Temperature reflects heat balance, not merely feeling warm.** Cellular metabolism continually generates heat while bodies exchange heat with their environment. An internal warm deviation can trigger greater surface heat transport and loss; a cold deviation can reduce loss and increase production. Skin sensation and regulated internal temperature are related but are not the same measurement. This lesson starts from a given internal deviation.

**Sweat must evaporate to remove the associated heat.** Wider cutaneous vessels increase surface blood flow and transport heat from the interior. With cooler air in this diagram, that can aid heat loss. Liquid sweat needs energy to become vapour and can remove heat from skin and nearby surfaces. More humid air reduces the water-vapour gradient, potentially limiting evaporation. Visible droplets alone cannot measure cooling.

**Cold responses include two different strategies.** Constricting skin vessels reduces some heat loss: conservation. Shivering muscle activity increases metabolic heat production: generation. Both can oppose cold deviation, but neither changes the weather or guarantees stability under extreme conditions. A humidity comparison must hold wind, clothing and other factors constant. Arrows do not give actual rates or safe exposure times.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：颤抖和血管收缩都是“把热量锁住”。反例：血管收缩主要减少表面热量运输，颤抖则通过肌肉代谢增加产热，是热量收支的不同侧。

### English

Misconception: shivering and vasoconstriction both merely trap heat. Counterexample: constriction reduces surface heat transport, whereas shivering adds metabolic heat production; they act on different sides of the heat budget.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

皮肤还通过辐射、对流和传导交换热，方向取决于环境温度等条件。如果空气比皮肤热，简单“扩张血管就能向空气散热”的说法不能直接套用。出汗也涉及水和盐的平衡，调节能力有限。本模型刻意固定较凉空气，不外推炎热、运动或医疗情境。

### English

Skin also exchanges heat by radiation, convection and conduction, with direction depending on environmental conditions. If air is hotter than skin, wider vessels cannot simply be assumed to lose heat to that air. Sweating also involves water and salt balance, and regulatory capacity is limited. This model fixes cooler air and does not extrapolate to heat exposure, exercise or medical situations.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

两段虚构记录只告诉你“汗滴数量相同”，未记录湿度、风或衣物。能否据此证明散热相同？列出两个需要控制或补充的条件。

### English

Two fictional observations report equal visible sweat but omit humidity, wind and clothing. Can they establish equal cooling? Identify two conditions to control or record.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **体温调节 / thermoregulation**：通过产热、散热等过程调节身体温度。
- **血管扩张 / vasodilation**：血管变宽，可改变某区域血流和热量运输。
- **血管收缩 / vasoconstriction**：血管变窄，可减少皮肤血流和相应散热。
- **蒸发 / evaporation**：液态水变为水蒸气的过程，需要能量。
- **颤抖 / shivering**：肌肉快速不自主活动，增加代谢产热。
- **热量传递 / heat transfer**：因温度差等发生的热能转移。

### English

- **thermoregulation / 体温调节**: Regulation of body temperature through heat production and heat exchange.
- **vasodilation / 血管扩张**: Widening of vessels, altering regional blood flow and heat transport.
- **vasoconstriction / 血管收缩**: Narrowing of vessels, which can reduce skin blood flow and heat loss.
- **evaporation / 蒸发**: Liquid water becoming vapour, requiring energy.
- **shivering / 颤抖**: Rapid involuntary muscle activity increasing metabolic heat production.
- **heat transfer / 热量传递**: Transfer of thermal energy associated with temperature differences and other exchange conditions.
