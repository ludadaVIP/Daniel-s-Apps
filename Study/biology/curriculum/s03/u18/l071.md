## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

虚构叶虫的体长和完整斑点数都不同，为什么不能用完全相同的分组方式？

### English

Fictional leafbugs vary in length and whole-spot count. Why should their graphs use different grouping rules?

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**先看变量，不只看记录形式。** 长度12与13之间可以有12.4等中间值，是连续测量。完整斑点数则只能取整数，不会有1.4个完整斑点。连续长度可能因尺子精度而记成整数，所以“表中都是整数”不是判断离散的充分理由。离散也不一定只有两类。

**每条记录只能分组一次。** 12只虚构叶虫的长度分入12至小于15、15至小于18、18至小于21，频数4、4、4。18不在“小于18”的第二组，而在第三组。0、1、2个斑点的频数则为3、4、5。原始数据、单位和样本量都保留，切换特征后横轴跟着改变，不让相同柱形误导读者。

**分布描述差异，不识别唯一原因。** 许多连续性状受多基因与环境共同影响，离散性状也不必由单个基因控制。三组长度频数相同只是这份虚构小数据的结果。要研究原因，还需亲缘、环境、测量误差与更广样本；不能只凭体长排出“更好”或“更进化”。

### English

**Examine the variable, not just recorded values.** Length can have intermediate values such as 12.4 between 12 and 13; whole-spot counts cannot be 1.4. Rounding continuous lengths to integers does not turn the underlying variable discrete. Discrete variables may also have many categories.

**Assign every record once.** Twelve invented lengths fall into 12–<15,15–<18,18–<21 with frequencies 4,4,4. The value 18 belongs only in the last interval. Spot categories 0,1,2 have frequencies 3,4,5. Raw records, units and sample size remain visible, while axis labels change with the selected trait.

**A distribution describes differences, not their unique cause.** Many continuous traits involve multiple genes and environments; discrete traits need not be single-gene. Equal frequencies here reflect a small invented dataset, not a natural law. Family information, conditions, measurement error and wider samples help investigate causes. Length does not rank worth or evolutionary advancement.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

连续不等于完全环境决定；离散不等于必为单基因；频数图本身不能证明遗传机制。

### English

Continuous does not mean purely environmental, nor discrete necessarily single-gene. Frequencies alone do not prove inheritance mechanisms.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

连续直方图区间相接，类别柱留空帮助表达类型。改变组距能改变图形外观，因此必须保留原始值和分组规则，不追求把所有数据画成钟形。

### English

Touching histogram intervals distinguish continuous bins from spaced category bars. Bin width changes appearance, so preserve raw values and rules rather than forcing a bell shape.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

尺子只能读整数毫米，是否说明体长本来只能是整数？解释测量精度和真实变量的区别。

### English

A ruler reads whole millimetres. Does length itself only take integers? Distinguish measurement precision from the underlying variable.

## Check / 理解检查
