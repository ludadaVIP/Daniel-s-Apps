## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

两个亲代都是Aa，子代必定一样吗？四个可能格是四个孩子的顺序表吗？

### English

Both parents are Aa. Must offspring match? Are four possibility boxes a birth-order schedule?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

### English

Choose an unscored prediction to test with the model: Every offspring of Aa and Aa must be Aa. / Four boxes show possible routes, not one guaranteed outcome per child. / One aa offspring uses up the chance of aa next time.

### English

Submit an unscored prediction, then investigate conditions and data to revise it.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**每方提供一个版本。** 虚构二倍体叶虫在某基因位置有A与a。体细胞组合可为AA、Aa、aa；每个配子在这个位置得到一个版本。受精把两个亲代贡献重新组合。这里只追踪单位置，不模拟完整减数分裂、整套基因组或所有遗传方式。

**同一组合可经两条路线。** Aa×Aa时，四条路线A+A、A+a、a+A、a+a得到AA、Aa、Aa、aa。两个Aa的亲代来源不同。假定配子机会相等且随机结合，AA概率1/4、Aa为1/2、aa为1/4；这些是可能程度，不是连续四个子代一定按四格出现。每次结合没有记忆，前一个aa不会用完下一次的机会。

**字母组合不是全部命运。** A不叫强大，a不叫弱小。显性与隐性描述特定表型关系，不代表优秀、常见或更易传递。这里不为字母指定复杂人类性状；真实特征还可能受许多基因、环境和发育影响。下一阶段研究细胞分裂与遗传概率时，会继续检查这个简单模型的条件。

### English

**Each parent contributes one version.** A fictional diploid leafbug has A/a versions at one locus. Somatic combinations are AA,Aa,aa; each gamete receives one version here. Fertilisation combines parental contributions. The model does not simulate full meiosis, genomes or every inheritance system.

**One combination can have two routes.** Aa×Aa yields A+A,A+a,a+A,a+a, producing AA,Aa,Aa,aa. The two Aa routes differ in parental origin. Assuming equally likely gametes and random union, probabilities are 1/4,1/2,1/4. These possibilities are not an obligatory schedule for four offspring. One aa outcome does not use up the chance for the next independent union.

**Letters are not destiny.** A does not mean strong and a weak. Dominance describes a particular phenotype relationship, not superiority, commonness or easier transmission. No complex human trait is assigned. Real traits can involve many genes, environments and development. Later division and probability lessons examine this simple model’s assumptions more deeply.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

四格不是生育顺序；显性不等于更强或必然传递；子代不是两个亲代所有性状的简单平均。

### English

Four boxes are not birth order; dominant is not stronger or guaranteed transmission, and offspring are not simple averages of every parental trait.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

小样本后代比例可能偏离期望；不同基因位置还可能有连锁与重组，因此不能把单位置的独立随机规则直接推广到所有基因。

### English

Small offspring samples can depart from expected ratios. Linkage and recombination can connect different loci, so a single-locus random rule cannot be extended automatically to every gene.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

Aa×Aa已经产生一个aa，能说下一个一定不能aa吗？在独立随机结合假设下解释。

### English

After Aa×Aa produces aa, must the next offspring not be aa? Explain under independent random-union assumptions.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

### English

Say: Each offspring receives one allele from each parent at this locus. The final phrase specifies the model’s scope.

## Check / 理解检查

### 中文

检查配子只得一个版本、AA×aa组合、四格不是出生顺序与gamete。订正时标两方来源，并说明为什么前一次结果不会用完下一次机会。

### English

Check one allele per gamete, AA×aa, possibility boxes versus birth order and gamete. Mark both parental origins and explain why one outcome does not use up the next chance.

## 中文

先检查概念，再用新情境解释证据。每题反馈指出理由与回看位置；英语术语独立记录。

### English

Check concepts and explain evidence in a new situation. Feedback gives reasons and a precise revisit; terminology is recorded separately.

### English
