## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**这些名词位于不同层次。** 典型动植物细胞的核内含染色体，DNA与相关蛋白质组织成染色体。DNA沿线有许多功能区段，基因是其中一类，不是每条染色体只有一个基因，也不是所有DNA都可简单称为蛋白质编码基因。

**信息在序列，不在颜色。** A、T、C、G是碱基简写，常见双链配对为A–T与C–G。局部序列版本可以字母不同，但颜色只是追踪标记，并不是基因的真实颜色。看到某一字母变化也不能不经证据就断言具体性状会改变；本模型没有建立序列到性状的计算。

**图形有时期和范围。** 染色体不是一直呈X形，X常表示复制后两条姐妹染色单体仍相连的状态，每条染色单体含一个DNA分子。本课用盘绕长线，避免把X当定义。线粒体和叶绿体也有DNA；细菌没有细胞核却有DNA。因此“核里有DNA”不能反过来推成“DNA只能在核里”。

### English

**The terms occupy different levels.** A typical animal or plant nucleus contains chromosomes, which organise DNA with associated proteins. Functional regions occur along DNA, including genes. One chromosome is not one gene, and not all DNA is protein-coding.

**Information lies in sequence, not colour.** A,T,C,G abbreviate bases, with typical A–T and C–G pairing. Local versions can differ in letters, while colours are tracing conventions rather than real gene colours. A sequence difference alone does not establish a particular trait effect; this model does not calculate sequence-to-phenotype consequences.

**Shapes have timing and scope.** Chromosomes are not permanently X-shaped. An X commonly shows joined sister chromatids after replication, each containing a DNA molecule. A coiled line avoids defining a chromosome by that temporary form. Mitochondria and chloroplasts also contain DNA. Bacteria have DNA without a nucleus: DNA being present in a nucleus does not imply that it exists only there.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

一条染色体不等于一个基因；图中线长或颜色不能判断生物能力与“高级程度”。

### English

A chromosome is not a gene; drawn length or colour cannot rank ability or biological advancement.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

二倍体物种许多体细胞的同源染色体来自两个亲代，同源不等于完全相同；姐妹染色单体由复制形成。后续细胞分裂课将分清这两种“成对”。

### English

Many diploid somatic cells have homologous chromosomes from two parents, not necessarily identical. Sister chromatids arise by copying. Later division lessons distinguish these two kinds of pair.

Correct “bacteria lack a nucleus, therefore lack DNA” and identify the reversed structural inference.
