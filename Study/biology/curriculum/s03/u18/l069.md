## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

两株虚构植物的叶片一样长，能推出它们的基因型相同吗？用同一组合换环境试一试。

### English

Two fictional plants have equally long leaves. Must their genotypes match? Try one genotype in different environments.

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选一条最符合你想法的预测，不计分；再用模型检查：相同叶长必定意味着相同基因型。；同一Aa换环境可改变叶长而保留Aa。；遮护条件会把a直接改写成A。

### English

Choose an unscored prediction to test with the model: Equal leaf lengths require equal genotypes. / Changing the environment can change leaf length while retaining Aa. / Shelter directly rewrites a into A.

## 中文

先提交一个想法，预测不计分。再操作条件与数据，用证据修正。

### English

Submit an unscored prediction, then investigate conditions and data to revise it.

### English

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**相似可以来自不同路径。** 后代继承亲代的信息，也经历营养、温度、光等条件。表型是看到或测到的特征，基因型是所讨论位置的等位基因组合。同表型未必同基因型；不同表型也不一定意味着DNA不同。

**比较时一次固定一边。** 本课纸叶草完全虚构。人为规定一个基因位置的AA、Aa、aa基础叶长为18、16、14个模型单位，遮护加4，暴露加0。同一Aa由16变20，改变的是环境条件，不是a变成A；固定环境再比较三种组合，才能在本规则中隔离基因型差异。

**相同结果不支持唯一反推。** 暴露AA与遮护aa都得18，所以只看18无法确定基因型。真实叶长、人体身高等通常受许多基因、环境与相互作用影响，未必符合简单相加。表格用于练习比较设计，不是某真实物种的实验，也不能用于评价人的能力、健康或价值。

### English

**Similarity can arise by different routes.** Offspring inherit information and experience nutrition, temperature, light and other conditions. Phenotype describes observations; genotype describes alleles at specified positions. Equal phenotypes need not imply equal genotypes, while different phenotypes need not imply different DNA.

**Hold one side fixed.** Paperleaf is invented. A stipulated locus gives AA, Aa and aa baseline lengths of 18,16,14 model units. Shelter adds four; exposure adds none. Moving Aa from 16 to 20 changes the environment, not a into A. Holding environment fixed then separates genotype effects under this particular rule.

**Equal results do not support unique reverse inference.** Exposed AA and sheltered aa both yield 18. The observed length alone therefore cannot identify genotype. Real leaf length and human height usually involve many genes, environments and interactions rather than simple addition. This table teaches comparative design, not a real species or a way to rank people’s ability, health or worth.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

环境影响表型不代表必然改写等位基因；一个单位置模型不能代表复杂人类性状。

### English

Environmental effects on phenotype need not rewrite alleles; one locus does not represent complex human traits.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

环境有时影响基因表达而不改变DNA序列。真实基因型对环境的反应可能不同而非平行加4；后续分子生物课将讨论序列、表达与发育之间的联系。

### English

Environment can affect gene expression without changing sequence. Real genotypes may respond differently rather than all gaining four. Later molecular lessons connect sequence, expression and development.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

比较同品种幼苗在两种光照下的表现，为何还要保持水、土和起始大小尽量一致？

### English

Why hold water, soil and initial size comparable when comparing seedlings in two light conditions?

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

The same genotype can produce different phenotypes in different environments. can描述可能，不代表每个性状都随环境改变。

### English

Say: The same genotype can produce different phenotypes in different environments. Can states a possibility, not every trait always changing.

## Check / 理解检查

### 中文

检查Aa换环境、固定环境比较基因型、两条得到18的路线与phenotype。订正时分别写清固定项、改变项和所测表型，不把叶长倒推成唯一基因型。

### English

Check Aa across environments, genotypes in one environment, two routes to 18 and phenotype. Identify fixed and changed factors and the measured phenotype without assigning a unique genotype from length alone.

## 中文

先检查概念，再用新情境解释证据。每题反馈指出理由与回看位置；英语术语独立记录。

### English

Check concepts and explain evidence in a new situation. Feedback gives reasons and a precise revisit; terminology is recorded separately.

## Mission / 今日小任务

### English

Design comparisons across environments and genotypes. Find two routes to 18 and explain the reverse-inference limit.
