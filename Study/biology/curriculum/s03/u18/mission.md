# U18｜虚构物种证据档案 / A fictional-species evidence file

## 中文

创造一个虚构物种的名称，使用课程提供的数据，不给自己或家人做基因判断。

- **比较表**：L069固定Aa，记录暴露与遮护的16、20；固定暴露环境比较AA18与aa14。再找同为18的暴露AA/遮护aa，解释只看表型不能唯一反推。
- **信息结构**：画DNA、基因、染色体与细胞核关系，注明核外DNA反例、图非按比例。
- **变异记录**：从L071选择体长或斑点，保留原始12条、单位、分组与频数；总频数仍应是12。
- **后代可能**：L072选两个亲代，每条路线各取一个版本，说明四格是可能性不是出生顺序。
- **结论边界**：写一个得到模型支持的结论、一项模型不支持的预测和一句英文。真实复杂性状通常涉及多基因、环境与发育，不套用此虚构单位置表。

在手帐的证据栏保留具体数字与路线，不只写“我看过动画”；推理栏解释为什么这一比较能支持或限制结论。

## English

Invent a species name and use supplied records, without inferring personal or family genetics.

- **Comparison table:** fix Aa and compare exposed 16 with sheltered 20. Fix exposure and compare AA18 with aa14. Explain why exposed AA and sheltered aa both yielding 18 prevents unique reverse inference.
- **Information structure:** connect DNA, genes, chromosomes and nuclei, including a counterexample to nuclear-only DNA and a not-to-scale note.
- **Variation:** retain all twelve raw length or spot records, units, groups and frequencies. Frequencies must still total twelve.
- **Offspring possibilities:** choose two L072 parents and trace one allele from each per route. Explain that four boxes are possibilities, not birth order.
- **Limits:** give one supported claim, one unsupported prediction and an English conclusion. Real complex traits commonly involve multiple genes, environments and development.

Keep numerical records and routes as evidence rather than merely saying that an animation was watched; explain why the comparison supports or limits the conclusion.
