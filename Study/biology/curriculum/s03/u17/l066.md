## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

更多水一定支持更多植物吗？补水之后，限制可能换成光或矿质元素。

### English

Does more water always support more plants? Light or minerals may become limiting after watering.

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选一条最符合你想法的预测，不计分；再用模型检查：水容量从3升到7，光8、矿质6时，共同容量会变7。；补水后矿质可能成为新的限制。；三种资源容量相加就是共同容量。

### English

Choose an unscored prediction to test with the model: Raising water capacity from 3 to 7 with light 8 and minerals 6 gives capacity 7. / Minerals may become limiting after watering. / Adding the three resource capacities gives the combined capacity.

## 中文

先提交一个想法，预测不计分。再操作条件与数据，用证据修正。

### English

Submit an unscored prediction, then investigate conditions and data to revise it.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

水、光、矿质元素三条同尺度容量柱，从零开始。数值是同一种人为幼苗单位，不能当作毫米雨量或光强。

### English

Water, light and mineral-capacity bars share a zero baseline. Values use the same invented seedling-unit, not rainfall or light-intensity units.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

依次选干燥、补水、遮光，预测谁限制，再逐步显示容量与最小值。

### English

Choose dry, watered and shaded scenarios; predict the limiting resource before revealing capacities and their minimum.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**需求常常同时存在。** 植物需要水、光与矿质元素，更多一种资源不能代替缺少的另一种。严重缺水可影响气孔与二氧化碳进入；光不足时即使有水，也不能维持同样的光合作用。温度、捕食与疾病也可约束种群，所以“限制”不总是缺食物。

**本模型比较相同单位的支持容量。** 人为规定水、光、矿质分别可支持3、8、6个幼苗单位，最小值3限制共同完成任务的容量。三项不能相加。水增为7时，新的最小值是矿质6；再遮光为2，光成为限制。这里没有测真实植物，也没模拟萌发、生长速度、死亡或多种资源的复杂相互作用。

**容纳量不是永久配额。** 季节、栖息结构与资源会改变，种群还有时间滞后和迁移。实际补水后未必立即增加数量：可能缺光，也可能还没有种子到达。结论应说明“其他条件相同时，资源变化可能影响可用条件”，并列出需要的对照证据，而不是把模型整数当成自然预测。

### English

**Needs operate together.** Plants require water, light and minerals; extra water cannot replace missing light. Water shortage can affect stomatal carbon-dioxide entry, while low light constrains photosynthesis despite adequate water. Temperature, predation and disease can also limit populations.

**The model compares support capacities in matching units.** Invented water, light and mineral supplies support 3, 8 and 6 seedling-units. The minimum, 3, constrains their combination; capacities are not added. Water raised to 7 makes minerals at 6 limiting, while shade reducing light to 2 makes light limiting. No real plants, growth rates, deaths or complex resource interactions are simulated.

**Carrying capacity is not a permanent quota.** Seasons, habitat structure and resources change, and populations show delays and movement. Water addition may not immediately increase abundance if light or seed arrival still limits it. A conclusion should specify comparable conditions and further evidence, not treat invented integers as natural forecasts.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

更多不总是更好；取最小值只是这项类比的规则，不是生态系统完整公式。

### English

More is not always better; the minimum rule is an analogy, not a complete ecosystem equation.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

竞争有时随密度增加而加强，干旱等因素则可影响不同密度的种群。真实限制因素须借助重复对照和机制证据判断；观察同时变化仍不够。

### English

Some competition intensifies with density, whereas drought can affect populations at different densities. Identifying actual limits needs replicated comparisons and mechanism evidence.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

盆栽补水后没长高，列两个替代解释和一项可检查信息，不改变真实照料。

### English

A watered pot did not grow taller. List two alternative explanations and useful information without changing its care.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

Water was limiting; after watering, minerals became limiting. became说明限制是条件性的。

### English

Say: Water was limiting; after watering, minerals became limiting. Became signals conditional change.

## Check / 理解检查

### 中文

检查共同容量、补水后的限制换位、真实补水未增数量的解释与limiting factor。订正时回看相同单位的三柱与最低虚线，并指出模型遗漏。

### English

Check combined capacity, the new limit after watering, explanations for no real increase and limiting factor. Revisit the three matching-unit bars and minimum line, and state an omitted process.

## 中文

先检查概念，再用新情境解释证据。每题反馈指出理由与回看位置；英语术语独立记录。

### English

Check concepts and explain evidence in a new situation. Feedback gives reasons and a precise revisit; terminology is recorded separately.

## Mission / 今日小任务

### 中文

用补水与遮光两次比较解释限制因素怎样换位，并注明模型遗漏。

### English

Explain the switch in limiting resources between watered and shaded scenarios and state an omission.
