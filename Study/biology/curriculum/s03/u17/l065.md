## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

池塘里有20只同种青蛙，也有蜗牛和水草。调查青蛙种群，与调查所有生命是同一个问题吗？

### English

A pond has 20 frogs of one species, plus snails and plants. Is studying the frog population the same as studying all life?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选一条最符合你想法的预测，不计分；再用模型检查：首尾都是20只，期间可能仍有出生和死亡。；首尾都是20只，就一定没有个体更替。；迁入2只与本地出生2只属于同一事件。

### English

Choose an unscored prediction to test with the model: A start and end count of 20 can conceal births and deaths. / A start and end count of 20 proves no turnover. / Two immigrants are the same event as two local births.

## 中文

先提交一个想法，预测不计分。再操作条件与数据，用证据修正。

### English

Submit an unscored prediction, then investigate conditions and data to revise it.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

边界内只统计某一种蛙，其他图样代表群落中的别种生物；出生、死亡和移动箭头分别标记。非按比例。

### English

Count one frog species inside the boundary; other organisms represent the wider community. Separate arrows mark birth, death and movement. Not to scale.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

比较迁入2只与6只，逐步更新四项账目。切换种群与群落视角，检查哪些对象进入蛙种群计数。

### English

Compare two versus six immigrants and update four flows stepwise. Switch population/community views to check what belongs in the frog count.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**先画研究边界。** 种群不是所有看起来相似或住在一起的生物，而是一定区域与时期内同一物种的个体集合。池塘中的一种蛙是研究种群；蛙、蜗牛与水草的不同种群组成群落的一部分。生态系统还包括光、水和温度等环境条件。研究边界可能是人为划定，个体仍能进出。

**数量和密度回答不同问题。** 20只蛙在10平方米中的密度为2只/平方米，在100平方米中则为0.2只/平方米。总数相同，分布与资源关系却可能不同。比较时须检查调查面积、时期、单位与方法，不能只比一个数字。

**数量是一份有流向的账。** 本虚构池塘从20只开始，出生5、死亡3、迁入2、迁出4，最终20+5−3+2−4=20。总数没变，不代表没有新个体与损失。只把迁入改为6，其他记录固定，结果变为24。模型核对已提供的事件数，不预测未来；如果仅看到末值24，不能唯一推出哪项事件发生了改变。

### English

**Define the study boundary.** A population consists of one species within a particular area and period. One frog species in a pond is a population. Frogs, snails and plants together belong to a wider community; an ecosystem also includes non-living conditions. Study boundaries need not prevent movement.

**Abundance differs from density.** Twenty frogs in 10 m² gives 2 per m², whereas twenty in 100 m² gives 0.2 per m². Totals can match while distributions and resource relationships differ. Check area, period, units and survey method before comparing values.

**Account for inputs and losses.** The invented pond starts at 20, with five births, three deaths, two immigrants and four emigrants: 20+5−3+2−4=20. Stable totals can conceal turnover. Change immigration alone to six and the result is 24. The model accounts for supplied events rather than forecasting them. An end count of 24 alone cannot identify which flow changed.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

数量不变不等于个体不变；“所有池塘动物”通常不是一个种群。

### English

Stable totals do not mean unchanged individuals; all pond animals do not form one population.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

调查者也会漏看、重复计数或遇到季节变化。一次记录更多不一定代表真实数量更多，也可能更容易看见。真实研究需要区分数量变化与探测概率变化。

### English

Observers can miss or double-count individuals, and seasons affect sightings. More sightings may reflect easier detection rather than greater abundance. Real surveys distinguish abundance from detectability.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

“今年看见更多蛙”可能来自真实数量增加，也可能调查时间不同。各提出一条解释与一项区分它们的证据。

### English

More frogs were seen this year. Suggest an abundance explanation, a survey explanation and evidence to distinguish them.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

The population stayed at 20, but individuals entered and left. stayed描述总数，不能据此断言个体相同。

### English

Say: The population stayed at 20, but individuals entered and left. Stayed describes the total, not individual identities.

## Check / 理解检查

### 中文

检查种群边界、四项数量账、稳定总数中的更替与density。订正时把出生和迁入、死亡和迁出分别标明，不只看首尾数字。

### English

Check population boundaries, four flows, turnover behind stable totals and density. Distinguish births from immigration and deaths from emigration rather than comparing endpoints alone.

## 中文

先检查概念，再用新情境解释证据。每题反馈指出理由与回看位置；英语术语独立记录。

### English

Check concepts and explain evidence in a new situation. Feedback gives reasons and a precise revisit; terminology is recorded separately.
