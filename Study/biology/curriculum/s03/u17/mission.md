# U17｜虚拟样方研究 / A virtual quadrat investigation

## 中文

1. 打开L067，用随机种子42先抽6格。记录每个Q编号、计数、面积。按“总计数÷6平方米×36平方米”求估计，并保留中间密度与单位。
2. 换到种子43，重复记录。比较两次估计；不能因一次不准就断言随机抽样无用。
3. 比较路边六格：计数4、面积6平方米、估计24。揭示教学普查96，解释路边偏低；再自己挑密集格，检查可能偏高。自选方案不得称为随机。
4. 用L068两次访问的原始数计算均值差，区分“观察关联”与“唯一原因”。提出一个替代解释、一项需要的额外数据。
5. 在手帐分别提交主张与计算、证据、推理与局限。写一句英文，例如“The estimate depends on which quadrats were sampled.”

这些是同一时期、同种虚构植物的原创网格与虚构访问记录，不能作为新西兰真实地点调查。普查总数不是环境容纳量。

## English

1. In L067 draw six quadrats using seed 42. Retain Q IDs, individual counts and area. Calculate total count ÷ 6 m² × 36 m², preserving density and units.
2. Repeat with seed 43. Compare estimates; one imperfect sample does not invalidate random sampling.
3. Compare the roadside sample: four plants in 6 m² estimates 24. Reveal census 96 and explain low roadside bias. Deliberately choose dense cells to test possible upward bias; manual choices are not random.
4. Use raw L068 records from two visits to calculate mean change. Separate association from a sole-cause claim, propose an alternative explanation and request useful additional data.
5. Submit claim/calculation, evidence and reasoning/limits separately. Add an English conclusion in your own words.

All grid counts represent one fictional plant species at one time; visit records are also invented. They are not observations of a real New Zealand site. Census is not carrying capacity.
