## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

雨多时记录到的植物也多，这就证明雨是唯一原因了吗？

### English

More plants were recorded on wetter visits. Does that prove rainfall was the only cause?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选一条最符合你想法的预测，不计分；再用模型检查：雨量与计数共同变化，只能先支持相关。；均值从3升到4说明每株植物都长高。；四次记录足以证明雨量是唯一原因。

### English

Choose an unscored prediction to test with the model: Rainfall and counts changing together initially support association. / A mean count rising from 3 to 4 means every plant grew taller. / Four visits establish rainfall as the sole cause.

## 中文

先提交一个想法，预测不计分。再操作条件与数据，用证据修正。

### English

Submit an unscored prediction, then investigate conditions and data to revise it.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

均值柱从零开始，保留每次3格的原始数；雨量另列毫米单位，不能共用同一纵轴。

### English

Mean bars start at zero and retain three raw records per visit; rainfall is listed separately in mm, not on the count axis.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

切换遮荫/开阔，逐步增加访问；用原始值核对均值，比较关联结论与唯一因果结论。

### English

Switch shade/open conditions and reveal visits stepwise; verify means from raw values and compare association versus unique-cause claims.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**图后面有真实规则与原始记录。** 本课4次访问均为虚构，每次在遮荫和开阔区各调查3个不同的1平方米样方。第一次遮荫2、3、4，平均3；第二次3、4、5，平均4。平均数帮助比较，却隐藏差异，因此必须能回看原始值。这不是对同一株的生长测量，不能把数量均值变大写成每株长高。

**总体方向不是每一步都上升。** 遮荫均值为3、4、5、4，最后一步回落。各柱共用零起点和相同尺度，样本量始终显示3。雨量12、25、41、18毫米表示每次访问之前同样长度时间段的记录。数量与雨量共同变化是关联线索；季节、温度、萌发、探测方式等也可能改变。

**好结论要留下替代解释。** “水增加可能帮助萌发”可以作为机制假设；“这些数证明雨是唯一原因”超出证据。下一步可增加可比地点的重复，同时记录温光和计数规则，或设计控制其他条件的虚拟比较。收集更多数据不自动变成因果实验，应指出哪些替代解释被排除、哪些还存在。

### English

**Graphs stand on rules and raw records.** Four invented visits each sample three distinct 1 m² quadrats in shade and three in open ground. Shade counts 2,3,4 give a mean of 3; the next visit’s 3,4,5 give 4. Means aid comparison but hide differences, so raw values remain visible. These counts do not measure individual plant growth.

**An overall pattern need not rise at every step.** Shade means are 3,4,5,4, including a final decline. Bars share zero and one scale, with n=3 shown. Rainfall of 12,25,41,18 mm covers equally long periods before visits. Joint change suggests association, while season, temperature, emergence and detection may also vary.

**Good conclusions retain alternatives.** More water might support germination, but these data do not prove rain the sole cause. Replicate comparable sites, record light, temperature and counting rules, or design a controlled virtual comparison. More observations do not automatically become a causal experiment. Specify which alternative explanations have been checked and which remain.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

均值不是每格真实数；两列一起升高不等于已证明唯一因果。

### English

A mean need not be any quadrat’s value; joint increase does not establish a unique cause.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

同一地点的连续访问可能有时间依赖，不能随意当独立重复。误差线须说明代表范围、标准差还是标准误；本课直接显示全部小样本值。

### English

Visits to one site can be temporally dependent, not independent replicates. Error bars require definitions such as range or standard error; this small dataset displays every raw value.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

“遮荫均值更高，所以遮荫造成更多植物”还缺哪些证据？写一个替代解释和一项补充记录。

### English

What is missing from “shade has a higher mean, therefore shade caused greater abundance”? Give an alternative and extra record.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

The records suggest an association, but do not establish a unique cause. suggest与prove表达不同确定程度。

### English

Say: The records suggest an association, but do not establish a unique cause. Suggest and prove convey different certainty.

## Check / 理解检查

### 中文

检查原始数的均值、3→4→5→4的趋势、雨量相关的证据边界与correlation。订正时区分植物计数、个体生长及未控制的其他条件。

### English

Check means from raw counts, the 3→4→5→4 trend, limits of rainfall evidence and correlation. Separate plant counts from individual growth and identify uncontrolled conditions.

## 中文

先检查概念，再用新情境解释证据。每题反馈指出理由与回看位置；英语术语独立记录。

### English

Check concepts and explain evidence in a new situation. Feedback gives reasons and a precise revisit; terminology is recorded separately.

## Mission / 今日小任务

### 中文

选两次访问，列原始数与均值变化，以“支持……但不能单独证明……”写结论。

### English

Choose two visits, record raw values and mean change, then conclude “supports … but does not alone prove …”.
