## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

36平方米的草地，抽6格能估计什么？只在路边挑方便的格子会怎样？

### English

What can six quadrats estimate about a 36 m² meadow? What happens if convenient roadside cells are chosen?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选一条最符合你想法的预测，不计分；再用模型检查：路边六格能保证代表整片草地。；随机抽六格每次都一定得到同一估计。；路边方案可能偏低，随机方案也会波动。

### English

Choose an unscored prediction to test with the model: Six roadside cells are guaranteed to represent the whole meadow. / Every random six-cell sample gives the same estimate. / Roadside sampling may underestimate, while random estimates still vary.

## 中文

先提交一个想法，预测不计分。再操作条件与数据，用证据修正。

### English

Submit an unscored prediction, then investigate conditions and data to revise it.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

6×6网格每格1平方米，数字表示同种植物的个体数。边框标记抽中位置。

### English

Each of 36 grid cells covers 1 m²; numbers count individuals of one plant species. Borders identify sampled positions.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

先选随机或路边方案，再逐步查看位置、计数、面积、密度与估计。比较6与12格、换随机种子、点选密集格，最后揭示普查。

### English

Choose random or roadside sampling, then reveal positions, counts, area, density and estimate. Compare 6/12 cells, change seed or select dense cells, then reveal the census.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**先定义数什么。** 这是原创虚拟草地，36格各1平方米，数字表示同一种可区分植物在同一时期的个体数。现实还须统一物种、边界规则、分株计数与观察时间。样方适合固定位置的植物，不宜原样套给快速移动的鸟。

**分母是抽查面积。** 路边6格共有4株，抽样面积6平方米，样本密度4÷6≈0.67株/平方米；乘全场36平方米，估计24株。不能把4除以36当作样本密度。这个推断假定样本可代表全场，但图中路边特别稀疏，便利抽样系统偏低；只挑最密集格又可能偏高。

**随机不保证一次准确。** 随机方案从全部位置不放回抽取，先生成位置，再看计数。换种子得到不同样本，所以估计会波动。增加样本通常减少随机误差，但不能保证每一次更接近真值。揭示普查得到96株，用来检查本次误差；真实调查往往不知道总数，不能把估计精确到小数就当作确定事实。

### English

**Define what is counted.** This original meadow has 36 cells of 1 m². Counts refer to distinct individuals of one fictional plant species at one time. Real studies need common species, boundary, clone-counting and timing rules. Quadrat methods fit stationary plants better than fast-moving birds.

**Use sampled area as denominator.** Six roadside cells contain four plants, giving 4÷6≈0.67 per m² and an estimated total of 24 over 36 m². Dividing by the whole field area would not give sample density. Extrapolation assumes representative coverage; the sparse roadside sample is biased low here. Selecting dense cells can bias upwards.

**Random does not guarantee one exact result.** Positions are drawn without replacement before counts are inspected. A new seed yields a different sample and estimate. Larger samples generally reduce random variation but need not improve every individual draw. Reveal the teaching census of 96 to check this estimate’s error. Real surveys often lack a known total, and decimal precision is not certainty.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

重复数同一格不等于增加独立空间样本；随机方法减少选择偏差但仍有抽样误差。

### English

Repeated counts of one cell are not extra independent spatial samples; random selection still permits sampling variation.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

若全场有不同生境，可在每层随机取样并按各层面积加权；面积不同时不能直接平均各层均值。本课先练等面积简单随机抽样。

### English

Different habitats can be randomly sampled within strata, then weighted by area. Unequal-area strata require weighted rather than simple means. This lesson practises equal-area simple random sampling.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

解释专挑花多区域与专挑路边可能产生相反偏差。提出一条在看到数量之前确定位置的规则。

### English

Explain opposite biases from choosing flower-rich cells or roadsides. Propose a position rule set before seeing counts.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

例如：The sample estimated 108 plants; the census counted 96. 108只对应样本计数18、抽样面积6平方米的演算例子，不代表当前随机样本。

### English

For example, a sample of 18 plants in 6 m² estimates 108 across 36 m², while this teaching census is 96. The current random sample may give a different estimate.

## Check / 理解检查

### 中文

检查计数÷抽样面积×总面积、先选位置的理由、样本量与随机波动、quadrat。订正时保留单位，并比较多次估计，避免把一次结果当成保证。

### English

Check count ÷ sampled area × total area, choosing locations first, sample size versus random variation and quadrat. Keep units and compare repeated estimates rather than treating one draw as a guarantee.

## 中文

先检查概念，再用新情境解释证据。每题反馈指出理由与回看位置；英语术语独立记录。

### English

Check concepts and explain evidence in a new situation. Feedback gives reasons and a precise revisit; terminology is recorded separately.

## Mission / 今日小任务

### 中文

记录6格随机位置、逐格计数、抽样面积、密度和估计；与路边方案比较，写一条不确定性。

### English

Record six random positions, counts, sampled area, density and estimate; compare roadside sampling and state an uncertainty.
