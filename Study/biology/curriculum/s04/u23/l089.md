## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

一群虚构甲虫只有A与a。重新配对能凭空产生第三种等位基因吗？皮肤细胞出现变化又意味着什么？

**学习目标（30分钟）**

- 区分突变产生新等位基因与重组重新排列已有等位基因。
- 联系生殖细胞谱系与后代，限定可遗传变异。
- 解释变异不按生物的需要定向发生。

### English

Fictional beetles carry only A and a. Can reshuffling create a third allele? What if a change occurs in a skin cell?

**Learning goals (30 minutes)**

- Distinguish new alleles from reshuffled existing alleles.
- Connect reproductive lineages to offspring and qualify heritability.
- Explain why variation is not directed by an organism’s needs.

Recombination changes combinations without necessarily creating new alleles; Useful mutations occur only when needed; Every cell mutation reaches offspring. Prediction is unscored.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

变异是比较个体时发现的差异，但差异的来源并不只有一种。DNA复制和修复并非绝对无误；一些序列改变形成突变，从而可能产生新的等位基因。突变是否有利取决于它影响什么以及所处环境。饥饿并不会指挥DNA准确写出一种需要的消化能力；环境可以影响突变发生率，却不等于按需要指定有利结果。

有性生殖还会重新组合已有信息。设两条亲代染色体携带AB与ab，形成配子时的重组可得到Ab或aB。这里组合从两种增加到四种，但在所讨论的两个位置，字母仍只有A、a及B、b，没有多出新版本。模型中的星号代表另一个序列版本，不能把单纯更换字母顺序说成突变。两个机制可共同增加种群遗传差异，却回答不同的问题。

能否遗传还要追踪变化经过哪些细胞。本课以动物为例：皮肤细胞突变可能影响个体，通常不经配子传给后代；生殖细胞谱系的突变若进入参与受精的配子，才有可能传下去，仍不保证每个后代都有。植物和无性繁殖的路径可能不同，不能把动物例子写成所有生物的绝对规则。比较序列、组合与传递路径，才算解释了变异，而不是只看到表面差异。还应控制观察条件，避免把环境影响误当作序列改变。

### English

Variation describes differences among individuals, but those differences have several sources. DNA copying and repair are not perfectly accurate. A change in sequence is a mutation and can introduce a new allele. Whether its effect is beneficial depends on the affected function and environment. Hunger does not instruct DNA to produce exactly the digestive ability an organism needs. Conditions can influence mutation rates without specifying useful outcomes.

Sexual reproduction also rearranges existing information. Consider fictional parental chromosomes carrying AB and ab at two loci. Recombination can produce Ab and aB. The number of combinations increases from two to four, yet the available versions remain A or a at one locus and B or b at the other. No additional allele is required. In our diagram a star marks a distinct sequence version; moving existing letters into a different combination does not itself create that version. Mutation and recombination can both contribute to genetic variation, through different mechanisms.

Inheritance requires a transmission route. In the animal example, a mutation confined to a skin cell may affect that individual but ordinarily does not enter offspring through gametes. A mutation in the reproductive lineage may be transmitted if the relevant gamete participates in fertilization; it need not reach every offspring. Plants and asexual organisms can have different transmission routes, so this animal comparison is not a rule covering every organism. To explain variation, trace the sequence change, the arrangement of existing alleles and the cells connecting one generation to the next. An observed difference alone does not establish any of these causes.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

突变不是为适应而有目的发生；重组不必创造新等位基因，动物体细胞突变通常不直接遗传。

### English

Mutations are not purposeful responses to need; recombination need not create alleles, and animal somatic mutations ordinarily are not inherited.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中桥梁：减数分裂的交叉互换和染色体独立分配增加组合；同一段DNA改变和等位基因频率变化属于不同描述层次。

### English

Senior bridge: crossing over and independent assortment increase combinations during meiosis. Sequence change and population allele-frequency change describe different levels.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

某动物皮肤的DNA改变，后代没有该变化；这是否反驳突变能产生可遗传变异？解释传递路径。

### English

A skin mutation is absent from offspring. Does this refute heritable mutation? Explain the transmission route.

## Vocabulary / 双语词汇

### 中文

- **突变 / mutation**：DNA序列的改变。
- **等位基因 / allele**：同一基因位置的不同版本。
- **重组 / recombination**：已有遗传信息形成新的组合。
- **可遗传 / heritable**：可通过繁殖传给后代。
- **生殖细胞系 / germline**：通向配子及后代的细胞谱系。
- **体细胞 / somatic cell**：动物体内通常不形成配子的细胞。

### English

- **mutation / 突变**: A change in DNA sequence.
- **allele / 等位基因**: A version at a gene locus.
- **recombination / 重组**: Existing genetic information forms new combinations.
- **heritable / 可遗传**: Capable of transmission through reproduction.
- **germline / 生殖细胞系**: Cell lineage leading to gametes and offspring.
- **somatic cell / 体细胞**: Animal body cell not ordinarily forming gametes.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] 只把AB/ab重组为Ab/aB，增加了什么？
   A. 新等位基因
   B. 已有等位基因的组合
   C. 每个位置的版本数一定翻倍
   答案：B。A/a、B/b仍是原有版本；改变组合不等于创造序列。 复习：回看重组路径的四组字母。

2. [concept] 本课动物皮肤细胞发生突变，通常怎样？
   A. 必传给所有后代
   B. 必定没有任何影响
   C. 可影响个体，通常不经配子遗传
   答案：C。细胞的位置和传递谱系决定遗传可能性；突变也可能影响个体。 复习：回看皮肤与生殖细胞路径。

3. [reasoning] 干旱前已有耐旱版本，干旱后其比例升高，支持什么？
   A. 环境按需要制造了该版本
   B. 已有变异可被筛选；仅此不能判断新突变率
   C. 所有差异都不能遗传
   答案：B。先存在的版本无需由干旱定向创造，比例变化与新序列来源应分开。 复习：重读Explain首段的“发生率”与“指定结果”。

4. [vocabulary] heritable最准确表示什么？
   A. 能通过繁殖传给后代
   B. 一定有利
   C. 出生后永不变化
   答案：A。可遗传描述传递能力，不保证有利，也不保证每个后代都得到。 复习：回看词汇“可遗传”。

### English

1. [concept] What increases when AB/ab becomes Ab/aB?
   A. New alleles
   B. Combinations of existing alleles
   C. Versions at each locus necessarily double
   Answer: B. A/a and B/b remain the existing versions; changing combinations does not create sequence. Revisit: Revisit the four letter combinations on the recombination route.

2. [concept] What ordinarily follows a mutation confined to an animal skin cell?
   A. It reaches all offspring
   B. It cannot affect anything
   C. It may affect the individual but ordinarily is not inherited through gametes
   Answer: C. Cell lineage determines transmission possibilities; a somatic change can still affect the individual. Revisit: Revisit skin versus reproductive lineages.

3. [reasoning] A drought-tolerant variant exists before drought and rises afterwards. What follows?
   A. The environment created the version on demand
   B. Existing variation can be selected; this alone does not determine mutation rate
   C. No differences can be inherited
   Answer: B. A pre-existing version need not be created on demand; frequency change and sequence origins are separate questions. Revisit: Revisit mutation rate versus specified outcome in Explain.

4. [vocabulary] What does heritable mean?
   A. Can pass through reproduction to offspring
   B. Always beneficial
   C. Never changes after birth
   Answer: A. Heritable concerns transmission, not benefit or guaranteed receipt by every offspring. Revisit: Revisit heritable in Vocabulary.
