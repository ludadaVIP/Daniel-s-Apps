## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

两种动物都有翅膀，是否就一定是最近的亲属？怎样把岩层、骨骼和DNA放到同一张证据桌？

**学习目标（30分钟）**

- 比较化石、同源结构与分子证据的推理链。
- 区分共同祖先与趋同的解释。
- 用不完整证据表达有边界的结论。

### English

Two animals have wings. Must they be closest relatives? How can rocks, bones and DNA inform one explanation?

**Learning goals (30 minutes)**

- Compare fossil, homologous-structure and molecular reasoning.
- Distinguish shared ancestry from convergence.
- Express bounded conclusions from incomplete evidence.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

演化研究不是只凭一块奇怪的骨头猜故事，而是问不同观察是否支持同一个历史解释。未受翻转或扰动的沉积岩层，通常下面早于上面；化石在其中提供形态与出现顺序的线索。相对先后不同于精确年龄，还需结合适当岩石的放射性测年等方法。保存机会不均、侵蚀与再搬运都可能造成缺口，因此没有找到某化石不等于该生物从未存在。

解剖证据关注对应细节。不同用途的肢体如果共享骨骼位置、连接与发育关系，就可能支持共同祖先；单独具有翅膀或流线形却不足以确定最近亲缘，因为相似环境可以独立筛选出相似功能，这叫趋同。研究者需比较一组性状，而不是挑一项外观相似就宣布结论。模型图的骨条是原创示意，不是某标本的复原。

分子比较增加另一条证据链。本课虚构对齐的100个位点中，A与B差4处，A与C差18处，B与C差17处；在位点可比较、变化过程相近的假设下，这支持A、B较近，却不能把4处直接换成四年，也不能由一段短序列断言整棵树绝无错误。若多段独立序列、详细解剖和可靠地层共同吻合，解释更稳健。若冲突，应检查取样、测年、同源性与模型，而不是隐藏不合意记录。

### English

Evidence for evolution comes from comparing observations with historical explanations. In sedimentary layers that have not been overturned or disturbed, lower layers are generally older than higher layers. Their fossils provide information about anatomy and relative appearance through time. Relative order is different from an exact age; suitable radiometric dating and geological context can add constraints. Unequal preservation, erosion and reworking create gaps. Failure to find a fossil therefore does not prove that an organism never existed.

Anatomical reasoning examines corresponding details. Limbs used for different purposes can share bone positions, connections and developmental relationships inherited from a common ancestor. A shared function alone is less informative. Wings or streamlined shapes can arise independently under similar environmental challenges, a process called convergence. Scientists compare sets of characters rather than declaring the closest relatives from one conspicuous feature. Our bone bars are original teaching schematics, not reconstructions of particular specimens.

Molecular comparisons provide another line of evidence. In our invented alignment of 100 sites, A and B differ at four, A and C at eighteen, and B and C at seventeen. If sites are comparable and their evolutionary processes sufficiently similar, these observations support a closer A–B relationship. Four differences do not mean four years, and one short sequence cannot establish an infallible whole tree. Agreement across independent sequences, detailed anatomy and reliable fossil context strengthens an explanation. Conflicting results should prompt checks of sampling, dating, homology and model assumptions rather than deletion of inconvenient evidence.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

一项相似特征不是独立证明；化石不完整也不意味着所有历史解释同样可靠。

### English

One similar trait is not proof by itself; incomplete fossils do not make every historical explanation equally supported.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中桥梁：分子钟需要速率模型及外部时间校准；基因树不一定逐处等同物种树。比较多个独立位点能检验而非消除全部不确定性。

### English

Senior bridge: molecular clocks require rate models and external time calibration. A gene tree need not match every aspect of a species tree; independent loci test uncertainty rather than eliminating it.

## Vocabulary / 双语词汇

### 中文

- **化石 / fossil**：保存下来的古代生物遗存或活动痕迹。
- **地层 / stratum**：沉积形成的岩层。
- **同源结构 / homologous structure**：因共同祖先而对应的结构。
- **趋同 / convergence**：不同谱系独立形成相似特征。
- **共同祖先 / common ancestor**：两个或多个谱系共享的祖先。
- **分子证据 / molecular evidence**：比较DNA或蛋白质等获得的证据。

### English

- **fossil / 化石**: Preserved remains or traces of past organisms.
- **stratum / 地层**: A sedimentary rock layer.
- **homologous structure / 同源结构**: Corresponding structures inherited from common ancestry.
- **convergence / 趋同**: Independent evolution of similar features.
- **common ancestor / 共同祖先**: An ancestor shared by lineages.
- **molecular evidence / 分子证据**: Evidence from comparisons such as DNA or proteins.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] 无扰动岩层中，通常哪层较早？
   A. 下层
   B. 上层
   C. 看化石大小决定
   答案：A。沉积先后支持相对顺序，前提是未翻转扰动；大小不是年代尺。 复习：回看岩层图的下至上顺序与前提。

2. [concept] 共同具有翅膀能单独证明最近亲缘吗？
   A. 能，功能完全决定亲缘
   B. 不能，需排查趋同并比较更多特征
   C. 不能，因为结构证据完全无用
   答案：B。功能相似可能独立形成；详细同源结构仍有证据价值。 复习：回看骨骼与趋同解释。

3. [reasoning] 100位点差异AB4、AC18、BC17，最合理结论？
   A. A与B必在四年前分开
   B. A与C一定无共同祖先
   C. 在可比速率等假设下支持A/B较近，需更多位点检验
   答案：C。差异数提供条件性亲缘证据，没有时间校准不能当年数。 复习：重读Explain的100位点算例。

4. [vocabulary] convergence指什么？
   A. 相同外形必来自最近祖先
   B. 不同谱系独立形成相似特征
   C. 所有DNA序列逐渐完全相同
   答案：B。趋同涉及独立起源，不能仅凭功能相似推最近亲缘。 复习：回看词汇“趋同”。

### English

1. [concept] In undisturbed sedimentary layers, which is generally older?
   A. Lower
   B. Upper
   C. Whichever has larger fossils
   Answer: A. Deposition supports relative order if layers remain undisturbed; size is not a clock. Revisit: Revisit the lower-to-upper ordering and its assumptions.

2. [concept] Can wings alone prove the closest relationship?
   A. Yes, function determines ancestry
   B. No; consider convergence and further characters
   C. No, because anatomy is useless
   Answer: B. Similar function can evolve independently; detailed homology still provides evidence. Revisit: Revisit anatomy and convergence.

3. [reasoning] For 100 sites, differences are AB4, AC18, BC17. Best conclusion?
   A. A and B split exactly four years ago
   B. A and C have no common ancestor
   C. Subject to comparability and rates, A/B appear closer; test further loci
   Answer: C. Differences support a conditional relationship; without calibration they are not years. Revisit: Revisit the 100-site worked example.

4. [vocabulary] What is convergence?
   A. Similar appearance necessarily comes from a recent ancestor
   B. Independent acquisition of similar features
   C. All DNA becomes identical
   Answer: B. Convergence concerns independent origins; shared function alone does not establish closest ancestry. Revisit: Revisit convergence in Vocabulary.
