## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

虚构Amber与Birch的分支交换上下位置，亲缘关系会改变吗？一条河又是否保证形成两个物种？

**学习目标（30分钟）**

- 联系基因流减少、分化与生殖隔离。
- 按共同祖先解读分支而非顶端顺序。
- 识别生物学物种概念的边界。

### English

If fictional Amber and Birch exchange positions on a tree, do relationships change? Does a river guarantee two species?

**Learning goals (30 minutes)**

- Connect reduced gene flow, divergence and reproductive isolation.
- Read branching by common ancestors rather than tip order.
- Recognize limits of the biological species concept.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

种群之间若持续交换基因，某些差异可能被混合；屏障减少基因流时，各地的突变、选择和随机过程可使谱系逐渐分化。如果形成交配时间、行为、配子相容性或后代繁殖能力等屏障，恢复接触后仍可能保持生殖隔离。在适用的有性生物中，这可支持物种形成的解释。地理隔开只是可能条件，时间很长也不能单凭一条河保证两个新物种；有些隔离也不需要地理分开。

演化树把亲缘假说画成连接关系，而不是通向最高级生物的梯子。本课Amber、Birch、Cobalt全部虚构。Amber与Birch共享一个较近节点，两者组成的分支再与Cobalt相连。因此Amber与Birch是姐妹群；不能因为Cobalt被画在右边就说它更先进，或把现代Amber直接当作Birch的祖先。三个末端都代表正在比较的谱系。

把Amber、Birch的上下位置互换，甚至绕节点旋转整个分支，连接和共同祖先没有改变，结论仍相同。此图的线长只为排版，不表示时间或变化量。若另有刻度，才可以按相应单位读长度。物种划分也需要明确证据与概念：繁殖标准对无性生物和化石不能直接应用，对存在杂交的群体还需细致判断。树是可被新数据修改的科学假说，稳定的判断来自所用性状、分子证据和节点关系，而不是图的朝向。

### English

Continued gene exchange can mix some differences between populations. When barriers reduce gene flow, mutation, selection and random processes can allow lineages to diverge. Differences in mating time, behaviour, gamete compatibility or offspring fertility may eventually maintain reproductive isolation even after renewed contact. For sexual organisms where it applies, this can support an explanation of speciation. Geographic separation is a possible condition, not a guarantee: a river alone does not establish that two new species have formed. Some reproductive barriers can also arise without geographic separation.

A phylogenetic tree represents a hypothesis about shared ancestry rather than a ladder towards superior organisms. Amber, Birch and Cobalt are entirely fictional taxa. Amber and Birch share a more recent branching node with one another than either shares with Cobalt. They are sister groups. Cobalt is not more advanced because it happens to be drawn farther right, and present-day Amber is not thereby the direct ancestor of Birch. Each tip represents a lineage being compared.

Exchanging the vertical positions of Amber and Birch, or rotating an entire branch around its node, preserves the connections and common ancestors. The relationship remains unchanged. Branch lengths in this diagram serve layout only; they do not indicate elapsed time or amount of change. Those interpretations require an explicit scale. Species identification likewise needs an explicit concept and evidence. Reproductive criteria do not apply directly to asexual organisms or fossils, and hybridizing populations require care. Trees can be revised with new evidence; their meaning depends on observations and branching connections, not drawing direction.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

演化树不是进步阶梯；隔离不自动保证成种，线长没有标尺就不能当时间。

### English

A tree is not a progress ladder; separation does not guarantee speciation, and unscaled branch lengths are not time.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中桥梁：区分合子前与合子后生殖隔离屏障；拓扑与带时间刻度的树不同，基因流及不完全谱系排序可能使简单分叉图不充分。

### English

Senior bridge: distinguish prezygotic and postzygotic barriers, topology and time-scaled trees. Gene flow and incomplete lineage sorting can complicate simple branching diagrams.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

两群虚构昆虫隔河分布，但接触后仍能持续产生可育后代。为何仅据河流不能断言新物种？

### English

Two fictional insect populations live across a river but continue producing fertile offspring when in contact. Why is the river insufficient evidence for new species?

## Vocabulary / 双语词汇

### 中文

- **物种形成 / speciation**：谱系分化形成不同物种。
- **生殖隔离 / reproductive isolation**：阻碍谱系间有效基因交流的屏障。
- **基因流 / gene flow**：遗传信息通过繁殖或迁移在种群间流动。
- **演化树 / phylogenetic tree**：描述共同祖先关系的分支假说。
- **节点 / node**：树中代表分歧或共同祖先的连接点。
- **姐妹群 / sister groups**：共享最近分支祖先的两个谱系。

### English

- **speciation / 物种形成**: Divergence of lineages into distinct species.
- **reproductive isolation / 生殖隔离**: Barriers to effective gene exchange between lineages.
- **gene flow / 基因流**: Movement of genetic information between populations.
- **phylogenetic tree / 演化树**: A branching hypothesis of common ancestry.
- **node / 节点**: A point representing divergence or common ancestry.
- **sister groups / 姐妹群**: Lineages sharing an immediate common branch ancestor.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] 树中谁共享较近祖先？
   A. Amber和Birch
   B. 看哪个名字在旁边就算
   C. 最右端和最左端一定最近
   答案：A。沿连接追到节点，Amber/Birch先相遇；相邻排版不是规则。 复习：沿树枝追踪Amber与Birch到共同节点。

2. [concept] 地理隔开为何不保证物种形成？
   A. 距离再短也必定永远不繁殖
   B. 还需分化与维持有效基因交流屏障的证据
   C. 物种形成只看身体大小
   答案：B。隔开能减少基因流，但接触后是否仍有屏障需要检验。 复习：回看屏障、分化与接触三个步骤。

3. [reasoning] 旋转树的Amber/Birch分支后怎样？
   A. Birch成为更高级生物
   B. Amber成为Birch的现代祖先
   C. 共同祖先关系不变
   答案：C。拓扑由连接确定，旋转保留连接，既不排名也不创造祖先。 复习：切换旋转并比较节点。

4. [vocabulary] reproductive isolation是？
   A. 阻碍有效基因交流的生殖屏障
   B. 只要住得远
   C. 身体看起来不一样
   答案：A。隔离关注有效繁殖与基因流，距离和外观只能提供部分线索。 复习：回看生殖隔离定义。

### English

1. [concept] Which pair shares the more recent common ancestor?
   A. Amber and Birch
   B. Any adjacent names
   C. The outermost tips are always closest
   Answer: A. Trace connections: Amber/Birch meet first; adjacency is not the rule. Revisit: Trace Amber and Birch back to their shared node.

2. [concept] Why does geographic separation not guarantee speciation?
   A. Any distance permanently stops reproduction
   B. Evidence of divergence and maintained reproductive barriers is needed
   C. Speciation depends only on body size
   Answer: B. Separation may reduce gene flow; whether barriers persist after contact needs evidence. Revisit: Revisit separation, divergence and renewed contact.

3. [reasoning] What follows rotating the Amber/Birch branch?
   A. Birch becomes more advanced
   B. Modern Amber becomes Birch’s ancestor
   C. Common-ancestor relationships remain unchanged
   Answer: C. Topology is determined by connections, which rotation preserves; rotation does not rank tips or create ancestors. Revisit: Toggle rotation and compare nodes.

4. [vocabulary] What is reproductive isolation?
   A. Reproductive barriers to effective gene exchange
   B. Simply living far apart
   C. Simply looking different
   Answer: A. Isolation concerns reproduction and gene flow; distance and appearance provide only partial clues. Revisit: Revisit reproductive isolation.
