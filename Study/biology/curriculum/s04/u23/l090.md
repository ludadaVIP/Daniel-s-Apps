## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

虚构微生物A、B各占一半。A每代贡献的后代是B的两倍，三代后比例还是一半吗？

**学习目标（30分钟）**

- 用可遗传差异与后代贡献解释跨代比例变化。
- 计算归一化后代比例并比较环境反转。
- 限定适合度与简化模型的适用范围。

### English

Fictional microbes A and B start equally common. A contributes twice as many offspring per generation. Will the proportions remain equal?

**Learning goals (30 minutes)**

- Explain generational frequency change using inherited differences and offspring contributions.
- Normalize offspring contributions and compare reversed conditions.
- Qualify fitness and the scope of a simplified model.

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

三代后A比例8/9，条件反转可改变方向；每个B努力变成A；更强壮的个体在所有环境都最适合。预测不计分。

### English

A reaches 8/9 after three generations; reversing conditions can reverse the direction; Each B tries to become A; The strongest individual is best in every environment. Prediction is unscored.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

自然选择需要先有差异，其中相关差异能够遗传，并且不同类型留下后代的成功不同。个体可能生长或学习，却不是靠努力把自己改成种群的下一种版本。跨过许多繁殖轮次，留下更多后代的类型在种群中的比例可能升高。因此演化的证据是跨代分布变化，不能只比较同一个体长大前后。

本课采用虚构的无性单倍体模型：A与B稳定复制各自类型，没有迁移、突变、随机死亡及密度限制。在某条件下，每单位A贡献2份后代，每单位B贡献1份。起点各50份，后代贡献为100与50，总数150，所以A频率为100/150＝2/3，而不是把100直接当成百分比。继续三轮得到A频率8/9。这是由假设推出的确定性比例，不是任何真实物种的数量预测。

把环境反转，贡献改为A一份、B两份，选择方向也会反转；同一类型不可能仅因名称就永远最好。这称为环境依赖的适合度，关注留下可继续繁殖后代的相对成功，不等同于肌肉强壮。若起点A为零且模型禁止突变和迁入，选择也不能凭空制造A。真实种群还会受到随机漂变、基因流和多基因性状影响，需要重复观测和独立证据检验，而不能把模型曲线直接贴到现实。

### English

Natural selection requires variation, inheritance of relevant differences and unequal success in contributing offspring. Individuals can grow or learn, but effort does not turn them into a new population variant. Across reproductive rounds, types contributing more offspring can become more frequent. Evidence for evolutionary change therefore concerns distributions across generations, rather than simply comparing one individual before and after growth.

Our fictional model uses asexual haploid organisms whose types copy faithfully. It excludes migration, mutation, random mortality and density limits. Under one condition each unit of A contributes two offspring units, while B contributes one. Starting with 50 units of each gives contributions of 100 and 50. Their total is 150, so A’s next frequency is 100/150, or two thirds. A contribution of 100 is not automatically a percentage. After three reproductive rounds A reaches eight ninths. This deterministic proportion follows from stipulated assumptions; it is not a numerical forecast for any real species.

Reverse the environment so A contributes one unit and B two: the direction changes. No type is always best simply because of its label. Fitness means relative reproductive success in a particular environment, rather than muscle strength. A benefit in one condition can carry a cost elsewhere. If A starts absent and the model excludes mutation and immigration, selection cannot invent A. Real populations also experience genetic drift, gene flow and complex inheritance. Repeated observations and additional evidence are needed before applying such a simplified explanation to a real population.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

适合度不是最强壮；个体不因努力演化；没有初始变异时本模型的选择不能制造它。

### English

Fitness is not strength, individuals do not evolve through effort, and selection in this model cannot create absent variation.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中桥梁：p′＝pwA/[pwA＋(1−p)wB]将贡献归一化；二倍体还须区分等位基因与基因型频率，不能直接套用此单倍体公式。

### English

Senior bridge: p′ = pwA/[pwA + (1−p)wB] normalizes contributions. Diploid models distinguish allele and genotype frequencies and require additional assumptions.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

某抗性版本在无药环境增长较慢。用环境依赖适合度解释撤去条件后比例可能改变，不给真实用药建议。

### English

A resistance variant reproduces more slowly without the selective agent. Explain how its frequency could change when conditions change.

## Vocabulary / 双语词汇

### 中文

- **自然选择 / natural selection**：可遗传差异导致不同繁殖贡献的过程。
- **适合度 / fitness**：特定环境中相对繁殖成功。
- **频率 / frequency**：某类型占总数的比例。
- **世代 / generation**：亲代到后代的一轮。
- **选择压力 / selection pressure**：影响相对存活繁殖的环境因素。
- **权衡 / trade-off**：一种条件下的益处可能伴随其他代价。

### English

- **natural selection / 自然选择**: Differential reproductive contributions associated with heritable differences.
- **fitness / 适合度**: Relative reproductive success in a specified environment.
- **frequency / 频率**: Proportion belonging to a type.
- **generation / 世代**: One parent-to-offspring round.
- **selection pressure / 选择压力**: Environmental influence on relative survival and reproduction.
- **trade-off / 权衡**: A benefit can carry costs under other conditions.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] 自然选择改变的核心是什么？
   A. 个体按需要改变遗传版本
   B. 有遗传差异的类型对后代贡献不同
   C. 所有存活个体繁殖相同
   答案：B。差异必须联系遗传与后代贡献；仅努力或仅存活不够。 复习：回看选择三代图的亲代与后代。

2. [concept] 若A初始为0且没有突变迁入，A更有利会怎样？
   A. A仍为0
   B. 一代产生50%A
   C. 所有B自动成为A
   答案：A。选择只能改变已有类型的相对贡献；本模型没有产生A的路径。 复习：把初始A切到0并重播。

3. [reasoning] A、B各50，贡献率A2/B1，下一代A比例？
   A. 50%
   B. 100%
   C. 100/150≈66.7%
   答案：C。100份A加50份B共150份，必须以总贡献作分母。 复习：回看Explain的归一化算例。

4. [vocabulary] fitness在本课指什么？
   A. 任何环境中的强壮程度
   B. 特定条件下相对繁殖成功
   C. 个体努力学习的程度
   答案：B。适合度是环境依赖的繁殖比较，不是体格排名。 复习：回看权衡与环境反转。

### English

1. [concept] What is central to natural selection?
   A. Individuals rewrite variants on demand
   B. Inherited variants contribute different amounts to offspring
   C. All survivors reproduce equally
   Answer: B. Variation must connect to inheritance and offspring contributions; effort or survival alone is insufficient. Revisit: Revisit parents and offspring across three rounds.

2. [concept] If A is absent and mutation and immigration are excluded, what does an A advantage do?
   A. A remains absent
   B. It creates 50% A in one round
   C. Every B automatically becomes A
   Answer: A. Selection changes existing contributions; this model contains no route introducing A. Revisit: Set initial A to zero and replay.

3. [reasoning] A and B start at 50 each; rates are A2/B1. Next A frequency?
   A. 50%
   B. 100%
   C. 100/150≈66.7%
   Answer: C. 100 A plus 50 B make 150; divide by the total contribution. Revisit: Revisit the normalization calculation in Explain.

4. [vocabulary] What does fitness mean here?
   A. Strength in every environment
   B. Relative reproductive success under specified conditions
   C. How hard an individual learns
   Answer: B. Fitness compares reproduction in an environment, rather than ranking strength. Revisit: Revisit trade-offs and environment reversal.
