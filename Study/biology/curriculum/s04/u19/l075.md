## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

如果四条染色体都复制过，怎样才能让两个子细胞各拿到每一条的一个副本，而不是只保证总数平均？

### English

Once four chromosomes are copied, how can each daughter receive one copy of every chromosome rather than merely half the total?

## Predict / 先做预测

### English

Choose an initial idea without a score; revisit it using evidence: Only the total amount matters, not chromosome identity; Mitosis sends homologues A and a to opposite daughters; Copy first, then send each chromosome’s sisters to opposite poles.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

DNA必须先复制，才能让两个子细胞各得到完整信息；但“复制完毕”本身仍不保证分配正确。有丝分裂把复制后的染色体有序分配到两个子核。前期染色质凝缩，分裂装置形成；核膜在随后阶段解体，纺锤体通过动粒连接染色体；中期各条复制染色体排列，正确连接为分离准备。中学常把前期与前中期合并讲述，本课五张卡也是这种简化，不代表各阶段之间有突然的物理界线。

关键事件发生在后期：姐妹分开，每条现在称为子染色体，分别走向两端。末期染色体到达两端，核膜重建，染色体逐渐去凝缩；胞质分裂随后或重叠进行，分开细胞质。动物细胞常形成分裂沟，植物细胞形成细胞板，因此图中外形不能代表全部生物。排序的依据是机制：未建立合理连接就不能正确分离，分配之后才能重建各自的核。

用2n=4例子，A/a和B/b是两对同源染色体，可能带不同等位基因；A1/A2才是A复制出的姐妹。正常左端为A1、a1、B1、b1，右端为A2、a2、B2、b2，两端各保留A、a、B、b。若A2误去左端，就出现5对3，右端缺A来源信息，即使总数还是8也不完整。只有假设复制忠实、没有新突变且分离正确，才可以说两子细胞获得相同核遗传信息；这不表示它们此后表达、功能或所有细胞质成分都必然相同。

因此除了数目，还应检查每端染色体的身份。两个子核各四条只是必要线索；如果把同一种错误重复两次，数量看似相等也可能缺少信息。

### English

DNA must be copied before two daughters can each receive complete information, but copying alone does not ensure accurate distribution. Mitosis organises replicated chromosomes into two daughter nuclei. In prophase, chromatin condenses and division machinery forms. Subsequently, the nuclear envelope breaks down and spindle microtubules attach through kinetochores. At metaphase, replicated chromosomes align with connections preparing them for separation. School accounts often combine prophase and prometaphase; our five cards follow that simplification rather than implying abrupt physical boundaries.

The crucial anaphase event is sister separation. Each separated copy becomes a daughter chromosome and moves towards one pole. In telophase, nuclear envelopes reform and chromosomes begin to decondense. Cytokinesis divides the cytoplasm, often overlapping these events. Animal cells commonly form a cleavage furrow, whereas plant cells build a cell plate. Ordering therefore follows a causal argument: appropriate connections must precede accurate separation, and distribution precedes formation of the new nuclei.

In the worked 2n=4 example, A/a and B/b are homologous pairs that may carry different alleles. A1/A2 are replicated sisters of A. Normal poles receive A1, a1, B1, b1 and A2, a2, B2, b2, retaining every homologue origin on each side. If A2 incorrectly goes left, the result is five versus three, with the right missing A-origin information despite conservation of eight overall. Identical nuclear information assumes faithful replication, no new mutation and correct segregation. It does not guarantee identical later expression, function or every cytoplasmic component.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：有丝分裂把同源染色体彼此分开，得到半套。那更接近减数第一次分裂的核心事件；有丝分裂分开姐妹，正常维持子细胞的染色体组数。

### English

Misconception: mitosis separates homologues to halve the sets. That resembles the central event of meiosis I; mitosis separates sisters and normally maintains chromosome sets.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中衔接：姐妹染色单体的黏连与动粒连接受严格调控，纺锤体检查点可延迟未正确连接时的分离。微管动态、运动蛋白与黏连解除共同参与，不是两根线随意拉走DNA。染色体不分离可造成非整倍性；模型只显示一种错误，不预测个体结局。

### English

Senior bridge: sister cohesion and kinetochore attachments are regulated, and the spindle checkpoint can delay separation when attachment is inappropriate. Microtubule dynamics, motors and cohesion release cooperate; DNA is not simply pulled by arbitrary strings. Nondisjunction can cause aneuploidy. This activity shows one error without predicting an individual outcome.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **有丝分裂 / mitosis**: 将复制后的核遗传信息分配到两个子核的过程。
- **同源染色体 / homologous chromosomes**: 含有相应基因位置、可有不同等位基因的一对染色体。
- **姐妹染色单体 / sister chromatids**: 同一染色体复制形成的相连副本。
- **纺锤体 / spindle**: 帮助染色体定位并分离的微管系统。
- **着丝粒 / centromere**: 姐妹相连且形成动粒连接区域的染色体区域。
- **胞质分裂 / cytokinesis**: 将细胞质及细胞边界分开的过程。

### English

- **有丝分裂 / mitosis**: Distribution of replicated nuclear genetic information into two daughter nuclei.
- **同源染色体 / homologous chromosomes**: A pair with corresponding gene loci that may carry different alleles.
- **姐妹染色单体 / sister chromatids**: Connected copies produced by replication of one chromosome.
- **纺锤体 / spindle**: A microtubule system positioning and separating chromosomes.
- **着丝粒 / centromere**: Chromosomal region associated with sister cohesion and kinetochore formation.
- **胞质分裂 / cytokinesis**: Division of the cytoplasm and cellular boundary.

## Check / 检索练习

### 中文

1. [concept] 哪项是有丝分裂的合理顺序？

   A. 姐妹分离→排列→凝缩→核重建
   B. 凝缩→排列→姐妹分离→核重建
   C. 凝缩→同源配对→同源分离→再复制

   答案: B. 复制在此前S期完成；有丝分裂的关键是把姐妹副本分往两端，不是减数第一次分裂的同源分离。

   用阶段卡把前期、中期、后期、末期排好，再接胞质分裂。

2. [concept] A、a是一对同源染色体，A1、A2是A的姐妹。正常分裂时怎样分？

   A. A1与A2各去一端，每端仍有A与a来源的染色体
   B. A与a必定各去一端，因此每端只留一条同源
   C. A1与A2一起去同一端，a去另一端

   答案: A. 子细胞保留两对同源；分开的是每条复制染色体的姐妹。

   在后期静帧逐个读左端和右端的A1/a1/B1/b1及A2/a2/B2/b2。

3. [reasoning] 错误对照中出现5条对3条，为什么不能说信息正确分配？

   A. 只要总数为8，两边都完整
   B. 两边拥有的染色体身份和数目不同
   C. 因为正常有丝分裂应产生4个细胞

   答案: B. A2错误进入左端，右端缺少A来源；总量守恒与每个子细胞信息完整是两件事。

   开启错误分离，比较每端身份而不只看8的总数。

4. [vocabulary] cytokinesis与mitosis的区别是什么？

   A. 胞质分裂分开细胞质，有丝分裂分配核遗传信息
   B. 两词都专指S期DNA复制
   C. 胞质分裂专指同源染色体配对

   答案: A. 核分裂与胞质分裂通常协调，但概念不同，有时可发生核分裂而未完成胞质分裂。

   回看末期与胞质分裂两张卡。

### English

1. [concept] Which sequence describes mitosis correctly?

   A. Sister separation → alignment → condensation → nuclear reformation
   B. Condensation → alignment → sister separation → nuclear reformation
   C. Condensation → homologue pairing → homologue separation → replication

   Answer: B. Replication occurred in the preceding S phase; mitosis separates sister copies rather than homologues as in meiosis I.

   Order prophase, metaphase, anaphase and telophase cards, then add cytokinesis.

2. [concept] A/a are homologues and A1/A2 are sisters. What normally happens?

   A. A1 and A2 go to opposite poles; each pole retains A and a origins
   B. A and a must go to opposite poles, leaving one homologue per pole
   C. A1 and A2 travel together to one pole, and a to the other

   Answer: A. Daughters retain both homologous pairs; sisters of each replicated chromosome separate.

   Read all labels at both poles in the anaphase snapshot.

3. [reasoning] Why does the faulty 5-to-3 comparison fail to show correct information distribution?

   A. A total of eight ensures both sides are complete
   B. The poles differ in chromosome identity and number
   C. Because normal mitosis should produce four cells

   Answer: B. A2 wrongly enters the left pole and the right lacks its A-origin copy; conservation differs from completeness per daughter.

   Enable faulty segregation and compare identities, not only the total eight.

4. [vocabulary] How does cytokinesis differ from mitosis?

   A. Cytokinesis divides cytoplasm; mitosis distributes nuclear genetic information
   B. Both mean DNA replication in S
   C. Cytokinesis means homologue pairing

   Answer: A. Nuclear and cytoplasmic division are usually coordinated but distinct; nuclear division can occur without completed cytokinesis.

   Revisit the telophase and cytokinesis cards.
