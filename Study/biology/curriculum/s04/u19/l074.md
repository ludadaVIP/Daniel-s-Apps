## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

细胞周期不是“细胞忽然一分为二”，而是一系列受调控的过程。G1期进行生长、合成与正常代谢；S期以原DNA为模板逐步复制；G2期继续准备，并检查进入分裂所需的条件；M期包括有丝分裂以及通常伴随的胞质分裂。G1、S、G2合称间期，虽然显微镜下未必出现明显分裂形态，内部活动却十分活跃。细胞也可能离开活跃周期进入G0，不能把所有细胞画成永不停转的钟。

本课用一个二倍体2n=4细胞，即两对同源染色体。G1有4条染色体，核DNA量记作2C；C是相对于单倍体基因组DNA量的单位。S结束后，每条染色体形成相连的两条姐妹染色单体，合计8条染色单体和4C DNA，但仍计4条复制染色体，倍性仍是2n。复制的是已有组内的DNA副本，不是新增一套独立同源组。到后期姐妹分开，各自成为子染色体，尚未分开的整个细胞可暂计8条；每个未来子核却各得4条。

例如在G2发现未修复DNA损伤，调控系统可以延缓进入M，为修复留下机会。模型把它简化为暂停开关，现实还涉及多种蛋白、信号、损伤程度与细胞命运。检查点能减少风险，却不能保证所有错误都被发现，也不能仅凭“周期继续”证明DNA没有损伤。在理想复制和正确分配下，每个子细胞恢复到2n=4、2C。这里关注的是一次正常有丝分裂周期，不包括减数分裂，也不把相同时间的动画格误作相同长度的真实时期。

记录时应把时期与计数对象一起写进表头，避免不同快照看似矛盾。

### English

A cell cycle is a regulated sequence rather than a sudden split. G1 includes growth, synthesis and ordinary metabolism. S progressively copies DNA using existing molecules as templates. G2 continues preparation and checks conditions for division. M includes mitosis and usually associated cytokinesis. Together, G1, S and G2 form interphase: an active interval even when dramatic division structures are absent. Some cells leave the active cycle for G0, so a continuously rotating clock is not a model for every cell.

Consider a diploid cell with 2n=4, meaning two homologous pairs. In G1 it has four chromosomes and 2C nuclear DNA, where C expresses DNA amount relative to one haploid genome. By the end of S, every chromosome consists of two connected sister chromatids. There are eight chromatids and 4C DNA, but still four replicated chromosomes and diploid ploidy. Replication copies DNA within the existing sets; it does not add independent homologous sets. When sisters separate in anaphase, each becomes a daughter chromosome. The undivided whole cell briefly contains eight, while each future daughter nucleus receives four.

For example, unrepaired damage at G2 can delay M entry, allowing time for repair. The activity represents this with a hold switch; real regulation depends on proteins, signals, damage and possible cell fates. Checkpoints reduce risk without detecting every error. Continued progression therefore does not prove undamaged DNA. With faithful copying and distribution, each daughter returns to 2n=4 and 2C. Equal animation intervals represent neither equal stage durations nor meiosis.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：S期DNA加倍就是倍性加倍。DNA量回答“有多少DNA”，倍性回答“有几套同源染色体”；相连姐妹的产生不把2n改为4n。

### English

Misconception: doubled DNA in S doubles ploidy. DNA amount asks how much DNA is present; ploidy asks how many homologous sets. Producing joined sisters does not turn 2n into 4n.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中衔接：染色体计数依赖着丝粒及姐妹是否已分离，必须注明时期和“整细胞/每核”。DNA含量分析可区分许多2C与4C细胞，却不能只凭4C判断它一定是四倍体；还要结合周期位置、核型与细胞类型。

### English

Senior bridge: chromosome counts depend on centromeres and whether sisters have separated; specify stage and whether counting a whole cell or each nucleus. DNA-content measurements can separate many 2C and 4C populations, but 4C alone does not establish tetraploidy. Cycle position, karyotype and cell type also matter.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **细胞周期 / cell cycle**: 细胞生长、复制DNA并完成分裂的有序过程。
- **间期 / interphase**: 包括G1、S、G2的活跃准备阶段。
- **DNA复制 / DNA replication**: 以已有DNA为模板合成DNA副本。
- **姐妹染色单体 / sister chromatids**: 一条染色体复制后相连的两份副本。
- **倍性 / ploidy**: 细胞具有的同源染色体组数。
- **检查点 / checkpoint**: 评估条件并可延缓周期进程的调控环节。

### English

- **细胞周期 / cell cycle**: Ordered growth, DNA replication and division.
- **间期 / interphase**: Active preparation comprising G1, S and G2.
- **DNA复制 / DNA replication**: Production of DNA copies using existing DNA as templates.
- **姐妹染色单体 / sister chromatids**: The connected copies of one replicated chromosome.
- **倍性 / ploidy**: Number of homologous chromosome sets in a cell.
- **检查点 / checkpoint**: A regulatory control that assesses conditions and can delay progression.

### 中文

1. [concept] DNA复制主要发生在哪一期？

   A. G1期
   B. S期
   C. 有丝分裂后期

   答案: B. S期进行DNA合成；M期主要处理复制后遗传信息的分配。

   回看周期图S与M的不同任务。

2. [concept] 一个原先2n=4的细胞在G2期，哪个描述正确？

   A. 4n=8，只有4条染色单体
   B. 2n=4，4条染色体由8条染色单体组成
   C. 2n=4，DNA量与G1完全相同

   答案: B. 复制增加DNA量和姐妹数，不增加同源染色体组数；相连姐妹仍按一条复制染色体计。

   比较Explore的G1与G2库存；M计数限定中期。

3. [reasoning] G2仍有DNA损伤时暂停进入M，对后续有什么作用？

   A. 给修复留下机会，降低把损伤传给子细胞的风险
   B. 证明每个错误已被完全纠正
   C. 代替S期，不再需要复制

   答案: A. 暂停提供修复与调控机会，既不等于修复完成，也不能证明检查点绝不失效。

   开启G2损伤条件，读暂停说明，再关闭作对照。

4. [vocabulary] interphase包含什么？

   A. 只有细胞完全休眠的时期
   B. 前期、中期、后期、末期
   C. G1、S、G2，细胞持续进行多种活动

   答案: C. 间期不是“没有事情发生”；生长、代谢、复制与准备都很活跃。

   回看Explain第一段及Vocabulary的间期。

### English

1. [concept] During which phase does DNA replication mainly occur?

   A. G1
   B. S phase
   C. Anaphase

   Answer: B. S is DNA synthesis; M primarily distributes previously copied genetic information.

   Revisit the different tasks of S and M in the cycle figure.

2. [concept] Which description fits a G2 cell originally 2n=4?

   A. 4n=8, with only 4 chromatids
   B. 2n=4, with 4 chromosomes comprising 8 chromatids
   C. 2n=4, with exactly the G1 DNA amount

   Answer: B. Replication increases DNA and sister copies, not homologous sets; connected sisters count as one replicated chromosome.

   Compare G1 and G2 inventories; the M count is specifically metaphase.

3. [reasoning] How can delaying M entry when DNA damage remains in G2 help?

   A. Allow repair and reduce the risk of passing damage to daughters
   B. Prove every error has been fully corrected
   C. Replace S so replication becomes unnecessary

   Answer: A. Delay allows repair and regulation; it neither proves repair is complete nor guarantees checkpoint success.

   Enable G2 damage, read the hold explanation, then compare without it.

4. [vocabulary] What does interphase include?

   A. Only complete cellular dormancy
   B. Prophase, metaphase, anaphase and telophase
   C. G1, S and G2, with continuing cellular activity

   Answer: C. Interphase is not inactivity: growth, metabolism, replication and preparation are active.

   Revisit Explain paragraph one and interphase in Vocabulary.
