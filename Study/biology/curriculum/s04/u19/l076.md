## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

表皮细胞、肌肉细胞与神经元大多保留相同的DNA，为什么形状、蛋白质与修复能力却不同？

### English

Epidermal cells, muscle cells and neurons largely retain the same DNA. Why do their shapes, proteins and repair capacities differ?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选一个想法，不评分。学后用证据重看：分化通常要把不需要的基因全部删掉；相同基因组可因表达不同而形成不同功能；任何成熟细胞都能自行分裂并变成任何细胞。

### English

Choose an initial idea without a score; revisit it using evidence: Differentiation usually deletes every unneeded gene; A shared genome can support different functions through expression; Any mature cell can divide and become any cell type.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

一个多细胞生物的许多体细胞来自同一个受精卵，通常保留相似的核基因组，却能形成不同结构与功能。分化的重要机制是基因表达受到不同调控：某些基因较活跃，另一些表达较低，形成不同RNA与蛋白质组合。蛋白质又参与细胞形状、收缩、信号传递或屏障形成。模型中的K、M、N代表相关功能基因，H代表共同维持功能；它们是帮助推理的虚构缩写，不是现实中仅有四个基因。

分化不等于把暂时不用的基因普遍删去，也不等于每种细胞只能表达一个基因。以表皮为例，基底层的干/祖细胞能够增殖，后代逐渐分化并向表面移动；终末分化的表面角质细胞不是仍在不断分裂的基底细胞。成熟肌纤维与许多成熟神经元也不能被画成随时一分为二的普通细胞。组织的细胞更新能力必须结合具体细胞位置、状态与种类说明。

例如一个组织要补充特定细胞，除遗传信息外，还需要合适的可分裂来源、局部信号、营养与血供，并协调炎症、细胞迁移和组织支架。把模型的祖细胞来源关闭，显示的是这条补充路线受限，不是“组织永远不能以任何方式修复”。某些修复形成瘢痕，完整结构和原有功能也未必恢复。干细胞的潜能有范围，不能说任何成熟细胞都能变成任何类型；“神经”选项更不是通常能让成体神经元大量再生的证据。本课只用安全的屏幕模型讨论机制，不观察或制造个人伤口。

### English

Many body cells originate from the same fertilised egg and usually retain similar nuclear genomes, yet acquire different structures and functions. A major differentiation mechanism is regulated gene expression. Some genes are relatively active and others less expressed, producing different RNA and protein combinations. Proteins support shape, contraction, signalling or barriers. Our K, M and N symbols represent identity-related functions, while H represents shared maintenance. These fictional abbreviations help reasoning; real cells do not have only four genes.

Differentiation does not generally delete temporarily unused genes, nor does each cell express only one gene. In epidermis, basal stem and progenitor cells can proliferate. Descendants differentiate and move towards the surface. Terminal surface keratinocytes are not still-dividing basal cells. Mature muscle fibres and many mature neurons likewise should not be pictured as ordinary cells ready to split. Replacement capacity must be described for a particular location, cell state and tissue.

For a worked example, replacing a particular cell type requires more than DNA: suitable proliferating sources, local signals, nutrients and blood supply must operate with migration, inflammation and tissue scaffolds. Turning off progenitor supply in the model limits this selected replacement route; it does not prove that every possible repair process stops forever. Some repair produces scar tissue rather than fully restoring original structure and function. Stem-cell potency has limits, and an arbitrary mature cell cannot normally become any other type. In particular, the neuronal option provides no evidence of general adult neuronal regeneration. This lesson examines mechanisms on screen and does not require observing or creating a personal wound.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：干细胞意味着能变成任何细胞。不同干细胞具有不同潜能范围，自我更新也受环境和调控限制。

### English

Misconception: every stem cell can become any cell type. Stem cells have different potency ranges, and self-renewal is regulated by their environment.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中衔接：转录因子、染色质状态和细胞间信号可稳定或改变表达网络。人工重编程显示某些分化状态并非绝对不可逆，但需要特定实验条件，不能据此推断正常成熟细胞都具有全能性。组织再生与瘢痕修复也是不同结局。

### English

Senior bridge: transcription factors, chromatin states and intercellular signals can stabilise or alter expression networks. Experimental reprogramming shows that some differentiated states are not absolutely irreversible, but requires specific conditions; it does not make ordinary mature cells totipotent. Regeneration and scar repair are also different outcomes.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

一则广告只说“有干细胞就能恢复任何组织”。用潜能范围、细胞环境与功能证据提出三个需要核查的问题，不设计人体实验。

### English

An advertisement claims that stem cells restore any tissue. Use potency, cellular environment and functional evidence to frame three questions to verify, without designing a human experiment.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **分化 / differentiation**: 细胞形成较专门结构与功能的过程。
- **基因表达 / gene expression**: 利用基因信息产生功能产物的过程。
- **蛋白质 / protein**: 承担结构、催化和调控等功能的大分子。
- **干细胞 / stem cell**: 能自我更新并产生一定类型后代的细胞。
- **祖细胞 / progenitor cell**: 具有一定分化方向且增殖潜力受限制的前体细胞。
- **组织修复 / tissue repair**: 恢复受影响组织完整性或功能的多过程反应。

### English

- **分化 / differentiation**: Development of more specialised cellular structures and functions.
- **基因表达 / gene expression**: Use of gene information to produce functional products.
- **蛋白质 / protein**: A macromolecule with structural, catalytic and regulatory roles.
- **干细胞 / stem cell**: A cell able to self-renew and generate a specified range of descendants.
- **祖细胞 / progenitor cell**: A precursor with a more restricted fate and proliferative capacity.
- **组织修复 / tissue repair**: Coordinated processes restoring tissue integrity or function.

## Check / 检索练习

### 中文

1. [concept] 为什么具有相似DNA的细胞能有不同功能？

   A. 它们表达的基因组合与水平不同
   B. 每次分化都必须更换整套DNA
   C. 蛋白质不影响细胞功能

   答案: A. 不同信号和调控产生不同蛋白组合，支持结构和功能差异；不需要把共同基因组整体替换。

   对照K/M/N/H图：基因列表相同，ON/low不同。

2. [concept] 哪项正确描述组织中细胞的分裂？

   A. 所有成熟细胞以相同速度分裂
   B. 成熟表面角质细胞不断自行分裂
   C. 某些干/祖细胞可分裂补充细胞，许多成熟细胞不分裂

   答案: C. 表皮补充依赖基底层增殖细胞；终末分化表面细胞不因此获得分裂能力。

   回看Explain第二段的基底细胞与表面细胞。

3. [reasoning] 模型保留相同DNA，只关闭合适祖细胞来源后补充受限，支持什么？

   A. 有DNA就足以保证组织完全再生
   B. 细胞来源也是修复的条件，DNA存在本身不足够
   C. 所有组织添加任意干细胞都能完全修复

   答案: B. 补充需要合适来源、信号与环境；模型不是普遍治疗证据，也不表明成体神经元通常可再生。

   保持细胞类型不变，比较有/无合适祖细胞，再读模型边界。

4. [vocabulary] gene expression指什么？

   A. 把所有基因平均分给两个子细胞
   B. 利用基因信息产生功能产物
   C. 把不表达的基因从DNA中全部剪去

   答案: B. 表达是信息的使用，不是遗传信息的分配或普遍删除。

   回看Vocabulary与信号→表达→蛋白质箭头。

### English

1. [concept] Why can cells with similar DNA perform different functions?

   A. They differ in combinations and levels of gene expression
   B. Every differentiation event must replace the entire genome
   C. Proteins do not affect cellular function

   Answer: A. Signals and regulation produce different protein combinations, supporting structural and functional differences without replacing the genome.

   Compare the K/M/N/H diagram: shared gene list, different ON/low pattern.

2. [concept] Which statement about division in tissues is correct?

   A. All mature cells divide at the same rate
   B. Fully mature surface keratinocytes continually divide themselves
   C. Some stem/progenitor cells divide to replace cells; many mature cells do not

   Answer: C. Epidermal replacement depends on proliferating basal cells; terminal surface cells do not gain division ability.

   Revisit basal versus surface cells in Explain paragraph two.

3. [reasoning] The model retains DNA but limits replacement when suitable progenitors are absent. What does this support?

   A. Having DNA guarantees complete tissue regeneration
   B. Cell supply is one repair condition; DNA presence alone is insufficient
   C. Any stem cell added to any tissue ensures complete repair

   Answer: B. Replacement needs suitable sources, signals and environment. This is neither treatment evidence nor a claim of general adult neuronal regeneration.

   Hold cell type constant, compare progenitor availability and read the limits.

4. [vocabulary] What does gene expression mean?

   A. Dividing all genes equally between daughters
   B. Using gene information to make functional products
   C. Cutting every unexpressed gene out of DNA

   Answer: B. Expression is use of information, not its distribution or general deletion.

   Revisit Vocabulary and the signals → expression → proteins arrows.
