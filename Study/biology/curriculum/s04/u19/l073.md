## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

把一个细胞的每条边放大两倍，它交换物质的表面与需要供应的内部，会一起增加两倍吗？

### English

If every edge of a cell doubles, do its exchanging surface and the interior needing supplies both merely double?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选一个想法，不评分。学后用证据重看：边长加倍，面积与体积都加倍；边长加倍，体积增长比面积更快；只要体积相同，任何形状的面积就相同。

### English

Choose an initial idea without a score; revisit it using evidence: Doubling length doubles both area and volume; Doubling length increases volume faster than area; Equal-volume shapes always have equal surface areas.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

细胞需要通过表面交换氧气、营养物与代谢产物，内部却有整个体积的物质需要供应。把细胞暂时简化为边长l的立方体，六个面的总面积为6l²，体积为l³，因此面积/体积比为6/l。若l用微米表示，面积单位是µm²，体积是µm³，比例则为µm⁻¹，不能把它当作没有单位的百分数。

例如l从2 µm变为4 µm，面积由24增加到96 µm²，增长四倍；体积从8增加到64 µm³，增长八倍。比例由3下降到1.5 µm⁻¹。每单位体积可以分配的表面较少，而且内部某些位置离膜更远。如果代谢需求随体积增长，这两件事就可能使单靠表面交换与短程扩散更难满足需求。这里的“可能”很关键：模型没有测出某个真实细胞的耗氧量或运输速度。

细胞也不是只能缩小。保持l³的体积，把三条边改为4l、l、l/4，扁平形的表面积会增加，某些扩散路径会缩短。褶皱、细长形状与内部运输也可能帮助解决供应问题，但有结构与维持成本。真实大小还受浓度梯度、膜运输蛋白数量、能量、基因调控及细胞功能影响。红细胞、卵细胞或长神经元各有不同结构与任务，所以不能从一个面积比推出所有细胞的统一最大尺寸，更不能把比例下降直接说成交换速率按相同比例下降。

比较形状时应保持体积与其他交换条件一致，才能把面积差异归因于形状。若同时改变膜厚或底物浓度，就增加了另一种解释；因此几何对照与真实生理实验不能混为一谈。

### English

Cells exchange nutrients, oxygen and waste through their surfaces, while an entire interior needs supplies. Temporarily model a cell as a cube of edge length l. Its six faces provide area 6l², and its volume is l³. Dividing gives an area-to-volume ratio of 6/l. With length in micrometres, area is in µm², volume in µm³ and the ratio in µm⁻¹. The ratio is not a percentage.

For a worked example, increasing l from 2 to 4 µm increases area from 24 to 96 µm², a fourfold change. Volume increases from 8 to 64 µm³, an eightfold change. The ratio falls from 3 to 1.5 µm⁻¹. Each unit of interior has less surface available, and some internal locations lie farther from the membrane. If metabolic demand grows with volume, surface exchange and short-distance diffusion may become less adequate. However, these calculations do not measure a real cell’s oxygen consumption or transport rate.

Shape offers another possibility. Dimensions 4l, l and l/4 retain volume l³ while increasing area and shortening some diffusion paths. Folds, elongated forms and internal transport can also help, with structural and maintenance costs. Actual limits depend on concentration gradients, membrane transport proteins, energy supply, regulation and cellular function. Red blood cells, eggs and long neurons meet different demands. Geometry therefore identifies a constraint rather than a universal maximum size, and halving the geometric ratio does not establish that measured exchange rate halves.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：细胞越小越好。小细胞也必须容纳必要结构；结构与功能需要权衡。面积/体积比是约束之一，不是唯一评分标准。

### English

Misconception: smaller is always better. Cells must accommodate necessary structures; function involves trade-offs. Area-to-volume ratio is one constraint, not a complete performance score.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中衔接：在其他条件相近时，扩散通量与可用面积、浓度差有关，与路径长度有关；扩散所需时间常随距离平方增大。这些关系各有假设，不能把它们与6/l合成一个适用于所有细胞的固定速率公式。主动运输与胞内流动还会改变简单扩散的适用范围。

### English

Senior bridge: under specified conditions, diffusive flux depends on available area, concentration difference and path length; characteristic diffusion time often grows with distance squared. These relationships have assumptions and cannot be combined with 6/l into one universal cellular rate formula. Active transport and internal flow further change the picture.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **表面积 / surface area**: 物体与外界接触的表面总面积。
- **体积 / volume**: 物体占据的三维空间大小。
- **面积/体积比 / surface-area-to-volume ratio**: 表面积除以体积，单位为长度的倒数。
- **扩散距离 / diffusion distance**: 物质由一处扩散到另一处需要跨越的距离。
- **浓度梯度 / concentration gradient**: 不同位置间的浓度差异。
- **代谢需求 / metabolic demand**: 细胞活动对物质和能量的需求。

### English

- **表面积 / surface area**: Total area of the surface exposed to the surroundings.
- **体积 / volume**: Amount of three-dimensional space occupied.
- **面积/体积比 / surface-area-to-volume ratio**: Surface area divided by volume, with inverse-length units.
- **扩散距离 / diffusion distance**: Distance a substance must cross by diffusion.
- **浓度梯度 / concentration gradient**: Difference in concentration across positions.
- **代谢需求 / metabolic demand**: Requirement for matter and energy supporting cellular activity.

### 中文

1. [concept] 边长l的立方体，面积/体积比是多少？

   A. 6/l，单位为长度⁻¹
   B. 6l，单位为长度
   C. l³，单位为体积

   答案: A. 六个面的面积6l²除以l³得6/l；比例不是无单位的数。

   回看Explain第一段，把µm²除以µm³。

2. [concept] 为什么扁平细胞在相同体积下可能更利于交换？

   A. 体积不变时所有几何量都相同
   B. 表面积可增加，内部到表面距离可缩短
   C. 扁平形不再需要运输蛋白

   答案: B. 相同体积并不保证相同面积；实际交换仍受膜性质和梯度等影响。

   比较Explore表中的两行边长、体积与面积。

3. [reasoning] 立方体边长由2 µm变为4 µm，哪组结果正确？

   A. 面积24→48；体积8→16
   B. 面积24→96；体积8→64；比例3→1.5 µm⁻¹
   C. 面积24→96；体积8→32；比例不变

   答案: B. 边长×2，面积×4、体积×8，因此比例减半；这不是交换速率减半的测量。

   把滑块先放2再放4，记录三列数值。

4. [vocabulary] diffusion distance最准确的含义是什么？

   A. 扩散需要跨越的路程
   B. 每秒通过膜的分子数
   C. 细胞表面与体积的比值

   答案: A. 距离、速率与几何比值是不同量；较长扩散距离通常使扩散运输更费时。

   回看Vocabulary的扩散距离，指认图中的内外位置。

### English

1. [concept] What is the area-to-volume ratio of a cube of edge l?

   A. 6/l, with units of length⁻¹
   B. 6l, with units of length
   C. l³, with volume units

   Answer: A. Six faces give 6l²; dividing by l³ gives 6/l. This ratio has units.

   Revisit Explain paragraph one and divide µm² by µm³.

2. [concept] Why may flattening favour exchange at the same volume?

   A. Every geometric quantity stays equal when volume is equal
   B. Area can increase and internal distances to the surface can shorten
   C. Flattened cells no longer need transport proteins

   Answer: B. Equal volume does not imply equal area; membrane properties and gradients still matter.

   Compare both rows of edges, volumes and areas in Explore.

3. [reasoning] A cube grows from 2 µm to 4 µm per edge. Which result is correct?

   A. Area 24→48; volume 8→16
   B. Area 24→96; volume 8→64; ratio 3→1.5 µm⁻¹
   C. Area 24→96; volume 8→32; ratio unchanged

   Answer: B. Doubling length multiplies area by four and volume by eight, halving the ratio; this does not measure a halving of exchange rate.

   Set the slider to 2 then 4 and record all three quantities.

4. [vocabulary] What does diffusion distance mean?

   A. The distance crossed by diffusion
   B. Number of molecules crossing a membrane per second
   C. Ratio of cellular surface area to volume

   Answer: A. Distance, rate and geometric ratio are different quantities; longer diffusion paths generally take more time.

   Revisit diffusion distance in Vocabulary and identify inner and outer positions.
