## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**ATP把能量转移接到具体工作。** 细胞合成物质、运输离子或改变蛋白质形状时，常把需要能量的变化与ATP水解耦联。常写ATP＋H₂O→ADP＋Pi；在通常细胞条件下，这个整体反应净释放可用自由能。蛋白质可通过磷酸基团转移或结构变化把反应连接起来，因此不是先产生一团“能量液体”，再随意倒给任何活动。

**断键本身并不放出净能量。** 断开化学键需要能量；整个水解反应同时涉及形成新键、产物相互作用及与水的作用。只有比较反应物与产物的整体状态，才能说明净变化有利。把ATP末端键说成“一剪断就无条件释放能量”，会掩盖反应体系。真实可用能量还依赖ATP、ADP、Pi的浓度等条件。

**短期载体要不断再生。** ADP与Pi可在输入来自呼吸或光反应的能量后重新形成ATP；这不是能量创造，也不是把水解倒放就免费恢复。ATP通常快速周转，脂肪和淀粉等更适合较长期储存。模型从3代币开始，需求5，无再生只能完成3、缺2；输入能量再生4后，总共可用7，完成5、余2。账式3＋4＝5＋2统计的是代币收支，不能推出每次真实泵动作都恰耗1ATP，也不能把数字理解为焦耳。

**供给和需求要分开问。** 保持初始三、再生四不变，若需求升到九，最多只能完成七项，仍缺两项；把滑块调大没有输入新能量。若需求只有一项，账面剩六代币，也不能理解成真实细胞会把ATP长期堆积不再调节。这个模型假定再生所需ADP与Pi可用，只练收支与耦联方向；完整细胞还会调节供能速度、底物利用和活动强度。

### English

**ATP connects energy transfer to particular work.** Cells often couple an energy-requiring change, such as ion transport or molecular synthesis, to ATP hydrolysis. The simplified expression is ATP + H₂O → ADP + Pi. Under typical cellular conditions the overall reaction releases usable free energy. Proteins can link the reactions through phosphate transfer or a change of shape. This is a physical coupling mechanism, not a puddle of energy poured into any process.

**Breaking a bond alone does not release net energy.** Bond breaking requires energy. Hydrolysis also includes formation of new bonds, product interactions and interactions with water. The net change depends on the overall difference between reactants and products. Describing the terminal bond as a switch that releases energy merely when cut hides this accounting. Actual free-energy change also depends on conditions including ATP, ADP and phosphate concentrations.

**A short-term carrier must be regenerated.** Energy supplied by processes such as respiration or light reactions can support formation of ATP from ADP and Pi. Regeneration transfers energy; it does not create it. ATP generally turns over rapidly, while substances such as starch and fats are better suited to longer storage. Our model starts with3 ATP tokens and requests5 actions. Without regeneration,3 finish and2 remain unmet. Energy input regenerating4 tokens provides7 in total;5 actions finish and2 tokens remain. The balance3+4=5+2 counts tokens, not joules or the ATP cost of every real molecular pump.

**Separate supply from demand.** With3 starting and4 regenerated tokens, demand9 permits7 actions and leaves2 unmet. Moving the demand slider supplies no energy. The model assumes ADP and phosphate are available for regeneration; real cells also regulate supply rate, substrate use and work intensity.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“ATP只要断键就创造能量。”反例：单独断键要耗能；需要考虑整个水解反应与耦联，而且ADP再生ATP必须输入能量。

### English

“Breaking an ATP bond creates energy.” Counterexample: breaking a bond requires energy; net hydrolysis and coupling must be considered, and regenerating ATP requires an energy input.

## Depth / 高中桥梁

### 中文

高中桥梁：用自由能变化ΔG描述整体反应方向。负ΔG过程可以通过共同中间体耦联正ΔG过程；ΔG不是只由某一根键决定。

### English

Senior bridge: use free-energy change ΔG to discuss overall reaction direction. A negative-ΔG process can couple to a positive-ΔG process through a shared intermediate; ΔG is not determined by one bond alone.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **ATP / ATP** — 腺苷三磷酸，常见的细胞短期能量转移载体。
- **ADP / ADP** — 腺苷二磷酸，可在输入能量后与Pi形成ATP。
- **无机磷酸 / inorganic phosphate (Pi)** — ATP水解产生并可在再生时利用的磷酸组分。
- **水解 / hydrolysis** — 水参与使化学键断裂的反应。
- **能量耦联 / energy coupling** — 把净有利过程与需要能量的过程联系起来。
- **磷酸化 / phosphorylation** — 把磷酸基团加入分子的过程。

### English

- **ATP / ATP** — Adenosine triphosphate, a common short-term cellular energy-transfer carrier.
- **ADP / ADP** — Adenosine diphosphate, which can form ATP with Pi when energy is supplied.
- **无机磷酸 / inorganic phosphate (Pi)** — Phosphate released in ATP hydrolysis and used in ATP regeneration.
- **水解 / hydrolysis** — A reaction involving water in the cleavage of a chemical bond.
- **能量耦联 / energy coupling** — Linking a net favorable process with one requiring energy.
- **磷酸化 / phosphorylation** — Addition of a phosphate group to a molecule.
