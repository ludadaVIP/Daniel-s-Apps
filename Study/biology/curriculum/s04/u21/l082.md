## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**速率由一套条件共同决定。** 叶片吸收光能，但还需要CO₂、水、适合的温度和正常工作的细胞。本模型固定充足水分、叶面积、矿质供应及其他条件，用无量纲的光照与CO₂指数和摄氏温度作输入，输出虚构相对速率。数字并非实测，不代表某种植物的每秒产糖量，更不能直接换算为长高或干物质增加。

**先做配对比较，再命名限制因素。** 模型把光照能力设为min(100,2×光照指数)，CO₂能力也相同；温度能力在25°C为100，离开25°C每度降低4。当前速率取三者最小值。光照50、CO₂20、25°C时，能力是100、40、100，速率40。只把光照加到100，最小值仍40；再把CO₂加到50，速率才达到100。这支持“在前一组条件下CO₂限制速率”，而不是“光照无用”。

**曲线形状有边界。** 固定低CO₂时，加光后平台很早出现；增加CO₂可抬高平台，直到其他因素限制。温度过低可使反应慢，过高又会妨碍细胞过程，所以不能一直增温。模型在0和50°C取零，是选定的教学边界，不是所有植物在这些温度必然停止光合作用。若两种能力同为40，单独提高其中一个仍可能无效，说明共同限制。真实响应常更平滑，还受气孔、水分胁迫等相互作用影响；这里的最小值只是帮助比较的规则。

**检验一个新的情境。** 光照15、CO₂60、20°C时，三项能力为30、100、80，速率30。只加CO₂仍无效；只把光照改40后，光能力变80，速率也到80，此时光照与温度共同限制。因而“哪种因素最重要”没有脱离条件的唯一答案。记录时写全输入与输出，才能让别人重现比较；一条没有条件说明的曲线不足以支持所有环境下的结论。

### English

**A rate belongs to a set of conditions.** A leaf needs light, carbon dioxide, water and functioning cells at a suitable temperature. This model holds water availability, leaf area, mineral supply and other conditions fixed. Light and CO₂ inputs are dimensionless indices, while temperature is in degrees Celsius. Output is an invented relative photosynthesis rate. It is neither a measured sugar-production rate for a species nor a prediction of height or dry-mass growth.

**Use a controlled comparison before naming the bottleneck.** Light capacity is min(100, twice the light index), and CO₂ capacity follows the same rule. Temperature capacity is 100 at 25°C and falls by four for every degree away from that value. The displayed rate is the smallest capacity. At light 50, CO₂ 20 and 25°C, capacities are 100, 40 and 100, giving rate 40. Raising only light to 100 still gives 40. Raising CO₂ to 50 then gives 100. This supports CO₂ limitation in the earlier conditions; it does not show that light is unnecessary.

**Interpret the shape within its boundaries.** With low CO₂, light response reaches a plateau early. More CO₂ can raise that plateau until something else limits. Temperature need not increase rate indefinitely: low temperatures can slow reactions, while excessive temperatures disrupt cellular processes. Zero values at 0°C and 50°C are chosen model boundaries, not universal biological thresholds. If two capacities tie at 40, raising either one alone can leave rate unchanged. Real responses are smoother and involve interactions such as water stress and stomatal closure. The minimum rule is a comparison aid, not a complete physiological mechanism.

**Test a new case.** At light15,CO₂60 and20°C, capacities30,100,80 give rate30. More CO₂ alone cannot help. Raising light to40 gives rate80, jointly limited by light and temperature. There is no single most important factor independent of conditions; preserve inputs so others can reproduce comparisons.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“曲线平台表示再也不需要该因素。”反例：把光照降到0，速率也降到0；平台只说明增加光照在当前其他条件下无额外效果。

### English

“A plateau means the factor is no longer needed.” Counterexample: reducing light to zero also reduces the modeled rate to zero. A plateau means extra light has no further effect under the current other conditions.

## Depth / 高中桥梁

### 中文

高中桥梁：真实光合气体交换常区分总光合作用与扣除呼吸后的净交换。测量CO₂交换不等于直接测量整株生长，仍需叶面积、呼吸和时间尺度等资料。

### English

Senior bridge: gas-exchange studies distinguish gross photosynthesis from net exchange after respiration. CO₂ exchange does not directly measure whole-plant growth; leaf area, respiratory losses and timescale also matter.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

设置光照20、CO₂20、25°C。先分别只增加一个指数到50，再同时增加两个。用40、40、40、100说明共同限制，写出公平比较应固定的条件。

### English

Set light 20, CO₂ 20 and 25°C. Raise either index alone to 50, then both. Use 40, 40, 40, 100 to explain joint limitation and name the conditions a fair comparison must hold fixed.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **限制因素 / limiting factor** — 在给定条件下约束速率的因素。
- **饱和 / saturation** — 继续增加某因素不再显著提高速率。
- **控制变量 / controlled variable** — 比较中保持不变的条件。
- **相对速率 / relative rate** — 用约定刻度表达的快慢，不一定有实际单位。
- **最适温度 / optimum temperature** — 特定条件下速率最高的温度，不是所有物种的常数。
- **共同限制 / co-limitation** — 多个因素同时约束当前过程的情况。

### English

- **限制因素 / limiting factor** — A factor constraining rate under specified conditions.
- **饱和 / saturation** — Further increase in a factor no longer substantially raises rate.
- **控制变量 / controlled variable** — A condition held constant in a comparison.
- **相对速率 / relative rate** — Rate expressed on a defined scale, not necessarily with physical units.
- **最适温度 / optimum temperature** — Temperature giving highest rate under specified conditions, not a universal constant.
- **共同限制 / co-limitation** — A situation in which several factors constrain the current process.
