## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**细胞呼吸不是吸气动作。** 肺部通气帮助人体交换气体，细胞中的代谢反应才把糖等物质的化学能转移到ATP等可用形式。糖酵解在细胞质中进行，一分子葡萄糖转为两个丙酮酸，先投入再生成ATP，净得2个ATP；这一步不直接使用氧。后续的有氧分解以氧为末端电子受体，通常能比仅靠糖酵解得到更多ATP，但实际产量受细胞与条件影响，不应给所有生物统一写36或38。

**缺氧不自动等于能量归零。** 糖酵解需要可循环使用的NAD⁺。人细胞的乳酸途径把丙酮酸还原为乳酸，从而再生NAD⁺，帮助糖酵解继续；酵母的典型酒精发酵则形成乙醇和CO₂。面团气泡可以与后者相联系，但不能据此说人的肌肉也会放出这些产物。发酵本身的再生步骤并不额外产生这2个ATP；这笔净收益来自糖酵解。

**术语要说明范围。** 一些学校用“无氧呼吸”宽泛指不使用氧获得能量，包括上述发酵。严格微生物学中，无氧呼吸使用电子传递链和氧以外的末端受体，例如某些途径使用硝酸盐；发酵不属于这种狭义呼吸。这里的三个按钮比较有氧路线与两种发酵，不代表所有无氧代谢。氧供应也不是所有细胞选择路线的唯一开关，真实细胞可能同时使用多条途径。

**把数值用于合适的范围。** 若给定五份葡萄糖都完成糖酵解，这一路径净得到十份ATP；不是说一个人的细胞总共只得到十份，因为其余反应、底物和实际时间还未指定。比较有氧与发酵时，应同时列出产物和途径证据，不只看有无气泡。没有气泡也不能证明没有代谢，因为乳酸路线不要求放出CO₂，而许多变化肉眼看不见。

### English

**Cellular respiration is not the act of breathing.** Ventilation helps the human body exchange gases; cellular reactions transfer chemical energy from substances such as glucose into usable forms including ATP. Glycolysis occurs in the cytosol. It converts one glucose into two pyruvates and produces a net two ATP after an initial ATP investment. This stage does not directly require oxygen. Later aerobic stages use oxygen as the final electron acceptor and generally yield more ATP than glycolysis alone. Yield varies with cells and conditions; 36 or 38 is not a universal total.

**Limited oxygen does not automatically mean zero usable energy.** Glycolysis needs a renewable supply of NAD⁺. In human lactate fermentation, reducing pyruvate to lactate regenerates NAD⁺, allowing glycolysis to continue. A representative yeast fermentation route produces ethanol and carbon dioxide instead. Gas bubbles in dough fit the yeast route, not a claim that human muscle makes ethanol. The regeneration steps of fermentation do not themselves supply the net two ATP: that accounting belongs to glycolysis.

**State the scope of the terminology.** Some school courses use anaerobic respiration broadly for energy metabolism without oxygen, including fermentation. In stricter microbiological usage, anaerobic respiration employs an electron-transport chain with a terminal acceptor other than oxygen, such as nitrate in some pathways. Fermentation lacks that respiratory chain. Our buttons compare aerobic metabolism with two fermentation routes, not all anaerobic metabolism. Oxygen supply is also not the sole switch determining every cell’s pathway; real cells can use several pathways together.

**Keep calculations within their scope.** Five glucose units completing glycolysis net ten ATP through that pathway, not necessarily ten ATP for an entire cell. Compare products and pathway evidence, rather than bubbles alone: the human lactate route need not release CO₂, and many metabolic changes are invisible.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“缺氧时所有细胞都制造乳酸。”反例：酵母典型酒精发酵产生乙醇和CO₂；某些微生物还可使用氧以外的受体进行无氧呼吸。

### English

“All cells make lactate without oxygen.” Counterexample: yeast alcoholic fermentation produces ethanol and CO₂; some microbes instead respire using non-oxygen electron acceptors.

## Depth / 高中桥梁

### 中文

高中桥梁：追踪NADH→NAD⁺，解释电子收支与底物水平磷酸化。比较线粒体电子传递链驱动的ATP合成，避免把全部ATP生成都归到线粒体。

### English

Senior bridge: track NADH → NAD⁺ and connect electron accounting to substrate-level phosphorylation. Compare ATP synthesis driven by mitochondrial electron transport, without placing all ATP production in mitochondria.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **糖酵解 / glycolysis** — 把葡萄糖转为丙酮酸并净获得少量ATP的途径。
- **有氧呼吸 / aerobic respiration** — 以氧为末端电子受体的细胞呼吸。
- **发酵 / fermentation** — 再生NAD⁺以支持糖酵解的途径，不使用呼吸电子传递链。
- **乳酸 / lactate** — 人细胞乳酸发酵途径的主要有机产物。
- **乙醇 / ethanol** — 酵母酒精发酵的一种有机产物。
- **电子受体 / electron acceptor** — 在电子转移过程中接收电子的物质。

### English

- **糖酵解 / glycolysis** — A pathway converting glucose to pyruvate with a small net ATP yield.
- **有氧呼吸 / aerobic respiration** — Cellular respiration with oxygen as the final electron acceptor.
- **发酵 / fermentation** — Routes regenerating NAD⁺ to sustain glycolysis without a respiratory electron-transport chain.
- **乳酸 / lactate** — The major organic product of the human lactate fermentation route.
- **乙醇 / ethanol** — An organic product of yeast alcoholic fermentation.
- **电子受体 / electron acceptor** — A substance receiving electrons during electron transfer.
