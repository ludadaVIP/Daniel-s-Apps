# 虚构叶片的限制因素证据档案 / A limiting-factor evidence file for a fictional leaf

## 中文

在L082保留至少4组设置和速率：光50/CO₂20/25°C；只把光改100；再只把CO₂改50；另测光20/CO₂20的共同限制。为每次公平比较说明固定条件。用数值证据解释何时加光无效、何时加CO₂有效，并画一条饱和曲线。联系L081区分碳来源与氧来源，联系L083/L084说明呼吸支持ATP再生。用一句英文注明：数据虚构、水分等条件固定、光合速率不能直接预测实际生长。

提交内容：主张、原始记录、计算、机制推理、一项不确定性与一句英文结论。评价关注证据是否与主张匹配，不能只报答案。屏幕内记录即可完成。

## English

Keep at least4 L082 settings and rates: light50/CO₂20/25°C; change only light to100; then only CO₂ to50; also investigate joint limitation at light20/CO₂20. State fixed conditions in each fair comparison. Use numerical evidence to explain ineffective extra light and effective extra CO₂, and draw a saturation curve. Connect L081 carbon and oxygen sources with L083/L084 respiration and ATP regeneration. Add an English limitation: invented data, water and other conditions fixed, and photosynthesis rate cannot directly predict actual growth.

Submit a claim, raw records, calculations, mechanistic reasoning, one uncertainty and an English conclusion. Judge whether evidence supports the claim rather than reporting answers alone. The task can be completed entirely on screen.
