## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**把方程当作物质账本。** 中学常用净式为6CO₂＋6H₂O→C₆H₁₂O₆＋6O₂，箭头注明光能输入。左侧有6个碳、12个氢、18个氧，右侧糖贡献6碳12氢6氧，氧气贡献12氧，合计相同。原子没有凭空出现，光能也不是一种加入糖中的原子。根提供水与矿质元素，但糖的碳骨架主要由空气中的CO₂贡献。

**来源不能只靠约分后的式子猜。** 用可识别的氧同位素追踪水，能发现叶片释放的O₂来自水的氧。若要画完整的水收支，可写6CO₂＋12H₂O→C₆H₁₂O₆＋6O₂＋6H₂O；约去两侧6H₂O才得到净式。因此不能拿净式左侧6个水的氧去逐个配给右侧12个氧气原子。来源图与净收支回答不同问题。

**总体反应不是机器内部录像。** 叶绿体中，吸收光能、分解水、传递电子、生成ATP及还原力，与把CO₂并入有机物有关联，却经过许多受酶控制的步骤。这里的葡萄糖表示总体碳水化合物收支，不意味着第一步直接产出一粒葡萄糖。光合作用需要能量输入，把部分光能转为化学能；呼吸释放可用能量，两者涉及不同反应体系。即使总体式看似相反，也不能把它们说成同一条通道倒着走。

**再做一次可检查的计算。** 若只把净物质收支放大到三份葡萄糖，系数应同时乘三：18CO₂、18H₂O对应3C₆H₁₂O₆和18O₂。糖中共18个碳，必须由18个CO₂的碳提供；不能只把糖的系数改三而保持输入不变。这只是扩大账本的比例，不表示真实叶片每次反应都会整齐分三组，也不能用这组整数算反应速度。

### English

**Read an equation as an account of matter.** A familiar net equation is 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂, with light energy supplied. Count six carbon, twelve hydrogen and eighteen oxygen atoms on each side. Sugar contributes six oxygen atoms on the right and oxygen gas contributes twelve. Atoms are rearranged rather than created. Light energy is not an extra atom added to sugar. Roots supply water and mineral nutrients, while the carbon skeleton of carbohydrate comes mainly from carbon dioxide.

**Accounting does not establish atom provenance.** Oxygen-isotope tracing establishes that released oxygen gas originates from water. To represent water before cancellation, an overall expression can use 6CO₂ + 12H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ + 6H₂O. Cancelling six waters from both sides gives the net expression. It would therefore be misleading to assign the six water oxygen atoms on the left of the net expression individually to all twelve oxygen atoms in the gas. A source trace answers a different question from a net balance.

**The overall equation is not a recording of the machinery.** Light absorption, water oxidation, electron transfer, ATP formation and carbon fixation involve many enzyme-controlled reactions. Glucose in the familiar equation summarizes carbohydrate accounting; it is not necessarily the first direct product. Photosynthesis transfers part of the incoming light energy into chemical energy. Respiration makes energy available through different pathways. Their overall equations may look opposite, but their mechanisms are not one pathway simply running backwards.

**Scale the account carefully.** Three glucose units require net coefficients18CO₂ and18H₂O, yielding3C₆H₁₂O₆ and18O₂. Their18 carbon atoms require18 carbon-dioxide inputs. Scaling coefficients preserves accounting; it neither groups real reactions into tidy batches nor specifies a reaction rate.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“O₂一定来自CO₂，因为式中有CO₂。”反例：标记水中的氧可在放出的O₂中追踪到标记；净式本身不显示这种来源证据。

### English

“O₂ must come from CO₂ because CO₂ appears in the equation.” Counterexample: labelled water oxygen can be traced into released O₂; the net equation alone does not reveal this evidence.

## Depth / 高中桥梁

### 中文

高中桥梁：区分光依赖反应与卡尔文循环。前者提供ATP和NADPH，后者使用它们进行碳同化；“不直接使用光子”不等于只能在黑夜运行。

### English

Senior bridge: distinguish light-dependent reactions from the Calvin cycle. The former supply ATP and NADPH; the latter uses them in carbon assimilation. Not directly using photons does not mean operating only at night.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **光合作用 / photosynthesis** — 利用光能把无机碳转化为有机物的过程。
- **净反应 / net reaction** — 抵消两侧相同物质后得到的总体收支。
- **反应物 / reactant** — 在所写反应中被消耗的物质。
- **产物 / product** — 在所写反应中形成的物质。
- **碳固定 / carbon fixation** — 把无机碳并入有机分子的过程。
- **同位素示踪 / isotope tracing** — 利用可识别的同元素原子追踪物质来源。

### English

- **光合作用 / photosynthesis** — Processes using light energy to incorporate inorganic carbon into organic molecules.
- **净反应 / net reaction** — Overall accounting after cancelling substances appearing on both sides.
- **反应物 / reactant** — A substance consumed in the reaction as written.
- **产物 / product** — A substance formed in the reaction as written.
- **碳固定 / carbon fixation** — Incorporation of inorganic carbon into organic molecules.
- **同位素示踪 / isotope tracing** — Following identifiable atoms of an element to investigate material origins.
