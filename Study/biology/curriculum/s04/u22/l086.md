## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**先说明为什么要减套数。** 本虚构生物体细胞有两对同源染色体，2n＝4；每个配子只保留一套n＝2，受精时2＋2恢复4。如果配子也有4条且都是两套，代代融合会使套数增加。单倍体不是“只有一条”，而是每类染色体一份。这个模型展示四个减数分裂产物；不同生物形成成熟配子的后续过程不一定完全相同。

**一次复制，分两次处理。** 起点设在S期之后：一个细胞中有4条复制染色体、8个DNA分子，记作2n、4C。同源染色体先配对，可在非姐妹间交换对应片段。分裂I把同源分到两个细胞，姐妹仍连接；每细胞有2条复制染色体、4个DNA分子，即n、2C。两次分裂之间不再复制DNA。分裂II把姐妹分开，形成四个细胞，每个有2条染色体、2个DNA分子，即n、1C。

**变化与守恒要同时检查。** 从起点到终点，所有产物合计的DNA分子仍为8，单个细胞的DNA量逐次减少；n表示套数，C表示DNA相对量。交叉互换交换的是同源的非姐妹之间对应片段，并非随便交换两种不同染色体的基因。第二对独立改变方向又会改变长短染色体的来源组合。模型只展示一种交换与两种排列，不宣称真实配子只有所画的几种；正常分配的计数也不包含不分离等异常。

**合计与每细胞不能混用。** 分裂I后的两个细胞各四个DNA分子，合计八；分裂II后四个细胞各两个，合计也八。此时所有细胞合计染色体数却从四变八，因为分开的姐妹各自获得染色体身份，不是又复制了一轮DNA。比较显微图时要先圈出单个细胞边界，再作计数；若把四个产物放在一栏，必须清楚标为所有产物的总计。

### English

**Explain the need to reduce chromosome sets.** Our fictional organism has two homologous pairs,2n=4. Each gamete receives one set,n=2, so fertilization restores4 chromosomes by2+2. If gametes retained two sets, repeated fusion would increase set number. Haploid means one of each chromosome type, not a total of one chromosome. The model shows four meiotic products; subsequent production of mature gametes differs among organisms.

**Replicate once, divide twice.** The starting cell has completed S phase:4 replicated chromosomes and8 DNA molecules, written2n,4C. Homologues pair, and corresponding segments can exchange between non-sister chromatids. Meiosis I separates homologues into two cells while sisters remain connected. Each cell now has2 replicated chromosomes and4 DNA molecules:n,2C. DNA does not replicate again between divisions. Meiosis II separates sisters, yielding four cells with2 chromosomes and2 DNA molecules each:n,1C.

**Check both change and conservation.** Across all products, total DNA molecules remain8 from the post-S starting point, while DNA amount per cell falls through the divisions. n measures sets and C measures relative DNA content. Crossing over exchanges corresponding segments of homologues, not arbitrary pieces from unrelated chromosome types. Reversing the orientation of the second homologous pair changes combinations of long and short chromosome origins. Our switches show one crossover and two orientations, not every possible gamete. The normal-segregation counts also exclude abnormalities such as nondisjunction.

**Do not mix totals with per-cell counts.** After I,two cells each have4 DNA molecules,total8; after II,four each have2,total8. Total chromosome count rises from4 to8 when separated sisters become individual chromosomes, without new DNA replication. Mark cell boundaries before counting.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“减数分裂两次，所以DNA复制两次。”反例：起点复制后合计8个DNA分子，经两次分裂分到四细胞仍合计8；第二次复制会破坏该账目。

### English

“Two divisions mean two DNA replications.” Counterexample: the post-S total of8 DNA molecules is distributed into four cells and remains8; a second replication would change that account.

## Depth / 高中桥梁

### 中文

高中桥梁：未考虑交换时，n对同源的独立排列可产生2^n种整条染色体来源组合，此模型n＝2得到4种潜在组合；不是说一次分裂必产生四种不同配子。

### English

Senior bridge: without crossing over, independent assortment of n pairs permits2^n whole-chromosome origin combinations; n=2 permits4. This does not mean one meiosis must yield four different gamete types.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **减数分裂 / meiosis** — 一次DNA复制后两次分裂，使染色体套数减少的过程。
- **配子 / gamete** — 参与受精的单倍体生殖细胞。
- **单倍体 / haploid** — 具有一套染色体，不等于只有一条染色体。
- **交叉互换 / crossing over** — 同源染色体非姐妹染色单体交换对应DNA片段。
- **分离 / segregation** — 染色体或等位基因副本分配到不同细胞。
- **独立分配 / independent assortment** — 不同同源对的方向与分离可形成不同组合。
- **受精 / fertilization** — 配子融合恢复两套等遗传贡献的过程。

### English

- **减数分裂 / meiosis** — Two divisions following one DNA replication, reducing chromosome-set number.
- **配子 / gamete** — A haploid reproductive cell participating in fertilization.
- **单倍体 / haploid** — Having one chromosome set, not necessarily one chromosome.
- **交叉互换 / crossing over** — Exchange of corresponding DNA segments between non-sister chromatids of homologues.
- **分离 / segregation** — Distribution of chromosome or allele copies into different cells.
- **独立分配 / independent assortment** — Different homologous-pair orientations and segregation generate combinations.
- **受精 / fertilization** — Fusion of gametes restoring genetic contributions including two chromosome sets in the model.
