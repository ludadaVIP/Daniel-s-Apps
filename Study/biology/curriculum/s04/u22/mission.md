# 灯豆遗传方格与重复抽样报告 / Glowbean Punnett and repeated-sampling report

## 中文

只用虚构灯豆：画Aa×Aa和Aa×aa方格，标每个等位基因来自哪方，并验证概率和为1。用减数分裂说明每配子一个等位基因。明确完全显性、配子等概率、独立随机结合和存活相同等假设。保留L088至少三批样本的亲代、数量、种子、各基因型期望和观察值；解释波动及前次结果不改变下次概率。最后给出多基因、环境和显性不等于常见的边界说明，不收集真实家庭信息。

提交内容：主张、原始记录、计算、机制推理、一项不确定性与一句英文结论。评价关注证据是否与主张匹配，不能只报答案。屏幕内记录即可完成。

## English

Use fictional glowbeans only. Draw Aa×Aa and Aa×aa squares, label parental allele origins and verify probabilities sum to1. Use meiosis to explain one allele per gamete. State complete dominance, equal gamete probabilities, independent random combination and equal survival. Keep parents, count, seed and genotype expectations/observations for at least3 L088 batches. Explain variation and why previous outcomes do not change next-trial probabilities. End with limitations involving multiple genes, environment and dominance versus frequency; do not collect family information.

Submit a claim, raw records, calculations, mechanistic reasoning, one uncertainty and an English conclusion. Judge whether evidence supports the claim rather than reporting answers alone. The task can be completed entirely on screen.
