## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**方格把分离与结合放在同一张纸上。** 虚构灯豆只研究一个常染色体基因座，两种等位基因为A与a。正常减数分裂后，每个配子在该位置只带一个等位基因。Aa亲代提供A或a的机会各1/2；受精时从两个亲代各取一个，重新组成两个等位基因。把两个配子槽交叉排列，得到四个各占1/4的组合。槽的概率相等和随机结合是模型假设，而不是图表本身保证的自然规律。

**先算基因型，再解释表型。** Aa×Aa四格是AA、Aa、aA、aa；aA与Aa只是写出来源的顺序不同，同属Aa，因此概率为1/4、1/2、1/4，总和为1。假设A对指定的发光表型完全显性，则AA与Aa都发光，aa不发光，表型概率为3/4与1/4，即3∶1。这一比例需要完全显性等前提；它不是所有基因型比，也不适用于所有性状。显性不意味着更好、更强或在人群更常见。

**换亲代才能检验理解。** Aa×aa时，aa亲代两个配子槽都写a，代表两份相同等位版本的等概率选择，而不是产生两个不同类型；子代Aa与aa各1/2。AA×aa则全部为Aa，即使两个亲代自己都不杂合。方格列出机会，不安排子代出生顺序，也不保证一组四粒种子恰好3粒发光。模型还固定配子成功率、受精机会与存活机会相同；若真实证据不满足这些条件，应修正模型，而不是强迫数据填进格子。

**用反例检查表型推理。** 若亲代改为AA×Aa，四格汇总为一半AA、一半Aa；在本设定下全部发光，但并非全部纯合。因此“看见发光”不足以唯一确定基因型，必须保留多个可能解释。反过来，把发光子代全写成AA，会丢失杂合版本传到下一代的可能性。方格的价值是把贡献与假设公开，便于检查推理，而不是用大小写给个体排名。

### English

**A square connects segregation with combination.** Our fictional glowbean model considers one autosomal locus with alleles A and a. After normal meiosis, a gamete carries one allele at this locus. An Aa parent contributes A or a with probability½ each. Fertilization takes one contribution from each parent, restoring two alleles. Crossing two equally likely gamete slots from each parent gives four combinations with probability¼ each. Equal gamete probabilities and random combination are assumptions, not guarantees supplied by the drawing.

**Calculate genotype before interpreting phenotype.** Aa×Aa yields AA,Aa,aA,aa. Aa and aA differ only in the order used to show parental origin, so they form one genotype class. The probabilities are¼AA,½Aa and¼aa, summing to1. If A shows complete dominance for the specified glow phenotype, AA and Aa glow while aa does not. Phenotype probabilities are¾ and¼, a3:1 ratio. This requires the stated dominance and other assumptions; it is neither the genotype ratio nor a universal rule for traits. Dominant does not mean stronger, better or more common.

**Change the parents to test the explanation.** In Aa×aa, both slots from the aa parent contain a. They represent equally likely copies of one allele version, not two distinct gamete types. Offspring are½Aa and½aa. AA×aa yields all Aa, even though neither parent is heterozygous. The square predicts chances, not birth order or an exact ratio among four seeds. It assumes equal gamete success, fertilization opportunity and survival; incompatible evidence calls for revising assumptions rather than forcing observations into the square.

**Check phenotype inference with a counterexample.** AA×Aa gives half AA and half Aa. All glow under our assumptions, but not all are homozygous. Observing glow therefore cannot uniquely identify genotype. Calling every glowing offspring AA would erase the possibility that an a allele passes to the next generation.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“两个aa配子槽都写a，所以只算一格。”反例：槽代表概率位置；Aa×aa仍有四个等概率槽组合，汇总后Aa与aa各一半。

### English

“Two a slots should count as one cell.” Counterexample: slots represent probability positions; Aa×aa still has four equally likely slot combinations, summing to½Aa and½aa.

## Depth / 高中桥梁

### 中文

高中桥梁：若A不完全显性或共显性，Aa可有不同表型，基因型概率仍可为1∶2∶1，而表型分类改变。连锁和配子选择也会改变更复杂模型的假设。

### English

Senior bridge: with incomplete dominance or codominance, Aa can have a distinct phenotype. Genotype probabilities may remain1:2:1 while phenotype grouping changes. Linkage and gamete selection alter assumptions in more complex models.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **遗传方格 / Punnett square** — 用亲代配子组合列出可能子代与概率的表。
- **基因型 / genotype** — 指定基因座的等位基因组合，或更广义的遗传组成。
- **表型 / phenotype** — 可观察或测量的特征，受基因型及环境等影响。
- **纯合 / homozygous** — 某基因座两个等位基因相同。
- **杂合 / heterozygous** — 某基因座两个等位基因不同。
- **完全显性 / complete dominance** — 在指定性状中，杂合子与一种纯合子表型相同。
- **概率 / probability** — 在给定假设下事件发生机会的数值。

### English

- **遗传方格 / Punnett square** — A table combining parental gametes to show possible offspring and probabilities.
- **基因型 / genotype** — The allele combination at a specified locus, or genetic makeup more broadly.
- **表型 / phenotype** — An observable or measurable feature influenced by genotype and environment.
- **纯合 / homozygous** — Having the same two alleles at a locus.
- **杂合 / heterozygous** — Having two different alleles at a locus.
- **完全显性 / complete dominance** — For a specified trait, the heterozygote has the same phenotype as one homozygote.
- **概率 / probability** — A numerical chance of an event under specified assumptions.
