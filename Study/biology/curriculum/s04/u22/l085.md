## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**信息有层次，版本有位置。** 一条未复制的染色体通常包含一条很长的DNA双链分子和相关蛋白质，DNA上有许多功能区段。基因不是整条染色体，也不是肉眼看到的性状本身。等位基因是同一基因的不同版本，如模型在对应位置标出的A与a；字母只是符号，不能把大写理解成分子体积更大或能力更强。

**同源与姐妹回答不同的来源问题。** 二倍体同源对中的两条染色体通常各来自一个遗传亲代，具有对应的基因位置，但等位基因可以不同。S期复制同一条染色体，产生两条相连的姐妹染色单体，每条含一个DNA双链分子。姐妹起初通常具有相同序列，但复制错误或后续交叉互换可使这种相同性不再完全成立；“姐妹永远相同”也不严谨。

**计数要先说在数什么。** 本模型两对同源染色体即2n＝4。复制前有4条染色体、4个DNA分子；复制后有4条由两条姐妹构成的染色体、8个DNA分子，倍性仍2n。X的两臂不等于两条已分开的染色体；以相连姐妹为一个复制染色体单位计数，直到姐妹分离成为各自的染色体。A与a各复制一份增加的是拷贝数，不是凭空产生两种新等位基因。因此报告“DNA加倍”时，不能顺手改写成“染色体套数加倍”。

**用等位基因拷贝再核对一次。** 指定A/a这一处基因座，复制前全细胞有一份A、一份a；复制后有两份A、两份a。版本种类仍为两种，拷贝数由二变四。这与全细胞四条染色体复制后的八个DNA分子并不矛盾，因为另一对染色体还含其他基因位置。应注明报告针对一处基因座还是整个细胞，避免把局部标记数当成全基因组数量。

### English

**Separate information, location and version.** An unreplicated chromosome normally contains a long double-stranded DNA molecule with associated proteins, and its DNA includes many functional regions. A gene is neither an entire chromosome nor the visible trait itself. Alleles are different versions of the same gene, such as A and a at the matching locus in our model. Letters are symbols: uppercase does not mean a larger molecule, a stronger version or a more frequent allele.

**Homologues and sisters have different origins.** A diploid homologous pair usually contains one chromosome from each genetic parent. The chromosomes have corresponding gene loci but can carry different alleles. Copying one chromosome during S phase produces two connected sister chromatids, each with a double-stranded DNA molecule. Sisters initially usually have the same sequence; replication errors or later crossing over can alter that similarity. Calling sisters permanently identical is therefore too strong.

**State what you are counting.** Our two homologous pairs give2n=4. Before replication there are4 chromosomes and4 DNA molecules. After replication there are still4 chromosomes, now each consisting of two sisters, giving8 DNA molecules. Ploidy remains2n. The arms of an X do not represent two already separated chromosomes. Connected sisters count as one replicated chromosome until separation makes them individual chromosomes. Copying A and a increases the number of copies, not the number of different allele versions. A report that DNA amount doubled therefore does not imply that chromosome-set number doubled.

**Check allele copies as well.** At the A/a locus, replication changes one A and one a into two copies of each. There are still two allele versions. These four locus copies differ from the cell’s eight DNA molecules because the other chromosome pair carries other loci. State the counting scope.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“一个X是两条同源染色体。”反例：一个X的两条姐妹来自同一条染色体复制；同源对在复制后是两个X。

### English

“One X is a homologous pair.” Counterexample: an X contains sisters copied from one chromosome; a replicated homologous pair is two Xs.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **基因 / gene** — DNA中有功能的区段，可影响RNA或蛋白质等产物。
- **等位基因 / allele** — 同一基因在相应位置的不同版本。
- **基因座 / locus** — 某基因或标记在染色体上的位置。
- **同源染色体 / homologous chromosomes** — 具有相应基因位置的一对染色体，版本可不同。
- **姐妹染色单体 / sister chromatids** — 同一条染色体复制产生、分离前连接的两份。
- **着丝粒 / centromere** — 与染色单体连接及分离相关的染色体区域。
- **二倍体 / diploid** — 具有两套染色体。

### English

- **基因 / gene** — A functional DNA region contributing to products such as RNA or protein.
- **等位基因 / allele** — An alternative version of a gene at the corresponding locus.
- **基因座 / locus** — The position of a gene or marker on a chromosome.
- **同源染色体 / homologous chromosomes** — A chromosome pair with corresponding gene loci, possibly carrying different alleles.
- **姐妹染色单体 / sister chromatids** — The two connected copies produced by replication of one chromosome.
- **着丝粒 / centromere** — A chromosome region involved in chromatid attachment and segregation.
- **二倍体 / diploid** — Having two chromosome sets.
