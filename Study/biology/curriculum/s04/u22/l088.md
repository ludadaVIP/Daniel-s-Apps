## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**期望值是平均的预测，不是预约名单。** Aa×Aa模型中aa概率为1/4；样本4的期望aa数量是4×1/4＝1，样本40则是10。某次4粒可以有0、1、2、3或4粒aa；例如5粒的期望为1.25，不存在四分之一粒种子，却仍是合理的平均预测。观察值必须是整数，而期望值可以不是。

**独立抽样不会记住欠账。** 在亲代配子机会、随机结合和存活等假设固定时，每次重新从每方抽一个等位基因。前三次都非aa，不会使第四次的aa概率自动升到1；它仍是1/4。计算机用种子启动可复现的伪随机序列，相同亲代、样本数和种子给出相同记录，换种子会换一批。种子是检查模型的工具，不是决定真实后代的生物原因。较大样本通常让比例更稳定，但不保证某批更靠近期望，也不保证绝对数量差更小。

**真实性状需要更丰富的模型。** 灯豆只给一个基因座和完全显性关系。现实中许多性状受多个基因、发育与环境共同影响；即使一个基因重要，也可能存在外显率或存活差异等条件。显性说的是杂合与纯合的表型关系，不是力量、价值或流行程度。罕见等位基因也可以显性，常见等位基因也可以隐性。所以不能用一张方格推断真实人的能力、性格或家庭关系，应先问假设与证据是否合适。

**把数量差换成比例再比较。** 四十粒中七粒aa的观察比例为7÷40＝17.5%，与理论25%相差7.5个百分点；另批十二粒aa则是30%，高出5个百分点。两批都可以在相同概率机制下出现，不能因为一批偏低就把概率永久改低。若许多独立大批次都持续偏离，才应进一步检验亲代判断、等概率配子、存活或记录方法，不能只用“随机”搪塞所有异常。

### English

**An expected count is an average prediction, not a reservation list.** In Aa×Aa, the modeled probability of aa is¼. A sample of4 therefore has expected aa count4×¼=1; a sample of40 has expectation10. One sample of4 can contain0,1,2,3 or4 aa offspring. For5 seeds the expectation is1.25: fractional seeds do not exist, but a fractional average is meaningful. Observed counts are integers; expected counts need not be.

**Independent draws do not remember a debt.** With gamete probabilities, random combination and survival assumptions fixed, each trial draws one allele from each parent anew. Three previous non-aa outcomes do not raise the fourth trial’s aa probability to1; it remains¼. A computer seed starts a reproducible pseudorandom sequence. The same parents, count and seed reproduce the same record, while a different seed provides another batch. The seed helps audit the model; it is not a biological cause determining real offspring. Larger samples usually have more stable proportions, but no particular batch is guaranteed closer to expectation, and absolute count differences need not shrink.

**Real traits require richer models.** Glowbeans assume one locus and complete dominance. Many real traits involve multiple genes, development and environment. Even when one gene matters greatly, penetrance or unequal survival can change predictions. Dominance describes a heterozygote’s phenotype relationship, not strength, value or population frequency. A rare allele can be dominant and a common one recessive. A single square cannot establish human ability, personality or family relationships; first examine whether its assumptions and evidence fit.

**Compare proportions as well as counts.** Seven aa among40 is17.5%,7.5 percentage points below25%; twelve is30%,5 points above. Either can occur under one mechanism. Persistent departures across many independent large samples deserve checks of parent identification, gamete probabilities, survival and records; randomness should not dismiss every discrepancy.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“多次没出现aa，下一次必定补回来。”反例：固定模型每次概率仍1/4；可能出现很长的非aa序列，这不构成改变后一次概率的机制。

### English

“Missing aa outcomes must be paid back next time.” Counterexample: the fixed model still assigns¼ each trial. Long non-aa runs can occur without a mechanism changing the next probability.

## Depth / 高中桥梁

### 中文

高中桥梁：独立固定概率试验的计数可用二项分布描述。Aa×Aa时，4粒全部非aa的概率为(3/4)^4≈0.316，约31.6%；“看不到aa”并不稀奇。

### English

Senior bridge: independent fixed-probability counts can be described by a binomial distribution. For Aa×Aa, the probability of4 non-aa seeds is(3/4)^4≈0.316, about31.6%; seeing no aa in four is unsurprising.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **期望值 / expected value** — 按概率计算的长期平均或加权平均，不保证单次取到。
- **观察值 / observed value** — 某次样本中实际记录的结果。
- **独立事件 / independent events** — 一个事件的结果不改变另一个事件的概率。
- **样本波动 / sampling variation** — 相同概率机制下不同样本产生的差异。
- **随机种子 / random seed** — 使伪随机计算可复现的起始设定。
- **多基因性状 / polygenic trait** — 受到多个基因座影响的性状。
- **显性 / dominance** — 指定条件下杂合子的表型关系，不代表更强或更常见。

### English

- **期望值 / expected value** — A probability-weighted average, not a guaranteed single realization.
- **观察值 / observed value** — The result actually recorded in a particular sample.
- **独立事件 / independent events** — Events for which one outcome does not change the probability of another.
- **样本波动 / sampling variation** — Differences among samples generated by the same probability mechanism.
- **随机种子 / random seed** — An initial setting making pseudorandom computation reproducible.
- **多基因性状 / polygenic trait** — A trait influenced by multiple gene loci.
- **显性 / dominance** — A heterozygote phenotype relationship under stated conditions, not strength or frequency.
