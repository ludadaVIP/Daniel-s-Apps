## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

同样的反应物和产物，加入少量酶后反应更快，难道酶向反应“灌进”了额外能量？

### English

The same reactants become the same products faster after adding a little enzyme. Has the enzyme poured extra energy into the reaction?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选一个想法，不评分。学后用证据重看：酶降低能垒，但不改变反应物与产物的能量差；酶把产物能量降得更低，所以平衡移动；每生成一份产物就永久消耗一份酶。

### English

Choose an initial idea without a score; revisit it using evidence: An enzyme lowers the barrier without changing the energy difference; An enzyme lowers product energy and shifts equilibrium; Each product permanently consumes one enzyme.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

细胞中许多反应即使在能量上可以发生，常温下仍可能慢到无法及时支持生命活动。因为反应物要先经历键的拉伸、电子分布调整等过程，达到短暂的高能过渡态，才形成产物。从反应物到过渡态所需克服的障碍叫活化能。酶是生物催化剂，多数是蛋白质，也有催化RNA；它们通过结合底物、安排适当位置与局部化学环境等方式，提供较低能垒的路径。

图中的自由能使用模型单位，反应物处于20、产物处于5。无酶的峰是90，所以正向活化能为90−20=70；有酶路径的峰是45，活化能为45−20=25。两条路的端点完全相同，因此反应的自由能变化ΔG都为5−20=−15。这个例子告诉我们要区分“路上的山有多高”和“终点比起点低多少”。酶降低山峰，并不把终点进一步降低，也不凭空给细胞提供能量。

对于一个可逆反应，酶加快正逆方向达到平衡的过程，却不改变给定条件下的平衡位置。反应速率回答每段时间转换多少，平衡组成回答最终正逆速率相等时各物质保持怎样的比例；两者不能混用。酶在催化循环中可能暂时结合底物或改变状态，但总体被再生，不是一份产物对应消耗一份酶。真实细胞可通过耦联反应、移走产物或持续供应底物推动整体过程，那是系统的物质与能量安排，不能归因于“酶单独改变了原反应的ΔG”。反应进程图也不是按时间记录的反应速率曲线。

### English

Many cellular reactions are energetically possible but would proceed too slowly under ordinary conditions to support life. Reactants must first undergo changes such as bond distortion and rearrangement of electron distributions, reaching a short-lived high-energy transition state. The barrier from reactants to that state is activation energy. Enzymes are biological catalysts, mostly proteins, with some catalytic RNA exceptions. Binding, orientation and a suitable chemical environment can provide a lower-barrier pathway.

The graph uses fictional free-energy units. Reactants start at 20 and products end at 5. Without enzyme, the peak is 90, so forward activation energy is 90−20=70. With enzyme, the peak is 45 and the barrier is 45−20=25. Both paths have identical endpoints, making the overall free-energy change ΔG=5−20=−15 in either case. This distinguishes the height of a hill along a route from the difference between starting and finishing elevations. The enzyme lowers the hill without lowering the final endpoint or supplying free energy.

For a reversible reaction, catalysis speeds approach to equilibrium in both directions without changing the equilibrium position under specified conditions. Rate describes conversion per time; equilibrium composition describes proportions when forward and reverse rates match. An enzyme may bind substrates and change temporarily during its cycle, but is regenerated overall. Cells can drive processes through reaction coupling, product removal and continued substrate supply. Those system-level arrangements do not mean that the enzyme alone changes the original reaction’s ΔG. Finally, a reaction-progress diagram is not a rate-versus-time measurement.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：ΔG为负就意味着不需要酶、反应一定很快。能量上有利与速度快是两个问题，较高活化能仍可能使反应很慢。

### English

Misconception: negative ΔG means a reaction needs no enzyme and must be fast. Energetic favourability and speed are different; a high barrier can still make the reaction slow.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中衔接：酶对过渡态的相对稳定、酸碱催化与共价催化等可降低有效活化自由能。温度与浓度也会影响速率；ΔG随浓度而变，而标准自由能变化与平衡常数有关。此处端点固定是条件受控的示意，不能把模型数字当作任意细胞的热力学数据。

### English

Senior bridge: transition-state stabilisation, acid–base catalysis and covalent catalysis can lower effective activation free energy. Temperature and concentrations affect rates; ΔG varies with composition, while standard free-energy change relates to equilibrium constants. Fixed endpoints here are a controlled schematic, not thermodynamic measurements for arbitrary cells.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **酶 / enzyme**: 催化特定生化反应、总体不被消耗的分子。
- **催化剂 / catalyst**: 改变反应路径、加快反应而总体被再生的物质。
- **活化能 / activation energy**: 反应达到过渡态所需克服的能量障碍。
- **过渡态 / transition state**: 反应路径上能量较高的短暂构型。
- **自由能变化 / free-energy change**: 产物与反应物的自由能差，记作ΔG。
- **动态平衡 / dynamic equilibrium**: 正逆过程速率相等、宏观组成稳定的状态。

### English

- **酶 / enzyme**: A molecule catalysing particular biochemical reactions without overall consumption.
- **催化剂 / catalyst**: A substance that provides a faster reaction pathway and is regenerated overall.
- **活化能 / activation energy**: Energy barrier to reaching the transition state.
- **过渡态 / transition state**: A short-lived high-energy configuration along a reaction pathway.
- **自由能变化 / free-energy change**: Difference in free energy between products and reactants, written ΔG.
- **动态平衡 / dynamic equilibrium**: A state of equal forward and reverse rates with stable overall composition.

## Check / 检索练习

### 中文

1. [concept] 酶主要怎样加快反应？

   A. 让反应物最终变成不同产物以避开原反应
   B. 提供活化能较低的反应路径
   C. 使产物与反应物的能量差总变为零

   答案: B. 酶可稳定过渡态并安排适合的化学环境，降低能垒；不是改写同一反应的端点。

   对照Explore的蓝色低峰与红色高峰。

2. [concept] 对同一可逆反应加入酶，哪项正确？

   A. 平衡位置不因催化而改变，但可更快接近平衡
   B. 平衡必定完全移向产物
   C. 酶是产物的永久组成部分

   答案: A. 酶促进正逆反应接近平衡，不改变平衡常数；净反应中酶被再生。

   回看Explain第三段，分清速度与最终比例。

3. [reasoning] 无酶与有酶图中反应物20、产物5，峰分别90、45，哪组计算正确？

   A. 活化能90→45，ΔG=+15
   B. 活化能70→25，ΔG均为−15
   C. 活化能70→25，ΔG由−15变−40

   答案: B. 能垒从反应物水平起算：90−20=70，45−20=25；ΔG=5−20=−15。

   在图上分别找起点、峰和终点，再做两种减法。

4. [vocabulary] activation energy是哪一个量？

   A. 反应物到过渡态的能量障碍
   B. 细胞储存的全部能量
   C. 产物与反应物的最终数量比

   答案: A. 活化能是路径上的能垒，不是储能总量或平衡比例。

   回看Vocabulary与能量图的峰相对起点高度。

### English

1. [concept] How does an enzyme principally accelerate a reaction?

   A. Replace the original products with different ones
   B. Provide a pathway with lower activation energy
   C. Make the reactant–product energy difference always zero

   Answer: B. Enzymes can stabilise transition states and arrange suitable chemistry, lowering a barrier without rewriting the reaction endpoints.

   Compare the lower blue and higher red peaks in Explore.

2. [concept] What is correct when an enzyme is added to the same reversible reaction?

   A. Catalysis leaves equilibrium position unchanged but can speed its approach
   B. Equilibrium must shift completely towards products
   C. The enzyme is a permanent product component

   Answer: A. An enzyme accelerates approach to equilibrium without changing its equilibrium constant and is regenerated overall.

   Revisit Explain paragraph three: speed versus final proportions.

3. [reasoning] Reactants are 20 and products 5; peaks are 90 without and 45 with enzyme. Which calculation is correct?

   A. Activation energy 90→45, ΔG=+15
   B. Activation energy 70→25, ΔG remains −15
   C. Activation energy 70→25, ΔG changes from −15 to −40

   Answer: B. Barriers are measured from reactants: 90−20=70 and 45−20=25; ΔG=5−20=−15.

   Locate start, peak and endpoint, then perform the two different subtractions.

4. [vocabulary] Which quantity is activation energy?

   A. The barrier from reactants to the transition state
   B. All energy stored by a cell
   C. The final product-to-reactant amount ratio

   Answer: A. Activation energy is a pathway barrier, not total stored energy or equilibrium proportion.

   Revisit Vocabulary and the peak height relative to reactants.
