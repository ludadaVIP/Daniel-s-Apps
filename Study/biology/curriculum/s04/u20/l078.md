## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

如果酶只是完全不动的一把锁，为什么底物结合后活性部位的形状还会调整？

### English

If an enzyme were a completely rigid lock, why could its active site change shape after a substrate binds?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选一个想法，不评分。学后用证据重看：只要分子大小相同，任何酶都能催化它；底物可与合适活性部位相互作用，引起适当调整；底物越多，每个酶每秒就必定无限加快。

### English

Choose an initial idea without a score; revisit it using evidence: Any enzyme catalyses any same-sized molecule; A suitable substrate interacts with an active site and promotes adjustment; More substrate makes each enzyme work infinitely faster.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

酶E的凹口代表活性部位，菱形A与方形B只是形状线索。真实选择性还依赖电荷与化学相互作用，不是二维拼图。

### English

The recess in E represents its active site. Diamond A and square B are shape cues; real selectivity also depends on charge and chemical interactions, not a two-dimensional puzzle.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

保持酶E不变，选A或B，并改变初始底物。用下一步、播放、暂停或重播观察四阶段；库存只在释放时记一次转换。把底物设0作为缺少底物对照。

### English

Keep enzyme E fixed, select A or B and vary starting substrate. Use Next, Play, Pause or Replay for four stages. The inventory records one conversion at release. Set substrate to zero as a no-substrate control.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

酶的活性部位不是一个只按外轮廓机械挑选分子的洞。参与结合的氨基酸残基具有电荷、亲水性与空间位置等性质，只有合适的相互作用才能有效结合底物并支持特定反应。模型用菱形A和方形B表示差异，是为了让选择性可见，不能据此认为现实中“大小形状相同就一定反应”，或一种酶只能接触一个绝对唯一的分子。

底物接近后，酶与底物相互作用可能使构象调整，更适合后续催化，这称为诱导契合。暂时形成酶—底物复合物，随后特定化学变化生成产物，产物释放，酶可以进入新一轮循环。这里的“调整”不是酶主动思考，也不是底物把酶永久改造成另一个物种。催化所需的条件还包括适当温度、pH，有些反应也需要辅助因子；仅仅碰到活性部位不等于反应必定完成。

例如初始4单位A和1单位E，一次模型循环结束后记为3单位底物、1单位转换产物和1单位E。模型单位用于记账；图中一单位底物可画成两个产物碎片，并不表示所有酶反应遵守一种分子计量。零底物时不应出现新产物，B在本选定反应中不转换。开始时增加底物可增加有效相遇；但固定酶量下活性部位逐渐饱和，继续加底物的速率收益会减小，不可能让每个酶无限加快。完整速率需要额外测量，本课四个阶段不是四个等长真实分子时间段。

比较条件时一次只改变一种因素。

### English

An active site is not a hole selecting molecules only by their outer outline. Its amino-acid residues have charges, chemical properties and spatial arrangements that determine binding and catalysis. Suitable interactions allow a substrate to participate in a particular reaction. Our diamond A and square B make selectivity visible, but real molecules with similar sizes can behave differently. Specificity also does not mean that every enzyme recognises exactly one unique molecular species.

As a substrate interacts with an enzyme, conformational adjustment may make the complex more suitable for catalysis: induced fit. A temporary enzyme–substrate complex forms, chemical changes generate products, products leave, and the enzyme can begin another cycle. Adjustment involves molecular interactions, not conscious decisions or permanent conversion into a different kind of enzyme. Suitable temperature and pH are also needed, and some reactions require cofactors. Encountering an active site does not guarantee completed reaction.

For example, begin with four A substrate units and one E enzyme unit. One completed model cycle leaves three substrate units, one converted-product unit and the enzyme still present. These units track conversion; a drawing of two product fragments is not a universal molecular stoichiometry. Zero substrate gives no new product, and B does not convert in this selected reaction. Initially, more substrate can increase productive encounters. With fixed enzyme amount, however, sites become saturated and extra substrate produces diminishing rate gains. The four visual stages are causal snapshots rather than four equal molecular durations, and the activity does not measure actual reaction rates.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：锁钥比喻证明酶完全刚性。这个比喻帮助理解选择性，但诱导契合强调动态结构与化学相互作用。

### English

Misconception: the lock-and-key analogy proves enzymes are rigid. It helps introduce selectivity, while induced fit emphasises dynamic structure and chemistry.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中衔接：许多简单酶促初始速率随底物浓度增加趋近最大值；Km与特定条件下的曲线有关，不能无条件当作结合强弱的唯一指标。竞争性抑制、变构效应和多底物反应可能改变曲线，当前四帧模型没有模拟它们。

### English

Senior bridge: many simple initial-rate curves approach a maximum as substrate concentration increases. Km describes a curve under specified conditions and is not always a direct measure of binding strength. Competitive inhibition, allostery and multiple substrates can alter behaviour; the four-frame model does not simulate them.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

实验中加倍底物，初始速率只稍微上升。提出一个基于饱和的解释，并说明为何还需要控制酶量、温度和pH。

### English

Doubling substrate causes only a small initial-rate increase. Explain this using saturation and state why enzyme amount, temperature and pH still need controls.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **底物 / substrate**: 由酶作用并转变的反应物。
- **活性部位 / active site**: 酶中参与底物结合与催化的区域。
- **特异性 / specificity**: 酶对一定底物与反应的选择性。
- **诱导契合 / induced fit**: 结合引起酶与底物适合催化的构象调整。
- **酶—底物复合物 / enzyme–substrate complex**: 酶与底物暂时结合形成的状态。
- **饱和 / saturation**: 多数活性部位持续被占用，增加底物收益趋小。

### English

- **底物 / substrate**: A reactant acted on and converted by an enzyme.
- **活性部位 / active site**: Enzyme region involved in substrate binding and catalysis.
- **特异性 / specificity**: Selectivity for particular substrates and reactions.
- **诱导契合 / induced fit**: Binding-associated conformational adjustment favouring catalysis.
- **酶—底物复合物 / enzyme–substrate complex**: Temporary association between enzyme and substrate.
- **饱和 / saturation**: Most sites remain occupied, limiting further gains from extra substrate.

## Check / 检索练习

### 中文

1. [concept] 诱导契合强调什么？

   A. 酶完全刚性，任何结合都不改变形状
   B. 适当结合伴随构象调整，有助催化
   C. 酶一旦结合就永久失去活性

   答案: B. 结合可调整活性部位与底物的位置和化学环境；不是永久消耗酶。

   逐步比较相遇与结合两帧的活性部位。

2. [concept] 酶E不能催化模型底物B，最合理的解释是什么？

   A. E与B的结合或化学条件不适合这个反应
   B. B不可能被任何其他酶作用
   C. 只要把B加足够多，E必定催化它

   答案: A. 特异性针对选定酶和反应；不能由E不作用推出所有酶都不作用。

   保持E不变，比较A与B，再读图后说明。

3. [reasoning] 固定1个酶、初始4单位底物，完成一次模型催化循环后是什么？

   A. 3底物、1产物、1酶
   B. 3底物、1产物、0酶
   C. 4底物、1产物、1酶

   答案: A. 一单位底物转换，酶再生；底物与产物的单位总数守恒。单位是转换记账，不表示真实反应一定1分子变1分子。

   将底物设4并到释放帧，读下方库存。

4. [vocabulary] substrate是指什么？

   A. 催化后总体再生的酶
   B. 由酶作用的反应物
   C. 任何反应的最终产物

   答案: B. 底物是本次酶促反应的反应物；产物在其他反应中也可能成为底物。

   回看Vocabulary并沿底物→产物箭头指认。

### English

1. [concept] What does induced fit emphasise?

   A. Enzymes are perfectly rigid and binding changes no shape
   B. Suitable binding is accompanied by conformational adjustment supporting catalysis
   C. Binding permanently destroys enzyme activity

   Answer: B. Binding can adjust active-site geometry and chemistry without consuming the enzyme permanently.

   Compare encounter and binding frames at the active site.

2. [concept] Why does enzyme E fail to catalyse model substrate B?

   A. E and B lack suitable binding or chemistry for this reaction
   B. No other enzyme could ever act on B
   C. Enough B must make E catalyse it

   Answer: A. Specificity concerns the selected enzyme and reaction; E’s failure does not exclude every other enzyme.

   Keep E fixed, compare A with B and read the caption.

3. [reasoning] With 1 enzyme and 4 substrate units, what remains after one model turnover?

   A. 3 substrate, 1 product, 1 enzyme
   B. 3 substrate, 1 product, 0 enzyme
   C. 4 substrate, 1 product, 1 enzyme

   Answer: A. One substrate unit converts and enzyme is regenerated. Units conserve conversion accounting, not a universal one-molecule-to-one-molecule stoichiometry.

   Set substrate to 4 and step to release; read the inventory.

4. [vocabulary] What is a substrate?

   A. The enzyme regenerated after catalysis
   B. A reactant acted on by the enzyme
   C. The final product of every reaction

   Answer: B. A substrate is a reactant in the selected enzyme reaction; a product may become a substrate in another reaction.

   Revisit Vocabulary and identify the substrate → product arrow.

### English
