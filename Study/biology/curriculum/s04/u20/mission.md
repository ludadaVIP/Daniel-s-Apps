# U20 · 用曲线、对照与恢复证据检验解释。 / Test an explanation with curves, controls and recovery.

## 中文

酶怎样改变速度，为什么相同检测温度不保证相同活性？

用L077标出相同ΔG和两种活化能；L079固定酶量1、底物8、pH7，记录新鲜E在5、40、75°C的速率，说明任意单位与独立样品。比较曾冷藏与曾75°C处理10分钟后，都回到40°C检测的结果；引用恢复/未恢复证据区分冷减慢与本模型变性，写一项控制与未模拟因素。再用L080解释为何胆汁不替代脂肪酶、淀粉酶不一步生成葡萄糖。写主张、具体数值、因果机制、英文句及局限，不做体液、尝味或家庭pH实验。

## English

How do enzymes change rate, and why can equal assay temperatures give different activity?

Use L077 to label unchanged ΔG and both barriers. In L079 fix enzyme1,substrate8,pH7 and record freshE at5,40,75°C, naming arbitrary units and separate samples. Compare previously chilled with previously75°C-for10min samples, both assayed at40°C. Use recovery versus persistent loss to separate cold slowdown from model denaturation; state a control and omitted factor. Use L080 to explain why bile cannot replace lipase and amylase does not directly complete glucose production. Include a claim, numerical evidence, causal mechanism, English sentence and limitation. No bodily fluids, tasting or home pH tests.
