## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

胆汁能把一大滴脂肪分成许多小滴，为什么还需要脂肪酶？淀粉酶是否一步就把淀粉全变成葡萄糖？

### English

Bile can disperse a large fat droplet into small droplets. Why is lipase still needed, and does amylase turn starch directly and completely into glucose?

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

消化既包括咀嚼、搅拌和乳化等物理处理，也包括酶催化的化学水解。较大的食物分子通常不能直接按原样大量跨消化道上皮吸收，需要先形成适合后续处理的小分子。不同底物具有不同化学键与结构，因此消化不能只靠一种万能酶；相同环境中有没有合适活性部位，是决定选定反应是否进行的重要条件。

淀粉酶催化淀粉中的部分糖苷键水解，形成麦芽糖和较短寡糖，不等于一步把全部淀粉变成葡萄糖。小肠其他糖消化酶继续处理，才得到可吸收的单糖。蛋白质路线则先由蛋白酶产生较短肽，再由进一步蛋白酶和肽酶形成氨基酸等适合吸收的产物。本课分两段画是为了保留因果次序；真实蛋白酶有不同切割位置，阶段可能重叠，不能说每种蛋白酶都只完成相同一步。

脂肪提供一个重要对照。胆汁中的胆盐有助于将脂肪分散成较小液滴，增加与水相接触的界面，但液滴内甘油三酯分子的化学键并未因此被水解。胆汁不是酶，乳化也不替代脂肪酶。小肠胰脂肪酶主要将甘油三酯水解为脂肪酸与单甘油酯，之后还需吸收和细胞内脂质处理。例如保持蛋白质不变，淀粉酶不产生本路线的肽，换合适蛋白酶才产生肽，这支持特异性，而不是证明前一种混合物“永远没有任何化学变化”。消化产物形成与跨上皮进入血液或淋巴属于不同过程；图上的分解箭头不等于已经吸收。

### English

Digestion includes physical processes such as chewing, mixing and emulsification, as well as enzyme-catalysed hydrolysis. Large food molecules generally need processing into suitable smaller components before substantial uptake across digestive epithelium. Different substrates contain different structures and bonds, so one universal enzyme cannot perform every digestive reaction. A suitable active site is an important condition for the selected chemical transformation.

Amylase hydrolyses selected glycosidic bonds in starch, forming maltose and shorter oligosaccharides. It does not complete every step directly to glucose. Other intestinal carbohydrate-digesting enzymes continue processing to absorbable monosaccharides. In the protein route, proteases generate shorter peptides, followed by further proteases and peptidases producing amino acids and other absorbable products. Splitting the route into stages preserves causal sequence; real enzymes have different cleavage preferences, and stages can overlap.

Fat illustrates the difference between physical and chemical processing. Bile salts help disperse fat into smaller droplets, increasing contact with the aqueous phase. This does not hydrolyse the triglyceride molecules within those droplets. Bile is not an enzyme and cannot replace lipase. In the intestinal pancreatic-lipase route, triglycerides mainly yield fatty acids and monoglycerides, followed by absorption and lipid processing. For a controlled example, keep protein fixed: amylase does not generate peptides in the selected route, whereas appropriate protease does. That supports specificity rather than proving absolutely no chemistry could ever occur in the first mixture. Finally, production of digestive fragments differs from transfer across epithelium into blood or lymph. A breakdown arrow does not mean absorption has already happened.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：胆汁是消化脂肪的酶，淀粉酶直接制造所有葡萄糖。胆汁乳化不水解，淀粉路线需要后续酶；要逐步标出中间产物。

### English

Misconception: bile is a fat-digesting enzyme and amylase directly makes all glucose. Bile emulsifies without hydrolysis, and starch digestion needs later enzymes; name intermediates explicitly.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **消化 / digestion**: 将食物机械处理并化学分解为较小组分。
- **水解 / hydrolysis**: 借助水参与断开化学键的反应。
- **淀粉酶 / amylase**: 催化淀粉形成麦芽糖与较短寡糖的酶。
- **蛋白酶 / protease**: 催化蛋白质或肽中肽键水解的酶。
- **脂肪酶 / lipase**: 催化脂质水解的酶，此处指小肠胰脂肪酶路线。
- **乳化 / emulsification**: 把脂肪分散成较小液滴而非断开分子化学键。
- **吸收 / absorption**: 消化产物等跨消化道上皮进入体内运输系统。

### English

- **消化 / digestion**: Mechanical processing and chemical breakdown of food into smaller components.
- **水解 / hydrolysis**: Bond cleavage involving water.
- **淀粉酶 / amylase**: An enzyme converting starch into maltose and shorter oligosaccharides.
- **蛋白酶 / protease**: An enzyme hydrolysing peptide bonds in proteins or peptides.
- **脂肪酶 / lipase**: An enzyme catalysing lipid hydrolysis; here the intestinal pancreatic route.
- **乳化 / emulsification**: Dispersion into smaller fat droplets without breaking molecular bonds.
- **吸收 / absorption**: Transfer of digested nutrients and other substances across gut epithelium into internal transport.

## Check / 检索练习

### 中文

1. [concept] 本课淀粉消化路线哪项准确？

   A. 淀粉→淀粉酶→麦芽糖/较短寡糖→其他酶→葡萄糖
   B. 淀粉→淀粉酶→直接只有氨基酸
   C. 淀粉→胆汁→直接只有葡萄糖

   答案: A. 淀粉酶开始特定糖苷键水解，还需小肠其他酶完成单糖形成；氨基酸来自蛋白路线。

   在Explore选择淀粉+淀粉酶，读后续产物图注。

2. [concept] 胆汁乳化与脂肪酶作用有什么区别？

   A. 胆汁和脂肪酶都是同一种酶
   B. 胆汁改变液滴分散状态，脂肪酶催化化学键水解
   C. 脂肪酶只把大液滴物理打散

   答案: B. 小液滴增加酶可接触界面，但分子仍是甘油三酯；胰脂肪酶主要产生脂肪酸与单甘油酯。

   保持甘油三酯，比较胆汁与脂肪酶两种条件。

3. [reasoning] 蛋白质+淀粉酶在本路线不转换，换蛋白酶后形成肽，支持什么？

   A. 只要液体混合，所有食物就会产生同样产物
   B. 所选酶和底物的特异性重要，还需后续肽酶等
   C. 这证明任何蛋白质都不能消化

   答案: B. 保持底物只换酶，是针对特异性的对照；蛋白酶所得肽仍可能需进一步水解为氨基酸。

   比较蛋白质下淀粉酶与蛋白酶，并读进一步路线。

4. [vocabulary] absorption与digestion的区别是什么？

   A. 吸收指跨上皮进入体内运输，消化指食物处理与分解
   B. 吸收专指牙齿咀嚼
   C. 两者都只指将脂肪打成液滴

   答案: A. 生成较小产物不等于已经进入血液或淋巴；还需要跨上皮运输等过程。

   回看Explain最后一段和Vocabulary的吸收。

### English

1. [concept] Which starch-digestion route is accurate here?

   A. Starch→amylase→maltose/shorter oligosaccharides→other enzymes→glucose
   B. Starch→amylase→only amino acids directly
   C. Starch→bile→only glucose directly

   Answer: A. Amylase begins selected glycosidic-bond hydrolysis; other intestinal enzymes complete monosaccharide formation. Amino acids belong to the protein route.

   Choose starch+amylase and read the later-product caption.

2. [concept] How does bile emulsification differ from lipase action?

   A. Bile and lipase are the same enzyme
   B. Bile changes droplet dispersion; lipase catalyses bond hydrolysis
   C. Lipase only physically disperses large droplets

   Answer: B. Smaller droplets increase accessible interface while molecules remain triglycerides; pancreatic lipase mainly yields fatty acids and monoglycerides.

   Keep triglyceride fixed and compare bile with lipase.

3. [reasoning] Protein+amylase shows no conversion, while protease yields peptides. What does this support?

   A. Mixing liquids makes every food yield identical products
   B. Enzyme–substrate specificity matters, with further peptidases needed
   C. This proves no protein can ever be digested

   Answer: B. Changing only enzyme for a fixed substrate tests specificity; resulting peptides may still need further hydrolysis to amino acids.

   Compare amylase and protease with protein, then read the onward route.

4. [vocabulary] How does absorption differ from digestion?

   A. Absorption crosses epithelium into internal transport; digestion processes and breaks down food
   B. Absorption means only chewing
   C. Both mean only dispersing fat into droplets

   Answer: A. Making smaller products does not mean they have entered blood or lymph; epithelial transfer is another process.

   Revisit Explain’s final paragraph and absorption in Vocabulary.
