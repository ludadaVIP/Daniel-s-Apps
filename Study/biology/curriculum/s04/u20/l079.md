## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

两份酶最后都在40°C检测，一份曾冷藏，一份曾充分加热，为什么活性仍可能不同？

### English

Two enzyme samples are both assayed at 40°C after one was chilled and the other strongly heated. Why might their activities still differ?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选一个想法，不评分。学后用证据重看：只要最后温度相同，所有处理历史都会被抹去；低温减慢与高温损伤可能不同，需要比较恢复；所有酶的最适温度都必定是37°C。

### English

Choose an initial idea without a score; revisit it using evidence: The same final temperature erases every treatment history; Cold slowdown and heat damage may differ; recovery is evidence; Every enzyme must have an optimum of 37°C.

### 中文

红点是当前检测条件，蓝线各点是独立样品的虚构初始速率。纵轴任意单位；温度横轴°C，pH横轴无单位。历史不同会使整条曲线不同。

### English

The red point marks current assay conditions; blue-curve points are fictional initial rates from separate samples. Rate uses arbitrary units, temperature°C and pH no units. Treatment history can alter the entire curve.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

温度能改变分子运动与有效相遇，也能影响酶结构。在一定范围内升温常使酶促反应加快，但继续升温可能破坏维持正确构象的相互作用，降低活性。低温通常主要减慢过程，并不等于酶已经变性。适宜范围取决于具体酶与测量方案，不存在所有酶统一的37°C最适点。当前虚构酶E把新鲜样品的模型高点设在40°C、pH7，只用于练习解释。

pH改变氨基酸基团与底物的带电状态，可影响结合、催化，极端条件也可能改变结构。曲线峰两侧活性下降不一定都意味着同一种永久损伤。恢复pH后能否恢复活性，需要另做恢复比较，不能只凭一张速率曲线判断。读图时要写清横轴条件、纵轴初始速率与单位，并保持酶量、底物浓度、检测时长和其他条件相同；曲线每个点是独立样品，不是同一份酶不断受热的录像。

例如本模型固定酶量1、底物8任意单位，40°C、pH7的新鲜速率为80。冷藏到5°C但保持结构的样品速率约16.8，回到40°C恢复80；此前75°C处理10分钟的另一份样品，假设92%失活，冷却回40°C也只剩6.4。相同检测温度而活性不同，说明先前处理历史可以持续影响结果。这个数字来自人为设定，不能用来决定真实酶的温度阈值或临床操作，也不能推断任何高温失活都绝对不可逆。模型没有测量噪声、真实折叠与暴露时长效应，真实结论需要重复测量、对照和具体蛋白证据。

### English

Temperature affects molecular motion and productive encounters, but also enzyme structure. Warming over a limited range often increases reaction rate. Further heating can disrupt interactions maintaining a functional conformation, reducing activity. Cold commonly slows molecular processes without denaturing the enzyme. Suitable ranges depend on the particular enzyme and assay; there is no universal37°C optimum. Our fictional fresh enzyme E has a model peak at40°C and pH7 solely for interpretation practice.

Changing pH alters charges on amino-acid groups and substrates, affecting binding and catalysis. Extreme conditions can also alter structure. A decline on either side of a pH peak does not identify one permanent damage mechanism. Whether activity returns after restoring pH requires a recovery comparison. Read both axes and units, and hold enzyme amount, substrate concentration, assay duration and other conditions constant. Curve points represent separately prepared samples, not a film of one progressively heated sample.

For example, with enzyme amount1 and substrate8 arbitrary units, fresh E has rate80 at40°C,pH7. An intact chilled sample gives about16.8 at5°C and returns to80 when assayed at40°C. A separate sample previously treated at75°C for10minutes is assumed92% inactivated, so cooling it to40°C leaves rate6.4. Equal assay temperature with unequal activity supports an effect of treatment history. These invented values establish neither real temperature thresholds nor clinical procedures, and do not prove that every heat-induced loss is irreversible. Noise, actual folding and exposure-time kinetics are omitted; real conclusions require controlled, repeated measurements and protein-specific evidence.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：活性低就是酶变性，回温必定都恢复。低温可使完整酶暂时变慢，而严重热损伤可能持续；要用相同检测条件的恢复证据区分。

### English

Misconception: low activity always means denaturation and rewarming always restores it. Intact cold enzymes may slow reversibly, while substantial heat damage may persist. Recovery evidence under matched assay conditions distinguishes these cases.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中衔接：测得的最适温度可能随检测时长变化，因为升温的速率增益与随时间积累的失活同时存在。pH曲线还可能反映多个可电离基团，真实数据应含重复、误差与样品处理信息。用初始速率减少底物耗尽影响，但不能自动消除所有测量偏差。

### English

Senior bridge: measured temperature optima may depend on assay duration because faster catalysis competes with time-dependent inactivation. pH profiles can reflect multiple ionisable groups. Real evidence should report replicates, uncertainty and preparation history. Initial-rate assays reduce substrate-depletion effects without automatically eliminating every measurement bias.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

研究者让一份样品从5°C一路加热到80°C并把结果当作独立温度效应。指出处理历史混杂，提出独立样品与相同检测时长的改进。

### English

A researcher warms one sample continuously from5°C to80°C and interprets every point as an independent temperature effect. Identify history as a confound and improve the design using separate samples and equal assay durations.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **初始速率 / initial rate**: 反应开始不久、底物尚未明显耗尽时的转换速率。
- **最适条件 / optimum conditions**: 在指定测量方案下产生较高活性的条件。
- **变性 / denaturation**: 分子结构改变，可能导致功能受损。
- **pH / pH**: 表示溶液酸碱性的对数尺度。
- **控制变量 / controlled variable**: 比较时保持相同以排除其他解释的因素。
- **处理历史 / treatment history**: 检测之前样品经历过的条件与时长。
- **可逆性 / reversibility**: 恢复条件后某种变化能够恢复的性质。

### English

- **初始速率 / initial rate**: Conversion rate near the start before substantial substrate depletion.
- **最适条件 / optimum conditions**: Conditions giving high activity under a specified assay.
- **变性 / denaturation**: Structural alteration that may impair molecular function.
- **pH / pH**: A logarithmic measure of acidity and alkalinity.
- **控制变量 / controlled variable**: A factor held constant to reduce alternative explanations.
- **处理历史 / treatment history**: Conditions and durations experienced before an assay.
- **可逆性 / reversibility**: Capacity for a change to reverse when conditions are restored.

## Check / 检索练习

### 中文

1. [concept] 本虚构酶E在40°C附近较快，能推出什么？

   A. 所有生物的酶都最适于40°C
   B. 仅说明E在本模型与检测条件下的趋势
   C. 任何温度高于40°C都会使所有酶立即全失活

   答案: B. 最适点依赖酶种类、条件和测量方案；模型40°C不是通用生物常数。

   回看曲线标题、固定条件与model optimum说明。

2. [concept] 为什么冷藏后回温可以恢复本模型活性？

   A. 模型假设冷藏主要减慢分子过程，结构仍保持
   B. 冷藏已把所有酶分子完全水解
   C. 回温会自动重新合成任意损伤的酶

   答案: A. 冷减慢与结构损伤不同；此处特意假设冷藏样品结构完好，不能泛化所有冻融过程。

   读冷藏→40°C对照行，并与热处理行比较。

3. [reasoning] 冷藏与热处理样品都回到40°C、pH7检测，速率约80和6.4，支持什么？

   A. 当前温度差解释全部差异
   B. 这个热处理历史留下持续失活；应控制酶量、底物和检测时长
   C. 低速证明热样品完全没有任何酶分子

   答案: B. 检测温度相同排除了该温差解释；残余6.4而非0，符合模型92%失活假设，并非全部分子消失。

   在表中读两行40°C结果，核对相同pH、酶量和底物。

4. [vocabulary] controlled variable是哪一项？

   A. 温度曲线中固定为7的pH
   B. 每次刻意改变的温度
   C. 图中得到的速率结果

   答案: A. 控制变量是保持不变的条件；主动改变的是自变量，得到的速率是因变量。

   回看Explore固定条件与Vocabulary。

### English

1. [concept] What follows from fictional enzyme E being faster near 40°C?

   A. All enzymes in all organisms work best at 40°C
   B. Only E’s trend under this model and assay conditions
   C. Every enzyme instantly loses all activity above 40°C

   Answer: B. Optima depend on enzyme, conditions and assay; model 40°C is not a universal constant.

   Revisit the graph title, fixed conditions and model-optimum caption.

2. [concept] Why does rewarming restore this model’s chilled enzyme activity?

   A. Chilling is assumed mainly to slow molecular processes while preserving structure
   B. Chilling completely hydrolysed every enzyme molecule
   C. Rewarming automatically synthesises any damaged enzyme

   Answer: A. Cold slowdown differs from structural damage; intact chilled samples are an explicit assumption, not a claim about every freeze–thaw process.

   Read the chilled →40°C row and compare the heat-treated row.

3. [reasoning] Chilled and heat-treated samples are assayed at 40°C,pH7 with rates about80 and6.4. What is supported?

   A. A current temperature difference explains everything
   B. This heat history leaves persistent inactivation; control enzyme amount, substrate and assay duration
   C. The low rate proves the heated sample has no enzyme molecules

   Answer: B. Equal assay temperatures remove that temperature difference; residual6.4 rather than0 fits assumed92% inactivation, not disappearance of all molecules.

   Read both40°C results and check matched pH, enzyme amount and substrate.

4. [vocabulary] Which is a controlled variable?

   A. pH fixed at7 in the temperature comparison
   B. The temperature deliberately varied each time
   C. The resulting rate measured on the graph

   Answer: A. A controlled variable stays constant; deliberately varied temperature is independent and resulting rate is dependent.

   Revisit fixed conditions in Explore and Vocabulary.
