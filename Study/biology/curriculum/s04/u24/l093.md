## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

一棵树长大增加的碳主要来自泥土还是空气？若燃烧燃料，碳会消失吗？

**学习目标（30分钟）**

- 区分碳库与碳通量，逐步核对守恒。
- 用光合、呼吸、凋落、分解与燃烧追踪碳。
- 区分物质循环与能量散失。

### English

Does a growing tree get most of its carbon from soil or air? Does fuel carbon disappear when burned?

**Learning goals (30 minutes)**

- Distinguish carbon reservoirs from fluxes and check conservation.
- Trace carbon through photosynthesis, respiration, litter, decomposition and combustion.
- Distinguish matter cycling from energy dissipation.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

树根吸收水和矿质元素，但植物有机物中的碳主要来自光合作用固定的二氧化碳。碳原子进入糖，也可进入纤维素、脂质和其他分子。碳库回答“碳现在在哪里”，碳通量回答“在所定时间里移动多少”。库的单位是碳量，通量还需时间边界；大库不一定移动快，小库也可能交换频繁，不能把储量直接当速度。

模型划出空气、植物、碎屑和燃料四个库，起点分别40、30、20、10份碳，共100份。光合作用把8份从空气移到植物后，空气32、植物38；植物呼吸再转回3份，变成35与35。凋落4份进入碎屑，碎屑分解中的微生物呼吸把2份释放为空气中的CO₂。此箭头概括分解的释放支路，没有单列微生物身体、动物、海洋和岩石等库，也没有宣称所有碎屑碳立即变成气体。

最后燃烧5份燃料，燃料从10到5，空气从37到42；不燃烧时空气为37。每一步四库总量都仍为100，没有碳原子凭空增加或消失。箭头上的数是人为规定的一个步骤的转移量，不代表全球真实速率。碳可以反复在有机和无机形式间转移，但推动过程的能量持续转化并以热散出，不能画成返回太阳的能量环。真实循环开放、库更多，若边界允许输入输出，也应把它们列入账本。

### English

Roots supply water and mineral nutrients, but most carbon in plant organic matter comes from carbon dioxide fixed during photosynthesis. Carbon atoms enter sugars and can then become cellulose, lipids and other molecules. A reservoir answers where carbon is stored. A flux answers how much moves between stores within a specified interval. A stock has units of carbon amount; a rate also needs a time denominator. A large store need not exchange rapidly.

Our model includes air, plants, detritus and fuel, initially holding 40, 30, 20 and 10 carbon units: a total of 100. Photosynthesis moves eight from air to plants, leaving 32 and 38. Plant respiration returns three, leaving 35 and 35. Litter transfers four plant units to detritus. A decomposer-respiration branch then releases two detrital units as atmospheric carbon dioxide. This arrow summarizes one release pathway during decomposition. Microbial bodies, animals, oceans and rocks are omitted as separate stores; not all detrital carbon immediately becomes gas.

Finally, combustion transfers five units from fuel to air. Fuel falls from ten to five and air rises from 37 to 42. Without combustion, air remains at 37. The total stays at 100 at every step. These are invented transfer amounts per model step, not global rates. Carbon can repeatedly change between organic and inorganic forms, while the energy driving processes is transformed and dissipated as heat. Energy does not cycle back to the Sun. A real system has additional stores and may exchange carbon across its boundary; those inputs and outputs must also enter the account.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

植物碳主要不来自土；碳循环不是能量循环；分解包含分解者呼吸与其他未画出的储存路径。

### English

Plant carbon mainly does not come from soil; carbon cycling is not energy cycling, and decomposition includes respiration and other omitted storage pathways.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中桥梁：净库变化＝流入−流出；净初级生产量与总光合固定减自养呼吸相关，但碳量与能量单位不可直接混算。

### English

Senior bridge: net stock change equals inflows minus outflows. Net primary production relates to gross fixation minus autotrophic respiration; carbon and energy units cannot simply be mixed.

## Vocabulary / 双语词汇

### 中文

- **碳库 / carbon reservoir**：在某边界内储存碳的部分。
- **通量 / flux**：一定时间内在库之间转移的量。
- **光合作用 / photosynthesis**：利用光能将无机碳固定进有机物。
- **呼吸作用 / respiration**：分解有机物释放可利用能量的过程。
- **分解 / decomposition**：死亡有机物被分解的过程。
- **燃烧 / combustion**：燃料与氧化剂反应并释放能量。
- **守恒 / conservation**：封闭边界内总量保持不变。

### English

- **carbon reservoir / 碳库**: A store of carbon within a defined boundary.
- **flux / 通量**: Amount transferred between stores over a time interval.
- **photosynthesis / 光合作用**: Use of light energy to fix inorganic carbon into organic matter.
- **respiration / 呼吸作用**: Breakdown of organic molecules to release usable energy.
- **decomposition / 分解**: Breakdown of dead organic matter.
- **combustion / 燃烧**: Reaction of fuel with an oxidant, releasing energy.
- **conservation / 守恒**: Constancy of a total within a closed boundary.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] 植物有机碳主要从哪里进入？
   A. 根直接吸入土壤糖
   B. 光合作用固定CO₂
   C. 光直接变成碳原子
   答案：B。光提供能量，CO₂提供碳；水和矿质供应不能替代碳来源。 复习：回看空气到植物的8份箭头。

2. [concept] 分解箭头把碎屑碳转为空气CO₂，能量怎样？
   A. 伴随转换与热散失，不能无损循环
   B. 全部返回太阳
   C. 因碳守恒所以可利用能量也不减少
   答案：A。原子守恒不表示可利用能量无损；微生物也会呼吸。 复习：比较Explain的碳账本与能量方向。

3. [reasoning] 燃烧前空气37、燃料10，转移5后是多少？
   A. 空气42、燃料10
   B. 空气37、燃料5
   C. 空气42、燃料5
   答案：C。转出库减5、转入库加5，四库合计仍100。 复习：切换燃烧并核对最后一步四库表。

4. [vocabulary] flux指什么？
   A. 库中全部存量
   B. 指定时间内在库间的转移量
   C. 碳原子的年龄
   答案：B。通量有方向和时间边界，不同于某时点存量。 复习：回看碳库与通量定义。

### English

1. [concept] Where does most plant organic carbon enter from?
   A. Roots mainly take up soil sugar
   B. Photosynthetic fixation of CO₂
   C. Light becomes carbon atoms
   Answer: B. Light supplies energy and CO₂ supplies carbon; water and minerals do not replace that carbon source. Revisit: Revisit the eight-unit air-to-plant arrow.

2. [concept] When decomposition releases detrital carbon as CO₂, what happens to energy?
   A. It is transformed and dissipated as heat rather than cycling without loss
   B. All returns to the Sun
   C. Carbon conservation keeps all energy usable
   Answer: A. Atom conservation does not imply lossless useful energy; microbes respire too. Revisit: Compare the carbon account and energy direction in Explain.

3. [reasoning] Air is 37 and fuel 10 before burning five units. Afterward?
   A. Air42, fuel10
   B. Air37, fuel5
   C. Air42, fuel5
   Answer: C. Subtract five from the source and add five to the destination; total remains 100. Revisit: Toggle combustion and check the final four-store table.

4. [vocabulary] What is a flux?
   A. The complete stock in a reservoir
   B. A transfer amount between stores over a specified interval
   C. The age of a carbon atom
   Answer: B. Flux has direction and a time boundary, unlike an instantaneous stock. Revisit: Revisit reservoir and flux definitions.
