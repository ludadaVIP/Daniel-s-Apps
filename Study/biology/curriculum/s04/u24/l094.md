## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

空气富含N₂，大多数植物为何仍需要根吸收含氮离子？一个氮原子能走哪些回路？

**学习目标（30分钟）**

- 准确标明固氮、氨化、硝化与反硝化方向。
- 追踪原子而非混淆分子数，比较分支路径。
- 联系微生物、植物吸收与其他矿质循环。

### English

Air contains abundant N₂. Why do most plants still take up nitrogen ions through roots? Which routes can one nitrogen atom follow?

**Learning goals (30 minutes)**

- Label fixation, ammonification, nitrification and denitrification directions accurately.
- Track atoms rather than confusing molecule counts and compare branches.
- Connect microbes, plant uptake and other mineral cycles.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

氮是蛋白质和核酸的组成元素，但空气中丰富的N₂并不等于根可以直接使用。多数植物缺少把N₂转成可利用形式的固氮能力，通常吸收铵NH₄⁺和硝酸盐NO₃⁻。某些自由生活或共生微生物能够固氮；豆科根瘤里的作用由相关微生物完成，不能简写成植物自己直接吸收N₂制蛋白。固氮首先改变化学形式，没有制造新的氮原子。

本课用一个氮原子作追踪标记，即N₂分子两个氮原子中的一个，不表示把一个N₂分子当成只有一个原子。吸收路线依次展示N₂→NH₄⁺→NO₂⁻→NO₃⁻→有机氮→NH₄⁺。前一步代表微生物固氮，中间两步代表硝化，植物吸收并同化形成有机氮，死亡或排泄有机物的分解再经氨化返回铵。植物也可直接吸收铵，模型路线只是一个可能路径，不是每个氮必须完整走完的传送带。

另一分支在硝酸盐之后通过反硝化回到N₂，常与低氧环境及相关微生物活动联系。每步仍追踪一个氮原子，化学式、电荷和储存位置改变，原子并未消失。实际过程还有气态中间产物、流失及多条支路；本图省略这些细节。磷等养分也在岩石、土壤、水和生物之间迁移，但没有必要照抄氮的主要气态回路。养分能循环也不表示可无限施加而无后果，过量流入水体可能改变群落和氧条件。

### English

Nitrogen is part of proteins and nucleic acids, but abundant atmospheric N₂ is not automatically accessible to roots. Most plants lack the capacity to fix N₂ and generally acquire ammonium, NH₄⁺, and nitrate, NO₃⁻. Certain free-living or symbiotic microbes perform nitrogen fixation. In legume root nodules the relevant microbes do this work; saying that the plant itself directly absorbs N₂ to make protein hides the mechanism. Fixation changes chemical form without creating nitrogen atoms.

Our marker follows one nitrogen atom, meaning one of the two atoms in an N₂ molecule. It does not redefine an N₂ molecule as a single atom. The uptake route shows N₂ → NH₄⁺ → NO₂⁻ → NO₃⁻ → organic nitrogen → NH₄⁺. The first step represents microbial fixation. The next two represent nitrification. Uptake and assimilation place nitrogen in plant organic molecules. Breakdown of dead or excreted organic material can return nitrogen to ammonium through ammonification. Plants can also take up ammonium directly; this displayed route is one possibility rather than a compulsory conveyor belt for every atom.

A different branch returns nitrate nitrogen to N₂ through denitrification, associated with suitable microbes and often low-oxygen conditions. Our tracked atom remains present while chemical formula, charge and location change. Real pathways include gaseous intermediates, losses across boundaries and additional branches omitted here. Other nutrients, including phosphorus, also move among rocks, soils, water and organisms, but need not share nitrogen’s major atmospheric route. Recycling does not make unlimited nutrient additions harmless: excess transfer into water can alter communities and oxygen conditions.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

固氮通常由相关微生物完成；植物可直接吸收铵而非必须先硝化；反硝化不是硝化的同方向过程。

### English

Relevant microbes perform fixation; plants can absorb ammonium without prior nitrification, and denitrification differs in direction from nitrification.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中桥梁：硝化是微生物氧化过程；反硝化涉及还原和电子受体。质量守恒需逐元素核算，不能拿分子数量直接替代氮原子数。

### English

Senior bridge: nitrification involves microbial oxidation; denitrification involves reduction and electron acceptors. Elemental mass balances count nitrogen atoms rather than simply counting molecules.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

水淹土壤中硝酸盐减少，能否只据此证明全部被植物吸收？提出另一条氮路径与待测证据。

### English

Nitrate declines in flooded soil. Does that prove plant uptake? Propose another nitrogen route and evidence to measure.

## Vocabulary / 双语词汇

### 中文

- **固氮 / nitrogen fixation**：把N₂转为氨或铵等可利用形式。
- **氨化 / ammonification**：有机氮分解成为氨或铵。
- **硝化 / nitrification**：铵经亚硝酸盐转为硝酸盐。
- **反硝化 / denitrification**：硝酸盐等最终转为N₂等气态产物。
- **同化 / assimilation**：把吸收的无机氮纳入有机分子。
- **铵 / ammonium**：NH₄⁺，植物可利用的含氮离子。
- **硝酸盐 / nitrate**：NO₃⁻，植物可吸收的含氮离子。

### English

- **nitrogen fixation / 固氮**: Conversion of N₂ into ammonia/ammonium forms.
- **ammonification / 氨化**: Conversion of organic nitrogen to ammonia/ammonium.
- **nitrification / 硝化**: Oxidation of ammonium through nitrite to nitrate.
- **denitrification / 反硝化**: Reduction of nitrate towards gaseous products including N₂.
- **assimilation / 同化**: Incorporation of acquired inorganic nitrogen into organic molecules.
- **ammonium / 铵**: NH₄⁺, a nitrogen ion usable by plants.
- **nitrate / 硝酸盐**: NO₃⁻, a nitrogen ion plants can take up.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] 大多数植物一般怎样获得氮？
   A. 根吸收NH₄⁺或NO₃⁻
   B. 直接吸收N₂再自行固氮
   C. 把碳原子改成氮
   答案：A。微生物固氮与植物离子吸收不同；元素不会这样互变。 复习：回看固氮与吸收的分工。

2. [concept] 硝化的方向是哪条？
   A. NO₃⁻→N₂
   B. 有机氮→NH₄⁺
   C. NH₄⁺→NO₂⁻→NO₃⁻
   答案：C。硝化是铵经亚硝酸盐到硝酸盐；另两条分别关联反硝化与氨化。 复习：回看路线第2、3步。

3. [reasoning] 一个标记氮原子从NO₃⁻进入有机氮，原子数怎样？
   A. 变为零，因为失去硝酸根
   B. 仍为一，化学形式改变
   C. 因有机物更大变为两个
   答案：B。追踪的是氮原子而非整个分子数，换形式不增减该原子。 复习：回看N₂中一个标记原子的说明。

4. [vocabulary] ammonification是什么意思？
   A. 有机氮转为氨或铵
   B. N₂转为硝酸盐的总称
   C. 植物用光能制造氮原子
   答案：A。氨化从有机氮释放铵，不是固氮或硝化的另一个名字。 复习：回看最后的有机氮→铵支路。

### English

1. [concept] How do most plants generally acquire nitrogen?
   A. Root uptake of NH₄⁺ or NO₃⁻
   B. Direct N₂ uptake and fixation by the plant
   C. Convert carbon atoms into nitrogen
   Answer: A. Microbial fixation differs from plant ion uptake; elements are not interconverted this way. Revisit: Revisit the distinction between fixation and uptake.

2. [concept] Which direction is nitrification?
   A. NO₃⁻→N₂
   B. Organic nitrogen→NH₄⁺
   C. NH₄⁺→NO₂⁻→NO₃⁻
   Answer: C. Nitrification runs ammonium through nitrite to nitrate; the other routes concern denitrification and ammonification. Revisit: Revisit route steps two and three.

3. [reasoning] One marked nitrogen atom moves from NO₃⁻ into organic nitrogen. Its atom count?
   A. Zero, because nitrate disappears
   B. Still one; chemical form changes
   C. Two, because organic molecules are larger
   Answer: B. We track an atom rather than molecule counts; a change of form does not add or remove it. Revisit: Revisit the marked atom within N₂.

4. [vocabulary] What does ammonification mean?
   A. Organic nitrogen becomes ammonia/ammonium
   B. Any N₂-to-nitrate process
   C. Plants create nitrogen atoms using light
   Answer: A. Ammonification releases ammonium from organic nitrogen; it is distinct from fixation and nitrification. Revisit: Revisit organic nitrogen→ammonium.
