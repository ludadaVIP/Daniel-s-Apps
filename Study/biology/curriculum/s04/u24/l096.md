## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

同样分解落叶的四种微生物一定比一种更耐旱吗？若四种都怕干燥，会发生什么？

**学习目标（30分钟）**

- 区分抵抗能力与扰动后的恢复。
- 联系功能冗余与响应差异，不把物种数当万能原因。
- 用虚拟重复条件的数据边界提出待检验预测。

### English

Must four litter decomposers withstand drought better than one? What if all four are drought sensitive?

**Learning goals (30 minutes)**

- Distinguish resistance from recovery after disturbance.
- Connect functional redundancy and response differences without treating richness as a universal cause.
- Use the limits of conditional virtual data to propose testable predictions.

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

只要物种多，所有扰动下必更稳定；功能替代与不同响应可能缓冲扰动，仍需比较；恢复快就表示从未受到影响。预测不计分。

### English

More species always guarantee stability under every disturbance; Functional substitutes with different responses may buffer disturbance; compare evidence; Rapid recovery means no initial effect. Prediction is unscored.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

生态系统稳定不是一个只由物种数决定的神奇分数。先指定所测功能、扰动强度和观察时间，才能区分抵抗能力与恢复。本课功能是虚构分解者群落的落叶分解速率，起点定为100指数单位，保持初始总功能和落叶输入相同。标准化短时干旱或水淹持续至第1天测冲击，随后恢复共同基线湿度并保持至第30天再测恢复。数值是原创规定的条件比较，不是真实野外实验，也不是未来物种数量预测。

四种分解者若都能分解同一落叶，具有功能冗余；若耐干旱、耐水淹的响应又不同，一些成员受影响时，其他存活者可能部分补位。模型的差异响应群落在干旱后由100到70，再到90。抵抗指标为70/100＝0.70，30天损失恢复比例为(90−70)/(100−70)＝2/3。前者看冲击时保留多少，后者看失去的功能在指定窗口补回多少，不能把两个指标混成同一件事。

反例很重要：同样四种但共同怕干旱的群落变成100、40、65，而一种耐旱分解者是100、65、88，物种较多这次却抵抗更低、恢复后功能也更低。换成水淹，排名可以再变，因为性状与扰动的匹配改变。真实结果还受相互作用、数量比例、环境历史和偶然性影响。本模型没有重复或误差条，不能计算可信的现实置信区间；应提出保持投入相同、重复比较不同响应组成的研究，检验机制而不只追求物种计数。

### English

Stability is not a magical score determined only by species number. First define the measured function, disturbance intensity and observation period. Then distinguish resistance during disturbance from subsequent recovery. Our function is the litter-decomposition rate of a fictional decomposer community. Its starting value is an index of 100, with equal initial total function and litter inputs. A standardized brief drought/flood pulse lasts through day-one measurement; common baseline moisture is then restored and maintained until day thirty. These are original stipulated comparisons, not field measurements or forecasts of species counts.

Four decomposers capable of using the same litter provide functional redundancy. If their moisture responses differ, some survivors may partly compensate when others are impaired. In the different-response community, drought changes the index from 100 to 70 and then 90. Our resistance indicator is 70/100 = 0.70. The fraction of lost function regained by day thirty is (90−70)/(100−70) = two thirds. One describes function retained at impact; the other describes recovery of the loss within a specified window. Neither is a universal scalar definition of ecosystem resilience.

A counterexample tests the claim. Four similarly drought-sensitive species give 100, 40 and 65, whereas one drought-tolerant decomposer gives 100, 65 and 88. Greater richness therefore performs worse in this comparison. Under flooding the ranking can change as traits match a different disturbance. Real outcomes also depend on interactions, relative abundances, environmental history and chance. Our model has no replication or error bars, so it cannot supply a real confidence interval. A test should hold inputs comparable and replicate communities with different response compositions, examining mechanisms rather than counting species alone.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

物种数增加不自动保证所有功能与扰动下更稳定；恢复指标必须明确功能、起点和时间窗。

### English

More species do not automatically stabilize every function under every disturbance; recovery measures need a function, baseline and time window.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中桥梁：稳定性还包括时间变异及多功能权衡；同样丰富度可能有不同功能组成。30天恢复比例不是恢复速率常数，也不能推广所有扰动。

### English

Senior bridge: stability also includes temporal variability and trade-offs among functions. Equal richness can hide different compositions. A thirty-day recovery fraction is not a rate constant or a result for every disturbance.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

比较四种共同敏感与一种耐旱群落；哪条数据反驳“越多必越稳”？再提出能检验补位机制的重复实验。

### English

Compare four similarly sensitive species with one drought-tolerant species. Which data refute a universal richness claim? Propose replication testing compensation.

## Vocabulary / 双语词汇

### 中文

- **生物多样性 / biodiversity**：生命在遗传、物种和生态系统等层面的差异。
- **物种丰富度 / species richness**：指定范围内的物种数。
- **抵抗能力 / resistance**：扰动期间维持所测功能的程度。
- **恢复能力 / resilience**：扰动后恢复功能的能力，需定义时间与指标。
- **功能冗余 / functional redundancy**：多个物种能承担相似功能。
- **响应多样性 / response diversity**：执行相似功能的物种对扰动反应不同。
- **扰动 / disturbance**：改变生态系统条件或结构的事件。

### English

- **biodiversity / 生物多样性**: Variation at genetic, species and ecosystem levels.
- **species richness / 物种丰富度**: Number of species in a defined area.
- **resistance / 抵抗能力**: Extent to which a measured function is maintained during disturbance.
- **resilience / 恢复能力**: Capacity for recovery after disturbance, requiring explicit timing and measures.
- **functional redundancy / 功能冗余**: Multiple species can perform similar functions.
- **response diversity / 响应多样性**: Different disturbance responses among species sharing functions.
- **disturbance / 扰动**: An event altering ecosystem conditions or structure.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] 抵抗能力与恢复能力怎样区分？
   A. 前者看冲击保留，后者看之后恢复
   B. 都只数物种
   C. 恢复越快就表示冲击为零
   答案：A。冲击大小与随后恢复是两个观察问题，需要分别记录。 复习：比较第1天与第30天的图点。

2. [concept] 功能冗余何时较可能缓冲干旱？
   A. 成员功能相同且全部同样敏感就必定足够
   B. 有存活替代者且成员响应存在差异
   C. 丰富度增加就无需研究性状
   答案：B。相似功能允许替代，响应差异使替代者更可能存活；仍是有条件机制。 复习：回看分解者的湿度响应与补位假设。

3. [reasoning] 100→70→90，30天损失恢复比例是多少？
   A. 90%
   B. 20%
   C. (90−70)/(100−70)≈66.7%
   答案：C。损失30、补回20，所以20/30；90%是最后值相对起点，含义不同。 复习：回看Explain的两个分母。

4. [vocabulary] response diversity表示什么？
   A. 执行相似功能的成员对扰动反应不同
   B. 所有物种做完全不同的功能
   C. 不同群落物种数一定不同
   答案：A。响应多样性关注面对扰动的差异，不等于只增加物种数或功能种类。 复习：回看响应多样性与功能冗余的区别。

### English

1. [concept] How do resistance and resilience differ?
   A. The first concerns retained function; the second concerns recovery
   B. Both only count species
   C. Faster recovery means zero impact
   Answer: A. Impact and subsequent recovery are separate observations requiring separate records. Revisit: Compare day-one and day-thirty points.

2. [concept] When is functional redundancy more likely to buffer drought?
   A. Shared function and identical sensitivity always suffice
   B. Surviving substitutes have differing responses
   C. Richness makes trait evidence unnecessary
   Answer: B. Shared function permits substitution and response differences can leave survivors; this is conditional. Revisit: Revisit moisture responses and compensation assumptions.

3. [reasoning] For 100→70→90, what fraction of lost function is recovered by day30?
   A. 90%
   B. 20%
   C. (90−70)/(100−70)≈66.7%
   Answer: C. The loss is30 and regain20, giving20/30;90% compares the last value with baseline and means something different. Revisit: Revisit the two different denominators in Explain.

4. [vocabulary] What is response diversity?
   A. Members sharing a function respond differently to disturbance
   B. Every species performs a completely different function
   C. Communities necessarily have different species counts
   Answer: A. Response diversity concerns responses to disturbance rather than simply species counts or function counts. Revisit: Revisit response diversity versus functional redundancy.
