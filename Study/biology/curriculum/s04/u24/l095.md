## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

生产者储存1000 kJ，下一营养级能得到多少？10%是自然界每次都精确执行的规则吗？

**学习目标（30分钟）**

- 核算下一营养级、碎屑与热的能量收支。
- 比较5%、10%、20%条件下的金字塔。
- 解释分解者跨营养级获得碎屑能量与持续能量输入。

### English

Producers store 1000 kJ. How much reaches the next trophic level? Is ten percent an exact rule everywhere?

**Learning goals (30 minutes)**

- Account for energy to the next level, detritus and heat.
- Compare pyramids at 5%, 10% and 20% transfer.
- Explain detrital energy from multiple levels and continuing energy input.

Every level always transfers exactly ten percent; Transfer varies; account for the next level, detritus and heat; Decomposers return energy to the Sun. Prediction is unscored.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

生态系统中的能量经食物关系流动。以光合生产者为起点，捕获的光能有一部分储存在新形成的有机物中；消费者只得到其中一部分，且还要用于呼吸、活动和维持。没有被摄食的物质、粪便与死亡身体可进入碎屑途径。分解者能从多个营养级的碎屑得到能量，它们自己也呼吸并散热，不是把能量重新送回生产者或太阳的电池。

本课用同一面积同一时期的生产量作比较，起点1000 kJ，分别假定传递效率5%、10%或20%，并为演示固定碎屑分支30%。若效率10%，下一层100 kJ，碎屑300 kJ，热600 kJ，三项相加为1000。若效率20%，则下一层200、碎屑300、热500；又一层从200开始，传递40、碎屑60、热100。这里只是完整账本的一种人工划分，不是所有食物链都有30%碎屑，真实测量也需明确边界和时间。

不同传递效率会改变上层可支持的生产量，解释为何能量金字塔通常向上变窄。10%是常用近似，不是自然界统一的常数。能量总量守恒与可利用程度降低并不矛盾：账本没有把能量销毁，却有越来越多转为环境中分散的热。物质原子可再次被吸收，维持光合生态系统的有用能量则需持续外部输入。读图时还要分清某一时刻的生物量和一段时间的生产量，它们不是同一指标。

### English

Energy moves through feeding relationships. Photosynthetic producers capture light energy and store part of it in new organic material. Consumers receive only part of that production and use energy for respiration, activity and maintenance. Uneaten material, waste and dead bodies can enter detrital pathways. Decomposers obtain energy from detritus originating at several trophic levels. They also respire and dissipate heat; they are not batteries returning energy to producers or the Sun.

We compare production over the same area and interval. Starting with 1000 kJ, we stipulate transfer efficiencies of five, ten or twenty percent and hold a detrital branch at thirty percent for demonstration. At ten percent, the next level receives 100 kJ, detritus receives 300 and heat receives 600. These sum to 1000. At twenty percent, the corresponding values are 200, 300 and 500. Starting another level with 200 then gives 40 to the next level, 60 to detritus and 100 to heat. This is an invented closed account, not a claim that all food chains allocate thirty percent to detritus. Real measurements require explicit boundaries and intervals.

Different efficiencies alter the production that upper levels can support, explaining why energy pyramids usually narrow upwards. Ten percent is a useful approximation rather than a universal constant. Conservation of total energy is compatible with declining availability: energy is not destroyed, but more becomes dispersed environmental heat. Atoms can be taken up again, while useful energy supporting a photosynthetic ecosystem requires continuing external input. Also distinguish biomass at one moment from production over an interval; they are different measurements.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

10%不是普适常数；分解者联系多个营养级，物质循环不等于能量可以循环利用。

### English

Ten percent is not universal; decomposers connect multiple levels, and matter cycling does not imply energy recycling.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中桥梁：传递效率包含摄食、同化与生产效率的影响；能量金字塔和可能倒置的瞬时生物量金字塔不可混同。

### English

Senior bridge: transfer efficiency depends on consumption, assimilation and production efficiencies. An energy pyramid differs from an instantaneous biomass pyramid, which can be inverted.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

两条食物链起点相同，效率分别5%和20%，两次传递后分别有2.5与40 kJ。为何不能据此断言真实动物数量？

### English

With identical inputs, 5% and 20% transfer give 2.5 and 40 kJ after two transfers. Why does that not directly predict animal numbers?

## Vocabulary / 双语词汇

### 中文

- **营养级 / trophic level**：食物关系中取得能量的位置。
- **生产量 / production**：一定时间面积内形成的新生物量或其能量。
- **传递效率 / transfer efficiency**：下一营养级生产量与前级生产量的比。
- **碎屑 / detritus**：死亡物质和排泄物等有机材料。
- **分解者 / decomposer**：从分解有机物取得物质和能量的生物。
- **热散失 / heat dissipation**：能量分散为难以继续驱动生物工作的热。

### English

- **trophic level / 营养级**: A feeding position in energy transfer.
- **production / 生产量**: New biomass or its energy formed over a specified interval and area.
- **transfer efficiency / 传递效率**: Ratio of next-level production to previous-level production.
- **detritus / 碎屑**: Dead material and organic wastes.
- **decomposer / 分解者**: An organism obtaining matter and energy from decomposing organic material.
- **heat dissipation / 热散失**: Energy dispersal as heat less available for biological work.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] 能量传递效率必为10%吗？
   A. 是，所有生态系统都精确如此
   B. 否，随生物和条件变化
   C. 否，因为能量不会散热
   答案：B。10%是近似示例，真实比例可变，呼吸和散热仍存在。 复习：切换5%、10%、20%比较金字塔。

2. [concept] 分解者从哪里得到能量？
   A. 只从最高营养级
   B. 直接从热变回可用光
   C. 来自多个营养级的死亡有机物等
   答案：C。碎屑有多个来源，分解者也呼吸，不能使能量无损回流。 复习：回看各层通往碎屑的箭头。

3. [reasoning] 输入1000 kJ、传递20%、碎屑30%，热是多少？
   A. 500 kJ
   B. 800 kJ
   C. 1000 kJ
   答案：A。1000−200−300＝500；把碎屑忘掉会重复或漏记能量。 复习：重读20%账本，核对三项总和。

4. [vocabulary] transfer efficiency表示什么？
   A. 个体体重之比
   B. 下一营养级与前级生产量之比
   C. 物质循环次数
   答案：B。效率比较同边界时期的生产量，不是个体体重或循环次数。 复习：回看生产量和传递效率定义。

### English

1. [concept] Must transfer efficiency be ten percent?
   A. Yes, exactly everywhere
   B. No, it varies with organisms and conditions
   C. No, because energy never becomes heat
   Answer: B. Ten percent is an illustrative approximation; actual efficiencies vary and heat dissipation remains. Revisit: Compare the 5%, 10% and 20% pyramids.

2. [concept] Where do decomposers obtain energy?
   A. Only from the highest level
   B. By converting heat back to usable light
   C. Dead organic material and wastes from multiple levels
   Answer: C. Detritus has multiple sources; decomposers respire and do not recycle energy losslessly. Revisit: Revisit arrows from multiple levels to detritus.

3. [reasoning] Input1000 kJ, next-level transfer20%, detritus30%. Heat?
   A. 500 kJ
   B. 800 kJ
   C. 1000 kJ
   Answer: A. 1000−200−300=500; omitting detritus leaves the account incorrect. Revisit: Revisit the twenty-percent account and sum all three outputs.

4. [vocabulary] What is transfer efficiency?
   A. Ratio of individual weights
   B. Ratio of next-level to preceding-level production
   C. Number of matter cycles
   Answer: B. Efficiency compares production with matched boundaries and interval, not individual weights or cycle counts. Revisit: Revisit production and transfer efficiency.
