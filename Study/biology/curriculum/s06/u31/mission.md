# 基因表达的有序证据链 / An ordered gene-expression evidence chain

## 中文

用原创短序列按DNA模板→RNA→核糖体→四残基短链排序，标5′/3′、细胞部位、RNA加工与UAA终止。不要把复制算作每次表达必需的第一步。以GCU/GCC比较说明简并；再固定转录输入2相对量 min⁻¹，比较翻译0.5/0.1 min⁻¹的5 min RNA/蛋白表，引用RNA3.875与蛋白4.453/0.891说明调控层。用CER写主张、至少两项带单位证据、机制及一个未排除解释；附一句英文。英语不计入生物判断，可先中文后借术语卡写英文。

**自查：** 按过程与部位排序；至少两条带单位/条件的数值证据；解释机制而非只抄结果；写一个模型没测的限制。若英语困难，先完成中文推理，再用术语卡补一句英文。

英语练习：Under these conditions, the evidence supports ___ because ___; the model does not test ___.

## English

Order the original sequence as DNA template→RNA→ribosome→four-residue chain, labelling ends, locations, processing and UAA stop. Replication is not required before every expression event. Compare GCU/GCC, then at transcription input 2 relative units min⁻¹ compare translation 0.5/0.1 min⁻¹: at 5 min RNA 3.875 and protein 4.453/0.891. Write a CER claim, at least two unit-labelled observations, mechanism and one unresolved alternative. Add one English sentence; biology reasoning may be written in Chinese first with vocabulary support.

**Self-check:** correct process/location order; at least two numerical observations with units and conditions; a mechanism rather than copied results; one unmeasured limitation. Complete biological reasoning first, then use vocabulary support for one English sentence.

English practice: Under these conditions, the evidence supports ___ because ___; the model does not test ___.
