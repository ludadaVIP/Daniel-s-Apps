## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**有信息不等于用同样多。** 不同细胞可共享大部分DNA，却表达不同的基因组合或不同数量的产物。基因表达的功能产物可以是RNA，也可以经过翻译形成蛋白质；蛋白功能再与其他基因和环境共同影响性状。因此“每个基因只决定一个性状”无法解释细胞分工与复杂性状。

**调控可以分层。** 转录因子等影响RNA生成；RNA加工、稳定性与定位影响可用RNA；翻译调控改变每份RNA支持的蛋白合成；蛋白的降解、修饰也能改变数量或活性。同一低蛋白结果可有不同原因，必须选择能区分解释的测量，不能只看终点就断言转录被关闭。对功能RNA基因，重点终点可能是RNA本身而非蛋白。

**用明确规则读时间表。** 原创模型每步为1 min，R和P分别表示RNA、蛋白相对量，不是可以直接相减的同种分子。规则为R下一步=0.5R＋转录输入，P下一步=0.75P＋翻译系数×旧R，初始都为0。固定输入2相对量 min⁻¹、翻译系数0.5 min⁻¹，1 min时R=2而P=0，因为本模型本步蛋白使用前一步RNA；5 min时R=3.875、P约4.453。只把翻译系数降到0.1，RNA仍3.875，蛋白约0.891。这支持“翻译层能产生这种差异”，而非证明任何真实细胞一定服从这些数值。

**稳定也需要流动。** 固定输入时R逐渐靠近4相对量，是因为每步新生成的量与移除量趋于平衡，不是转录与降解都停止。蛋白需要更久响应，观察时间不同可能改变判断。本模型固定降解比例，省略随机性、细胞分裂稀释和复杂反馈，参数不是生理常数。迁移到实验时，至少报告指标单位、采样时间和对照条件，再用RNA与蛋白共同数据定位可能层次；若要区分低翻译与高降解，还应增加能分别测合成和移除的证据。

**对照需要固定采样窗口。** 比较两次处理时，若一次在一分钟采样，另一次在五分钟采样，蛋白差异可能来自观察时点而非调控条件。应保持起始量、细胞数量与采样时间可比，并区分每个细胞的量和整份样品的总量。相对单位需要同一指标内部的参考标准；RNA与蛋白分别归一化后，即使柱高相等也不代表分子数相等。这些测量条件决定数据能支持哪一层结论。

### English

Possessing information does not ensure equal use. Different cell types can share most DNA yet express different combinations or amounts of gene products. A functional product may be RNA itself or a protein made through translation. Protein functions then interact with other genes and environmental conditions. One gene–one visible trait is therefore an inadequate account of cell specialization or complex traits.

Regulation operates at several levels. Transcription factors influence RNA production. Processing, RNA stability and location affect available RNA. Translation control changes protein synthesis supported by that RNA. Protein degradation and modification can change amount or activity. The same low-protein endpoint can arise through several mechanisms, so measurements must distinguish explanations rather than infer transcriptional shutdown from a final protein measurement. For a functional-RNA gene, RNA itself may be the relevant endpoint.

Read the time table using explicit rules. In this original model each step represents one minute. R and P are separate relative RNA and protein scales, not interchangeable molecular quantities. Next R=0.5R+transcription input; next P=0.75P+translation coefficient×previous R. Both start at zero. With input 2 relative units min⁻¹ and translation coefficient 0.5 min⁻¹, after one minute R=2 but P=0 because the update uses RNA from the preceding step. At five minutes R=3.875 and P≈4.453. Reducing only the translation coefficient to 0.1 leaves RNA at 3.875 but lowers protein to about 0.891. A translation-level change can therefore generate this pattern; no claim is made that real cells follow these invented numerical constants.

Stable amounts still require molecular flow. At fixed input, R approaches four relative units because production approaches removal, not because transcription and degradation both stop. Protein responds later, making sampling time important. This model fixes loss proportions and omits noise, division dilution and complex feedback; its parameters are not physiological constants. For an experiment, report units, sampling time and matched controls before using RNA and protein evidence to identify possible levels of control. Distinguishing reduced translation from increased degradation requires additional measurements that separately assess production and removal. A single endpoint rarely identifies one unique mechanism.

Controls also need a matched sampling window. Comparing one treatment at one minute with another at five minutes can produce a protein difference caused by timing rather than regulation. Keep starting amounts, cell numbers and sampling times comparable, and distinguish amount per cell from total amount in a sample. Relative units require a reference standard within each measured indicator. When RNA and protein are normalized separately, equal bar heights do not imply equal molecule numbers. These measurement conditions determine which level of explanation the observations can support.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

表达调控不只发生在转录；相对RNA量与蛋白量不能当作一比一产率。

### English

Regulation is not confined to transcription; RNA and protein relative scales do not define a one-to-one yield.

## Depth / 高中桥梁

### 中文

连续模型可写dR/dt=转录−降解，dP/dt=翻译−降解。此处用离散步帮助看清因果顺序，不把每分钟丢一半当普适半衰期。

### English

A continuous model can write production-minus-loss equations. Discrete steps expose ordering here; losing half per minute is not a universal biological half-life.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：同一处理前后RNA均8相对量，蛋白由6降到2，采样均10 min。给两种机制及区分证据。自查：低翻译或高蛋白降解都可；需独立测新合成及消失速率，不能断言基因缺失。

### English

New situation: RNA remains 8 relative units while protein falls 6→2 at matched 10-min sampling. Give two mechanisms and distinguishing evidence. Self-check: reduced translation or increased protein degradation; separately measure new synthesis and loss rather than claim gene absence.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **基因表达** — 利用基因信息产生功能RNA或蛋白产物。
- **调控** — 改变过程强弱、时间或位置的控制。
- **转录因子** — 参与调节转录的蛋白质。
- **翻译效率** — 给定RNA条件下形成蛋白的有效程度。
- **降解** — 分子被分解移除的过程。
- **稳态** — 生成与移除相平衡而量近似稳定的状态。
- **时间滞后** — 上游变化与下游响应之间的延迟。
- **功能RNA** — 以RNA形式执行功能、不必翻译的基因产物。

### English

- **gene expression** — Using gene information to produce functional RNA or protein.
- **regulation** — Control of a process amount, timing or location.
- **transcription factor** — A protein involved in transcription control.
- **translation efficiency** — The effectiveness of protein production from available RNA.
- **degradation** — Breakdown and removal of molecules.
- **steady state** — A near-constant amount maintained by balanced production and removal.
- **time lag** — A delay between an upstream change and downstream response.
- **functional RNA** — A gene product acting as RNA without requiring translation.
