## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**信息指导装配，不是材料变身。** 翻译发生在核糖体上，mRNA提供顺序信息，氨基酸是装配原料，tRNA把密码子识别与正确氨基酸递送联系起来。核糖体连接氨基酸形成多肽，mRNA仍然是RNA，可被再次读取或后来降解。蛋白质的完整功能还依赖折叠、修饰、定位与环境，所以看到一条氨基酸链不等于已证明某项细胞功能。

**三联体要有阅读框。** 本例从5′端方向的起始AUG建立分组，核糖体沿mRNA的5′→3′方向逐个读密码子。AUG既在此处担当起始信号，也编码甲硫氨酸Met；不能把起始和终止都说成“不编码氨基酸”。tRNA反密码子与密码子反平行配对，例如5′-GCU-3′对应3′-CGA-5′。末端UAA由释放因子识别并终止，不加入所谓“停止氨基酸”。本图用标准遗传码，不模拟特殊重编码。

**把序列算到底。** 原创教学RNA为5′-AUG GCU UUU GGA UAA-3′，15个核苷酸组成5个密码子，却只形成Met–Ala–Phe–Gly四残基短链。把第二密码子GCU换成GCC，二者都指定Ala，所以此短链的氨基酸序列不变。这叫遗传码简并：多种密码子可以指定同一氨基酸，并非一个密码子可随意解释。不同同义密码子的使用仍可能影响翻译等层次，本交互没有模拟这些效应，不能由短链相同推出所有表达量和功能都相同。

**边界帮助迁移。** 这里极短序列用于检验密码子与计数，不是一个真实天然蛋白的完整基因。若删除一个核苷酸，后续三联体可能重新分组，影响远超一个位置；若只给内部六个碱基又未给阅读框，不能贸然翻译成“完整蛋白”。回答序列问题时，先标mRNA方向与起点，再列密码子、对应氨基酸和终止位置，最后说明采用标准码及省略的上下文，比凭单个字母猜性状可靠。

**插入与替换需要分别分析。** 假设在同一阅读框内把一个三联体换为另一个，通常先检查该位置氨基酸；若插入一个核苷酸，后续分组会整体改变，原来的终止位置也可能不再被按原框读取。相反，插入三个核苷酸不必造成下游整体移框，但增加的残基仍可能影响折叠。因此突变大小不能只凭改变几个字母判断，应具体比较分组后的序列、终止位置和未经检验的功能层次。

### English

Translation assembles material under sequence guidance. Ribosomes use mRNA as an information source, amino acids as building blocks and tRNAs as adaptors linking codon recognition to amino acid delivery. Amino acids are joined into a polypeptide; mRNA remains RNA and may be read again or later degraded. A protein's eventual function also depends on folding, modification, location and environment. Merely displaying a chain does not demonstrate a cellular function.

Triplets require a reading frame. In this example, an initiating AUG establishes grouping, and the ribosome reads mRNA 5′→3′. AUG acts here as a start signal and encodes methionine, Met. It is therefore wrong to say that both start and stop signals encode no amino acid. A tRNA anticodon pairs antiparallel with its codon: 5′-GCU-3′ corresponds to 3′-CGA-5′. The final UAA is recognized by a release factor and adds no “stop amino acid.” This lesson uses the standard genetic code without exceptional recoding.

Work through the full sequence. The original teaching RNA 5′-AUG GCU UUU GGA UAA-3′ contains fifteen nucleotides in five codons, yet produces only the four-residue chain Met–Ala–Phe–Gly. Changing the second codon from GCU to GCC leaves Ala at that position, so this amino acid sequence is unchanged. Genetic-code degeneracy means several codons can specify the same amino acid, not that one codon has arbitrary meanings. Synonymous codon usage can still influence other expression properties. Those effects are omitted here; equal short-chain sequences do not prove identical expression levels or biological functions.

Scope helps transfer the method. The fragment is deliberately short to expose codon counting and is not a complete natural protein gene. Removing one nucleotide may regroup many downstream triplets rather than changing just one residue. Conversely, six internal bases without a defined frame cannot establish a whole protein sequence. When solving an unfamiliar sequence problem, label mRNA direction and the starting position, list codons and amino acids, then mark termination. State use of the standard code and missing biological context. This produces a defensible sequence explanation instead of guessing a visible trait from a single changed letter.

Analyze insertions separately from substitutions. Replacing one complete triplet within a fixed reading frame first requires checking the amino acid at that position. Inserting one nucleotide instead can regroup downstream triplets, so the original stop may no longer be read in its former frame. Inserting three nucleotides need not shift every downstream triplet, yet the additional residue can still affect folding. Counting changed letters alone therefore does not establish the size of a mutation's effect. Compare the regrouped sequence, termination position and still-untested functional consequences.

### English

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **翻译** — 核糖体依据mRNA装配氨基酸链。
- **密码子** — mRNA中按阅读框读取的三核苷酸单位。
- **反密码子** — tRNA上与密码子反平行配对的三核苷酸。
- **转运RNA** — 连接密码子识别与氨基酸递送的RNA。
- **阅读框** — 决定连续三联体分组的起点与分组方式。
- **终止密码子** — 通常由释放因子识别、不指定氨基酸的信号。
- **多肽** — 由肽键连接的氨基酸链。
- **简并** — 遗传码中多个密码子可指定同一种氨基酸。

### English

- **translation** — Ribosomal assembly of an amino acid chain using mRNA.
- **codon** — A three-nucleotide mRNA unit read within a frame.
- **anticodon** — A tRNA triplet pairing antiparallel with a codon.
- **transfer RNA** — RNA linking codon recognition with amino acid delivery.
- **reading frame** — The starting position and grouping defining consecutive triplets.
- **stop codon** — A signal normally recognized by release factors, not specifying an amino acid.
- **polypeptide** — An amino acid chain linked by peptide bonds.
- **degeneracy** — Multiple codons can specify the same amino acid.

### English

1. Which teaching chain follows AUG GCU UUU GGA UAA?
   - A. Met–Ala–Phe–Gly, then stop
   - B. Met–Ala–Phe–Gly–a Stop amino acid
   - C. Five RNA blocks become five amino acids

2. How is the antiparallel anticodon to 5′-GCU-3′ written?
   - A. 5′-CGA-3′
   - B. 3′-CGA-5′
   - C. 3′-GCU-5′

3. In the same frame, GCU→GCC while other codons stay unchanged; both specify Ala. Which conclusion follows?
   - A. Amino acid sequence remains, but all expression effects need not be identical
   - B. Any base change leaves protein unaffected
   - C. The whole gene is unchanged

4. What is degeneracy?
   - A. A codon can mean anything
   - B. Multiple codons can specify one amino acid
   - C. All codons specify one amino acid

<details>
<summary>Reveal after answering</summary>

1. A. Four amino-acid codons precede a stop signal; stop is not a fifth amino acid. Inspect I01 frame 5 and the termination record.

2. B. Complementary CGA must carry 3′→5′ end labels; correct letters alone do not establish correct orientation. Return to I01 frame 2 anticodon ends.

3. A. This is a particular synonymous substitution, not a rule for every substitution or insertion; RNA has changed. Toggle I01 GCU/GCC and compare the four residues.

4. B. Degeneracy is many-to-one, not arbitrary ambiguity; GCU has a defined meaning in the standard code. Revisit the frame 2 GCU/GCC comparison.

</details>
