### English

One strand grows left and the other right. How can both new strands grow 5′→3′?

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**先分清复制什么。** DNA复制增加DNA副本，为细胞后续分裂提供遗传材料；转录则只按所需区域产生RNA。复制时双链局部解开，每条亲代链各自作为模板。A与T、G与C互补，使新链序列受旧链约束。完成后每个子代双链都含一旧一新，叫半保留复制；旧链没有被切碎后平均分配，也不是两条旧链始终留在同一个子代分子里。

**方向由化学端点决定。** 配对两链反平行。DNA聚合酶只能向已有新链的3′端添加核苷酸，因此新链总以5′→3′延伸，而聚合酶沿模板3′→5′前进。图中向右移动的叉，上方新链能连续跟随解链方向；下方新链必须反复起始成短片段，再处理引物并连接骨架。它们叫领导链和后随链，是相对于这个复制叉的名称；换到另一个方向的叉，不能机械地把“上方”永远叫领导链。

**用数目追踪物质。** 假设一个双链连续完成三轮复制，无降解、无不完整复制，分子数依次为1、2、4、8。每个双链两条链，所以末尾共16条，其中原来两条仍只有2条，另外14条是新合成的。三轮后只有2个双链仍含最初的标记旧链，占2/8；不能把“旧”一词每轮重命名后再声称最初标记链变多。此账本追踪指定起点的分子，不是测量复制速度。

**互补不等于绝不出错。** 配对、校对和修复提高保真度，但误配可发生并可能形成突变；本交互没有随机错误，不证明真实复制零误差。图也省略双螺旋扭转、全部辅助蛋白与真实片段长度，只突出模板、端点和连续性。遇到新图先标复制叉运动方向与四个端点，再判断哪条连续、哪条分段，比背图的上下位置可靠。

**证据还要能排除替代模型。** 若最初重标记的双链转到轻原料中，一轮后只有中间密度带，可排除“两旧链原封留一起、另造全新双链”的保留模型，却还不能单独排除每条链都混杂新旧片段的分散模型。第二轮出现轻带与中间带，结合完整链结构证据，才更有力支持半保留。密度证据、分子数证据与方向机制回答不同问题，不能让其中一种替代全部论证。

### English

DNA replication produces DNA copies for later cell division. Unlike transcription, which makes RNA from selected regions, replication copies genetic material as DNA. Local unwinding exposes each parental strand as a template. Complementary A–T and G–C pairing constrains the sequence of a new partner. Each completed daughter duplex contains one old and one new strand: semiconservative replication. The two parental strands do not remain together in one daughter duplex, and old strands are not shredded and evenly mixed into both new strands.

Direction comes from chemical ends, not page orientation. Paired strands are antiparallel. DNA polymerase adds nucleotides to an existing new strand's 3′ end, so new DNA always grows 5′→3′ while its template is read 3′→5′. At the right-moving fork shown, the upper new strand can follow unwinding continuously. The lower new strand requires repeated starts, short fragments, primer processing and backbone joining. Leading and lagging describe synthesis relative to this fork. At an oppositely moving fork, the strand roles differ; “upper” is not a universal definition of leading.

Count material through a worked example. Start with one duplex and allow three complete rounds without degradation or incomplete copying. Duplex numbers are 1, 2, 4 and 8. Eight duplexes contain sixteen strands. Only two are the originally labelled strands; fourteen have been synthesized since the starting point. Two of eight duplexes retain an original labelled strand. Calling a newly made strand “old” in the next round must not be confused with multiplying the two originally labelled molecules. This ledger tracks a defined starting material rather than replication speed.

Complementarity does not guarantee zero errors. Pairing, proofreading and repair improve fidelity, yet mismatches can occur and persist as mutations. The deterministic interaction omits errors and therefore cannot demonstrate perfect biological accuracy. It also omits helical twisting, many helper proteins and actual fragment lengths. Its purpose is to reveal templates, ends and continuity. For an unfamiliar diagram, label fork motion and strand ends before deciding which synthesis is continuous or fragmented; this method transfers better than memorizing the picture's upper and lower positions.

Evidence must also distinguish alternative models. If initially heavy-labelled DNA is copied using light raw materials, a single intermediate-density band after one round rules out the conservative model, in which both original strands stay together beside an entirely new duplex. That result alone does not exclude a dispersive model mixing old and new material within each strand. Light and intermediate bands after the second round, together with evidence about intact strand structure, support semiconservation more strongly. Density measurements, molecule counts and synthesis direction answer different questions; none should replace the entire argument.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“后随链3′→5′合成”混淆模板读取与新链延伸；“半保留”不是保留一半基因。

### English

“Lagging synthesis runs 3′→5′” confuses template reading with new-strand extension; semiconservative does not mean retaining half the genes.

## Depth / 高中桥梁

### 中文

引物提供可延伸的3′端；片段随后连接。本课不要求背全部酶名。用脉冲标记可研究新合成DNA，但标记信号必须与完整分子数区别。

### English

A primer provides an extendable 3′ end; fragments are later joined. Memorizing every enzyme is unnecessary. Pulse labelling can track newly synthesized DNA, but signal is distinct from complete molecule counts.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：2个起始双链共4条原始标记链，完成2轮复制。写出双链数与原始链占全部链比例。自查：8个双链、16条链、4/16=25%；前提无降解且每轮完整，不把25%说成只有25%基因被复制。

### English

New situation: two starting duplexes contain four labelled original strands and complete two rounds. Give duplex count and original fraction of all strands. Self-check: 8 duplexes, 16 strands, 4/16=25%; assume complete rounds without loss. This does not mean only 25% of genes were copied.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **半保留复制** — 每个子代双链含一条亲代链和一条新链。
- **模板链** — 为互补新链提供碱基顺序的旧链。
- **反平行** — 配对两链的5′到3′方向相反。
- **复制叉** — 双链局部解开并合成新链的分叉区域。
- **领导链** — 在给定复制叉持续向叉方向合成的新链。
- **后随链** — 在给定复制叉以短片段远离叉合成的新链。
- **DNA聚合酶** — 向已有链的3′端添加脱氧核苷酸的酶。
- **连接酶** — 连接DNA骨架缺口的酶。

### English

- **semiconservative replication** — Each daughter duplex contains one parental and one new strand.
- **template strand** — An existing strand specifying a complementary new sequence.
- **antiparallel** — Paired strands run in opposite 5′ to 3′ directions.
- **replication fork** — A branching region where DNA unwinds and new strands are made.
- **leading strand** — The new strand synthesized continuously toward a given fork.
- **lagging strand** — The new strand synthesized in fragments away from a given fork.
- **DNA polymerase** — An enzyme adding deoxyribonucleotides to an existing 3′ end.
- **ligase** — An enzyme sealing gaps in the DNA backbone.

## Check / 理解检查

### 中文

1. 半保留复制完成一次后，每个双链是什么组合？
   - A. 两条旧链
   - B. 一旧一新
   - C. 两条新链

2. 后随链片段怎样延伸？
   - A. 3′→5′，因为它向左
   - B. 5′→3′，以其自身端点判定
   - C. 没有方向

3. 最初1个双链含2条标记旧链；完整复制3轮且无降解，原始旧链／双链分子各多少？
   - A. 8／8
   - B. 2／8
   - C. 2／3

4. replication fork在本课指？
   - A. 两条新链拼成蛋白质处
   - B. DNA局部解开与合成的分叉区
   - C. 细胞分裂出的两个细胞

<summary>作答后查看答案与解释</summary>

1. B. 每条旧链分别作模板；“两旧留一起”是保留复制模型，不符合本模型。 回到D01蓝旧链与金新链的配对及完整记录末句。

2. B. 纸面左右不是分子方向；聚合酶仍向3′端添加，沿模板3′→5′读取。 看I01帧3–4下方片段两端5′/3′。

3. B. 双链数1×2³=8，原始物质仍只有2条；增加的是新合成链，不是标记旧链。 把I01轮数调到3，检查独立于动画的链数账本。

4. B. 复制叉是DNA上的动态结构，不是细胞分裂本身，更不是翻译地点。 看D01右侧分叉点。

</details>

### English

1. After one semiconservative round, what does each duplex contain?
   - A. Two old strands
   - B. One old and one new
   - C. Two new strands

2. How does a lagging fragment extend?
   - A. 3′→5′ because it moves left
   - B. 5′→3′ according to its own ends
   - C. Without direction

3. One duplex starts with two labelled original strands. After three complete rounds without loss, how many original strands/duplexes remain?
   - A. 8 / 8
   - B. 2 / 8
   - C. 2 / 3

4. What does replication fork mean?
   - A. Where new strands form protein
   - B. A DNA unwinding and synthesis branch
   - C. Two daughter cells

<details>
<summary>Reveal after answering</summary>

1. B. Each old strand templates a new partner; keeping both old strands together is the conservative alternative. Return to D01 old/new strand pairing and the final static record.

2. B. Page direction is not molecular direction; polymerase adds to the 3′ end while reading template 3′→5′. Inspect I01 frames 3–4 and the lower fragment ends.

3. B. Duplexes double to 1×2³=8; only two original strands exist. New synthesis does not multiply original labelled matter. Set I01 rounds to 3 and inspect the separate strand ledger.

4. B. A replication fork is a dynamic DNA structure, not cell division or a translation site. Look at the branch on the right of D01.

</details>
