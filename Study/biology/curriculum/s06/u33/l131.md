## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

为什么能减小偏差的反馈回路，仍可能慢慢回稳或暂时越过目标？

**学习目标（38分钟）**

- 追踪刺激、感受、控制、效应与反馈。
- 比较增益和延迟如何改变模型轨迹。
- 用胰岛素或ADH举例，区分机制与人体预测。

### English

Why can a corrective feedback loop return slowly or temporarily pass its target?

**Learning goals (38 minutes)**

- Trace stimulus, sensing, control, effectors and feedback.
- Compare how gain and delay change a model trajectory.
- Use insulin or ADH to distinguish mechanisms from human predictions.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

稳态不是身体永远不变化，而是持续调节，使重要条件保持在合适范围。先把回路画完整：某变量偏离工作范围，感受装置检测变化，控制过程改变信号，效应器作出响应，结果减弱原来的偏差。负反馈中的“负”说的是回路方向，不是所有量都要下降。若原变量太低，负反馈也可以推动它升高。

血糖升高时，胰腺胰岛β细胞的胰岛素分泌通常增加。胰岛素信号促进一些组织摄取和储存葡萄糖，也抑制肝糖输出，血糖向较低方向变化，原先刺激分泌的条件随之减弱。低血糖相关调节还涉及胰高血糖素等作用，不能把全系统缩成只有一个激素。水调节的另一例子是失水后体液渗透浓度上升，ADH释放增多，肾脏增加水重吸收，原刺激因保水而减弱。

为了单独研究回路的方向，我们用虚构偏差x，不用人体浓度。设初值10任意单位，无延迟时每步修正当前偏差的30%，得到10、7、4.9、3.43。第2步计算是7−0.3×7=4.9；每次修正量会随偏差变小而减少，所以不会每步固定减3。横轴一步没有分钟或小时换算。

再比较增益0.6和一步延迟。本模型把开始前的偏差也设为10，不是刚刚施加的新扰动。控制器依据前一步偏差，开始为10、4、−2、−4.4；修正尚未追上变化时会越过目标。负偏差表示变量到了目标另一侧，不是激素浓度小于零。这一模型只说明延迟和响应强度足以改变轨迹；一次波动既不能证明调节失效，也不能据此诊断疾病。真实回路有阈值、饱和、多重激素及不同组织反应。

评价证据时，需要问测量的究竟是变量、激素还是效应；相关变化还不足以确认整个闭环。可以预测：若感受或效应连接中断，原偏差可能难以被抵消；若时间延迟变长，回稳过程可能改变。写下预测的条件和可观察结果，再承认模型不包含真实生理时间，才是把反馈图用于科学解释，而不是把图当成个人健康说明书。

### English

Homeostasis is active regulation, not a promise that internal conditions never vary. Draw a complete loop: a variable deviates, sensors detect it, control processes change a signal, effectors respond, and the resulting change reduces the original deviation. “Negative” describes this opposing direction. It does not mean every quantity decreases. A variable below its working range may be driven upward by negative feedback.

When blood glucose rises, pancreatic beta cells usually increase insulin secretion. Insulin signalling promotes glucose uptake and storage in responsive tissues and suppresses liver glucose output. Falling glucose weakens the stimulus for secretion. The whole system also involves other hormones, including glucagon; one hormone is not the complete controller. For water regulation, water loss can increase osmolarity, increasing ADH release and kidney water reabsorption. Retaining water reduces the initial stimulus.

To isolate feedback direction, our fictional model uses deviation x in arbitrary units, not a human concentration. Starting at 10, a controller with no delay corrects 30% of the current deviation each step: 10, 7, 4.9, 3.43. The second calculation is 7 − 0.3 × 7 = 4.9. Corrections shrink with the remaining deviation rather than subtracting a constant three units. A model step has no conversion to minutes or hours.

The pre-start deviation is also set to 10; this is not a newly imposed disturbance with unknown history. With gain 0.6 and a one-step delay, correction uses older information. The sequence begins 10, 4, −2, −4.4, temporarily crossing the target. A negative deviation means being on the other side of the target, not a negative hormone concentration. This establishes that lag and response strength can alter a corrective trajectory. It does not diagnose disease or make every oscillation pathological. Real regulation includes thresholds, saturation and multiple interacting tissues.

When evaluating evidence, distinguish measurements of the regulated variable from measurements of hormone and effector activity. Correlated changes alone do not demonstrate the entire closed loop. A useful prediction specifies which connection changes and what observation should follow. State the units and limits: these trajectories illustrate control principles, not a timetable for a person's physiology.

## Depth / 高中延伸

### 中文

可选高中桥梁：反馈方向可由沿环路影响的符号判断；延迟、增益和饱和会影响稳定性。曲线有越调不等于正反馈；要检查响应是否抵消原偏差。

### English

Bridge: signs around the loop identify feedback direction; lag, gain and saturation affect stability. Overshoot does not by itself make a loop positive feedback; inspect whether the response opposes the original deviation.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：“负反馈必须瞬间恢复”“波动全部是病”。虚构延迟回路会暂时越调，却仍用反向响应减小原偏差；模型不是诊断工具。

### English

Misconceptions: negative feedback restores instantly, and all fluctuations indicate disease. A delayed opposing response can overshoot; this model is not a diagnostic tool.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新控制器初始偏差8任意单位，无延迟每步修正25%。自查：8→6→4.5；第二次修正1.5而非2。若效应器失去响应，预测偏差更难被本回路抵消；仍不能推算人体血糖。

### English

A new controller starts eight arbitrary units from target and corrects 25% per step without lag. Self-check: 8→6→4.5; the second correction is 1.5, not 2. An unresponsive effector would prevent correction through this loop, but these values cannot predict human glucose.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **homeostasis / 稳态**：通过调节使内部条件保持在可维持的范围。
- **negative feedback / 负反馈**：响应削弱最初偏差或刺激的回路。
- **effector / 效应器**：执行调节响应的细胞或器官。
- **gain / 增益**：本模型中每步修正偏差的比例参数。
- **delay / 延迟**：感知与效应之间的时间间隔。
- **insulin / 胰岛素**：胰腺分泌、参与降低血糖的激素。
- **ADH / 抗利尿激素**：参与增加肾水重吸收的激素。

### English

- **homeostasis / 稳态**: Regulation maintaining internal conditions within workable ranges.
- **negative feedback / 负反馈**: A loop whose response reduces the initial deviation or stimulus.
- **effector / 效应器**: A cell or organ carrying out a corrective response.
- **gain / 增益**: The model parameter controlling correction per step.
- **delay / 延迟**: A lag between sensing and the resulting effect.
- **insulin / 胰岛素**: A pancreatic hormone participating in lowering blood glucose.
- **ADH / 抗利尿激素**: A hormone involved in increasing kidney water reabsorption.

## Mission / 放到生活里

### 中文

给胰岛素或ADH画闭环，标明原刺激、效应和返回箭头；再改变一个连接，写有条件预测。

检索：不看图说出关键机制、一个带单位证据和一个限制。英语另练：The evidence supports … under …, but does not establish …。现实连接：观察课本或科学新闻是否交代模型条件，不必做人体实验或培养微生物。

### English

Draw a closed insulin or ADH loop, identifying stimulus, effect and returning arrow. Change one connection and write a conditional prediction.

Recall without the diagram: name the mechanism, evidence with units and one limit. Practise separately: The evidence supports … under …, but does not establish … . Real-world connection: check whether a textbook or science report states its model conditions; no human experiment or microbial culture is needed.
