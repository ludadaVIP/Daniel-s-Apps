## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

强刺激会让每个神经电峰更高，还是让一秒内的峰更多？

**学习目标（38分钟）**

- 用等幅峰和频率解释刺激强度编码。
- 区分轴突膜电位传播与化学突触传递。
- 用ms、mV和Hz读图并限定模型结论。

### English

Does stronger stimulation make each nerve spike taller, or produce more spikes per second?

**Learning goals (38 minutes)**

- Explain intensity coding using equal-amplitude spikes and frequency.
- Distinguish propagation along an axon from chemical synaptic transmission.
- Read ms, mV and Hz and limit claims from the model.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

神经元的信息不是一股“电液”从头流到尾，也不是递质一路沿轴突奔跑。静息时，膜内外离子分布和膜对不同离子的通透性造成膜电位。钠钾泵用能量维持离子浓度梯度，漏通道也很重要。当局部刺激使膜达到阈值，电压门控通道的活动让膜电位快速升高又下降。每一次峰的快速变化主要由通道介导的离子流形成，不是钠钾泵逐峰按压出来的。

在给定细胞和条件下，动作电位具有“全或无”的特征。未达到触发条件可能没有峰；一旦触发，不是把刺激增加一倍就把峰高加倍。模型里弱刺激100 ms出现2峰，强刺激出现5峰，两者都从−70 mV到+30 mV。100 ms等于0.1 s，所以频率分别为2/0.1=20 Hz和5/0.1=50 Hz。峰高都约100 mV，但每秒事件数不同。

频率不是唯一编码方式。更强刺激也可能募集更多感受器或神经元；刺激持续时间、同步性等也会影响信息。模型固定同一神经元，只帮助隔离“相同峰幅，不同频率”这一点。不应期限制了可发生的峰率，因此不能把输入和输出关系无限延长。

沿轴突传播时，局部电流促使相邻膜段产生新的动作电位，像一排依次触发的事件。到达化学突触末端后，递质才被释放到很短的细胞间隙，扩散并结合突触后受体，改变下一细胞的活动。突触后细胞会整合多个兴奋或抑制输入，所以收到一次化学信息不保证立即产生新峰。释放装置主要在突触前、相应受体在突触后，这种结构分工解释化学突触的方向。

读图时先报单位，再分开波幅、峰数和观察窗口。如果只比较两幅图的高度，容易漏掉频率差。若真实记录的峰高改变，还应查细胞种类、位置、记录方式及状态，不能据此否定全或无原则，也不能把教学波形当作所有神经元的标准数值。

### English

Neural information is neither an electrical fluid travelling down a tube nor neurotransmitter flowing along the entire axon. Ion gradients and selective membrane permeability create a resting potential. The sodium–potassium pump uses energy to maintain concentration gradients, while leak channels also matter. When local stimulation reaches threshold, voltage-gated channel activity produces the rapid rise and fall of an action potential. Channel-mediated ion flows generate each rapid spike; the pump does not push out individual peaks.

For a given neuron under matched conditions, action potentials are approximately all-or-none events. Below threshold there may be no spike. Once triggered, doubling stimulus strength does not normally double the height of that spike. The teaching trace shows two spikes during 100 ms under weaker stimulation and five under stronger stimulation. Both traces begin near −70 mV and peak at +30 mV. Since 100 ms is 0.1 s, their frequencies are 2/0.1 = 20 Hz and 5/0.1 = 50 Hz. The voltage excursion is about 100 mV in both; events per second differ.

Frequency is not the only code. Stronger stimulation may recruit more receptors or neurons, and timing can also carry information. This model fixes the neuron to isolate frequency from amplitude. Refractory periods limit the rate of repeated spikes, so the relation cannot be extended indefinitely.

Along an axon, local current helps adjacent membrane generate another action potential. The moving signal is regenerated membrane activity. At a chemical synapse, arrival at the terminal leads to transmitter release across a tiny gap. Binding to postsynaptic receptors changes the receiving cell. Several excitatory and inhibitory inputs can be integrated, so a single input need not trigger a new spike. Release machinery on one side and receptors on the other explain the usual direction of this chemical junction.

When reading evidence, state the voltage unit, observation window and spike count separately. Real traces vary with cell type, recording site and recording method. The numerical values here illustrate a mechanism and are not universal measurements for every neuron.

## Depth / 高中延伸

### 中文

可选高中桥梁：髓鞘减少膜泄漏，使节点间局部电流能有效触发下一节点；所谓跳跃传导仍是膜电位事件再生。兴奋/抑制取决于受体与离子条件，不只取决于递质名字。

### English

Bridge: myelin reduces leakage so local current can trigger successive nodes; saltatory conduction still regenerates membrane events. Excitation or inhibition depends on receptors and ion conditions, not only transmitter names.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：“强刺激必使峰更高”“每个峰都是泵的作用”。本图强弱峰同高；泵维持梯度，门控通道介导快速峰。

### English

Misconceptions: stronger stimuli must make taller spikes; pumps generate each peak. The traces have equal peaks; pumps maintain gradients and gated channels mediate rapid spikes.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新记录在200 ms有8峰，另一记录4峰，峰值相同。自查：40 Hz与20 Hz；支持频率差，不支持波幅加倍。若不是同类细胞/同记录条件，需要补充对照；不能由频率单独推算主观感觉。

### English

New recordings show 8 versus 4 equal-height spikes in 200 ms. Self-check: 40 versus 20 Hz, a frequency difference rather than doubled amplitude. Match neuron type and recording conditions; frequency alone does not calculate a subjective sensation.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **action potential / 动作电位**：达阈值后产生的可再生膜电位事件。
- **threshold / 阈值**：触发再生电位的关键膜状态条件。
- **frequency / 频率**：单位时间的重复次数，此处以Hz表示。
- **axon / 轴突**：沿其膜传播动作电位的神经元突起。
- **synapse / 突触**：细胞间传递信息的连接。
- **neurotransmitter / 神经递质**：化学突触中释放并作用于受体的信号。
- **refractory period / 不应期**：一次电位后短暂不易再触发的时期。

### English

- **action potential / 动作电位**: A regenerative membrane-voltage event after threshold.
- **threshold / 阈值**: A membrane condition for triggering a regenerative event.
- **frequency / 频率**: Events per unit time, here expressed in Hz.
- **axon / 轴突**: A neuronal extension along whose membrane spikes propagate.
- **synapse / 突触**: A junction for communication between cells.
- **neurotransmitter / 神经递质**: A signal released at chemical synapses to act on receptors.
- **refractory period / 不应期**: A short period of reduced ability to trigger another spike.
