# 修复一张反馈控制图 / Repair a feedback control diagram

## 中文

以胰岛素或ADH为真实机制例，标出刺激、感受/控制、信号、受体、效应与返回箭头。再用虚构偏差模型初值10任意单位、k0.3无延迟给10→7→4.9；改为k0.6且延迟1步给10→4→−2。预测改变受体或效应器条件后的响应，说明负偏差不是负激素浓度。写CER：有条件主张、至少两数值及单位、逐箭头机制、一个未测步骤和一条模型局限；画图区分轴突电活动与突触释放，或说明免疫两分支何以不能互相替代。英语另练“The response would … if … because …; the model does not establish …”。生物推理可中文完成，不以英语准确度锁定。

自查：主张有条件；证据有数值、分母和单位；推理逐箭头解释；局限和下一步有具体对象。允许保存草稿并回课图修订，英语术语与生物理解分开。

## English

Use insulin or ADH as a real mechanism example and label stimulus, sensing/control, signal, receptor, effector and return arrow. In the fictional deviation model use x0=10 arbitrary units and k0.3 without lag:10→7→4.9; compare k0.6 with one-step lag:10→4→−2. Predict the consequence of changing a receptor or effector condition; negative deviation is not negative hormone concentration. Write CER with a conditional claim, two values and units, arrow-by-arrow mechanism, an unmeasured step and a limitation. Distinguish axonal electrical activity from synaptic release, or explain why immune branches are not interchangeable. Practise separately: “The response would … if … because …; the model does not establish …”. Chinese reasoning is accepted; English accuracy does not lock biology progress.

Self-check: qualified claim; numerical evidence with denominators and units; arrow-by-arrow reasoning; a specific limitation and follow-up. Save and revise using lesson diagrams. English vocabulary and biological understanding are assessed separately.
