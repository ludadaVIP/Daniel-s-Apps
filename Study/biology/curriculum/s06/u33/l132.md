### English

Antibodies bind extracellular targets. Who deals with infected cells, and what if a cooperating branch is missing?

- Connect the roles of APCs, helper T, B/plasma and CD8 cells.
- Use conditional removal comparisons to investigate cooperation.
- Distinguish antibody, cellular killing, memory and infection outcome.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

免疫防御需要识别目标、传递信息和执行效应。抗原呈递细胞可摄入材料、处理抗原并把信息呈递给相应T细胞，还提供激活所需的其他信号。辅助T细胞通过信号帮助其他免疫细胞。B细胞具有识别抗原的受体，在合适条件下增殖分化，其中浆细胞分泌抗体，部分细胞形成记忆。不能把这条路线误读为APC直接制造抗体，或辅助T细胞变成浆细胞。

抗体在细胞外结合特定抗原，可能阻挡病原体进入细胞，或帮助其他成分识别目标。它是蛋白质，不是能吞噬的细胞。已经被感染的宿主细胞则可以成为CD8细胞毒性T细胞等效应细胞的目标。杀伤感染细胞与中和游离病毒是不同作用；只测抗体不能直接知道细胞杀伤是否正常，更不能把某一免疫读数等同于整体保护。

本模型选择一种需要辅助T帮助的抗体响应。关掉帮助条件时，上方B到浆细胞分支不再激活，APC的呈递仍可出现。下方开关表示已有可工作的CD8效应细胞，因此该分支单独控制；真实CD8启动和扩增也受呈递与协调影响。模型没有声称所有B反应都依赖T帮助，也没有模拟全部细胞通信。

原创虚构培养记录展示为什么要分开读数：完整条件下抗体指标为80相对单位、靶感染细胞杀伤比例为60/100；去掉已有CD8效应细胞，抗体仍为78单位、杀伤降到5/100。在这个对照里，抗体读数近似不变而杀伤差55个百分点，支持两种效应有分工。相对单位不能当抗体分子数，也不能把体外杀伤比例换成一个人的感染概率。

反应结束后，部分特异性B和T细胞可长期保留，使再次接触相似抗原时的响应更快或更强。但病原体变化、免疫记忆随时间和位置不同，都会影响保护，记忆不是永不感染的保证。可靠解释要引用本条件下具体效应指标，说明哪个协作连接受影响，并承认一个培养模型不能包含完整组织和感染过程。

### English

Immune defence combines recognition, communication and action. Antigen-presenting cells can take up material, process antigens and present information to suitable T cells, alongside other activation signals. Helper T cells send coordinating signals. B cells recognise antigen and, under appropriate activation conditions, expand and differentiate. Plasma cells secrete antibodies, while some cells contribute to memory. These connections do not mean that APCs manufacture antibodies or that helper T cells become plasma cells.

Antibodies bind particular antigens outside cells. Some block entry into host cells; others help additional defence components recognise a target. An antibody is a protein, not an engulfing cell. Already infected host cells can be recognised and killed by effectors including CD8 cytotoxic T cells. Killing an infected cell and neutralising an extracellular virus are different actions. An antibody measurement alone does not measure all cellular killing or overall protection.

Our model selects one T-dependent antibody response. Turning off helper support leaves presentation visible but prevents the selected B-to-plasma branch. The lower switch explicitly represents already functional CD8 effectors, allowing their action to be examined separately. In reality, presentation and coordination also influence CD8 activation and expansion. The model neither makes every B-cell response helper-dependent nor includes the full immune network.

Original fictional culture records illustrate the need for separate measurements. The complete condition gives an antibody index of 80 relative units and killing of 60/100 target infected cells. Without the available CD8 effectors, the index is 78 units while killing is 5/100. Similar antibody readings alongside a 55-percentage-point difference in killing support distinct effector roles here. Relative units are not antibody molecule counts, and culture killing cannot be converted into a person's probability of infection.

Some specific B and T cells persist after a response, enabling changed responses on later exposure. Protection still depends on antigen, time and location. Memory does not guarantee that infection never occurs. A strong interpretation identifies a measured effect, quotes its denominator and explains the affected connection, while recognising that a culture or logical model omits the organisation of a whole organism.

## Depth / 高中延伸

### 中文

可选高中桥梁：抗原识别通常还需要共刺激和局部信号；不同细胞呈递与识别条件不同。把网络分支清楚，比死记全部CD标记或细胞因子名称更有用。

### English

Bridge: antigen recognition usually operates alongside costimulation and local signals, with different presentation/recognition requirements across cells. Track the network before memorising CD markers or cytokine lists.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：“抗体吞噬”“有记忆就不会再感染”。抗体结合目标，吞噬由细胞完成；记忆改变响应条件，但不能消除所有感染机会。

### English

Misconceptions: antibodies engulf, or memory prevents every infection. Proteins bind targets, cells engulf, and memory changes later responses without eliminating every opportunity for infection.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新培养对照：有无辅助T帮助时，抗体指标90与12相对单位，靶细胞杀伤均50/100且CD8已激活。自查：抗体分支下降78单位，不能推出全部免疫停止；说明CD8预先激活的条件，建议测浆细胞数及细胞活性。

### English

New cultures with/without helper support have antibody indices 90 and 12 relative units, while both kill 50/100 targets with preactivated CD8 cells. Self-check: the antibody branch falls by 78 units; all immunity has not stopped. State the preactivation condition and propose plasma-cell counts and viability checks.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **antigen-presenting cell / 抗原呈递细胞**：处理并呈递抗原信息以帮助T细胞激活的细胞。
- **helper T cell / 辅助T细胞**：通过信号帮助协调适应性反应的T细胞。
- **B cell / B细胞**：可在适当激活后形成浆细胞和记忆细胞的淋巴细胞。
- **plasma cell / 浆细胞**：分泌抗体的分化B细胞。
- **CD8 effector cell / CD8效应细胞**：能识别并杀伤某些感染细胞的T细胞。
- **antibody / 抗体**：结合特定抗原的蛋白质，可帮助中和或标记。
- **immune memory / 免疫记忆**：使后续特异响应不同的持久细胞状态或细胞群。

### English

- **antigen-presenting cell / 抗原呈递细胞**: A cell that processes and presents antigen information to help activate T cells.
- **helper T cell / 辅助T细胞**: A T cell helping coordinate adaptive responses through signals.
- **B cell / B细胞**: A lymphocyte that can form plasma and memory cells after suitable activation.
- **plasma cell / 浆细胞**: A differentiated B cell that secretes antibody.
- **CD8 effector cell / CD8效应细胞**: A T cell capable of recognising and killing certain infected cells.
- **antibody / 抗体**: An antigen-binding protein that can help neutralise or tag targets.
- **immune memory / 免疫记忆**: Persistent cellular states or populations changing later specific responses.
