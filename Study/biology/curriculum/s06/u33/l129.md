## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

同一信号到达两种细胞，为什么可能只有一种响应？即使两者都有受体呢？

**学习目标（38分钟）**

- 用受体→传导→响应解释条件性响应。
- 用对照区分不能结合与结合后不能传导。
- 用数值证据提出有边界的机制解释。

### English

Why might only one of two cells respond to the same signal, even when both have a receptor?

**Learning goals (38 minutes)**

- Explain conditional responses through receptor → relay → response.
- Distinguish failed binding from a failed downstream relay.
- Use numerical evidence for a qualified mechanism claim.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

细胞接收信息并不等于把信号分子整个吞进去。图中的信号留在膜外，与跨膜受体结合，使受体的状态改变；胞内分子随后把变化传下去，最后改变酶活性、运输或基因表达。这个例子要分清三个层次：接收、传导和响应。受体像接收装置，但比“锁和钥匙”更复杂：结合会改变活动状态，数量和细胞所处条件也会影响结果。

先在模型中保留受体和下游，按下一步观察。第一步尚无激活；第二步有结合；第三步出现胞内传导；第四步才有响应。关闭受体时，路线从结合处中断。保留受体但关闭下游时，受体仍能结合，后面却没有响应。因此“没有最终响应”至少有两种解释，单测终点不能定位故障，需要同时观察结合与胞内变化。

看一组原创虚构记录：给三组各100个细胞相同信号和观察时间。甲有80个结合、60个出现指定响应；乙有78个结合、5个响应；丙无结合、无响应。乙与甲的结合比例接近，而响应相差55个百分点，支持差异可能在结合之后。它不能单独证明是哪一种蛋白出了变化，也不能把乙的全部细胞都判为死亡；还需活性、受体数量及检测阈值等对照。

同一种信号甚至同一种受体，在不同细胞里也可能接到不同下游网络。有的细胞改变代谢，有的改变分裂或分泌。另一方面，有些能穿膜的信号会与胞内受体作用，所以膜外模型不是全部信号的统一路线。两种情况的共同原则是：信息作用要看接收装置、传导条件和细胞状态，不能只看信号有没有到达。

写解释时先说比较条件和测量单位，再连接机制：“相同刺激下，乙78/100个结合而只有5/100个响应；结合后传导受限是一个可检验解释。”最后补充局限，提出同步检测胞内中间步骤。这样既比背诵‘细胞会交流’更精确，也避免把模型的一条箭头当成已经证明的真实分子通路。

### English

A cell can receive a message without importing the whole signalling molecule. In this diagram, a ligand remains outside the membrane and binds a surface receptor. The receptor changes activity, an intracellular relay carries the information onward, and a response changes transport, enzyme activity or gene expression. Reception, transduction and response are different events. The lock-and-key comparison helps with recognition but does not explain every detail of receptor activity.

Keep both model switches on and advance the steps. Binding appears before the relay, and the response appears last. Removing the matching receptor interrupts the first connection. Keeping the receptor but disabling the relay allows binding without the final response. Consequently, an absent response has several possible explanations. Measuring only the endpoint cannot locate the interruption; an observation of binding adds useful evidence.

Consider original fictional records for three groups of 100 cells under the same signal and observation period. Group A has 80 binding events and 60 responding cells. Group B has 78 binding events but only 5 responding cells. Group C has neither. Binding fractions in A and B are similar, whereas response fractions differ by 55 percentage points. This supports investigating a difference after binding. It does not identify a particular protein, establish that B cells are dead, or exclude differences in receptor abundance and measurement thresholds. Independent viability controls and a measured intermediate would strengthen the explanation.

Even the same receptor can connect to different downstream machinery in different cell types. One cell may alter metabolism while another changes secretion. Some other signals enter cells and bind internal receptors; the surface-receptor route is one example rather than a universal pathway. What transfers between examples is the conditional chain from recognition through intracellular processing to a response.

A useful explanation therefore states matched conditions, gives a numerator and denominator, proposes a mechanism and names a limit. For example: “Under the matched exposure, 78/100 B cells bound the signal but only 5/100 responded. A downstream limitation is plausible; measuring relay activation would help test it.” This is a testable interpretation, not a claim that the schematic has identified every molecular step.

## Depth / 高中延伸

### 中文

可选高中桥梁：级联可放大信号，也可出现交叉、终止和脱敏；箭头代表依赖关系，不保证一对一分子数量。先测中间步骤再定位，不要求背所有通路名称。

### English

Bridge: cascades may amplify, cross-connect, terminate or desensitise. Arrows indicate dependencies, not one-to-one molecular counts. Measure intermediates before naming a pathway; memorising all pathway names is unnecessary.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：“有受体就一定产生相同响应。”反例是保留结合而关闭下游的模型；同受体也可连接不同细胞网络。

### English

Misconception: a receptor guarantees the same response. The relay-off comparison retains binding without a response; different cells can connect the same receptor to different networks.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：两种虚构藻细胞各50个，处理后甲40个结合、30个发光，乙41个结合、2个发光。提出一个后续测量。自查：引用30/50与2/50，结合率相近支持查胞内步骤；不能直接宣布乙缺受体，需测中间信号与细胞活性。

### English

New case: of 50 fictional algal cells per group, A has 40 binding and 30 glowing; B has 41 binding and 2 glowing. Propose a follow-up. Self-check: quote 30/50 versus 2/50; similar binding motivates an internal-relay measurement and viability control. It does not support simply declaring B receptor-free.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **signalling molecule / 信号分子**：携带细胞间信息、可与受体相互作用的分子。
- **receptor / 受体**：识别并结合合适信号的蛋白质。
- **ligand / 配体**：与特定分子结合的分子，此处为受体的结合对象。
- **signal transduction / 信号传导**：把接收信号转成胞内变化的过程。
- **target cell / 靶细胞**：在相应条件下能对信号作出响应的细胞。
- **cellular response / 细胞响应**：信号后出现的细胞活动变化。
- **control / 对照**：帮助比较特定条件效应的参照。

### English

- **signalling molecule / 信号分子**: A molecule carrying information between cells and interacting with a receptor.
- **receptor / 受体**: A protein that recognises and binds a suitable signal.
- **ligand / 配体**: A molecule binding another molecule; here the receptor-binding partner.
- **signal transduction / 信号传导**: Conversion of a received signal into intracellular changes.
- **target cell / 靶细胞**: A cell able to respond to a signal under appropriate conditions.
- **cellular response / 细胞响应**: A change in cellular activity following a signal.
- **control / 对照**: A reference comparison for investigating a condition.

## Mission / 放到生活里

### 中文

画一条有条件的接收→传导→响应链，给一个中断位置和能区分它的观测。

检索：不看图说出关键机制、一个带单位证据和一个限制。英语另练：The evidence supports … under …, but does not establish …。现实连接：观察课本或科学新闻是否交代模型条件，不必做人体实验或培养微生物。

### English

Draw a conditional reception → transduction → response chain, mark one interruption and identify an observation that could locate it.

Recall without the diagram: name the mechanism, evidence with units and one limit. Practise separately: The evidence supports … under …, but does not establish … . Real-world connection: check whether a textbook or science report states its model conditions; no human experiment or microbial culture is needed.
