## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

一种植物在强光下更高，能说它的基因一定更“高大”吗？

**学习目标（38分钟）**

- 用两品系×两环境设计区分来源。
- 计算重复数据均值与环境响应差异。
- 说明多基因、相关与遗传率的条件边界。

### English

If a plant is taller in brighter light, does that prove its genes make it taller?

**Learning goals (38 minutes)**

- Use a two-lineage × two-environment design to distinguish influences.
- Calculate replicate means and differences in environmental responses.
- Explain the conditional limits of polygenic effects, correlation and heritability.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

许多性状不是一个“高大基因”开或关的结果。多个位点可通过生长、代谢和调节共同影响表型，光、水、温度等条件也会改变结果。多基因不代表环境不重要，受环境影响也不代表没有遗传差异。要把两者分开研究，需要有能比较的设计，而不是看到一株高一株矮就决定原因。

本课用两种遗传背景较一致的虚构植物品系A、B，各自在较低和较高光照中培养。每格3株独立植株，随机分配处理与位置，匹配水、土、温度，并统一测21天株高，单位cm。两品系都放在两环境，避免“A总在暗处、B总在亮处”的完全混杂。多个读数来自同一株时，不能冒充多株独立重复。

平行情景中，A低光9、10、11 cm，均值10；高光15、16、17 cm，均值16。B低光13、14、15，均值14；高光19、20、21，均值20。两品系高低光均相差6 cm，同环境B比A高4 cm。这个有限条件下，品系差和环境效应可以同时出现；平行线没有显示交互，不代表其他温度、时间或品系也不会交互。

交互情景保留低光数据，A高光均值改为20 cm，B高光为16 cm。A环境响应为+10 cm，B为+2 cm；品系排序由B更高变为A更高。因此环境效应依赖品系，图上连线交叉。线连接不同处理组的均值，绝不是把同一植株同时放在两个环境，也不是按时间追踪同一株。单凭四格表仍不能找出具体是哪几个基因起作用。

相关性提供线索，因果解释还要设计和机制证据。遗传率描述特定群体、环境和测量中的变异关系，不是某个个体“多少百分比被基因决定”，更不保证改变环境无效。本课不把复杂的人类能力或性格归结为单基因。好的结论写成“在21天和本光照条件下，两品系响应不同”，引用均值、cm和重复数，并提出更多品系、环境水平及独立批次检验。

### English

Many traits are not controlled by a single on/off “tallness gene”. Multiple loci can influence growth through metabolism and regulation, while light, water and temperature also affect phenotype. Polygenic does not mean environmentally independent, and an environmental effect does not exclude genetic differences. Separating influences requires comparisons rather than simply assigning causes to one tall and one short plant.

Our fictional plant lineages A and B have relatively consistent genetic backgrounds. Each occurs under lower and higher light, with three independently treated plants per cell. Positions and treatments are randomised; water, soil, temperature and day-21 height measurements are matched. Including both lineages in both environments avoids completely confounding lineage with light. Repeated measurements of one plant would not create additional independent plants.

In the parallel scenario, A has lower-light heights 9, 10 and 11 cm, mean 10, and higher-light heights 15, 16 and 17 cm, mean 16. B means are 14 and 20 cm. Both lineages differ by six centimetres across environments, and B exceeds A by four centimetres in either environment. Genetic-background differences and environmental effects can coexist. Parallel responses here do not establish that interaction never occurs under other conditions.

In the interaction scenario, lower-light observations stay the same, but higher-light means become 20 cm for A and 16 cm for B. A gains ten centimetres and B gains two. Their ranking reverses, so the environmental response depends on lineage. Lines connect means from different treatment groups; they do not show one plant simultaneously occupying two environments or being followed through time. Four cells still cannot identify the causal genes.

Correlation supplies clues, while causal explanations need design and mechanistic evidence. Heritability concerns variation in a particular population and environment, not the percentage of an individual's destiny controlled by genes. It does not imply that changing environment is ineffective. State a qualified result with means, centimetres and replicate counts, then propose further lineages, environmental levels and independent batches. Complex human abilities and personalities should not be assigned to a single gene from this plant model.

## Depth / 高中延伸

### 中文

可选高中桥梁：在给定统计模型中可把表型变异分成遗传、环境及交互等来源；分法依赖设计与假设。只有两个品系和两环境不足以估计广泛群体遗传率，也不能推出具体基因数。

### English

Bridge: a specified statistical model may partition variation into genetic, environmental and interaction components. The partition depends on design and assumptions. Two lineages and environments cannot estimate broad-population heritability or count causal genes.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：“遗传率高就无法改变”“一条连线是一株植物的旅程”。遗传率有群体条件，连线是独立组均值；同一品系在新环境可有明显不同表型。

### English

Misconceptions: high heritability means unchangeable, or a line tracks one plant. Heritability is population-specific; the line joins independent-group means, and a lineage can show different phenotypes in another environment.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新试验A在两温度均值8和12 cm，B为10和14 cm，每格4株独立随机植物。自查：两者环境差均4 cm，品系差均2 cm，未显示本条件交互；再测多批次和温度水平。若只把A冷处与B暖处比较，来源完全混杂。

### English

New trial: A means 8 and 12 cm across two temperatures; B means 10 and 14 cm, four independent randomised plants per cell. Self-check: both environmental differences are four centimetres and lineage differences two; no interaction is shown here. Add batches and temperatures. Comparing only cool A with warm B completely confounds sources.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **polygenic trait / 多基因性状**：受两个或更多基因位点影响的性状。
- **phenotype / 表型**：在一定条件下可观察或测量的性状。
- **environment / 环境**：影响生长和表达等过程的外部条件。
- **reaction norm / 反应规范**：同一基因型或品系在不同环境下的表型响应模式。
- **interaction / 交互作用**：某因素的效应随另一因素条件而改变。
- **replicate / 重复**：按设计独立处理并测量的实验单位。
- **heritability / 遗传率**：特定群体条件下表型变异中与遗传差异相关的统计量。

### English

- **polygenic trait / 多基因性状**: A trait influenced by two or more gene loci.
- **phenotype / 表型**: An observable or measurable trait under specified conditions.
- **environment / 环境**: External conditions influencing growth, expression and other processes.
- **reaction norm / 反应规范**: The phenotype-response pattern of a genotype or lineage across environments.
- **interaction / 交互作用**: An effect of one factor that changes with another factor.
- **replicate / 重复**: An experimental unit independently treated and measured in the design.
- **heritability / 遗传率**: A population- and condition-specific statistic relating phenotypic variation to genetic differences.

## Mission / 放到生活里

### 中文

从四格数据写一段CER，包含两种响应差、单位、重复数、机制可能性和一条不能推出的结论。

检索：不看图说出关键机制、一个带单位证据和一个限制。英语另练：The evidence supports … under …, but does not establish …。现实连接：观察课本或科学新闻是否交代模型条件，不必做人体实验或培养微生物。

### English

Write a CER from the four cells, including both response differences, units, replication, a possible mechanism and one unsupported conclusion.

Recall without the diagram: name the mechanism, evidence with units and one limit. Practise separately: The evidence supports … under …, but does not establish … . Real-world connection: check whether a textbook or science report states its model conditions; no human experiment or microbial culture is needed.
