## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

把一棵树的两条枝上下交换，A与B会突然不再是近亲吗？

**学习目标（38分钟）**

- 按共同祖先和clade而非上下顺序读树。
- 说明旋转节点为何不改变拓扑。
- 用共享衍生特征和分子证据评价有边界假说。

### English

If two branches swap vertical positions, do A and B stop being close relatives?

**Learning goals (38 minutes)**

- Read trees by common ancestors and clades, not vertical order.
- Explain why rotating a node preserves topology.
- Evaluate a qualified hypothesis using shared derived and molecular characters.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

系统发育树是关于演化关系的假说图，不是从低等排到高等的阶梯。枝端表示研究的类群，节点表示谱系的共同祖先或分歧关系。在本课虚构树中，A和B先汇合到一个节点，C和D先汇合到另一个节点，再往根部才汇合。所以A与B共享的最近共同祖先，比A与C共享的更近；“近”说的是分支关系，不是纸上直线距离。

演化支包含一个祖先及其全部后代。这里AB、CD和ABCD都是图中内部节点对应的演化支。选择A和C却排除B、D，不能构成这棵树上的完整演化支，因为它们的共同祖先也留下被排除的后代。把根部上下两组旋转，写法从((A,B),(C,D))变成((C,D),(A,B))，节点下的成员没有改变，因此亲缘关系相同。

图的上方、下方、左侧和右侧只是排版选择，不能解释为先进程度。枝端的现代类群不是另一个现代枝端的祖先；它们可能共享已经不在图中单独取样的祖先。只有标清时间或分子变化比例尺时，枝长才有相应定量含义。本图无标度，不能根据A线条更长就说A更古老或突变更多。

研究者用证据比较不同树。假设外群和更多资料提示▲是衍生特征，A、B共享它会支持AB分组；一段同源DNA中相同位置的字符也能提供证据。这里仅用一个虚构位点G/G/T/T帮助读图，不足以确定真实历史。相同形态可能独立出现为趋同，分子位点也可能多次变化；不同基因的历史还可能不同。

作答不能只说“两种生物长得像”。更强的说明是：指出比较的同源结构或位点、哪些共享状态支持哪个节点，以及还有哪些替代解释。增加独立位点、合理外群和统计支持，可检验假说，但不把支持当绝对确定。新证据允许修订树；能追踪节点和后代，比记住树长什么样更能迁移到新图。

### English

A phylogenetic tree is a hypothesis about evolutionary relationships, not a ladder from inferior to superior organisms. Tips represent sampled taxa, and internal nodes represent ancestral branching relationships. In our fictional tree, A joins B at one node and C joins D at another before the branches meet nearer the root. A and B therefore share a more recent common ancestor than A and C. “Closer” refers to branching relationships, not straight-line distance on the page.

A clade contains an ancestor and all its descendants. AB, CD and ABCD correspond to internal-node clades here. Selecting A and C while excluding B and D does not produce a complete clade: their common ancestor also has the excluded descendants. Rotate the two root branches and the notation changes from ((A,B),(C,D)) to ((C,D),(A,B)). Membership under each node stays the same, so the topology is unchanged.

Vertical order is a layout choice, not a scale of progress. One modern tip is not automatically the ancestor of another modern tip. Branch lengths acquire quantitative meaning only when the diagram supplies a time or change scale. This tree has none, so a longer drawn line cannot establish greater age or more mutations.

Evidence helps compare alternative trees. If outgroup information and further observations identify the triangle as derived, its presence in A and B supports their grouping. Corresponding positions in homologous DNA can also provide evidence. Our single fictional G/G/T/T site is deliberately insufficient to establish a real history. Similar forms can evolve independently by convergence, sites can change repeatedly, and different genes can sometimes have different histories.

A stronger explanation names the character or homologous position, identifies the node it supports and considers alternatives. More independent characters, an appropriate outgroup and statistical support can test the hypothesis. Support is not absolute certainty. New evidence can revise a tree; tracking nodes and descendant sets transfers more reliably than memorising a drawing's orientation.

## Depth / 高中延伸

### 中文

可选高中桥梁：基因树不必完全等同物种树；多次突变、基因流或不完全谱系排序等会增加不确定性。这里只要求理解独立证据和假说修订，不背全部算法。

### English

Bridge: a gene tree need not exactly match a species tree. Repeated changes, gene flow and incomplete lineage sorting can add uncertainty. Focus on independent evidence and revision rather than memorising inference algorithms.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：“上面更先进”“旋转改变亲缘”“没标度也能读年代”。旋转后AB仍共享同一节点；纸面方向和无标度枝长不提供这些结论。

### English

Misconceptions: higher tips are advanced, rotation changes relationships, or unscaled lengths give ages. AB retains the same node after rotation; layout and unscaled length do not support those claims.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新树((E,(F,G)),H)。问F与G是否形成演化支，E与F能否在排除G时组成同样的演化支。自查：FG可以；EF不可以，二者共同祖先也有G。只有E、F外表相似不能直接推翻这棵树，还要检验趋同与同源证据。

### English

New tree: ((E,(F,G)),H). Is FG a clade? Can EF exclude G and remain a clade? Self-check: FG yes; EF no, because their common ancestor also has G. Similar E/F appearance alone does not overturn the tree; investigate convergence and homologous evidence.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **phylogeny / 系统发育**：类群演化关系与历史。
- **common ancestor / 共同祖先**：两个或更多后代谱系共有的祖先。
- **clade / 演化支**：一个共同祖先及其全部后代。
- **shared derived character / 共享衍生特征**：相对祖先状态新出现并由一组后代共享的特征。
- **convergent evolution / 趋同进化**：不同谱系独立演化出相似特征。
- **homology / 同源**：由于共同祖先而有的对应关系。
- **topology / 拓扑**：树中分支连接的模式，不是纸上朝向。

### English

- **phylogeny / 系统发育**: Evolutionary relationships and history among taxa.
- **common ancestor / 共同祖先**: An ancestor shared by two or more descendant lineages.
- **clade / 演化支**: A common ancestor and all its descendants.
- **shared derived character / 共享衍生特征**: A derived character shared by descendants relative to an ancestral state.
- **convergent evolution / 趋同进化**: Independent evolution of similar features in different lineages.
- **homology / 同源**: Correspondence due to shared ancestry.
- **topology / 拓扑**: The branching connection pattern, not its drawing orientation.

## Mission / 放到生活里

### 中文

用旋转前后两棵树列相同clade，再给一条支持分组的证据与一条替代解释。

检索：不看图说出关键机制、一个带单位证据和一个限制。英语另练：The evidence supports … under …, but does not establish …。现实连接：观察课本或科学新闻是否交代模型条件，不必做人体实验或培养微生物。

### English

List matching clades before and after rotation, then give supporting evidence and one alternative interpretation.

Recall without the diagram: name the mechanism, evidence with units and one limit. Practise separately: The evidence supports … under …, but does not establish … . Real-world connection: check whether a textbook or science report states its model conditions; no human experiment or microbial culture is needed.
