## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

40%的植株是Aa，能直接说A占种群基因的40%吗？

**学习目标（38分钟）**

- 从二倍体基因型计数求等位基因频率。
- 区分个体比例、拷贝频率与抽样范围。
- 在明确假设下使用可选Hardy–Weinberg基准。

### English

If 40% of plants are Aa, does A make up 40% of the allele copies?

**Learning goals (38 minutes)**

- Calculate allele frequencies from diploid genotype counts.
- Distinguish organism fractions, allele-copy frequencies and sampling scope.
- Use an optional Hardy–Weinberg benchmark under explicit assumptions.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

一个个体的基因型与一个种群的等位基因频率，回答不同层次的问题。本课只研究虚构二倍体植物的一个常染色体位点，有A和a两个等位版本。AA、Aa和aa是个体的组合；种群中A的频率则要把所有个体带的A拷贝放在一起数。Aa不是“半个个体”，而是一个携带一个A和一个a拷贝的植株。

初始样本有20株AA、40株Aa、40株aa，共N=100株。A拷贝数为2×20+40=80，a为2×40+40=120，总拷贝2N=200。所以p(A)=80/200=0.4，q(a)=120/200=0.6，p+q=1。Aa个体比例恰好也是0.4只是巧合：若只有100株Aa，Aa比例为1，而p=q=0.5。把个体数与拷贝数分开，才能避免分母混用。

移动滑块会改变样本组成和总植株数，不代表同一个体在一瞬间变换基因型。若所有计数为0，没有可用拷贝，频率未定义，而不是A已灭绝的0频率。非空样本全是aa才得到p=0；全是AA才得到p=1。真实抽样还要说明地点、时间及方法，小样本频率不一定等于整个种群频率。

可选的Hardy–Weinberg基准提出一个有假设的问题：若随机交配、种群足够大并忽略选择、迁移和突变，等位基因频率p、q对应的基因型比例可写为p²、2pq、q²。p=0.4时给出0.16、0.48、0.36，即100株的期望数16、48、36。初始实测是20、40、40；差异值得调查，但样本波动、非随机交配等多种原因都可能造成差异，不能直接指定自然选择。

比较两代时，应保持位点与采样定义一致。频率变化可以发生而外表未明显改变，频率不变也可能伴随基因型比例变化。一个群体统计不能说明每个个体的命运。写证据先交代二倍体、两版本和N，再给计数公式，最后把样本描述与依赖假设的理论预测分开。

### English

A genotype describes an organism, whereas an allele frequency describes a population or sample. We study one autosomal locus with alleles A and a in fictional diploid plants. AA, Aa and aa are individual combinations. To calculate the frequency of A, pool allele copies across the sample. An Aa plant is one organism carrying one A and one a, not half an organism.

Initially there are 20 AA, 40 Aa and 40 aa plants: N = 100. The A count is 2 × 20 + 40 = 80; the a count is 2 × 40 + 40 = 120. With 200 total copies, p(A) = 80/200 = 0.4 and q(a) = 120/200 = 0.6. Their sum is one. The Aa organism fraction happens to equal 0.4 here, but this is a coincidence. A sample of 100 Aa plants has an Aa fraction of one and p = q = 0.5. Individual and copy denominators must remain separate.

The sliders change sample composition; they do not turn a single plant into another genotype. If every count is zero, frequency is undefined because no copies were sampled. That is different from a nonempty all-aa sample with p = 0. Real sampling also needs a location, period and method; sample frequency need not equal the frequency throughout a population.

The optional Hardy–Weinberg benchmark asks a conditional question. Under random mating, a sufficiently large population and no selection, migration or mutation, expected genotype proportions are p², 2pq and q². At p = 0.4 these are 0.16, 0.48 and 0.36, or expected counts 16, 48 and 36 out of 100. The observed 20, 40 and 40 differ. Sampling variation and nonrandom mating are among possible explanations; the difference alone does not identify selection.

Across generations, use the same locus and sampling definition. Allele frequencies can change without an obvious visible change, and genotype proportions can change while allele frequencies remain constant. State the diploid assumption, denominator and calculation before moving from a sample description to an assumption-dependent population inference.

## Depth / 高中延伸

### 中文

可选高中桥梁：p+q=1在本课两等位、完整计数下是恒等式；p²+2pq+q²用于交配模型的基因型期望则要条件。增加第三等位或性染色体情景需重写计数，不机械沿用。

### English

Bridge: p+q=1 is an identity for complete counts of two alleles here; genotype expectations p², 2pq and q² need mating-model assumptions. A third allele or sex-linked locus requires revised counting.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：“显性等位一定常见”“Aa比例就是A频率”。显隐性描述表达关系，不决定频率；全Aa的反例给Aa比例1但A频率0.5。

### English

Misconceptions: dominant alleles must be common, or the Aa fraction is A frequency. Dominance concerns expression, not abundance; an all-Aa sample has Aa fraction 1 but A frequency 0.5.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新样本12株AA、16株Aa、12株aa。自查：N=40，A=40、总80、p=0.5；AA比例12/40=0.3。若采用平衡假设，期望10/20/10而非实测12/16/12；差异本身不能锁定选择。

### English

New sample: 12 AA, 16 Aa, 12 aa. Self-check: N=40, A=40 of 80 copies, p=0.5; AA fraction=12/40=0.3. Equilibrium assumptions predict 10/20/10 rather than observed 12/16/12. The difference alone cannot establish selection.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **allele / 等位基因**：同一基因位置的不同版本。
- **genotype / 基因型**：本课指一个位点上的等位基因组合。
- **gene pool / 基因库**：指定种群中可传递的遗传变异总集合。
- **allele frequency / 等位基因频率**：某等位版本占该位点全部拷贝的比例。
- **diploid / 二倍体**：每个相应常染色体位点通常有两个拷贝。
- **heterozygote / 杂合子**：某位点有两个不同等位基因的个体。
- **random mating / 随机交配**：相对研究位点的基因型不影响配偶组合。

### English

- **allele / 等位基因**: A version at a gene locus.
- **genotype / 基因型**: Here, the allele combination at one locus.
- **gene pool / 基因库**: The transmissible genetic variation in a specified population.
- **allele frequency / 等位基因频率**: The fraction of locus copies that are a particular allele.
- **diploid / 二倍体**: Usually having two copies of an autosomal locus.
- **heterozygote / 杂合子**: An organism with two different alleles at a locus.
- **random mating / 随机交配**: Mating independent of genotype at the studied locus.

## Mission / 放到生活里

### 中文

为一份样本写两个分母，分别报Aa个体比例与A拷贝频率，并写一条不能由样本推出的结论。

检索：不看图说出关键机制、一个带单位证据和一个限制。英语另练：The evidence supports … under …, but does not establish …。现实连接：观察课本或科学新闻是否交代模型条件，不必做人体实验或培养微生物。

### English

Report an Aa organism fraction and an A-copy frequency with their different denominators, then state one unsupported conclusion.

Recall without the diagram: name the mechanism, evidence with units and one limit. Practise separately: The evidence supports … under …, but does not establish … . Real-world connection: check whether a textbook or science report states its model conditions; no human experiment or microbial culture is needed.
