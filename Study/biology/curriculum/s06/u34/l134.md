## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

种群的等位基因频率变化，不自动说明“需要它，所以它变多”。自然选择需要可遗传差异与繁殖贡献差异：在特定环境中，某基因型平均留下更多进入后代基因库的拷贝，相关等位基因才可能上升。适合度不是努力、强壮或道德评价，也不保证同一个版本在所有环境都更有利。

模型延续上一课的二倍体位点，起点p=0.4。打开选择时，AA、Aa、aa相对适合度为1.4、1.2、1。先以随机交配基准得到0.16、0.48、0.36，再乘适合度得0.224、0.576、0.36，总和1.16。下一频率为(0.224+0.576/2)/1.16≈0.441。归一化很重要：适合度是相对权重，不是把p随意加0.4。

漂变来自有限后代的随机取样。模型每代从频率给定的基因池抽2N个拷贝形成N株二倍体植物，没有偏向“更好”的版本。同条件换随机种子会走出不同路径；相同种子可重复检查。N=20通常比N=200波动更强，因为抽样误差相对更大，但不能要求每对单一路径都按大小排序。大群体仍有漂变，只是一般较弱。

迁入必须更新分母。20株居民p=0.4，迁入10株来源p=0.8，混合频率为(20×0.4+10×0.8)/(20+10)=16/30≈0.533。不能直接平均得到0.6，除非两群数量相同。因居民和迁入者都是二倍体，分子分母共同的2可约去；本模型混合后抽样/形成的子代仍恢复为N株，迁入不是让数量永久累加。

实际过程可同时发生。模型顺序为选择、迁入、再可选漂变，没有突变；若A已为0且没有迁入带A，选择和漂变都不能重新创造A。仅凭一条上升轨迹无法区分三种机制，需要重复群体、繁殖成功、迁入来源等证据。有效解释应比较有记录的条件、数值与随机变化范围，避免把某次幸运抽样叫作适应。

### English

A rising allele frequency does not automatically mean that a population needed that allele. Natural selection requires heritable differences associated with unequal reproductive contributions. Under a particular environment, some genotypes leave more copies in later gene pools. Fitness is a relative contribution, not effort or moral worth, and an advantage need not persist in every environment.

The model continues the diploid locus from the previous lesson, starting at p = 0.4. With selection on, AA, Aa and aa fitness values are 1.4, 1.2 and 1. Random-mating starting proportions 0.16, 0.48 and 0.36 become weighted values 0.224, 0.576 and 0.36. Their sum is 1.16. The next A frequency is (0.224 + 0.576/2)/1.16 ≈ 0.441. Normalisation matters; selection does not simply add the fitness advantage to p.

Drift comes from finite random sampling. Each model generation samples 2N copies to form N diploid offspring, without favouring a “better” allele. Different seeds produce different paths; the same seed and conditions reproduce a run. N = 20 generally fluctuates more than N = 200 because relative sampling variation is larger. This is a statement about distributions, not a guarantee about every pair of individual paths. Drift also occurs in large populations.

Immigration changes the denominator. Twenty residents at p = 0.4 plus ten immigrants at p = 0.8 give (20 × 0.4 + 10 × 0.8)/(20 + 10) = 16/30 ≈ 0.533. Simply averaging to 0.6 would require equal group sizes. Both groups are diploid, so the common factor of two cancels. The model forms a new cohort of N plants after pooling; population size does not accumulate each generation.

Selection, migration and drift can operate together. Here the order is selection, immigration and optional sampling, with no mutation. An absent allele cannot reappear through selection or drift alone. One upward path cannot identify the cause. Repeated populations, reproductive-success data and migration records provide stronger evidence than an endpoint interpreted as adaptation.

## Depth / 高中延伸

### 中文

可选高中桥梁：这是随机交配、加性基因型适合度的Wright–Fisher式抽样模型，不是所有生命史。迁入改变基因池后重新形成固定N子代。真实选择与有效群体大小也可能随代变化。

### English

Bridge: this is a random-mating, additive-genotype-fitness, Wright–Fisher-style sampling model, not every life history. Immigration changes the pool before a fixed N offspring cohort forms. Real fitness and effective population size may vary.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：“漂变有目的”“只在小群体”“升高就证明优势”。随机波动无方向，有限大群体也存在；重复对照和繁殖证据才能帮助区分。

### English

Misconceptions: drift is purposeful, occurs only in small populations, or any increase proves advantage. Sampling has no intended direction, occurs in finite large populations too, and needs repeated comparisons and reproductive evidence.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新群体80株p=0.25，迁入20株p=0.75，无选择。自查：加权(80×0.25+20×0.75)/100=0.35，不是0.5。若下一代实际0.31，有限抽样可能参与；需多次独立重复，不能直接声称迁入模型错误。

### English

New population: 80 plants at p=0.25 plus 20 immigrants at p=0.75, without selection. Self-check: (80×0.25+20×0.75)/100=0.35, not 0.5. A realised next-generation value of 0.31 could include finite sampling; use independent repeats before rejecting the mixing mechanism.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **natural selection / 自然选择**：可遗传差异造成相对繁殖贡献不同。
- **fitness / 适合度**：特定条件下对后代基因库的相对贡献。
- **genetic drift / 遗传漂变**：有限群体随机抽样引起的频率变化。
- **gene flow / 基因流**：迁入并进入繁殖群体等过程造成的等位基因流动。
- **random seed / 随机种子**：使同一随机模型能够复验的初始编码。
- **fixation / 固定**：某等位基因频率达到1的状态。
- **bottleneck / 瓶颈**：群体数量大幅减少、随机遗传取样可增强的事件。

### English

- **natural selection / 自然选择**: Heritable differences causing unequal relative reproductive contributions.
- **fitness / 适合度**: Relative contribution to later gene pools under specified conditions.
- **genetic drift / 遗传漂变**: Frequency changes caused by random sampling in finite populations.
- **gene flow / 基因流**: Movement of alleles through migration into breeding populations and related processes.
- **random seed / 随机种子**: An initial code enabling a random model run to be reproduced.
- **fixation / 固定**: A state in which an allele frequency reaches one.
- **bottleneck / 瓶颈**: A sharp population reduction that can intensify genetic sampling effects.

## Mission / 放到生活里

### 中文

对比选择单独与漂变单独两情景，记录N、种子、起终频率；提出区分两者还需要的证据。

检索：不看图说出关键机制、一个带单位证据和一个限制。英语另练：The evidence supports … under …, but does not establish …。现实连接：观察课本或科学新闻是否交代模型条件，不必做人体实验或培养微生物。

### English

Compare selection-only and drift-only scenarios, recording N, seed and starting/ending frequencies. Propose evidence needed to distinguish their mechanisms.

Recall without the diagram: name the mechanism, evidence with units and one limit. Practise separately: The evidence supports … under …, but does not establish … . Real-world connection: check whether a textbook or science report states its model conditions; no human experiment or microbial culture is needed.
