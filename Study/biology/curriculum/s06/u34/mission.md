# 给两条频率轨迹做机制鉴别 / Compare mechanisms behind two frequency trajectories

## 中文

比较两情景，起点pA=.4、N20、10代：A仅选择，AA/Aa/aa适合度1.4/1.2/1；B仅漂变，种子17，再改种子29并比较N200。记录每条起终频率与重复条件；频率是拷贝比例，不是植株数。解释选择的繁殖权重与漂变随机取样，不能仅凭一条上升路径定因；提出多群体重复、繁殖成功或迁入记录。另算20株p.4混合10株p.8得.533，注明子代恢复N的模型假设。用CER给两种机制、有数值条件证据和一条局限；可补树旋转不改clade或品系×环境对照，避免把适合度等同努力、遗传率等同命运。英语另练“Under …, the frequency changed from … to …; this is consistent with … but does not distinguish …”。中文科学解释完整可达标。

自查：主张有条件；证据有数值、分母和单位；推理逐箭头解释；局限和下一步有具体对象。允许保存草稿并回课图修订，英语术语与生物理解分开。

## English

Compare two scenarios starting pA=.4,N20,10 generations: A selection only, AA/Aa/aa fitness1.4/1.2/1; B drift only,seed17,then seed29 and N200. Record starting/ending frequencies and replication conditions; frequency is an allele-copy fraction, not plant count. Explain reproductive weights versus random sampling; a single increase does not establish cause. Propose replicated populations,reproductive-success or migration records. Also calculate pooling20 plants at.4 with10 at.8=.533 and state the fixed-N offspring assumption. Write CER with both mechanisms,conditional numerical evidence and a limitation. Optionally add invariant clades under tree rotation or a lineage×environment comparison. Fitness is not effort and heritability is not destiny. Practise separately: “Under …, the frequency changed from … to …; this is consistent with … but does not distinguish …”. Complete Chinese scientific reasoning can meet the biology goal.

Self-check: qualified claim; numerical evidence with denominators and units; arrow-by-arrow reasoning; a specific limitation and follow-up. Save and revise using lesson diagrams. English vocabulary and biological understanding are assessed separately.
