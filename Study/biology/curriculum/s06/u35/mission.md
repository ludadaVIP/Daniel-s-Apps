# U35｜虚拟DNA证据听证会 / A Virtual DNA Evidence Hearing

## 任务 / Task

仅用屏幕上的虚构材料，不开展PCR、电泳、培养或编辑。提交一份主张–证据–推理–局限（CER）与可修订的研究决定。

Use fictional on-screen material only: no PCR, electrophoresis, culturing or editing. Submit a claim–evidence–reasoning–limitation account and a revisable research decision.

## 证据包 / Evidence pack

- 对照有效时，样本与参照约400 bp条带只能支持大小相近；ATCG/AGCT等长不同序列。空白有信号或阳性失败时先查来源与方法；普通阳性成功不能排除样本特异抑制。
- With satisfactory controls, approximately 400 bp bands support similar size only; ATCG/AGCT illustrate equal length with different order. A signalling blank or failed positive requires investigation; a normal positive does not exclude sample-specific inhibition.
- 虚构植物100份只测20份：12预期、5其他修复、3未改；12/20=60%只用于已测子样本，80未测。靶位点不能证明无脱靶或预期性状。
- Of 100 fictional plant samples, only 20 were assessed: 12 intended, five other repair, three unchanged. The 60% denominator is 20; 80 are unassessed. A target-site result does not prove off-target absence or a trait.

## 作品 / Product

写有限主张、至少两条含单位/分母证据、连接证据的推理、一项局限及下一步。支持“有条件继续封闭研究”或“暂缓扩大先补证据”均可；呈现另一立场的强理由，列受影响者、充分信息与自愿参与、公平、生态、独立监督/停止条件及会改变决定的新证据。不可把未知写成已证实危害，也不可把潜在效益当自动许可。

Give a bounded claim, at least two pieces of evidence with units/denominators, reasoning, a limitation and next evidence. Support either contained research or pausing expansion, present the other position’s strongest reason, and address stakeholders, informed voluntary participation, fairness, ecology, independent oversight/stopping rules and evidence that would change your decision. Unknown is not proven harm; potential benefit is not automatic permission.

## 自查 / Self-check

评价事实准确、证据层级、分母、诚实不确定与论证质量，不按政策立场评分。生物解释语言自选；补英文句框：“The evidence supports … in the assessed samples, but does not establish … . I would revise this decision if … .”

Assess factual accuracy, evidence layers, denominators, honest uncertainty and argument quality, never policy allegiance. Explain biology in either language and complete the English scaffold above.
