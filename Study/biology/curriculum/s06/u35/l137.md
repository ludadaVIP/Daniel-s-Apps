## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

没有目标模板却运行很多轮，会不会“复制”出目标？空白也有信号时，样本信号还能直接相信吗？

**学习目标**

- 连接模板、引物和聚合酶，解释目标区段选择性复制。
- 有条件使用理想倍增公式，比较效率。
- 用空白/阳性对照解释污染、抑制和未检出局限。

### English

Can many cycles copy a target that was never present? Can a sample signal be trusted if the blank also signals?

**Learning goals**

- Connect template, primers and polymerase to selective target copying.
- Use ideal doubling conditionally and compare efficiencies.
- Use blank/positive controls to interpret contamination, inhibition and non-detection.

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

- 有模板、合适引物与聚合酶才可复制目标区段。
- 没有目标也会因循环多而必然产生目标。
- 空白有信号时反而说明样本更可靠。

预测不计分；操作后可修订理由。

### English

- A target template, suitable primers and polymerase enable target copying.
- Enough cycles necessarily produce the target without a template.
- A signalling blank makes the sample more reliable.

Unscored prediction; revise the reason after exploring.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**先抓机制。** PCR不是从无到有创造一种DNA，也不是把细胞全部遗传物质一模一样地复制一遍。已有DNA提供模板；引物与模板上互补位置结合，界定所关注区段；DNA聚合酶从起点延伸新链。新产生的链又可成为以后循环的模板，因此选定区段的拷贝量可以迅速增加。图中的灰色侧翼提醒我们：“目标增加”不等于“全基因组增加”。

**再追过程。** 概念顺序是模板两链分开、引物结合、聚合酶延伸，再重复。它利用与细胞复制相通的互补配对和模板思想，但发生情境、起始方式与调节不同。这里不提供真实引物、温度、时间或操作方案。动画显示的是信息复制逻辑，绝不是可照着做的实验流程；PCR也不会把原序列改成想要的新序列。

**算一个有限模型。** 若起始目标N₀=2份，且每轮全部有效翻倍，则N=N₀×2ⁿ，4轮为32份。固定效率e=0.5时，模型改为N=N₀(1+e)ⁿ，4轮约10.125期望份；小数表示模型期望量，不表示半个DNA分子。效率固定、无资源耗尽是重要假设，真实反应会受抑制、试剂变化及平台期影响，不能无限指数外推。

**证据要过对照关。** 模型空白若含目标，提示目标污染，对照不通过。阳性初始2份，必须运行后实际新增，才能验证本模型的扩增；0轮或效率0不通过。共有抑制把有效效率设0，阳性与含2份目标样本始终保留2份，新增0，绝不是DNA消失。现实阳性若未出现预期扩增信号，不能支持简单阴性判断；样本还可能有特有抑制，普通阳性成功不能排除它。

**把结论停在边界。** 起始目标为0时，本复制模型永远得到0；但现实的“未检出”也可能来自取样未含目标、量太少或方法检出能力不足。有效对照下出现预期目标信号，可支持“这份材料提供了与目标相符的证据”，不能直接推出整个对象的身份、健康或活性。回看目标、对照与限定语，三者合起来才是检测推理。

还要区分“模型有几份目标”和“仪器是否检出信号”。这里表中是目标期望拷贝量，没有模拟检出阈值、低拷贝随机性或仪器噪声；0轮阳性有模板也不等于现实检测已成功。空白信号在现实还可能涉及非特异扩增等情况，本模型只简化成目标污染，不能用于诊断或替代专业质量控制。

### English

**Mechanism first.** PCR increases copies of a selected DNA region. Existing DNA supplies a template, complementary primers mark the region of interest and provide starting points, and DNA polymerase extends new strands. Copies can become templates in subsequent rounds. The grey flanks in the diagram matter: increasing one target region is different from copying an entire genome.

**Follow the conceptual sequence.** Strands separate, primers bind, polymerase extends, and copying repeats. Complementary pairing connects this idea to cellular DNA replication, although the setting, initiation and regulation differ. This lesson supplies no real primers, temperatures, timings or experimental instructions. The animation shows information-copying logic. It is not a laboratory procedure, and copying is not genome editing.

**Calculate with assumptions visible.** Starting with two target copies and ideal 100% efficiency, N=N₀×2ⁿ gives 32 copies after four cycles. In our alternative fixed-efficiency model, N=N₀(1+e)ⁿ. At e=0.5, four cycles give 10.125 expected copies. A fractional expectation is not a partial molecule. Fixed efficiency and unlimited resources are simplifications; real amplification can be inhibited and eventually plateau.

**Controls constrain interpretation.** Target in the model blank indicates contamination and invalid controls. The positive starts with two copies: only an actual increase after running verifies model amplification. Zero cycles or zero efficiency do not pass. Shared inhibition sets effective efficiency to zero: the positive and a two-template sample retain two copies, adding zero, rather than losing DNA. Real failure of an expected positive amplification signal prevents a simple negative interpretation; a normal positive cannot exclude sample-specific inhibition.

**Stop at the evidence boundary.** With no starting target, this copying model cannot create one. In real testing, however, non-detection can also reflect little target, an unrepresentative subsample or limited detection capability. A suitable signal with satisfactory controls supports a carefully specified statement about the material tested. It does not by itself establish an organism’s identity, viability or health. Always connect the selected target, the controls and the qualification, rather than treating a large copy number as a complete answer.

Also distinguish modeled copies from detected instrument signal. This table models no threshold, low-copy randomness or instrument noise; a positive containing template at cycle zero is not a demonstrated successful real test. A real blank signal can also involve non-specific amplification, whereas this model assumes target contamination.

## Depth / 高中延伸

### 中文

高中桥梁：推导Nₙ₊₁=Nₙ+eNₙ。e=0表示没有增加，不代表模板已消失；随轮次变化的e不能套固定公式。

### English

Bridge: derive Nₙ₊₁=Nₙ+eNₙ. At e=0 there is no increase, not disappearance of template. Varying efficiency cannot be represented by one fixed-e formula.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“PCR阳性=活的生物/完整基因组/疾病确诊。”扩增检测某目标证据；这些是另一些需要额外证据的问题。

### English

“Positive PCR means a living organism, complete genome or diagnosis.” A target signal addresses a narrower question; those claims need additional evidence.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

虚构树叶检测的仪器信号（不是目标份数，任意单位a.u.）：样本0、空白0、阳性0。写两句判断与下一步。自查要点：阳性未获预期信号，使阴性不可解释；不代表模板消失，先查方法/抑制，不宣称叶片无目标，也不在家实验。

### English

Fictional leaf-test instrument signals (not target copies, arbitrary units a.u.): sample 0, blank 0, positive 0. Give an interpretation and next step. Self-check: the missing expected positive signal prevents a negative conclusion; it does not mean templates vanished. Investigate method/inhibition rather than claiming absence or conducting a home experiment.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **template / 模板**: 供互补链复制的信息来源。
- **primer / 引物**: 与模板互补并为延伸提供起点的短核酸。
- **DNA polymerase / DNA聚合酶**: 延伸DNA新链的酶。
- **amplification / 扩增**: 增加选定区段的拷贝量。
- **cycle / 循环**: 本概念模型中一轮重复复制过程。
- **blank control / 空白对照**: 不含目标模板的比较，用于检查污染等。
- **positive control / 阳性对照**: 预期能给出信号的已知比较。
- **inhibition / 抑制**: 使预期扩增受阻的影响。

### English

- **template / 模板**: The information source for a complementary copy.
- **primer / 引物**: A short nucleic acid binding complementarily and providing an extension start.
- **DNA polymerase / DNA聚合酶**: An enzyme extending a new DNA strand.
- **amplification / 扩增**: Increasing copies of a selected segment.
- **cycle / 循环**: One repeated copying round in this conceptual model.
- **blank control / 空白对照**: A no-target comparison checking contamination and related problems.
- **positive control / 阳性对照**: A known comparison expected to yield a signal.
- **inhibition / 抑制**: An influence that obstructs expected amplification.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] PCR在本课主要做什么？
   A. 复制选定DNA区段
   B. 改写所有基因
   C. 直接读全部碱基
   答案: A. 引物界定目标、聚合酶复制；扩增不等于编辑或测序。 复习: 回看图中目标两端与灰色侧翼。

2. [concept] 空白有目标信号应怎样解释？
   A. 不影响任何结论
   B. 提示污染，先检查再解释样本
   C. 证明样本中目标很多
   答案: B. 空白不应有目标。其信号使来源不确定，不能把所有信号归样本。 复习: 回看空白列与对照情境。

3. [reasoning] 2份目标、4轮、理想100%效率得多少？
   A. 8份，所有真实样本都如此
   B. 32份，只在理想假设下
   C. 0份，因为PCR不能复制
   答案: B. 2×2⁴=32；50%固定效率时为2×1.5⁴=10.125期望份，真实过程还有平台期。 复习: 回看循环4与效率按钮。

4. [vocabulary] inhibition指什么？
   A. 确定不存在目标
   B. 把DNA改写
   C. 阻碍预期扩增
   答案: C. 抑制阻止新增而不删除原有模板；本模型阳性仍2份、新增0，对照不通过。现实未检出不等于不存在。 复习: 回看共有抑制时阳性2份、新增0和独立对照状态。

### English

1. [concept] What does PCR principally do here?
   A. Copy a selected DNA segment
   B. Rewrite every gene
   C. Read every base directly
   Answer: A. Primers bound a target and polymerase copies it; amplification is neither editing nor sequencing. Revisit: Revisit the target boundaries and grey flanking DNA.

2. [concept] How should a target signal in the blank be treated?
   A. It affects no conclusions
   B. Suspect contamination; investigate before interpreting samples
   C. It proves abundant target in the sample
   Answer: B. A target-free blank should not signal. Its signal makes origin uncertain, so not all signal can be attributed to the sample. Revisit: Revisit the blank column and control scenario.

3. [reasoning] With two target copies and four ideal 100%-efficient cycles, how many result?
   A. 8, in every real sample
   B. 32, only under ideal assumptions
   C. 0, because PCR cannot copy
   Answer: B. 2×2⁴=32; fixed 50% efficiency gives 2×1.5⁴=10.125 expected copies, while real processes also plateau. Revisit: Revisit cycle 4 and efficiency settings.

4. [vocabulary] What does inhibition mean?
   A. Proven target absence
   B. Rewriting DNA
   C. Obstructing expected amplification
   Answer: C. Inhibition prevents new copies without deleting templates: the model positive remains at two, adds zero and fails controls. Real non-detection is not absence. Revisit: Revisit the inhibited positive’s two copies, zero increase and separate control status.
