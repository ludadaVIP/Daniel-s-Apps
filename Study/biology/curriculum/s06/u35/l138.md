## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

两条带出现在相近高度，是否意味着碱基顺序相同？为什么ATCG与AGCT能有相同长度？

**学习目标**

- 解释DNA向正极迁移与常见小片段迁得较远。
- 用marker估计大小并承认误差与分辨力。
- 区分电泳大小、测序顺序和身份主张的证据层级。

### English

Do bands at similar heights have the same base order? Why can ATCG and AGCT have equal length?

**Learning goals**

- Explain migration towards the positive pole and generally farther movement of smaller fragments.
- Use markers to estimate size while recognising error and resolution.
- Distinguish size, sequence and identity claims as different evidence levels.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**先问工具测什么。** 电泳利用电场和介质使分子迁移；DNA骨架带负电，通常向正极移动。在本课讨论的常见线性DNA片段与凝胶条件下，较小片段通常更容易穿过介质、迁得更远。不能把这句话推广到任意分子或任意DNA构象。起点、正负极与marker必须一起读，不能凭条带在屏幕上“比较低”就猜身份。

**用标准而不是凭感觉。** marker提供已知大小。图中1600、800、400、200、100 bp按倍数变化，使用对数大小示意坐标；它们在图上可等距，但相邻大小差不是同样多的bp。样本在400 bp附近，只支持一个带误差的大小估计。本图没有真实距离单位，不是可用于实际标定的仪器曲线。

**算一次测量边界。** 模型坐标y=90+170×log₂(1600/bp)/4。400 bp在175，450 bp约167.8，相差约7.2示意坐标单位。若实际读数或分辨力未知，不能因为绘图软件画出两线就说真实样本一定可区分。读数过度精细也不会创造真实精度；标准、重复检测和记录来源有助于判断可靠性。

**再换信息层次。** 测序回答碱基怎样排列。虚构四碱基窗口ATCG与AGCT都长4个碱基，却顺序不同；完整片段也可同为约400 bp。电泳相近与局部测序不同可以同时成立。窗口只是教学符号，不是真实可执行目标，也不代表已读完全部400 bp或全基因组。一个清楚条带往往对应大量相近分子，而不是一条分子的照片。

**最后评价主张。** “大小相近”“这个窗口序列不同”“样本来自同一对象”是逐渐增加要求的判断。身份还涉及多个有区分力的位点、样本取样与保管、混合来源和统计比较；行为或责任更不能由一条带推出。先确认空白、阳性等对照，再把单位、误差和证据层级写清，才不会把漂亮图形误当完整证明。

若同一材料重复读数不同，先查看标准、图像质量和记录过程，而不是挑最接近期望的一次。若两样本在误差范围内位置接近，诚实说“未能由此分辨大小差别”，不等于它们必相同。图形只保存一部分信息；样本标签、原始图与对照记录也是证据链的一部分，失去来源后再清晰的带也不能补回缺失的身份信息。

### English

**Ask what the tool measures.** Electrophoresis moves molecules through a medium using an electric field. DNA’s backbone is negatively charged, so it generally moves towards the positive pole. For the common linear DNA fragments considered here, smaller fragments usually migrate farther through a gel. Other molecular types, DNA shapes and conditions require additional care. Read the origin, electrical direction and marker together.

**Estimate using a standard.** A marker supplies fragments of known size. Our diagram uses 1600, 800, 400, 200 and 100 bp. Equal ratios are equally spaced on this schematic logarithmic size scale, although adjacent differences in base pairs are unequal. A band near the 400 bp marker supports an approximate fragment-size estimate, not an exact sequence. These coordinates are not calibrated physical distances.

**Work through a measurement limit.** The teaching model uses y=90+170×log₂(1600/bp)/4. A 400 bp fragment is at coordinate 175, while 450 bp is approximately 167.8: about 7.2 schematic units apart. Actual resolution and reading uncertainty are unspecified. Two lines being visibly separate in software does not establish that real fragments would be distinguishable. More decimal places cannot manufacture precision.

**Change the information layer.** Sequencing determines base order. The toy windows ATCG and AGCT each contain four bases but differ in order. Longer fragments containing them could both be approximately 400 bp. Similar gel positions and different local sequences therefore do not contradict one another. The short windows are teaching symbols, not actionable targets or complete 400 bp sequences. A band also represents many molecules rather than a photograph of one molecule.

**Match the claim to the layer.** Similar size, a difference in one sequence window and common sample identity are increasingly demanding claims. Identity requires suitable additional markers, sample provenance, consideration of mixtures and appropriate comparisons. Behaviour or responsibility cannot follow from a single band. Check controls, units, uncertainty and the exact information supplied before converting an attractive diagram into a stronger conclusion than it supports.

If repeated readings differ, inspect the marker, image quality and records instead of selecting the preferred result. Failure to resolve a size difference is not proof of equality. Sample labels, original images and control records also belong to the evidence chain; a clear band cannot restore missing provenance.

## Depth / 高中延伸

### 中文

高中桥梁：对数坐标把倍数关系转成间距。想一想200到400与400到800为什么可同间距，但都不等于“每毫米相同bp”。

### English

Bridge: logarithmic coordinates turn size ratios into distances. Explain equal spacing for 200→400 and 400→800 without claiming a fixed number of bp per millimetre.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“同一高度条带就是同一序列甚至同一人。”同样长度的文字也能内容不同；DNA还需适当序列与多位点证据，身份不等于行为。

### English

“Same-height bands mean the same sequence or person.” Equal-length texts can differ in content; DNA needs suitable sequence/multilocus evidence, and identity is not behaviour.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

虚构种子A和B均有约800 bp条带。有人说“同品种且一定同来源”。写可支持、尚不能支持和新证据各一句。自查：只支持相近大小；测序/适当多位点与来源记录；不能从单带断身份。

### English

Fictional seeds A/B both show approximately 800 bp bands. Someone claims the same variety and certain common origin. Give supported, unsupported and next-evidence statements. Self-check: similar size only; suitable sequence/multilocus and provenance records are needed; a band alone cannot identify origin.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **electrophoresis / 电泳**: 用电场使分子迁移并分离的技术。
- **band / 条带**: 许多迁移位置相近分子的可见信号。
- **marker / 标记标准**: 提供已知片段大小的比较。
- **base pair / 碱基对**: 双链DNA片段大小常用单位bp。
- **sequencing / 测序**: 确定核酸碱基的排列顺序。
- **resolution / 分辨力**: 区分接近信号的能力。
- **migration / 迁移**: 分子在介质中位置发生变化。
- **measurement error / 测量误差**: 读数与所测量量的差异或不确定。

### English

- **electrophoresis / 电泳**: A technique separating molecules by movement in an electric field.
- **band / 条带**: Visible signal from many molecules at similar migration positions.
- **marker / 标记标准**: A comparison containing fragments of known sizes.
- **base pair / 碱基对**: A common unit, bp, for double-stranded DNA fragment size.
- **sequencing / 测序**: Determining the order of nucleic-acid bases.
- **resolution / 分辨力**: Ability to distinguish nearby signals.
- **migration / 迁移**: Change of molecular position through a medium.
- **measurement error / 测量误差**: Difference or uncertainty associated with a measured value.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] 本课常见DNA片段迁移方向是什么？
   A. 向正极
   B. 永远停在起点
   C. 因为正电而向负极
   答案: A. DNA骨架带负电，电场中向正极；介质与大小也影响迁移。 复习: 回看右侧正极箭头和marker。

2. [concept] 一条约400 bp的带直接提供什么？
   A. 完整基因组序列
   B. 相对于标准的片段大小证据
   C. 唯一身份和行为
   答案: B. 条带位置支持大小估计，不逐个读碱基，更不证明行为。 复习: 回看底部ATCG与AGCT等长反例。

3. [reasoning] 样本与参照同约400 bp；给定窗口分别ATCG和AGCT。最佳结论？
   A. 同大小所以序列完全相同
   B. 窗口不同，片段大小仍可相同；全基因组未知
   C. 测序否定电泳原理
   答案: B. 两工具测不同层次；局部序列差异与相同长度并不矛盾。 复习: 切换只有电泳/再给测序并比较能说什么。

4. [vocabulary] resolution在此指什么？
   A. 政策决议
   B. 把每条带变成一个人
   C. 区分相近信号的能力
   答案: C. 画出两个位置不保证实际仪器可区分；要知道分辨力和误差。 复习: 回看400/450 bp与7.2示意单位差。

### English

1. [concept] Which direction do typical DNA fragments move here?
   A. Towards the positive pole
   B. Always remain at the origin
   C. Towards negative because DNA is positive
   Answer: A. DNA’s backbone is negatively charged, so migration is towards the positive pole; medium and size also matter. Revisit: Revisit the positive-pole arrow and marker.

2. [concept] What does an approximately 400 bp band directly provide?
   A. A complete genome sequence
   B. Fragment-size evidence relative to a marker
   C. Unique identity and behaviour
   Answer: B. Position supports a size estimate, not base-by-base reading or behaviour. Revisit: Revisit the equal-length ATCG/AGCT counterexample.

3. [reasoning] Sample and reference are both about 400 bp; stated windows are ATCG and AGCT. Best conclusion?
   A. Same size means identical sequence
   B. Windows differ while fragment sizes can match; whole genomes remain unknown
   C. Sequencing disproves electrophoresis
   Answer: B. The tools assess different evidence layers; local sequence differences are consistent with equal lengths. Revisit: Switch gel-only/add-sequence and compare permitted statements.

4. [vocabulary] What does resolution mean here?
   A. A policy decision
   B. Assigning each band to a person
   C. Ability to distinguish nearby signals
   Answer: C. Two drawn positions do not guarantee instrumental distinguishability; resolution and error matter. Revisit: Revisit 400/450 bp and their 7.2 schematic-unit separation.
