## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

把DNA在靶位点切开，是否等于每个细胞都获得同一个预期结果？12/20能写成100份里已有60份成功吗？

**学习目标**

- 区别PCR扩增、引入遗传材料与靶向编辑。
- 用切割后修复解释混合结果。
- 以真实分母和检测范围评价成功率、脱靶与性状主张。

### English

Does a targeted cut give every cell the same intended result? Does 12/20 mean 60 of 100 were observed to succeed?

**Learning goals**

- Distinguish PCR amplification, introduced genetic material and targeted editing.
- Explain mixed outcomes through repair following a cut.
- Evaluate success, off-target and trait claims using tested denominators and coverage.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**区分三种目的。** PCR让选定DNA区段的拷贝增多，主要不是改写序列。基因工程是改变遗传材料的广义技术集合；转基因强调引入外源遗传材料，编辑强调针对基因组位置作改变。它们不是处处互斥的盒子：有些编辑不保留外源DNA，有些可引入片段。不能把所有改变都说成完全相同的过程或风险。

**把切割和结果拆开。** 本课展示一种“识别靶位点→切割→细胞修复→检查”的概念路径。靶向工具不是万能橡皮擦；切开后还要由修复过程形成产物，不同细胞或材料可能得到不同改变，也可能仍未得到预期改变。并非所有编辑工具都造成双链断裂，本图只解释一种常见思路，不是所有方法的通用流程，更不提供实际实验方案。

**算清分母。** 虚构植物材料共100份，只检查其中20份：12份预期产物、5份其他修复、3份未改变。已测样本预期比例12/20=60%；其他修复25%，未变15%。三个数加100%是对这20份的分类，不表示全体100份都已了解。其余80份未测，样本是否随机、有无优先挑成功者也未知，因此不能直接写“已知60份成功”。

**再查检测范围。** 看见靶位点符合预期，不等于其他位置没有改变；那是脱靶问题。即使某项检查未发现异常，也要说明检查了哪些位点、多少材料以及方法能发现什么。未完全编辑、材料混合和检测范围有限是不同局限，不能用一句“技术精准”同时消除它们。重复研究应增加有代表性的材料和适当的其他位点证据。

**从序列走向性状仍需证据。** 预期序列改变可能影响蛋白质或调控，但生长与生态表现还受多个基因、环境及发育影响。虚构耐旱植物即使获得目标序列，也不能凭这一页证明田间节水、无生态后果或人人公平受益。评价应同时询问目标收益、可能损害、样本边界与替代方案；在下一课把这些经验问题与社会价值分开讨论。

还应区别“没有发现”和“证明确实没有”。只测少数位点或少量材料的阴性结果，会留下尚未覆盖的范围。报告可写本次检查未见某变化，但应同时给方法范围。合理比较还需未经编辑材料与合适检测对照，才能判断差异是否与所讨论的改变有关，而不把原有差异也算作编辑效果。

### English

**Separate the purposes.** PCR increases copies of a chosen DNA segment; it does not principally rewrite the sequence. Genetic engineering broadly uses technology to alter genetic material. Transgenesis emphasises introduced foreign genetic material, while genome editing emphasises targeted genomic changes. The categories can overlap. Some edits introduce a fragment; others leave no foreign DNA. Different methods should not be assumed to share identical processes or risks.

**Separate a cut from its outcome.** Our schematic follows target recognition → cutting → cellular repair → assessment. A targeting tool is not a perfect eraser. Repair and differences among cells or samples influence the resulting products. Some products match the intention, some contain other changes, and some remain unchanged. Not every editing tool makes a double-strand break, so this diagram illustrates one common idea rather than every method. No practical editing instructions are supplied.

**Calculate the tested denominator.** There are 100 fictional plant samples, but only 20 are assessed: 12 intended products, five other repair products and three unchanged. The intended fraction among tested samples is 12/20=60%; other repair is 25%, unchanged 15%. These sum to 100% of the assessed subset, not complete knowledge of all 100 samples. Eighty remain unassessed. Sampling was not specified as random, so “60 of 100 are known successful” is unjustified.

**Check assessment coverage.** A correct target-site result does not establish that every other site is unchanged. That is a separate off-target question. Even “no changes detected” needs a statement of tested sites, sampled material and detection capability. Incomplete editing, mixed material and limited coverage are distinct limitations. Calling a tool precise does not eliminate them all.

**Traits require another evidence step.** Sequence changes can affect proteins or regulation, but growth and ecological outcomes also depend on environment, development and other genes. An intended sequence in a fictional drought-tolerant plant does not demonstrate water savings in every setting, ecological safety or fair access. Evaluate potential benefits, harms, sampling boundaries and alternatives. The next lesson separates these empirical questions from the values that also shape decisions.

Distinguish “not found” from “shown absent”. Testing few sites or samples leaves unassessed regions. Report what was checked alongside non-detection. Suitable unedited comparisons and assessment controls also help separate pre-existing differences from changes relevant to the proposed edit.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“基因编辑=一定转入外来基因=每个细胞必成功。”方法范围有重叠与区别，靶向、修复、检测、性状验证各是一步。

### English

“Editing always inserts foreign genes and succeeds in every cell.” Categories overlap and differ; targeting, repair, assessment and trait validation are separate steps.

### English

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **genetic engineering / 基因工程**: 用技术改变遗传材料的广义做法。
- **genome editing / 基因组编辑**: 针对基因组位置进行改变的方法集合。
- **transgenesis / 转基因**: 将外源遗传材料引入生物的做法。
- **target site / 靶位点**: 计划影响的基因组位置。
- **repair / 修复**: 细胞处理DNA损伤或断裂的过程。
- **off-target change / 脱靶**: 预期位置以外的改变。
- **incomplete editing / 未完全编辑**: 部分材料或细胞仍未获得预期改变。
- **representativeness / 代表性**: 样本反映所讨论总体的程度。

### English

- **genetic engineering / 基因工程**: A broad use of technology to alter genetic material.
- **genome editing / 基因组编辑**: Methods for making targeted genomic changes.
- **transgenesis / 转基因**: Introducing foreign genetic material into an organism.
- **target site / 靶位点**: A genomic location intended to be affected.
- **repair / 修复**: Cellular processes responding to DNA damage or breaks.
- **off-target change / 脱靶**: A change outside the intended location.
- **incomplete editing / 未完全编辑**: Some material or cells lack the intended change.
- **representativeness / 代表性**: How well a sample reflects the population being discussed.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] PCR和编辑的主要区别是什么？
   A. PCR增拷贝；编辑改变序列
   B. 两者都必然加入外源DNA
   C. PCR能任意指定新性状
   答案: A. 复制信息与改写信息是不同目标；有的编辑不引入外源DNA。 复习: 回看靶向、切割、修复的顺序。

2. [concept] 为什么切割不等于指定产物？
   A. DNA不能修复
   B. 修复可产生不同改变或仍未改变
   C. 每次都会产生新物种
   答案: B. 工具影响靶位点后，细胞修复与材料差异仍影响结果，需检测产物。 复习: 回看预期12、其他5、未变3分支。

3. [reasoning] 100份中测20份，12预期。哪句诚实？
   A. 已知100份里60份成功
   B. 已测20份的60%预期，80份未知
   C. 其余80份一定失败
   答案: B. 12/20=60%，不能把未测变成已知成功或失败；取样代表性也未知。 复习: 回看图题只测20/100和底部80未测。

4. [vocabulary] off-target change指什么？
   A. 目标DNA复制增多
   B. 预期位置外的改变
   C. 任何有益性状
   答案: B. 靶位点正确不证明其他位点都没改变；检测覆盖范围要说清。 复习: 回看尚需检查其他位点的提示。

### English

1. [concept] What principally distinguishes PCR from editing?
   A. PCR increases copies; editing changes sequence
   B. Both necessarily add foreign DNA
   C. PCR specifies any new trait
   Answer: A. Copying and rewriting information have different goals; some editing does not introduce foreign DNA. Revisit: Revisit targeting, cutting and repair.

2. [concept] Why does cutting not guarantee a specified product?
   A. DNA cannot be repaired
   B. Repair can yield different changes or no intended change
   C. Every cut creates a new species
   Answer: B. Cellular repair and material differences still affect outcomes after targeting, so products need assessment. Revisit: Revisit branches: intended 12, other repair 5, unchanged 3.

3. [reasoning] Of 100 samples, 20 assessed and 12 intended. Which statement is honest?
   A. 60 of 100 are known successful
   B. 60% of 20 assessed are intended; 80 are unknown
   C. The other 80 certainly failed
   Answer: B. 12/20=60%; unassessed samples are neither known successes nor failures, and representativeness is unspecified. Revisit: Revisit the 20/100 title and 80-unassessed note.

4. [vocabulary] What is an off-target change?
   A. More copies of target DNA
   B. A change outside the intended location
   C. Any beneficial trait
   Answer: B. A correct target does not establish absence of changes elsewhere; assessment coverage must be specified. Revisit: Revisit the note about assessing other sites.
