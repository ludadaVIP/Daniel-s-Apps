## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

同一份有限的植物编辑证据，为什么有人支持封闭研究，有人支持先补证据再扩大？谁应该参与决定？

**学习目标**

- 区分经验事实、不确定与价值判断。
- 比较至少两种可辩护决定并回应反方理由。
- 把同意、公平、生态与治理写成可修订条件。

### English

Why might the same limited plant-editing evidence support contained research or a pause before expansion? Who should participate?

**Learning goals**

- Separate empirical facts, uncertainties and value judgements.
- Compare two defensible decisions and answer a counterargument.
- Turn consent, fairness, ecology and governance into revisable conditions.

- Uncertainty means harm has been proven.

Unscored prediction; revise the reason after exploring.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**先分三类句子。** “已测20份中12份目标产物”是可核查的经验陈述；“长期生态影响还不清楚”指出证据空白；“应优先避免难逆生态后果”表达价值优先。三类都可进入讨论，却不能互相冒充。实验不能直接测出公平应占多少权重，价值偏好也不能把12/20变成100/100。讨论先把事实说准确，分歧才有真正对象。

**再列受影响者。** 虚构耐旱植物可能关系农户、当地社群、消费者、研究团队和生态管理者。谁获得潜在收益，谁承担成本与不确定风险，谁能参与或拒绝，未必一致。知情同意要求充分信息与自愿；本课不涉及实际人体试验，但尊重人的信息参与是思考线索。HHS伦理原则提供框架，不能当成本植物研究的法律许可或统一生态法规。

**同一事实容许合理分歧。** 一种立场是有条件继续封闭研究：更多证据可能帮助解决问题，封闭条件可减少部分接触。它仍须承认研究成本、意外外逸与不公平分配，设置独立检查和停止条件。另一种立场是暂缓扩大、先补证据：优先避免难逆后果，调查生态路径与取样代表性。它也应承认延迟可能推迟效益，不能把所有未知说成已证实危害。

**把理由变成可检查承诺。** 若支持继续，写清只在哪些边界内、谁监督、发现什么就停；若支持暂缓，写清缺什么证据、如何获得、何时重审。两种立场都应回应最强反方，而非只挑最弱理由。12/20=60%只用于已测子样本，不能计算成“60%安全”或“60%值得做”；成功率、生态风险和伦理接受度不是同一个量。

**决策是一条公开的推理链。** 好答案同时包含准确事实、诚实未知、可解释价值、受影响者声音与修订条件。公平还包括谁负担监测成本、谁有机会获得收益，以及信息是否易懂。科学证据能改变风险判断，社会讨论能改变价值权重，治理让这些变化能影响行动。这里评价论证品质，不按赞成或反对某技术决定学生是否“道德正确”。

可以先用另一方也接受的事实复述其理由，再说明自己把哪种价值放得更重。若新证据会改变经验判断，承认修订不是输掉辩论；若分歧仍来自价值，也应准确指出分歧所在。公开理由比口号更便于监督，学生无须透露个人隐私或被迫代表某个社群发言。

### English

**Separate three kinds of statement.** “Twelve of twenty assessed samples have the intended product” is empirically checkable. “Long-term ecological effects remain unclear” identifies uncertainty. “Avoiding irreversible ecological consequences should come first” expresses a value priority. All three belong in discussion, but they cannot substitute for each other. Measurement does not determine how much fairness should matter, and preference cannot turn 12/20 into 100/100.

**Identify affected people.** A fictional drought-tolerant plant may affect farmers, communities, consumers, researchers and ecological managers. Benefits, costs, risks and influence may be unevenly distributed. Informed consent involves adequate information and voluntary agreement. There is no actual human experiment here; respect for informed participation is a reasoning principle. HHS ethics principles are a framework, not legal permission or a universal ecological rule for this plant scenario.

**Compare defensible alternatives.** Conditional continuation of contained research may generate useful evidence while limiting some exposure. Its supporters must still acknowledge cost, possible escape and unequal access, with independent checks and stopping conditions. Pausing expansion to gather evidence may prioritise avoiding hard-to-reverse consequences. Its supporters must acknowledge delayed benefits and specify the missing ecological and sampling evidence, rather than presenting every unknown as proven harm.

**Make commitments checkable.** Continuing requires explicit boundaries, oversight and a rule for stopping or revising. Pausing requires an evidence plan and review point. Either position should address the strongest counterargument. The sample result 12/20=60% is not “60% safe” or “60% ethically acceptable”: product success, ecological risk and ethical judgement are different quantities.

**Keep the reasoning public and revisable.** A strong submission combines accurate facts, honest uncertainty, explicit values, stakeholder participation and conditions for changing course. Fairness also concerns who pays for monitoring and whether information is accessible. New evidence may change risk estimates; deliberation may change value priorities. Governance connects these changes to action. This activity evaluates the quality of reasoning, never whether a student adopts one supposedly mandatory policy stance.

Restate the other side using shared facts before naming your different priority. Revising an empirical judgement is not losing a debate. If a disagreement remains about values, identify it accurately. Public reasons aid accountability; students need not disclose personal information or speak for a community.

## Depth / 高中延伸

### 中文

高中桥梁：区分“风险发生概率的估计”与“愿意承担多少风险”的价值问题。没有概率数据时不可编一个安全百分比。

### English

Bridge: distinguish estimating risk probability from deciding acceptable risk. Without probability data, do not invent a safety percentage.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“科学会自动给出唯一政策”或“价值不同就不需要证据”。两者都错：证据约束可说的事实，价值与治理影响可采取的行动。

### English

“Science automatically chooses one policy” and “different values make evidence unnecessary” both fail. Evidence constrains factual claims; values and governance also shape action.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

虚构无外源DNA的编辑作物获目标序列，但田间资料缺失。给继续封闭研究和暂缓扩大各一条强理由，再给共同可接受的新证据。自查：不把无外源DNA等同零风险；写生态/效益、受影响者、独立检查。

### English

A fictional edited crop retains no foreign DNA and has the target sequence, but field evidence is missing. Give one strong reason for each policy and shared useful evidence. Self-check: no foreign DNA is not zero risk; discuss ecology/benefit, stakeholders and independent assessment.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **bioethics / 生物伦理**: 对生命科学行动中的价值和责任进行论证。
- **empirical fact / 经验事实**: 能用观察或测量检查的陈述。
- **uncertainty / 不确定性**: 证据尚不能确定的范围或问题。
- **stakeholder / 利益相关者**: 受到决定影响或有相关责任的人群。
- **informed consent / 知情同意**: 在获得充分信息并自愿情况下作出同意。
- **equity / 公平**: 关注利益、风险和参与机会如何分配。
- **governance / 治理**: 作决定、监督、追责和修订的安排。
- **trade-off / 权衡**: 在不同效益、风险或价值间作选择。

### English

- **bioethics / 生物伦理**: Reasoning about values and responsibilities in life science.
- **empirical fact / 经验事实**: A statement checkable through observation or measurement.
- **uncertainty / 不确定性**: Questions or ranges not settled by available evidence.
- **stakeholder / 利益相关者**: People affected by a decision or bearing relevant responsibilities.
- **informed consent / 知情同意**: Voluntary agreement based on adequate information.
- **equity / 公平**: Attention to distribution of benefits, risks and participation.
- **governance / 治理**: Arrangements for decisions, oversight, accountability and revision.
- **trade-off / 权衡**: A choice involving competing benefits, risks or values.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] 哪句是本情境的经验事实？
   A. 已测20份有12份目标产物
   B. 任何成本都值得承担
   C. 公平不重要
   答案: A. 12/20可核查；值得和重要表达价值，不能混成实验直接结论。 复习: 回看事实/未知/价值三圆。

2. [concept] 哪个治理条件最能支持可修订决定？
   A. 永不公开失败
   B. 独立检查、停止条件和重审时间
   C. 只问支持者
   答案: B. 监督与重审让新证据真正影响行动，也需要受影响者有意义参与。 复习: 回看决定反馈中的新证据与重审条件。

3. [reasoning] 两人分别支持继续封闭研究和暂缓扩大，怎样评价？
   A. 按是否同意老师
   B. 看事实准确、权衡透明、回应反方和修订条件
   C. 只按最后立场
   答案: B. 两种决定都可合理；评证据与理由品质，不把一个政策当唯一道德答案。 复习: 切换两个决定，比较各自成本和理由。

4. [vocabulary] stakeholder在这里指谁？
   A. 仅实验工具
   B. 仅研究者
   C. 受影响者和有相关责任者
   答案: C. 农户、当地社群、研究者、生态管理者和消费者可能承受不同利益与风险。 复习: 回看受影响者、信息参与与公平提示。

### English

1. [concept] Which is an empirical fact in this scenario?
   A. 12 of 20 assessed samples have the intended product
   B. Any cost is worth paying
   C. Fairness does not matter
   Answer: A. 12/20 can be checked; worth and importance express values, not direct experimental findings. Revisit: Revisit the fact/unknown/value circles.

2. [concept] Which governance condition best supports revision?
   A. Never disclose failures
   B. Independent checks, stopping conditions and review timing
   C. Consult supporters only
   Answer: B. Oversight and review allow new evidence to change action, with meaningful stakeholder participation. Revisit: Revisit new-evidence and review conditions in the feedback.

3. [reasoning] One person supports contained research, another a pause before expansion. How should they be evaluated?
   A. By agreement with the teacher
   B. By accurate facts, transparent trade-offs, counterarguments and revision conditions
   C. By final stance alone
   Answer: B. Both can be defensible; evaluate evidence and reasoning, not one policy as the only moral answer. Revisit: Switch both decisions and compare their costs and reasons.

4. [vocabulary] Who is a stakeholder here?
   A. Only laboratory equipment
   B. Only researchers
   C. Affected people and relevant responsible parties
   Answer: C. Farmers, communities, researchers, ecological managers and consumers can face different benefits and risks. Revisit: Revisit participation, informed agreement and fairness.
