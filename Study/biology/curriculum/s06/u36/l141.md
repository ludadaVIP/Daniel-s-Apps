## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

“树越多鸟就越多吗？”很有趣，但两地点几次观察能回答因果吗？描述变化的研究是否同样有价值？

**学习目标**

- 将兴趣改成有对象、指标、地点与时期的问题。
- 区分描述、比较和因果问题及所需设计。
- 选官方公开数据或安全观察并检查可行性与来源。

### English

“Do more trees mean more birds?” is interesting, but can a few visits establish causality? Can description be valuable research?

**Learning goals**

- Turn curiosity into a question with organisms, measures, place and period.
- Distinguish descriptive, comparative and causal questions and their designs.
- Select official public data or safe observations and check feasibility and provenance.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**兴趣还不是研究问题。** “鸟喜欢树吗”能引发好奇，却没有说明哪些鸟、哪里、哪段时间以及“喜欢”怎样记录。一个可回答的问题把概念转成指标，同时承认证据的适用范围。比如“在这份虚构表的第1、3、10天，两地点相同5分钟访问的鸟类记录数有何差异？”它具体、能核查，也避免把记录数冒充真实鸟总数。

**先选问题类型。** 描述性问题问发生了什么、如何分布或变化；比较问题问明确条件下两组记录有何不同；因果问题问改变一个因素是否导致响应改变。三者都有科学价值。研究不一定要主动操控生物；官方长期观察数据可回答描述或比较问题。只是观察设计通常更难排除混杂，不能把比较结果直接改写成原因。

**做一次数值预检。** 原创练习表A在第1、3、7、10天记录2、4、缺失、0，B为4、6、8、2。B第7天观察10分钟，其余有效访问5分钟。若研究同日同努力差异，可比日期为1、3、10，B-A各2记录/5分钟。第7天不是“差8”，因为A未访；问题先明确可用证据，才能避免之后为了想要的答案乱改分母。

**让来源进入问题。** 毕业项目使用官方公开生态数据或安全家庭观察。公开表要记录机构、原始页面或报告、数据时期、访问日期、单位与方法；自己的观察要记录真实时间、地点条件和努力，不得把预填示例当实测。NPS年末狼群年龄表可问“这一日期两个地区的已记录幼狼比例有何差别”，但不能仅凭表问全年出生率或地区的因果效应。

**最后检查可行性。** 好问题可以很小，却应值得解释。确认可取得数据、变量定义一致、时间允许、观察安全，不打扰生物或进入危险地方。若原想回答的因果问题目前做不到，缩为诚实的描述或比较并不是失败；把新的机制问题列作下一步。预测是事先说明可能出现什么及理由，不是承诺数据必须配合；发现不同模式也能学到东西。

把问题写定并不禁止以后修订，但修订时要注明看过哪些数据、为什么改变。先看到图再挑最突出差异的问题，也能产生探索线索，却不应伪装成事先预测。这样保留探索与验证的区别，别人才能理解你的结论有多大支持力，并知道下一次独立研究应检验什么。

### English

**Curiosity needs a measurable question.** “Do birds like trees?” is interesting but leaves organisms, place, period and the meaning of “like” unspecified. An answerable question translates an idea into a recorded measure and states its scope. For example: how do bird records differ between two sites on matched five-minute visits on days 1, 3 and 10 in the fictional table? This asks about records, without pretending to count every bird present.

**Choose the question type.** Description asks what occurred, how it was distributed or how it changed. Comparison contrasts records under specified conditions. A causal question asks whether changing a factor changes a response. All three are useful. Research does not always require manipulating organisms: systematic observations and official long-term data can answer descriptive or comparative questions. Observational differences, however, usually leave alternative explanations.

**Pre-check with numbers.** The practice table gives A records of 2, 4, missing and 0 on days 1, 3, 7 and 10; B gives 4, 6, 8 and 2. B’s day-7 visit lasted ten minutes; other valid visits lasted five. Equal-effort matched comparisons use days 1, 3 and 10, each with B−A=2 records per five minutes. Day 7 is not a difference of eight: A was not visited. Defining the question first prevents convenient denominator changes later.

**Include provenance.** The graduation project uses official public ecological data or safe home observations. Record the institution, original page/report, data period, access date, units and methods. For your own observations, record real times, conditions and effort; prefilled examples are not measurements. An NPS year-end wolf age-composition table can support a question about recorded pup proportions on that date, rather than annual birth rates or causal regional effects.

**Check feasibility and value.** A small question can still teach something worthwhile. Confirm access, consistent definitions, time and safe observation without disturbing organisms. Narrowing an unanswerable causal ambition to an honest comparison is productive. A prediction states an anticipated pattern and reason before analysis; it does not require the data to cooperate.

Questions may be revised, but record what data had already been seen and why the revision occurred. A striking pattern can generate an exploratory question; do not present it as a prior prediction. This distinction helps others understand support and plan an independent follow-up.

## Depth / 高中延伸

### 中文

高中桥梁：把“概念→指标→可用数据→允许结论”写成一条链。因果问题的兴趣可以保留，但别让现有数据承担无法区分的机制。

### English

Bridge: connect concept → measure → available data → permitted inference. Keep a causal interest as a future question rather than forcing current data to distinguish mechanisms they cannot test.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“官方数据所以什么都能证明”与“不操控就不是研究”都不成立。数据来源可信度、指标适配与设计强度是不同检查。

### English

“Official data prove anything” and “without manipulation it is not research” both fail. Source credibility, measure suitability and design strength are separate checks.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

官方年末表记录某地区幼狼17、总狼53。把“这里繁殖一定更好”改成可回答问题。自查：限定年末日期、记录种群、17/53约32.1%幼狼比例；不能推全年繁殖率或因果。

### English

An official year-end table records 17 pups among 53 wolves in a region. Repair “breeding is definitely better here.” Self-check: specify the year-end date and recorded population; ask about 17/53≈32.1% pups, not annual reproductive rate or causality.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **research question / 研究问题**: 明确所要回答及其边界的问题。
- **descriptive study / 描述性研究**: 记录对象的分布、数量或变化。
- **comparison / 比较**: 在明确条件下对照不同记录。
- **causation / 因果**: 某因素改变导致响应改变的关系。
- **operational definition / 操作性定义**: 把概念落实成可记录指标的说明。
- **scope / 范围**: 研究适用的对象、地点、时期等边界。
- **data provenance / 数据来源**: 数据由谁、何时、如何形成的记录。
- **feasibility / 可行性**: 资源、时间与安全允许研究完成的程度。

### English

- **research question / 研究问题**: A question specifying the intended answer and its scope.
- **descriptive study / 描述性研究**: A study describing distribution, quantity or change.
- **comparison / 比较**: Contrasting records under specified conditions.
- **causation / 因果**: A relationship in which changing one factor changes a response.
- **operational definition / 操作性定义**: A definition translating a concept into a recordable measure.
- **scope / 范围**: The organisms, place, period and other boundaries of a study.
- **data provenance / 数据来源**: A record of who produced data, when and how.
- **feasibility / 可行性**: Whether resources, time and safety allow a study to be completed.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] 哪题用本表可回答？
   A. 两地点同日5 min记录数怎样比较？
   B. 所有森林因树量增加了多少鸟？
   C. 全部鸟的终生存活率多少？
   答案: A. 同日等努力记录是表内指标；没有树量操控或终生跟踪。 复习: 回看问题漏斗的对象、时期、单位。

2. [concept] 描述性问题是否必须主动操控变量？
   A. 必须，否则不算科学
   B. 不必，规范观察或官方数据也可回答
   C. 不必，所以不用方法记录
   答案: B. 问题类型决定设计；描述仍需明确指标、来源与局限。 复习: 回看描述A变化的可回答示例。

3. [reasoning] 把“鸟很多”改成什么最可核查？
   A. 很自然的感觉
   B. 地点A，第1/3/10天每5 min鸟记录数
   C. 任何地点所有鸟都喜欢树
   答案: B. 对象、地点、时间与努力单位让记录可复核；不把记录数偷换成总体。 复习: 回看修测量与边界按钮。

4. [vocabulary] data provenance强调什么？
   A. 图的颜色
   B. 只有最大数
   C. 数据的来源、时间和形成方法
   答案: C. 机构、原表、日期、单位和方法使别人能追查，官网身份不消除数据局限。 复习: 回看研究问题与来源自查。

### English

1. [concept] Which question can this table answer?
   A. How do records compare on matched five-minute visits?
   B. How many birds did trees add in every forest?
   C. What is lifetime survival of every bird?
   Answer: A. Matched equal-effort records are measured here; trees were not manipulated and lifetimes were not tracked. Revisit: Revisit organisms, period and units in the question funnel.

2. [concept] Must a descriptive question manipulate a variable?
   A. Yes, otherwise it is not science
   B. No; systematic observation or official data can answer it
   C. No, so methods need not be recorded
   Answer: B. Question type shapes design; description still needs measures, provenance and limits. Revisit: Revisit the answerable descriptive example for site A.

3. [reasoning] Which replacement for “many birds” is most checkable?
   A. A natural feeling
   B. Bird records at A per five-minute visit on days 1/3/10
   C. All birds everywhere like trees
   Answer: B. Organisms, place, dates and effort make records checkable; they do not turn records into population abundance. Revisit: Revisit measurement-and-scope options.

4. [vocabulary] What does data provenance emphasise?
   A. Chart colours
   B. Only the largest value
   C. Data origin, timing and production method
   Answer: C. Institution, original table, dates, units and methods allow checking; an official source does not erase limitations. Revisit: Revisit the question and source checks.
