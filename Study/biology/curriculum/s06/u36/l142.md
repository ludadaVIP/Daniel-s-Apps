## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

同一地点访问四次，是四个独立地点吗？没去观察的那天能不能在表里填0？

**学习目标**

- 明确取样单位、重复测量和配对或独立设计。
- 检查观察努力、偏差、混杂与缺失。
- 让采集记录与研究问题一致并保留原始来源。

### English

Are four visits to one site four independent sites? Can a missed visit be recorded as zero?

**Learning goals**

- Specify sampling units, repeated measures and paired or independent designs.
- Check effort, bias, confounding and missingness.
- Align records with the question and preserve original provenance.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**设计从单位开始。** 先写清要讨论什么：这两个地点的记录，还是一类生境的通常表现？本表每一行是一次地点访问，地点只有A和B两个。对同一地点反复记录能了解时间变化，却没有增加独立地点数。若要推广到生境类型，需要多个真正独立地点，而不是复制表格行数或把同一地点再访问很多次。

**按问题选比较结构。** 本练习可以把同一日期的A与B合理配对，减少部分日期条件差异；日期1、3、10是完整且相同努力的三对。配对并不消除两地点之间所有差别，也不保证不同日期独立。独立地点设计则应另采多个地点并预先说明选择方法、条件与单位。按钮只比较设计，不能把现有两地点资料变成新的独立重复。

**努力是数据的一部分。** 原表B第7天记录8，但观察10分钟，其他有效访问5分钟。直接比较8与别天的数可能混入时间投入影响；除以分钟也不必然完全校正发现概率，因为观察越久不一定线性增加新个体。此处选择同日同5分钟完整记录，可明确保持比较条件，却仍须保存被排除记录与理由，不能装作第7天从未存在。

**把缺失与零分开。** A第7天未访，记NA、努力0分钟；第10天确实观察5分钟、记录0，两者含义不同。用0补NA会把未知变成“观察未记录到”，改变分母与趋势。若遗漏常发生在坏天气，还可能系统性漏掉特定条件，不能简单认为剩余数据代表全部时期。记录缺失原因能帮助评价这种风险。

**控制与观察各有边界。** 实验可通过控制、随机安排等加强因果判断；观察研究则要记录可能混杂的天气、时段、植被、食物与噪声，诚实限制结论。只选容易到达或鸟多地点会带来取样偏差。预定访问规则、统一努力、多个地点、保留原始表与修订记录能提高可靠性，但不会自动消除全部偏差；把改进对应到具体问题，才是好的设计。

预先写一份简短记录字典很有帮助：地点编号代表哪里，日期从何时算起，记录数计什么，观察分钟如何保存，NA有哪些原因。两个记录者使用同一定义，才不会把鸟叫次数与见到个体数混在同列。即使后来发现定义需要改变，也应保留版本说明，不能悄悄把前后的不同指标接成同一条趋势。

### English

**Begin with the unit.** Are you describing records at these two sites, or typical differences between habitat types? Each row in this table is a site visit; only two sites exist. Revisiting a site adds information about time, not independent sites. Generalising to a habitat type requires several genuinely independent sites, not duplicated rows or repeated visits alone.

**Match the comparison structure.** A and B can be paired on the same dates, partly accounting for shared date conditions. Days 1, 3 and 10 provide three complete pairs with equal effort. Pairing does not remove every difference between sites, and successive dates may be dependent. An independent-site study would require additional sites and a planned selection method. A software button cannot turn two-site records into new independent replication.

**Treat effort as data.** B’s day-7 count is eight, but that visit lasted ten minutes; other valid visits lasted five. Direct comparison can mix biological differences with observation time. Simply dividing by minutes does not necessarily solve detectability, because additional observations need not accumulate linearly. Our restricted comparison uses same-day five-minute complete records while preserving excluded rows and their reasons.

**Separate missingness from zero.** A was not visited on day 7: record NA and zero minutes of effort. On day 10, A was actually observed for five minutes with zero records. These mean different things. Replacing NA with zero invents an outcome and changes denominators and patterns. Missing visits may also be systematic—for example, bad weather may prevent visits—so remaining records need not represent the full period.

**Address bias specifically.** Experiments can use controlled conditions and random allocation to strengthen causal inference. Observational work should record possible confounders such as weather, time, vegetation, food and noise, then limit its claims. Choosing only accessible or bird-rich sites creates sampling bias. Planned visits, consistent effort, independent sites and an auditable original table improve reliability, but do not automatically eliminate every bias. Link each proposed improvement to a named weakness rather than writing only “collect more data”.

A brief data dictionary specifies site codes, day origin, what counts as a record, effort and missing-value reasons. Consistent definitions prevent mixing calls with individuals in one column. If definitions change, preserve a version note rather than silently joining incompatible measures into a trend.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“更多行总是更多独立样本”“缺失就是0”。独立性由取样单位与处理决定；缺失必须保留原因。

### English

“More rows always mean more independent samples” and “missing equals zero.” Independence follows sampling and treatment units; preserve missingness and reasons.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

虚构两盆同品种植物每天各量3次，持续7天。有人称42株。自查：只有2株、42读数；分处理时盆可能是处理单位；应增加独立盆并预定量法，不伪造样本数。

### English

Two fictional pots of one variety are each measured three times daily for seven days; someone claims 42 plants. Self-check: two plants, 42 readings; pot may be the treatment unit. Add independent pots and planned measurement, not fictional sample size.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **sampling unit / 取样单位**: 被选择并作为观察或处理基础的对象。
- **repeated measures / 重复测量**: 对同一单位在不同时间等条件下再次记录。
- **paired design / 配对设计**: 按同一单位或合理匹配条件比较。
- **independent sample / 独立样本**: 不是同一单位重复记录的独立取样。
- **sampling effort / 观察努力**: 如观察时间、面积等采集投入。
- **sampling bias / 取样偏差**: 取样过程系统性偏向某些对象或条件。
- **confounding / 混杂**: 其他因素与比较因素共同变化，使解释混在一起。
- **missing value / 缺失值**: 未获得所需记录，不等同观察为0。

### English

- **sampling unit / 取样单位**: The entity selected as a basis for observation or treatment.
- **repeated measures / 重复测量**: Recording the same unit again over time or conditions.
- **paired design / 配对设计**: A comparison using the same units or justified matches.
- **independent sample / 独立样本**: Sampling distinct independent units rather than repeated records of one.
- **sampling effort / 观察努力**: Collection effort such as observation time or area.
- **sampling bias / 取样偏差**: Systematic preference for some units or conditions during sampling.
- **confounding / 混杂**: Other factors vary with the comparison factor and mix explanations.
- **missing value / 缺失值**: An unavailable record, different from an observed zero.

   B. 0，因为没有看见
   C. 复制上次4
   答案: A. 未访没有观察结果；0要求确实按方法观察却未记录到。 复习: 回看第7天A的NA和努力0 min。

### English

1. [concept] How should A’s missed day-7 visit be recorded?
   A. NA, explaining the missed visit
   B. 0, because none was seen
   C. Copy the previous four
   Answer: A. An unmade visit has no outcome; zero requires an actual observation with no records. Revisit: Revisit A day 7: NA and zero minutes of effort.

2. [concept] Do eight rows represent eight independent sites?
   A. Yes; row count equals site count
   B. No; they are repeated visits to two sites
   C. Yes, because values differ
   Answer: B. Sampling level follows entities and design, not row count or differing values. Revisit: Revisit the A/B repeated-visit diagram.

3. [reasoning] What is the best initial comparison for this table?
   A. Compare B ten minutes with A missing as zero
   B. Use complete five-minute pairs on days 1/3/10 and document exclusions
   C. Delete all unwelcome values
   Answer: B. Equal-effort matched dates reduce some differences; retain the original table and exclusion reasons. Revisit: Revisit complete paired dates and B day-7 effort.

4. [vocabulary] What is sampling effort?
   A. Whether the researcher likes the result
   B. Length of a paper title
   C. Collection time, area and related effort
   Answer: C. More records can result simply from longer observation; save effort alongside the outcome. Revisit: Revisit the min column.
