# U36｜研究方案、数据图与双语结论 / Research Plan, Chart and Bilingual Conclusion

## 练习与真实研究 / Practice and a genuine investigation

课堂鸟表是原创虚构练习，不能冒充实测。用它练问题、单位、配对、努力、缺失、图和结论，再迁移到官方公开生态数据或真实安全家庭观察。描述或比较可成立，不强迫操控生物来追因果。无需购买设备，不培养未知微生物，不采集体液或打扰野生生物。

The classroom bird table is fictional practice, not measured data. Use it to practise questions, units, pairing, effort, missingness, charts and claims, then transfer to official public ecological data or genuine safe home observations. Description or comparison is valid; do not manipulate organisms merely to demand causality. No equipment purchase, unknown-microbe culture, body-fluid collection or wildlife disturbance is needed.

## 提交内容 / Submission

1. 一个有对象、地点、时期、指标和范围的问题；机构/原表URL/数据日期/访问日期或自己的真实记录条件。
2. 取样单位、配对/独立、努力、控制或混杂、缺失与真实0的规则，保留可重算的原表。
3. 含单位、可读比例尺、正确时间间隔、n与缺失说明的图；范围不能标CI，未做适当分析不能宣布显著。
4. 数据→推理→局限结论、一个有针对性的下一步、参考来源。
5. 实际英文摘要与中英同义图注，以及3–5分钟讲稿或分镜；生物解释语言自选，英语用句框支架，按自己语速排练，计时不是科学质量分数。

6. A question with organisms, place, period, measure and scope; institution/original URL/data date/access date or real self-recording conditions.
7. Sampling unit, paired/independent structure, effort, controls/confounders and missing/zero rules, retaining recalculable raw data.
8. A chart with units, readable scale, actual time intervals, n and missingness; range is not CI, and significance requires appropriate analysis.
9. A data → reasoning → limitation conclusion, a targeted next step and references.
10. An actual English abstract, scientifically equivalent bilingual caption and a 3–5 minute script/storyboard. Explain biology in your chosen language; scaffold English and rehearse at your own pace. Timing is not a science-quality score.

## 官方数据衔接例 / Official-data bridge

NPS《Yellowstone Wolf, Cougar and Elk Project Annual Report 2025》物理第5页记录2025-12-31年龄组成：Northern 17 pups/53 total≈32.1%，Interior 0/31=0%。可比较这一年末记录比例；不能推出地区造成差异、全年繁殖/存活率、0幼狼意味着从未出生、或总体密度。原表8个pack/group（7 pack+1 group）另有两个0值loner类别，类别不是独立处理重复。

Physical page 5 of the NPS 2025 annual report gives age composition on 2025-12-31: Northern 17 pups/53 total≈32.1%, Interior 0/31=0%. Compare recorded year-end proportions, without inferring a regional cause, annual reproduction/survival, that zero pups means none were born, or population density. Eight pack/group rows (seven packs plus one group) and two zero-valued loner categories are not independent treatment replicates.
