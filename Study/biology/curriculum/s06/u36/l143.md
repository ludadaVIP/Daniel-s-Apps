## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

A的缺失若填0，均值为什么从2变成1.5？均值柱更高就能宣布“显著”或因果吗？

**学习目标**

- 保留单位、分母、缺失和真实时间间隔作图。
- 计算均值与范围并区别置信区间。
- 用数据→推理→局限修正过强结论。

### English

Why does filling A’s missing value with zero change its mean from 2 to 1.5? Does a taller mean bar establish significance or causality?

**Learning goals**

- Plot with units, denominators, missingness and actual time intervals.
- Calculate mean and range and distinguish them from confidence intervals.
- Repair overstrong conclusions using data → reasoning → limitation.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**先保留数据的意思。** 分析不是先挑好看的图，而是核对指标、单位、时期、缺失与设计。鸟类记录数是访问时记录的指标，不等同真实总体密度。本表日期1、3、7、10间距分别2、4、3天；只用可比日期1、3、10时，间距2与7天更不能画成相同时间距再解释变化速度。图轴和图注应把这些边界留下。

**分母改变会改答案。** A的有效记录为2、4、0，均值(2+4+0)/3=2记录/5分钟；第7天缺失并没有一个可加入的0。若错误补0，均值变6/4=1.5。若又删去真实0，变6/2=3。三种数各对应不同处理，后两种会歪曲数据含义。报告应保留原表、有效n与排除理由，让读者能重算。

**比较也要同条件。** B全表均值(4+6+8+2)/4=5，但8来自10分钟访问。只保留同5分钟可比日期，B为4、6、2，均值4。三对B-A均2记录/5分钟，描述了两地点在这些日期的差异。A最小0最大4，B最小2最大6，范围都4记录；范围是样本散布，不是均值估计的置信区间，更不是“95%保证”。

**让图帮助而不代替推理。** 均值柱图简洁却隐藏每次观察；原始点展示变化与真实0。没有合适的统计方法、设计假设与推断对象，不能凭两根柱子宣布“统计显著”。图看起来分开也不能证明因果；两地点的树量、食物、天气和发现概率可能共同不同。明确局限不意味着否定全部描述价值，而是防止越界。

**写一条完整证据链。** “在3个同日5分钟完整配对中，B均值4、A均值2，差2记录/5分钟，与B记录较高的描述一致。各类只有一个地点、日期可能相关且发现概率未控制，不能证明树量效应或总体密度；下一步增加独立地点、预定等努力访问并记录天气。”每个改进都回应一个具体局限，远胜于只写“存在人为误差”。

若发现特别高的值，先查原始记录和单位，不能因为它影响均值就删掉。必要时并列含该值与待核查情景的结果，说明为何暂不作最终解释。还可检查纵轴是否从0开始、截断是否清楚标明；真实数值没变，视觉差距仍可能被放大。可读图形应帮助比较，而不是把读者推向事先决定的故事。

### English

**Preserve meaning before calculating.** Check the measure, units, period, missingness and design before choosing a chart. Bird records are a visit-based index, not population density. Days 1, 3, 7 and 10 have unequal gaps; the comparable dates 1, 3 and 10 are separated by two and seven days. Equal horizontal spacing would misrepresent rates of change. Axes and captions should preserve these boundaries.

**Changing the denominator changes the answer.** A’s observed values are 2, 4 and 0: mean (2+4+0)/3=2 records per five minutes. The missing day-7 visit contributes no observed zero. Incorrectly filling it gives 6/4=1.5. Dropping the real zero gives 6/2=3. The last two procedures invent or discard evidence. Keep the original table, observed n and exclusion reasons so someone else can recalculate.

**Compare equivalent conditions.** B’s complete-table mean is (4+6+8+2)/4=5, but eight comes from a ten-minute visit. Restricting to comparable five-minute dates gives 4, 6 and 2, mean four. Each matched B−A difference is two records per five minutes. A spans 0–4 and B 2–6, so both ranges equal four records. A range describes sample spread; it is not a confidence interval or a 95% guarantee about a mean.

**Use charts without letting them replace inference.** Mean bars hide visit-level values; raw points reveal variation and observed zeros. Statistical significance requires a suitable analysis, assumptions and inferential target, not merely different bar heights. Separation also cannot establish causality: vegetation, food, weather and detectability may differ between sites. Limits do not remove the descriptive value of the observations.

**Write the evidence chain.** A bounded conclusion reports means four and two across three complete equal-effort dates, then restricts the interpretation to these records. One site per setting, possible temporal dependence and unmeasured detectability prevent a tree-cover effect or population-density claim. Add independent sites, planned equal-effort visits and weather records as specific next steps. Each improvement should answer a named limitation, rather than simply invoking “human error”.

Investigate an unusually high value and its units rather than deleting it because it affects the mean. Show transparent alternative scenarios while verification is pending. Check whether the vertical axis begins at zero and whether truncation is clearly marked; unchanged numbers can still be presented misleadingly.

## Depth / 高中延伸

### 中文

高中桥梁：样本散布、测量不确定与参数估计不确定不是同一种量。增加小数位不减测量误差；更多相关读数不等于更多独立证据。

### English

Bridge: sample spread, measurement uncertainty and parameter-estimation uncertainty differ. More decimals do not reduce measurement error; correlated readings do not add independent evidence one-for-one.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“柱子差大=显著=因果”“范围=置信区间”。数值差、推断与机制证据分别检查，不把同一个图承担三层结论。

### English

“Large bar difference=significant=causal” and “range=confidence interval” confuse numerical difference, inference and mechanism evidence.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

虚构植物观察第1、2、8天高10、12、18 cm。哪段平均每天增高更快？自查：(12−10)/1=2 cm/day；(18−12)/6=1 cm/day，不能把两段按等日距解释；同株不能推广所有植物。

### English

A fictional plant is 10, 12 and 18 cm tall on days 1, 2 and 8. Which interval has faster average growth? Self-check: 2/1=2 cm/day versus 6/6=1 cm/day. Do not treat intervals as equal; one plant does not represent all plants.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **mean / 均值**: 有效观察值之和除以其个数。
- **range / 范围**: 最大值减最小值，描述样本散布。
- **confidence interval / 置信区间**: 用明确统计方法估计参数不确定性的区间。
- **denominator / 分母**: 比例或平均所依据的基数。
- **scale / 比例尺**: 图上位置与数值之间的对应。
- **correlation / 相关**: 变量共同变化的统计关系，不自动表因果。
- **statistical significance / 统计显著**: 依赖假设和适当分析的推断结论。
- **limitation / 局限**: 限制结论强度或适用范围的具体条件。

### English

- **mean / 均值**: Sum of observed values divided by their number.
- **range / 范围**: Maximum minus minimum, describing sample spread.
- **confidence interval / 置信区间**: An interval from a specified statistical method describing parameter uncertainty.
- **denominator / 分母**: The base used for a fraction or average.
- **scale / 比例尺**: The mapping between plotted position and value.
- **correlation / 相关**: A statistical pattern of covariation, not automatically causation.
- **statistical significance / 统计显著**: An inference requiring assumptions and appropriate analysis.
- **limitation / 局限**: A specific restriction on inference strength or scope.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] A为2、4、NA、0，均值是什么？
   A. 2，分母3个已观察值
   B. 1.5，缺失必须算0
   C. 3，只留非零
   答案: A. 0是有效记录而NA不是；(2+4+0)/3=2，不能随意换分母。 复习: 回看原表NA、0与均值输出。

2. [concept] A的0–4记录，范围4表示什么？
   A. 95%置信区间
   B. 样本最大减最小的散布
   C. 总体真实值必在0–4
   答案: B. 范围描述观察值散布；没有指定推断方法，不能标成CI或总体保证。 复习: 回看原始点与“未画置信区间”。

3. [reasoning] 本表最合适的结论是哪句？
   A. 树量导致全部鸟显著翻倍
   B. 可比3日期B记录均比A多2/5 min，仍不能推因果或总体密度
   C. 记录有局限就完全没价值
   答案: B. 差异有描述价值；地点、努力、发现概率与设计限制更强结论。 复习: 回看修正结论反馈和完整配对。

4. [vocabulary] limitation应怎样使用？
   A. 只写“人为误差”就够
   B. 隐藏使报告更漂亮
   C. 指具体边界并连接下一步改进
   答案: C. 一个地点/类型→增加独立地点；努力不同→预定统一访问，比空泛标签更有用。 复习: 回看局限→标准化与独立地点建议。

### English

1. [concept] A has 2, 4, NA and 0. What is its mean?
   A. 2, with three observed values
   B. 1.5, because missing must count as zero
   C. 3, retaining only nonzero values
   Answer: A. Zero is observed; NA is not. (2+4+0)/3=2, without arbitrary denominator changes. Revisit: Revisit NA, zero and the mean output.

2. [concept] What does A’s range of four records, from 0 to 4, describe?
   A. A 95% confidence interval
   B. Sample spread: maximum minus minimum
   C. Population truth must lie between 0 and 4
   Answer: B. Range describes observed spread; without an inferential method it is neither a CI nor a population guarantee. Revisit: Revisit raw points and “no confidence interval shown”.

3. [reasoning] Which conclusion best fits the table?
   A. Trees significantly doubled all birds
   B. Across three comparable dates B has two more records/5 min, without establishing causality or density
   C. Limitations make the records worthless
   Answer: B. The difference has descriptive value; sites, effort, detectability and design limit stronger claims. Revisit: Revisit repaired-claim feedback and complete pairs.

4. [vocabulary] How should limitation be used?
   A. Write only “human error”
   B. Hide it to improve presentation
   C. Name a specific boundary and link it to a next improvement
   Answer: C. One site per setting → add independent sites; unequal effort → standardise planned visits. Specific links beat vague labels. Revisit: Revisit limitations linked to standardisation and independent sites.
