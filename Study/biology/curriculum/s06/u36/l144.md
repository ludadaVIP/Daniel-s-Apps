## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

同一图能被讲成“树让所有鸟翻倍”或“本记录B较高”。怎样让听众核查哪一步有证据、哪一步仍未知？

**学习目标**

- 用问题、方法、图、结论、局限与来源组织研究表达。
- 修订英文摘要和科学含义一致的双语图注。
- 准备3–5分钟讲稿/分镜并按自己语速排练。

### English

The same chart can become “trees double all birds” or “B is higher in these records”. How can listeners check what is supported and what remains unknown?

**Learning goals**

- Organise communication around question, method, chart, conclusion, limitations and sources.
- Revise an English abstract and scientifically aligned bilingual caption.
- Prepare a 3–5 minute script/storyboard and rehearse at your own pace.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**表达是让推理可核查。** 研究报告不是把最多术语塞在一起，也不是把图画漂亮后宣布成功。先说问题与数据来源，再用方法解释记录如何形成；展示含单位、时间和样本说明的图；回答问题，说明哪些结论仍受限制。读者应该能找到原表，重算主要数字，知道你何时是在描述证据、何时是在提出可能机制。

**把一句夸张话拆开修。** “树让所有鸟翻倍”混入了未测树量因果、总体数量和无限推广。练习中可说：“3个同日5分钟完整配对，A均值2、B均值4记录，每对差2记录/5分钟。”数字支持本记录B较高；不能说明总体密度或树量效应。范围均4记录描述样本散布，不是置信区间，也不能凭图宣布显著。科学语言靠边界具体，不靠随便加一个“可能”。

**英文摘要要有真信息。** 可用句框“We compared … . Mean records were … . These observations support … but do not establish … .”填入对象、时期、努力、关键结果与局限。英语支架帮助表达，而非把生物理解锁在流利度后；你可以任选语言解释推理，再借模板做出真正包含科学内容的英文摘要。不要只把标题翻译后称为双语报告。

**图注两种语言要同义。** 中文写缺失，英文也要写；英文写记录数，中文不能悄悄改总体鸟数。图注说明第1、3、10天、每5分钟记录、原始点、A第7天缺失与B该日10分钟排除。真实项目另注明机构、报告页/表、数据日期、URL与访问日期，或自己的真实观察条件。原创虚构练习不得冒充实测，公开来源也不代表没有局限。

**把讲解编成可调整分镜。** 约40秒问题来源、60秒方法、80秒图表数值、60秒推理局限，可作为约4分钟起点。若英文稿420词，自己语速105词/分钟约4分钟；换成140则约3分钟，还未计停顿。3–5分钟是排练目标，不是科学分数，中文也不按空格算词。最后让听众问一个质疑，依据证据修订；课程在这里结束，研究仍可继续。

排练时请听众复述一句结论和一项局限，能否复述比记住所有术语更能检查表达。若听众误以为你已证明因果，修改图题、单位或过渡句，而不是只责怪听众没听懂。报告留存修订版本与来源，使以后新增观察时可以检验结论是否保持，而不必把旧结果说成永远正确。

### English

**Communication makes reasoning checkable.** A report should let another person find the original evidence and understand how the conclusion follows. Introduce the question and source, explain how records were generated, present a chart with units, dates and samples, then answer with limitations. Readers should be able to recalculate key numbers and distinguish description from a proposed mechanism.

**Repair an overstrong sentence.** “Trees double all bird populations” invents a tree-cover effect, population abundance and universal scope. The practice comparison instead has three complete matched five-minute dates: A mean two, B mean four, and each paired difference two records per five minutes. These figures support higher recorded values at B under those conditions. They establish neither density nor a tree effect. Ranges of four records describe spread, not confidence intervals; the chart alone establishes no statistical significance.

**Give the abstract real information.** A scaffold is: “We compared … . Mean records were … . These observations support … but do not establish … .” Insert organisms, period, effort, main result and scope. English support should help expression without making fluency a gate for biological understanding. Explain reasoning in your preferred language, then use scaffolds to produce an English abstract with actual scientific content. Translating only the title is not a bilingual report.

**Align both caption languages.** If one version identifies missingness, the other must too. Neither should turn records into total abundance. The practice caption specifies days 1, 3 and 10, five-minute visits, raw points, A’s missed day-7 visit and exclusion of B’s ten-minute visit. Real projects also cite the institution, report page/table, data date, URL and access date, or genuine self-recorded conditions. Fictional practice data must not become invented observations.

**Rehearse an adjustable storyboard.** Allocate time to question/source, method, chart and numbers, then reasoning and limits. A 420-word English script at 105 words/minute is approximately four minutes; at 140 it is approximately three, before pauses. Three to five minutes is a rehearsal target, not a science grade. Chinese does not use this space-based word counter. Invite one challenge and revise using evidence.

Ask a listener to restate one finding and one limitation. If they infer causality you did not establish, repair the title, units or transitions. Keep revised versions and sources so later observations can test whether the finding persists, without treating the first report as permanently correct.

## Depth / 高中延伸

### 中文

高中桥梁：摘要压缩的是篇幅，不是科学边界。口头解释、图表、数据表、摘要四种表征应保持同一分母和指标。

### English

Bridge: abstracts compress length, not scientific boundaries. Speech, chart, data table and abstract must retain the same denominators and measures.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“英语流利=科学准确”“删局限=更有说服力”。流利表达也可能越界；可核查数值和诚实边界才支撑说服力。

### English

“Fluent English means accurate science” and “removing limitations strengthens persuasion” both fail. Checkable numbers and honest boundaries support a claim.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

给同伴看一张没有单位、只写“B更好”的图。改英文图题和双语图注，另写一句不能推什么。自查：指标、单位、日期/对象、n与缺失；不把记录多换成生态一定更好。

### English

A peer shows a unit-free chart saying only “B is better”. Repair the English title and bilingual caption, then state an unsupported inference. Self-check: measure, units, dates/objects, n and missingness; more records are not automatically better ecology.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **abstract / 摘要**: 概括问题、方法、主要结果和边界的短文。
- **caption / 图注**: 说明图显示什么、单位、条件与必要边界。
- **claim / 主张**: 用证据回答研究问题的陈述。
- **evidence / 证据**: 与主张有关的可核查记录。
- **reasoning / 推理**: 说明证据为什么支持有限主张的逻辑。
- **citation / 引用**: 指向所用信息原始来源的标记。
- **storyboard / 分镜**: 按顺序安排图、讲解与停顿的计划。
- **revision / 修订**: 根据证据或反馈改进表达与结论。

### English

- **abstract / 摘要**: A short account of question, methods, main results and scope.
- **caption / 图注**: Text explaining a figure, units, conditions and needed boundaries.
- **claim / 主张**: A statement answering the research question with evidence.
- **evidence / 证据**: Checkable records relevant to a claim.
- **reasoning / 推理**: Logic connecting evidence to a bounded claim.
- **citation / 引用**: A reference directing readers to a source used.
- **storyboard / 分镜**: A sequence planning visuals, explanation and pauses.
- **revision / 修订**: Improving communication and claims using evidence or feedback.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] 图注最需要包括哪项？
   A. 记录指标、单位、日期、样本与排除说明
   B. 只有好看的标题
   C. 未计算的显著星号
   答案: A. 图注让读者在不听讲的情况下仍知道图的对象与边界。 复习: 回看可编辑双语图注的5 min与缺失。

2. [concept] 科学理解和英语应怎样评价？
   A. 口音不标准就科学不及格
   B. 推理可任选语言，另提供英文摘要和双语图注支架
   C. 只写英文，不需数值
   答案: B. 生物推理不被英语流利度锁住；成品仍应有实际的双语科学信息。 复习: 回看图底部语言分工和摘要模板。

3. [reasoning] 如何修“Trees double all bird populations”？
   A. 只加definitely
   B. 改为本3配对日期记录均值2/4，不能推树量因果或总体
   C. 删掉所有局限
   答案: B. 修订要补对象、数据、单位和推断边界；不是只换一个语气词。 复习: 比较过强摘要和含证据示范稿。

4. [vocabulary] abstract在研究报告里指什么？
   A. 装饰图案
   B. 从来不能改的标题
   C. 问题、方法、结果与边界的简短概括
   答案: C. 摘要帮助读者快速判断研究回答了什么，并保留关键局限。 复习: 回看英文摘要首句、数值句、局限句。

### English

1. [concept] What does the caption most need?
   A. Measure, units, dates, samples and exclusions
   B. Only an attractive title
   C. Uncomputed significance stars
   Answer: A. A caption lets readers understand what the chart shows and its boundaries without hearing the talk. Revisit: Revisit five-minute units and missingness in the editable caption.

2. [concept] How should science understanding and English be evaluated?
   A. An accent means science failure
   B. Reason in a chosen language, with scaffolds for an English abstract and bilingual caption
   C. English alone, with no data
   Answer: B. English fluency must not gate biological reasoning; the product still needs actual bilingual scientific information. Revisit: Revisit the language support and abstract model.

3. [reasoning] How should “Trees double all bird populations” be repaired?
   A. Only add definitely
   B. Report means 2/4 across three paired dates, without inferring tree causality or population abundance
   C. Remove all limitations
   Answer: B. Repair requires organisms, data, units and inference limits, not a single qualifying word. Revisit: Compare the overstrong abstract with the evidence-based model.

4. [vocabulary] What does abstract mean in a research report?
   A. A decorative pattern
   B. An unchangeable title
   C. A brief account of question, methods, findings and scope
   Answer: C. An abstract helps readers identify the answer and its key limits quickly. Revisit: Revisit the question, numerical and limitation sentences in the abstract.
