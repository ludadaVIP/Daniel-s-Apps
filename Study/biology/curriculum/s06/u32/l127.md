## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**光反应与碳固定各有工作。** 典型叶绿体的类囊体膜承载光吸收、电子传递和ATP生成系统。光能支持电子从水出发进入传递路径，水被氧化使O₂释放；电子最终帮助形成NADPH，质子梯度支持ATP合成。光是能量输入，不是制造碳原子的原料。光合作用释放的氧主要来自水，不能把CO₂的C与放出的O₂混为一条原子路线。

**碳固定在基质使用供能产物。** Calvin循环把CO₂的碳并入有机分子，再利用ATP和NADPH进行还原与受体再生。它不直接吸收光子来做每一步，却依赖光反应供能及光下调控，因此“暗反应只能在黑暗中进行”是误解。循环大部分产物用于再生受体，只有一部分作为净产物退出；一次固定一个CO₂并不是立刻造出一个六碳葡萄糖。

**用一份有限预算算限制。** 理想化计量下，净获得1 μmol三碳G3P需要固定3 μmol CO₂，并消耗9 μmol ATP和6 μmol NADPH，已把受体再生计入。若可用CO₂=6、ATP=18、NADPH=12 μmol，三项各支持2 μmol净G3P，固定碳共6 μmol。若只把NADPH降至6，输出最多1 μmol净G3P，即使ATP提高到36也无助，因为还原力先不足。G3P可用于进一步合成糖等物质，但2个G3P并不意味着图中已模拟完成葡萄糖合成。

**预算不是叶片速率。** 交互把三种资源当作给定的一次性可用量，以最小预算求输出，单位为μmol，不是μmol每秒。真实叶片有资源持续再生、气孔和CO₂运输、温度、酶活性及光呼吸等条件，本图都未计算。光反应ATP与NADPH供应比例也可因电子路径变化而改变，所以滑块独立输入仅用于识别资源限制，不代表某一灯强的实测产率。迁移时应先问资源、部位与单位，再区分“碳从哪来”和“能量从哪来”，守住物质与能量两个账本。

**剩余资源也是证据。** 在还原力不足的那组预算中，造一份净三碳产物只消耗九份ATP，因此原有十八份中仍剩九份；没有用完的ATP并不能独立把碳继续还原。把剩余量列出，可以检查自己是否错误地把所有资源都按总和平均分配。实际光合还涉及ATP与还原力再生，有限预算只回答这批给定资源最多支持多少净产物；若要预测每分钟产量，必须另有供给速率与酶反应的数据。

### English

Light reactions and carbon fixation perform different jobs. In a typical chloroplast, the thylakoid membrane houses light absorption, electron transfer and ATP production systems. Light supports an electron pathway beginning with water. Water oxidation releases O₂; electron transfer ultimately supports NADPH formation, and a proton gradient supports ATP synthesis. Light supplies energy rather than carbon atoms. The oxygen released by photosynthesis comes principally from water, so CO₂ carbon and released O₂ must not be combined into one atom route.

Carbon fixation uses energy-transfer products in the stroma. The Calvin cycle incorporates CO₂ carbon into organic molecules, then uses ATP and NADPH for reduction and acceptor regeneration. Its individual chemical steps do not directly absorb photons, but they depend on light-reaction supplies and light-associated regulation. Calling it a dark reaction that occurs only in darkness is misleading. Most cycle material regenerates the acceptor; only part leaves as net product. Fixing one CO₂ does not instantly yield a six-carbon glucose.

Calculate a finite resource budget. Idealized stoichiometry for one net μmol of three-carbon G3P requires three μmol CO₂, nine μmol ATP and six μmol NADPH, including acceptor regeneration. If available CO₂=6, ATP=18 and NADPH=12 μmol, each budget supports two μmol net G3P and six μmol fixed carbon. Reducing only NADPH to six limits net G3P to one μmol. Increasing ATP to thirty-six cannot solve the shortage of reducing power. G3P can supply later carbohydrate synthesis, but two G3P equivalents do not mean that this interaction has simulated a complete glucose-synthesis pathway.

A budget is not a leaf rate. The interaction treats three resources as finite available amounts and takes their smallest supported output. Units are μmol, not μmol per second. Real leaves continuously regenerate supplies and depend on stomata, CO₂ transport, temperature, enzyme activity and photorespiration, omitted here. Alternative electron paths can also alter ATP:NADPH supply ratios. Independent sliders therefore illustrate resource limitation rather than an observed yield at a given lamp intensity. For an unfamiliar problem, identify resources, locations and units, then keep carbon origin separate from energy supply so both matter and energy accounts remain clear.

Unused resources provide evidence too. In the reducing-power-limited budget, making one net three-carbon product equivalent consumes only nine ATP equivalents, leaving nine of the original eighteen. Unused ATP cannot independently continue carbon reduction without reducing power. Listing leftovers checks whether resources have mistakenly been pooled or averaged as interchangeable inputs. Actual photosynthesis continuously regenerates ATP and reducing power. A finite budget answers only how much net product this supplied batch can support; predicting output per minute additionally requires supply-rate and enzyme-reaction evidence.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

Calvin循环不只在暗处发生；O₂来自水，净G3P不是一轮直接产生葡萄糖。

### English

Calvin cycling is not confined to darkness; O₂ comes from water and net G3P is not one-turn direct glucose production.

### English

RuBP is the regenerated CO₂ acceptor. Fixation, reduction and regeneration matter more than memorizing all intermediates. Cyclic electron flow can supplement ATP; electron-path adjustment is omitted.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **类囊体** — 叶绿体中承载光反应系统的膜结构。
- **基质** — 叶绿体中类囊体外的内部液相区域。
- **光反应** — 用光能支持电子转移并形成ATP/NADPH的过程。
- **碳固定** — 把无机CO₂碳并入有机分子的过程。
- **Calvin循环** — 利用ATP/NADPH固定并还原碳且再生受体的循环。
- **NADPH** — 供还原反应用的电子载体。
- **G3P** — 三碳糖磷酸，可作为形成其他有机物的原料。
- **限制资源** — 在给定条件下最先限制输出的可用资源。

### English

- **thylakoid** — A chloroplast membrane structure housing light-reaction machinery.
- **stroma** — The chloroplast internal compartment outside thylakoids.
- **light reactions** — Processes using light to support electron transfer and ATP/NADPH supply.
- **carbon fixation** — Incorporation of inorganic CO₂ carbon into organic molecules.
- **Calvin cycle** — A cycle using ATP/NADPH for carbon fixation, reduction and acceptor regeneration.
- **NADPH** — An electron carrier supplying reducing power.
- **G3P** — A three-carbon sugar phosphate supplying organic synthesis.
- **limiting resource** — An available resource that first constrains output under given conditions.

   - A. Thylakoid membrane / stroma
   - B. Nucleus / mitochondrion
   - C. Stroma / nucleus

2. What supplies released photosynthetic O₂ and carbohydrate carbon respectively?
   - A. Water / CO₂
   - B. CO₂ / O₂
   - C. ATP / photons

   - A. 2 μmol, CO₂
   - B. 1 μmol, NADPH

<summary>Reveal after answering</summary>

1. A. Membrane transfer/gradients support supply; stromal cycling uses ATP/NADPH. Connected processes have distinct locations. Return to the two D01 chloroplast regions.
