## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**ATP不是用断键魔法供能。** ATP水解可概括为ATP＋水→ADP＋无机磷酸。断开原有化学键需要输入能量；整个反应同时形成新的键与相互作用，产物状态在给定条件下自由能较低，才使净ΔG为负。“高能磷酸键”是描述较大的水解自由能变化的简写，不能解释成只要折断一个键就凭空放出能量，物质也没有消失。

**数值必须带条件。** ΔG表示反应在指定温度、pH及浓度条件下的自由能变化；负值有利于该方向，正值表示需要驱动。常见标准转化条件下ATP水解值约−30.5 kJ mol⁻¹，细胞内的实际值还依赖ATP、ADP和磷酸等浓度，本图滑块是预先给定的条件值，不由浓度计算，也不是一个细胞每秒的功率。ΔG不直接告诉反应多快，酶降低活化障碍却不改变同条件下反应前后的ΔG。

**做一笔能量与机制账。** 假设同条件下A→B需要+20 kJ mol⁻¹，ATP净水解为−30 kJ mol⁻¹，并有共享中间体或构象变化将两者真正连接，总ΔG=20−30=−10 kJ mol⁻¹。本模型据此判定满足驱动条件；若把需能改成+35，总和+5，就不满足。若保持−10总和却断开实际连接，也不能说ATP已经驱动A→B：水解可只耗散为热，A并未获得正确的化学路径。

**可再生，但不是免费循环。** 细胞通过呼吸或光合作用等途径利用能量把ADP和磷酸再生为ATP；ATP再水解支持运输、合成和运动。再生需要能量，不是永动机。本图A–P仅代表一种抽象磷酸化中间体，不声称所有偶联都有相同结构，也没模拟酶速率与浓度随时间变化。迁移到陌生案例时，先写完整反应与单位，再问是否有真实共享过程，最后给条件限制，比仅把两个数字相加更接近机制解释。

**自由能与守恒并不冲突。** 负ΔG不表示全部能量消失，意味着在指定状态变化下可用于驱动其他过程的自由能减少，部分能量会以热等形式分散。ADP、磷酸及水中原子仍须进入完整物质账本。若一个示意图只画出“ATP变能量”而遗漏产物，就既不能解释物质去向，也难以判断是否真正偶联。好的机制图应保留反应物、产物、连接步骤和给定条件，而不是只画闪电符号。

### English

ATP supplies free-energy transfer through whole reactions, not magical bond breaking. Hydrolysis can be summarized as ATP plus water producing ADP and inorganic phosphate. Breaking an existing chemical bond requires energy. The complete reaction also forms new bonds and interactions; lower-free-energy products under given conditions make the net ΔG negative. “High-energy phosphate bond” is shorthand for a substantial hydrolysis free-energy change, not a claim that snapping a bond alone releases energy or makes matter disappear.

Values require conditions. ΔG describes free-energy change at specified temperature, pH and concentrations. A negative value favors the stated direction; a positive value requires driving. A commonly quoted transformed standard ATP hydrolysis value is about −30.5 kJ mol⁻¹, whereas cellular values depend on ATP, ADP and phosphate concentrations. The sliders supply conditional values rather than calculating them from concentrations. Their unit is energy per mole, not cellular power per second. ΔG also does not specify speed. Enzymes lower activation barriers without changing the free-energy difference between reactants and products at the same conditions.

Keep both an energy ledger and a mechanism. Suppose A→B costs +20 kJ mol⁻¹ and ATP hydrolysis is −30 kJ mol⁻¹ under the same conditions. If a shared intermediate or conformational process physically links them, total ΔG=20−30=−10 kJ mol⁻¹. This meets the model's driving conditions. Raising the work cost to +35 produces +5, failing that condition. Alternatively, keeping the −10 sum but disconnecting the mechanism does not establish ATP-driven A→B. Hydrolysis might merely dissipate energy as heat without providing the required chemical path.

ATP is regenerated rather than supplied free of cost. Respiration and photosynthesis transfer energy to regenerate ATP from ADP and phosphate; hydrolysis then supports synthesis, transport and movement. Regeneration requires energy, so the cycle is not perpetual motion. The schematic A–P represents an abstract phosphorylated intermediate, not the universal structure of coupling. Rates and changing concentrations are omitted. For an unfamiliar example, identify complete reactions and units, check a physical shared process, and state the conditional limits before treating an arithmetic sum as a mechanism.

Free-energy change is consistent with conservation. Negative ΔG does not mean all energy disappears: the specified change lowers the free energy available to drive other processes, while some energy disperses as heat or through other transfers. Atoms in ADP, phosphate and water still belong in a complete material ledger. A sketch showing only “ATP becomes energy” while omitting products cannot account for matter or establish coupling. A useful mechanism diagram retains reactants, products, the connecting step and the stated conditions rather than relying on an energy-flash symbol.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

ΔG为负不等于反应立刻很快；相加为负不等于两过程自动连接。

### English

Negative ΔG does not imply immediate speed, and a negative sum does not automatically connect processes.

## Depth / 高中桥梁

### 中文

高中可进一步用ΔG=ΔG°′＋RT ln Q联系浓度；本课只使用给定条件值，不要求算活度。自由能差与活化能在图上应分别标记。

### English

Later study connects concentrations using ΔG=ΔG°′+RT ln Q. This lesson uses supplied conditional values without activity calculations. Free-energy difference and activation barriers are distinct.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：泵送过程+40 kJ mol⁻¹，配对水解−35 kJ mol⁻¹且有连接。能否据此判断正向自发？自查：总+5，不满足；需要不同条件或其他能量输入，不能说加酶就把ΔG变负。

### English

New situation: pumping costs +40 kJ mol⁻¹ and linked hydrolysis supplies −35 kJ mol⁻¹. Self-check: total +5, so this does not favor the forward direction; different conditions or another input are needed, not merely an enzyme changing ΔG.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **ATP** — 可参与自由能转移与反应偶联的核苷酸。
- **ADP** — ATP少一个磷酸基的相关核苷酸。
- **水解** — 有水参与的化学分解反应。
- **自由能变化** — 给定条件下反应前后可用自由能的差，以ΔG表示。
- **需能反应** — 给定条件下ΔG为正的反应。
- **放能反应** — 给定条件下ΔG为负的反应。
- **偶联** — 通过共享过程使一种反应驱动另一种过程。
- **中间体** — 反应过程中形成并进一步反应的物质。

### English

- **ATP** — A nucleotide participating in free-energy transfer and reaction coupling.
- **ADP** — A related nucleotide with one fewer phosphate group than ATP.
- **hydrolysis** — A chemical breakdown reaction involving water.
- **Gibbs free-energy change** — The free-energy difference between products and reactants under stated conditions, ΔG.
- **endergonic reaction** — A reaction with positive ΔG under given conditions.
- **exergonic reaction** — A reaction with negative ΔG under given conditions.
- **coupling** — Linking reactions through a shared process so one can drive another.
- **intermediate** — A substance formed and used during a reaction sequence.

   - B. −5 kJ mol⁻¹；断开不再由此保证驱动
   - C. +55 kJ mol⁻¹；断开更好

### English

1. Which correctly explains net energy release in ATP hydrolysis?
   - A. Bond breaking alone releases energy
   - B. The whole reaction has lower-free-energy products under stated conditions
   - C. Matter disappears into energy

2. Does total ΔG<0 prove two reactions are coupled?
   - A. Yes, if placed together
   - B. No, a shared intermediate or another physical connection is needed
   - C. Yes, any enzyme automatically connects them

3. At fixed conditions A→B is +25 and ATP hydrolysis −30 kJ mol⁻¹. What is the linked total, and what if disconnected?
   - A. −5 kJ mol⁻¹; disconnected still guaranteed
   - B. −5 kJ mol⁻¹; disconnected gives no such guarantee
   - C. +55 kJ mol⁻¹; disconnection helps

4. What does endergonic mean under given conditions?
   - A. ΔG<0
   - B. ΔG>0
   - C. Always fast

<details>
<summary>Reveal after answering</summary>

1. B. Bond breaking costs energy; hydrolysis includes new interactions, and the complete reaction determines the net change. Inspect the D01 net-hydrolysis box and whole-reaction record.

2. B. Thermodynamic favorability does not supply a mechanism; energy dissipated as heat need not drive the other reaction. Toggle I01 connection with both ΔG values unchanged.

3. B. 25−30=−5; a negative sum is one condition, but connection remains essential. Adding magnitudes loses the sign. Set I01 to 25 and −30, then toggle the shared intermediate.

4. B. Endergonic means positive ΔG, not reaction speed; activation barriers and rates are separate. Return to the D01 work box and Explain conditions.

</details>
