How can a cell reduce one pathway when product is abundant without stopping every reaction?

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**代谢需要协调而非最大速度。** 细胞要使原料、能量供给和需求相配。若一条合成路径的产物已足够，继续高速生产会浪费资源；产物可以回过头影响路径较早的关键酶，降低自身合成通量，这叫反馈抑制。它针对具备相应结合与响应机制的酶，不是任何产物都能同样抑制所有反应。

**先分活性与数量。** 调节分子可结合酶的非活性位点，改变构象与催化表现，属于变构调节的一类。已有酶活性可在数量不变时变化；基因表达调控则可改变酶的合成量，降解也影响数量。两层可共同参与，但如果实验只看到速率下降，不能立刻说基因被关闭、酶少了一半或蛋白被破坏。解除抑制后速率恢复，支持可逆活性变化，却仍需其他证据判断具体结合机制。

**把对照算清楚。** 原创示例固定酶量、温度与pH，底物S和产物P单位为μmol L⁻¹，敏感酶通量v=12S/(2＋S)/(1＋P/4)，单位μmol min⁻¹。取S=6时无产物抑制v=12×6/8=9；P=4时除以2得4.5，P=12时除以4得2.25。不敏感对照不含最后抑制项，三种P下都为9。将敏感酶的P从12调回0，模型恢复9，说明这里没有假定酶被永久损坏。这是特定数学教学规则，并不是所有抑制都服从同一公式。

**稳态不是停摆。** 在真实系统中产物一边生成一边被使用，反馈可使两种通量趋近，产物量保持在范围内；量稳定仍有分子不断周转。本交互只计算指定条件下的瞬时通量，未模拟产物随时间累积或消耗，因此不能仅凭一幅静态图证明系统已经达到稳态。迁移时要固定底物、比较敏感与不敏感对照、报告每分钟物质量，并把浓度和通量分开。若要声称细胞稳态，还需追踪产物量随时间及生成和消耗两侧证据。

**对照的意义在于排除混杂。** 若提高产物时同时升高温度，速率变化就不只由产物解释；若所谓不敏感对照的酶量本来较少，也不能直接比较原始通量。教学模型把这些条件固定，才让差异定位到是否含反馈项。真实实验还要检查底物是否被耗尽、产物是否影响测量信号以及重复间变异。即使对照支持抑制，也不能仅从一组曲线确定抑制位点的三维结构或分子结合常数。

### English

Metabolism needs coordination rather than maximum speed everywhere. Cells must match resources and energy supply to demand. If a synthesis pathway already has enough product, continued rapid production wastes resources. The product may act on a particular earlier enzyme and lower its own synthesis flux: feedback inhibition. This depends on a specific enzyme's binding and response mechanism, rather than every product equally suppressing every reaction.

Separate enzyme activity from enzyme amount. A regulator can bind away from an active site and influence conformation and catalysis, one form of allosteric regulation. Existing enzyme activity can change without a change in enzyme quantity. Gene-expression control can instead alter synthesis of enzyme molecules, while degradation affects quantity too. Both levels may operate together. A falling rate alone therefore does not prove that a gene was switched off, enzyme quantity halved or protein was destroyed. Recovery after removing an inhibitor supports reversible activity regulation, though further evidence is needed to identify a particular binding mechanism.

Calculate the controls explicitly. The original example fixes enzyme amount, temperature and pH. S and P have concentration units μmol L⁻¹. Sensitive-enzyme flux is v=12S/(2+S)/(1+P/4), in μmol min⁻¹. At S=6 with no product inhibition, v=12×6/8=9. P=4 divides this by two, giving 4.5; P=12 divides it by four, giving 2.25. The insensitive control omits the final inhibitory term and remains 9 at all three P values. Returning sensitive-enzyme P from 12 to 0 restores 9, because this model does not assume permanent enzyme damage. This is a particular teaching equation, not a universal law for every inhibitor.

Steady state is not inactivity. In a real system, product is continuously made and used. Feedback can help bring these fluxes into balance, keeping product amount within a range while molecules still turn over. The interaction calculates instantaneous flux at specified conditions; it does not simulate product accumulation or consumption over time. A static picture therefore cannot establish a system's steady state. For a new experiment, hold substrate fixed, compare sensitive and insensitive controls, report amount per time and keep concentration distinct from flux. A steady-state claim additionally needs time measurements of product amount and evidence concerning both production and consumption.

Controls help rule out confounding explanations. If temperature rises while product concentration increases, a rate change cannot be attributed to product alone. If the insensitive control starts with less enzyme, raw fluxes are not directly comparable either. Fixing those conditions in the teaching model isolates the difference associated with the feedback term. Real experiments must also check substrate depletion, interference with the measurement signal and variation between repeats. Even evidence supporting inhibition does not reveal the binding site's three-dimensional structure or a molecular binding constant from one set of curves.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

半速不自动等于酶量减半；浓度μmol L⁻¹不是通量μmol min⁻¹；稳态不等于零反应。

### English

Half speed does not automatically mean half enzyme amount; concentration is not flux, and steady state is not zero reaction.

## Depth / 高中桥梁

### 中文

式中12为最大通量参数，2为底物响应尺度，4为抑制浓度尺度；数值皆虚构。该乘法抑制规则不拟合真实竞争/非竞争抑制，也不推断结合常数。

### English

The parameters 12, 2, 4 denote maximal flux, substrate-response scale and inhibition scale. All are invented. This multiplicative rule is not a fit to competitive/noncompetitive kinetics and does not infer binding constants.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：P=4 μmol L⁻¹，S从2升6；敏感酶通量从3升4.5 μmol min⁻¹。学生说“有抑制就加底物完全无效”。自查：数据否定“完全无效”，但S6时仍低于无抑制9；只适用于本模型条件，不能推广所有抑制。

### English

New situation: at P 4 μmol L⁻¹, S rises 2→6 and sensitive flux rises 3→4.5 μmol min⁻¹. Self-check: added substrate is not completely ineffective, but flux remains below uninhibited 9 at S 6. Limit the claim to this model rather than all inhibition mechanisms.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **代谢通量** — 单位时间通过某反应或路径的物质量。
- **反馈抑制** — 产物反过来抑制路径中较早步骤。
- **酶活性** — 给定条件下酶支持反应的能力或速率表现。
- **变构调节** — 分子结合非活性位点并影响酶功能的调节。
- **底物** — 被酶作用的反应物。
- **产物** — 反应生成的物质。
- **特异性** — 相互作用依赖特定分子与结构条件。
- **动态稳态** — 持续生成与消耗平衡、量近似不变。

### English

- **metabolic flux** — Amount passing through a reaction or pathway per time.
- **feedback inhibition** — An end product inhibits an earlier step in its pathway.
- **enzyme activity** — An enzyme reaction capacity or rate under stated conditions.
- **allosteric regulation** — Regulation through binding away from the active site and changing function.
- **substrate** — A reactant acted on by an enzyme.
- **product** — A substance formed by a reaction.
- **specificity** — Dependence of an interaction on particular molecules and structural conditions.
- **dynamic steady state** — Near-constant amount maintained by ongoing balanced production and use.
