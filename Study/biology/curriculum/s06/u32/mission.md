# 代谢过程—部位—物质匹配档案 / A process–location–material matching file

## 中文

匹配糖酵解/细胞质、丙酮酸氧化与循环/线粒体基质、电子传递与ATP合成/内膜、光反应/类囊体膜、Calvin循环/基质。分列6C→6CO₂、O₂接受电子→水、光合水→O₂与CO₂→有机碳。引用ATP偶联+20−30=−10 kJ mol⁻¹且须实际连接、光合CO₂6/ATP 18/NADPH 6 μmol→净G3P1 μmol及反馈S6/P0→4时通量9→4.5 μmol min⁻¹。用CER连接至少两组数据到机制，写固定条件和不适用边界；附一句英语，可借词卡，英语弱不锁生物任务。

**自查：** 按过程与部位排序；至少两条带单位/条件的数值证据；解释机制而非只抄结果；写一个模型没测的限制。若英语困难，先完成中文推理，再用术语卡补一句英文。

英语练习：Under these conditions, the evidence supports ___ because ___; the model does not test ___.

Match glycolysis/cytosol, pyruvate oxidation+cycle/matrix, electron transfer+ATP synthesis/inner membrane, light reactions/thylakoid membrane, Calvin cycling/stroma. Separate 6C→6CO₂, O₂ accepting electrons→water, photosynthetic water→O₂ and CO₂→organic carbon. Cite coupling +20−30=−10 kJ mol⁻¹ with a physical link; CO₂ 6/ATP 18/NADPH 6 μmol supporting 1 μmol net G3P; and feedback at S 6/P 0→4 reducing flux 9→4.5 μmol min⁻¹. Connect at least two datasets to mechanisms using CER, fixed conditions and limits. Add one supported English sentence; vocabulary support is allowed and English does not gate biology completion.

**Self-check:** correct process/location order; at least two numerical observations with units and conditions; a mechanism rather than copied results; one unmeasured limitation. Complete biological reasoning first, then use vocabulary support for one English sentence.
