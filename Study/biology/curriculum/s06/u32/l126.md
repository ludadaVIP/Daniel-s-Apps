### English

Respiration uses oxygen and releases CO₂. Does inhaled O₂ directly become exhaled CO₂?

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**呼吸是多步氧化，不只是呼吸动作。** 本课用典型真核细胞完全氧化葡萄糖的简化路径。细胞质糖酵解把一个6碳葡萄糖变成两个3碳丙酮酸，净得2 ATP并产生2 NADH。丙酮酸进入线粒体，基质中转化为乙酰基时每葡萄糖放2 CO₂，随后柠檬酸循环再放4 CO₂，另净得约2 ATP当量并产生还原电子载体。不能把所有步骤都画在线粒体内。

**电子与碳走不同的账。** 完全氧化每葡萄糖合计可记10 NADH与2 FADH₂，携带12对电子到线粒体内膜的电子传递系统。电子转移支持把质子泵到膜间隙，质子再经ATP合酶回流支持ATP形成。O₂在路径末端接受电子并与质子形成水；它不含碳，不会直接提供糖碳或把自己变成CO₂。CO₂的碳来自有机物氧化。此处是化学角色简图，不用于逐原子追踪氧同位素交换。

**数量可以核账但不是普适常数。** 教学例设糖酵解2、循环2、氧化磷酸化约26 ATP，总约30 ATP/葡萄糖；真实产量受穿梭系统、膜泄漏、运输及细胞条件影响，不能背成所有生物固定38。碳账是6C→6 CO₂，即6个碳仍存在；ATP数字与碳数不是同一单位，不能相互替代。

**无氧切换有必要前提。** 本课无氧选项明确改用能进行乳酸发酵的系统：2个丙酮酸接受NADH电子成为2个3碳乳酸，再生NAD⁺，使糖酵解可继续净得2 ATP。没有NAD⁺再生，有限的氧化型载体用尽后糖酵解也不能无限持续。乳酸路径不额外产生该6 CO₂，较少ATP反映仍有化学能留在有机产物中，不是碳凭空消失。微生物还可有其他发酵或无氧呼吸路径，本开关并不代表所有缺氧细胞的唯一结局。

**分区能产生可检验预测。** 若选择性阻断线粒体内膜电子传递，先直接影响电子受体再生和梯度供能，随后可间接拖慢依赖氧化型载体的基质过程；这不等于抑制剂直接作用糖酵解每个酶。若有发酵能力，细胞质路径可能暂时维持一部分ATP。因此解释干预时要区分直接作用位置与后续系统效应，记录供氧、底物及时间，不能只凭最终ATP下降就指定唯一被阻断的步骤。这些预测必须写成限定条件下的可能结果，再由时间序列检验。

### English

Cellular respiration is a multistep oxidation pathway, not just breathing movements. This lesson simplifies complete glucose oxidation in a typical eukaryotic cell. Cytosolic glycolysis converts one six-carbon glucose into two three-carbon pyruvates, giving net two ATP and two NADH. Pyruvate enters mitochondria; matrix conversion to acetyl groups releases two CO₂ per glucose, and the citric acid cycle releases four more. The cycle also supplies about two ATP equivalents and reduced electron carriers. The entire pathway must not be placed inside mitochondria because glycolysis occurs outside them.

Keep electron and carbon ledgers separate. Complete oxidation can yield ten NADH plus two FADH₂ carrying twelve electron pairs to the inner-membrane electron-transfer system. Electron transfer supports proton pumping into the intermembrane space. Protons returning through ATP synthase support ATP formation. O₂ accepts electrons at the end and combines with protons to form water. It contains no carbon and neither supplies sugar carbon nor directly turns into CO₂. CO₂ carbon comes from oxidized organic material. This chemical-role diagram is not an atom-by-atom simulation of oxygen-isotope exchange.

Quantities support accounting without becoming universal constants. The teaching example assigns two glycolytic ATP, two cycle ATP equivalents and approximately twenty-six oxidative ATP, totaling about thirty ATP per glucose. Actual yields vary with shuttle systems, membrane leakage, transport and cellular conditions; a universal fixed thirty-eight is inappropriate. Carbon accounting gives 6C→6 CO₂: six carbon atoms remain in products. ATP yield and carbon count have different units and cannot substitute for each other.

The oxygen-off option has a necessary assumption. It explicitly selects a system capable of lactate fermentation. Pyruvate accepts NADH electrons, producing two three-carbon lactates and regenerating NAD⁺ so glycolysis can continue with net two ATP. Without NAD⁺ regeneration, a finite oxidized-carrier supply would eventually limit glycolysis. This lactate pathway does not additionally generate those six CO₂. Its lower ATP yield leaves substantial chemical energy in organic products rather than making carbon disappear. Microorganisms can use other fermentation or anaerobic-respiration pathways; this switch does not represent every possible outcome of oxygen shortage.

Compartments support testable predictions. Selectively blocking inner-membrane electron transfer first affects electron-carrier reoxidation and gradient-supported ATP production. It can subsequently slow matrix processes that require oxidized carriers; this does not show that the inhibitor directly targets every glycolytic enzyme. With fermentation capacity, the cytosolic pathway may temporarily maintain some ATP production. Distinguish the intervention's direct location from later system effects, and record oxygen supply, substrate and time. A final ATP decrease alone cannot uniquely identify the blocked step. State these predictions conditionally and test them with a time series.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

O₂是末端电子受体，CO₂的碳来自糖；发酵不等于没有氧的同一电子传递链。

### English

O₂ is the terminal electron acceptor and CO₂ carbon comes from sugar; fermentation is not the same chain without oxygen.

## Depth / 高中桥梁

### 中文

氧化还原表示电子转移。NADH被再氧化为NAD⁺，并非载体分子永远只用一次。ATP合酶依赖跨膜电化学梯度，本图省略电位与具体质子数。

### English

Redox concerns electron transfer. NADH is reoxidized to reusable NAD⁺. ATP synthase depends on an electrochemical gradient; voltage and exact proton counts are omitted.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：线粒体内膜被人为设为质子自由通过，供氧及糖仍足够。预测哪部分ATP先下降，并说明CO₂不必立刻为零。自查：氧化磷酸化因梯度耗散下降；底物水平磷酸化可暂保留，需测ATP和碳通量，不能把全部呼吸看作一个开关。

### English

New situation: protons freely leak across the inner membrane despite adequate oxygen and glucose. Self-check: oxidative ATP falls as the gradient dissipates; substrate-level ATP may persist temporarily. CO₂ need not instantly vanish. Measure ATP and carbon flux rather than treat respiration as one switch.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **糖酵解** — 在细胞质把葡萄糖转为丙酮酸并净得少量ATP的路径。
- **基质** — 线粒体内膜所围的内部空间。
- **柠檬酸循环** — 氧化乙酰基并产生CO₂和还原载体的循环。
- **NADH** — 可向其他反应传递电子的还原载体。
- **电子传递链** — 通过电子转移建立跨膜质子梯度的系统。
- **末端电子受体** — 电子传递路径末端接收电子的物质。
- **氧化磷酸化** — 电子转移与质子梯度支持的ATP生成。
- **发酵** — 通过有机受体再氧化NADH以维持糖酵解的路径。

### English

- **glycolysis** — A cytosolic pathway converting glucose to pyruvate with a small net ATP yield.
- **matrix** — The compartment enclosed by the mitochondrial inner membrane.
- **citric acid cycle** — A cycle oxidizing acetyl groups to CO₂ and reduced carriers.
- **NADH** — A reduced carrier transferring electrons to other reactions.
- **electron transport chain** — A system transferring electrons and establishing a transmembrane proton gradient.
- **terminal electron acceptor** — A substance accepting electrons at the end of a transfer pathway.
- **oxidative phosphorylation** — ATP production supported by electron transfer and a proton gradient.
- **fermentation** — A pathway reoxidizing NADH with an organic acceptor to sustain glycolysis.

### English

1. Where is the respiratory electron transport chain in a typical eukaryotic cell?
   - A. Mitochondrial inner membrane
   - B. On nuclear DNA
   - C. Freely anywhere in the cytosol

2. What is O₂ doing in aerobic respiration?
   - A. Supplying carbon in CO₂
   - B. Accepting electrons at the end and forming water
   - C. Directly becoming all ATP

3. Per 6C glucose: aerobic model gives 6 CO₂ and ≈30 ATP; lactate fermentation gives two 3C lactates and net 2 ATP. Which comparison is valid?
   - A. Fermentation loses four carbons because ATP is lower
   - B. Both conserve six carbons; substantial chemical energy remains in lactate
   - C. Lactate fermentation directly gives six CO₂

   - A. A substance accepting electrons at the end of a pathway
   - B. The final sugar carbon
   - C. A general name for every enzyme

<summary>Reveal after answering</summary>

1. A. The inner membrane separates compartments for a proton gradient; glycolysis is cytosolic, while pyruvate oxidation/cycling occur mainly in matrix. Return to the D01 compartments and I01 frame 3.

3. B. Carbon conservation and ATP yield are different ledgers: 2×3=6 carbons remain in lactate; lower ATP does not mean lost carbon. Toggle I01 routes and compare 6 CO₂ with 2×3C.
