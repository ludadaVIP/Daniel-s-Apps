## 作品目标 / Outcome

完成一份能让读者复核的研究报告：明确问题、来源和方法，保留原始数据与纳入范围，用数值和图支持有限主张，区分机制假设与实测，考虑局限、替代解释和进一步研究。提供真实双语结构化摘要、双语图注、引用、自核与修订，以及五段共计划180–300秒的口头稿或分镜。

Write a checkable investigation: define the question, provenance and method; retain raw records and inclusion scope; support a bounded claim with numbers and a graph; separate measured evidence from hypotheses; consider limitations, alternatives and a further study. Include an assisted bilingual structured abstract, bilingual figure caption, citations, manual review, revisions, and a five-part oral script or storyboard planned for 180–300 seconds.

## 两条数据路径 / Two data paths

### 官方生态数据 / Official ecological data

使用NPS《Yellowstone Wolf, Cougar, and Elk Project Annual Report 2025》物理第5页年末狼群表。观察日期为2025-12-31，核验日期为2026-11-15。十行原表转录见`yellowstone-2025.json`，包括7个pack、1个新group和2个零独居类别。汇总只作校验，不能加入原始行再次求和。

North：36成年+17幼狼=53；Interior：31成年+0幼狼=31；合计67成年+17幼狼=84。北部合并幼狼份额17/53≈32.08%，全园17/84≈20.24%；不能等权平均狼群比例。独居类别0/0未定义，Interior的0/31为0%。

这是年末年龄组成快照，不是出生率、存活率、密度或2026当前数量；0只年末幼狼不等于全年没有出生。原表不是84条逐只记录，10行不是10个独立实验重复。地区差异不能证明因果，疾病、迁出、观测差异需要额外证据。不得从历史柱图猜测或数字化未核验的时间序列。

Use the NPS year-end age-composition table on physical page 5. The ten transcribed rows contain seven packs, one new group and two zero loner categories. Sum raw rows once; published totals are cross-checks. Pooled shares use total wolves as the denominator. The snapshot does not estimate birth rates, survival, density, regional causes or current 2026 abundance. No historical chart values are digitized.

选择全园、北部或内部范围；至少两条有效不同记录可被引用。绘制分类计数条形图或地区合并幼狼比例图，不把类别画成时间线。不同记录不是不同独立来源。

Choose all, Northern Range or Interior scope. Cite at least two distinct valid records, recognizing that records are not independent sources. Draw categorical counts or pooled regional pup shares, never a fictitious time series.

### 安全家庭植物观察 / Safe home plant observations

默认空白，不能把产品预填或测试fixture称作实测。观察熟悉的原有室内植物，保持正常照料，不摘样，不进行健康测量。说明自录来源、同一高度参照点、尺和单位、条件及真实起始日期。录入3–100条记录：稳定记录ID、植株ID、真实经过日（允许不等间隔整数）及高度cm或明确缺失NA。

可保留多株原始记录，但图表与计算一次只分析一株，至少三个不同日的有效高度。0保留为真实测值；缺失不补0，图中不跨缺失点连线。输出末次减首次差值，相对变化分母为首次有效高度；首值0时相对变化未定义。单株多次测量是纵向n=1，不是多个独立植株。全部原始行、未纳入行及原因均随报告导出。数据为学生自述，未经外部核验。

Start blank. Observe an existing familiar indoor plant during normal care, without collecting samples or measuring health. Record provenance, a consistent height reference point and ruler, conditions and a real start date. Enter 3–100 stable-ID records with plant ID, actual elapsed integer day and height in cm or explicit NA. Analyse one plant with at least three valid distinct days; retain other plants and exclusions. Keep real zero, never replace missing with zero, and break graph lines at missing readings. Relative change is undefined when the first valid height is zero. Repeated readings of one plant remain independent plant n=1. Records are self-reported, not externally certified.

## 报告与口头稿 / Report and oral script

学生正文可选中文或英文，英语不是生物进度门槛。可选另一语言练习和英语句型帮助表达；数据生成的双语摘要明确为辅助结构化支架，不冒充自由文字自动翻译。图注、单位、实际范围和所有原始数据必须一致。

五个有标题的段落分别为：问题与边界；来源/方法/数据；分析/证据/机制假设；局限/替代；下一研究/结论。每段由学生填写自己的笔记（任一语言），可选另一语言；整数秒计划合计180–300秒。必须排练、自核并记录修订的what和why；计划秒数不证明实际说完。

The learner may write prose in Chinese or English. Optional starters and second-language practice support expression. The automatically populated bilingual abstract is explicitly an assisted structured scaffold, not a translation of prose. Each of five titled segments needs the learner’s own notes and a whole-second budget, totaling 180–300 seconds. Rehearse, manually review, and record what changed and why; planned timing is not proof of actual delivery.

## 保存、理解检查与阶段完成 / Saving, checking and completion

两条路径分别保留草稿与savedAt。编辑只撤除当前题材保存标记；删改记录或改变范围/日期/植株会清理失效引用。切题材不清空另一题材。报告结构保存与理解检查分开，不把错误学生选择在报告中冒充标准答案。

六题跨U31–U36：L123翻译与同义替换；L127净G3P化学计量限制；L131延迟负反馈偏差；L134加权基因流；L138片段长度与证据范围；L143缺失、真实零与独立重复。允许回课和订正。S06完成条件是学生自己的24课、6任务、至少一个有效已保存题材，加上六题全答且至少五题正确。144课已制作/available不等于学生毕业，也不等于正式published。

Drafts and saved states are independent. Edits revoke only the edited case’s saved state, while changed data or scope clear invalid citations. Saving the report is separate from answering all six cross-unit checks with at least five correct. Learner completion also requires their own 24 lessons and six missions. Course availability is not learner completion or formal publication.

## 自动检查边界 / Automated-check boundaries

自动检查只验证结构、数据合法性、计算、引用和图表，不判定自由文字的科学质量。人工自核要求检查来源、单位、范围、机制假设、局限、替代解释、独立重复、可挑战结果与排练修订。最终输出为可离线阅读、可打印的独立HTML，所有学生文本转义，保留完整图、原始数据、摘要、正文、引用、五段讲稿、修订与理解检查状态。

Automated checks cover structure, valid data, arithmetic, citations and graphs, not the scientific quality of free prose. The complete offline, printable HTML includes escaped student text, the actual graph, all raw records, scope, summary, sources, prose, citations, five script segments, revision, manual review and actual comprehension choices with separate answered/correct counts.
