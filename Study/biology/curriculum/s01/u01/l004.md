## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文
细菌是单细胞生物。病毒不是细胞：它有遗传物质和蛋白质衣壳，部分病毒还有包膜，但没有独立完成代谢和复制的细胞系统。

在合适的宿主细胞内，病毒的遗传信息参与产生新病毒。模型以几个步骤概括接触、利用宿主系统、产生与释放副本；不同病毒的真实过程并不完全相同。

为什么存在争论？病毒有遗传信息，病毒群体能演化；但它们没有细胞结构，也不能独立代谢和复制。采用细胞生命的标准时，病毒通常不被视为独立生物。回答需要说明采用的标准与证据。

### English
Bacteria are unicellular organisms. Viruses are not cells: they contain genetic material and a protein capsid, sometimes with an envelope, but lack cellular systems for independent metabolism and replication.

Inside a suitable host cell, viral genetic information participates in making new viruses. The model summarises contact, use of host systems, production and release. Actual processes differ between viruses.

Why the debate? Viruses carry genetic information and viral populations can evolve, but viruses are acellular and cannot metabolise or replicate independently. Under cellular criteria they are usually not treated as independent organisms. Explain the criteria and evidence behind your answer.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文
“病毒是很小的细菌。”细菌有细胞结构，病毒没有；大小不是最重要的区分依据。并非所有细菌都有害，也并非病毒能感染任意细胞。

### English
“Viruses are tiny bacteria.” Bacteria have cellular structure; viruses do not. Size is not the key distinction. Not all bacteria are harmful, and viruses cannot infect just any cell.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文
细菌细胞中DNA不被细胞核包围；病毒基因组可以是DNA或RNA。遗传信息不意味着必须有细胞核。是否把病毒看作生命，取决于生命定义；这是可以基于证据讨论的科学边界。

### English
Bacterial DNA is not enclosed in a nucleus. Viral genomes may be DNA or RNA, so genetic information does not imply a nucleus. Viruses illustrate how definitions create scientific boundary cases.
