## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文
种子中有胚，也有保护结构，通常有储存养分。活种子可能处于低活动的休眠状态；没有明显变化不代表一定死亡。

许多种子萌发需要水分、氧气和适宜温度。种子先吸水，再恢复活跃的代谢，根通常先伸出。早期生长利用储存养分，幼叶展开后逐渐通过光合作用制造糖。

不萌发有很多可能：缺水、氧气不足、温度不合适、休眠条件尚未解除，或种子失去活力。光照要求也因物种而异。一次失败不能直接证明死亡。

### English
A seed contains an embryo, protective structures and usually stored nutrients. A living seed may be dormant with low activity. No visible change does not establish that it is dead.

Many seeds need water, oxygen and a suitable temperature to germinate. Water uptake helps restore active metabolism, and a root usually emerges first. Early growth uses stored nutrients; developing leaves later make sugars by photosynthesis.

Failure to germinate may reflect unsuitable water, oxygen or temperature, unresolved dormancy, or loss of viability. Light requirements vary among species. One failure does not establish death.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文
“水越多越好。”完全浸没可能限制氧气供应。“任何种子在相同条件下都会萌发”也不对，种类和活力很重要。

### English
“More water is always better.” Full submersion can limit oxygen. Seeds also differ in species and viability, so identical conditions do not guarantee germination.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文
本模型假设种子有活力、无病害、休眠已经解除，且氧气充足时为普通豆种的适宜条件。温度阈值是教学用简化值，阶段时间不是生长预测。不同植物可能需要光、低温处理或其他刺激。

### English
The model assumes viable, healthy seeds with dormancy already broken, representing common beans. Temperature thresholds are simplified teaching values; stage timing is not a growth forecast. Other species may require light, chilling or other cues.
