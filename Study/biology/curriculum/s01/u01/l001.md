## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文
移动、变大或使用能量都不能单独证明生命。机器人能移动，火焰能蔓延；植物虽不走路，仍有细胞并进行生命活动。

科学家综合细胞结构、代谢、调节、生长发育、反应与遗传等证据研究生命。每个个体不一定在每一时刻明显展示所有特征。

**观察 → 解释**：“第二天出现一条小根”是观察；“这颗种子可能有生命”是基于证据的解释。解释可以随新证据修正。

### English
Movement, getting bigger or using energy alone cannot establish life. Robots move and flames spread. Plants have cells and carry out life processes even though they do not walk.

Scientists consider cellular structure, metabolism, regulation, development, responses and heredity together. Not every individual visibly shows every characteristic at every moment.

**Observation → inference**: “A small root appeared the next day” is an observation. “This seed may be alive” is an inference that can be revised with evidence.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文
“不会动就是没有生命。”植物和可萌发的干种子就是反例；火焰虽然变大，却不具有细胞结构。

### English
“If it does not move, it is non-living.” Plants and viable dry seeds are counterexamples. Flames grow without cellular structure.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文
细胞是生命的基本结构与功能单位。病毒是边界案例，留到 L004 比较。不能仅用一条表面特征把所有对象绝对分类。

### English
Cells are basic units of life’s structure and function. Viruses are a boundary case explored in L004. One superficial feature cannot classify every object.
