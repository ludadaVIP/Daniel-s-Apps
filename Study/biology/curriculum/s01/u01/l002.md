## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文
植物通过光合作用利用光能，由水和二氧化碳制造糖。土壤提供水和矿质养分；植物不是从土里直接“吃到食物”。

植物的细胞也进行细胞呼吸，释放可用能量，用于生长、运输和维持生命。细胞呼吸可以昼夜发生；它不是肺部吸气呼气。

一些新茎会随着生长向光弯曲。这是对刺激的反应，不是植物像动物那样转身。模型把几天的变化压缩成几个步骤。

### English
Plants use light energy to make sugars from water and carbon dioxide through photosynthesis. Soil supplies water and mineral nutrients; it does not directly provide the plant’s food.

Plant cells also carry out cellular respiration, releasing usable energy for growth, transport and maintenance. This can occur day and night and is different from breathing.

Some young shoots bend towards light as they grow. This response is gradual, not a plant turning around like an animal. The model compresses days into a few steps.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文
“植物白天光合作用，晚上才细胞呼吸。”细胞呼吸昼夜都可进行，二者不是按白天夜晚轮班。

### English
“Plants photosynthesise in the day and respire only at night.” Cellular respiration can occur both day and night; these are not alternating shifts.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文
向光性与茎两侧生长速度的差异有关；这涉及激素调节，后续再研究。比较光照方向时，应控制植物种类、水量和温度等条件。

### English
Phototropism involves different growth rates on the two sides of a shoot, with hormonal regulation explored later. Tests should control plant type, water and temperature.
