## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

拿起书的一瞬间，骨、肌肉和关节怎样配合？一块肌肉能像电动杆一样，又推又拉吗？

### English

As you pick up a book, how do bones, muscles and joints cooperate? Can one muscle actively push and pull like a powered rod?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先猜弯臂时是谁改变形状、谁保持长度。模型会让你检查这个猜想。

### English

Predict what changes shape and what keeps its length during bending. Use the model to check.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

器官地图的肌肉不是独自工作：还需要神经信号、氧与养分。点击其他器官，找一个合作伙伴。

### English

Muscle does not work alone: it needs nerve signals, oxygen and nutrients. Select another organ and find a partner.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

选弯臂或伸臂，逐步推进。观察骨在肘关节处的角度、彩色肌肉的形状和连接到骨的虚线肌腱。

### English

Choose bending or straightening and advance step by step. Observe the elbow angle, coloured muscle shapes and dashed tendon connections.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**骨提供支撑，关节允许运动。** 手臂的骨不是一根能自动折弯的软管。上臂与前臂的骨在肘关节处发生相对转动，不是靠骨突然缩短。不同关节允许的动作不同，头骨的很多连接处几乎不动。

**肌肉产生牵拉。** 骨骼肌收缩能产生力；肌腱把肌肉连接到骨，传递牵拉。弯臂时，以肱二头肌为代表的屈肌参与拉动；伸臂时，以肱三头肌为代表的伸肌参与相反方向的拉动。模型省略其他肌肉与精细附着位置，只比较两种方向。

**放松不是主动推。** 一块肌肉放松，并不等于它主动把骨推回去。相反方向的肌肉牵拉、重力或其他外力，可以改变位置。真实动作中，肌肉还会共同稳定关节，因此“永远一块全开、一块全关”也太简单。

**运动是一场合作。** 神经参与协调，血液送来氧和营养物质，细胞提供可用能量。手臂模型的角度和帧数只是教学设置，不代表每个人的活动范围或实际用时。

### English

**Bones support; joints permit movement.** An arm is not a soft pipe that folds itself. Upper-arm and forearm bones turn relative to one another at the elbow without suddenly shortening. Different joints allow different movements; many skull connections barely move.

**Muscles pull.** Skeletal muscle contraction generates force. Tendons connect muscle to bone and transmit pulls. Flexors, including biceps, participate in bending; extensors, including triceps, participate in opposing straightening. The model omits other muscles and detailed attachment sites to compare directions.

**Relaxation is not active pushing.** A relaxed muscle does not actively push a bone back. Opposing muscles, gravity or other external forces can change its position. Real muscles can also cooperate to stabilise a joint; one fully on and one fully off is too simple.

**Movement involves systems.** Nerves help coordinate action, blood brings oxygen and nutrients, and cells supply usable energy. Model angles and frame counts are teaching choices, not personal movement ranges or real timing.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“骨变短，所以手臂弯了。”模型固定骨长，只改变关节角度。“一块肌肉主动推回去”也把放松与牵拉混淆了。

### English

“Bones shorten to bend the arm.” The model keeps bone lengths fixed while changing joint angle. “One muscle pushes it back” also confuses relaxation with pulling.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

肌腱连接肌肉与骨，韧带主要连接骨与骨。肌肉内部怎样产生牵拉？后续会用蛋白质相互作用和能量供给解释；目前先抓住结构合作。

### English

Tendons connect muscles to bones; ligaments mainly connect bones to bones. Later lessons examine protein interactions and energy supply inside muscle. Begin with cooperation between structures.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

弯腿是否也只靠骨自己变形？尝试迁移“骨—关节—肌肉—肌腱”的合作解释，不必把所有关节都想成同一形状。

### English

Does bending a leg rely on bones changing shape? Apply bone–joint–muscle–tendon cooperation without assuming every joint has the same form.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

关节joint；骨骼肌skeletal muscle；肌腱tendon；拮抗作用antagonistic action。用“Muscles pull bones at joints”连起来。

### English

Use joint, skeletal muscle, tendon and antagonistic action. Connect them: Muscles pull bones at joints.

## Check / 理解检查

### 中文

比较骨长与角度，指出肌腱的作用，解释伸臂时为什么不能只说“屈肌推回去”。

### English

Compare bone length with angle, identify tendon function and explain why straightening is not a flexor pushing back.

## Mission / 放到生活里

### 中文

观察一次日常拿书动作，或回看模型，用自己的话讲出谁拉、哪里转、什么长度不变。不需要举重、竞赛或重复动作。

### English

Observe an ordinary book-picking action or revisit the model. Explain what pulls, what pivots and what length stays fixed. No weights, competition or repetitions needed.
