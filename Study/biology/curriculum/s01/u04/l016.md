## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

窗边的沙沙声可能来自猫，也可能是窗帘。你听见什么，和你认为发生什么，是同一句话吗？

### English

A rustle by a window might come from a cat or a curtain. Are what you hear and what you think happened the same statement?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先猜耳朵怎样与脑合作，再想想一条声音线索是否足以确定原因。

### English

Predict how ears cooperate with the brain and whether one sound identifies its cause.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

选择“只有声音”或“再看一眼”，逐步查看刺激、感受器、神经信号、脑的解释。这里只用文字提示沙沙声，不播放声音；新视觉线索会改变可以提出的解释。

### English

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**感官收集不同线索。** 光可刺激眼内的感受细胞，声音的振动可影响耳内的感受细胞，皮肤也能感受触碰等变化。刺激是可被感受系统检测的变化；不同感受器对不同类型的刺激作出反应。

**信息被转换和传递。** 耳朵不是把一小团“声音物质”塞进脑。感受细胞参与把振动转为神经系统信号，信号沿有关通路传递。图中的金色点代表信号传播，不是空气颗粒，也不是一根神经只有一个信号。

**脑参与解释。** 脑处理感官信息，也结合背景和经验。听见沙沙声是一个线索；“窗边有猫”是对原因的推测。同样的声音可能有不同来源。

**多一条线索，可以修正解释。** 再看见窗帘在动，让“窗帘可能是声源”更有根据，但仍不排除其他来源。感官不是没有用，也不是永不出错。明确什么是观察、什么是解释，能帮助我们继续调查。

### English

**Senses gather different clues.** Light affects sensory cells in eyes, vibrations affect sensory cells in ears, and skin detects changes including touch. A stimulus is a detectable change. Different receptors respond to different stimulus types.

**Information is converted and carried.** Ears do not put lumps of sound into the brain. Sensory cells help convert vibrations into nervous-system signals that travel through relevant pathways. Gold dots represent signal propagation, not air particles or a single signal in an entire nerve.

**The brain interprets.** The brain processes sensory information with context and experience. Hearing a rustle is a clue; a cat by the window is an inferred cause. Similar sounds can have different sources.

**Another clue can revise an explanation.** Seeing a moving curtain supports the idea that it may be the source, without excluding every other source. Senses are useful but not infallible. Separating observations from interpretations helps further investigation.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“只要听到一种声音，就只有一种原因。”这是把线索当成了唯一解释。另一个误区是“感官会有局限，所以什么都不能知道”。我们可以用更多证据检验解释。

### English

“One sound has only one possible cause” mistakes a clue for a unique explanation. “Senses have limits, so we can know nothing” is also mistaken. More evidence can test explanations.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

人的感觉不只通常列举的视、听、嗅、味、触，还包括身体位置与平衡等。真实神经通路有许多细胞和中继；四站图只概括信息方向。L053、L104会进一步连接神经与行动。

### English

Human senses include body position and balance as well as the familiar five. Real nervous pathways have many cells and relays; the four-stop diagram only summarises direction. L053 and L104 connect nervous systems with action.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

看到地面湿，能立刻断言刚下雨吗？洒水也能让地面湿。用今天的“线索→可能解释→再找证据”方法想一想。

### English

Does wet ground prove recent rain? Watering can wet it too. Apply clue → possible explanation → further evidence.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

刺激stimulus；感受器receptor；神经nerve；知觉perception。句式：I observe …; this may suggest … .

### English

Use stimulus, receptor, nerve and perception. Try: I observe …; this may suggest … .

## Check / 理解检查

### 中文

指出传递信息的神经信号，区分声音线索与猫的推测，并用新线索修正解释。

### English

Identify nerve signals, separate a sound clue from a cat inference and revise an explanation with another clue.

## Mission / 放到生活里

### 中文

安静地观察熟悉物体，用两种可用的感官记录线索；也可只用课中的文字和图。不要尝陌生物、制造强光或大声音。

### English

Quietly record clues about a familiar object using two available senses, or use the lesson’s text and diagram. No unfamiliar tasting, bright lights or loud sounds.
