## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**呼吸让空气更新。** 吸气和呼气让肺中的空气与外界交换。肺不是制造氧的工厂：空气中原有的氧到达肺泡附近。

**气体跨过交换表面。** 肺泡是小气囊，周围有细小血管。氧跨过薄的交换表面进入血液，二氧化碳从血液进入肺泡气体。两种气体都有自己的交换方向，不是把整团空气直接吹进血管。

**心脏泵血，血液运输。** 从肺返回的富氧血流到左心，再被泵向身体；血液在组织中把氧送往细胞。身体回来的血液经右心泵向肺。完整血流可连成：身体→右心→肺→左心→身体。心脏本身也需要氧和养分。

**细胞才使用氧。** 氧可参与有氧细胞呼吸，帮助细胞从有机物中获得可用能量；二氧化碳经血液回到右心、肺，随后呼出。呼吸运动与细胞呼吸不是同一件事。肌肉活动、消化系统供应养分和神经协调，组成更大的合作网络。

### English

**Breathing renews air.** Inhalation and exhalation exchange lung air with the surroundings. Lungs do not manufacture oxygen; oxygen already in air reaches the alveoli.

**Gases cross exchange surfaces.** Alveoli are small air sacs surrounded by tiny vessels. Oxygen crosses thin surfaces into blood, while carbon dioxide moves from blood into alveolar air. Each gas has its own exchange direction; intact air bubbles are not blown into vessels.

**The heart pumps and blood transports.** Oxygen-rich blood returning from lungs reaches the left heart, which pumps it towards the body. Blood delivers oxygen to tissues. Blood returning from the body goes through the right heart towards lungs. The connected flow is body → right heart → lungs → left heart → body. Heart tissue needs oxygen and nutrients too.

**Cells use oxygen.** Oxygen participates in aerobic cellular respiration, supporting usable energy supply from organic substances. Carbon dioxide travels in blood back through the right heart to lungs and is exhaled. Breathing movements and cellular respiration are different. Muscle activity, nutrient supply and nervous coordination form a larger partnership.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“肺制造氧，心脏输送空气。”肺交换气体，心脏主要推动血液。空气通路和血液通路在交换表面相邻，却不是一条敞开的管。

### English

“Lungs make oxygen and the heart transports air.” Lungs exchange gases; the heart pumps blood. Air and blood routes meet at exchange surfaces without forming one open pipe.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

为什么交换表面薄、面积大很有帮助？为什么右心和左心的泵血工作不同？L038、L039和L101会深入。模型不显示真实血流速度、心率或血氧数值；颜色只区分路线，实际血液不会变成蓝色。

### English

Why do thin, large exchange surfaces help? Why do right- and left-heart pumping jobs differ? L038, L039 and L101 explore further. The model shows no real flow speed, heart rate or oxygen reading. Colours distinguish routes; real blood does not turn blue.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

睡觉时不在跑步，心肺能全部停下吗？细胞仍需维持生命活动，所以供氧、运输与排出二氧化碳仍在进行。

### English

Can heart and lungs stop completely while you sleep? Cells still maintain life, so oxygen delivery, transport and carbon dioxide removal continue.
