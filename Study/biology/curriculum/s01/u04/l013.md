## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

鸟啄种子，牛吃草，我们吃午饭。取食的方式不同，为什么食物仍要经过一段旅程才能帮到身体？

### English

A bird pecks seeds, a cow eats grass and we eat lunch. Why must food travel through several steps before it helps the body?

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**食物先被取得。** 动物需要从其他生物或它们产生的物质取得有机物。牙齿、喙和过滤结构等，帮助不同动物取得和处理食物；动物不一定都靠咀嚼。

**颗粒变小，不等于分子都变小。** 咀嚼是机械处理。消化酶参与化学分解：淀粉的消化从口腔开始，继续在小肠进行，最终可得到葡萄糖；蛋白质主要从胃开始被分解，在小肠继续消化成可吸收的产物，包括氨基酸。胃并不完成一切工作。

**越过边界，才是吸收。** 多数营养物质在小肠吸收。图中葡萄糖或氨基酸跨过肠壁进入血液，再经循环运到细胞。真实血液路线还会经过肝脏等部位，图中省略。

**细胞再使用材料。** 葡萄糖可以参与细胞呼吸，为活动供应可用能量；氨基酸可以用于建造蛋白质。食物不是原样塞进肌肉。未被消化吸收的物质继续沿消化道前行。

### English

**First, obtain food.** Animals obtain organic substances from other organisms or their products. Teeth, beaks and filtering structures support different feeding methods; not every animal chews.

**Smaller pieces are not necessarily smaller molecules.** Chewing is mechanical processing. Enzymes help with chemical breakdown: starch digestion starts in the mouth and continues in the small intestine, eventually yielding glucose. Protein breakdown begins mainly in the stomach and continues in the small intestine, producing absorbable products including amino acids. The stomach does not do everything.

**Absorption crosses a boundary.** Most nutrients are absorbed in the small intestine. In this model, glucose or amino acids cross the intestinal wall into blood, which circulates towards cells. Real routes include the liver and other structures omitted here.

**Cells use materials.** Glucose can support cellular respiration and usable energy supply. Amino acids can help build proteins. Intact food is not inserted into muscle. Unabsorbed material continues along the digestive tract.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“嚼碎就是消化完，胃把整块食物送到肌肉。”颗粒处理、分子分解、跨肠壁和血液运输是不同工作。

### English

“Chewing finishes digestion and the stomach sends chunks to muscle.” Processing pieces, breaking molecules down, crossing the gut wall and blood transport are different jobs.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

酶怎样选择要分解的物质？小肠为什么有很大的吸收表面积？后续L037、L102会深入。这里的血液路线只跟踪葡萄糖和氨基酸；许多脂肪消化产物先经淋巴，所以不能把它当作全部营养物质的唯一通路。

### English

How do enzymes act on particular substances? Why does the intestine have a large absorbing surface? L037 and L102 explore further. This blood route tracks glucose and amino acids; many fat products first enter lymph, so it is not the only route for all nutrients.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

喝下水是否也要先被酶拆成葡萄糖？不需要。水能被吸收，提醒我们“吸收”和“消化”不等同。

### English

Must water be enzymatically broken into glucose before absorption? No. Water can be absorbed, showing that absorption and digestion are different.
