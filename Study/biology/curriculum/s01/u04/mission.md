# U04 身体的接力路线 / Body relay map

## 中文

画一个想象中的跑步者，把氧与养分到达肌肉细胞的路线连起来。氧：空气→肺→血液→左心→身体细胞；养分：食物→消化→小肠吸收→血液运输→细胞。再补上二氧化碳：细胞→血液→右心→肺→呼出。神经协调肌肉，肌肉通过肌腱牵拉骨在关节处运动。

用主张、证据和推理记录：为什么跑步不是腿部肌肉独自完成？指出路线图的简化，如省略肝脏、心脏各腔室、血管细节和真实速度。模型箭头是教学线索，不是直接看见的体内血流。不需要实际跑步或测指标。

## English

Sketch an imagined runner and connect oxygen and nutrient delivery to muscle cells. Oxygen: air → lungs → blood → left heart → body cells. Nutrients: food → digestion → intestinal absorption → blood transport → cells. Add carbon dioxide: cells → blood → right heart → lungs → exhalation. Nerves coordinate muscles, which pull bones through tendons at joints.

Record a claim, evidence and reasoning: why is running more than leg muscles working alone? Identify omissions, including liver, heart chambers, detailed vessels and real speeds. Model arrows are teaching clues, not directly observed internal blood flow. No actual running or body measurements needed.
