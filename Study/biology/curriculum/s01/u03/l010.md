## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文
根在土里，叶在阳光下。它们怎样分享自己得到的东西？

### English
Roots are in soil; leaves are in light. How do they share the materials they obtain?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文
预测水与糖的来源、路线和去向。把一株植物当作协作系统，而不是几棵小植物拼起来。

### English
Predict the sources, routes and destinations of water and sugars. Treat one plant as a cooperating system.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文
点根、茎和叶，查看各自能做什么。图中蓝色代表水，金色代表糖；箭头为功能示意，不是真实管道尺寸。

### English
Inspect roots, stem and leaves. Blue marks water and gold marks sugars; arrows show functions, not actual vessel sizes.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文
选择追踪水或追踪糖，逐步观察来源、经过茎和到达目标。糖的路线同时指向根和上方新芽。

### English
Choose water or sugars. Advance from the source through the stem to destinations. Sugar routes reach both roots and growing buds above.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文
**根在地下也需要能量。** 许多根吸收水与矿质营养，帮助固定植物。它们的细胞需要有机物供能，不能把泥土直接当成糖。

**叶片制造，其他部位也使用。** 许多植物的叶片是重要光合器官，制造的糖可运往根、果实或正在长大的芽。根供给的水与营养也支持叶片活动，所以两者相互依赖。

**茎是连接的一部分。** 茎不仅撑住叶片，还含运输组织。追踪水时，本模型展示根→茎→叶；追踪糖时，叶片→茎→根或新芽。路线不同，不该背成“所有东西只向上”或“糖永远向下”。

**生活中能看见一部分，推测另一部分。** 窗台植物的茎、叶可能直接可见，盆里的根不一定能看见。画图时给看不见的根加虚线和“推测”标记，比拔出植物求证更合适。

### English
**Roots need energy too.** Many roots take up water and mineral nutrients and anchor plants. Their cells need organic substances for energy; soil is not directly consumed as sugar.

**Leaves produce, other parts use.** Leaves are major photosynthetic organs in many plants. Sugars can travel to roots, fruits and growing buds. Water and nutrients supplied by roots support leaves, creating interdependence.

**Stems help connect the system.** Stems support leaves and contain transport tissues. This model traces water from roots through stems to leaves; sugars move from a leaf towards roots or buds. These routes should not become universal rules that everything moves up or sugars always move down.

**Observe some parts and infer others.** A windowsill plant’s stems and leaves may be visible while its roots are hidden. Marking inferred roots with dashed lines is useful; pulling the plant out is unnecessary.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文
“茎只是架子。”若只有支撑没有运输，根与叶得到的物质就无法按这些路线交换。“所有植物的叶都负责光合作用”也太绝对；仙人掌的绿色茎是后续反例。

### English
“Stems are only scaffolding.” Support alone cannot explain transport between roots and leaves. Nor do all plants rely on broad leaves for photosynthesis; green cactus stems provide a later counterexample.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文
后续L042会认识木质部xylem与韧皮部phloem：水与矿质营养主要由木质部运输，糖等由韧皮部从来源运向需求部位。细胞间还有更复杂的交换；这里不模拟推动水上升的压力或运输速率。

### English
L042 introduces xylem and phloem: xylem mainly carries water and minerals, while phloem moves sugars and other substances from sources to sinks. This introductory model omits transport pressures, rates and detailed cell exchanges.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文
一个新芽在叶片上方，它是否只能等自己长出叶子后才得到糖？结合来源与需求解释，不能只看上下方向。

### English
Must a bud above a leaf wait until it grows its own leaves to receive sugars? Use sources and destinations, not direction alone.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文
根root；茎stem；叶leaf；运输transport。沿一条箭头，用一句话说明来源、路线和作用。

### English
Use root, stem, leaf and transport to describe a source, route and function along one arrow.

## Check / 理解检查

### 中文
解释水路线、糖供根、方向误区和stem的含义，每题都有回看提示。

### English
Explain water routes, sugar supply to roots, directional misconceptions and the term stem; each question includes a revisit cue.

## Mission / 放到生活里

### 中文
画一株熟悉植物，蓝箭头追踪水，金箭头追踪糖。不见的部分注明推测；无需拔根或切茎。

### English
Draw a familiar plant with blue water arrows and gold sugar arrows. Label unseen structures as inferences; do not uproot or cut it.
