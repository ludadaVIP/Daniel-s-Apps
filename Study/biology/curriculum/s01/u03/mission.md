# U03 · 窗台植物的双向路线图 / A windowsill plant’s two-way route map

## 中文

选择已有盆栽、熟悉植物或照片，不必买材料。观察叶、茎和环境，用纸画图：蓝箭头表示水由根经茎到叶，金箭头表示光合部位制造的糖到根或新芽。看不到根就用虚线并写“推测”，不要拔根。

在发现手帐记录：

1. **主张**：说明一条物质路线及其作用。
2. **证据**：直接观察到的结构是什么？哪些是借助课程推测的内部过程？
3. **推理**：这些器官为什么相互依赖？静态外观能否证明内部运输？还需要怎样的证据？

可选延伸：找一处叶形与环境的联系，使用“可能帮助……，但还需……”句式。无需触摸刺、尝未知果实，或改变真实植物的照料。

## English

Choose an existing plant or photo. Observe leaves, stems and surroundings. Draw blue arrows for water from roots through stems to leaves and gold arrows for sugars from photosynthetic parts to roots or buds. Use dashed lines for unseen roots, labelled “inference”; do not uproot the plant.

Record in the notebook:

1. **Claim**: describe one material route and its function.
2. **Evidence**: which structures are directly visible, and which internal processes are inferred using the lesson?
3. **Reasoning**: why do the organs depend on one another? Can a static appearance establish internal transport, and what further evidence would help?

Optional extension: connect a leaf shape to its environment, using “It may help …, but further evidence …”. No touching spines, tasting unknown fruits or altered care is needed.
