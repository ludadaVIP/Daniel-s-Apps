## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文
暗柜里的薄荷有水、有土，为什么还是长不好？它的“食物”究竟从哪里来？

### English
A watered mint plant sits in a dark cupboard. Why might it struggle, and where does its food come from?

### English
Predict what a plant needs, keeping soil and sugars as distinct ideas.

### English
Follow three inputs: water, carbon dioxide from air and light energy. Locate sugars made and oxygen released.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文
**植物没有把土当午饭。** 根吸收水和矿质营养；绿色的光合部位利用光能，用水和二氧化碳等原料制造糖等有机物。糖中有来自原料的原子，并储存化学能。土能支持植物、提供水与营养，却不是一袋现成的糖。

**糖能去两个方向。** 一部分用来建造身体或储存；另一部分供细胞呼吸释放可用能量，支持细胞活动。光合作用与细胞呼吸可以同时发生，并非只能轮流。

**夜里也要生活。** 本模型的无光条件不支持制造新糖；假设植物健康、有储存糖和氧气，仍可细胞呼吸。真实植物若长期缺光、缺水或其他条件不合适，会受到限制；一晚没有光不等于立刻死亡。

**盆栽照料要看条件。** 适宜光照与水量因种类而异。肥料提供矿质营养，不能代替光能，也不是越多越好。没有土也能水培，但水、营养与根部氧气等条件仍需满足。

### English
**Soil is not lunch.** Roots take up water and mineral nutrients. Photosynthetic parts use light energy and materials including water and carbon dioxide to make sugars and other organic substances. Sugars contain atoms from the starting materials and store chemical energy. Soil can provide support, water and nutrients, but is not a bag of ready-made sugars.

**Sugars have several uses.** They can contribute to growth or storage, and cellular respiration releases usable energy from organic substances. Photosynthesis and respiration can occur together; they are not processes that must take turns.

**Life continues at night.** This dark model does not make new sugars. Given a healthy plant, stored sugars and oxygen, respiration can continue. Prolonged unsuitable conditions constrain real plants; one dark night does not mean instant death.

**Care depends on conditions.** Suitable light and water vary across plants. Fertiliser supplies mineral nutrients, cannot replace light energy and is not always beneficial in larger quantities. Hydroponic plants need no soil, but still need suitable water, nutrients and root oxygen.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文
“植物只有白天呼吸，夜里休息。”植物细胞在有合适原料和条件时，昼夜都可细胞呼吸。另一个误区是用更多肥料解决缺光，肥料不能提供光能。

### English
“Plants respire only in daylight and rest at night.” Plant cells can respire day and night given suitable materials and conditions. More fertiliser also cannot replace missing light energy.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文
光合作用涉及许多步骤，这里只建立物质与能量的入门关系。模型把条件简化为有/无，不模拟速率、萎蔫、营养缺乏或死亡；不能拿来制定真实植物的浇水量。后续L034、L083会深入能量与碳的路线。

### English
Photosynthesis involves many steps; here we establish the roles of matter and energy. Binary model conditions do not simulate rates, wilting, nutrient deficiency or death, and cannot prescribe watering amounts. L034 and L083 revisit energy and carbon routes.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文
一株暗处的盆栽长得较差：先列可能原因，再设计只改变光照的比较。为什么一次观察不足以证明唯一原因？

### English
A darker pot plant grows poorly. List possible causes and design a comparison varying light alone. Why is one observation insufficient to establish a unique cause?

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

### English
Use photosynthesis, carbon dioxide, mineral nutrients and cellular respiration to explain inputs and outputs.

## Mission / 放到生活里

### 中文

### English
Record a familiar plant’s light, water and air conditions and explain one care decision. No deliberate harm or purchase is needed.
