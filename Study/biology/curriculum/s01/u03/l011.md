## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文
一只豌豆荚既不甜也不像苹果，为什么也能叫果实？里面的豌豆又是什么？

### English
A pea pod is not sweet and looks unlike an apple. Why is it a fruit, and what are the peas inside?

### English
Predict the connections among pollen, fertilisation, fruits and seeds without substituting culinary categories for botanical structures.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文
**花粉不是微型种子。** 花粉把雄性生殖细胞的形成和运输过程联系起来。传粉是花粉到达柱头，之后适合的花粉可萌发花粉管，帮助生殖细胞到达胚珠并完成受精。传粉与受精并非同一件事。

**豌豆荚是一份发育证据。** 成功受精并继续发育后，胚珠形成种子，子房通常发育成果实。豌豆荚的外壁属于果实，里面的豌豆是种子。并非每朵花都成功结实，也有其他繁殖方式；模型只追踪一种开花植物的典型路线。

**传播是另一段旅行。** 果实和种子可以借风、水、动物或自身结构传播。蒲公英被叫作“飞着的种子”的部分，准确地说是带着种子的小果实；绒毛帮助风带走它。带钩的果实可附在动物皮毛上，再在别处脱落。

**换地方不等于成功长大。** 传播后的种子仍要面对适宜的萌发条件、空间和资源。图上的旅行路线不预测距离，也不保证新植物长成。

### English
**Pollen is not a miniature seed.** It is involved in producing and transporting male reproductive cells. Pollination is pollen reaching a stigma. Suitable pollen can grow a tube, allowing reproductive cells to reach an ovule and complete fertilisation. These are different events.

**A pea pod records development.** Following successful fertilisation and development, ovules form seeds and the ovary usually develops into a fruit. The pod wall belongs to the fruit and the peas inside are seeds. Not every flower sets fruit, and plants have other reproductive routes; this model follows one typical flowering-plant pathway.

**Dispersal is another journey.** Wind, water, animals and plant structures can move fruits and seeds. A dandelion’s familiar flying unit is a small seed-containing fruit, with hairs assisting wind transport. Hooked fruits can attach to fur and later detach.

**Arrival does not guarantee growth.** Dispersed seeds still need suitable germination conditions, space and resources. Animated routes do not predict distance or successful establishment.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文
“花粉落到柱头，就已经变成种子。”传粉之后还需受精与发育。“不甜就不是果实”也不对：豌豆荚就是反例。

### English
“Pollen lands on a stigma and immediately becomes a seed.” Fertilisation and development must follow. A pea pod also refutes the idea that only sweet structures are fruits.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文
开花植物存在双受精等更复杂过程，留到后续L044及更高阶段。本图画三枚胚珠，只追踪一条代表性花粉管；果荚阶段假设三枚均成功发育，画三颗种子只是示意，不是每朵花的固定产量。

### English
Flowering plants have additional processes such as double fertilisation, revisited in L044 and later stages. Three ovules are shown, with one representative pollen tube for tracking. The pod assumes all three develop successfully; three illustrated peas are not a fixed yield per flower.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文
看到绒毛或钩子，提出传播方式的假说，再说明还需观察什么。只凭一个结构能否推断精确距离？

### English
Use hairs or hooks to suggest a dispersal mechanism and identify observations still needed. Can one structure establish an exact travel distance?

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文
传粉pollination；受精fertilisation；果实fruit；传播dispersal。用自己的故事区分花粉移动与种子传播。

### English
Use pollination, fertilisation, fruit and dispersal to distinguish pollen movement from seed dispersal.

## Mission / 放到生活里

### 中文
观察已有食材或照片，分清果实与种子；看窗外或照片中的绒毛果实，记录一个有待检验的传播猜想。不尝未知果实。

### English
Inspect existing foods or photos for fruit and seed structures. Record a testable dispersal suggestion from a hairy fruit seen outside or in a photo. Do not taste unknown fruits.
