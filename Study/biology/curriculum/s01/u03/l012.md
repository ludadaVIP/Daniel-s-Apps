## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文
仙人掌与睡莲的叶和茎差别很大。同一种形状能解决所有环境的难题吗？

### English
Cacti and water lilies have very different leaves and stems. Can one form solve every environmental challenge?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文
先猜结构为何与环境有关，留意“想变成”和“长期可遗传变化”并非一回事。

### English
Predict how structure relates to habitat, distinguishing desire from inherited changes across generations.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文
比较仙人掌刺叶与厚绿色茎，以及睡莲浮在水面的宽叶。颜色标记帮助解释，不代表每种植物的实际外观。

### English
Compare cactus spines and a thick green stem with broad floating water-lily leaves. Colours explain the model, not every species’ appearance.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文
选择干燥开阔地或水面环境，再选仙人掌或睡莲结构。播放结构→作用→帮助→限制四帧。换环境不会把植物变成另一种。

### English
Choose dry open land or a water-surface habitat, then select cactus or water-lily form. Advance through structure → function → benefit → limitation. Changing habitat does not transform species.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文
**把结构与问题连起来。** 干燥环境中，失去水分可能限制生活。许多仙人掌的叶退化成刺，减少暴露叶面积；绿色厚茎储水并可光合作用。刺还可能减少动物取食，不能把所有刺都解释成只为省水。

**水面是另一个问题。** 睡莲宽叶可在水面接受光，叶柄把叶与水下部分联系起来。浮叶适合它的生活方式，不代表所有宽叶都属于水生植物，也不表示它能无水生活。

**优势有条件。** 储水与减少叶面积在干旱条件下可能有益，但有刺不意味着零失水；适应干旱也不意味着根适合长期积水。宽叶增加接收光的机会，也增加暴露面积，实际失水还受气孔、外皮和环境影响。没有一种结构在所有地方都最好。

**植物没有预先设计。** 个体会生长和作生理反应，但仙人掌没有因为今天想省水就立即把所有叶变成刺。可遗传差异在许多代中与不同的生存、繁殖成功相联系，形成适应。这里先建立正确方向，之后再认识自然选择。

### English
**Connect structures to problems.** Water loss can constrain life in dry habitats. Many cacti have reduced spine-like leaves, limiting exposed leaf area, while thick green stems store water and can photosynthesise. Spines can also deter herbivores, so water conservation is not their only possible function.

**The water surface presents different conditions.** Broad floating water-lily leaves intercept light, and stalks connect them to submerged parts. This suits their way of life, without implying that every broad-leaved plant is aquatic or that lilies can live without water.

**Benefits depend on conditions.** Water storage and reduced leaves can help in drought. Spines do not eliminate water loss, and drought adaptation does not imply tolerance of persistent waterlogging. Broad leaves can intercept light but expose more surface; water loss also depends on stomata, coverings and conditions. No structure is best everywhere.

**Plants do not plan new organs.** Individuals grow and respond physiologically, but a cactus does not instantly transform all leaves because it wants to save water today. Inherited differences, linked to unequal survival and reproduction over generations, contribute to adaptation. Later lessons develop natural selection.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文
“环境一变，植物就有意识地长出需要的器官。”换环境可能触发生长或生理反应，却不会随意把一种植物变成另一种。另一个误区是“有刺就不需水”。

### English
“A changed habitat makes a plant consciously grow whatever organ it needs.” Environments can affect growth and physiology without instantly converting one species into another. Spines also do not remove the need for water.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文
自然选择不是“所有个体努力改变”。群体已有或新出现的可遗传差异，经过不同繁殖成功而改变分布。不是每个特征都能只用一个适应故事解释，比较结构仍需要演化、功能和环境证据；后续L095、L115会深入。

### English
Natural selection is not every individual trying to change. Existing or newly arising inherited variation can change in frequency through unequal reproductive success. Not every trait has a simple single-function story; evolutionary, functional and environmental evidence matter. L095 and L115 return to this.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文
看到针状叶，就能断定附近一定是沙漠吗？松树是反例。先记录现象，再提出有限的功能假说。

### English
Do needle-like leaves prove a desert habitat? Pine trees offer a counterexample. Record observations before suggesting a limited functional hypothesis.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文
栖息地habitat；适应adaptation；蒸腾transpiration；代价trade-off。使用“可能帮助…，但也受…限制”的解释句。

### English
Use habitat, adaptation, transpiration and trade-off in a sentence stating a possible benefit and a limitation.

## Check / 理解检查

### 中文
概念题比较刺叶、厚茎与积水限制；推理题纠正目的性演化解释；英语题识别trade-off。

### English
Concept questions examine spines, stems and waterlogging; reasoning corrects intentional evolution, and vocabulary identifies trade-off.

## Mission / 放到生活里

### 中文
不触摸刺，不改变植物生长环境。观察熟悉植物或照片，记录形状、环境和一个需要继续找证据的解释。

### English
Do not touch spines or alter habitats. Observe a familiar plant or photo, record form and conditions, and propose an explanation needing further evidence.
