## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文
为什么有时把图片放得更大，仍旧只得到一大团模糊？来做一次“更大”与“更清楚”的比较。

### English
Why does a larger picture sometimes remain a blurry blob? Compare “bigger” with “clearer”.

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文
猜一猜：高倍视野一定更大吗？两颗模糊合并的小点，单纯放大后会分开吗？

### English
Predict whether higher power shows a larger area, and whether enlarging merged dots can separate them.

### 中文

### English

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文
先用4×物镜定位模型标本，再换10×或40×。移动对焦滑块，让图像清晰；对比分辨率小实验中的“两个点”和“合并的点”。

### English
Locate the model specimen with the 4× objective, then try 10× or 40×. Adjust focus and compare separate dots with merged dots.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文
**放大是“看起来多大”。** 在本复合显微镜模型中，目镜为10×，物镜为4×、10×或40×。总放大倍数=目镜倍数×物镜倍数，所以分别是40×、100×、400×。它们是仪器的名义倍数，网页图片本身还可能被调整尺寸。

**清楚不只靠放大。** 分辨能力指能否把相近结构区分开；对焦则让目标平面的图像更清晰。原图已合并的两个点，不会因为单纯数字放大就恢复成两个真实细节。对焦失误也不能只靠不断加倍放大解决。

**高倍看得近，也看得少。** 在本模型保持其他光学条件相同时，提高物镜倍数使标本图像变大，却让视野包含更小的标本范围。因此先低倍定位、居中目标，再提高倍数更稳妥。真实学校仪器的使用由老师指导；不要让物镜碰到玻片。

### English
**Magnification describes apparent size.** This compound-microscope model uses a 10× eyepiece with 4×, 10× or 40× objectives. Multiplication gives nominal totals of 40×, 100× or 400×. The displayed web image may also be resized.

**Clarity requires more than enlargement.** Resolving ability separates nearby structures. Focus makes the target plane appear sharp. If two dots were merged in the original image, digital enlargement cannot reconstruct the missing separation. Increasing power also does not automatically fix poor focus.

**Higher power shows less area.** With other model conditions unchanged, a higher-power objective enlarges the specimen image but shows a smaller specimen area. Locate and centre a target at low power first. Follow a teacher’s instructions with real equipment, and avoid lens contact with the slide.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文
“高倍什么都更好。”如果目标还没定位、图像没有对焦，或原始细节无法分开，高倍只是把问题放大。不同显微镜有不同用途。

### English
“Higher power always means a better view.” Without locating, focusing or resolving the target, higher power may just enlarge the problem. Different microscopes serve different purposes.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文
在同一套光学条件下，视野直径与物镜倍数近似成反比。本模型假设4×物镜的视野直径为2mm，10×时为0.8mm，40×时为0.2mm。真实设备需要校准，不能把这些值当作所有显微镜的规格。

### English
For the same optical setup, field diameter is approximately inversely related to objective power. This model assumes 2mm at 4×, 0.8mm at 10× and 0.2mm at 40×. Real equipment needs calibration; these are not universal specifications.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文
如果目标在低倍视野边缘，换高倍后看不到，是细胞消失了，还是视野范围变了？用模型支持你的解释。

### English
If a target lies near the low-power field edge and disappears at high power, did the cell vanish or the viewing range change?

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文
放大倍数magnification；分辨率resolution；视野field of view；对焦focus。

### English
Magnification, resolution, field of view and focus describe different aspects of an image.

## Check / 理解检查

### 中文
计算一次总倍数，解释一次模糊照片的局限，并解决一个重新定位的问题。

### English
Calculate total power, explain a blurry photo’s limits and solve a relocation problem.

## Mission / 放到生活里

### 中文
拍一张包装小字的照片，再放大同一张图；记录字是否只是变大，以及哪些细节仍旧不可辨认。

### English
Photograph small print on a label, enlarge that same photo and record which details stay indistinguishable.
