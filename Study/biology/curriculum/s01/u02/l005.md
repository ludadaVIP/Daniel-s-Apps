## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文
鲸鱼巨大，是不是因为每个细胞巨大？试着换一种观察尺度：一株植物 → 一片叶子 → 表皮组织 → 一个细胞。

### English
Is a whale huge because every cell is huge? Change your observation scale: a plant → a leaf → epidermal tissue → a cell.

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文
先猜“大身体”与“细胞大小”是什么关系。不要因为图片占满屏幕，就认为它很大。

### English
Predict how body size relates to cell size. A picture filling the screen need not represent a large object.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文
图中由植物、叶片、组织、细胞逐步缩小观察对象。它们是组成关系，不是植物变成了一个细胞。

### English
The diagram links plant, leaf, tissue and cell. These are relationships between parts and wholes, not a plant turning into one cell.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文
切换叶片、组织和细胞视野。比较右下角的单位和比例尺。细胞模型宽50µm，组织视野宽500µm；数一数同样宽度里能放几个模型细胞。

### English
Switch between leaf, tissue and cell views. Compare units and scale bars. The model cell is 50µm wide and the tissue view is 500µm wide; compare how many model widths fit.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文
**先看整个，再看组成。** 你能看到一片叶子，却通常不能用裸眼分清构成它的大多数单个细胞。放大观察有助于发现新的结构层次，但镜头没有让真实细胞长大。

**从细胞到身体。** 许多细胞及相关物质组成组织；不同组织形成叶片这样的器官；器官在个体内协作。鲸鱼和人类都由大量细胞构成，体型差异不能简化为每个细胞按同样比例变大。细胞类型、大小、数量和组织方式都有差异。

**单位把两个世界连起来。** 1mm=1000µm，50µm=0.05mm。比例尺告诉你图中一段长度代表多少真实长度。把一张图片在手机上放大，比例尺应一起放大；屏幕尺寸变了，物体的真实尺寸没有变。

### English
**Begin with the whole, then inspect its parts.** A leaf is visible, but most individual cells within it are not distinguishable by eye. Magnified views reveal another level of structure without making real cells grow.

**From cells to bodies.** Cells and associated material form tissues; different tissues form organs such as leaves. Organs cooperate within an organism. Whales and humans contain many cells. A difference in body size is not a uniform enlargement of every cell: cell type, size, number and organisation all matter.

**Units connect the views.** 1mm=1,000µm, and 50µm=0.05mm. A scale bar relates a length in an image to a real length. If you resize an image, its scale bar should resize with it. Screen dimensions can change without changing the specimen.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文
“一张细胞图比鲸鱼图大，所以细胞更大。”不同图可能使用不同尺度，必须看单位与比例尺。模型的50µm也不表示所有细胞都一样大。

### English
“The cell picture is bigger than the whale picture, so the cell is bigger.” Images can use different scales. Check units and bars. The model’s 50µm does not describe every cell.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文
较大的细胞会遇到物质交换和内部运输的限制。后续L073会把这个问题与表面积、体积联系起来。此时先建立组成层次与尺度的观念；不要推断所有组织都是整齐的小方格。

### English
Larger cells encounter constraints on exchange and internal transport. L073 later connects this to surface area and volume. For now, build the ideas of organisation and scale; real tissues are not universally tidy grids.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文
两张图片同样宽，但比例尺分别表示50µm与500µm。哪些实际尺寸结论需要先换算单位？

### English
Two equally wide images have scale bars labelled 50µm and 500µm. Which real-size conclusions require checking units first?

## Mission / 放到生活里

### 中文
观察一片常见落叶：分别记录整个叶片、叶脉和边缘。提出一个需要显微镜才能继续调查的问题。

### English
Observe a familiar fallen leaf: record the whole leaf, veins and edges. Ask one question needing a microscope.
