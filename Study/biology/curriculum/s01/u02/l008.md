## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文
酵母和洋葱表皮都是一格格细胞。为什么其中一格可能是一个个体，另一格只是植物的一部分？

### English
Yeast and onion skin both have cells. Why can one cell be an organism while another is part of a plant?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文
先判断：六个酵母细胞在同一画面，是一个六细胞个体，还是可能有六个单细胞个体？

### English
Predict whether six yeast cells in one picture must form one six-celled organism.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文
左边是一个酵母个体，右边是洋葱表皮组织的一部分。它们都是教学示意，不是仅凭一张照片就能证明的全部生命活动。

### English
The left model represents a yeast organism; the right shows part of onion epidermal tissue. These teaching diagrams cannot alone establish all life functions.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文
比较一个酵母、多个酵母个体与植物合作。逐步查看酵母获取物质、释放可用能量、生长和出芽；植物模式追踪根、叶之间的水与糖。

### English
Compare one yeast organism, several yeast organisms and plant cooperation. Step through yeast uptake, energy release, growth and budding; trace water and sugars between plant parts.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文
**一个细胞也能是一个生命。** 面包酵母是单细胞真菌。一个细胞能获取所需物质、释放可用能量、生长，并在适合条件下产生后代。单细胞不意味着少了一半生命功能。

**看个体，而不是数画面。** 图中有很多细胞，可能代表多个单细胞个体，也可能是一个多细胞个体的一部分。只数格子不够，还要了解这些细胞之间的组织关系和生命活动。酵母与细菌都可为单细胞，但不是同一类生物。

**多细胞带来分工，也带来依赖。** 一株植物的根吸收水，叶片制造糖，其他组织负责运输；它们协作维持整个个体。洋葱表皮的一格细胞仍是活细胞，但不能因此当成一整株独立洋葱。我们的图用简化箭头表示协作，不表示只有叶片需要水或只有根需要糖。

### English
**One cell can be a whole living thing.** Baking yeast is a unicellular fungus. One cell can obtain substances, release usable energy, grow and reproduce under suitable conditions. Unicellular life is not half a life.

**Count cells per organism, not per picture.** Many visible cells may represent several unicellular organisms or part of a multicellular organism. Cell count alone is insufficient: organisation and life functions matter. Yeast and bacteria can both be unicellular without being the same kind of organism.

**Many cells enable specialisation and dependence.** Roots take up water, leaves make sugars and transport tissues connect plant parts. Together they maintain one organism. An onion epidermal cell is living but is not thereby an entire independent onion plant. Simplified arrows illustrate cooperation, not exclusive needs: water and sugars are needed beyond only leaves or roots.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文
“小就是单细胞，大就是多细胞。”“许多酵母细胞挤在一起就是一个多细胞个体。”大小或数量不能替代对个体结构与功能的调查。

### English
“Small means unicellular; large means multicellular.” “A crowd of yeast cells is one multicellular organism.” Size and count do not replace investigation of organisation and function.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文
自然界有群体、菌落和更复杂的协作，不能用“一起出现”作唯一分类标准。有些植物细胞在适当的培养条件下可以形成新植株，但不是每格细胞都在日常条件下独立生活。本课不进行培养实验。

### English
Nature includes groups, colonies and more complex cooperation. Appearing together is not a classification rule. Some plant cells can regenerate plants under suitable culture conditions, but not every cell lives independently in ordinary conditions. No culturing is part of this lesson.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文
一张酵母群体图与一张植物组织图都有多格细胞。你需要补充哪些功能和组成证据，才能讨论它们是不是一个多细胞个体？

### English
Yeast-group and plant-tissue diagrams both contain multiple cells. What functional and organisational evidence would help identify a multicellular organism?

### English
Unicellular, multicellular, division of labour and interdependence connect structure to function.
