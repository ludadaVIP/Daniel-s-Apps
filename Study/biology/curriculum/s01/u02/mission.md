# U02 · 厨房里的细胞侦探 / Kitchen cell detective

## 中文

回看 L008 的酵母与洋葱表皮示意图，用自己的话写三句话：

1. **主张**：一格酵母与一格洋葱表皮细胞，哪一个可以代表完整个体？
2. **证据**：图上能看见什么？例如边界、核、相邻的细胞、芽体。把实际观察和课程提供的信息分开。
3. **推理**：一个酵母细胞可完成自身生命活动；洋葱表皮细胞是植物的一部分，依赖其他组织和器官的协作。静态图能支持哪些比较？它不能单独证明哪些功能？

可看看厨房里洋葱的外形和面包酵母包装。包装与裸眼外形不能直接显示细胞结构；无需购买显微镜，也无需培养微生物。知道证据的限度，也是发现。

## English

Revisit the L008 yeast and onion epidermis diagrams. Write three sentences in your own words:

1. **Claim**: which single cell can represent a whole organism?
2. **Evidence**: what is visible—boundaries, nuclei, neighbouring cells or a bud? Separate visible observations from information supplied by the lesson.
3. **Reasoning**: a yeast cell can perform its own life processes; an onion epidermal cell belongs to a plant and depends on cooperating tissues and organs. Which comparisons do the static diagrams support, and which functions can they not establish by themselves?

Look at a kitchen onion and a labelled baking-yeast packet if available. Their appearance and labels do not directly reveal cell structure. No microscope purchase or microbial culture is needed. Recognising the limits of evidence is a discovery too.
