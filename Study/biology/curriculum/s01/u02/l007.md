## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文
洋葱表皮像一排排小房间。谁给每个房间划出边界？物质又怎样到达内部？

### English
Onion skin resembles tiny rooms. What defines each boundary, and how do substances reach the inside?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文
猜一猜：细胞壁和细胞膜是不是同一层？没有核的细菌，是否就没有遗传信息？

### English
Predict whether wall and membrane are the same layer, and whether a bacterium without a nucleus lacks genetic information.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文
比较洋葱细胞与细菌细胞。点选图中的膜、细胞质、核或DNA、壁，联系位置与功能。色彩是人为标注，不代表真实观察颜色。

### English
Compare onion and bacterial models. Inspect membrane, cytoplasm, nucleus or DNA, and wall. Colours are explanatory, not real microscopy colours.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文
逐步追踪两个假设颗粒。一个在模型条件下可通过膜，另一个被挡在膜边界；观察“壁”与“膜”不是同一个位置。

### English
Trace two hypothetical particles. One can cross under the model conditions; the other stops at the membrane. Notice that wall and membrane occupy different positions.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文
**不是空盒子。** 活细胞内部有进行生命活动的物质和结构。细胞膜帮助界定内部与外部，并调节物质进出；细胞质包含许多参与反应和活动的成分。图解只画入门结构，省略了多数细胞器。

**植物有壁，也有膜。** 细胞壁位于膜外，支持和保护细胞；膜并没有因为有壁而消失。洋葱鳞茎表皮细胞通常不呈现绿色叶片那样的叶绿体，不能说每个植物细胞都必须有可见叶绿体。

**核与遗传信息也要分开。** 典型植物和动物细胞的细胞核包围大部分DNA；细菌有DNA，却没有膜包围的核。核图标不能代表所有细胞。真实细胞膜的交换涉及物质性质和膜蛋白等条件，本课只先建立边界与交换的概念。

### English
**More than an empty box.** A living cell contains substances and structures supporting life. Its membrane defines an inside and outside and regulates exchange. The cytoplasm contains components involved in reactions and activities. This introductory diagram omits most organelles.

**Plants have both wall and membrane.** The wall lies outside the membrane and supports and protects the cell. A wall does not replace the membrane. Onion-bulb epidermal cells do not typically show the chloroplasts familiar from green leaves, so visible chloroplasts are not required in every plant cell.

**A nucleus is not the same as genetic information.** Typical plant and animal nuclei enclose most DNA. Bacteria have DNA without a membrane-bound nucleus. A nucleus icon is not a universal cell template. Real membrane transport depends on substance properties, proteins and other conditions; this lesson introduces boundary and exchange.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文
“没有核就不是细胞。”“壁就是膜。”“洋葱表皮的真实颜色一定像图中。”分别混淆了遗传信息、结构功能与图解表达。

### English
“No nucleus means no cell.” “Wall means membrane.” “Real onion cells must have the diagram’s colours.” These confuse genetic information, structural roles and illustration choices.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文
膜具有选择性，但不是有意识的门卫。颗粒A/B只是模型预设，不能推断某种真实分子必定能通过。后续扩散、渗透与主动运输课程会研究不同交换机制。

### English
A membrane is selective, not a conscious gatekeeper. Particles A and B have preset model behaviour; they do not establish how a real molecule travels. Later lessons examine diffusion, osmosis and active transport.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文
模型中一类颗粒没通过膜，是否说明细胞与外界完全隔绝？用需要获取物质的生命活动反驳这个推断。

### English
Does one blocked model particle mean a cell is completely isolated? Use its need for substances to challenge that inference.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文
细胞膜cell membrane；细胞质cytoplasm；细胞核nucleus；细胞壁cell wall。

### English
Cell membrane, cytoplasm, nucleus and cell wall label distinct structures.

## Check / 理解检查

### 中文
区分壁与膜、解释无核细菌、判断模型能支持什么结论，再识别一个术语。

### English
Distinguish wall and membrane, explain a bacterium without a nucleus, assess a model inference and identify a term.

## Mission / 放到生活里

### 中文
对照厨房洋葱表皮与模型。写一条图示帮助解释的发现，以及一条裸眼无法验证的事；需要切洋葱时由成人协助。

### English
Compare kitchen onion skin with the model. Record one helpful explanation and one fact invisible by eye. Ask an adult for help if cutting is needed.
