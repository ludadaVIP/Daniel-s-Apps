# S01 · 生命侦探野外笔记 / Life Detective field notebook

资源ID：S01-P01、S01-P01-D01。状态：家庭试学稿。

## 中文

把第一阶段学到的生命、结构、环境和证据连在一起。在熟悉、安全的地方记录5种生物；不知道种名，可用清楚的外观描述。每条写：名称或描述、观察证据（时间和来源）、栖息环境、至少一个疑问。最后中英文各写一句总结。

不方便外出时，使用APP内原创虚拟后院：草叶簇、白花植物、蜗牛样动物、有斑点的小甲虫、停枝的小鸟。标“课中虚拟场景”，不要把它写成亲眼见过的真实野外记录。静态图提供外观和位置线索，不证明物种名、实际食谱或种群变化。不能把同一种的5个个体当成5种。

例子只示范写法，不替你完成作品：

- 观察：“虚拟场景里，小甲虫有斑点背部和可见的腿，停在叶片上。”
- 环境：“植物叶面，旁边是草叶和土壤。”
- 问题：“它吃什么？还要哪些观察？”
- 不支持的断言：“只凭斑点证明它一定吃蚜虫。”

APP按输入保存草稿，可分次完成。写满5条与两句总结后保存作品。综合理解检查有5题，连接生命证据、细胞与个体、光合作用、食物箭头和结论范围；可回看并订正。4/5作为建议理解线，英语单独由自己或家人校对，不自动判语言成绩。24课、6单元任务、已保存作品和综合检查达线共同组成第一阶段探索完成。

评价时请实际阅读作品：证据是否来自对应来源，描述是否把现象与解释分开，五种是否重复，问题能否继续观察，双语总结是否讲清联系。自动的非空检查只确认结构，不代表科学内容已经审核。无需捕捉、喂食或改变生物。

## English

Connect life, structure, habitat and evidence from the first stage. Record five kinds of organisms in a safe familiar place. A clear appearance description is enough when a name is unknown. Include a name or description, observation evidence with time and source, habitat and at least one question for each. Finish with one summary sentence in Chinese and one in English.

A screen-only alternative uses the original fictional garden: a grass tuft, white-flowered plant, snail-like animal, spotted small beetle and perched bird. Label it as the lesson’s fictional scene. A static illustration offers appearance and position clues, not proof of species identity, diet or population change. Five individuals of one kind are not five kinds.

Example of method, not a completed notebook: “In the fictional scene, a spotted beetle has visible legs and rests on a leaf.” Its habitat is a plant leaf beside grass and soil. Ask “What does it eat, and what observations would help?” Spots alone do not establish an aphid diet.

Drafts save as you type and can be completed in several visits. Save the notebook after filling five records and both summaries. Five understanding questions connect life evidence, cells and organisms, photosynthesis, food-web arrows and conclusion scope. Revisit and revise; 4/5 is a suggested threshold. English is reviewed separately with family rather than automatically graded. Completing 24 lessons, six unit missions, a saved notebook and the understanding check completes first-stage exploration.

Read the work itself: do evidence and source match, are observation and interpretation distinct, are the five kinds distinct, can questions guide further observations, and do summaries explain a connection? Automatic non-empty checks only confirm structure, not scientific review. No catching, feeding or alteration of organisms is needed.
