## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

叶、蚜虫、瓢虫、毛虫和小鸟，怎样被取食关系连成一张网？

### English

How do feeding links connect leaves, aphids, ladybirds, caterpillars and a bird?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先猜箭头指向食物还是取食者。图例的约定会帮你检查。

### English

Predict whether arrows point to food or feeder. Use the legend to check.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

看植物→蚜虫→瓢虫的示例。点线索认识生产者、消费者和箭头；瓢虫以一种食蚜类型为例，不能推广为所有瓢虫的食谱。

### English

Study plant → aphid → ladybird. Select producer, consumer and arrow clues. This aphid-eating ladybird example does not describe every ladybird diet.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

从两条初始关系开始，逐张加入毛虫吃叶、鸟吃毛虫、鸟吃蚜虫的虚拟观察。先看文字版，播放时每步加入一条；暂停后也能单步操作，重播回到初始图。

### English

Start with two links and add virtual observations: caterpillar eats leaves, bird eats caterpillars, bird eats aphids. Play to add one per step, pause or advance manually; replay restores the starting graph.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**植物把光能带进这张网。** 这里的绿色植物进行光合作用，制造有机物，因此称为生产者。它们也会细胞呼吸，并非不使用自己的能量。

**取食把材料与能量传下去。** 蚜虫取食植物汁液，食叶毛虫吃叶，课中的食蚜瓢虫吃蚜虫；食虫小鸟可利用蚜虫与毛虫。箭头从食物指向取食者，例如蚜虫→瓢虫。它表示传递方向，不是追捕方向。

**一条链与一张网不同。** 植物→蚜虫→瓢虫是一条食物链。加入植物→毛虫→鸟，以及蚜虫→鸟，多条路线连接成食物网。真实动物食谱可能随种类、季节和生命阶段变化，这张网只列选定关系。

**箭头需要证据，也有局限。** 靠近不等于取食。课程卡片使用虚拟取食观察；真实调查要找可靠食性资料或直接观察。箭头不告诉我们取食频率、种群数量或传递比例；并非食物中全部能量都到下一级。分解关系和更多物种暂未画入。

### English

**Plants bring light energy into this web.** These green plants photosynthesise and make organic substances, so they are producers. They also respire and use energy themselves.

**Feeding transfers materials and energy.** Aphids feed on plant sap, leaf-eating caterpillars eat leaves, and our aphid-eating ladybirds eat aphids. The insect-eating bird can feed on aphids and caterpillars. Arrows run from food to feeder, such as aphid → ladybird, rather than following pursuit.

**A chain differs from a web.** Plant → aphid → ladybird is one chain. Plant → caterpillar → bird and aphid → bird connect routes into a web. Real diets vary with species, season and life stage; these are selected links.

**Links need evidence and have limits.** Proximity does not establish feeding. Course cards specify virtual observations; real investigations need reliable diet information or direct observations. Arrows do not show feeding frequency, population size or transfer proportion, and not all energy reaches the next level. Decomposition and additional species are omitted here.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“箭头表示捕食者扑向食物。”本图用食物→取食者。“每种瓢虫都吃蚜虫”也不成立，有些吃真菌或植物。

### English

Arrows here mean food → feeder, not pursuit. Some ladybirds feed on fungi or plants, so the aphid diet is not universal.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

能量为什么不能全部传到下一层？呼吸与活动会使部分能量散为热，另有未取食、未消化或进入分解路线的部分。L049、L063以后会研究更多证据，不在这里硬套固定百分比。

### English

Respiration and activity disperse energy as heat; material may also remain uneaten, undigested or enter decomposition pathways. L049 and L063 develop this without imposing a fixed percentage here.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

如果一个动物既吃果实又吃昆虫，它的图里会只有一条进来的箭头吗？尝试画出多条来源，注明取食证据。

### English

Would an animal eating fruit and insects have only one incoming arrow? Sketch multiple sources and identify feeding evidence.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

生产者producer；消费者consumer；食物链food chain；食物网food web。Food → feeder 用来说明箭头。

### English

Use producer, consumer, food chain and food web. Food → feeder explains the arrow convention.

## Check / 理解检查

### 中文

正确读箭头，区分网与链，评价“在旁边”是否足以证明取食。

### English

Read arrows, distinguish webs from chains and assess whether proximity proves feeding.

## Mission / 放到生活里

### 中文

用虚拟证据卡画图，不需捕捉、饲养或喂食野生动物。真实关系不知道时写“待查”，不要为了补满图而猜。

### English

Draw from virtual evidence cards without catching, keeping or feeding wildlife. Mark unknown real links as needing evidence rather than guessing to fill the graph.
