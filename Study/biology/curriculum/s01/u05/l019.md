## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**分解者也要生活。** 部分真菌与细菌从落叶等有机残体取得物质与能量。它们通过酶等参与有机物分解，并吸收可利用的物质。不是每种真菌与细菌都在做同样工作。

**碎叶不等于全部分解。** 踩踏、风和取食碎屑的动物也可使叶子变碎，增加微生物能作用的表面；仅凭碎片外观，不能鉴定分解者或测出速度。

**物质可以换形式再利用。** 分解、微生物利用与后续释放等过程，使叶中一些元素进入土壤中的无机营养形式，植物可由根吸收。模型跟踪一种矿质元素，不是叶的全部成分。碳也有回到二氧化碳等其他路线；植物制糖的碳主要来自空气中的二氧化碳。

**能量的故事不同。** 分解者用有机物支持细胞呼吸与生命活动；部分能量转化、最终散为环境中的热，并非凭空消失。矿质元素能在生态系统中循环，但散开的热不会原路回到同一片叶的化学能中。植物持续制造有机物仍需要新的光能输入。

### English

**Decomposers live too.** Some fungi and bacteria obtain matter and energy from organic remains such as litter. Enzymes support breakdown and organisms absorb usable substances. Not every fungus or bacterium does the same job.

**Fragments are not complete decomposition.** Trampling, wind and detritus-feeding animals can fragment leaves and expose more surface. Appearance alone cannot identify decomposers or establish a rate.

**Matter changes form and can be reused.** Decomposition, microbial use and subsequent release can return some elements to inorganic nutrient forms in soil for root uptake. The model tracks one mineral element, not the whole leaf. Carbon has other routes, including carbon dioxide; carbon in new plant sugars mainly comes from atmospheric carbon dioxide.

**Energy follows a different story.** Organic material supports decomposer respiration and activities. Some energy transforms and eventually disperses as environmental heat rather than vanishing. Mineral elements cycle, but dispersed heat does not return along the same route as chemical energy in one leaf. Continued plant production needs new light-energy input.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“落叶直接变成植物的糖。”矿质营养不是现成糖，植物制糖仍需光、水和二氧化碳。“能量循环回植物”也把物质与能量混淆了。

### English

Litter does not directly become ready-made plant sugar: photosynthesis needs light, water and carbon dioxide. Energy returning in the same cycle also confuses energy with matter.

## Mission / 放到生活里

### 中文

只观察现有落叶或照片，不触碰霉菌，不培养微生物，不闻腐烂材料。把可见变化与解释分开。

### English

Observe existing litter or photos without touching mould, culturing microbes or smelling decaying material. Separate visible changes from explanations.
