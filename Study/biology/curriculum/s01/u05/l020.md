## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

一种食蚜瓢虫不见了，没有直接吃它们的植物为什么也可能受影响？

### English

Why might plants be affected when an aphid-eating ladybird species disappears locally?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先预测可能的关系，再检查“必然”两个字是否需要更多证据。

### English

Predict links, then check whether a guaranteed outcome requires more evidence.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

点图中线索，区分蚜虫→瓢虫的直接联系，与经蚜虫影响植物的间接路线。箭头是能量传递约定，推理影响时可沿相连关系追踪。

### English

Select clues to distinguish aphid → ladybird feeding from effects transmitted through aphids to plants. Energy arrows and causal reasoning are different uses of the connections.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

切换瓢虫在/不在，再切换鸟是否取食蚜虫。逐步查看初始网→直接变化→可能连锁→证据与局限。节点大小不是数量，图上没有真实种群预测值。

### English

Toggle ladybird presence and the bird–aphid link. Follow starting web → direct change → possible cascade → evidence and limits. Node size is not abundance; no real population values are predicted.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**先确认哪条关系改变。** 课中选定的一种食蚜瓢虫在观察范围内消失，蚜虫就失去这一种捕食者。这里讨论本地范围，不等于这个物种已从世界灭绝。

**直接变化可能向外传递。** 捕食压力减少，蚜虫在其他条件允许时可能增多；更多蚜虫取食植物汁液，可能增加植物承受的取食影响。这是经蚜虫传递的间接影响，不需要瓢虫直接吃植物。

**食物网还有其他路线。** 若鸟也吃蚜虫，另一条捕食联系仍在。但鸟是否多吃、增加多少，以及能否缓冲变化，需要鸟的数量、取食选择与环境证据。保留箭头不等于保留完全相同的取食量。

**可能不等于必然。** 蚜虫的繁殖、天气、植物资源、其他捕食者和病原体等，都可能影响结果。这张五节点图省略了许多因素。模型用来提出解释和需要的证据，不计算后院未来，也不指导移除或引入生物。

### English

**Identify the changed link first.** Local disappearance of the selected aphid-eating ladybird removes one predator of aphids in the study area. Local absence does not mean worldwide extinction.

**Direct changes may spread.** Reduced predation may allow aphids to increase under suitable conditions. More aphids feeding on plant sap may increase feeding impacts on plants. This is an indirect effect through aphids, without ladybirds eating plants.

**Other routes remain in a web.** If birds also eat aphids, another predation link remains. Whether birds eat more and buffer change depends on their numbers, feeding choices and environment. Retaining an arrow does not guarantee the same feeding amount.

**Possible is not inevitable.** Aphid reproduction, weather, plant resources, other predators and pathogens can affect the result. The five-node graph omits many factors. It helps generate explanations and evidence needs rather than calculating the garden’s future or recommending species removal or introduction.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“不直接取食，就绝无影响”忽略间接关系。“有另一种捕食者就保证没变化”又把一条联系当成固定取食量。

### English

No direct feeding does not mean no indirect effect. Another predator does not guarantee unchanged conditions: a link is not a fixed feeding quantity.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

种群改变会随时间、季节和密度产生反馈，真实关系也有竞争、疾病和非生物条件。L062、L068和L094将进一步研究生态稳定、扰动与自然选择。

### English

Population feedback depends on time, season and density, alongside competition, disease and abiotic conditions. L062, L068 and L094 extend stability, disturbance and selection.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

花园喷洒后既少了蚜虫又少了其他昆虫，就能仅凭这一现象断定所有变化的原因吗？记录时间、处理和对照证据；这里只提出调查问题，不进行喷洒。

### English

If aphids and other insects decline after a garden treatment, does that observation establish every cause? Record timing, treatment and comparisons. This is a question, not a spraying activity.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

种群population；捕食predation；间接影响indirect effect；不确定性uncertainty。句式：It might … because …; we still need … .

### English

Use population, predation, indirect effect and uncertainty. Try: It might … because …; we still need … .

## Check / 理解检查

### 中文

找直接改变的联系，分析另一种捕食者，修正“瓢虫不吃植物就无影响”的说法。

### English

Identify the direct change, analyse another predator and revise the claim that no plant feeding means no plant impact.

## Mission / 放到生活里

### 中文

用虚拟后院完成一张网，或只观察熟悉安全的真实环境。未知关系标待查，不捕捉、喂食、移除或引入生物，不用药剂测试。

### English

Use the virtual garden or observe a safe familiar real place. Mark unknown links. Do not catch, feed, remove, introduce or chemically test organisms.
