# U05 后院食物网 / Garden food web

## 中文

选课程虚拟后院或一个安全熟悉的真实地方。至少画植物、一个植食者和一个捕食者；如果证据支持，再加入第二条相连的取食路线。箭头规定为食物→取食者，不是追捕方向。

写下主张、证据和推理：一种生物减少，为什么可能影响另一种？把直接观察、课程虚拟证据卡和可靠食性资料分开。指出未知关系，写一项需要继续调查的证据。图不需要“补满”，不需更改真实环境；不捕捉、喂食、移除或引入生物。

## English

Choose the lesson’s virtual garden or a safe familiar real place. Draw at least a plant, a plant-feeding animal and a predator; add a second connected feeding route when evidence supports it. Arrows mean food → feeder, not pursuit.

Record a claim, evidence and reasoning: how might fewer of one organism affect another? Separate direct observations, virtual evidence cards and reliable diet information. Mark unknown links and one further evidence need. Do not fill gaps by guessing or change real habitats; no catching, feeding, removal or introduction.
