## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

隔几步的树荫与铺路，为什么可能给蜗牛不同的生活条件？

### English

Why might shade and paving a few steps apart offer different conditions to a snail?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先猜小角落是否也会有不同条件。不要把“一个地方对某种生物合适”当成“对所有生物都合适”。

### English

Predict whether small corners differ. Suitability for one organism does not mean suitability for all.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

点图下的水分、光照与生物线索，连接看见的地方和可能影响生活的条件。土壤含有生物，不能把整团土看成绝无生命。

### English

Select moisture, light and living clues. Connect a visible place to living conditions. Soil contains organisms; it is not entirely lifeless.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

选择树荫落叶处或阳光铺路，查看地点→物理条件→资源与生物→有限解释四帧。这是虚拟情境，不是你后院的测量。

### English

Choose a shaded leaf patch or sunny paving. Follow site → physical conditions → resources and organisms → limited explanation. This is a virtual scenario, not a measurement of your garden.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**栖息地不只是门牌。** 生物生活的地方有水分、温度、光照，也有可利用的资源和遮蔽。巢或洞可能是栖息地的一部分，但觅食范围也重要。

**生物因素与非生物因素共同作用。** 植物、动物、真菌与细菌参与生物关系；水分、光和温度属于非生物条件。植物的叶能遮荫，改变周围条件，说明两类影响相互联系。

**一个后院也有差别。** 在课中的虚拟情境里，阴影中的落叶处较湿、有遮蔽；铺路暴露于阳光、较干、植物资源较少。这可以支持关于常见陆生蜗牛的栖息条件假说，却不证明真实后院一定如此，也不适用于所有物种。

**生态系统关注联系。** 当我们把这个范围内的生物、非生物环境及相互作用一起研究，就在使用生态系统的视角。先记录观察，再解释；一张图、一次出现或没看见，都不能说明全部关系。

### English

**A habitat is more than an address.** A living place offers moisture, temperature, light, resources and cover. A nest or burrow may be part of it, but feeding areas matter too.

**Living and nonliving factors interact.** Plants, animals, fungi and bacteria form living relationships. Moisture, light and temperature are nonliving conditions. Leaves can shade a site and change nearby conditions, connecting both kinds of influence.

**One garden has differences.** Our virtual shaded leaf patch is damp and sheltered; the sunny paving is exposed, drier and has fewer plant resources. This supports a habitat hypothesis for a common land-snail example, without establishing conditions in your garden or suitability for every species.

**An ecosystem perspective studies connections.** Consider organisms, nonliving surroundings and their interactions together. Record observations before explanations: one diagram, sighting or failure to see something cannot reveal every relationship.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“栖息地只有一个地名”忽略了资源与条件。“没看见生物就没有生物”也忽略了时间、隐蔽和观察方式。

### English

A place name alone omits resources and conditions. Failure to see organisms also depends on timing, concealment and observation methods.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

一个花盆并非封闭世界：水、空气、光和生物都能跨过观察范围。生态系统边界常是研究者划定的观察边界，不一定是实体围墙。后续L045–L048会深入。

### English

A pot is not closed: water, air, light and organisms cross its observation boundary. Ecosystem boundaries can be study choices rather than physical walls. L045–L048 extend this.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

同一条路上的树下与空旷处适合相同的植物吗？先比较光、水和其他条件，不凭地名作结论。

### English

Would the same plant suit shade and an open spot on one street? Compare light, water and other conditions before concluding.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

栖息地habitat；生物因素biotic factor；非生物因素abiotic factor；生态系统ecosystem。

### English

Use habitat, biotic factor, abiotic factor and ecosystem.

## Check / 理解检查

### 中文

辨别光照与生物关系，解释同一后院的小差别，并评价一次蜗牛观察的局限。

### English

Distinguish light from living interactions, explain small-scale differences and evaluate a single snail sighting.

## Mission / 放到生活里

### 中文

从窗边或安全熟悉的地方观察两个角落。记录时间、可见差异与疑问；不要翻石头、扰动落叶、搬动物或走进陌生地方。

### English

Observe two corners from a window or safe familiar spot. Record time, visible differences and a question without turning stones, disturbing leaves, moving animals or entering unfamiliar places.
