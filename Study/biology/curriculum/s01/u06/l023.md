## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

每组平均只有一个数，幼苗各自的故事在哪里？

### English

A group mean is one number. Where are the individual seedling stories?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

猜平均值是否代表每株，再读六条原始记录。所有数字是原创虚拟教学数据。

### English

Predict whether the mean represents every seedling, then read six raw records. All values are authored fictional teaching data.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

点原始值、平均、范围，查看图表和相同尺度。纵轴为7天茎高度增量（cm），从0到10；颜色不表示健康评分。

### English

Select Raw values, Mean and Scope. The vertical axis is stem-height gain over seven days in cm, from 0 to 10; colours do not indicate a health score.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

在“每株”与“组平均”之间切换。四帧依次看测量定义、原始差异、平均计算和结论边界。横轴是光照组别，不是时间。

### English

Switch between individual values and group means. Follow measurement definition, raw variation, mean calculation and scope. The horizontal axis shows light groups, not time.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**数字先要有来历。** 课中两组各3株虚拟幼苗，都从4 cm开始。第7天弱光组为10、11、12 cm，较强光组为7、8、9 cm；逐株减去初始值，得到6、7、8和3、4、5 cm。原始表与图使用同一份数据。

**图表要能读回数字。** 柱高表示高度增量，纵轴有0起点与cm单位，横轴标组别。较高的柱只是“此指标更大”，没有自动变成健康评分。两组使用相同刻度，避免把相同增量画成不同大小。

**平均不是每株。** 弱光组(6+7+8)÷3=7 cm；较强光组(3+4+5)÷3=4 cm。两组均值相差3 cm，但各组都有个体差异。保留六个原始数，别人才能核对计算、样本数和变异。

**图回答的范围有限。** 这张图描述这些虚拟幼苗这7天的茎高度变化，不是所有植物的规律，也未测糖产量或整体健康。没有采集的量不能靠换个标题“读出来”。下一课会加入不同类型的记录。

### English

**Give numbers a traceable origin.** Each fictional group has three seedlings starting at 4 cm. Day-7 heights are 10, 11 and 12 cm in weaker light, and 7, 8 and 9 cm in stronger light. Subtracting each starting value gives gains of 6, 7, 8 and 3, 4, 5 cm. The table and chart use the same records.

**A chart should let us recover values.** Bar heights show height gains on a common centimetre scale starting at zero. The horizontal axis identifies groups. A higher bar means a larger measured outcome, not an automatic health score.

**A mean is not every individual.** The means are (6+7+8)/3=7 cm and (3+4+5)/3=4 cm. Their difference is 3 cm, but seedlings vary within each group. Keeping all six raw values lets others check calculations, sample sizes and variation.

**Respect the graph’s scope.** It describes these fictional seedlings over seven days. It neither establishes a universal plant rule nor measures sugar production or overall health. Renaming a graph cannot create a measurement that was never collected.

## Misconception / 一个常见误区

### 中文

误区：“平均增加7 cm，就每株增加7 cm。”反例：6、7、8的平均为7，但只有中间一株的记录等于平均。不要为符合均值而删除不同的值。

### English

Misconception: “A mean gain of 7 cm means each plant gained 7 cm.” The values 6, 7 and 8 average to 7, but only one equals the mean. Do not delete variation to make values match.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

弱光组最大最小相差2 cm，较强光组也相差2 cm。相同差值不表示所有分布都相同。这里只有3株，没有进行统计显著性检验；不要把图上分开直接说成普遍因果证明。

### English

Each group’s maximum minus minimum is 2 cm. Equal ranges do not make all distributions identical. With three seedlings per group and no statistical test, visual separation is not universal causal proof.

## Apply / 把知识用起来

### 中文

画两组平均柱，标标题、7天、组别、cm和0起点。旁边保留个体增量，写一句图未测的东西。无需下载工具。

### English

Draw two group-mean bars labelled with title, seven days, groups, cm and zero baseline. Retain individual gains and name an unmeasured outcome. No extra tool is needed.

## Vocabulary / 小词典

### 中文

数据 data；平均数 mean；单位 unit；变异 variation。

### English

Data: recorded information; mean: sum divided by count; unit: measurement scale; variation: differences among records.

## Check / 检索练习

### 中文

检索平均数、纵轴含义、个体差异和mean词义。若算错，回看增量而不是总高度。

### English

Recall means, the vertical-axis meaning, individual variation and the word mean. If mistaken, revisit gains rather than total heights.

## Mission / 生活探究

### 中文

用同一份原始记录画图、核对平均，写“本图显示…但没有测…”。

### English

Chart the same raw records, check the means and write “This graph shows … but does not measure …”.
