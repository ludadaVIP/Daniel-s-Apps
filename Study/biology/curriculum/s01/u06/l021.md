## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

幼苗一周变高，叶色却更浅。我们能直接记录什么？又在解释什么？

### English

A seedling becomes taller but paler in one week. What can we record directly, and what are we interpreting?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

选择一个初始想法。预测不评分。先别把“量到高度”当成“量到了健康”。

### English

Choose an initial idea; predictions are unscored. Measuring height is not measuring health.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

点记录、解释、检验三张线索，看同一幼苗与尺子的示意。图不按比例；读数来自课中虚拟记录，不是图上像素。

### English

Select Record, Interpret and Test clues. The plant and ruler are schematic; readings come from fictional records rather than pixels.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

看A2幼苗第0天4 cm、第7天11 cm，再比较叶色记录。点选笔记，并判断它是观察还是解释。按播放或下一步回看线索；这不是真实生长速度模型。

### English

View A2 at 4 cm on day 0 and 11 cm on day 7, then compare leaf-colour records. Select notes and classify each as an observation or interpretation. Playback revisits clues; it is not a real growth-rate model.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**先写发生了什么。** 在虚拟笔记中，A2的茎高由4 cm变成11 cm。测量从土面到茎顶，用相同方法；还记录了日期和单位。这样的观察比“它长得不错”更容易让别人检查。

**再写可能为什么。** “低光可能促使茎伸长”把现象与可能原因连接，是需要检验的解释。一个有用的假说还要产生可以比较的预测：如果只改变光照，其他相关条件尽量一致，记录的高度增量是否不同？

**线索不等于唯一原因。** 叶片颜色浅是定性观察；照片的照明与拍摄条件会影响判断。水分、营养、品种和温度也可能影响状态。我们可以写“不确定”，列出还需核对的条件。

**解释能改，记录要诚实。** 新线索可能使旧解释变弱或变强。把记录和解释分开，别人才能看出哪一步还需要证据。科学探索会来回检查，不是一次走完就永远结束。

### English

**Record what happened first.** In the fictional notebook, A2 goes from 4 to 11 cm. Stem height is measured from soil surface to stem tip using the same method, with dates and units. This is easier to check than “It is doing well.”

**Then propose why.** “Low light may promote stem elongation” links the pattern to a possible cause. Testable ideas suggest comparisons: if light changes while relevant conditions stay similar, do height gains differ?

**A clue need not identify a unique cause.** Pale leaves are a qualitative observation. Lighting and photographing conditions influence colour judgement; water, nutrients, variety and temperature may also matter. Uncertainty can be recorded alongside what needs checking.

**Revise explanations honestly.** Further observations may strengthen or weaken an idea. Separating records from interpretations shows where evidence is still needed. Inquiry can loop back rather than following a rigid once-only recipe.

## Misconception / 一个常见误区

### 中文

误区：“观察只包括数字，解释只是瞎猜。”叶色和形状也能按明确方法记录；解释应结合已有知识并能接受检验。反例：两株同高，但叶色与茎粗不同，高度不能单独概括全部状态。

### English

Misconception: “Only numbers count as observations; interpretations are random guesses.” Colour and shape can be recorded systematically too. Interpretations use knowledge and remain testable. Two equally tall plants may have different leaf colours and stem thicknesses.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

工具扩大观察范围，但读数仍有误差。同一幼苗的弯曲、尺子倾斜、起点选择会影响高度。约定方法、保存原始记录，比事后把数改整齐更有用。

### English

Instruments extend observation but do not remove error. A curved stem, tilted ruler or changed starting point can affect readings. Agree on a method and retain raw records rather than tidying values afterwards.

## Apply / 把知识用起来

### 中文

对现有植物或课中图写两句：“我在…看见/量到…”与“可能…还需…”。观察可从照片完成，不需要改变植物照料。

### English

For an existing plant or lesson image, write “At … I observed/measured …” and “Possibly … we still need …”. A photograph is enough; no changed plant care is needed.

## Vocabulary / 小词典

### 中文

观察 observation；推断 inference；假说 hypothesis；预测 prediction。

### English

Observation: recorded information; inference: evidence-based interpretation; hypothesis: testable explanation; prediction: an expected observation under an idea.

## Check / 检索练习

### 中文

检索：哪句带测量方法？哪句需要检验？一张照片不能确定什么？hypothesis是什么意思？每题查看反馈，再回看相关线索。

### English

Recall: which statement gives a measurement method? Which needs testing? What can one photograph not establish? What does hypothesis mean? Read feedback and revisit clues.

## Mission / 生活探究

### 中文

留下一条带时间或来源的观察、一个可能解释和一个还需的证据。

### English

Record an observation with time or source, a possible explanation and one further piece of evidence needed.
