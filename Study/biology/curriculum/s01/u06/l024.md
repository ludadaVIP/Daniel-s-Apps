## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

弱光组长得更高。如果叶色更浅、茎更细，还能把“高”直接改成“健康”吗？

### English

The weaker-light group is taller. If its leaves are paler and stems thinner, can “taller” simply become “healthier”?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选择一个解释，不评分。接下来查看新线索，允许自己修改想法。

### English

Choose an unscored interpretation. Inspect new clues and allow your idea to change.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

点主张、证据、局限，连接数字、幼苗形态与适用范围。虚拟照片以原创图示代替，不能按像素自行量高。

### English

Select Claim, Evidence and Limits to connect numbers, plant form and scope. Original schematics stand in for fictional photographs; do not measure their pixels.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

点“加入叶色记录”，比较只看高度与同时看叶色时的解释。四帧依次看主张、数据、可能机制与下一步。新增记录不会改掉原高度数。

### English

Add leaf-colour records and compare interpretations with and without them. Follow claim, data, possible mechanism and next step. New records do not overwrite height values.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**让主张说清范围。** “本例7天内弱光组平均茎高度增量更大”与数据相符。“所有植物在弱光下都更健康”把样本、时间和指标全部扩大，超出了记录。

**相关证据不只一种。** 高度是定量记录；叶色与茎形态可作为按方法记录的定性线索。虚拟弱光组还有较浅叶色与较细长茎，说明高度不能单独代表所有状态。颜色需要一致拍摄条件，不能单凭照片确定原因。

**推理要有机制，也要可修正。** 部分幼苗在不足光照下会伸长、显得细长，种子储存的材料与能量能支持早期生长；茎变长不等于光合作用更多或长期表现更好。这里只给出一个可能机制，不靠新颜色记录宣布唯一原因。

**把局限变成下一步。** 每组3株、观察7天、虚拟教学数据，并未测全部健康指标。先定义想了解的状态，再考虑叶数、颜色、后续存活或其他合适指标；增加重复、核对控制和测量，保留不合预期的记录。

### English

**Scope the claim.** “In this example over seven days, weaker-light seedlings had greater mean stem-height gain” fits the data. “All plants are healthier in weaker light” extends the sample, time and outcome beyond the records.

**Use relevant evidence of different kinds.** Height is quantitative; colour and form can be systematic qualitative observations. Fictional weaker-light records also show paler leaves and more slender stems. Height therefore cannot represent every aspect of condition. Colour comparisons need consistent photographing conditions and cannot establish a cause alone.

**Reason with mechanisms that remain revisable.** Some seedlings elongate and become spindly in insufficient light. Stored seed resources support early growth; a longer stem does not establish greater photosynthesis or better long-term performance. This is a possible explanation, not a uniquely proven cause from colour.

**Turn limits into further work.** Three seedlings per group, seven days and fictional data do not answer every health question. Define the condition of interest before choosing leaf number, colour, later survival or other indicators. Add replicates, check controls and measurements, and retain unexpected records.

## Misconception / 一个常见误区

### 中文

误区：“承认局限就是承认全错。”反例：我们可以确认图中平均高度增量不同，同时承认没有测整体健康。好解释把已支持和未支持的部分分开。

### English

Misconception: “Admitting limits means everything is wrong.” We can describe different mean gains in the records while acknowledging that overall health was not measured. Good explanations separate supported and unsupported parts.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

若不同条件只是两个不同窗台而非受控试验，位置也可能混入温度等因素。不要把相关模式直接升级为单独光照的因果效应。研究结论需要与实际方法一起评价。

### English

If groups are simply on different windowsills rather than in a controlled test, location may also change temperature and other factors. Do not upgrade an association into an isolated light effect; judge conclusions alongside the actual method.

## Apply / 把知识用起来

### 中文

写一份三句小报告：主张有范围；引用相关数值或记录；解释机制并说出还需哪种证据。可以修正初始预测。

### English

Write a three-sentence report: a scoped claim, relevant values or records, and reasoning with further evidence needed. Revising an initial prediction is welcome.

## Vocabulary / 小词典

### 中文

主张 claim；证据 evidence；推理 reasoning；局限 limitation。

### English

Claim: a scoped judgement; evidence: relevant records; reasoning: why evidence matters; limitation: what remains unanswered.

## Check / 检索练习

### 中文

检索：哪句有范围？新叶色线索改变什么？下一步测什么？limitation对应哪个词？

### English

Recall a scoped statement, the effect of new leaf-colour clues, a further measurement and the meaning of limitation.
