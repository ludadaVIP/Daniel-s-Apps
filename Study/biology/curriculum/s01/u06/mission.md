# U06 · 光照与幼苗的公平比较 / A fair light–seedling comparison

## 中文

问题：两种光照强度与7天茎高度增量是否有关？只设计也可以，不要求实际让植物缺光。

1. 写预测，说明改变的是光照强度，不是同时改光、水和品种。
2. 测量：同一方法由土面至茎顶，以cm记录第0天和第7天；逐株计算末次减初始。
3. 重要控制：同品种、年龄与起始状态相近，相同花盆与土、照射时长、测量时间与方法；制定一致水分计划，并核对实际土壤水分和温度。
4. 每组用多株，随机分组，保留每株记录。没有“3株保证可靠”的规则；3只是课中小样本例子。
5. 写局限与下一步：窗台位置可能同时改变温度；高度不等于整体健康。还需哪些观察？

## English

Question: how do two light intensities relate to seven-day stem-height gain? A design-only plan is sufficient; no real light deprivation is required.

1. Predict an outcome and change light intensity without changing water and variety with it.
2. Measure soil surface to stem tip consistently in cm on day 0 and day 7; calculate final minus initial for each plant.
3. Keep important conditions similar: variety, age, starting condition, pots, soil, exposure duration, measurement timing and method. Use a consistent watering plan and check actual moisture and temperature.
4. Use several seedlings per group, assign randomly and retain individual records. Three is a small sample example, not a reliability guarantee.
5. State limits and further work: windowsill location may also change temperature; height is not overall health. What additional observations are needed?
