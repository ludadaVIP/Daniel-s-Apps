## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

两组幼苗不只光不同，水、品种与温度也不同。结果到底说明哪个因素？

### English

Two seedling groups differ in light, water, variety and temperature. Which factor do their results identify?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

猜想能否把差别全归给光，再操作方案。预测不计成绩。

### English

Predict whether the difference can be attributed wholly to light, then inspect the plan. Predictions are unscored.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

两组图标只展示方案，没有显示生长结果。点改变、测量、控制，明确本次问题的三个角色。

### English

The two-group schematic displays a plan, not growth results. Select Change, Measure and Control to distinguish the three roles in this question.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

点击水分方案、品种与温度卡，把不同条件修成尽量一致，再看“问题→混杂→控制→重复”四帧。随时可把一项改回，看看解释为何变难。

### English

Select watering plan, variety and temperature cards to make conditions similar. Follow question → confounding → control → replication. Change a card back to inspect why interpretation becomes harder.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**先把问题缩到能检验。** 本次问两种光照强度是否影响7天的茎高度增量。改变光照强度，照射时长尽量一致；测量第7天高度减第0天高度。不要把“健康”当成已经定义好的单一数字。

**其他因素别跟着组别一起换。** 若强光组还换了品种、提高温度并改变水分，结果有多种解释。修正这些混杂因素后，比较更容易解释，但仍要检查实际条件。相同浇水量不保证相同土壤水分，光和温度会影响蒸发，所以要监测相关条件。

**重复与随机分组有作用。** 用同品种、年龄和起始状态相近的多株幼苗，随机分到两组；花盆、土和测量时间尽量一致。个体并不完全相同，重复让我们看见变异，减少把单株偶然表现当作普遍规则。

**公平不等于保证结果。** 一个设计更好的试验仍可能有误差、样本太少或未想到的因素。模型中三项“已对齐”只说明修正了列出的混杂，不保证所有真实条件都完美。这里只设计，不要求让植物缺光。

### English

**Make the question testable.** This comparison asks how two light intensities relate to stem-height gain over seven days. Change light intensity while keeping exposure duration similar; measure day-7 height minus day-0 height. “Health” needs a clearer definition before measurement.

**Avoid changing other factors with the group.** Different varieties, temperatures and watering create alternative explanations. Aligning these conditions makes the comparison easier to interpret. Equal watering volume does not guarantee equal soil moisture: light and temperature can affect evaporation, so monitor relevant conditions.

**Replicate and assign groups randomly.** Use several seedlings of the same variety with similar ages and starting conditions. Keep pots, soil and measurement times comparable. Individuals still differ; repeated observations reveal variation and reduce reliance on one unusual plant.

**Fairer design does not guarantee certainty.** Error, small samples and overlooked factors remain possible. Matching the three listed cards repairs those specific confounders; it does not guarantee perfect real conditions. This is a planning exercise, with no real deprivation required.

## Misconception / 一个常见误区

### 中文

误区：“只改变一个因素，就一定证明原因。”反例：只各放一株，恰好一株原先受损，也会造成差别。控制重要因素、随机分组、重复与核对测量共同增加可信度。

### English

Misconception: “Changing one factor automatically proves a cause.” With only one seedling per group, an already damaged plant can distort the comparison. Controls, random assignment, replication and checking measurements work together.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

本课是简单的单因素比较。科学还使用调查、长期观察和多因素设计；复杂系统不能总靠一个简单试验回答。变量的角色取决于当前问题，不是某个物体永久的标签。

### English

This is a simple one-factor comparison. Science also uses surveys, long-term observations and multifactor designs. Variable roles depend on the question; they are not permanent labels attached to objects.

## Apply / 把知识用起来

### 中文

把“哪种照料最好？”改写成一个具体问题，并列出目标改变、测量指标、尽量一致的因素、重复与时间。可完全使用屏幕方案。

### English

Turn “Which care is best?” into a specific question. List the target change, measured outcome, similar conditions, replicates and timing. A screen-only plan is sufficient.

## Vocabulary / 小词典

### 中文

自变量 independent variable；因变量 dependent variable；控制变量 controlled variable；混杂因素 confounding factor。

### English

Independent variable: deliberate target change; dependent variable: measured outcome; controlled variable: similar condition; confounding factor: another change mixed with the target.

## Check / 检索练习

### 中文

检索：本次改变什么？水与光一起变化的问题是什么？为何需要多株和随机分组？控制变量对应哪个词？

### English

Recall the target factor, the problem with changing water and light together, why replication and random assignment help, and the term for a similar condition.

## Mission / 生活探究

### 中文

设计U06的光照与植物生长比较方案，记录问题、测量起终点、重要控制、每组株数和观察时长。实际条件未知要标待核对。

### English

Design the U06 light–growth comparison with question, measurement endpoints, important controls, plants per group and observation period. Mark unknown real conditions for checking.
