## 我的作品 / My project

### 中文

想象窗台盆栽的一片成熟绿叶，与正在生长的根合作。给水、CO₂、光和糖四条路线加“物质”“能量”标签；各写一句来源→边界→去向的解释，再用中英各一段把路线联系起来。屏幕图解即可，也可以自己画在纸上，再记录解释。

### English

Imagine a mature green leaf on a windowsill plant cooperating with a growing root. Label four routes—water, CO₂, light and sugar—with matter/energy categories. Explain source → boundary → destination for each, then connect them with Chinese and English summaries. Use the on-screen diagram or a paper drawing with recorded explanations.

## 四条路线 / Four routes

### 中文

- **水 / Water：物质。** 土壤→根→木质部/茎→叶。需要适宜水势与运输条件。部分水参与光合作用，许多从湿表面蒸发→气隙→气孔→空气。
- **二氧化碳 / Carbon dioxide：物质。** 空气→气孔→叶肉细胞/叶绿体；CO₂提供有机物中的碳。图固定向内净扩散的条件，不表示所有气体总是朝同一方向。
- **光 / Light：能量输入。** 光到叶，部分被叶绿体吸收；光供能，不提供糖的碳。光、水、CO₂等条件合作，不是光单独变成糖。
- **糖 / Sugars：物质，也储存化学能。** 光合作用产物可形成蔗糖，沿韧皮部从成熟叶这一源到生长根这一库。糖可以供生长材料与细胞呼吸。不是所有韧皮部永远向下；储存根也可供芽。

### English

- **Water: matter.** Soil → root → xylem/stem → leaf, under suitable water-potential and transport conditions. Some participates in photosynthesis; much evaporates from moist surfaces → air spaces → stomata → air.
- **Carbon dioxide: matter.** Air → stomata → mesophyll cells/chloroplasts. CO₂ supplies carbon in organic material. This drawing assumes inward net diffusion, not one direction for every gas.
- **Light: energy input.** Some light reaching the leaf is absorbed by chloroplasts. It supplies energy rather than carbon. Light, water, CO₂ and other conditions cooperate; light alone does not become sugar.
- **Sugars: matter storing chemical energy.** Photosynthetic products can form sucrose that travels through phloem from the mature-leaf source to a growing-root sink. Sugars support growth material and respiration. Phloem is not always downward; storage roots can supply buds too.

## 做法 / Workflow

### 中文

1. 按名称选择一条路线，播放、暂停、逐步或重播机制说明。
2. 选择它的标签，并用自己的话解释。标签会立即出现在图上，是自己的选择。
3. 完成四条与中英总结后保存，查看标签提示。错标不清空作品，可以回到该路线修改；文字不自动判对错或语法。
4. 下载SVG，得到四条双语标签、完整路线解释与两段总结；也可只在浏览器查看。SVG保留自己的文字和标签，分享前自行核对。
5. 完成五道跨单元题，可以回看相关课程并订正。内容不是考试大纲认证。

### English

1. Select a named route and play, pause, step or replay its explanation.
2. Choose its category and explain it in your own words. Labels appear immediately as your choices.
3. Save four route cards and both summaries, then inspect category feedback. Wrong labels do not erase work; revise the route. Writing and grammar are not automatically graded.
4. Download an SVG with bilingual labels, all explanations and summaries, or view in the browser. The SVG preserves your choices; check before sharing.
5. Answer five cross-unit questions, revisit lessons and revise. This is not certification against an examination syllabus.

## 模型边界 / Model limits

### 中文

彩色圆点安排解释顺序，不追踪真实分子，也不提供秒数、通量、生长量或健康结果。图固定成熟绿叶和生长根，水蒸气另画支路；O₂、矿质营养、其他糖源库与完整细胞结构未画出。叶与根也呼吸，夜晚不等于植物全部生命活动停止。箭头是机制图，不是一次真的测量。

### English

A coloured dot orders explanations, not real molecular tracking, seconds, fluxes, growth or health outcomes. The drawing fixes a mature green leaf and growing root, with a vapour branch. O₂, minerals, other sources/sinks and full cellular structures are omitted. Leaves and roots also respire; night does not halt every plant process. Arrows describe mechanisms rather than an experiment.
