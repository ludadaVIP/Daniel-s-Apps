## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

一只tūī需要花蜜等食物，一只katipō需要沙丘里的隐蔽空间。给地图贴上生物名字之后，还得问：这里有哪些资源？如果一种资源改变，能推断到哪一步？

### English

A tūī needs foods such as nectar; a katipō needs shelter in dunes. After naming organisms on a map, ask what resources are available and how far an explanation can follow a change.

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

预测拿掉图中一项资源，是否等于立即知道生物数量。

### English

Predict whether removing one drawn resource reveals organism numbers immediately.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

森林/林缘图选卷心菜树、银蕨、tūī、kererū和惠灵顿树wētā；海岸图选沙丘katipō及沙丘后方湿地的卷心菜树。这里只是教学焦点，不是完整分布图。

### English

The forest/edge focus includes cabbage tree, silver fern, tūī, kererū and Wellington tree wētā. The coast focus shows dune katipō and cabbage tree on moist ground behind dunes. These are teaching selections, not full range maps.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

选择森林/林缘或海岸，再按生物名称选择焦点。逐步看特征→资源→连接→局限。可比较花蜜来源减少或沙丘遮蔽物减少；保留生物图，说明资源影响而不生成数量。

### English

Choose forest/edge or coast, then a named organism. Step through trait → resource → connection → limits. Compare fewer nectar sources or reduced dune cover; organism images remain while resource effects are described without invented counts.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**地方与生命通过资源相连。** 森林里有不同光照、植物材料与隐蔽空间；海岸也有潮线、干沙丘和后方湿地等不同条件。katipō的沙丘隐蔽处不是海水里的家，卷心菜树后方湿地也不是所有植物都适合的环境。

**原生不自动等于特有。** 原生描述自然出现，特有还限定自然分布范围；引入描述人带来。许多新西兰生物有独特演化历史，但具体名称和分布需要查资料，而不是凭外观猜。

**改变资源，先找机制。** 花蜜来源减少可能改变tūī的觅食选项；沙丘遮蔽物改变可能影响katipō隐蔽空间。数量结果还需其他食物、移动、繁殖、捕食、季节和真实调查，模型不代替监测。

### English

**Places connect through resources.** Forests offer varied light, plant materials and shelters. Coasts include tide lines, dry dunes and moist ground behind dunes. Katipō dune cover is not a home in seawater; moist cabbage-tree ground does not suit every plant.

**Native does not automatically mean endemic.** Native refers to natural occurrence; endemic restricts natural range; introduced refers to human transport. Many New Zealand organisms have distinctive histories, but names and ranges need references rather than appearance-based guesses.

**Follow a resource mechanism first.** Reduced nectar sources can change tūī feeding options; altered dune cover can affect katipō shelter. Population outcomes also depend on other foods, movement, reproduction, predation, seasons and surveys. Models do not replace monitoring.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

地图出现同一生物不意味着它处处都能生活；没画出来也不意味着当地不存在。引入与原生描述来源，不直接证明每一个体有害或有益。

### English

A drawn organism is not suitable everywhere; an omitted one is not necessarily absent. Introduced/native describe origin rather than proving every individual harmful or beneficial.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

保护可以从具体资源与威胁链入手，也需考虑人与地方的关系。毛利名称保留长音符用于尊重名称与准确交流；本课不推定传统用途。银蕨的资料名为Alsophila dealbata，也可能见旧名Cyathea dealbata；名称更新不意味着植物当天变了。

### English

Conservation can examine resources and threat chains while considering people and place. Māori names retain macrons for accurate communication; no traditional use is inferred here. Silver fern is listed as Alsophila dealbata, with the older name Cyathea dealbata also encountered. Updated names do not mean the plant changed that day.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

用课内虚拟地图即可。若远看熟悉地方，只记录位置、时间、结构与行为，不移动枯木、翻找沙丘或接近蜘蛛；尚未确认的物种保留描述。

### English

The virtual map is enough. From a distance in a familiar place, record location, time, structure and behaviour without moving cover, searching dunes or approaching spiders. Unconfirmed species can retain descriptive names.

### English

### English

### English
