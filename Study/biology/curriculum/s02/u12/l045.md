## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

一片蕨叶像羽毛，一只昆虫和蜘蛛都有分节的腿。别急着背名字：每问一个能检查的问题，就让候选集合变小。看不清也可以停在“未知”。

### English

A fern frond resembles a feather; an insect and a spider both have jointed legs. Instead of memorising names, ask checkable questions to shrink the candidate set. Unclear clues can remain unknown.

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先猜同为绿色的蕨叶和鸟羽毛是否应直接归为同类。

### English

Predict whether green fronds and green feathers should be grouped together immediately.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

六张原创图示带有可核对特征：卷心菜树带状叶、银蕨分裂叶、tūī喉白簇、kererū白胸腹、树wētā六腿、成年雌katipō八腿。图不按比例。

### English

Six original pictures show straps, divided fronds, tūī throat tuft, kererū white breast, six wētā legs and eight adult-female katipō legs. Pictures are not to scale.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

选择一张匿名样本，读图和文字线索，再选当前分支。正确特征让候选减少；不符时提示重看。点“看不清，保留未知”暂停判断，之后可重走。完整检索表可展开作参考。

### English

Choose an anonymous sample, inspect picture and written clues, then take a branch. Consistent choices reduce candidates; mismatches prompt another look. “Unclear: keep unknown” pauses the conclusion, with restart available. Expand the full key as a reference.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**分类让比较有依据。** 把生物按共同特征组织，有助交流、研究与辨认。科学分类还考虑结构、遗传、发育和演化亲缘等证据；不是按颜色或喜好贴标签。

**检索表把大问题拆小。** 每个二分节点有两个清楚选项。我们的六样本表先看叶片，再分蕨叶/带状叶；动物再看羽毛，分别比较白羽位置或完整图的腿数。每一步要有能核对的证据。

**范围决定结论。** 这张表只分六种已标定的教学图。现实世界还有大量相似生物，成体、幼体和遮挡也影响线索；走到末端并不等于完成真实物种鉴定。

### English

**Classification makes comparison explicit.** Grouping by shared evidence supports communication and study. Scientific classification also uses structure, genetics, development and evolutionary relationships rather than colours or preferences alone.

**A key breaks identification into choices.** Each node has two clear alternatives. Here leaves split into fronds/straps, while animals split by feathers and then white markings or complete leg counts. Every step needs checkable evidence.

**Scope limits conclusions.** This key separates six labelled teaching illustrations. Real life includes many similar organisms and changes with age or visibility. A terminal label is not a complete real-world species identification.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

蕨叶像羽毛不意味着它是鸟的一部分；蜘蛛不是八腿昆虫。我们的叶片/动物分支也没有囊括真菌、细菌与所有植物。

### English

A feather-like frond is not a bird part, and spiders are not eight-legged insects. The leaves/animals branch does not cover fungi, bacteria or all plants.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

辨认工具可按实用特征安排顺序，演化树则表达共同祖先的关系，两者用途不同。真正分类有层级，例如鸟类包含许多物种；现代分类也会随新证据修正。

### English

A practical key can order convenient traits, while an evolutionary tree describes common ancestry. Classification has levels, with many species inside birds, and can be revised with new evidence.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

用课内图就能完成，不需采集。观察窗外生物时只写能看到的特征；腿不完整或羽色模糊就记未知，不触摸或捕捉蜘蛛。

### English

The lesson pictures are enough. Record visible traits outside; incomplete legs or unclear colours can remain unknown. Do not handle or catch spiders.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

试说：A key narrows the candidates. I can check this trait. The evidence is unclear.

### English

Try: “A key narrows the candidates. I can check this trait. The evidence is unclear.”

## Check / 挑战自己

### 中文

检查每个答案用了什么特征，以及检索表覆盖哪些样本。

### English

Check the trait behind each answer and the key’s sample scope.

## Mission / 今日小任务

### 中文

为六个课程样本写一张二分检索表，先用叶片/动物，再分蕨叶/带状叶、羽毛/分节腿、两种白羽位置与六/八腿。

### English

Write a binary key for six lesson samples: leaves/animals, fronds/straps, feathers/jointed legs, white feather locations, and six/eight legs.
