## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

两株幼苗都想接收到光；蜘蛛捕获昆虫；tūī从花取蜜，又可能带走花粉。同在一张图里，关系并不都一样，也不是“好生物”和“坏生物”的比赛。

### English

Seedlings both need light, a spider catches an insect, and a tūī takes nectar while possibly carrying pollen. Sharing a scene does not mean sharing a relationship, or a contest of good and bad organisms.

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先预测一只鸟只是停在花旁，能不能直接称双方互利。

### English

Predict whether a bird merely beside a flower establishes mutual benefit.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

三个独立示意：两株幼苗、蜘蛛与昆虫、tūī与两朵harakeke花。颜色与位置只帮助阅读，不模拟数量。

### English

Three independent schematics show seedlings, spider/insect and tūī/two harakeke flowers. Colours and positions aid reading, not population modelling.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

选择一种关系逐步四格。访花例可切换“接触并递送花粉”与“只确认取蜜”；两支都有鸟获得花蜜，只有前者有花粉转移线索。

### English

Choose a relationship and step through four views. The flower case compares pollen contact/delivery with nectar feeding only. Both supply the bird, while only the first includes pollen-transfer evidence.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**竞争看共同而有限的需求。** 光、水、矿质元素、食物或空间可能成为资源。两株都需要的资源有限，彼此使用或遮挡可影响机会；无需把生物画成打架的人。

**捕食看取食关系。** 捕食者获得材料与化学能，猎物受到损失。食物网箭头是猎物→捕食者；关系存在不直接给出未来数量。

**互利看双方的作用。** tūī从花蜜得到食物，接触并把相容花粉递送到另一花的柱头可帮助传粉。只见取蜜不能证明这个第二环节，也不能把传粉当成已经成功结实。

### English

**Competition concerns shared limited demand.** Light, water, minerals, food and space may be resources. Limited access can be changed by others’ use or shade without human-like fighting.

**Predation concerns feeding.** The predator gains materials and chemical energy while prey is harmed. Food arrows run prey → predator; a relationship alone gives no future population count.

**Mutual benefit concerns both effects.** Nectar feeds tūī; contact and delivery of compatible pollen to another stigma can support pollination. Nectar feeding alone does not establish that second link or successful fruit development.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

共生不全是互利；寄生通常一方受益、另一方受损，偏利关系一方受益而另一方未见明显影响。自然关系也不是道德评分。

### English

Symbiosis is not always mutual benefit. Parasitism typically benefits one while harming the other; commensalism benefits one with no clear effect on the other. These are not moral scores.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

“共生”常强调长期紧密联系；鸟访花不需长期同居也能互利。关系效果会随环境变化。我们只用资源与转移机制解释例子，不以单次行为证明长期净利益。

### English

Symbiosis often emphasises close persistence; bird visits can be mutually beneficial without cohabitation. Effects vary with context. Resource and transfer mechanisms explain these cases without proving lifetime net benefit from one event.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

在熟悉地方远看植物与访花动物，记录行为和时间，不干预。看见同处一地只记共现；看见取食才能写相应行为线索。课内模型也可完成任务。

### English

Observe plants and visitors from a distance without intervention. Shared location is co-occurrence; feeding provides another kind of clue. The lesson model is enough for the task.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

试说：They use a limited resource. The prey is food for the predator. A visit alone does not prove mutual benefit.

### English

Try: “They use a limited resource. The prey is food for the predator. A visit alone does not prove mutual benefit.”

## Check / 挑战自己

### 中文

先找资源与行为，再判断双方作用，最后问哪些结果尚未测量。

### English

Find resource and behaviour, consider both effects, then ask what remains unmeasured.

## Mission / 今日小任务

### 中文

### English
