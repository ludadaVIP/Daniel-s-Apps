# U12 · 六样本检索表 / A six-sample key

为卷心菜树、银蕨、tūī、kererū、惠灵顿树wētā和成年雌katipō图设计二分检索表。每步两个可核对选项，不用单一颜色或屏幕大小；用两个样本试走。写一句英文和一条局限，保留“看不清/表外样本”的未知出口。可用文字描述，不需采集。

Design a binary key for the cabbage tree, silver fern, tūī, kererū, Wellington tree wētā and adult-female katipō illustrations. Use checkable paired choices rather than colour alone or screen size. Test two samples, add an English sentence and one limit, and retain an unknown exit for unclear or out-of-scope samples. Text is enough; no collection needed.
