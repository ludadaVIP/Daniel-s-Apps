## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

tūī的羽毛有时看起来深黑，有时闪着绿蓝光。kererū也可能有绿色光泽。颜色这条线索，到底能告诉我们多少？

### English

Tūī plumage can look dark or iridescent green-blue, while kererū can also look green-toned. How far can colour take an explanation?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

预测相同羽色是否比羽毛、喉部与胸腹等多条特征更可靠。

### English

Predict whether colour alone is more reliable than several structural clues.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

两幅成鸟示意只改变羽色明暗或选择另一鸟种；不是鸟瞬间演化。部分视图只有一片羽色，标签留未知。

### English

Two adult bird schematics vary viewing colour or show another species, not instant evolution. The partial view retains an unknown label.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

选“同种不同视角”“两种鸟”或“只有局部”，逐步四格：观察→补充资料→分层判断→局限。新资料是课程给定身份，不是模型生成DNA或繁殖实验。

### English

Choose same-species views, two species or a partial view. Step through observation → supplied reference → layered conclusion → limits. Information is provided by the lesson, not generated DNA or a breeding experiment.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**外观不是全部身份。** 同种个体可因年龄、性别、遗传差异或环境而不同；同一个体的羽色也可因光线与角度不同而改变。不能见到颜色变化就宣布新物种。

**共同特征有层级。** tūī和kererū都有羽毛，属于鸟类，却是不同物种。共同类群不等于单一物种；彼此不同也不意味着完全没有亲缘。

**物种边界需多证据。** 对许多有性生殖生物，科学家会研究自然条件下种群间是否通常交换基因、是否有生殖隔离，并结合结构、遗传、行为和生态等线索。这里不给活鸟做实验，只用资料练习证据范围。

### English

**Appearance is not the whole identity.** Individuals can differ with age, sex, inheritance or environment; one individual can also look different under changed light or angle. A colour change does not declare a new species.

**Shared traits have levels.** Tūī and kererū share feathers and belong to birds, but are different species. One broader group is not one species, and difference does not mean no relationship.

**Species boundaries need multiple lines of evidence.** For many sexually reproducing organisms, scientists examine usual natural gene exchange and reproductive isolation, alongside structural, genetic, behavioural and ecological evidence. We use references rather than experiments on live birds.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

“鸟类”不是一种鸟，“绿色”也不是物种名称。身体差异与观察角度造成的颜色变化是不同来源，不应混成一种机制。

### English

“Birds” is not one bird species, and green is not a species name. Biological variation and colour changes caused by viewing conditions have different sources.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

生物学物种概念对自然有性生殖群体很有用，但不能原样套到无性繁殖生物或化石。杂交也可能发生，界限不总是一句口号。分类研究可采用不同物种概念，比较演化谱系和更多证据。

### English

The biological species concept is useful for natural sexual populations but does not transfer unchanged to asexual organisms or fossils. Hybridisation can occur, so boundaries are not a slogan. Researchers use suitable species concepts, lineages and multiple lines of evidence.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

远看一只鸟，记时间、光线和可见结构；同一照片的颜色不能当作不同个体的遗传测量。可只记“有羽毛的鸟，具体物种未确认”。

### English

Observe a bird from a distance and record time, light and visible structure. Colour in a photo is not a genetic measurement. “Feathered bird, species unconfirmed” is acceptable.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

试说：They share bird traits. They are different species. This view is not enough to identify one.

### English

Try: “They share bird traits. They are different species. This view is not enough to identify one.”

## Check / 挑战自己

### 中文

判断是在说个体、物种、共同类群，还是观察条件。

### English

Decide whether a statement concerns individuals, species, groups or viewing conditions.
