## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

一片叶子在夜晚没有光，但细胞仍要保持边界、更新材料。它是等到第二天才工作，还是能利用已有糖？

### English

Without light at night, leaf cells still maintain boundaries and materials. Do they wait until morning or use existing sugar?

### English

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**呼吸在细胞内发生。** 有氧细胞呼吸通过多步反应把糖等有机物中的化学能转移，一部分用于ATP再生成，部分最终散为热。糖分解的一些开始步骤在细胞质，许多后续有氧步骤在真核细胞的线粒体。不是糖在里面燃烧着火。

**植物也要呼吸。** 白天叶肉有光、合适原料等条件时可以光合作用，同时也可呼吸；夜晚没有光，仍可利用已有糖呼吸。根通常没有本课叶肉式的叶绿体，但也可呼吸，依赖其他部位送来的有机物。

**供氧不是制造能量。** 氧支持完整有氧路线，糖的碳可进入CO₂，物质的化学组合变化。肺通气、气体交换和循环把氧与细胞连接，细胞呼吸是内部反应。缺氧时这条路线受限，不代表所有供ATP过程都不存在。

### English

**Respiration happens inside cells.** Aerobic respiration transfers chemical energy from sugars through multiple reactions, partly supporting ATP regeneration and ultimately releasing heat. Initial sugar-breakdown steps occur in cytoplasm; many later aerobic steps in eukaryotic cells occur in mitochondria. This is not a literal fire.

**Plants respire too.** Mesophyll can photosynthesise with suitable daylight and materials while also respiring. At night it can use existing sugar. Roots usually lack the chloroplasts of green mesophyll but can respire using organic compounds delivered from elsewhere.

**Oxygen supports a route rather than creating energy.** Glucose carbon can appear in CO₂ as chemical arrangements change. Ventilation, gas exchange and circulation connect oxygen supply to cells; cellular respiration is the internal reaction process. Missing oxygen restricts this route without eliminating every ATP-supplying process.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

植物不是“白天光合作用、夜晚才呼吸”。氧不是食物化学能的替代品。缺氧开关不预测细胞死亡时间，也不表示所有ATP立即归零。

### English

Plants do not respire only at night. Oxygen does not replace food chemical energy. The oxygen switch predicts neither time to cell death nor an immediate loss of all ATP.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

有氧呼吸总体可概括为C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O，并伴随能量转移。它不等于把光合作用机械倒放，两者路径与酶不同。糖酵解可以不直接需要氧，某些细胞通过发酵相关过程维持它，少量供ATP；本模型不比较真实ATP产量或速率。

### English

An overall aerobic relation is C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O with energy transfer. It is not photosynthesis mechanically played backwards; pathways and enzymes differ. Glycolysis does not directly require oxygen, and some cells sustain it through fermentation-related processes with smaller ATP supply. Real yield and rates are not compared here.

### English
