# U09 · 阳光到动物活动 / From sunlight to animal activity

选一条路线：阳光→叶片制糖→食物→细胞呼吸/ATP→动物活动与散热。补一个枯落物→分解者支路。用不同颜色画物质和能量；说明糖的碳主要来自CO₂，不来自光。

Choose sunlight → leaf sugar-making → food → respiration/ATP → animal activity and heat. Add detritus → decomposers. Use distinct matter/energy colours and explain sugar carbon coming from CO₂ rather than light.

## 三格证据 / Three fields

1. 主张：追踪什么食物路线，哪些是物质、哪些是能量？ / Claim: which food route, and which parts are matter versus energy?
2. 证据：写明课程模型条件、六个碳示踪与一个假设能量账本；直接生活观察另列。 / Evidence: label model conditions, six carbon tracers and an assumed energy account; separate everyday observations.
3. 推理：植物也呼吸；双向生命过程不等于能量循环。写英文：Carbon can cycle; energy is transferred and dissipates as heat. / Reasoning: plants respire too; life processes do not mean energy cycles. Add the English sentence.

不要求实际食物/植物实验。账本是假设E单位，不能预测真实速率、效率、种群或个人身体指标。

No real food/plant experiment is needed. Assumed E units predict no real rate, efficiency, population or personal body measurement.
