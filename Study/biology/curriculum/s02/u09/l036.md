## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

叶片→毛虫→鸟。鸟得到的是原来所有叶片能量吗？毛虫活动、呼吸，或产生枯落物后，能量还有哪些去向？

### English

Leaf → caterpillar → bird. Does the bird receive all original leaf energy? What happens through caterpillar activity, respiration and detritus?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先猜下一层能得到多少，再问其余是不是全部消失。别把常见的10%近似当作每个真实食物链的精确比例。

### English

Predict onward transfer, then ask whether the remainder vanishes. Do not treat the familiar 10% approximation as exact for every real food chain.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

箭头从食物指向取食者。金色波线表示能量，蓝色碳路线可回到空气CO₂。植物起始1000E是有机物化学能，不是照到叶片的全部太阳能；E为虚构单位。

### English

Arrows point food → feeder. Gold waves show energy; blue carbon routes can return to atmospheric CO₂. Initial plant 1000E is organic chemical energy, not all incident sunlight. E is an invented unit.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

选择能量或碳视角。能量视角可换5%、10%、20%假设转移比例；五站导览加入毛虫、鸟、枯落物与热。展开账本核对各层输入=传下一层+枯落物+热。三条柱使用同一零起点尺度，细柱也有数字。

### English

Choose energy or carbon. Energy view compares assumed 5%, 10% and 20% transfers. Five stages add caterpillars, birds, detritus and heat. Open the account to check input = onward transfer + detritus + heat. Three bars share a zero-based scale and readable values.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**取食传递部分能量。** 植物有机物中的化学能可通过取食传给毛虫，再传给鸟。但并非所有植物被吃、所有吃下的物质被吸收，或所有吸收能量都留在下一层的新有机物中。活动与呼吸使部分能量最终散为热。

**未传递不等于全是热。** 模型10%情境中，植物1000E分成传毛虫100E、枯落物200E、热700E。枯落物仍有化学能，分解者能利用，自己也呼吸和散热。模型账本暂把枯落物保留在这个库，不计算分解时间。

**碳循环，能量流。** 碳可由CO₂进入植物有机物，再经取食、死亡与分解移动，呼吸后回到CO₂，被新一轮光合作用利用。这里需要新的光能输入；分解者不会把分散的热重新变成阳光。模型比例用于推理，不是后院实测，也不预测鸟的数量。

### English

**Feeding passes some energy onward.** Plant organic chemical energy can reach caterpillars and birds. Not all plants are eaten, eaten material absorbed or absorbed energy retained in new organic matter at the next level. Activity and respiration ultimately dissipate part as heat.

**Non-transfer is not all heat.** The 10% scenario splits plant 1000E into 100E onward, 200E detritus and 700E heat. Detritus retains chemical energy for decomposers, which also respire and release heat. The account leaves detritus in its pool without calculating decomposition time.

**Carbon cycles; energy flows.** Carbon can move from CO₂ into plants, through feeding and detritus, then return through respiration to CO₂ for reuse. Another round needs new light energy. Decomposers do not convert dispersed heat back into sunlight. Fractions are reasoning scenarios, not garden measurements or bird-population predictions.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

10%不是自然定律；其余90%不是全已散成热，更不是凭空消失。能量柱不表示生物数量、体积或大小。碳回到CO₂不等于能量回到阳光。

### English

Ten percent is not a natural law; the other 90% is neither all already heat nor gone. Energy bars are not organism counts, sizes or volumes. Carbon returning to CO₂ does not mean energy returning to sunlight.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

真实转移受被吃比例、消化吸收、呼吸需求与环境影响，各环节可以不同。本模型两条取食环节用相同假设比例，固定20%进枯落物，余量算热；最后一层不设置下一取食者。这是封闭的教学账本，不是实际生态系统。分解者连接多个营养层，不只是食物链最末的固定一级。

### English

Actual transfers depend on consumption, assimilation, respiratory demands and environment, and links can differ. Our two feeding links use one assumed fraction, fixed 20% detritus and remaining heat, with no onward feeder from the terminal level. This is a closed teaching account, not a real ecosystem. Decomposers connect multiple trophic levels, not just a fixed final level.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

回看U05虚拟后院的食物网，把某一条路线改画成能量流。用已有照片或模型即可，不需喂食、捕捉或改变真实生物；观察“鸟吃虫”也不能直接测出转移比例。

### English

Revisit a U05 virtual garden feeding route and draw energy flow. Use models or existing photos without feeding, catching or changing organisms. Seeing a bird eat an insect does not measure transfer efficiency.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

试说：Energy is transferred and dissipates as heat. Detritus still contains chemical energy. Carbon can be reused. 用can表示路线可能性，不把示例比例说成必然。

### English

Try: “Energy is transferred and dissipates as heat. Detritus still contains chemical energy. Carbon can be reused.” Can marks a possible route rather than a guaranteed exact scenario.

## Check / 挑战自己

### 中文

用账本核对去向，再判断哪些是模型假设。英语题分清energy flow与carbon cycle，而不是背一个孤立的10%。

### English

Check fates with the account and identify assumptions. Distinguish energy flow from carbon cycle rather than memorising an isolated 10%.
