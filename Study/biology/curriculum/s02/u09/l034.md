## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

一片绿叶制造糖，糖里的碳从哪里来？土壤、水、空气和光各自提供了什么？

### English

Where does a green leaf obtain sugar carbon? What different roles do soil, water, air and light play?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先猜碳来源与光的作用。看完模型再解释为什么“土壤就是植物的食物”遗漏了制糖过程。

### English

Predict carbon’s source and light’s role. Then explain what “soil is plant food” misses about sugar-making.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

六个蓝色碳点先属于CO₂，随后进入糖的示意组；光用金色波线表示，不加新碳点。只追踪碳，水与氧图标不按数量显示，糖的六点组不是化学结构式。

### English

Six blue carbon tracers start in CO₂ and later appear grouped in sugar. Gold waves represent light without adding carbon. Only carbon is traced; water/oxygen icons are not molecule counts and the sugar group is not a structural formula.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

调光到无光、较弱或较亮；选择可用水、可用CO₂是否存在。每次换条件重置四站导览。模型固定叶肉有叶绿体、温度和酶状态适宜，并假定没有储备支撑缺条件时的这条制糖路线。

### English

Set light to none, dimmer or brighter and switch usable water/CO₂ availability. Changes reset four stages. Mesophyll chloroplasts, suitable temperature and enzyme conditions are fixed, with no reserve sustaining the depicted route when a prerequisite is absent.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**光与原料分工不同。** 光提供能量，水和CO₂参与物质转化。绿色叶肉的叶绿体中，叶绿素等参与吸收光；供能过程与碳的组装相互配合，最终形成糖等有机物。土壤提供水和矿质元素，但不是现成糖的主要来源。

**碳没有消失。** 本图只追踪六个碳：开始分散在CO₂中，后来出现在一组代表葡萄糖的示意里。它们仍是碳原子，只是化学组合改变；光不变成它们。植物可把制得的有机物用于呼吸、运输、储存或生长。

**条件不等于产量。** 光、水和CO₂等都要考虑。这里的两档有光只显示制糖路线是否具备条件，不能比较真实速率；更亮不保证永远更快。叶片同时可能呼吸，本图没有计算制糖与消耗相减后的净糖量。

### English

**Energy and materials have different jobs.** Light supplies energy while water and CO₂ participate in material conversion. Chlorophyll and other components in mesophyll chloroplasts absorb light, connecting energy supply to carbon assembly into organic compounds. Soil supplies water and minerals rather than being the main source of ready-made sugar.

**Carbon remains carbon.** Six tracers begin in separate CO₂ molecules and later form a group representing glucose. Their chemical arrangement changes; light does not become atoms. Products can support respiration, transport, storage or growth.

**Conditions are not yield.** Light, usable water and CO₂ matter. Two light settings only indicate prerequisites; they do not compare actual rates. Brighter does not always mean faster. Leaves may simultaneously respire; net sugar production minus consumption is not calculated.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

光不是糖里的碳原料；土壤养分不是光合作用制造的糖。模型“缺可用水”不等于现实中一天没浇水；植物可能已有储备，不能照按钮去改变真实照料。

### English

Light is not sugar’s carbon material, and soil minerals are not photosynthetic sugar. Missing usable water is not the same as a day without watering; real plants may have reserves. The buttons are not care instructions.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

总体关系可写CO₂ + 水 —光能→ 糖 + 氧气；进阶常用6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂概括净反应。实际过程分多步，并非叶绿体一步直接造葡萄糖；释放的氧主要来自水。只按CO₂和氧图标画直线，不能推断每个氧原子的来源。

### English

An overall relation is CO₂ + water —light energy→ sugar + oxygen; the net equation is often summarised as 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂. Actual reactions involve many steps and do not directly form glucose in one step. Released oxygen originates mainly from water; icon connections alone do not establish oxygen-atom origins.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

看现有绿叶或照片，想象气孔、内部叶肉与根送来的水怎样配合。外观绿色是线索，不直接测出光合作用速率。无需遮叶、密封或改变浇水。

### English

Observe an existing leaf or photo and consider stomata, mesophyll and root-supplied water. Green appearance is a clue, not a photosynthesis-rate measurement. No covering, sealing or changed watering is needed.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

试说：CO₂ supplies carbon. Light supplies energy. The same carbon atoms enter sugar. 用reactant表达物质原料，别把光说成碳原料。

### English

Try: “CO₂ supplies carbon. Light supplies energy. The same carbon atoms enter sugar.” Use reactant for material inputs, without calling light a carbon material.
