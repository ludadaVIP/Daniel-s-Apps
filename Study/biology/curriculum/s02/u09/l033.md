## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**生活不只有跑跳。** 细胞制造新材料、维持内部条件、主动运输和更新结构，都涉及供能反应与工作之间的配合。身体不动时，许多活动仍在进行。

**早餐是一段接力。** 小麦用光合作用制造含能量的有机物，部分成为种子里的淀粉。吐司消化后，可吸收的材料经循环到细胞。细胞呼吸等反应把化学能转移，一部分与ATP的再生成耦联；ATP参与支持细胞工作。植物细胞也用已有糖等材料支持工作，不是只有动物才用ATP。

**光不变成碳。** 阳光供能，CO₂等物质供原子。含碳物质可被组装成身体材料，也可经呼吸回到CO₂；能量在这些过程中转移并散为热。图展示路线关系，不计算早餐热量、ATP数量或个人体能。

### English

**Life is more than movement.** Assembly, internal regulation, active transport and renewal involve energy-supplying reactions coupled to work. Many activities continue while your body is still.

**Breakfast is a relay.** Wheat makes organic compounds by photosynthesis, with some stored as seed starch. After digestion, absorbed materials reach cells through circulation. Respiration and other reactions transfer chemical energy, partly coupled to ATP regeneration. ATP reactions help support cellular work. Plant cells also use stored sugars and other materials.

**Light does not become carbon.** Sunlight supplies energy; CO₂ and other substances supply atoms. Carbon can enter body materials or return to CO₂ through respiration. Energy transfers and dissipates as heat. The drawing does not calculate meal calories, ATP yield or personal performance.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

能量不是一种从太阳跑进身体的金色原子。ATP不是永久充满的电池，不移动也不代表不用能量。光能与有机物的碳来源应分别解释。

### English

Energy is not a gold atom travelling from the Sun. ATP is not a permanently charged battery, and stillness is not absence of energy use. Explain light energy and carbon sources separately.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

ATP的反应可与需要能量的反应耦联；再生成ATP也需要供能。不能简单说“断一个键就凭空释放能量”：断键需要能量，形成新键会释放能量，要看整体反应变化。多数熟悉的地表食物链以光供能，有些生态系统则由利用化学反应能量的生产者支持。

### English

ATP reactions can couple to energy-requiring reactions, while ATP regeneration needs energy supply. Breaking a bond alone does not create energy: bond breaking requires energy and bond formation releases it, so consider the overall reaction. Many familiar surface food chains depend on light, while some ecosystems depend on chemical-energy-using producers.
