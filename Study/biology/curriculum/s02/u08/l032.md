## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

家人把食材切小后，接触调味料的外表面可能增多。细胞也面临表面与内部的关系，但切菜块不是活细胞模型。用图形做比较，无需刀具。

### English

Smaller food pieces can expose more surface to seasoning. Cells also face a surface–interior relationship, but cut food blocks are not living cells. Compare drawings without knives.

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

如果总体积一样，八块会比一块多多少外露面积？薄盒到中心的最近距离是否一定和大立方体一样？

### English

At equal total volume, how does eight-piece exposed area compare with one block? Must a thin box have the same nearest centre distance?

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

图只画立体物的三个可见面，数值计算全部六面。八个小块分开放置，每面都接触外界；贴在一起的隐藏接触面不能这样计数。单位是虚构长度u，不是微米。

### English

Only three faces are visible in the perspective drawing, but calculations include all six. Eight cubes are separated with every face exposed; hidden touching faces would not count. The invented length unit u is not a micrometre.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

切换大立方体、八个小块和薄盒，逐步比较总体积、总面积与中心到最近表面距离。三种体积均为8u³；导览是比较顺序，不是粒子移动的计时动画。

### English

Switch among a large cube, eight cubes and a thin box, then step through volume, area and nearest centre distance. All total 8u³. The tour orders comparisons; it does not time particle movement.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**总量一样，表面可以不同。** 大立方体边长2：体积2×2×2=8，面积6×2×2=24。八个边长1的分离小块：总体积8，面积8×6=48。它们有更多接触外界的面，但材料总量没有增加。

**变薄也有用。** 薄盒4×2×1体积仍8，面积2×(4×2+4×1+2×1)=28。中心到最近表面沿最薄方向，仅0.5；大立方体为1。薄形状既能改变接触面积，也能缩短某些扩散距离。

**几何是线索，不是全部。** 更多外表面积可提供更多交换位置；较短距离有利于扩散联系内部。真实细胞还需要适合的膜通透性、运输蛋白、驱动力与周围供给。小肠吸收细胞的表面突起增加面积，但有面积也不意味着任何东西都能进来。

### English

**Equal amount, different surface.** A cube of side 2 has volume 2×2×2=8 and area 6×2×2=24. Eight separate cubes of side 1 total volume 8 and area 8×6=48. They expose more surface without adding material.

**Thinness can help.** A 4×2×1 box still has volume 8, with area 2×(4×2+4×1+2×1)=28. Its centre is only 0.5 from the nearest face along its thinnest direction, compared with 1 in the large cube. Thinness can change contact area and shorten some diffusion distances.

**Geometry is one clue.** More exposed area can offer more exchange sites, and shorter distances can connect interior regions more readily by diffusion. Real cells also need suitable permeability, proteins, driving conditions and surrounding supply. Intestinal projections increase area without making every substance freely pass.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

表面积大不是体积大；面积单位u²，体积u³，不能直接相减。中心到最近表面不是所有分子的路程。八块拼回一块时，内部接触面不再暴露，不能仍用48作外表面积。

### English

Area is not volume: u² and u³ cannot simply be subtracted. The centre-to-nearest-face distance is not every molecule’s journey. Reassembling eight cubes hides internal contact faces, so 48 no longer describes exposed area.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

把同形立方体边长放大k倍，面积放大k²倍，体积放大k³倍，面积/体积比缩小到1/k。这解释尺寸增大时每单位内部对应的外表面减少。真实细胞可有薄形状、膜折叠、内部运输或不同代谢需求，不能只凭一个比值判断生命能否存在。

### English

Scaling a similar cube’s side by k scales area by k² and volume by k³, reducing area/volume by 1/k. Increasing size thus supplies less outer area per internal volume. Real cells can use thin forms, membrane folds, internal transport or different demands; one ratio does not decide viability.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

回看L028小肠吸收细胞的表面小突起：它们为什么不是装饰？再用图解释薄叶或许多小块的接触面。生活食材用于外观对照，不把切块当作细胞分裂或保证交换速度的实验。

### English

Revisit L028 intestinal-cell projections: why are they more than decoration? Explain a thin leaf or separated pieces using surface contact. Food appearance is an analogy, not cell division or an experiment guaranteeing exchange speed.

## Check / 挑战自己

### 中文

### English

Check six faces and the thinnest direction in the concept questions. In reasoning, identify conditions missing for real exchange. Start by explaining equal amounts in different shapes.
