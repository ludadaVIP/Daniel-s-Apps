## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

盐拌黄瓜后来可能变软、外面变湿。水来自哪里？它是否必须等盐先穿过细胞膜？

### English

Salted cucumber may soften and become wet. Where could the water come from? Must salt cross the cell membrane first?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选解释，再比较外侧相对溶质浓度1、4、8。细胞内起始为4，初始压力与温度相同，溶质暂不穿过模型膜。

### English

Predict first, then compare outside relative solute concentrations 1, 4 and 8. Inside starts at 4; initial temperature and pressure match, and solute does not cross the model membrane.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

绿色外框是壁，蓝色内边界是膜，金色点是溶质。外框固定，内边界可与壁分开。两支水箭头表示双向移动，粗细只表示起始净趋势，不是流速测量。

### English

The green outline is a wall, the blue inner boundary a membrane and gold dots solute. The fixed wall can separate from the inner boundary. Two water arrows show both directions; widths indicate initial net tendency, not measured rates.

### English

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**先看水，不是盐。** 水分子可以双向运动；当膜允许水通过而限制某种溶质时，溶质差与压力等条件影响水的净方向。本模型初始温度、压力相同，所以较稀外液使水起始净向内，较浓外液使水起始净向外。相同外液没有起始净偏向，不是水停止运动。

**膜与壁要分清。** 植物细胞失水时，内容物和膜可能缩离壁，壁仍提供外框。入水时内容物更贴近壁，产生膨压；这种支持有助于组织挺立。壁不是本课限制溶质通过的选择性边界。

**起始方向不等于最终状态。** 随着体积和压力改变，水的净移动也可能改变。模型只给示意趋势，不计算最终浓度、膨压或真实时间。不要把最后一帧当作具体测量，更不要断言水会无限进入。

### English

**Follow water, not salt.** Water moves both ways. When a membrane passes water but restricts a solute, solute differences and pressure conditions influence net direction. With equal starting temperature and pressure, dilute outside solution gives an initial inward tendency and concentrated solution an outward one. Matching solution has no initial net preference; water still moves.

**Distinguish wall and membrane.** During water loss, membrane and contents can shrink away from a plant wall. With water entry, contents press against the wall, generating turgor that helps support tissue. The wall is not the selective solute barrier in this model.

**A starting tendency is not an endpoint.** Changing volume and pressure can change net movement. This schematic does not calculate final concentrations, turgor or real time. Its last picture is not a measurement or a claim of endless entry.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

水向较浓溶液净移动，不代表盐必须跨膜。相同起始浓度的两支等粗箭头也不代表没有水运动。细胞壁不因内容物收缩而消失。

### English

Net water movement towards a more concentrated solution does not require salt crossing. Equal arrows do not mean no water motion. The wall does not vanish when contents shrink.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

更完整的解释用“水势”：溶质通常降低水势，压力可以提高水势，水总体沿水势差移动。因此“水总向溶质多的地方走”只在指定条件下适用。真实细胞还可能调节溶质，溶质也可能经其他路线移动；本模型不包含这些过程。

### English

A fuller explanation uses water potential: solutes usually lower it, while pressure can raise it. Net water movement follows a water-potential difference. “Always towards more solute” only works under stated conditions. Real cells can regulate solutes or provide other routes; those processes are omitted here.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

回想家人做好的盐拌蔬菜或照片，不用你切菜或改植物照料。渗透能帮助解释部分水分变化，但外面有水也可能受组织破损等影响；外观不能独立证明只有一种原因。

### English

Recall prepared salted vegetables or a photo without cutting or changing plant care. Osmosis can explain part of the water change, but tissue damage can contribute. Appearance alone does not establish a single cause.

### English

## Check / 挑战自己

### 中文

解题先核对：水能通过吗？溶质能通过吗？压力条件是什么？再说起始净方向。

### English

Before answering, check water passage, solute passage and pressure conditions. Then state an initial net direction.
