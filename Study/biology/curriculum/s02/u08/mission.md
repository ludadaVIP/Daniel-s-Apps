# U08 · 给扩散写一份数据解释 / Explain diffusion with records

从L030任选起始条件，记录两帧的模型步、左右数量及该段两个方向的穿越事件。核对左侧减少数 = 左→右 − 右→左。说明为什么双向运动仍可以有净变化，再换一个轨迹检查结论范围。

Choose starting conditions in L030. Record two snapshot ticks, left/right counts and crossing events during that interval. Check left decrease = left-to-right − right-to-left. Explain why two-way motion can produce net change, then compare another trace.

## 保存三格笔记 / Save three fields

1. 主张：哪种条件、哪一段，整体怎样变？ / Claim: which conditions and interval, and what changed?
2. 证据：起终数量、两方向事件、等体积条件。注明模型数据；生活果汁观察另列。 / Evidence: initial/final counts, directional events and equal-volume condition. Label model records and separate real juice observations.
3. 推理：净值相减；单粒不认路；一段可能波动。写一句英文：Particles move both ways, but the net change is … . / Reasoning: subtract directions; individuals do not find routes; an interval may fluctuate. Add the English sentence.

这是解释任务，不要求购买器材或进行食物/膜实验。模型没有真实时间换算；不由短段记录推断所有情境、渗透终点或真实交换速率。

This explanation task requires no equipment or real food/membrane experiment. No physical time conversion is available; a short trace does not predict every context, osmosis endpoint or real exchange rate.
