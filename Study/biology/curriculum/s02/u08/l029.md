## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

吃过午饭，养分需要进入细胞，废物还得出去。如果细胞膜像塑料袋一样完全封闭，会怎样？如果像破网一样谁都能过，又会怎样？

### English

After lunch, nutrients must reach cells and wastes must leave. What if a membrane were sealed plastic? What if everything crossed freely?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先猜氧气、钠离子和蛋白质能否走同一条路线。预测不评分；试完可以改变解释。

### English

Predict whether oxygen, sodium and a protein can use the same route. This is unscored; revise your explanation after trying.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

找两排磷脂头部和朝内的尾部，再找穿过两层的运输蛋白。膜不是一片硬板，植物细胞壁在膜外侧，不代替膜。图的颜色、大小和间距均为示意。

### English

Find two rows of phospholipid heads, inward tails and a protein spanning both layers. A membrane is not a rigid board; a plant wall lies outside it. Colours, sizes and gaps are schematic.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

选择氧气、钠离子或蛋白质，播放四步路线。只改变“钠通道打开”，比较谁的路线改变。这个按钮是对模型条件的选择，不表示真实细胞用人类按钮开门。

### English

Choose oxygen, sodium or protein and play the four-step route. Change only whether the sodium channel is open. Which route changes? The button sets a model condition; real cells do not use human buttons.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**边界也能交换。** 膜的基本框架是磷脂双层。朝水的头部和朝内的疏水尾部形成不同环境，膜上还嵌有蛋白质。它柔软、动态，并不是一层有固定圆洞的金属筛。

**性质决定路线。** 不带电的氧气可以经过脂质内部。钠离子虽然小，却带电，直接经过这一内部很困难；适合的开放通道能提供路线。这里的一种钠通道不会顺便让大蛋白质通过。大小、电荷、与脂质的相容性和蛋白质都值得考虑。

**先认识被动路线。** 图只追踪一粒从外侧到内侧的可能路线，假设存在适合向内的驱动力。真实粒子可向两边运动；整体方向取决于条件。下一课会区分一粒的随机运动与总体净变化。

### English

**A boundary can exchange.** A phospholipid bilayer forms the membrane framework. Water-facing heads and inward hydrophobic tails create different environments, with proteins embedded in the membrane. The structure is flexible and dynamic.

**Properties shape routes.** Uncharged oxygen can pass through the lipid interior. A small charged sodium ion cannot readily take that route; a suitable open channel provides another. That sodium channel does not also admit a large protein. Size, charge, lipid compatibility and proteins all matter.

**Start with passive routes.** The drawing follows one possible inward journey under a suitable inward driving condition. Real particles can move both ways; collective change depends on conditions. The next lesson separates individual motion from net change.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

“选择性”不等于膜会思考；“小”也不等于一定通过。蛋白质在这两条路线中被挡住，不代表任何蛋白质永远不能进入细胞：其他机制未在本模型中展示。

### English

Selectivity is not conscious thought, and small does not guarantee passage. A protein blocked by these routes is not necessarily excluded by every possible cellular mechanism; other mechanisms are omitted.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

疏水内部让带电离子的通过不容易。离子的净被动运输还受膜两侧电荷差影响，不只是浓度。这里不计算电压，也不展示主动运输、载体或内吞；通道不是消耗ATP把离子逆向泵过去的泵。

### English

The hydrophobic interior hinders charged ions. Net passive ion transport depends on electrical differences as well as concentration. This model does not calculate voltage or show carriers, active transport or endocytosis. A channel is not an ATP-driven pump.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

回想叶片、洋葱和身体细胞：它们需要的交换各不相同，但都要有边界。用“谁、什么性质、走哪条路线”解释一个例子，比记住“膜像门”更有用。

### English

Think of leaf, onion and body cells: exchange needs differ, but boundaries matter to each. Explaining which substance, which property and which route is more useful than memorising “a membrane is a door”.
