## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

一杯果汁的颜色能散开。如果把水当成无数分子组成的世界，一粒分子会认出“淡的地方”吗？真实果汁还可能受水流和温度差影响，这节把这些因素先分开。

### English

Colour can spread through juice. In a molecular world, does one molecule recognise a paler region? Real juice can also mix through currents and temperature differences; here we separate those effects.

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先猜左侧12粒、右侧4粒会怎样。再问：同样的总体趋势，是否要求每个粒子都向右？

### English

Predict what happens from left 12, right 4. Does an overall trend require every particle to move right?

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

左右空间一样大，中线只是计数边界，不是细胞膜。紫色点代表同种粒子，金色点是其中一粒；折线显示它上一段的随机路线。点的大小不代表分子大小。

### English

Equal-sized halves share an imaginary counting boundary, not a membrane. Purple dots are the same substance; gold is one tracked particle. Its line shows the previous interval’s random route. Dot sizes are not molecular sizes.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

选12:4、8:8或4:12，播放或逐步走。每帧相隔32模型步，不是真实秒。看金点会往返，再看“左→右”和“右→左”穿越事件；展开记录表可抄两帧。重播保留同段轨迹，“换一段轨迹”给出另一组随机路线。

### English

Choose 12:4, 8:8 or 4:12 and play or step. Snapshots are 32 model steps apart, not real seconds. Track gold’s returns, compare both crossing counts, and open the record table. Replay repeats this trace; Another trace changes the random routes.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**单粒随机，整群有趋势。** 粒子不断运动，与周围粒子的相互作用让方向变化。模型用随机步表达这件事，不让粒子读取浓度或选择目的地。开始高浓度一侧有更多粒子，因此有更多粒子有机会随机离开，形成整体从高到低的净趋势。

**净值是相减。** 如果一段中左→右10次、右→左8次，净向右2次，左侧就少2粒。反向运动没有消失。一个粒子可能重复穿越，所以事件数可以大于粒子数。两侧等体积，数量才可在这里直接反映浓度大小。

**均匀不等于冻结。** 达到动态平衡，粒子仍随机运动。小模型的数量会起伏，某段净值甚至会反向；多次运行的整体趋势比一段短记录更可靠。本课没有细胞主动消耗ATP驱动随机扩散，但热运动仍与温度和能量有关。

### English

**Random individuals, collective trends.** Interactions with surrounding particles change movement direction. Random steps represent this without particles reading concentrations or choosing destinations. An initially concentrated side offers more particles a chance to leave, giving an overall high-to-low tendency.

**Net means subtraction.** Ten left-to-right and eight right-to-left crossings give net two to the right, decreasing the left count by two. Reverse motion continues. A particle can cross repeatedly, so event counts can exceed particle counts. Equal volumes allow counts to represent relative concentrations here.

**Uniform does not mean frozen.** Random motion continues at dynamic equilibrium. A small model fluctuates, sometimes even reversing net direction in one interval. Repeated runs support a trend more reliably than one short trace. Cells do not actively spend ATP to drive this diffusion, but thermal motion still involves temperature and energy.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

“从高到低”说的是整体净趋势，不是每个粒子的路线。不要把每帧都更接近8:8当作必须条件，也不要把某一次恰好8:8当作永远平衡。

### English

High to low describes a collective net tendency, not every particle’s route. Each snapshot need not approach 8:8, nor does one 8:8 snapshot guarantee permanent balance.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

真实世界有大量分子，少量粒子的波动显得特别大。这个模型把分子当独立点，在二维网格随机走，边缘不能漏出；它省略碰撞细节、溶剂流动和温度变化，没有换算真实扩散速率。扩散可在没有膜的空间发生，跨膜扩散还要有能通过的路线。

### English

Real materials contain vast numbers of molecules; fluctuations look large with only a few particles. Our independent points walk a 2D lattice with closed edges. Collision details, solvent flow and temperature changes are omitted; no real rate is calculated. Diffusion can occur without a membrane; membrane diffusion additionally needs a permeable route.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

回想搅拌果汁：勺子带动水流，比单纯扩散更快地混合。看见颜色均匀不能独立证明只有扩散。无需做新的实验，用模型与生活现象的差别练习解释。

### English

Think of stirring juice: a spoon moves liquid and mixes it faster than diffusion alone. Uniform colour does not establish diffusion as the only cause. Compare model and familiar observation without performing a new experiment.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

试说：Particles move both ways. The net change is two to the right. 用concentration而不是单说“更多”，记得体积条件。

### English

Try: “Particles move both ways. The net change is two to the right.” Use concentration and remember the volume condition.

## Check / 挑战自己

### 中文

挑战会把“一粒怎么走”和“整体怎么变”分开。推理题请用两方向事件的差值，而不是只看单向箭头。

### English

The challenge separates individual motion from collective change. Use the difference between directional events in the reasoning question.

## Mission / 今日小任务

### 中文

选一段真实显示的模型记录，用相邻两帧检验：左侧减少数 = 左→右次数 − 右→左次数。写清模型步、起始条件和限制，不冒充果汁实测。

### English

Use two displayed model snapshots to check: left decrease = left-to-right minus right-to-left crossings. State model steps, starting conditions and limits; do not label it measured juice data.
