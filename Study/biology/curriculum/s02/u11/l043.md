## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

窗边一片叶子在用光制造有机物，CO₂得从空气进来。叶片里却也有水蒸气往外走。一个小孔，为什么会带来两种相反方向的旅程？

### English

A sunlit leaf uses light to make organic materials, requiring air CO₂. Meanwhile water vapour leaves it. Why can one small pore support two opposite net journeys?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

预测缩小同一个孔，会不会只限制水而完全不影响CO₂通路。

### English

Predict whether narrowing the same pore restricts only water without affecting CO₂ access.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

图是局部叶片的截面，上方为叶内、下方为空气。两枚保卫细胞夹着孔；细胞壁上的水是液体，叶内空隙和外界的水是蒸气。

### English

The local cross-section has leaf interior above and outside air below. Paired guard cells flank a pore. Cell-wall water is liquid; water in air spaces and outside is vapour.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

选择较开/较窄孔隙与较干/较湿空气，逐步四格。蓝点在细胞壁时表示液态水，进入气隙后以空心圈表示水蒸气；金色CO₂走相反方向。比较孔隙通路与水蒸气差异两张卡，不把箭头粗细当作测量。

### English

Choose a wider/narrower pore and drier/humid air, then step through four views. A blue liquid-water dot becomes a hollow vapour ring in the air space; gold CO₂ travels inward. Compare separate access and vapour-difference cards; arrow width is not a measurement.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**气孔连接叶内与外界。** 叶脉送来液态水，一部分到达叶肉细胞壁表面并蒸发。水蒸气经叶内气隙、气孔等路径向外移动；在常见较干外界条件下有净失水。这种蒸腾也与木质部拉力相关。

**同孔，不同净方向。** 本课假设叶片正在净吸收CO₂做光合作用：空气CO₂净进入，水蒸气净离开。植物的呼吸一直可发生，真实气体净方向取决于过程与条件，不是所有叶在所有时刻都固定这样。

**开度是取舍的一部分。** 保卫细胞调节孔隙；缩小会限制CO₂通路，也有助限制气孔失水。外界更湿通常减小同温下的水蒸气差异。图不让植物自动响应：按钮设定比较情境，真实开度还受多种信号影响。

### English

**Stomata connect interior and outside.** Veins supply liquid water; some reaches mesophyll walls and evaporates. Vapour moves through internal air spaces, stomata and other paths, with net loss under common drier-air conditions. Transpiration also connects to xylem tension.

**One pore, different net directions.** Here the leaf is assumed to be a net CO₂-assimilating photosynthetic leaf: CO₂ enters while vapour leaves. Respiration can continue. Real net gas directions depend on processes and conditions, not a fixed rule for every leaf at every moment.

**Opening brings a trade-off.** Guard cells regulate the pore. Narrowing restricts CO₂ access and helps restrict stomatal water loss. More humid air usually reduces the vapour difference at the same temperature. Controls impose comparison cases rather than automatic plant responses; real opening depends on multiple signals.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

气孔不是呼吸用的嘴；不是所有植物只有叶下表面有气孔。孔变窄不等于水损失归零，也不等于光合作用停止到某个精确数值。

### English

Stomata are not mouths; not every plant has them only below leaves. Narrowing does not imply zero water loss or a precisely calculated photosynthetic shutdown.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

离子移动与水势变化影响保卫细胞水分和膨压，从而改变孔隙。角质层也可能有少量失水。某些植物如许多CAM植物常在夜间打开气孔，因此不能把白天开、夜晚关背成普遍规律。

### English

Ion movement and water-potential changes affect guard-cell water and turgor, changing the pore. Some water loss also occurs through the cuticle. Many CAM plants commonly open stomata at night, so day-open/night-closed is not universal.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

观察已有叶片的表面与叶脉，记录阳光与环境。肉眼通常看不到气孔，也不能从一张下垂叶照片断定失水原因；无需闷袋、暴晒或改变浇水。

### English

Observe an existing leaf surface, veins and light conditions. Stomata are usually invisible unaided, and one wilted-leaf photo cannot establish the cause. No bagging, extra sun or changed watering is needed.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### English

Try: “Water evaporates inside the leaf. Vapour can leave through a stoma. Guard cells regulate the opening.”

## Check / 挑战自己

### English

Distinguish liquid from gas, then compare each net direction and the model’s fixed conditions.
