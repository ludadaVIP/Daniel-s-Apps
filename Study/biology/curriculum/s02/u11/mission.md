# U11 · 一片叶子的三条路线 / Three routes for a leaf

选择已有叶片或课程模型，画水从土壤经根、木质部到叶的路线，补上蒸发与气孔失水；画空气CO₂进入叶片、光供能制造有机物，以及糖经韧皮部从源到库。标一个开度取舍、一个模型条件，并写一句英文。把直接观察与机制解释分开，不需实验或改变照料。

Choose an existing leaf or lesson model. Draw soil water through roots and xylem to the leaf, then evaporation and stomatal vapour loss. Add air CO₂ entering, light supplying energy for organic-material production, and phloem sugars from source to sink. State a pore trade-off, a model condition and one English sentence. Separate observation from mechanism; no experiment or changed care is needed.
