## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

树没有心脏，叶子却能收到根来的水。叶子造出的糖还能去地下；春天，地下储备也能支持新芽。选一种货物，找它的来源和目的地。

### English

A tree has no heart, yet leaves receive root water. Leaf-made sugars can reach underground parts; stored materials can later support buds. Choose a cargo and find its source and destination.

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

预测糖是否只能向下运输，地下储备能否成为来源。

### English

Predict whether sugars only travel downward and whether underground stores can become sources.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

蓝线标木质部，金线标韧皮部。中央两列是运输功能放大图，并非所有植物茎的真实截面。

### English

Blue marks xylem and gold marks phloem. The two central lanes enlarge functions, not the actual cross-section of every stem.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

选水、成熟叶输出糖或储存根输出糖，再逐步五站。看水到叶后变为水蒸气；看糖的源与库改变，进入不同方向的韧皮部路线。

### English

Choose water, mature-leaf sugar or stored-root sugar and follow five stops. See water become vapour after reaching leaves; source and sink changes select differently directed phloem routes.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**水路线通常向上。** 木质部的导管与管胞提供相连通路，许多导水细胞成熟时不再有活的细胞内容物。叶肉细胞壁上的水蒸发，水蒸气经气孔等离开；水分子之间的内聚力帮助连续水柱传递张力，支持根来的水向上。茎也靠运输组织加强支持。

**糖路线看源与库。** 成熟光合叶可输出有机物，生长根、芽和果实可接收。储存器官释放储备时也能成为源。韧皮部以蔗糖等形式输送有机物，筛管中的压力差推动从源到库的整体流动。

**同一株可有不同方向。** 不同筛管路线可同时通向上方或下方；这不是每粒糖在一根管中同时双向主流。水是糖运输液的重要部分，两种组织的功能区分也不意味着完全隔离。

### English

**Water commonly travels upward.** Xylem vessels and tracheids form connected pathways; many conducting cells lose living contents at maturity. Evaporation at mesophyll walls and vapour loss through stomata and other routes create tension. Cohesion between water molecules helps transmit tension through connected water columns, supporting upward root water transport. Transport tissues also support stems.

**Sugar routes depend on source and sink.** Mature photosynthetic leaves can export organic materials; growing roots, buds and fruits can receive them. Stores can become sources when mobilising reserves. Phloem carries sucrose and other organic materials; pressure differences support bulk flow from source to sink.

**One plant can have different directions.** Separate sieve-tube routes may carry toward upper and lower sinks. That is not simultaneous opposing bulk flow in one tube. Water is part of phloem sap, and different tissue functions do not imply complete isolation.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

没有心脏不等于没有推动条件。糖不永远向下，根不永远是库；模型播放速度不是实际流速。

### English

No heart does not mean no driving conditions. Sugar is not always downward, roots are not always sinks, and playback speed is not sap speed.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

源处装载糖与库处卸载，影响筛管内溶质和压力。伴胞支持活的筛管分子；不同植物装载方式不同。根压有时参与木质部流动，但不能独自解释高大树木的全部输水。本课不计算压力或树高。

### English

Loading at sources and unloading at sinks affect solutes and pressure. Companion cells support living sieve-tube elements, and loading mechanisms vary. Root pressure sometimes contributes to xylem flow but cannot alone explain all transport in tall trees. No pressure or height is calculated.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

看已有叶片的叶脉、胡萝卜的储存根或正在长的新芽。可记录形状，运输组织和糖方向则是机制推断；无需切割或染色。

### English

Observe veins in an existing leaf, a carrot storage root or a growing bud. Record visible forms; tissue identity and sugar direction are mechanistic inferences. No cutting or dye is needed.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

试说：Xylem carries water. Phloem connects a source to a sink. A storage organ can become a source.

### English

Try: “Xylem carries water. Phloem connects a source to a sink. A storage organ can become a source.”

## Check / 挑战自己

### 中文

先找货物，再找组织、源与库；不要仅凭上下位置猜路线。

### English

Identify cargo, tissue, source and sink rather than guessing from height alone.

## Mission / 今日小任务

### 中文

画水的根→茎→叶路线；再画叶→根或储存根→新芽的糖路线，写明组织、源与库。

### English

Draw root → stem → leaf water, then leaf → root or storage root → bud sugar. Name tissue, source and sink.
