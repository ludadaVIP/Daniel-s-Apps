## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

豌豆花最后能长出果荚，里面有新植物的起点。但蜜蜂来过不等于已经成功。跟着一颗花粉到柱头，再把镜头交给花粉管和精细胞。

### English

A pea flower can develop a pod containing beginnings of new plants, yet a bee visit does not guarantee success. Follow a pollen grain to the stigma, then pass the camera to its tube and sperm cells.

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

预测不相容花粉即使停在柱头上，是否一定能长出种子。

### English

Predict whether incompatible pollen on a stigma must lead to seeds.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**先递送花粉。** 花药产生花粉，花粉到柱头是传粉。动物、风或其他方式可协助，也有自花传粉；豌豆通常可自花传粉，因此蜜蜂不是必需的唯一渠道。

**再递送生殖细胞。** 相容花粉在合适条件下萌发，花粉管经花柱朝胚珠生长，递送精细胞。精细胞与卵融合形成受精卵，这是受精的一部分，不是花粉整个变成胚。

**成功发育才有下一代起点。** 受精卵分裂形成胚，胚珠发育成种子，子房可发育成果实；豌豆的果荚就是果实。传粉、受精和后续结实是不同关卡，均不能用“看见一朵花”保证。

### English

**Deliver pollen first.** Anthers produce pollen; arrival at a stigma is pollination. Animals, wind and other routes can help, and self-pollination also occurs. Peas commonly self-pollinate, so bees are not the sole necessary channel.

**Then deliver reproductive cells.** Compatible pollen germinates under suitable conditions. A tube grows through the style toward an ovule and delivers sperm cells. Sperm–egg fusion forms a zygote as part of fertilisation; the whole grain does not become an embryo.

**Development creates a new beginning.** The zygote divides to form an embryo, an ovule develops into a seed and the ovary can develop into a fruit. A pea pod is a fruit. Pollination, fertilisation and fruit/seed development are separate stages, none guaranteed by seeing a flower.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

花粉不是种子，蜜蜂不是受精卵。花朵鲜艳不证明已结实；花粉到达也不保证相容、受精或种子有活力。

### English

Pollen is not a seed, and a bee is not a zygote. Bright flowers do not prove fruit set; arrival does not guarantee compatibility, fertilisation or seed viability.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

被子植物有双受精：一个精细胞与卵融合形成受精卵，另一个参与与中央细胞融合，形成胚乳的起点。胚乳可供发育营养；不同种子成熟时储存位置不同，豌豆主要在子叶。主图只追踪前一条，不把后一条说成不存在。

### English

Angiosperms have double fertilisation: one sperm fuses with the egg to form a zygote, and another fuses with the central cell to initiate endosperm. Endosperm can nourish development; mature stores vary, with peas storing mainly in cotyledons. The main graphic tracks the first fusion without denying the second.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

看已有食物中的豌豆荚或豆粒，或者观察一朵花与访花动物，不采摘。记录直接看到的部位与行为，再写一句可能解释，保留未确认环节。

### English

Look at an existing pea pod or peas, or observe a flower and visitor without picking it. Record visible parts and behaviour, then offer an explanation while keeping unconfirmed stages explicit.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

试说：Pollination delivers pollen. A pollen tube delivers sperm cells. An ovule can develop into a seed.

### English

Try: “Pollination delivers pollen. A pollen tube delivers sperm cells. An ovule can develop into a seed.”

## Check / 挑战自己

### 中文

每题先确认当前阶段：到达、管生长、融合，还是发育。

### English

Identify the stage: landing, tube growth, fusion or development.

## Mission / 今日小任务

### 中文

写花药→柱头→花粉管→精与卵融合→种子；把“到达”与“成功发育”圈成不同阶段。

### English

Write anther → stigma → pollen tube → sperm–egg fusion → seed, marking arrival and successful development separately.
