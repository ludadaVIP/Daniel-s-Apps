## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

窗台植物的叶在土壤上方，水却常从地下进入。把视线缩到一根根毛：没有嘴，也没有水泵，水为什么会跨进来？

### English

Leaves stand above the soil, yet water often enters underground. Zoom into a root hair: without a mouth or pump, why does water cross into it?

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**细小突起，大接触面。** 根毛是根表皮细胞的一部分，可接触土壤颗粒间的水。细胞壁允许水通过，细胞膜具有选择性；水可经膜和水通道蛋白移动。

**有条件的净方向。** 通常吸水时，土壤的总水势高于根细胞，水净进入。两侧相同不等于分子停止；外侧总水势较低时，水也可能净流出。水势同时受溶质、压力和土壤束缚等影响，不能只看“水多不多”。

**接力进入运输组织。** 水穿过皮层，可沿细胞壁间或细胞内部等多条路线前行。内皮层的凯氏带阻挡绕过细胞膜的壁间路线，使进入中央运输区的材料需要经过膜的选择性环节；矿质离子的运输不能等同于水的渗透。

### English

**A small extension adds contact.** A root hair is part of an epidermal cell, contacting water between soil particles. The wall allows water through; the membrane is selective. Water can cross membranes and aquaporins.

**Net direction has conditions.** During usual uptake, soil total water potential is higher than that of root cells. Equal potential does not stop molecular motion, and lower outside potential can allow net outward flow. Solutes, pressure and soil binding all influence potential; visible wetness alone is insufficient.

**Routes join transport tissues.** Water crosses cortex through wall spaces, cell interiors and other routes. The endodermal Casparian strip interrupts wall-space bypass, requiring a selective membrane crossing into the central transport region. Mineral-ion transport differs from water osmosis.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

根毛不是吸管口；根不把土壤“吃”成糖。面积更大增加接触机会，不决定水势差，也不保证健康或吸水速率。

### English

Root hairs are not straw mouths; roots do not eat soil to make sugar. More area adds contact, not a guaranteed gradient, uptake rate or health outcome.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

凯氏带阻断的是特定壁间通路，不是完全挡住所有水。图只画一条跨膜路线，省略完整根截面、周鞘和所有并行通道。根细胞吸收部分矿质离子会用到能量，而水的渗透本身是被动过程。

### English

The Casparian strip blocks a particular wall-space route, not all water. The diagram follows one membrane route, omitting the full cross-section, pericycle and parallel paths. Uptake of some mineral ions uses energy, while osmosis itself is passive.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

从已有盆栽外观记一条观察：叶片挺立或下垂、表土颜色、观察时间。不要挖根或改变照料；把“根内发生渗透”单列为解释，不冒充看见分子。

### English

Record an existing plant’s posture, surface-soil appearance and time without digging or changing care. List root osmosis as an explanation, not a directly seen molecule.
