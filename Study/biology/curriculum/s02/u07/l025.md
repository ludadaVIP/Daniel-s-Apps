## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

一格脸颊细胞，没有嘴也没有胃，怎样维持自己？

### English

How can a cheek cell maintain itself without a mouth or stomach?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选择你的解释，预测不评分。留意“细胞核做所有工作”是否遗漏了协作。

### English

Choose an unscored initial explanation. Consider whether “the nucleus does everything” leaves out cooperation.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

选择膜、细胞质、核、线粒体、核糖体；图和名称按钮都可操作。转90°后再找同一部位。模型中的相对位置、颜色、数量与大小是教学安排，不是口腔细胞实测。

### English

Select membrane, cytoplasm, nucleus, mitochondria or ribosomes using the diagram or name buttons. Turn 90° and find the same part. Relative positions, colours, counts and sizes are teaching choices, not cheek-cell measurements.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

播放五站结构导览，暂停、下一步、重播都保留。观察转动后的结构仍做同一类工作：位置不是功能。图中不展示真正三维切面，也不模拟活细胞的转动或代谢速率。

### English

Play a five-stop structural tour, with pause, step and replay. Parts keep their jobs after rotation: position is not function. This is neither a true 3D section nor a simulation of living-cell rotation or metabolic rates.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**从边界开始。** 口腔内表面由许多细胞组成。每格细胞膜把内部与外界分开，又允许适合的物质通过；它不会有意识地挑选。不同物质的交换受膜结构、通道和条件影响，下一单元再深入。

**内部不是空水袋。** 细胞质包含含水的液体、细胞内结构与许多反应。图中的淡色背景表示这个区域，不代表液体是这个颜色，也不代表没有画出的地方完全空着。

**信息、组装与能量分工。** 典型口腔上皮细胞的核保存大部分DNA。信息可通过副本参与调节蛋白质制造；核不是会思考的大脑。核糖体把氨基酸组装成蛋白质，线粒体承担有氧细胞呼吸的重要步骤，把养分中的化学能转移到ATP等可用形式。这几类工作互相支持。

**模型比一张普通光学图能画得更多。** 经过合适制备的光学细胞图可能显示细胞轮廓和核，但通常不能清楚分辨小核糖体。模型把研究支持的结构画出来，不等于你用这张图实际看见每种结构正在工作。

**典型并不等于全部。** 动物细胞的形状和结构配置因工作而异。成熟人的红细胞没有细胞核和线粒体，本课选的典型上皮细胞不能替代这个例外。

### English

**Start at the boundary.** Many cells line the mouth. Each membrane separates inside from outside while allowing suitable exchanges. It does not make conscious choices: its structures, channels and conditions affect passage. The next unit explores this further.

**Inside is not an empty water bag.** Cytoplasm contains watery fluid, structures and many reactions. The pale background represents a region, not a literal liquid colour or evidence that unpictured spaces are empty.

**Information, assembly and energy have different jobs.** A typical oral epithelial cell’s nucleus stores most DNA. Information copies help regulate protein production; the nucleus is not a thinking brain. Ribosomes assemble amino acids into proteins. Mitochondria carry out major stages of aerobic respiration, transferring nutrient energy into usable forms such as ATP. These jobs support one another.

**A model can show more than an ordinary light image resolves.** Suitable preparation may reveal cell outlines and a nucleus, while tiny ribosomes usually cannot be clearly resolved. The model depicts structures supported by research; it is not a direct observation of each process working.

**Typical does not mean universal.** Animal cells vary with their jobs. Mature human red blood cells lack nuclei and mitochondria. This representative epithelial model does not describe that exception.

## Misconception / 一个常见误区

### 中文

误区：“细胞核是脑袋，其他部分只是装饰。”核提供与调节信息，不能代替膜的交换、核糖体的组装或线粒体的能量转移。把图转过来，各部分名称与工作关系仍不变。

### English

Misconception: “The nucleus is a brain and everything else decoration.” Information regulation does not replace exchange, assembly or energy transfer. Turning the drawing preserves each structure–job relationship.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

DNA不是只存在于核：线粒体也有少量自己的DNA。核糖体不是有膜的小袋子；“细胞器”的用法在教材中略有不同，本课把核糖体称为细胞内功能结构。细胞膜与细胞质也是重要部分，不必把每一项都叫器官。

### English

DNA is not confined to the nucleus: mitochondria have a small genome too. Ribosomes are not membrane-bound bags; usage of “organelle” varies between textbooks. Here they are intracellular functional structures. Membrane and cytoplasm are important parts without being organs.

## Apply / 迁移到生活

### 中文

朋友说：“我的显微镜没看见线粒体，所以动物细胞没有线粒体。”你会补问观察工具、制备与分辨能力的什么信息？指出“没看清”与“证明没有”的差别。

### English

A friend says, “I saw no mitochondria with my microscope, so animal cells lack them.” What would you ask about the tool, preparation and resolution? Distinguish failure to resolve from evidence of absence.

## Vocabulary / 术语

### 中文

细胞膜 cell membrane；细胞质 cytoplasm；细胞核 nucleus；线粒体 mitochondrion（复数mitochondria）；核糖体 ribosome；DNA 遗传信息分子。用“部位→工作”连接，不只抄名字。

### English

Cell membrane; cytoplasm; nucleus; mitochondrion (plural mitochondria); ribosome; DNA. Link each part to a job rather than only copying names.
