## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

洋葱鳞叶是植物的一部分，为什么细胞图没有绿色小叶绿体？

### English

Why can an onion bulb cell be a plant cell without little green chloroplasts?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先判断“所有植物细胞都有叶绿体”和“壁代替膜”是否合理。预测不评分。

### English

Consider whether every plant cell has chloroplasts and whether a wall replaces a membrane. Predictions are unscored.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

比较绿色叶肉细胞、典型地下洋葱鳞叶表皮细胞与典型动物细胞。先找共同部分，再找壁、液泡与叶绿体。转动改变图的位置，不改变细胞类型。

### English

Compare green mesophyll, typical underground onion bulb epidermis and a typical animal cell. Find shared parts before walls, vacuoles and chloroplasts. Rotation changes orientation rather than cell type.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

选择叶肉或洋葱鳞叶模型，点击名称或图上部位。导览会按该模型实际包含的结构逐步展开；洋葱模型不画叶绿体。可再切换动物细胞作对照。

### English

Select mesophyll or bulb epidermis and inspect parts by names or diagram. The tour visits the depicted structures; the bulb model omits chloroplasts. Switch to an animal cell for comparison.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**两道边界，作用不同。** 植物细胞壁在细胞膜外侧，含纤维素，帮助支持细胞。膜仍位于里面，参与选择性物质交换。壁不是一层完全不透的包装，也不代替膜。

**植物仍有共同细胞工作。** 图中的叶肉与鳞叶表皮细胞都包含膜、细胞质、核、线粒体与核糖体。活的植物细胞要维持结构、制造蛋白质并获取可用能量，不是有光就能省去线粒体。

**大的中央区域不是空气洞。** 成熟植物细胞常有一个大中央液泡，里面是细胞液，由膜包围。液泡可储存物质，液泡与细胞中的水分状态参与产生膨压，与壁一起支持柔嫩部分。具体水分进出机制留到渗透一课。

**叶绿体随位置和工作而异。** 绿色叶肉细胞中的叶绿体进行光合作用。典型地下洋葱鳞叶的表皮通常没有叶绿体；鳞叶是储存器官的一部分。根中的许多细胞也没有叶绿体。不能从“属于植物”直接推出每格都能光合作用。

**图是比较工具，不是完美身份证。** 真细胞不都方方正正，叶绿体数量、液泡大小和核的位置也有变化。来源、结构证据与观察条件比单凭颜色或外形可靠。

### English

**Two boundaries have different jobs.** A plant wall lies outside the membrane, contains cellulose and helps support the cell. The inner membrane still regulates exchange selectively. A wall is not impermeable packaging and does not replace the membrane.

**Plants retain shared cellular jobs.** Both modelled plant cells contain membrane, cytoplasm, nucleus, mitochondria and ribosomes. Living plant cells maintain structures, make proteins and obtain usable energy; having light does not remove their need for mitochondria.

**The large central space is not air.** Mature plant cells often contain a large central vacuole with cell sap, enclosed by a membrane. It stores substances; water status contributes to turgor pressure, which works with walls to support soft parts. A later osmosis lesson explains water movement.

**Chloroplasts depend on location and role.** Green mesophyll chloroplasts carry out photosynthesis. Typical epidermis from an underground onion bulb usually lacks them; bulb scales are part of a storage organ. Many root cells lack chloroplasts too. Plant identity does not imply photosynthesis in every cell.

**A drawing is a comparison tool, not a perfect identity card.** Real cells are not all rectangles. Chloroplast numbers, vacuole size and nuclear position vary. Source, structural evidence and observation conditions are stronger than colour or shape alone.

## Misconception / 一个常见误区

### 中文

误区：“没有叶绿体就不是植物细胞。”地下洋葱鳞叶和绿色叶肉提供反例：共同结构仍在，工作配置不同。也别把中央液泡叫空白或细胞核。

### English

Misconception: “No chloroplasts means not a plant cell.” Bulb epidermis and green mesophyll share structures while differing in jobs. The central vacuole is neither blank space nor the nucleus.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

细胞壁也不是植物独有：面包酵母属于真菌，也有壁而没有叶绿体。本课只比较三种代表细胞，不能把“有壁”当成所有生物分类的唯一规则。

液泡和叶绿体也有膜，但它们的膜不等于包围整个细胞的细胞膜。叶绿体有少量自己的DNA。植物壁外还有相邻细胞及连接；本图只画独立一格，省略连接和许多内部结构。

### English

Walls are not unique to plants: baking yeast is a fungus with a wall but no chloroplasts. These three profiles cannot turn wall presence into a universal classification rule.

Vacuoles and chloroplasts have membranes distinct from the membrane surrounding the whole cell. Chloroplasts contain a small genome too. Real walls adjoin neighbouring cells and connections; this isolated model omits those and many internal structures.

## Apply / 迁移到生活

### 中文

同学只按“方的属于植物、圆的属于动物”分类。用本课证据改写他的规则，并说明一张简化图仍不能证明哪些活动正在进行。

### English

A classmate classifies square cells as plants and round cells as animals. Improve the rule using structural evidence and state which activities a simplified drawing cannot establish.

## Vocabulary / 术语

### 中文

细胞壁 cell wall；叶绿体 chloroplast；液泡 vacuole；叶肉 mesophyll；纤维素 cellulose。说出壁在膜外、液泡有细胞液、叶绿体与光合作用相关。

### English

Cell wall; chloroplast; vacuole; mesophyll; cellulose. Explain wall outside membrane, sap inside vacuole and chloroplasts associated with photosynthesis.

## Check / 检索与反馈

### 中文

四题检查壁与膜、洋葱反例、可靠比较方法与vacuole。资料来源与细胞类型必须一起考虑。

### English

Four questions check wall versus membrane, the onion counterexample, reliable comparison and vacuole. Consider source together with cell type.

## Mission / 生活探究

### 中文

以现有洋葱或照片与绿叶作生活对照，画出两种细胞的共同结构和一个差别；细胞层次的结论标为借助模型，不声称裸眼看见叶绿体。

### English

Compare an existing onion or photo with a green leaf. Sketch shared cell structures and one difference, labelled model-based rather than claiming naked-eye chloroplast observations.
