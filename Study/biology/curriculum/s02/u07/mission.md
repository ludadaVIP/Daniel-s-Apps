# 两格细胞的比较地图 / A two-cell comparison map

### 中文

在课中典型动物、绿色叶肉、地下洋葱鳞叶表皮模型中选两格。纸上画或用文字描述都可以。

1. 标出至少三种共有结构，给膜、核、线粒体、核糖体中的两种写作用。
2. 用不同颜色标出一个差异：壁、液泡或叶绿体；写清细胞类型，不能只说“所有植物都一样”。
3. 用“我从模型知道……，这能解释……，但还不能证明……”写证据与推理。普通光学图通常不能分辨核糖体，模型不提供真实活动速率。
4. 用英语写一个简单比较句，如“Both model cells have …, but …”。不自动评英语成绩。

例：叶肉模型有叶绿体，典型地下鳞叶表皮模型没有；两者仍有膜和线粒体。模型解释不同工作配置，不能单凭方形就判断某张真实图的来源。

无需显微镜、染色或身体采样；可用现有洋葱和叶片照片作外观对照，把直接看见与借助模型理解分开。

### English

Choose two lesson models: typical animal cell, green mesophyll or underground onion bulb epidermis. Sketch on paper or describe in words.

1. Label at least three shared parts and explain two jobs among membrane, nucleus, mitochondria and ribosomes.
2. Mark one difference involving wall, vacuole or chloroplasts. Specify cell types rather than treating all plants as identical.
3. Write “The model shows …, this helps explain …, but does not establish …”. Ordinary light microscopy usually cannot resolve ribosomes; models give no real activity rates.
4. Add a simple English comparison: “Both model cells have …, but …”. English is not automatically graded.

For example, green mesophyll has chloroplasts while typical underground bulb epidermis lacks them; both retain membranes and mitochondria. The models explain different job configurations, but rectangular shape alone cannot identify a real image’s source.

No microscope, staining or body sampling is needed. Compare an existing onion and leaf photo externally, distinguishing direct observations from model-based understanding.
