## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

一个会光合作用的细胞，能替代整片叶子吗？

### English

Can one photosynthesising cell replace a whole leaf?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

选择初始解释，不评分。特别区分“一种功能”与“器官的全部合作”。

### English

Choose an unscored explanation, distinguishing one job from the full cooperation of an organ.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

跟随叶肉细胞→叶肉组织→叶片→植物，或吸收细胞→上皮组织→小肠→人体。这是组成与尺度关系，不是同一细胞变成整株植物或整个人。

### English

Follow mesophyll cell → mesophyll tissue → leaf → plant, or absorptive cell → epithelium → intestine → person. These are organisation and scale, not one cell becoming a whole organism.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

选择叶片或小肠路线，播放或点击四个层次。细胞图只选一个代表；组织图只展示部分细胞；器官图必须结合不同工作的文字线索理解。模型不按真实比例。

### English

Select leaf or intestine, then play or select four levels. One cell is representative; tissue shows only some cells. Read multiple cooperating jobs alongside the organ image. Models are not to scale.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**细胞有适合工作的结构。** 绿色叶肉细胞的叶绿体帮助光合作用，小肠吸收细胞的表面有细小突起，增加与肠内物质接触的面积。特化不意味着一个细胞只做一件事：维持边界与获取能量等基本工作仍需要协作。

**组织不是一堆随意放在一起的细胞。** 相关细胞与周围物质组成具有共同功能的结构。叶肉参与大量光合作用，上皮组织形成覆盖或内部衬里。组织内的细胞不必每个都完全相同；结构、连接与周围材料也重要。

**器官由多种组织组成。** 叶片不只有叶肉。表皮提供保护与内外界面，气孔参与气体交换，木质部与韧皮部参与物质运输。气孔不是一种独立组织，而是表皮上的结构。不同组织让一片叶的制糖活动与整株植物联系起来。

**小肠同样是合作结构。** 上皮参与吸收，肌肉组织帮助推动和混合内容物，结缔组织与血管提供支持和运输，神经组织参与协调。吸收细胞不能单独代替一个小肠，更不能从“看见一种细胞”证明整个器官的所有功能正常。

**回到个体。** 植物的叶、茎、根彼此依赖，人体的消化、循环等系统也互相联系。四层路线只是本课聚焦的组成关系，中间还可讨论器官系统；它不是忽略系统存在的完整层级清单。

### English

**Cells have structures suited to jobs.** Chloroplasts support photosynthesis in green mesophyll. Tiny surface projections on intestinal absorptive cells increase contact area with intestinal contents. Specialisation does not remove shared jobs such as boundary maintenance and energy use.

**A tissue is more than a random pile.** Related cells and surrounding material form a structure with associated functions. Mesophyll contributes much photosynthesis; epithelia form coverings or linings. Tissue cells need not be identical: connections, organisation and surrounding material matter too.

**An organ contains different tissues.** A leaf includes mesophyll, epidermis and vascular tissue. The epidermis provides protection and an interface; stomata participate in gas exchange. Xylem and phloem transport substances. Stomata are epidermal structures rather than a separate tissue type. Cooperation connects sugar-making with the rest of the plant.

**The intestine cooperates similarly.** Epithelium participates in absorption, muscle mixes and moves contents, connective tissue and blood vessels support and transport, and nervous tissue helps coordinate. One absorptive cell cannot replace an intestine. Seeing one cell type does not prove every organ function is normal.

**Return to the organism.** Leaves, stems and roots depend on one another; digestive and circulatory systems cooperate too. The four-stop route highlights selected organisational levels. Organ systems can be discussed between organs and organisms; the route is not an exhaustive hierarchy that denies them.

## Misconception / 一个常见误区

### 中文

误区：“能制糖的组织就是一片叶；细胞器是很小的器官。”叶肉组织只有部分工作，叶片还有其他组织。叶绿体在细胞内，叶片由组织组成，两者所属层次不同。

### English

Misconception: “Photosynthetic tissue is a whole leaf; an organelle is a tiny body organ.” Mesophyll contributes only part of a leaf’s work. A chloroplast is inside a cell, while a leaf contains tissues: different levels.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

多细胞生物中的细胞需要联系、协调与物质交换，器官不是细胞简单加起来的名字。显微图观察一个局部，需要谨慎连接到整个器官；结构证据可以提出功能解释，但功能往往还需实验或其他观察。

### English

Multicellular cells communicate, coordinate and exchange materials. Organs are not merely a name for adding cells. A microscopic view samples only a local region; structural evidence suggests functional explanations, which often need experiments or further observations.

## Apply / 迁移到生活

### 中文

若只取一小块叶肉组织，你预期还缺哪些完整叶片的协作条件？选保护、交换或运输中的两项，说明它们怎样支持光合作用，而不只是列两个名词。

### English

If you take a small piece of mesophyll, what cooperation of a whole leaf is missing? Choose two of protection, exchange or transport and explain how they support photosynthesis rather than only naming them.

## Vocabulary / 术语

### 中文

特化 specialisation；组织 tissue；器官 organ；上皮组织 epithelial tissue；运输组织 vascular tissue。叶片是organ，叶绿体是organelle。

### English

Specialisation; tissue; organ; epithelial tissue; vascular tissue. A leaf is an organ; a chloroplast is an organelle.

## Check / 检索与反馈

### 中文

四题检查叶片多组织协作、小肠组成路线、单一功能证据的限制，以及organ/organelle区别。

### English

Four questions check leaf tissue cooperation, intestine organisation, limits of one-function evidence and organ versus organelle.

## Mission / 生活探究

### 中文

画四层路线，并给器官标出至少两类工作。可以使用课中图或已有叶片照片，不采集身体组织、不破坏植物。

### English

Sketch four levels and at least two organ jobs using lesson diagrams or an existing leaf photo. No body-tissue collection or plant damage is needed.
