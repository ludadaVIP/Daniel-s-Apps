## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

夜晚叶片看起来安静。细胞里的所有工作也停了吗？

### English

A leaf looks quiet at night. Does every cellular job stop?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选择夜晚的解释，不评分。记住：没有光与没有已有养分不是一回事。

### English

Choose an unscored night-time explanation. Lack of light differs from lack of existing nutrients.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

信息副本从核到核糖体，能量支持来自线粒体；两类箭头不是同一种东西。叶绿体负责需光的制糖任务，不负责所有细胞工作。

### English

An information copy connects nucleus to ribosomes, while energy support comes from mitochondria. These arrows represent different things. Chloroplasts support light-dependent sugar-making, not every cellular job.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

选择叶肉、洋葱鳞叶或动物细胞，再逐步检查边界、白天、夜晚与组装线索。前两帧代表有光，后两帧代表无光；不是测出的真实一天。模型固定假定细胞活着，氧、已有糖、氨基酸及其他条件适宜。

### English

Choose mesophyll, bulb epidermis or an animal cell. Step through boundary, day, night and assembly clues. The first two frames have light; the last two do not. This is not a measured day. Cells are assumed viable with oxygen, existing sugar, amino acids and other suitable conditions.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**先把三条线分开。** 原料是能组成新物质的东西，信息规定如何组装，能量支持过程进行。它们不是三种名字相同的“燃料”。膜参与交换，细胞质是许多反应与结构所在的区域。

**制造蛋白质需要合作。** 核内DNA中的信息可通过RNA副本参与指导核糖体。核糖体把氨基酸连接成蛋白质；线粒体参与供应可用能量。这个入门图省略内质网、高尔基体以及信息调节的许多步骤。不是把整个DNA搬出核，也不是先做完一种工作才轮到另一种。

**植物也进行细胞呼吸。** 线粒体承担有氧细胞呼吸的重要步骤，养分中的化学能转移到ATP等形式。能量并非无中生有，ATP也不是细胞吃的午饭。细胞呼吸的某些步骤发生在细胞质，不应说全部过程都只在一个小椭圆里。

**光合作用与呼吸的条件不同。** 绿色叶肉的叶绿体在适宜光照下可利用水与二氧化碳制造糖。没有光时这项需光活动不能照常进行；若已有糖、氧和其他条件适宜，细胞仍可呼吸并支持蛋白质更新。白天也会呼吸，不是“白天制糖，夜晚才呼吸”的开关。

**比较不同细胞。** 洋葱鳞叶模型和典型动物细胞都没有叶绿体，但仍有线粒体与核糖体。课中亮起的卡表示模型条件允许一类作用，不提供速率或产量，也不证明任意真实细胞正在做相同强度的工作。

### English

**Separate three routes.** Materials build new substances, information guides assembly and energy supports processes. They are not interchangeable fuels. Membranes participate in exchange, while many reactions and structures lie in the cytoplasm.

**Protein production needs cooperation.** RNA copies of nuclear DNA information help guide ribosomes, which join amino acids into proteins. Mitochondria help supply usable energy. This introductory diagram omits endoplasmic reticulum, Golgi apparatus and many regulatory steps. Whole DNA is not moved out, and these jobs need not happen one after another.

**Plants respire too.** Mitochondria perform major stages of aerobic respiration, transferring nutrient chemical energy into forms such as ATP. Energy is not created from nothing; ATP is not a meal. Some respiration stages occur in cytoplasm, so the whole process is not confined to one little oval.

**Photosynthesis and respiration need different conditions.** In suitable light, mesophyll chloroplasts can use water and carbon dioxide to make sugars. Without light, light-dependent activity cannot continue as usual. With existing sugar, oxygen and other suitable conditions, respiration and protein renewal can continue. Respiration happens by day too, rather than switching on only at night.

**Compare cell types.** Modelled bulb epidermis and typical animal cells lack chloroplasts but retain mitochondria and ribosomes. An active card means the assumed conditions allow a job, not a rate, yield or proof that any real cell works at the same intensity.

## Misconception / 一个常见误区

### 中文

误区：“植物靠叶绿体，动物靠线粒体，双方各选一种。”叶肉细胞同时有两者：制糖与把已有养分的能量转成可用形式是不同工作。夜晚模型不制造新糖，仍可利用已有糖。

### English

Misconception: “Plants use chloroplasts and animals use mitochondria, one each.” Mesophyll has both. Sugar-making differs from making nutrient energy usable. The night model uses existing sugar without new photosynthetic production.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

ATP可快速参与能量转移，但不是长期大量储能的唯一形式。真实细胞可调节蛋白质种类与活动，信息不等于“核永远命令每个动作”。本课没有模拟温度、底物消耗或氧供应变化，因此不能据此算生长或呼吸速度。

### English

ATP participates in rapid energy transfer rather than serving as the sole long-term bulk energy store. Cells regulate protein types and activity; information is not a nucleus commanding every movement. Temperature, substrate depletion and changing oxygen are not simulated, so growth and respiration rates cannot be calculated.

## Apply / 迁移到生活

### 中文

观察夜晚的绿叶，解释“外观没动”不能推出“细胞没活动”。写清推理需要的条件，再说明为什么图中的亮卡不能告诉你真实叶片每分钟用了多少糖。

### English

Explain why a still night-time leaf does not imply inactive cells. State needed conditions and why active cards cannot reveal real sugar use per minute.

### English
