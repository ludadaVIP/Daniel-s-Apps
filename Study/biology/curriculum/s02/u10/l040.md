## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

想象拿起一本书：肌肉细胞利用材料和能量，但肺不会直接把一口空气送进手臂。追踪氧、葡萄糖和CO₂碳，检查少了哪条连接会改变新递送。

### English

Imagine lifting a book. Muscle cells use materials and energy, yet lungs do not send a pocket of air into an arm. Trace oxygen, glucose and CO₂ carbon; inspect how one missing link changes new delivery.

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

### English

Are oxygen in lungs, materials in blood and cellular use the same level of evidence?

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**来源不同，细胞会合。** 本例葡萄糖由消化和吸收进入血，先经肝；氧由肺泡交换进入血。循环将两者运到组织，经组织间液和细胞膜到细胞。细胞内部的有氧反应支持ATP再生成，ATP反应与工作耦联，并伴随散热。

**回程同样依赖连接。** CO₂碳可经血液从组织返回右心、肺，再形成CO₂跨进肺泡并呼出。吸入的氧不是简单变成CO₂里的碳；碳来自有机物。

**一条图线只能说明一条路线。** 隐藏新吸收不会删除已有血中养分与储备。隐藏肺交换或运输表示本模型所选完整新递送受限，不计算实际存量、ATP速率、调节与健康后果。

### English

**Different sources meet at cells.** Here glucose enters blood through digestion and absorption and first passes the liver. Oxygen enters through alveolar exchange. Circulation delivers both to tissue, followed by tissue-fluid and membrane crossing. Aerobic cellular reactions support ATP regeneration; ATP reactions couple to work with heat transfer.

**Return links also matter.** CO₂ carbon travels from tissue through blood and right heart to lung, reforms CO₂ and crosses into alveolar air for exhalation. Inhaled oxygen does not become CO₂ carbon: that carbon comes from organic compounds.

**One diagram link describes one route.** Hiding new absorption does not erase nutrients already in blood or reserves. Hiding exchange or transport limits the selected complete new-delivery route, without calculating real inventories, ATP rates, regulation or health outcomes.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

刚吃的午饭不是活动唯一能量来源；已有储备没有在图里画全。肺通气、气体交换、血液运输和细胞呼吸不能用一个“呼吸”词全部代替。

### English

A recent meal is not the only source supporting activity; reserves are not fully shown. Ventilation, exchange, blood transport and cellular respiration are distinct processes.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

循环也送往肝、脑、心肌等组织，运输热与许多其他物质。神经和激素调节多系统合作；本课固定这些条件，不以按钮估算跑步表现或真实身体中断后的时间。

### English

Circulation also serves liver, brain and heart muscle, transporting heat and other materials. Nerves and hormones regulate cooperation; these are fixed rather than using buttons to predict exercise performance or timing after a real interruption.

### English

Choose reading, holding a book or sleeping and explain why cellular maintenance still needs cooperative supply. Sketch without exercise, breath-holding, changed diet or measurements.
