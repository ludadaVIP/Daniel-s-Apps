## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

早餐米饭先经过一条食物通道。血管在通道外面，细胞却需要材料。选一种分子，看看“变小”和“穿过去”是否同一件事。

### English

Breakfast travels through a food passage with vessels outside it. Cells need materials. Choose a molecule and compare becoming smaller with crossing into the body.

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

预测淀粉、蛋白质和代表性纤维是否沿同一路线进入血液。

### English

Predict whether starch, protein and representative fibre enter blood by the same route.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

选择淀粉、蛋白质或代表性纤维，逐步走六站。珠子的连接展示示意性分解；只有葡萄糖与氨基酸支路画跨肠壁进入血液，接着到肝。纤维支路继续进入大肠。

### English

Choose starch, protein or representative fibre and follow six stages. Connected beads illustrate breakdown. Only glucose and amino-acid branches cross the displayed wall into blood, then reach the liver. The fibre branch continues into the large intestine.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**食物通道不等于血液。** 咀嚼和胃肠混合把食物处理成碎块，酶催化化学消化。淀粉消化从口腔开始，小肠内继续，经多种酶作用产生葡萄糖；蛋白质在胃开始较明显的分解，并在小肠继续提供氨基酸。

**吸收需要跨边界。** 小肠绒毛和细胞的微绒毛增加表面。葡萄糖和氨基酸经上皮转运进入毛细血管，先沿门静脉到肝，再经循环分配；不是食物整块进入血液。

**没有走金色支路的不一定没用。** 人的酶不能消化本例纤维；一部分膳食纤维可被大肠微生物发酵。大肠也吸收水和某些物质，余下内容最终形成粪便。

### English

**The food passage is distinct from blood.** Chewing and mixing change physical pieces; enzymes catalyse chemical digestion. Starch breakdown starts in the mouth and continues in the small intestine to glucose through several enzymes. Protein breakdown begins prominently in the stomach and continues in the intestine to amino acids.

**Absorption crosses a boundary.** Villi and microvilli increase surface. Glucose and amino acids cross epithelium into capillaries, travel via portal blood to the liver, and then circulate. Whole pieces of food do not enter blood.

**A different route can still matter.** Human enzymes cannot digest the example fibre. Some dietary fibre is fermented by large-intestinal microbes. The colon also absorbs water and other substances; remaining contents form faeces.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

胃不是唯一消化器官；咬碎不等于分子已经全部分解。吸收不是只靠粒子小就自动穿过，肠壁转运有选择性。

### English

The stomach is not the only digestive organ. Chewing does not complete molecular breakdown, and small size alone does not guarantee crossing: intestinal transport is selective.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

绒毛内有毛细血管和淋巴管。许多长链脂类吸收后重新组装成脂质颗粒，先进入淋巴，再入血；所以本课的葡萄糖路线不能套到全部养分。消化道的推进依靠肌肉活动，不只是重力。

### English

Villi contain blood and lymph vessels. Many absorbed long-chain lipids are packaged into particles and enter lymph before blood, so the glucose route does not apply to every nutrient. Gut propulsion involves muscular action, not gravity alone.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

观察已有食物标签或回想一口米饭，不改变饮食。把“看见碎块”标为直接观察，把“分子变成葡萄糖”标为课程解释。

### English

Look at an existing food label or recall rice without changing your diet. Label visible fragments as observation and molecular breakdown as a lesson-based explanation.

### English

Before answering, ask whether material remains in the food passage or has crossed its wall.
