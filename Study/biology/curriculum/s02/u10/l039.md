## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

想象一辆随血流移动的小观察车。选氧、葡萄糖或CO₂的碳，在每站看它在哪里、怎样被携带。你会经过心脏两侧，却不会从右侧直接穿墙到左侧。

### English

Imagine a small observer travelling with blood. Choose oxygen, glucose or CO₂ carbon and inspect each stop and carrier. The route connects both heart sides without passing directly through the dividing wall.

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

预测肺动脉与肺静脉的方向，看看是否支持“动脉总是氧多”。

### English

Predict pulmonary-vessel directions and test “arteries always have more oxygen”.

## See / 看图寻找线索

### 中文

路线按站连线，不是人体比例图。每条连线旁的文字给出组织与血管，颜色用于区分路线；血液始终是红色系。

### English

Connected stations are not an anatomical scale drawing. Labels identify tissues and vessels; colours distinguish routes while real blood remains shades of red.

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

选三种追踪对象，逐步导览或点站名。氧从肺泡跨入血，经肺静脉、左心和体动脉到组织；CO₂碳沿组织、右心、肺的回程。葡萄糖先沿小肠毛细血管、门静脉到肝，然后随静脉和双循环送往组织。

### English

Choose a tracer, step or select a station. Oxygen crosses from alveolus into blood, then passes pulmonary vein, left heart and systemic artery to tissue. CO₂ carbon returns from tissue through right heart to lung. Glucose first passes gut capillaries and portal blood to liver, then veins and both circuits to tissue.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**血液有不同运输方式。** 大部分氧与红细胞内血红蛋白可逆结合，少部分溶解。葡萄糖溶解在血浆。CO₂的碳大部分转换成碳酸氢根并主要随血浆运输，也有溶解及蛋白结合部分；在肺重新形成可呼出的CO₂。

**心脏推动两条相接循环。** 右心→肺动脉→肺毛细血管→肺静脉→左心；左心→体动脉→组织毛细血管→体静脉→右心。两侧心脏由隔壁分开，不靠直接混合。

**血管名字讲方向。** 动脉离心，静脉回心。肺动脉通常氧较少，肺静脉较多。组织处物质跨毛细血管壁与组织间液到细胞，红细胞不需要进入肌肉细胞才能递送氧。

### English

**Blood uses distinct carriers.** Most oxygen binds red-cell haemoglobin reversibly with a smaller dissolved portion. Glucose dissolves in plasma. Much CO₂ carbon becomes bicarbonate carried mainly in plasma; smaller portions dissolve or bind proteins. At lungs it reforms exhalable CO₂.

**The heart drives connected circuits.** Right heart → pulmonary artery → lung capillaries → pulmonary vein → left heart. Left heart → systemic arteries → tissue capillaries → systemic veins → right heart. A septum separates the heart sides; blood does not take a direct mixing shortcut.

**Vessel names describe direction.** Arteries lead away from the heart and veins toward it. Pulmonary arteries usually carry less oxygen and pulmonary veins more. Materials cross capillary walls and tissue fluid to cells; red cells need not enter muscle cells to deliver oxygen.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

所有血都不是同一种“运氧水”。白细胞参与防御，血小板参与止血，本课不画它们的详细工作。CO₂碳示踪不声称同一个CO₂分子原封不动全程旅行。

### English

Blood is not one uniform oxygen liquid. White cells support defence and platelets support clotting, which are not modelled in detail. Carbon tracking does not claim one unchanged CO₂ molecule travels the whole route.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

心房接收，心室泵出，瓣膜帮助定向。图中把每侧两腔合成一站；门静脉先连接肠与肝的两组毛细血管。肝可处理或储存材料，本路线只跟随仍继续循环的一部分葡萄糖，不说所有吸收糖都会马上进入肌肉。

### English

Atria receive, ventricles pump and valves support one-way flow. Each heart side combines two chambers into one station here. Portal blood connects gut and liver capillary beds. The liver processes or stores material; the route follows a portion of glucose that continues rather than every absorbed molecule immediately reaching muscle.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

用两种箭头画离心与回心，再写肺动脉这个反例。把熟悉的早餐材料与L037的吸收连接起来，无需测脉搏或做运动。

### English

Draw away-from and toward-heart arrows, then use the pulmonary artery as a counterexample. Connect breakfast materials with L037 absorption without pulse measurements or exercise.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### English

Try: “Arteries carry blood away from the heart. Veins return blood. Most oxygen binds haemoglobin.”

### English

## Mission / 今日小任务

### 中文

选氧或CO₂碳，画肺与身体之间的一条路线。标两侧心脏、动静脉方向、毛细血管与运输形式。

### English

Choose oxygen or CO₂ carbon and map a route between lung and body, labelling heart sides, vessel directions, capillaries and carrier forms.
