## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

肺像树枝通向很多微小气囊。先做同体积球模型，再看氧和CO₂为什么能沿不同方向跨过同一片薄壁。

### English

Lung branches lead to many tiny sacs. Compare equal-volume spheres, then see why oxygen and CO₂ can cross one thin wall in different directions.

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

同体积下1个大球与8个半径减半的小球，预测总表面；再判断两种气体的方向。

### English

Predict the total surface of one sphere versus eight half-radius spheres, then the two gases’ directions.

### English

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

选择单球/八球和氧/CO₂；气体页还能选通常、相同或反向分压。后两种是假设比较，不描述你的身体。数值是虚构相对单位p，固定不随时间计算；箭头只示净趋势。

### English

Choose one/eight spheres and oxygen/CO₂. Compare usual, equal or reversed partial pressures. The latter are hypothetical, not personal states. Values are invented relative p units held fixed; arrows show net tendency only.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**很多小气囊提供表面。** 同样总体积拆成较小空间可以增加表面；本例8个半径减半的小球，总体积相等、总表面加倍。真实肺泡相互连接、有共享壁，几何例子说明原则，不能替代真实面积测量。

**气体跨薄屏障。** 肺泡上皮和毛细血管内皮形成很短的交换距离。在通常肺部条件，氧从较高分压的肺泡空气净进入血，CO₂从血净进入肺泡空气。每种气体按自己的分压差判断。

**维持差异需要合作。** 通气更新空气，血流带来和带走物质。没有净箭头不等于分子停止随机运动；若分压相同，两个方向运动的总体净值为零。

### English

**Small sacs provide surface.** Dividing a fixed total volume into smaller spaces can increase surface. Eight half-radius spheres have equal total volume and double surface. Real alveoli connect and share walls; this geometric example cannot replace measured lung surface.

**Gases cross a thin barrier.** Alveolar epithelium and capillary endothelium create a short exchange distance. In usual lung conditions oxygen enters blood from higher-partial-pressure air, while CO₂ enters air from blood. Judge each gas separately.

**Maintaining differences takes cooperation.** Ventilation renews air and blood flow brings and removes materials. No net arrow does not mean molecules stop moving: equal partial pressures give zero net movement while random motion continues.

## Misconception / 容易误会的地方

### 中文

空气不是直接流进血管；氧与CO₂也不是互相变出来的。肺泡气体交换、肺通气和细胞呼吸分别是跨屏障、空气移动和细胞反应。

### English

Air does not flow directly into vessels, and oxygen and CO₂ do not turn into each other here. Exchange, ventilation and cellular respiration mean barrier crossing, air movement and cellular reactions.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

气体溶解性不同，不能把空气与血中同样的“浓度数字”简单相减当分压。肺泡中的表面活性物质帮助稳定气囊；本球模型省略液膜、形变和循环变化。

### English

Different gas solubilities mean air and blood concentration numbers cannot simply substitute for partial pressures. Surfactant helps stabilise alveoli; the sphere model omits liquid films, deformation and circulatory changes.

## Apply / 把知识带回生活

### 中文

看已有植物叶片、纸片折叠或回想U08的形状：表面增加是结构线索，不是所有交换过程的完整证据。无需呼吸训练或测量身体。

### English

Compare a familiar leaf or folded paper with U08 geometry: more surface is a structural clue, not complete evidence for exchange. No breathing exercise or body measurement is needed.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

试说：Ventilation renews air. Gases diffuse across a thin barrier. Each gas follows its own partial-pressure difference.

### English

Try: “Ventilation renews air. Gases diffuse across a thin barrier. Each gas follows its own partial-pressure difference.”

## Check / 挑战自己

### 中文

把分压差与表面分开判断：前者帮助判断方向，后者与交换能力有关。

### English

Separate pressure difference from surface: the first helps determine direction, while surface relates to exchange capacity.

## Mission / 今日小任务

### 中文

为氧画空气→肺泡→薄屏障→血液箭头，为CO₂画反方向。写出表面为何有帮助但不能单独预测速率。

### English

Draw oxygen from air through alveolus and barrier into blood, and CO₂ in reverse. Explain why surface helps without predicting rate alone.
