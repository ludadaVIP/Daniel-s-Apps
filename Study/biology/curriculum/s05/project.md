# 生物学证据报告 / Biology evidence report

## 任务 / Task

### 中文

用给定原创虚构马铃薯数据制作证据报告，不要求切割或做真实实验。四个独立块茎A–D各提供五条，块内随机分配至0、0.2、0.4、0.6、0.8 mol/L蔗糖外液；每条独立杯。共20条/20处理单位、四个生物块、每浓度n=4。每条初终质量配对，不能当作20个独立块茎或40个独立样本。

控制22°C、60 min、每杯50 mL、名义尺寸30×10×10 mm、同批材料和一致擦干；初质量2.90–3.10 g，天平分辨率0.01 g。全部20条原始ID、块身份、浓度、初/终质量在工作台可见。先算100×(终−初)/初，再求四条百分比的均值和min–max，不用总质量比率代替每条百分比均值。

### English

Build a report from the supplied original fictional potato data. No cutting or real experiment is required. Four independent tubers A–D each provide five strips, randomly assigned within block to 0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6 and 0.8 mol/L external sucrose. Each strip has its own cup: 20 strips / 20 treatment units, four biological blocks, n=4 per concentration. Before/after readings belong to the same strip; these are not 20 independent tubers or 40 independent samples.

Conditions are 22°C, 60 min, 50 mL per cup, nominal dimensions 30×10×10 mm, same batch and consistent blotting. Starting masses are 2.90–3.10 g; balance resolution is 0.01 g. All 20 IDs, block identities, concentrations and paired masses are visible. Calculate 100×(after−before)/before for each strip before calculating group means and min–max. A percentage based on total masses is a different weighted quantity.

## 科学边界 / Scientific limits

### 中文

0.2 mol/L组均值为正、0.4为负，可以线性内插约0.30 mol/L的60 min零净质量变化外液浓度。它不是精确胞内蔗糖浓度，不证明已达平衡。解释假设为60 min内蔗糖近似不透膜、其他净质量变化可忽略；水势差驱动水的净移动。质量数据单独不能证明机制，擦干差异、组织损伤或溶质移动均可能影响结果。min–max仅为四条观测值范围，不是置信区间，不报告p值或“显著”。

### English

The mean is positive at 0.2 mol/L and negative at 0.4 mol/L, allowing linear interpolation of about 0.30 mol/L for zero net mass change at 60 min. This is not an exact intracellular sucrose concentration or proof of equilibrium. An osmotic interpretation assumes sucrose is approximately impermeant during 60 min and other net mass changes are negligible; a water-potential difference drives net water movement. Mass alone does not establish mechanism; blotting differences, tissue damage or solute movement may affect the result. Min–max is only the range of four observations, not a confidence interval. No p-values or significance claims are made.
