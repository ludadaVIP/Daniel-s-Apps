# 温室叶片证据报告 / Greenhouse leaf evidence report

## 主张 / Claim

原创模型：20°C、光100、风0，湿度50%时蒸腾5、湿度80%时2相对单位/时；湿度50%把风0→3则5→10。写带条件主张，引用两组数值，解释梯度与边界层，并讨论气孔关闭及吸水计代理局限。联系木质水流、光合CO₂条件和结籽需要后续成功。中文即可，可选英文图题和一句限制。

Original model: at 20°C,light 100,wind 0,loss is 5 at humidity 50% and 2 at 80% relative units/time; at 50% humidity,wind 0→3 changes 5→10. Make a conditional claim with two numerical comparisons, explain gradients/boundary layers, and discuss stomatal closure and the uptake-proxy limit. Link xylem flow, photosynthetic carbon conditions and later seed-set stages. Chinese is sufficient; English titles or a limitation sentence are optional.

## 证据与机制 / Evidence and mechanism

用至少两组带单位或相对尺度的数值，标固定条件、改变变量与图中结构。用因为—所以把结构/梯度/信息或分配机制连到主张，避免只重述趋势。

Use at least two numerical comparisons with units or a relative scale, fixed conditions, the changed variable and a diagram structure. Link mechanism to claim instead of merely restating the trend.

## 局限与下一步 / Limitations and next step

指出原创理想化模型省略的一项条件及可能影响；提出只改一个条件、有重复的下一步比较。不能把模型数值说成真实物种或人群数据。

Identify one omitted condition, its potential consequence and a repeated comparison changing one variable. Do not present invented values as real species or human-population measurements.

## 自查 / Self-check

- 主张有适用条件；证据有两组具体数值或明确结构；推理说明机制；局限解释为什么限制结论。
- 条件性解释完成即可记录单元任务；英文词汇另行复习，不锁生物进度。
- Claim has scope; evidence includes two numerical comparisons or specified structures; reasoning explains a mechanism; the limitation explains its consequence.
- A valid biological explanation completes the mission; English vocabulary can be reviewed separately.

## 可选英文练习 / Optional English practice

In this model, under ___ conditions, ___ changes from ___ to ___. This supports ___ because ___. However, ___ was not tested.

中文作答同样有效，无需额外购买、培养、采样或收集家庭遗传资料。

Chinese answers are equally valid. No purchases, culturing, specimen collection or family genetic records are required.
