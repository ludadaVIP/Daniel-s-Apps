## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**速率问题要写明条件。** 光合作用既需要光能，也需要CO₂作为有机物碳源，并依赖适合温度、水分和叶片功能。当一项条件不足以支持更高速率时，改变另一个已经充足的因素可能无效。“限制因素”描述特定时刻和条件下的作用，不是永远只有光或永远只有CO₂。真实系统有时还会共同限制。

**曲线必须连着轴读。** 本课横轴为相对光照，纵轴为相对光合速率，两条曲线分别固定CO₂供应40和70。温度、水和叶面积相同，折线是原创理想模型，不是某物种实测。低光区两曲线重叠，表示增加CO₂尚不足以提速；高光区分开，表示同一光强下CO₂条件会改变输出。平台表示继续增加横轴变量无效，不表示过程停止。

**计算让结论可检查。** 在CO₂40时，光20给速率20，光60给40，光90仍40。把光60增至90而速率不变，只能先判“此处再加光没有效果”。再固定光90，把CO₂40增至70，速率40增至70，增幅为30÷40＝75%，这才支持此条件下CO₂限制。若光20时相同加CO₂操作仍为20，就不能把高光结论无条件套到低光。

**观测指标有自己的边界。** 水草气泡计数可用来比较一定时间内净产气趋势，但气泡体积、气体溶解、呼吸消耗及其他气体都会影响读数。不能把每泡当作等体积纯氧，更不能直接当总光合碳固定。进入温室新情境，即使加CO₂有效，叶面积、夜间呼吸、营养与时间也会影响生物量；短时相对速率不是产量保证。合理答题应依次报告趋势、引用数值、说明机制，并限定比较条件。

### English

Rate claims need conditions. Photosynthesis requires light energy and carbon dioxide as a carbon source, alongside suitable temperature, water supply and functioning leaves. If one requirement restricts the rate, increasing another already sufficient input may have little effect. A limiting factor is therefore conditional, not a permanent title for light or carbon dioxide. Real systems can also be jointly limited.

Read each curve with its axes. Here x is relative light and y is relative photosynthetic rate. Two curves fix carbon supply at 40 and 70 while temperature, water and leaf area remain equal. The original straight-line model is not a measured species curve. Overlap at low light shows that extra carbon alone cannot raise output there. Separation at high light shows a response to carbon under the same illumination. A plateau means extra x has little effect, not that the process has stopped.

Use numbers to test the explanation. At carbon 40, light 20 gives rate 20, light 60 gives 40 and light 90 still gives 40. Increasing light 60→90 without a rate change shows extra light is ineffective at that setting. Now hold light 90 and increase carbon 40→70: rate rises 40→70, an increase of 30÷40=75%. This supports carbon limitation there. At light 20, the same carbon increase leaves rate 20, so the high-light conclusion cannot be applied unconditionally to low light.

Bubble counts from aquatic plants can indicate net gas-production trends over a fixed period. Bubble size, gas dissolution, respiration and gas composition affect the count, so each bubble cannot be assumed an equal volume of pure oxygen or an exact measure of gross carbon fixation. In a greenhouse transfer scenario, a short-term rate improvement also cannot guarantee yield: leaf area, respiration, nutrition and elapsed time matter. A strong answer reports a trend, cites numbers, connects a mechanism and states the controlled conditions.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“平台就是零速率”混淆斜率与纵坐标；“加光永远增产”忽略其他限制及时间尺度。

### English

“Plateau means zero rate” confuses slope with y-value; “more light always increases yield” ignores other limitations and timescales.

## Depth / 高中桥梁

### 中文

平台与斜率不同：斜率接近零，速率仍可大于零。本模型用min函数突出瓶颈，真实反应常平滑且可共同限制，不能把折角当真实生理突变。

### English

Slope and rate differ: near-zero slope can coexist with positive rate. The min model highlights bottlenecks; real responses are smoother and may be jointly limited.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：固定CO₂和水，15°C到25°C时净产氧由3升至5 mL/h；光也同时增加。自查：可报上升2 mL/h，不能独归因温度；设计固定光、只改温度并重复，不从两点推温度最适值。

### English

New situation: net oxygen production rises 3→5 mL/h as temperature rises 15→25°C, but light also increases. Self-check: report the 2 mL/h rise without attributing it solely to temperature; hold light fixed, vary temperature and repeat. Two points do not establish an optimum.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **限制因素** — 当前条件下限制过程速率的因素。
- **平台** — 横轴继续增大而纵轴变化很小的区段。
- **光强** — 单位面积接收光的强弱。
- **碳固定** — 把无机碳并入有机分子的过程。
- **控制变量** — 公平比较时保持相同的因素。
- **净速率** — 同时发生的相反过程相减后的速率。
- **相对单位** — 在规定参照尺度上表达的量。

### English

- **limiting factor** — A factor restricting process rate under current conditions.
- **plateau** — A region where increasing x produces little change in y.
- **light intensity** — The strength of light received per area.
- **carbon fixation** — Incorporating inorganic carbon into organic molecules.
- **controlled variable** — A factor kept constant in a fair comparison.
- **net rate** — Rate remaining after opposing processes are accounted for.
- **relative unit** — A quantity expressed on a stated reference scale.

## Check / 理解检查

### 中文

1. 曲线平台直接说明什么？
   - A. 光合作用完全停止
   - B. 当前条件下继续加光未明显提高速率
   - C. 所有植物都不需要光

2. 研究CO₂作用时哪项设计最公平？
   - A. 只改变CO₂，固定光、温度、水和叶面积
   - B. 同时换灯、换植物和增CO₂
   - C. 只记最终气泡数，不记时间

3. 光20时CO₂40→70，速率20→20；光90时同改动，40→70。哪项解释最好？
   - A. CO₂在所有光强都无影响
   - B. 高光时CO₂增加使速率提高75%，低光时仍受光限制
   - C. 低光时速率提高150%

4. limiting factor中的limiting最接近？
   - A. 总是完全消失
   - B. 只在夜间起作用
   - C. 当前条件下限制速率

<details>
<summary>作答后查看答案与解释</summary>

1. B. 平台仍可有正速率；它只描述横轴改变的效果，不能单凭平台识别所有限制。 读光60→90的两点和平台纵坐标40。

2. A. 单变量比较才能把速率变化与CO₂联系；实际还需重复和适应时间。 比较同一横坐标的两条曲线。

3. B. 高光增幅(70−40)/40=75%；低光输出不变说明这时增加CO₂不足以提速。 将光分别设20和90，各比较CO₂40/70。

4. C. 限制因素随条件改变，不是给某个变量永久贴标签。 对照低光区与高光平台。

</details>

### English

1. What does the plateau directly show?
   - A. Photosynthesis has stopped completely
   - B. Extra light does not increase rate under these conditions
   - C. All plants have no need for light

2. Which design fairly investigates CO₂?
   - A. Change only CO₂; hold light, temperature, water and leaf area constant
   - B. Change lamp and plant while increasing CO₂
   - C. Record final bubble count without time

3. At light 20, CO₂40→70 gives rate 20→20; at light 90 it gives 40→70. Best explanation?
   - A. CO₂ never matters at any light level
   - B. At high light CO₂ raises rate 75%; low light remains limiting
   - C. At low light the rate rises 150%

4. What does limiting mean in limiting factor?
   - A. Always completely absent
   - B. Acting only at night
   - C. Restricting rate under present conditions

<details>
<summary>Reveal answers and explanations after answering</summary>

1. B. A plateau may have a positive rate. It describes the effect of the x-variable, not every possible limitation. Read light 60→90 and the plateau rate 40.

2. A. A one-variable comparison links a change to CO₂; real experiments also need repeats and equilibration time. Compare the two curves at the same x-coordinate.

3. B. The high-light increase is(70−40)/40=75%; at low light, extra CO₂ alone does not raise the output. At light 20 and 90 separately, compare CO₂40/70.

4. C. Limitation changes with conditions; it is not a permanent label attached to one variable. Compare the low-light region with the high-light plateau.

</details>
