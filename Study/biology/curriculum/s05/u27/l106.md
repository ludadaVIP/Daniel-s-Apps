## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

有风通常加快失水，为什么炎热干燥时植物关气孔却可能失水更慢？吸水计真的在量蒸腾吗？

### English

Wind often increases water loss. Why might a hot, dry plant lose less after closing stomata? Does a potometer directly measure transpiration?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

- 固定气孔时，湿度更高通常减小失水梯度。
- 风的作用不受气孔关闭影响。
- 吸水计每一单位吸水都等于同刻蒸腾。

### English

- With aperture fixed, higher humidity usually reduces the loss gradient.
- Wind effects are unaffected by stomatal closure.
- Every unit of uptake equals simultaneous transpiration.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**水先换状态，再离开叶。** 叶片内部湿润的细胞壁表面，液态水蒸发进入细胞间隙。叶内空气通常较湿，水蒸气主要经气孔扩散到外界；净扩散方向取决于梯度。蒸腾不是根把水加压“喷”出孔，也不要求叶表出现可见水滴。叶失水还影响木质部水柱拉力，根的供水和叶的散失共同影响水分平衡。

**环境条件彼此交互。** 固定其他条件，空气湿度升高往往减小内外水蒸气梯度，风吹走贴叶的湿空气、减薄边界层，常加快失水。温度升高可增强蒸发和饱和水蒸气条件，光常促使气孔开启。但水分胁迫时气孔关闭会限制通路，因此“越热、风越大，蒸腾永远越快”不成立。种类、叶面积和供水状态也是重要条件。

**先读单位，再作比较。** 原创模型在20°C、光100、风0、气孔响应光时，湿度50%给出5相对单位/时，湿度80%给出2。下降量3，百分比下降为3÷5＝60%。若同时把风从0加到3，就改变两个条件，不能把全部差值归因湿度。固定湿度50%只把风0改3，输出由5变10，才是这组模型条件下的风效应。

**装置量到的过程需要命名。** 吸水计通过气泡或液面位移估计枝条吸水，而不是直接收集叶片散失的全部水。短时间内吸水可用于储水和生长，失水也可能由原有储水提供；漏气和装置温度变化另会干扰。本模型用吸水＝蒸腾＋0.4，0.4表示这段时间净保留的水，使用同一任意单位/时，绝不是现实通用常数。若新情境中植物先失水、后补回储水，吸水与蒸腾的时间变化更不必同步。

### English

Transpiration combines a phase change and diffusion. Liquid water evaporates from moist mesophyll cell walls into leaf air spaces. Vapour then diffuses mainly through stomata towards drier outside air. Water is not simply sprayed from pores by root pressure, and visible droplets are not required. Leaf loss contributes to tension in the xylem water column, while uptake and stored water help maintain the plant water balance.

Environmental effects interact. With other conditions fixed, higher humidity commonly reduces the vapour gradient. Wind removes moist air near the leaf and reduces boundary-layer resistance. Warming can increase evaporation and alter vapour conditions, while light commonly promotes stomatal opening. However, water stress can close stomata and restrict the pathway. Consequently, stronger wind or higher temperature does not guarantee faster loss under every condition. Leaf area, species and water supply also matter.

Read the units before interpreting a comparison. In this original model, at 20°C, light 100, wind 0 and light-responsive stomata, humidity 50% gives 5 relative units/time and humidity 80% gives 2. Loss falls by 3 units/time, or 3÷5=60%. Changing wind simultaneously would confound the humidity comparison. Keeping humidity 50% and changing only wind 0→3 instead changes loss 5→10, describing the wind effect under those particular conditions.

A potometer estimates uptake from liquid or bubble displacement; it does not directly collect all transpired water. Uptake can supply storage and growth, while stored water can temporarily supply loss. Leaks and temperature changes can also disturb the apparatus. Here uptake=loss+0.4 represents net water retained during the selected interval, in the same arbitrary units/time. The 0.4 is an invented teaching budget, never a universal correction. In a new scenario where a plant first depletes and later refills water stores, uptake and transpiration need not peak together. Measuring a related process requires explicitly stating what is observed and what is inferred.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“吸水计读数就是蒸腾”混淆代理和目标；“关孔后完全不失水”忽略角质层等通路。

### English

“Potometer uptake equals transpiration” confuses proxy and target; “closed stomata mean no loss” ignores cuticular and other pathways.

## Depth / 高中桥梁

### 中文

水势把溶质和压力等作用联系起来；本课不计算完整水势方程。边界层阻力和气孔阻力可共同控制水蒸气通量。

### English

Water potential links solute and pressure effects; a full equation is beyond this lesson. Boundary-layer and stomatal resistance jointly influence vapour flux.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：相同叶面积两枝条10 min内吸水0.8和1.2 mL，但第二装置漏气。自查：能说第二读数高0.4 mL，不能断言蒸腾高50%；解释吸水代理、装置泄漏，需密封检查和重复。

### English

New situation: equal-area shoots show 0.8 and 1.2 mL uptake in 10 min, but the second apparatus leaks. Self-check: report a 0.4 mL reading difference; do not conclude 50% more transpiration. Name the proxy, leak confounder and need for sealing checks and repeats.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **蒸腾** — 植物表面向外散失水蒸气的过程。
- **蒸发** — 液态水进入气态的过程。
- **气孔** — 保卫细胞调节的表皮孔隙。
- **湿度** — 空气含水蒸气的程度。
- **边界层** — 贴近叶面的较静止空气层。
- **代理指标** — 与目标过程有关但不等同的测量。
- **吸水计** — 估计枝条吸水速率的装置。

### English

- **transpiration** — Loss of water vapour from plant surfaces.
- **evaporation** — Liquid water entering the vapour phase.
- **stoma** — An epidermal pore regulated by guard cells.
- **humidity** — The amount of water vapour in air.
- **boundary layer** — Relatively still air next to the leaf surface.
- **proxy** — A related measurement that is not identical to the target process.
- **potometer** — An apparatus estimating shoot water uptake rate.

1. 蒸腾主要包含哪条先后关系？
   - A. 细胞壁水蒸发，再经气孔扩散
   - B. 液态水全部直接由筛管喷出
   - C. 根把水转为水蒸气后送到叶

   - A. 关闭把叶内所有水变为糖

   - A. 湿度升高使失水增加150%
   - B. 升温导致失水降低
   - C. 湿度升高使叶内外梯度变小，失水降低60%

### English

1. Which sequence describes the main transpiration route?
   - A. Wall water evaporates, then vapour diffuses through stomata
   - B. All liquid water sprays directly from sieve tubes
   - C. Roots turn all water into vapour before transport

2. Why can drought closure reduce loss even in bright light?
   - A. Closure converts all leaf water into sugar
   - B. The pore pathway becomes restricted, modifying environmental effects
   - C. Bright light has no remaining energy

3. At 20°C, light 100 and wind 0, loss is 5 at 50% humidity and 2 at 80% (relative units/time). Which explanation is supported?
   - A. Higher humidity increases loss 150%
   - B. Warming caused the reduction
   - C. Higher humidity reduces the gradient and loss falls 60%

4. What does a potometer directly track?
   - A. The number of individual stomata
   - B. Shoot water uptake or its rate
   - C. Exact photosynthetic rate

<details>
<summary>Reveal answers and explanations after answering</summary>

1. A. Evaporation occurs at the cell walls; vapour diffusion near the pore follows a vapour gradient. Trace the route from leaf wall water to outside the pore.

2. B. Closure restricts diffusion. Other surface losses remain, so closure does not guarantee zero transpiration. Compare open and closed at the same humidity, wind and temperature.

3. C. The reduction is(5−2)/5=60%. Temperature did not change. The comparison applies to these controlled leaf and aperture conditions. Reset mentally to defaults, change only humidity 50→80 and read outputs.

4. B. Uptake is often related to transpiration, but storage, growth and leaks prevent exact equality. Compare the distinct uptake and loss readings.

</details>

## Mission / 放到生活里

### English

Keep three fair comparisons, state a conditional humidity or wind claim with numerical evidence, and explain the uptake-proxy limitation.
