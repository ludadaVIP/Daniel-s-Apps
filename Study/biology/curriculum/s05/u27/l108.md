## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**先分清三个尺度的事件。** 花药产生花粉，花粉从花药转移到柱头叫授粉；即使花粉不相容，到达柱头仍是授粉，只是后续可能失败。相容花粉在适宜条件下萌发形成花粉管，向胚珠生长并输送雄配子。雄配子核与卵细胞核结合形成合子才是受精。此后胚发育，胚珠可形成种子；花粉既不是种子，也不是已经形成的胚。授粉、受精、成熟结籽之间存在多个可能失败的环节。

**繁殖同时依赖整株资源。** 种子和果实发育常成为糖的库，糖来自光合器官或储藏物质，需通过运输通路供应。植物也需要氮来合成氨基酸、核酸等，需要镁构成叶绿素并参与其他作用。缺素可影响生长和光合能力，进而影响可分配的资源，但这不是“矿物盐直接变糖”。糖的碳骨架主要来自CO₂，矿质不能替代光能或碳源。

**数据不能跨越未检查的步骤。** 原创对照中，同种植物各20朵花，人工补充授粉组形成14颗种子，未人工补充组6颗。差为8颗，支持此条件下授粉处理与结籽增加有关，却不能说所有授粉花都受精、每花只一胚珠，或未人工授粉组没有自然花粉。要增强解释，应控制植株大小、营养、水温条件，记录花数、胚珠数和重复组。

**症状与原因要分开。** 叶片发黄可能与缺氮、缺镁、病害或其他条件有关，单靠颜色无法可靠诊断。若新情境中肥料增加后仍不结籽，应检查授粉相容性、花粉管成功与环境，而不是无限加肥。模型把繁殖步骤与矿质功能并列，是为了区分资源支持和事件顺序；不表示某一矿物一补上就必然结籽。高中将进一步讨论开花植物双受精，本课保留卵与雄配子结合形成合子的主线。

### English

Separate the events before linking them. Pollen is produced in anthers, and its transfer to a stigma is pollination. Incompatible pollen can also arrive, but later recognition, germination or fertilisation may fail. Compatible pollen under suitable conditions can germinate, forming a tube that grows towards an ovule and delivers male gametes. Fertilisation occurs when a male gamete nucleus unites with an egg nucleus to form a zygote. An embryo subsequently develops, and the ovule may develop into a seed. Pollen is therefore neither a seed nor an already formed embryo. Several steps can fail after successful transfer.

Reproduction also depends on whole-plant resources. Developing seeds and fruits commonly act as sugar sinks supplied by photosynthetic tissues or reserves. Nitrogen supports amino acids, nucleic acids and other molecules; magnesium is a component of chlorophyll and has other cellular roles. Deficiency can reduce growth or photosynthetic function and thus available resources. This does not mean mineral salts turn directly into sugar. Sugar carbon comes mainly from CO₂; minerals cannot replace light energy or a carbon source.

An original comparison follows 20 flowers in each of two groups. Added pollination yields 14 seeds, while no added pollination yields 6. The difference is 8 seeds and supports increased seed set under these conditions. It does not show that every pollinated flower was fertilised, that each flower had one ovule, or that the untreated group received no natural pollen. Control plant size, nutrition and environmental conditions; record ovule numbers and replicate groups before making stronger claims.

Symptoms do not uniquely identify causes. Yellowing may reflect nitrogen or magnesium deficiency, disease or other conditions. In a new scenario where fertiliser does not restore seed production, investigate compatibility, tube success and environment rather than assuming more fertiliser must solve it. The model juxtaposes reproductive sequence and mineral function to distinguish resource support from event order. Later study can expand the second fusion of double fertilisation; here the central route follows the zygote and embryo.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“授粉＝受精”“花粉＝种子”把前后事件混为一谈；“土壤给植物食物”忽略糖碳来自CO₂。

### English

“Pollination=fertilisation” and “pollen=seed” conflate stages; “soil feeds ready-made food” ignores CO₂ as sugar carbon.

### English

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：两组同样补镁，A花粉相容、B不相容，A有种子而B无。自查：引用相同镁条件，提出相容性或后续事件解释；不能因B不结籽断定缺镁，也不能只靠种子结果确定受阻步骤。

### English

New situation: both groups receive equal Mg; A gets compatible pollen and B incompatible pollen, with seeds only in A. Self-check: cite equal Mg conditions, propose compatibility or later-stage failure, and explain why seed outcomes alone do not locate the exact failed step.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **授粉** — 花粉从花药转移到柱头，不要求后续相容或受精成功。
- **受精** — 雄雌配子核结合。
- **胚珠** — 含雌性生殖结构并可发育为种子的结构。
- **花粉管** — 向胚珠输送雄配子的生长通路。
- **矿质元素** — 植物从无机来源获得的必需元素。
- **氮** — 参与氨基酸、核酸等组成的元素。
- **镁** — 叶绿素组成元素，并参与其他细胞作用。

### English

- **pollination** — Transfer of pollen from anther to stigma, without requiring subsequent compatibility or fertilisation.
- **fertilisation** — Union of male and female gamete nuclei.
- **ovule** — A structure containing female reproductive tissues that may develop into a seed.
- **pollen tube** — A growing route delivering male gametes towards an ovule.
- **mineral nutrient** — An essential element obtained from inorganic sources.
- **nitrogen** — An element in amino acids, nucleic acids and other molecules.
- **magnesium** — An element in chlorophyll with additional cellular roles.

### English

1. Pollen reaches a stigma without gamete-nucleus fusion. What has occurred?
   - A. Fertilisation
   - B. Pollination
   - C. Seed germination

2. Which function is most directly linked to magnesium supply?
   - A. Being a component of chlorophyll
   - B. Replacing CO₂ in all sugar carbon skeletons
   - C. Directly becoming a whole seed inside pollen

3. Same-species plants:20 flowers each produce 14 seeds with added pollination and 6 without. Best conclusion?
   - A. Added pollination guarantees one seed per flower
   - B. Six seeds prove all unfertilised flowers become fertilised
   - C. Supports increased seed set here; natural pollination, ovule number and repeats still matter

4. Which best relates ovule and seed?
   - A. An ovule may become a seed after suitable fertilisation and development
   - B. An ovule is pollen landing on the stigma
   - C. Every ovule is an already germinated seed

<details>
<summary>Reveal answers and explanations after answering</summary>

1. B. Step 1 is pollen transfer; step 3 is fertilisation. Pollination does not guarantee later success. Compare structures in steps 1 and 3.

2. A. Mg is part of chlorophyll; sugar carbon comes mainly from CO₂. Minerals support functions rather than supplying ready-made carbohydrate food. Read the Mg→chlorophyll label.

3. C. The difference is 8 seeds, not 20; a flower may contain several ovules. No added pollination does not mean no pollen arrived. Review steps between pollination and seed formation.

4. A. Structures develop, but a pre-fertilisation ovule and mature seed are not the same stage. Locate the ovule and inspect the final step.
