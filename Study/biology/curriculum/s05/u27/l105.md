## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**先分物质，再分通道。** 植物不是把根吸收的所有物质都塞进同一条“血管”。常见蒸腾条件下，水及溶解矿质沿木质部由根送往叶。成熟导管分子失去活的细胞内容物，端部连接形成低阻通道；木质化的壁提供支撑。死导管仍能运输，说明每一段都主动泵水不是必要条件。叶片失水产生的拉力、水分子之间的内聚等共同维持水柱，本课只追踪整体方向。

**糖看源和库，不看上下。** 成熟光合叶常净输出蔗糖，是源；生长点、根、果实或储藏组织可能是库。叶到根的路线向下，叶到新梢的路线向上，能够在不同筛管中同时发生。不能把图上两条箭头合并成“一条管内两个相反的整体流”。春季储藏器官分解储备并输出糖时可成为源，后来接受新叶制造的糖又可成为库。

**装载与长距离流动不是同一步。** 一些植物在源端主动装载蔗糖，需要能量和伴胞参与；这改变溶液条件，水可由邻近组织渗透进入，使源端压力提高。库端卸载和利用糖，帮助维持压力差。筛管中长距离的糖水整体运动主要由压力差驱动，并非沿路每个糖分子都被细胞逐次主动搬运。装载方式也存在物种差异。

**把数据放回模型。** 假设源叶输出20相对单位糖，6小时后新梢收到8、根收到12，8＋12＝20支持这份简化收支，但不意味着真实植物没有呼吸消耗、储存或分支。若另一次发现根部标记减少、新梢增加，应先问源–库关系是否改变，不能立即宣布韧皮部坏了。迁移到幼苗时，应辨认子叶储备能否作为源，而不是坚持只有绿色叶才有输出资格。

### English

Separate cargo from conduit. Under usual transpiring conditions, xylem carries water and dissolved minerals from roots towards leaves. Mature vessel elements have lost their living contents and connect into conduits. Lignified walls support these channels. Transport through dead vessel elements shows that active pumping at every segment is unnecessary. Leaf water loss, water cohesion and the resulting tension help maintain movement; the diagram summarises the route rather than every physical contribution.

Phloem direction depends on source and sink. A mature photosynthetic leaf commonly exports sucrose and acts as a source. Growing shoots, roots, fruits and storage organs can receive sugar as sinks. Separate tubes may carry sugar upwards to a shoot and downwards to roots simultaneously. This does not mean one tube carries opposing bulk flows at the same instant. During sprouting, a storage organ can export reserves and become a source, later becoming a sink again.

Loading differs from long-distance flow. In some plants, energy-dependent loading at the source, supported by companion cells, increases solute concentration. Water can enter by osmosis, raising pressure. Unloading and sugar use at the sink help maintain a pressure difference. Bulk flow moves the sugar solution along the tube; active loading does not mean each sugar molecule is actively pumped along the entire journey. Loading mechanisms differ among plants.

For an original accounting example, a leaf exports 20 relative sugar units and, six hours later, a shoot has received 8 and roots 12. The sum 8+12=20 closes this simplified budget. Real plants also respire, store sugar and have branching routes, so endpoint amounts are not direct flow-rate measurements. If root label falls while shoot label rises, investigate changing source–sink relationships. In a new seedling scenario, cotyledon reserves may supply a growing shoot even before mature green leaves exist.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“韧皮部只能向下”忽略源–库关系；“木质部全部是死细胞”也过度概括，成熟导管分子死亡而组织还含其他细胞。

### English

“Phloem only goes down” ignores source–sink roles; “all xylem cells are dead” also overgeneralises beyond mature vessel elements.

## Depth / 高中桥梁

### 中文

把主动装载、渗透进水、压力差、整体流动分成四个因果环节。水势还包括压力等影响，不能只比较糖浓度就断定整条路线方向。

### English

Separate loading, osmotic water entry, pressure difference and bulk flow. Water potential includes pressure effects; sugar concentration alone does not determine every route.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：幼苗子叶4天内糖由30降到18 mg，新梢由2升到7 mg。写源–库主张。自查：用mg和时间，说明子叶可供糖；不能把12 mg减少全部当成新梢增加，尚有呼吸与其他库；此为原创账本。

### English

New situation: over 4 days, cotyledon sugar falls 30→18 mg while shoot sugar rises 2→7 mg. Self-check: identify a possible cotyledon source using units and duration; the 12 mg loss is not all shoot gain because respiration and other sinks matter. This is an original budget.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **木质部** — 主要运输水和矿质并支持植物的组织。
- **韧皮部** — 运输糖等有机溶质的活组织系统。
- **导管** — 成熟时由死的、木质化细胞形成的运输通道。
- **筛管** — 筛管分子相连形成的活运输通路。
- **源** — 在所考察时段净输出糖的部位。
- **库** — 净接受糖以利用或储存的部位。
- **压力流** — 压力差驱动溶液整体流动。

### English

- **xylem** — Tissue mainly transporting water and minerals and supporting plants.
- **phloem** — Living tissue system transporting sugars and other solutes.
- **vessel** — A conduit formed from dead, lignified cells at maturity.
- **sieve tube** — A living transport route formed from connected sieve elements.
- **source** — A region exporting sugar during the period considered.
- **sink** — A region receiving sugar for use or storage.
- **pressure flow** — Bulk solution movement driven by a pressure difference.

### English

1. Which structure fits a typical mature xylem vessel?
   - A. Living complete end walls repeatedly pumping sugar
   - B. Connected dead lignified cells forming a water conduit
   - C. Only a lipid membrane, with no cell wall

2. When a non-photosynthetic shoot receives sugar from a store, which is the source?
   - A. The shoot
   - B. Always a mature leaf
   - C. The exporting storage organ

3. Six hours after sugar labelling, a shoot contains 8 relative units and roots 12. What follows?
   - A. Consistent with different tubes supplying two sinks; not proof of opposing flow in one tube
   - B. Every tube must flow both ways simultaneously
   - C. Sugar must travel only through xylem

4. What does source mean in a plant-transport question?
   - A. Any underground organ
   - B. A net sugar-exporting region
   - C. The only place losing water

<details>
<summary>Reveal answers and explanations after answering</summary>

1. B. Lignified walls support conduits under tension. Mature vessel elements are dead, not the whole plant or every xylem cell. Inspect the blue xylem route and its cargo.

2. C. Source means net exporter. A storage organ can change from sink to source without becoming a leaf. Select reserve mobilisation and trace tube A.

3. A. Both destinations received label, but endpoint amounts alone do not map every route or measure instantaneous flow. Compare the separate arrows in tubes A and B.

4. B. Source describes sugar balance rather than vertical position; a sink is a net recipient. Read endpoint labels under both source–sink conditions.

</details>
