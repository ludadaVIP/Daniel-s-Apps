## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

肺动脉运输相对低氧血，为什么仍叫动脉？左心室壁又为什么更厚？

### English

Why is the pulmonary artery an artery despite carrying relatively oxygen-poor blood, and why is the left ventricular wall thicker?

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

人的心脏有左右两侧，每侧有接收血液的心房和把血泵出的心室。右心把从身体回来的相对低氧血送到肺；肺部气体交换后，血回左心，再被送到身体组织。血液完成一次身体—肺—身体的大回路，要经过心脏两次，因此称双循环。两侧通过心内间隔分开，减少不同含氧状态血液直接混合，路线图上的相邻方框不表示心腔之间都有孔相通。

依序追踪：体静脉→右心房→右心室→肺动脉→肺毛细血管→肺静脉→左心房→左心室→主动脉→体毛细血管→体静脉。动脉把血带离心脏，静脉把血带回心脏，名称由方向决定。因此肺动脉虽相对低氧仍是动脉，肺静脉虽相对高氧仍是静脉；低氧血不是完全没有氧。毛细血管提供薄壁交换位置，不是主要高压泵。

心肌收缩造成压力变化，压力差推动血流；瓣膜的瓣片受压力差开闭，限制倒流，不能自己代替肌肉泵血。模型单向瓣膜前12、后8 kPa时，前压较高，允许正向开放；前8、后12时则关闭。数字只是为比较方向而设，不能当成心内各处实际压力，也不代表一秒的真实心动周期。

左心室要支持体循环较高的压力需求，肌壁通常比右心室厚；右心室服务肺循环较低压力需求。两侧长期平均输出量应匹配，不能把“壁厚”解释成左心每次一定泵两倍的血。读结构图时还需检查左右的标注视角、血管连接与箭头，不能靠红蓝颜色或图上大小猜路线。

### English

The human heart has two sides, each with an atrium receiving blood and a ventricle pumping blood out. The right side sends relatively oxygen-poor blood returning from the body to the lungs. After gas exchange, blood returns to the left side, which supplies body tissues. A complete body–lung–body journey passes through the heart twice: double circulation. The septum separates the sides, reducing direct mixing; adjacent boxes on a route map do not imply openings between all chambers.

Trace systemic veins → right atrium → right ventricle → pulmonary artery → lung capillaries → pulmonary vein → left atrium → left ventricle → aorta → body capillaries → systemic veins. Arteries carry blood away from the heart; veins carry it towards the heart. Therefore the relatively oxygen-poor pulmonary artery is still an artery, and the oxygen-rich pulmonary vein is a vein. Oxygen-poor blood is not oxygen-free. Capillaries provide thin exchange surfaces rather than serving as the main pressure pump.

Cardiac muscle changes pressure, and pressure differences drive flow. Valve leaflets open or close in response to those differences, limiting backflow rather than actively pumping. In our hypothetical one-way valve comparison, upstream 12 and downstream 8 kPa permit forward opening. Reversing the pressures closes it. These numbers illustrate a mechanism; they are not measured pressures throughout a heart or a timed cardiac cycle.

The left ventricle usually has thicker muscle to support the higher pressure requirements of systemic circulation. The right ventricle serves the lower-pressure pulmonary circuit. Their long-term average outputs must match; thicker muscle does not imply that the left side continuously pumps twice as much blood. Read orientation, connections and arrows rather than inferring anatomy from colour or drawn size.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：蓝色血无氧且只在静脉。反例：肺动脉相对低氧、肺静脉相对高氧；身体中的血液实际都是不同深浅的红色，图色是编码。

### English

Misconception: blue blood contains no oxygen and occurs only in veins. Pulmonary arteries carry relatively low-oxygen blood and pulmonary veins higher-oxygen blood. Actual blood is red; diagram colours are codes.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中衔接：压力与流量不同，阻力也影响流量。左右心平均流量匹配不要求压力相同。心动周期中房室瓣与半月瓣随不同压力差改变，本图只演示通用单向瓣膜，未模拟完整周期或冠脉。

### English

Senior bridge: pressure differs from flow, which also depends on resistance. Matched average flows do not require equal pressures. Atrioventricular and semilunar valves respond to different pressure differences during the cycle; this model omits full-cycle and coronary dynamics.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：未知血管X连接心室并把血送到气体交换器官，测得含氧量低。它应如何命名？自查：使用“离开心脏”的方向证据；判为动脉，在人体肺情境为肺动脉；低氧不能改名静脉；若要确认左右，须检查连接到右心室而非只凭颜色。

### English

New case: vessel X leaves a ventricle for a gas-exchange organ and carries low-oxygen blood. Name its type. Self-check: use the away-from-heart direction; it is an artery, specifically pulmonary artery in this human context. Low oxygen does not make it a vein; confirm its right-ventricle connection rather than relying on colour.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **心房 / atrium**: 接收回到心脏血液的心腔。
- **心室 / ventricle**: 把血泵出心脏的心腔。
- **动脉 / artery**: 把血液带离心脏的血管。
- **静脉 / vein**: 把血液带回心脏的血管。
- **肺循环 / pulmonary circulation**: 右心与肺之间及返回左心的循环路线。
- **体循环 / systemic circulation**: 左心经身体组织返回右心的路线。
- **瓣膜 / valve**: 受压力差影响开闭并限制倒流的结构。
- **毛细血管 / capillary**: 连接微小血管并支持与组织交换的薄壁血管。

### English

- **心房 / atrium**: A chamber receiving blood returning to the heart.
- **心室 / ventricle**: A chamber pumping blood out of the heart.
- **动脉 / artery**: A vessel carrying blood away from the heart.
- **静脉 / vein**: A vessel carrying blood towards the heart.
- **肺循环 / pulmonary circulation**: The circuit from right heart through lungs to left heart.
- **体循环 / systemic circulation**: The circuit from left heart through body tissues to right heart.
- **瓣膜 / valve**: A structure responding to pressure differences and limiting backflow.
- **毛细血管 / capillary**: A thin-walled vessel supporting exchange with tissues.

## Check / 检索练习

### 中文

1. [concept] 肺静脉的定义与血液状态是哪项？

   A. 离心脏、通常相对低氧
   B. 回心脏、通常相对高氧
   C. 回心脏、完全无氧

   答案: B. “静脉”由回心方向定义；肺交换使它相对高氧。低氧也不等于无氧。

   回路线5→6→7，找到肺与左心房。

2. [concept] 左心室壁较厚，最合理解释是什么？

   A. 支持体循环较高压力需要
   B. 必须持续泵出右侧两倍血量
   C. 只因示意图画得较大

   答案: A. 肌壁厚与压力需求相关；两侧长期平均流量应匹配。示意比例不是证据。

   比较左心→全身与右心→肺路线。

3. [reasoning] 模型瓣膜前压从12降到8，后压保持10 kPa，预期怎样？

   A. 一直打开，因为血是红色
   B. 瓣膜主动加压到12
   C. 从可正向打开变为关闭以限制倒流

   答案: C. 前12>后10时可正向开；前8<后10时压差反转使单向瓣关闭。颜色不控制流向，瓣膜不是泵。

   切换两压力对照并回Explain压力例。

4. [vocabulary] double circulation指什么？

   A. 一次完整体肺循环经过心脏两次
   B. 任何动脉有两层血
   C. 每次心跳左侧泵两次

   答案: A. 两条相连的肺/体循环使完整路线经过心脏两次，不是单侧多跳一次。

   沿1→10再回1，圈出左右心各一次。

### English

1. [concept] Which describes a pulmonary vein?

   A. Away from heart, usually lower oxygen
   B. Towards heart, usually higher oxygen
   C. Towards heart, entirely oxygen-free

   Answer: B. Vein means towards the heart; lung exchange gives relatively high oxygen. Low oxygen does not mean none.

   Trace steps 5→6→7 from lungs to left atrium.

2. [concept] Why is the left ventricular wall thicker?

   A. It supports systemic circulation’s higher pressure requirement
   B. It must continuously pump twice the right side’s volume
   C. Only because it is drawn larger

   Answer: A. Wall thickness relates to pressure demand; average outputs must match. Drawing scale is not evidence.

   Compare left-heart→body with right-heart→lungs.

3. [reasoning] Upstream model pressure falls from 12 to 8 while downstream stays 10 kPa. What follows?

   A. It stays open because blood is red
   B. The valve actively raises pressure to 12
   C. It changes from forward opening to closing against backflow

   Answer: C. 12>10 permits forward opening; 8<10 reverses the pressure difference and closes the valve. Colour does not control flow; a valve is not a pump.

   Switch pressure comparisons and revisit the pressure example.

4. [vocabulary] What does double circulation mean?

   A. A complete systemic–pulmonary circuit passes the heart twice
   B. Every artery contains two layers of blood
   C. The left side pumps twice per heartbeat

   Answer: A. Connected pulmonary and systemic circuits take blood through the heart twice, not an extra contraction by one side.

   Trace 1→10→1 and circle one passage through each side.
