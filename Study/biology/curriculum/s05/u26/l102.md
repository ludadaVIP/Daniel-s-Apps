## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

小肠已经把淀粉分解，为什么还不能说葡萄糖已经进入血液？脂肪又走哪里？

### English

If starch has been digested in the small intestine, why has glucose not necessarily entered blood, and where do fats go?

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

消化与吸收相连，却不是同一过程。消化通过机械处理与酶促水解，把食物中的大分子变为较小组分；吸收要求物质跨过肠上皮，进入体内运输系统。肠腔中的葡萄糖已经很小，但仍须跨膜并离开上皮细胞，才算走到血中。食物碎块变小也不等于所有化学键已经被水解。

淀粉酶先把淀粉变成麦芽糖和较短寡糖，小肠其他酶进一步形成葡萄糖，后者进入血毛细血管。蛋白酶产生肽，随后肽酶等继续作用，通常以氨基酸等形式被吸收，进入血液。脂肪路线要区分乳化与消化：胆汁把脂肪分散成较小液滴，增加酶接触的界面，但不是酶，不能替代脂肪酶水解。胰脂肪酶主要产生脂肪酸与单甘油酯。多数长链脂肪在上皮细胞内重组并包装，进入乳糜管，再经淋巴汇入血液；不能将所有脂肪酸都绝对归到同一路线。

绒毛增加小肠可交换面积，细胞表面的微绒毛进一步增大面积。薄上皮缩短路径，血液和淋巴及时带走产物帮助维持转运条件；上皮也有特定运输蛋白与能量供应。因此，大面积不是唯一原因，消化充分也不能单独保证吸收速率。

结构比较例：假设两模型均固定通透性、路径与梯度，每平方毫米每分钟通过2模型量单位，面积3与6 mm²时，总量分别6与12单位/分钟。这是理想面积对照，不是真实小肠测量。若营养物供应不足或载体饱和，实际增加可能小于两倍。考试应先说清比较条件，再把面积、路径和运输输出连接起来，而不是背一句“绒毛有利吸收”。

### English

Digestion and absorption are connected but distinct. Digestion uses physical processing and enzyme-catalysed hydrolysis to produce smaller components. Absorption requires material to cross intestinal epithelium and enter internal transport systems. Glucose in the lumen still has to cross membranes and leave the epithelial cell before reaching blood. Smaller food fragments also do not imply that all relevant chemical bonds have been hydrolysed.

Amylase produces maltose and shorter chains from starch; other intestinal enzymes complete glucose production. Glucose then enters blood capillaries. Proteases produce peptides, and further enzymes yield absorbable products such as amino acids that reach blood. Fat processing requires another distinction: bile emulsifies fat into smaller droplets, increasing the interface available to enzymes, but does not replace lipase hydrolysis. Pancreatic lipase mainly produces fatty acids and monoglycerides. Most long-chain fats are rebuilt and packaged within epithelial cells, then enter lacteals and eventually blood through lymph. Not every fatty acid follows precisely this route.

Villi enlarge exchanging area, and microvilli enlarge epithelial-cell surfaces further. Thin epithelium shortens crossing distances; blood and lymph remove products. Transport proteins and energy supply also matter. Digestion alone or surface area alone therefore cannot guarantee a particular absorption rate.

For an ideal structural comparison, suppose permeability, path length and gradient are fixed, with transfer of 2 model amount units per mm² per minute. Areas of 3 and 6 mm² give totals of 6 and 12 units/minute. These are invented model values, not measurements of an intestine. Limited nutrient delivery or saturated carriers could reduce the increase. In exam explanations, identify controlled conditions and connect area, path length and transport outputs rather than repeating that villi aid absorption.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：胆汁消化脂肪，所有产物直接进血。反例：乳化不等于水解；多数长链脂肪先由上皮包装进入乳糜管，葡萄糖主要进血毛细血管。

### English

Misconception: bile digests fat and all products enter blood directly. Emulsification differs from hydrolysis; most packaged long-chain fats enter lacteals first, while glucose mainly enters capillaries.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中衔接：葡萄糖跨上皮涉及有方向的运输蛋白与离子梯度，脂肪包装涉及细胞内加工。这里只追踪腔→上皮→血/淋巴，不要求背全部酶或分子包装细节。

### English

Senior bridge: glucose absorption involves directional transport proteins and ion gradients, while fat packaging requires cellular processing. Trace lumen→epithelium→blood/lymph without memorising every enzyme or packaging step.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：某人造肠模型把上皮厚度加倍，消化酶量与产物浓度相同。若吸收下降，能说酶消化变慢吗？自查：区分吸收与消化；引用只改路径长度的条件；解释跨越距离增加，需测酶反应速率再判断消化；不能由吸收量倒推消化量。

### English

New case: an artificial intestinal model doubles epithelial thickness while enzyme amount and product concentration stay fixed. Reduced absorption proves slower digestion? Self-check: distinguish the processes; cite the changed path length; explain crossing distance; measure enzyme reaction rate before drawing a digestion conclusion.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **消化 / digestion**: 把食物分解为更小组分的机械与化学过程。
- **吸收 / absorption**: 物质跨肠上皮进入身体内部的过程。
- **绒毛 / villus**: 小肠内壁的指状突起，内有血毛细血管和乳糜管。
- **微绒毛 / microvilli**: 上皮细胞表面的微小突起，增加面积。
- **上皮 / epithelium**: 覆盖表面和内腔的细胞层。
- **乳糜管 / lacteal**: 绒毛中接收多数长链脂肪运输颗粒的淋巴管。
- **乳化 / emulsification**: 将大脂滴分散成较小液滴而不水解脂肪。

### English

- **消化 / digestion**: Mechanical and chemical breakdown of food into smaller components.
- **吸收 / absorption**: Movement across intestinal epithelium into the body.
- **绒毛 / villus**: A finger-like intestinal projection containing capillaries and a lacteal.
- **微绒毛 / microvilli**: Tiny epithelial-cell projections increasing surface area.
- **上皮 / epithelium**: A cell layer lining surfaces and cavities.
- **乳糜管 / lacteal**: A villus lymphatic receiving most packaged long-chain fats.
- **乳化 / emulsification**: Dispersing fat into smaller droplets without hydrolysis.

## Check / 检索练习

### 中文

1. [concept] 淀粉酶作用之后通常还需要什么？

   A. 无需其他酶就已全部成为葡萄糖
   B. 其他小肠酶进一步生成葡萄糖，再跨上皮
   C. 胆汁把麦芽糖变成氨基酸

   答案: B. 淀粉酶产生麦芽糖/短寡糖，不一跳完成葡萄糖吸收；胆汁主要乳化脂肪。

   回淀粉路线第2→3→4步。

2. [concept] 多数长链脂肪吸收后的首条输出路线是哪项？

   A. 上皮重组包装后进乳糜管
   B. 全部直接进同一血毛细血管
   C. 无需跨上皮就进入细胞核

   答案: A. 上皮加工后主要进淋巴乳糜管，再汇入血；本规则不对每种脂肪酸作绝对归类。

   切换长链脂肪，找绿色路线。

3. [reasoning] 固定每mm²每分钟通过2模型单位，面积由3变6 mm²，正确推断是什么？

   A. 总量6→12单位/分钟，限于相同其他条件
   B. 必是真实人小肠加倍，任何条件都成立
   C. 单位不必写，因为只是图

   答案: A. 面积乘单位面积通量给总量；这是假设数据，不能越过梯度、供应或饱和限制。

   回Explain数值例并指出固定了哪些条件。

4. [vocabulary] absorption与digestion的区别是哪项？

   A. 吸收只使食物碎块变小
   B. 二者都等于排出粪便
   C. 吸收跨上皮；消化把食物分解

   答案: C. 跨边界进入体内与分解大分子是不同事件；排出未消化物又是排遗。

   回图定位肠腔、上皮与两条输出。

### English

1. [concept] What normally follows amylase action on starch?

   A. Everything is already glucose without other enzymes
   B. Other intestinal enzymes form glucose, followed by epithelial crossing
   C. Bile converts maltose to amino acids

   Answer: B. Amylase yields maltose/short chains, not one-step glucose absorption. Bile mainly emulsifies fat.

   Trace starch steps 2→3→4.

2. [concept] What is the main initial output route for most absorbed long-chain fats?

   A. After epithelial rebuilding/packaging, into lacteals
   B. All directly into the same blood capillary
   C. Into nuclei without crossing epithelium

   Answer: A. After epithelial processing they mainly enter lacteals, then blood via lymph. This is not an absolute rule for every fatty acid.

   Select long-chain fats and find the green route.

3. [reasoning] At fixed 2 model units per mm² per minute, area changes 3→6 mm². What follows?

   A. Total 6→12 units/minute, under the same other conditions
   B. Every real intestine must double its rate under all conditions
   C. Units are unnecessary because it is a diagram

   Answer: A. Area times transfer per area gives total. These assumed values do not override gradient, supply or saturation limits.

   Identify controlled conditions in the numerical example.

4. [vocabulary] How does absorption differ from digestion?

   A. Absorption only breaks food into pieces
   B. Both mean passing faeces
   C. Absorption crosses epithelium; digestion breaks down food

   Answer: C. Crossing into the body differs from molecular breakdown; egestion removes undigested material.

   Locate lumen, epithelium and both outputs.
