## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

葡萄糖进入滤液后，为什么正常情况下仍能大部分回到血里？水分不足时又发生什么？

### English

After glucose enters filtrate, why is most normally recovered into blood, and what changes when water is scarce?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选一个解释，不评分；学后用具体证据修正：过滤把血细胞全部筛进尿液；过滤后仍需选择性重吸收并调节水；ADH直接把尿中水泵回

### English

Choose an initial explanation without a score; revise using specific evidence: Filtration sends all blood cells into urine；Filtration is followed by selective recovery and water regulation；ADH directly pumps water back from urine

### 中文

### English

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

肾脏处理的是血液中的水和溶质，不是把整个血液变成尿。肾小球的压力使一部分血浆水与小分子通过选择性过滤屏障，进入肾小囊成为滤液。正常情况下，血细胞和大部分大蛋白保留在血中；葡萄糖、尿素与离子等小分子则可以进入滤液。因此，过滤不是有目的地只挑废物，也不是简单扩散顺浓度差的同义词。压力与屏障性质是这里的关键。

滤液随后经过肾小管，葡萄糖、适量离子与大量水被选择性重吸收，回到血液。有些物质还会从血分泌到管液。葡萄糖出现在初期滤液，并不意味着它必定在最终尿液中；重吸收涉及不同运输蛋白及被动、主动过程。水主要通过渗透返回，不由ADH一颗颗主动泵回。尿素是氨基酸代谢相关的含氮废物之一，也不能说滤液里只有尿素和水。

原创水量例固定100 mL滤液：若总回收90%，回收90、余留10 mL；假设较高ADH使总回收达98%，则回收98、余留2 mL。两例都守恒100。它们是专为比较设置的比例，非人体每日实测，也不描述所有肾小管段的独立比例。

水分不足可使血浆渗透浓度上升，下丘脑感受相关变化，经垂体后叶释放更多ADH；ADH增加集合管等处的水通透性，在适宜水势梯度下使更多水回收，尿量减少、常较浓，原始变化减弱，构成负反馈。条件恢复时调节信号也可减弱。实际还有血容量等信号及其他激素。排泄移除代谢废物和过量物质；排遗是排出消化道残余，不能因为都“排出”就混用，也不能凭本图自行诊断。

### English

Kidneys process water and solutes in blood rather than turning whole blood into urine. Glomerular pressure drives part of the plasma water and small substances through a selective filtration barrier into the renal capsule. Cells and most large proteins normally remain in blood. Small glucose, urea and ions can enter filtrate, so filtration does not exclusively select wastes. It is also not simply another name for diffusion down a concentration gradient: pressure and barrier properties are central.

Tubular processing then selectively returns glucose, appropriate ions and much water to blood. Some substances are also secreted from blood into tubular fluid. Glucose entering initial filtrate therefore does not imply glucose remaining in final urine. Reabsorption involves multiple proteins and both passive and active processes. Water mainly returns by osmosis; ADH does not directly pump individual water molecules. Urea is one nitrogenous waste associated with amino-acid metabolism, not the only solute in filtrate.

Our original balance fixes filtrate at 100 mL. Assumed 90% recovery returns 90 mL and leaves 10 mL. At assumed 98% recovery, 98 mL returns and 2 mL remains. Both conserve 100 mL. These invented total fractions support comparison; they are not daily human measurements or separate fractions for every nephron segment.

Water shortage can raise plasma osmotic concentration. Hypothalamic detection supports greater ADH release from the posterior pituitary. ADH increases water permeability in regions including collecting ducts. With a suitable water-potential gradient, more water returns, urine volume falls and the original change is reduced: negative feedback. Signals can diminish as conditions recover. Blood-volume signals and other hormones also contribute. Excretion removes metabolic wastes and excess substances, whereas egestion removes gut remains. Shared outward movement does not make these processes identical or turn a teaching diagram into a diagnostic test.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：滤液就是尿，血中的有用物质都不能被过滤。反例：小分子葡萄糖可进入滤液，随后通常被回收；血细胞和大部分大蛋白则正常保留。

### English

Misconception: filtrate is urine and useful substances cannot be filtered. Small glucose molecules can enter filtrate and normally be recovered; cells and most large proteins are retained.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中衔接：不同肾段有不同通透性和运输机制，维持髓质梯度也重要。ADH在下丘脑合成、由垂体后叶释放；本课只建反馈与水量收支，不建立完整逆流浓缩或分泌模型。

### English

Senior bridge: nephron segments differ in permeability and transport, and medullary gradients matter. ADH is synthesised in the hypothalamus and released from the posterior pituitary. This model omits full countercurrent concentration and secretion mechanisms.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：同量100 mL滤液，A回收95 mL、B回收97 mL。B的尿量比A少几成？自查：先用守恒得A=5、B=3 mL；下降(5−3)/5=40%，不是2%；仅这份模型比较，不可据此诊断ADH或肾功能，其他控制未给。

### English

New case: from 100 mL filtrate, A recovers 95 and B 97 mL. How much lower is B’s urine volume? Self-check: conservation gives 5 versus 3 mL; reduction is (5−3)/5=40%, not 2%. This model comparison alone cannot diagnose ADH or kidney function; other conditions are unspecified.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **排泄 / excretion**: 移除代谢废物和过量物质。
- **排遗 / egestion**: 排出消化道内未消化/未吸收的食物残余。
- **肾单位 / nephron**: 肾中执行过滤与后续处理的功能单位。
- **滤液 / filtrate**: 经肾小球屏障进入肾小囊的液体。
- **重吸收 / reabsorption**: 物质由肾小管液返回血液。
- **抗利尿激素 / ADH**: 影响集合管等处水通透性的调节激素。
- **负反馈 / negative feedback**: 响应减弱引发响应的原始变化。
- **尿素 / urea**: 氨基酸代谢产生的含氮废物之一，主要经尿排出。

### English

- **排泄 / excretion**: Removal of metabolic wastes and excess substances.
- **排遗 / egestion**: Removal of undigested/unabsorbed food remains from the gut.
- **肾单位 / nephron**: Kidney functional unit performing filtration and subsequent processing.
- **滤液 / filtrate**: Fluid entering the renal capsule through the glomerular barrier.
- **重吸收 / reabsorption**: Movement from tubular fluid back into blood.
- **抗利尿激素 / ADH**: A hormone regulating water permeability in regions including collecting ducts.
- **负反馈 / negative feedback**: A response reducing the original change that triggered it.
- **尿素 / urea**: A nitrogenous waste from amino-acid metabolism, mainly removed in urine.

## Check / 检索练习

### 中文

1. [concept] 哪种物质正常可进入滤液后被大量重吸收？

   A. 完整红细胞
   B. 大部分大血浆蛋白
   C. 葡萄糖

   答案: C. 葡萄糖足够小可进入滤液，再被选择性回收；细胞和大部分大蛋白正常保留在血中。

   回图左侧过滤与回收箭头，分别标去向。

2. [concept] 水分不足后ADH升高的典型机制是什么？

   A. 增加集合管水通透性，适宜梯度下增加回收
   B. ADH直接泵水进尿
   C. 关闭所有肾过滤且永久停止

   答案: A. ADH调节通透性而非直接搬水；模型不支持所有过滤停止的绝对说法。

   读静态反馈链，圈出“原变化减弱”。

3. [reasoning] 同100 mL滤液，回收90和98 mL时，尿量如何比较？

   A. 90与98 mL，因为回收就是尿量
   B. 10与2 mL，较高回收组少80%
   C. 10与2 mL，证明任何人的ADH浓度

   答案: B. 尿量=滤液−回收；(10−2)/10=80%。守恒例不能单独推断个人激素水平。

   核对水量表，再以较低回收组10作分母。

4. [vocabulary] excretion和egestion的正确配对是什么？

   A. 排泄尿素；排遗消化道食物残余
   B. 二者都只是排出粪便
   C. 排泄就是过滤红细胞

   答案: A. 排泄按代谢废物/过量物质定义，排遗按消化道残余定义；并非一切排出都一样。

   回Vocabulary与Explain末段。

### English

1. [concept] Which can normally enter filtrate and then be largely reabsorbed?

   A. Intact red cells
   B. Most large plasma proteins
   C. Glucose

   Answer: C. Small glucose enters filtrate and is selectively recovered; cells and most large proteins normally remain in blood.

   Label the filtration and recovery arrows separately.

2. [concept] What typically follows higher ADH during water shortage?

   A. Greater collecting-duct water permeability and recovery with a suitable gradient
   B. ADH pumps water directly into urine
   C. All filtration permanently stops

   Answer: A. ADH changes permeability rather than directly carrying water; the model does not imply all filtration stops.

   Read the feedback chain and mark reduction of the original change.

3. [reasoning] For 100 mL filtrate with 90 and 98 mL recovered, compare urine.

   A. 90 and 98 mL because recovery equals urine
   B. 10 and 2 mL; higher recovery gives 80% less
   C. 10 and 2 mL, proving anyone’s ADH concentration

   Answer: B. Urine=filtrate−recovery; (10−2)/10=80%. A conservation example alone cannot infer an individual hormone level.

   Check the balance table and use 10 as the comparison denominator.

4. [vocabulary] Which correctly pairs excretion and egestion?

   A. Excretion of urea; egestion of gut food remains
   B. Both only mean passing faeces
   C. Excretion means filtering out red cells

   Answer: A. Excretion concerns metabolic wastes/excess substances; egestion concerns gut remains. Outward movement alone does not equate them.
