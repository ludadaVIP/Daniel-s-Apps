## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

同一激素随血液接触许多细胞，为什么不是每个细胞都作出相同响应？

### English

If one hormone reaches many cells in blood, why do they not all respond identically?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选一个解释，不评分；学后用具体证据修正：所有激素都慢，所有神经效果都短；路线与受体共同决定响应，速度需要证据；激素到达哪里，哪里必然同样响应

### English

Choose an initial explanation without a score; revise using specific evidence: All hormones are slow and all neural effects brief；Routes and receptors shape response, while speed needs evidence；Every cell reached by a hormone responds identically

四格路线把起点、运输、接触与靶响应分开。神经格对应轴突与突触，激素格对应分泌与血液运输。每一格不是固定的真实时长。

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

身体要把变化的信息传递给能够响应的部位。神经系统沿神经元传递电信号，在许多突触处通过局部化学信号影响下一个细胞；内分泌细胞释放激素，激素经血液等体液送到多个位置。两种系统的路线不同，但都依赖细胞识别信号及产生响应的机制。神经并不是一条空心管把激素运到肌肉，激素也不是身体中所有物质的统称。

信号到达不等于一定有效。靶细胞需要适当受体和下游条件；同种激素接触的许多细胞中，只有合适的靶细胞能作出相应响应，且不同细胞可能产生不同效果。图的“无受体”分支说明本信号无法触发所设响应，不表示这个细胞没有任何功能或所有受体。

原创数据例把刺激起点和可检测响应统一定义：选定神经响应起效0.05 s、持续0.2 s，选定激素响应起效5 s、持续60 s。此例激素起效时间是神经的100倍、持续时间是300倍；这是两种选定响应的对照，不能推出所有激素都比所有神经慢。肾上腺素相关反应可在较短时间发生，神经调控也可能引发持续的系统变化。考试应看时间轴单位、测量起点及响应指标，再说快慢。

两套系统可以协同：外界刺激引发神经活动，神经又可促进某些内分泌细胞释放激素，激素支持更广泛的反应。调节水分时，下丘脑感受相关变化，ADH释放影响肾集合管，回收水增加后原始变化减弱。完整解释应连接刺激、传递、靶细胞和反馈，而不是背“神经快、激素慢”就结束。本图不模拟动作电位细节或个人身体反应时间。

### English

Coordination sends information about change to locations capable of responding. Neurons conduct electrical signals; at many synapses, local chemical signals affect another cell. Endocrine cells release hormones that travel through blood or other body fluids to many locations. Routes differ, but both systems require cellular recognition and response mechanisms. A nerve is not a hollow pipe carrying hormones to muscle, and hormone is not a name for every chemical in the body.

Arrival does not guarantee an effect. A target requires appropriate receptors and downstream conditions. Many cells may encounter the same hormone, while only suitable targets respond; different targets can produce different responses. Our missing-receptor branch means the selected signal cannot initiate the specified response. It does not mean the cell lacks every receptor or has no function.

Our original timing example defines a common stimulus start and detectable-response criterion. A selected neural response begins after 0.05 s and lasts 0.2 s; a selected endocrine response begins after 5 s and lasts 60 s. The endocrine onset is 100 times longer and duration 300 times longer in this comparison. These values do not establish that every hormone is slower than every neural response. Adrenaline-related effects can begin relatively rapidly, while neural regulation can initiate lasting systemic changes. Read units, timing origins and response measures before comparing speed.

The systems can cooperate. A stimulus changes neural activity, which can promote secretion by endocrine cells, supporting wider responses. In water regulation, hypothalamic detection, ADH release and kidney target responses cooperate; increased water recovery reduces the original disturbance. A full explanation connects stimulus, route, target and feedback rather than ending with “nerves fast, hormones slow”. This diagram does not simulate action-potential details or an individual’s reaction time.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：激素随血到全身，所以所有细胞同样响应。反例：缺少匹配受体的细胞虽接触信号，不能启动本设定响应；受体还不是决定真实效果的唯一因素。

### English

Misconception: blood distributes hormones widely, so every cell responds alike. A cell lacking a matching receptor can encounter the signal without initiating this response; receptors are not the sole determinant of real effects.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：信号P经血运输，细胞A有受体却被阻断下游过程，B没有受体；二者均不响应，能说P不是激素吗？自查：激素由分泌/运输/靶作用定义，不由这两次阴性结果否定；B提示识别条件，A提示下游机制条件；需要未阻断且有受体的阳性对照。

### English

New case: blood-borne signal P reaches A, whose downstream response is blocked despite a receptor, and B, which lacks the receptor. Neither responds. Is P disproved as a hormone? Self-check: use secretion, transport and target action; B tests recognition, A downstream machinery. Add an unblocked receptor-positive control.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **神经元 / neuron**: 传递神经信息的特化细胞。
- **轴突 / axon**: 神经元中把信号传向末端的长突起。
- **突触 / synapse**: 神经元与另一细胞间传递信息的连接处。
- **激素 / hormone**: 内分泌细胞释放并作用于靶细胞的化学信号。
- **受体 / receptor**: 能识别特定信号并参与响应的分子结构。
- **靶细胞 / target cell**: 具备相应受体和响应机制的细胞。
- **协调 / coordination**: 多个过程相互配合以形成适宜响应。
- **起效时间 / onset time**: 从所定起点到可检测响应的时间。

### English

- **神经元 / neuron**: A specialised cell transmitting neural information.
- **轴突 / axon**: A neuronal projection conducting signals towards terminals.
- **突触 / synapse**: A junction transmitting signals between a neuron and another cell.
- **激素 / hormone**: A chemical signal released by endocrine cells and acting on targets.
- **受体 / receptor**: A molecular structure recognising a signal and supporting response.
- **靶细胞 / target cell**: A cell with suitable receptors and response machinery.
- **协调 / coordination**: Processes working together to produce suitable responses.
- **起效时间 / onset time**: Time from a defined start to a detectable response.

## Check / 检索练习

### 中文

1. [concept] 某激素接触细胞但无匹配受体，最合理预测是什么？

   A. 一定产生与所有细胞相同响应
   B. 不能启动本设定受体依赖响应
   C. 该细胞完全没有生命活动

   答案: B. 接触与识别不同；缺少本受体不代表细胞没有其他功能。真实响应还需下游机制。

   保持激素路线，只切受体有/无。

2. [concept] 哪句话正确描述协同？

   A. 神经可影响内分泌释放，两者共同参与响应
   B. 两系统永远互不联系
   C. 所有内分泌信号沿轴突传递

   答案: A. 神经和激素可相互影响；路线不同不等于不能协作。激素典型经血，而非沿轴突。

   读Explain的刺激→神经→内分泌例。

3. [reasoning] 本表神经0.05 s、激素5 s起效，支持哪项？

   A. 所有激素永远慢100倍
   B. 本例激素起效时间为100倍，不能推广全部系统
   C. 5−0.05=100 s

   答案: B. 5/0.05=100是比值，不是时间差。证据只支持这两种选定响应。

   回时间表，先标单位s再选分母。

4. [vocabulary] target cell最准确指什么？

   A. 所有被血液经过的细胞
   B. 只要细胞尺寸大
   C. 有适当受体和响应机制的细胞

   答案: C. 靶性来自识别和响应条件，不是血液接近或大小。

   回图靶细胞与受体开关。

### English

1. [concept] A hormone reaches a cell without its matching receptor. What follows?

   A. It must produce the same response as every other cell
   B. It cannot initiate the specified receptor-dependent response
   C. The cell has no life processes

   Answer: B. Contact differs from recognition; lacking this receptor does not remove other cell functions. Downstream machinery also matters.

   Keep hormone signalling and toggle receptor presence.

2. [concept] Which describes coordination?

   A. Neural activity can affect endocrine release, supporting a shared response
   B. The systems never interact
   C. All endocrine signals travel down axons

   Answer: A. Nerves and hormones can interact. Different routes do not prevent cooperation; hormones typically travel in blood rather than along axons.

   Trace the stimulus→nerve→endocrine example.

3. [reasoning] The table gives neural onset 0.05 s and hormonal onset 5 s. What is supported?

   A. Every hormone is always 100 times slower
   B. This example’s endocrine onset is 100 times longer; it is not universal
   C. 5−0.05 equals 100 s

   Answer: B. 5/0.05=100 is a ratio, not a time difference. Evidence covers these selected responses only.

   Read seconds in the timing table and choose the denominator.

4. [vocabulary] What best defines a target cell?

   A. Every cell near blood
   B. Any large cell
   C. A cell with suitable receptors and response machinery

   Answer: C. Target specificity depends on recognition and response conditions, not proximity to blood or size.

   Revisit the target panel and receptor control.
