## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

20个细菌中原有2个耐药，选择后为什么耐药比例升高，却不一定有更多耐药菌？

**学习目标（30分钟）**

- 区分已有变异、差异存活与繁殖。
- 分别计算耐药数量与比例。
- 区别突变与水平基因转移，避免目的性解释。

### English

Two of 20 bacteria are initially resistant. Why can their proportion rise without an initial increase in their number?

**Learning goals (30 minutes)**

- Distinguish existing variation, differential survival and reproduction.
- Calculate resistant counts and fractions separately.
- Distinguish mutation and horizontal gene transfer without purposeful explanations.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

耐药性是微生物的性状，不是人的身体对抗生素“免疫”。抗生素影响细菌的结构或过程，不能把对细菌的机制直接套给病毒。细菌之间可有遗传差异；某些差异使细胞在特定抗生素存在时较易存活。环境因而改变谁留下更多后代，这叫选择，不表示每个细菌都因需要而主动改变。

本模型从20个细菌开始：18个敏感，2个已有耐药性。规定选择后18个敏感菌全部消失、2个耐药菌存活，因此耐药数量仍为2，占比却由2/20＝10%变成2/2＝100%。随后每个存活者产生4个后代，得到8个耐药菌。数量增加发生在繁殖步骤，比例上升已经发生在差异存活步骤。没有选择时，两类都各产生4个后代，敏感72、耐药8，比例仍为10%。

把初始耐药数改为0会得到不同结果：本模型在选择后没有存活者，占比没有定义，不能说“0/0＝0%”。模型没有突变或基因转移，因而不能凭空产生耐药菌。现实中新突变可以产生变异，但不按生存需要定向产生；某些耐药基因还可通过质粒等在细胞之间水平转移。这是获得遗传信息的另一条路线，不等于药物把每个敏感细胞教会了耐药。

示范作答：“选择提高了本模型存活者中的耐药比例：2/20到2/2，但耐药数在这一阶段仍为2。敏感菌被去除造成分母下降，随后耐药菌繁殖到8个。这里支持对已有变异的选择，不证明选择时发生了新突变。”现实种群还受迁入、空间结构、代价和不同存活率影响。图中的全杀灭和四倍繁殖只是简化条件，不能用于判断具体感染或安排用药。

补查分母时还要标同一观察窗口；不能拿一组的起点与另一组的后代直接比较，随后再把差异归给选择。

### English

Resistance is a microbial trait, not a human body becoming immune to antibiotics. Antibiotics act on bacterial structures or processes; that mechanism does not automatically work against viruses. Bacteria can differ genetically, and some variants survive better under a particular antibiotic. Selection changes which variants leave descendants. It does not mean that every cell deliberately changes because resistance is needed.

The model starts with 20 cells: 18 sensitive and 2 already resistant. Under the stipulated pressure all sensitive cells disappear while both resistant cells survive. The resistant count is still 2, but its fraction changes from 2/20 = 10% to 2/2 = 100%. Each survivor then produces four descendants, giving 8 resistant cells. The count increases during reproduction; the fraction had already risen during differential survival. Without pressure, both types produce four descendants, giving 72 sensitive and 8 resistant cells, still 10% resistant.

Starting with zero resistant cells changes the outcome. Under pressure there are no survivors, so the fraction is undefined: 0/0 is not 0%. This model contains neither mutation nor gene transfer and cannot create resistant cells. In real populations, new mutations can generate variants without being directed by need. Resistance genes can also move between cells, for example on plasmids. Horizontal transfer is a different genetic route from parent-to-offspring inheritance, not evidence that a drug teaches every sensitive cell resistance.

A worked answer is: “Selection increased the resistant fraction among survivors from 2/20 to 2/2, while the resistant count remained 2. Removing sensitive cells reduced the denominator. Reproduction subsequently raised the resistant count to 8. This supports selection of existing variation, not a conclusion that new mutations occurred during selection.” Migration, spatial structure, fitness costs and varied survival are omitted. These all-or-none survival rules are teaching assumptions, not a way to assess an infection or plan medicine use.

## Depth / 高中延伸

### 中文

高中延伸：区分性状来源与性状频率改变；水平转移能改变一个细胞的遗传组成，选择决定这些细胞在群体中的相对后代贡献。此处不展开分子耐药机制。

### English

Depth: Separate the origin of a trait from its frequency change. Horizontal transfer can alter a cell’s genetic composition; selection changes relative descendant contribution. Molecular resistance mechanisms are beyond this model.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

比例100%不等于数量增加；没有存活者时比例未定义。

### English

A 100% fraction does not itself mean the count rose; with no survivors the fraction is undefined.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新种群初始36S+4R，选择后3S+3R。自查：R数4→3而比例10%→50%，差异存活仍可提高比例；未测遗传变化，不能断言新突变或水平转移。

### English

A new population changes from 36S+4R to 3S+3R. Self-check: R count falls 4→3 while its fraction rises 10%→50%; differential survival can still increase frequency. Unmeasured genetic changes cannot establish new mutation or horizontal transfer.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **antibiotic / 抗生素**: 杀死细菌或限制其生长的物质。
- **resistance / 耐药性**: 微生物在相应药物作用下仍能存活繁殖的能力。
- **selection pressure / 选择压力**: 使不同性状具有不同存活或繁殖结果的条件。
- **mutation / 突变**: DNA序列发生改变。
- **horizontal gene transfer / 水平基因转移**: 不是亲子繁殖而在细胞间传递遗传物质。
- **frequency / 频率**: 某类在明确总体中的比例。
- **offspring / 后代**: 由繁殖产生的新个体或细胞。

### English

- **antibiotic / 抗生素**: A substance that kills bacteria or restricts their growth.
- **resistance / 耐药性**: Microbial ability to survive and multiply despite a relevant drug.
- **selection pressure / 选择压力**: A condition causing different survival or reproduction among traits.
- **mutation / 突变**: A change in DNA sequence.
- **horizontal gene transfer / 水平基因转移**: Genetic transfer between cells outside parent-offspring reproduction.
- **frequency / 频率**: A fraction of a type in a defined total.
- **offspring / 后代**: New individuals or cells produced by reproduction.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] 耐药性首先是谁的性状？
   A. 人的肌肉
   B. 药物的记忆
   C. 细菌等相关微生物
   答案: C. 耐药是微生物在药物作用下存活繁殖的能力，不是人变耐药。 复习: 回看S/R细胞标记。

2. [concept] 初始无R且模型不含变异，选择后会怎样？
   A. 自动产生R
   B. 没有存活者；比例未定义
   C. 每个S都变成R
   答案: B. 选择不能创造本模型中不存在的变异；零分母不能计算比例。 复习: 把初始耐药设0，检查存活栏。

3. [reasoning] 18S+2R变为0S+2R支持什么？
   A. 耐药比例10%到100%，耐药数仍为2
   B. R增加了90个
   C. 药物创造了18个突变
   答案: A. 分母从20变2；数量与频率是不同问题。后代步骤才让R变8。 复习: 比较表的起点和存活两行。

4. [vocabulary] horizontal gene transfer强调什么？
   A. 从亲代分裂得到后代
   B. 药物主动选择突变位置
   C. 非亲子途径的细胞间遗传物质传递
   答案: C. 水平转移区别于垂直遗传，也区别于DNA中新发生的突变。 复习: 回看Depth的性状来源。

### English

1. [concept] Whose trait is antibiotic resistance?
   A. Human muscle
   B. A medicine’s memory
   C. Relevant microbes such as bacteria
   Answer: C. Resistance concerns microbial survival and reproduction, not a person becoming resistant. Revisit: Revisit the S/R cell labels.

2. [concept] With no initial R and no variation-generating process, what follows pressure?
   A. R appears automatically
   B. No survivors; fraction undefined
   C. Every S becomes R
   Answer: B. Selection cannot create absent variation in this model; a zero denominator gives no fraction. Revisit: Set initial resistance to 0 and inspect survivors.

3. [reasoning] What follows from 18S+2R becoming 0S+2R?
   A. Resistant fraction rises 10% to 100%; resistant count stays 2
   B. R rises by 90 cells
   C. The drug creates 18 mutations
   Answer: A. The denominator falls from 20 to 2. Count and frequency differ; only the descendant step changes R to 8. Revisit: Compare start and survivor rows.

4. [vocabulary] What does horizontal gene transfer emphasise?
   A. Parent-to-descendant division
   B. A drug choosing mutation sites
   C. Genetic transfer between cells outside parent-offspring routes
   Answer: C. Horizontal transfer differs from vertical inheritance and from new sequence mutation. Revisit: Revisit trait origins in Depth.
