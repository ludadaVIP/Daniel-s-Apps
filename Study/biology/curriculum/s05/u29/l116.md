## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

虚构宣传“青禾饮品让事件风险减半”，10对20个事件就够了吗？

**学习目标（30分钟）**

- 用分子分母区分绝对与相对风险差。
- 检查随机分配、对照、盲法与失访。
- 用研究设计限定相关与因果结论。

### English

A fictional “Qinghe drink” advert says event risk is halved. Are 10 versus 20 events enough?

**Learning goals (30 minutes)**

- Use numerators and denominators to distinguish absolute and relative differences.
- Inspect random allocation, controls, blinding and loss to follow-up.
- Limit association and causal claims using study design.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

“青禾饮品”是本课虚构名称，不对应真实品牌。宣传给出A组10次、B组20次虚构症状事件，却隐去人数。若两组各200人、每人只记是否至少出现一次指定事件，则风险为10/200＝5%与20/200＝10%。以B为参考，绝对降低为10%−5%＝5个百分点，相对降低为5%/10%＝50%。两者可以同时正确，但“减半”若不配基线，会让读者误以为少了50个百分点。

分母会改写故事：若A是10/50＝20%，B仍为20/200＝10%，A事件数虽少，风险反而高。还要问指定观察期间、事件定义、谁进入样本和谁完成随访。若A最初200人中有30人失访，仅170人有记录，其中10人有事件，完整病例率为10/170约5.88%；不能假设失访者都没有事件；极端边界是10/200＝5%到40/200＝20%，已足以改变与B的比较。

随机分配能在长期重复中减少处理组与对照组的系统差异，但不能保证小样本所有因素完全平衡。对照组帮助比较若不接受目标处理会怎样；在可行时盲法能降低预期和评估偏差；失访仍可能破坏比较。自选饮品的人可能也睡得更多，睡眠就是替代解释，单张相关图不能排除它。观察研究仍可发现关联、罕见影响或产生假设，只是不能凭本图单独宣布因果。

示范作答：“无失访的等大完整样本中，A为5%、B为10%，相差5个百分点，相对B低50%。但若分组自选或A失访30人，这不能证明饮品造成变化。应核对原始分母、随访与随机对照设计，并预先定义同一结局。”这里学习的是审查证据的写法，不根据虚构数据判断任何个人的健康。

一个实用检查顺序是先画出分组人数、完成记录人数和事件人数三层，再计算比例。宣传词可以省略细节，科学作答应主动补回；样本有差异时不应只比较柱子的高度。

### English

“Qinghe drink” is an invented name, not a real brand. Its advert reports 10 fictional symptom events in A and 20 in B without stating group sizes. If each group has 200 people and each person is counted once for having a specified event during the stated period, the risks are 5% and 10%. The absolute reduction is 5 percentage points. Relative to B, the reduction is 5%/10% = 50%. Both statements can be correct, but “halved” without the baseline may sound like a 50-percentage-point improvement.

Denominators can reverse the story. If A contains 50 people, 10/50 = 20%, compared with B at 20/200 = 10%. Fewer events now accompany a higher risk. Ask about the observation period, event definition, selection and follow-up. If 30 of A’s original 200 participants lack outcome records, only 170 have records and the complete-case rate is 10/170, about 5.88%; treating missing participants as event-free is unjustified. Extreme bounds are 10/200 = 5% through 40/200 = 20%, enough to change the comparison with B.

Random allocation reduces systematic group differences across repeated trials but does not guarantee perfect balance in every small sample. A control group supports a comparison with the absence of the target treatment. Blinding, when feasible, reduces expectation and assessment biases; missing outcomes can still undermine the comparison. People who choose the drink may also sleep longer. That alternative explanation prevents a correlation plot alone from establishing causation. Observational research can still reveal associations, rare effects and hypotheses.

A worked answer is: “In the equal-sized no-loss scenario, A has 5% and B 10%, a 5-percentage-point difference or 50% relative reduction. Self-selection or 30 missing A outcomes prevents this alone proving that the drink caused the difference. Check original denominators, follow-up and allocation.” These invented data support evidence appraisal, not any judgement about a person’s health.

## Depth / 高中延伸

### 中文

高中延伸：失访边界是极端情境敏感性检查，不是估计最可能真实风险；随机分配处理混杂，随机抽样处理样本代表性，二者不同。

### English

Depth: Extreme missing-outcome bounds are a sensitivity check, not an estimate of the most likely risk. Random allocation addresses confounding; random sampling addresses representativeness.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

相对降低50%不是降低50个百分点；研究被随机分配也不能让失访自动消失。

### English

A 50% relative reduction is not 50 percentage points; random allocation does not erase missing outcomes.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

另一虚构材料：A为6/100、B为12/300。自查：A风险6%、B4%，相差2个百分点；事件少不等于风险低，设计未知不能断因果。

### English

Another fictional report: A has 6/100 and B 12/300. Self-check: risks are 6% and 4%, a 2-percentage-point difference; fewer events need not mean lower risk, and unknown design cannot establish causation.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **risk / 风险**: 指定期间指定结局的发生比例。
- **absolute risk difference / 绝对风险差**: 两组风险直接相减。
- **relative reduction / 相对降低**: 风险差除以明确参考组风险。
- **random allocation / 随机分配**: 用机会机制分配处理以减少组间系统差异。
- **confounder / 混杂因素**: 同时关联分组和结局、可提供替代解释的因素。
- **blinding / 盲法**: 在可行时不让相关人员知道分组。
- **loss to follow-up / 失访**: 分组后未获得预定结局记录。

### English

- **risk / 风险**: The proportion with a specified outcome over a specified period.
- **absolute risk difference / 绝对风险差**: Direct subtraction of two risks.
- **relative reduction / 相对降低**: Risk difference divided by a stated reference risk.
- **random allocation / 随机分配**: Assigning treatments by chance to reduce systematic group differences.
- **confounder / 混杂因素**: A factor associated with exposure and outcome that offers another explanation.
- **blinding / 盲法**: Keeping allocation unknown to relevant people when feasible.
- **loss to follow-up / 失访**: Missing planned outcome records after allocation.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] 10/200对20/200，哪项表达正确？
   A. 绝对降低50个百分点
   B. 绝对降低5个百分点，相对B降低50%
   C. A风险10%
   答案: B. 5%与10%相差5个百分点；除以B的10%才得到50%。 复习: 回看两组分母和风险栏。

2. [concept] 自选分组的观察图为什么仍可有价值？
   A. 能显示关联和产生假设，但替代解释未排除
   B. 它一定证明因果
   C. 它不可能提供任何证据
   答案: A. 证据价值不是全有全无；结论强度须匹配混杂等局限。 复习: 回看睡眠混杂例。

3. [reasoning] A原200人，10事件且30失访；哪项最稳妥？
   A. 30人都无事件
   B. 随机分组保证结果不变
   C. 全体风险极端可为5%至20%，要查失访
   答案: C. 未观察不等于零；若失访者全有事件则40/200=20%。这是边界，不是置信区间。 复习: 回看失访提示和完整/缺失切换。

4. [vocabulary] confounder最接近哪项？
   A. 任何拼写错误
   B. 关联分组与结局、提供替代解释的因素
   C. 两组的样本量
   答案: B. 睡眠可能同时影响饮品选择和结局；它不是单纯的随机波动。 复习: 回看饮品←睡眠→结局的分叉。

### English

1. [concept] For 10/200 versus 20/200, which statement is correct?
   A. Absolute reduction is 50 percentage points
   B. Absolute reduction is 5 percentage points; relative to B it is 50%
   C. A risk is 10%
   Answer: B. 5% and 10% differ by 5 percentage points; dividing by B’s 10% gives 50%. Revisit: Revisit denominators and risks.

2. [concept] Why can a self-selected observational graph still be useful?
   A. It can reveal associations and hypotheses while alternatives remain
   B. It necessarily proves causation
   C. It cannot provide evidence
   Answer: A. Evidence is not all-or-nothing; claim strength must match confounding and other limits. Revisit: Revisit the sleep confounder.

3. [reasoning] A originally has 200 people, 10 events and 30 missing outcomes. What is justified?
   A. All 30 had no events
   B. Random allocation guarantees no effect on results
   C. Extreme full-group bounds are 5%–20%; investigate missingness
   Answer: C. Unobserved is not zero. If all missing participants had events, 40/200=20%. This is a bound, not a confidence interval. Revisit: Revisit the missing-outcome switch.

4. [vocabulary] Which best describes a confounder?
   A. Any spelling error
   B. A factor linked to group and outcome that offers an alternative explanation
   C. The sample size alone
   Answer: B. Sleep may affect both drink choice and outcomes; it is not merely random fluctuation. Revisit: Revisit drink ← sleep → outcome.
