## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

- 用分类计数支持有边界的机制解释。

### English

Barriers, phagocytes and antibodies help defence. Are they doing the same job?

**Learning goals (30 minutes)**

- Connect barriers, pattern recognition and specific clonal responses.
- Distinguish the basic roles of B, helper T and cytotoxic T cells.
- Use classified counts to support a qualified mechanism explanation.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

免疫不是一个“看到坏东西就吞掉”的单一动作。完整皮肤和黏液先减少病原体进入；进入组织的微生物可能被先天免疫细胞的受体识别。先天受体能检测一些微生物共有的分子特征，所以“非特异性”不等于完全没有识别能力。吞噬细胞可以包围、摄入并分解某些微生物，还能通过信号与抗原呈递帮助启动适应性反应。两套系统互相协作，不是严格轮流工作的两堵墙。

适应性反应中，具有合适受体的少数B或T淋巴细胞被激活并增殖，形成特异性克隆。B细胞可分化成分泌抗体的浆细胞；抗体结合抗原，有的能阻挡病原体进入细胞，有的帮助其他防御成分识别目标。抗体本身不是细胞，不能吞噬。辅助T细胞帮助协调反应，细胞毒性T细胞能杀伤某些被感染的细胞；这不同于直接吞掉游离病毒。部分细胞保留为记忆细胞，使以后对同一抗原的反应不同。

图中虚构的100条防御观察记录，60条是屏障阻挡，25条是吞噬，15条是特异B细胞克隆扩增。按本课分类，前85条属于先天路线，后15条属于适应性路线。这不是人体防御贡献百分比：记录单位、抽样地点和时间会改变计数，三类事件也可能发生在同一场感染中。

示范作答：“这些记录支持防御包含多条协作路线，因为100条记录中85条涉及屏障或吞噬，15条涉及B细胞克隆。前者减少进入或清除目标，后者产生特异性效应及记忆。但该分类样本不能比较两类防御对所有感染的重要程度。”把数值的分母和机制写出，比只写‘免疫保护人’更能让读者检查推理。

### English

Immunity consists of cooperating processes. Skin and mucus reduce entry. Innate receptors detect shared molecular features of microbes, so “non-specific” does not mean unable to recognise anything. Phagocytes engulf some targets and send signals; antigen presentation helps connect innate defence to adaptive responses. The systems can overlap in time rather than working as two strictly sequential walls.

In an adaptive response, selected B or T lymphocytes with suitable receptors become activated and multiply. B cells can develop into antibody-secreting plasma cells. Antibodies bind particular antigens: some block entry into host cells, while others help immune components recognise a target. Antibodies are proteins, not engulfing cells. Helper T cells coordinate responses. Cytotoxic T cells kill certain infected cells, which is different from swallowing free viruses. Some activated cells become memory cells.

The diagram contains 100 fictional observations: 60 barrier events, 25 phagocytosis events and 15 specific B-cell clonal-expansion events. Under this classification, 85 observations belong to innate routes and 15 to an adaptive route. They are event counts, not percentages of all protection supplied by each system. Timing, location, sampling and the definition of an observation would change those numbers. Several event types can occur during the same infection.

A worked answer is: “The records support several cooperating defence routes. Of 100 observations, 85 concern barriers or phagocytosis and 15 concern B-cell clones. The former reduce entry or remove targets; the latter generate specific effects and memory. This selected sample cannot rank their importance for every infection.” A transferable answer names the claim, quotes a denominator, explains a mechanism and limits the inference. A different pathogen may be controlled by a different combination of routes; classification alone does not tell us the clinical outcome.

## Depth / 高中延伸

### 中文

高中延伸：识别抗原不必直接产生同一种效应；受体、信号与细胞分化之间还有步骤。这里不展开抗原呈递分子或信号通路，也不把“先天无记忆”当成对训练性先天反应的绝对断言。

### English

Depth: Recognition need not directly produce one fixed effect; signalling and differentiation intervene. Molecular presentation and signalling pathways are beyond this lesson. The contrast with adaptive clonal memory is not an absolute denial of trained innate responses.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“先天没有识别”“抗体吞噬”均错误：共有模式识别不同于特异克隆识别；吞噬需要细胞。

### English

“Innate defence cannot recognise” and “antibodies engulf” are errors: shared-pattern recognition differs from specific clonal recognition; engulfment requires a cell.

### English

A new 40-record sample has 12 phagocytosis, 8 barrier and 20 specific T-cell-clone events. Self-check: 20/40 innate and 20/40 adaptive; T cells are not antibodies. Equal event fractions do not prove equal protection; outcomes and sampling details are missing.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **innate immunity / 先天免疫**: 屏障及快速识别共有特征的防御。
- **adaptive immunity / 适应性免疫**: 涉及抗原特异性淋巴细胞克隆及记忆的防御。
- **antigen / 抗原**: 能被适应性免疫受体识别的分子结构。
- **phagocytosis / 吞噬作用**: 细胞包围并摄入颗粒的过程。
- **antibody / 抗体**: 由浆细胞分泌、结合特定抗原的蛋白质。
- **clone / 克隆**: 从同一细胞增殖而来的细胞群。
- **T cell / T细胞**: 参与协调或杀伤感染细胞的淋巴细胞。

### English

- **innate immunity / 先天免疫**: Barriers and rapid defences recognising shared features.
- **adaptive immunity / 适应性免疫**: Defence involving antigen-specific lymphocyte clones and memory.
- **antigen / 抗原**: A molecular structure recognised by adaptive immune receptors.
- **phagocytosis / 吞噬作用**: Cellular engulfment of particles.
- **antibody / 抗体**: A protein secreted by plasma cells that binds a particular antigen.
- **clone / 克隆**: Cells descended by division from one cell.
- **T cell / T细胞**: A lymphocyte involved in coordination or killing infected cells.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] 先天识别与适应性识别怎样区分？
   A. 先天识别共有特征；适应性涉及特异克隆
   B. 先天没有受体
   C. 所有细胞只认一种相同抗原

2. [concept] 抗体的基本作用是什么？
   A. 自己吞掉所有病原体
   B. 结合抗原并可帮助中和或标记目标
   C. 代替全部T细胞工作
   答案: B. 抗体是蛋白质；吞噬细胞才完成摄入。T细胞还有协调和杀伤感染细胞的角色。 复习: 回看图中Y形蛋白与细胞圆圈。

3. [reasoning] 60屏障、25吞噬、15克隆记录支持什么？
   A. 先天贡献人体全部保护的85%
   B. 适应性没有价值
   C. 本样本85/100条归先天，不能当保护贡献率

4. [vocabulary] phagocytosis指什么？
   A. 细胞包围并摄入颗粒
   B. 抗体在血中扩增分裂
   C. 病原体记住抗原

### English

1. [concept] How do innate and adaptive recognition differ?
   A. Innate receptors detect shared features; adaptive responses involve specific clones
   B. Innate cells have no receptors
   C. Every cell recognises one identical antigen
   Answer: A. Innate defence recognises features too; clonal selection and antigen-specific memory are the key contrast here. Revisit: Revisit pattern recognition and the B/T branches.

2. [concept] What is a basic antibody action?
   A. Engulf all pathogens itself
   B. Bind an antigen and help neutralise or tag a target
   C. Replace all T-cell functions
   Answer: B. An antibody is a protein; a phagocyte engulfs. T cells also coordinate and kill infected cells. Revisit: Compare the Y-shaped protein with the phagocyte circle.

3. [reasoning] What do 60 barrier, 25 phagocytosis and 15 clone records support?
   A. Innate immunity supplies 85% of all human protection
   B. Adaptive immunity has no value
   C. 85/100 sampled events are innate; this is not a protection contribution rate
   Answer: C. The denominator is 100 observations, not all infection outcomes. Quote the data and limit the scope. Revisit: Check the card counts and observation unit.

4. [vocabulary] What does phagocytosis mean?
   A. A cell engulfs particles
   B. Antibodies divide in blood
   C. Pathogens remember antigens
   Answer: A. The term names cellular engulfment, not antibody division. Revisit: Revisit the phagocyte engulfment arrow.
