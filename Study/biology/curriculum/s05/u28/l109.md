## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**把字母放回分子。** DNA由核苷酸连接而成，每个核苷酸含脱氧核糖、磷酸和一种碱基。糖与磷酸构成链的骨架，碱基朝向双链内部。常见双链DNA扭成双螺旋，本课把它展平以便读图；这不是说真实DNA总像直梯，也不是完整基因组只有几对碱基。

**配对与方向要同时满足。** A与T配对，G与C配对，两条链反平行。一条按图左到右为5′–AGTC–3′，另一条同方向写成3′–TCAG–5′。若题目要求另一条也以5′开头，就必须从图右往左读，得到5′–GACT–3′。只写TCAG却不标方向，会隐藏一个重要条件；把两条左端都写5′则画成了平行链。

**信息在次序，不在色块。** AAGC与AGAC具有相同字母总数，排列不同，信息仍可不同。示意图颜色只是帮助分辨，换配色不改变真实碱基序列。一个短片段的变化可能影响功能，也可能不造成可见性状变化；必须知道它的位置、是否影响产物及细胞环境，不能看到变一个字母就断言生病或出现新性状。

**配对也支持定量推理。** 原创题给完整配对双链100个核苷酸，A占30个，则T也为30，剩40个由G和C平均分，各20。这里100个核苷酸是50对碱基，不是100对。A＝T、G＝C是对整段完整双链统计；单独一条链不必满足。迁移到未知片段时，先检查题目说的是单链、双链、核苷酸数还是碱基对数，再做计算，能避免把两个不同计数单位混在一起。

### English

Put the letters back into the molecule. DNA consists of linked nucleotides, each containing deoxyribose sugar, phosphate and a base. Sugar and phosphate form the backbone; bases face the interior of the double strand. Common cellular DNA forms a double helix. Flattening it into a ladder helps us inspect orientation but does not imply that real DNA is straight or that six pairs constitute an entire genome.

Pairing and direction must both be correct. A pairs with T and G with C, while the two strands run antiparallel. If the upper strand reads 5′–AGTC–3′ from left to right, the lower strand reads 3′–TCAG–5′ in that same visual direction. To report the lower strand in its own 5′→3′ direction, read from the right:5′–GACT–3′. Writing only TCAG hides orientation; labelling both left ends 5′ incorrectly draws parallel strands.

Information depends on sequence rather than colour. AAGC and AGAC have identical base totals but different order. Recolouring a diagram does not change its DNA sequence. A sequence change may affect function or may have no visible phenotypic consequence. Its location, effects on products and cellular context matter, so changing one letter alone does not establish disease or a new trait.

Pairing also supports numerical reasoning. In an original fully paired double-stranded fragment containing 100 nucleotides,30 A imply 30 T. The remaining 40 are split equally into 20 G and 20 C. The 100 nucleotides represent 50 base pairs, not 100 pairs. Equality of A and T, and G and C, describes the whole paired double strand, not necessarily either isolated strand. In a new fragment question, identify whether the count concerns strands, nucleotides or base pairs before calculating. This distinguishes a structural rule from an unwarranted assumption about every single strand.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“互补就是照抄”“A=T在每条单链都成立”分别忽略配对和统计范围。颜色、字母数和序列是不同信息层。

### English

“Complement means copying” and “A=T in every single strand” ignore pairing and counting scope. Colour, totals and sequence are different information levels.

## Depth / 高中桥梁

### 中文

5′与3′来自糖结构位置，用来标链方向；无需在此背完整化学式。配对帮助复制但复制的酶和机制留到S6；不要把本模型当DNA编辑工具。

### English

The 5′ and 3′ labels refer to sugar positions and strand orientation. Detailed chemistry and replication enzymes belong to later study; this model is not a DNA-editing tool.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：双链DNA共80对碱基，其中G有18个。自查：总核苷酸160，C18，A=T=(160−36)/2=62；说明完整配对假设，不能把80当总核苷酸。

### English

New situation: double-stranded DNA has 80 base pairs and 18 G nucleotides. Self-check:160 nucleotides total; C18 and A=T=(160−36)/2=62. State complete pairing and avoid treating 80 as the nucleotide total.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **DNA** — 常见细胞遗传信息载体，双链脱氧核糖核酸。
- **核苷酸** — 包含糖、磷酸和碱基的基本单位。
- **碱基** — A、T、G、C所代表的含氮部分。
- **互补** — 特定配对关系，如A–T、G–C。
- **反平行** — 配对链的化学方向相反。
- **序列** — 核苷酸或碱基沿链的排列次序。
- **基因组** — 生物一套遗传信息的整体。

### English

- **DNA** — The usual cellular genetic-information molecule, deoxyribonucleic acid.
- **nucleotide** — A building unit containing sugar, phosphate and a base.
- **base** — The nitrogen-containing component represented by A,T,G,C.
- **complementary** — Specific pairing relationships, such as A–T and G–C.
- **antiparallel** — Paired strands having opposite chemical orientations.
- **sequence** — The order of nucleotides or bases along a strand.
- **genome** — An organism’s complete set of genetic information.

## Check / 理解检查

### 中文

1. DNA核苷酸包含哪组结构？
   - A. 只有一种碱基
   - B. 脂肪酸、甘油和蛋白质
   - C. 脱氧核糖、磷酸和碱基

2. 上链5′–AGTC–3′，下链按同一左→右顺序应写？
   - A. 3′–TCAG–5′
   - B. 5′–TCAG–3′
   - C. 3′–AGTC–5′

3. 双链片段共100个核苷酸，其中A有30个。哪组总量可成立？
   - A. T20、G25、C25
   - B. T30、G20、C20
   - C. T30、G30、C10

4. antiparallel指什么？
   - A. 两链颜色必须相反
   - B. 两链一个是蛋白质
   - C. 一链5′→3′方向对应另一链3′→5′

<details>
<summary>作答后查看答案与解释</summary>

1. C. 图把骨架和内侧字母分开；字母只是核苷酸的一部分。 读糖–磷酸骨架与内侧碱基说明。

2. A. A配T、G配C，且化学方向相反；若下链也要求5′→3′，应写5′–GACT–3′。 观察四个端点，按位置配对再检查方向。

3. B. 完整配对双链中A=T=30；剩40分给G=C，各20。此规则不必对孤立单链成立。 用A–T、G–C规则核对配对和总和100。

4. C. 反平行是分子方向，不是上下位置或示意色。 看上链与下链左端不同的端点。

</details>

### English

1. Which components make a DNA nucleotide?
   - A. A base alone
   - B. Fatty acids, glycerol and protein
   - C. Deoxyribose, phosphate and a base

2. For upper 5′–AGTC–3′, how is the lower strand written left→right?
   - A. 3′–TCAG–5′
   - B. 5′–TCAG–3′
   - C. 3′–AGTC–5′

3. A double-stranded fragment has 100 nucleotides, including 30 A. Which totals fit?
   - A. T20,G25,C25
   - B. T30,G20,C20
   - C. T30,G30,C10

4. What does antiparallel mean?
   - A. The strands must have opposite colours
   - B. One strand is protein
   - C. One 5′→3′ direction aligns with the other 3′→5′

<details>
<summary>Reveal answers and explanations after answering</summary>

1. C. The diagram separates backbone and internal letters; a base is only part of a nucleotide. Read the backbone and internal-base labels.

2. A. Use A–T and G–C with opposite orientation. Reporting the lower strand 5′→3′ instead gives 5′–GACT–3′. Inspect all four ends; pair positions before checking orientation.

3. B. For a fully paired double strand,A=T=30; the remaining 40 areG=C=20 each. An isolated single strand need not satisfy these totals. Use pairing and the total 100 together.

4. C. Antiparallel describes molecular orientation, not vertical position or display colour. Compare the different left endpoints.

</details>
