# 从DNA到表现型的分层因果图 / A layered causal map from DNA to phenotype

## 主张 / Claim

画DNA→RNA→蛋白→细胞功能→表现型，另加功能RNA路径和环境箭头，标转录/翻译位置。用2n=4减数账本解释信息如何分配。引用L112 G=3时环境0→2、性状16→28，及G=0/6环境增量6/18单位，支持条件化交互主张。写结构证据和不能一基因一性状/不能外推人类的局限；英文标签与限制句可选，不作为生物通关条件。

Map DNA→RNA→protein→cell function→phenotype, with functional RNA and environmental arrows; locate transcription/translation. Use the 2n=4 meiotic budget to explain information distribution. Cite L112:G=3,E0→2 gives 16→28, while G=0/6 environmental increments are 6/18 units, supporting a conditional interaction claim. Include structural evidence and limits on one-gene/one-trait and human extrapolation. English labels and limitation sentences are optional, never a biology gate.

## 证据与机制 / Evidence and mechanism

用至少两组带单位或相对尺度的数值，标固定条件、改变变量与图中结构。用因为—所以把结构/梯度/信息或分配机制连到主张，避免只重述趋势。

Use at least two numerical comparisons with units or a relative scale, fixed conditions, the changed variable and a diagram structure. Link mechanism to claim instead of merely restating the trend.

## 局限与下一步 / Limitations and next step

指出原创理想化模型省略的一项条件及可能影响；提出只改一个条件、有重复的下一步比较。不能把模型数值说成真实物种或人群数据。

Identify one omitted condition, its potential consequence and a repeated comparison changing one variable. Do not present invented values as real species or human-population measurements.

## 自查 / Self-check

- 主张有适用条件；证据有两组具体数值或明确结构；推理说明机制；局限解释为什么限制结论。
- 条件性解释完成即可记录单元任务；英文词汇另行复习，不锁生物进度。
- Claim has scope; evidence includes two numerical comparisons or specified structures; reasoning explains a mechanism; the limitation explains its consequence.
- A valid biological explanation completes the mission; English vocabulary can be reviewed separately.
