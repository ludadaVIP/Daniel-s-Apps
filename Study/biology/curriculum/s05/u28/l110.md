## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**基因是可被使用的信息区域。** DNA包含许多区域，基因提供产生功能产物的信息。对蛋白编码基因，信息先转录到RNA，再由信使RNA引导蛋白质合成；另一些基因产生本身有功能的RNA，不以蛋白质作为终点。因此“基因＝一段一定直接变成蛋白质的DNA”过于简单。DNA也不在过程结束时耗尽或变身为蛋白质。

**分子角色和位置要分开。** 在本课真核细胞简图里，DNA留在细胞核，转录形成RNA；经过必要加工的mRNA可到细胞质，被核糖体读取。核糖体连接氨基酸形成多肽，随后蛋白质折叠并参与催化、运输或结构等功能。核糖体本身包含RNA和蛋白，说明RNA不只是临时传话纸。本图不要求背全套密码子，也不代表所有生物都有细胞核。

**用证据区别“有”和“用”。** 原创例中，两种细胞都含基因X，A测得RNA相对量8、蛋白相对量6，B分别为1和0.5。这与两细胞表达水平不同相容，却不表示A的DNA更多或B缺失基因。RNA和蛋白的生成、降解及测量时间都会影响数量，不能把8与6直接当一一换算比例。若只测蛋白低，也不能立刻定位是转录还是翻译阶段受影响。

**功能到性状还有层级。** 一个蛋白可参与多个过程，多种蛋白及环境也能共同影响同一性状。所以路线图显示信息流，并不承诺一基因决定一个可见结果。迁移到根细胞和叶细胞时，它们可共享大部分DNA却使用不同信息组合；要解释功能差异，应研究表达与细胞环境，而不是凭细胞形状断言基因组全不同。S6再展开转录、翻译及调控细节，这里先建立可以定位证据的框架。

### English

Genes provide usable information. A DNA gene region supplies information for a functional product. For a protein-coding gene, transcription produces RNA and messenger RNA guides protein synthesis. Other genes produce RNA that itself performs a function, without a protein endpoint. Defining every gene as DNA that directly becomes protein is therefore too simple. DNA is not used up or physically transformed into protein during expression.

Separate molecular roles from locations. In this eukaryotic overview, DNA remains in the nucleus and transcription forms RNA. After necessary processing, mRNA can reach the cytoplasm, where ribosomes read it and join amino acids into a chain. Folding then supports protein functions such as catalysis, transport or structure. Ribosomes themselves contain RNA and proteins, illustrating that RNA is more than a temporary message. A complete codon table is unnecessary here, and the nuclear location does not apply to all organisms.

Distinguish possessing information from using it. In an original example, both cell types contain gene X. Cell A has relative RNA8 and protein 6, whereas cell B has 1 and 0.5. These observations fit different expression levels; they do not show that A has more DNA or B lacks the gene. Production, degradation and sampling time affect RNA and protein amounts. The numbers 8 and 6 are not a direct one-to-one conversion ratio. Low protein alone also cannot locate an effect specifically at transcription or translation.

Further levels connect function to phenotype. One protein may participate in several processes, while multiple proteins and environmental conditions may influence the same trait. The arrow diagram therefore represents information flow, not one gene determining one visible outcome. Applying this to root and leaf cells, largely shared DNA can support different functions through different information use. Investigate expression and cellular context instead of inferring entirely different genomes from cell shape. Later lessons will expand transcription, translation and regulation; here the goal is a framework for locating evidence.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：处理后RNA相对量仍5，蛋白由4降至1。自查：数据支持蛋白层变化，不足以证明转录减少；可提出翻译或降解改变并说明须进一步测量。注明各数为不同指标的相对量，不作4比5产率。

### English

New situation: after treatment,RNA remains 5 relative units while protein falls 4→1. Self-check: identify a protein-level change without asserting reduced transcription; propose translation or degradation changes needing further measurements. The two scales are different indicators, not a 4/5 yield.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **基因** — 产生功能RNA或蛋白产物所需信息的DNA区域。
- **转录** — 以DNA信息形成RNA的过程。
- **RNA** — 参与信息传递、结构或其他细胞功能的核酸。
- **信使RNA** — 可供核糖体读取蛋白序列信息的RNA。
- **翻译** — 核糖体依据信使RNA连接氨基酸的过程。
- **核糖体** — 由RNA和蛋白组成、进行翻译的结构。
- **氨基酸** — 构成蛋白质链的基本单位。

### English

- **gene** — A DNA region supplying information for functional RNA or protein products.
- **transcription** — Production of RNA using DNA information.
- **RNA** — A nucleic acid involved in information transfer, structure and other functions.
- **messenger RNA** — RNA read by ribosomes for protein-sequence information.
- **translation** — Ribosomal assembly of amino acids using mRNA information.
- **ribosome** — An RNA–protein structure performing translation.
- **amino acid** — A building unit of protein chains.

1. 蛋白编码基因的主要信息路线是？

   - A. B肯定完全没有DNA
   - B. 有同一基因不保证同等表达，需查表达条件与测量
   - C. A的全部DNA已变成蛋白质

4. translation在本课指什么？
   - A. 把中文科学术语改成英文
   - B. 复制整套DNA
   - C. 核糖体读取mRNA信息并连接氨基酸

3. B. 区分拥有信息和使用信息；低检测值也有测量阈值，不足以证明基因消失。 查看DNA仍留核内和RNA产物逐步出现。

4. C. 这是生物分子过程专名，不是语言翻译，也不是DNA复制。 看最后一步核糖体与氨基酸链。

### English

1. What is the main information route for a protein-coding gene?
   - A. Protein→RNA→DNA in every case
   - B. DNA→RNA→protein
   - C. DNA directly becomes a complete protein

2. What do functional-RNA and protein-coding genes share?
   - A. Both involve transcription of DNA information into RNA
   - B. Both must translate into the same protein
   - C. Both determine exactly one trait

3. Cells A/B both contain gene X; A has its RNA and protein, B has little of either. Best interpretation?
   - A. B certainly has no DNA
   - B. Having the gene does not ensure equal expression; investigate conditions and measurements
   - C. All A DNA has turned into protein

4. What does translation mean here?
   - A. Changing Chinese science terms to English
   - B. Copying the whole genome
   - C. A ribosome reading mRNA information and joining amino acids

<details>
<summary>Reveal answers and explanations after answering</summary>

1. B. Transcription makes RNA, then ribosomes translate it; DNA is not consumed as protein material. Compare nuclear DNA with cytoplasmic ribosomes.

2. A. Functional RNA need not be translated; the shared level is RNA production, not necessarily protein production. Compare the first two steps of both product routes.

3. B. Distinguish possessing information from using it. Low detection also involves thresholds; it does not establish loss of the gene. Observe DNA remaining in the nucleus while RNA products appear.

4. C. This is a molecular process, not language translation or DNA replication. Inspect the ribosome and amino-acid chain in the final step.

</details>
