### English

### English

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**变异是需要解释的结果。** 个体表现型可能受多个基因、环境以及它们的交互共同影响。基因型描述所考察的遗传组成，表现型描述可观察的特征。相同基因型在不同环境可有不同表现，不同基因型在同一环境也可不同；二者并不互相取消。不要把人类复杂能力、性格或社会结果套进单基因故事。

**先看这个模型假设了什么。** 原创虚构植物有三个独立两等位基因座，每个等位基因在组合中等频且独立。把贡献等位基因总数记G＝0至6，64种等概率组合的期望频数为1、6、15、20、15、6、1，总和64。图展示的是七个期望组，不是真实采到64株，也不是真正平滑连续曲线；加入更多基因和环境差异可出现更多中间值。

**用成对比较分开作用。** 模型性状Y＝10＋2G＋3E＋GE，环境指数E从0至2，单位完全虚构。固定G＝3，E从0增到2，Y由16增到28，环境效应12单位。固定E＝0，G由0到6，Y由10到22，显示遗传组成作用。再比较环境效应：G＝0时增加6单位，G＝6时增加18单位；同样环境改变的效果依赖G，就是交互作用，不只是给每株加同一个常数。

**分布形状不能单独证明原因。** 连续分布不表示完全遗传，离散类别也不保证单基因。阈值可把多因素结果分成两类，环境也会改变类别。迁移到温室两品系试验，要把两品系都种在两环境、有重复并随机安排位置，才能比较环境响应是否不同。仅看一个直方图无法分离遗传、环境和测量误差，更不能用本虚构图计算真实遗传率或预测某个孩子的能力。

### English

Variation is an outcome requiring explanation. Phenotypes may reflect several genes, environmental conditions and interactions between them. Genotype describes the genetic constitution under consideration; phenotype describes observable characteristics. Matching genotypes can differ across environments, and different genotypes can differ within one environment. Neither observation cancels the other. Complex human abilities, personality and social outcomes should not be reduced to a single-gene story.

Inspect the model assumptions. This original fictional plant has three independent two-allele loci. Allele combinations are equally frequent and independent. Let G count contributing allele units from 0 to 6. Among 64 equally likely combinations, expected group counts are 1,6,15,20,15,6,1, summing to 64. These are seven expected groups, not a real sample of 64 plants or a smooth continuous curve. More loci and environmental differences can generate many more intermediate values.

Use paired comparisons to separate effects. The invented trait formula is Y=10+2G+3E+GE, with environment index E between 0 and 2 and entirely fictional units. Holding G=3, changing E0→2 raises Y16→28, an environmental response of 12 units. Holding E=0, changing G0→6 raises Y10→22, showing genetic contribution in the model. Environmental effects also differ: the E0→2 change adds 6 units at G=0 but 18 at G=6. An environmental effect depending on genotype is an interaction, rather than the same constant added to every plant.

Distribution shape alone does not establish cause. Continuous variation need not be purely genetic, and discrete categories need not imply a single gene. Thresholds can divide multifactorial outcomes into categories. For a new greenhouse study, grow both varieties in both environments with repeats and randomised positions to compare responses fairly. One histogram cannot separate genetics, environment and measurement error, estimate real heritability or predict a child’s abilities. The model illustrates comparison logic, not a validated prediction system.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“遗传或环境只能选一个”“连续必遗传、离散必单基因”都不受分布本身支持。

### English

“Either genes or environment” and “continuous means genetic; discrete means single-gene” do not follow from the distribution alone.

## Depth / 高中桥梁

### 中文

遗传率是特定群体和环境下的变异统计，不是个体性状有多少百分比由基因做成；此模型无真实抽样，不计算遗传率。S6再讨论研究方法。

### English

Heritability is a population-and-environment-specific variation statistic, not the percentage of an individual made by genes. This unsampled model cannot estimate it.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：A品系在低/高水分为12/18 cm，B为15/30 cm，每条件5株均值。自查：A增6、B增15 cm，符合不同响应；需误差范围和重复判断可靠性，不能从均值直接断言某基因或推广所有植物。

### English

New situation: variety A averages 12/18 cm under low/high water, B 15/30 cm, with 5 plants per condition. Self-check: increases are 6 and 15 cm, consistent with different responses; need variability and repeats to judge reliability, without assigning a specific gene or generalising to all plants.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **变异** — 个体之间的差异。
- **基因型** — 所考察个体的遗传组成。
- **表现型** — 基因和环境共同影响的可观察特征。
- **多基因** — 受多个基因影响。
- **环境** — 影响生物的外部条件。
- **交互作用** — 一个因素的效果随另一个因素而变化。
- **连续变异** — 某特征在一定范围呈许多中间值。

### English

- **variation** — Differences among individuals.
- **genotype** — The genetic constitution being considered.
- **phenotype** — Observable characteristics influenced by genes and environment.
- **polygenic** — Influenced by multiple genes.
- **environment** — External conditions affecting organisms.
- **interaction** — An effect of one factor that changes with another factor.
- **continuous variation** — Variation with many intermediate values across a range.

## Check / 理解检查

### 中文

1. 同基因型环境改变后株高改变，说明？
   - A. 基因必定没有作用
   - B. 环境必定改变了全部DNA序列
   - C. 表现型可受环境影响，不能据此排除遗传作用

2. 离散的两类表现一定由一个基因决定吗？
   - A. 不一定，多个因素或阈值也能产生类别
   - B. 一定，只能是显性和隐性
   - C. 一定，环境不能产生类别

3. 环境0→2时G=0株高10→16，G=6为22→40。哪项最准确？
   - A. 环境效果对所有G都相同
   - B. 两种G分别增加6和18单位，符合基因×环境交互
   - C. 基因型高的个体没有环境响应

4. polygenic中的poly表示？
   - A. 只受环境影响
   - B. 完全相同
   - C. 多个，表示多个基因参与

<details>
<summary>作答后查看答案与解释</summary>

1. C. 控制基因型可比较环境效应；环境有作用不等于基因无作用。 固定G=3，比较环境0与2的16和28。

2. A. 分布是结果形状，机制需遗传与环境证据；类别化测量还可能把连续量切分。 读静态步骤关于连续与离散的边界。

3. B. 同一环境改变产生不同增量，不只是整体平移；这是本虚构公式中的交互，不能外推人类。 选择G=0和6，分别读环境0→2数值。

4. C. 多基因不等于没有环境，也不意味着每个基因贡献完全相同。 看三个基因座贡献合并为G的说明。

</details>

### English

1. If the same genotype changes height across environments, what follows?
   - A. Genes must have no effect
   - B. Environment must have changed the entire DNA sequence
   - C. Phenotype can respond to environment without excluding genetic influence

2. Must a two-category phenotype be controlled by one gene?
   - A. No; multiple factors or thresholds can produce categories
   - B. Yes, it must be dominant/recessive
   - C. Yes, environments cannot produce categories

3. From environment 0→2,G=0 height 10→16 andG=6 height 22→40. Best interpretation?
   - A. Environmental effects are equal for every G
   - B. The increases 6 and 18 units fit gene×environment interaction
   - C. The higher-G plants do not respond to environment

4. What does poly mean in polygenic?
   - A. Environmental influence only
   - B. Completely identical
   - C. Many, indicating multiple genes contribute

<details>
<summary>Reveal answers and explanations after answering</summary>

1. C. Matching genotype helps investigate environmental effects; environmental influence does not imply genetic irrelevance. Hold G=3 and compare 16 versus 28 at environment 0 and 2.

2. A. Distribution is an outcome shape. Mechanism needs genetic and environmental evidence; thresholds can divide a continuous measure into categories. Read the static explanation of continuous/discrete limits.

3. B. The same environmental change produces different increments, not merely a common shift. This is interaction in a fictional formula, not a human prediction. Select G=0 and 6 and read each environment 0→2 comparison.

4. C. Polygenic does not exclude environment or imply identical effects for every gene. Read how three loci combine into G.

</details>
