## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

**先约定计数单位。** 本课虚构生物二倍体2n＝4，表示两套染色体，每套n＝2条。C不是条数，而是一套未复制染色体的相对DNA量。起点细胞有4条染色体、4条染色单体和2C。复制以后有4条已复制染色体，每条连接两条姐妹，共8条染色单体、4C；仍是两套，不是四套。图中一个X是复制后的一条染色体。

**有丝和减数分离的对象不同。** 有丝分裂把姐妹染色单体分开，完成后形成两个各2n＝4、4条染色单体、2C的子细胞，通常保持起点套数。减I把同源染色体分往两侧，姐妹通常仍连接；完成后每细胞一套、2条已复制染色体、4条染色单体、2C。于是“单倍体”不等于永远只有1C。

**两次分裂不等于两次复制。** 减I前复制一次，减I与减II之间不再次复制。减II再分离姐妹，得到四个各n＝2、2条染色单体、1C的细胞。复制后总量4C，减I后2×2C＝4C，减II后4×1C＝4C，显示DNA被分配而不是每分一次就再制造一次。这些数字是完成检查点的每细胞计数，不把后期尚未分胞的瞬时条数混入表中。

**结构相似不表示遗传完全一样。** 同源染色体对应位置有相同类别的基因，却可有不同等位基因。减数中的交叉互换和独立分配能够改变组合，不是细胞知道未来需要哪种性状才挑选。图为清楚计数省略交叉互换，不能据此断言产物都相同。迁移到2n＝6物种时，先确定n＝3，再按同样逻辑检查套数与复制状态，不要把本图的4条背成所有生物的常数。

### English

Define the units first. In this fictional diploid species,2n=4 means two sets with n=2 chromosomes per set. C measures the relative DNA amount in one unreplicated haploid set, not chromosome number. The starting cell has 4 chromosomes,4 chromatids and 2C DNA. After replication it has 4 replicated chromosomes,8 chromatids and 4C, while retaining two sets. An X represents one replicated chromosome with two connected sisters, not two independent chromosomes.

The divisions separate different structures. Mitosis separates sister chromatids and yields two cells, each normally 2n=4 with 4 chromatids and 2C. MeiosisI separates homologous chromosomes while sisters generally remain connected. Each completed meiosisI cell has one set,2 replicated chromosomes,4 chromatids and 2C. Thus haploid describes chromosome sets and does not always mean 1C DNA.

Two divisions do not require two replications. DNA replicates once before meiosisI, with no new replication between meiosisI andII. MeiosisII separates sisters to form four cells, each n=2 with 2 chromatids and 1C. Total replicated DNA is 4C; after divisionI it is 2×2C=4C and after divisionII4×1C=4C. DNA has been distributed into more cells rather than manufactured again before each division. These are per-cell counts at completed checkpoints. Do not mix them with transient chromosome counts during anaphase before the cell divides.

Similar structure does not guarantee identical genetic information. Homologues have corresponding gene positions but may carry different alleles. Crossing over and independent assortment change combinations without choosing what future offspring will need. The drawing omits crossing over to clarify counts, not to imply identical meiotic products. For a new 2n=6 species, first infer n=3 and then apply the same accounting logic. Four chromosomes in this diagram are an example, not a universal biological constant.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

“复制让2n变4n”“减I后必只有1C”“减II前再复制”都把套数和DNA量混淆。

### English

“Replication changes 2n to 4n,” “afterI must be 1C” and “replicate again beforeII” confuse sets with DNA amount.

## Depth / 高中桥梁

### 中文

姐妹分离后各自计为染色体，后期瞬时计数取决于是否按整细胞或每极；本课固定完成检查点避免歧义。植物减数产物并非总直接成为配子，生命周期细节以后展开。

### English

Once sisters separate, each counts as a chromosome; anaphase totals depend on counting the whole cell or each pole. Completed checkpoints avoid ambiguity. Plant meiotic products are not always directly gametes.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：2n=6物种完成减I。自查：每细胞n=3、3条染色体、6条染色单体、2C；与原复制前2C相同DNA量但套数不同。说明C尺度固定，不从DNA量单独推套数。

### English

New situation: a 2n=6 species completes meiosisI. Self-check: each cell has n=3,3 chromosomes,6 chromatids and 2C. DNA amount matches the original pre-replication 2C but set number differs. Keep the C scale fixed and do not infer ploidy from DNA amount alone.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **有丝分裂** — 通常保持套数、分离姐妹染色单体的核分裂。
- **减数分裂** — 一次复制、两次分裂，使套数减少的过程。
- **同源染色体** — 有对应基因位置的一对染色体。
- **姐妹染色单体** — 一条染色体复制后相连的两份。
- **单倍体** — 具有一套染色体。
- **二倍体** — 具有两套染色体。
- **DNA含量** — 细胞含DNA的量，可用C作相对单位。

### English

- **mitosis** — Nuclear division generally maintaining ploidy while separating sister chromatids.
- **meiosis** — One replication and two divisions reducing chromosome sets.
- **homologous chromosomes** — A pair with corresponding gene positions.
- **sister chromatids** — The connected copies of one replicated chromosome.
- **haploid** — Having one chromosome set.
- **diploid** — Having two chromosome sets.
- **DNA content** — Amount of cellular DNA, expressible in relative C units.

## Check / 理解检查

### 中文

1. 2n=4细胞复制后、分裂前的正确账本？
   - A. 4套、8条染色体、2C
   - B. 2套、4条染色体、8条染色单体、4C
   - C. 1套、2条染色体、1C

2. 减数第一次分裂主要分离什么？
   - A. 同源染色体，姐妹通常仍相连
   - B. 全部DNA都降解
   - C. 姐妹染色单体，且同源都去同一细胞

3. 一个4C细胞完成减数分裂得4个各1C细胞，这说明？
   - A. 分裂之间必复制两次
   - B. DNA总量由4C变1C，丢了四分之三
   - C. 总量4×1C=4C，分配到更多细胞而非再复制

4. haploid最准确是？
   - A. DNA含量永远只能1C
   - B. 具有一套染色体
   - C. 一条染色体都没有

<details>
<summary>作答后查看答案与解释</summary>

1. B. 复制让每条染色体有两条姐妹，但同源组数仍是两套。X按连接的着丝粒单位计一条。 看步骤2复制检查点的4个X和4C。

2. A. 减I降低套数，减II再分姐妹；两次分裂之间不再复制。 看减数路线步骤3：每细胞两个X但只有一套。

3. C. 每细胞与全部细胞总和不同；四个1C保留复制后的4C总量，模型忽略损失和错误。 把每步细胞数乘以每细胞C值。

4. B. 减I后每细胞已是单倍体，但染色体仍已复制，DNA为2C。单倍体描述套数。 比较减I后1套/2C与减II后1套/1C。

</details>

### English

1. What is correct after replication but before division in a 2n=4 cell?
   - A. 4 sets,8 chromosomes,2C
   - B. 2 sets,4 chromosomes,8 chromatids,4C
   - C. 1 set,2 chromosomes,1C

2. What separates during meiosisI?
   - A. Homologous chromosomes; sisters generally remain joined
   - B. All DNA is degraded
   - C. Sisters, with all homologues entering one cell

3. One 4C cell completes meiosis to yield four 1C cells. What follows?
   - A. Two more replications must have occurred
   - B. Total DNA fell 4C→1C, losing three quarters
   - C. Total 4×1C=4C: distribution into more cells, not another replication

4. What best defines haploid?
   - A. DNA amount must always be 1C
   - B. Having one chromosome set
   - C. Having no chromosomes

<details>
<summary>Reveal answers and explanations after answering</summary>

1. B. Replication makes two sisters per chromosome while retaining two homologous sets. A joined X counts as one chromosome unit. Inspect the four X units and 4C at checkpoint 2.

2. A. MeiosisI reduces sets; meiosisII separates sisters, without intervening replication. Inspect meiotic checkpoint 3: two X chromosomes but one set per cell.

3. C. Per-cell amount differs from the total. Four 1C cells preserve the replicated 4C total in this idealised error-free model. Multiply cell count by per-cell C at each checkpoint.

4. B. After meiosisI the cell is haploid but chromosomes remain replicated, with 2C DNA. Haploid describes sets. Compare one set/2C afterI with one set/1C afterII.

</details>
