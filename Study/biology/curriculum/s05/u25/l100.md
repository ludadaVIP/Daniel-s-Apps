## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

把交换表面折出两倍面积，是否就能让矿物离子持续从稀处进入浓处？

### English

If exchange area doubles, can mineral ions continuously move from dilute surroundings into a concentrated cell without energy?

### English

Choose an initial explanation without a score; revise using specific evidence: Enough area removes the energy requirement for uphill transport；Area and energy address different constraints；All protein-assisted transport directly uses ATP

### English

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

结构与功能题不只是写“面积大所以好”。交换物质需要可用的表面、适宜通路和推动净移动的条件。其他条件相同，更多表面可提供更多并行跨越的位置，较薄屏障缩短路径；血流、通气或代谢移走产物又能帮助维持浓度差。这些因素相互配合，而不是某一条独自保证高效。

原创比较模型设相对被动交换容量=A×ΔC/d，固定通透性。面积A=1、梯度ΔC=3、距离d=1，容量为3；面积改2得6；再把距离减至0.5得12。这里面积、距离、梯度都使用相对模型单位，结果只是相对容量，不是实际人体的毫升每分钟。若同时把面积和距离改变，应承认两项都参与，不能把四倍增加全归给面积。真实扩散还受分子性质、膜蛋白与血流供应等限制。

当物质需要从较低浓度处进入较高浓度处，简单顺浓度扩散不能持续完成任务。主动运输利用细胞代谢提供的能量和特定载体，帮助物质逆浓度或电化学梯度运输。能量可能由ATP直接驱动泵，也可由先前耗能建立的离子梯度间接提供；所以“所有主动运输都在同一载体直接分解ATP”过于绝对。相反，有蛋白参与的顺梯度易化扩散不必直接消耗代谢能。

模型另设2个可用载体、底物充足：有能量时逆梯度量2，无能量时为0。增加左侧交换面积不会替右侧载体付款，也不会让有限载体无限加速。真实细胞的载体数量、供能、梯度与工作状态共同决定表现。本课只比较机制，不从按钮推断任何人的吸收或器官健康。

### English

A structure–function explanation needs more than “large area is good”. Exchange requires available surface, a suitable route and conditions supporting net movement. Other factors being equal, larger area supplies parallel crossing sites, while a thinner barrier shortens the path. Blood flow, ventilation or metabolism can remove substances and maintain concentration differences. These features work together rather than guaranteeing performance independently.

Our original comparison uses relative passive capacity=A×ΔC/d with fixed permeability. With area 1, gradient 3 and distance 1, capacity is 3. Doubling area makes it 6. Then halving distance makes it 12. All inputs use relative model units; these are not measured human flow rates. If area and distance both change, both contribute. A fourfold capacity increase cannot be attributed entirely to area. Actual exchange may also be limited by molecular properties, membrane proteins and delivery of substances.

Simple downhill diffusion cannot continuously move a substance from lower to higher concentration. Active transport uses metabolic energy and specific carriers to support movement against a concentration or electrochemical gradient. Some pumps use ATP directly. Other carriers exploit ion gradients established through earlier energy expenditure. It is therefore too absolute to claim that every active carrier directly breaks down ATP. Conversely, protein-assisted diffusion down a gradient need not directly consume metabolic energy.

A separate model assumes two functioning carriers and ample substrate. Uphill transport is 2 with energy and 0 without it. Increasing exchange area does not supply the missing energy or make a finite carrier population infinitely fast. Real cells depend jointly on carrier availability, energy supply, gradients and working conditions. These controls compare mechanisms, not an individual’s absorption or organ health.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：有蛋白就是主动运输。反例：开放通道可让离子顺适宜电化学梯度被动通过；能否逆梯度与能量来源才是关键问题。

### English

Misconception: protein involvement means active transport. An open channel can pass ions passively down a suitable electrochemical gradient. Ask about gradient direction and energy source.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中衔接：对离子需合并浓度与电势形成电化学梯度。能量耦联的细节留到S6；本课不将A×ΔC/d应用为主动运输的通用公式。

### English

Senior bridge: ion transport depends on electrochemical gradients combining concentration and electrical effects. Detailed coupling belongs in S6; A×ΔC/d is not a universal active-transport formula.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：两张人造交换片A与B面积相同，B距离是A的一半，却实测速率未加倍。能说扩散规律错误吗？自查：相同面积、距离单位明确；模型只在通透性与梯度固定时预测两倍；检查血流供应、梯度或载体限制，区分理想容量与实测速率。

### English

New case: artificial sheets A and B have equal areas; B is half as thick, yet measured rate does not double. Is diffusion disproved? Self-check: state the geometry and units; the model predicts doubling only at fixed permeability and gradient; check delivery, gradient and carrier limits; separate ideal capacity from measured rate.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **主动运输 / active transport**: 利用代谢能支持物质逆浓度或电化学梯度运输。
- **载体蛋白 / carrier protein**: 通过结合物质与构象变化参与运输的蛋白。
- **代谢能 / metabolic energy**: 细胞代谢提供的可用于工作的能量。
- **交换表面 / exchange surface**: 物质跨越进入或离开系统的界面。
- **扩散距离 / diffusion distance**: 扩散需跨越的路径长度。
- **梯度维持 / gradient maintenance**: 通过供给、移走等过程保持差异。
- **饱和 / saturation**: 可用载体等达到限制而不能无限提高速率。

### English

- **主动运输 / active transport**: Metabolic energy supports movement against a concentration or electrochemical gradient.
- **载体蛋白 / carrier protein**: A transport protein using binding and shape changes.
- **代谢能 / metabolic energy**: Usable energy supplied by cellular metabolism.
- **交换表面 / exchange surface**: An interface across which material enters or leaves a system.
- **扩散距离 / diffusion distance**: The path length crossed by diffusion.
- **梯度维持 / gradient maintenance**: Maintaining a difference through supply or removal.
- **饱和 / saturation**: A carrier or other capacity limit preventing indefinite rate increases.

### English

1. [concept] What supports sustained transport against a concentration gradient?

   A. Only more area
   B. Metabolic energy and a specific transport mechanism
   C. Only a smaller drawing

   Answer: B. Area does not provide the energy for uphill transport; an appropriate carrier mechanism and energy source are needed.

   Compare the energy switch at the right of the figure.

2. [concept] Which statement is more accurate?

   A. Every carrier directly consumes ATP
   B. Active transport never needs proteins
   C. Active transport needs energy that may come directly or indirectly from metabolism

   Answer: C. Some carriers use established ion gradients; protein-assisted downhill transport can be passive.

   Separate protein involvement from energy in Explain paragraph 3.

3. [reasoning] At gradient 3, how does A=2,d=0.5 compare with A=1,d=1?

   A. Fourfold; both area and distance changed
   B. Twofold, considering area only
   C. Actual human rate must be 12 mL/min

   Answer: A. 12/3=4. Area contributes ×2 and shorter distance ×2. The result is relative model capacity, not measured mL/min.

   Mark changed columns in all three geometry rows.

4. [vocabulary] What is a carrier protein?

   A. A sugar only forming cell walls
   B. A transport protein that binds and changes shape
   C. A concentration unit for diffusion

   Answer: B. A carrier is a molecular mechanism, not a sugar or unit; carriers can participate in different transport types.

   Revisit the right-hand carrier and vocabulary.
