## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

同一外液让植物细胞与动物细胞进水，为什么后果可能不同？

### English

The same external solution causes water entry into plant and animal cells. Why may their outcomes differ?

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

渗透关注的是水，不是溶质穿过膜。水分子仍作双向运动，但在有选择透过性且允许水通过的膜两侧，初始净流方向由总水势差决定：从较高总水势到较低总水势。−0.2 MPa比−0.5 MPa高，因此模型外−0.2、内−0.5时水净进入。不能把“负得更多”误读为更高，也不能把溶质扩散或主动运输叫作渗透。

在同温、压力等条件可比较时，增加不能通过膜的溶质通常降低水势，常可据此预测水趋向溶质较浓一侧。但水势也受压力影响；若植物细胞已有显著膨压，单看溶质浓度可能误判。图给的是初始总水势，不是只给溶质势。进水或失水会改变浓度、体积与压力，因此初始方向不能直接算出最终状态。

植物失水时膨压可先下降；失水足够多时，原生质体才可能收缩离开细胞壁，出现质壁分离。细胞壁通常仍在原位，不能说细胞壁也缩成一团。植物进水会增加膨压，壁的支撑限制进一步膨胀。动物没有细胞壁，失水可皱缩，过量进水可使膜破裂；红细胞破裂称溶血。并非任何进水都会立刻破裂，也不是每次失水都会发生质壁分离。

数据例：虚构同类植物组织在等温外液中处理相同时间，初质量2.00 g，终质量2.10 g，则变化=(2.10−2.00)/2.00×100%=+5%。这支持该条件下组织净吸水，但不是直接测出胞内溶质浓度。组织不完全相同，表面残水、其他物质移动也会影响质量；需要重复并统一擦拭、大小与处理时长。零质量变化外液只帮助估计该方法下的零净变化条件。

### English

Osmosis concerns water, not solute crossing a membrane. Water molecules still move both ways, but the initial net direction across a suitable selectively permeable membrane is from higher to lower total water potential. In our model, −0.2 MPa outside is higher than −0.5 MPa inside, so water initially enters. More negative does not mean higher. Solute diffusion and active transport are different processes.

At comparable temperature and pressure, adding an impermeant solute generally lowers water potential. This often predicts movement towards the more concentrated solution. Pressure also matters, however. A plant cell’s turgor can change the result, so solute concentration alone is insufficient when pressure differs. Our displayed values are initial total water potentials. Water entry or loss changes volume, concentration and pressure, meaning initial direction cannot directly determine the final state.

Water loss can first reduce plant-cell turgor. Only sufficient loss may withdraw the protoplast from the wall: plasmolysis. The wall generally remains in place. Water entry raises turgor, and the wall limits further expansion. Animal cells lack this support; they may shrink on water loss and rupture after excessive entry. Red-cell rupture is haemolysis. Neither all water entry nor all water loss guarantees these extreme outcomes.

For a worked data example, fictional plant tissue begins at 2.00 g and reaches 2.10 g after a fixed treatment. Percentage change is (2.10−2.00)/2.00×100%=+5%. This supports net water gain under those conditions; it is not a direct measurement of internal solute concentration. Surface liquid, tissue differences and other material movements can affect mass. Replicates and consistent blotting, size and treatment duration strengthen the comparison. A zero-change solution estimates the method’s zero-net-change condition, with uncertainty.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：植物一失水就质壁分离。反例：少量失水可仅降低膨压，膜仍贴近细胞壁；要观察足够收缩的证据才使用质壁分离。

### English

Misconception: every water loss causes plasmolysis. Small losses may only reduce turgor while the membrane remains close to the wall. Plasmolysis requires evidence of sufficient withdrawal.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中衔接：简化总水势Ψ=溶质势+压力势，单位可用MPa；需要说明其他项忽略及同温条件。平衡总水势相等，不要求溶质浓度相等；外界压差也能影响水流。

### English

Senior bridge: a simplified total Ψ equals solute plus pressure potential, in MPa, with other terms neglected and temperature specified. Equal total potentials do not require equal solute concentrations; pressure differences affect water flow.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：同条件3份组织在0.4 mol/L外液下变化−1%、0%、+1%，可称精确测出胞内浓度0.4吗？自查：术语用“零净质量变化估计”；引用范围−1%到+1%；解释水势平衡与压力、组织差异；需重复及更密浓度梯度，不把估计变精确值。

### English

New case: three tissues in 0.4 mol/L solution change −1%, 0% and +1%. Has internal concentration been measured exactly as 0.4? Self-check: call this a zero-net-mass-change estimate; cite the −1% to +1% range; discuss water potential, pressure and tissue variation; request replicates and finer solution steps.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **渗透 / osmosis**: 水通过选择透过性膜沿总水势降低方向净移动。
- **水势 / water potential**: 描述水移动趋势的量，需考虑溶质和压力等。
- **膨压 / turgor pressure**: 细胞内容物对细胞壁产生的压力。
- **质壁分离 / plasmolysis**: 植物充分失水时原生质体收缩离开细胞壁。
- **溶血 / haemolysis**: 红细胞破裂释放内容物。
- **选择透过性膜 / selectively permeable membrane**: 让水与某些物质通过而限制其他物质的屏障。
- **净质量变化 / net mass change**: 终质量减初质量，可结合初值求百分比。

### English

- **渗透 / osmosis**: Net water movement across a selectively permeable membrane down total water potential.
- **水势 / water potential**: A quantity describing water movement tendency, including solute and pressure effects.
- **膨压 / turgor pressure**: Pressure of cell contents against the cell wall.
- **质壁分离 / plasmolysis**: Withdrawal of the protoplast from the wall after sufficient water loss.
- **溶血 / haemolysis**: Rupture of red blood cells, releasing contents.
- **选择透过性膜 / selectively permeable membrane**: A barrier passing water and some substances while restricting others.
- **净质量变化 / net mass change**: Final minus initial mass; divided by initial mass for percentage change.

## Check / 检索练习

### 中文

1. [concept] 初始外−0.8、内−0.5 MPa，水净向哪里？

   A. 进细胞，因为−0.8更高
   B. 出细胞，从−0.5向−0.8
   C. 溶质代替水移动

   答案: B. −0.5高于−0.8；渗透定义水的净移动。方向不自动给出最终细胞状态。

   回图核对初始总水势与箭头说明。

2. [concept] 为什么植物少量进水后不一定破裂？

   A. 细胞壁支撑并使膨压增加
   B. 水不能穿植物细胞膜
   C. 植物没有细胞膜

   答案: A. 壁提供机械限制，水仍可跨膜。是否达到某终态还取决于水势、压力与时间。

   回图区分绿色细胞壁与蓝色膜。

3. [reasoning] 虚构组织2.50 g变2.40 g，质量百分变化和结论是什么？

   A. +4%，说明溶质进入
   B. −0.1%，精确给出胞内浓度
   C. −4%，支持该条件下净失水但需控制表面残液

   答案: C. −0.10/2.50×100%=−4%。克的差不能直接当百分数；质量本身不精确测浓度，也需排除残液等解释。

   回Explain的数据公式及静态推理步骤1–3。

4. [vocabulary] plasmolysis描述哪种现象？

   A. 红细胞破裂
   B. 植物充分失水后原生质体与壁分离
   C. 水被主动泵入植物

   答案: B. 质壁分离涉及植物的原生质体和细胞壁；红细胞破裂是溶血。渗透不是水泵。

   回静态表第2步，比较植物/动物条件。

### English

1. [concept] With outside −0.8 and inside −0.5 MPa, where does water initially move?

   A. Into the cell because −0.8 is higher
   B. Out of the cell, from −0.5 to −0.8
   C. Solute moves instead of water

   Answer: B. −0.5 is higher than −0.8. Osmosis concerns water; direction alone does not establish final cell state.

   Check initial total potentials and the directional caption.

2. [concept] Why need a plant cell not rupture after modest water entry?

   A. The wall supports the cell as turgor rises
   B. Water cannot cross plant membranes
   C. Plants lack membranes

   Answer: A. The wall provides mechanical constraint while water crosses the membrane. Final state depends on potentials, pressure and time.

   Distinguish the green wall from the blue membrane.

3. [reasoning] Fictional tissue changes from 2.50 to 2.40 g. Which result is justified?

   A. +4%, proving solute entry
   B. −0.1%, giving exact internal concentration
   C. −4%, supporting net water loss with surface-fluid controls

   Answer: C. −0.10/2.50×100%=−4%. A mass difference is not a percentage. Mass alone does not precisely measure concentration; residual liquid is an alternative factor.

   Revisit the mass formula and static reasoning steps 1–3.

4. [vocabulary] What does plasmolysis describe?

   A. Red-cell rupture
   B. Plant protoplast withdrawal from the wall after sufficient water loss
   C. Water actively pumped into plants

   Answer: B. Plasmolysis concerns a plant protoplast and wall. Red-cell rupture is haemolysis; osmosis is not water pumping.

   Compare plant and animal conditions in static step 2.
