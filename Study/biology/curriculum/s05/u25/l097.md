## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

氧气和更小的钠离子一起到达膜，为什么小的离子反而不容易直接穿过？

### English

Oxygen and a smaller sodium ion reach a membrane. Why may the smaller ion have more difficulty crossing directly?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选一个解释，不评分；学后用具体证据修正：任何小分子都能直接穿膜；疏水核心与蛋白共同决定选择性；膜蛋白完全固定不动

### English

Choose an initial explanation without a score; revise using specific evidence: Any small molecule crosses directly；The hydrophobic core and proteins shape selectivity；Membrane proteins are completely fixed

## Explore / 动手操作

### 中文

先选氧气、无匹配蛋白，再选离子。保持离子不变，只把匹配蛋白改为有。比较葡萄糖。此活动是结构—通透性的静态比较，无生理时间播放。

### English

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

细胞膜把细胞内外分开，又必须让物质有选择地交换。磷脂的头部亲水，尾部疏水；在两侧都有水的环境中，两层头部分别朝向细胞外液与细胞质，尾部相向，形成疏水核心。因此，膜既不是一层头朝外的单排小球，也不是毫无间隙的硬墙。膜的边界作用来自分子的相互作用，不是膜能思考哪些物质“有用”。

氧气等小而不带电的非极性分子比较容易穿过脂质核心；带电离子即使很小，也不容易这样通过。葡萄糖较大且亲水，通常需要匹配的运输蛋白。通道提供亲水路径，载体则与物质结合并改变构象。是否存在蛋白与是否需要代谢能是两个问题：经蛋白顺梯度运输仍可属于被动运输。图中“有匹配蛋白”假设蛋白开放、工作正常且方向适宜，不能据此断言真实细胞一定有同样速率。

结构分析例：在同温、同浓度差的两张模型膜上，一张只有脂质，另一张另有开放的匹配离子通道。前者允许氧气通过却阻碍离子；后者增加离子通路。由此支持“化学性质和蛋白参与选择性”，不能推出通道让所有物质自由通过。应分别比较同一物质在有、无匹配蛋白时的结果。

“流动”表示许多脂质和蛋白可在膜平面内侧向移动；有的蛋白受细胞骨架锚定，并非都自由漂移。镶嵌表示组成和功能多样，不是随机装饰。示意图没有画出所有胆固醇、糖链与锚定结构，也不是电子显微镜照片。判断模型好坏，要问它能否解释选择性与动态证据，同时承认未呈现的部分。

### English

The cell membrane separates two watery environments while allowing selective exchange. A phospholipid has a hydrophilic head and hydrophobic tails. Heads face extracellular fluid on one side and cytoplasm on the other, while tails face one another to form a hydrophobic core. This arrangement is neither a single row of beads nor a solid wall. Selectivity follows molecular interactions; a membrane does not decide what a cell finds useful.

Small non-polar molecules such as oxygen cross the lipid core relatively easily. Charged ions do not readily follow that route, even when small. Hydrophilic glucose usually requires suitable transport proteins. Channels provide aqueous routes, while carriers bind substances and change shape. Requiring a protein does not automatically mean requiring metabolic energy: protein-assisted movement down a gradient can be passive. Our matching-protein option assumes an available, functioning route under suitable conditions; it does not predict a measured cellular rate.

Consider a structural comparison at the same temperature and concentration difference. A lipid-only model membrane allows oxygen passage but strongly restricts ions. Adding an open channel specific to the selected ion supplies a route for that ion. The evidence supports roles for chemistry and protein specificity. It does not show that the channel transports every substance. Compare the same substance with and without its matching protein before drawing a conclusion.

Fluid means that many lipids and proteins move laterally within the membrane. Some proteins are anchored to other structures, so mobility varies. Mosaic refers to varied components and functions. The drawing omits many carbohydrates, cholesterol molecules and attachments. It is an explanatory model, not a microscopy image. Evaluate it by what evidence it explains and what it leaves unresolved.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：通道是给所有物质开的洞。反例：匹配离子通道不自动成为葡萄糖载体；选择性涉及孔径、电荷及结合特征。

### English

Misconception: a channel is a hole for everything. A matching ion channel does not automatically transport glucose; size, charge and binding properties matter.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中衔接：膜蛋白可有运输、受体、酶与锚定功能。离子运动还受电势差影响；本课只用适宜浓度梯度，不用“能通过”推算电生理方向或速率。

### English

Senior bridge: membrane proteins can transport, receive signals, catalyse and anchor. Electrical differences also affect ions; this lesson does not infer electrophysiological direction or rate from permission to cross.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：A、B膜具有相同脂质面积，B比A多一种葡萄糖载体。能断言B的氧气透过速率更大吗？自查：先区分载体选择性；题中只改变葡萄糖载体；氧气主要走脂质核心；须控制温度、厚度与氧气梯度，不能从葡萄糖条件推出氧气结果。

### English

New case: A and B have equal lipid areas, but B has more glucose carriers. Must oxygen cross B faster? Self-check: name carrier specificity; identify the changed glucose-carrier condition; trace oxygen through the lipid core; control temperature, thickness and oxygen gradient before comparing rates.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **磷脂 / phospholipid**: 具有亲水头与疏水尾的膜脂分子。
- **双层 / bilayer**: 两层磷脂的尾部相向组成的结构。
- **亲水 / hydrophilic**: 可与水形成有利相互作用。
- **疏水 / hydrophobic**: 与水接触不利的性质。
- **流动镶嵌模型 / fluid mosaic model**: 脂质和蛋白构成动态而非全部固定的膜。
- **选择透过性 / selective permeability**: 不同物质跨膜难易不同。
- **通道蛋白 / channel protein**: 为特定物质提供亲水通路的蛋白。

### English

- **磷脂 / phospholipid**: Membrane lipid with a hydrophilic head and hydrophobic tails.
- **双层 / bilayer**: Two phospholipid layers with tails facing inward.
- **亲水 / hydrophilic**: Forms favourable interactions with water.
- **疏水 / hydrophobic**: A property making contact with water unfavourable.
- **流动镶嵌模型 / fluid mosaic model**: A dynamic membrane of lipids and proteins, not entirely fixed.
- **选择透过性 / selective permeability**: Different substances cross with different ease.
- **通道蛋白 / channel protein**: Protein providing a hydrophilic route for particular substances.

## Check / 检索练习

### 中文

1. [concept] 磷脂双层的正确方向是什么？

   A. 头朝两侧水、尾相向
   B. 尾朝两侧水、头在中央
   C. 所有头都只朝细胞外

   答案: A. 内外都有水，因此两侧都需要亲水表面；疏水尾形成内部。把膜画成单排会遗漏内侧水环境。

   回图找两排蓝头及中央黄尾。

2. [concept] 小离子不易直接穿脂质核心，主要说明什么？

   A. 只有大小决定透过
   B. 电荷与疏水核心的相互作用很重要
   C. 所有膜都没有蛋白

   答案: B. 带电粒子跨疏水核心有障碍；小不保证易通过。膜蛋白可提供选择性通路，但并非无蛋白。

   保持离子不变，对照有无匹配蛋白。

3. [reasoning] 为检验离子通道的作用，哪组比较最有力？

   A. 同时更换离子和温度
   B. 比较氧气与离子而不控制膜
   C. 相同离子、面积、梯度与温度，只改变匹配通道

   答案: C. 只改变目标因素减少其他解释；换分子或温度会同时改变通透性条件。模型结果仍需真实实验检验。

   回图和静态结构表，说明通道提供了哪条路径。

4. [vocabulary] fluid mosaic model中的fluid指什么？

   A. 膜整体是水
   B. 许多组分可侧向移动，部分受锚定
   C. 每个蛋白完全固定

   答案: B. 流动性是组分的动态性质；不等于膜是纯水，也不要求每个蛋白都同样自由。

   回图右下“蛋白可侧向移动”，再读Depth中的锚定。

### English

1. [concept] How are phospholipids oriented?

   A. Heads face water on both sides; tails inward
   B. Tails face water; heads at centre
   C. All heads face only outward

   Answer: A. Both sides contain water, requiring hydrophilic surfaces; tails form the core. A single row omits the inner watery environment.

   Find both blue head rows and the yellow core.

2. [concept] Why does a small ion not readily cross the lipid core?

   A. Size alone determines crossing
   B. Charge and interactions with the hydrophobic core matter
   C. All membranes lack proteins

   Answer: B. Charge creates a barrier in the hydrophobic core; small does not guarantee passage. Membranes can contain selective transport proteins.

   Keep the ion and compare matching-protein presence.

3. [reasoning] Which comparison best tests an ion channel’s role?

   A. Change ion and temperature together
   B. Compare oxygen and ions using different membranes
   C. Same ion, area, gradient and temperature; vary only matching channel

   Answer: C. Varying only the target factor reduces alternatives. Changing substance or temperature changes other permeability conditions; model results still require experimental testing.

   Use the figure and structural table to identify the added route.

4. [vocabulary] What does fluid mean in fluid mosaic model?

   A. The membrane is made entirely of water
   B. Many components move laterally; some are anchored
   C. Every protein is fixed

   Answer: B. Fluidity describes dynamic components, not pure water or unrestricted movement of every protein.

   Revisit lateral movement and anchored proteins.
