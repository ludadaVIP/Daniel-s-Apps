## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

两侧粒子一样多时，看不见净变化，是否意味着所有粒子都停下了？

### English

If both sides contain equal amounts and show no net change, have all particles stopped?

## Predict / 先做预测

### 中文

先选一个解释，不评分；学后用具体证据修正：粒子只会从多的一侧走向少的一侧；双向都有运动，但净量可不同；浓度相等时粒子不再运动

### English

Choose an initial explanation without a score; revise using specific evidence: Particles move only from the crowded side；Movement occurs both ways, with unequal net transfer；Equal concentrations stop particle motion

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

扩散不是粒子知道哪里拥挤，然后有目的地奔向空处。在适宜温度下，粒子持续作随机运动；两侧粒子都可能越过能透过的边界。在体积相同、其他条件相同时，粒子较多的一侧通常会有更多粒子在同一时间内跨出，因此形成从高浓度向低浓度的净移动。“净”是把两个方向相减后的结果，不表示低浓度侧没有粒子反向运动。

本原创密闭双室模型不发生化学反应，两侧体积各为1单位，每一步都假设各侧现有量的四分之一跨膜。初始左16、右4，左向右为4，右向左为1，净左向右3；于是左剩13、右为7，总量仍为20。下一步两侧交换的量会随现有量改变，不能每一步都固定搬3。小数表示大量粒子总体的期望量，不是把一个真实分子切成四分之一；步骤也不是秒，不能当成测得的扩散速率。

若两侧都是10，每步两向各2.5，净量为零，但交换继续，这就是动态平衡。两侧平均浓度稳定与微观运动停止不是同一句话；若给一侧粒子作可追踪标记，仍可能看到它们跨到另一侧。对比若膜完全不透过所选物质，即使16对4的差异存在，模型也没有跨膜净运输。梯度提供方向条件，通路是否可用则是另一必要条件。

解释题应先确认比较的是同种物质的浓度，而不是所有圆点或不同体积的总量。真实体系还可能有流动、代谢消耗和持续供应，使梯度长期存在。本课不把这些开放体系硬套成最终各半的密闭模型。

### English

Diffusion does not require particles to know which side is crowded. Thermal motion is random, and particles on both sides can cross an available boundary. With equal volumes and otherwise similar conditions, the side containing more particles generally supplies more crossings over the same interval. Net transfer is therefore down the concentration gradient. Net means one directional amount minus the opposite amount; it does not mean reverse crossings disappear.

Our original closed model has equal compartment volumes and no chemical reactions. Each step transfers one quarter of each side’s current amount. Starting with 16 on the left and 4 on the right gives forward transfer 4 and reverse transfer 1. Net transfer is 3, leaving 13 and 7. The total remains 20. The following step uses these new amounts, so net transfer does not remain fixed at 3. Fractions represent expected amounts across many particles, not fragments of individual molecules. Steps are not seconds and do not establish a measured rate.

Starting with 10 on each side gives transfers of 2.5 in both directions. Net transfer is zero while exchange continues: dynamic equilibrium. Stable average concentration does not imply stopped microscopic motion. If selected particles were labelled, some could still cross to the opposite side. In contrast, an impermeable barrier prevents this substance from crossing even when the initial amounts are 16 and 4. A gradient and an available route are separate conditions.

When interpreting evidence, compare concentrations of the same substance, not the total number of different symbols or quantities in unequal volumes. Real cells can consume, supply and transport substances, maintaining gradients in open systems. The eventual equal-sharing result of this closed model cannot simply be imposed on every living system.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

误区：平衡是静止。反例：10/10时两向各2.5，净0而非两向都0。不可透膜造成的不交换，与动态平衡不同。

### English

Misconception: equilibrium means rest. At 10/10 each direction transfers 2.5, giving net 0. Failure to exchange across an impermeable barrier is a different situation.

## Depth / 再想深一点

### 中文

高中衔接：一定条件下净通量与浓度梯度、面积、扩散系数有关；温度和扩散距离也影响过程。本课的1/4交换不是自然常数，不能由此预测任意分子的扩散系数。

### English

Senior bridge: under specified conditions, net flux relates to gradient, area and diffusion coefficient; temperature and distance matter. The one-quarter exchange rule is not a natural constant or a way to infer every molecule’s diffusion coefficient.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新情境：A室含8单位、体积1；B室含12单位、体积3。哪边初始浓度高？自查：浓度=量/体积，A=8、B=4单位/体积；可透膜且条件相同时净A→B，虽然B总量较多。写出通透性与等温条件，不能只数圆点。

### English

New case: A contains 8 amount units in volume 1; B contains 12 in volume 3. Which initial concentration is higher? Self-check: amount/volume gives A=8 and B=4 units per volume. With suitable permeability and equal temperature, net transfer starts A→B despite B containing more material.

## Vocabulary / 双语小词典

### 中文

- **扩散 / diffusion**: 随机运动造成物质顺梯度的净分散。
- **浓度梯度 / concentration gradient**: 不同位置间同种物质浓度的变化。
- **净移动 / net movement**: 一个方向的量减去反方向的量。
- **动态平衡 / dynamic equilibrium**: 相反过程持续发生且平均速率相等。
- **通透性 / permeability**: 屏障允许某种物质通过的程度。
- **守恒 / conservation**: 封闭且无反应时同种物质总量不变。

### English

- **扩散 / diffusion**: Net spreading down a gradient arising from random motion.
- **浓度梯度 / concentration gradient**: Change in concentration of one substance across positions.
- **净移动 / net movement**: Transfer in one direction minus the reverse transfer.
- **动态平衡 / dynamic equilibrium**: Opposite processes continue at equal average rates.
- **通透性 / permeability**: How readily a barrier lets a substance cross.
- **守恒 / conservation**: An unchanged total amount in a closed non-reacting system.

## Check / 检索练习

### 中文

1. [concept] 16/4第一步两向4与1，净量是多少？

   A. 5，左向右
   B. 3，左向右
   C. 4，且无反向运动

   答案: B. 净量为4−1=3；5是两向交换总量。反向1必须计入。

   回全表第1步，核对13+7=20。

2. [concept] 平衡时为什么仍能交换？

   A. 双向平均量相等，净量为零
   B. 粒子不再有动能
   C. 膜必须完全封闭

   答案: A. 动态平衡仍有运动；封闭通路造成的零跨膜交换不是同一机制。

   选择10/10并查看两向2.5。

3. [reasoning] 密闭等体积模型第一步后左13右7，下一步净左→右应是多少？

   A. 仍是3，因为初始梯度不变
   B. 5，因为总量20的1/4交换
   C. 1.5，因为13/4−7/4

   答案: C. 两侧现有量决定下一步双向3.25与1.75，净1.5。总量守恒不表示每步净量不变。

   回表第2步，比较梯度缩小。

4. [vocabulary] concentration gradient指什么？

   A. 同种物质浓度随位置的变化
   B. 物质跨膜的总数量
   C. 所有粒子停止运动

   答案: A. 梯度描述空间差异，不是跨膜量，也不是平衡时运动停止。

   回图先读两室体积与量。

### English

1. [concept] With transfers 4 and 1, what is first-step net transfer?

   A. 5, left to right
   B. 3, left to right
   C. 4, with no reverse movement

   Answer: B. Net transfer is 4−1=3; 5 is gross two-way exchange. Reverse transfer must be counted.

   Check ledger step 1 and verify 13+7=20.

2. [concept] Why can exchange continue at equilibrium?

   A. Equal average two-way amounts give zero net transfer
   B. Particles lose all kinetic energy
   C. The membrane must be sealed

   Answer: A. Dynamic equilibrium retains motion; a blocked route gives zero exchange by a different mechanism.

   Choose 10/10 and inspect the two transfers of 2.5.

3. [reasoning] After 13/7, what is the next net left-to-right transfer?

   A. Still 3 because the initial gradient stays fixed
   B. 5 because one quarter of total 20 transfers
   C. 1.5 because 13/4−7/4

   Answer: C. Current amounts give 3.25 and 1.75, net 1.5. Conservation does not require a constant net transfer.

   Inspect step 2 as the gradient declines.

4. [vocabulary] What is a concentration gradient?

   A. A change in one substance’s concentration across positions
   B. Total material crossing a membrane
   C. All particles stopping

   Answer: A. A gradient describes spatial differences, not transferred amount or motion stopping.

   Read compartment volumes and amounts first.
