## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

“8小时光照让所有植物长高三分之一”这句话哪里超出了9与12 cm的数据？

**学习目标（30分钟）**

- 写出主张、含单位数据、机制和限定语。
- 让结论强度匹配设计，不把支持写成证明。
- 把具体局限连接到下一项可行检验。

### English

How does “eight hours of light makes all plants grow one-third taller” exceed the 9 and 12 cm data?

**Learning goals (30 minutes)**

- Write a claim, data with units, mechanism and qualification.
- Match claim strength to design without equating support with proof.
- Connect each specific limitation to a feasible next test.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

科学结论不是把图上的最大数抄成绝对规律。先问设计允许回答什么。若光照与水量同时变化，只能描述两组关联，不能将差异单归于光。若控制水量、块内随机分配光照并使用独立植株，证据对本实验条件下的光照效应更有支持力；但少量样本、一次批次和一种植物仍不能保证总体因果规律，更不意味着有4株就达到任何通用统计标准。

修复后的虚构4小时组增量8、9、10、9 cm，8小时组11、12、12、13 cm；各4株，7天均值9与12 cm，差3 cm。相对4小时均值的增幅是3/9约33.3%，不是每株都增加33.3%，也不是最终株高增加三分之一。因变量是7天株高增量，不能悄悄换成总株高、质量、产量或健康。范围均2 cm描述样本散布，不是置信区间。

机制推理可以是较长光照在本条件下提供更多光合作用机会，促进有机物形成并支持生长。但我们没有直接测碳固定、糖或干质量，所以机制是与现有生物知识一致的解释，不能写成已经由这张株高图直接证明。局限要具体而有后果：“一种豆苗、7天”限制推广；下一步用多个品种和更长预定时程检验，而不是泛泛写“人为误差”。

示范完整答案：“在水量一致并块内随机安排的本批豆苗中，8小时光照的7天株高增量均值12 cm，高于4小时的9 cm，差3 cm，与较长光照促进本条件下生长的预测一致。光合作用机会增加是可行机制，但未直接测量；每组4株和一个品种限制推广。应以新独立批次和其他品种重复，并增加有机物指标。”这既说明证据的价值，也把结论留在数据能够承担的范围内。

### English

A scientific conclusion does not turn the largest plotted value into a universal law. First ask what the design can answer. If illumination and water vary together, the difference describes an association rather than isolating light. Matching water, randomly allocating duration within blocks and using independently treated seedlings strengthen support for an illumination effect under these conditions. A small sample, one batch and one variety still restrict conclusions; four seedlings are not a universal statistical adequacy threshold.

The repaired fictional four-hour increases are 8, 9, 10 and 9 cm; eight-hour increases are 11, 12, 12 and 13 cm. Four seedlings per group have seven-day means of 9 and 12 cm, differing by 3 cm. Relative to the four-hour mean, the increase is 3/9, about 33.3%. It does not mean every plant increased by 33.3%, nor that final plant height rose by one-third. The response is seven-day height change, not total height, mass, yield or overall health. Both sample ranges are 2 cm, not confidence intervals.

Longer illumination could provide more photosynthetic opportunity, supporting organic-matter production and growth. However, carbon fixation, sugar and dry mass were not measured directly. That mechanism is consistent with biological knowledge; a height graph alone does not prove it. A specific limitation includes a consequence: one variety over seven days restricts generalisation. A useful next study tests more varieties and a pre-planned longer period rather than merely writing “human error”.

A worked conclusion is: “With matched water and random allocation within blocks, eight-hour seedlings had a mean seven-day height increase of 12 cm versus 9 cm at four hours, a 3 cm difference consistent with the prediction. Greater photosynthetic opportunity is plausible but unmeasured. Four plants per group and one variety limit generalisation. Repeat with new independent batches and other varieties, adding an organic-matter measure.” This preserves the evidence’s value while keeping the claim within its support.

## Depth / 高中延伸

### 中文

高中延伸：随机分配有助内部因果解释，扩大取样范围有助外部适用性；两者不能互相替代。统计不确定性、机制测量与设计偏差也是不同问题。

### English

Depth: Allocation helps internal causal interpretation; broader sampling helps external applicability. Neither replaces the other. Statistical uncertainty, mechanism measurements and design bias are also distinct questions.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

有局限不等于毫无证据；随机分配不等于代表所有植物；机制合理不等于已经测得机制。

### English

Limitations do not mean no evidence; random allocation does not represent every plant; a plausible mechanism is not a measured mechanism.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

迁移：另一品种6株/组，5天根长均值4与5 cm。自查结论：该品种、该条件下差1 cm；不能照搬7天株高33.3%。提议重复独立批次并测根质量，不宣称所有植物。

### English

Transfer: another variety has six plants/group and five-day root means of 4 and 5 cm. Self-check: a 1 cm difference for that variety and setting; do not copy the seven-day height figure of 33.3%. Repeat independent batches and measure root mass without claiming all plants.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **claim / 主张**: 证据试图支持的回答。
- **evidence / 证据**: 与问题相关的观察或数据。
- **reasoning / 推理**: 把证据与主张连接的逻辑和机制。
- **limitation / 局限**: 限制结论强度或适用范围的条件。
- **causation / 因果**: 改变一个因素导致响应改变的关系。
- **generalisation / 推广**: 把样本结论扩展到其他对象或条件。
- **replication / 重复研究**: 用新独立材料检验结果能否再出现。

### English

- **claim / 主张**: The answer evidence is used to support.
- **evidence / 证据**: Observations or data relevant to a question.
- **reasoning / 推理**: Logic and mechanism linking evidence to a claim.
- **limitation / 局限**: A condition restricting claim strength or scope.
- **causation / 因果**: A relationship in which changing a factor changes a response.
- **generalisation / 推广**: Extending a conclusion to other organisms or conditions.
- **replication / 重复研究**: Testing whether findings recur with new independent material.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] 结论中的机制句应怎样限定？
   A. 株高图证明了糖的全部变化
   B. 光合作用机会增加是可行解释，但糖未直接测
   C. 机制完全不需要知识
   答案: B. 数据测株高，不测糖；机制连接证据，但不能伪装成直接观察。 复习: 回看机制框与测量框的区别。

2. [concept] 哪项局限与下一步最匹配？
   A. 一个品种→多品种独立重复
   B. 样本少→把同株复制成多行
   C. 位置混杂→只加小数位
   答案: A. 新独立材料扩大适用范围；复制读数或小数不能修复取样和设计。 复习: 回看局限→下一步箭头。

3. [reasoning] 均值9与12 cm，哪个33.3%说法合适？
   A. 每株最终高度都增33.3%
   B. 所有植物产量增33.3%
   C. 本样本7天平均增量相对9 cm高约33.3%
   答案: C. 分母9 cm是参考组平均增量；不能换成每株、最终高度或产量。 复习: 核对数据框的7天株高增量标签。

4. [vocabulary] limitation在科学结论中表示什么？
   A. 结论没有任何价值
   B. 限制强度或适用范围的具体条件
   C. 必须隐藏的坏消息
   答案: B. 局限让别人知道何处可用、何处还需新证据。 复习: 回看一个品种/7天的限定语。

### English

1. [concept] How should a mechanism sentence be qualified?
   A. The height graph proves every sugar change
   B. Greater photosynthetic opportunity is plausible, but sugar was not measured
   C. Mechanisms require no biological knowledge
   Answer: B. Height, not sugar, was measured. A mechanism connects evidence without pretending to be a direct observation. Revisit: Revisit mechanism versus measurement boxes.

2. [concept] Which limitation matches its next study?
   A. One variety → independent repetitions across varieties
   B. Few samples → duplicate rows from one plant
   C. Position confounding → add decimals
   Answer: A. New independent material broadens scope; duplicate readings and decimals do not fix sampling or design. Revisit: Revisit limitation → next test.

3. [reasoning] For means 9 and 12 cm, which 33.3% statement is justified?
   A. Every final plant height increased by 33.3%
   B. All plant yields rose by 33.3%
   C. The sample mean seven-day increase is about 33.3% higher relative to 9 cm
   Answer: C. The denominator is the reference mean increase of 9 cm, not individual height, final height or yield. Revisit: Check “seven-day height increase” in the data box.

4. [vocabulary] What does limitation mean in a scientific conclusion?
   A. The conclusion has no value
   B. A specific restriction on claim strength or scope
   C. Bad news that must be hidden
   Answer: B. A limitation shows where an inference applies and where new evidence is needed. Revisit: Revisit the one-variety/seven-day qualification.
