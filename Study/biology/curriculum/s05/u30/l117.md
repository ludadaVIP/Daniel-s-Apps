## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

“光让豆苗更健康”如何改成别人能重复检验的句子？

**学习目标（30分钟）**

- 将含糊用语改成可测量的变量、时间与单位。
- 区分问题、解释性假设和可观察预测。
- 设计一个可能反驳原假设的结果。

### English

How can “light makes seedlings healthier” become a statement others can test?

**Learning goals (30 minutes)**

- Replace vague words with measurable variables, time and units.
- Distinguish a question, explanatory hypothesis and observable prediction.
- Describe a result that could challenge the hypothesis.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

一个问题可以很有趣，却还不能直接检验。“豆苗喜欢光吗？”把喜欢当成人的感受，没有说明观察什么；“更多光一定更好”中的更多和更好也没有量尺。操作性定义把模糊词换成可重复的动作：同品种同龄豆苗，每天光照4小时与8小时，7天后用同一方法测量株高变化，单位厘米。研究对象、比较条件、观察窗口和结果指标都清楚了。

假设是暂定解释，例如“在其他条件相同且本范围内，较长光照可能增加制造有机物的机会，从而促进生长”。预测则是从解释推出的可观察结果：“8小时组的7天平均株高增量高于4小时组”。这是有方向的预测，不是保证；株高并不等于全部健康，光强、温度、缺水和初始状态都可能改变结果。把水量、土壤、容器和温度固定，才能让光照时长成为主要安排改变的因素。

虚构预试验中，4小时组增量为8、9、10、9 cm，平均9 cm；8小时组为11、12、12、13 cm，平均12 cm，差3 cm。这个结果与预测一致，不能写成假设已被永久证明。只有4株一组且只测一种豆苗，仍可能受样本变异影响；若两个组同时使用不同水量，结果还无法分开光和水的效应。

示范作答：“我预测在同样水量和温度下，8小时组7天株高增量均值高于4小时组；本例12 cm对9 cm与预测一致。较长光照可能增加光合作用机会，但未直接测糖的生成。若重复的公平比较显示均值相等或反向，该解释在本条件下会受到挑战。”先写可能失败的观察，能防止看到数据后才悄悄改预测。进一步迁移可改测干质量，但要重新写单位和测量方法，不能继续把厘米当作质量。

### English

An interesting question is not automatically testable as written. “Do seedlings like light?” gives no measurable meaning to “like”; “more light is better” leaves both “more” and “better” undefined. An operational definition replaces them with repeatable actions: use seedlings of one variety and age, compare four versus eight hours of light daily, and measure height change in centimetres after seven days using the same method. Organisms, comparison, time window and response are explicit.

A tentative hypothesis is that, within this range and under otherwise matched conditions, longer illumination provides more opportunity to make organic matter and promote growth. A prediction is the observable consequence: the eight-hour group will have a greater mean seven-day height increase. The prediction is not a guarantee. Height is only one growth measure, and light intensity, temperature, water and initial condition can alter the result. Keep water, soil, containers and temperature matched so that light duration is the deliberately varied factor.

In the fictional pilot, four-hour changes are 8, 9, 10 and 9 cm, averaging 9 cm. Eight-hour changes are 11, 12, 12 and 13 cm, averaging 12 cm. The 3 cm difference agrees with the prediction but does not permanently prove the hypothesis. Four seedlings per group from one variety leave sampling variation and limited generalisation. If water also differs, light and water effects cannot be separated.

A worked answer is: “I predict a higher mean seven-day height increase under eight hours with water and temperature matched. The example means, 12 versus 9 cm, agree. Longer illumination could increase photosynthetic opportunity, but sugar production was not measured directly. Equal or reversed means in repeated fair comparisons would challenge the explanation under these conditions.” Stating a potentially conflicting result before seeing data guards against quietly rewriting a prediction afterwards. Changing the response to dry mass requires a new measurement method and units.

## Depth / 高中延伸

### 中文

高中延伸：支持不等于唯一解释；生长机制还包括呼吸消耗及分配。这里的假设可被修正，不以“任何反例都算成功”的写法规避检验。

### English

Depth: Support does not establish a unique explanation; growth also involves respiration and allocation. A useful hypothesis is revisable and must not redefine every possible result as success.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

假设不是愿望；预测与数据一致不是永久证明，更不能把未测的“健康”当作株高同义词。

### English

A hypothesis is not a wish. Agreement does not permanently prove it, and unmeasured “health” is not a synonym for height.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

迁移到盐水：比较0与5 g/L盐浓度下5天根长变化。自查：自变量为浓度，因变量为cm根长变化，固定水量/光/品种；根长结果不能直接断言所有植物更健康。

### English

Transfer to salt: compare five-day root-length change at 0 and 5 g/L. Self-check: concentration is the factor, root change in cm the response; match water/light/variety. Root length cannot establish all aspects of plant health.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **hypothesis / 假设**: 可用证据检验的暂定解释。
- **prediction / 预测**: 在指定条件下预期观察到的结果。
- **operational definition / 操作性定义**: 说明怎样施加条件或测量变量。
- **independent variable / 自变量**: 研究者安排改变的条件。
- **dependent variable / 因变量**: 随处理测量的响应。
- **falsifiable / 可反驳**: 可能有证据与该解释不符。

### English

- **hypothesis / 假设**: A tentative explanation testable with evidence.
- **prediction / 预测**: An expected observation under specified conditions.
- **operational definition / 操作性定义**: A description of how a condition is applied or a variable measured.
- **independent variable / 自变量**: The condition deliberately varied.
- **dependent variable / 因变量**: The measured response.
- **falsifiable / 可反驳**: Capable of conflicting with possible evidence.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] 哪句最可直接检验？
   A. 豆苗更快乐
   B. 光是最好的
   C. 同水温下比较4/8 h光照的7天株高增量cm
   答案: C. 量值、时间、响应和固定条件使别人能重复；快乐和最好未定义。 复习: 回看假设修复卡的三个空位。

2. [concept] 哪项是预测而非机制解释？
   A. 8 h组平均增量将高于4 h组
   B. 光可为光合作用提供能量
   C. 叶片含叶绿体
   答案: A. 预测描述指定条件下预期读数；机制说明为什么可能这样。 复习: 对照Explain的假设和预测句。

3. [reasoning] 12 cm对9 cm，但两组水量也不同，怎样修复？
   A. 删除较矮株
   B. 固定水量再比较光照，不能单归因于光
   C. 差3 cm就足够证明
   答案: B. 水是同时改变的替代原因；增加测量精度不能解除这个混杂。 复习: 回看固定条件开关。

4. [vocabulary] operational definition是什么？
   A. 写出肯定正确的结论
   B. 给研究取漂亮名字
   C. 说明实际怎样施加或测量变量
   答案: C. 它让模糊词落到动作、时间、单位和仪器上。 复习: 回看4/8 h、7 d、cm标签。

### English

1. [concept] Which statement is most directly testable?
   A. Seedlings are happier
   B. Light is best
   C. Compare seven-day height changes in cm at 4/8 h light with matched water and temperature
   Answer: C. Values, timing, response and controls enable replication; happier and best are undefined. Revisit: Revisit the three hypothesis-repair fields.

2. [concept] Which is a prediction rather than a mechanism explanation?
   A. The 8 h group will have a higher mean increase than the 4 h group
   B. Light can supply photosynthetic energy
   C. Leaves contain chloroplasts
   Answer: A. A prediction states expected readings under specified conditions; a mechanism explains why. Revisit: Compare the hypothesis and prediction sentences.

3. [reasoning] Means are 12 versus 9 cm, but water differs too. How should the study be repaired?
   A. Remove shorter seedlings
   B. Match water and compare light again; the current result cannot isolate light
   C. A 3 cm difference proves it
   Answer: B. Water is an alternative cause varied with light; precision alone cannot remove this confounding. Revisit: Revisit the matched-conditions switch.

4. [vocabulary] What is an operational definition?
   A. A certainly correct conclusion
   B. An attractive study name
   C. A description of how a variable is applied or measured
   Answer: C. It turns vague words into actions, timing, units and measurement methods. Revisit: Revisit the 4/8 h, 7 d and cm labels.
