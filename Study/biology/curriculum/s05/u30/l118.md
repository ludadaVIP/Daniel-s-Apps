## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

一株豆苗量12次，能代替12株各量一次吗？窗边都放8小时组公平吗？

**学习目标（30分钟）**

- 修复光照与水量、位置同时变化的混杂。
- 区分独立生物重复与同一样本的重复读数。
- 使用分块及块内随机分配提高可比性。

### English

Can twelve readings of one seedling replace twelve seedlings? Is it fair to place every eight-hour seedling by the window?

**Learning goals (30 minutes)**

- Repair confounding between illumination, water and position.
- Distinguish independent biological replicates from repeated readings.
- Use blocks and within-block random allocation to improve comparability.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

坏实验把4小时组放远窗、每天水10 mL，8小时组放近窗、每天水20 mL。即使观察到增量9 cm和12 cm，也无法知道是时长、水量、位置还是组合造成差异。修复首先让水量、土壤、品种和测量日相同；4小时条件是比较基线，不是“什么都不给”的无光无水条件。对照必须服务于所问问题，不能引入额外差别。

位置影响难完全消除时可分块：近窗和远窗各放4与8小时处理，并在每个位置块中用机会机制分配独立植株，避免研究者把较壮者全放一组。虚构数据近窗4/8小时分别增10/13 cm，远窗为8/11 cm。每块差都是3 cm，比只拿近窗13与远窗8比较得到5 cm，更接近在共同位置条件下研究光照时长的问题。分块处理已知位置差异，随机化降低其他系统安排偏差；两者都不是保证所有干扰归零。

独立生物重复必须有多个独立生物单位。将一株量12次，得到12条读数，生物样本数仍为1；将4株各量3次，是12条读数、4个生物单位。重复读数可以帮助发现读尺不稳定，但不能测量植株之间的变异。若同一盆植物共用处理，盆可能才是处理单位；不能无条件把盆内每叶都算独立处理重复。

示范作答：“应保持水量相同，在近远窗块内分别随机安排两种光照，使用独立盆栽重复。已有块内差均为3 cm，比混合位置的5 cm更可比；一株12次读数仍只有1个生物单位。还需增加独立样本和不同批次，检查结果能否重复。”修复设计是在让证据回答问题，不是用更多小数或更多同株照片制造强证据。

还应在记录表保留每株身份和处理编号，先把同株读数汇总，再比较独立植株；记录结构本身就是防止伪重复的工具。

### English

The faulty experiment puts the four-hour group far from the window with 10 mL water daily and the eight-hour group near the window with 20 mL. Means of 9 and 12 cm cannot identify whether duration, water, position or their combination caused the difference. First match water, soil, variety and measurement day. The four-hour condition is the comparison baseline; it need not mean no light and no water. A useful control serves the question without adding other differences.

When position cannot be eliminated, block by it. Place both four- and eight-hour treatments near and far from the window, assigning independent seedlings by chance within each block. This avoids deliberately allocating the strongest seedlings to one treatment. Fictional near-window changes are 10 and 13 cm; far-window changes are 8 and 11 cm. Both within-block differences are 3 cm. Comparing only near-window 13 with far-window 8 gives 5 cm and mixes position with duration. Blocking addresses the known nuisance factor; randomisation reduces other systematic allocation biases. Neither guarantees that every influence vanishes.

Independent biological replication requires independent units. Measuring one seedling twelve times gives twelve readings but one biological unit. Measuring four seedlings three times each gives twelve readings and four biological units. Repeated readings may reveal unstable measurement but cannot represent differences between plants. If plants share one treatment pot, the pot may be the experimental unit; every leaf is not automatically an independent treatment replicate.

A worked answer is: “Match water, allocate both light treatments randomly within near/far blocks, and use independently treated potted seedlings. The existing within-block differences are both 3 cm, more comparable than the mixed-position 5 cm difference. Twelve readings of one plant still give one biological unit. More independent units and batches are needed.” Design repair makes evidence answer the question; extra decimal places or repeated photographs do not create independent evidence.

## Depth / 高中延伸

### 中文

高中延伸：实验单位是独立接受处理的单位；生物来源与处理单位可能不相同。配对可比较同一株前后增量，但前后两值不是两个独立样本。

### English

Depth: The experimental unit independently receives treatment; biological sources and treatment units can differ. Pairing before/after values measures within-plant change, but the two values are not independent samples.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

重复读数不能增加独立生物样本数；随机分配和保持变量相同解决不同问题。

### English

Repeated readings do not increase independent biological sample size; random allocation and matched conditions address different problems.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

迁移：3盆各测4片叶，共12读数。自查：若处理独立施于盆，处理单位是3盆，不能声称n=12个独立处理重复；需要更多独立盆与块内随机分配。

### English

Transfer: measure four leaves from each of three pots, twelve readings total. Self-check: if treatment is independently applied to pots, there are three treatment units, not twelve independent treatment replicates. Add independent pots and allocate within blocks.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **control / 对照**: 用于比较的参考条件或处理。
- **controlled variable / 控制变量**: 保持相同或按设计平衡的条件。
- **biological replicate / 生物重复**: 独立生物单位的重复观察。
- **repeated reading / 重复读数**: 对同一样本多次测量。
- **pseudoreplication / 伪重复**: 把非独立读数当成独立样本。
- **blocking / 分块**: 按已知干扰因素分组，块内包含各处理。
- **randomisation / 随机化**: 用机会机制决定安排。

### English

- **control / 对照**: A reference condition or treatment for comparison.
- **controlled variable / 控制变量**: A condition held equal or balanced by design.
- **biological replicate / 生物重复**: An observation from an independent biological unit.
- **repeated reading / 重复读数**: Multiple measurements of the same sample.
- **pseudoreplication / 伪重复**: Treating non-independent readings as independent samples.
- **blocking / 分块**: Grouping by a known nuisance factor with treatments within blocks.
- **randomisation / 随机化**: Using chance to determine allocation.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] 同一株量12次，独立生物样本数是多少？
   A. 12
   B. 1
   C. 测得准就是无限
   答案: B. 读数和生物单位不同。多次测同株可改善读数，却未增加植株差异信息。 复习: 回看样本ID连线。

2. [concept] 分块的正确安排是什么？
   A. 每个位置块都有两种处理，并块内随机分配
   B. 近窗全8小时、远窗全4小时
   C. 只把同一株换名字
   答案: A. 块内比较让位置不再与处理完全重合；换名字不会造出独立样本。 复习: 回看近/远窗两个块。

3. [reasoning] 近窗10/13 cm，远窗8/11 cm，合理比较是？
   A. 只比13−8=5 cm证明光效应
   B. 平均所有数后不分处理
   C. 各块8 h减4 h都是3 cm，仍需重复
   答案: C. 13与8跨位置，混入位置差；两个3 cm来自共同位置的比较。 复习: 核对分块图的每一行。

4. [vocabulary] pseudoreplication是什么？
   A. 重复实验的正常英文
   B. 把非独立读数当独立样本
   C. 随机安排处理
   答案: B. 关键不是读数多，而是把它们错误当成独立证据。 复习: 回看同株12读数案例。

### English

1. [concept] What is biological n for twelve readings of one seedling?
   A. 12
   B. 1
   C. Infinite if precise
   Answer: B. Readings differ from biological units. Re-measuring improves readings but adds no between-plant variation. Revisit: Revisit sample-ID links.

2. [concept] Which is a correct blocked design?
   A. Both treatments in each position block, allocated randomly within it
   B. Only 8 h near and 4 h far
   C. Rename the same plant
   Answer: A. Within-block comparison separates position from treatment; renaming creates no independent units. Revisit: Revisit near/far blocks.

3. [reasoning] Near-window values are 10/13 cm and far-window 8/11 cm. Which comparison is appropriate?
   A. Use only 13−8=5 cm as the light effect
   B. Average everything without treatment labels
   C. Within each block 8 h minus 4 h is 3 cm; replication remains needed
   Answer: C. 13 versus 8 mixes positions; both 3 cm differences compare treatments under shared positions. Revisit: Check each row of the blocked diagram.

4. [vocabulary] What is pseudoreplication?
   A. A synonym for a repeated experiment
   B. Treating non-independent readings as independent samples
   C. Random treatment allocation
   Answer: B. The problem is not many readings but incorrectly treating them as independent evidence. Revisit: Revisit twelve readings of one plant.
