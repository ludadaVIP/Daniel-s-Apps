# 修复豆苗试验 / Repair a seedling experiment

## 坏实验 / Faulty experiment

4 h组远窗、10 mL水；8 h组近窗、20 mL水；一株12读数写n12。株高图没单位，28 cm读数因不合预测被删除。全为虚构记录。

The 4 h group is far from the window with 10 mL water; the 8 h group near with 20 mL. Twelve readings of one plant are called n12. Axes lack units and 28 cm is discarded for disagreeing with prediction. All records are invented.

## 修复与数据 / Repair and data

定义因变量为7天株高增量cm，安排4/8 h光照时长；同水、土、品种、温度；近远位置各包含两处理、块内随机，独立盆栽处理。4株各3读数仍为4个生物单位。前后同株读数用于配对增量，不算2株。

| Light / 光照 | Four independent plant changes / 四株增量 cm | Mean / 均值 cm | Min–max / 端点 cm | Range / 范围 cm |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------- | -------------- | ----------------- | --------------- |
| 4 h          | 8,9,10,9                                     | 9              | 8–10              | 2               |
| 8 h          | 11,12,12,13                                  | 12             | 11–13             | 2               |

Use random allocation within position blocks, independent pots and matched conditions. Plot raw values and the true min–max endpoints with “Seven-day seedling height increase” as the English title. Range is sample spread, not ruler error or a confidence interval.

## CER与局限 / CER and limitations

本修复情景均值差3 cm，可能与较长光合作用机会相符，但没有直接测糖。每组4株不是通用足够样本标准，一批一种限制推广；新独立批次、多品种与有机物指标是对应检验。原始第一组10若被未核实28替代，均值13.5 cm、范围20 cm，方向改变；查原记录并报告，不因不合预测删点。

Claim: a conditional 3 cm difference in seven-day mean height increase. Evidence: report n, means, units and raw points. Reasoning: photosynthetic opportunity is plausible but unmeasured. Limitation: one variety/batch; propose independent replication and mechanism measurements. An unverified 28 cm changes the first mean to 13.5 cm: investigate and report sensitivity.

## 英语练习与自查 / English practice and self-check

“In these conditions, … compared with … . This is consistent with …, but … was not measured.” 可中文解释，不以英语阻断。检查独立单位、随机/分块、单位、范围端点、异常值处理和局限→下一步。屏幕内完成，不替代学校实际操作训练。
