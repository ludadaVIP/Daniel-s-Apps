## Hook / 今天的谜题

### 中文

两组平均值不同，是否每株都不同？一个28 cm读数不顺眼就能删吗？

**学习目标（30分钟）**

- 从原始数据计算均值、最小最大与范围。
- 补齐图轴单位，区分范围、测量误差和置信区间。
- 追查异常值并透明比较保留与更正的结果。

### English

If means differ, must every plant differ? Can an inconvenient 28 cm reading simply be deleted?

**Learning goals (30 minutes)**

- Calculate mean, minimum, maximum and range from raw data.
- Repair axis units and distinguish range, measurement error and confidence intervals.
- Investigate an unusual value and compare retained versus documented-corrected results.

## Explain / 真正的科学解释

### 中文

图表要让读者看清测了什么、以什么单位测、多少独立单位和怎样汇总。虚构4小时组株高增量8、9、10、9 cm，平均9 cm，最小8、最大10，范围为2 cm；8小时组11、12、12、13 cm，平均12 cm，范围同为2 cm。两组均值差3 cm，本组每个读数的分布还应保留。画范围线时端点必须是8到10、11到13，不能把“均值±范围”误画成7到11和10到14。

范围描述这批样本的散布，既不是尺子误差，也不是均值的置信区间。范围没有说明抽样分布；仅凭范围不重叠，也不能自动宣布统计显著。若同一值读尺时上下波动，可通过重复读数估计部分随机读数误差；若尺子零点整体偏移，多量几次不能消除同方向的系统误差。株高增量若由同一尺子前后读数相减，某些固定零点偏差可能抵消，但这不使所有误差都消失。

现在把4小时组第三值10改成28 cm，数据变为8、9、28、9，均值13.5 cm，范围20 cm。它可能是真实变异，也可能抄错、单位错或测量对象错，不能因“不支持预测”就删除。先查原始记录；只有发现有据可查的错误，才按说明更正。若原表确认是10而被录成28，报告更正及前后结果；若无法确认，保留数据并说明其影响，继续调查。

示范作答：“原记录两组均值9与12 cm，相差3 cm，范围均2 cm。加入未核实28 cm后第一组均值变13.5 cm，方向改变，说明结果对该点敏感；应追查而非随意删点。范围不能提供总体均值的置信区间。”轴从哪里开始、是否断轴也影响视觉夸大，因此表格和明确单位是检验图像印象的工具。

还要报告量尺分辨率及读数方法，不用计算器多出的位数假装更准确。柱状图的高度比较通常需要零基线；散点图可用清楚标注的非零范围，但应避免夸大。

### English

A graph should identify the response, units, number of independent units and summary method. Fictional four-hour height changes are 8, 9, 10 and 9 cm: mean 9 cm, minimum 8, maximum 10 and range 2 cm. Eight-hour values are 11, 12, 12 and 13 cm: mean 12 cm and range 2 cm. The mean difference is 3 cm. Keep the raw points visible. A range line must run from 8 to 10 or 11 to 13, not from mean minus range to mean plus range.

Range describes the spread of this sample. It is neither ruler error nor a confidence interval for a mean. It gives no sampling distribution, and non-overlapping ranges do not automatically establish statistical significance. Re-reading may help estimate some random reading variation. A ruler with an offset zero introduces systematic error that repeated readings do not remove. Subtracting before/after readings on the same ruler may cancel a fixed zero offset, but not every source of error.

Replace the four-hour value 10 with an unverified 28 cm. The dataset becomes 8, 9, 28 and 9: mean 13.5 cm and range 20 cm. The unusual value might be real variation, a transcription error, a unit error or a measurement of the wrong object. Its disagreement with a prediction is not grounds for deletion. Inspect original records. Correct a documented error transparently, reporting what changed; if the source cannot be verified, retain the observation, explain its influence and investigate further.

A worked answer is: “The original means are 9 and 12 cm, a 3 cm difference, with ranges of 2 cm. Including the unverified 28 cm value changes the first mean to 13.5 cm and reverses the comparison, showing sensitivity to that point. Investigate rather than discard it. Range does not provide a population-mean confidence interval.” Clear axis units and a data table also protect against misleading visual impressions from truncated axes.

## Depth / 高中延伸

### 中文

高中延伸：范围依赖样本量，不能用更宽范围直接断言测量更不准确。准确度涉及接近参考值，精密度涉及重复读数接近程度；本课不计算显著性或置信区间。

### English

Depth: Range depends on sample size and does not directly measure accuracy. Accuracy concerns closeness to a reference; precision concerns agreement among repeated readings. No significance tests or confidence intervals are calculated here.

## Misconception / 容易搞错的地方

### 中文

范围=最大−最小，不是均值±范围，不是仪器误差，更不是CI。异常值需要原因证据。

### English

Range=max−min, not mean±range, instrument error or a CI. An unusual observation requires evidence about its cause.

## Apply / 用到新情境

### 中文

新数据5、6、7 cm与7、8、9 cm。自查：均值6与8 cm，差2 cm，两范围均2 cm且端点相接；不能仅据此宣称无差异或显著。

### English

New data: 5, 6, 7 cm versus 7, 8, 9 cm. Self-check: means 6 and 8 cm, difference 2 cm, ranges 2 cm with touching endpoints. This alone establishes neither no effect nor statistical significance.

## Vocabulary / 生物学英语

### 中文

- **mean / 均值**: 数据总和除以个数。
- **range / 范围**: 最大值减最小值。
- **random error / 随机误差**: 重复测量中方向不固定的波动。
- **systematic error / 系统误差**: 使结果持续偏向同一方向的偏差。
- **outlier / 异常值**: 与其余观察明显不同、需要追查的数据。
- **confidence interval / 置信区间**: 基于抽样方法与统计假设对参数不确定性的区间。
- **unit / 单位**: 说明数值测量尺度的标准。

### English

- **mean / 均值**: The sum divided by the number of values.
- **range / 范围**: The maximum minus the minimum.
- **random error / 随机误差**: Measurement variation without a fixed direction.
- **systematic error / 系统误差**: A consistent directional measurement bias.
- **outlier / 异常值**: An unusually different observation requiring investigation.
- **confidence interval / 置信区间**: An interval for parameter uncertainty based on sampling methods and statistical assumptions.
- **unit / 单位**: A standard specifying a measurement scale.

## Check / 检查理解

### 中文

1. [concept] 8、9、10、9 cm的范围是多少？
   A. 2 cm
   B. 9 cm
   C. 8–10是95% CI
   答案: A. 范围10−8=2 cm；9 cm是均值；没有统计方法不能把端点叫CI。 复习: 回看散点图的8与10端点。

2. [concept] 对可疑28 cm读数首先怎样处理？
   A. 不合预测就删
   B. 查原始记录、单位与方法，再透明报告
   C. 改成均值而不说明
   答案: B. 真实生物变异也可能异常；只在证实错误后有理由更正。 复习: 回看待查值开关及均值变化。

3. [reasoning] 8、9、28、9 cm均值13.5，对照组12 cm，应如何写？
   A. 删除28后一定证实原假设
   B. 范围大说明尺子一定坏
   C. 未核实值改变比较方向，报告敏感性并追查
   答案: C. 13.5大于12，而原9小于12；这种敏感性需透明说明，不能自动归因或删点。 复习: 比较原始与28 cm状态的数据表。

4. [vocabulary] systematic error是哪一种？
   A. 同株读数时高时低
   B. 尺子零点使读数持续偏高
   C. 样本范围为2 cm
   答案: B. 同方向偏移是系统误差；重复不能简单消除它。 复习: 回看随机与系统误差对比。

### English

1. [concept] What is the range of 8, 9, 10, 9 cm?
   A. 2 cm
   B. 9 cm
   C. 8–10 is a 95% CI
   Answer: A. Range is 10−8=2 cm; 9 cm is the mean. No CI follows without a statistical method. Revisit: Revisit endpoints 8 and 10.

2. [concept] What should happen first to a suspicious 28 cm reading?
   A. Delete it if it disagrees with the prediction
   B. Check original records, units and method, then report transparently
   C. Replace it with the mean silently
   Answer: B. Real variation can be unusual; documented error provides a reason for correction. Revisit: Revisit the unverified-value switch and mean changes.

3. [reasoning] Averages are 13.5 cm for 8,9,28,9 and 12 cm for the other group. What should be written?
   A. Deleting 28 proves the hypothesis
   B. A wide range proves the ruler is broken
   C. The unverified value reverses the comparison; report sensitivity and investigate
   Answer: C. 13.5 exceeds 12 while the original 9 is below 12; report this sensitivity without automatic attribution or deletion. Revisit: Compare original and 28 cm tables.

4. [vocabulary] Which is systematic error?
   A. Readings randomly high and low
   B. A ruler-zero offset consistently raises readings
   C. A sample range of 2 cm
   Answer: B. A consistent directional offset is systematic; repetition alone does not remove it. Revisit: Revisit random versus systematic errors.
