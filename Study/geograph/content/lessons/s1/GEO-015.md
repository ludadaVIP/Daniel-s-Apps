<!-- section:hook -->
<!-- zh -->

今天先把地名折起来，只保留影像、日期和观察线索。四个候选案例里，哪些形状能帮你排除一个猜测？又有哪些线索容易让你过度确定？
<!-- en -->

Today the place name starts folded away, leaving imagery, dates and observation clues. Among four candidates, which patterns help reject a guess, and which tempt overconfidence?
<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 先用眼睛，不急着报地名

看密集建筑与水道、长条沙丘、矩形田块与分汊河道，或海岛山脉。把“我看见……”写成不带地名的描述，这样才能检查它是否在图中真实存在。

雪、冰、云都可能白，森林、草地、农田都可能绿。照片里没有看见道路，也可能是分辨率、云或视角所限，不能直接断言道路不存在。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Observe before naming

Look for dense structures/waterways, long dunes, rectangular fields/braided channels, or island mountains. Describe “I see …” without a place name so you can check whether the pattern is actually visible.

Snow, ice and cloud may be white; forest, grassland and fields may be green. Roads not visible may reflect resolution, clouds or viewpoint rather than absence.
<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 候选卡是额外资料

四张资料卡分别介绍海湾城市、干旱沙海、农业平原和有山脉的海岛。气候背景来自资料，不是从一个颜色凭空读出的全年天气。先比较地形与人造形状，再用第二条独立线索核对。

在这四个候选里最吻合，仍不意味着全世界只有这个地方吻合。保留“较可能”，会让你的解释更诚实、更容易修正。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Candidate cards add information

Four reference cards describe a harbour metropolis, arid sand sea, farming plain and mountainous island. Climate context is supplied by references, not year-round weather invented from a colour. Compare terrain and constructed patterns, then cross-check a second distinct clue.

The best fit among four candidates is not necessarily unique worldwide. “More likely” makes your explanation easier to revise and more faithful to evidence.
<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 推测之后，查出处

展开来源，读取实际地点、拍摄日期与图像处理说明。纽约照片为2022年斜视、南方在上；坎特伯雷与纳米布影像分别为2015与2019年；南岛图为2007年。日期与视角不能混成同一次调查。

如果资料核查确认了地点，请说“来源显示”，而不把它伪装成自己单凭照片证明。科学推理也包含发现局限、修正猜测和记录证据从哪里来。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Check the origin after inferring

Reveal source, actual place, capture date and processing notes. New York is a 2022 oblique view with south at top; Canterbury and Namib imagery dates to 2015 and 2019; South Island to 2007. Dates/viewpoints do not form one simultaneous survey.

When metadata confirms a place, say “the source shows”, rather than claiming the photograph alone proved it. Reasoning includes limits, revised hypotheses and evidence provenance.
<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：侦探任务不是靠猜中地名证明聪明。一条线索常有多个解释；两个线索也不自动消除所有不确定性。来源可自由看，任务记录的是你怎样把观察与核查连接起来。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** this is not a contest of clever place-name guesses. One clue often allows several explanations; two clues do not automatically remove all uncertainty. Sources are freely accessible; the task records how observations connect to checking.
<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

依次完成四张影像：选择候选，留下两条可观察的线索，说明“候选中较吻合”而非唯一确定，再展开出处并记录来源核查。选错时先读提示，可以重新观察与修正。
<!-- en -->

For each of four images, choose a candidate, record two observable clues, state a better fit rather than unique certainty, reveal origin and log the source check. Read hints and revise after any mismatch.
<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

问：“如果把地名遮住，你的两条线索仍能指出来吗？还能想出一个别的地方？”纸笔替代：用四张打印图或手绘线索卡，写观察、候选、另一种可能、需要核查的资料。日期和出处保留在卡片背面。无需拍摄人脸或收集他人个人资料。
<!-- en -->

Ask: “With the name hidden, can you still point to two clues? Can another place fit?” Paper alternative: use four printed images or drawn clue cards, writing observation, candidate, another possibility and a reference to check. Keep dates and origins on the reverse. No faces or others’ personal information are needed.
