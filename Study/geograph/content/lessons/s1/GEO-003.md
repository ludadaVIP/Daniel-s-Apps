这是 **2007 年 12 月 7 日** Aqua 卫星 MODIS 传感器观测的新西兰南岛。找出亮白山脉、两侧不同的颜色和海岸。它能告诉你从基督城开车到哪里吗？又能告诉你今天是否下雨吗？
<!-- en -->

Aqua’s MODIS sensor observed New Zealand’s South Island on **7 December 2007**. Look for bright mountain areas, contrasting colours and the coast. Can this view tell you where to drive from Christchurch? Can it establish whether it is raining today?
<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 影像也是一次观测

卫星传感器记录反射或发出的信号，经过处理形成影像。这张真彩色影像帮助观察地表外观，但云能遮住地面，季节会改变景象，分辨率会限制能看清的细节。它是 2007 年的观测，不是实时画面。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / An image is an observation

A satellite sensor records reflected or emitted signals, which are processed into an image. This true-colour view helps us inspect surface appearance. Clouds can obscure the ground, seasons alter the scene, and resolution limits detail. It records 2007 rather than a live view.
<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 地图有目的，也有图例

道路图突出城市和连接通道；地形图突出山地与平原。读地图时，先看题名，再读图例。本课道路与地形图是依据真实海岸位置绘制的**教学示意图**：城镇位置近似，连线和山地区域简化；它们不是测绘导航图，也没有真实高程色带。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Maps have a purpose and a key

A road map emphasises towns and connections; a terrain map emphasises mountains and plains. Read the title and legend first. Our road and terrain views are **teaching schematics** based on real coastline positions. Town locations are approximate, and connections and mountain areas are simplified. They are not navigation charts or measured elevation maps.
<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 选图，就是选择证据

观察拍摄时的地表外观，选影像；看主要城镇的连接关系，选道路图；比较山地与平原位置，选地形图。三张图讲的是同一区域，但影像视角、范围和示意图不同，不能逐像素叠加。一个问题常需多份证据，今天路况则需要更新的道路资料。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Choosing a view means choosing evidence

Choose imagery for surface appearance at capture time, roads for major town connections, and terrain for approximate mountain–plain relationships. These views describe the same region, but have different perspectives and extents; they cannot be overlaid pixel for pixel. Many questions need more than one source. Current road conditions require updated road information.
<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：地图上的棕色不是地面涂了棕色；绿色的含义要看当前图例。2007 年影像也不能证明今天的天气、积雪和道路状况。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** brown on a map does not mean the ground was painted brown. Read the current legend to interpret green. Imagery from 2007 cannot establish today’s weather, snow cover or road conditions.
<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

切换影像、道路和地形三个视图。为“拍摄时地表是什么样”“城镇如何相连”“山地与平原在哪里”各选择最合适的图，并读出一条图例或影像线索。选错时，先看提示，再换一种证据。
<!-- en -->

Switch among imagery, roads and terrain. Choose the most useful view for surface appearance at capture time, town connections, and mountain–plain locations. Cite one image clue or legend item. If a choice misses the purpose, use the hint and try a different source.
<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

问：“你为什么选这张图？它省略了什么？”孩子若只按颜色猜，让他读图例，并比较三张图为什么不会完全重合。纸笔替代：给家里一个房间画两张图，一张突出家具位置，一张只突出门与通道；比较同一空间的不同信息取舍。
<!-- en -->

Ask “Why choose this view?” and “What does it leave out?” If the child guesses by colour alone, read the legend and compare why the views do not align exactly. Paper alternative: draw one room twice, emphasising furniture in one view and doors and passageways in the other.

如果地图上的北箭头指向右边，“往东走”是朝屏幕上方还是下方？下一课转动地图试一试。
<!-- en -->

If a map’s north arrow points right, does travelling east mean moving up or down the screen? Rotate a map in the next lesson to find out.
