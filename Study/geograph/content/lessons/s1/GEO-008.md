新西兰在十二月接近夏季，伦敦却接近冬季。如果整颗地球到太阳的距离几乎相同，“离太阳远”能解释这两种季节吗？先看两侧地轴和光照，提出一个能同时解释南北半球的猜想。
<!-- en -->

December brings summer towards New Zealand and winter towards London. If both hemispheres share almost the same distance to the Sun, can “farther away” explain both? Inspect the axes and sunlight, then propose an explanation that works for north and south together.
<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 一天，不是一年

自转是绕自身地轴转动，带来一地的昼夜。公转是地球绕太阳运行，约一年一圈。先固定公转位置，只推进自转滑块：同一个观察点会进入光照、再进入黑夜。

这证明什么？它说明每天的明暗变化不需要地球远离太阳，却不能单独解释一年里白昼为什么变长或变短。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / A day is not a year

Rotation is spinning about the axis, producing a place’s day/night cycle. An orbit is Earth’s journey around the Sun, taking about a year. Hold orbital position fixed and advance rotation: the same observer enters sunlight and then darkness.

What does this establish? Daily light and dark do not require Earth to move farther away, but rotation alone does not explain changing daylight duration through the year.
<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 轴不每天改指向，朝光关系却改变

地轴相对轨道面垂线倾斜约23.4°，一年里指向近似保持不变。公转改变地球与太阳的位置关系。六月北半球较朝向太阳；十二月换成南半球，两地季节因此相反。

透视图没有按真实大小与距离画。左右两个至点的地轴朝向一致，不是每隔半年把地轴翻过去。三月、九月分点附近，两半球光照较均衡；请在下面比较昼长。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / A nearly fixed axis, a changing sunlight relation

The axis tilts about 23.4° from perpendicular to the orbital plane, keeping nearly the same direction through the year. Orbit changes Earth’s relation to the Sun. The north faces towards it more in June and the south in December, giving opposite seasons.

The perspective diagram is not to scale. Both solstice axes point the same way rather than flipping every six months. Near March and September equinoxes, hemispheric illumination is more balanced; compare daylight below.
<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 更高的太阳，更长的白昼

一个半球朝向太阳时，其中纬度地区常有更长白昼与更高正午太阳高度。同样一束阳光，高角度照射时较集中，低角度则铺得更开。光照变化有助于解释季节，但不能单凭模型预测某一天气温。

比较六月与十二月奥克兰、伦敦附近的光照条。再把倾角改成0°，保持纬度、自转和圆形轨道不变：昼夜仍存在，主要季节光照变化却消失。纬度差异还在，所以不能说各地一样热。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Higher Sun, longer daylight

When a hemisphere faces the Sun more, its mid-latitudes generally have longer days and a higher noon Sun. The same beam is more concentrated at a high angle and spread wider at a low angle. Sunlight helps explain seasons but cannot alone forecast a day’s temperature.

Compare June and December light bars near Auckland and London. Set tilt to zero while keeping latitude, rotation and a circular orbit fixed: day/night remains, but the main seasonal light changes disappear. Latitude differences remain, so places do not all become equally warm.
<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：真实地球轨道略呈椭圆，但不是“离太阳远就让全球冬天”。本课圆轨道对照用来单独观察倾角作用。四季光照、地方气候和当天的天气也不同；热带可能更适合描述干季、雨季。理想昼长忽略大气折射等因素，不能当作实测日出时刻。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** the real orbit is slightly elliptical, but “farther means winter everywhere” fails. Our circular-orbit comparison isolates tilt. Seasonal sunlight, local climate and daily weather are different; wet/dry seasons can describe some tropical places better. Ideal day length omits effects such as refraction and is not a measured sunrise time.
<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

先在0°与180°自转帧记录观察点的昼、夜。再记录23.4°倾角下六月、十二月两地的相反光照证据。最后改成0°，记录对照。五条证据齐全后，用“一天／一年／半球／倾角”解释，别只报季节名称。
<!-- en -->

Record the observer’s day and night at rotation frames 0° and 180°. At 23.4° tilt, record the opposite hemispheric light patterns in June and December. Then record the zero-tilt comparison. Explain all five observations using day/year, hemisphere and tilt, rather than merely naming seasons.
<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

每次只问一个变量：“刚才改的是自转、公转位置，还是倾角？”纸笔替代：用台灯与球，先固定球的位置转动它；再保持轴方向，把球移到灯的两侧，比较南北半球。也可直接画四幅图。灯具由成人操作；不直视太阳，不用高温或强光实验。
<!-- en -->

Ask one variable at a time: “Did you change rotation, orbital position or tilt?” Paper alternative: use a lamp and ball, rotating at one fixed position first. Then keep the axis direction fixed and move to opposite sides of the lamp, comparing hemispheres. Four drawings work too. An adult handles the lamp; do not look at the Sun or use hot or intense-light equipment.
