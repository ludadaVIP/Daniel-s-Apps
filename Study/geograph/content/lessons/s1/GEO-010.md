俯视图上圈圈都是线，怎样知道哪边上山更陡？先选一个高度，再切换到剖面，追踪那条线在山坡上的位置。
<!-- en -->

How do rings on a plan reveal the steeper side of a hill? Select a height, switch to the section, and trace its position on the slope.
<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 一层一层切同一座山

想象用水平面在100、200、300米切山，切面与地表相交的位置连起来就是等高线。它们不是道路。俯视图里不容易看见高度，所以每条线需要高度数字。

切换东西剖面：高亮200米，找到两侧与山坡的交点。地图与剖面来自同一个虚构斜锥山，不是分别随意画出的两座山。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Slice the same mountain

Imagine horizontal slices at 100, 200 and 300 m. The intersections with the ground form contours. They are not roads. A plan hides vertical height, so labels give elevation.

Switch to the west–east section. Highlight 200 m and find the intersections on both slopes. Plan and section come from the same fictional oblique cone, rather than two unrelated drawings.
<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 线数会变，山不会

把等高距从100米改成50米，会出现更多高度层；峰顶还是400米，两侧坡形也没变。等高距是高度差，不是纸上两条线之间的厘米数。

在同一图里，西侧需要较长水平距离达到下一层高度，东侧较短。比较不同地图时，必须先核对比例尺、等高距与高度单位，不能直接数线。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / More lines, same hill

Change interval from 100 to 50 m: more height levels appear. The summit stays at 400 m and both slopes keep their shape. The contour interval is a vertical difference, not centimetres between printed lines.

Here the west side needs a longer horizontal run to reach the next height than the east. Before comparing different maps, check scale, interval and height units; do not just count lines.
<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 用同样升高来比较

这座山每升高100米，西侧水平前进425米，东侧175米。所以东侧更陡，西侧更缓。俯视图东侧等高线较密；剖面与距离数值支持同一个判断。

剖面纵向高度为了阅读而放大，因此不要直接拿屏幕上的角度当真实坡角。真实山路还受岩石、天气、路况影响；“缓坡”不等于“可以安全走”。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Compare the same rise

A 100 m rise requires 425 m horizontally on the west side and 175 m on the east. The east is steeper and the west gentler. Closer eastern contours, the section and distance values support the same conclusion.

The section exaggerates vertical height for readability; screen angles are not true slope angles. Rocks, weather and paths also affect real terrain. Gentler does not automatically mean safe to walk.
<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：封闭圈不一定都代表山峰，还可能是凹地，须看高度标注或凹地符号。本课仅展示向内升高的单峰模型。等高线之间的高度需要估计，不能凭空读出精确值。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** closed loops can describe peaks or depressions; read height labels or depression symbols. This lesson shows only a single hill rising inwards. Heights between contours require estimation, not invented precision.
<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

分别查看并记录俯视图与剖面，再回答同一条200米线的高度。选择更陡与更缓的坡面，并指出100米升高对应的水平距离。可以改变等高距检验“线多就是山更高”的猜想。
<!-- en -->

View and record both plan and section. Answer the equal-height question about the 200 m line, identify steeper and gentler slopes, and point to the horizontal runs for a 100 m rise. Change interval to test whether more lines means a higher hill.
<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

问：“你说这边陡，是因为它高，还是因为相同升高发生在更短距离？”纸笔替代：用纸板叠出一边缓一边陡的山，描各层边缘；或直接画同一山的剖面与三层等高线。比较同一比例，避免剪刀等工具由孩子独自操作。
<!-- en -->

Ask: “Is it steeper because it is high, or because the same rise happens over less distance?” Paper alternative: stack card layers into an uneven hill and trace their edges, or draw a profile and three contours. Keep the comparison scale consistent; an adult handles cutting tools.
