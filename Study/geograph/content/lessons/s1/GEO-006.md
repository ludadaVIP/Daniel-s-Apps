“太平洋里有一个点”还不够精确。把世界想成有编号的参考网格：先找到一条南北位置线，再找到一条东西位置线。它们的交点，能否让另一个人找到同一片区域？
<!-- en -->

“A point in the Pacific” is not enough. Imagine Earth covered by numbered reference lines. Choose a north–south location, then an east–west location. Could their intersection help someone else find the same region?
<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 纬度告诉你赤道哪一侧

赤道是0°纬线。向北，到北极为90°N；向南，到南极为90°S。纬线环绕东西方向，却描述南北位置，别把线延伸的方向与它测量的方向混淆。

本课滑块用正数表示北纬、负数表示南纬。将点从30°N移到30°S，哪个数改变了？它们是否都离赤道同样的角度？
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Latitude locates you north or south

The equator is latitude 0°. Latitude reaches 90°N at the North Pole and 90°S at the South Pole. Parallels circle east–west while describing north–south location: a line’s direction and what it measures are different.

Our slider uses positive values for north and negative values for south. Move from 30°N to 30°S. Which number changes? Are both the same angular distance from the equator?
<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 经度需要一个约定起点

经线从北极连到南极，测量以0°经线为参考的东西位置。向东记E，向西记W，各到180°；180°E与180°W指同一条参考经线。

赤道有自然的几何定义，0°经线的选择则有历史约定。本课只用区域尺度，不用来找精确门牌。观察球体上经线的会聚：靠近极地，相同经度差所跨的东西距离会变短。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Longitude needs an agreed starting line

Meridians join the poles and locate east–west positions relative to 0° longitude. E means east and W west, each reaching 180°. The 180°E and 180°W labels describe the same reference meridian.

The equator has a natural geometric definition; the choice of 0° longitude has a history of agreement. Our regional-scale exercise does not identify exact addresses. Notice meridians converging towards the poles, where the same longitude difference spans a shorter east–west distance.
<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 两个数，两条线，一个位置

40°S、175°E的点在赤道以南、0°经线以东，新西兰附近。按纬度、经度的顺序读，再让两条参考线相交。负纬度对应S，负经度对应W；不要把两个负号都当成“往南”。

矩形图方便定位，却拉伸了球面的几何。看同一经纬网在球体图和矩形图中的差别：为什么整齐格子的宽度不能证明它们实地一样宽？
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Two numbers, two lines, one point

40°S, 175°E lies south of the equator and east of 0°, near New Zealand. Read latitude first, longitude second, then intersect their lines. Negative latitude means S; negative longitude means W. The two minus signs do not both mean south.

A rectangular view makes location convenient while stretching spherical geometry. Compare the globe and flat grids. Why can identical cell widths on paper not establish identical ground widths?
<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：经纬度以角度表示，不是公里；参考线并未真的画在地面上。南北极位置可以确定，但在那里所有经线交会，经度不再区分不同地表点。专业数据还要说明坐标基准、顺序和精度。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** coordinates are angular degrees, not kilometres, and the reference lines are not painted on Earth. The poles have defined locations, but all meridians meet there, so longitude does not distinguish separate surface points. Professional data also needs its reference system, coordinate order and precision.
<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

用两个滑块依次标出新西兰附近、撒哈拉内陆和太平洋赤道区域。每次先读N/S、E/W，再移动参考线。标记前说明点大致落在陆地还是海洋。滑块以5°为步长，目标是找到区域，不是精确城市地址。
<!-- en -->

Use both sliders to mark a point near New Zealand, one inside the Sahara and one in the equatorial Pacific. Read N/S and E/W before moving the lines. Before marking, describe whether the point appears on land or ocean. The 5° steps locate broad regions, not precise city addresses.
<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

让孩子先说“赤道哪一侧”，再说“0°经线哪一侧”。如果经纬互换，回到球体图分别指线。纸笔替代：画矩形参考网格，标出赤道与0°经线；一人给坐标、一人标点，再用橘子或球展示参考线会聚。不要写家庭真实坐标。
<!-- en -->

Ask which side of the equator, then which side of 0° longitude. If the values are swapped, point to each family on the globe drawing. Paper alternative: draw a grid with the equator and 0° meridian. One person gives coordinates and the other marks the point; use an orange or ball to show convergence. No real home coordinates are needed.
<!-- section:door -->
<!-- zh -->

同一经线上地点不一定共享同样钟表规则。同一时刻的新西兰与伦敦，也可能用不同日期。下一课把地球的空间网格与时间联系起来，再看看钟表里的约定。
<!-- en -->

Places on one meridian need not share a clock rule. At the same instant, New Zealand and London may even display different dates. Next, connect Earth’s spatial grid to time and examine the agreements behind clocks.
