如果只说“新西兰有山有海”，朋友能找到你介绍的地方吗？先读北箭头和两岛轮廓，再用城市、山脉、火山和河流，把宽泛描述变成有位置的证据。
<!-- en -->

If you only say “New Zealand has mountains and sea”, could a friend locate your example? Read north and the island outlines, then use cities, ranges, volcanoes and rivers to turn a broad statement into spatial evidence.
<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 先建立位置骨架

北岛在北、南岛在南，中间是Cook Strait。Auckland在北岛北部，Wellington靠北岛南端；Christchurch在南岛东侧平原临海地区。国家还包括其他岛屿，本图没有全部画出。

A–G只是大致位置。海岸来自Natural Earth资料，山脉与河道用概略线说明关系，不是真实测量线，也不能用来驾车、登山或判断洪水安全。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Build a location framework

North Island lies north of South Island, separated by Cook Strait. Auckland is in northern North Island, Wellington near its southern end, and Christchurch on the eastern South Island coastal plains. The country includes other islands not all shown here.

A–G are approximate regions. Natural Earth supplies coast geometry; ranges and rivers are schematic connections, not measured lines for driving, hiking or flood safety.
<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 用真实高程比较

NASA图把2000年雷达高程变成颜色与阴影：较低处绿色，经黄、棕色到较高处白色。这和照片里绿色代表植物不是同一回事。图于2005年发布，不是今天的卫星外观。

北岛中央有Ruapehu等火山与高地，也有其他山脉和低地。南岛中部偏西有长南阿尔卑斯山脉，东侧有坎特伯雷平原。因此“两岛的地形安排不同”比“北岛全平、南岛全高”更准确。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Compare using actual elevation

NASA’s map converts 2000 radar heights into colour/shading: green lower terrain, through yellow and brown to white heights. This differs from vegetation appearing green in a photo. Released in 2005, it is not today’s satellite appearance.

Central North Island has Ruapehu and other volcanic/high terrain, plus other ranges and lowlands. Western-central South Island contains the long Southern Alps with Canterbury Plains to the east. Different arrangements is more accurate than north-all-flat/south-all-high.
<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 让河流与城市进入解释

怀卡托河从陶波湖地区经北岛向西北出海；克卢萨河连接南岛内陆湖区与东南海岸。地图只是概略画出方向，真实河道有弯曲、支流与设施。城市与河流、海岸和地形有联系，但不能由一张图证明所有选址原因。

2022年北岛斜视照片与2007年南岛影像有不同日期、视角和云量。拿照片比较时，先读图注，再用地形图与位置图交叉核对。Aotearoa New Zealand及当地地名有文化意义，本课使用常用名称并保留资料来源。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Add rivers and cities to the explanation

The Waikato links the Taupō region with a northwest outlet; the Clutha links inland South Island lakes to the southeast coast. The teaching lines simplify real bends, tributaries and structures. Cities relate to water, coasts and terrain, but one map cannot establish every settlement cause.

The 2022 North Island oblique photo and 2007 South Island image differ in dates, viewpoints and cloud. Read captions and cross-check with relief and location maps. Aotearoa New Zealand and local place names carry cultural meaning; common names and sources are retained.
<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：主岛教学图不代表新西兰的全部领土；“北岛火山较突出”也不等于“北岛只有火山”。城市点不显示人口或具体家庭位置。两个视觉范围不同，不能逐像素叠加。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** a main-island teaching map is not the entire territory. Prominent North Island volcanism does not mean only volcanoes. City points do not encode population or home locations. Different image extents cannot be overlaid pixel for pixel.
<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

选一个地名，再用A–G和文字线索定位。完成三城、山脉、火山、两条河流七个区域。最后结合真实高程图选出更合理的南北岛比较，并举一个反对“全都”的实例。
<!-- en -->

Choose a feature, then locate it using A–G and written clues. Complete three cities, a range, volcano and two rivers. Use the real elevation map for the island comparison and give a counterexample to an “all” claim.
<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

问：“这个点表示一座建筑、整条河，还是附近的区域？”纸笔替代：画两大岛简图，加北箭头和海峡，贴七张地名卡；每张写一句与相邻地物的关系。可以用资料在家完成，不需收集当前位置或安排实地旅行。
<!-- en -->

Ask: “Does this point mean one building, an entire river, or a surrounding region?” Paper alternative: sketch the two islands with north and the strait, then attach seven feature cards, describing a neighbouring relation for each. Work from references at home without location collection or travel.
