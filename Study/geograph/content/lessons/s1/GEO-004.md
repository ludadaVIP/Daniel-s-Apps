一张虚构社区地图：学校、图书馆和河流都在固定的位置。先找北箭头，再转动地图。图书馆看起来换了方向，它真的搬家了吗？要过河，能直接走一条直线吗？
<!-- en -->

A fictional community map places a school, library and river at fixed locations. Find its north arrow, then rotate the map. The library appears in a different direction on the screen. Has it moved? Can a straight line take you safely across the river?
<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 上方不一定是北

北在上方是常见制图习惯，不是所有地图必须遵守的自然法则。先找到北箭头：从北顺时针依次是东、南、西。地图转动时，地点和箭头一起转；地理位置没有改变。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Up need not mean north

North-up is a common map convention, not a law of nature. Locate the north arrow. Clockwise from north comes east, south and west. When the map rotates, locations and its arrow rotate together while their geographic relationships stay the same.
<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 地图北与指南针北

真北指向地理北极；磁性指南针指向磁北，两者通常有差别，称为磁偏角，随地点和时间变化。本课地图用真北箭头，交互只是旋转地图，没有读取设备传感器或模拟当地磁场。现实中使用指南针还要考虑磁偏角和附近金属干扰。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Map north and compass north

True north points towards the geographic North Pole. A magnetic compass indicates magnetic north. The difference, magnetic declination, varies by location and time. Our map uses a true-north arrow. Rotating it neither reads device sensors nor simulates a local magnetic field. Real compass use also needs declination and nearby metal to be considered.
<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 方位与可行路线

“图书馆在学校北面”描述相对位置，不代表可以一直向北走。本图河流只有东侧的桥可以通过。先从学校向东到桥下，再向北过桥，最后向西到图书馆。绕路是地理条件带来的取舍：方向、障碍和连接都要一起考虑。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Direction and a workable route

“The library is north of the school” describes relative location, not an instruction to walk straight north. This model allows crossing only at the eastern bridge. Travel east from the school, north over the bridge, then west to the library. Direction, obstacles and connections all shape a workable route.
<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：地图倒过来，北不会变成南；屏幕方向与地理方位不是同一回事。没有手机信号时，指南针仍可能工作，但必须会读地图和处理真实环境；本课程不替代实际导航。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** turning a map upside down does not turn north into south. Screen direction and geographic direction are different. A magnetic compass can work without a phone signal, but real navigation requires map skills and attention to conditions; this lesson does not replace it.
<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

先转动地图，找出图书馆相对学校的方位。然后逐个点击相邻地点，从学校经过桥到图书馆；地图拒绝直接跨河，并告诉你原因。倒转地图后再完成一次，你能保持同样的地理路线吗？
<!-- en -->

Rotate the map and identify the library’s direction from the school. Then click adjacent stops to reach the library via the bridge. A direct river crossing is blocked with an explanation. Turn the map upside down and repeat. Can you follow the same geographic route?
<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

让孩子先指北，再说东。可以把纸地图转动 90°、180°，检查他能否使用箭头而不是记住屏幕位置。纸笔替代：在家中画虚构寻宝地图，标上北箭头与障碍，描述三段路线；不需要出门，不使用真实家庭地址。
<!-- en -->

Invite the child to point north before naming east. Turn a paper map by 90° and 180° to check that the arrow, rather than screen position, guides the answer. Paper alternative: draw a fictional treasure map at home with a north arrow and obstacles, then describe three route segments. No trip or real home address is needed.
