### 01 / 缩小的是画面里的世界

地图把一大片空间缩到纸或屏幕上。线段尺给出图上一段长度所代表的实地长度。把 A–B 与尺比较：大约几段？乘上每段公里数，才能得到距离。

现在从1×改为2×。地点间的画面长度加倍，固定画面长度的尺代表的公里数减半。用新尺再量，实地距离应该相同。你能指出两处一起改变的证据吗？
<!-- en -->

### 01 / A smaller view of a larger place

A map fits a broad space onto paper or a screen. Its graphic bar relates a drawn length to a ground length. Count how many bar lengths fit along A–B, then multiply by kilometres per bar.

Change from 1× to 2×. The drawn separation doubles, while this fixed-width bar represents half as many kilometres. Use the updated bar to check the same ground distance. Which two changes support your explanation?
<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 同单位，才能读懂比例

纸图的1:100万，表示地图1厘米对应实地100万厘米。100万厘米是10公里，所以2厘米对应20公里。大数字分母并不表示“细节更多”：同样大小纸张上，1:100万通常比1:1万覆盖更大范围、取舍更多细节。

图里的尺只是计算示意，不能把屏幕显示的“2厘米”拿真实尺子去量。想比较缩放过的图片，优先使用随图片一起缩放的线段尺。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Match the units first

On a printed 1:1,000,000 map, 1 cm represents 1,000,000 cm on the ground: 10 km. So 2 cm represents 20 km. A larger denominator does not mean more detail. On the same paper size, 1:1,000,000 usually covers more territory with fewer details than 1:10,000.

The illustrated ruler shows a calculation, not physical screen centimetres. For a resized image, use a graphic bar resized together with the image.
<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / “多远”先说明沿哪里量

青色虚线连接两点，棕色通路绕行。直线10公里与沿路16公里可以同时正确：它们量的对象不同。路线由3、10、3公里三段组成，逐段相加就是沿路长度。

为什么沿路距离更适合估计这条通路的步行长度？即使知道16公里，还缺哪些资料才能估计所需时间？想想坡度、路面和行走速度。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Distance along what?

Teal dashes connect the points directly; the brown path takes a detour. A 10 km direct distance and a 16 km path can both be correct because they measure different things. Add its 3, 10 and 3 km segments for the route total.

Why does path distance better answer a walking-distance question here? What else would you need to estimate travel time, even after finding 16 km? Consider slope, surface and walking speed.
<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：放大地图不是扩大真实地球；屏幕厘米不等于指定印刷尺寸。世界地图的投影也会影响距离，不能把任意两点的画面直线直接当作球面最短路。本课是已声明尺度的虚构局部平面模型。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** zooming does not enlarge the real Earth, and screen centimetres do not equal specified printed centimetres. World-map projections also affect distance, so a screen line need not be the shortest path over a sphere. This activity is a fictional local flat model with declared distances.
<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

分别在1×、2×视图，用线段尺估计 A–B 的直线公里数，并记录两次结果。再计算棕色通路三段相加的长度。选错后回到图上找证据，最后解释两种“距离”为何不同。
<!-- en -->

Estimate A–B in kilometres using the bar at both 1× and 2×, recording each result. Then add the three brown path segments. If a result does not fit, inspect the drawing again. Explain why the two kinds of distance differ.
<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

先问“你的尺上写着什么单位？”再让孩子估计，不要先报答案。纸笔替代：画两点相距4厘米，声明1厘米代表500米；算出2公里。把图和线段尺一起放大两倍，再比较；添一条折线路径，逐段相加。无需实际外出。
<!-- en -->

Ask “What unit is on your bar?” before inviting an estimate, rather than giving the answer. Paper alternative: draw points 4 cm apart and declare 1 cm = 500 m, giving 2 km. Enlarge both the drawing and graphic bar together, compare again, and add a bent path whose segments can be summed. No trip is needed.
