<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 名字帮助组织世界

本课采用常见的七大洲教学划分：亚洲、欧洲、非洲、北美洲、南美洲、大洋洲、南极洲。大洲不是国家，也不一定是被海水完全隔开的单块陆地。海岸形状是真实地理信息，分类方式还包含历史与教学约定。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Names organise the world

We use a common seven-region teaching model: Asia, Europe, Africa, North America, South America, Oceania and Antarctica. A continent is not a country, nor must each be an entirely separate landmass. Coastlines describe physical geography; classifications also reflect history and teaching conventions.
<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 为什么还有其他答案？

欧洲和亚洲相连，有时合称“欧亚大陆”；有些教学体系也把南、北美洲合称美洲。英文资料有时把澳大利亚列作洲，而用 **Oceania** 指更广的大洋洲区域。本课“大洋洲”包括澳大利亚、新西兰与太平洋众多岛屿。比较地图时，先问它采用了什么划分。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Why are there other answers?

Europe and Asia connect and may be described as Eurasia. Some teaching systems group the Americas together. English-language materials sometimes name Australia as the continent and use **Oceania** for the wider region. Here, Oceania includes Australia, New Zealand and many Pacific islands. Ask which convention a map uses before comparing counts.
<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 五个名字，一片相连的海洋

常见五大洋是太平洋、大西洋、印度洋、北冰洋和南大洋。南大洋围绕南极洲，教学中常用南纬 60° 附近描述它的北界；这是一种界定约定，不是海上的围墙。太平洋在某些地图的两边出现，因为把球面展开时需要切开一个地方。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Five names, one connected ocean

The five commonly named regions are the Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Arctic and Southern oceans. The Southern Ocean surrounds Antarctica; about 60°S is a commonly used northern boundary. It is a convention, not a wall. The Pacific can appear at both map edges because a globe must be cut somewhere to make a flat map.
<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：新西兰在大洋洲，不等于新西兰在澳大利亚国内。两端的太平洋是同一个大洋；这张等距圆柱投影图不能用于直接比较各洲真实面积。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** being in Oceania does not put New Zealand inside Australia. The Pacific at both edges is one ocean. This equirectangular map does not preserve continental areas for direct size comparisons.
<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

先选一个地名标签，再点地图编号位置；也可以把标签拖到位置上。为七大洲、五大洋各放好一个标签，最后找到新西兰。允许先借助线索，再重试。点“太平洋居中”，解释为什么地图边缘换了地方。
<!-- en -->

Select a place label, then click its numbered position, or drag the label onto that position. Place all seven continent-region and five ocean labels, then find New Zealand. Use clues and retry as needed. Centre the Pacific and explain why the map edges have moved.
<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

重点不是限时背名词，而是让孩子说出“在哪里”“旁边有什么”。问：“把地图拼回球面，左右边缘会怎样？”纸笔替代：看编号地图，写一份编号与洲洋的对应表，再用手指追踪新西兰附近的海水如何与其他海洋相连。
<!-- en -->

Focus on “where” and “what is nearby”, with no time limit. Ask what happens to the two edges when the map becomes a globe again. Paper alternative: write a numbered place key, then trace how water near New Zealand connects with other ocean regions.
