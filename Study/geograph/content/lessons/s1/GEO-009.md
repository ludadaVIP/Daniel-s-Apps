把一张地图上的山“压平”在纸上，还能分辨它与平原吗？先看A、B、C的形状，再猜：看颜色就足够了吗？
<!-- en -->

If a mountain is flattened onto a map, can we still distinguish it from a plain? Inspect A, B and C, then predict whether colour alone is enough.
<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 高度与形状是两条线索

山地峰谷起伏明显；平原宽广、起伏小；盆地的中心比周围低。沿地面切出一道剖面，能看出这些不同。请用手指沿A、B、C走一遍。

海拔回答“相对海平面多高”，相对高差回答“附近地面变化多大”。盆地底部可能仍很高；平坦高地也可能是高原。不要把“平”直接翻译成“低”。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Height and shape give different clues

Mountains have prominent peaks and valleys; plains are broad with little relief; basins have lower centres than their surroundings. A ground section reveals these differences. Trace A, B and C with a finger.

Elevation asks how high above a reference such as sea level; local relief asks how much nearby ground varies. A basin floor may still be high, and flat high land may be a plateau. Flat does not automatically mean low.
<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 这不是一张彩色照片

NASA/JPL西部青藏高原图把高程资料变成颜色，并用模拟光源产生阴影。紫色低地约200米，喜马拉雅部分白色高点超过7700米；这些颜色来自本图的高度编码，不是气温或植被。

在图中比较细碎的山脊与较平缓的高原内部。同样海拔高的地方，起伏程度仍可不同。资料包含2000年SRTM与GTOPO30，2004年发布；不是今天的地表照片。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / This is not a colour photograph

NASA/JPL’s western Tibet map encodes elevation as colour and adds simulated relief lighting. Purple lowlands are around 200 m; some white Himalayan heights exceed 7700 m. These colours encode height here, not temperature or vegetation.

Compare intricate ridges with the plateau’s gentler interior. High elevation does not guarantee equally rugged ground everywhere. The map combines SRTM from 2000 with GTOPO30 and was released in 2004; it is not a photograph taken today.
<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 回到真实地表

2024年的塔里木盆地影像里，观察较低的沙漠与周边山地；沙尘遮住了部分区域，不能假装每条边界都清楚可见。2007年的南岛影像里，比较中央山脉与东侧较宽平原。两图不在同一区域，也不在同一时刻。

真彩色影像会记录植被、岩石、云和光线。照片帮助看外观，高程图帮助看高度；互相核对，比“棕色就是山”更可靠。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Return to the actual surface

In the 2024 Tarim Basin image, compare the lower desert with surrounding mountains. Dust hides part of the scene; not every boundary is visible. In the 2007 South Island image, compare central mountains with the broader eastern plains. These are different places and dates.

True-colour imagery records vegetation, rocks, clouds and light. Photos show appearance; elevation maps show height. Cross-checking is stronger than assuming brown means mountain.
<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：海拔高不等于地面一定陡；盆地不一定有水，也不一定低于海平面。颜色必须依图例解释，不能把所有地图的绿色当成同一种信息。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** high elevation does not necessarily mean steep ground. A basin need not contain water or lie below sea level. Interpret colours through each map’s legend.
<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

为A、B、C三个剖面配上山地、平原、盆地标签。每次选之前，指一条形状证据。再选择真实照片应怎样核对地形；完成后用一句话区别海拔与相对高差。
<!-- en -->

Match sections A, B and C to mountains, plain and basin. Before each choice, point to a shape clue. Choose how to check terrain in real photos, then distinguish elevation from local relief in a sentence.
<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

追问：“盆地底部比周围低，能说明它一定低于海平面吗？”纸笔替代：画三个剖面，在各图下画同一海平面基准，再把整条平坦地面抬高，讨论它与高原的关系。无需外出或收集位置。
<!-- en -->

Ask: “If a basin is lower than its surroundings, must it be below sea level?” Paper alternative: draw three profiles above the same sea-level reference. Raise a flat surface and discuss its relation to a plateau. No trip or location collection is needed.
