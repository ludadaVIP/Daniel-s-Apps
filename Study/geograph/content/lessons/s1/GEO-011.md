<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 向低处，不是向屏幕下方

重力让河水沿地势总体向较低处流。源头与支流汇合，再经过弯道到河口；读海拔还必须检查河道连接。两个点中较低的一个，也可能隔着山脊，水不能任意横穿。

本图主河总体向东北流。虚构模型用几个高程点简化连续河床，只表达连接与方向，不预测速度、洪水或真实路线。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Downhill is not screen-down

Gravity drives river water generally towards lower ground. Sources and tributaries join, then reach the mouth through bends. Read heights and inspect channel connections. A lower point may lie beyond a ridge; water cannot cross arbitrarily.

The main river here runs generally northeast. This fictional model simplifies a continuous bed into a few heights, showing connections and direction without predicting speed, floods or real routes.
<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 真正的河网像一棵树吗

NASA图中的细支流汇入较大的河道，最后进入密西西比河出口。深蓝突出主河，其他线表示河网；颜色不是水深，也不是州界。沿主河向南追到墨西哥湾，比较它与支流的关系。

这是2016年发布的可视化，河网来自USGS HYDRO1K、背景来自Blue Marble资料。它表达汇水路径，不是当天拍下的蓝色河流照片；线宽与颜色也不能当作流速。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Does a real river network resemble a tree

NASA’s map shows smaller tributaries joining larger channels and eventually the Mississippi outlet. Dark blue highlights the main river; other lines trace the network. Colours do not mean depth or state borders. Trace the main river south to the Gulf of Mexico and compare tributary connections.

Released in 2016, this visualization combines USGS HYDRO1K drainage with Blue Marble imagery. It represents drainage paths, not blue rivers photographed that day; line widths and colours do not measure flow speed.
<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 河道以外还有整个汇水区

流域是向共同出口汇水的土地，分水岭把相邻流域分开。模型里的较高山脊，把主河与东侧内流湖分开。美国Harney盆地也有流入湖泊、却没有地表出海出口的河流。

不是所有降雨都立即变成河水：一部分会蒸发、被植物使用或渗入地下。今天只追踪地表通道，不能由此判断每一滴水的最终去向。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Land matters beyond the channel

A catchment is land draining to a shared outlet, separated from neighbouring drainage by divides. The model’s higher ridge separates the main river from an eastern closed lake. The US Harney Basin also contains rivers feeding lakes without surface outlets to the sea.

Not all rain immediately becomes river flow: some evaporates, is used by plants or infiltrates. We trace surface channels here, without determining every drop’s ultimate destination.
<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：“顺流而下”的下游不是地图的下边，也不是一定在南方。真实河流可流向任何罗盘方向；局部潮汐、回水与人造设施会使情况更复杂。本课只研究总体自然排水连接。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** downstream is not map-bottom or necessarily south. Rivers can flow in any compass direction. Tides, backwater and structures complicate local flow; this lesson examines broad natural drainage connections.
<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

从已标记的400米源头开始，每次选择相连且较低的下游点，走到河口。试一次隔着山脊的内流湖，读反馈。再辨认分水岭，并选择内流湖能给“所有河流通海”提出什么限制。
<!-- en -->

Start at the marked 400 m source. Choose a connected lower downstream neighbour each time until reaching the mouth. Try the closed lake across the divide once and read the feedback. Identify the divide and the limit this lake places on “all rivers reach the sea”.
<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

问：“你选择这里，是因为它在屏幕下面，还是因为河道相连且海拔低？”纸笔替代：画山脊与两侧河道，用箭头标出向各自出口汇水的坡面；转动纸张，检查流向解释是否仍成立。用纸箭头即可，不需真正倒水或靠近河岸。
<!-- en -->

Ask: “Did you choose this point because it is lower on screen, or because it is connected and lower in elevation?” Paper alternative: draw a ridge and channels on both sides, adding arrows from slopes to their outlets. Rotate the paper and check your explanation. Paper arrows suffice; no pouring water or visiting riverbanks is needed.
