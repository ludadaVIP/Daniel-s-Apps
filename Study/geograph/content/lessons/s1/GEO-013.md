<!-- section:hook -->

从上面看，街区像棋盘，农田也像棋盘。如果都由矩形组成，怎样判断它们在做什么？先比较建筑密度、水道与田块边缘，不只看颜色。
<!-- en -->

From above, street blocks and fields can both resemble chessboards. If both use rectangles, how can we infer their use? Compare building density, waterways and plot boundaries rather than colour alone.
<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 看得见的覆盖

纽约与新泽西的2022年斜视照片有密集建筑、道路与水道，也有公园和湿地。照片能帮助提出“哪里是建成地”的问题，却不能为每栋楼指定用途。南方在照片上方；别把图片顶端当北。

土地覆盖描述外观，土地利用描述人们怎样使用土地。两者有关，却不是同一个词。相同屋顶外观可能对应住宅、办公室或仓库，需要其他资料核查。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Visible surface cover

The 2022 oblique New York/New Jersey photo contains dense structures, roads and waterways, with parks and wetlands too. It helps ask where built land lies, without assigning every building a use. South is at the top; photo-top is not north.

Land cover describes appearance; land use describes how people use ground. They are related but distinct. Similar roofs may serve housing, offices or storage, needing additional evidence.
<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 边缘不是一条简单分界线

2009年的凤凰城都市区影像中，蓝灰色建成地与绿色、棕色矩形田块交错。这里采用城市边缘案例，比较的是一种混合格局，不是凭照片给城市画精确行政边界。

城市、郊区和乡村通过交通、工作、食物和水相互联系。城市里可有农田，乡村里也可有商业与工业活动。不能据此把每个人的生活方式归为一种。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / An edge is not one simple boundary

In the 2009 Phoenix metropolitan image, blue-grey built land mixes with green/brown rectangular fields. This urban-edge case illustrates a mixed pattern, not a precise administrative boundary inferred from imagery.

Cities, suburbs and countryside connect through transport, work, food and water. Cities can contain farming and rural areas can contain commerce or industry. An area’s pattern does not prescribe every resident’s life.
<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 规则田块也有复杂故事

2015年的坎特伯雷平原影像里，矩形农田旁有林地与Waimakariri河的分汊河道。田块形状和组织方式支持农业推测；不能仅用颜色读出作物种类、收成或农民收入。

三幅图来自不同地区、日期与视角，并非同一比例的面积比较。更好的结论会同时说“我观察到什么”“我推测什么”“还需要什么证据”。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Regular fields, complex stories

The 2015 Canterbury Plains image places rectangular fields beside forest and braided Waimakariri channels. Field shapes and organization support an agricultural inference; colour alone cannot establish crops, yield or farmers’ income.

The three images differ in region, date and viewpoint; they are not equal-scale area comparisons. Stronger conclusions state an observation, an inference and the further evidence needed.
<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：绿色不一定是天然原始森林，城市也不只由灰色水泥组成。影像是一个时点，不能独自讲完人们的生活。没有一种城乡生活方式天然优于另一种。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** green does not necessarily mean original natural forest, and cities contain more than grey concrete. An image captures a moment, not complete lives. Neither rural nor urban life is inherently superior.
<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

分别查看海湾城市、城市边缘、农田与河流三幅影像。为每幅选择最受图像支持的描述，再判断影像对人们生活的证据限制。每次回答前，指一条具体形状线索。
<!-- en -->

Inspect harbour city, urban edge, and fields/river. Choose a description supported by each image, then identify a limit to claims about people’s lives. Point to a specific shape clue before answering.
<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

问：“这是你看见的，还是你根据外观推测的？”纸笔替代：画三幅鸟瞰小图，加入道路、屋顶、田块与绿地；让孩子为每幅写一条观察和一条还不知道的事。无需询问陌生人、拍摄个人或记录家庭地址。
<!-- en -->

Ask: “Is that directly observed, or inferred from appearance?” Paper alternative: draw three overhead sketches with roads, roofs, fields and green spaces. Write one observation and one unknown for each. No interviews, personal photos or home address are needed.
