青藏高原比一些更靠北的低地还冷，地图是不是画错了？先读图例、年份和温度类型，再找三个纬度不同的样点，最后检查一组相近纬度的高原与低地。
<!-- en -->

The Tibetan Plateau is colder than some lowlands farther north. Is the map wrong? Read the legend, years and temperature type; compare three latitudes, then a plateau/lowland pair at similar latitude.
<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 先问这张图在平均什么

这张世界地图表示1991–2020年近地面空气温度年平均，单位°C。资料来自NOAA的NCEP/NCAR再分析1：历史观测与模型结合，不是每个格子放了一支温度计。Study把十二个月长期均值按月份日数加权，再由开尔文转为摄氏度。

点击热带、温带、格陵兰样点，读位置和数值。蓝色较冷、红色较暖；地图高纬度面积被矩形投影放大，不能按屏幕面积直接求全球均值。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Ask what the map averages

This world map shows 1991–2020 annual mean near-surface air temperature in °C. NOAA’s NCEP/NCAR Reanalysis 1 combines historical observations and a model; it does not place a thermometer in every cell. Study weights twelve monthly long-term means by days and converts kelvin to Celsius.

Select tropical, temperate and Greenland cells and read coordinates and values. Blue is colder, red warmer. The rectangular projection enlarges high-latitude areas, so screen area cannot directly supply a global average.
<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 同一束光，铺开面积不同

低角度阳光摊在较大地面，高角度较集中。这有助于解释低纬度与高纬度多年能量背景的差异。图中相同宽度光束，以90°与30°入射到平面，后一束铺开的地面长度是前者两倍。

这只控制角度，并没有计算温度；真实世界还要考虑昼长、云、大气、海洋输送和地面反射。极地夏季也可以有长白昼，不能把图理解为“高纬度永远没太阳”。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / The same beam covers different ground

Low-angle sunlight spreads over more ground; high-angle sunlight is more concentrated. This helps explain different long-term energy backgrounds across latitudes. Equal-width beams at 90° and 30° hit a plane; the second spans twice the ground length.

This controls angle without calculating temperature. Day length, clouds, atmosphere, ocean transport and reflection also matter. Polar summers can have long daylight; high latitude does not mean permanently no Sun.
<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 找一个例外，改进解释

两个样点都在约31.4°N：青藏高原网格约−7.6°C，相近纬度低地约15.3°C。高海拔是较冷高原的重要线索，说明纬度不是唯一因素；这组比较也没有控制海洋、环流等所有条件，不能把全部温差都归给海拔。

原始网格约1.9°，只适合区域教学比较，不能给山路或具体城市下精确结论。年平均和多年平均都会平滑季节与日变化；今天是否需要外套，仍要读当天的天气。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Use an exception to improve the explanation

Both cells are near 31.4°N: the Tibetan Plateau cell averages about −7.6°C and the comparison lowland about 15.3°C. Elevation is an important clue to the colder plateau, showing that latitude is not the only factor. This pair does not control oceans, circulation and every other condition, so elevation cannot be assigned the entire difference.

The original grid is about 1.9°, suitable for regional teaching rather than precise city or trail conclusions. Annual and long-term averages smooth seasonal and daily changes; today’s jacket still requires today’s weather.
<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：这张图是空气温度，不是卫星测得地表表皮温度，也不是相对某基准的“温度异常”。“低纬通常较暖”是趋势，不能排除高山例外或某日天气变化。图像颜色显示数据，不造成气候。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** this map shows air temperature, not satellite surface skin temperature or an anomaly relative to a baseline. Warmer low latitudes is a trend compatible with mountain exceptions and daily weather changes. Colours display data; they do not cause climate.
<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

逐个点击五个样点，读它们的坐标、温度与统计时段。先比较前三个纬度样点，再比较相近纬度的高原与低地。完成三个证据判断：大趋势、海拔例外、气候与今日天气的区别。
<!-- en -->

Select all five cells and read their coordinates, temperature and averaging period. Compare the first three latitudes, then the similar-latitude plateau and lowland. Make three evidence judgments: broad trend, elevation exception, and climate versus today’s weather.
<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

问：“你的解释能同时说明大趋势和这个例外吗？这幅图还有哪些信息没给你？”纸笔替代：写五张卡片，注明坐标、均温和1991–2020。先按纬度排序，再把两张31.4°N卡片并排；另画同样光束不同角度的地面足迹。无需联网收集天气或个人位置。
<!-- en -->

Ask: “Can your explanation cover both the broad trend and the exception? What information is missing?” Paper alternative: make five cards with coordinates, mean temperature and 1991–2020. Order by latitude, then put the two 31.4°N cards together. Draw footprints for equal beams at different angles. No online weather collection or personal location is needed.
