<!-- en -->

Imagine a call with a friend in London: after dinner in Auckland, around breakfast in London. Neither clock is broken. One instant can carry different local labels. Try a different date: does the clock gap stay the same all year?
<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 同一颗地球，不同光照时刻

太阳照亮地球的一侧；自转把同一地点带进光照、再带到背光侧。从北极上方看，自转为逆时针。把观察点转半圈：太阳不必绕它走一圈，就能出现昼夜变化。

这个赤道剖面是理想示意，暂时隔离地轴倾斜。图上白昼与黑夜各半，不代表每座城市全年每天都各12小时。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / One Earth, different lighting moments

The Sun illuminates one side of Earth. Rotation carries a place into light and then darkness; seen above the North Pole, the spin is counterclockwise. Move the observer halfway round: day can become night without the Sun circling that point.

This ideal equatorial section temporarily isolates tilt. Equal light and dark halves do not imply 12-hour days for every city throughout the year.
<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 太阳时解释趋势，时区规定钟表

地球约一天转360°，平均每小时约15°。不同经度的太阳时因此不同；向东的地点较早到达当地太阳正午。不过国家和地区不会把钟表边界机械画成整齐经线，有些偏移不是整小时。

UTC是比较钟表的共同参考。“UTC+9”表示当地钟表在相同时刻比UTC快9小时。只知道经度，不足以确定真实城市的当地钟表时间。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Solar time explains a pattern; civil time sets clocks

Earth turns about 360° per day, averaging roughly 15° an hour. Solar time differs with longitude, with eastern places reaching their solar noon earlier. Jurisdictions do not draw clock boundaries mechanically along neat meridians, and some offsets are not whole hours.

UTC is a shared clock reference. UTC+9 means a local clock is nine hours ahead of UTC at the same instant. Longitude alone cannot determine a real city’s clock time.
<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 先问哪天，再问几点

本课比较2026年1月15日与7月15日。奥克兰在这两天分别采用UTC+13、UTC+12；伦敦分别采用UTC+0、UTC+1。两地差距由13小时变为11小时，因为两国夏令时安排不同。

拨UTC滑块，留意当地日期：UTC加上偏移超过24小时，就进入下一天。改钟不会增加一天的阳光，只改变它在钟面上的时间标签。下一次约活动，你会先核对哪些信息？
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Ask which date before which hour

Compare 15 January and 15 July 2026. Auckland uses UTC+13 and UTC+12 respectively; London uses UTC+0 and UTC+1. Their clock gap changes from 13 to 11 hours because their daylight-saving arrangements differ.

Move the UTC slider and watch local dates: adding an offset beyond 24 hours enters the next day. A clock adjustment adds no sunlight; it changes sunlight’s clock label. What information would you check before your next international event?
<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：时区、日期变更线与经线不是同一种线；东、西两地也不一定使用同一日期。夏令时不是全球统一规定，更不能把两座城市的时差记成永久常数。这里只给明确日期的学习案例，不显示“实时世界时钟”。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** time-zone boundaries, the date line and meridians are different kinds of lines. Two places can have different dates. Daylight saving is not universal, and a city pair’s gap is not a permanent constant. These are dated learning examples, not a live world clock.
<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

用UTC小时滑块安排一次虚构通话，让奥克兰在19:00–21:00、伦敦在07:00–09:00。一月与七月各记录一次，再说出两地UTC偏移的变化。观察另外两座城市的日期，说明为什么安排全球活动只写“晚上8点”不够。
<!-- en -->

Choose a UTC hour for a fictional call with Auckland at 19:00–21:00 and London at 07:00–09:00. Record one January and one July example, then explain the changing offsets. Inspect the other cities’ dates and explain why “8 pm” is insufficient for a global event.
<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

不要先教孩子死记城市时差。请他指两张时钟和日期，说明同一UTC时刻。纸笔替代：用本课静态对照，将UTC07:00分别加13、0、12、1小时；比较一月与七月。再试UTC18:00，练习跨日。无需联系任何人，避免填写真实行程。
<!-- en -->

Avoid memorising city gaps first. Ask the child to point to two clocks and dates representing one UTC instant. Paper alternative: use the static table and add 13, 0, 12 and 1 hours to UTC07:00, comparing January with July. Then try UTC18:00 to practise date rollover. No contact or real itinerary is needed.

<!-- zh -->

改钟改变时间标签，自转改变一地昼夜。可是为什么冬天常常白昼更短，南北半球季节还相反？下一课固定一种变量、改变另一种，检验四季的原因。
<!-- en -->

Clock rules change labels; rotation changes a place’s day and night. Why is winter daylight often shorter, with opposite seasons in the two hemispheres? Next, hold one variable fixed and change another to test the explanation.
