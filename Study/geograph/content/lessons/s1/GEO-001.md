<!-- section:hook -->
<!-- zh -->

这是阿波罗 17 号宇航员在 **1972 年 12 月 7 日**拍到的地球。先当一位太空侦探：找一处蓝色、一处棕色，再找一团白色。它们可能是什么？同样是白色，会不会有不同的解释？
<!-- en -->

Apollo 17 astronauts photographed Earth on **7 December 1972**. Be a space detective: find something blue, something brown and something white. What could each be? Could two white areas have different explanations?
<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 蓝色不是一层涂料

照片里的大片深蓝区域是海洋，非洲大陆的棕色和绿色露在海面上。全球测量告诉我们：海洋约占地球**表面积的 71%**。这不是说地球内部有 71% 是水，也不能只数这张照片里的蓝色像素来得到全球比例。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Blue is not a coat of paint

The broad dark-blue regions are ocean, while brown and green areas reveal Africa. Global measurements put ocean coverage at about **71% of Earth’s surface**. This is not the percentage of water inside Earth, and counting blue pixels in this one image cannot establish global coverage.
<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 同样白色，不同线索

卷曲、延伸的白带常常是云。云由微小水滴或冰晶组成，能遮住海洋和陆地。照片下方还有南极的冰。先看位置和形状，再作解释；只有颜色，证据还不够。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / One colour, different clues

Swirling white bands often indicate clouds. Tiny droplets or ice crystals form clouds, which can hide both ocean and land. Antarctic ice also appears at the bottom. Use location and shape as well as colour before explaining what you see.
<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 观察与推测要分开

“这里是一条弯曲的白带”是观察；“它可能是云”是解释。照片有用，但只显示一个视角和一个时刻。背面的新西兰并没有消失，我们只是看不到它。比较别的影像、地图和测量，才能更全面地了解地球。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Separate observing from explaining

“I see a curved white band” is an observation. “It may be cloud” is an explanation. A photograph records one view at one moment. New Zealand has not disappeared on the far side; it is simply outside our view. Other images, maps and measurements extend the evidence.
<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：白色不一定是雪，蓝色也不能单凭颜色就认定为海洋。“71%”指全球地表面积，不是这一面的照片面积。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** white need not be snow, and blue alone does not establish ocean. The 71% figure describes global surface area, rather than the area in this one view.
<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

在真实照片上找到 **海洋、陆地、云** 三种线索，再观察“100 格地球”模型。说出一种颜色判断可能出错的情况。热点给的是观察样本，不代表全部地表分类。
<!-- en -->

Locate **ocean, land and cloud** in the real photograph, then inspect the 100-square Earth model. Describe one situation where colour alone might mislead you. The hotspots are observation samples, not a complete surface classification.
<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

先问“你看到了什么形状？”再问“你用什么证据判断？”不要急着纠正猜想。让孩子指出云下的地表看不清，并用 100 格图说明“表面”与“内部”不同。纸笔替代：画 10×10 方格，将 71 格标为海洋、29 格标为陆地，注明这是比例示意，不是世界地图。
<!-- en -->

Ask “What shapes do you see?” before “What evidence supports your idea?” Give guesses time. Invite the child to identify hidden ground and distinguish surface coverage from Earth’s interior. Paper alternative: draw a 10×10 grid with 71 ocean squares and 29 land squares, labelled as a proportion model rather than a world map.
<!-- section:door -->
<!-- zh -->

下次把地球展开成一张地图：海洋明明连在一起，为什么还要取五个名字？你能找到照片背面的新西兰吗？
<!-- en -->

Next, unfold Earth into a map. If the ocean is connected, why give its regions five names? Can you find New Zealand beyond this photograph’s view?
