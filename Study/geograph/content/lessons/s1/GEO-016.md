<!-- section:hook -->
<!-- zh -->

这张世界底图还没有你的发现。怎样让一个没上过课的朋友读懂它？先决定地图介绍什么，再选大致区域与图例，让图像和解释互相支持。
<!-- en -->

This world base has none of your discoveries yet. How could a friend who has not taken the lessons understand it? Decide its purpose, choose a coarse region and legend, and make the map support the explanation.
<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 第一回合：地图有一个目的

用七洲、五洋的常用约定加入标签，核对它们与真实陆海轮廓的关系。可切换大西洋视角和太平洋居中，观察同一地球怎样被放到纸上。标签点不是完整洲界。

选择你所在或愿意介绍的大致区域，找到北箭头、赤道与图例。这个项目不用精确家庭地址。换配色后重新核对图例；颜色不是自动变成温度或海拔数据。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Session one: give the map a purpose

Add labels using the familiar seven-continent/five-ocean convention and check them against real land/ocean outlines. Switch Atlantic and Pacific-centred views of the same world. Label points are not complete regional borders.

Choose a broad region where you live or wish to introduce. Find north, equator and legend without a precise address. Check the legend after recolouring; colours do not automatically become temperature or elevation data.
<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 第二回合：三条发现都带证据

每条先写“我发现了”，再补“我从这幅图／这个操作观察到”。例如缩放后实地距离不变、同样升高的坡面距离不同，或气候均值不是当天温度。选择对应课程，报告会保留资料来源。

可以先用熟悉语言记录，另一语言口头介绍也可以。家长听的是你的推理，不是考翻译长句。机器只记录清单，不自动判断自由解释正确；错误与疑问值得保留和讨论。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Session two: three discoveries with evidence

For each, write a discovery and an observation from a diagram or interaction: unchanged ground distance after zoom, different runs for equal rises, or climate means versus daily weather. Select the evidence lesson so exports retain its sources.

Write first in your comfortable language; use an oral introduction for the other. A parent listens for reasoning rather than long-sentence translation. The checklist records process without automatically judging free explanations; questions and mistakes remain useful.
<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 让家人能打开与核查

指图用中文与英文各介绍至少一洲、一洋，并讲一条发现，可以使用短句。由家庭勾选介绍记录，再导出地图集报告、矢量地图或文字稿。HTML报告自带地图，可离线打开并打印；草稿也能导出。

这是教学作品，不是全球精确测距图。矩形投影改变形状和面积，标记是大致区域。把这些限制写在作品里，会帮助朋友知道它能回答什么问题。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Make it openable and checkable

Point to at least one continent and ocean, then explain a discovery in Chinese and English; short sentences suffice. Family records the introduction, then exports an atlas report, SVG map or notes. HTML embeds the map for offline viewing/printing; drafts can also be exported.

This teaching atlas is not a precise global distance map. Rectangular projection distorts shape/area and the marker is coarse. Stating these limits helps a friend know what it can answer.
<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：勾选“介绍过”不是机构认证，也不是机器证明你的解释正确。地图美观与解释证据不是同一件事；清楚的图例、来源和限制，比增加装饰更能帮助家人读懂。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** ticking an introduction is not certification or machine proof of correctness. Visual appeal and evidential explanation differ; clear legends, sources and limits help family read the work.
<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

可分两次各30–45分钟。先命名地图、逐个核对七洲五洋、选择大致区域、核对图例与方向。再写三条发现及证据，选来源课程，向家人作中文和英文短介绍。完成清单后仍可修改；导出离线报告作为第一阶段作品。
<!-- en -->

Work in two sessions of 30–45 minutes. Name the atlas, check each continent/ocean, choose a coarse region and verify legend/direction. Add three discoveries with evidence and source lessons, then give short Chinese and English family introductions. Keep editing after the checklist; export the offline report as your first-stage work.
<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

只追问一条：“这条发现的证据能在图里指出来吗？”确认双语介绍由孩子完成，可帮记短句，不代写推理。纸笔替代：在世界底图上写洲洋、北箭头、图例与大致区域，贴三张证据卡，作双语介绍。默认不收集照片、家庭地址、姓名或GPS。
<!-- en -->

Ask one focused question: “Can you point to the evidence for this discovery?” Family checks that the child gives the bilingual introduction; help record short phrases without writing the reasoning for them. Paper alternative: annotate a world base with continents/oceans, north, legend and a coarse region, attach three evidence cards, then introduce it bilingually. No photo, home address, name or GPS is collected by default.
