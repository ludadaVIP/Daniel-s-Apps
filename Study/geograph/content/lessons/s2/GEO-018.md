南美洲东岸与非洲西岸像能拼合的碎片。仅靠轮廓够吗？先把两片拉近，寻找两组化石标记，再对照真实板块图，看边界是否沿海岸走。
<!-- en -->

Eastern South America and western Africa resemble puzzle pieces. Are outlines sufficient? Move fragments closer, compare two fossil symbols, then inspect whether real plate boundaries follow coasts.

<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 岩石圈拼图，比大陆更大

GEO-017中的岩石圈分成许多板块。它们相对移动，大陆作为其中的一部分随之改变位置。海水不是拖着大陆走的传送带。

USGS全球图标出主要板块及边界；有些边界在海洋中，有些经过大陆。点名新西兰附近的太平洋板块与澳大利亚板块。线条是简化概括，真实边界可能是较宽的变形区；此图没有注明数据时段，不能当实时测量。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / A puzzle larger than continents

The lithosphere in GEO-017 is divided into plates. Their relative movement changes continental positions; seawater is not the conveyor carrying continents.

The USGS map shows major plates/boundaries through both oceans and continents. Find the Pacific and Australian plates near New Zealand. Lines simplify potentially broad deformation zones. The map gives no data period and is not a live measurement.

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 重建过去，要多条线索

大陆轮廓只是线索之一。同类型、相近地质年龄的化石与相应岩层，也可支持过去相连的解释。大陆曾组成盘古大陆，后来分散，但古海岸并不与现代海岸完全一致。

我们只用南美洲与非洲的概略碎片演示关系：三角符号代表相应化石线索，不是实际采样点；滑块只改间距，不代表年代或真实漂移路线。说出两条证据，再说一条这个模型没有告诉你的事。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Reconstruct with several clues

Outlines are one clue. Corresponding fossils of similar geological age and matching rock formations support past connections. Continents were assembled in Pangaea and later dispersed; ancient coasts differ from modern ones.

Our South America/Africa fragments illustrate a relationship only. Triangles are conceptual fossil clues, not sample sites; the slider changes a gap, not age or measured paths. Give two clues and one unknown.

<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 看相对运动，不只背名字

分离时两块彼此远离；汇聚时彼此靠近，可能发生俯冲或碰撞；错动时沿边界滑过。按帧0→4观察箭头与间距，比较哪个方向改变。

地球内部热量、地幔流动、俯冲板片下沉等共同参与运动，不是每块都由一条简单环形“传送带”推动。课堂帧没有时间或速度刻度，不能从点按钮的次数算出板块一年走多远。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Describe relative motion

Divergence separates neighbours; convergence brings them together, with subduction or collision possible; transform motion passes along the boundary. Compare arrows and spacing from frames 0→4.

Internal heat, mantle flow and sinking slabs participate in motion, not one universal conveyor for every plate. Frames have no time/speed calibration, so button counts cannot give annual movement.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：板块不是大陆，海岸不是板块边界。拼图相像不等于完整证明；模型中的同一帧号也不等于真实运动同样快。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** plates are not continents and coasts are not boundaries. A shape fit is not complete proof, and equal frame numbers do not mean equal real speeds.
