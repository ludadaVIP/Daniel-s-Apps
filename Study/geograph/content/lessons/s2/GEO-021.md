<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 先定位，后解释

南阿尔卑斯山脉沿南岛延伸。SRTM高程图以绿色表示较低地面、白色表示较高地面，不能把绿色直接读成森林。概略地图给出山脉位置，照片则显示表面外观；它们都没有拍到地下结构。

北岛鲁阿佩胡的火山堆积和南岛的主要造山机制不同。“高山”描述外形，不是统一的成因。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Locate before explaining

The Southern Alps extend along South Island. SRTM relief colours run from green lower ground to white higher ground; green does not directly mean forest. Our overview locates the range, while a photograph shows surface appearance. Neither photographs the interior.

Ruapehu’s volcanic accumulation differs from South Island’s main mountain-building mechanism. “High mountain” describes a form, not one universal origin.

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 材料没有凭空增加

太平洋板块与澳大利亚板块在南岛相对运动。阿尔卑斯断层附近既有沿边界错动，也有挤压分量。岩层可以褶皱、断裂和缩短；地壳增厚与抬升可构建山脉，深部也发生变化。

操作矩形类比：同一截面积，宽度缩短就需厚度增加。真实地壳不是均匀矩形，山顶也不是平的；模型只让你看“同量材料怎样重新分配”，不需要加入熔岩，也不能把帧数换成年数。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Material does not appear from nowhere

The Pacific and Australian plates move relative to one another across South Island. Around the Alpine Fault this includes along-boundary slip and a compressional component. Rocks may fold, fault and shorten; crustal thickening and uplift build a range, with changes at depth too.

Operate the rectangular analogy: with constant sectional area, shortening requires thickening. Real crust is not uniform and summits are not flat. Our model isolates redistribution, requires no added lava and cannot convert frames into years.

<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 向上抬，与向外搬

河流、冰川和重力也在移走山地物质，碎屑可到低地、平原或海底。岩石抬升量不能直接当作山顶长高量；山形取决于内力与外力的共同作用。

固定虚构抬升为每段3单位，比较移除1、3、5。相对初值，山顶分别升高、大致不变、降低。真实地形的侵蚀会反馈到抬升，降雨、岩性、冰川和时间尺度也不同；这里的数值不是南阿尔卑斯山的米／年。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Upward motion, outward removal

Rivers, glaciers and gravity remove mountain material, which can reach lowlands, plains or the seafloor. Rock uplift is not identical to summit growth: internal and surface processes act together.

Fix fictional uplift at 3 units per interval and compare removal of 1, 3 and 5. Relative to the starting height, the summit rises, stays similar or lowers. Real erosion feeds back on uplift, with varying rainfall, geology, glaciers and timescales. These numbers are not Southern Alps metres/year.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：“会抬升”不代表“只抬升”；“有断层”不代表全部运动只有错动；“高山”不代表全部是火山。不要拿示意厚度、四帧或2007照片算今天的高度变化。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** uplift can coexist with removal; a fault need not have only sideways motion; high mountains need not be volcanoes. Do not calculate current height changes from exaggerated thickness, four frames or a 2007 photograph.

定位南阿尔卑斯山，观察挤压帧0和4，用模型解释已有材料如何增厚。比较错动与挤压分量，再分别把三个高度收支情景看至帧4，判断山顶变化；每个判断说一条图中证据。
<!-- en -->

Locate the Southern Alps, view compression frames 0 and 4, and explain how existing material thickens. Compare slip/compression components, then view all three height-budget cases through frame 4. Give one diagram clue for each conclusion.

<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

问：“山顶没变高，能说明岩石没有抬升吗？”鼓励孩子用初值虚线比较。纸笔替代：画一块同面积宽薄矩形和窄厚矩形，再用+3、−1／3／5箭头解释。可选深入：查一份带测量方法与时段的抬升研究，区别岩石抬升、地表抬升与剥露；不要用纸折叠力气估算真实压力。
<!-- en -->

Ask: “If the summit stays the same height, does that prove no rock uplift?” Use the dashed initial outline. Paper alternative: draw wide/thin and narrow/thick equal-area rectangles, then +3 and −1/3/5 arrows. Optional investigation: read a dated uplift study with methods, distinguishing rock uplift, surface uplift and exhumation. Paper-folding force cannot estimate real stress.
