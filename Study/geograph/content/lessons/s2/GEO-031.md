### 01 / 同一边界，三种相对关系

新西兰跨澳大利亚板块与太平洋板块。北岛东侧Hikurangi一带，太平洋板块俯冲到澳大利亚板块之下。南岛Alpine Fault附近以斜向相对运动、侧向错动并伴挤压抬升为特征。西南Puysegur一带，俯冲关系反过来：澳大利亚板块进入太平洋板块下方。

图中三条虚线与山带为Study概略教学叠层，不是测绘断层坐标；一条线也不能画出整个宽广、三维的变形区。编号帮助讨论区域，不标震源位置。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / One boundary, three relative relationships

New Zealand spans the Australian and Pacific plates. At Hikurangi east of the North Island, the Pacific plate subducts beneath the Australian plate. Near the South Island Alpine Fault, oblique motion combines lateral displacement with compression/uplift. At Puysegur in the southwest, the Australian plate subducts beneath the Pacific plate.

Study’s dashed lines and mountain belt are approximate teaching overlays, not surveyed fault coordinates. A line cannot represent a broad three-dimensional deformation zone. Numbered markers identify discussion regions, not earthquake origins.

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 山脉记录累积，地震记录事件

南阿尔卑斯山的形成与板块挤压、抬升有关；河流、冰川、风化和侵蚀又移除材料。地形是这些作用长期共同塑造的结果，不是一场地震瞬间造出整条山脉。地震记录断层滑动释放能量的事件，不等于所有板块运动都以大地震出现。

开关山带与边界，看看空间联系，再与真实地形图对照。山带位置和地震点可以提供不同线索；因果机制要结合地质研究，不能只凭“点离线近”断言全部关系。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Mountains record accumulation; quakes record events

Plate compression/uplift contributes to the Southern Alps, while rivers, glaciers, weathering and erosion remove material. Relief results from their long-term interaction, not one quake instantly creating the whole range. Quakes record events releasing energy during fault slip; not all plate motion appears as large earthquakes.

Toggle the mountain/boundary layers and compare real relief. Mountains and quake points give different clues; geological research supplies mechanisms. Proximity alone cannot prove every causal relationship.

### 03 / 历史目录也有选择条件

本图来自GeoNet：2020年UTC、164°E–180°E／48°S–33°S范围、震级至少4、目录类型为earthquake，共190条。原查询191条，其中一条被目录标为其他类型，按上述条件排除。圆形为深度小于40km、方形40至小于100km、菱形100km及以上；这些是教学分组，不是危险等级。

点按震源深度分形状，但平面位置只画经纬度，没有显示震源三维轨迹。阈值外、范围外、其他年份、小震和未记录事件不在此图；不能用一年样本预测下一场地震，空白也不能证明零风险。可展开文字表核对日期、位置、震级、深度与事件ID。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / A historical catalogue has selection conditions

GeoNet supplies 190 earthquake-type events in 2020 UTC, between 164°E–180°E and 48°S–33°S, magnitude at least 4. The query returned 191 records; one had a different catalogue type and was excluded. Circles are depth below 40km, squares 40 to below 100km, diamonds 100km or deeper. These teaching bands are not danger levels.

Shapes encode origin depth; map points show longitude/latitude without three-dimensional source paths. Other years, smaller/outside/unrecorded events are absent. One year cannot predict the next quake; blank areas do not prove zero risk. Expand the table to inspect UTC time, location, magnitude, depth and event ID.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：新西兰边界不是处处同一种俯冲方向，山脉不是一次震动的产物。190点是有条件的历史样本，既不是所有地震，也不是危险性排序图。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** subduction direction is not identical everywhere in New Zealand; a range is not the product of one shake. The 190 points are a selected historical sample, not every quake or a hazard ranking.
