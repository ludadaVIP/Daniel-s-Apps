<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 水有许多去路

地表雨水可以形成径流，进入河流；也可以下渗，成为土壤水。根吸水后，植物蒸腾把水送入空气；土壤水也可直接蒸发。一部分下渗水继续补给地下水，地下水可经孔隙或裂隙运动、排入河流。地下水不等同一条埋在地下的大河。

追踪图的箭头表示允许的连接，不代表所有水都沿同一条线路，也不表示速度或停留时间。它省略雪、冰、湖泊等储存与路径；我们选三条路线帮助发现分支。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Water has alternative paths

Surface rainwater can run into rivers or infiltrate into soil. Roots take up soil water; transpiration returns water to air, and soil water can evaporate directly. Some infiltrated water recharges groundwater, which can move through pores/fractures and discharge into streams. Groundwater is not simply a large buried river.

Arrows show possible connections, not a compulsory itinerary or travel times. Snow, ice, lakes and other stores/paths are omitted. Our three selected journeys reveal branches.

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 看得见的云，不是水汽本身

蒸发使液态水变成气态水汽；水汽本身看不见。凝结使水汽变成小液滴，云由悬浮小液滴和／或冰晶组成。雨、雪等降水把水带向地表。本课只画一种云滴到雨的简化连接，不展开冰晶形成的全部过程。

太阳能提供蒸发的重要能量，重力促使降水落下、推动地表水向低处运动。水循环没有唯一的起点，也没有必须逐站完成的圆圈；水能在一些储存处停留很久。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Visible clouds are not vapour itself

Evaporation changes liquid water into invisible water vapour. Condensation makes small liquid drops; clouds contain suspended droplets and/or ice crystals. Precipitation, including rain and snow, returns water to the surface. Our cloud-to-rain connection omits the full ice-cloud processes.

Solar energy supplies much evaporation energy; gravity brings precipitation down and drives downhill surface flow. The cycle has no unique beginning or mandatory circle. Water can remain in stores for long periods.

<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 同样的雨，去路会变化

模型把一场虚构雨分成10个等量水份：较可渗地表为3径流、6下渗、1暂留；较封闭地表为7径流、2下渗、1暂留。三个数始终合计10。切换地表后，比较方向变化，而不是记住“真实城市必有70%径流”。暂留也可能随后蒸发或流走。

真实分配还受降雨强度、已有含水量、土壤、植被、坡度与排水设施影响。在新西兰的山地和平原可寻找这些条件，在世界任何地区也要先检查条件；这组数没有测量现实洪水。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / The same rain can take different paths

Our invented rain has 10 equal parcels: a more permeable surface sends 3 to runoff, 6 to infiltration and 1 to temporary storage; a more sealed surface sends 7, 2 and 1 respectively. Each totals 10. Compare the change, without learning “real cities always have 70% runoff”. Stored water may later evaporate or leave.

Actual partition depends on rain intensity, previous wetness, soil, vegetation, slope and drainage. Look for these conditions in New Zealand mountains/plains and elsewhere. These numbers measure no real flood.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：水汽不可见；云是液滴／冰晶。水不必经过海洋才能回到空气。下渗不等于所有水立即变为地下水；教学份数不是真实流域比例。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** vapour is invisible; clouds contain droplets/ice. Water need not visit the ocean before returning to air. Infiltration does not instantly make all water groundwater; teaching parcels are not real catchment fractions.

<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

从地表雨水出发，分别完成“径流—河流—海洋—水汽—云”“土壤—植物—水汽—云”“土壤—地下水—河流”三条路线；可重置出发点，已发现的路线会保留。试一条不相连的过程并读提示。匹配五种主要过程，切换两种地表并解释云的状态、能量／重力与模型局限。
<!-- en -->

Start at surface rainwater and complete the river/ocean/cloud, soil/plant/cloud and soil/groundwater/river journeys. Reset the start while retaining discoveries. Try an unconnected process and read its hint. Match five main processes, compare both surfaces and explain cloud states, energy/gravity and model limits.
