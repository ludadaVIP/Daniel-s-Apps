照片中的鲁阿佩胡在新西兰北岛，山顶有雪，也有火山口。白色不是“火山停止”的证据。把这个位置与大西洋中脊、夏威夷放在同一地图，猜它们是否有相同的岩浆来源条件。
<!-- en -->

Ruapehu rises on New Zealand’s North Island with snowy summits and a crater. White does not prove volcanic inactivity. Place it alongside the Mid-Atlantic Ridge and Hawaiʻi and predict whether magma forms under identical conditions.

<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 真实山峰，与地下模型

NASA照片拍于2013-09-30，能观察山体、雪与周围地面，不能直接看到岩浆来源。照片来自太空斜视，不是垂直剖面或今天的状态。

地下的熔融岩石物质称岩浆，到地表称熔岩。岩浆可沿裂隙上升，也可在地下冷却，未必喷出。地表的熔岩与火山灰等反复累积，可构建火山；不是每座火山都像一个尖锥。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Real mountain, modelled interior

NASA’s photograph was taken on 30 September 2013. It shows land, snow and the mountain, not the magma source. It is an oblique space view, neither a vertical section nor today’s state.

Molten rock material below ground is magma; at the surface it is lava. Magma may rise through cracks or cool underground without erupting. Repeated lava/ash accumulation can build volcanoes, which need not be pointed cones.

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 两种边界，不同熔融条件

俯冲环境中，下沉板片释放流体，帮助上方地幔局部熔融。鲁阿佩胡属于新西兰北岛的俯冲相关火山环境，不是整块板片被摩擦烧成岩浆。

在大西洋中脊，板块分开，热地幔上升时压力降低，可发生部分熔融。地图点代表中脊一个概略区段，不是特定火山测站。逐帧追踪局部熔融、岩浆上升与到达地表；问“条件变了什么”，比只背边界名更有用。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Two boundaries, different melting conditions

At subduction settings, fluids from the descending slab help mantle above partially melt. Ruapehu belongs to North Island’s subduction-related volcanic environment; friction does not melt the entire slab.

At the Mid-Atlantic Ridge, separating plates allow hot mantle to rise; decreasing pressure permits partial melting. Our point represents a ridge segment, not a station. Follow melting, ascent and arrival at the surface and identify the changed condition.

<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 板块内部也有例外

夏威夷大岛位于太平洋板块内部的热点上方。板块经过较固定的岩浆来源，可留下逐渐较老的火山链。较热地幔上涌是一种常用解释，热点具体来源与演化仍在研究。

因此“全部火山在边界”不成立；“每条边界处处有火山”也不成立。地图只定位三个环境案例，剖面是成因教学模型，没有深度、时间或喷发预测比例。要了解当前火山状态，需另查官方监测，不能从旧照片、雪色或模型判断。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / An exception within a plate

Hawaiʻi lies above an intraplate hotspot in the Pacific Plate. Movement over a relatively fixed magma source can leave progressively older volcanoes. Hotter mantle upwelling is a common explanation, with origins/evolution still studied.

Not all volcanoes lie on boundaries, and not every boundary is volcanic. Our map locates three cases; the sections lack calibrated depth/time and do not forecast eruptions. Current status requires official monitoring, not old photos, snow or models.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：岩浆局部生成不等于整个地幔液化；地核不是火山共用的储液罐。看到雪、没有烟或位于某个板块，都不能断定今天安全或将喷发。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** local magma generation does not liquefy the whole mantle, and the core is not a shared storage tank. Snow, no plume or plate location cannot establish current safety or eruption timing.
