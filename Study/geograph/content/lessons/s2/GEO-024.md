<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 窄谷：不只水，也有坡

河流沿相连的较低河道流动。岩质河段有足够侵蚀能力时，河床可逐渐下切，谷坡的风化与重力移动也塑造两侧，形成V形或峡谷景观。它不代表河谷是水一次划开的缝。

新河峡谷照片来自美国西弗吉尼亚州；它不是源头照片。陡坡、岩性、抬升和长期过程可以让较远河段仍有窄谷，所以不能只凭“V形”就把任何照片判为上游。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Narrow valleys: channel and slopes

Water follows connected falling channels. Where a bedrock reach has sufficient erosive capacity, incision deepens the bed, while weathering and gravity-driven slope movement shape its sides into V-shaped valleys or gorges. A gorge is not one instant cut.

New River Gorge is in West Virginia, USA, not at the source. Gradient, geology, uplift and long processes can maintain narrow valleys farther along a river. V-shape alone cannot label an unfamiliar photograph “upper course”.

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 宽谷：河道也能横向移动

宽谷里，单一河道可出现曲流。水的流动与河岸材料相互作用，外弯常侵蚀、内弯常沉积，河道可向侧面迁移。洪泛平原是邻近可能被洪水淹没的较平地面，不代表全年水下，也不保证没有洪水风险。

阿拉斯加照片显示曲流和周围山地共存。上游、中游、下游是沿河路径的相对位置；可以用它们概括常见变化，但外形不能代替实际位置图。下一课再细看弯道内外两侧。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Wide valleys: channels move sideways too

A single channel in a wide valley may meander. Flow interacts with bank material, often eroding outer bends and depositing on inner bends, allowing lateral migration. Floodplains are adjacent low ground that can inundate, not permanently submerged or flood-free land.

The Alaskan view shows bends with surrounding mountains. Upper, middle and lower describe relative positions along a flow path. They summarise common tendencies, but form cannot replace a location map. Next lesson examines inner and outer bends.

### 03 / 平原的反例，速度的反例

坎特伯雷影像有平原，也有绕砂砾洲分叉重汇的Waimakariri河道。较多粗颗粒供应、易变河岸与水量变化等条件可支持辫状形态；“到了平原就一定是曲流”不成立。水量、粒径、植被、岩性和工程都可能影响形态。

较缓坡也不证明下游流速必较低：流量增加、河道变深与阻力变化都会影响速度。条件图只表达可能的形态和过程，没有实测流速。排序活动给的是虚构相连河道的高程；真实照片来自不同国家，不是同一条河的一组镜头。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Plains and velocity have exceptions

Canterbury shows plains and Waimakariri channels splitting/rejoining around gravel bars. Abundant coarse sediment, changing banks and discharge variations can favour braiding: a plain need not contain a single meander. Grain size, vegetation, geology and engineering also affect form.

Gentler slope does not prove slower downstream flow. Added discharge, deeper channels and changing friction affect velocity. Our condition diagrams show possible forms/processes, without measured velocities. The sorting task supplies elevations of a fictional connected river; actual photos come from different countries, not one river sequence.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：上中下游不是固定的年龄阶段，也不是照片形态的唯一标签。下游并非只沉积、不侵蚀；上游也能有局部沉积。排序题的300、100、0是任意高程单位，不是照片地点实测。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** reaches are not fixed age stages or unique photographic forms. Downstream reaches can erode and upper reaches can deposit locally. The sorting task’s 300, 100 and 0 are arbitrary supplied elevations, not measurements of photographed places.
