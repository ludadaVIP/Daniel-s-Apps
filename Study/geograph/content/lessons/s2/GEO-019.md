<!-- en -->

These dots are not random decoration: they come from USGS’s 2020 larger-earthquake catalogue. Look for belts, then ask why slow plate motion can produce sudden shaking.

<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 从点到分布，再到限制

图上每个圆点是2020年UTC起震时间、USGS首选震级至少6的一次地震震中。与板块图并排比较，寻找太平洋周边等集中区域。许多地震与板块相互作用有关，内陆也会发生。

这不是全部地震：其他年份与较小地震没有画出。圆点大小相同，不表示震级、深度、当地摇晃或损失。目录也可修订；一年样本适合观察，不能替代长期危险评估，更不能预测下一次日期。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / From points to patterns and limits

Each circle marks a 2020 UTC earthquake epicentre with USGS preferred magnitude at least 6. Compare the plate map and look around the Pacific. Many events relate to plate interactions; intraplate earthquakes also occur.

Other years and smaller events are excluded. Equal dots encode neither magnitude nor depth, shaking or damage. Catalogues can be revised; a one-year sample supports observation, not long-term hazard assessment or next-event prediction.

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 慢慢积累，突然释放

断层两侧可能被摩擦锁住，周围岩石仍随运动逐渐形变。突然滑动时，一部分积累的弹性能释放，地震波向外传播，地面因此摇晃。

逐帧比较锁住、形变、滑动与波传播。不要把模拟帧数当成真实日期：不同断层的过程非常复杂。这个剖面只帮助解释机制，不提供应力、震级或时间预测。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Slow accumulation, sudden release

Friction can lock a fault while nearby rock gradually deforms. Sudden slip releases some stored elastic energy as seismic waves, producing shaking.

Compare locking, deformation, slip and spreading waves. Frame counts are not dates: real faults are complex. The section explains a mechanism without forecasting stress, magnitude or time.

<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 起点、地表位置与行动

地下开始破裂的A是震源，正上方地表B是震中。断层斜着到达地表的C不一定是震中；震中也不一定是损失最严重的地方。后果还与建筑、地面条件和人群暴露有关。

按新西兰民防指引，普通站立者在室内摇晃时先趴下、掩护、抓牢，保护头颈，不冲到外面或站门框。靠海遇到持续超过一分钟或强到难以站立的地震，摇晃结束后迅速向高处或内陆、离开海啸撤离区。家长结合家庭、学校指引练习；本课不是实时警报。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Starting point, surface point and action

A is the underground focus; B directly above is the epicentre. C at the surface fault trace need not be the epicentre or worst-damaged place. Buildings, ground conditions and exposure affect consequences.

NZ Civil Defence advises people standing indoors to Drop, Cover and Hold, protecting head/neck rather than running outside or using doorways. Near the coast, after shaking longer than a minute or too strong to stand, quickly move to high ground or inland beyond tsunami evacuation zones. Practise with family/school guidance; this lesson is not a live alert.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：历史地震多不代表能精确预测下一次；地图没点也不代表不会震。震级、某处摇晃强度与灾害损失不是同一个量。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** historical concentration does not enable exact prediction, and no dots do not mean no quakes. Magnitude, local shaking and losses are different quantities.

观察帧0、2、3、4，说明慢与快；在剖面找震源与震中；读分布图的筛选限制，并选出室内基本保护动作。可以回看和重试。

Inspect frames 0, 2, 3 and 4 and explain slow/fast stages. Locate focus/epicentre, interpret map selection limits and identify basic indoor protective actions. Revisit and retry.

<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

问：“哪个部分慢，哪个部分突然？这个模型有没有告诉我们哪天？”纸笔替代：画四格漫画，标锁住、形变、滑动、波传播；再画斜断层与竖直投影。用平静语言练习民防动作，不推倒家具、不晃动孩子，也不把课堂图当预报。
<!-- en -->

Ask: “Which part is slow, which sudden? Does the model supply a date?” Paper alternative: draw locking/deformation/slip/waves, then an inclined fault and vertical projection. Practise official actions calmly; do not tip furniture, shake a child or treat the diagram as a forecast.
