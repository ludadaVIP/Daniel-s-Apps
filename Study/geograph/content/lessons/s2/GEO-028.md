### 01 / 母质与生命，共同成土

岩石风化形成矿物颗粒，搬来的河流沉积、风积物或火山灰也可成为母质。有机残体被分解、转化，根、动物与微生物改变土壤结构和物质循环。母质、气候、生物、地形和时间一起影响成土；不是石头只要变碎，就立即等同成熟土壤。

Noatak照片有根、层次和量尺，来源图像创建记录为2013-08-07。它来自阿拉斯加苔原，不能直接代表新西兰农田。我们用另一个原创土层实例解释字母，并没有凭照片颜色鉴定它的全部土层。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Parent material and life form soil together

Weathered rock supplies mineral particles; transported river/wind deposits or volcanic ash may also be parent material. Organic residues decompose/transform. Roots, animals and microbes alter structure and material cycling. Parent material, climate, organisms, relief and time interact; fragmentation alone does not instantly make developed soil.

Noatak’s photo shows roots, layers and a tape; NPS records an image-create date of 7 August 2013. This Alaskan tundra does not represent a New Zealand field. Our separate original profile explains letters without diagnosing all photo horizons from colour.

### 02 / 搭一种剖面，不背全球配方

本例从上到下是O有机残体层、A表层矿质土、B下层土、C较少受成土作用改变的母质，再到非土壤的R坚硬基岩。A与B的区别涉及成土过程与多项性质，不是固定的两种颜色；C也不保证由正下方R产生。

真实土壤可能没有O，可能出现淋失较明显的E，或没有本图某些层。厚度、颜色、矿物和结构随地点变化。按指定实例拼装能理解位置与性质；把实例当所有土壤的模板则会误判。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Build one profile, not a global recipe

Our example has organic O, mineral topsoil A, subsoil B, less soil-altered parent material C, then hard bedrock R, which is not soil. A/B distinctions involve soil formation and multiple properties, not two fixed colours; C need not derive from the R beneath it.

Real soils may lack O, have an eluviated E, or lack other pictured horizons. Thickness, colour, minerals and structure vary. Building the specified example clarifies relationships; prescribing it universally creates misidentifications.

<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 孔隙不是空无，植物也不只要土

这个人为设定的100体积单位矿质土样中，45是矿物、5是有机质，另外50为孔隙。孔隙可容水或空气；加水到50时，示意中孔隙空气减少到0。比例不代表所有土，也不是“25水＋25气”一定最好。实际土壤还会排水、收缩或膨胀，根和生物影响结构。

土壤为自然植物提供支撑、水和养分环境，并支持生命、循环和储水。但无土生长并非不可能：水培人为供水、养分、根系氧和支撑，光与适宜温度等也仍需满足。农田影像只显现植被与土地利用，不能单凭颜色判断土壤健康、pH或养分。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Pores have contents; plants have needs beyond soil

Our invented 100-volume-unit mineral sample has 45 minerals,5 organic matter and 50 pores. Pores contain water/air; raising water to 50 leaves 0 pore-air units in this box. These are not universal proportions or an optimum 25/25 recipe. Real soils drain, shrink/swell and have root/organism-modified structure.

Soil supports natural plant growth, water/nutrient environments, life, cycling and storage. Hydroponics can supply water, nutrients, root oxygen and support artificially; light, temperature and other plant needs remain. A field image shows vegetation/land use, without measured soil health, pH or nutrients.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：土壤不只是碎石，也不一定由正下方岩石直接形成。基岩R不是土壤。土层顺序和体积比例都是本例，不能拿来给所有土诊断；无土水培也不等于植物没有资源需求。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** soil is more than rock fragments and need not come directly from bedrock below. R is not soil. Our sequence/proportions describe examples, not all soils; hydroponics does not remove resource needs.

问：“土壤里除了颗粒还需要什么？模型是哪一种实例？”纸笔替代：画五层卡拼一个剖面；画100格，选45矿物、5有机质，其余50格以蓝/白表示水/空气，另画根与分解者连接。可选调查：查公开土壤资料比较两个地区的层序，不挖沟或采未知材料；这不是施肥、配土或农场适用性建议。
<!-- en -->

Ask: “What else is present besides particles? Which example does this model show?” Paper alternative: order five horizon cards and draw 100 squares with 45 mineral/5 organic/50 water-or-air, adding roots/decomposers separately. Optional investigation: compare public soil profiles for two regions without digging or collecting unknown material; this is not a fertiliser, soil-mix or farm-suitability recommendation.
