<!-- en -->

This blue-white wall is the Pedersen Glacier front in Alaska. The photo shows fractures, colour and water, but not a day’s ice movement. How can apparently stationary solid ice help carve a large valley?

<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 固体也可以慢慢流

积雪经过压实、重新结晶等逐渐成为冰川冰。足够厚的冰体在重力下发生形变，有的冰川底部也沿岩面滑动。这不是整条冰川先融成河再往下走。冰可携带岩石，碎屑磨蚀床面；冰也可拔走已有裂隙中的岩块。

Pedersen冰川照片与蒙大拿Avalanche Lake谷地照片来自不同地方，不是同地“有冰/无冰”前后照。前者看现存冰体，后者看NPS标明的过去冰川谷地；一张静态照片不能量冰流速度。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / A solid can flow slowly

Accumulated snow compacts/recrystallises into glacier ice. A thick ice body deforms under gravity; some glacier bases also slide over rock. It need not first melt into a liquid river. Carried debris abrades surfaces, and ice can pluck blocks from existing joints.

Pedersen Glacier and Montana’s Avalanche Lake are different places, not same-site before/after views. One shows existing ice; the other is an NPS-identified glacial valley. A still photo cannot measure ice velocity.

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 比较横剖面，不只比较名字

常见河谷的河床向下侵蚀，谷坡风化与重力作用也塑造两侧，可概括为底窄的V形。谷地冰川占据较宽的谷底，运动冰与夹带碎屑作用于底部和侧壁，可留下宽底、陡壁的U形谷。两图同为横剖面，不能与俯视河曲直接比字母。

地形受岩性、裂隙、不同年代过程等影响，真实谷形不必是标准字母。新西兰Fiordland有冰川雕刻的谷、湖和峡湾；河流后来可进入旧冰川谷，海水也可淹没低于海面的谷底。现在看到的水，不一定是最初雕刻者。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Compare cross sections, not just names

River bed incision plus slope weathering/gravity commonly yields a narrow-bottomed V summary. Valley glaciers occupy more of the floor; moving ice/debris erodes bed and walls, often leaving a broad-floor, steep-wall U form. Both diagrams are cross sections, unlike a plan-view river bend.

Geology, joints and successive processes modify real outlines. New Zealand’s Fiordland has glacially carved valleys, lakes and fiords. Rivers may later occupy glacial valleys and seawater may flood below-sea-level floors. Present water need not be the original carver.

<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 冰在走，边界却在退

分别追踪红点与冰川末端。三种情景里，红点都向低处流；末端送来的冰超过损失时可推进，两者相当时可近似不变，损失更大时可退缩。退缩不是冰倒着流，而是边缘消失得比冰被送来更快。

整条冰川还受到积雪、消融、崩解、厚度、地形等影响，质量变化与末端移动的响应时间不一定相同。我们的帧让三种可能结果同步显现，属于示意，不是某年某冰川的预测。真实监测要重复测量厚度、流动、范围与收支。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Ice moves while the edge retreats

Track the red marker separately from the terminus. It moves down-valley in all three scenarios. A front can advance when delivery exceeds loss, remain approximately stationary when they balance, or retreat when loss exceeds delivery. Retreat means the edge disappears faster than flowing ice replaces it, not reverse flow.

Snow, ablation, calving, thickness and terrain influence a whole glacier. Mass changes and terminus position need not respond on identical timescales. Our synchronous frames illustrate possible outcomes, without a dated forecast. Real monitoring repeats thickness, flow, extent and budget measurements.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：冰川是运动冰体，不是任何积雪或湖面冰。U形是形成线索，不是单凭一个字母就定论。冰川退缩时仍可向谷下流；质量收支变化不保证末端立即作同幅度回应。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** a glacier is moving ice, not any snow patch or frozen lake. U shape is a clue, not a complete verdict. Retreating glaciers can still flow down-valley; mass-balance changes do not guarantee immediate equal terminus responses.
