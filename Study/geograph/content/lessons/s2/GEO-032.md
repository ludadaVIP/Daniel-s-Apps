<!-- section:hook -->
<!-- zh -->

如果要给朋友介绍一座山、一条冰川谷或一处海柱，你会只给地名，还是能解释它如何形成？选择新西兰或世界案例；报告从你的问题开始，正文不自动填入标准答案。
<!-- en -->

To introduce a mountain, glacial valley or sea stack to a friend, can you explain its formation beyond naming it? Choose a New Zealand or world case. Your report begins with your question; no standard explanation is filled in automatically.

<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 先定位，再确定你要解释什么

可选南阿尔卑斯山、Ruapehu、美国Avalanche Lake谷地或Third Beach海柱，也可自选公开可查地貌。案例图有独立署名与图注；圆点只定位介绍区域，不表示每条山脉／海岸的边界。自选案例手填大致经纬度与双语地名，精度0.1°，不需要家庭地址、定位或上传。

地图北向上，使用Natural Earth世界陆海底图、等距圆柱投影；形状与面积会失真。照片能显示形态，但一个画面通常不足以证实全部形成过程。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Locate the place and define your question

Choose the Southern Alps, Ruapehu, Avalanche Lake valley or Third Beach stacks, or another publicly documented landform. Case images have their own credits/captions. The dot locates a region, not its full boundary. Custom cases use a bilingual name and manual coarse coordinates to 0.1°, without home addresses, location access or uploads.

The north-up map uses Natural Earth land/ocean and equirectangular projection, which distorts shapes/areas. Photos show form; one image rarely proves the entire formation history.

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 三步图解，是解释而不是固定公式

从挤压抬升、断层错动、熔融喷发、冰川作用、风化、侵蚀搬运、沉积中选择三个过程卡，可重复并调整顺序。每一步至少用一种语言写自己的描述，联系具体案例。比如山地可同时抬升与被侵蚀；海柱涉及岩体、裂隙与波浪，不能强迫所有案例套同一流程。

三个框是你选的解释结构，不是实测的三个等长时期，也不证明先后。若资料尚不足，写清不确定处，修改后再勾选核对。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Three diagram steps explain rather than prescribe

Select three cards from compression/uplift, fault motion, melting/eruption, glacial action, weathering, erosion/transport and deposition. Repeat/reorder cards and describe each in at least one language for your case. Uplift and erosion can overlap; stack formation involves rock, fractures and waves. Different cases need different explanations.

The boxes structure your reasoning, not three measured equal periods or proven chronology. State uncertainties where evidence is insufficient and recheck after editing.

<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 观察、推论与局限分开写

报告需要两条证据。每条选择S2课程来源，或填写公开资料标题与http/https链接；再分别写“我观察到什么”“它支持哪个推论”“它不能告诉我什么”。两条应从不同角度支持机制，而不是把同一句话复制两遍。原始影像、数据与研究说明可以互相补充，不能把教学箭头冒充现场测量。

最后写自己的中文与英文讲解，也可分别记录已完成的口头讲解。系统只检查记录是否齐全，证据是否贴合案例、因果是否合理仍由你和家人核对。报告自动保存在本机，可导出独立HTML、Markdown和两张SVG；不上传。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Separate observation, inference and limitation

Provide two pieces of evidence. Select an S2 source lesson or enter a public source title and http/https link. Write what you observe, what inference it supports and what it cannot tell you. Aim for complementary angles rather than duplicating a sentence. Images, data and research can support each other; teaching arrows are not field measurements.

Finish with your own Chinese and English explanations, or separately record completed oral explanations. Software checks record completeness; you/family judge relevance and causal reasoning. The local draft exports to standalone HTML, Markdown and two SVGs without uploading.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：已勾选不等于科学结论已被证明，两条证据不等于两句猜测；步骤框不等于真实时间轴。更换案例后保留草稿文字，但必须重新核对位置、过程与证据是否适用。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** a checked box is not scientific proof; two guesses are not two pieces of evidence. Boxes are not a measured timeline. Changing case keeps draft writing but requires renewed checks of location, process and evidence relevance.

<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

完成四项：双语标题与位置地图、三张过程卡及自己的描述、两条含观察／推论／局限的有来源证据、中英文讲解。修改后核对，查看四项完成状态；导出草稿或完整作品。再回到整课写一句总结、完成回顾并记录探索。
<!-- en -->

Complete four parts: bilingual title/location map; three process cards with your descriptions; two sourced observation/inference/limit entries; Chinese/English explanations. Recheck after edits and inspect the four-part status. Export a draft or completed work, then return to the lesson for your summary, recall and exploration record.

<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

问：“这条证据实际能支持你的哪句话？如果换一个地貌，还会用同一过程吗？”纸笔替代：在地图点地点，画三格过程图，将两条来源、观察、推论、局限写成卡，再分别中英文讲给家人。不用所有框都写长段，但要具体；先肯定可核查的观察，再帮助修正因果与不确定性。
<!-- en -->

Ask: “Which claim does this evidence support? Would another landform need the same process?” Paper alternative: mark a map, draw three process boxes, write two source/observation/inference/limit cards and explain in both languages. Keep entries specific rather than long. Start with checkable observations, then refine causal reasoning and uncertainty.
