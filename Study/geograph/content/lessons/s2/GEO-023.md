### 01 / 原地改变：风化

黄石岩壁可见裂隙与植物。水结冰、盐晶体或根系生长等可扩大部分裂隙；物理风化使岩石变碎，不必改变矿物化学组成。化学风化则包括矿物溶解或氧化等改变。温度、水、岩性与已有裂隙会影响过程。

照片的裂隙是观察线索，不能断定每条都由冰或植物造成，也不能从单张照片算出形成多久。风化不是“石头必须已经被风吹走”。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Change in place: weathering

The Yellowstone wall shows cracks and vegetation. Ice, salt crystals or roots can enlarge some openings; physical weathering breaks rock without necessarily changing mineral chemistry. Chemical weathering includes dissolution or oxidation. Water, temperature, geology and pre-existing joints affect these processes.

Cracks are observable clues, not proof that every one was caused by ice or roots, nor a clock for its age. Weathering does not require the rock to have already been blown away.

### 02 / 离开原处，再到别处

选取一段简化故事：岩块变碎→水流带走颗粒→颗粒在别处沉积。第一步突出原地风化；第二步突出侵蚀与搬运；第三步是物质停下或积累。移动的水、风、冰与重力都可能参与地表物质移动；水流也能磨蚀基岩。

模型中八个符号始终保留。“移走”改变地点，不意味着消失。它只画固体颗粒，没画化学风化产生的溶解物；真实岩坡可能一边风化，一边被侵蚀，旁边同时沉积。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Leave one place, arrive elsewhere

Choose a simplified sequence: bedrock breaks up → water moves particles → particles settle elsewhere. The first highlights weathering in place, the second erosion/transport, and the third deposition. Water, wind, ice and gravity can move surface material, while flowing water can abrade bedrock too.

All eight symbols remain in the model. Removal changes location, not existence. Only solid particles are drawn; chemically produced solutes are omitted. Real slopes can weather while being eroded, with deposition nearby at the same time.

### 03 / 变化证据，不等于全部解释

帕德玛河影像来自NASA同一Landsat系列；采于1988-01-02与2018-01-20，都是当地干季的日期。先以标注城镇作位置参照，看Harirampur附近河岸和河道的差异，区分水域、陆地与砂洲。它们不是一年内四季的对照。

河岸物质可被剥蚀移走，别处又有沉积。洪水、水量、岸坡材料与工程等都可能改变河道。两张处理影像直接支持位置变化；不能独自证明每次变化原因、每年速率或受影响家庭数量。颜色差异还可能受传感器、处理和水位影响。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Evidence of change is not a complete explanation

These NASA Landsat-series views were captured on 2 January 1988 and 20 January 2018, both dry-season dates. Use labelled settlements as reference points, comparing banks/channels near Harirampur and distinguishing water, land and bars. This is not a within-year seasonal comparison.

Banks can lose material while deposition builds elsewhere. Floods, discharge, bank composition and engineering may affect channels. Two processed images directly support positional change, not every cause, annual rate or number of affected households. Sensors, processing and water level can also affect colour differences.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：侵蚀不只是风，风化也不只是风；沉积不等于沉积岩立即形成。平缓地面也会侵蚀，绿色也不保证河岸稳定。滑到50%是同时看两年各一部分，不是2003年的影像。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** erosion and weathering are not just wind; deposition does not instantly create sedimentary rock. Gentle ground may erode and green banks may be unstable. A 50% slider reveals parts of two years, not an image from 2003.

Which statement is observed, which explained?” Paper alternative: use eight paper dots for three location panels, or list observations/inferences/missing data beside two images. No cliff base, fast water, landslide or acid experiment is needed; local observation uses a parent-approved safe place.
