### 01 / 岩岸与沙滩，是材料和过程的故事

波浪冲击、夹带砂砾磨擦、岩石裂隙与坡体移动等可改变海岸。侵蚀岸坡照片来自弗吉尼亚George Washington Birthplace，显示水边陡岸；我们没有其年退岸速率。Olympic海蚀柱是周围材料逐渐被移去后的岩石遗体，岩性与裂隙影响哪些部分先被侵蚀。

Point Reyes沙滩有松散沉积物，也邻近陡岸和湿地，说明侵蚀形态与堆积形态能相邻。海蚀拱塌顶可留下柱，但不是每根柱都必经一套固定阶段。照片来自不同地点，不是同一片海岸从崖变柱再变沙滩。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Rock coasts and beaches tell material/process stories

Wave impact, abrasion by sediment, joints and slope movement reshape coasts. Virginia’s George Washington Birthplace scarp shows a steep water-edge bank without an annual retreat rate. Olympic’s stacks are rock remnants after surrounding removal; geology/joints influence differential erosion.

Point Reyes combines loose sediment, steep banks and wetlands: erosional and depositional forms can coexist. An arch roof may collapse into a stack, without a compulsory path for every stack. The photos show different places, not a cliff→stack→beach time series.

### 02 / 往返也有净搬运

把红点当成一粒沙：波浪斜着到岸，上冲可把沙带向斜上方，水沿滩坡返回海侧。一次往返后，沙可能没有回到原来的沿岸位置。改变来浪方向，示意净位移也反过来。

这是选取的上冲/回流路径，不是每粒沙的录像。沿岸流也能持续搬运泥沙，潮流、地形和风暴会影响方向与强度。沙还可在近岸、较远海底与滩面之间交换；本图只强调沿岸位置，不测流速。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Back-and-forth can give net transport

Treat the red dot as a sand grain. Oblique swash moves it diagonally up-beach; backwash returns it more downslope. After a cycle, its alongshore position may differ. Reversing approach reverses net displacement in our chosen route.

This is a selected swash/backwash path, not footage of every grain. Longshore currents also transport sediment; tides, terrain and storms affect direction/strength. Sand exchanges between beach, nearshore and farther seabed too. This view isolates alongshore displacement without measuring speed.

### 03 / 沙滩也有库存账本

选定一段沙滩，把“原有＋进入−离开”记下来。我们的三个盒从6单位开始：进3出1后是8，进2出2后还是6，进1出3后是4。侵蚀掉的材料可能进入别处库存，不是消失。单位是任意示意，不等于立方米或沙滩宽度。

真实泥沙来源可包括河流、岸坡、近岸沉积物和生物形成材料；波浪和海流再分配它们。今天宽的沙滩也可能在某个季节缩窄，潮位改变还会影响照片中的露出宽度。若想判断趋势，要同范围、可比潮位的时序与收支证据，不能只挑一张“最宽”的照片。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / A beach has a stock account

For one beach segment, record initial + in − out. Three invented boxes start at 6 units:3 in/1 out gives 8;2/2 stays 6;1/3 gives 4. Removed material may enter another area’s stock rather than disappear. Units are arbitrary, not cubic metres or beach widths.

Rivers, eroding banks, nearshore deposits and biological material can supply real beaches. Waves/currents redistribute them. A wide beach today may narrow seasonally; tide affects exposed width in photos. Trends require matched extents, comparable tides, repeated observations and budget evidence.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：海浪不只破坏，也参与搬运、留下材料。海蚀柱是硬岩遗留，不是新沙堆；沙滩与陡岸可以相邻。库存净增不自动等于任何时刻露出的沙滩加宽，单照也不能确认趋势。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** waves also transport/deposit. Stacks are rock remnants rather than new sand piles; beaches can adjoin steep banks. Net sediment gain does not automatically mean a wider exposed beach at every tide, and one image cannot establish a trend.

Match three coastal photos with main forms/processes. Trace frames 0/8 under both wave directions and infer net motion. Open all three sediment boxes and compare gain/balance/loss. State what one wide-beach photo cannot prove.
