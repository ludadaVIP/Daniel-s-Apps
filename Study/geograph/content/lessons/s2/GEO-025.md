### 01 / 同一个弯，两种变化

水沿相连的较低通道走，不是选择地图上两点间最短的直线。河道受局部地形、材料和水流影响；弯曲出现后，流速分布与河道形状相互作用。常见曲流的较强水流偏向外弯，容易带走河岸物质；内弯较容易留下沙粒，形成沉积洲。这里比较的是同一弯的相对倾向，不是“每一点外侧永远最快”。

在图上，A与D都是外弯，却朝向纸面不同的一侧。B与E在内弯。先用手沿河道追踪，再看内外，别把左、右当过程标签。河岸材质、植被、工程和洪水都会修正实际作用。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / One bend, two kinds of change

Water follows connected falling channels, not simply the shortest line between map points. Local terrain, materials and flow affect its path; once a bend exists, flow and channel shape interact. In a common meander, stronger flow near the outer bank helps remove material; the inner bend more readily retains sand as a point bar. This is a relative tendency within a bend, not a claim that every outer point is always fastest.

A/D are outer bends facing different page directions; B/E are inner bends. Trace the channel, then identify inside/outside. Bank material, vegetation, engineering and floods modify actual behaviour.

### 02 / 搬走和留下，河道会迁移

拖动帧0到帧4：两处外弯向外移，内弯留下材料，曲流可逐渐迁移。沙不是“消失了”：部分被搬到别处，来源与去处要结合整段河流调查。动画只让机制可见，帧不等于年份，图也没有实测的侵蚀速率。

真实曲流还可向下游迁移，有时弯道被截弯取直，旧河道留下牛轭湖等痕迹；本图只展示侧向变大，没有模拟截弯。也不是所有河都曲流：上一课的坎特伯雷辫状河道就是需要不同条件解释的反例。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Removal and deposition let a channel migrate

Move frame 0 to 4: outer banks shift outward while inner bends retain material. Sediment is relocated rather than vanishing; its sources/destinations require a reach-scale study. Frames make a mechanism visible without years or measured erosion rates.

Real bends may also migrate downstream or be cut off, sometimes leaving oxbow lakes. This diagram shows lateral growth, without cutoff simulation. Not all rivers meander: the preceding Canterbury braided example needs different conditions.

### 03 / 平地不是永远的干地

洪泛平原是河道旁较平、可能受漫岸洪水影响的地面。普通水位下它可以长草、耕作或有建筑；水位升高时，河水可能越过河岸、在较低地面流动，减速后留下沉积物。长期沉积与河道迁移共同塑造这里。

切换水位，C从干地变成图中漫水地。我们人为设定了蓝色范围，不能据此判断真实住宅安全。真实范围需要高程、流量、堤防状况、历史洪水及官方资料。没有下雨的一张照片不能证明“永远不会淹”。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Flat ground is not permanently dry

Floodplains are relatively flat land beside a channel that may inundate. They can support grass, farms or buildings at ordinary water. Higher water may overflow banks, cross low ground and leave sediment as it slows. Deposition and channel migration shape the plain over time.

C becomes inundated in our invented high-water view. That extent cannot assess a real home. Actual boundaries need elevations, discharge, defences, flood history and official information. One dry photograph cannot establish “never floods”.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：侵蚀与沉积可以同时发生在相邻两侧。外弯/内弯不等于固定的左岸/右岸；洪泛平原不是全年淹水的湖底。图中常见机制不能直接代替真实流速、侵蚀速率或洪水边界。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** adjacent banks can erode/deposit simultaneously. Inner/outer does not mean fixed left/right; floodplains are not permanently flooded lakebeds. Common-process diagrams do not replace measured velocities, erosion rates or flood extents.

问：“你按弯道还是纸的左右判断？变化后的材料可能去了哪里？”纸笔替代：画S形河道，贴五个字母，给两外弯画侵蚀箭头、两内弯画沙洲，河道外涂洪泛平原。可选调查：比较带日期的公开河道地图，记录能支持的变化与未知的原因；无需接近河岸或涉水。
<!-- en -->

Ask: “Are you using the bend or page sides? Where might moved material go?” Paper alternative: draw an S channel, label five sites, add outer-bank arrows/inner bars and shade adjacent floodplain. Optional investigation: compare dated public channel maps, recording supported changes and unknown causes; no bank visit or wading is needed.
