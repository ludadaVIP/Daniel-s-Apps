我们在GEO-009看过地表高低，但地球深处没有摄影机带回全景。科学家凭什么画出剖面？先找模型的薄外皮，再猜它是否每一层都液态。
<!-- en -->

We mapped surface relief in GEO-009, but no camera has returned a panorama of the deep interior. How can scientists draw a cutaway? Find the thin outer skin and predict whether every layer is liquid.

<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 从薄外皮到金属核心

地壳是外面的岩石薄层；地幔在它下方，延伸很深。地核主要为金属，分成液态外核和固态内核。这里把地壳加厚，是为了让你看见它，不是它真实占这么大比例。

沿A→D由外到内比较：既要说“是什么材料”，又要说“什么状态”。越深就一定越液态的猜测，会被固态内核这个例子挑战。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / From a thin skin to a metallic centre

Crust forms a thin rocky skin; the mantle extends beneath it. The mainly metallic core has a liquid outer part and solid inner part. The drawing exaggerates the crust so it is visible.

Follow A→D and distinguish material from state. A solid inner core challenges the idea that greater depth always means more liquid.

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 地壳不是整个板块

地壳与最上部坚硬地幔共同组成岩石圈，板块是岩石圈的大块碎片。因此“地壳”和“板块”不能互换。图中的括号包含两种成分层的一部分。

下方地幔主要仍是固态岩石，在高温高压和漫长时间下可缓慢变形与流动。部分地点会局部熔融产生岩浆，并不表示整个地幔是一片液态海洋。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Crust is not a whole plate

Crust and rigid uppermost mantle together form the lithosphere. Plates are large pieces of it, so crust and plate are not interchangeable. The bracket spans parts of two compositional layers.

Mantle below remains mostly solid rock but can deform and flow over long times under heat and pressure. Local partial melting creates magma without making the entire mantle a liquid ocean.

<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 看不见，也能检验

地震波穿过不同材料时，速度和方向会变化。P波可通过固体和液体，S波不能通过液体。许多地震与地面仪器的记录，帮助约束外核为液态的解释。

图上的直线只表示能否穿过，并非真实波路。实际还有折射、反射和波型转换；科学家还结合岩石、重力、磁场及实验。一个剖面是多种证据支持的模型，不是亲眼看到的内部。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Unseen, but testable

Seismic-wave speeds and directions change across materials. P waves pass through solids and liquids; S waves cannot cross liquids. Records from many earthquakes and instruments constrain a liquid outer core.

Straight lines show transmission only, not real paths. Refraction, reflection and wave conversion occur; rocks, gravity, magnetic fields and experiments add evidence. A cutaway is a supported model, not a direct view.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：“地幔能流动”不等于“地幔全液态”。图上橙色也不是地下真实颜色；地核不是所有火山的岩浆库。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** mantle flow does not mean an entirely liquid mantle. Orange is a diagram colour; the core is not the reservoir feeding all volcanoes.
