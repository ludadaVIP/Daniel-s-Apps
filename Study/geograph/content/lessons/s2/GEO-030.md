### 01 / 海水为什么流动

风推动许多表层流动；温度和盐度改变海水密度，密度差参与环流；潮汐也产生水流。地球自转的科里奥利效应、海岸与海底地形影响路径。海浪的起伏与持续输送海水的洋流并非同一种现象，海洋也不是只由一个原因推动。

本图只挑三例：W墨西哥湾流及北大西洋延伸总体把较暖水向北、再向东北输送；C加那利寒流沿北非西侧总体向低纬流；E南极绕极流总体向东绕南极。暖／寒是相对周围水体的比较，不表示暖流永远超过25°C或寒流永远结冰。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / What drives seawater

Wind drives much surface flow. Temperature/salinity affect seawater density; density differences participate in circulation. Tides produce currents too. Earth’s rotation through the Coriolis effect, coasts and seabed shape paths. Wave oscillation differs from currents transporting water; no single cause drives the whole ocean.

Our map selects three examples: W combines the Gulf Stream and its North Atlantic extension carrying relatively warm water broadly north/northeast; C is the relatively cool Canary Current flowing broadly equatorward off northwest Africa; E is the broadly eastward Antarctic Circumpolar Current. Warm/cool compare surrounding waters, not fixed 25°C or freezing thresholds.

### 02 / 用真实读数检验，不凭箭头猜温度

NOAA OI SST V2将卫星与现场观测等组合为1°网格产品。本课采用1991–2020的一月／七月长期平均、单位°C；A在34.5°N、70.5°W，B在34.5°N、14.5°W。一月A20.3、B17.9；七月A26.4、B21.4。两个月A都较暖，两点也都在七月较暖。

我们按原始网格绘色，无额外插值，并用同源陆海掩膜将陆地置灰。网格读数不是海岸气温、海流速度或一台浮标今天的测量。同纬度控制一个因素，并没有控制风、混合、深度、海岸和全部环流；两点不能单独证明一条洋流造成全部差异。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Check real readings rather than infer temperatures from arrows

NOAA OI SST V2 combines satellite and in-situ observations into a 1° grid. We use 1991–2020 January/July means in°C: A at 34.5°N,70.5°W and B at 34.5°N,14.5°W. January A20.3/B17.9; July A26.4/B21.4. A is warmer in both months; both cells are warmer in July.

Colours retain source cells without extra interpolation; the matching land/sea mask makes land grey. A cell is not coastal air temperature, flow speed or today’s buoy reading. Equal latitude controls one factor, not winds, mixing, depth, coasts and all circulation. Two cells cannot isolate one current’s causal contribution.

### 03 / 搬运热量，气候还要看多个条件

把较暖／较冷水团移向虚构海岸，观察它带来的热量环境变化。移动帧没有秒、速度或温度计算；真实海气热交换还受风、湿度和温差等影响。洋流能影响周边气候，但纬度、盛行风、海陆分布与地形也参与，不能说一条暖流决定所有天气。

联系新西兰：东奥克兰洋流沿北岛东北岸总体向东南输送相对暖的水；南岛东岸的Southland Current总体向东北。南极绕极流位于更南方海域，不直接流经新西兰主岛岸边。世界图箭头经过简化，既不画全部分支／涡旋，也不用于航行。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Transport heat; climate has multiple controls

Move relatively warm/cool water parcels towards a fictional coast and consider the heat environment they bring. Frames have no seconds, speed or temperature calculation. Real air–sea exchange also depends on wind, humidity and temperature differences. Currents influence nearby climate alongside latitude, prevailing winds, land/sea distribution and relief; one warm current does not determine all weather.

New Zealand connection: the East Auckland Current carries relatively warm water broadly southeast along the northeast North Island; the Southland Current flows broadly northeast off the eastern South Island. The Antarctic Circumpolar Current is farther south, not directly along the main islands. Our arrows omit branches/eddies and are not navigation routes.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：温度图的红蓝颜色不表示速度，箭头宽度也不是流量。寒暖为相对概念。长期海表温度不等于今天海岸气温；比较两点不是完整气候归因实验。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** red/blue temperature colours do not show speed, and arrow widths do not measure transport. Warm/cool are relative. Climatological SST is not today’s coastal air temperature; a two-cell comparison is not a complete climate attribution experiment.
