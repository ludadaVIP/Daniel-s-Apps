<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 三类，是三种形成故事

花岗质露头是火成岩例子：熔融物质在地下冷却结晶，后来被剥露。砂岩是沉积岩例子：砂粒沉积后，经压实和矿物胶结成岩。NPS来源注明照片中的Marble Canyon条纹岩壁为变质岩案例：已有岩石在较高温压等条件下，主要保持固态而改变矿物与结构。

照片可发现层次、条带或颗粒线索，但三类按成因分，不按颜色分。变质岩不一定有条带，沉积岩也不全由碎屑压成；石灰岩等还有化学或生物形成途径。岩壁照片可看条纹形态，不能鉴定每层岩性或所有矿物；峡谷地名本身也不是岩石鉴定。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Three groups, three origin stories

The granitic outcrop illustrates igneous rock: melt crystallised underground and was later exposed. Sandstone illustrates sedimentary rock: deposited sand was compacted and cemented. NPS identifies the striped Marble Canyon wall as metamorphic: existing material changed minerals/texture under elevated temperature/pressure, mainly while solid.

Photos offer layers, bands or grains as clues, but origin defines the groups. Not all metamorphic rocks are banded, nor are all sedimentary rocks compacted fragments: limestone includes chemical/biological origins. The wall shows banded form, not every layer’s rock type or mineral; the place name itself is not an identification.

<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 把过程接上，不只背箭头

A：岩浆冷却结晶→火成岩。B：岩石在地表风化、侵蚀搬运→松散沉积物。C：沉积物沉积、压实胶结→碎屑沉积岩。D：已有岩石受热、受压等，保持主要固态→变质岩。E：发生熔融→岩浆。

五个字母是一条可行路径，不是必须依次通过的五站。我们的C箭头简化合并了沉积与成岩，不是“一落水底立刻变硬”。冷却也不等于地表风化。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Connect processes, not just arrows

A: magma cools/crystallises → igneous rock. B: surface weathering and erosion/transport → loose sediment. C: deposition, compaction/cementation → clastic sedimentary rock. D: heat/pressure while mainly solid → metamorphic rock. E: melting → magma.

These letters show one possible route, not five mandatory stations. C combines deposition with later lithification: settling does not instantly make hard rock. Cooling is also different from surface weathering.

<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 捷径，比完整一圈更真实

火成岩可直接变质，不必先成为沉积岩；变质岩也可以先被剥露、风化，不必先熔融。同一块岩体不同部分还可能经历不同条件，形成混合的记录。

在旅行操作中，从火成岩选“受热／受压，保持固态”走捷径，再尝试熔融、冷却、风化与成岩。按钮只展示状态可怎样转换；不规定各过程时间长短，也不表示所有岩石都曾熔融或都将走完一圈。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Shortcuts are part of the story

Igneous rock can metamorphose directly, without first becoming sedimentary. Metamorphic rock can be exposed and weathered without melting first. Different parts of a rock body may experience different conditions and retain mixed records.

Start from igneous material and use heat/pressure while solid for a shortcut. Then try melting, cooling, weathering and lithification. Buttons explore possible state changes, without durations or a claim that every rock once melted or must complete a loop.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：变质主要保持固态；熔融后那部分成为岩浆。沉积物和沉积岩不同，沉积之后还要成岩。不同颜色可以属于同类，相似条带也可能有不同成因。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** metamorphism mainly stays solid; melted material becomes magma. Loose sediment differs from sedimentary rock, with lithification after deposition. Different colours may share an origin, and similar bands may have different origins.

Ask: “Which material state must this arrow start from?” Paper alternative: draw five state cards, connect them with conditions and add a shortcut. Optional investigation: compare two existing photographs or lawfully obtained specimens, listing observations and unknowns rather than naming by colour. No smashing, heating, acid washing, licking or collecting from protected land is needed.
