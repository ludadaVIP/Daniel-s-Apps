<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 上升不是更靠近太阳取暖

气块升到外界压力更低处，会膨胀并冷却。未饱和气块的理想干绝热冷却率约9.8°C/km；这里把抬升作为外加条件，不计算气块是否会自行上升。水汽本身不可见，接近饱和时可在凝结核上形成小液滴；云也可含冰晶。

真实成云还有地形抬升、空气汇合、锋面、地表受热等通路。本课只选“抬升冷却”一条，不能把它当作所有云的唯一来源。云的形状也不能独自给出完整天气过程。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Rising air cools by expansion

A parcel lifted into lower surrounding pressure expands and cools. An ideal unsaturated parcel’s dry adiabatic cooling rate is about 9.8°C/km. Lift is imposed here; we do not solve whether the parcel rises on its own. Vapour is invisible; near saturation it can form droplets on condensation nuclei. Clouds can also contain ice crystals.

Terrain, convergence, fronts and surface heating can lift air. This lesson selects one pathway, not the sole cause of every cloud; cloud shape alone does not reveal a full weather process.

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 同温度，水汽条件改变云底

两例地面都是20°C，露点分别16°C与6°C。露点是饱和温度指标，较接近初温表示较湿条件。未饱和上升时假设温度每km降9.8°C、露点约降2°C；于是估计云底为温度与露点差除7.8，约0.51 km与1.79 km。

先在1 km比较：较湿例有云、较干例没有。再升到2 km：两例都超过估计云底。较干不等于永远不能成云。过云底后用6°C/km示例表示凝结释放潜热后冷却较慢；真实湿绝热率并不固定。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Equal temperature, different cloud bases

Both surface parcels start at 20°C; dew points are 16°C and 6°C. Dew point indicates saturation temperature; being closer to initial temperature indicates moister conditions. We approximate unsaturated ascent with temperature falling 9.8°C/km and dew point about 2°C/km. Estimated base is their initial difference divided by 7.8: about 0.51/1.79 km.

At 1 km the moister case is cloudy, the drier case is not. At 2 km both exceed estimated bases. Drier does not mean cloud is impossible. Above base, a chosen 6°C/km shows slower cooling with latent-heat release; actual moist lapse rate varies.

### 03 / 看见云，还不能宣布下雨

小液滴和冰晶散射光，让云可见；白色不是水汽气体的颜色。小云滴下落很慢，可随气流悬浮。要形成降水，粒子需增长，现实可涉及液滴碰并或冰晶过程；降下的水也可能在到地面之前蒸发。

增长开关只是假设“仍小／已经长大”的对照，不是按一下就真实生成雨，也不模拟等待时长。新西兰山地和世界其他地方，都须查看实际水汽、抬升、稳定度等资料。下一课会把山地抬升应用到迎风与背风一侧。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Visible cloud does not establish rain

Droplets/ice scatter light, making clouds visible; white is not the colour of vapour gas. Tiny cloud drops settle slowly and can remain aloft with air motion. Precipitation particles must grow, through drop collisions/coalescence or ice processes among other mechanisms; falling water may evaporate before reaching ground.

The growth control only compares assumed tiny versus grown particles. A click does not generate real rain or simulate waiting time. New Zealand mountains and other regions require actual moisture, lift, stability and other evidence. Next apply terrain lift to windward and leeward sides.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：云不等于可见水汽气体；较干不等于永远不成云；有云不一定下雨。滑块表示km而非分钟，6°C/km仅为示例。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** clouds are not visible vapour gas. Drier does not mean cloud impossible; cloud does not guarantee rain. Slider units are km, not minutes; 6°C/km is a chosen example.

Record both parcels at 0 km and a cloudy height; compare at 1 km on the way. While cloudy, compare tiny drops/grown-particle conditions. Explain expansion cooling, visible cloud material, precipitation conditions, moisture difference and forecast limits.

Ask: “Which parcel becomes cloudy first? Did you change moisture or initial temperature? Why is rain uncertain?” Paper alternative: draw 0/1/2 km and both estimated cloud bases, expanding parcels and droplets. With existing cloud photos, write observed/inferred/missing-evidence columns; appearance alone is no precise forecast.
