### 01 / 先找推动的差异

空气受气压梯度力作用时，力指向较低气压。虚构例把同一距离两端设为1014/1010 hPa，或1022/1010 hPa。切换4与12 hPa，看数字差怎样改变；更大梯度在其他条件相同下产生更强加速作用，但本图不把它换算成m/s。

蓝色等压线相差2 hPa。比较不同地图时必须同时核对线间数值与距离比例。温度受热差异等过程会影响气压分布；这里把气压作为已知条件，并未模拟其全部成因。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Find the driving difference

Pressure-gradient force points toward lower pressure. At fixed distance our invented endpoint pairs are 1014/1010 hPa or 1022/1010 hPa. Switch 4/12 hPa to compare numerical differences. With other conditions equal, a larger gradient gives stronger acceleration; this graphic does not convert it to m/s.

Blue isobars differ by 2 hPa. Across different maps, check both contour intervals and distance scales. Uneven heating and other processes affect pressure distributions; here pressure is prescribed rather than its full origins simulated.

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 力不等于最后的风向

仅气压梯度力例中，初始静止气块开始向低压加速。真实大尺度空气还受地球自转影响：沿运动方向看，北半球偏右，南半球偏左。高压在左时初始向东；北半球右侧在图上是南，南半球左侧是北。交换高低压，初始向西，右侧就变成北。

曲线仅展示这个相对方向规则，不是计算所有力后得到的轨迹。现实风还受到摩擦等影响，高处大尺度风常大致沿等压线，近地面风可能斜穿等压线。不要把红色力箭头当成真实风速预报。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Force is not the final wind direction

With pressure-gradient force alone, a parcel initially at rest accelerates toward lower pressure. Rotation also affects large-scale motion: right relative to motion in the Northern Hemisphere, left in the Southern. With higher pressure left, initial motion is east; northern right is map south, southern left is north. Swap pressures: initial motion west makes right point north.

The curve only illustrates that relative-direction rule, not a fully solved trajectory. Real wind also depends on friction; large-scale flow aloft often roughly follows isobars, while near-surface flow may cross them obliquely. The red force arrow is not a real wind-speed forecast.

<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 来自哪里，才是风名

从西吹向东叫西风，从东吹向西叫东风。箭头常画空气去向，风名说来向，两者不能混用。南半球的新西兰与北半球的欧洲使用同一来向命名，但偏转规则左右相反。

同压例没有气压梯度加速；因为本例初始静止并省略其他驱动，它显示不移动。现实中已有运动不会因为这一个力消失就立即停下。照片中的云也不是风速计；要判断真实风需相应的观测与天气资料。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Wind names describe origin

Air moving west to east is a west wind; east to west is an east wind. Arrows often show destination, while names give origin. New Zealand and Europe use the same naming convention but opposite hemispheric deflection rules.

The equal-pressure case has no pressure-gradient acceleration; it shows no motion because our parcel starts at rest with other drivers omitted. Existing real motion does not instantly stop when one force disappears. Clouds in a photo are not an anemometer; real wind needs suitable observations/weather evidence.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：高低压指相对气压，不是高度。北右南左相对于运动；等压线需读数值和地图尺度。零气压梯度不等于任何现实场景都无风。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** high/low pressure means relative pressure, not altitude. Northern right/southern left are relative to motion. Read isobar values and map scales. Zero pressure gradient does not mean no wind in every real situation.
