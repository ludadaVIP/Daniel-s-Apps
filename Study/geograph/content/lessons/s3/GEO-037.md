同一张南岛影像里，山脉两侧会遇到相同的天气吗？1981–2010年机场站年降水常年值：格雷茅斯2452毫米，基督城594毫米。先猜差异的原因，再检验风向、水汽与山高；照片是一刻，数字则是长期统计。
<!-- en -->

Would both sides of the Southern Alps experience identical weather? Airport annual rainfall normals for 1981–2010 are 2452 mm at Greymouth and 594 mm at Christchurch. Predict a cause, then test flow, moisture and ridge height. The photograph is a moment; the numbers are long-term statistics.

<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 用长期对照找到问题

原创地图只放两站的年常年值，并未把它们插值成全岛雨量图。点是大致城市参照位置；山带和西风箭头也为概略。数值同属1981–2010基准，可比较这两站，却不是今天预报或每个地点的情况。

南岛位于中纬度常见西风影响区，西侧常迎接来自塔斯曼海的湿气流。两站对照与地形、风和水汽的过程证据结合，才能支持解释；它没有单独排除所有其他原因。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Find a question with long-term evidence

The original map plots two annual normals without interpolating a whole-island rain field. Dots are coarse city references; mountain band and westerly arrow are approximate. Both values share 1981–2010, so these stations can be compared. They are not today’s forecast or conditions everywhere.

South Island lies in the midlatitude westerly region; its west often receives moist Tasman Sea airflow. Combine the station contrast with terrain, wind and moisture evidence to support a mechanism. The two values alone do not eliminate every other cause.

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 让气流爬坡，再反转

本例气块初温20°C，露点16°C或6°C，山高可取0、1、2 km。抬升膨胀冷却，达到估计云底才出现云；本例再假设云粒子增长，凝结水在山顶前全部降出。背风下降按无云气块的9.8°C/km增温，相对湿度降低。

沿用上一课干／湿近似率，不计算降水量或效率。西风先爬西坡，东风先爬东坡；迎风与背风是相对于来流的关系，不是山永远固有的东西标签。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Lift airflow, then reverse it

Our parcel begins at 20°C, with dew point 16°C or 6°C and ridge heights 0/1/2 km. Lift expands/cools it; cloud appears only above estimated base. We additionally assume particle growth and all condensate removed before the crest. Cloud-free lee descent warms at 9.8°C/km, lowering relative humidity.

The previous lesson’s dry/moist approximations calculate no precipitation amount or efficiency. Westerlies rise on the west first; easterlies rise on the east first. Windward and lee are relations to flow, not permanent east/west labels.

<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 有山不等于必定有雨

较干气块经过1 km山峰时不到估计云底；到2 km山顶却能成云。去掉山峰后，本模型没有这一路抬升，不能理解为现实平原永远不下雨。现实还有锋面、汇合、地表受热等抬升，降水也可能溢过山脊。

南岛长期西侧较湿，不表示每个东风天气也一样。世界其他雨影区，如北美大盆地，也要查看当地山脉、水汽路径和长期证据。本课的帧不是小时，山峰不是实测剖面。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / A ridge does not guarantee rain

The drier parcel stays below cloud base over a 1 km ridge but reaches cloud conditions at a 2 km crest. Removing the ridge removes this lift pathway, not all possible rain on real plains. Fronts, convergence, surface heating and other processes can lift air; precipitation can spill across a crest.

A wetter western South Island climatology need not describe each easterly event. Other rain-shadow regions, such as North America’s Great Basin, need their own terrain, moisture paths and long-term evidence. Frames are not hours and this ridge is not surveyed.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：迎风取决于来流；背风不等于永远无雨。水汽减少与相对湿度下降不同，增温也会影响后者。两站常年值不是全岛地图，假定云水全降出不是真实降水效率。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** windward depends on flow; lee is not permanently rain-free. Vapour loss and lowered relative humidity differ; warming also affects the latter. Two normals are not a whole-island field, and complete condensate removal is not a measured precipitation efficiency.

<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

读取两站常年值。湿气块与2 km山峰下，分别看西风、东风的山顶帧2和离开帧4；再比较较干气块1/2 km山顶，以及0 km无山的中央位置。完成来向、冷却、背风较干、降水条件和时间尺度推理。
<!-- en -->

Read both station normals. With moist air and a 2 km ridge, inspect crest frame 2 and leaving frame 4 for west/east flow. Then compare drier 1/2 km crest cases and 0 km no-ridge centre. Explain origin direction, cooling, drier lee, precipitation conditions and time scale.

<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

问：“哪些数字是实测汇总，哪些条件是我们设定的？风反向后哪一坡先爬？”纸笔替代：标两站数值，画同一山峰，用纸气块从两侧移动；用两条云底线比较1/2 km山顶。可以口述原因链，不要求背所有率值。
<!-- en -->

Ask: “Which numbers summarise observations and which conditions did we choose? With reversed flow, which slope rises first?” Paper alternative: label the two normals, move a paper parcel across one ridge from either side, and compare 1/2 km crests with both cloud-base lines. Explain the chain aloud; rate memorisation is unnecessary.
