<!-- section:hook -->
<!-- zh -->

2020年1月11日南岛附近红点网格估计有1.04 mm/day降水。能因此把这里叫“多雨气候”吗？先预测，再切到1月15日；两个日期都已经过去，地图不是今天的天气。
<!-- en -->

On 11 January 2020, the South Island vicinity red-point cell has estimated precipitation of 1.04 mm/day. Does that establish a wet climate? Predict, then switch to 15 January. Both dates are historical; this is not today’s weather.

<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 一天能回答多大的问题？

两张降水图是各日00–24 UTC的日平均率换算，单位mm/day液态水当量。红点在42.8564°S、172.5°E，网格东西间隔1.875°，并非基督城气象站。它结合模型与观测重建天气，降水是模型估计；不能用来断言某一屋顶是否淋雨。

1月15日红点显示0.00，是保留两位小数后的值。两个日期特意用于比较，不是随机样本，也没有表示该月雨天比例。先读日期、单位、位置与来源，再谈结论。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / How much can one day answer?

The precipitation maps convert daily mean rates for 00–24 UTC into mm/day liquid-water equivalent. The red point is 42.8564°S, 172.5°E; longitude spacing is 1.875°, not a Christchurch station. Reanalysis combines modelling and observations; precipitation is model-estimated and cannot establish whether an individual roof was wet.

The red point’s 0.00 on 15 January is rounded to two decimals. These days were selected for comparison, not sampled randomly; no rainy-day fraction is shown. Read date, units, position and source before making claims.

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 平均不是每天的承诺

长期图汇总1991–2020年各月份，每月有30个有效年份。一月温度15.2°C是模型地表上2 m的月平均；一月平均每日降水率2.06 mm/day不是月总量，更不是每天一定下2.06 mm。气候还包括波动、极值和出现频率，本图仅保留平均。

温度线和降水柱放在两张独立纵轴图，避免把°C和mm/day误当同一单位。月平均每日率若要估计月总量还须乘该月天数；这里不需要做这个换算。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / A mean is not a daily promise

Long-term charts combine 1991–2020, with 30 valid years for every month. January temperature 15.2°C is a monthly mean at 2 m above model surface. January mean daily precipitation rate 2.06 mm/day is neither a monthly total nor a promise of 2.06 mm each day. Climate also includes variation, extremes and frequencies; only means are retained here.

Temperature and precipitation have separate plots/axes so °C and mm/day cannot be mistaken for one unit. Converting a mean daily rate to an estimated monthly amount also requires the month’s day count; this activity does not require that conversion.

<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 季节与趋势，分开读

南岛网格一月比七月暖；欧洲网格七月比一月暖。这个比较支持两地的季节模式不同，却不支持“全球每个地方都一样”。月份1–12的横轴也不能告诉我们1991到2020是否逐年变暖：跨年信息已被汇总。

将“某天降水图”“三天预报”“30年一月统计”“几十年跨年份趋势”分类。最后说明：要判断多雨气候，还需要长期、有代表性的降水资料及比较参照，而非一场雨。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Read season and trend separately

The South Island cell is warmer in January than July; the European cell shows the reverse. This supports different seasonal patterns at these two cells, not identical patterns everywhere. A month 1–12 axis cannot reveal whether 1991–2020 warmed year by year: those years have been combined.

Classify a daily rain map, three-day forecast, 30-year January statistic and multi-decade yearly trend. Then explain why judging a wet climate needs representative long-term precipitation evidence and a comparison reference, beyond one shower.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：气候不只等于平均；一次事件不决定长期特征。常年值不是预报。平均每日降水率不是月总量；0.00是舍入值；网格不等于城市。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** climate includes more than means; one event cannot establish long-term characteristics. Normals are not forecasts. Mean daily rate is not monthly total; 0.00 is rounded; a grid cell is not a city.

Inspect both historical days and both hemisphere cells; classify four time-scale cards. Use graphics to judge daily claims, mean interpretation and seasonal pattern. The task checks specified chart relations; your own explanation must include an evidence limit.
