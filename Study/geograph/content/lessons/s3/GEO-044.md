<!-- section:hook -->
<!-- zh -->

为什么有些海上低压会组织成强大的热带气旋，而另一些不会？就算两场风暴的风很强，它们造成的风险会一样吗？这课用安全的概念图学习条件和风险，不追逐风暴，也不把模型当预报。
<!-- en -->

Why do some ocean low-pressure systems organize into powerful tropical cyclones while others do not? Even if two storms have strong winds, will their risks be the same? This lesson uses safe concept diagrams for conditions and risk; it does not chase storms or treat a model as a forecast.

<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 一条概念能量链

热带气旋通常在温暖的热带或副热带海洋上发展。暖海帮助蒸发，把水汽和能量带入近海面的空气；湿空气上升、冷却并凝结成云雨时会释放潜热，进一步支持上升和低压环流。地球自转、已有的扰动和合适的环境结构也参与其中。

这是一条概念链，不是“暖水一出现就有气旋”的开关。课程图没有地点、日期、海温观测或风场资料，不能预报任何一场风暴。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / A conceptual energy chain

Tropical cyclones usually develop over warm tropical or subtropical oceans. Warm ocean supports evaporation, putting moisture and energy into near-surface air; when moist air rises, cools and condenses into cloud/rain, latent heat release can further support rising motion and low-pressure circulation. Earth’s rotation, a pre-existing disturbance and suitable environmental structure also matter.

This is a concept chain, not a switch saying “warm water always makes a cyclone.” The diagram has no location, date, sea-surface observation or wind field, and cannot forecast a storm.

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 增强与减弱都看多个条件

热带气旋需要持续的暖水和湿空气等支持条件；强的垂直**风切变**可把不同高度的云和环流拉开，较冷水、干空气、陆地摩擦和地形也可能削弱或改变系统。每场风暴的路径和强度还受更大尺度环流影响。

因此不能从一张海温图断言某地必有风暴，也不能从一次安静天气否定长期危险。真实警报请看当地权威气象和应急部门的最新信息。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Intensification and weakening use many conditions

Tropical cyclones need support such as sustained warm water and moist air. Strong vertical **wind shear** can pull clouds and circulation at different heights apart; cooler water, dry air, land friction and terrain can weaken or alter a system. Each storm’s track and intensity are also shaped by larger-scale circulation.

Do not declare a storm from one sea-temperature map, or use one quiet day to deny long-term hazard. For real alerts, use current information from local official weather and emergency services.

<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 天气危险不等于灾害风险

强风、暴雨和风暴潮是**危险性**；有多少人、房屋、道路或服务位于可能受影响的地方，是**暴露度**；建筑质量、预警、资源、健康和恢复能力等会影响**脆弱性**。它们共同塑造风险。相同风速经过人口稀少的海面和经过低洼密集社区，潜在后果可能很不同。

这个框架不能责怪受影响的人，也不能算出某处的精确风险。它帮助我们提出公平的问题：谁得到预警？谁能够安全撤离或修复？
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Weather hazard is not disaster risk

Strong wind, heavy rain and storm surge are **hazards**. How many people, homes, roads or services are in a potentially affected place is **exposure**. Building quality, warnings, resources, health and ability to recover influence **vulnerability**. Together they shape risk. The same wind crossing open ocean and a low-lying dense community can have very different potential consequences.

This framework does not blame people affected by disasters or calculate an exact local risk. It helps ask fair questions: who receives warnings, and who can evacuate or recover safely?

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：暖海是支持条件之一，不是保证；风暴强度不等于风险；概念图不等于实时预报。不要前往海岸、河道或危险地点观察风暴。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** warm ocean is one support, not a guarantee; storm intensity is not the same as risk; a concept diagram is not a live forecast. Do not visit coasts, rivers or other hazardous places to observe a storm.

<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

追踪暖海—湿空气—云雨的概念能量链，比较支持或削弱风暴的多个条件，再把危险性、暴露度和脆弱性分开。用自己的话解释为什么模型不能预测某地明天的风暴。
<!-- en -->

Trace the warm-ocean → moist-air → cloud/rain conceptual energy chain, compare multiple conditions that support or weaken a storm, then separate hazard, exposure and vulnerability. Explain why the model cannot predict tomorrow’s storm at a particular place.
