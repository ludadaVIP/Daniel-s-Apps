<!-- section:hook -->
<!-- zh -->

同样是一片陆地，为什么有的地方长成森林，有的开阔成草原，有的只在短暂夏季长出低矮植物？温度和水分提供重要线索，但它们能不能单独画出每一处植被？
<!-- en -->

Why does one land area grow forest, another stay open grassland, and another support low plants only during a short summer? Temperature and water give important clues, but can they draw every place’s vegetation by themselves?

<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 两个轴，先做一个有用的猜测

**生物群系**是用长期气候、植被和生物关系概括出的较大生态类型。入门时，可把温度和可利用水分放在两条轴上：长期温暖且水分较多的地点更可能支持森林；温暖但水分较少时，开阔草本植被或干旱植被更可能出现；寒冷、短生长季的陆地环境可接近苔原。

这是概念图，不是完整分类钥匙。图中的“更可能”不能改成“必定”。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Two axes make a useful first guess

A **biome** is a broad ecological type summarized from long-term climate, vegetation and living relationships. As a first model, put temperature and available water on two axes. Places that are warm with more water may support forest; warm places with less water may have more open grass or dry vegetation; cold land with a short growing season can be closer to tundra.

This is a concept graph, not a complete classification key. “More likely” must not become “guaranteed.”

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 同样气候，不保证同样景观

土壤深浅和养分、火、食草动物、洪水、坡向、海拔以及人类种植或砍伐，都能改变植物实际长成的样子。例如火可以维持开阔地；同一纬度上高海拔通常较冷；一次牧场或种植园的航拍图也不必等于自然群系。

所以地图上的颜色需要图例、时间、尺度和来源。它可以帮我们提出“这里为什么是灌丛？”但不能替我们回答全部问题。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Same climate does not guarantee the same landscape

Soil depth/nutrients, fire, grazers, flooding, slope aspect, altitude and planting or clearing can change what plants actually grow. Fire can maintain open land; high altitude at the same latitude is often cooler; an aerial image of pasture or plantation need not equal a natural biome.

Map colours therefore need a legend, time, scale and source. A map can prompt “Why is there shrubland here?” but cannot answer every question for us.

<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 从气候线索到现场证据

较好的地理推理是一条链：长期温度与水分 → 对可能植被的假设 → 现场或可靠资料检查。例如比较山坡两侧时，可记录海拔、坡向、土壤、遮阴、水分、火史和土地利用，再看植物覆盖是否不同。选择相邻地点并使用相同观察方法，结论才更可比。

群系边界在自然界往往是渐变或镶嵌，而不是课本上的粗线。人们也会用不同目的和尺度划分名称，因此先说清使用哪一种资料和范围。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / From climate clue to field evidence

Better geographic reasoning is a chain: long-term temperature/water → a hypothesis about possible vegetation → a field or reliable-source check. Comparing two slopes might record altitude, aspect, soil, shade, moisture, fire history and land use, then compare plant cover. Nearby places and the same observation method make conclusions more comparable.

Biome boundaries in nature are often gradual or patchy, not thick textbook lines. People also classify for different purposes and scales, so say which source and extent you are using.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：生物群系不是只由气温决定，也不是每一格地图只能有一种植被。下雪的一天不能证明苔原；一片树也不能自动证明雨林。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** biomes are not determined by temperature alone, and each map cell does not hold only one vegetation type. One snowy day does not prove tundra; trees alone do not prove rainforest.

<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

匹配温暖潮湿、温暖较干和寒冷案例，检查概念图中森林、草原与苔原的位置。再把土壤、火、食草动物、海拔或土地利用加入解释，说明为什么还需要现场证据。
<!-- en -->

Match warm/wet, warm/drier and cold cases, checking forest, grassland and tundra on the concept graph. Then add soil, fire, grazers, altitude or land use to an explanation and say why field evidence is still needed.

<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

问：“如果两个地方同样温暖，还可能有什么不同？”“这张图告诉你什么，又没有告诉你什么？”纸笔替代：画温度—水分十字轴，把三张地点卡放上去；再在卡片背面写一个必须查证的当地因素。
<!-- en -->

Ask: “If two places are equally warm, what else may differ?” “What does this diagram tell you, and what does it not tell you?” Paper alternative: draw temperature/water axes, place three location cards, then add one local factor to check on each card’s back.
