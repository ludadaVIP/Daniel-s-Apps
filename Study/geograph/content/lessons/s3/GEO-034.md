<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 五层是理解工具

按温度随高度的变化，可把大气主要分成对流层、平流层、中间层、热层、外逸层。图中对流层顶取12 km作为示例；现实约6–20 km且随地点、季节变化。多数天气在对流层；臭氧层主要位于平流层，吸收太阳紫外线；许多流星在中间层发光烧蚀。

国际空间站约400 km位于热层。很高的气体温度不等于空气密集，因为那里气体稀薄。外逸层逐渐过渡到太空，层界不是坚硬外壳。图块不按高度或质量比例画。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Five layers help organise evidence

Temperature changes with height define five main atmospheric layers: troposphere, stratosphere, mesosphere, thermosphere and exosphere. Our chosen tropopause is 12 km; actual heights roughly span 6–20 km and vary with place/season. Most weather occurs in the troposphere; stratospheric ozone absorbs solar UV; many meteors glow and ablate in the mesosphere.

The ISS at roughly 400 km lies in the thermosphere. High gas temperature does not imply dense air; gas there is thin. The exosphere fades gradually into space, without a rigid shell. Bands represent neither height nor air mass to scale.

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 太阳光与地面热辐射

太阳辐射以较短波长为主；地面吸收能量后，会发出较长波长的热红外辐射。水汽、二氧化碳等与部分红外相互作用，吸收并发射，包括向下与向上。不同气体、云和波段的作用不同。

点击红外透明例与单层吸收例。透明例太阳输入7，地面发7到太空；吸收例太阳仍输入7，大气吸收地面发出的14，再向上发7、向下发7。地面得到7+7，发出14；整体入7出7。内部交换没有创造能量。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Sunlight and surface thermal radiation

Solar radiation has mainly shorter wavelengths; the warmed surface emits longer-wave thermal infrared. Water vapour, carbon dioxide and other constituents interact with parts of this IR, absorbing and emitting both upward and downward. Gases, clouds and spectral bands differ.

Compare the IR-transparent and single absorbing-layer cases. Transparent: solar input 7 and surface 7 escape to space. Absorbing: solar input remains 7; the layer absorbs surface 14 and emits 7 upward plus 7 downward. Surface receives 7+7 and emits 14; the whole system receives 7 and emits 7. Internal exchange creates no energy.

<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 被子的比喻哪里不够？

同样太阳输入下，吸收例地面需要较大的红外发射才能平衡，代表辐射平衡地面更暖的方向，不是14°C或升温7°C。现实大气不是全部红外不透明的一层；还有反射、对流、蒸发、云与复杂光谱。本模型不估算真实地球温度。

“被子”能提示能量散失受影响，却不能解释所有辐射路径，更不表示大气把热永久封住。2011年7月31日照片可观察地球边缘亮带；月球在大气之外，视觉邻近不代表空间邻近。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Where the blanket analogy runs out

For equal solar input, the absorbing case needs greater surface IR emission to balance, indicating a warmer equilibrium surface—not 14°C or 7°C warming. Real air is not one fully IR-opaque layer; reflection, convection, evaporation, clouds and spectral complexity also matter. This model estimates no real Earth temperature.

A blanket analogy suggests changed energy loss but does not explain every radiative path or permanent trapping. The 31 July 2011 photograph provides a visible limb band. The Moon lies outside the atmosphere; apparent proximity does not imply physical proximity.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：高度层界可变；图块不按比例。臭氧吸收太阳紫外线与温室红外交换要分开。辐射模型份数不是°C，大气也发射能量。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** layer boundaries vary and bands are not to scale. Distinguish ozone’s solar UV absorption from greenhouse IR exchange. Model energy units are not °C; atmosphere also emits energy.

把天气、臭氧、流星、空间站、外逸边缘五个现象匹配到层；切换两种红外条件并逐边界核对收支。最后判断再发射机制和层界是否固定。

Match weather, ozone, meteors, ISS and outer transition to layers; compare both IR conditions and check each boundary’s budget. Explain re-emission and variable layer boundaries.
