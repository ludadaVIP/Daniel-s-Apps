<!-- section:hook -->
<!-- zh -->

南极海岸与南阿尔卑斯山高处都可能很冷。它们是不是同一种环境？如果一只动物有厚毛，它的厚毛究竟证明了什么，又不能证明什么？
<!-- en -->

Antarctic coasts and high parts of the Southern Alps can both be cold. Are they the same environment? If an animal has thick fur, what does it show—and what does it not show?

<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 共同的冷，不同的组合

极地环境的低太阳高度、漫长冬夜、海冰或冰盖会影响温度、季节和可获得资源；但极地内部、海岸和海洋边缘仍有差异。高山环境也会冷，却由高海拔带来较低气压、较稀薄空气、强风、陡坡和可能较强的紫外线暴露。山地的坡向和积雪又会制造小尺度差异。

因此“很冷”只是一个起点。先比较条件，再讨论生命怎样应对。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Shared cold, different combinations

In polar environments, low sun angle, long winter darkness, sea ice or ice sheets affect temperature, season and available resources; interior, coast and ocean edge still differ. Alpine environments can also be cold, but altitude brings lower pressure, thinner air, wind, steep slopes and potentially stronger UV exposure. Slope aspect and snow can create small-scale mountain differences.

So “very cold” is only a starting point. Compare conditions before discussing how life responds.

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 适应要带着条件讲

**适应**不是“某个动物努力后选择的超能力”。在世代尺度上，某些遗传特征或行为在特定条件下可能提高生存和繁殖机会。厚毛、脂肪层、紧凑体形、避风处、迁徙、休眠或短生长季快速开花，都是需要说明对象和条件的例子。

例如保温可以减少散热，但形成和维持组织也需要能量；迁徙可避开严酷季节，却需要路线和资源。好处总要连同限制与证据来描述。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Explain adaptation with conditions

An **adaptation** is not a superpower an animal chooses through effort. Over generations, inherited features or behaviours can improve survival and reproduction in particular conditions. Thick fur, fat layers, compact shape, sheltering, migration, dormancy or rapid flowering in a short growing season are examples that need an organism and conditions named.

Insulation can reduce heat loss, but building and maintaining tissue needs energy; migration can avoid harsh seasons but needs routes and resources. Describe a benefit alongside limits and evidence.

<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 从照片到可检验问题

一张照片显示厚毛、雪地或矮小植物，只能提供线索。更好的问题是：“这个物种在哪个季节、哪个海拔、用什么行为和身体特征减少散热？”“它在食物不足或快速变暖时会遇到什么限制？”回答需要长期观察、物种资料和当地环境数据。

本课图解是原创概念模型，不代表某一物种的完整机制，也不预测种群数量、天气或气候变化后果。尊重适应的复杂性，比把生命讲成永远战胜环境更准确。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / From a photo to testable questions

A photo showing thick fur, snow or low plants supplies only a clue. Better questions are: “In which season and at what altitude does this species reduce heat loss through behaviour and body features?” “What limits might it face when food is scarce or warming is rapid?” Answers need long-term observations, species information and local environmental data.

This lesson’s diagrams are original concept models. They do not represent one species’ full mechanism or predict population, weather or climate-change outcomes. Respecting complexity is more accurate than presenting life as always defeating an environment.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：极地和高山都冷，不等于条件相同。一个特征也不保证在所有情境有利；适应不是个体临时决定长出来的，更不能用来预测明天的天气。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** polar and alpine places may both be cold without having identical conditions. One trait is not beneficial in every situation; adaptation is not a feature an individual grows on demand, and it cannot predict tomorrow’s weather.

<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

比较极地和高山的条件，选择一个保温或季节性策略，按“条件→特征→好处→取舍”解释。最后判断为什么同一张动物照片不足以说明全部适应过程。
<!-- en -->

Compare polar and alpine conditions. Choose one insulation or seasonal strategy and explain “condition → trait → benefit → trade-off.” Finally decide why one animal photo cannot establish the entire adaptation process.

<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

问：“这个特征帮它解决哪一个具体问题？”“可能有什么代价？”纸笔替代：做四张箭头卡（条件、特征、好处、取舍），把一项假想观察连成完整句子。不要要求孩子背极地动物名单或把任何地方当作冒险目的地。
<!-- en -->

Ask: “Which specific problem could this trait help with?” “What might its cost be?” Paper alternative: make four arrow cards—condition, trait, benefit, trade-off—and connect one imagined observation into a complete sentence. No polar-animal list memorisation or adventure activity is needed.
