印度西岸附近的长期图中，6–9月的雨柱为什么会高起来？先预测原因，再比较陆地和海洋的季节冷热。主线以南亚印度洋路径为例；其他亚洲地区也有不同季风与天气系统，不能用一个图包办。
<!-- en -->

Why are rainfall bars higher in 6–9 at the west India vicinity cell? Predict, then compare seasonal land/ocean heating. This case focuses on South Asian Indian Ocean pathways; other Asian regions have differing monsoons/weather systems that one diagram cannot fully explain.

<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 陆海反应不同，但不只是海风变大

海洋的热容量、混合与蒸发等使它与陆地受热和冷却的反应不同。暖季大陆相对较暖可支持低压倾向，周边海面相对高压，低层气流趋向陆地；另一季节的热力对比可能反转。

季风是区域性季节环流，还涉及热带辐合带移动、地形、自转和天气系统。每天昼夜海陆风与它尺度不同。滑块−2到+2是自选冷热比较级，不是实际°C、hPa或月份。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Different land/ocean responses, beyond a large sea breeze

Ocean heat capacity, mixing, evaporation and other processes differ from land responses. Relatively warmer continental conditions can favour lower-pressure tendencies with higher pressure over surrounding ocean and surface flow toward land; seasonal contrast may reverse.

Monsoons are regional seasonal circulation involving convergence-zone shifts, terrain, rotation and weather systems. Daily land/sea breezes differ in scale. Slider−2…+2 is a chosen contrast level, not actual°C, h Pa or a month.

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 风把水汽带到哪里？

较暖陆地例画海→陆的广泛路径，较凉例画陆→海。H/L表示概念高低压，不是今日气压图；自转与其他力使真实风不会只按直线力箭头走。气流经过海面可得到水汽，抬升冷却与粒子增长等条件合适才可能形成降水。

凉季也不是全亚洲统一无雨。东北季风路径经过孟加拉湾可参与印度东南部降水；具体区域还受其他系统影响。来自某方向的风，不能单独决定沿途雨量。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Where does airflow carry moisture?

Warmer-land arrows indicate broad sea-to-land pathways; cooler-land arrows indicate land-to-sea. H/L are conceptual pressures, not today’s pressure map. Rotation/other forces mean real wind does not simply follow a straight force arrow. Ocean paths can gain moisture; lift/cooling and particle growth must also permit precipitation.

The cool season is not rain-free across all Asia. Northeast-monsoon paths across the Bay of Bengal can contribute to southeastern Indian rain, alongside other systems. Origin direction alone cannot determine rainfall along the path.

<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 用两个雨图检验概括

1991–2020 GPCP月图汇总卫星与雨量计资料。西岸附近18.75°N/73.75°E格子6–9月较湿；东南附近13.75°N/81.25°E格子10–12月也有较高雨量。后者含近海，不是钦奈城区的站点记录。

这里把mm/day长期月平均率乘相应平均月天数，得到mm/month；与GEO033的每日率不同。季节图支持不同模式，却不能单独证明全部成因，也不能预报今年何日开始。年份、地区、雨季内间歇都可能不同。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Test broad claims with two rain charts

1991–2020 GPCP combines satellite/gauge evidence. The west-vicinity 18.75°N/73.75°E cell is wetter in 6–9; the SE-vicinity 13.75°N/81.25°E cell also has higher rain in 10–12. The latter includes near-coast ocean, not Chennai city station observations.

Here monthly mean daily rates in mm/day are multiplied by mean month days to obtain mm/month, unlike GEO033’s daily-rate charts. Seasonal patterns alone neither establish every cause nor forecast this year’s onset. Regions, years and within-season breaks can differ.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：季风不是一次暴雨，也不是每天海风。海到陆不必然下雨，凉季不必然全无雨。月图的mm/month与每日率不同；滑块不含真实日期或风速。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** a monsoon is neither one downpour nor a daily sea breeze. Sea-to-land need not rain; cool seasons need not be entirely dry. mm/month differs from a daily rate; the slider has no actual date or wind speed.

<!-- en -->

Compare warmer/cooler/equal land–ocean contrast and both India-vicinity rainfall charts. Explain pressure tendencies, seasonal scale, rain conditions, the SE wet-season exception and model limits. Treat monsoon as a seasonal system requiring multiple conditions.
