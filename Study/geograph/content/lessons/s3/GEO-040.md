<!-- en -->

The 20 December 2025 southern Gobi image shows snow on mountains, steppe and some sandy terrain, alongside uncovered dunes. The 2012 Sahara photograph shows dunes/outcrops. Can snow or yellow sand alone establish climate? Predict the main condition worth comparing for deserts.

<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 先看水，再看温度

沙漠是长期水分供应很少的干燥环境，不要求全年炎热或地面全是沙。常见入门参考为年降水少于约250 mm，但正式干旱判断还要看温度、季节与蒸散需求。图上250毫米只是分档，不是自动分类程序。

切换1991–2020全球年降水与气温。降水为GPCP卫星／雨量计融合估计，温度为NCEP再分析2 m气温；原网格不同，样点切换后略移。这些格子不能等同整个沙漠或某个城市。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Read water, then temperature

Deserts have very limited long-term water supply, without requiring year-round heat or an entirely sandy surface. A common introductory reference is below about 250 mm annually; fuller aridity diagnosis also considers temperature, season and evaporative demand. Our 250 mm bin is not an automatic classification.

Compare 1991–2020 annual precipitation/temperature maps. GPCP combines satellite/gauge precipitation estimates; NCEP reanalysis supplies 2 m air temperature. Different original grids make markers shift slightly. Cells do not represent whole deserts or individual cities.

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 少雨有不同通路

撒哈拉的一个重要背景是副热带下沉空气较不利成云。戈壁受大陆内部水汽供应与山地屏障影响，冬季可冷、夏季可热。阿塔卡马附近冷海流、下沉与山地共同作用，沿岸雾不等于大量降雨。

南极内陆极冷，水汽供应少，年降水可很低；海岸可更湿。这些机制并非互斥或每一格唯一原因。世界图显示分布，需要过程资料来解释；不能从颜色反推出所有过程。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Different paths lead to dryness

Subtropical subsidence often inhibits Sahara clouds. Gobi moisture supply is affected by continental interior/mountain barriers; winters can be cold and summers hot. Cold currents, subsidence and terrain combine near Atacama; coastal fog is not heavy rain.

Antarctic interior is extremely cold with limited moisture and can have very low precipitation; coasts can be wetter. Mechanisms are not mutually exclusive or unique to every cell. Maps show patterns; process evidence explains them. Colour alone does not reveal every cause.

<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 避免三种扩大结论

戈壁温度样点一月−19.2°C、七月25.0°C，“冷沙漠”不是全年都零下。南极的多年冰储量也不是每年新落下的水；低降水与厚冰可以同时成立。照片中的雪，只证明拍摄时部分地表有雪。

全球降水图也包括海洋格子，不应称低雨海面为陆地沙漠。新西兰基督城比格雷茅斯较干，也不能仅凭相对比较宣布整个坎特伯雷为沙漠。先限定地点、统计基准与证据类型，再提出分类。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Avoid three enlarged claims

The Gobi temperature cell has January −19.2°C/July 25.0°C: cold desert does not mean below freezing year-round. Antarctic accumulated ice is not the annual input of new water; low precipitation and thick ice can coexist. A snowy photo establishes snow on some surfaces at capture time.

Precipitation maps include ocean cells; low-rain ocean is not land desert. Christchurch being drier than Greymouth cannot alone classify all Canterbury as desert. Limit claims by place, baseline and evidence type before classifying.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：干燥、炎热和沙丘不是同一个概念。一次雪不决定长期湿润，厚冰不等于年降水多。低降水地图不是完整沙漠分类；两种数据网格不是同一气象站。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** dryness, heat and dunes are different concepts. One snowfall does not establish wet climate; thick ice is not high annual rain. Low-precipitation maps are not complete desert classifications; two grids are not one station.

<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

比较两张真实影像、两个数据图层与四个少雨案例，匹配不同机制。判断共同的干燥特征、冬雪／冰储存、250 mm参考与新西兰相对干湿比较的局限。用证据说明至少一个冷热反例。
<!-- en -->

Compare two real images, both data layers and four dryness cases; match mechanisms. Explain shared dryness, snow/ice storage, the 250 mm reference and limits of relative NZ wet/dry comparisons. Support at least one hot/cold counterexample with evidence.
