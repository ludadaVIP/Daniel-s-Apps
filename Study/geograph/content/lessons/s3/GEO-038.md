全球年平均气温图通常呈现赤道附近较暖、高纬较冷的趋势，但青藏高原为何在相近纬度中偏冷？先猜纬度是否足够。颜色是1991–2020年模型2 m气温，不是今天温度或地表皮肤温度。
<!-- en -->

Annual temperature often shows warmer low latitudes and colder high latitudes. Why is the Tibetan Plateau colder than a nearby-latitude lowland? Predict whether latitude is sufficient. Colours show 1991–2020 model 2 m air temperature, not today’s weather or surface skin temperature.

<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 纬度带是起点，不是完整诊断

本图取23.4°回归线和66.6°极圈，整理热带、两个中纬度温带与两个高纬带。这是与地轴倾角有关的纬度热量带。回归线、极圈不能把每个地方的降水、月份温度或植被全部决定。

实际分类，例如Köppen，按温度、降水与季节统计规则区分类型；边界并不必然沿纬线。本课不要求背分类代码。先定位，再用真正气候图检验概括。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / Latitude belts are a starting point

Chosen 23.4° tropics and 66.6° polar circles organise a tropical belt, two temperate belts and two high-latitude belts. These astronomical heat belts relate to axial tilt. The boundary lines do not determine every place’s rain, monthly temperature or vegetation.

Actual schemes such as Köppen use temperature, precipitation and seasonal statistics; boundaries need not follow parallels. You need not memorise codes here. Locate first, then test broad claims with climate evidence.

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 同一光束，投到不同面积

在分点几何中，赤道正午太阳较高，75°N太阳较低。同一光束斜照时投到更宽地面，每单位面积分到的直接光能较少。切换六月与十二月：75°N出现极昼／极夜，43°S则呈相反季节的光照。

模型给正午太阳高度和白昼小时，不计算真实气温。它忽略天气、折射和地形，也没有用正午强度替代日总能量；极昼不等于一年里永远温暖。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / The same beam covers different areas

At equinox, the equatorial noon Sun is higher than at 75°N. Oblique incidence spreads one beam over more ground, reducing direct energy per unit area. Switch June/December: 75°N shows polar day/night while 43°S follows opposite-season geometry.

The model calculates noon altitude/daylight, not real temperatures. Weather, refraction and terrain are omitted; noon incidence is not total daily energy. Polar day is not permanent annual warmth.

<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 给纬度趋势加修正

原网格中，青藏高原与东亚低地两点同为31.428°N，年平均为−7.6和15.3°C。高海拔是重要线索；两格还存在其他差异，不能声称仅海拔精确造成22.9°C差。

洋流输送热量，海气交换与风影响海岸；海洋也可减小沿岸季节温差。新西兰约43°S位于中纬度，却不能简单套用欧洲相近纬度的冬季。温度、降水、地形、海洋与季节需要一起解释。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Add modifiers to the latitude pattern

The original-grid Tibetan and East Asian lowland cells share 31.428°N yet average−7.6/15.3°C annually. Elevation is an important clue; other differences remain, so elevation alone has not been shown to cause exactly 22.9°C difference.

Currents move heat; air–sea exchange and winds affect coasts, while oceans can moderate seasonal ranges. New Zealand near 43°S is midlatitude but cannot simply inherit European winters at a similar latitude. Explain temperature, rain, terrain, oceans and seasons together.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：纬度热量带不同于完整气候分类。光束面积不是温度，正午入射不是日总量。高海拔例外也不是全部由一个因素造成的实验证明。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** latitude heat belts differ from complete climate classification. Beam area is not temperature; noon incidence is not daily total. A highland contrast is not an experiment proving one factor explains the whole difference.

<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

查看纬度带与真实气温，给五个样点按纬度位置分类。比较分点0°与75°N，再比较75°N和43°S的六月／十二月。判断季节、海拔、洋流修正与分类局限。
<!-- en -->

Inspect latitude belts and real temperature; classify five cells by latitude position. Compare equinox 0°/75°N and June/December at 75°N/43°S. Explain seasonal, elevation, ocean-current modifiers and classification limits.
