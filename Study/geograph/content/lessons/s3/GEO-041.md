<!-- section:hook -->
<!-- zh -->

想象你从雨林的地面抬头：头顶不是一片平整的绿色天花板，而是高矮不同、明暗不同的许多层。为什么同一座森林里会有那么多不同的动物、植物和真菌？先不要把答案缩成“因为雨多”。
<!-- en -->

Imagine looking up from a rainforest floor. Above you is not one flat green ceiling, but many layers at different heights and light levels. Why can one forest contain so many different animals, plants and fungi? Do not shrink the answer to “because it rains.”

<!-- section:story-1 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 01 / 一座有高度的森林

热带雨林常年较温暖、较湿润，但最有用的观察起点是**垂直层次**：突出层的少数高树高过其他树冠；林冠层像较连续的叶屋顶；林下层得到较少直射光；林地接收的光通常最少。层次不是整齐楼层，树高、空隙、季节和天气都会改变光照。

不同高度的光、风、湿度、枝叶和食物并不相同。它们提供不同的生活位置，而不是保证每一层都有相同数量的物种。
<!-- en -->

### 01 / A forest with height

Tropical rainforests are often warm and wet for much of the year, but **vertical layers** are a useful starting observation. A few emergent trees rise above other crowns; the canopy is a relatively continuous leafy roof; the understorey receives less direct light; and the forest floor usually receives least. These are not neat floors: tree height, gaps, season and weather alter light.

Light, wind, humidity, branches and food differ with height. They offer different places to live; they do not guarantee the same number of species in every layer.

<!-- section:story-2 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 02 / 不是一个原因，而是一组机会

温暖和水分可以让植物在较长时间内生长；植物又改变光、庇护处和食物。树洞、附生植物、花、果实、腐木和阴湿土壤都可能成为不同生物利用的资源组合。生物使用资源并与其他生物发生关系的方式和位置叫作**生态位**。

“可能有更多生态位”是解释，不是精确物种计数。土壤、历史、干扰、面积、与其他栖息地的连接及人类活动也会影响多样性。不要从一项条件推到唯一原因。
<!-- en -->

### 02 / Not one cause, but many opportunities

Warmth and water can let plants grow for long periods; plants then change light, shelter and food. Tree holes, epiphytes, flowers, fruit, dead wood and damp soil can become different resource combinations. A **niche** is the way and place an organism uses resources and relates to other organisms.

“May create more niches” is an explanation, not a precise species count. Soil, history, disturbance, area, connection to other habitats and human activity also affect diversity. Do not turn one condition into the only cause.

<!-- section:story-3 -->
<!-- zh -->

### 03 / 看见绿色，不等于完成调查

一张雨林照片可以显示拍摄时看见的树、光线或水汽；它不能直接告诉我们所有物种、数量、夜行动物、地下生命或一整年的变化。要比较多样性，需要清楚的调查问题，例如在林冠空隙与阴暗林下，用相同方法记录植物种类或幼苗数量，并说明时间和范围。

本课的三张图是原创教学示意：它们用于比较关系，不是按比例雨林地图、物种名录或真实遥感图。图解让我们提出好问题，再由观察和可靠资料检验。
<!-- en -->

### 03 / Seeing green is not completing a survey

One rainforest photo can show trees, light or moisture visible at capture time. It cannot directly tell us every species, abundance, nocturnal animals, underground life or a whole year of change. Comparing diversity needs a clear question: for example, record plant types or seedlings with the same method in a canopy gap and shaded understorey, then state time and area.

The three diagrams in this lesson are original teaching schematics. They compare relationships; they are not to-scale rainforest maps, species inventories or real satellite imagery. Diagrams help us ask useful questions, which observations and reliable sources can test.

<!-- section:misconception -->
<!-- zh -->

**容易混淆**：雨多不自动等于“每个地方物种最多”。“林冠层”也不是所有树一样高的盖子。一张绿树照片是线索，不是完整的生物多样性调查。
<!-- en -->

**Watch out:** more rain does not automatically mean “most species everywhere.” A canopy is not a lid of identical trees. One green-tree photograph is a clue, not a full biodiversity survey.

<!-- section:mission -->
<!-- zh -->

切换层次、资源组合和关系网三幅图；找出林冠层，解释不同光照和庇护怎样创造不同机会。再判断哪一种结论需要实地或长期调查才站得住脚。
<!-- en -->

Switch among layer, resource-combination and relationship-web diagrams. Find the canopy, explain how different light/shelter can create different opportunities, then identify a conclusion that needs field or long-term investigation.

<!-- section:parent -->
<!-- zh -->

问：“图里的哪一层光更多？你为什么这样想？”“如果只拍了一张照片，什么还不知道？”纸笔替代：画一棵四层大树，在每层写一个可能不同的条件；用箭头连接一种资源和一种生物，但不要求孩子背物种名单。
<!-- en -->

Ask: “Which layer has more light in the diagram, and why?” “What remains unknown after one photo?” Paper alternative: draw a four-layer tree, add one changing condition per layer, and connect one resource to one organism. No species-list memorisation is needed.
