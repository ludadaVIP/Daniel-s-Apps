## 模型复核

- 比例尺：`图上两点长度 / 图上线段尺长度 × 线段尺公里数`，在1×、1.25×、1.5×、1.75×、2×都得到10公里。
- 坐标：40°S、175°E在新西兰附近；25°N、15°E在撒哈拉；0°、150°W在太平洋赤道。不是精确城市坐标或真实家庭地址。
- 时区：跨日必须更新日期；UTC07:00对应一月奥克兰20:00／伦敦07:00，七月奥克兰19:00／伦敦08:00。檀香山均在前一当地日期。
- 光照：理想球体太阳赤纬取分点0°、六月＋23.4°、十二月−23.4°。太阳正午高度为 `90° − |纬度 − 赤纬|`。昼长按几何地平线的日周弧计算。忽略太阳盘面、大气折射、地形和天气，结果不用于预测真实日出、日落或气温。
- [NOAA计算说明](https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/calcdetails.html)用于识别折射等真实计算因素。其在线计算器已注明不再积极维护；本项目没有调用它，也不把理想模型说成其观测结果。

所有新图为Study原创SVG或已有公共领域底图上的参考网格，见 `asset-manifest.json`。每幅图有双语替代说明；动画使用滑块或按钮逐帧控制，不依赖自动播放或联网。

后续引入当前国家边界、人口、灾害、气候、土地使用等数据时，应另记录年份、统计口径和许可，不直接把本轮示意数字移作实测数据。

## GEO-017–020 · 2026-10-13

- GEO-017: [USGS Interior](https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/interior/index.html), [USGS glossary](https://www.usgs.gov/glossary/earthquake-hazards-program), [seafloor model](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/1999/ofr-99-0132/) cross-check compositional layers, rigid lithosphere and slow deformation of predominantly solid mantle. The core is metallic, outer liquid/inner solid. Colours are illustrative and crust thickness exaggerated. P/S transmission diagram is conceptual, not a ray-tracing model: real paths curve, reflect and convert; many observations and laboratory constraints support inference. No core photography or universal magma ocean is implied. Some older USGS publications use “semi-solid”; the learner-facing wording is “mostly solid, slowly deforming”.
- GEO-018: [USGS fossil exercise](https://www.usgs.gov/educational-resources/wegeners-puzzling-continental-drift-evidence) supports corresponding fossil type/geological age and rock formations as evidence beyond outlines. Original South America/Africa fragments show only a relationship within Pangaea, not an actual palaeogeographic reconstruction, sampled fossil map or dated trajectory. Modern coasts do not fit perfectly. Sliders are gap controls, not time. [Plate nutshell](https://volcanoes.usgs.gov/about/edu/dynamicplanet/nutshell.php) and glossary support lithospheric rather than purely continental plates; relative divergent/convergent/transform motions are distinct. Convergent plan view does not simulate slab intersection; actual subduction/collision is described. Slab sinking, mantle flow and internal heat are presented as interacting mechanisms, not a universal single conveyor.
- The unmodified [USGS plate map](https://www.usgs.gov/media/images/tectonic-plates-earth) is explicitly Public Domain on its media page. It is an undated general reference. No data year is invented, and it is shown separately from the 2020 events rather than overlaid as a co-temporal measured boundary dataset. Source GIF is shipped unchanged; missing-year UI no longer incorrectly assigns a Natural Earth version to every undated asset.
- GEO-019: [USGS Science of Earthquakes](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/earthquake-hazards/science-earthquakes) supports elastic loading, slip, waves, hypocentre and its vertical surface projection. The fault model has no stress/depth/time scale and is not a countdown. [USGS prediction FAQ](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/can-you-predict-earthquakes) distinguishes precise date/location/magnitude prediction from long-term hazard assessments. “No dot” is not no hazard. Consequences are not inferred from magnitude alone.
- USGS [FDSN catalogue](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/fdsnws/event/1/) query and original-response SHA256 are retained in `content/earthquake-data.json`: origin time `2020-01-01T00:00:00 <= t < 2021-01-01T00:00:00 UTC`, preferred magnitude >=6, all depths/contributors, 121 earthquake events. API endtime is inclusive; the rebuild script explicitly excludes the next year's boundary. Every record type, range, finiteness and ID uniqueness is validated. Equal points encode epicentres only; depth and magnitude type are retained in the optional accessible table. Catalogues may be revised; this snapshot was retrieved 2026-10-13. One year/threshold is a classroom selection, not all seismicity, a complete detection assessment or personal risk forecast. Natural Earth v5.1.2 base distorts shape/distance.
- [NZ Civil Defence](https://getready.govt.nz/emergency/earthquakes) checked for indoor Drop, Cover and Hold, avoiding running outside/ordinary doorways, and coastal Long or Strong, Get Gone after shaking. The example is explicitly for a person standing indoors; family/school instructions and mobility-specific official guidance remain applicable. No destructive exercise or simulated alarm is included.
- GEO-020: [USGS plate explanation](https://volcanoes.usgs.gov/about/edu/dynamicplanet/nutshell.php), [2026 volcanic-fields review](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/pp1890A/full), [hotspot FAQ](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/what-a-hotspot-and-how-do-you-know-its-there), and [2014 GNS subduction study](https://www.gns.cri.nz/news/another-piece-in-the-tectonic-plate-mosaic/) cross-check slab fluids facilitating melting above a slab, decompression of upwelling mantle, and intraplate hotspots. They are different conditions for local melting, not whole-mantle liquefaction. Hotspot column depicts a common hypothesis; origins are not fully resolved. No claim that every convergent boundary is volcanic. Map points are coarse cases, not a complete volcano inventory; Mid-Atlantic Ridge point represents a segment, not a named monitoring station.
- The [NASA Ruapehu/Tongariro](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/ruapehu-volcano-and-tongariro-volcanic-complex-82244/) original ISS037-E-5089 photograph was acquired 30 September 2013, published 28 October 2013; NASA cropping, contrast enhancement and lens-artifact removal are recorded. Study has not altered pixels. Snow/clouds and the article's contemporaneous eruption descriptions do not establish current status. No current warning level or eruption forecast is presented. Eruptions may accumulate lava/ash, but cone shape is not universal and magma can cool underground.

Rebuild the historical catalogue: download the exact recorded query, then run `python geograph/scripts/prepare-earthquakes.py /path/source.geojson --retrieved-on YYYY-MM-DD`. It reads a local file, validates observations, hashes original bytes and retains source/selection metadata. Learning pages run offline with no live catalogue, location or external tile requests.

- **Mountain building:** [GNS Alpine Fault](https://www.gns.cri.nz/our-science/land-and-marine-geoscience/our-plate-boundary/alpine-fault/) and [Geology of NZ](https://www.gns.cri.nz/our-science/land-and-marine-geoscience/geology-of-new-zealand/) support South Island deformation/uplift with both along-fault slip and compression. - **Mountain models:** Original equal-area block analogy has area17600 arbitrary drawing units, width440→260 and thickness40→67.69; compression redistributes existing material. Its rectangle does not reproduce a mountain profile, folding, crustal rheology or calibrated dimensions. The separate surface budget starts10 with simultaneous +3 uplift and −1/3/5 removal each interval; frame4 gives18/10/2. No metres/year or Southern Alps forecast. Real feedbacks and isostatic adjustment are explicitly omitted; rock uplift and surface-height change remain distinct. Water can abrade bedrock, and chemical dissolution can feed solute transport: no implication that only visible fragments count or that real processes must occur separately. Upper/middle/lower are relative reaches, not universal chronological ages. Plan views of bends/braiding are explicitly distinguished from the V-valley section; conditions support possibilities, not forecasts or unique reach labels. Downstream may erode and upper reaches may deposit locally.
