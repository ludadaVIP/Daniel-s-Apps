153 usable lessons: 19 in Stage 0, 20 in Stage 1 (four measurement courses, five motion courses, four force courses, three gravity courses and four density courses), and thirty-two in Stage 2 (six energy courses, five work/power courses, five introductory thermal courses, four phase-change courses, five sound courses and seven light courses), plus fifty-two Stage 3 courses (six pressure, seven buoyancy, six simple-machine, seventeen electricity, eight magnetism and eight Earth/space courses), plus twenty-four Stage 4 bridge courses (eight algebra, seven graph, five vector and four problem-solving courses), plus six Stage 5 kinematics courses.
