## 一次观察流程

1. 家长先预览目标、模型和两个例题，确认自己理解整体、单位与题意。
2. 让学习者先猜，再改变一个控件；问“什么变了，什么没变”。先记原话，不急着给答案。
3. 做一题主动生成答案的练习，记是否独立、用了哪层提示、是否只是照抄图中文字。
4. 做一个解释或迁移任务；允许画图、中文解释或必要的英文读题协助，分开记录数学与语言困难。
5. 需要休息就分两次，不以速度评价理解。一次短观察不代表掌握整个单元。
6. 下一次换一个数字或图示再问同一个概念，检查是否能迁移，而不是只记住上次答案。

Preview the goal and examples first. Let the learner predict, change one control and explain the change. Record whether an answer was independent or supported, separating language assistance from mathematical hints. Allow a break or a second session. Revisit the concept with a changed example on another occasion; one successful attempt is not permanent mastery.

## 从反馈到修改

Use observed evidence to identify a concept, calculation, reading or representation issue, leaving uncertainty explicit. Reproduce the specific problem, revise the relevant material, verify it technically, then gather a changed-example follow-up. Do not invent trial outcomes to close a checklist.
