# 二项式系数与杨辉三角

(x+b)^n表示n个相同形式的因子相乘，n是非负整数。把因子位置标成1到n，每次展开都从每个因子选一项。**相同代数项出现几次，是一个选位置的组合问题。**

## 位置子集产生系数

要得到x^(n−k)b^k，选k个位置贡献b，其他位置贡献x，选法有C(n,k)种。因此(x+b)^n=Σ C(n,k)x^(n−k)b^k，k从0到n。若问x的某个幂的系数，还要乘b^k；二项式系数C(n,k)本身不包含这个常数幂。

杨辉三角从第0行的1开始，第n行是C(n,0),…,C(n,n)。两端为1。内部递推C(n,k)=C(n−1,k−1)+C(n−1,k)，因为k个位置的集合，要么含第n位置，要么不含；两类互斥且穷尽。n=0时展开为1，一个空位置选择保持边界一致。

令x=b=1，系数和为2^n，也就是每个位置选或不选得到的全部子集数。不能把一行有n+1个数，误认为每种k同样常见。

### 例1：六种位置选择与常数幂

(x+y)^4中要x²y²，取y的位置可为12,13,14,23,24,34，共6种，所以系数为C(4,2)=6。

把y换成2，六种选择每种贡献x²×2²，故(x+2)^4中x²系数是6×4=24，不是6。若展开(x−2)^3，x²来自一个位置取−2，系数C(3,1)(−2)=−6。完整展开x³−6x²+12x−8；奇数次负常数幂为负，偶数次为正。

### 例2：相同计数，概率权重不同

每轮独立抛4枚公平硬币。四个位置各有正反两种，16个有序结果等可能。恰两枚正面相当于选两个正面位置，有6种，所以理论概率6/16=3/8。

按正面个数0,1,2,3,4归组，串数为1,4,6,4,1。这五组不等可能。模拟20轮可能某一组比另一组更多，甚至没有出现某个可能结果；有限频率不必等于理论，也不必每加一批都更接近。若硬币偏斜或互相依赖，仍可数位置子集，但不能再给每个串同样的1/16权重。

## 工作台怎样连接两种表示

先选幂n、取常数次数k、常数b，预测x^(n−k)的系数。展开核验显示精确杨辉三角，高亮的是C(n,k)，旁边才乘b^k。改变代数常数b，不改变独立公平硬币实验；改变每轮硬币数n，则必须清除旧实验记录。

实验由可复现伪随机序列产生，每轮重新抛n枚；逐轮记录保留实际正面数。理论列、模拟次数列和观察频率列明确分开，零轮频率显示undefined。换种子会清空旧模拟，不能把新旧不同设置混成一组证据。

出口从“是否选最后位置”解释递推，再说清模拟与理论的区别。家长可让孩子为三角里一个内部数找出两个互斥来源，检验他是否理解系数在数什么。

# Binomial coefficients and Pascal’s triangle

For a nonnegative integer n, (x+b)^n multiplies n factors of the same form. Label the factor positions 1 through n. Every expansion choice takes one term from each factor. **The number of copies of an algebraic term is a combination problem about positions.**

## Position subsets create coefficients

To obtain x^(n−k)b^k, choose k factor positions to contribute b and x from the rest. There are C(n,k) such subsets. Thus (x+b)^n=Σ C(n,k)x^(n−k)b^k for k from zero through n. The coefficient of a power of x also includes b^k; C(n,k) alone omits that constant power.

Pascal’s triangle starts with row zero containing one. Row n contains C(n,0),…,C(n,n), with ones at both edges. For an interior entry, partition the k-position subsets according to whether they contain the last position. Inclusion leaves k−1 positions from the first n−1, while exclusion leaves k. These disjoint exhaustive cases give C(n,k)=C(n−1,k−1)+C(n−1,k). At n=0 the expansion is one, matching the single empty position choice.

Substituting x=b=1 shows that each row sums to 2^n, the number of all position subsets. Having n+1 entries does not make different subset sizes equally common.

### Example 1: Six choices and a constant power

For x²y² in (x+y)^4, the two positions contributing y can be 12,13,14,23,24,34. There are six, giving C(4,2)=6.

Substitute y=2. Each choice now contributes x²×2², so the coefficient of x² in (x+2)^4 is 6×4=24. For x² in (x−2)^3, choose one factor to contribute −2: C(3,1)(−2)=−6. The full expansion is x³−6x²+12x−8. Odd powers of the negative constant are negative; even powers are positive.

### Example 2: Counts become probability weights

Independently flip four fair coins per trial. The sixteen ordered strings are equally likely. Exactly two heads means choosing two head positions, giving six strings and theoretical probability 6/16=3/8.

Grouping by head counts zero through four gives multiplicities 1,4,6,4,1. These five groups are not equally likely. A twenty-trial simulation may miss a possible outcome or give uneven frequencies. Finite frequency need not equal theory or approach it monotonically as trials are added. For biased or dependent coins, position subsets still exist, but assigning every string equal probability 1/16 would be unjustified.

## Connect the workbench representations

Choose n, k and b, then predict the coefficient of x^(n−k). The exact triangle highlights C(n,k); the audit separately multiplies by b^k. Changing the algebraic constant does not alter the independent fair-coin experiment. Changing n does alter each trial, so it clears the previous simulation.

A reproducible pseudorandom sequence supplies fresh n-flip trials. The original record retains each trial’s head count. Theory, simulated counts and observed frequencies have separate columns. Frequency at zero trials is undefined. Changing the seed clears the record rather than mixing incompatible experiments.

For the exit, explain the recurrence using inclusion or exclusion of the last position, then distinguish finite simulation from theory. A parent can ask for the two disjoint sources of one interior entry, checking what that entry actually counts rather than whether its shape has been memorised.
