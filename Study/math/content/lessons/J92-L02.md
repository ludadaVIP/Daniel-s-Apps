# 因式分解法

## 零积法的前提是“零”

实数乘积 uv=0，当且仅当 u=0 或 v=0，这里的“或”允许两者同时为零。为什么？如果 u 不为零，就能两边除以 u 得 v=0；若 u=0，已经满足第一分支。这个论证同时解释了条件与分支完整性，不能把结论机械套在 uv=4 上。乘积为四时，任一因子为零都会使等式不成立。

解题顺序是：把整个方程整理为一边为零，完整分解另一边，逐个非恒定因子为零求解，合并相同根，再代回原方程。分解恒等式与求解方程是两个步骤：写 (x−2)(x−3) 是给出乘积形式；写 {2,3} 才是在给出根集。题目问什么，就交付什么形式。

### 例 1：不能除以可能为零的 x

x²−3x=0 提出公因式 x，成为 x(x−3)=0。第一分支 x=0；第二分支 x−3=0，所以 x=3。完整实根为0、3。原式核验：0²−3×0=0，3²−3×3=0。

若直接两边除以 x，得到 x−3=0，只剩3。错误不是算术，而是偷偷加入 x≠0 的条件，删掉原式允许的零。也可先讨论 x=0，再在 x≠0 的分支里相除，但通常保留因子更清楚。对3x²=6x，同样移项为3x(x−2)=0；常数3不能为零，不提供新分支，根只有0、2。

### 例 2：右边非零，先整体移项

解 x(x−3)=4。不能把 x 或 x−3 直接等于零，因为原乘积不是零。先展开、移项：x²−3x−4=0，再分解为 (x−4)(x+1)=0，得到 x=4 或 x=−1。检查4×1=4，(−1)×(−4)=4，都通过原等式。反之0和3使原左边为零，与四不同。

非首一式也可分解：2x²+x−1=(2x−1)(x+1)，所以根为1/2与−1。展开核因式时，中间项2x−x=x，常数−1。不要把因子2x−1的零点写成1；求根仍需完成一次方程2x=1。分解式中的数字与根值不能直接照抄。

## 重根、检验与完整性

(x+2)²=0 的两个因子相同，都给−2。称它二重根，但列不同实根时只写一次。检查根时用原式最可靠，尤其原右边非零或先前有移项时。代回证明每个候选有效；“已全部列出”则由乘积为零的充分必要分支保证。这两个任务都要完成。

工作台接受交换因子次序和成对改号，但因式题要求完成分解，而非仅写一个等值展开式。遇到一时分解不出的二次式，不能据此断言无根；下一课配方及之后的公式会提供更通用方法。家长重点听“乘积现在等于什么”和“这次相除是否可能除以零”，不只看最后数字。

# Solving by factorisation

## The zero-product premise

For real numbers, uv=0 exactly when u=0 or v=0, possibly both. If u is nonzero, division gives v=0; otherwise the first branch already holds. This argument explains both the condition and exhaustive branches. It does not apply to uv=4: a zero factor would contradict a product of four.

First collect the entire equation with zero on one side. Factor the other side completely, set each nonconstant factor to zero, combine repeated values and substitute into the original. Factoring an expression and solving an equation are separate tasks: (x−2)(x−3) is a product form, while {2,3} is a solution set.

### Example 1: division can lose zero

Factor x²−3x=0 as x(x−3)=0. The branches give x=0 and x=3. Both pass the original: 0²−3×0=0 and 3²−3×3=0. Dividing immediately by x keeps only three because it silently assumes x≠0, deleting an allowed solution.

One may instead check x=0 separately, then divide on the x≠0 branch. Keeping the factors is usually clearer. Likewise 3x²=6x becomes 3x(x−2)=0. The constant factor three is never zero and supplies no extra branch; the roots are zero and two.

### Example 2: move a nonzero right side first

For x(x−3)=4, neither x=0 nor x=3 follows. Expand and move the whole equation to x²−3x−4=0, then factor (x−4)(x+1)=0. Thus x=4 or −1. Checks give 4×1=4 and (−1)(−4)=4. The tempting zero and three instead make the original product zero.

A nonmonic example is 2x²+x−1=(2x−1)(x+1). Expansion gives middle term 2x−x=x and constant −1. Setting each factor to zero gives 1/2 and −1. The zero of 2x−1 is not one: solving that linear branch still requires dividing by two.

## Repetition and completeness

Both factors of (x+2)²=0 give −2. This double root is one distinct value, listed once. Substitution into the original establishes that each candidate works; the exhaustive zero-product branches establish that none are missing. These are different responsibilities.

The factor task accepts reordered factors and balanced sign changes, but requires a completed product rather than an equivalent expanded expression. Failure to find integer factors does not prove no real roots. Completing the square and the formula will provide more general methods. A parent should listen for the actual product value and the nonzero condition behind any division, not just check the final digits.
