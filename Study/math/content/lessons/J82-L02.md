# 平方差分解

## 找两个完整平方，也要看相减符号

上一单元从和差乘积展开得 `(A−B)(A+B)=A²−B²`。现在逆用它，把平方差分成两个因式。先确认两个完整项分别是谁的平方，再确认中间是相减。公式里的 A、B 是整体，不一定只是一个字母或常数。

仍然先检查公因式。如果整式各项共同含二，先提出二，之后再识别括号。每次分解后检查新因式，不以“出现两个括号”作为完成标准。本单元的完成范围是整数系数，不要求进入其他数域。

### 例 1：整体平方根与符号

`x²−9=(x−3)(x+3)`，因为九是三的平方。展开为 `x²+3x−3x−9`，中项相消。它不是 `(x−3)²`，后者有 −6x 中项且常数正九。

`4x²−25=(2x−5)(2x+5)`。第一整体是 2x，因为 `(2x)²=4x²`；不能把 A 错认成四 x。平方和 `x²+9` 则不能写成同样的和差乘积，否则常数会变成负九。若假设整数一次因式 `(x+r)(x+s)`，需 r+s=0 而 rs=9，但 s=−r 会使积非正，不能满足正九。

### 例 2：分解之后继续检查

`x⁴−16` 中，A=x²、B=4，所以先得 `(x²−4)(x²+4)`。第一括号仍为平方差，继续成 `(x−2)(x+2)`，最终 `x⁴−16=(x−2)(x+2)(x²+4)`。

在整数系数范围，x²+4 保留：若两个整数一次因式的常数和为零，积就不可能是正四。不能把它强拆成 `(x−2)(x+2)`，那会给 x²−4。另如 `2x²−18`，先提二成 `2(x²−9)`，再到 `2(x−3)(x+3)`；外部二不能漏掉。

## 展开可以检查每个整体

将三因式的前两项合回 x²−4，再乘 x²+4，可恢复 x⁴−16。回查既检符号，也检次数和数系数。只代一个 x，特别是零或一，容易让遗漏项碰巧消失，不能替代完整展开。

几何可以把正的平方差理解为去角后重排面积，但长度条件要另外说明。比如 x=5，去掉边长三的角块，剩十六；因式长度 x−3=2 与 x+3=8，乘积也是十六。代数恒等式则对任意实数 x 成立，即使图中某个长度不再为正。

家长请孩子按“共同因子—平方结构—符号—新因式—展开”顺序解释。对不匹配的式子，只能说本公式不适用；在规定整数范围内证明没有相应因式时，也不要扩大成对所有数域的结论。

# Factoring a difference of squares

## Identify whole squares and the subtraction

Read (A−B)(A+B)=A²−B² backwards. Identify the two entire squared terms and verify subtraction. A and B represent whole expressions, not necessarily individual letters or constants.

Check common factors first. If all terms share two, extract it before examining the bracket. After each step inspect every new factor; two visible brackets do not guarantee completion. This unit works over integer coefficients rather than requiring other number domains.

### Example 1: whole square roots and signs

x²−9=(x−3)(x+3), since nine is three squared. Expansion gives x²+3x−3x−9, with cancelling middle terms. This differs from (x−3)², whose middle is −6x and constant positive nine.

For 4x²−25, the factors are (2x−5)(2x+5) because the first whole square root is 2x, not 4x. The sum x²+9 cannot use the same product: conjugate constants would give negative nine. Integer factors (x+r)(x+s) would need r+s=0 and rs=9, but s=−r makes the product nonpositive.

### Example 2: continue inspecting new factors

For x⁴−16, use A=x² and B=4 to obtain (x²−4)(x²+4). Split the first bracket again, giving (x−2)(x+2)(x²+4).

Retain x²+4 over the integers: offsets with sum zero cannot have positive product four. Splitting it as (x−2)(x+2) would change the sign of its constant. For 2x²−18, first extract two, then continue from 2(x²−9) to 2(x−3)(x+3). Keep the outside multiplier.

## Expansion checks the whole relationship

Multiply the first two factors of the three-factor result back to x²−4, then by x²+4 to recover x⁴−16. This checks signs, degrees and coefficients. One input, particularly zero or one, may conceal missing terms and does not replace full expansion.

A positive-length area can illustrate the result: at x=5, removing a square of side three leaves sixteen, equal to side lengths two and eight multiplied. That picture has its own length conditions. The algebraic identity holds for all real x, including values outside a positive-length drawing.

A parent asks for the shared content, squared whole terms, sign, remaining reducible factors and complete expansion. A formula’s failure only rules out that method. Even a justified conclusion about integer factors must not silently become a statement about every number domain.
