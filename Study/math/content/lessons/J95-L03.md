# 切线性质

## 直角必须在切点

若整条直线PT与圆在T处相切，那么连接圆心O与切点T，半径OT垂直切线。它不是“半径与圆外任意线段都垂直”，也不是“在任意位置垂直某半径的线都是切线”。反向判定还要求这条线经过半径的圆上端点T，并恰在那里垂直。

可以用圆心距解释：经过T且垂直OT的直线，到圆心的最短距离恰为半径r，所以只有T一个交点。若直线在半径别的位置与其垂直，距离可能小于r成为割线，也可能大于r不相交。写清切点位置比在草图上标一个直角更重要。

### 例 1：切线段长度用平方差

P在圆外，PT在T处相切，OT=3、OP=5 cm。直角在T，因此OP是△OTP的斜边，而不是半径。勾股给OP²=OT²+PT²，故PT²=25−9=16，PT=4 cm。长度取正，不把−4也列为答案，也不是直接算5−3=2。

若OT=5、OP=13 cm，同理PT²=169−25=144，PT=12 cm。若OP改成7，PT²=49−25=24，PT=√24=2√6 cm。精确长度用根式，4.90只是近似小数，不能冒充精确答案。所有数值必须采用相同长度单位，再作平方差。

### 例 2：同外点的两条切线

从同一个外点P向同一圆引两条切线段PT、PS。OT⊥PT、OS⊥PS，因此△OTP与△OSP都是直角三角形，共有斜边OP，且OT=OS为同圆半径。斜边、一直角边全等，所以PT=PS。若PT=8 cm，则PS也是8 cm；不需要把两条相加当另一条的长度。

若两个切点的小圆心角∠TOS=100°，四边形OTPS在T、S各有90°。四角总和360°，于是∠TPS=360−90−90−100=80°。这里明确用两条切线段在P的夹角，以及两个切点之间的小圆心角，不能把100°的半数误当P角。

## 定理的适用条件

两切线段相等，需要同一外点、同一圆及相切。两个不同外点各画一条切线，长度不必相等；两条只是连接圆上点的线段，也不一定垂直半径。P在圆上只有一条切线方向，P在圆内没有实切线，不能继续套“两个外点切线段”的图形。

工作台先比较3–5、5–13和5–7的长度案例，再切到夹角案例。移除相切条件后，图形改为没有右角标记的一般线段；此时应回答条件不足，而非继续调用平方差。仅旋转或缩放显示不会改变预测，改变真正的案例则重新计算。

家长让孩子指出斜边、切点和直角的来源，并说明为何只取正根。自动检查精确长度或夹角，不能替代两三角形全等证明。离堂请补完整“垂直半径则相切”的反向条件，再给一个在错误位置垂直却不是切线的例子。这样能把图像记忆变成可核查的逻辑条件。

# Tangent properties

## The right angle is at the contact

If the infinite line PT is tangent to a circle at T, the contact radius OT is perpendicular to it. This does not say every exterior segment is perpendicular to a radius. The converse requires the line to pass through the on-circle endpoint T of the radius and be perpendicular there.

Its perpendicular centre distance then equals r, giving exactly one intersection. A perpendicular at another position may instead be a secant or disjoint line. State the contact location rather than relying on a right-angle mark in an arbitrary sketch.

### Example 1: subtract squares

Let P be external, PT tangent at T, OT=3 and OP=5 cm. T is the right-angle vertex, so OP is the hypotenuse of OTP, not a radius. Pythagoras gives PT²=OP²−OT²=25−9=16 and positive length PT=4 cm. Do not include a negative length or subtract ordinary lengths to obtain two.

For OT=5 and OP=13, the squared difference is 169−25=144, giving twelve centimetres. For OT=5 and OP=7, it is 49−25=24, giving exact length √24=2√6 cm. The decimal 4.90 is approximate and does not replace an exact radical. Unify the length units before squaring.

### Example 2: two tangents from one external point

Draw PT and PS tangent to the same circle from the same external P. Right triangles OTP and OSP share hypotenuse OP and have equal radii OT=OS. Hypotenuse-leg congruence therefore gives PT=PS. If PT is eight centimetres, so is PS; their sum is not the length of either segment.

If minor central angle TOS=100°, quadrilateral OTPS has right angles at both contacts. Its interior-angle sum 360° gives TPS=360−90−90−100=80°. This uses the angle between the tangent segments at P and the minor angle between the contact radii. Halving one hundred would apply the wrong relationship.

Equality needs one external point, one circle and genuine tangency. Tangents from different external points need not have equal lengths. Two arbitrary segments to circle points need not be perpendicular to their radii. An on-circle point has one tangent direction and an interior point has no real tangent, so neither gives two distinct external tangents.
