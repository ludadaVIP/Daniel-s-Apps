# 勾股逆定理

## 先有三角形，再谈它的角

正向勾股定理由“已知直角”推出 a²+b²=c²。逆定理则说：一个已存在的三角形，取最长边 c，若两短边满足 a²+b²=c²，则 c 所对的角是直角。已知与结论的方向不同，不能把待证的直角先画上标记充当依据。

使用三边前先检查它们均为正，且两短边的和严格大于最长边。若相等，三个点只能落在退化边界；若更小，连三角形也围不成。没有非退化三角形，就不谈锐角、钝角或直角。题目给数字的顺序不代表大小，最后一个数不自动是斜边。

### 例 1：排序后的直角判断

给三边五、三、四，先排序为三、四、五。三加四大于五，存在。3²+4²=9+16=25=5²，逆定理证明最长边五所对的角是直角。没有必要先量图；量到近似九十度反而不能替代精确等式。

为何这不是循环论证？另构一个两腿为三、四的直角三角形，前向勾股算其斜边为五。新三角形三边与原来分别相等，由 SSS 全等，原最长边所对角才被证为九十度。一般正 a/b/c 同理，正长度保证相同平方对应相同的非负长度。

对照一、二、三：一加二等于三，先验存在就失败，必须回答不能成非退化三角形。不能因为三平方大于另外两平方和，就继续称其为钝角三角形。

### 例 2：不相等时，还要哪条比较性质

三边二、三、四可以成三角形，4²−2²−3²=3，不是零，因此先能排除直角。若要进一步判钝角，还需额外的几何比较性质：固定两条边，它们的夹角越大，对边越长。另构以二、三为腿的直角三角形，斜边为 √13，小于四；原同两边所夹角更大，故大于九十度，是钝角三角形。

四、四、五则有 5²−4²−4²=−7。原最长边比相应直角比较边短，所以它所对最大角小于九十度，其余角也不大于它，整个三角形为锐角。这部分用到了边角比较，不能声称只由“平方不等于”就推出某一种角型。

因此在三角形存在、c 最长的前提下，平方差为零给直角；正给钝角、负给锐角需要配合上述比较性质。它是与逆定理连用的拓展检查。把“任何不相等都叫钝角”会错判四、四、五。

## 精确检验与证明

工作台先排序、检查存在，再显示精确平方差；在主动核验以前不提前画直角标记。无法构成三角形时不画假图。极端比例若超出图形分辨率，仍保留精确三边结论，并提示以数值为依据；绘图失败不改数学判断。

边长若是测量近似值，需要另外讨论测量误差，不能把不精确的平方“凑成相等”来当精确证明。本课使用题设精确值，家长核原始条件、最长边和正逆方向，再看孩子能否解释 SSS 的中间桥梁，而不只背三四五。

# Converse of Pythagoras

## A triangle must exist first

Forward Pythagoras starts with a right angle and derives a square equality. The converse starts with a valid triangle and a²+b²=c², with c longest, and establishes the angle opposite c as right. Do not assume the target right angle as a premise.

Check positive sides and that the two shorter sides sum strictly above the longest. Equality is degenerate; a smaller sum cannot form a triangle. Listing order does not identify the longest side or a hypotenuse.

### Example 1: sort before testing

For sides five, three, four, sort to three, four, five. Existence holds, and 9+16=25=5², proving rightness by the converse. Approximate screen measurement adds no exact evidence.

To justify the converse, construct a separate right triangle with legs three and four. Forward Pythagoras gives its hypotenuse five. SSS makes it congruent to the original, proving the original angle right without assuming it. For general positive a,b,c, equal squares identify equal positive lengths in the same way.

Sides one, two, three fail existence because 1+2=3. A positive square difference does not turn a nonexistent triangle into an obtuse one.

### Example 2: unequal squares need an additional comparison

Sides two, three, four form a triangle and give square difference three, excluding rightness. Obtuse classification additionally uses the hinge property: with two sides fixed, a larger included angle has a longer opposite side. The right comparison triangle on legs two and three has hypotenuse √13, below four, so the original included angle exceeds 90°.

For four, four, five the square difference is minus seven. Even the largest angle, opposite the longest side, is below the right comparison. The triangle is acute. Inequality alone does not establish which non-right type occurs.

Thus zero difference establishes rightness; positive/negative differences classify obtuse/acute only with existence, longest-side identification and that extra side-angle comparison property. This is an extension alongside the converse, not a claim that every unequal case is obtuse.

## Exact evidence

The workshop sorts and checks existence before displaying exact differences. A right-angle mark appears only after verification. Impossible data do not produce false triangles; extreme display proportions do not alter the exact result.

Measured approximate sides would need a separate error discussion. Do not round a difference into equality and call it proof. A parent checks givens, the longest side and forward versus converse direction, including the SSS bridge rather than only memorised triples.
