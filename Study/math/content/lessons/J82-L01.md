# 提公因式

## 把分配律反过来读

展开把乘积分成和，因式分解把整式写成乘积。`kA+kB=k(A+B)` 是同一条分配律的两个方向。因式分解不是解方程：原式没有等于零，就不需要找 x 的零点，也不能把一个乘积改成一列 x 的值。

本单元在整数系数范围内分解。共同的数因子取系数绝对值的最大公因数；每个字母取全部项都拥有的最低次数。不是只看首项，也不是取出现过的最高次数。提出后，每一项都必须仍能写成整式余项，括号里的系数不再留共同整数因子。

### 例 1：同时核共同性与最大性

对 `6x²+12x`，系数六与十二的最大公因数是六，x 的次数二与一，共同最低为一。所以两项分别是 `6x·x` 和 `6x·2`，合成 `6x(x+2)`。

`3x(2x+4)` 展开也正确，但括号还留数因子二，尚未完成“提出最大公因式”的要求。`6(x²+2x)` 仍留共同 x。若提 `6x²`，第二项需余 `2/x`，不再是本课整式分解。区分“关系错误”和“等值但未完成”，才能知道下一步该改什么。

### 例 2：负号与两个字母

`−6x²+9x` 可提正三 x，得 `3x(−2x+3)`；也可提负三 x，得 `−3x(2x−3)`。两种符号安排都展开为原式，因式不唯一，但任何改号都必须保持总乘积。不能把外部变负、括号却原样保留。

对 `8x²y−12xy²`，数公因数为四，x 与 y 的共同最低次数都一，所以 `4xy(2x−3y)`。第一余项仍有一个 x，第二仍有一个 y；若只提出四 x，括号还有共同 y。各字母分别核，不将总次数混为一个指数。

## 分解不丢掉零输入

等式 `6x²+12x=6x(x+2)` 对每个实数 x 成立，x=0 时两边都零。它来自分配律，没有将方程两边除 x，不需要附 x≠0。若以后解方程，要单独遵守方程变形规则；不能把那种取消因子的限制偷带到这里。

若所有项都含 x²，例如 `6x³+9x²`，共同次数二，结果 `3x²(2x+3)`。提出的因子乘回每一个余项，是最直接的回查。若某项提完没有字母，它的余项可以是常数，甚至是一，不能因为“没剩字母”就漏掉这一项。

工作台提供有效但非最大、最大和过度提取的候选。先判断每项都含什么，再自行写完整乘积，最后展开逐项余项。家长请孩子说明数因子、各字母最低次数、括号余项与零输入；点中最大候选本身不等于会解释。

# Taking out common factors

## Read distribution in reverse

Expansion changes a product into a sum; factorisation writes a polynomial as a product. kA+kB=k(A+B) is the same distributive law read backwards. Factorisation is not solving an equation: without a statement that the expression equals zero, do not replace its factors with a list of roots.

This unit factors over integer coefficients. Use the greatest common divisor of absolute numerical coefficients and the minimum exponent shared by every term for each letter. Do not use only the first term or the greatest visible exponent. Each remainder must remain a polynomial, with no shared integer numerical content left inside.

### Example 1: commonness and greatest content

For 6x²+12x, six and twelve have GCD six, and the x exponents two and one share a minimum of one. Write the terms as 6x·x and 6x·2, giving 6x(x+2).

The product 3x(2x+4) expands correctly but leaves common numerical factor two inside. Likewise, 6(x²+2x) still leaves shared x. Extracting 6x² would require remainder 2/x for the second term, outside polynomial factorisation here. Distinguish a false relationship from an equivalent but unfinished form.

### Example 2: signs and two letters

For −6x²+9x, either 3x(−2x+3) or −3x(2x−3) is correct. Both expand to the source. Factor arrangements need not be unique, but any sign change must preserve the overall product. Changing the outside sign alone would change the polynomial.

For 8x²y−12xy², the numerical divisor is four and both letters have minimum shared exponent one. The result is 4xy(2x−3y). One x remains in the first remainder and one y in the second. Extracting only 4x leaves a shared y. Count letters separately rather than merging total degrees.

## A factorisation retains zero inputs

The identity 6x²+12x=6x(x+2) holds for every real x, including zero. Reverse distribution did not divide an equation by x, so no condition x≠0 was introduced. Equation solving later has its own rules; do not transfer cancellation restrictions to this identity rewrite.

For 6x³+9x², the minimum shared x exponent is two, giving 3x²(2x+3). Multiply the common factor into every remainder to check. A remainder can be a constant, even one, and must not disappear simply because it has no letters.

The workshop includes a common but nongreatest factor, the greatest factor and an excessive proposed extraction. Check every source term, write the product and reveal the individual remainders. A parent asks for the coefficient GCD, each minimum exponent, all remainders and the retained zero input. Selecting a button alone does not demonstrate that reasoning.
