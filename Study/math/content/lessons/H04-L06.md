# 解简单三角方程

计算器给arcsin(1/2)=π/6，回答的是主值范围内的一个角；它没有自动回答“所有实根”或“指定区间里的全部解”。周期与单位圆的另一分支必须一起考虑。

## 一条核心想法

**一周期找全分支，加周期生成候选，反解内部角，再按原区间筛选。** sin对应相同纵坐标，cos对应相同横坐标。sin和cos基本周期2π，tan周期π且须排除cos=0的位置。求有限解集时，不能把相差整周的两个合法输入合并成一个同终边代表。

给定区间的开闭端点是问题条件，不是图窗边缘。原式中的倍率与平移改变内部角范围；不能只改方程而沿用原来的内部区间。候选最后代回原方程，核对值、定义域和单位。

### 例1：两分支及开闭端点

解sinx=1/2，x∈[0,2π)。单位圆上同高度给x=π/6与5π/6，两者都在原域。全体实根可写π/6+2kπ或5π/6+2kπ，k为整数；在此区间只留下两个。

sinx=0的候选为kπ。在[0,2π]有{0,π,2π}，在[0,2π)有{0,π}，在(0,2π]有{π,2π}。同一方程只改端点条件，完整解集就会不同。tanx=1在[−π,π)则由π/4+kπ筛得{−3π/4,π/4}，两个输入的cos都非零。

### 例2：内部倍率和平移

解sin(2x)=1/2，x∈[0,2π)。令u=2x，内部域是[0,4π)，有两周期的四个候选π/6、5π/6、13π/6、17π/6。全部除以2，得到x=π/12、5π/12、13π/12、17π/12。只在u∈[0,2π)找解会漏掉一半。

解2sin(x−π/4)+1=2。先除去竖直变换得sin u=1/2，u=x−π/4∈[−π/4,7π/4)。加回π/4并筛原域，得x=5π/12或13π/12；代回原式都为2。没有被原区间包含的其他周期候选不能留下。

## 完整解集实验

选择sin、cos或tan、内部倍率、相位和原区间，先写全部角再显示交点核验。精确解可以用{pi/6,5pi/6}，顺序不影响集合；缺一支、漏周期、加错端点都会改变答案。sin或cos目标2时空集写∅，因为范围只有[−1,1]。

图像画给定原域，开端点为空心，闭端点为实心；正切各分支在无定义点断开。图上采样只帮助观察，解的数量由确切分支和端点筛选决定，不以像素距离判断两个角是否相等。

# Solving basic trig equations

A calculator’s arcsin(1/2)=π/6 gives one angle in its principal range. It does not automatically provide every real root or every root in a stipulated interval. The other unit-circle branch and periodic repetitions matter.

## One central idea

**Find every branch in one period, add periods, reverse the inner angle and filter the original interval.** Sine means equal vertical coordinates; cosine equal horizontal coordinates. Their basic periods are 2π. Tangent has period π and excludes positions with cos=0. For a finite solution set, two admissible inputs separated by a full turn remain distinct solutions, even when coterminal.

Open and closed bounds are problem conditions, not graph-window edges. Inner multipliers and shifts change the domain of the inner angle; transforming an equation without transforming that domain loses roots. Finally substitute candidates into the original equation and check values, domain and units.

### Example 1: Two branches and interval endpoints

For sinx=1/2 on [0,2π), the common unit-circle height gives π/6 and 5π/6. Both are allowed. Every real root is π/6+2kπ or 5π/6+2kπ for integer k; filtering this interval leaves two.

The candidates for sinx=0 are kπ. On [0,2π] the set is {0,π,2π}; on [0,2π) it is {0,π}; on (0,2π] it is {π,2π}. Changing only endpoint ownership changes the complete answer. For tanx=1 on [−π,π), filter π/4+kπ to obtain {−3π/4,π/4}, both with nonzero cosine.

### Example 2: Inner multipliers and shifts

For sin(2x)=1/2 on x∈[0,2π), let u=2x. Its domain is [0,4π), containing four candidates over two periods: π/6, 5π/6, 13π/6 and 17π/6. Dividing all by two yields x=π/12, 5π/12, 13π/12, 17π/12. Searching only u∈[0,2π) would miss half the roots.

For 2sin(x−π/4)+1=2, remove the vertical transformation to get sin u=1/2 with u=x−π/4 in [−π/4,7π/4). Add π/4 back and filter the original x domain to obtain 5π/12 and 13π/12. Both substitute to two in the original rule; other periodic candidates lie outside the interval.

## Complete-set experiment

Choose a function, inner multiplier, phase and original interval. Predict all angles before revealing the intersection audit. Exact sets can use {pi/6,5pi/6}; ordering does not affect a set. Missing a branch or period, or adding an excluded endpoint, changes the answer. A sine or cosine target of two gives ∅ because the range is only [−1,1].

The graph uses the stipulated original domain, with open or filled endpoint markers. Tangent branches are not joined across poles. Sampling supports observation; exact branches and endpoint filtering decide the roots, rather than pixel proximity between angles.

## Family challenge and parent review

A fictional viewing wheel follows H(t)=8+6sin(πt/10−π/2) for 0≤t≤20, with t in minutes. Heights range from two to 14 metres and a turn takes 20 minutes. For height eight, the inner sine must be zero, giving allowed times five and 15 minutes. Explain every branch and the original interval, not only the first time.

This ideal model assumes uniform motion and omits real facility details. A parent reviews the inner domain, periods, substitution and units in the written argument. The exit is a complete explanation, not an automatically graded collection of keywords such as “π” or “period”.
