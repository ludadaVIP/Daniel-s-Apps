# 移项的本质是等式变形

## 变号背后有左右两边的操作

“移到另一边变号”可以缩短书写，但不能代替理由。一个项没有穿过等号的魔法；我们是在两边同加或同减一个完整的式子，再合并。可逆操作保证原方程与新方程的解集相同，既不丢原解，也不凭空增加解。

两边同加或同减定义良好的式子，可以用相反操作还原。两边同乘或同除一个已知非零常数，也能还原。常数可以为负或分数，关键在非零；本课不把含 x 的因子当作已知非零常数，因为它可能在某个值为零，也可能改变方程次数。

### 例 1：把两侧的 x 集中

解 `5x+2=2x+11`。

1. 两边同减 `2x`：`5x+2−2x=2x+11−2x`，合并成 `3x+2=11`。
2. 两边同减二：`3x=9`。
3. 两边同除非零三：`x=3`。
4. 代回原式，左边 `15+2=17`，右边 `6+11=17`。

说“右边 2x 移到左边成为 −2x”时，完整理由是两边同减 `2x`。右边的项抵消成零，而不是凭空消失。逆着看，先乘三、再加二、再加 `2x`，可以回到原式，说明各步保留解集。

### 例 2：负系数同样能除

解 `2−3x=11`。两边同减二得 `−3x=9`；两边同除负三得 `x=−3`。负三非零，除法合法，符号由 `9÷(−3)` 决定。检验 `2−3×(−3)=2+9=11`。

再比较 `7x−3=4x+2`：同减 `4x` 得 `3x−3=2`，同加三得 `3x=5`，所以 `x=5/3`。原式两边均为 `26/3`。不用为了得到整数而改题，也不能把 `1.67` 当作精确解。

## 同做还不够，还要检查可逆

把 `5x+2=2x+11` 两边同乘零，得到 `0=0`。原来只有三满足，现在任何有理数都满足，限制丢失了。乘零虽然让两边仍相等，却不是通用的保解变形。零没有乘法逆元，不能从 `0=0` 还原原式。除零则本身没有定义，不是可以做完再补救的一步。

只给左边减二，通常会改变解；即使某个特别的方程在某次单侧操作后碰巧同解，也不能据此把单侧操作当通用规则。工作台允许记录这类反例，会分别说明“当前是否同解”和“所用规则是否通用可逆”。这两个判断不能混为一谈。

从默认方程尝试同减 `2x`、同减二、同除三，每次先预测下一行再操作。重置后试乘零、除零和仅左侧变形，比较原因与解集提示。最多保留八步，以便读清推理链。家长应追问每步两边做了什么、如何反向还原，以及最终是否检验原式。

# Equation balance rather than magic transposition

## A sign change abbreviates an operation

“Move a term and change its sign” is a shorthand, not a justification. We add or subtract the same complete expression on both sides and collect terms. A reversible step preserves the full solution set: it neither loses original solutions nor invents new ones.

Adding or subtracting the same defined expression can be undone. Multiplying or dividing both sides by a known nonzero constant can also be undone. Negative and fractional constants are allowed; zero is the crucial boundary. A factor containing x is not automatically a known nonzero constant: it may vanish and may change the degree of the equation.

### Example 1: gather the variable terms

Solve `5x+2=2x+11`. First subtract 2x from both sides, writing `5x+2−2x=2x+11−2x`, then collect to `3x+2=11`. Subtract two from both sides to obtain `3x=9`, then divide both by nonzero three: `x=3`. The original sides become seventeen and seventeen.

The phrase “move 2x left as −2x” abbreviates subtraction on both sides. The right term cancels to zero; it does not disappear without a reason. Reverse the chain by multiplying by three, adding two and adding 2x to recover the original. These inverse steps justify equal solution sets.

### Example 2: divide by a negative coefficient

For `2−3x=11`, subtract two from both sides: `−3x=9`. Divide both by negative three to obtain `x=−3`. Negative three is nonzero, and `9÷(−3)` determines the sign. Substitution gives `2−3×(−3)=11`.

For `7x−3=4x+2`, subtract 4x, add three and divide by three, always on both sides. The result is `x=5/3`, and both original sides are `26/3`. There is no need to change conditions to get an integer; 1.67 is an approximation, not the exact solution.

## Equal operations must also be reversible

Multiplying both sides of the original example by zero produces `0=0`. The original restricts x to three, while the new identity allows every rational. The restriction was lost. Although equal sides remain equal, zero multiplication is not a general solution-preserving transformation because it cannot be reversed. Division by zero is undefined from the start.

Changing only one side usually changes the solution set. A particular one-sided operation might coincidentally preserve a special equation, but that coincidence does not justify a general rule. The workshop reports both the actual solution-set comparison and whether the operation is a generally reversible step. These are different questions.

Predict and record subtraction of 2x, subtraction of two and division by three. Reset, then try zero multiplication, zero division and left-only operations. Read the reasons as well as the current equation. Up to eight recorded steps keep the chain readable. A parent asks what happened on each side, how to undo it and whether the final value checks in the original.
