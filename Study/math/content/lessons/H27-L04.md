# 模拟抽样变异

同一总体按同一规则抽样，结果仍会波动。本课先把全部可能样本算清，再用固定种子的伪随机模拟观察经验频率；精确理论与有限模拟各自回答不同问题。

## 四个身份，不是两种数字

有限总体A=0、B=0、C=4、D=4，总体均值2。A与B数值相同但不是同一个人，C与D同理。简单随机不放回抽n个成员，意味着每个n元子集等可能。每轮内部不重复身份，下轮恢复完整总体；不要把“不放回”误解为整个模拟过程中永不恢复。

样本均值在所有可能样本之间形成抽样分布。其方差围绕该分布自己的期望计算，分母为可能样本数（或按各样本概率加权），不是把这张完整分布当成一个待估计的小样本再除m−1。

### 例1：枚举六个样本

n=2时，AB、AC、AD、BC、BD、CD共六种，均值依次0、2、2、2、2、4。它们等可能，所以均值2的概率4/6=2/3，均值0和4的概率各1/6。

分布期望(0+2+2+2+2+4)/6=2，恰等总体均值。方差为[(0−2)²+四个0+(4−2)²]/6=8/6=4/3。这是均值的抽样方差，不是原始成员数值的方差4。

### 例2：样本量、重复数和偏差

n=1的四个均值0、0、4、4，方差4。n=3时四个均值4/3、4/3、8/3、8/3，方差4/9。n=4只有全体一个样本，均值恒2，方差0。

这是有限总体不放回的结果。若用公式核对，方差=σ²/n·(N−n)/(N−1)，这里N=4、σ²=4；不能直接套独立有放回抽样的σ²/n，后者在n=4会错给1而不是0。全部枚举已经足以证明本课数值，无需先背公式，也无需套正态近似。

固定n=2，增加模拟轮数12到192不会把每个样本扩大。它改变经验频率对固定理论分布的观察，通常更稳定，但不保证每个具体种子/轮数都逐次更贴近。若选择规则永远只取A、B，均值恒0、方差0，期望却偏离目标2，偏差−2；重复任意多次也不修复选择机制。抽齐全体的普查是明确例外，不能把“便利样本变大”一般说成必然去偏。

## 工作台与证据

紫柱是精确概率，绿柱是模拟频率，同一均值并列比较，横位置保留4/3等真实分数。改变种子可以复现另一序列，固定种子则完整复现；这说明软件可核查，不把确定性种子当真实随机性的证明。

本工具先均匀选择一个允许子集再取成员均值，并用拒绝法避免整数索引的简单取模偏差。伪随机模拟用于探索，不替代全部样本表；便利设计旁列自身期望、方差和偏差，不拿总体均值冒充估计期望。

出口需列六个身份组合，说明分母、期望、方差和两类参数。家长可让孩子指认“每轮n”“重复数”“总体人数”三个不同数字，再追问为什么稳定的错误仍是错误。模拟轮数和样本量分开记录，不能以动画次数当成学习掌握或抽样证据质量。

# Simulating sampling variability

Repeated samples from the same population and rule still vary. Enumerate every allowed sample first, then use seeded pseudorandom simulation to observe empirical frequencies. Exact theory and a finite run serve different purposes.

## Four identities, not just two values

The finite population is A=0, B=0, C=4, D=4, with population mean two. Equal values do not merge people. Simple random sampling without replacement makes every n-member subset equally likely. A member cannot repeat inside one sample, but the full population is restored before the next repetition.

Sample means across all allowed samples form a sampling distribution. Compute its variance around its own expectation, dividing by the number of equally likely samples or weighting by their probabilities. This complete distribution is not a partial sample that needs an m−1 variance estimator.

### Example 1: Enumerate six equally likely samples

For n=2, the six samples AB,AC,AD,BC,BD,CD have means zero, two, two, two, two and four. Mean two therefore has probability 4/6=2/3; means zero and four each have probability 1/6.

The distribution’s expectation is twelve divided by six, or two, matching the population mean. Its variance is [4+0+0+0+0+4]/6=4/3. This is sampling variance of the mean, distinct from the original members’ population variance four.

### Example 2: Size, repetitions and bias differ

With n=1, means zero, zero, four and four have variance four. With n=3, the four means are 4/3,4/3,8/3,8/3, with variance 4/9. A census with n=4 has one possible mean, two, and variance zero.

These results use a finite population without replacement. The formula σ²/n·(N−n)/(N−1), with N=4 and σ²=4, provides a cross-check. The independent with-replacement formula σ²/n would incorrectly give one instead of zero for the census. Enumeration proves these particular results without requiring a memorised formula or a normal approximation.

Keeping n=2 while increasing repetitions from twelve to 192 does not enlarge individual samples. More rounds help approximate a fixed theoretical distribution, but do not guarantee that every realised seed and repetition count gets closer monotonically. Always selecting A,B instead yields mean zero with variance zero and bias−2 relative to target two. Repetition cannot repair that selection rule. A full census is an explicit exception; merely enlarging a convenience sample is not a general guarantee against bias.

## Explore reproducibly

Purple bars show exact probabilities and green bars empirical frequencies, paired at the same mean. Fractional mean positions such as 4/3 remain exact. A fixed seed reproduces a run; changing it generates a different sequence. Reproducibility makes software auditable but does not prove genuine randomness.

The tool selects an allowed subset and averages its members, using rejection to avoid simple integer-modulo bias in index selection. Pseudorandom runs support exploration; the complete sample table establishes exact probabilities. Convenience designs list their own expectation, variance and bias rather than substituting the target population mean.

For the exit, list all six identity combinations and explain denominators, expectation, variance and the two controls. A parent can ask the learner to distinguish population size, n per sample and repetition count, then explain why a stable error remains an error. Animation counts are neither mastery evidence nor a substitute for a sound sampling design.
