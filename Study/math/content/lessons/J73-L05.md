# 方程应用：年龄与数量

## 先定义未知量，再找相等关系

建模不从猜一个数开始。先写“设 x 表示什么”，补单位与取值条件，再把题干中相等的两种数量放到等号两边。不同对象之间有差或倍数时，要用同一个 x 表达关联，而不是把不同年龄或份量都叫 x。

本课明确区分精确年龄与整岁报告。精确年龄以年为单位，可以是非负分数；“完整袋数”“卡片张数”则是非负整数。方程可能有唯一代数解却不满足这些条件，建模时就应保留范围，不能等解出来后只看数值外观。

### 例 1：年龄差与时间一起表达

虚构两人的精确年龄相差八年、年龄和三十年。设较小年龄为 x 年，x≥0，较大年龄为 `x+8` 年。因此原年龄和与三十相等：`x+(x+8)=30`。合并后 `2x+8=30`，同减八、同除二，得 x=11。较大十九，核对十九减十一为八、十九加十一为三十，两人都非负。

若年龄差五、和二十四，则 `x+(x+5)=24`，得 `x=19/2`，即 9.5 年；另一人为 14.5 年。这不是人数，精确分数年数符合条件，不能擅自舍入十年。

再看同一时间线：现年龄差六，四年后较大为较小两倍。现较小 x、较大 x+6；四年后分别为 `x+4`、`x+10`，所以 `x+10=2(x+4)`，求得 x=2。现二与八，未来六与十二。写 `2x+4` 是只把原年龄翻倍再加四，和两倍未来年龄不同。

### 例 2：按袋重复量与一次余量

已有两支笔，另有 x 个完整袋，每袋三支，总共二十支。设 x 为非负整数袋数。按袋重复量 `3x` 加固定两支，原总量式为 `3x+2`，于是 `3x+2=20`。求得六袋，检查 `3×6+2=20`，且六是非负整数。

不能把它列成 `5x=20`，因为固定两支不会每袋再给一次。也不能把 x 当支数同时又乘每袋三支，未知量的意义必须前后一致。对于两盒相邻整数张卡片，总十七张，设较少 x、另一盒 x+1，列 `x+(x+1)=17`，得八与九，核对总量和相邻条件。

## 原数量建模与同解变形是两种任务

工作台与指定建模题要求先展示题干两种原数量，允许展开、合并或交换左右，如 `2x+8=30`、`30=x+x+8`。`2x=22` 或 `x=11` 与年龄原式数学上同解，但省掉了所要求展示的原数量关系。反馈会说明“解集相同，需补原建模式”，而不是宣称这些等式数学错误。

求解题则按精确数值判，年龄答案可带年或岁；原数量建模题要写一个等号及两边表达式。工作台滑块可以构造不可能条件，例如年龄和小于年龄差，或总支数不能由完整袋数组成。保留条件并指出无符合条件解，不修改数据凑整数。

家长检查的顺序是未知量定义、两边各代表什么、变形理由、代回原式及全部情境限制。孩子能说清每项来源，比只报一个正确数字更能说明建模已掌握。

# Age and quantity word problems

## Define the unknown before building the equality

Begin with what x measures or counts, its units and its allowed values. Then put the two equal original quantities on opposite sides. Use x to express related ages or amounts; do not call different quantities x without accounting for their difference.

This lesson distinguishes exact age in years from an age reported in completed years. Exact age may be a non-negative fraction. Whole bags and card counts require non-negative integers. State these domains before solving, because a unique algebraic root may still violate the context.

### Example 1: age difference and a shared timeline

Two fictional exact ages differ by eight and sum to thirty years. Let the younger be x years, with x≥0. The older is x+8. The original relationship is `x+(x+8)=30`. Collect, subtract eight and divide by two to get x=11. The older is nineteen; their difference is eight, their sum thirty and both ages are non-negative.

With a gap of five and sum twenty-four, `x+(x+5)=24` gives x=19/2, or 9.5 years. The other age is 14.5. These are ages rather than counts of people; fractional exact years are allowed and must not be rounded to ten.

For a gap of six and the older being twice the younger four years later, current ages are x and x+6. Future ages are x+4 and x+10, so `x+10=2(x+4)`. This gives x=2. Current ages two and eight become six and twelve. The expression 2x+4 doubles the current age before adding four, which is different from doubling the entire future age.

### Example 2: repeated bag contents and fixed extras

There are two fixed pencils plus x whole bags of three, totalling twenty. Define x as a non-negative integer bag count. The original total is 3x+2, so `3x+2=20`. Solving gives six bags, checking as `3×6+2=20` with the required integer domain.

The model 5x=20 incorrectly repeats the two fixed extras for every bag. Also keep the variable meaning consistent: x cannot switch between bag count and pencil count. For consecutive card counts totalling seventeen, the quantities are x and x+1. The equation `x+(x+1)=17` yields eight and nine, satisfying both total and consecutiveness.

## Showing the model is a distinct task

The workshop and explicitly labelled modelling questions ask for the two original quantities before transposition. Expansion, collection and side reversal are allowed, including `2x+8=30` and `30=x+x+8`. Equations `2x=22` and `x=11` have the same solution set but omit the original quantities this task asks you to display. Feedback identifies equivalent solutions and asks for the original model; it does not call these mathematically false equations.

Numerical solving questions accept an exact age with optional year units. Modelling questions require one equality with an expression on each side. Sliders may deliberately create impossible data, such as an age sum smaller than its gap or a total incompatible with whole bags. Retain the conditions and state no allowed solution rather than altering data to force an integer.

A parent checks the definition of x, the meaning of each side, reversible reasoning, the original substitution and every domain condition. Explaining where each term came from shows more modelling understanding than reporting a correct number alone.
