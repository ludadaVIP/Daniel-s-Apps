# 圆的标准方程

圆不是“长得圆的图”，而是到定点C的距离恒为正数r的点集。将距离条件变成方程，才能说明中心符号、半径平方和空轨迹。

## 从距离到标准式

设中心C(h,k)，任意点P(x,y)。由勾股，CP²=(x−h)²+(y−k)²。要求CP=r>0，就得到(x−h)²+(y−k)²=r²。反过来，若点满足此式，非负距离CP的平方等于r²，故CP=r。这是双向对应，不只验证几个点。

方程描述圆周，不含整个圆盘。CP²<r²是圆内，CP²>r²是圆外；中心到自身距离0，在r>0时不是圆周点。

当右端R=0时，两项非负平方必须同时为0，轨迹仅有(h,k)。当R<0时没有实点。我们把“圆”用于r>0的非退化情形，并分别称另外两种为单点与空集，不画一个虚假的圆。

### 例1：读中心，检查圆周点

(x−1)²+(y+2)²=25中，y+2=y−(−2)，中心为(1,−2)，半径√25=5而不是25。点P(4,2)与中心坐标差(3,4)，距离平方9+16=25，在圆上。

中心C(1,−2)代入左边为0，不等于25，所以C不在圆周上。点(1,3)也在圆上，因为纵向差5。这个圆不是必须以原点为中心，也不是所有点都离原点5。

### 例2：一般式配方恢复距离

对x²+y²−4x+6y−12=0，先移常数：x²−4x+y²+6y=12。补平方时保持等式平衡，得到(x−2)²+(y+3)²=12+4+9=25。中心(2,−3)，半径5。

检查P(5,1)：坐标差(3,4)给距离5；代原式25+1−20+6−12=0，也满足。若某次配方右端得到0，则只剩中心；若得到−1，则无实点。不能在未核对右端符号前把任何含x²+y²的等式都叫圆。

## 工作台与迁移

选择中心坐标、右端r²与待检验点P。先写轨迹方程，再判circle、point或empty，并按距离平方判P的位置。P是待检验点，不保证自动落在圆上；图中圆心也不是待求圆周点。

核验表把r²与r分开显示。改待检验点只重做相关位置任务；改中心或半径平方重做方程。图窗只截取图形，不改变数学集合。负右端保留坐标基准C，但不画圆；零右端只有C这个轨迹点。

出口需从一般式完成配方、给中心和半径、代点验证。家长可要求两种验证：距离条件与原方程代入。后续三种圆锥曲线也将先写距离定义，再写标准式，避免只按图形外观认名称。

# Standard equation of a circle

A circle is the set of points at a fixed positive distance r from centre C. Starting with this condition explains the centre signs, squared radius and empty-locus boundary.

## From distance to an equation

For C(h,k) and P(x,y), Pythagoras gives CP²=(x−h)²+(y−k)². Requiring CP=r>0 gives (x−h)²+(y−k)²=r². Conversely, an equation point has nonnegative distance whose square is r², so CP=r. This proves both directions, rather than checking a few examples.

The equation describes the circumference, not the whole disc. Squared distance smaller than r² means inside; larger means outside. The centre has distance zero and is not on a positive-radius circle.

If the right side R=0, both nonnegative squares must vanish, leaving (h,k) alone. If R<0, there are no real points. Here “circle” means the nondegenerate case r>0; the other loci are a singleton and the empty set.

### Example 1: Read the centre and test a point

In (x−1)²+(y+2)²=25, write y+2=y−(−2). The centre is (1,−2) and radius √25=5, not 25. At P(4,2), coordinate differences (3,4) give squared distance 25, so P is on the circle.

Substituting C(1,−2) gives zero on the left, not 25: the centre is not on the circumference. Point (1,3) is on it because its vertical displacement from C is five. Distances are measured from C, not necessarily the origin.

### Example 2: Complete squares to recover distance

Rearrange x²+y²−4x+6y−12=0 to x²−4x+y²+6y=12. Completing squares while balancing both sides gives (x−2)²+(y+3)²=12+4+9=25. Thus the centre is (2,−3) and radius five.

For P(5,1), coordinate differences (3,4) give distance five. Substitution in the original equation gives 25+1−20+6−12=0 as well. A completed right side zero would leave just the centre; a right side −1 would give no real points. Check that sign before calling an equation a circle.

## Workbench and transfer

Choose centre coordinates, right side r² and a test point P. Write the locus equation, classify circle, point or empty, then compare squared distances to classify P. The test point is not guaranteed to be on the circle; neither is the centre.

The audit separates r² and r. Changing P affects its position task; changing the centre or radius square changes the equation. Window changes do not alter the set. A negative right side keeps C as a coordinate reference but draws no circle; zero gives C alone as the locus.

The exit completes squares, states centre and radius, and verifies a point. Ask for both a distance check and original-equation substitution. The next conics follow the same approach: distance definition first, standard form second.
