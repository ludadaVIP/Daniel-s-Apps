## 先说清楚“一个整体”是谁

一根绳子、一杯原有的果汁、一张纸，都可以在一个问题里作为整体。分数的 **1** 指这个被选定的整体，不是所有东西都必须长一米或重一克。把这个整体等分成若干份，取其中一份，就是本课的单位分数。

**单位分数的分子是 1，分母表示这个整体的等份数。** 本课先探索分成两份及以上的情形。分母不能为零；“分了四块”还不足以叫四分之一，四块必须代表相同大小的量。面积图要检查面积，长度图要检查长度。

### 例 1 · 八等份中的一份

1. 先把一整根绳子作为整体。
2. 等分成 8 段，每一段的长度一样。
3. 一段是这根绳子的 **1/8**，两段的总量是 2/8。
4. 每段的单位仍为 1/8；取的段数不会改变小单位的名称。
5. 八段合起来是 8/8，也就是原整体。

### 例 2 · 两根绳子的一半

一根长 12 米，另一根长 6 米，分别把各自整根作为整体。

1. 12 米绳子的 1/2 是 12÷2 = **6 米**。
2. 6 米绳子的 1/2 是 6÷2 = **3 米**。
3. 两份都叫“一半”，却是不同大小整体的一半。
4. 同样的分数表示同样的比例，不保证实际长度相等。

### 分数、份数与实际量分别在说什么？

1/6 说明一个整体被分成六等份后的一份；2 米说明一段的实际长度。12 米平均分成六段时，一段既是整根的 1/6，又是 2 米。这两种说法互相配合，不要把 1/6 与 2 当成互相矛盾的答案。

若画出的三块不等大，不能因为块数是三，就给每块贴 1/3。先明确测量的是长度、面积还是容量，再检查等分。不同形状的部分也可能同面积；仅凭外形不一样，不能直接断言不等大。

工作台中，灰色轨道使用同一个长度标尺，整体轮廓可以变长或变短。一份始终是分母指定的单位分数，但其实际长度随整体改变。先预测，再改变整体与等份数，检查小份的大小。

**小检查：** 1 升与 2 升果汁都分成八等份，每份都占各自总量的 1/8。每份实际容量是否一样？解释整体改变了什么。

## Identify what counts as one whole

A rope, the juice originally in a jug, or a sheet of paper can be the whole in a problem. The fraction's **one** refers to that chosen whole; it does not require every object to be one metre or one gram. Divide the whole into equal parts and take one part to obtain a unit fraction.

**A unit fraction has numerator one, and its denominator counts equal parts of the whole.** This lesson starts with at least two parts. The denominator cannot be zero. Four pieces do not automatically mean quarters: the pieces must represent equal amounts. Compare area for area models and length for length models.

### Example 1 · One of eight equal lengths

1. Choose the entire rope as the whole.
2. Divide it into eight equally long lengths.
3. One length is **1/8** of that rope; two lengths total 2/8.
4. Each part's unit remains one eighth. Taking more parts does not change the unit's name.
5. Eight parts make 8/8, recovering the original whole.

### Example 2 · Halves of two ropes

One rope is twelve metres and the other six metres. Each entire rope is its own whole.

1. Half of twelve metres is 12÷2 = **six metres**.
2. Half of six metres is 6÷2 = **three metres**.
3. Both portions are called half, but they are halves of different-sized wholes.
4. A matching fraction gives a matching proportion, not necessarily matching actual lengths.

### Fraction, count and actual amount

1/6 describes one of six equal parts of a whole; two metres describes an actual length. If twelve metres is shared into six equal lengths, each is both one sixth of the rope and two metres. These descriptions work together rather than contradict each other.

Three unequal pieces cannot each be labelled one third just because there are three. Identify whether the quantity is length, area or volume before checking equality. Differently shaped regions can have equal areas; different appearance alone does not prove unequal amounts.

The workshop uses a fixed length scale along the grey track. The whole outline can grow or shrink. One selected part retains the unit fraction specified by the denominator, while its actual length follows the whole's size. Predict before changing whole length or part count.

**Quick check:** one litre and two litres of juice are each split eight ways. Each share is one eighth of its own total. Do the actual volumes match? Explain the change in the whole.
