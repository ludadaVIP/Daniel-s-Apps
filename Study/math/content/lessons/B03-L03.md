## 两条边都拆开，出现四块面积

长方形的面积可以用“每行多少单位方格×行数”计算。两位数乘法也能用这一个数量结构解释。把两条边各拆成整十与个位，长方形会分成四块；四块没有重叠、没有空缺，面积之和等于整体。

长度单位与面积单位要分清：边长以厘米计，面积以平方厘米计。一个 1 cm×1 cm 方格面积是 1 cm²，不能把长与宽简单相加得到面积。

### 例 1 · 23×14 的四块

1. 宽 23=20+3，高 14=10+4。
2. 四块分别是 **20×10=200、3×10=30、20×4=80、3×4=12**。
3. 合并 **200+30+80+12=322**，所以 23×14=322。
4. 高十的两块合计 200+30=230，等于 23×10。
5. 高四的两块合计 80+12=92，等于 23×4。竖式部分积可记成 230+92。

### 例 2 · 32×21，与竖式联系

1. 两边拆为 30+2 与 20+1。
2. 四块为 30×20=600、2×20=40、30×1=30、2×1=2。
3. 面积合计 **600+40+30+2=672**。
4. 按两条横带合并，也可做 **32×20+32×1=640+32=672**。
5. 第二个因数的十位 2 表示二十。乘它的那一行若只写成 64 而没有表示十位，就把 640 缩小了十倍。

### 为什么不能漏掉十位的单位

23×14 中，14 的 1 表示十，不是一个一。92+23=115 算的是 23×(4+1)，不是 23×(4+10)。竖式向左一位或补零，是为了记录部分积的位值，不能只记成一个无意义的排版动作。

40×12 的宽可以写成 40+0。有零宽的区域，面积为零，不需要画成一块有面积的图；仍然必须算 40×10+40×2=400+80=480。少一块为零与漏掉一块正面积不是同一回事。

18 cm×25 cm 的纸板面积为 200+160+50+40=450 cm²。43 cm 是两条边相加，86 cm 是周长，都不是面积。先确认题目要覆盖多少方格，还是沿边绕多远。

工作台在两条轴使用同一个单位比例，图形的宽、高随数值同步变化。先预测四块，再展开部分积；面积相等依赖乘法与分割关系，不依赖肉眼觉得两块“差不多大”。

**小检查：** 用 31=30+1、12=10+2 找四块，说明哪两块组成 31×10，再求整体。

## Split both sides to obtain four areas

A rectangle's area counts unit squares per row times the number of rows. The same structure explains two-digit multiplication. Splitting each side into tens and ones creates four regions. They have no overlap or gaps, so their areas sum to the whole.

Length and area units differ. Centimetre side lengths produce square centimetres. A 1 cm by 1 cm square has area 1 cm²; adding the side lengths does not count covered squares.

### Example 1 · Four regions for 23×14

1. Width 23=20+3 and height 14=10+4.
2. The regions are **20×10=200, 3×10=30, 20×4=80 and 3×4=12**.
3. Combine **200+30+80+12=322**, giving 23×14=322.
4. The ten-high regions total 200+30=230, equal to 23×10.
5. The four-high regions total 80+12=92, equal to 23×4. These become written partial products 230+92.

### Example 2 · Connect 32×21 to written calculation

1. Split the sides into 30+2 and 20+1.
2. Four regions give 30×20=600, 2×20=40, 30×1=30 and 2×1=2.
3. Combine **600+40+30+2=672**.
4. Grouping into two horizontal bands instead gives **32×20+32×1=640+32=672**.
5. The 2 in the second factor's tens place represents twenty. Writing its partial product as 64 without its place value shrinks 640 by a factor of ten.

### Keep the tens unit

In 23×14, the 1 represents ten rather than one. Adding 92+23=115 calculates 23×(4+1), not 23×(4+10). Shifting left or recording a zero preserves the partial product's place value; it is not an arbitrary formatting trick.

For 40×12, width is 40+0. A zero-width region has zero area and should not appear as a positive-area rectangle. Still include 40×10+40×2=400+80=480. A zero region is different from accidentally omitting a positive region.

An 18 cm by 25 cm card has area 200+160+50+40=450 cm². Adding two sides gives 43 cm, while perimeter is 86 cm; neither is the area. Identify whether the task asks for covered squares or distance around the edge.

Both workshop axes use the same unit scale and the sides change with their numerical values. Predict all four regions before revealing partial products. Equality comes from multiplication and the partition, not from two regions looking approximately alike.

**Quick check:** split 31=30+1 and 12=10+2 into four regions. Identify the two that form 31×10 before finding the whole product.
