# 定积分的几何意义

定积分带上下限，结果是一个有方向的数。若横坐标从小到大，曲线在横轴上方的部分贡献为正，下方为负。几何面积则把每块面积大小相加，不允许用负面积抵消。两者可能相等，也可能差很远。

## 先分块，再处理方向

设a<b，正区域面积P，负区域的面积大小N，则∫ₐᵇf=P−N，而与横轴间的总几何面积为P+N=∫ₐᵇ|f|。先找全部区间内零点，在这些点分块，再核每段符号。零点未必变号，例如x²在0处两侧都非负；分块虽可保守多分一次，不能凭“有根”就断言方向改变。

交换上下限定义∫ᵦₐf=−∫ₐᵇf。几何区域没有因此变成负面积；“总面积”仍按从小到大坐标计量。相同上下限a=b时，区间长度零，积分为零，与f(a)是否为零无关。

### 例1：正负抵消与反向积分

取f(x)=x−1，区间[0,3]。根在1。0到1下方三角形底1、高1，面积大小1/2；1到3上方三角形底2、高2，面积2。因此有向积分2−1/2=3/2，总几何面积2+1/2=5/2。

用原函数F=x²/2−x独立核验，F(3)−F(0)=9/2−3=3/2。若倒过来积分，从3到0得−3/2，但同一片几何区域面积仍5/2。取定积分的绝对值只得3/2，不能找回被抵消的那1单位面积。

图上绿色与橙色表示相对横轴的符号，不是材料数量。若f是速度，积分单位为位移米；若两轴本来都以米计，几何面积单位才是平方米。像素颜色本身不能决定单位。

### 例2：零积分不等于零函数

取f(x)=2−x，在[0,4]。根在2。左侧正三角形面积2，右侧负区域大小2，所以积分0、总面积4。函数显然并非处处零，只有正负贡献抵消。

另外∫₂²(2−x)dx=0来自区间长度零；即使改成∫₂²x²dx，仍是0，而被积函数在2处值4。把零积分归因于“高度必须零”会混淆这两个原因。

连续非负函数在非零长度闭区间上若积分零，则处处零，这需要非负与连续等条件。当前有符号例子不能套用这个更强结论。对始终负的−x²，在[−2,2]积分−16/3，总面积16/3；此时没有正负相消，但积分仍是负数。

## 让图与算式各尽其职

工作台可切换曲线与有序上下限。几何着色固定按递增横坐标分块，标题保留实际积分方向；反向时只改变有向数，不能交换区域的正负身份。相同上下限只显示退化区间，不画成有宽度的矩形。

先预测积分与总面积两个数，再核验每段贡献。出口说明需要交代根、每段符号、端点方向以及单位。家长可问：“你相加的是有符号变化还是区域大小？”和“把上下限交换后，哪些量应变号？”只背上减下不能代替完整分块。

# Geometric meaning of definite integrals

A definite integral has ordered bounds and produces a signed number. With increasing horizontal coordinate, regions above the axis contribute positively and regions below it negatively. Geometric area adds magnitudes and cannot cancel positive against negative pieces.

## Separate pieces from orientation

For a<b, let P be the positive-region area and N the magnitude of the negative-region area. Then ∫ₐᵇf=P−N, while total geometric area is P+N=∫ₐᵇ|f|. Locate every interior zero and check the sign on each resulting piece. A root does not necessarily reverse sign: x² stays nonnegative on both sides of zero.

Reversing bounds gives ∫ᵦₐf=−∫ₐᵇf. The geometric region remains unchanged. Total geometric area is still measured across increasing coordinates. Equal bounds give zero interval length and zero integral regardless of the point height.

### Example 1: Cancellation and reversed bounds

For f(x)=x−1 on[0,3], the root is one. The negative triangle on[0,1] has magnitude1/2. The positive triangle on[1,3] has area two. Thus the signed integral is2−1/2=3/2, whereas total area is2+1/2=5/2.

An independent primitive F=x²/2−x gives F(3)−F(0)=9/2−3=3/2. Integrating from three to zero gives−3/2. Reversing direction does not change geometric area5/2. Taking the absolute value of the net integral gives only3/2 and cannot restore the cancelled contributions.

Colours identify signs relative to the axis, not physical units. If f is velocity and the horizontal axis is time, its integral represents displacement in metres. Geometric square metres require both coordinate axes to carry length units. Pixel area alone determines neither interpretation.

### Example 2: Zero integral does not mean zero function

For f(x)=2−x on[0,4], the root is two. The positive and negative triangles each have area magnitude two. The integral is zero and total area four. The function is clearly not identically zero; its signed contributions cancel.

The integral from two to two is also zero, but now because the interval has zero length. Replacing the function by x² leaves ∫₂²x²dx=0 although its point value at two is four. Do not infer zero height from a zero-length integral.

A continuous nonnegative function on a nonzero closed interval with zero integral must be identically zero there. Nonnegativity and continuity are conditions behind that stronger statement. They do not apply to the signed cancellation example. For−x² on[−2,2], the integral is−16/3 and geometric area16/3: an always-negative curve gives a negative integral without any positive cancellation.

## Coordinate the picture with the calculation

The workbench shades pieces using increasing coordinates while retaining the ordered bounds in its caption. Reversal changes the signed result, preserving the area prediction. Equal bounds show a degenerate interval rather than inventing a positive-width rectangle.

Predict both quantities, then audit signed and absolute contributions separately. An exit explanation should name the roots, signs, direction and units. Parent prompts should ask what is being added and which quantities change sign when bounds reverse. A remembered endpoint subtraction rule does not replace sign analysis or identify the requested quantity by itself.
