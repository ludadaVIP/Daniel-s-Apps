# 单位圆与三角函数

锐角直角三角形用正边长比定义三角函数。把角转到120°或225°后，直角三角形的那套“全部为正”不够了；需要用带方向的坐标扩展定义。

## 一条核心想法

**单位圆上的点P=(cosθ,sinθ)。** 圆心在原点、半径1，角从正横轴量起。横坐标给余弦，纵坐标给正弦；坐标可以为负，半径和边长仍非负。只要cosθ≠0，tanθ=sinθ/cosθ。

第一至第四象限的(cos,sin)符号依次为(+,+)、(−,+)、(−,−)、(+,−)。坐标轴上的角单独读取，不硬贴某个象限。参考角给坐标大小，所在象限给符号；负角按顺时针转，超过整圈的角保留输入但可用同终边读取函数值。

### 例1：先参考角，再定符号

θ=2π/3=120°，在第二象限，参考角π/3。cosθ=−1/2，sinθ=√3/2；横坐标负而纵坐标正。因此tanθ=(√3/2)/(−1/2)=−√3。没有任何边长因此变成负的。

θ=5π/4=225°在第三象限，参考角π/4，两坐标都是−√2/2。13π/6=2π+π/6与π/6同终边，所以sin(13π/6)=1/2。完整角13π/6与π/6不同，但周期函数输出可以相同。

### 例2：轴上点与反向旋转

θ=π/2时P=(0,1)，正弦1、余弦0；tan需要1/0，所以无定义。不能把它记成0，也不能把计算器屏幕上的一个极大近似数当作确切函数值。

θ=π时P=(−1,0)，tanπ=0/(−1)=0，横轴点不会让正切失效。θ=−π/3是π/3关于横轴的反射，横坐标保持1/2，纵坐标变为−√3/2，说明cos(−θ)=cosθ、sin(−θ)=−sinθ。

## 单位圆实验

用可键盘操作的角度滑杆先预测三个值，再揭示精确坐标表。图用同单位标尺显示投影，不把“到横轴的距离”冒称负长度。输入π或sqrt表达式保留确切值；√2/2与1/√2等值，0.707只是近似。

拖到θ=π/2或−π/2时，正切任务接受undefined并解释分母条件。改变显示表格不会清除预测；改变输入角会清除对应旧答案。图中P的位置和表里的(cos,sin)必须按同一次输入核验。

## 迁移与家长核对

出口给P=(−√3/2,−1/2)。先根据两坐标符号确定第三象限，再由纵坐标大小1/2得到参考角π/6，故[0,2π)中的角为7π/6。家长可交换两个坐标，让孩子指出会改变什么。下一课将同一个坐标沿角度轴展开成函数图，恒等式课再用单位圆方程连接两坐标。

# Unit circle trigonometry

Acute right triangles define trig functions using positive side lengths. Extending to 120° or 225° requires more than an “all positive” rule. Signed coordinates supply the extension.

## One central idea

**The unit-circle point is P=(cosθ,sinθ).** Its centre is the origin, radius one, and angles start from the positive horizontal axis. Horizontal position is cosine and vertical position sine. Coordinates may be negative while radius and side lengths stay nonnegative. Where cosθ≠0, tanθ=sinθ/cosθ.

In quadrants I to IV, the coordinate signs (cos,sin) are (+,+), (−,+), (−,−), (+,−). Read axis points separately instead of assigning them to quadrants. A reference angle gives magnitudes; the quadrant supplies signs. Negative angles rotate clockwise. Angles beyond a full turn retain their input while coterminal rays help read function values.

### Example 1: Reference angle, then sign

θ=2π/3=120° is in quadrant II with reference angle π/3. Thus cosθ=−1/2 and sinθ=√3/2: horizontal negative, vertical positive. Consequently tanθ=(√3/2)/(−1/2)=−√3. No side length has become negative.

At θ=5π/4=225°, the reference angle is π/4 and both coordinates are −√2/2. Also 13π/6=2π+π/6 is coterminal with π/6, so sin(13π/6)=1/2. The complete angles differ even though a periodic function can give equal outputs.

### Example 2: Axis points and reversed rotation

At θ=π/2 the point is (0,1), giving sine one and cosine zero. Tangent would require 1/0 and is undefined. It is neither zero nor an enormous real value suggested by a calculator’s numerical error near the pole.

At θ=π, P=(−1,0), so tanπ=0/(−1)=0: horizontal-axis positions do not make tangent undefined. The angle −π/3 reflects π/3 across the horizontal axis, preserving cosine 1/2 and reversing sine to −√3/2. This illustrates cos(−θ)=cosθ and sin(−θ)=−sinθ.

## Unit-circle experiment

Use the keyboard-accessible angle slider to predict three values before revealing the exact coordinate table. Equal unit scales show signed projections without describing a negative coordinate as a negative distance. Pi and sqrt expressions retain exact values: √2/2 and 1/√2 are equivalent, whereas 0.707 is approximate.

At θ=π/2 or −π/2, the tangent task accepts undefined and explains its denominator condition. Revealing the table preserves predictions; changing the mathematical input resets them. Audit the displayed point and its (cos,sin) row using the same angle.

## Transfer and parent check

For the exit point (−√3/2,−1/2), the two negative coordinates identify quadrant III. Vertical magnitude 1/2 gives reference angle π/6, so the angle in [0,2π) is 7π/6. A parent can swap the coordinates and ask what changes. Next, unwrap one coordinate against angle into a graph; later use the unit-circle equation to connect both coordinates in an identity.
