# 平均数与异常值

## 特别的一条记录先核查，不先删除

某条数值远离其余数据，是进一步核查的信号，不自动证明它错误。它可能来自单位混用、录入失误，也可能是真实而少见的情况。先看来源与原始记录；若证实错误，说明依据并记录更正，若有效就保留，报告它怎样影响分布和摘要。

均值使用全部数值的总和，中位数使用排序后的中间位置，两者对改动的反应不同。不能简单背“平均数受异常值影响、中位数永远不变”；也不能为了得到好看的平均数，事后随意选择删哪些观测。

### 例 1：八改五十，条数不变

原记录四、五、六、七、八，条数五，总和三十，均值六，中位数六。只把最后八改成五十，新记录为四、五、六、七、五十，总和七十二。

新均值 `72/5=14.4`，中位数仍为排序第三条六。总和增加 `50−8=42`，固定五条使均值增加 `42/5=8.4`，不是增加四十二。这个均值可能明显高于大多数记录，因此应同时说明分布和中位数，而不是藏掉五十。

### 例 2：改为零，中位数也会动

把原来的八改成零，得到四、五、六、七、零。总和二十二，仍有五条，所以均值 `22/5=4.4`。零是有效数值，不是没填，不能把分母变成四。

排序为零、四、五、六、七，中间第三条五，说明中位数也能改变。上例不变来自具体排序位置，并不是一个对所有修改都成立的定理。若有相同数值，也逐条保留，不把重复计数删掉。

再看一、二、三、四、二十：均值六，中位数三。若二十已核实有效，两个摘要都可报告，并解释大值把均值向右拉；不能把一到四的均值二点五冒充原五条的均值。

## 共同刻度保留变化的真实大小

工作台保持两图零至六十共同尺度，只替换一条记录并保留原编号位置，不自动删除或补值。近处点可能聚在一起，旁边同时列出精确记录供核算；刻度不为某一图偷偷改变。切换新值后，旧均值、中位数答案与核查标记会清空。

“已核原记录”的勾选仅表示在这个虚构例子中完成了核查，不改变数值或统计公式。未勾选也不自动剔除；真实任务需要依据与可追踪处理。异常的定义还依情境、量纲与比较群体，不能用本例五十作为所有问题的固定阈值。

家长请孩子先说明条数是否保持，再比较总和与中间位置，最后解释数据处理决定。计算与来源核查是两件事；一个数影响很大，正是需要透明报告的原因，不是偷偷消除它的理由。

# Means and outliers

## Check an unusual record before removing it

A value far from the rest prompts investigation rather than automatic rejection. It may reflect mixed units or an entry error, or a valid unusual observation. Check the source. Correct verified errors transparently, while retaining valid data and reporting its influence.

Mean uses every value through the total; median uses ordered positions. Their responses can differ. Do not replace this with the slogan that median never changes, or choose deletions after seeing which mean looks preferable.

### Example 1: replace eight by fifty, keeping five records

The original 4,5,6,7,8 totals thirty, with mean and median six. Replacing only eight by fifty gives total seventy-two and mean 72/5=14.4. The ordered middle remains six.

The total change is forty-two, so with count five fixed, mean change is 42/5=8.4. It is not forty-two. The mean now exceeds most observations; report the distribution and median as well rather than hiding fifty.

### Example 2: zero can change the middle too

Replacing eight by zero gives 4,5,6,7,0, total twenty-two and mean 22/5=4.4. Zero remains a record, not a missing response, so the denominator stays five.

Ordering gives 0,4,5,6,7 with median five. The median can therefore change. Its stability in the fifty example follows from those particular ordered positions, not a universal immunity to modification. Duplicate values also remain separate observations.

For 1,2,3,4,20, mean is six and median three. If twenty is verified, reporting both summaries can explain its influence. The mean 2.5 of the first four is not the mean of the original five observations.

## Common scales preserve the size of the change

The workshop retains a zero-to-sixty scale on both plots and replaces one record without deleting its identity. Closely spaced values may cluster visually; the exact records are also printed for calculation. Changing the replacement clears prior answers and the prior verification mark.

The checkbox records a source check within this fictional context, without altering the data or formula. An unchecked record is not automatically removed either. Real analysis needs evidence and traceable decisions. Unusualness depends on context, units and comparison group, not a universal threshold of fifty borrowed from this example.

A parent asks whether count stayed fixed, how the total changed and which positions became central, then reviews the data-handling decision. Arithmetic and verification are separate. A large influence calls for clear reporting rather than concealment.
