# 整式加减

## 先把每个整式当作完整对象

整式加减由去括号和合并同类项组成，但要先看清外部运算作用于哪一个完整对象。`A−B` 的意思是加上 B 的整体相反数；A 保留不变，B 的每项乘负一。若是 `A+B`，则 B 各项原本的正负号保持，而不是一见括号就全部变号。

字母部分仍决定哪些项同类。平方项、一次项、常数分别处理；两个字母的 xy 项与 y 项也分开。整式没有把这些不同类型变成可随意拼成一个项的数。

### 例 1：减去三个项，三项都处理

设 `A=2x²−x+3`，`B=x²+2x−4`，求 `A−B`。

1. 保留 A：`2x²−x+3`。
2. B 的整体相反数为 `−x²−2x+4`，不是只把 x² 改负。
3. 按类型合并：平方系数 `2−1=1`，一次系数 `−1−2=−3`，常数 `3−(−4)=7`。
4. 结果 `x²−3x+7`。

用逆关系核验：结果加原 B，`x²−3x+7+x²+2x−4=2x²−x+3`，恢复 A。若反查时把 B 也换成相反数，就不是这个核验。把 A 额外取反同样改变问题。

工作台先给 A、B 的完整式子，再展开外部符号；符号卡按同类项分组，同时保留来源身份。改变 B 的一次系数时，A 的项没有被悄悄修改。

### 例 2：加法与分数系数

`(x²+3x−2)+(2x²−x+5)` 的外部加号保留第二式符号，得到 `x²+3x−2+2x²−x+5`，合并为 `3x²+2x+3`。若把第二式所有项变号，算成的就是减法，不是原题。

对比 `[(1/2)x²+x]−[(1/3)x²−2x]`：外部减号使第二式成为 `−(1/3)x²+2x`。平方系数 `1/2−1/3=1/6`，一次系数 `1−(−2)=3`，所以结果 `(1/6)x²+3x`。通分发生在数值系数上，字母指数不变。

## 零和嵌套符号

相同整式相减，如 `(x²+2x+1)−(x²+2x+1)`，每一类都抵消，整体恒为零。应该明确写零，不留下 `x²−x²` 当作已经合并完的答案，也不把“没有项剩下”当成没有结果。

嵌套关系也要按完整对象处理：`A−(B−C)=A−B+C`。括号内原本的负 C 再乘外部负一变成正 C，而不是 A−B−C。可以先将 B−C 当一个对象，标出它的两项，再执行分配律。

## 相等需要什么证据

本课允许重排项和等值分数系数，但要求完成去括号和合并。仍有重复字母部分的中间式，系统会说明“已等值，还需完成写法”，避免把概念正确与任务完成混为同一状态。

一般等值关系来自每类精确系数相等，或来自分配律、加法性质和逆关系。代一个 x 可发现漏项，几次结果相同却不能证明恒等。向家长说明时，请指出每个项来自 A 还是 B、是否受到外部符号作用、与哪项同类，以及加回如何还原。

# Adding and subtracting expressions

## Treat each expression as a whole object

Polynomial sums and differences combine bracket removal with collection, but first identify the complete object affected by the outside operation. `A−B` adds the opposite of all of B: retain A and multiply every B term by negative one. In `A+B`, preserve B’s existing signs.

Variable parts still determine like terms. Squares, linear terms and constants are separate, as are xy and y. A polynomial is not permission to merge unlike types into one term.

### Example 1: subtract every term of the second expression

Let `A=2x²−x+3` and `B=x²+2x−4`. Find `A−B`.

1. Retain `2x²−x+3`.
2. The opposite of B is `−x²−2x+4`, not just a negative first term.
3. Collect square coefficient `2−1=1`, linear coefficient `−1−2=−3` and constant `3−(−4)=7`.
4. The result is `x²−3x+7`.

Check the inverse relationship by adding original B: `x²−3x+7+x²+2x−4=2x²−x+3`, recovering A. Negating B again during the check would check a different relationship. Nor should A be negated in the original subtraction.

The workshop first presents both full expressions, then reveals the outside sign’s action. Grouped symbolic cards retain their source identities. Changing B’s linear coefficient does not silently change A.

### Example 2: addition and rational coefficients

In `(x²+3x−2)+(2x²−x+5)`, addition preserves the second signs, giving `3x²+2x+3`. Negating the second group instead would calculate a subtraction that was not asked for.

For `[(1/2)x²+x]−[(1/3)x²−2x]`, the second expression becomes `−(1/3)x²+2x`. The square coefficient is `1/2−1/3=1/6`, and the linear coefficient is `1−(−2)=3`. The result is `(1/6)x²+3x`. Common denominators change numerical coefficient representations, not variable exponents.

## Zero and nested signs

Subtracting a polynomial from itself cancels every type. Write zero explicitly rather than retaining an unfinished `x²−x²`. No remaining terms still means a defined zero expression.

For nested objects, `A−(B−C)=A−B+C`: the inside negative C receives an outside negative-one factor. Identify the two complete inside terms before distributing.

Term order and equivalent rational coefficients are accepted, but finish distribution and collection. An equivalent intermediate with repeated variable parts receives a form reminder rather than being mistaken for a completed task. General equality comes from matching exact coefficients or justified algebraic relationships. Trial substitutions can expose a missing term, but a few matches do not prove identity. Explain term sources, sign scope, matching types and the inverse check.
