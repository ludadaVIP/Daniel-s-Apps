# 地图和摄影中的相似

## 比例来自模型，不来自外观

地图、照片和影子都能提供比例问题，但必须先说明哪种几何模型适用。本课数据均为虚构。原印图上的厘米、屏幕中的像素和现实距离是不同数量；一张看起来像矩形的斜照片，也不自动拥有全局统一长度倍率。计算之前先列条件，最后核单位和结论适用范围。

### 例 1：原印图与屏幕缩放

虚构原印图比例尺1:50000，表示原印图每1 cm对应实际50000 cm，不是50000 km。AB原图直线长3 cm，则实际直线距离为150000 cm=1500 m=1.5 km。逆向若实际2 km，先换200000 cm，再除50000，得原图4 cm。用实际km直接除50000会把单位混在一起。

把这张图在屏幕放大两倍，AB像素长度也翻倍，但现实AB仍为1.5 km。表示1 km的图示比例尺必须同步放大两倍，AB与比例尺条的长度比仍为3/2。原印刷1:50000数值不是任意屏幕上物理厘米的比例；屏幕缩放、设备密度和显示尺寸都会影响它。这里算的是两点直线距离，不能没有路线信息就叫道路里程或旅行路程。

### 例 2：照片框与保留内容

完整4×3 cm照片等比缩放到宽10 cm，k=10/4=5/2，新高3×5/2=15/2 cm，面积75 cm²，面积倍率25/4。图中圆形也按同倍率成为圆形。若强行做成10×6，宽比5/2、高比2不统一，圆被拉成椭圆，是拉伸。

若先从4×3从下方裁去一条，只保留4×2，再等比放大到宽10，保留框成为10×5。保留下来的图案没有被压扁，它们与裁切前相应部分之间仍是等比缩放；但新完整框10×5与最初完整框4×3的长宽比不同，两个完整框不相似。裁切删除内容，拉伸改变保留内容的形状，不能混为一谈。

斜拍平面矩形时，透视可改变不同位置和方向的长度尺度，平行线也可能在图中相交。仅有一处长度标定，不足以保证整幅图具有统一相似倍率；需要更多几何信息或校正。工作台用实际透视变换展示轮廓和图案，不把一个任意梯形当成已经标定的厘米图。

## 影长应用先核AA

虚构条件明确为同时同地的平行光、同一水平地面、物体均直立。两组高度与影长构成直角三角形，直角相等，光线和地面的夹角相等，因此AA相似。1.2 m杆影0.8 m，另一个物体影3 m，则h/3=1.2/0.8，h=4.5 m。若没有这些前提，不能只把两条影长代入就宣布唯一高度。

切换地图、照片、影长案例，先判断证据是否充分，再计算。地图缩放只改显示；照片操作会改题目；关闭影长前提后，应回答条件不足，工作台也不再把两个直角三角形当作已知展示。家庭挑战用纸上虚构街区图和照片框即可：记录来源、单位、对应、倍率及面积，并写一个模型不适用的例子。家长核推理链，不需要真实出行、摄影、购买或上传个人资料。

# Similarity in maps and images

## State the model before calculating

All data in this lesson are fictional. Original-print centimetres, screen pixels and real distances are different quantities. An oblique photograph does not automatically provide one global length factor. State the geometric premises, convert units and limit the conclusion to what the model supports.

### Example 1: a print and its displayed copy

An original printed map at 1:50000 represents 50000 real centimetres for each print centimetre. A straight-line AB measurement of three centimetres therefore represents 150000 cm=1500 m=1.5 km. Conversely two real kilometres are 200000 cm, represented by four centimetres on that original print. Converting before dividing prevents unit errors.

Doubling the screen display doubles AB's pixel length without changing the real distance. The 1 km scale bar must double too, preserving the route-to-bar ratio 3/2. The original numerical print ratio cannot be applied directly to physical centimetres measured on an arbitrarily resized screen. Display zoom and device density matter. The calculated quantity is straight-line distance; road distance requires route information.

### Example 2: resize, stretch and crop

A complete 4×3 cm photo uniformly resized to width ten has factor 5/2, height 15/2 cm and area 75 cm². Its area factor is 25/4. A circular motif remains circular. Forcing the complete frame to 10×6 instead uses width factor 5/2 and height factor two, distorting a circle into an ellipse.

Cropping first to a retained 4×2 frame and then uniformly enlarging to width ten gives a 10×5 frame. Retained content is not squashed; it remains uniformly scaled relative to the corresponding retained portion of the original. However, the complete original 4×3 frame and final 10×5 frame have different aspect ratios and are not similar. Cropping removes content, whereas stretching changes its shape.

Perspective photography may vary scale with position and direction. One local length calibration does not establish similarity over the whole image. Additional geometric information or rectification is required. The lab uses a projective transformation for the outline and motif, without claiming calibrated centimetre dimensions for that perspective image.

For the shadow model, explicitly assume simultaneous parallel light, one level ground plane and upright objects. Right angles and equal light angles establish AA. A 1.2 m rod with 0.8 m shadow and another shadow of three metres then give h/3=1.2/0.8, hence h=4.5 m. Without those premises, the same shadow information does not establish a unique height.

Switch between the application cases and judge evidence before calculating. Map zoom preserves an answer; a different photo operation changes the task. Removing the shadow premises removes the justified triangle illustration. The family challenge uses a fictional paper map and image frames to record correspondence, units, length and area factors, together with a case where the model fails. A parent reviews the reasoning without requiring travel, purchases or personal uploads.
