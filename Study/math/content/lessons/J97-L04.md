# 样本偏差

## 先说明想了解谁

本课用虚构资料卡，不要求收集真实个人资料。目标总体是A、B、C三组各8张，共24张卡。抽样框是抽取办法真正能碰到的编号范围，不一定等于目标总体。样本则是实际被选中的一部分，回答者又可能只是被邀请者的一部分。

把总体、框、选择与回应分开，才能判断一份比例描述了谁。只问身边A组，可能漏掉与A不同的B/C；即使把A组全部问完，这种遗漏仍存在。样本量增大通常能减少适当随机设计中的部分随机波动，却不会自动修复固定的覆盖遗漏或回应差异。

### 例 1：A组全部不是总体全部

模型真值：A组8张中2张是，B组4张是，C组6张是。因此A比例2/8=1/4，全部比例(2+4+6)/24=1/2。只用A组得到的1/4正确描述A，但不能直接说总体也是1/4。A组全体回答也仍少了另外16张。

从全24编号按已知机会随机抽取，覆盖条件比只取A好，但一个8张小样本仍可能恰多是或恰少是，甚至没有每组都出现。这种单次波动不自动证明办法有偏，偶然抽到恰好1/2也不证明每次都完美代表。评估办法不能只看本次离真值多近。

### 例 2：未回答不能当作否

邀请12张，8张回答，其中6张是、2张否，另外4张未回答。回答者中是的比例6/8=3/4。若报告6/12=1/2，那个数是已知是占邀请者的比例，不是回答者中的比例；它也不能通过把未回答都设为否来冒充总体是比例。

回应率是8/12，而回答者中是比例是6/8；两者分母对象不同。缺失者若更倾向某种答案，结果还可能有未回答偏差。工作台让全框抽取的C组卡全部未回答，明确保留缺失标记，不改成否。无人回答时，是/回答数成为0/0，比例未定义而非0%。

## 什么能够支持什么结论

如果完整24张都回答、无遗漏且记录准确，就能知道这24张卡的精确比例，这是本情境的普查。它不自动代表另一个学校或另一时间的总体。若只掌握样本，通常只能带着设计依据和局限作估计，不能宣布精确总体真值已经确定。

改进可以包括扩大到正确的全体抽样框、说明随机选择机会、记录未回答并尝试恰当跟进。分组覆盖也有帮助，但不能仅为凑出“每组一样多”就保证总体估计正确；组大小与实际回应机制也要考虑。本课先练清楚对象与分母，不把复杂估计公式当万能补丁。

先报告邀请数、回答数、是与未回答，再判断是否足够确定目标总体的精确比例。反转记录的显示顺序不改变资料，换抽样种子或办法会形成新样本。家长阅读理由时核目标、覆盖、回应与单次波动，不能只奖励接近1/2的结果。

# Sampling bias

## Identify the target population

Use fictional cards rather than collecting real personal information. The target is 24 cards: eight each in A,B,C. A sampling frame is the IDs the selection procedure can reach. It may omit part of the target. The selected sample is not necessarily the set of respondents, because some invitations receive no answer.

Separate population, frame, selection and response. Nearby A-only selection can omit systematically different B/C groups. Asking every A card does not restore them. Increasing sample size can reduce some random variation under an appropriate design, but does not automatically repair fixed undercoverage or differential response.

### Example 1: all of A is not all of the population

Model truth is two yes in A, four in B and six in C, each group of size eight. A proportion is 1/4 while the all-24 proportion is 12/24=1/2. The A result accurately describes A, without directly describing the target population. An A census still omits 16 cards.

Full-frame random selection improves coverage opportunities, but any eight-card sample can contain more or fewer yes cards and need not include every group. One unusual sample does not prove a biased procedure; one sample matching 1/2 does not guarantee perfect representation in every run. Audit the method rather than only its realised closeness to truth.

### Example 2: nonresponse is not no

Twelve invitations yield eight responses: six yes and two no, with four missing. The respondent yes proportion is 6/8=3/4. Six divided by twelve describes known yes among invitees, a different quantity. Treating missing as no cannot turn it into a valid population yes proportion.

The response rate is 8/12, whereas yes among respondents is 6/8. Name what each denominator counts. Nonresponse may introduce bias if missing answers differ systematically. The lab’s C cards fail to respond under one scenario, and remain explicitly missing. With no responses, a yes proportion is undefined, never 0%.

An accurate complete census of all 24, without omissions, determines this population’s exact proportion. It does not automatically generalise to another school or time. A sample normally supports an estimate subject to design assumptions and limitations, rather than a declaration of exact population truth.

Improvements can include the correct full frame, known selection opportunities, recording missing responses and appropriate follow-up. Group coverage also matters, but equal numbers from groups do not automatically guarantee a valid population estimate; group sizes and response mechanisms still count. Begin with clear targets and denominators.

Report invitations, responses, yes and missing before judging whether an exact target proportion is established. Reversing record order changes only the display; changing seed or method produces a new sample. A parent reviews coverage, response and finite variation without simply rewarding estimates nearest 1/2.
