# 复合函数求导法则

把x先送入一台机器g，再把输出送入另一台机器F，得到F(g(x))。求导时不能只看外面那层操作；内层把输入放快、放慢或反向，都影响最后变化率。

## 内值与内导不是同一件事

设z=g(x)、y=F(z)。当g在x处可导、F在g(x)处可导时，dy/dx=F′(g(x))g′(x)。外导F′必须在内层输出g(x)处计算，再乘内导g′(x)。这不是任意分数约分的许可证，链式法则有明确的函数与可导条件。

对正整数n，([g(x)]ⁿ)′=n[g(x)]ⁿ⁻¹g′(x)。本课自动工具只处理总次数不超过4的有理系数多项式；规则的数学意义不只限于这个展示范围。涉及根、对数或分母的函数时还需核定义域与可导条件，不能只凭外形套用。

### 例1：立方外层接收的输入不是原来的x

f(x)=(2x+1)³。内层g(x)=2x+1，g′=2；外层F(z)=z³，F′(z)=3z²。所以f′=3(2x+1)²·2=6(2x+1)²。

在x=1时，内值z=3，外导F′(3)=27，内导2，最终f′(1)=54。若误用F′(1)=3，就在错误输入求了外导；若只答27，就漏掉内层快两倍。原函数f(1)=27恰好与此处外导同数，也不代表它们是同一个量。

展开独立核验：f=8x³+12x²+6x+1，所以f′=24x²+24x+6。把6(2x+1)²展开也得同式。恒等核验覆盖所有x，而不是用一个“看起来对”的数掩盖漏层。

### 例2：内导可以反向或暂时为零

对q(x)=(1−x)³，内导为−1，q′=−3(1−x)²。负号不是可有可无，它表示内层随着x增加而减少。原式展开1−3x+3x²−x³，逐项求导−3+6x−3x²也验证此式。

对r(x)=(x²+1)²，内导2x，外导2(x²+1)，所以r′=4x(x²+1)。在0，内值1、外导2，但内导0，最终变化率0。漏内导的答案2(x²+1)在0错误地给2。常数内层g=2也可用规则：无论外层的导数是多少，乘内导0，复合结果恒定，导数0。

## 机器链与乘积的区别

(2x+1)³是把一个内层输出立方，优先用链式法则；(x²+1)(x−2)是两个输出相乘，优先用乘积法则。有些表达式两者都需要，但一次微课先把结构辨认清楚。先写“谁先接收输入”，再画箭头x→g(x)→F(g(x))，最后把两个变化率对应到箭头。

工作台把内值、外导在内值处的值、内导分别列出，避免三者碰巧相同就混淆角色。完整导数题允许因式或展开等价式。改变求值点时完整表达式保留；改变显示视窗不更改任何数学答案。出口需要层次、推导、代入与展开核验；家长可让孩子口头说出“外导在哪里算”和“内导为什么还要乘”。

# Chain rule

Feed x into a machine g, then feed its output into a machine F. The result is F(g(x)). The derivative must account for both layers: the inner machine can speed up, slow down or reverse the input received by the outer one.

## Inner value differs from inner rate

Write z=g(x), y=F(z). If g is differentiable at x and F is differentiable at g(x), then dy/dx=F′(g(x))g′(x). Evaluate the outer derivative at the inner output and multiply by the inner derivative. This is a theorem with conditions, not permission to treat every derivative symbol as an ordinary cancellable fraction.

For a positive integer n, the derivative of [g(x)]ⁿ is n[g(x)]ⁿ⁻¹g′(x). Our automatic tool uses rational-coefficient polynomials of total degree at most four. The mathematical rule extends beyond that display bound when its hypotheses hold. Roots, logarithms and variable denominators require additional domain and differentiability checks rather than visual pattern matching.

### Example 1: The outer cubic receives a different input

For f(x)=(2x+1)³, the inner function g(x)=2x+1 has derivative two. The outer function F(z)=z³ has derivative 3z². Thus f′=3(2x+1)²·2=6(2x+1)².

At x=1, the inner value is z=3, the outer derivative F′(3)=27 and the inner rate is two. Their product gives derivative value 54. Using F′(1)=3 evaluates the outer derivative at the wrong input. Stopping at 27 omits the doubled inner rate. The original function value f(1)=27 happens to equal the outer derivative’s value here, but their mathematical roles remain distinct.

Expand independently: f=8x³+12x²+6x+1, so f′=24x²+24x+6. Expanding 6(2x+1)² gives the same polynomial for every x. This identity check covers the full rule rather than accepting one plausible number.

### Example 2: Inner rates may reverse or vanish

For q(x)=(1−x)³, the inner derivative is −1. Therefore q′=−3(1−x)². The minus sign records the decreasing inner input. Expanding the source to 1−3x+3x²−x³ gives derivative −3+6x−3x² and confirms the result.

For r(x)=(x²+1)², the inner derivative is 2x and the outer derivative at the inner value is 2(x²+1). Hence r′=4x(x²+1). At zero the inner value is one and the outer derivative two, but the inner rate is zero, making the final rate zero. Omitting the inner derivative would incorrectly give two there. A constant inner function g=2 also works: multiplication by its zero derivative makes the composite derivative zero, regardless of the outer derivative’s value.

## Distinguish composition from multiplication

The expression (2x+1)³ sends one inner output into a cubic operation, so the chain rule exposes its structure. The expression (x²+1)(x−2) multiplies two outputs and calls for the product rule. Some problems need both, but first identify which operation is at the outside. Describe which machine receives x first, draw x→g(x)→F(g(x)), and assign a rate to each arrow.

The audit separately lists inner value, outer derivative at that value and inner derivative, keeping their roles clear even when numbers coincide. Full derivative answers accept equivalent expanded or factored polynomials. Changing the evaluation point preserves the full-expression prediction, and changing the display window preserves all mathematical answers. The exit requires identification of layers, derivation, evaluation and an expansion check. A parent can ask where the outer derivative is evaluated and why the inner rate must still be multiplied.
