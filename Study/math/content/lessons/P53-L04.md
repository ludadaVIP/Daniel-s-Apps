## 切得更细，总量可以保持不变

等值分数是同一个数的不同分数表示。先选**同一个整体**，保持涂色区域不变，再把每一份重新等分。小份变小了，涂色小份的个数同时增加；两种改变配合起来，总量才能保持不变。

例如一半可以表示为 1/2、2/4 或 3/6。条形必须代表一样大的整体，每次切分必须等大；不同大小巧克力的一半，不能只凭分数就判断实际量相同。

### 例 1 · 2/3 改写成六分之一

1. 把同样大小的整体分成三等份，涂色两份。
2. 每个三分之一再分成两个相等小份。
3. 整体现在有 3×2=6 小份，涂色区域有 2×2=4 小份。
4. 因此 **2/3=4/6**。涂色端点没有移动，小份数却改变了。
5. 可以重合两条分数条检查，但不能用“看起来接近”冒充精确等值。

### 例 2 · 3/5 改写成二十分之一

1. 目标分母为 20，原分母 5 需要乘 4。
2. 每个五分之一分成四份，整个整体共有 20 小份。
3. 原来涂色的三份变成 3×4=12 小份。
4. 因此 **3/5=12/20**。
5. 分子与分母都乘同一个正整数，代表同一次等分操作。

### 为什么只改分母不行？

2/3 改成 2/6 时，每份变成原来一半，但仍只取两份，总量也变成原来一半。若要保留原涂色区域，新的小份数必须成为四份，所以等值式应是 2/3=4/6。

分子分母同时乘同一个正整数，会保持分数值。反向把若干相同小份合成一个新组，也可以保持数量；这时分子分母要同时除以能整除它们的同一个正整数。不能乘或除以零，分母必须保持有意义。

本课填空题有时在问分子，有时在问分母。3/4=9/? 中，分子乘了 3，分母也乘 3，缺少的是 12；不要把 9 当成分母的答案。

工作台固定整体长度与涂色量。改变细分倍数前先预测新的分子和分母，再点击重合检查：粗分线仍表示原来的单位，细分线表示新的单位。两组表示的涂色端点完全重合，数量由等分结构保证，而不是由颜色相似保证。

**出口发现：** 说明 4/6 与 2/3 为什么等值，再写另一个等值分数。要讲清每份怎样变、份数怎样变，不能只写一个等号。

## Finer cuts can preserve the amount

Equivalent fractions are different fractional representations of one number. Choose **the same whole**, keep its shaded region fixed, then repartition each part equally. Smaller parts and a larger shaded-part count must change together to preserve the amount.

A half can be written 1/2, 2/4 or 3/6. The strips must represent equal-sized wholes with equal cuts. Halves of differently sized bars do not automatically have equal actual amounts.

### Example 1 · Rewrite 2/3 in sixths

1. Divide a fixed whole into thirds and shade two.
2. Split every third into two equal smaller parts.
3. The whole now has 3×2=6 parts and the shading has 2×2=4 parts.
4. Thus **2/3=4/6**. The shaded endpoint stays fixed while the part count changes.
5. Overlay the strips to check, but do not replace exact structure with a claim that the drawings merely look close.

### Example 2 · Rewrite 3/5 in twentieths

1. The target denominator twenty requires multiplying five by four.
2. Split each fifth into four parts, making twenty per whole.
3. Three shaded fifths become 3×4=12 smaller parts.
4. Therefore **3/5=12/20**.
5. Multiplying both numbers by the same positive whole number describes one common repartitioning.

### Why does changing only the denominator fail?

Changing 2/3 into 2/6 halves each part but still takes just two, halving the total. Preserving the original shading requires four new parts: the equivalent expression is 2/3=4/6.

Multiplying numerator and denominator by the same positive whole number preserves value. Grouping equal small parts back together also preserves amount when both numbers divide by the same common positive factor. Do not multiply or divide by zero; the denominator must remain meaningful.

A blank may ask for the numerator or the denominator. In 3/4=9/?, the numerator has tripled, so the denominator triples too. The missing number is twelve, not nine.

The workshop fixes whole length and shading. Predict both new numbers before changing the refinement factor, then reveal the overlay. Thick lines retain original units and fine lines show the new units. Both shaded endpoints coincide exactly because of equal-part structure, not matching colour alone.

**Exit discovery:** explain why 4/6 and 2/3 are equivalent and produce another equivalent fraction. Describe changes in part size and count instead of only write an equals sign.
