# 不放回抽取

## 排除同一个编号，不是排除同颜色

从袋中连续抽两次，不放回表示第一球在第二抽前不回到袋里。R1被拿走后不能再抽R1，但R2仍在，因此两次红可以发生。不放回不是“第二次必须换颜色”，也不自动表示任意两个事件都相依；应对具体事件和规则判断。

把三红两蓝编号为R1、R2、R3、B1、B2。行是第一抽，列是第二抽。5×5表格的同编号对角线五格都排除，其余5×4=20格是等可能有序基本结果。R1→R2与R2→R1是不同顺序，全部保留。

### 例 1：用编号核颜色不同

先红后蓝共有3×2=6个编号有序对；先蓝后红共有2×3=6个。两组不重叠，故异色概率(6+6)/20=3/5。用树也得到3/5×2/4+2/5×3/4=3/5，两种表示指向同一份完整空间。

四种颜色路径RR、RB、BR、BB对应的编号有序对数分别6、6、6、2。因此它们概率3/10、3/10、3/10、1/10不相等，不能数“异色占四条中的两条”而交1/2。计数法的关键是基本结果等可能，而非类别名称的个数。

### 例 2：至少一次蓝与补事件

至少一次蓝包含RB、BR、BB，概率3/10+3/10+1/10=7/10。它也等于没有蓝的RR的补事件：1−3/10=7/10。至少一次不能只数BB；两次蓝是更窄的事件，概率1/10。

换成一红三蓝，不放回时RR不可能，概率0，因为第一红后已没有红。BB仍能发生，概率3/4×2/3=1/2。这个例子专门反驳“不放回就不能同色”的误解。零概率来自缺少第二个该颜色编号，不来自拒绝所有同色格。

## 放回与顺序的对照

五球放回并混匀有5×5=25个等可能编号有序对，对角线也可发生。RR为9/25，异色为12/25；去掉放回规则后，分母20且RR为6/20、异色12/20。事件符合数可能保持或改变，不能只看到分子相同就认为概率相同。

本课统一使用有序结果。也可以另建无序抽样空间，但必须重新核它的等可能性，并在分子分母中一致使用无序组合。不能把“六种无序异色对”除以“二十种有序全部结果”，也不能遇到相同颜色就把两个编号的不同顺序漏掉。

工作台先展示全部编号对，孩子填概率后再高亮所求事件。改变显示为树保留原预测，改变球数、事件或放回规则才清空。家长核验排除条件、编号身份、顺序、等可能依据与完整分母；图中颜色只是辅助，R/B和编号确保不用颜色也能读懂。

# Sampling without replacement

## Exclude an ID, not a whole colour

No replacement means the first token is not returned before draw two. Removing R1 excludes R1, while R2 can remain. Two reds can still occur. This rule does not mean the next colour must differ, nor that every conceivable pair of events is dependent; examine the particular events and model.

Label three reds R1,R2,R3 and two blues B1,B2. Rows represent the first draw, columns the second. Exclude the five same-ID diagonal cells from a five-by-five grid. The remaining 5×4=20 ordered basic outcomes are equally likely. R1→R2 and R2→R1 retain their different orders.

### Example 1: audit different colours by IDs

RB contains 3×2=6 ordered ID pairs and BR another six. These disjoint groups give 12/20=3/5. The conditional tree agrees: 3/5×2/4+2/5×3/4=3/5. Both representations describe the same complete space.

RR,RB,BR,BB contain 6,6,6,2 pairs respectively, so their probabilities are 3/10,3/10,3/10,1/10. Counting two different-colour labels out of four labels incorrectly assumes equal categories. Favourable/total counting requires equally likely basics, not just a list of names.

### Example 2: at least one blue

At least one blue combines RB,BR,BB for 7/10. Equivalently, complement the no-blue event RR: 1−3/10=7/10. Both blue is narrower, with probability 1/10, and cannot replace at least one.

With one red and three blue, RR is impossible without replacement because the sole red has already gone. BB remains possible, with probability 3/4×2/3=1/2. This refutes the claim that no replacement prohibits every same-colour result.

With replacement and remixing, all 25 ordered ID pairs are available, including the diagonal. For three red and two blue, RR=9/25 and different colours=12/25. Without replacement these become 6/20 and 12/20. A numerator staying the same does not keep the probability unchanged when the eligible space changes.

This lesson consistently uses ordered results. An unordered approach can also work, but must justify equal likelihood and recount both numerator and denominator consistently. Never divide an unordered favourable count by an ordered total, or omit reverse orders simply because the colours match.

Predict before revealing event highlights. Changing to tree view preserves the task, while counts, event or replacement changes require a new prediction. A parent reviews exclusions, IDs, order, equal-likelihood assumptions and the whole denominator. R/B letters and numbered labels keep the model readable without relying on colour alone.
