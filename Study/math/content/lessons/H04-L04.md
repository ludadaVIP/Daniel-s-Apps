# 振幅、周期与相位

把同一条正弦规则拉高、压缩或移动，会改变什么？追踪一个源点，比看到符号就背“左加右减”更可靠，尤其当x前还有倍率时。

## 一条核心想法

**把模型整理为y=A sin(B(x−h))+D，再追踪内部角u=B(x−h)。** 振幅是最大值与最小值差的一半，即|A|；中线是y=D。A<0表示竖直反射，振幅本身不负。

当A≠0且B≠0，内部角增加整周2π所需的最小正输入变化为T=2π/|B|。源正弦输入u映到新横坐标x=h+u/B，输出变成D+A sin u。h是题式给定的水平相位参数，可以有相差整周期的等价表示，不要求从图上唯一恢复。

### 例1：先分解内部，再找源最高点

y=3sin(2x−π)+4应写成3sin(2(x−π/2))+4。振幅3，中线4，最小正周期π，按此形式向右移动π/2。直接把常数π当作移动量，忽略了内部倍率2。

源正弦最高点在u=π/2、输出1。新横坐标x=π/2+(π/2)/2=3π/4，输出4+3=7；最低值4−3=1。把x=3π/4代回原式，内部角2×3π/4−π=π/2，可以核对整个追踪。

### 例2：负外倍率与常数边界

y=−2sin(2(x−π/4))+1的振幅2、中线1、周期π。x=π/4时内部角0，所以y=1；内部角π/2时源值1被反射为输出−1，内部角3π/2时源值−1变为输出3。最大值与最小值是3和−1，振幅(3−(−1))/2=2。

若A=0，模型恒为D。任意正T都是周期，又总能取更小的T/2，因此没有最小正周期。不能把2π/|B|当作这个常数函数的最小正周期；图也不能唯一识别B和h。本课工作台要求B≠0，避免把另一种常数退化藏进除法。

## 参数工作台

调整A、B、给定h和D，分别预测振幅、最小正周期与当前输入的输出。改D不应清掉振幅答案，改查询x也不应清掉周期答案。图上先看内部角映到哪里，再看竖直变换；窗口变化不改变数学值。

当前x按弧度计，给定h的控件填写π的系数。如果以后输入改成分钟，B必须带相应的弧度/分钟意义，不能把一个时间参数误作π倍角。

## 家长核对与迁移

出口要求整理原式、列四个参数意义并追踪源最高点，不只报一串数字。家长优先检查2x−π的因式分解和A负时的上下翻转。用同一原点代回原式，是独立于“看起来移动了多少”的核验。后续方程课先反解内部角，再按原区间筛选全部输入。

# Amplitude, period and phase

Changing a sine rule can stretch, compress, reflect or shift its graph. Tracking one source point is more reliable than applying a sign slogan, particularly when x has an inner multiplier.

## One central idea

**Write y=A sin(B(x−h))+D and track the inner angle u=B(x−h).** Amplitude is half the range width, |A|, and the midline is y=D. A negative A gives vertical reflection, not a negative amplitude.

For nonzero A and B, a full 2π change in the inner angle requires least positive input change T=2π/|B|. Source input u maps to x=h+u/B, and output becomes D+A sin u. The stated h is a horizontal phase parameter; equivalent descriptions may differ by complete periods, so uniqueness is not claimed.

### Example 1: Factor the inner expression and track a maximum

y=3sin(2x−π)+4 becomes 3sin(2(x−π/2))+4. Its amplitude is three, midline four, least positive period π and, in this representation, shift right π/2. Calling the shift π would ignore the inner multiplier two.

The source sine maximum occurs at u=π/2 with output one. Its new input is x=π/2+(π/2)/2=3π/4, and new output four plus three equals seven. The minimum is four minus three, or one. Substituting x=3π/4 into the original expression recovers inner angle 2×3π/4−π=π/2 and audits the tracking independently.

### Example 2: Negative scale and the constant boundary

y=−2sin(2(x−π/4))+1 has amplitude two, midline one and period π. At x=π/4 its inner angle is zero, giving y=1. Inner angle π/2 has source value one, reflected to output −1. Inner angle 3π/2 has source value −1, becoming output three. The range width is 3−(−1)=4, so amplitude is two.

With A=0, the model is constant D. Every positive T is a period, and T/2 is always a smaller one; there is no least positive period. The expression 2π/|B| cannot supply a least positive period for that constant function, and its graph cannot identify B or h uniquely. The workbench requires B≠0 rather than hiding another constant degeneration inside division.

## Parameter workbench

Adjust A, B, the stated h and D. Predict amplitude, least positive period and the output at a selected input independently. Changing D should preserve the amplitude answer, and changing a query should preserve period evidence. Track the inner angle first and apply the vertical transformation second. Changing a viewing window changes no mathematical value.

Here x is in radians, and the h selector supplies its coefficient of π. If a later model uses minutes, B needs the corresponding radians-per-minute interpretation; a time parameter cannot be silently treated as a multiple of π.

## Parent check and transfer

The exit requires factoring the original rule, explaining four parameters and tracking a source maximum, not merely reporting numbers. Check the factorisation of 2x−π and the vertical reflection for negative A first. Substitution into the original rule audits the result independently of visual impressions. The equation lesson will reverse the inner angle and filter every candidate by the original interval.
