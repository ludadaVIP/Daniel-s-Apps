## 从一行格子走向面积公式

长方形面积是在数它覆盖多少个单位正方形。用边长一厘米的格子铺一厘米单位的长方形：横边决定每行有几格，纵边决定有几行，所以面积为长乘宽。单位是平方厘米，不是厘米。

正方形也是长方形，只是相邻边相等；边长乘自身得到面积，不是乘四。乘四数的是正方形外边界的长度。旋转图形只交换观察到的横纵方向，不改变格子总数。

### 例 1 · 六乘四的覆盖

1. 长方形长 **6 cm**、宽 **4 cm**，选择每格 **1 cm²**。
2. 一行六格，四行共有 **6×4=24** 格。
3. 面积 **24 cm²**。每个格只覆盖一次，不漏空白也不重复计数。
4. 周长为 **6+4+6+4=20 cm**，它沿边界走，而不是数覆盖格。
5. 旋转后四格一行、六行，**4×6=24 cm²**；面积保持不变。

### 例 2 · 两边单位先对齐

1. 长方形长 **4 m**、宽 **50 cm**，求平方米面积。
2. 先改宽：**50 cm=0.5 m**。
3. **4×0.5=2 m²**。也可全用厘米：**400×50=20000 cm²**。
4. 再换成平方米：**20000÷10000=2 m²**。
5. 直接 4×50 后写 m²，会把厘米错误当成米；即使数字算乘法没错，量也不成立。

### 小数边长与逆向问题

2.5 m×1.2 m 的长方形也按同一个覆盖关系计算。可先换为 250 cm×120 cm 得 30000 cm²，再写成 3 m²。不需要把小数边长强行取整；格子不整齐落在图边时，可以用更小单位或部分单位来解释。

若面积 20 cm²，已知宽 4 cm，长为 20÷4=5 cm。代回 5×4=20 cm² 核查。面积除一个边长得到另一个边长，所得单位是厘米；这是求未知边，不是把平方厘米直接换成厘米。

周长相同不保证面积相同。2 cm×4 cm 与 3 cm×3 cm 周长都为 12 cm，分别覆盖八格与九格。不能只看数值哪个大就比较面积和周长，因为两者单位不同。图形“看着更大”也不能替代给定标尺和覆盖数。

工作台演示整数厘米边长以便逐格数；正方形模式两边联动，旋转交换横纵边，变化后收起旧计算。显示周长是为了对照另一种量，不把它当面积的另一写法。练习中小数和混合边长单位仍适用同样关系。

**带走一个发现：** 先对齐边长单位，再数每行与行数。面积数覆盖，周长数边界；逆向求边时要说清算出的是什么量。

## From a row of cells to the area formula

Rectangle area counts covering unit squares. With one-centimetre squares, the horizontal edge sets cells per row and the vertical edge sets rows. Multiplying counts the coverage, in square centimetres.

A square is a rectangle with equal adjacent edges. Its area is edge times itself; four times the edge is perimeter. Rotation changes observed directions without changing the total covering cells.

### Example 1 · Six by four coverage

1. A rectangle has edges **6 cm** and **4 cm**, with **1 cm²** cells.
2. Six cells per row and four rows give **24** cells.
3. Area is **24 cm²**, counting each covering cell once.
4. Perimeter is **6+4+6+4=20 cm**, a boundary length.
5. Rotating gives four cells per row and six rows: **4×6=24 cm²**.

### Example 2 · Align edge units first

1. A rectangle is **4 m** by **50 cm**. Find area in m².
2. **50 cm=0.5 m**.
3. **4×0.5=2 m²**. Alternatively **400 cm×50 cm=20000 cm²**.
4. **20000÷10000=2 m²**.
5. Multiplying 4×50 and labelling m² treats centimetres as metres. Correct numerical multiplication does not repair a mismatched quantity.

### Decimal edges and inverse questions

For 2.5 m×1.2 m, use 250 cm×120 cm=30000 cm²=3 m². Decimal edges do not need truncation; smaller units or parts of unit squares explain partial boundary cells.

If area is 20 cm² and width 4 cm, length is 20÷4=5 cm. Substitute back to check 5×4=20 cm². Dividing area by one edge finds a new length, rather than directly converting square units into length units.

Equal perimeter does not ensure equal area. A 2 cm×4 cm rectangle and 3 cm×3 cm square both have perimeter 12 cm but cover eight and nine unit squares. Comparing raw area and perimeter numbers is inappropriate because their units differ. Apparent size without a scale is not measurement evidence.

The workshop uses integer-centimetre edges for clear cell counting. Square mode links edges; rotation swaps them; changes hide old results. The perimeter readout contrasts a different quantity. Decimal and mixed-unit practice still uses the same coverage relationship.

**Takeaway:** align edge units, then count cells per row and rows. Area measures coverage and perimeter the boundary. In an inverse problem, identify the new quantity being found.
