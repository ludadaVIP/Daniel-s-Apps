# 独立性与贝叶斯直觉

本课研究一个问题：看到新信息F后，怎样更新事件A的占比？先正向分流，再在F里反向归因，能把贝叶斯公式中的每一部分变成可解释的质量。

## 用正向两条路重建反向分母

来源分为A与Aᶜ，互斥且穷尽。被标记F的总概率为P(F)=P(A)P(F|A)+P(Aᶜ)P(F|Aᶜ)。两来源都有正概率时可直接这样写；若一个来源概率0，应用四格的联合质量表达，避免对空来源定义条件比。

P(F)>0时，P(A|F)=P(A∩F)/P(F)。分子来自A且被标记，分母包含**所有来源**的标记。这就是贝叶斯更新的直觉：正向率、基础占比和反向条件各司其职。不能只把条件符号调头。

独立定义为P(A∩F)=P(A)P(F)。若P(F)>0，这等价于P(A|F)=P(A)：标记不改变来源占比。互斥却不等于独立；正概率互斥事件交集0而乘积大于0。零概率事件也按乘积定义处理，相关条件比仍可能未定义。

### 例1：标记率很高，反向占比仍可能不高

虚构包装模型中，A厂占5%，A厂包装标记率80%，其他来源标记率10%。用1000份**模型质量**想象：A有50份，其中40标记；其他950份，其中95标记。标记总质量135，其中A占40/135=8/27，约29.6%。

80%=P(F|A)，8/27=P(A|F)，两者分母不同。这里的“1000份”是按假定率重表达的理论质量，不是一次调查实测，也不承诺实际1000件必有40和95件被标记。

只把A基础占比改成50%，同样标记率得到A中400份标记、其他50份标记，反向概率400/450=8/9。设备正向规则没变，先验构成已改变，看到标记后的来源构成也改变。

### 例2：没有信息与没有条件

若两来源标记率都20%，A占5%时，A标记质量10，其他190，反向10/200=5%，仍等原占比。P(A∩F)=1/100，P(A)P(F)=(1/20)(1/5)=1/100，故独立。

若两来源都从不标记，P(F)=0，则P(A|F)未定义；但交集0=概率乘积0，按定义A与F仍独立。再若A根本不可能，P(F|A)未定义，而乘积判据依然成立。工作台允许填写假定分流率，即使某来源质量为0；该空来源的条件概率读数仍显示undefined，不把参数当作由0/0得到的事实。

# Independence and Bayes intuition

How should evidence F update the share belonging to A? Build the forward branches first, then look backwards inside F. This gives every component of Bayes’ rule an interpretable probability mass.

## Rebuild the reverse denominator from forward paths

Sources A and Aᶜ form a disjoint exhaustive partition. Their flagged masses sum to P(F)=P(A)P(F|A)+P(Aᶜ)P(F|Aᶜ). This conditional expression is directly valid when both source probabilities are positive. For a zero-mass source, use joint cell masses instead of assigning an ordinary conditional ratio to an empty source.

For P(F)>0, P(A|F)=P(A∩F)/P(F). Its numerator is mass from A that is flagged; its denominator includes flagged mass from **every source**. Forward likelihood, base rate and reverse probability play different roles. Reversing the conditioning symbol alone does not reverse a probability correctly.

Independence means P(A∩F)=P(A)P(F). With positive P(F), this is equivalent to P(A|F)=P(A): observing a flag leaves source proportions unchanged. Disjoint positive-probability events are not independent, because their intersection is zero while their product is positive. Zero-probability events still use the product definition even when a conditional ratio is unavailable.

### Example 1: A high forward rate need not imply a high reverse share

In a fictional package model, A supplies 5%, with an 80% flag rate; other sources have a 10% flag rate. Express this per 1,000 units of **model mass**. Of fifty A units, forty are flagged. Of 950 others, ninety-five are flagged. Among 135 flagged units, the A share is 40/135=8/27, approximately 29.6%.

The 80% is P(F|A), whereas 8/27 is P(A|F). Their denominators differ. The thousand-unit representation expresses assumed probabilities; it is not an observed survey or a promise that a real batch must contain exactly those flagged counts.

Now change only A’s base rate to 50%. The same flag rates yield four hundred flagged A units and fifty flagged other units, so P(A|F)=400/450=8/9. The forward sorter rule has stayed fixed while the source composition, and therefore the flagged composition, has changed.

### Example 2: No information versus no conditioning event

If both sources flag 20% and A supplies 5%, the flagged masses are ten from A and 190 from elsewhere. The posterior is 10/200=5%, unchanged from the prior. Also P(A∩F)=1/100=(1/20)(1/5)=P(A)P(F), establishing independence.

If neither source ever flags, P(F)=0 and P(A|F) is undefined. Nevertheless, the zero intersection equals the zero probability product, so A and F are independent. If A itself is impossible, P(F|A) is undefined but the product criterion again holds. The workbench permits an assumed branch-rate parameter for a source of zero mass, while its displayed conditional probability remains undefined; the parameter was not inferred from 0/0.

## Interpret the area model and exit

The rectangle represents a thousand model units. Source proportions determine widths; each source’s flag rate determines its gold height. Gold areas therefore encode the two joint flagged masses. A 5% source is a narrow column, so comparing heights alone misses its small base rate. The exact table retains both masses and the posterior fraction.
