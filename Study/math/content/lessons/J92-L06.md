# 二次模型应用

## 先说数量与范围，再列方程

应用题不只问“某个二次式的根”。先定义未知数表示什么、用什么单位、允许哪些值，再用两种原数量写等式。随后整理、求全部代数根、分别代回原式和检查情境范围，最后用单位回答。等式成立与范围合格要分别核验，不能用其中一项替代另一项。

本课情境均为虚构，用于学习模型，不是现实设备或材料的测量数据。矩形宽度要求正，卡阵边卡数要求非负整数，指定时间段要求落在闭区间内。这些限制从原题来；不能看见负根就一律删除，也不能为了得到整数答案随意四舍五入。最终若没有可行解，应说明是等式本身无实根，还是实根全部不合题设。

### 例 1：矩形的负根通过等式，却不能作长度

设矩形宽 x m，长比宽多3 m，面积40 m²。原模型为 x(x+3)=40，范围x>0。左边是宽乘长的面积，右边是已知面积，单位都是m²；x+x+3=40把长度和面积混在一起，模型错误。

移项得 x²+3x−40=0，分解为 (x−5)(x+8)=0，全部代数根5、−8。代入5，原左为5×8=40且宽、长均正，因此宽5m、长8m。代入−8，原左为(−8)×(−5)=40，等式也成立，但负边长违反范围，所以排除。不能说负根“代回不成立”，它失败的是另一个条件。

列原模型的题要求保留两种来源数量在等号两边。展开为 x²+3x=40 或交换为40=x(x+3)仍展示相同关系；直接交 x=5 或移项后的零式虽可与求解相关，却没有完成这一列式任务。求解阶段当然可以使用等价零式。

### 例 2：有实数根，但卡阵无法安排

五张完整卡要排成两个相同的 x×x 正方阵。每阵x²张，两阵2x²张，因此模型2x²=5，且x为非负整数。代数根为±√(5/2)=±√10/2。负根不合非负条件，正根虽为正却不是整数，因此没有可行卡阵。不是因为判别式小于零；此方程的实根确实存在。

把正根四舍五入到2，会用2×2²=8张；改成1则用2张，都不等于5。整数情境不能靠舍入修复严格等式。若题目允许近似尺寸，那是另一个模型，需要先明说容许误差，不能擅自改题。

## 区间筛选与最终解释

虚构信号 x²−6x+5 在0≤x≤2 min内何时为零？因式为 (x−1)(x−5)，全部代数零点1、5。两个均通过原信号为零的核验，但5在考察时段外，故只有开始后1min。范围端点包含0、2，也不表示端点一定是根；必须同时满足等式。

收尾可用小矩形 x(x+1)=2 解释完整链：定义正宽x cm，列原面积关系，求1、−2，两根代回分别为2，但只接受正宽1cm、长2cm。家长阅读数量来源、变形依据、完整分支和两项条件，不把一个正确数字当作已经理解建模。家庭挑战可在纸上设计虚构标牌，换面积后比较方法，不需购买或上传真实信息。

# Quadratic modelling problems

## Define the quantity and domain

State what x represents, its unit and permitted values before modelling. Equate the two original quantities, solve for every algebraic root, substitute into the original, check the contextual domain and answer with units. Equation validity and contextual admissibility are separate checks.

These scenarios are fictional teaching models. Widths must be positive, square-array edge counts nonnegative integers, and times inside the stated interval. Restrictions come from the problem. Do not delete every negative root automatically or round to invent a feasible integer. If none remain, distinguish no real equation roots from real roots that all fail the context.

### Example 1: a negative root can pass the equation

A rectangle has width x m, length x+3 m and area 40 m². Its original model is x(x+3)=40 with x>0. Both sides measure area; x+x+3=40 incorrectly mixes lengths and area.

Collect x²+3x−40=0 and factor (x−5)(x+8)=0. The complete algebraic roots are five and −8. Five gives 5×8=40 and positive dimensions, so width 5 m and length 8 m. The negative root gives (−8)(−5)=40 too, passing the equation but failing positive lengths. It is inaccurate to say it fails substitution.

For an original-model task, x²+3x=40 or 40=x(x+3) retains the two quantity sources. A final x=5 or transposed zero form does not complete that requested layout, though the zero form is valid during solving.

### Example 2: real roots but no card arrangement

Five whole cards must form two identical x×x square arrays. Each array uses x² cards, so 2x²=5 with x a nonnegative integer. Algebra gives ±√(5/2)=±√10/2. The negative root fails nonnegativity; the positive root fails integrality. Thus no arrangement is feasible even though real roots exist and the discriminant is not negative.

Rounding to two uses 2×2²=8 cards; one uses two cards. Neither equals five. A whole-count model cannot be repaired by rounding. Approximate dimensions with a stated tolerance would be a different problem, not an assumption to add silently.

## Interval filtering and explanation

A fictional signal x²−6x+5 is studied only for 0≤x≤2 min. Factoring (x−1)(x−5) gives algebraic zero times one and five. Both pass substitution, but only one lies in the interval. The answer is 1 min after the start. Included interval endpoints are not automatically roots; they still need to satisfy the equation.

For an exit example, model a rectangle by x(x+1)=2 with positive width x cm. Its roots one and −2 both pass the original area equality, but only one is a width, giving dimensions 1 cm by 2 cm. A parent reviews quantity sources, equivalent transformations, complete branches and both checks. The paper-based fictional-sign challenge can change areas and compare methods without purchases or personal data.
