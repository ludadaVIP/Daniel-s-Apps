# 多项式与指数函数求导复习

求导前先问：变量在什么位置？x³与3ˣ都出现3，却有不同结构。本课把已有幂规则和链式法则连到指数函数，重点是辨结构再用规则，不是背一个没有条件的口诀。

## 三条规则及条件

固定正整数n的幂函数满足(xⁿ)′=nxⁿ⁻¹；常数项导数0，和的导数逐项相加。自然指数满足(eˣ)′=eˣ。这里e约为2.71828；ln是自然对数，以e为底。

正底数a的实数指数函数有(aˣ)′=ln(a)aˣ。a=1时恒为1，导数0，与ln1=0一致；a≤0不能直接套这个全体实数定义域的公式。负底的某些分数指数没有实值，零底也无法覆盖所有实指数。

复合函数要保留内导：(eᵘ)′=u′eᵘ。指数函数在求导后仍保留原指数；它不是变量底的幂xⁿ，不能把指数x“降一次”。自然指数规则可由极限(eʰ−1)/h→1建立；本课在该标准结果上复习应用，有限图表只用来观察，不声称证明此极限。

### 例1：同一和式，逐项辨结构

f(x)=3x³−2x+eˣ。第一项用幂规则得9x²，第二项得−2，第三项仍eˣ，所以f′(x)=9x²−2+eˣ。

在0处，f(0)=1，而f′(0)=−1。函数值与变化率不同，并不矛盾。过P=(0,1)的切线是y=1−x；图中原函数、导函数与切线各有角色，不能把导函数点(0,−1)误当原函数切点。

作为独立核查，幂部分导数可由精确系数运算得到；指数部分用标准导数规则。挑几个输入看数值是否吻合可以查错，但不能据此认定任意实数输入都成立。

### 例2：指数内层与底数因子

g(x)=5e^(2x)，外层导数保留指数，内层2x的导数是2，故g′(x)=10e^(2x)，不是5e^(2x)，也不是10eˣ。g′(0)=10。

h(x)=2ˣ的导数为ln(2)2ˣ，在0为ln2，约0.693，而函数值2⁰=1。保留精确ln2；不能把0.69当精确因子。另有p(x)=e^(−x)，内导−1，所以p′(x)=−e^(−x)。衰减来自负内导，不是把e换成负底。

若函数是x eˣ，须用乘积法则得到eˣ+x eˣ=(x+1)eˣ；这说明规则可以组合，但本课核心仍是分清固定幂与指数结构。

## 输入和解释都要能核验

工作台可切换四种函数。先输入导函数，再输入0处斜率；显示切线只改视图，不清已有预测。改变函数后，导函数任务更新；恰好相同的斜率预测可以保留。

公式输入支持有理系数、括号与题中同一指数内层，如e^x、exp(x)、e^(2*x)、ln(2)*2^x。系统把已核验的函数原子交给精确多项式核验，接受展开、合并、因式形式，不用浮点试几个值判恒等。该输入语法不是任意计算机代数系统；完整底数条件及为何用这条规则的解释仍由家长复核。

# Derivative review: polynomials and exponentials

Locate the variable before differentiating. Both x³ and3ˣ contain a three, but their structures differ. Connect the power and chain rules to exponentials by identifying structure rather than applying an unconditional slogan.

## Rules with conditions

For fixed positive integer n, (xⁿ)′=nxⁿ⁻¹. Constants differentiate to zero and sums differentiate term by term. The natural exponential satisfies(eˣ)′=eˣ. The number e is approximately2.71828 and ln denotes its logarithm.

For a positive base a, (aˣ)′=ln(a)aˣ. Base one gives constant one and derivative zero, consistent with ln1=0. Nonpositive bases do not support this rule on the entire real line: negative bases fail for some fractional real exponents and base zero does not cover every real exponent.

The chain rule gives(eᵘ)′=u′eᵘ. Retain the original exponent. Unlike xⁿ, an exponential does not have its variable exponent “lowered by one”. The natural-exponential rule follows from the standard limit(eʰ−1)/h→1. This lesson uses that result; finite plots illustrate it rather than proving the limit.

### Example 1: Differentiate a sum by structure

For f(x)=3x³−2x+eˣ, the terms differentiate to9x²,−2 and eˣ. Thus f′(x)=9x²−2+eˣ.

At zero, f(0)=1 and f′(0)=−1. Value and instantaneous rate need not agree. The tangent through P=(0,1) is y=1−x. The original curve, derivative curve and tangent play different roles; the derivative point(0,−1) is not the original curve’s tangent point.

Exact coefficient operations independently verify the polynomial part, while the exponential part uses its standard rule. Testing selected inputs can reveal errors but cannot establish identity for every real input.

### Example 2: Retain inner rates and base factors

For g(x)=5e^(2x), the inner derivative is two, giving g′(x)=10e^(2x). Neither5e^(2x) nor10eˣ retains all required structure. At zero the derivative is ten.

For h(x)=2ˣ, the derivative is ln(2)2ˣ and h′(0)=ln2, approximately0.693. Its value at zero is one. Keep the exact logarithm rather than substituting a rounded coefficient such as0.69. For p(x)=e^(−x), the inner derivative−1 gives p′(x)=−e^(−x). Decay comes from the negative inner rate, not a negative exponential base.

If the function were x eˣ, the product rule would give eˣ+x eˣ=(x+1)eˣ. Rules combine, but the primary goal remains distinguishing a fixed power from an exponential.

## Make formulas and explanations checkable

Switch among four functions in the workbench. Predict the derivative and slope at zero before auditing. Toggling a tangent changes display without erasing thinking. A changed function updates the derivative task; a slope prediction can remain when its actual answer is unchanged.

Formula inputs accept rational coefficients, brackets and the stated exponential argument, such as e^x,exp(x),e^(2*x),ln(2)*2^x. Verified function atoms are compared by exact polynomial algebra, accepting reordered, factored and expanded forms without floating-point sample matching. This supported grammar is not a general computer algebra system. A parent still reviews the complete reasoning, base conditions and justification for selecting the rule.
