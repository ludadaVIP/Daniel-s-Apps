## 三个数量，正向与反向寻找未知量

**组数 × 每组量 = 总量**。知道组数与每组量，用乘法求总量；知道总量与一个非零因数，用除法恢复另一因数。这就是本课乘除互逆的数量解释。

不要把“互逆”只记成四行符号。每一条式子都要说出哪个数计组、哪个数计每组物体、哪个数计全部物体。数值相同的算式，在不同故事中可能回答不同未知量。

### 例 1 · 三组每组四个

1. 三组，每组四个，合计 **3×4=12 个**。
2. 已知总量十二和三组，每组量为 **12÷3=4 个**。
3. 已知总量十二和每组四个，组数为 **12÷4=3 组**。
4. 两条除法都可以用相应乘法检查，十二恢复为原总量。
5. 4×3=12 也是同一批十二个物体的一种等组计数；但要说明新的组数与每组量。

### 例 2 · 找缺少的因数

1. 有四行小球，总共三十二个，每行同样多。
2. 写出 **4×每行量=32**，先明确未知量的角色。
3. 用 32÷4=8，得到每行八个。
4. 检查 4×8=32；若算 32−4=28，只是在减去四个物体，没有表达四个等组。

### 除数必须非零

0×5=0，并不能推出 0÷0=5。因为 0×8=0、0×12=0，很多数都符合乘回去的零结果，除以零无法唯一恢复那个因数。

另一方面，0÷5=0 是有效的：五组，每组零个，合计零。这里的除数是五，不是零。不能因为看见总量零，就把所有除法都当成无效。

若组数与每组量相同，例如三组三个，有些反向式会写得相同；数量角色仍可以分别解释。不需要为了凑四条不同符号而改变数字。

已知 7×8=56，可以从七个托盘与总量五十六求每盘八个，也可以从每盘八个与总量五十六求七个托盘。单位说明了商在数什么：每盘物体数不是托盘数。

工作台改变组数或每组量后，同步显示总量与合法的反向除法。选择零组或零每组量时，检查哪条除法的除数会为零；模型不显示那条无效除法。物体总数仍由等组图准确表示。

**小检查：** 用“六组共四十二个”写出两条有意义的除法，说明每个商的单位。

## Find an unknown quantity forwards or backwards

**Group count × amount per group = total**. Knowing the two factors finds the total by multiplication. Knowing the total and a non-zero factor recovers the other factor by division. This is the quantity meaning of inverse multiplication and division.

Do not memorise only four symbol lines. State which number counts groups, which counts objects per group and which counts all objects. Similar numerical expressions can answer different unknowns in different stories.

### Example 1 · Three groups of four

1. Three groups of four total **3×4=12 objects**.
2. Given twelve and three groups, amount per group is **12÷3=4 objects**.
3. Given twelve and four per group, group count is **12÷4=3 groups**.
4. Check each division by the corresponding multiplication, recovering the original twelve.
5. 4×3=12 also counts twelve objects in equal groups, but explain the changed group count and amount per group.

### Example 2 · Find a missing factor

1. Four equal rows contain thirty-two counters.
2. Write **four × amount per row = thirty-two**, identifying the unknown role first.
3. Calculate 32÷4=8, giving eight per row.
4. Check 4×8=32. Subtracting 32−4=28 removes four objects and does not model four equal groups.

### The divisor must be non-zero

0×5=0 cannot justify 0÷0=5. Zero times eight, twelve and many other values is also zero. Division by zero cannot uniquely recover that factor.

By contrast, 0÷5=0 is valid: five groups with zero each total zero. Five is the divisor, not zero. A zero total does not make every division invalid.

If group count equals amount per group, as in three groups of three, some inverse expressions coincide. The two quantity roles can still be explained. Do not invent different numbers just to force four distinct symbol lines.

Knowing 7×8=56 can find eight objects per tray from seven trays and a total of fifty-six, or seven trays from eight per tray and the same total. Units distinguish the quotient roles: objects per tray are not tray count.

The workshop changes either factor and synchronises the total with valid inverse divisions. For a zero factor, identify which proposed division would have zero divisor; the model omits that invalid division. The equal-group picture still shows the correct object total.

**Quick check:** write two meaningful divisions for six groups totalling forty-two objects and identify each quotient's unit.
