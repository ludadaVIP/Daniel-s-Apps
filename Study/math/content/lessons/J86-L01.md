# 全等的含义

## 同形，也同大小

两个图形能经过平移、旋转或反射而完全重合，称为全等。允许换位置和方向，但不允许偷偷拉伸或缩放。对应长度、角度保持；颜色、描边粗细、在纸上朝左还是朝右都不是图形大小的条件。

三角形全等要写清对应顺序。`△ABC≅△DEF` 表示 A 对 D、B 对 E、C 对 F，随后才能判断 AB 对 DE、BC 对 EF、AC 对 DF，及各顶点的角。符号不是把六个字母随便放在两边，而是在表达一个特定的身份匹配。

### 例 1：反射后仍按身份对应

原三角形三边为四、五、三，第二个由它反射、旋转后移到另一处，顶点 A、B、C 分别改名 D、E、F。每条长度和每个角都保持，所以两图全等，按 ABC 顺序写右边为 DEF。

若第二图看起来 D 在右、E 在左，也不能按“从左到右”改写。要看身份与给定对应：边 AB 的两个端点是 A/B，匹配 D/E，故为 DE。线段 DE 与 ED 表示同一条无向边，但把整个三角形 DEF 写成 DFE，会把 B/C 的身份交换，不再是同一有序对应。

现在只把第二图放大至 1.5 倍，原边四变成六、五变成七点五、三变成四点五，角仍保持，但长度不再相同。因此只同形而不同大小，不能全等。检验重合时若又偷偷缩小第二图，就已经改变了允许的操作。

### 例 2：不同字母顺序的边角

已知 `△ABC≅△DFE`，先逐位写 A↔D、B↔F、C↔E。于是 AB↔DF、BC↔FE、AC↔DE；角 B 对角 F，而不是角 E。

如果 AC=7 cm，则 DE=7 cm，因为先有全等，再有对应边相等。不能直接说所有边都等于七，BC 并不因此等于 AC。若问 C 对应谁，只填 E；若问边 AB 对应谁，填 DF 或 FD；若问另一有序三角形，则须完整保留 DFE。问题所求的对象不同，答案格式也不同。

## 已知、外观与后续结论

一个相等边不能保证两个三角形全等，甚至三个角相等也不能固定大小。后续会学习哪些对应条件足够。当前先建立顺序：把已知对应写清，再判断可否全等，最后取对应边角结论。不能把正要证明的边相等预先当作已知条件。

工作台保持横纵相同单位长度，旋转和反射不改变数据。重合按钮撤销位置与方向变化，却保留放大倍率。两个颜色叠在一起是观察证据，精确条件才是后续证明依据；屏幕像素、线宽和近似量角不能代替数学论证。

家长可先遮住图，按一个全等式问三次顶点对应，再问一条边与一个角。若孩子只按位置猜，换一个旋转角度；若把同形当全等，用四变六的具体长度追问“这还算同大小吗”。

# Meaning of congruence

## Same shape and same size

Congruent figures can coincide through translation, rotation or reflection without resizing. Corresponding distances and angles remain fixed. Colour, stroke width and left/right placement do not determine size.

Triangle notation records correspondence. △ABC≅△DEF means A↔D,B↔E,C↔F, which then matches AB/DE, BC/EF and AC/DF. The letter order is part of the mathematical claim rather than decoration.

### Example 1: reflection retains identities

A triangle with side lengths four, five and three is reflected, rotated and translated, renaming A/B/C as D/E/F. Every length and angle stays fixed, so the triangles are congruent in the order ABC↔DEF.

Do not reorder the second triangle by screen position. AB matches DE because its endpoints correspond. DE and ED name the same undirected segment, but DEF and DFE exchange two vertex identities and describe different ordered matches.

Enlarging the second triangle by 1.5 changes four to six, five to 7.5 and three to 4.5 while retaining angles. Equal shape no longer means equal size. Shrinking it again during an overlay would introduce an operation outside rigid congruence.

### Example 2: reading a changed order

Given △ABC≅△DFE, match A↔D,B↔F,C↔E. Therefore AB↔DF,BC↔FE,AC↔DE and angle B matches F.

If AC=7 cm, then DE=7 cm because congruence was already established. Other sides need not all be seven. Asked for C’s partner, enter E; asked for AB’s partner, enter DF or FD; asked for the ordered triangle, retain DFE. Different requested objects need different answer forms.

## Givens and conclusions

One equal side is insufficient, and even three matching angles leave scale free. Later lessons identify sufficient conditions. First state correspondence, then establish congruence, then use matching sides and angles. Do not assume the target side equality as a premise.

The workshop uses a common unit length in both directions. The overlay undoes position and orientation while retaining any enlargement. Pixels and approximate measurements support observation without replacing exact proof.

A parent can hide the drawing and ask for vertex, side and angle matches from notation alone. Rotate a figure to challenge position-based guessing, and use four becoming six to distinguish equal shape from equal size.
