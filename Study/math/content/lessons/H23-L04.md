# 直线与平面的关系

空间位置关系要同时检查**方向与位置**。投影中两条线交叉不保证空间相交；一个点积为0，也不会自动把直线“放进”某个平面。

## 把几何关系变成参数方程

直线可写X=A+tu，t取所有实数，u≠0；平面可写n·X=d，n≠0。n是法向量，平面内所有方向都与它垂直。把直线代入平面，得到n·A+t(n·u)=d。

若n·u≠0，参数t=(d−n·A)/(n·u)唯一，直线与平面交于一点。若n·u=0且n·A=d，等式对所有t成立，整条直线在平面内；若n·u=0但n·A≠d，则没有交点，直线严格平行于平面。本课把“parallel”用于外部严格平行，和contained分开。

线面垂直要求u与n平行，即u是n的非零倍数，反而不是u·n=0。垂直线也属于唯一相交情形；“相交”是位置关系，“垂直”还说明方向角，两个词回答的问题不同。

### 例1：同一法向，四种配置

平面z=0的法向为(0,0,1)。直线(0,0,1)+t(1,0,0)与法向点积0，但所有点z=1，严格平行。若只把基点改为原点，整线z=0，便是contained；方向条件相同，位置结论不同。

直线(0,0,1)+t(1,0,−1)给z=1−t，t=1时交于(1,0,0)，唯一相交但不垂直。改方向为(0,0,−1)时与法向平行，交点(0,0,0)，此时才线面垂直。

### 例2：直线异面与平面常数

直线l=(t,0,0)、m=(0,1+s,1)的方向分别为(1,0,0)、(0,1,0)，方向夹角90°，但l全在z=0、m全在z=1，永远没有公共点。方向不平行又不相交，属于skew异面直线。可以讨论异面直线方向的较小夹角，却不能据此宣称两线相交。

两个平面α:z=0、β:−2z=−4，法向平行，但β是z=2，严格平行而非重合。若β改为−2z=0，法向与右端都是α的同一倍数，才重合。若法向不平行，如z=0与x=0，则交成一条直线；法向点积0还说明这两个平面垂直。

## 工作台保留哪些条件

切换线面、两直线、两平面配置，先读完整方程与基点，再预测位置和诊断点积。图只显示直线片段和平面有限色块，边缘不是数学边界。两平面重合时色块叠加，交线可能超出色块；判断仍以无限对象方程为准。

核验后显示点积、基点代入差与交点参数。换相机不改作答。非零方向、非零法向是定义对象的前提：零方向只剩一个点；零法向配不同常数会给全空间或空集，不能拿来代表一个有效平面。

出口要求从同一参数等式推导三个分支，并说明垂直的方向条件。家长把“方向测试”和“位置测试”各写一行，防止用一张图或单个点积取代整个论证。

# Relationships between lines and planes

Spatial relations require both **direction and position**. Projected crossings do not prove spatial intersections, and one zero dot product does not place a line inside a plane.

## Convert the geometry to one parameter equation

Write a line as X=A+tu for real t and nonzero u, and a plane as n·X=d for nonzero normal n. Tangent directions in the plane are perpendicular to n. Substitution gives n·A+t(n·u)=d.

If n·u≠0, exactly one parameter gives an intersection. If n·u=0 and n·A=d, every parameter works and the line is contained. If n·u=0 but n·A≠d, no parameter works and the line is strictly parallel outside the plane. The workbench distinguishes this parallel case from contained.

A perpendicular line has direction u parallel to n, not perpendicular to it. Such a line also intersects uniquely. Intersection classifies position; perpendicularity adds directional information.

### Example 1: Four configurations with one normal

For z=0, normal n=(0,0,1). Line (0,0,1)+t(1,0,0) has u·n=0 but stays at z=1, so it is strictly parallel. Moving its base point to the origin makes it contained while retaining the direction.

Line (0,0,1)+t(1,0,−1) has z=1−t and intersects at t=1, point (1,0,0), obliquely. Direction (0,0,−1) instead is parallel to n and gives a perpendicular line intersecting at the origin.

### Example 2: Skew lines and plane constants

Lines l=(t,0,0),m=(0,1+s,1) have perpendicular directions but stay at z=0 and z=1 respectively. They never meet and are not parallel, so they are skew. Their directions can define a smaller angle without an actual intersection of the lines.

Planes α:z=0 and β:−2z=−4 have parallel normals, but β is z=2, so the planes are distinct and parallel. Changing β to −2z=0 scales both normal and right side consistently, giving coincidence. Nonparallel normals, as for z=0 and x=0, give an intersection line; zero normal dot product additionally identifies perpendicular planes.

## Keep all premises in the workbench

Choose line-plane, line-line or plane-plane examples. Read complete equations and base points before predicting relation and diagnostic dot product. Displayed line pieces and plane patches have artificial edges. Coincident patches overlap and an intersection may fall outside a displayed patch; full infinite-object equations decide the relation.

The audit gives dot products, incidence residuals and intersection parameters. Camera changes preserve answers. Nonzero directions and normals are defining premises: zero direction gives only a point; zero normal can give all space or no points, not a genuine plane.

The exit derives the three parameter branches and the perpendicular direction condition. Separate a direction test from a position test so neither a picture nor one dot product replaces the argument.
