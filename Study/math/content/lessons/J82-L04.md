# 简单二次三项式分解

## 两个条件来自完整展开

本课研究首项系数为一的整数二次式 `x²+px+q`，寻找整数 r、s 使它等于 `(x+r)(x+s)`。展开得 `x²+sx+rx+rs=x²+(r+s)x+rs`，所以必须同时满足 `r+s=p` 和 `rs=q`。

积控制常数，和控制一次项系数。只找一对乘积相符的数，会漏掉中间项。先看常数正负决定符号关系，再用和判断大小和正负，最后完整展开回查，而不是把找到的数直接写成 x 的解。

### 例 1：积六还需要和五

`x²+5x+6` 中，整数一与六虽然积六，和却为七；候选 `(x+1)(x+6)` 展开是 x²+7x+6，不是原式。二与三积六、和五，所以得到 `(x+2)(x+3)`。

也要考虑负号。常数为正六，两个数同号；一次项系数为正五，选择两正。若原式为 `x²−5x+6`，则两负的 −2、−3 符合，结果 `(x−2)(x−3)`。因式交换次序仍是同一个乘积，不需要只背一种排列。

### 例 2：负常数与零常数

`x²+x−6` 的常数负六，两个数异号。三与负二积负六、和正一，故 `(x+3)(x−2)`。若换成 −3 与二，和负一，会得到另一个整式 `x²−x−6`。只匹配绝对值会错过关键符号。

`x²+4x` 的常数零，先用公因式方法得 `x(x+4)`，等价于一组偏移零与四。不用列出无穷多个“零乘任意数”的配对，因为中项还要求和四。若中项也零，则 x² 本身已可理解为 x·x 或重复因子 x²。

## 没有整数配对时，说清范围

对 `x²+x+1`，整数积一的无序配对只有 (1,1) 与 (−1,−1)，和分别二与负二，都不是一。因此它不能写成两个整数一次因式的乘积。这个结论依赖首项系数一、整数系数和完整检查，不应扩大成对所有数域或其他多项式的判断。

若首项不是 x² 而是 2x²，本课的简单积和规则不能原样套用；需另行处理系数或用后续方法。本课宁可保留范围，也不把一个方便口诀当所有二次式的通法。

正偏移可在 x>0 的长方形分块中寻找边长；有负偏移时，用有符号配对表理解交叉项，不画负宽度。工作台可改变 p、q，自行填两个整数并分别核积与和，再展开所有整数乘积对。常数零时明确切换到提 x 的方法。

家长问“这对整数的积和分别是多少？”“它们为什么取同号或异号？”“当前结论只覆盖哪个范围？”每一次猜测都应由两个条件和展开支持。

# Factoring simple quadratics

## Both constraints come from full expansion

This lesson studies monic integer quadratics x²+px+q and seeks integers r,s for (x+r)(x+s). Distribution gives x²+(r+s)x+rs, requiring both r+s=p and rs=q.

The product controls the constant; the sum controls the linear coefficient. Matching only a product omits the middle. Use the constant’s sign to relate signs, then use the sum and expand back. The offsets are parts of factors, not a requested list of solutions for x.

### Example 1: product six also needs sum five

For x²+5x+6, one and six multiply to six but add to seven. Their proposed factors expand to x²+7x+6, not the source. Two and three match both constraints, giving (x+2)(x+3).

Positive constant six requires same-sign offsets. Positive sum five selects both positive. For x²−5x+6, select −2 and −3 instead, giving (x−2)(x−3). Reversing factor order preserves the same product.

### Example 2: negative and zero constants

For x²+x−6, the negative constant requires opposite signs. Three and −2 multiply to −6 and sum to one, giving (x+3)(x−2). The reversed signs −3 and two sum to −1 and produce x²−x−6 instead. Absolute values alone do not decide the factors.

For x²+4x, extract common x to get x(x+4). The offsets are zero and four. There is no need to list infinitely many zero-product pairs because the sum must also be four. If the linear coefficient is zero too, x² already represents the repeated factor x·x.

## State the scope of a missing integer pair

For x²+x+1, the unordered integer product-one pairs are (1,1) and (−1,−1), with sums two and −2. Neither sum is one. It therefore has no product of two integer linear factors in this monic setting. This uses the leading coefficient, number domain and a complete search; do not generalise it to every domain or polynomial.

For a leading term such as 2x², this simple offset rule cannot be copied unchanged. Coefficients require another treatment. Keep the scope explicit instead of turning a useful special method into a universal quadratic algorithm.

Positive offsets can be pictured as rectangle partitions for x>0. Negative offsets belong in signed algebraic pairs rather than negative physical widths. The workshop lets you change p,q, enter two integers and check product and sum separately before revealing product pairs. For zero constant it explicitly uses common x.

A parent asks for both numerical constraints, the sign reasoning and the scope of the conclusion. Every candidate should be supported by both conditions and a full expansion.
