# 一般式、顶点式与因式形式

## 同一函数，选择合适的观察窗口

二次函数一般式y=ax²+bx+c直接给出整理后的系数，c是纵轴截距；顶点式y=a(x−h)²+k直接显示顶点(h,k)、对称轴x=h和全实数最值；若有实根，还可用因式形式y=a(x−r₁)(x−r₂)读零点。a≠0是共同前提。三种写法必须对所有输入表示同一函数，不能仅在几个点或零点处相同。

“等值”与“完成指定形式”是两件事。题目要顶点式，原一般式虽然表示同一函数，也还没有展示移位平方；题目要一般式，未展开的平方同样没有完成要求。工作台会先核恒等，再核表达结构，不把等值但未完成说成函数不同。输入框处理有理系数，含无理因子的数学结论另以精确根和纸笔解释核验。

### 例 1：一条曲线的三个面貌

x²−4x+3配方为x²−4x+4−1=(x−2)²−1，也可分解为(x−1)(x−3)。所以同一函数可以写为y=x²−4x+3=(x−2)²−1=(x−1)(x−3)。一般式读纵轴截距3；顶点式读(2,−1)与轴x=2；因式形式读零点1、3。

将后两式展开，二次、一次、常数系数都回到1、−4、3，才保证每一个x对应相同输出。2(x−1)(x−3)虽然也有零点1、3，却是原函数的两倍：x=0时原函数3，新函数6；x=2时原函数−1，新函数−2。相同根集不能替代相同函数，外系数不能丢。

### 例 2：外系数与补偿

2x²−4x−6先提2得到2(x²−2x−3)。配方时括号内写(x−1)²−4，所以顶点式为2(x−1)²−8；分解时x²−2x−3=(x−3)(x+1)，所以因式形式为2(x−3)(x+1)。展开核三个系数2、−4、−6。把配方补偿误写成−4会改变函数，漏外部2同样错误。

负外系数也不能跳步：−2x²−8x+1=−2[(x+2)²−4]+1=−2(x+2)²+9。里面减四，乘外部负二后贡献正八，再加一为九。写成−2(x+2)²−7，通常是忽略乘法符号或漏了整个补偿项。

## 因子存在，要说明数的范围

x²+1对所有实数至少为1，没有实零点；等价地判别式−4<0。因此它不能写成两个实一次因子的乘积。这一结论不是靠“试了几对整数，没找到”得到的。x²−2也没有整数一次因子，却有实根±√2，可写成(x−√2)(x+√2)。整数、有理数、实数的分解范围不能混用。

若只有一个不同实根，因式形式可有重复因子：−(x−2)²就是−(x−2)(x−2)，在x=2处为零。若根中出现零，因子x仍要保留。对给定形式，交换因子次序或相应改号可以仍恒等，不能仅按某个固定文本判断。家长可让孩子根据任务选择形式，再逐项展开核系数，最后说明这次改写帮助看见了哪一条性质。

# Standard, vertex and factored forms

## Choose a useful view of one function

Standard form y=ax²+bx+c exposes collected coefficients and y-intercept c. Vertex form y=a(x−h)²+k exposes (h,k), axis x=h and the unrestricted extremum. When real roots exist, factored form y=a(x−r₁)(x−r₂) exposes zeros. All require a≠0 and must agree for every input, not just a few points.

Identity and completed form are separate requirements. An unchanged standard expression is equivalent to itself but does not complete a request for a shifted-square form. An unexpanded square likewise does not complete standard form. The laboratory checks identity and structure separately. Its expression field uses rational coefficients; irrational factors are handled with exact roots and a written explanation.

### Example 1: three views of one curve

Complete x²−4x+3 as x²−4x+4−1=(x−2)²−1, or factor it as (x−1)(x−3). Standard form gives y-intercept three, vertex form gives (2,−1) and x=2, and factored form gives zeros one and three.

Expanding both alternatives returns coefficients 1,−4,3, establishing equality at every input. The expression 2(x−1)(x−3) has the same zeros but doubles other outputs: at zero it gives six instead of three, and at two it gives −2 instead of −1. Equal root sets do not establish equal functions; retain the outside multiplier.

### Example 2: compensate for the outside coefficient

Start 2x²−4x−6 as 2(x²−2x−3). Completing inside gives 2[(x−1)²−4]=2(x−1)²−8. Factoring inside gives 2(x−3)(x+1). All expand to coefficients 2,−4,−6. Subtracting only four in vertex form or omitting the outer two changes the function.

For a negative coefficient, −2x²−8x+1=−2[(x+2)²−4]+1=−2(x+2)²+9. Inside −4 contributes outside +8 before adding one. Writing a constant −7 usually indicates a missed multiplication sign or compensation term.

## State the coefficient domain

Since x²+1≥1 for every real x, it has no real zeros and cannot be a product of two real linear factors. Its discriminant −4 gives the same conclusion. An unsuccessful integer search alone would not establish that result. The expression x²−2 has no integer linear factors but does have real roots ±√2, yielding (x−√2)(x+√2). Distinguish integers, rationals and reals.

A repeated root can appear as a squared factor: −(x−2)²=−(x−2)(x−2). A zero root retains factor x. Factor order and balanced sign changes may vary without changing the function. A parent should ask which form suits the task, verify every expanded coefficient, and hear which feature becomes easier to read after rewriting.
