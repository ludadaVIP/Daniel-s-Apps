# 正比例与一次函数对比

## 固定变化率不等于固定比值

本单元一次函数 y=mx+b 要求 m≠0。b=0 时形式为 y=mx，叫正比例函数，是一次函数的特例；对于非零输入，y/x=m 恒定。x=0 时得到点 (0,0)，但不能计算 0/0 来验证比值，因为除零未定义。

一次但非正比例的关系有固定变化率，却有不为零的固定项。它不通过原点，其 y/x 会随输入变化。不要把“每增加一单位加同样多”与“输出总是输入的同一倍数”混在一起；前者描述增量，后者描述总量之间的比。

### 例 1：相同斜率，不同比例

比较 y=2x 与 y=2x+3。两式每增加一个 x 单位，y 都增加二，斜率相同。第一式在 x=1、2 时输出二、四，比值都为二；且零输入输出零，所以为正比例。

第二式在 x=1、2 时输出五、七，比值分别五与 7/2，并不相同。它的 m=2 非零，仍是一次函数，但 b=3 非零，所以不是正比例。图像是一条平移后的直线，固定费用三就能产生这种结构，不因“长得仍像直线”而变成正比例。

负系数也可以正比例。例如 y=−x/2，m=−1/2 非零，b=0；输入一、二输出负半、负一，非零输入比值固定为负半。“正比例”的“正”不意味着比例系数必须为正数，本课按 y=mx 的非零系数形式判断。

### 例 2：过原点与零函数陷阱

完整公式 y=x² 也通过原点，但输入一、二、三分别输出一、四、九，y/x 分别为一、二、三，不固定；它含平方项，不属 mx+b 形式。过原点只是正比例图像的一个特征，不足以反过来证明正比例。两三个点看起来接近直线，同样不能证明未知关系的全局形式。

常函数 y=4 的 m=0、b=4，水平、输出不变；它仍然是函数，按本单元一次函数非零系数约定归常函数。y=0 虽过原点且非零输入比值为零，仍因 m=0 被本课的正比例定义排除，归常函数。若阅读其他资料采用更宽泛的 linear 定义，要先对齐约定，不能把名称差异当计算矛盾。

这些对照把“函数”“直线形状”“一次”“正比例”“常函数”拆开判断。竖直线甚至不是 y 关于 x 的函数，不能仅用它也是直线就归入一次关系。

## 工作台与复盘

选择完整公式，观察实际图线与 x、y、y/x 表。零输入比值明确显示未定义；这不表示函数在零没有输出，例如 2x 在零仍给零。图形窗口只显示有限部分，分类依据给定完整规则，不能把表格样本当作未知整体规律的证明。

家长可依次问：它能写成 mx+b 吗？m 是否非零？b 是否零？如果拿“过原点”作为理由，要求给一个能排除 x² 的额外条件。让孩子用具体输入比值与增量说明区别，解释仍需人工检查。

# Direct proportion vs linear functions

## Constant rate and constant ratio differ

This unit’s first-degree form y=mx+b requires m≠0. If b=0, y=mx gives direct proportion. For nonzero inputs, y/x=m is constant. At zero the function outputs zero, but the quotient 0/0 is undefined and cannot test the ratio.

A non-direct first-degree rule retains a constant rate while having a nonzero fixed term. Its total output/input ratio changes. “The same increment per extra input unit” differs from “the output is always the same multiple of the input”.

### Example 1: equal slope, different ratios

Compare y=2x and y=2x+3. Both increase output by two per x unit. The first gives outputs two and four at inputs one and two, with ratio two each time. Its zero intercept makes it direct proportion.

The second gives five and seven, with ratios five and 7/2. It is first-degree because m=2 is nonzero, but not direct because b=3. A fixed fee creates this structure: being a straight line is insufficient.

A negative coefficient can also give direct proportion here. For y=-x/2, m=-1/2 is nonzero and b=0, with constant ratio minus one half at nonzero inputs. Decrease does not disqualify the rule.

### Example 2: origin crossing and the zero function

The complete rule y=x² passes through the origin but gives ratios one, two and three at inputs one, two and three. Its square term is outside mx+b. Origin crossing is insufficient to prove direct proportion. A few nearly straight sample points likewise cannot establish an unknown global formula.

The rule y=4 is constant with m=0,b=4, still a function but not first-degree under this unit’s convention. The zero function y=0 also has m=0 and is classified constant here, despite origin crossing and zero ratios at nonzero inputs. Other English linear/affine conventions may differ, so compare conditions rather than labels alone.

These examples separate function status, straight shape, first degree, direct proportion and constancy. A vertical line is straight but is not y as a function of x.

## Investigate

Select a supplied complete formula and inspect its graph and x,y,y/x table. At zero the ratio is explicitly undefined, which does not remove the function’s output there. A viewing window displays only a portion; classification follows the full supplied rule, not finite samples as proof of unknown behaviour.

A parent asks whether the rule has form mx+b, whether m is nonzero and whether b is zero. If the reason is only origin crossing, ask for a condition excluding x². Check concrete ratios and increments rather than terminology alone.
