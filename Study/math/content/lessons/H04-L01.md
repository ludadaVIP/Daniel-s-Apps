# 角度与弧度

同一个转角放在不同大小的圆上，大圆截出的弧更长。若把弧长除以半径，这个比值却保持不变。弧度把角与圆上的长度关系直接连接起来。

## 一条核心想法

**弧度数等于有向弧量除以半径：θ=s/r。** 正向逆时针为正，顺时针为负；长度单位必须一致。若s专指非负的累计弧长，则s=r|θ|；正向角时可以直接写s=rθ。弧度是长度与长度的比，没有长度量纲，仍常写rad标明角的度量方式。

当弧长等于半径时，转角是1 rad，并非1°。整周弧长2πr，所以一周2π rad=360°，半周π rad=180°。这是确切换算；1 rad约57.3°只是近似。

### 例1：完整有向角的换算

150°×π/180°=5π/6 rad。反过来，−3π/4 rad×180°/π=−135°。π在第二个式中消去，方向负号保留。不能因为计算器当前在DEG模式，就把题中π/2当作π/2度。

−450°×π/180°=−5π/2 rad。它的终边与−π/2相同，却多顺时针转了一整圈。求三角函数值时可以借同终边化简；转换完整转动量时不能删去那一周。同样720°=4π rad，不是0 rad。

### 例2：从弧长读角，从角读弧长

半径3 cm、正向角2π/3 rad，弧长s=3×2π/3=2π cm。若角写成120°，须先换为2π/3 rad，不能把120直接代入s=rθ而得到360 cm。

纸上车轮半径0.4 m，正向弧长0.6 m。θ=0.6/0.4=1.5 rad；计算的比值已经是弧度数，不再乘π。如果半径40 cm、弧长60 cm，同样得到60/40=1.5 rad；混用0.6与40会破坏单位一致性。

## 预测与弧长实验

先选有向角，预测弧度，再预测当前半径上的累计弧长。改变半径时角度答案保留，弧长需要重新核对。图中半径使用同一长度标尺，圆的大小实际改变；负角按顺时针扫过。多圈轨迹可能重叠，标签仍保留完整θ，图形不能替你删掉整圈。

## 迁移与家长核对

拿线段在纸圆上比较“一段半径长的弧”，解释为什么r变而s/r不变。家长先问单位是否一致，再问需要完整角还是同终边代表。出口1.5 rad若写成1.5π，应回到弧长与半径的比值，而不只提醒“少乘或多乘了什么”。下一课用弧度记录单位圆输入；在图像和周期课再次复习整周2π。

# Degrees and radians

The same turn cuts a longer arc on a larger circle. Dividing arc length by radius gives an unchanged ratio. Radians connect an angle directly to the geometry of a circle.

## One central idea

**The radian measure is directed arc measure divided by radius: θ=s/r.** Counterclockwise is positive and clockwise negative, with matching length units. If s instead means nonnegative travelled arc length, use s=r|θ|. For a positive turn this reduces to s=rθ. A radian is a ratio of lengths and has no length dimension, although rad usefully names the angle convention.

An arc as long as the radius subtends one radian, not one degree. A full circumference is 2πr, so one turn is 2π rad=360° and half a turn is π rad=180°. This relationship is exact; one radian being about 57.3° is an approximation.

### Example 1: Convert a complete directed rotation

150°×π/180°=5π/6 rad. In reverse, −3π/4 rad×180°/π=−135°. Pi cancels in the second calculation and the negative direction remains. A calculator being in DEG mode does not turn a question’s π/2 into π/2 degrees.

−450°×π/180°=−5π/2 rad. Its terminal ray matches −π/2, but the rotation includes an extra clockwise turn. Coterminal angles help evaluate trig functions; they cannot replace the requested complete rotation during conversion. Likewise 720°=4π rad, not zero radians.

### Example 2: From arc to angle and back

With radius three centimetres and positive angle 2π/3 rad, the arc length is s=3×2π/3=2π cm. An angle supplied as 120° must first become 2π/3 rad. Substituting 120 directly into s=rθ would incorrectly produce 360 cm.

A paper wheel has radius 0.4 m and a positive arc of 0.6 m. Its angle is θ=0.6/0.4=1.5 rad. That ratio is already a radian measure; do not multiply it by π. Radius 40 cm and arc 60 cm give the same 60/40=1.5 rad, while mixing 0.6 and 40 would lose unit consistency.

## Predict and explore arc length

Choose a directed angle, predict its radian measure, then predict travelled arc length at the current radius. Changing the radius preserves the angle prediction but resets the arc task. The drawing uses one length scale, so radius changes actually resize the circle. Negative angles sweep clockwise. Multiple turns can overlap visually, but the θ label retains the complete rotation; the picture cannot remove turns for you.

On paper compare an arc with a piece of string one radius long. Explain why changing r preserves s/r for a fixed angle. A parent first checks matching units, then whether the task asks for the complete angle or a coterminal representative. If the exit answer is written as 1.5π, revisit the ratio rather than simply saying which factor to remove.
