## 让读者知道答案为什么成立

一个完整解释把数量关系、计算、单位和情境回答连起来。不是每题都要写长文章，但别人应能看出每个数代表什么，为什么选这一步，以及结果怎样回答原问题。只写一个正确数字，无法区分理解、猜到、抄到或算式不成立。

核查也要有作用。把送出的量加回，应恢复原有；把总价与余钱相加，应恢复预算。它们从另一方向检验关系，比把原算式又抄一遍更有帮助。核查必须采用给定条件，不能拿自己的答案作为它自己的证明。

### 例 1 · 同一个十七，两种可靠程度

1. 五袋卡片，每袋四张，总共送出三张。
2. 正确关系为 **5×4=20 张**原有，再 **20−3=17 张**剩余。
3. 回答“还剩十七张”已有数、单位与情境，但没有说明计算关系与核查。
4. “5+4−3=17 张”更有问题：袋数与每袋张数应相乘，且左边实际等于六。
5. 即使最后数字碰巧写对，也不能修好错误理由；用 **17+3=20** 核查剩余与原有，才能支持解释。

### 例 2 · 把钱数放回预算情境

1. 预算 **NZ$20**，买三本每本 **NZ$4**的本子。
2. 第一步求总价 **3×4=NZ$12**，不是剩十二本。
3. 余钱 **20−12=NZ$8**。用话回答：“买后还剩八元。”
4. 核查 **8+12=20**，余钱加花掉的钱恢复预算。
5. “20−3=17”只扣本数，没有算钱数；“十二”只得到花的钱，也没有回答余钱问题。

### 用自己的话，不按固定句子评分

你可以画图、讲给家长听，或写短句。关键是关系合理、数与单位相符、未知被回答、核查有效，不必使用某个固定关键词。程序不能仅凭“因为”“所以”就判断解释好，也不能因为句子与示例不同就判错。

面积问题同样需要单位含义：四厘米乘三厘米，四格一行共三行，覆盖 **12 cm²**。平方厘米说明计的是覆盖方格；十二厘米则计长度，不能当作同一答案。把三个四格行相加恢复十二格，是另一种计数核查。

出口题会换故事：两包每包九张，另有五张散卡，一共 **2×9+5=23 张**。这里五张是加入，不是送出；不能照搬上一例的减法。核查二十三减去五得到十八，再十八除二得到每包九张，回到给定条件。

工作台让你比较三种说明，找到缺少关系、错误算式或已经连贯的解释。它只核对这些明确示例的分类；你在练习与出口中的自己的理由仍需家长按情境复核。抄示例不等于独立理解，可请家长换一个数或问题再听你说明。

**家庭挑战：** 用纸卡建立假想小铺。四包每包六张、每包五元，预算四十元，另有没买的三元笔。求余钱与送三张后的剩余卡，分别标单位并加回核查；若问总质量，说明缺什么资料。无需真实购买、计时或得分。

## Show why the answer is supported

A sound explanation connects relationships, calculation, units and an answer to the story. It need not be a long essay. Readers should understand what numbers represent, why a step applies and how the result answers the question. A correct number alone cannot distinguish understanding from guessing, copying or invalid reasoning.

Verification should test the relationship. Adding given cards back should recover original cards; adding cost and remaining money should recover budget. These reverse-direction checks offer more than repeating an expression. Use supplied conditions rather than treat your answer as its own proof.

### Example 1 · The same seventeen, different reasoning

1. Five bags hold four cards each, with three given away in total.
2. **5×4=20 cards** are original; **20−3=17 cards** remain.
3. “Seventeen cards remain” gives number, unit and context, but lacks the relationship and verification.
4. “5+4−3=17 cards” is invalid: bags times cards per bag should multiply, and the left side actually equals six.
5. A coincidentally correct final number cannot repair bad reasoning. Check **17+3=20** against the original count.

### Example 2 · Return money to its budget context

1. Budget **NZ$20**, three notebooks priced **NZ$4 each**.
2. Cost is **3×4=NZ$12**, not twelve notebooks remaining.
3. **20−12=NZ$8** remain. State: “Eight dollars remain after buying the notebooks.”
4. Check **8+12=20**, restoring the budget from remaining and spent money.
5. **20−3=17** subtracts notebook count from money; “twelve” answers cost rather than remaining money.

### Use your own words rather than a required sentence

Use a diagram, speak to a parent or write short sentences. Sound relationships, quantities, units, a relevant answer and effective verification matter more than fixed words. Software cannot establish understanding merely from “because” or reject a different sentence automatically.

Area also needs unit meaning. A four-by-three-centimetre rectangle covers **12 cm²**, four cells in each of three rows. Twelve centimetres instead describes length. Adding three four-cell rows provides another count check.

A new story with two packs of nine and five loose cards gives **2×9+5=23 cards**. The five are added rather than given away, so copying subtraction from an earlier example would fail. Check twenty-three minus five equals eighteen, then eighteen divided by two recovers nine per pack.

The workshop classifies supplied explanations; it does not automatically assess your own written reasoning. Practice and exit explanations still need parent review in context. Copying a sample is not independent understanding: try a changed number or question with your own explanation.

**Family challenge:** build a fictional paper-card shop: four packs of six at five dollars per pack, budget forty dollars, and three-dollar pens not bought. Find money remaining and cards remaining after giving three away, naming units and checking by adding back. For total mass, identify missing information. No real purchase, timer or score is needed.
