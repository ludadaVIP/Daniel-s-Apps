# 基本乘法求导法则

两个函数相乘时，两个因子都会随输入变化。把它们的导数直接相乘会漏掉“原来已有多少”对变化的影响。本课用两项贡献解释乘积法则，再用先展开的办法独立核验。

## 两个贡献，而非两个率直接相乘

若u、v在所讨论输入处可导，则(uv)′=u′v+uv′。第一项让u变化并保留v，第二项保留u并让v变化。每一项只求导一个因子；最后相加。常数倍法则是它的特例，因为常数的导数为0。

为什么不是u′v′？在非零h下，乘积增量可精确分解为Δ(uv)=vΔu+uΔv+ΔuΔv。除h后，前两项趋向vu′+uv′，交叉项(Δu/h)Δv趋向0，因为可导使Δu/h趋有限数、Δv趋0。不能在有限h时就说交叉项精确为0。

也可在u(x+h)v(x+h)−u(x)v(x)中加减u(x)v(x+h)，直接得到[Δu/h]v(x+h)+u(x)[Δv/h]。连续性使v(x+h)趋向v(x)。两种推导都强调保留另一个因子的值。

### 例1：两项可以恰好抵消

f=(x²+1)(x−2)，设u=x²+1、v=x−2，u′=2x、v′=1。导数为2x(x−2)+(x²+1)=3x²−4x+1。

在x=1，u=2、v=−1、u′=2、v′=1。两项分别−2与2，所以变化率0。原乘积值−2是函数高度；错误的导数乘积2也不对。零变化率只是这个时刻，不表示原乘积整段恒定。

独立核验：原式展开x³−2x²+x−2，逐项求导3x²−4x+1，与结构法恒等。若只在x=1比出相同值，还不能证明两个任意表达式在全部输入上相同；这里用系数逐项对应完成核验。

### 例2：常数因子与两个相同因子

对5(x²+1)，把u=5代入乘积法则：0·(x²+1)+5·2x=10x。保留5是因为它不变，而不是因为所有因子都能原封不动搬到导数前。

对x²·x²，得到2x·x²+x²·2x=4x³。先合并为x⁴再求导也得4x³。错误的(2x)(2x)=4x²在x=1碰巧等于4，但在x=2给16而正确值32，暴露“只检查一个点”的危险。

## 从面积看单位，再回到符号

假设长u(t)和宽v(t)都以米计，面积A=uv以平方米计。u′v的单位是米/秒乘米，uv′的单位是米乘米/秒，两项都为平方米/秒，可相加。u′v′单位是平方米/秒²，根本不是面积的一阶时间变化率。此单位核验帮助判断公式角色，不代替可导前提。

# Product rule

When two functions are multiplied, both factors can change with the input. Multiplying their derivatives loses the effect of how much of the other factor is already present. Use two contributions, then check independently by expanding the source.

## Two contributions

If u and v are differentiable at the input in question, (uv)′=u′v+uv′. The first term changes u while retaining v; the second retains u while changing v. Differentiate one factor in each term, then add. The constant-multiple rule is a special case because the derivative of a constant is zero.

For a nonzero increment h the exact product change can be written Δ(uv)=vΔu+uΔv+ΔuΔv. After dividing by h, the first two terms tend to vu′+uv′. The cross term (Δu/h)Δv tends to zero because differentiability gives a finite limit for Δu/h and makes Δv approach zero. It is not exactly zero at a finite increment.

Alternatively, add and subtract u(x)v(x+h) in the original product difference. Its quotient becomes [Δu/h]v(x+h)+u(x)[Δv/h]. Continuity makes v(x+h) tend to v(x), giving the same result. Both arguments retain the other factor’s value rather than multiplying two rates.

### Example 1: The contributions can cancel

Let f=(x²+1)(x−2), with u=x²+1 and v=x−2. Then u′=2x and v′=1. The derivative is 2x(x−2)+(x²+1)=3x²−4x+1.

At x=1, u=2, v=−1, u′=2 and v′=1. The two contributions are −2 and two, so the instantaneous rate is zero. The original product value −2 is a height. The incorrect product of slopes gives two. Neither answers the derivative question. A zero rate at this input also does not establish constancy nearby.

For an independent check, expand the source to x³−2x²+x−2 and differentiate term by term. The result 3x²−4x+1 agrees as a polynomial identity. Checking only x=1 would not prove arbitrary expressions equal for every input; matching their coefficients does.

### Example 2: A constant factor and repeated factors

For 5(x²+1), the product rule gives 0·(x²+1)+5·2x=10x. Five remains because it is constant, not because every factor can be copied unchanged in front of a derivative.

For x²·x², the result is 2x·x²+x²·2x=4x³. Simplifying the source to x⁴ before differentiating confirms this. The incorrect (2x)(2x)=4x² happens to agree at one, but gives sixteen at two instead of the correct thirty-two. This exposes the danger of a one-point check.

## Units connect the rule to area

If length u(t) and width v(t) are measured in metres, area A=uv is measured in square metres. The term u′v has units m/s times m, and uv′ has units m times m/s. Both are m²/s and can be added. In contrast, u′v′ has units m²/s², which are not area’s first time-rate units. This dimensional check supports the formula’s interpretation without replacing its differentiability conditions.
