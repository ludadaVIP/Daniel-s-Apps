# 双变量散点图

## 一行记录，一对坐标

单变量分布研究一列数，双变量研究同一对象的两个量。虚构学习卡的一行同时记录练习轮数 x（轮）与得分 y（分），点 (x,y) 的两个数必须来自同一编号。先横后纵与以前坐标课相同；不同变量可用不同单位，纵横单位长度也不必相同，但每根轴必须均匀、有名称和刻度。

散点图通常不按编号连线，因为编号本身不是时间或连续路径。分别排序两列再配对会改变问题：它可能制造整齐的上升图，却破坏原记录。要排序表格，就把整行一起移动。重复位置可能代表不止一个对象，不能随手去重；探索图把重合身份合并标注，原表仍逐行保留。

### 例 1：有局部下降的整体正向

五条完整记录为 P1=(1,2)、P2=(2,3)、P3=(3,2)、P4=(4,5)、P5=(5,5)。P6 的 x=6，y 缺失。可画五个点；P6 留在表中但不画 (6,0)，因为零分是一条明确观察，不是“不知道”。

P3 的横坐标三和纵坐标二同属 P3，不能为了图更整齐而借 P2 的三。整体看，较大 x 往往伴随较大 y，可描述这批记录有正向关联。二到三处 y 从三降到二，并不推翻整体方向；正相关不是“每走一步必须严格增加”，也不是 y 一定等于某个固定公式。

如果只凭五个点断言“多练一轮一定提高两分”，就越过了证据。样本有限，没有给每个可能 x 的确定函数，更没有随机安排练习处理来排除其他因素。

### 例 2：向下、散布与 U 形

点 (1,8)、(2,6)、(3,7)、(4,3)、(5,2) 整体向右向下，可称负向关联，虽然中间有一次上升。再看 x 为 1、3、5 时都各有 y=2 和 y=6：点在高低两条水平带中均衡出现，未见明显向上或向下线性趋势。准确说“这批点没有明显线性关联”，不要据此证明任何关系都不存在。

U 形数据 (1,9)、(2,4)、(3,1)、(4,0)、(5,1)、(6,4)、(7,9) 先降后升。它有很清楚的弯曲关联，不能只因一条直线概括不了就说完全无关。此构造满足 y=(x−4)²，但从实际有限散点发现形状，不代表自动知道总体的精确规律。以后学习相关系数时还要注意：一个线性指标不能概括所有非线性关系。

## 描述不等于推断

观察先说方向与形状，再说是否紧密、是否有例外或单个远点。没有计算标准化指标时，不必把视觉判断包装成精确的相关系数。极端点可能影响整体方向，应核来源而非自动删除。

改变横轴窗口会改变图上斜陡，却不会改变原数对；不能由“线看起来陡”直接判关系更强。变量单位与显示比例也会影响外观。本工作台只画散点并提供逐行定位：选 P3 显示它的位置，选缺失行不会虚构一个点。

结论应带“在这些记录中”的范围。相关表示共同变化的模式，既不说明谁导致谁，也不保证观察到的趋势适用于所有人或未来。家长核对配对身份、缺失条数和孩子对 U 形的解释，再进入因果证据一课。

# Bivariate scatter plots

## One row supplies one pair

A univariate display studies one variable; a bivariate display studies two measurements on the same observational unit. A fictional card records practice rounds x and score y together. Each point must retain the same row’s identity, x first and y second. Variables may use different units and display scales, but each axis needs a uniform labelled scale.

Do not join points merely by record ID: identity is not time or a continuous path. Sorting columns independently can manufacture a tidy rising picture by destroying the observations. Sort whole rows instead. Repeated locations may represent multiple records; retain their identities and rows rather than deleting duplicates.

### Example 1: an overall rise with an exception

Complete pairs are P1=(1,2), P2=(2,3), P3=(3,2), P4=(4,5), P5=(5,5). P6 has x=6 and missing y. Plot five points and retain P6 in the table. Do not invent (6,0): zero score is an observation, while missing means unknown.

Both coordinates of P3 belong to P3. Borrowing P2’s score changes the data. Overall, larger x tends to accompany larger y, a positive association in these records. The local decrease from P2 to P3 does not cancel the overall pattern. Positive association does not require every successive score to increase or follow an exact formula.

Five points do not justify “each additional practice round always adds two points”. A small sample gives neither a deterministic rule for every x nor a randomised intervention that excludes other explanations.

### Example 2: falling, balanced and curved patterns

Pairs (1,8),(2,6),(3,7),(4,3),(5,2) show an overall negative association despite one local increase. At each x=1,3,5, suppose y is both two and six. These balanced high/low pairs show no clear rising or falling linear trend. Say “no clear linear association in these points”, not “proof of no relationship”.

The U-shaped pairs (1,9),(2,4),(3,1),(4,0),(5,1),(6,4),(7,9) have a clear curved relationship. A straight line is an unsuitable summary. This constructed example follows y=(x−4)², but a finite real scatter plot does not automatically identify an exact population formula. Later, a linear correlation coefficient will also need this limitation.

## Description is not inference

Describe direction, shape, clustering and exceptions. Without computing a standardised measure, do not present a visual impression as an exact correlation coefficient. Check unusual records against sources instead of automatically deleting them.

A changed x-axis window alters apparent steepness without changing pairs. Units and display ratios also affect appearance; steepness is not correlation strength. Select a table ID to locate its point. Selecting a missing row creates no false point.

Keep claims scoped to these records. Association identifies co-variation, not causal direction or a guarantee about everyone and the future. A parent checks row identity, missing counts and the explanation of a U shape before moving to causal evidence.
