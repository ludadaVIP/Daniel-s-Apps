# 弧长与扇形面积

## 同一个份额，乘不同的整体

扇形由两条半径和它们之间选定的弧围成。设半径r，选定圆心扫角θ度，占一整圈的份额为θ/360。因此弧长l=(θ/360)·2πr，扇形面积S=(θ/360)·πr²。份额相同，但所乘整体不同：一个是圆周长度，一个是圆面积。先问所求量的种类，再选择公式。

同一对端点之间通常有小弧与大弧，不能默认只取小角。工作台用明确的逆时针扫角，可超过180°。图中彩色曲线是所选弧，彩色区域是与该扫角对应的扇形。连接端点的直线是弦，其长度不等于弧长；弦与弧围成的是弓形，其面积也不等于这里的扇形面积。

### 例 1：六十度扇形的两种量

r=6 cm，θ=60°，份额60/360=1/6。整圆周长12π cm，所以弧长(1/6)×12π=2π cm。整圆面积36π cm²，所以扇形面积(1/6)×36π=6π cm²。2π和6π回答不同的量，单位分别为cm和cm²，不能互换。

完整扇形周长还包含两条半径，因此为2π+6+6=12+2π cm。弧长只计算弯曲边；把2π直接写成整个扇形周长，会遗漏两条直边。把面积6π cm²交到周长题，则是量纲错误，不只是数字算错。

### 例 2：大扇形与反求角度

仍取r=6，但选定300°大扇形，份额5/6。大弧长(5/6)×12π=10π cm，面积(5/6)×36π=30π cm²。它们分别与60°小部分的2π和6π合成整圆12π和36π，这是一个方便的核验。不能看到同一条弦，就把300°擅自换成60°。

反过来，若r=6且选定弧长3π cm，它占整圆周长的份额为3π/(12π)=1/4，因此扫角θ=90°。π约去，并不需要先代入3.14。精确结果保留π；只有题目明确要求近似时，才按指定精度计算小数。

## 变化与边界条件

角份额保持不变、半径扩大两倍，弧长扩大两倍，扇形面积扩大四倍。这与上一单元的相似长度k、面积k²一致。若角度也改变，不能只看半径倍率；须重新计算θ/360的份额。例如半径翻倍、角度减半，弧长不变而面积变为两倍。

本课实验研究0<θ<360°的扇形，周长可写l+2r。当θ恰为360°时区域是整圆，半径只是内部接缝，外周界不能再额外加2r。明确完整边界比背公式更可靠。

先在工作台选择弧长或面积，再给精确π表达式。显示旋转缩放不改答案，真实半径、扫角或所求量改变则重新作答。家长要求孩子先指边界或区域、写份额，再核单位；完整解释由人审阅。离堂用给定弧长反求角度，检验他是否理解公式可以双向使用，而不只是套一次乘法。

# Arc length and sector area

## One fraction, different wholes

A sector is bounded by two radii and a selected arc. For radius r and central sweep θ degrees, its fraction of a full turn is θ/360. Multiply full circumference 2πr by this fraction for arc length, or full area πr² for sector area. The fraction is shared, but the whole quantity differs. Identify the requested measurement before choosing a formula.

The same endpoints usually determine a minor and a major arc. This lab states a counterclockwise sweep, possibly above 180°. The coloured curve is the selected arc and the shaded region its sector. The straight endpoint segment is a chord, not an arc. A circular segment bounded by chord and arc differs from a sector bounded by radii and arc.

### Example 1: a sixty-degree sector

For r=6 cm and θ=60°, the fraction is 1/6. Full circumference is 12π cm, so arc length is 2π cm. Full area is 36π cm², so sector area is 6π cm². These answer different questions and carry different units.

The complete sector perimeter includes both radii as well as the arc: 12+2π cm. Giving only 2π omits the straight edges. Giving 6π cm² confuses an area with a length; it is a dimensional error rather than merely an arithmetic mistake.

### Example 2: a major sector and an inverse question

For the same radius with sweep 300°, the fraction is 5/6. Major arc length is 10π cm and sector area 30π cm². Adding the respective sixty-degree quantities recovers full circumference 12π and full area 36π. The same chord endpoints do not justify replacing the given 300° sweep with the smaller sixty-degree sweep.

Conversely, for radius six and selected arc length 3π, divide by full circumference 12π to obtain one quarter of a turn. Thus θ=90°. Pi cancels without substituting 3.14. Retain exact pi expressions unless an approximation and precision are explicitly requested.

At a fixed sweep, doubling radius doubles arc length and quadruples area, connecting with similarity factors k and k². If the sweep changes too, include its new fraction. Doubling radius while halving sweep preserves arc length but doubles area.

The interactive sectors use 0<θ<360°, where complete perimeter is l+2r. At a full 360° the region is a disk: the radius is an internal seam and contributes nothing extra to its exterior perimeter. Count the actual boundary rather than extending a formula blindly.
