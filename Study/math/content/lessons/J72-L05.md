# 从文字题建立代数式

## 定义变量后，才选择运算

建模不是把题目里所有数字和字母凑成一个式子。先写变量表示什么、单位是什么、允许哪些值；再确定问题求总费用、支数、周长还是面积。同一组数字可能支持不同问题，目标量决定关系。

人数 n 通常为非负或正整数，要按本题条件明确；长度的数值可允许正分数，不必局限整数。若 n 表示以厘米计的宽，长比宽多二厘米，长的数值就是 n+2。两个 n 在同一题中不能一个当人数、另一个当长度。

### 例 1：每人费用与一次固定费

虚构团体 n 人，n 为正整数，每人门票 NZ$4，另付一次整组费用 NZ$6。求一般总费用。

按人数重复的是四元，所以票费 `4n`；六元只在整组收一次，合起来 `4n+6`，金额单位 NZD。`n(4+6)=10n` 错误地把组费重复 n 次。

n=3 时费用十八，n=5 时二十六。这两个数用于检错，不能代替一般式。一般式的依据是每人费用与固定量的组成。若情境改为三袋铅笔、每袋 n+2 支，则两支在每袋里面，要重复三次，关系为 `3(n+2)=3n+6`，不是 3n+2。

### 例 2：同一个长方形，两种目标

长方形长 n+2 cm、宽 n cm，n>0，这里 n 是宽的厘米数值。

周长是两长两宽，`2(n+2)+2n=4n+4`，单位 cm；面积是长乘宽，`n(n+2)=n²+2n`，单位 cm²。两式中都有 n 和二，却不能互相替换，因为边界长度与覆盖量不同。

n=3 时，周长十六厘米，面积十五平方厘米。工作台把同一长方形保持比例，再切换边界与覆盖目标。n 的样例变动时，正确一般式仍成立，具体代入值需要重算。长度模型本身也适用其他正长度，并不因为滑块只列整数就限制真实的数学条件。

## 固定优惠与更多边长

虚构购买 n 本练习本，每本五元，一次整单优惠二元，n 为正整数。付款 `5n−2`；`3n` 把优惠当成每本都有，换了条件。若题目说每本优惠二元，才会得到 `n(5−2)`。先定位优惠属于哪一个整体，比看见“优惠”就减一个数更可靠。

长方形改为长 2n+1、宽 n+3，仍求周长，先写 `2[(2n+1)+(n+3)]`，再整理为 `6n+8`。本课允许这种因式形式，因为目标是表达数量关系；不像去括号练习那样要求一定展开。写一个等号或带单位文本进入表达式框不符合输入约定，单位应在解释中说明。

## 检错与一般推导各有作用

挑两个允许值，可以快速发现把固定费重复、把周长当面积等错误；但如果两式在这两点吻合，不能就证明恒等。一般推导来自“每份重复几次”“每类边有几条”“覆盖量是长乘宽”等关系，等值表达可以再按精确系数核对。

家长可要求孩子指明每个项的来源、变量范围、目标单位，再改一个条件问式子哪部分必须跟着改。家庭任务完全可以纸上虚构，不需要实际购买。能保留情境意义，同时写出适用于全部允许值的关系，才是这课的目标。

# Modelling word problems with expressions

## Define the variable before choosing operations

Modelling is not assembling every number and letter in a story. Define what the variable measures or counts, its unit and its permitted values. Then identify whether the target is cost, pencil count, perimeter or area. The target quantity determines the relationship.

A people count may be a positive or non-negative integer according to the problem. A length can include positive fractions. If n is a width’s numerical value in centimetres and the length is two centimetres greater, its numerical value is n+2. The same n cannot silently change its meaning within a problem.

### Example 1: per-person and fixed charges

A fictional group has positive integer n people. Tickets cost NZ$4 each, with one NZ$6 group fee. The repeated amount is four, giving ticket cost `4n`. Add six once for total `4n+6`, measured in NZD. The expression `n(4+6)=10n` incorrectly charges the group fee n times.

At n=3 the cost is eighteen, and at n=5 it is twenty-six. Those values check examples but do not replace the general expression. The derivation comes from the charging relationship. By contrast, three pencil bags each containing n+2 pencils need `3(n+2)=3n+6`: the extra two repeat inside each bag.

### Example 2: one rectangle, two targets

A rectangle is n+2 cm long and n cm wide, with n>0. Here n is the numerical width in centimetres. Perimeter adds two lengths and two widths: `2(n+2)+2n=4n+4`, in cm. Area multiplies length and width: `n(n+2)=n²+2n`, in cm².

Both expressions use n and two, but boundary length and coverage are different quantities. At n=3, perimeter is sixteen centimetres and area fifteen square centimetres. The workshop preserves one proportional rectangle while switching targets. A valid general expression remains when the sample n changes, while its numerical answer needs recalculation. Integer slider samples do not restrict the underlying length model to integers.

## Locate the scope of a fixed adjustment

For a fictional order of n five-dollar notebooks and one two-dollar order discount, payment is `5n−2`. The expression `3n` would give the discount per notebook instead. If that were the stated condition, `n(5−2)` would be appropriate. Identify the whole to which the adjustment applies.

For rectangle sides 2n+1 and n+3, perimeter is `2[(2n+1)+(n+3)]=6n+8`. This modelling task accepts equivalent factored forms because its goal is the relationship, unlike a task explicitly requiring expansion. Omit equal signs and units from the expression input and explain the units separately.

Two permitted test values may reveal a repeated fixed fee or an area/perimeter confusion. Matches do not prove identity. General reasons come from repeated costs, side counts and area relationships, with exact coefficients checking equivalent forms. Explain the source of every term, domain and unit, then change a condition and identify which part of the expression must change. A fictional paper task needs no actual purchase.
