# 平行线与角度关系

## 平行是条件，角的位置也要给清

在同一平面内，两条不同直线永不相交时称平行。图上的一对匹配箭形记号表示题设的平行条件，不是装饰。有限图中暂时没有画出交点，并不能说明无限延长也不相交；“看起来差不多平行”不能替代条件。

一条截线分别穿过两线，在上交点按区域标 A、B、C、D，在下交点标 E、F、G、H。先辨角的位置，再用关系。A/E 在两交点相同位置，称同位；C/E 位于两线内部、截线两侧，称内错；D/E 位于两线内部、截线同侧，称同旁内角。名称不由数值相等决定。

### 例 1：已知平行，逐条推出角度

已知 ℓ∥m 且 A=65°。

1. A/E 同位，由平行线性质得 E=A=65°。
2. C/E 内错，由平行线性质得 C=E=65°；也可用同一上交点的对顶关系 A=C。
3. D/E 同旁内角，由平行线性质得 D+E=180°，因此 D=115°。

不是所有跨交点的角都相等。同旁内角一般是互补；只有特别情形如两个直角时，它们又相等又互补。若是 `(2x+10)°` 与 `(3x−5)°` 这一对同旁内角，就列和式 `5x+5=180`，得到 x=35，两角为八十与一百。

### 例 2：性质与判定的方向不同

若没有平行已知，但给定一对指定同位角都为 66°，可用“同位角相等，两直线平行”的判定推出平行。这是从角关系到直线关系，方向与上一例相反。

若只知道 A=65°，没有下线方向或其他跨交点关系，E 无法确定。下线可以转动而 A 不变，E 会变；即使某个画法碰巧令 E 也像六十五度，也没有获得题设保证。工作台在无平行模式允许更换未标度数的下线外形，用不同可能性暴露条件缺口。

写“图像平行，所以同位角相等，再由同位角相等证明平行”是假定了要证明的结论，是循环论证。应从独立的已知或已证明关系出发；没有足够条件就承认无法确定，不能补造标记。

## 去掉平行后，什么还保留

每个交点自身的两条直线仍然相交，所以对顶相等、邻补和一百八十仍成立。这些局部关系不需要两条被截线平行。跨交点使用同位、内错或同旁内角的数值性质时，才要核对平行前提或相应的反向判定条件。

操作前先说平行是已知还是待证，再指出角在截线哪侧、两线内部还是外部。家长可以删去平行标记，询问原结论还能否保证；再保留一个独立的指定同位角相等条件，让孩子恢复有效判定。条件与推理方向必须一起读。

# Parallel lines and angle relationships

## Parallelism and angle positions are premises

Distinct coplanar lines are parallel when they never meet. Matching chevrons state a given parallel condition. A finite drawing without a visible intersection does not establish what happens under indefinite extension. Nearly parallel appearance is insufficient.

A transversal forms angles A/B/C/D at the upper intersection and E/F/G/H below. A/E occupy matching positions and correspond. C/E lie between the lines on opposite transversal sides and are alternate interior. D/E lie inside on the same side and are co-interior. Position defines each pair, not equality of its measures.

### Example 1: start from given parallel lines

Given ℓ∥m and A=65°, corresponding equality gives E=65°. Alternate-interior equality gives C=65°, also obtainable from A/C opposite equality at the upper intersection. Co-interior supplementarity gives D+E=180°, hence D=115°.

Not every cross-intersection pair is equal. Co-interior angles are generally supplementary; a special right-angle pair can be both equal and supplementary. For co-interior measures `(2x+10)°` and `(3x−5)°`, their sum gives 5x+5=180, so x=35 and the angles are eighty and one hundred degrees.

### Example 2: a property and its converse run in different directions

If parallelism is not given but a specified corresponding pair is known to measure 66° each, the corresponding-angle converse establishes parallel lines. This goes from an angle relationship to a line relationship, reversing the direction of the property.

With only A=65° and no lower-line direction or cross-intersection premise, E is undetermined. The lower line may rotate while A stays fixed. Even a drawing where E happens to look like 65° does not guarantee it from the givens. The workshop offers several unmeasured lower-line shapes to expose this missing condition.

Arguing “the lines look parallel, so corresponding angles are equal, therefore they are parallel” assumes the target conclusion. Obtain an independently given or proved corresponding equality instead. If none exists, report insufficient information rather than inventing marks.

## What remains without parallelism?

Each individual intersection still has two straight lines, so vertically opposite equality and adjacent straight-angle sums remain valid locally. Those relationships do not require the two crossed lines to be parallel. Cross-intersection numerical properties need their parallel premise or the appropriate converse conditions.

Before calculating, state whether parallelism is given or is the target, then locate each angle relative to the transversal and the two lines. A parent can remove the parallel marks and ask what is still guaranteed, then supply an independent corresponding equality to build a valid converse. Read conditions and reasoning direction together.
