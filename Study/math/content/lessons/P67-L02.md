## 先问要回答什么，再选统计量

平均数、中位数和众数描述数据的不同特征，不是三个争夺“永远最好”称号的答案。平均数把**完整总量均分**；中位数描述**排序中间位置**；众数指出**最高频数的值或类别**。选择要依据问题、数据类型和需要保留的信息。

较大观察值会明显影响总量和平均数。中位数主要由排序位置决定，只改变末端远处的数值可能不动中间位置。这不意味着中位数总比平均数正确，也不意味着可以悄悄删除不方便的记录。

### 例 1 · 同一批等待可有平均数八、中位数三

1. 五次假想等待 **2、3、3、4、28 分钟**，完整总量为四十。
2. 若问把总量均分五份，每份 **40÷5=8 分钟**，用平均数。
3. 若明确要用稳健的中间位置描述这五次，第三位为三，用中位数 **3 分钟**。
4. 三出现两次，是这组数的众数；二十八只出现一次。
5. 平均数八、中位数三不矛盾，它们回答了不同问题，也都不声称每次实际等于该数。

把最后一条从八改为二十八时，总量由二十变四十，平均数从四变八；中位数仍为三。工作台保留这一条观察，不把它称作自动可删的“错误”。是否属于录入错误，需要另有证据；不能因为结果不好看就删掉。

### 例 2 · 需要均分总量时保留精确平均数

1. 三次等待 **1、2、2 分钟**，总量五分钟。
2. 均分三份得 **5/3 分钟**，不是截成一，也不是必须选原数据里的二。
3. 中位数和众数都为二，但它们不能替代“均分五分钟”的任务。
4. 若记录为 **0、2、4、6**，总量十二除以四，平均数为三。
5. 零仍是一条记录，不能删掉后用十二除以三。

类别数据要先看有没有数值尺度。纸卡类别 A、A、B、C，最常见的是 A，可报告众数类别。字母在这里是名称，不能按字母表随意赋数求出“平均字母”；对没有规定顺序的类别也不能声称有数值中位数。

一个统计量会丢掉一些信息。只说中位数三，看不出末端等待八还是二十八；只说平均数八，也不能知道三最常出现。说明数据和问题，比孤立报一个数字更有解释力。

这些结论只描述给出的五次或三次记录。小样本中一次较大等待不代表所有人的等待，也不确定下一次。家庭挑战：先保留全记录，分别写一个“总量均分”问题和一个“中间位置”问题，让家长指出各自要哪种量。带走一个发现：**问题决定统计量；真实记录不随结果喜好删改，样本描述不冒充未来保证。**

## Choose the summary to answer the question

Mean, median and mode describe different features. Mean **shares the full total equally**; median describes an **ordered middle position**; mode identifies **highest frequency**, including categories. Choice depends on the question, data type and information needed, rather than a claim that one summary is always best.

A large observation affects the total and mean. Changing a distant endpoint may leave the central positions and median unchanged. That resistance does not make median universally correct or justify quietly deleting inconvenient observations.

### Example 1 · Mean eight and median three can both be valid

1. Fictional waits **2,3,3,4,28 minutes** total forty.
2. Sharing the complete total into five equal parts gives mean **8 minutes**.
3. For a specifically requested resistant middle position, the third observation gives median **3 minutes**.
4. Three occurs twice and is the mode; twenty-eight occurs once.
5. Eight and three answer different questions, without asserting that every actual wait equals either summary.

Changing the final observation from eight to twenty-eight changes total twenty to forty and mean four to eight, while median stays three. The workshop retains this observation. Calling it an error requires evidence beyond its size; an unattractive summary is not a reason to remove it.

### Example 2 · Preserve an exact equal-share mean

1. Waits **1,2,2 minutes** total five.
2. Dividing into three equal parts gives **5/3 minutes**, rather than a truncated one or an observed-value-only answer two.
3. Median and mode are two, but neither substitutes for sharing the total five.
4. For **0,2,4,6**, divide total twelve by four to get mean three.
5. Zero is an observation, not a reason to divide by only three.

For nominal card categories A,A,B,C, frequency identifies modal category A. The letters are names without a numerical scale: arbitrary alphabetical coding does not create a meaningful numerical mean or median. One summary also loses information. Median three cannot reveal whether the final wait was eight or twenty-eight, while mean eight cannot reveal that three was most common. Describe the data and question alongside the number.

These summaries concern the stated observations, not everyone or the next wait. For a family task, retain all records and write one equal-sharing question and one middle-position question, then explain why their summaries differ. The takeaway matches the summary to the question, preserves genuine records and keeps a sample description separate from a future guarantee.
