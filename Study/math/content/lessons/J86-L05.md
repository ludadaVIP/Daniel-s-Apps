# 平行四边形的性质与判定

## 先问图形是否已经给定

本课讨论简单、非退化四边形，顶点按周界依次命名 ABCD，不把交叉连线的错误顺序当普通四边形。平行四边形的定义是两组对边分别平行：AB∥CD 且 BC∥AD。由定义可以证明对边相等、对角相等、邻角互补、对角线互相平分。

性质从“已是平行四边形”走向结论；判定从一些条件走向“它是平行四边形”。把一条性质倒过来用时，必须核它是否真是充分判定、是否遗漏另一组条件。看起来像一个斜长方形不等于已经知道两组平行。

### 例 1：从定义推性质

给 AB∥CD、BC∥AD，连 AC。平行线内错角给 BAC=DCA、BCA=DAC，公共边 AC=CA，于是 △ABC≅△CDA（ASA）。按对应得 AB=CD、BC=DA，且角 B=角 D。角 A/C 可由两小角相加得到对应等角；邻角由平行线同旁内角互补。

所以已知平行四边形 A=70°，相邻 D=110°，对角 C=70°。不能把“平行四边形的对边相等”直接拿来作为正在从定义证明对边相等的理由，那会把目标当已知。学习证明过程时应显式使用平行角、公共边和全等。

若对角线内部交于 O，可以由平行角与已得相等对边，证相对的两小三角形全等，推出 AO=OC、BO=OD。AO=3 cm 时整条 AC=6 cm；“互相平分”要求两条都被平分，而不是只给某一条的一半。

### 例 2：完整判定与梯形反例

简单四边形若两组对边分别相等，连 AC，用 AB=CD、BC=DA、AC=CA 得 SSS 全等，再由对应内错角相等的逆判定推出两组平行，故是平行四边形。这里使用的是“角相等推出平行”，不预先假设平行。

另一充分条件是**同一组**对边同时平行且相等，例如 AB∥CD 且 AB=CD。连 AC 可用夹角与公共边作 SAS，进而推出另一组平行。还可使用两对角线在内部交点互相平分的判定，两个等分条件必须同时给出。

对照 AB∥CD 且 AD=BC：平行条件与等长条件落在不同组。等腰梯形可满足它们，底边长度不同，另一组不平行，因此不能保证平行四边形。仅给一组平行或一组对边等长也不够；一个符合原条件但不满足结论的合法图形就构成反例。

## 操作与家长提示

工作台明确选择“只给这些条件”。充分条件的模型可改变斜移量而保条件，显示对角线不清空作答；不足条件使用实际简单四边形反例，不通过画得不准来冒充反例。新增对角线只是辅助连接，不自动增加等长或平行前提。

家长先问“已知它是平行四边形，还是正在证明它是？”再问平行与相等是否同一对边、两条对角线是否都等分。让孩子用一句具体缺失条件解释错误，比只说“这是性质不是判定”更清楚。

# Parallelogram properties and tests

## Is the shape already established?

Use a simple, nondegenerate quadrilateral named ABCD around its boundary. A parallelogram has AB∥CD and BC∥AD. From this definition follow equal opposite sides/angles, supplementary adjacent angles and mutually bisecting diagonals.

A property starts from an established parallelogram; a converse test establishes that status. Reversing a property requires complete sufficient premises. A slanted rectangular appearance is not a parallelism premise.

### Example 1: properties from the definition

Draw AC with both opposite pairs parallel. Alternate angles give BAC=DCA and BCA=DAC. Common AC=CA yields ABC≅CDA by ASA, hence AB=CD,BC=DA and B=D. Combining corresponding component angles gives A=C; parallel-line facts give supplementary neighbours.

Thus if A=70°, adjacent D=110° and opposite C=70°. When proving equal opposite sides from the definition, simply citing that same unproved property would be circular. State the parallel angles, shared side and congruence.

If diagonals meet internally at O, parallel-angle facts and an established equal opposite side give congruent opposite small triangles, hence AO=OC and BO=OD. AO=3 cm gives the entire AC=6 cm. Mutual bisection requires both equalities, not just one diagonal being halved.

### Example 2: complete tests and a trapezoid counterexample

If both opposite side pairs are equal, AC is common and SSS gives ABC≅CDA. Equal corresponding alternate angles then establish both parallel pairs. Do not assume the parallelism being proved.

The same opposite pair being parallel and equal, such as AB∥CD with AB=CD, is also sufficient via AC and SAS. Another complete test has both diagonals bisecting each other at an internal intersection.

In contrast, AB∥CD with AD=BC concerns different pairs. An isosceles trapezoid satisfies these but has unequal bases and no second parallel pair. One parallel or one equal opposite pair alone is insufficient. A valid figure satisfying premises without the target defeats a claimed universal guarantee.

## Investigate

The workshop states exactly which conditions are supplied. Shear a sufficient-case model while preserving them; revealing diagonals retains the answer. Insufficient cases use genuine simple-quadrilateral counterexamples. Drawing an auxiliary diagonal adds no unstated equality or parallelism.

A parent asks whether parallelogram status is given or targeted, whether parallelism/equality concern the same pair, and whether both diagonals are bisected. Name the concrete missing premise rather than relying only on a slogan about properties and tests.
