# 不定积分与原函数

求导把函数变成变化率；原函数问题反过来问：哪些函数求导后等于给定f？如果F′=f，就说F是f的一个原函数。不定积分符号∫f(x)dx表示完整原函数族，不是带上下限的数值面积。

## 一个、完整一族、带初值

若F是原函数，那么F+C也是，其中C为任意实常数。在同一连通区间上，任意两个原函数之差的导数为零，由中值定理其差为常数。因此完整写法为F+C。连通区间的条件不能省：定义域分成互不相连的部分时，各部分可各有常数。

幂规则倒过来给∫xⁿdx=xⁿ⁺¹/(n+1)+C，只在n≠−1时使用；n=−1的原函数是ln|x|，需分开讨论不跨零的区间。本课自动输入以有理系数多项式为主，不用此语法冒充所有函数的积分器。

“给一个原函数”时可以选择常数0、5或其他明确数；“给完整族”时必须保留任意实常数；“满足初值”时需要算出唯一适用常数。三个任务不能只靠同一格式机械作答。

### 例1：从完整族到初值

f(x)=3x²−4。逐项增幂再除以新幂，得F=x³−4x+C。求导回查：3x²−4，常数项消失，所以确为原函数族。漏掉C只给一个原函数，不是完整族。

若再要求F(0)=5，代入得C=5，故F=x³−4x+5。另一函数x³−4x+7导数也对，却不满足初值。核验必须同时检查全式导数和给定点值；只在几个点导数恰巧吻合不足以证明恒等。

也可写x³−4x−C，因为−C仍跑遍全部实数；x³−4x+2C+5也覆盖相同族。但写C²只允许非负偏移，漏掉负常数，不能描述完整族。此处输入器支持非零一次C；更复杂参数化超出当前自动语法，应改写为常规+C。

### 例2：反向辨结构并回查

对4x³，原函数族x⁴+C，因为(x⁴)′=4x³。对零函数，所有常数的导数为零，因此完整族是C，而“一个原函数”可以写0或8。不能把零被积函数理解成只有零原函数。

指数与三角函数也可由已学导数逆向识别：∫eˣdx=eˣ+C，∫sin x dx=−cos x+C；后一式的负号由(−cos x)′=sin x回查。若是e^(2x)，原函数为(1/2)e^(2x)+C，链式因子2在逆向时要除掉。三角公式默认弧度。这些式子需要在阅读和纸笔中解释，当前多项式自动框不接受任意超越函数表达式。

## 看懂垂直平移图

工作台画几个F₀+C，而非把它们误叫被积函数。同一个横坐标处，曲线高度随C变，斜率不变。显示常数只是从完整族中挑成员，并不把题目里的任意C固定为显示数。

先选择任务类别，再输入表达式。完整族核验逐项求导相等并检查任意常数的覆盖；初值任务另算点值。不填等号、积分号或近似系数。家长可问：“你给的是一个还是全部？”以及“为何初值确定常数而不改变导数？”出口说明还要保留区间条件，不能把不同连通部分强行绑为同一个常数。

# Antiderivatives and indefinite integrals

An antiderivative reverses differentiation: F is a primitive of f when F′=f. The indefinite integral ∫f(x)dx denotes the full family of primitives. Unlike a definite integral, it is not a numerical area with specified endpoints.

## One primitive, a full family, an initial condition

If F is a primitive, so is F+C for every real constant C. On a connected interval, two primitives differ by a constant: the derivative of their difference is zero, so the mean value theorem applies. On disconnected domain components, constants may be chosen independently; the interval condition matters.

Reverse the power rule to obtain xⁿ⁺¹/(n+1)+C when n≠−1. The exponent−1 is excluded because division by zero is invalid; its primitive is ln|x| on intervals avoiding zero. This workbench automatically checks rational-coefficient polynomials rather than claiming to integrate every function.

A request for one primitive permits any specified numerical constant. A full-family request requires an arbitrary real constant. An initial condition determines the appropriate numerical constant. The three tasks have different completeness requirements.

### Example 1: A family and then an initial value

For f(x)=3x²−4, reverse each power derivative to get F=x³−4x+C. Differentiating gives3x²−4 and eliminates the constant. Omitting C gives one primitive rather than the full family.

If F(0)=5 is also required, substitution yields C=5, so F=x³−4x+5. The expression x³−4x+7 has the right derivative but fails the initial condition. Check both global derivative identity and the specified point value. Matching derivatives at a few selected inputs does not establish identity.

Using−C gives the same full family because−C ranges over all real numbers. Likewise2C+5 covers every real offset. Using C² misses negative offsets and therefore loses primitives. The input checker supports nonzero affine occurrences of C. More complicated parameterizations should be rewritten in conventional+C form before automatic checking.

### Example 2: Reverse structure and verify signs

For4x³, the full family is x⁴+C, since differentiating x⁴ gives4x³. For the zero function, every constant differentiates to zero, so its family is C. Zero and eight are two possible individual primitives; zero integrand does not imply a unique zero primitive.

Known exponential and trigonometric rules can also be reversed. The integral of eˣ is eˣ+C. The integral of sin x is−cos x+C because differentiating−cos x gives sin x. For e^(2x), divide by the inner derivative to obtain(1/2)e^(2x)+C. Trigonometric formulas assume radians. Explain these rules in reading and on paper; the polynomial response box does not claim to accept arbitrary transcendental expressions.

## Interpret vertical translations

The plot displays several members F₀+C, not several different integrands. Their heights differ at the same input while their slopes agree. Selecting a displayed numerical constant chooses a member for illustration; it does not fix the arbitrary C in a full-family task.

Choose the task type, enter only the expression, then differentiate back. The checker compares exact polynomial coefficients and, where required, the initial value. A parent should ask whether the answer provides one member or all members, why an initial condition determines the constant, and where the connected-interval assumption enters. Preserve exact rational coefficients rather than rounded decimals that no longer differentiate to the intended integrand.
