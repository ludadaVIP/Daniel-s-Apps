# 顶点与对称轴

## 让平方项恰好为零

把二次函数写成 y=a(x−h)²+k，a≠0，便能直接读出顶点(h,k)与对称轴x=h。这里顶点是一个有序数对，对称轴是一条竖直线，两者不是同一种对象。a>0时平方项非负，k是全实数范围的最小值；a<0时平方项非正，k是最大值。后面处理实际范围时，还要另外确认输入h是否允许。

括号里写减h，因此x−2在x=2时为零；x+3则等于x−(−3)，在x=−3时为零。不要靠“看见减号就往左”的口令猜图，直接问括号什么时候零，符号关系就清楚了。改变h移横向位置，改变k移纵向位置，改变a控制开口与离轴高度。

### 例 1：读顶点，并用等距点验证

y=(x−2)²−1的顶点为(2,−1)，对称轴x=2。输入1和3都离轴一单位，输出均0；输入0和4都离轴两单位，输出均3。用一般距离d表示：输入h−d后括号为−d，输入h+d后为d，平方相同，两个输出均为ad²+k。这解释整条图像的对称，而不只是某两个点碰巧重合。

对y=−2(x+3)²+5，顶点为(−3,5)，轴x=−3，向下。负a不会把横向移位再反号。d=0时两个示意输入合成顶点这一个位置；不能为了凑“两点”画出重复的不同位置。若改变图像窗口，顶点可能暂时看不见，但计算所得坐标没有改变。

### 例 2：一般式先配方

求y=2x²−8x+3的顶点。先提出二次和一次项的公因数2，得到2(x²−4x)+3；括号内同加减4：2[(x−2)²−4]+3=2(x−2)²−5。因此顶点(2,−5)，轴x=2。补进去的四被外部2倍乘，所以补偿是减八，不能只减四。

也可由h=−b/(2a)=8/4=2，再算k=f(2)=8−16+3=−5。公式只是配方关系的简写，k必须代入计算，不等于一般式常数c=3。c表示x=0处的纵轴截距；顶点在x=h处，除非h=0，两者通常不同。

## 分数与退化边界

对y=−(1/2)x²+3x−2，h=−3/(−1)=3，k=−9/2+9−2=5/2，顶点为(3,5/2)。用精确分数可避免把图上近似位置当作计算依据。坐标按先横后纵填写，(5/2,3)是另一个点。对y=−(x+1)²+4，顶点(−1,4)，纵轴截距却是3，正好可核查“c就是顶点高度”的误解。

工作台的距离d只改变示意的等距点，改变它应保留当前答案；改变a、h或k则改变函数，需要重新预测。a=0时函数退化为常数，所有输入输出相同，不存在唯一抛物线顶点，也不能把某个任意h宣布为唯一对称轴。家长让孩子分别指出“点”“线”“输入”“输出”，能帮助后续区分最值与达到它的时刻。

# Vertex and axis of symmetry

## Make the squared term zero

For y=a(x−h)²+k with a≠0, the vertex is (h,k) and the axis is vertical x=h. A vertex is an ordered pair; an axis is a line. Positive a gives unrestricted minimum k, while negative a gives maximum k. Contextual domains will later require checking whether h is allowed.

The bracket subtracts h: x−2 vanishes at two, while x+3 means x−(−3) and vanishes at −3. Asking when the bracket is zero is more reliable than guessing from a visible sign. Parameters h,k move the position; a controls opening and output displacement.

### Example 1: read and verify symmetry

The function y=(x−2)²−1 has vertex (2,−1) and axis x=2. Inputs one and three are distance one from the axis and both output zero; zero and four are distance two and output three. Generally h−d and h+d produce brackets −d and d, whose squares both equal d². Their outputs therefore equal ad²+k for every d, explaining the whole symmetry.

For y=−2(x+3)²+5, the vertex is (−3,5), axis x=−3 and opening downward. Negative a does not reverse h again. At d=0 both illustrative inputs coincide at one position, the vertex. Changing the window may hide a point without changing its coordinate.

### Example 2: complete the standard form

Write 2x²−8x+3 as 2(x²−4x)+3. Add and subtract four inside to obtain 2[(x−2)²−4]+3=2(x−2)²−5. The vertex is (2,−5) and axis x=2. The inserted four is multiplied by outside two, so its compensation is eight, not four.

Alternatively h=−b/(2a)=8/4=2 and k=f(2)=8−16+3=−5. This formula abbreviates completing the square. The vertex height is not standard-form constant c=3: c is the y-intercept at x=0, while vertex height occurs at x=h. They coincide only in the appropriate special case h=0.

## Fractions and boundaries

For y=−(1/2)x²+3x−2, h=−3/(−1)=3 and k=−9/2+9−2=5/2, giving vertex (3,5/2). Retain exact fractions instead of estimating from pixels, and keep x before y. For y=−(x+1)²+4, vertex (−1,4) differs from its y-intercept three.

Changing illustrative distance d preserves the same function and prediction; changing a,h or k creates a new task. At a=0 the function is constant, with no unique parabola vertex or unique symmetry axis. A parent can ask the learner to identify the point, line, input and output separately, preparing for the distinction between an extremum and where it occurs.
