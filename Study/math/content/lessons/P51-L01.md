## 大数也要先保留运算关系

数变大以后，运算顺序没有改变。没有括号的同一层，先算乘除，再算加减；乘与除同级，加与减同级，各自按原式从左往右处理。不能因为最左数字最大，就从它开始把所有符号一路算过去。

每一行可以用已经算出的值替换一个完整部分，但要保留其他数与符号。替换后整行应仍与原表达式等值。把一段没算完的乘法拆成“先加一下再乘”，会改变它作用的数量关系。

### 例 1 · 先求乘积与商

1. 原式 **1200+36×25−480÷6**。
2. 乘积 **36×25=900**，商 **480÷6=80**。
3. 用两者替换，得到 **1200+900−80**；不是先做 1200+36。
4. 同级加减从左往右：**2100−80=2020**。
5. 乘回 **80×6=480**，也可由 **2020+80−900=1200** 回到开头量，核查局部与整体。

### 例 2 · 除后乘，不是先乘后除

1. 原式 **3600÷9×4+275**。
2. 除与乘同级，先做 **3600÷9=400**。
3. 再 **400×4=1600**，最后 **1600+275=1875**。
4. 不能擅自做 9×4 后让 3600 除以 36；那相当于新增括号。
5. 商四百的两个零属于位值：**9×400=3600**，不是把商写成四十。

### 位值、单位与核查

18×125 可以用 18×(100+25)=1800+450=2250，也可用 20×125−2×125=2500−250=2250。不同有效策略可以得到同一乘积，但每一步的关系都要说明。接着计算 5000−2250+120，得到 2870；乘积算好后，原式里的减号不会因为数字变大而消失。

零乘 875 是零，不是 875；所以 0×875+2400÷6=0+400=400。零因数与零除数不同：乘零有效，除零没有定义。本工作台的除法都选除数非零且整除的例子，不把有余量的普通商随意截成整数。

假想预算三千元，十八套每套一百二十五元，先求总价 2250 元，再求余钱 750 元。每一步的对象都是钱数；预算减“十八套”不是同单位关系。用余钱加总价恢复三千元，是情境核查，不只是再按一次同样算式。

工作台先保留完整原式，再逐行展开。你可以纸笔预测下一段，按钮只显示下一次等值替换。改变开头量会收起旧步骤，避免上一题的结果留在新数里。这里的步骤核对不代替练习中的独立作答与自己的解释。

**带走一个发现：** 我会先标需要计算的部分，每次只替换一个完整值，并保留其余关系与位值；核查不只看答案有没有“差不多大”。

## Retain operation relationships when numbers grow

Larger numbers do not change operation order. Within a level without brackets, multiplication and division precede addition and subtraction. Multiplication/division share a level, as do addition/subtraction, with equal-level operations handled left to right. A large leftmost number does not authorise calculating every symbol straight across.

Replace a complete calculated part by its value while retaining all other quantities and signs. Each resulting line should remain equivalent to the original expression. Adding into an unfinished product changes what multiplication acts upon.

### Example 1 · Find product and quotient first

1. Begin with **1200+36×25−480÷6**.
2. **36×25=900** and **480÷6=80**.
3. Substitute to obtain **1200+900−80**, without first calculating 1200+36.
4. Equal-level addition/subtraction gives **2100−80=2020**.
5. Check **80×6=480** and recover the opening quantity through **2020+80−900=1200**.

### Example 2 · Divide then multiply, not automatically multiply first

1. Begin with **3600÷9×4+275**.
2. Equal-level division/multiplication starts with **3600÷9=400**.
3. Then **400×4=1600**, followed by **1600+275=1875**.
4. Multiplying 9×4 first and dividing 3600 by 36 introduces brackets absent from the expression.
5. Retain the quotient’s zero places: **9×400=3600**, rather than writing forty.

### Place value, units and checks

For 18×125, use 18×(100+25)=1800+450=2250 or 20×125−2×125=2500−250=2250. Different valid strategies can give the same product when each relationship is justified. In 5000−2250+120, retain the subtraction to obtain 2870.

Zero times 875 is zero, so 0×875+2400÷6=400. Multiplying by zero differs from division by zero, which is undefined. Workshop divisions use nonzero divisors and exact whole quotients rather than truncate a non-whole result.

A fictional three-thousand-dollar budget buys eighteen sets at NZ$125 each. Cost is NZ$2250 and remaining money NZ$750. Subtracting eighteen sets directly from dollars mismatches units. Remaining plus spent money restores the budget and checks the story.

Predict the next part on paper before revealing one equivalent replacement. Changing the leading quantity clears earlier steps. Workshop inspection does not replace independent practice or your own explanation.

**Takeaway:** mark each complete part, substitute its value and retain relationships and place values. Check more than whether the answer looks approximately plausible.
