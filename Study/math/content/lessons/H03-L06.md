# 指数与对数模型

指数回答“过了这些步，变成多少”，对数回答“要达到这个量，需要几步”。一个计算值还不是完整情境答案：必须说明单位、输入范围、目标方向和模型假设。

## 一条核心想法

**先写模型，再反求时间，最后回到原情境核验。** 正值倍率模型N(t)=N₀aᵗ，N₀>0、a>0且a≠1。达到目标B>0时，先除初量，aᵗ=B/N₀，得t=log(B/N₀)/log a。这里两次log用同底，t的单位必须与倍率的时间步长一致。

若只按整天记录，首次达到不是四舍五入到最近整数；要找最早满足不等式的允许整数。增长通常问N≥B，衰减常问N≤B。因log a在衰减时为负，解不等式时除它要反向；用前一步和本步代回可以核验方向及边界。

### 例1：连续2.8天不等于第2天

虚构纸上模型N(t)=5×2ᵗ，t≥0以天计。达到35时，2ᵗ=7，t=log₂7≈2.8073549天，取最近0.1天是2.8天。计算器中间值先保留，最后才按所问精度舍入。

如果只问整天n=0,1,…首次至少35，第2天N(2)=20<35，第3天N(3)=40≥35，所以第3天。即使连续时间舍入到2.8，也不把第2天当作已经达到。目标若恰为40，连续交点就是3，首次整天仍是3，不额外加一天。目标初始已满足时，首次为0。

### 例2：衰减与时间单位

虚构存储量N(t)=80×(1/2)ᵗ，每天保留一半。连续达到11的时间log(11/80)/log(1/2)≈2.8625天，最近0.1天为2.9。仅整天读数时，第2天20>11，第3天10≤11，首次第3天。

若倍率是每小时1/2而非每天1/2，一天的倍率是(1/2)²⁴。不能把时间从小时改成天却保留同一个倍率。类似每年增长10%用1.1，不是0.1；100单位两年后为121，而非120。

## 阈值工作台

选择增长或衰减，先独立预测连续交点和首次整天，再显示六行数表。两种答案分开核验；改变目标会重置对应数学任务，显示表格保留输入。表格高亮首次，但解释仍须引用前一天和当天的原模型值，并用单调性排除所有更早天数。

# Exponential and logarithmic models

Exponentials answer how much remains after a given number of steps. Logarithms answer how many steps reach a quantity. A computed number becomes a contextual answer only after its unit, input domain, target direction and assumptions are stated.

## One central idea

**Specify the model, invert for time, then audit in the original context.** In the positive multiplier model N(t)=N₀aᵗ, require N₀>0, a>0 and a≠1. To reach positive target B, divide by the initial quantity: aᵗ=B/N₀, so t=log(B/N₀)/log a. Use the same logarithm base in numerator and denominator. The time unit must match the period attached to the multiplier.

For whole-day observations, the first crossing does not mean rounding to the nearest integer. Find the earliest admissible integer satisfying the target inequality. Growth typically asks for N≥B and decay for N≤B. Since log a is negative for decay, dividing an inequality by it reverses direction. Substituting both adjacent whole steps audits the direction and boundary.

### Example 1: A crossing at 2.8 days does not mean day two

The fictional paper model N(t)=5×2ᵗ uses t≥0 in days. Reaching 35 requires 2ᵗ=7, hence t=log₂7≈2.8073549 days, or 2.8 days to the nearest tenth. Retain intermediate calculator digits and round only the requested final result.

If observations occur only on whole days n=0,1,…, day two gives N(2)=20<35 and day three gives N(3)=40≥35. The first whole day is three. Rounding the continuous crossing to 2.8 never makes day two sufficient. For target 40, the continuous crossing is exactly three and the first whole day is also three, without an extra day. A target met initially has first day zero.

### Example 2: Decay and time units

A fictional storage quantity N(t)=80×(1/2)ᵗ retains half daily. Its continuous time to reach 11 is log(11/80)/log(1/2)≈2.8625 days, or 2.9 to the nearest tenth. For whole-day readings, day two gives 20>11 and day three gives 10≤11, so the first qualifying day is three.

If the multiplier were one half per hour instead, the daily multiplier would be (1/2)²⁴. Changing the time unit from hours to days cannot leave the multiplier unchanged. Similarly, 10% annual growth uses 1.1, not 0.1. Starting at 100 units gives 121 after two years, not 120.
