# 两步随机实验的树状图

## 第二步沿着第一步后的状态走

树状图把实验按步骤展开。每个第一步分支之后，单独检查袋内还剩什么，再写第二步分支。沿一条完整路径乘首步与条件概率，互斥路径相加得到事件概率。这个方法不要求第二步与第一步独立；相反，条件变化正是树要显示的信息。

本课每个球有不同编号，同样大小、混匀后每个现存编号等机会被取到。R、B 只记录红蓝颜色，颜色名称不是等权对象。放回时还要混匀，才恢复本课设定的原抽取机制；不放回则总数与被取颜色都减少。

### 例 1：两红一蓝，放回与不放回

编号 R1、R2、B1。抽两次不放回，首红概率 `2/3`；沿首红走后，剩一红一蓝，次红条件概率 `1/2`。两红概率 `2/3×1/2=1/3`，不能继续用原来的三分之二。

若抽蓝在先，袋里没有蓝了，BB 路径概率为零。树上可用虚线标这个不可能分支，但不能把它算进可发生的样本空间。完整可发生颜色路径是 RR、RB、BR，本例各三分之一，是具体数量带来的结果，不是颜色路径普遍等可能的定理。

若每次放回混匀，次步恢复红 `2/3`、蓝 `1/3`。RR、RB、BR、BB 概率依次为 `4/9、2/9、2/9、1/9`，总和一，明显不是各四分之一。

### 例 2：合并事件路径并用编号空间验算

三红一蓝不放回，不同色有两条互斥路径。RB 为 `3/4×1/3=1/4`；BR 为 `1/4×3/3=1/4`，相加得二分之一。第二条次红为一，因为抽走唯一蓝后全是红，不是继续写四分之三。

两红一蓝不放回时，编号空间有 R1-R2、R1-B1、R2-R1、R2-B1、B1-R1、B1-R2，六条等可能路径。RR 包含前者中两条不同编号路径，所以概率二除六。颜色合并后，不能把一条颜色路径当作一条等权编号结果。

放回时“至少一蓝”也可用补事件：`1−P(RR)=1−4/9=5/9`。或把 RB、BR、BB 相加，但要确保没有重复计数；“至少一”不是“恰一次”，BB 也符合。

## 树完整，不表示能猜中下一次

工作台改变红蓝个数、放回方式与事件时，会重新计算每条条件分支，清空旧任务。零概率分支明确标不可能，编号结果列表可展开核计数，所有叶概率总和一。更换条件时，分母和颜色数一起核，不能只把总数减一。

概率描述给定机制的可能性，不预告下一次一定结果。随机观察频率也可能偏离理论值，前面抽到某色不产生“欠另一色一次”的补偿规则。家长请孩子沿某条路径说出实物余量、每步分数和合并事件，理由比背一条乘法口诀重要。

# Tree diagrams for two-step experiments

## Follow the state after the first branch

A tree expands an experiment by steps. After each first branch, check what remains before writing second branches. Multiply first and conditional probabilities along a path, then add mutually exclusive paths for an event. This does not require independent steps: the tree makes dependence visible.

Each token has a distinct ID and is equally eligible among the remaining mixed tokens. R/B record colours rather than equal-weight objects. Replacement includes remixing to restore the stated selection mechanism; without replacement both total and drawn-colour count change.

### Example 1: two red and one blue

IDs are R1,R2,B1. Without replacement, first red has probability 2/3. After that branch one red and one blue remain, so conditional second red is 1/2. The product is 1/3, not 4/9 from incorrectly retaining the original chance.

If blue is first, no blue remains and BB has probability zero. A dashed impossible branch may be displayed, but it is not a possible sample-space outcome. RR,RB,BR each happen to have probability one third in this case; that is not a general equal-colour-path rule.

With replacement and remixing, second chances return to 2/3 red and 1/3 blue. RR,RB,BR,BB have probabilities 4/9,2/9,2/9,1/9, totalling one and not equal quarters.

### Example 2: add exclusive paths and verify with IDs

For three red and one blue without replacement, different colours comprise RB and BR. RB gives 3/4×1/3=1/4; BR gives 1/4×3/3=1/4. Add them to obtain one half. Conditional red after the only blue is removed is certain.

For two red and one blue without replacement, the ID space is R1-R2,R1-B1,R2-R1,R2-B1,B1-R1,B1-R2. These six ordered ID paths are equally likely. Colour RR aggregates two ID paths, so its chance is two sixths rather than one arbitrarily weighted colour outcome.

With replacement, at least one blue can be found as 1−P(RR)=5/9, or by adding RB,BR,BB. At least one differs from exactly one; BB belongs to the former. Do not count overlapping cases twice.

## Completeness does not predict the next draw

Changing counts, replacement or event updates conditional branches and clears the previous task. Zero branches are explicitly impossible, the ID list verifies counting and all leaf probabilities sum to one. Recheck both the denominator and the colour count after removal.

Probability describes possibilities under the mechanism, not the next realised result. Observed frequencies may deviate from theory, and past colours do not create a compensating debt. A parent asks for the actual remaining tokens, each conditional fraction and the event paths rather than only a memorised multiplication rule.
