# 换一种表示方式

语言、表格、图形和代数可以讲同一件事，但换表示必须保留变量、单位和定义域。本课把纸模活动的计价问题换几种表示，寻找更容易发现的结构。

## 先辨认横轴数什么

沿用每人3张、备用5张、库存4张、每包8张整包采购、容量20人的规则。正整数人数n对应k=⌈(3n+1)/8⌉包。方案A费用7+6k，方案B费用19+4k，单位均为虚构筹码。n=0取消，两费用都0；取消分支不沿用正活动固定费。

人数n与包数k是两个不同变量。人数变一次，包数未必变；费用对人数呈台阶。工作台人数图只画整数点，避免连线使14.5人看似是可实施输入。包数图中两条仿射关系容易比较，但只在整数包数处采购。当前图显示正活动可能出现的k=1…8，取消点另外说明。

### 例1：先解包数，再换回人数

令7+6k=19+4k，得到2k=12、k=6，费用43。这个横坐标是6包，并不是6人。要找对应人数，使用⌈(3n+1)/8⌉=6，当且仅当5<(3n+1)/8≤6。

所以40<3n+1≤48，得到13<n≤47/3。整数解是14、15。检查14人需求47、缺43张；15人需求50、缺46张；两者都要6包，均费用43。

若题问完整域0≤n≤20中费用相等的人数，还要加入n=0取消。故完整集合是{0,14,15}。只写{14,15}漏分支，只写{6}混淆变量。集合可以按任意顺序写，重复项不会增加一个新人数。

### 例2：表格揭示决策中的台阶

比较13、14、15、16人：缺口依次40、43、46、49张，包数依次5、6、6、7。A费用37、43、43、49，B费用39、43、43、47。正活动差B−A=12−2k，所以包数小于6时A更便宜，大于6时B更便宜。不能说“B每包便宜，所以总是便宜”，因为固定费不同。

若预算改51，A最多7包，仍最多18人；B最多8包，可供20人（需求65、缺61、8包），费用51且不超容量。B的预算材料上界其实可到21人，但容量20将其截断。只比较费用交点还不足以给出最终人数决策。

## 表示之间相互核验

文字写规则，公式概括规则，表格保留具体整数输入，图形突出趋势和相交。图上的重叠点可通过不同点形与精确表核验，不能靠像素猜测相等。横轴切换不改变同一费用命题的预测；显示核验也不清除已输入的思考。

包数图若延长到k=0会得到仿射截距7和19，但这不是本活动的取消费用0。更换问题假设后，可能出现正活动库存足够而k=0，此时仍须支付固定费；这与n=0又不同。出口说明需要连起这几种表示及分支，家长核验变量、取整区间、整数集合和结论范围，而不只核最后一个数。

# Switching representations

Words, tables, graphs and algebra can represent the same situation. A change of representation must retain variables, units and domain. This lesson uses the workshop prices to uncover structure that is easier to see in a different form.

## Identify the horizontal variable

Keep three cards per person, five spares, four stocked cards, whole packets of eight and capacity twenty. Positive whole attendance n requires k=⌈(3n+1)/8⌉ packets. A costs 7+6k; B costs 19+4k, in fictional toy credits. At n=0 both cancel for zero, outside the positive-event pricing branch.

Attendance n and packet count k are different variables. Adding a person need not add a packet, so attendance costs have steps. The attendance plot uses integer dots instead of suggesting that 14.5 people form an admissible event. Packet-cost lines expose affine relationships, implemented only at whole packet counts. The displayed positive-event packet domain is one through eight; cancellation is described separately.

### Example 1: Solve for packets, then translate to people

Equate 7+6k and 19+4k to obtain 2k=12, hence k=6 and equal cost 43. The intersection counts six packets, not six people. Translate back using the ceiling condition: ⌈(3n+1)/8⌉=6 exactly when 5<(3n+1)/8≤6.

Thus 40<3n+1≤48, or 13<n≤47/3. Whole solutions are fourteen and fifteen. Fourteen need 47 cards, shortage 43; fifteen need fifty, shortage 46. Both require six packets and both plans cost 43.

If the question asks for the complete equality set over 0≤n≤20, cancellation adds zero. The answer is {0,14,15}. Writing only {14,15} misses a branch; {6} confuses variables. Set order is immaterial and duplicate entries do not create new attendances.

### Example 2: Use a table for a discrete decision

At attendance thirteen, fourteen, fifteen and sixteen, shortages are forty, 43, 46 and 49. Packet counts are five, six, six and seven. A costs 37,43,43,49, while B costs 39,43,43,47. For positive events B−A=12−2k: A is cheaper below six packets and B above six. A lower per-packet charge alone does not make B always cheaper because the fees differ.

With budget 51, A permits seven packets and at most eighteen people. B permits eight packets and twenty people: demand 65, shortage 61, eight packets, cost 51. Its material/budget bound alone would allow twenty-one, but capacity limits the event to twenty. Finding a price intersection is therefore only one part of the final decision.

## Let representations check each other

Words state the rules, formulas summarise them, tables retain exact integer cases and plots expose trends and intersections. Different marker shapes and the exact table audit overlapping points; pixels alone do not prove equality. Switching the horizontal variable changes display, not the underlying prediction. Opening an audit also preserves entered thinking.

Extending the packet lines to k=0 would give intercepts seven and nineteen. Those are not the cancellation cost zero. Under changed stock assumptions a positive event might need no purchased packets and would still pay its fee; that case differs from zero attendance. An exit explanation must connect the representations and branches. Parent review checks variables, ceiling intervals, integer solution sets and scope, rather than just a final number.
