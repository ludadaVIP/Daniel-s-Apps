## 划去一些份，剩余份的大小不变

同分母分数减法以相同整体和相同分数单位为前提。从七个八分之一中划去三个八分之一，剩四个八分之一。分子相减，分母保留，因为划去操作没有把每一份重新切成别的大小。

要先辨认**原有量、去掉量、剩余量**。它们满足“原有量=去掉量+剩余量”。减法不能像加法那样任意交换先后。此单元用实物条研究非负差；若去掉量大于原有量，工作台会提示边界，不擅自调换两量或把结果改成零。负数将在后续课程研究。

### 例 1 · 从七份中去掉三份

1. 原有 **7/8**，去掉 **3/8**，整体标尺相同。
2. 每份都是 **1/8**，七份中划去三份，剩 **7−3=4** 份。
3. **7/8−3/8=4/8**，不是 4/0。
4. 两个八分之一合成一个四分之一，再成组，**4/8=1/2**；涂色端点相同。
5. 加回核查：**4/8+3/8=7/8**。约分只改变表示方式，不丢失任何剩余量。

### 例 2 · 原量超过一个整体

1. 有 **11/10 L** 果汁，倒出 **3/10 L**，求剩余量。
2. 原量是一个整升加十分之一升；仍用 **1/10 L** 作每份。
3. **11−3=8**，得到 **8/10 L**。
4. 约成 **4/5 L**。差小于原量，且非负，符合倒出的情境。
5. **8/10 L+3/10 L=11/10 L**，恢复原量；单位仍是升，不是毫升或米。

### 零差与整数原量

3/7 L−3/7 L=0 L。零表示水用完了，不表示分母变成零。0/7 与 0 表示同量；0/0 没有定义，不能作分数答案。若题目要求最简表示，可写 0。

一米彩带剪去 2/5 m：先把 1 m 写成 5/5 m，再求 5/5−2/5=3/5 m。把 1 直接与分子 2 相减，漏掉了一米共有五个五分之一。9/8−5/8=4/8=1/2，同样可以先完整表示超过一条的原量，再划去。

工作台的叉号明确表示划去，剩余区保留涂色；没有划去的空白格仍属于整体标尺。颜色辅助观察，叉号和已知量标签也表达关系。先预测剩下几份，再展开结果，最后用加回去的方法核查，而不是只重复按一次减法。

**带走一个发现：** 减去的是同单位份的数目。零差合法、零分母不合法；约分不改变剩余量。用剩余加去掉量恢复原有量。

## Removing pieces does not resize the remainder

Like-denominator subtraction requires the same whole and fraction-piece unit. Remove three eighths from seven eighths and four eighths remain. Subtract numerators but retain the denominator, because the remaining pieces have not been cut differently.

Identify the **original, removed and remaining amounts**: original=removed+remaining. Subtraction order matters. This unit’s material-strip model explores non-negative differences. If removal exceeds what is available, the workshop explains the boundary instead of swapping operands or forcing a zero result. Later courses introduce negative numbers.

### Example 1 · Remove three of seven pieces

1. Start with **7/8** and remove **3/8**, using one whole reference.
2. Every piece is **1/8**. Seven minus three leaves **4** pieces.
3. **7/8−3/8=4/8**, not 4/0.
4. Regroup eighths without moving the shaded endpoint: **4/8=1/2**.
5. Add back to check: **4/8+3/8=7/8**. Simplification changes representation, not the amount remaining.

### Example 2 · An original amount above one whole

1. There is **11/10 L** of juice; pour out **3/10 L**.
2. The original amount is one litre plus one tenth. Every piece remains **1/10 L**.
3. **11−3=8**, giving **8/10 L**.
4. Simplify to **4/5 L**. A non-negative difference below the original fits the story.
5. **8/10 L+3/10 L=11/10 L** restores the original. The unit remains litres.

### Zero differences and integer originals

3/7 L−3/7 L=0 L means the water has been used up; it does not create a zero denominator. 0/7 equals 0, whereas 0/0 is undefined. Write 0 when simplest representation is requested.

For one metre of ribbon with 2/5 m cut off, rewrite 1 m as 5/5 m. Then 5/5−2/5=3/5 m. Subtracting the numerator two directly from the integer one ignores the five fifths in a metre. Similarly, 9/8−5/8=4/8=1/2 retains the original above one strip before removal.

Crosses explicitly identify removed pieces in the workshop. Remaining shading and unused background preserve the whole reference. Labels and crosses convey relationships as well as colour. Predict the remainder before revealing it, then use addition to check rather than merely repeating subtraction.

**Takeaway:** subtract counts of equal-size pieces. A zero difference is valid; a zero denominator is not. Simplifying preserves the remainder. Add removed and remaining amounts to recover the original.
