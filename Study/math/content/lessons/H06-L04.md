# 空间距离与角

“从A到G有多远”可能在问沿棱走过的路程，也可能在问两点之间的最短距离。点到平面、点到直线又是别的问题。把所问对象说清，往往比立即套公式更重要。

## 一条核心想法

**用真正的垂足和正投影，把空间问题放进合适的直角三角形。** 两点距离用连接它们的线段；点到直线或平面用垂直距离；两条异面线之间用同时垂直两线的公垂线段。端点必须属于题设对象。

本课线面角取直线与其在平面上的正投影所成的锐角；当直线平行或垂直平面时还应分别处理0°、90°边界。本工作台参数全为正，使AG与底面的角严格在0°与90°之间；不把“所有线面角都锐”当通用结论。

### 例1：先在底面勾股，再到空间勾股

设A=(0,0,0)、B=(3,0,0)、C=(3,4,0)、G=(3,4,12)，并按长方体对应顶点命名。底面对角线AC=√(3²+4²)=5 cm。GC垂直整个底面，所以也垂直面内经过C的AC。

在直角三角形ACG中，AG=√(5²+12²)=13 cm。而A→B→C→G沿三棱走3+4+12=19 cm，不能把19当两点直线距离。点G到底面的垂足是C，故点面距离另为GC=12 cm，不是斜线AG的13。

AG在底面的正投影是AC，线面角θ=∠GAC，因此cosθ=AC/AG=5/13，sinθ=GC/AG=12/13。AG与竖直方向的锐角是它的余角；12/13可作那个角的余弦，不能不说明角就换用。

### 例2：点线距离与两异面线距离

G到完整直线AB的垂足是B，GB=√(4²+12²)=4√10 cm。若把AB上任一点写为P=(p,0,0)，则GP²=(p−3)²+4²+12²，最小值在p=3处取得，独立说明B的最短性。图上看着靠近的点不能替代这个条件。

AB与CG异面，BC连接B∈AB和C∈CG，并同时垂直两线，长度4 cm。再用任意P=(p,0,0)、Q=(3,4,q)核对：PQ²=(p−3)²+16+q²≥16，等号在P=B、Q=C时成立。于是4是两线距离，而非3、12或13。

这里只用长方体中的简单垂直关系与平方和说明最短性；一般空间向量计算留到后续课程。不要看到一个斜面的短边，就不查两端属于谁而称它为公垂线。

## 距离与角工作台

分别选择所求距离和所求角，三项预测是底面对角线、指定距离、指定角的余弦。高度改变保留底面对角线；换视角或展开表格保留所有预测。

紫线AG与青线AC构成线面角，若改研究AG与AB，青线随所问角改变，正确余弦改为3/13。金线指出当前最短线段，不能把它与角的两边混同。所有根式与分数按精确值核验，长度题只填数值、不附单位。

## 家长核对与迁移

出口比较19、13、12，要求各自说清对象和理由。家长可故意把线面角余弦写成12/13，让孩子指出这是正弦或余角的余弦。再让孩子换观察方向，解释为什么距离和余弦都不改变。组合体将继续用实际尺寸和面身份求量，不从空间投影图直接量面积。

# Spatial distances and angles

“How far from A to G?” may ask for an edge-route distance or a shortest point-point distance. Distances from a point to a plane or line ask still different questions. Identifying the requested objects often matters more than immediately substituting into a formula.

## One central idea

**Use actual perpendicular feet and orthogonal projections to find the right right-angled triangle.** Point-point distance uses the joining segment; point-line and point-plane distances use perpendicular segments. Skew-line distance uses a common perpendicular. Its endpoints must belong to the requested objects.

This lesson uses the acute angle between a line and its orthogonal projection for an oblique line-plane angle. Parallel and perpendicular cases require the0° and90° boundaries separately. Positive workbench dimensions keep AG strictly oblique; they do not justify saying all line-plane angles are acute.

### Example 1: Pythagoras in the base, then in space

Let A=(0,0,0),B=(3,0,0),C=(3,4,0),G=(3,4,12), with matching cuboid labels. The base diagonal is AC=√(3²+4²)=5 cm. Because GC is perpendicular to the base plane, it is perpendicular to AC through C.

In right triangle ACG, AG=√(5²+12²)=13 cm. The edge route A→B→C→G travels3+4+12=19 cm, which is not the straight point-point distance. The perpendicular foot from G to the base is C, so point-plane distance is separately GC=12 cm.

The orthogonal projection of AG onto the base is AC. Thus θ=∠GAC, cosθ=5/13 and sinθ=12/13. The acute angle with the vertical direction is complementary:12/13 is its cosine, not the cosine of the original line-plane angle. Name the angle before choosing a ratio.

### Example 2: Point-line and skew-line distances

The perpendicular foot from G to full line AB is B, giving GB=√(4²+12²)=4√10 cm. For any P=(p,0,0) on AB, GP²=(p−3)²+4²+12², minimised at p=3. This independently proves the shortest-point claim instead of judging by visual closeness.

Lines AB and CG are skew. Segment BC joins B on AB to C on CG and is perpendicular to both, with length4 cm. For arbitrary P=(p,0,0) and Q=(3,4,q), PQ²=(p−3)²+16+q²≥16. Equality occurs at B and C, proving the distance four rather than three, twelve or thirteen.

These arguments use simple cuboid perpendicularity and sums of squares; general spatial-vector methods come later. A visually short edge is not a common perpendicular unless its endpoint membership and both perpendicularities are established.

## Distance and angle workbench

Choose distance and angle targets separately. Predict the base diagonal, requested distance and requested angle cosine. Changing height preserves the base diagonal; camera changes and audit displays preserve all predictions.

Purple AG and teal AC define the line-plane angle. If the target changes to AG with AB, teal changes accordingly and the cosine becomes3/13. Gold identifies the current shortest segment, which should not be confused with the angle rays. Exact radicals and fractions are checked symbolically; length tasks ask for numerical values without unit suffixes.

## Parent check and transfer

The exit compares19,13 and12, naming each object and justification. Deliberately propose12/13 as the line-plane cosine and ask the learner to identify the sine or complementary-angle cosine. Change the camera and explain why distances and cosines remain fixed. Composite solids next use actual dimensions and face identities rather than measuring areas from a projected picture.
