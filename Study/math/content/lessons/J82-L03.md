# 完全平方分解

## 两端平方，中项也必须匹配

完全平方分解是把 `A²+2AB+B²` 写成 `(A+B)²`，或把 `A²−2AB+B²` 写成 `(A−B)²`。末项在两种情况都为正，平方括号的正负由中间项决定。不能只看到首尾平方就宣布整个三项式是平方。

先找所有项的整数公因子，再核三个系数。A 可能含有数系数，平方与二倍乘积都必须使用完整 A。写成平方表示同一个因式重复两次，也可写两个相同因式相乘，二者都应展开回查。

### 例 1：中项控制加号或减号

`x²+6x+9` 的两端是 x 与三的平方，中项 `2·x·3=6x`，所以等于 `(x+3)²`。若中项改成 −6x，则为 `(x−3)²`；末项仍是正九，不能写成平方差。

`x²+4x+9` 虽有相同首尾，四 x 却不等于正负六 x，因此不是这两个候选平方。不能把题目中项改成六来套公式。工作台只改变中项，正是让你看到三个条件需同时满足，而不是只认图片的两端。

### 例 2：复合首项与外部公因子

`4x²−12x+9` 中，A=2x、B=3。平方首项为四 x²，负二倍乘积为 `−2·2x·3=−12x`，所以结果 `(2x−3)²`。若写 `(4x−3)²`，首项会变成十六 x²。

对 `2x²+12x+18`，先提二，余 `x²+6x+9`，再写成 `2(x+3)²`。`(2x+6)(x+3)` 展开相同，但第一个非恒定因式的系数仍有公因数二，应移到外部。既要关系恒等，也要按本单元要求完成整数分解。

## 负号、重复因式与范围

`x²−2xy+y²=(x−y)²`，两个交叉项都是 −xy，末项由两个负 y 相乘得正 y²。`(y−x)²` 也是同一结果，因为两个因式同时改号，总乘积不变；只改一个因式的符号则会改变原式。

把 `(x−3)²` 展开为 `x²−3x−3x+9`，再合并，是对整个结构的检查。因式次序与平方写法可变化，所有展开系数仍须相同。一个 x 的数值吻合不足以证明；反例则足以否证错误主张。

正长度面积图可帮助解释两块交叉项；差平方减条图需 x>b>0，重减角块补一次。分解恒等关系不局限于这个图的长度范围。家长请孩子指出两个完整平方根、二倍乘积、符号与外部公因子，再解释展开为何还原所有项。

# Factoring perfect squares

## Square ends need a matching middle

Read A²+2AB+B²=(A+B)² or A²−2AB+B²=(A−B)² backwards. The final square is positive in both cases; the middle controls the sign inside the bracket. Square ends alone do not establish the pattern.

Extract common integer content first, then check all three coefficients. A may include a numerical coefficient, which belongs in both its square and twice-product term. A squared bracket represents two identical factors and may also be written as their product.

### Example 1: choose the sign from the middle

For x²+6x+9, the square roots of the ends are x and three and twice their product is 6x, giving (x+3)². With middle −6x, the result is (x−3)², still with positive final nine.

The expression x²+4x+9 has the same ends but its middle is neither +6x nor −6x. It is not either candidate square. Do not change the given coefficient to fit a formula. Varying only the workshop’s middle term demonstrates why all three conditions matter.

### Example 2: a composite first term and outside content

For 4x²−12x+9, take A=2x,B=3. The first square is 4x² and negative twice the full product is −12x, giving (2x−3)². Using 4x as the first whole term would instead give 16x².

For 2x²+12x+18, first extract two, then recognise x²+6x+9, giving 2(x+3)². The equivalent product (2x+6)(x+3) still leaves numerical content two in a nonconstant factor. Extract it to satisfy the unit’s completed integer-factor form as well as identity.

## Signs, repeated factors and domain

x²−2xy+y²=(x−y)² because both cross terms are −xy and the final product of two negative y terms is positive y². The form (y−x)² also works: both repeated factors change sign, preserving their product. Changing only one sign would change the source expression.

Expand (x−3)² as x²−3x−3x+9 and collect to check the whole structure. Factor order and power notation may vary while every expanded coefficient remains fixed. One matching input does not prove an identity, whereas a counterexample can disprove a proposal.

Area diagrams explain the two separate cross regions. The squared-difference removal picture requires x>b>0 and restores a doubly removed corner once. The factorisation identity is not confined to that picture’s domain. A parent asks for the full square-root terms, twice their product, signs, outside content and complete reverse expansion.
