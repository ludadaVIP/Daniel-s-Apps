## 倾斜改变外形，不一定改变面积

想象一个可以左右错移上边的框：底保持 6 cm，两条平行线间的垂直距离保持 4 cm。它越来越斜，覆盖的面积就越来越大吗？先预测，再沿高切拼。我们关心的是同底、同垂直高的覆盖量，不是斜边的长度。

平行四边形有两组对边平行。高是所选底与对面平行边之间的垂直距离。梯形这里先指只有一组对边平行的四边形；英文也可能遇到 trapezium。本课程沿规划使用 trapezoid，并在图里明确标出两条平行底，避免只靠名称猜图形。

### 例 1 · 把平行四边形补成矩形

1. 底 **6 cm**，垂直高 **4 cm**，斜边 5 cm。先指出哪个长度与底垂直。
2. 在上边错移不超过一个底宽的图中，沿垂线切下边角，平移到另一侧。
3. 切块没有缩放，拼合没有重叠或空缺。得到底 6 cm、高 4 cm 的矩形。
4. 原图面积 **6×4=24 cm²**。斜边 5 cm 不能代替高算成 30 cm²。
5. 反向把切块移回去，仍是相同的 24 cm²。这里一直只有一份图，因此不除二。

工作台允许上边向左或向右错移。格子保持相同单位，切块仅平移一个底宽。改变倾斜程度会改变部分边长和周长，但在底、高固定时不会改变面积。更大错移仍适用面积公式，本工作台只选择方便一次切拼的范围。

### 例 2 · 梯形的长底从哪里来

1. 梯形上底 **3 cm**、下底 **7 cm**、垂直高 **4 cm**。
2. 另做一份全等梯形，旋转半圈，与原图拼成平行四边形。
3. 新底由原来的两条底首尾接成，长 **3+7=10 cm**；高仍是 4 cm。
4. 继续切拼矩形，两份合计 **10×4=40 cm²**。
5. 原来一份面积 **(3+7)×4÷2=20 cm²**。核查：两份二十合为四十。

梯形面积 **A=(a+b)h÷2**。两底要相加，因为拼成新底的是两段长度；把两底相乘既没有对应这个矩形底，也漏掉垂直高。若两底是 5 cm、8 cm，高 3 cm，面积为 **13×3÷2=19.5 cm²**，半个平方单位需要保留。

把工作台两底调得相等，会成为平行四边形。按这里的狭义命名不再叫梯形，但同一面积关系变成 **(b+b)h÷2=bh**，仍然正确。名称边界不使数量关系突然失效。两底大小交换也不改变和；侧边可以不同斜度，高仍是平行线间垂直距离。

面积 35 cm²、高 5 cm 的平行四边形，底是 **35÷5=7 cm**，代回七乘五核查。遇到米和厘米先统一单位，再计算；平方单位只标在面积上。

**一起说清楚：** 每一步有一份还是两份？矩形底对应原图哪些线段？家长可让孩子沿线指出答案，再解释何时除二。出口题用新两底与高，不照抄工作台的结果。

## A slant changes shape without necessarily changing area

Imagine shearing a frame sideways while its base stays 6 cm and its perpendicular height stays 4 cm. Does becoming more slanted increase area? Predict, then use dissection. The relevant quantities are base and perpendicular separation, rather than slanted-side length.

A parallelogram has two pairs of parallel sides. Height is the perpendicular distance between the chosen base and its opposite parallel side. Here we first use trapezoid for a quadrilateral with exactly one parallel-side pair. You may also encounter the English name trapezium. This course follows the plan’s label trapezoid and identifies the parallel bases explicitly.

### Example 1 · Complete a rectangle

1. The parallelogram has base **6 cm**, perpendicular height **4 cm** and slanted side 5 cm. Identify the perpendicular measurement.
2. For a shear within one base width, cut a corner along a perpendicular and translate it to the opposite side.
3. The piece neither stretches nor overlaps another. The resulting rectangle has base 6 cm and height 4 cm.
4. Original area is **6×4=24 cm²**. Using slanted side 5 cm would incorrectly give 30 cm².
5. Moving the piece back preserves the same 24 cm². There was only one copy throughout, so no halving is required.

The workshop supports shearing left or right. Grid units stay fixed and a cut piece moves one base width. Side lengths and perimeter can change while base and height remain fixed. Larger shears still obey the area relationship; the workshop uses a range convenient for one dissection.

### Example 2 · Where the joined base comes from

1. A trapezoid has bases **3 cm** and **7 cm**, with perpendicular height **4 cm**.
2. Make a congruent copy, rotate it half a turn and join the copies into a parallelogram.
3. Its base joins the two original bases, giving **3+7=10 cm**. Height remains 4 cm.
4. Rearrange into a rectangle. The pair’s total area is **10×4=40 cm²**.
5. One original copy has **(3+7)×4÷2=20 cm²**. Check that two twenties make forty.

Therefore **A=(a+b)h÷2**. Add bases because two lengths join end to end. Multiplying them does not describe the new rectangle and leaves out height. With bases 5 cm and 8 cm and height 3 cm, area is **13×3÷2=19.5 cm²**; retain the half square.

Equal parallel bases produce a parallelogram. Under the definition used here, it is no longer called a trapezoid, but the relationship becomes **(b+b)h÷2=bh** and still works. A naming boundary does not invalidate a quantity relationship. Exchanging the two base lengths also leaves their sum unchanged.

For a parallelogram of area 35 cm² and height 5 cm, base is **35÷5=7 cm**. Substitute to check. Align metres and centimetres before calculating, and reserve square units for area.

**Explain together:** identify whether each stage contains one copy or two, and trace the original segments forming the rectangle’s base. A parent can ask when halving is justified. Use new measurements for the exit ticket, with a reason for the joined-base formula.
