## 先确定结果和条件，再说机会

本课讨论明确的**非空有限模型**。没有任何符合结果的事件叫不可能，概率零；覆盖所有结果的事件叫必然，概率一；既有符合又有不符合结果，叫可能但不必然。可能不是“下一次一定会发生”，也不是“所有名称机会相同”。

等可能需要依据。理想均匀角度转盘中，一个扇区的机会与它的角度成比例；只有等角扇区才有相同机会。若相同字母出现几次，应把它包含的各扇区合并，不能只数不同字母有几种。

### 例 1 · 三种字母不必各三分之一

1. 理想转盘 A 占半圆，B、C 各占四分之一。
2. 均匀抽取角度时，**P(A)=1/2**，**P(B)=P(C)=1/4**。
3. A、B、C 虽然是三个名称，却不是三个等可能事件。
4. 这个转盘没有 D，故 P(D)=0；A 或 B 或 C 覆盖整盘，概率一。
5. A 既占了一部分也没有覆盖全部，属于可能但不必然，而非必然。

这里假设理想抽角，不是看到纸上画了圆就证明实际手拨转盘公平。轴的偏差、摩擦或操作方式会影响实物；若没有明确机制和证据，不能只凭名称宣布等可能。工作台的条件是明示的数学模型。

### 例 2 · 等扇区与等字母是两层判断

1. 把转盘等分四份，标 **A、A、B、C**。
2. 四个编号扇区各有 **1/4** 机会。
3. 事件 A 包含两份，所以 **P(A)=2/4=1/2**。
4. B 和 C 各一份，分别为 1/4。
5. 因而“基本扇区等可能”成立，“不同字母等可能”不成立。

换成 A、B、C 等分三份，三个字母各三分之一才有依据。切换结构时，要重新核对扇区与字母合并，不能把上一个模型的机会照搬。图中用字母和 S 编号共同区分，不只依靠颜色。

概率一半不安排每两次必须一次的配额。即使理想独立重复，AA、AB 等短序列都可能出现；过去连续 A 并不迫使下一次非 A。下一课先算单次事件比例，末课再看真实生成的模拟记录，不能提前把理论比例硬塞进记录。

家长请孩子先指出“哪些基本结果才等可能，为什么”，再让他圈出事件包含的全部结果。判断必然要覆盖整个结果集合；判断不可能要在已给模型中没有符合结果，不能把“少见”当“不可能”。带走一个发现：**结果名称不保证等可能，扇区大小和随机机制才给依据；事件要合并基本结果，可能不等于必然。**

## Identify outcomes and assumptions before judging chance

This lesson uses explicit **nonempty finite models**. An event with no matching outcomes is impossible, with probability zero. An event containing all outcomes is certain, with probability one. An event with matching and nonmatching outcomes is possible but uncertain. Possibility neither determines the next result nor makes all names equally likely.

Equal likelihood needs a reason. Under ideal uniform-angle sampling, a spinner sector’s chance is proportional to its angle; equal angles give equal sector chances. Combine repeated labels to find a letter event rather than counting distinct names.

### Example 1 · Three letters need not mean three equal chances

1. A occupies half an ideal spinner, while B and C occupy one quarter each.
2. Uniform angle sampling gives **P(A)=1/2** and **P(B)=P(C)=1/4**.
3. Three names do not create three equally likely events.
4. D is absent, so P(D)=0. A or B or C covers the whole spinner, giving probability one.
5. A occupies part but not all of the spinner, so it is possible but uncertain.

The ideal angle assumption does not prove a physical hand-spun device fair. Axle bias, friction and technique may matter. The workshop states its mathematical mechanism instead of inferring fairness merely from a drawing.

### Example 2 · Equal sectors differ from equal letter events

1. Four equal sectors are labelled **A,A,B,C**.
2. Each numbered sector has chance **1/4**.
3. Event A contains two sectors: **P(A)=2/4=1/2**.
4. B and C each have probability 1/4.
5. Elementary sectors are equally likely, but distinct letter events are not.

For equal thirds A,B,C, equal letter probabilities one third are justified. Changing structure requires recounting and regrouping; do not copy chances from the preceding model. Letters and S identifiers supplement colours in the diagram.

Probability one half does not allocate exactly one success to every pair of trials. In independent ideal repetition, short sequences can vary, and earlier A outcomes do not force a non-A next. Later lessons distinguish the single-trial model from a generated simulation record. A parent can ask which elementary outcomes are equally likely and why, then request the complete set included in an event. Certainty requires every outcome; impossibility requires none within the stated model, rather than mere rarity. The takeaway bases chance on a mechanism and sector sizes, combines elementary outcomes into events and distinguishes possibility from certainty.
