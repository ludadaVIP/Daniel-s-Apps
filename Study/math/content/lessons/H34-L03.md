# 置信区间的直觉

点估计只有一个数，区间估计还报告与抽样波动相容的一段数值。置信区间的含义属于一个重复产生区间的方法：固定总体参数，重复抽样时区间变化，方法在其条件成立时按规定比例覆盖真参数。

## 本课另外指定一个模型

这里不再是上一课六个固定编号的不放回问题。另设n条独立同分布正态观测，总体标准差σ确实已知。样本均值的标准误为σ/√n，给定临界值z*，构造x̄±z*σ/√n。

已知σ是一条重要假设，不能把同一小样本估计的s直接塞进去就声称保留完全相同的正态覆盖。未知σ、依赖、选择偏差或有限总体设计需要另外检查方法。区间用于总体均值，不是预测单个新观测。

### 例1：构造区间并检查宽度

给定观测均值10分钟、已知σ4分钟、n16、z*=2。SE=4/√16=1分钟，误差界2×1=2分钟，闭区间[8,12]。

若均值仍10、σ仍4、z*仍2，但n改64，则SE=1/2、误差界1，区间[9,11]。样本量增至四倍才使此标准误减半，而不是增加一倍就减半。

若同一数据改用更大的z*，区间会更宽、理论覆盖更高，但精度也更低。不能只凭“更宽”就说方法全面更好；设计是否有偏、模型是否适用比宽窄本身更基本。

### 例2：固定参数，重复不同区间

假设演示真μ10，重复生成各组n16的原始正态观测，每组算均值再构造区间。有些均值偏高、有些偏低，区间一起移动。真μ不会为了落入区间而移动。

z*=2时理论覆盖为P(−2≤Z≤2)=2Φ(2)−1，约0.9545，即95.45%，不是精确95%。z*=1.96给覆盖约95%。模拟10组可能全中或有几组未中，不能把轨迹硬调成固定命中比例，也不能靠一次命中或失手证明方法好坏。

区间已经观察得到后，样本与端点都固定。频率解释并不直接赋予固定μ“有95%概率在这个已得区间里”；若要对参数建立概率分布，需要另述模型与推断框架。也不能改说95%个体会落在均值区间内，那是不同问题。

## 审核重复过程

工作台把单个给定观测区间与重复覆盖演示分开。工作台选平方样本量便于精确手算，公式本身不要求n为平方数。给定均值用于预测SE和闭区间；演示真值只用于检查命中，不参与现实区间计算。每次模拟实际生成n条观测，核验表可看第一组原始值和全部R组的区间与覆盖，而不是把n与重复数R混成一个控制。

图只画前12条区间，覆盖计数使用全部R组；实线覆盖、虚线未覆盖，颜色之外也有线型区别。显示的原始数值有舍入标记，覆盖判定用未先舍入的计算值。零组时命中率未定义。

公式与频率解释核对[NIST置信区间说明](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section1/prc14.htm)。出口报告要一起说明置信水平、样本量、σ来源、模型条件及区间范围，不把区间当作消除所有不确定性的保证。

# Intuition of confidence intervals

An interval estimate reports a range together with a procedure. Its frequentist confidence level concerns repeated random intervals covering a fixed population parameter, under the procedure’s assumptions.

## Specify a different model here

This is not the preceding six-unit without-replacement experiment. Assume n iid normal observations with genuinely known population SD σ. The sample mean has SE=σ/√n, and a supplied critical value z* gives x̄±z*SE.

Known σ matters. Replacing it by an estimate from a small sample does not automatically retain the same normal coverage. Unknown scale, dependence, biased selection or a finite-population design needs another method check. A confidence interval for the mean is not an individual prediction interval.

### Example 1: Build an interval and track its width

With observed mean ten minutes, known σ=4 minutes, n=16 and z*=2, SE is one minute and the margin two. The closed interval is [8, 12].

Keep the same mean, σ and critical value but use n=64. SE becomes one-half, margin one, and the interval [9, 11]. Multiplying n by four halves this SE; merely doubling n does not.

Increasing z* on the same data widens the interval and raises theoretical coverage, while reducing precision. Width alone neither certifies overall quality nor repairs a biased design or invalid model.

### Example 2: Repeat intervals around a fixed truth

Set demonstration truth μ=10. Generate new groups of sixteen original normal observations, calculate each mean and construct its interval. High and low sample means move their intervals; μ itself stays fixed.

For z*=2, coverage is 2Φ(2)−1≈0.9545, or 95.45%, rather than exactly 95%. Using 1.96 gives approximately 95% coverage. Ten simulated intervals may all hit or several miss. Do not alter a trace to force a nominal hit count, or judge a general method from one hit or miss.

After observing an interval, its endpoints are fixed. The frequentist coverage statement does not directly assign a 95% probability to the fixed μ lying inside that realised interval. A parameter probability distribution requires another stated inferential model. Nor does a mean interval contain 95% of individual observations.

## Audit the repeated procedure

The workbench separates a supplied observed-mean task from the coverage simulation. Square sample sizes keep the workbench arithmetic exact; the formula itself does not require square n. Revealed truth is used only to check simulated hits, not to construct the observed interval. Each repetition generates n actual normal observations, with the first raw sample and all R intervals available for inspection.

Only twelve intervals are drawn, while counts use all R repetitions. Solid and dashed lines distinguish hits and misses without relying only on colour. Display values are rounded and labelled; coverage uses unrounded calculations. Zero repetitions give an undefined hit rate.

The formula and repeated-coverage interpretation were checked against the [NIST confidence-interval reference](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div 898/handbook/prc/section 1/prc 14.htm). Report confidence level, n, σ information, assumptions and endpoints together rather than presenting the interval as an unconditional guarantee.
