## 整数表示组数，小数表示每组多少

一个小数乘非负整数，可以看成相同小数量的重复组。**整数是组数，小数是每组量**。计算后仍要保留原来的计量单位。

小数点位置不是随便决定的：把每组量改用十分之一、百分之一或千分之一计数，整数乘法算出多少个这样的单位，再写回小数。

### 例 1 · 0.325 × 4

1. 每组有 325 个千分之一，4 组是 325 × 4 = 1,300 个千分之一。
2. 1,000 个千分之一是一个整体；剩下 300 个千分之一。
3. 所以 **0.325 × 4 = 1.300 = 1.3**。
4. 检查：0.325 四次相加也得 1.300。四组比一组大，0.13 显然不合理。

### 例 2 · 1.25 × 6

1. 1.25 = 125 个百分之一。
2. 125 × 6 = 750 个百分之一。
3. 750/100 = **7.50 = 7.5**。
4. 拆分也能检查：6 个 1 加 6 个 0.25，得到 6 + 1.5 = 7.5。

### 零组与整数结果

2.306 × 0 表示没有任何一组，所以总量为 **0**。不要因为原来每组量很大就保留原数。

乘积也可以刚好是整数。例如 0.375 × 8 = 3.000 = **3**。不能因为原题是小数就坚持答案必须还有非零小数位。

生活题中，0.125 升牛奶做 4 次相同配方，需要 0.125×4 = **0.5 升**。这里的 4 是次数，不是额外的 4 升；0.125+4 不对应这个情境。

探索器把相同大小的组画在一起。先预测总量大概在哪，再观察“每组多少 × 几组”。显示的整数乘法是在计小单位，最终读回小数时必须把单位解释清楚。

**小检查：** 0.6 × 4 为什么是 2.4，而不是 0.24？不要只说“移动小数点”。

## The whole number counts groups; the decimal gives each amount

Multiplying a decimal by a non-negative whole number can be modelled as repeated equal groups. **The whole number is the group count; the decimal is the amount per group.** Retain the original measurement unit in the result.

The decimal point is not placed by guessing. Count the amount in tenths, hundredths or thousandths, perform whole-number multiplication on those units, then express the result as a decimal.

### Example 1 · 0.325 × 4

1. Each group is 325 thousandths. Four groups total 325 × 4 = 1,300 thousandths.
2. One thousand thousandths make one whole; three hundred thousandths remain.
3. Thus **0.325 × 4 = 1.300 = 1.3**.
4. Adding 0.325 four times gives the same total. Four positive groups exceed one group, so 0.13 would be unreasonable.

### Example 2 · 1.25 × 6

1. 1.25 is 125 hundredths.
2. 125 × 6 = 750 hundredths.
3. 750/100 = **7.50 = 7.5**.
4. Check by splitting: six ones plus six lots of 0.25 give 6 + 1.5 = 7.5.

### Zero groups and whole-number results

2.306 × 0 means there are no groups, so the total is **zero**. The amount per group does not remain when the group count is zero.

A product can also be a whole number: 0.375 × 8 = 3.000 = **3**. Decimal inputs do not require a result with non-zero decimal digits.

Four baking batches needing 0.125 litre of milk each require 0.125×4 = **0.5 litre**. Four is a batch count, not an extra four litres; 0.125+4 does not model this situation.

The explorer shows repeated groups of the same size. Estimate the total first, then inspect “amount per group × group count”. Its whole-number multiplication counts small units, whose meaning must be retained when reading the decimal result.
