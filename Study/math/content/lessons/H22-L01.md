# 直线方程的多种形式

同一条直线可以有不同方程。判断是否同一条线，要看它们描述的**点集**，不能只看写法相同。本课用已知点和方向选形式，并保留竖直线。

## 先有点与方向，再选形式

经过P₀(x₀,y₀)且斜率为m的非竖直线，可写点斜式y−y₀=m(x−x₀)。移项得到斜截式y=mx+b，其中b=y₀−mx₀。m是纵向改变量除以横向改变量；横向改变量必须非零，才可用这个有限斜率。

一般式Ax+By+C=0要求A、B不同时为0。向量(A,B)是法向量，沿直线的方向可取(B,−A)，两者点积AB−AB=0。B≠0时m=−A/B；B=0时A≠0，方程是x=−C/A的竖直线，斜率无定义。A=0、B≠0则为水平线，斜率0。

移项、展开和两边乘同一个非零常数不改点集。乘0会把条件抹掉，不能作为等价变形。一般式中的0x+0y+0=0含整个平面，0x+0y+1=0没有点，二者均不是一条直线。

### 例1：同一点与斜率，三种写法

经过(1,3)，斜率2：y−3=2(x−1)。展开得y=2x+1，再移项得2x−y+1=0。代入(1,3)，三式均满足；沿方向(1,2)移动也保留关系。法向量(2,−1)与(1,2)点积0。

−4x+2y−2=0是最后一式的−2倍，仍为同一直线。y=2x+3却不是：代(1,3)给3≠5。相同斜率只保证平行或重合，还要核对所给点。

### 例2：竖直线与截距式的条件

两点(2,−1)、(2,4)横坐标相同。它们决定x=2，任意实数y均可。Δy/Δx=5/0无定义，不能写“斜率无限大”再当普通实数代公式；也不能写成水平线y=2。

另一条线经过(2,0)、(0,3)，两个非零截距分别为2、3，可写x/2+y/3=1，等价于3x+2y=6。截距式需要两个有限非零截距。过原点的y=2x不能把截距0放进分母；竖直线x=2没有唯一纵截距，仍用一般式处理。

## 工作台与解释任务

工作台先给P₀与方向(B,−A)，选择斜线、竖直线、水平线或非零倍数表示。先写方程，再预测斜率或undefined。方程检查比较两边差的多项式是否成同一个非零常数倍，支持合理的移项与展开；不接受含变量分母的式子，因为除法可能排除本来应保留的点。

图窗只显示直线的一部分，不能把它误读为有端点的线段。改变图窗保留答案；换直线重做相关预测。精确坐标和方程在核验表中，图形只用于连接点与方向。

出口同时构造斜线与竖直线，须代点并解释形式条件。家长追问“固定的是哪个坐标？”、“斜率0与无定义哪里不同？”下一课沿法向量寻找真正最短距离。

# Forms of a line equation

Different equations can describe the same line. Compare their **sets of points**, rather than their appearance. Start with a point and direction, then choose a form that includes the relevant cases.

## A point and direction before a formula

A nonvertical line through P₀(x₀,y₀) with slope m has point-slope form y−y₀=m(x−x₀). Rearrangement gives y=mx+b with b=y₀−mx₀. Slope divides vertical change by horizontal change; the horizontal change must be nonzero.

General form Ax+By+C=0 requires A and B not both zero. Vector (A,B) is normal to the line; (B,−A) is a direction along it, since their dot product is zero. When B≠0, m=−A/B. When B=0 and A≠0, the line is vertical, x=−C/A, with undefined slope. A=0 and B≠0 gives a horizontal line with slope zero.

Rearranging, expanding and multiplying both sides by the same nonzero constant preserve the point set. Multiplication by zero erases the condition. Equation 0x+0y+0=0 describes the whole plane; 0x+0y+1=0 describes no points. Neither is one line.

### Example 1: Three forms from one point and slope

Through (1,3) with slope two, write y−3=2(x−1). Expand to y=2x+1 and rearrange to 2x−y+1=0. Substitution verifies (1,3) in each form. Moving in direction (1,2) preserves the relation; normal (2,−1) has zero dot product with that direction.

Equation −4x+2y−2=0 is −2 times the last form and describes the same line. Equation y=2x+3 does not: at (1,3) it gives 3≠5. Equal slope allows parallel lines; the supplied point still matters.

### Example 2: Vertical lines and intercept-form conditions

Points (2,−1) and (2,4) share x-coordinate two, giving x=2 for arbitrary real y. Slope would require 5/0, which is undefined. Do not treat an infinite slope as an ordinary real coefficient, or confuse x=2 with the horizontal line y=2.

A different line passes through (2,0) and (0,3). Its finite nonzero intercepts give x/2+y/3=1, equivalently 3x+2y=6. Intercept form needs both intercepts finite and nonzero. For y=2x, a zero intercept cannot be a denominator. A vertical line has no unique y-intercept; use general form.

## Workbench and reasoning

The workbench gives P₀ and direction (B,−A), including slanted, vertical and horizontal examples and a scaled equation. Write an equation before predicting slope or undefined. The checker compares polynomial differences up to a nonzero constant. It accepts rearrangement and expansion, but excludes variable denominators that could remove valid points.

The viewing window shows only part of an infinite line. It does not create endpoints. Changing the window preserves predictions; changing the line requires a new relevant prediction. The audit gives exact coordinates and equations.

The exit constructs both a slanted and a vertical line, checks their points and explains why forms have conditions. Ask which coordinate is fixed, and how zero slope differs from undefined slope. The next lesson uses a normal direction to locate shortest distance.
