# 绝对值不等式

|x−2|是数轴上x到2的距离。距离不到3的点在两边界之间；距离至少3的点跑到两侧。集合的“且”“或”在这里不再只是圆圈游戏，而决定解集是一段还是两段。

## 一条核心想法

**先读绝对值的非负性，再把距离条件翻译成完整解集。** 对c>0：|u|<c等价于−c<u<c，两条件同时成立，是交集；|u|>c等价于u<−c或u>c，是两条射线的并集。≤与≥分别在相应边界包含等号。

本课x取全体实数，图上的视窗只是展示范围，不是定义域。空心圆不含端点，实心圆含端点；箭头表示继续超出视窗。区间如(−1,5)不含端点，[-1,5]包含端点，无穷远从不是可以包含的数。

### 例1：同中心，两种距离条件

解|x−2|<3：写−3<x−2<3，三部分同加2，得−1<x<5。两端距离恰为3，不满足“不到3”。代回中心x=2，距离0，满足；代回−1或5，距离3，不满足。

解|x−2|≥3：写x−2≤−3或x−2≥3，得到x≤−1或x≥5，即(−∞,−1]∪[5,∞)。不能把或换成且，否则要求同一个数同时在两侧，会误得空集。只写x≥5漏掉整条左射线，也不是完整答案。

### 例2：系数、负阈值与退化

解|2x−1|≤3：−3≤2x−1≤3，加1得−2≤2x≤4，再除正数2，得−1≤x≤2。若系数为负，除法时两个不等号均反向，随后按从小到大重排；例如|−2x+1|≤3有同样解集。

不要盲目套公式：|x+1|<−2无解，因为绝对值≥0；|x+1|≥−2对所有实数成立。阈值为0时，|x+1|≤0只允许x=−1，|x+1|>0却是x<−1或x>−1；|x+1|≥0为全体，<0为空。若a=0，先化成常数比较，例如|0x+2|≤2恒真，所有实数都满足，不能除以0求一个“边界”。

## 预测、代回、检查完整性

工作台控制|ax+b|与阈值c，可用四种不等号。先写解集，再显示数轴；仅改变视窗不改变解集，也不清除原预测。切换a、b、c或不等号才需要重算。遇到a=0或负阈值，先说为什么恒真或恒假。

再用候选x代回，特别检查边界、中心和两侧。一个数满足能证实它是解，却不能证明你的解集完整；需要等价变形或穷尽情况。输入接受单区间、两个区间的并集、带且或的界限、空集∅、全体实数ℝ；不要把分号当或，分号在这里表示同时成立。

## 收尾与迁移

虚构传感器读数x与目标2的偏差不超过3，对应|x−2|≤3；若题目另给读数非负，还需与x≥0取交集。这是数学练习而非真实仪器标准。家长核对孩子能否从“偏差”“超过”“不超过”说明端点和且/或，而不只是背口诀。回到集合语言，把距离、边界与完整解集连成同一个论证。

# Absolute-value inequalities

|x−2| is the distance from x to two on a number line. Points less than three units away lie between two boundaries. Points at least three units away lie on two outer sides. Intersection and union now decide whether the solution has one interval or two rays.

## One central idea

**Use nonnegativity first, then translate the distance condition into a complete solution set.** For c>0, |u|<c means −c<u<c: both bounds must hold, an intersection. For c>0, |u|>c means u<−c or u>c: a union of two rays. The non-strict versions include the appropriate boundary equalities.

The domain is all real numbers. A displayed window is only a view, not a domain restriction. Open circles exclude endpoints, filled circles include them, and arrows continue beyond the window. (−1,5) excludes its endpoints; [-1,5] includes them. Infinity is not a number that can be included as an endpoint.

### Example 1: Same center, different distance condition

Solve |x−2|<3. Translate to −3<x−2<3, then add two to all three parts: −1<x<5. The boundaries have distance exactly three and fail “less than three”. Substituting x=2 gives distance zero and succeeds; −1 and five give distance three and fail.

For |x−2|≥3, use x−2≤−3 or x−2≥3. Thus x≤−1 or x≥5, equivalently (−∞,−1]∪[5,∞). Replacing or with and demands that one number lie on both outer sides and incorrectly gives an empty set. Giving only x≥5 omits an entire ray and is incomplete.

### Example 2: Coefficients and exceptional thresholds

Solve |2x−1|≤3. Write −3≤2x−1≤3, add one to get −2≤2x≤4, then divide by positive two: −1≤x≤2. With a negative coefficient, dividing reverses both comparisons; rewrite the final bounds in increasing order. For example |−2x+1|≤3 has the same solution set.

Do not apply a memorized rule without checking its assumptions. |x+1|<−2 has no solutions because absolute value is nonnegative; |x+1|≥−2 holds for every real. At threshold zero, |x+1|≤0 leaves only x=−1, whereas |x+1|>0 gives x<−1 or x>−1. The ≥0 version is all reals and the <0 version empty. If a=0, compare constants first: |0x+2|≤2 is always true. Never divide by the zero coefficient to invent a boundary.

## Predict, substitute, then audit completeness

The workbench controls a,b,c and four comparison signs. Predict a complete set before revealing its line. Changing only the display window does not change the mathematics or clear your prediction. Changing the expression or sign requires recalculation. For zero coefficients or negative thresholds, explain why the comparison is always true or always false first.

Substitute candidate values, especially a boundary, center and both outer sides. A successful candidate proves that particular number is a solution, not that the entire set is complete. Use equivalent transformations or exhaustive cases. Accepted answers include intervals, unions, simultaneous bounds, ∅ and ℝ. A semicolon means and here; use or or ∪ for union.

## Transfer and parent review

A fictional sensor value x differing from target two by at most three satisfies |x−2|≤3. If a separate premise requires nonnegative readings, intersect with x≥0. This is a mathematics scenario, not an actual instrument standard. The parent checks whether the learner connects distance language, strictness, endpoints and and/or in one argument, rather than merely repeating a rule.
