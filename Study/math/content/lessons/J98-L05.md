# 综合项目：设计一个滑板坡道

## 先把设计变成明确的数学任务

本项目只做纸上虚构模型，不是实际施工图。侧面是题目给定的直角三角形，竖升h、水平距r、斜长s；宽w垂直于侧面，坡面是一块长方形。练习仅计这块坡面，不包括厚度、支撑、浪费或真实场地条件。费率NZ$60/m²与以下约束均为课堂设定，不代表真实价格或建设标准。

纸上要求h≥0.6 m，r≤2.4 m，坡度h/r≤25%，坡面面积≤3 m²，费用不超过给定预算。满足一条不等于满足全部，先统一cm与m，再分别列计算链：s²=h²+r²，A=sw，C=60A。坡度用水平r作分母，面积用斜长s作一边。

### 例 1：默认方案逐项核算

默认h=60 cm、r=240 cm、w=100 cm、预算NZ$150。换成3/5、12/5、1 m，s²=153/25，所以s=3√17/5≈2.473863 m。坡度(3/5)/(12/5)=1/4=25%，坡角约14.0°，两者单位与含义不同。

坡面A=s×1=3√17/5 m²，费用C=36√17≈148.4318，最后到分为NZ$148.43。高度与水平距恰满足边界，坡度等于上限；面积和费用也需独立核验。由于量为正，可比较A²=153/25≤9、C²=22032≤22500，得到精确约束判断，不先把s粗舍入为2.5而误把费用算150。

### 例 2：只增加宽度的近似方案

保留侧面，把w改为1.2 m。斜长s与坡度h/r都不变，但面积与费用乘1.2：A≈2.968636 m²仍小于3，C≈178.1182，即NZ$178.12，超过150预算。因此这不是满足全部约束的方案，不能因坡度仍25%就判全部合格。

反过来改宽0.8 m，侧面仍相同，面积与费用减为原来0.8倍。所有长度统一放大则不同：s乘k、坡度保持、面积和按面积费用乘k²，高度或水平范围还可能失效。报告要说清具体改了哪个量，不能只写“变大一些”。

## 报告保留精确值、近似与理由

先比较精确关系，最后才为显示或金额舍入。对正量使用平方比较可避免根式近似在边界引起错误；不能对任意带符号量未经分析就套同样办法。计算器给出一个小数，不代表所有前提、范围与约束已经核过。

工作台可分别预测全部约束、精确斜长和费用，再揭示逐项核验。只改宽度，斜长预测应保留；改变竖升或水平距则重新计算。显示缩放只改侧视图，不能改变真实尺寸或预算。论证草稿写数量、方法、全部约束、另一方案和模型局限。

# Capstone: design a ramp

## Define a mathematical design task

This is a fictional paper model, not a construction drawing. A right-triangle side is stipulated, with rise h, horizontal run r and incline s. Width w is perpendicular to that side, making one rectangular inclined surface. Exclude thickness, supports, waste and actual site conditions. NZ$60/m² and the constraints below are worksheet assumptions, not real prices or construction standards.

Require h≥0.6 m, r≤2.4 m, grade h/r≤25%, surface area≤3 m² and cost within the stated budget. Convert centimetres to metres before using s²=h²+r², A=sw and C=60A. Grade uses horizontal run; inclined area uses s. One passing constraint does not establish every constraint.

### Example 1: audit the default design

Rise 60 cm, run 240 cm and width 100 cm become 3/5,12/5,1 metres, with budget NZ$150. Then s²=153/25 and positive s=3√17/5≈2.473863 m. Grade is 1/4=25%, while angle≈14.0°: different quantities and units.

Area is 3√17/5 m² and cost 36√17≈148.4318, rounded finally to NZ$148.43. Height, run and grade meet their boundaries, while area and budget need separate checks. Positive quantities permit exact comparisons A²=153/25≤9 and C²=22032≤22500. Rounding s first to 2.5 would change the displayed cost to 150 unnecessarily.

### Example 2: a width-only near miss

Keep the side and increase width to 1.2 m. Incline and grade remain fixed, but area and cost multiply by 1.2. Area≈2.968636 m² still meets three, while cost≈178.1182 rounds to NZ$178.12 and exceeds 150. An unchanged 25% grade does not establish overall feasibility.

Width 0.8 instead scales area and cost down to 0.8 of their former values. Uniformly scaling all lengths is different: incline scales by k, area/cost by k² and grade remains fixed, while height or horizontal bounds may fail. Specify the actual changed quantity.

Retain exact relationships for comparisons and round only final displays or money. Squaring inequalities here is valid because all quantities are positive; it is not an unrestricted rule for signed values. A calculator decimal does not audit premises, domains or every constraint.
