# 勾股定理的拼图证明

## 直角是前提，平方表示面积

给定非退化直角三角形，两条直角边为 a、b，斜边为 c，勾股定理为 `a²+b²=c²`。斜边在直角对面，是最长边；不能把任意第三条边叫斜边。a、b、c 是长度，平方后是对应正方形面积，单位也从 cm 变为 cm²。

这个关系不说 c=a+b。非退化三角形两短边和必须大于长边，直接把长度相加反而落到退化边界。我们要用面积守恒证明一般正 a/b 的关系，而不是只验三四五等几个数。

### 例 1：同一个大正方形，同样四块

用四块直角边 a、b 的全等直角三角形拼在边长 a+b 的外正方形内。每块面积 ab/2，总面积 2ab。第一排法的中央空白有四条边，每条都是原三角形斜边 c。

还需证明中央是正方形，而非只有四边等长的菱形。原直角三角形两锐角 α+β=90°，中央每个内角补上相邻的 α/β 组成平角，所以中央内角为 180−90=90°。有四边 c、四个直角，中央面积才确实为 c²。

重排同样四块，既不放大、也不重叠或丢块。第二排法留下两个正方形，边长分别 a、b，面积 a²、b²。两个外框大小相同，减去同样四块，剩余面积相等，故 c²=a²+b²。编号让我们确认移动的是同样四块，不靠颜色数量猜测。

### 例 2：代数核验与长度开方

外正方形面积为 (a+b)²，减掉四块 ab/2，剩 `(a+b)²−2ab=a²+2ab+b²−2ab=a²+b²`。这与几何重排一致，也说明不是针对某一组整数的巧合。

当 a=2 cm、b=3 cm 时，外面积二十五，每块三，四块十二，空白面积十三 cm²。所以 c²=13 cm²；c 的长度不是十三 cm。记 √13 为平方等于十三的非负数，它是精确长度的写法，之后会学习怎样使用。三点六一只是近似，不等于 √13。

若 a=3、b=4，则 c²=25，c=5；若 a=5、b=12，则 c²=169，c=13。平方等式还允许代数上正负两个根，但边长取非负根；本课三角形的边严格为正。不要把负十三当作另一种允许长度。

## 拼图的证据是什么

工作台允许改变 a/b，保持每块为真正的直角三角形，两框使用同样的单位长度。主动重排后才能看到第二组空白；理由逐条揭示并可撤回。它展示构造，普遍结论仍来自直角、全等、无重叠和面积论证，不因滑杆只列一至五就把样本检验当证明。

家长先问“为什么中央有直角”，再问“减掉的是否同样四块”。如果孩子只说中间看起来是方形，要求指出 α+β 的角依据；若把二十五填作斜边，追问现在求的是面积还是长度。

# Visual proof of Pythagoras

## A right angle is the premise

For a nondegenerate right triangle with legs a,b and hypotenuse c, Pythagoras states a²+b²=c². The hypotenuse faces the right angle and is longest. Squared lengths represent square areas, changing units from cm to cm².

This does not say c=a+b, which would be a degenerate triangle boundary. We need a general area argument for positive a,b rather than a few numerical examples.

### Example 1: one outer square and the same four pieces

Place four congruent right triangles in a square of side a+b. Each has area ab/2, giving total 2ab. In the first arrangement, every central-gap edge is a hypotenuse of length c.

Equal edges alone could make a non-square rhombus. The triangle’s acute angles satisfy α+β=90°. Each central angle supplements adjacent α/β, giving 90°. Thus the gap is a square of area c².

Rearrange the same pieces without resizing, overlap or loss. Two remaining squares have sides a,b and total area a²+b². Equal outer squares minus the same pieces leave equal areas, proving the identity. Numbered pieces retain their identities independently of colour.

### Example 2: algebra and a length root

Subtracting the triangles gives (a+b)²-2ab=a²+b². This symbolic argument agrees with the construction for general legs.

For a=2 cm,b=3 cm, outer area twenty-five minus four triangles of area three leaves thirteen cm². Therefore c²=13, but c is not thirteen centimetres. The notation √13 means the nonnegative number whose square is thirteen, an exact length; 3.61 is only approximate.

For legs three and four, c²=25 gives c=5. For five and twelve, c²=169 gives c=13. Although an algebraic square equation has positive and negative roots, a length uses the nonnegative one; these nondegenerate triangle sides are positive.

## What establishes the proof?

Changing the workshop legs preserves genuine right triangles and a common unit scale. Reveal the second arrangement and step through reasons. The finite slider examples illustrate a general proof based on right angles, congruence, non-overlap and area, rather than replacing it with sampling.

A parent asks why the central angles are right and whether exactly the same four pieces were removed. Distinguish “looks square” from the angle argument and area from length.
