## 比较时先对齐单位，不是数写了几位

小数的每一位代表固定大小的单位。把小数点对齐，从整数部分开始，再逐位比较。**第一次不同的高位决定大小**，后面的低位不能推翻它。

需要时可在末尾补零，写成相同位数；补零帮助看清位置，没有改变数值。

### 例 1 · 0.8、0.75 和 0.805

1. 写成 **0.800、0.750、0.805**。
2. 十分位分别是 8、7、8，0.750 先被排在后面。
3. 剩下两个的百分位都为 0；千分位是 0 与 5。
4. 因此 **0.750 < 0.800 < 0.805**。0.805 比 0.8 多五个千分之一，不只是“看着差不多”。

### 例 2 · 1.995 到最近的百分之一

1. 相邻的两个百分之一是 **1.99 与 2.00**。
2. 中点是 1.995，两边距离均为 0.005。
3. 本课对非负小数使用“正好一半取上边”的约定，结果是 **约 2.00**。
4. 进位跨过两个 9 到整数部分；不能写成 1.100。

### 精确比较与近似报告，是两件事

0.804 和 0.806 精确值不同，相差 0.002；到十分之一时，却都报告成“约 0.8”。舍入会丢掉部分信息，所以**同一个近似数不能证明精确值相等**。

1.24 km + 2.67 km 若先各自估到十分之一，得到 1.2 + 2.7 = **约 3.9 km**。精确合计是 3.91 km；两者接近，但应用时必须保留“约”和精度要求。

与整数舍入一样，直接从原数舍入到目标位置。不要先舍入到另一位置再舍入一次，也不要把逗号、位数长短或图上很小的距离当成精确判据。

探索器会把两数的小数位对齐，并指出第一次不同的位置。先从位值判断，再用数轴观察顺序；显示到某一位的舍入结果另外标为近似。

## Align units, rather than count digits

Each decimal place has a fixed unit size. Align decimal points, compare whole-number parts, then move right place by place. **The first differing higher place decides the order**; later places cannot reverse it.

Add trailing zeros if useful to align representations. The zeros clarify positions without changing values.

### Example 1 · 0.8, 0.75 and 0.805

1. Write **0.800, 0.750 and 0.805**.
2. Tenths are 8, 7 and 8, so 0.750 is smallest.
3. The remaining two have zero hundredths, but their thousandths are 0 and 5.
4. Therefore **0.750 < 0.800 < 0.805**. The difference between 0.805 and 0.8 is exactly five thousandths, not merely a visual guess.

### Example 2 · Round 1.995 to hundredths

1. The neighbouring hundredths are **1.99 and 2.00**.
2. The midpoint is 1.995, with distance 0.005 to each side.
3. This lesson uses half-up rounding for non-negative decimals, giving **about 2.00**.
4. Carrying crosses both 9s into the whole-number part. The result is not 1.100.

### Exact comparison and approximate reporting differ

0.804 and 0.806 differ by 0.002, but both round to about 0.8 at tenths. Rounding loses information, so **matching approximations cannot prove exact equality**.

For 1.24 km + 2.67 km, first rounding each to tenths gives 1.2 + 2.7, or **about 3.9 km**. The exact sum is 3.91 km. They are close, but the report must retain “about” and the requested precision.

As with whole numbers, round directly from the original value to the requested place. Avoid double rounding. Digit length, commas and a tiny drawn distance are not exact comparison methods.

The explorer aligns the two numbers and identifies their first differing place. Use place value first and the number line to observe order. Rounded reports are shown separately as approximations.
