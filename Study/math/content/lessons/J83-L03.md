# 加减消元法

## 消掉一个字母，不是丢掉一条关系

等式两边同乘非零数，再把两条等式对应相加或相减，可以让一个字母的系数相消。必须处理左右全部项，不是只乘想消去的那一项。得到的一元关系与保留的一条原关系共同工作，才能恢复两个未知数。

更准确地说，保留第二式，用“第一式的非零倍数，加上第二式的任意倍数”替换第一式。可将第二式的倍数减回，再除非零倍数，还原原第一式。这解释了等价性，也解释为何不能把第一式系数取零，或把两式都扔掉只留合成式。

### 例 1：直接相加与保留式

对 2x+y=11、x−y=1，两式相加得 `3x=12`，同时保留 x−y=1。于是 x=4，回代得 y=3。代回两原式分别十一与一，都符合。

若只保留 3x=12，y 可以任意取；(4,0) 也会通过这一条，却不符合原组。消去 y 是为了先找 x，不表示原来关于 y 的条件消失。相减同样需要先核系数是否会抵消，不能看到两个 y 就任意删去。

### 例 2：倍乘整条等式

解 2x+3y=13、3x−2y=0。第一式乘二，得 `4x+6y=26`；第二式乘三，得 `9x−6y=0`。相加为 `13x=26`，保留一条可回代关系，得到 x=2、y=3。

三个地方都要留意：第一式右边十三也乘二；第二式中负二 y 也乘三；合并 x 系数是四加九为十三，不是二加三。只把 3y 改成 6y，却不改同式其余项，会改变关系而不是等价变形。

## 零倍数为何会丢信息

仍看 2x+y=11、x−y=1。若“零倍第一式加一倍第二式”替换第一式，又保留第二式，两条都变成 x−y=1。数对 (1,0) 通过新组，却使原第一式左值二而非十一；原约束已经丢失。

若合法消元产生 0=0，须保留另一式，通常是同线无穷多共同解；产生 0=非零则矛盾无解。不能因表面一个字母消失，就把这两类都当作同一结论，也不能除零求值。

工作台固定保留第二式，并展示每条倍数、替换后的两式和是否真正消去 y。某组倍数可能等价但未消去目标字母，这不是关系错误；零倍数导致不可还原则应拒绝。家长请孩子说明如何从新组恢复旧组，以及为何求一个字母后还要回代并核两条原式。

# Elimination method

## Eliminate a letter without discarding a relationship

Scale complete equations on both sides, then add or subtract corresponding sides so one variable’s coefficients cancel. Every term participates, not only the term to be eliminated. The new one-variable relation works together with a retained original equation.

Retain equation two and replace equation one by a nonzero multiple of one plus a multiple of two. Subtract the retained multiple back and divide by the nonzero multiplier to recover the original. This explains equivalence and why zeroing the replaced row’s contribution or retaining only one combined equation loses information.

### Example 1: direct addition and the retained equation

For 2x+y=11 and x−y=1, addition gives 3x=12. Retain x−y=1, solve x=4 and back-substitute y=3. Both originals then give eleven and one.

If only 3x=12 remains, any y is permitted: (4,0) passes that row but fails the original system. Eliminating y helps find x first; it does not remove the original condition involving y. Likewise, subtraction requires coefficients that actually cancel rather than arbitrary deletion of two visible y terms.

### Example 2: scale every term

For 2x+3y=13 and 3x−2y=0, scale the first equation by two to get 4x+6y=26 and the second by three to get 9x−6y=0. Addition yields 13x=26. Retain an equation for back-substitution, giving x=2,y=3.

The right-side thirteen must also be doubled; the negative y coefficient must be tripled; and the combined x coefficient is four plus nine, not two plus three. Altering only the chosen y term changes rather than preserves the equation.

## Why a zero multiplier loses information

For 2x+y=11 and x−y=1, replacing row one by zero times one plus one times two makes both rows x−y=1. The pair (1,0) passes the new system but fails the old first row. Its independent constraint was lost.

A legitimate elimination producing 0=0 still retains another equation, typically a coincident-line set of infinitely many solutions. A zero row with nonzero right side is a contradiction. Do not confuse these outcomes or divide by zero.

The workshop retains row two and displays the full replacement, checking reversibility separately from whether y was eliminated. Some multipliers preserve the system without cancelling the chosen variable; this is unfinished strategy rather than a false relationship. A parent asks how the original can be recovered and why back-substitution and both original checks remain necessary.
