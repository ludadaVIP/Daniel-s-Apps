# 分段函数与实际规则

“超过3小时的部分加价”和“超过3小时整件换价”不是同一条规则。分段函数不只是把多个式子排在一起，还要说明每个式子适用哪些输入，以及边界恰好归哪一段。本课金额均为虚构纸上费率，不表示任何现实商家的报价。

## 一条核心想法

**先按输入选分支，再使用该分支完整规则。** 总定义域应明确；各分支合起来无遗漏，同一输入也不应给两个冲突输出。通常用开闭端点分工，让每个边界恰好属于一段。若公式在交界碰巧给同样数值，也不改变题目写明的归属。

图像每段分别画。跳跃两端之间没有给定的中间值，就不要用斜线接起来。原域结束后，最后一个式子不自动无限延长。求“全部满足预算的输入”时，则每段解条件、与本段域相交，最后取并集，而不是只查几个点。

### 例1：先免费、后逐段累计

虚构停车模型t为小时，0≤t≤5：0≤t≤1时C=0；1<t≤3时C=2(t−1)；3<t≤5时C=4+3(t−3)，费用单位NZD。首小时免费，中间两小时每小时2，之后部分每小时3。

t=3属于中段，C=2(3−1)=4；t=4属于后段，前3小时累计4，再加1小时的3，得7。直接用3×4=12是把全部时间按最后费率重算；只写3则漏掉之前累计。预算4允许全部[0,3]，不是只有查询过的0、1、2、3几个点，也不是包含负时间的x≤3。超过5小时不在这个模型域内。

### 例2：整件档价与每开始一小时

虚构包裹按总重量m kg收整件费：0<m≤1时4；1<m≤3时7；3<m≤5时10。m=1属于第一段，收费4；m=1.5属于第二段，整件费7，不是先收4再加半公斤某个没有给出的费率。0 kg不在寄件域内。预算7允许(0,3]。

另一计时模型t=0收费0，0<t≤4每开始一小时收3：0<t≤1为3，1<t≤2为6，以此类推。1.5小时已经开始第二小时，收费6而非4.5；3小时9，3.5小时12。与停车累计的3小时4、3.5小时5.5比较，能看出整数边界归属和跳跃的不同。预算7只允许[0,2]，其中0必须保留。

## 工作台：一个输入与整个集合

改变查询点，观察当前命中分支，再预测收费；同时预测整个预算可行集合。换预算只重置集合预测，不改变固定查询的收费；换查询只重算收费，不清掉同一预算的完整集合；只改窗口则两者保留。这样能区分“一个值”与“整组允许输入”两种问题。

先读给定分支，不急着看图。特别试1、3、0以及刚超过边界的值。空心点不含，实心点包含；未给域内的输入用outside-domain，不能把它硬塞进最后一段。图线用于观察，分支定义才是端点和收费的依据。

## 迁移与家庭挑战

在纸上自拟两种虚构费率，写清总域、各段、单位、边界和预算，列几行数表并画分段图。用一次完整的分段解集论证补上“列几个点不证明全部”的缺口。家长检查有没有重复或遗漏输入、累计是否保留前段、整件档价有没有被擅改为按量累计；不需要实际购买或收集同学信息。

# Piecewise functions in context

“Charge extra only for time beyond three hours” differs from “reprice the whole item beyond three hours”. A piecewise function needs not only formulas but the inputs to which each applies and ownership of exact boundaries. All amounts here are fictional worksheet tariffs.

## One central idea

**Locate the input branch first, then evaluate its complete rule.** State the total domain. Branches should cover it without missing inputs or conflicting outputs. Open and closed boundaries usually assign each endpoint once. If two formulas happen to agree at a boundary, that does not change their stipulated ownership.

Draw branches separately. A diagonal across a jump invents intermediate values that the rule never supplied. The last formula does not extend itself beyond the stated domain. For a complete budget-feasible set, solve the inequality in each branch, intersect its domain, then take the union. A few queried inputs are insufficient.

### Example 1: Free time followed by marginal accumulation

Let t be hours in a fictional parking model, 0≤t≤5. Use C=0 for 0≤t≤1; C=2(t−1) for 1<t≤3; and C=4+3(t−3) for 3<t≤5, in NZD. The first hour is free, the next two cost two per hour, and only the later part costs three per hour.

At t=3, the middle branch gives four. At t=4, retain four already accumulated and add three for the next hour, giving seven. Computing 3×4=12 reprices all hours at the last rate; giving only three omits earlier charges. Budget four admits the whole interval [0,3], not just a few tested integer times and not all negative times in x≤3. Times beyond five are outside this model.

### Example 2: Whole-item bands and started hours

A fictional parcel fee uses total mass m in kg: four for 0<m≤1; seven for 1<m≤3; ten for 3<m≤5. At one kg, use the first band and charge four. At 1.5 kg, the whole-parcel fee is seven, not four plus an invented marginal half-kg charge. Zero kg is excluded. Budget seven admits (0,3].

A different timed rule charges zero at t=0, then three per started hour for 0<t≤4. Thus 0<t≤1 costs three, 1<t≤2 costs six, and so on. At 1.5 hours the second hour has begun, giving six rather than 4.5. Three hours costs nine; 3.5 costs twelve. Compare parking’s four and 5.5 at those times to see how boundary ownership and jumps differ. Budget seven gives [0,2], retaining zero.

## One query versus a complete set

Move the query, inspect its highlighted branch and predict its charge. Separately predict the entire budget-feasible input set. Changing the budget resets that set prediction but preserves a charge at a fixed query. Changing the query resets its charge but preserves the complete set for the same budget. A window change preserves both.

Read the branches before revealing the graph. Test zero, one, three and values just beyond boundaries. Open markers exclude and filled markers include. An excluded input receives outside-domain, not an automatic value from the last branch. The definitions establish boundaries and charges; the graph supports observation.
