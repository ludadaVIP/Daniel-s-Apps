# 体积与表面积综合

两块立体拼起来，占有的空间可以直接相加，能涂色的表面却会减少。相加或相减之前，先说明正在计算体积、单块表面积、接触面积还是外露面积。

## 一条核心想法

**无内部重叠时体积相加；拼合表面积要去掉两份实际接触片。** 接触面有面积，却没有三维体积。表面积和来自两个原立体，每个原立体都贡献了一份接触区，所以要从和中扣两次。

这条规则有前提：模型两块内部不穿入，接触面平整、零厚度且没有胶缝。若一块穿入另一块，体积不能直接加；若还要做裁切、边缝或真实涂料采购，则需另外提供损耗、厚度和覆盖条件。

### 例1：部分接触，不是整个大顶面消失

底块6×4×2 cm，体积48 cm³，完整表面积2(24+12+8)=88 cm²。顶上小块3×2×2 cm，体积12 cm³，完整表面积2(6+6+4)=32 cm²。

小块底面完全落在大块顶面内，实际接触面积3×2=6 cm²。总体积48+12=60 cm³；外露面积88+32−2×6=108 cm²。错误114只扣了一份，错误120完全没有扣，若把大块24 cm²顶面整个去掉又会扣太多。

可以按外露片另算来核对：大块仍露88−6=82，小块露32−6=26，两者和108。表面位置虽只是一处接触，两个原表面积中却各数过一份面身份。

### 例2：相同模型，长度倍率不等于面积或体积倍率

把上述模型每一长度放大2倍，面积倍率是2²=4，体积倍率是2³=8。新外露面积108×4=432 cm²，体积60×8=480 cm³，接触面积也要从6变为24 cm²。

家庭挑战改为每1 cm模型长度代表实际10 cm。体积60×1000=60000 cm³=60 L；外露面积108×100=10800 cm²=1.08 m²。换算平方厘米到平方米需除10000，不能只除100；立方厘米与升则1000 cm³=1 L。

每张材料覆盖0.5 m²时，1.08/0.5=2.16张是面积份额，不是可购买的整张数。至少买3张，合计覆盖1.5 m²。在忽略裁切损耗的本题假设下，多出0.42 m²；真实采购若有额外条件，需要再建模，不能把此数字当保证。

## 拼合与外露工作台

可把小块放顶面、放侧面、改为跨满宽的顶块或两块分开。小块从顶改侧，接触片从3×2变为2×2，体积保持60，外露面积却从108变112。分开后接触为0，完整面积和120全部外露。

跨满宽的顶块改为3×4×2，体积变72，接触12，外露面积116。所有长度同步缩放；图中绿色接触片是透明观察时看见的内部片，不能又把它涂色。显示核验或换视角保持预测，位置改变但体积未变时体积作答也保留。

## 家长核对与迁移

单独的无盖6×4×2盒子，面片材料为底24加四壁40，共64 cm²；它与两个完整块拼合是不同问题，不混用“去顶”规则。家长要求先画实际接触区，再说两次扣除各对应哪一个原表面，最后核对单位和倍数。

出口保留模型假设、60 L、1.08 m²和3整张的完整解释。后续空间向量、坐标几何与综合建模还会复现真实对象、前提和多种量的区分；一次算对并不意味着无需继续复习。

# Combined volumes and surface areas

Joining two solids can add their occupied space while reducing the exposed paintable surface. Before adding or subtracting, identify volume, separate surface area, contact area or exposed area.

## One central idea

**With disjoint interiors, volumes add; exposed area removes two copies of the actual contact patch.** A face has area but no three-dimensional volume. Each original solid contributes a contact patch to the sum of separate surface areas, so both copies must be removed.

The rule assumes no interpenetration, flush zero-thickness contact and no glue seams. Interpenetrating blocks do not have a simple volume sum. Real fabrication or procurement may additionally require cutting losses, thickness and coverage conditions; those require separate data.

### Example 1: Partial contact does not hide the entire large top

A6×4×2 cm base block has volume48 cm³ and full surface area2(24+12+8)=88 cm². A3×2×2 cm top block has volume12 cm³ and full area2(6+6+4)=32 cm².

Its base lies fully within the large top, giving contact area3×2=6 cm². Total volume is48+12=60 cm³, while exposed area is88+32−2×6=108 cm². The answer114 removes only one copy,120 removes none, and removing the entire24 cm² large top would discard still-exposed portions.

Audit by exposed pieces: the base contributes88−6=82 and the small block32−6=26, totaling108. A single contact location was counted twice in the separate-area sum, once for each original face identity.

### Example 2: Length, area and volume have different scale factors

Doubling every model length multiplies area by2²=4 and volume by2³=8. Exposed area becomes108×4=432 cm², volume60×8=480 cm³, and contact area6×4=24 cm².

For the family challenge,1 cm of model represents10 cm physically. Volume becomes60×1000=60000 cm³=60 L. Exposed area becomes108×100=10800 cm²=1.08 m². Square centimetres convert to square metres by dividing10000, not100;1000 cm³ equals one litre.

If each covering sheet covers0.5 m², the exact quotient1.08/0.5=2.16 is an area share, not an available whole purchase count. At least three sheets provide1.5 m². Under the no-cutting-loss assumption,0.42 m² remains. Extra practical conditions require a revised model rather than treating these figures as a real procurement guarantee.

## Assembly and exposure workbench

Place the small block on top, at the side, enlarge it across the base width, or separate the blocks. Moving it from top to side changes contact from3×2 to2×2. Volume stays60 while exposed area changes from108 to112. Separation gives zero contact and full exposed area120.

The full-width top block measures3×4×2, giving volume72, contact12 and exposed area116. Every length scales together. The green contact patch is shown through a transparent model but remains buried and unpainted. Camera and audit changes preserve predictions; moving a block without changing its volume also preserves the volume answer.

## Parent check and transfer

A separate open-top6×4×2 box uses bottom24 and four walls40, totaling64 cm² of face material. This differs from joining two complete blocks; do not apply the lid-removal rule to the assembly. Ask the learner to draw the actual contact patch and identify which original face each subtraction removes, then audit units and scale factors.

The exit retains assumptions and the full explanation for60 L,1.08 m² and three whole sheets. Later spatial vectors, coordinate geometry and integrated modelling retrieve object identification, premises and the distinction between different quantities. A single successful calculation does not remove the need for further retrieval.
