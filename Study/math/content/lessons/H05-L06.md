# 向量解决几何问题

一个正方形旋转后不再横平竖直，仍可通过边向量确认。向量证明的力量来自等式背后的几何条件，不来自一张看起来像答案的图。

## 一条核心想法

**把位置关系写成向量等式，再用模长、数量积和非退化条件完成论证。** 对按A,B,C,D相邻排列的平行四边形，设u=AB、v=AD，则C=A+u+v=B+D−A。

两条对角线中点为(A+C)/2与(B+D)/2，由上述关系可证相同。若要得到通常的二维非退化平行四边形，相邻边必须非零且不共线。满足一个位置等式或共同中点，并不自动排除四点共线的退化情形。

### 例1：第四点与斜置正方形

A=(0,0)、B=(4,0)、D=(1,3)，第四点C=B+D−A=(5,3)。两条对角线中点都为(5/2,3/2)，可按两组点分别计算核验，而不是直接根据图上的交点读一个近似值。

另取A=(0,0)、B=(3,1)、D=(−1,3)，则C=(2,4)。相邻边u=(3,1)、v=(−1,3)，点积−3+3=0，平方模都是10；两边不共线。已有平行四边形关系，且邻边等长、成直角，所以是正方形。共同中点(1,2)，形状不因斜放而改变。

### 例2：缺前提与退化反例

取A=(0,0)、B=(4,0)、D=(2,0)、C=(6,0)。仍满足C=B+D−A，对角线中点也都为(3,0)，但四点共线，面积0，不能据此称为通常的非退化平行四边形。

“对角线等长就一定是矩形”也缺少前提。等腰梯形A=(−2,0)、B=(2,0)、C=(1,2)、D=(−1,2)的两条对角线平方长都为13，但上下底长4与2，不是平行四边形。需要先有非退化平行四边形，等长对角线条件才可进一步推出矩形。

## 几何证明工作台

选择一般邻边、垂直不等长、等长不垂直、等长且垂直或共线边界，分别预测C、中点和最具体分类。共同平移会改变C与中点位置，却不改变边向量或分类。图中虚线为对角线，精确表帮助核验起点与分量。

分类顺序是先排退化，再查直角与等长：只有直角是矩形，只有等长是菱形，两者兼有是正方形。这里“只有”指在已知平行四边形的这些条件中区分最具体类别，不是忽略其他四边形所需前提。

## 家庭挑战与家长核对

纸上公园模型每格10 m，使用斜置正方形的四点。两边路线长20√10 m，直接对角线长20√5 m；两条路线的净位移同为(20,40) m。交点位置为(10,20) m，单位转换要作用在坐标长度上。

家长审核“已有平行四边形关系”“非共线”“点积0”“平方模相同”是否都说明，再看最终类别。绘图、计算和文字论证互相核对；不能因为点到了一个正确按钮，就把完整证明视为已掌握。

# Vectors in geometry

A square can be tilted away from the coordinate axes and still be certified using side vectors. A vector proof depends on the geometric premises behind equations, not a picture that resembles the expected shape.

## One central idea

**Translate positional relationships into vector equations, then use magnitudes, dot products and nondegeneracy to justify the conclusion.** For consecutive parallelogram vertices A,B,C,D, let u=AB and v=AD. Then C=A+u+v=B+D−A.

The diagonal midpoints are (A+C)/2 and (B+D)/2, equal by this relation. For an ordinary nondegenerate plane parallelogram, adjacent sides must be nonzero and non-collinear. A positional equation or common midpoint alone does not exclude a collinear degeneration.

### Example 1: Fourth vertices and a tilted square

With A=(0,0), B=(4,0), D=(1,3), the fourth vertex is C=B+D−A=(5,3). Both diagonal midpoints are (5/2,3/2). Calculate them from the two pairs independently rather than merely reading a plotted intersection approximately.

Now let A=(0,0), B=(3,1), D=(−1,3), so C=(2,4). Adjacent vectors (3,1) and (−1,3) have dot product −3+3=0 and equal squared magnitudes ten, and are not collinear. Under the parallelogram premise, equal adjacent sides and a right angle certify a square. The common midpoint is (1,2); tilting does not alter the classification.

### Example 2: Missing premises and degenerate counterexamples

Let A=(0,0), B=(4,0), D=(2,0), C=(6,0). The relation C=B+D−A and common midpoint (3,0) still hold, but all four points are collinear and area is zero. They do not form an ordinary nondegenerate parallelogram.

“Equal diagonals always give a rectangle” also omits a premise. The isosceles trapezium A=(−2,0), B=(2,0), C=(1,2), D=(−1,2) has both diagonal squared lengths 13, yet base lengths four and two show it is not a parallelogram. First establish a nondegenerate parallelogram; only then does equal-diagonal evidence imply a rectangle.

## Geometry proof workbench

Choose general sides, unequal perpendicular sides, equal nonperpendicular sides, equal perpendicular sides or the collinear boundary. Predict C, midpoint and most specific shape separately. Translating the site changes positions of C and the midpoint while preserving side vectors and classification. Dashed lines show diagonals; the exact table audits tails and components.

First exclude degeneracy, then check right angles and equal side lengths. Under the given parallelogram premise, right angles alone give the rectangle case, equal adjacent sides alone the rhombus case, and both the square case. These conditions distinguish the most specific category without discarding premises for other quadrilaterals.

## Family challenge and parent check

Use the tilted-square points for a paper park at ten metres per square. The edge route has length 20√10 m and the direct diagonal 20√5 m. Both have net displacement (20,40) m. The intersection position is (10,20) m; unit conversion applies to coordinate lengths.

A parent checks the parallelogram relation, non-collinearity, zero dot product and equal squared magnitudes before approving the final class. Drawing, calculation and written proof should audit one another. A correct button alone does not demonstrate mastery of the complete argument.
