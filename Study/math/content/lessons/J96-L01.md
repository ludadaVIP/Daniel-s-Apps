# 正弦是对边比斜边

## 先说你看的是哪个角

本单元先研究非退化直角三角形中的锐角，范围是0<θ<90°。若△ABC的直角在C，斜边就是直角对面的AB，不由它在屏幕上是否“斜着”决定。选A作参照，A对面是BC，接触A的另一条直角边是AC。选B作参照时，对边变AC，邻直角边变BC；斜边AB仍固定。

正弦的定义是sinθ=对边长度/斜边长度。这里的“对边”总是相对于当前参照锐角。两个长度先用同一单位，比值无单位。可以记O/H，但记号要建立在真实匹配上：O表示opposite，H表示hypotenuse，不是看到某个数字就随便相除。

### 例 1：同一个三角形，换参照角

已知∠C=90°，AC=4、BC=3、AB=5 cm。参照A，对边BC=3、斜边AB=5，所以sinA=3/5。参照B，对边AC=4、斜边仍5，所以sinB=4/5。两个答案不同，因为问的是两个不同的角，而不是一个公式前后矛盾。

把整幅图转九十度或反射，点A、B、C的身份随点移动，C仍是给定直角。不能根据新的“下面那条边”重新认邻边，也不能把最接近水平的斜边改叫直角边。工作台保留点名，让孩子先指出直角与参照角，再输入边名与比值。

### 例 2：等腰直角三角形的精确值

两直角边都为1的等腰直角三角形，两个锐角各45°。由勾股，斜边长√(1²+1²)=√2。因此sin45°=1/√2=√2/2。精确分数根式可以有多种等价写法；0.71只是近似值，不能在要求精确时替代它。

在30°–60°–90°三角形中，若30°对边为1、斜边为2，则sin30°=1/2。特殊角不是靠背表突然出现，仍来自同一条边比定义。之后用计算器求一般锐角时，也要保留“它是哪两条边的比”的含义。

## 为什么只由角决定

两个直角三角形若还有一个对应锐角相等，由AA相似。对应对边与斜边都乘同一个正倍率k，因此(k·对边)/(k·斜边)=原对边/原斜边。把3–4–5放大成6–8–10，sinA仍为6/10=3/5。统一缩放改变长度，却没有改变同一锐角的正弦。

这不是说任意形状里都能直接套sin=对/斜。若未给直角或足够条件证明直角，就不能把一条边叫本定义中的斜边。三边数据有时能先用勾股逆定理建立直角，但工作台的缺前提反例没有这些数据，不能从草图外观补上。

先预测边比，再揭示O、A、H身份。仅改变统一倍率、旋转或反射，应保留同角比值的预测；改变参照角则重新匹配。家长核对为什么对应、为什么能用AA和为什么单位约掉。数值答对不代替这些解释；孩子也可在纸上画相似的两个三角形，直接用长度比检查自己的说法。

# Sine as opposite over hypotenuse

## Name the reference angle

This unit first studies nondegenerate right triangles with an acute reference angle, 0<θ<90°. If C is the right angle of ABC, AB is the hypotenuse because it lies opposite C, regardless of screen orientation. For reference A, BC is opposite and AC the adjacent leg. For reference B, those leg roles swap while AB remains the hypotenuse.

Define sinθ=opposite length/hypotenuse length. Opposite is always relative to the selected acute angle. Convert lengths to a common unit before dividing; the ratio is dimensionless. O/H is useful shorthand for opposite/hypotenuse, but does not replace matching the actual sides.

### Example 1: the same triangle, another reference

Given C=90°, AC=4, BC=3 and AB=5 cm, reference A gives sinA=BC/AB=3/5. Reference B instead gives sinB=AC/AB=4/5. These are different questions about different angles, rather than contradictory uses of one formula.

A quarter turn or reflection preserves vertex identities and the given right angle. Do not rename sides from their new left, right or lower positions. The lab keeps the labels attached to the points so you can identify the right angle and reference before entering side letters and a ratio.

### Example 2: an exact special value

An isosceles right triangle with legs one has two 45° angles and hypotenuse √2 by Pythagoras. Therefore sin45°=1/√2=√2/2. Equivalent exact radicals are allowed; 0.71 is only approximate and cannot replace an exact answer.

In a 30°–60°–90° triangle with opposite side one and hypotenuse two for the 30° angle, sin30°=1/2. Special values still come from the same side-ratio definition. Calculator values for other acute angles must retain that geometric meaning.

Why does the angle determine the ratio? Two right triangles with one equal acute angle are similar by AA. Their corresponding opposite sides and hypotenuses share one positive scale factor k, which cancels in the quotient. Enlarging 3–4–5 to 6–8–10 leaves sinA=6/10=3/5. Lengths change while the same-angle sine does not.

Without a given or justified right angle, these right-triangle definitions do not apply directly. Sufficient side data might establish a right angle using the converse of Pythagoras, but the missing-premise example supplies none. Appearance is not evidence.

Predict before revealing the O/A/H audit. Uniform scaling and display transforms preserve a same-angle prediction; changing the reference requires new matching. A parent reviews the correspondence, similarity argument and cancellation of units separately from the checked value.
