## 同一条除法式，可能有不同的数量角色

除法可以寻找未知的每份量，也可以寻找未知的份数。先读清楚给定的是什么，才知道商在数什么。不是看到除号就只说“平均分”，然后忽略具体情境。

### 例 1 · 已知分给几人

1. 24 个小球，平均分给 6 人。
2. 6 是人数，未知量是每人几个。
3. **24÷6=4**，每人四个。
4. 检查六份：6×4=24，所有小球都分尽，且每人一样多。

### 例 2 · 已知每盒几个

1. 仍有 24 个小球，但每盒固定装 6 个。
2. 6 是每盒量，未知的是盒数。
3. **24÷6=4**，可以装四盒。
4. 检查四盒：4×6=24。
5. 两个例题得数一样，6 和 4 的单位角色却不同；不要把“四人”当成“四个球”。

### 不能分尽时，余量不能消失

23 个球，每盒五个，四个满盒装了二十个，余三颗另放。写出精确的数量检查：**23=5×4+3**。四是满盒数，不是所有物体已经装入的证明。

若问“需要几个盒子才能放下全部球”，还要加一个未满盒，答案会是五盒。这与“有几个满盒”不同，要先明确问题。不要把完整组数四当成普通除法的精确商：23÷5 的数值为 4.6；这里只在计完整盒，余下三颗仍存在。

若是把二十三颗完整小球平均分给五人、不拆小球，可以先每人四颗，余三颗另放。若允许切分的量，例如纸带或液体，平均分的最终每份量可能是分数或小数；不能把“不拆分的球”模型偷偷当成所有除法的唯一情形。

总量为零，分给四人，每人零个，0÷4=0。除数仍为四。分给零个人则没有一个可定义的“每人量”，不能做除以零；零总量与零除数要区分。

工作台切换已知人数与已知每组量，保持原总数。每个框都显示组，余量在框外独立列出。先预测商的角色，操作后检查“完整组总量+余量=原总量”。

**小检查：** 22 个完整小球分给五人，每人先分四个，余下两颗在哪里？怎样写出恢复总量的式子？

## One division expression can carry different quantity roles

Division may find an unknown amount per share or an unknown share count. Identify the known quantities before deciding what the quotient counts. Do not say only “sharing” while overlooking the situation.

### Example 1 · Known recipient count

1. Share twenty-four counters equally among six people.
2. Six counts recipients; the unknown is counters per person.
3. **24÷6=4**, so each receives four.
4. Check all six shares: 6×4=24. Every counter is assigned and the shares match.

### Example 2 · Known amount per box

1. Again use twenty-four counters, but pack six per box.
2. Six counts counters per box; box count is unknown.
3. **24÷6=4**, so four boxes can be filled.
4. Check the four boxes: 4×6=24.
5. The numerical answer matches, but the sixes and fours have different roles. Four boxes are not four counters.

### A remainder cannot disappear

Twenty-three balls packed five per box fill four boxes, using twenty balls and leaving three outside. The exact quantity check is **23=5×4+3**. Four is the full-box count, not proof that all balls are packed.

Asking for all boxes needed to hold every ball adds one partly filled box, giving five. That differs from asking for full boxes. Four is not the exact numerical value of 23÷5, which is 4.6; this task counts complete boxes and retains three leftover balls.

Sharing twenty-three indivisible counters among five people can give four whole counters to each while setting three aside. For divisible amounts such as rope or liquid, the final equal share may be a fraction or decimal. Do not silently generalise an indivisible-counter model to every division problem.

A zero total shared among four people gives zero each: 0÷4=0. The divisor remains four. With zero recipients, there is no meaningful amount per recipient; division by zero is not defined. Distinguish a zero total from a zero divisor.

The workshop switches between known recipient count and known group size while retaining the original total. Groups appear in boxes and leftovers outside them. Predict the quotient's role, then check complete-group total plus remainder equals the original total.

**Quick check:** with twenty-two indivisible counters and five people, four each leaves two. Where are the two, and what equation recovers the total?
