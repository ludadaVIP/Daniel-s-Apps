# 弦、切线、圆心角

## 一个圆上三种不同对象

圆是平面内到固定点O距离都等于r的点组成的图形，O为圆心，r>0为半径。连接圆上两点的线段叫弦；经过圆心的弦叫直径，长度为2r。所以直径也是弦，普通弦则不一定经过圆心。讨论切线时，对象是整条直线，而不是屏幕中有限的一小段。

整条直线与圆有两个公共点时叫割线，两交点之间的线段是弦；只有一个公共点时叫切线，公共点叫切点；没有公共点时不相交。有限线段只画出一个交点，不足以说明延长后不会遇到第二个点。判断不能只数画框里看见的点。

### 例 1：垂直距离决定交点数

圆半径5 cm。从O向直线作垂线，垂足M，垂距d=OM。d=3<5时有两交点A、B；d=5时只有垂足一个公共点，此处M也就是切点T；d=6>5时无公共点。这里必须使用垂直距离，随便连一条从O到线上的斜线并不是“圆心距”。

在d=3的图中，△OMA与△OMB是直角三角形，共用OM，斜边OA=OB=5为同圆半径。由斜边、一直角边全等，AM=MB。因此AM²=5²−3²=16，AM=4，整弦AB=8 cm。不要求得半弦4后直接当整弦答案。d=0时弦经过O成为直径，AB=10 cm；此时OM为零，单独处理，不能继续把三个共线点叫非退化直角三角形。

### 例 2：圆心角提供等腰结构

圆心角的顶点在圆心，两边是经过圆上端点的射线。若∠AOB=120°，同圆半径给OA=OB，△OAB是等腰三角形。两个底角相等，总和为180−120=60°，每个30°。这个30°不是本课尚未引入的圆周角折半，而是三角形内角和与等腰性质共同推出的底角。

工作台明确用0<θ≤180°的小圆心扫角，避免同一对射线的大、小角混淆。θ=180°时A、O、B共线，AB成为直径，不能再用“两个三角形底角”描述这个退化布局。下一课讨论圆周角时，还会出现与大弧对应的反角扫角，届时必须明确所选弧。

## 用条件解释观察

移动直线，使垂距依次小于、等于、大于半径，先预测交点关系，再核验。旋转整幅图或改变显示缩放，不改变圆心距与半径的比例、交点数和点的身份。工作台按给定数值计算，不能用屏幕尺量像素代替精确长度。

家长可以让孩子用自己的话区分“直径是弦”和“弦都是直径”，指出定义的包含方向。再让他说明为什么半弦可以用勾股，哪个条件给出了直角，哪个条件保证两半相等。解释由人阅读；输入正确分类名称或长度，只完成了结论部分。若把距离写成一条斜线，先修正垂直距离的含义，再继续计算。

# Chords, tangents and central angles

## Distinguish the objects

A circle consists of points at one positive distance r from its centre O. A chord is a segment joining two points on the circle. A diameter is a chord through O, of length 2r. Every diameter is therefore a chord, while an ordinary chord need not pass through the centre.

A secant is an infinite line with two circle intersections. Its between-intersection segment is a chord. A tangent is an infinite line with exactly one common point, called the contact. A line with no common points is disjoint. A short drawn segment showing only one crossing does not establish tangency: extending it might reveal a second intersection outside the picture.

### Example 1: perpendicular centre distance

Take radius five centimetres and perpendicular OM from O to a line. Distance three gives two intersections because three is less than five. Distance five gives one common point at the foot, which is also contact T. Distance six gives none. An arbitrary sloping segment from O to the line is not the perpendicular centre distance.

At OM=3, right triangles OMA and OMB share OM and equal hypotenuses OA=OB=5. Hypotenuse-leg congruence gives AM=MB. Pythagoras then yields AM²=25−9=16, so AM=4 and the entire chord AB=8 cm. Four is only half the chord. At zero centre distance, the chord is a diameter of length ten. Treat this separately: a zero-length OM does not produce a nondegenerate right triangle.

### Example 2: equal radii and a central angle

A central angle has vertex O and rays through its circle endpoints. For AOB=120°, equal radii OA=OB make OAB isosceles. Its equal base angles total 180−120=60°, so each is thirty degrees. This calculation uses the triangle angle sum and equal-radii structure, not the inscribed-angle theorem introduced next.

This lab specifies a minor central sweep with 0<θ≤180°. At θ=180°, A, O and B are collinear and AB is a diameter; do not describe this as a nondegenerate triangle with two base angles. Later, an intercepted major arc may require a reflex central sweep, so always name the selected arc.

Predict line classification before revealing the audit. Rotating the complete diagram or changing display zoom preserves the given distances and intersection count. Screen pixels are not exact measurements. A parent checks which premise supplies the right angle, why the chord is bisected, and whether a calculated length is a half-chord or full chord. Automatically accepting a classification or number does not replace that explanation.
