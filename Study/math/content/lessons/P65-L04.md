## 天平思想：两边同做，还要能倒回来

方程是含未知数的等式；**解**是代入后使原等号两边值相同的数。等号表示关系，不是“请在右边写答案”的箭头。2x=10 与 10=2x 是同一个条件，未知数可以写在任意一边。

天平帮助理解两边同做同样的事，但本课展示的是**数值关系板**，不把负数当成负质量。候选值代入后，左右数值相同才显示相等；这个检查不能代替对变形是否等价的判断。

### 例 1 · 两边同除二，保留原来的解

1. 方程 **2x=10** 表示两份 x 的总值为十。
2. 两边同除非零的二，得 **x=5**。
3. 代回原式：**2×5=10**，核验通过。
4. 若只把左边除二，会写成 x=10；代回原式左为二十、右为十，不相等。
5. 正确一步可同乘二倒回原式。保持相同操作并可逆，说明解集没有增减。

“两边同做”不是说任意操作都能保持完全相同的解。把 2x=10 两边同乘零会得到 **0=0**，所有数都满足新式，原来的限制消失。原解仍能通过新式，不代表新式的所有解都能返回原式。除零则没有定义。本课求解时乘除都要求非零数。

### 例 2 · 解可以是负数或分数

1. 解 **x+5=2**，两边同减五，得 **x=2−5=−3**。
2. 代回 **−3+5=2**，负数解符合原条件；不能因为负号不习惯就改为正三。
3. 解 **4x=2**，两边同除四，得 **x=2/4=1/2**。
4. 核验 **4×1/2=2**。没有要求整数时，不可把半截去或四舍五入。
5. 对 **7=2x** 同除二得 x=7/2，未知数在右也不改变求解原则。

负系数的方程如 −3x=12，可两边同除 −3 得 x=−4。核查可把 −3x 看成 3x 的相反数：当 x=−4，三份负四为 −12，其相反数为十二，所以 (−3)×(−4)=12。若需要，先用旧课分数乘除和有符号数运算补桥，再处理代数符号。

工作台保留原方程和逐步变形记录。先设候选值，观察当前两边是否相同；再尝试两边同做，或只改左边查错。即使新式某次显示相等，也必须回答它是否仍保留原解集。最后按“代回原方程核查”，核的是原条件，不是被改坏的新条件。

家长请孩子说明每一步做了什么、对哪两边做、为何可以逆回；答案正确但过程只写“移过去变号”还不足以解释。带走一个发现：**保持完全相同的解，需要合法可逆的同做；最终核验以原式为准。**

## Balance reasoning: act on both sides and preserve reversibility

An equation is an equality involving an unknown. A **solution** makes the original sides equal after substitution. The equal sign states a relationship, rather than an instruction to place an answer on its right. 2x=10 and 10=2x express the same condition, with the unknown on either side.

The balance idea motivates equal actions, but this model is a **numerical relationship board**, not a physical scale with negative mass. Substituting a candidate compares current numerical values. That check differs from deciding whether a transformation preserves exactly the original solutions.

### Example 1 · Divide both sides by two

1. **2x=10** says that two copies of x total ten.
2. Divide both sides by nonzero two to obtain **x=5**.
3. Substitute in the original: **2×5=10**.
4. Dividing only the left would give x=10, whose original left value is twenty rather than ten.
5. The correct step can be reversed by multiplying both sides by two. Equal reversible actions preserve exactly the solutions.

Not every identical action is reversible. Multiplying both sides of 2x=10 by zero produces **0=0**, satisfied by every number. The original solution still passes, but additional solutions have appeared because the constraint was lost. Division by zero is undefined. This solving workshop therefore requires nonzero factors and divisors for multiplication and division.

### Example 2 · Solutions may be negative or fractional

1. For **x+5=2**, subtract five from both sides: **x=−3**.
2. Check **−3+5=2**. Unfamiliarity with negative values does not justify changing the sign.
3. For **4x=2**, divide both sides by four: **x=1/2**.
4. Verify **4×1/2=2**. Without an integer restriction, do not discard or round the fraction.
5. For **7=2x**, dividing both sides by two gives x=7/2. Writing the unknown on the right changes no solving principle.

A negative coefficient is handled by the same reversible operation: −3x=12 divided by −3 gives x=−4. To check, view −3x as the opposite of 3x. At x=−4, three copies give −12, whose opposite is twelve. Thus (−3)×(−4)=12. Return to signed arithmetic and fraction operations if these numerical checks are uncertain; the algebra depends on their meanings.

The workshop retains the original equation and a transformation history. Assign a candidate and compare current sides, then try equal actions or a deliberately one-sided change. A new equation may balance at a particular value while having a different solution from the original. The final original-substitution check always uses the initial condition, not a transformed condition that may have been damaged. Ask which operation was used, which sides received it and how to reverse it. A correct answer accompanied only by a move-and-change-sign slogan does not explain preservation of the relationship. The takeaway is equal reversible operations and final verification against the original equation.
