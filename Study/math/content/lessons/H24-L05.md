# 条件概率

P(A|B)读作“已知B发生，A发生的概率”。竖线右侧是条件，左侧是要研究的事件。**新的分母只保留满足B的结果**；这是更新结果空间，不是从一个概率里随意减掉一些数。

## 交集占条件的份额

当P(B)>0，P(A|B)=P(A∩B)/P(B)。在有限等可能编号模型中，这等于|A∩B|/|B|；若结果不等可能，必须累加各结果的概率权重，不能只数点。四格图的矩形大小也不能代替权重。

同一交集可写成P(A∩B)=P(B)P(A|B)。若P(A)>0，也可写成P(A)P(B|A)。这是首事件概率乘该事件发生后的条件概率，适用于相依事件，无需先假设独立。

P(B)=0时，上述比值没有定义，不能填0。空条件中“没有有利结果”与在一个非空条件中“有利结果数0”不同。后者才可能是合法零概率。

### 例1：同一四格的四种提问

十个等可能编号结果分成四格：同时A与B有3个，仅A有1个，仅B有2个，皆非有4个。A总数4，B总数5，交集3，总数10。

所以P(A)=4/10=2/5，P(A∩B)=3/10，P(A|B)=3/5，而P(B|A)=3/4。两个条件概率共享分子3，但条件决定不同分母，不能互换。反过来验算：(1/2)(3/5)=(2/5)(3/4)=3/10，都回到交集。

若另一模型B为空，P(A|B)未定义。若B非空但与A没有交集，则P(A|B)=0。不要仅看到交集为空就忽略分母。

### 例2：无放回的条件改变

袋内有编号红球R1,R2,R3和蓝球B1,B2，均匀无放回抽两次。已知第一次红，袋中剩2红2蓝，因此第二次红的条件概率2/4=1/2。两次都红的概率是(3/5)(2/4)=3/10。

3/5是第一次红的概率，不是已知第一次红后第二次的概率。如果放回并重新均匀抽，第二步才保持3/5。条件可改变可用对象，也可只改变我们知道的信息；两种情形都应重新说明分母。

## 让条件变成看得见的筛选

工作台给每个等可能结果一个编号。选择P(A|B)时，B外的点变灰；选择P(B|A)则改筛A外的点。点的原身份不变，只有当前条件空间改变。图的四个色片大小相同，它们不是概率面积，需按点计数。

预测未定义时输入undefined。核验表同时保留总数、A、B、交集及两个方向的精确比例，帮助检查是不是忘了换分母。不要拿某一次模拟出现次数为0，推断理论条件概率必为0；这里的编号图是明确给定的理论模型。

出口要解释四种概率，并比较空B和非空但无交集的B。家长可先盖住B以外的点，让孩子在剩余集合里说“几份中的几份”，再把同一句话写成符号。

# Conditional probability

P(A|B) means the probability of A **given that B occurred**. The right of the bar supplies the condition; the left names the target event. The denominator now contains only outcomes satisfying B. This restricts the outcome space rather than arbitrarily subtracting from an old probability.

## The intersection as a share of the condition

For P(B)>0, P(A|B)=P(A∩B)/P(B). In a finite equally likely numbered model this becomes |A∩B|/|B|. Without equal likelihood, add probability weights instead of counting dots. The apparent size of a cell in a drawing does not supply a probability weight either.

Rearranging gives P(A∩B)=P(B)P(A|B). When P(A)>0, it also equals P(A)P(B|A). Multiply the first event’s probability by the relevant conditional branch. This product rule works for dependent events; independence is not a prerequisite.

When P(B)=0 the ordinary ratio is undefined, not zero. Having no outcomes in the condition differs from having zero favourable outcomes inside a nonempty condition. Only the latter can yield a legitimate zero conditional probability.

### Example 1: Four questions about one table

Ten equally likely numbered outcomes have four disjoint cells: three satisfy both A and B, one satisfies A only, two satisfy B only and four satisfy neither. Thus A contains four, B five, and their intersection three.

The probabilities are P(A)=4/10=2/5, P(A∩B)=3/10, P(A|B)=3/5 and P(B|A)=3/4. The two conditional questions share numerator three but use different conditions and denominators. Reversing the bar changes the question. Check the product rule: (1/2)(3/5)=(2/5)(3/4)=3/10.

If another model has empty B, P(A|B) is undefined. If B is nonempty but disjoint from A, the conditional probability is zero. Looking only at an empty intersection misses this essential denominator distinction.

### Example 2: Sampling without replacement

A bag contains numbered reds R1,R2,R3 and blues B1,B2. Draw twice uniformly without replacement. Given a red first draw, two red and two blue balls remain, so the second-red conditional probability is 2/4=1/2. Both-red probability is (3/5)(2/4)=3/10.

The original 3/5 describes a red first draw, not the second-red probability after knowing the first was red. With replacement followed by another uniform draw, the second step would retain 3/5. Conditions can change available objects or simply change our information; either way, state the new denominator explicitly.

## See conditioning as a filter

The workbench gives every equally likely outcome a unique number. For P(A|B), dots outside B turn grey; for P(B|A), the filter instead excludes dots outside A. Outcome identities stay fixed while the conditioned space changes. The four cell backgrounds have equal visual sizes but are not probability areas: count their dots.

Enter undefined for an undefined condition. The audit retains totals for the whole space, A, B and their intersection, together with exact ratios in both directions. It helps detect an unchanged denominator. This is a specified theoretical model; observing zero instances in a finite simulation would not establish that a theoretical condition has zero probability.

For the exit, distinguish the four probabilities and compare empty B with a nonempty B disjoint from A. A parent can cover the dots outside B and ask for “how many out of how many” inside the remaining set, then translate that sentence into symbols.
