## 数字变小，不等于数量变小

约分是把相同小份重新合成较大的组。例如十二个十八分之一，不一定要一直按十八分之一计数；可以把每六份合成一组。原来的整体与涂色区域不变，新分子与新分母变小。

**分子和分母必须同时除以同一个共同因数。** 约分后的数值与原分数相等。最简分数还要求分子分母没有大于 1 的共同因数；“等值”和“已经最简”是两个不同检查。

### 例 1 · 12/18 一步约到最简

1. 12 与 18 都能被 6 整除。
2. 把每六个十八分之一合成一个新组，整个整体有 18÷6=3 组。
3. 涂色部分有 12÷6=2 组。
4. 因此 **12/18=2/3**，总量没变。
5. 2 与 3 只有共同因数 1，已经最简。

### 例 2 · 先约成 6/9，再继续

1. 12/18 的分子分母先同时除以 2，得到 **6/9**。
2. 6/9 与原分数等值，所以这一操作正确。
3. 但 6 与 9 还共有因数 3，可以继续同时除以 3。
4. 得到 **2/3**。两个不同的合法分组路径，最后表示同一个最简分数。
5. 若题目要求最简，仅填 6/9 是中间结果；系统会说明数量等值，但还需要完成写法要求。

### 怎样检查“不能继续约分”？

不要只看分子是不是奇数，也不要认为两个数相邻才算最简。检查是否有大于 1 的整数能同时整除分子和分母。7/12 就已经最简，虽然两数不相邻；9/12 还可同时除以 3，得到 3/4。

分子分母同时减去相同数，并不对应共同分组，不能当作约分。例如 3/5 与 2/4 不等值：三份五分之一超过一半，而两份四分之一恰好是一半。数字看似一起变小，不保证比例保持。

本课先用较小的正整数，尝试不同共同因数。若已经没有可用的共同因数，工作台说明已经最简；它不会假装任何数都能继续约。输入最简答案时要写分数，整数结果可直接写整数。等值小数属于另一种表示，不是本题要求的最简分数写法。

牛奶 9/12 升约成 3/4 升时，单位仍为升，实际容量也不变。约分没有倒掉牛奶，改变的是计量小单位及其个数。

## Smaller written numbers need not mean a smaller amount

Simplification groups equal small parts into larger units. Twelve eighteenths can be regrouped with six original parts per group. The original whole and shaded region stay fixed while numerator and denominator become smaller.

**Divide both numerator and denominator by the same common factor.** This preserves value. Simplest form also requires no common factor greater than one; equivalence and full simplification are separate checks.

### Example 1 · Simplify 12/18 in one step

1. Twelve and eighteen are both divisible by six.
2. Group each six eighteenths into one new unit: the whole contains 18÷6=3 groups.
3. Shading contains 12÷6=2 groups.
4. Thus **12/18=2/3**, with unchanged total amount.
5. Two and three share only factor one, so the fraction is simplest.

### Example 2 · Reach 6/9 and continue

1. Divide both numbers in 12/18 by two to obtain **6/9**.
2. This is an equivalent fraction, so the step is mathematically valid.
3. Six and nine still share factor three; divide both by three next.
4. Obtain **2/3**. Different valid grouping paths reach the same simplest fraction.
5. When simplest form is requested, 6/9 is an intermediate answer. The app distinguishes equal value from completing the requested form.

### How do you know simplification is finished?

Do not only check whether the numerator is odd or whether the numbers are adjacent. Find whether an integer above one divides both. 7/12 is already simplest despite non-adjacent numbers. By contrast, 9/12 still divides by three to give 3/4.

Subtracting an equal number from numerator and denominator is not common grouping and does not preserve fractions in general. For example, 3/5 differs from 2/4: three fifths exceed a half, while two quarters are exactly a half. Making both written numbers smaller does not guarantee an unchanged proportion.

The workshop uses small positive integers and permits different common factors. It explains when no further factor is available rather than pretend every fraction can keep reducing. Use a fraction for a simplest-form answer; an integer result may be entered as an integer. An equivalent decimal is another representation, not the requested simplest fractional form.

Reducing 9/12 litre of milk to 3/4 litre preserves both litres and actual volume. No milk is removed; only the counting unit and its count change.
