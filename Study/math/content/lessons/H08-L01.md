# 变量类型与数据清理

一张表不是一堆可任意平均的数字。先问每行代表哪个对象、哪次记录，每列表示什么，才能决定哪些计算有意义。本课只做一件事：建立**保留来源、按字段核对的分析数据**。

## 一条核心想法

变量按意义分类，不按电子表格外观分类。颜色是无序类别（nominal）；低、中、高满意等级是有序类别（ordinal），但相邻等级未必等距；件数为离散数值（discrete），允许真实的0；长度为连续数值（continuous），即使仪器只显示整厘米，原长度仍可在两个整数之间。类别用1、2、3编码，不自动获得加减平均的意义。

清理不是让图好看。确认单位、缺失、无效值和重复身份；需要修正时同时保留原值、采用值、原因和来源。一个极端但有效的记录不能因为影响均值就删除。

### 例1：相同长度与重复身份是两回事

虚构纸卡记录R1长20 cm，R2长200 mm。先换算200÷10=20 cm，这两个不同ID的长度相同，仍是两次观测。若又导入同次记录的R2，须核来源确认是重复导入，再合并这个重复身份；不能仅按长度去重。

工作台7行的ID依次为R1、R2、R3、R4、R5、R6、R2，末行是确认重复的R2。因此有6个不同记录，重复导入1次。R2件数真实为0，仍有效。R3未填长度，用“—”表示缺失；填0会声称实际测得零长，并改变总和、分母及意义。

### 例2：有来源的修正与字段分母

六个不同记录的原长度为20 cm、200 mm、缺失、−5 cm、200 cm、24 cm。给定原始量尺记录确认R5是20 cm，200 cm为录入错误；采用修正时保留这条依据。负长度−5与长度定义不符，待核且暂不进入长度均值；它所在行的有效件数仍可用于件数统计。

换单位、合并重复ID并采用来源修正后，有效长度是20、20、20、24 cm，共4个，总和84 cm，均值21 cm。报告应写“4个有效长度，均值21 cm；另1缺失、1无效待核”。不能用6作长度均值分母，也不能把缺失填0。

若暂不采用已确认修正，原编码对照为20、20、200、24，均值66 cm。这个值展示错误影响，不能作为最终清理结论。修正后也没有凭空恢复缺失个体；清理不会自动消除缺失带来的代表性问题。

## 审计工作台

逐行对照原长度与统一cm列，再切换来源修正。先预测不同记录数、变量类型和当前长度均值；展开核验检查各字段去向。改来源修正只影响相关均值，不改变记录身份；改变变量类型问题只清该问题。重复值保留，重复导入只计一次。

纸上补一列“处理理由”，试着让另一个人不听口头说明也能复现21。若无法判断重复身份或真实单位，应标记待核，不能猜一个看起来合理的数。

## 家长核对与迁移

追问：“删的是重复ID，还是不同人的相同值？”再问：“这个0实际记录了吗？”出口需讲清来源修正、单位、字段分母与有效零，不能只背“先清理”。下一课用清理后的原始点观察分布，仍保留每次真实观测。

# Variable types and data cleaning

A table is not a collection of numbers that can all be averaged meaningfully. Identify the object and occasion represented by each row and the quantity or category in each column. This lesson develops one idea: **traceable analysis data with field-specific checks**.

## One central idea

Classify a variable by its meaning. Colour is nominal: categories have no intrinsic order. Low, medium and high satisfaction are ordinal, but their gaps need not be equal. Counts are discrete numerical quantities, including a genuine zero. Length is continuous even when an instrument records only whole centimetres; the underlying measurement can lie between integers. Coding categories as 1,2,3 does not make their arithmetic mean meaningful.

Cleaning is not cosmetic. Check units, missingness, validity and repeated identities. A correction must retain the original, adopted value, reason and source. Do not remove an unusual but valid observation merely because it affects a summary.

### Example 1: Equal measurements differ from duplicate identities

Fictional record R1 has length 20 cm and R2 has length 200 mm. Conversion gives 200÷10=20 cm. These are two distinct IDs with equal lengths, so both observations remain. A later import of that same R2 record is different: after confirming it is the same occasion and record, merge the duplicate import rather than counting it twice.

The workbench has seven row IDs: R1,R2,R3,R4,R5,R6,R2. The last is a confirmed repeated import. There are six distinct records and one duplicate import. R2's count of zero remains a valid observed quantity. R3 has no recorded length, displayed as a dash. Replacing the dash with zero would assert an actual zero-length measurement and alter the denominator and meaning of the mean.

### Example 2: A sourced correction and a field-specific denominator

Six distinct records originally have lengths 20 cm,200 mm,missing,−5 cm,200 cm,and 24 cm. The supplied measurement source confirms that R5 was 20 cm: 200 cm was an entry error. Keep that source when adopting the correction. Negative length is invalid for this variable and remains under review. Its row may still contain a valid count, which must not be erased merely because length is invalid.

After unit conversion, duplicate-ID merging and source correction, the usable lengths are 20,20,20,24 cm. Four observations total 84 cm and have mean 21 cm. Report the denominator and decisions: four usable lengths, one missing and one invalid pending review. Dividing by all six records or filling the missing value with zero would describe a different, unsupported dataset.

Before applying the known correction, the coding comparison is 20,20,200,24 with mean 66 cm. That value illustrates the error's effect; it is not a reconciled conclusion. The corrected four measurements still do not recover the missing individuals. Cleaning does not automatically make incomplete data representative.

## Audit workbench

Compare original measurements with the standardized centimetre column. Predict record counts, variable types and the current length mean before revealing the audit. Applying the source correction affects the relevant mean but not record identity. Switching the variable question changes that prediction only. Equal values remain; the duplicate import counts once.

Add a written reason column on paper. Could another person reproduce 21 cm from the retained information without an oral explanation? If identity or units are unknown, record the uncertainty and seek the source rather than guessing a plausible number.

## Parent review and transfer

Ask whether a removed row was the same imported identity or a different individual with an equal value. Ask whether zero was actually observed. The exit explanation must connect source correction, units, field-specific denominators and genuine zero. The next lesson describes distributions while retaining every real observation.
