# 角度与比值的变化

## 固定斜边，观察两条直角边

本课普通三角形仍限定0<θ<90°。固定斜边，把参照锐角逐渐增大，对边变长，邻直角边变短。因此sinθ=对/斜增大，cosθ=邻/斜减小。正切的正分子增大、正分母减小，所以tanθ也增大。这些变化不是一次函数的固定加法关系，不能以为角度翻倍，比值必然翻倍。

锐角sin、cos均严格介于0和1，因为直角边短于斜边。正切的两个直角边没有固定大小顺序，可以小于、等于或大于1。图像与表格帮助观察趋势，但有限几个点不能代替关于全部锐角的理由。

### 例 1：三个特殊角的精确比较

由30°–60°–90°三角形和等腰直角三角形，可得sin30°=1/2、sin45°=√2/2、sin60°=√3/2，依次增大。余弦按相反顺序为√3/2、√2/2、1/2。正切为√3/3、1、√3，最后一个已超过1。

这些都是精确值。例如tan45°=1来自两直角边相等，而tan60°=√3来自对边√3、邻边1。如果题目要求精确值，1.732只是√3的近似，不能代替根式。若题目明确要求四位小数，则应按相应表示要求作答。

### 例 2：近似格式与图窗边界

sin30°精确等于1/2；要求四位小数时写0.5000。tan45°要求四位小数时写1.0000。1/2与0.5000数值相等，但后者才完成本题指定的格式；这不意味着分数在数学上错误。尾零用于表达本题精度，不应随意删掉。

tan85°约为11.4301，若图窗纵轴只画到4，这个点在窗外，不能说函数没有这个值，也不能把它钳到4造一段水平线。工作台保留真实纵坐标并裁切曲线，另外说明当前值在窗外；不是把视窗当作函数的取值范围。

## 九十度不是一个巨大有限正切

当锐角越来越接近90°，固定斜边下邻边越来越接近零，对边接近斜边，tan越来越大。恰为90°时，普通三角形退化且邻边分母为零，tan90°无定义。不能把浮点库对近似π/2给出的巨大有限数字当成精确数学结果。

本课不把0°、90°当普通锐角三角形。曲线的端点用空心标记说明锐角域不包含它们；更一般的三角函数定义会在后续课程扩展。当前先把合法锐角、趋近边界和真正的零分母区别清楚。

先预测随角变化的方向，再拖动。精确题和近似题按各自要求检查；显示缩放只改变图像大小，改变真实角或所求函数则产生新问题。家长核验的是边变化如何解释比值变化，而不是只听到“图上是这样”。离堂解释tan90°，需要同时说清定义域、分母和浮点近似，避免把计算器输出误当数学定义。

# How trig ratios vary with angle

## Keep the hypotenuse fixed

Ordinary triangles here still require 0<θ<90°. With fixed hypotenuse, increasing the reference angle increases the opposite leg and decreases the adjacent leg. Thus sine increases and cosine decreases. Tangent increases because its positive numerator grows while its positive denominator shrinks. These changes are not linear: doubling an angle does not generally double a ratio.

Acute sine and cosine lie strictly between zero and one because a leg is shorter than the hypotenuse. Tangent compares two legs and can be below, equal to or above one. Tables and plots support observation, but finitely many samples are not a general argument.

### Example 1: exact special values

The 30°–60°–90° and isosceles right triangles give sine values 1/2, √2/2 and √3/2 at 30°, 45° and 60°, increasing in that order. Cosine takes the reverse order. Tangent takes √3/3, one and √3; the last is above one.

These values are exact. Equal legs give tan45°=1, and opposite √3 with adjacent one gives tan60°=√3. If an exact answer is requested, 1.732 does not replace √3. A task prescribing decimal places asks for a different representation.

### Example 2: precision and the graph window

sin30° is exactly 1/2; to four decimal places write 0.5000. tan45° becomes 1.0000. The fraction and decimal can be numerically equivalent while only the requested decimal format completes the task. This does not make the fraction mathematically false. Retain the specified trailing zeros.

tan85°≈11.4301. If the vertical window ends at four, this point lies outside it; the value still exists. Do not clamp it to four and create a false plateau. The lab clips the genuine curve and reports when the selected value is off-window, keeping the viewing window distinct from the function's range.

As θ approaches 90° from within the acute domain, the adjacent leg approaches zero and tangent grows. At 90° the ordinary triangle is degenerate and the denominator zero, so tan90° is undefined. A huge finite output at a floating-point approximation to π/2 is not the exact endpoint value.

Zero and ninety degrees are excluded from these ordinary acute triangles. Open markers show the domain endpoints; later courses extend the definitions. Predict trends before dragging, and distinguish a true parameter change from display zoom. A parent reviews the geometric reason for the trends and the domain, denominator and approximation explanation at the boundary.
