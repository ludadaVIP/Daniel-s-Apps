# 截面与展开图

截面是在立体中与一个平面真正相交的形状；展开图则把原有表面沿棱打开到平面上。两者都要追踪真实的点或面，不能只看一张轮廓图猜答案。

## 一条核心想法

**截面保留交点的位置，展开保留每块面的身份。** 平面与立方体的交集不一定是多边形：可能为空、一个点、一条线段，或与已有面重合。形成多边形时，其顶点来自棱的交点，共同顶点只数一次。

展开不创造或删除面片面积。正方体需要六个全等正方形，但“六格相连”仍不足以保证折成六个不同面；如果两格折后占同一面，面积即使正确也无效。

### 例1：正方形、三角形和六边形截面

取0≤x,y,z≤4 cm的正方体。z=2与四条竖棱相交，得到边长4的正方形，面积16 cm²；z=0则与整个底面重合，交集仍为正方形，不是额外创造了一个原立体的外表面。

x+y+z=2与三条从原点出发的棱分别交于(2,0,0)、(0,2,0)、(0,0,2)。任意两点距离2√2，得到等边三角形。把它分成两个直角三角形，高为√((2√2)²−(√2)²)=√6，因此面积为(2√2)×√6/2=2√3 cm²。

x+y+z=6时，顶点为(4,2,0)的六种坐标排列；相邻距离都是2√2，形成正六边形，可分成六个相同的上述面积三角形，总面积12√3 cm²。纸面投影可能看起来压扁，不能拿投影面积代替真实切面面积。

### 例2：同样六格，为什么有的折不成

把四格排成一带，在第二格上、下各接一格，保留编号1至6。沿共边折90°，带上的四格围成一圈，两瓣补上另外两面；六个面的方向各不相同。追踪可得1号与3号相对，5号与6号相对。

完整2×3矩形也有六格且相连，面积同样六个单位，但折转会出现面方向重叠或闭环要求冲突，不能覆盖六个不同正方体面。检查的不是轮廓好不好看，而是每格最终占哪一面，公共边能否一致相接。

若原正方体边长4 cm，所有六个面片面积和始终6×16=96 cm²。面积和正确是必要检查，却不足以证明一个候选排布是合法展开图。

## 截面与折面工作台

截面模式可改变切平面方向和参数，先数不同顶点/端点，再预测面积。x+y=0在非负坐标约束下强制x=y=0，得到一条竖棱，面积0；x+y+z=0只留下原点；平面移到立体外则空交集。系统保留这些边界，不伪造四边形。

展开模式先预测有效性与1号的对面，再切换折面核验。编号贯穿展开和折后图，重叠编号保留，不能为了看清就把几块本应重叠的面移开。切换观察方向和核验表保留预测，截面与展开两个活动各自保留输入。

## 家长核对与迁移

解释题要求说明“六格、面积、连通”为什么仍不够，出口再区别重合面与相切棱。家长检查孩子是否用真实面对应和坐标约束支持判断，而非只记某个展开图外形。下一课用三维中的实际线段求距离，组合体则继续追踪被接触埋住的两个面身份。

# Cross-sections and nets

A cross-section is the actual intersection of a solid with a plane. A net opens original surface faces onto a plane. Both require tracking real points or face identities, not merely guessing from an outline.

## One central idea

**Sections preserve intersection positions, while nets preserve face identities.** A plane-cube intersection may be empty, one point, a segment, a polygon, or a whole existing face. Polygon vertices come from edge intersections, with shared vertices counted once.

Unfolding preserves face areas. A cube needs six equal squares, but six connected squares alone do not guarantee six distinct folded faces. Two tiles folding onto one face invalidate the net despite the correct total area.

### Example 1: Square, triangle and hexagon sections

Take the cube0≤x,y,z≤4 cm. Plane z=2 meets its four vertical edges in a square of side four and area16 cm². Plane z=0 coincides with the whole base: still a square intersection, without creating an additional exterior face of the original solid.

Plane x+y+z=2 intersects the three origin edges at (2,0,0),(0,2,0),(0,0,2). Each side between those points is2√2. Split this equilateral triangle into two right triangles: its altitude is √((2√2)²−(√2)²)=√6. Its area is therefore (2√2)×√6/2=2√3 cm².

For x+y+z=6, the vertices are the six permutations of (4,2,0). Adjacent vertices remain2√2 apart, giving a regular hexagon. Six equilateral triangles of the preceding area give12√3 cm². Its drawing may look compressed, but projected area is not true section area.

### Example 2: Six tiles that do or do not fold

Place four squares in a belt with one square above and one below the second belt square, retaining IDs1–6. Quarter-turn folds wrap the belt around four sides, and the two flaps fill the remaining faces. All six face directions are distinct. Tracking IDs gives face1 opposite3 and face5 opposite6.

A full2×3 rectangle also has six connected squares with the same area, but quarter-turn folds create overlapping face directions or inconsistent cycles. It cannot cover six distinct cube faces. The criterion is which face each tile occupies and whether shared edges join consistently, not visual resemblance to a familiar outline.

For a cube of side4 cm, the six face areas always total6×16=96 cm². Correct area is a necessary audit but does not certify every proposed arrangement as a valid net.

## Section and fold workbench

In section mode change the cutting plane and parameter, predict the distinct vertex/endpoint count, then area. With nonnegative coordinates, x+y=0 forces x=y=0 and leaves a vertical edge of zero area. Plane x+y+z=0 leaves only the origin, while a plane beyond the solid may give an empty intersection. These boundary cases are retained instead of inventing a quadrilateral.

In net mode predict validity and the face opposite1 before switching to the fold audit. IDs persist throughout. Overlapping labels stay overlapping rather than moving tiles apart artificially. Camera and audit-table changes preserve predictions, and section/net activities retain their own inputs when you switch between them.

## Parent check and transfer

The reasoning item explains why tile count, area and connectivity are insufficient; the exit contrasts a coincident face with a tangent edge. Check for actual face correspondences and coordinate constraints, rather than recognition of a memorised silhouette.
