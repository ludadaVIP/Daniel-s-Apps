## 周长量的是边界一整圈

由 π=C/d 可得 **C=πd**。同圆 d=2r，所以也有 **C=2πr**。两个公式描述同一周边界，不是需要先乘直径、再额外乘二的两道步骤。先把已知长度标为 r 或 d，能防止最常见的倍数错误。

周长是长度，用 cm、m 等一次单位。圆内部的覆盖面积用平方单位，是下一课的量；不能因为图是圆就把每个答案都写成平方厘米。屏幕缩放、旋转或改变标注方式不改变原圆的数学长度。

### 例 1 · 半径五与直径五不同

1. 给 **半径 r=5 cm**，先求同圆直径 **d=10 cm**。
2. 精确周长 **C=πd=10π cm**，也可直接用 2π×5。
3. 10π 不是十；π 不能无故省掉。
4. 若题目给的是 **直径 d=5 cm**，精确周长则为 **5π cm**，只有前者一半。
5. 核查：两圆半径倍数二，边界周长也按二倍缩放。

保留 π 的式子是精确值。输入可用 `10π`、`10pi` 或等值的 `20π/2`，不需要把 π 展开成小数。若题目要求精确答案，31.4 不能替代 10π；数值接近不等于精确相等。

### 例 2 · 指定近似时再代入

1. 给 **直径 8 cm**，要求按 **π≈3.14** 计算。
2. 用 C=πd，得到 **C≈3.14×8=25.12 cm**。
3. 不再乘二，因为八已经是直径。
4. 同圆的精确写法仍是 **8π cm**，25.12 来自指定近似约定。
5. 若按同一约定给 C 的计算值 31.4 cm，先求 d=31.4÷3.14=10，再折半得半径五厘米。

题目明确近似方法时，判的是按这个方法所得数值。不能把另一种 π 近似或计算器更多位数字混入同一道题，再只在最后改精度。若要比较两种方法，先分别说明各自约定。

轮子无滑动滚动四整周时，路程为四个周长。例如 d=3 cm，每周 3π cm，四周 **12π cm**。这里需要完整周数与无滑动条件；只有半圈或发生打滑时，不能不加说明套四个周长。

**半圆边界也需先看对象。** 半周弧长是整周一半，但半圆形材料的完整边框还包括那条直径。不能把“弧的一半”与“整个半圆边框”自动当成同一个问题；本课自动题明确问整圆一周或整周滚动。

工作台切换 r、d 时保持同一圆，只换已知长度的描述；再次预测后核对。家长请孩子在草图上指着边界绕一周，再说出给定线段。带走一个发现：**先辨半径直径，再选等值公式；精确保留 π，近似交代约定，周长始终是长度。**

## Circumference measures a complete boundary turn

From π=C/d, obtain **C=πd**. Since d=2r in the same circle, **C=2πr** as well. These are equivalent descriptions of one boundary turn, not successive instructions to multiply by diameter and then double again. Label the known length r or d before calculating.

Circumference is a length in centimetres or metres. Covering the interior is area, measured in square units. Drawing a circle does not make every quantity an area. Rotating, zooming or relabelling the same circle does not change its mathematical length.

### Example 1 · Radius five differs from diameter five

1. With **r=5 cm**, the diameter is **d=10 cm**.
2. Exact circumference is **C=10π cm**, also obtained from 2π×5.
3. 10π is not ten; retain π.
4. If the given length were **d=5 cm**, the result would instead be **5π cm**, half as much.
5. A radius scale factor of two gives the same circumference scale factor of two.

Forms such as `10π`, `10pi` and `20π/2` preserve the exact value. There is no need to expand π into a decimal. When exactness is requested, 31.4 cannot replace 10π merely because they are close.

### Example 2 · Substitute the specified approximation

1. Given **diameter 8 cm**, use **π≈3.14** as requested.
2. Compute **C≈3.14×8=25.12 cm**.
3. Do not double again: eight already denotes diameter.
4. The exact expression remains **8π cm**; 25.12 follows the chosen approximation.
5. Conversely, a calculated circumference of 31.4 cm under the same convention gives d=31.4÷3.14=10 cm and radius five centimetres.

A specified approximation question checks the value produced by that convention. Do not silently mix another approximation or calculator digits into the same calculation. If comparing methods, state each convention separately.

A wheel rolling four complete turns without slipping covers four circumferences. With d=3 cm, each turn covers 3π cm and four turns **12π cm**. The complete-turn and no-slip conditions matter. A semicircular arc is half a circumference, but the whole border of a semicircular sheet also includes a diameter. Identify the requested boundary before using a fraction of the full turn. Automatic questions here explicitly ask about whole circles or complete wheel turns.

Switching r and d in the workshop retains the same circle while changing the given-length description. Predict again before checking. A parent can ask the learner to trace one boundary turn and identify the given segment. The takeaway is radius versus diameter first, equivalent formulas second, exact π or a stated approximation, and linear units throughout.
