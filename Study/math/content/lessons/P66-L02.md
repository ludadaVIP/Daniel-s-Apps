## 读数的商与精确圆周率分开说

圆周长 C 是边界绕一整圈的长度，直径 d 是穿过圆心的直线距离。对理想圆，**π=C/d** 是不随圆大小改变的恒定比。它不是周长除以半径；除以半径会得到 2π，约为 6.28。π 没有厘米单位，因为分子与分母是同单位的长度。

工作台的滚动是**理想无滑动模型**，一整周的路程与圆周长相同。几何由数学值绘制，表格再按选定尺精度给模拟读数；不是从真实物体采来的数据。细尺和粗尺能给不同读数商，这有助于理解测量误差，但不能用这张模拟表假装做过真实实验。

### 例 1 · 商是三点一五，不必强改成三点一四

1. 给定假想读数 **C=12.6 cm、d=4 cm**。
2. 先核对都为厘米，且 C 指整条周长。
3. **C÷d=12.6÷4=3.15**。
4. 三点一五是这组读数的商，不能因记得 3.14 就改掉所给数据。
5. 它也不表示这个圆的精确 π 改变了；周长读数取近似、尺精度和绕线方式都会影响数据。

数学中的 π 是精确量，**3.14 是有限小数近似**。写 π≈3.14，不写 π=3.14。测量误差不是 π 自己忽然变了，也不能用有限几次读数证明 π 的无理性或精确求出它的全部数字。

### 例 2 · 单位不统一会把商缩小十倍

1. 假想读数 C=31.4 cm，而 d=100 mm。
2. 先换算 **100 mm=10 cm**。
3. 得 **31.4÷10=3.14**，这是这组数据的商。
4. 若直接算 31.4÷100=0.314，就把厘米与毫米当成了同一单位。
5. 也可都换成毫米，314÷100 仍是三点一四，说明比不依赖统一选用哪一种长度单位。

粗读数如 C=18 cm、d=6 cm，商是三。请如实报告，不把“读数商”与“理想圆的恒定比”偷换。工作台允许另填同单位的假想或家庭纸笔读数，系统算出实际商，不强迫它靠近 3.14。直径为零无法相除，负长度不在这个测量情境内。

可以用纸上圆与纸条粗比一周和直径，记录是“粗测”，而非追求许多小数位。纸条要绕完整一圈、不过度拉伸，尺读同单位。看不清或弯曲造成偏差时，讨论数据如何得来，比宣布孩子“测错了所以答案一定是 3.14”更有价值。

家长追问：“除的是直径还是半径？这是实测、模拟还是给定数？用什么精度？”带走一个发现：**理想恒定比叫 π；有限精度读数的商要按数据诚实计算，并交代来源与近似。**

## Separate reading quotients from exact pi

Circumference C measures one complete boundary turn; diameter d measures a straight segment through the centre. For ideal circles, **π=C/d** is a constant independent of size. Dividing by radius instead gives 2π, about 6.28. The ratio has no centimetre unit because matching length units cancel.

The workshop shows an **ideal no-slip rolling model**. One full turn covers a distance equal to circumference. Its geometry uses mathematical values, while the table rounds them to a selected simulated ruler precision. These are not observations collected from a physical object. Coarse and fine readings can yield different quotients; that illustrates precision without pretending the simulation is a real experiment.

### Example 1 · Keep a quotient of 3.15

1. Fictional readings are **C=12.6 cm and d=4 cm**.
2. Both use centimetres and C covers a complete turn.
3. **C÷d=12.6÷4=3.15**.
4. Report the requested data quotient rather than forcing a memorized 3.14.
5. This does not mean exact π changed. Rounding, ruler precision and wrapping technique affect readings.

π is exact, whereas **3.14 is a finite decimal approximation**: write π≈3.14, not π=3.14. Measurement error is not a change in π. Finitely many readings cannot determine every digit or prove irrationality.

### Example 2 · Match units before dividing

1. Let C=31.4 cm and d=100 mm.
2. Convert **100 mm=10 cm**.
3. Then **31.4÷10=3.14**, the quotient of these readings.
4. Directly using 31.4÷100 would incorrectly treat centimetres and millimetres as the same unit.
5. Alternatively convert both to millimetres: 314÷100 again gives 3.14. The ratio does not depend on which common length unit is chosen.

Coarse readings C=18 cm and d=6 cm produce quotient three. Report it honestly while distinguishing it from the ideal constant. The workshop accepts additional fictional or family paper readings without forcing them toward 3.14. Diameter zero makes division invalid; negative lengths are outside this measurement setting.

For an optional paper investigation, compare a drawn circle’s boundary and diameter with a paper strip, recording the result as a coarse measurement. Wrap a complete turn without stretching and use one unit. Discuss unclear marks, bending and precision rather than replacing the data with the expected approximation. A parent can ask whether the divisor is diameter or radius, whether the source is observation or simulation, and which precision was used. The takeaway names π as the ideal constant while computing finite reading quotients from the actual stated data and reporting their source and approximation.
