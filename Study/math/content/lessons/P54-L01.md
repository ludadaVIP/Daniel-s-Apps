## 合并的是份数，保留的是每份大小

分数同时说明两件事：分母说明一个整体等分多少份，分子说明取了其中多少份。计算同分母分数加法前，还要确认它们以**同样大的整体**为参照。两个八分之一加三个八分之一，是五个八分之一；每一份并没有因合并而变小，分母仍是八。

“分母相同”只是相同份大小的线索，不替代整体检查。半块小饼和半块大饼的实际量不同。如果用升表示水量，每个完整整体可以统一为 1 L；壶本身可以不同大小，分数条仍使用同一个测量标尺。

### 例 1 · 五个八分之一

1. 已知同一整体的 **3/8** 与 **2/8**，求合计量。
2. 两组每一份都是 **1/8**，不需要重新分割。
3. 把三个小份与两个小份接在一起：**3+2=5** 份。
4. 合计 **3/8+2/8=5/8**，不是 5/16。
5. 用逆运算核查：**5/8−2/8=3/8**。画出的五格仍占一整条的五个八分之一。

### 例 2 · 和超过一个整体

1. 两壶各有 **3/4 L** 水。求合并后的水量，容器足够大。
2. 每份 **1/4 L**，共有 **3+3=6** 份，所以 **6/4 L**。
3. 四份组成 **1 L**，另剩两份：**6/4 L=1 2/4 L**。
4. 约分得 **3/2 L**，也可写 **1 1/2 L**；涂色实际量没有减少。
5. 总量减一壶：**6/4 L−3/4 L=3/4 L**，恢复另一壶。和大于一升完全合理，不能强迫它小于 1。

### 从数量等值到按题意表示

1/6+3/6=4/6=2/3。4/6 是正确的中间数量；若题目要求最简分数，应继续约为 2/3。APP 会区分“数值等价但形式未完成”和数量错误。题目特意要求某个分母时，应按指定形式写；没有这个要求时，不应把等值分数说成不同量。

0/9+4/9=4/9：零表示没有新增份，不会让另一组的份消失。5/8+7/8=12/8=3/2，跨过一个整体后仍数同样大的八分之一。多条完整条都表示同样大小的一个整体，不能把第二条画得更短来凑答案。

工作台可以改变分子和分母。在同分母模式，两组会一起使用同一分母。先纸笔预测，再查看等单位份、合并与最简表示。改动已知量会收起旧结果。图形只帮助观察，不代替练习里的独立答案。

**带走一个发现：** 加法数的是同大小份的总数。保留整体与每份大小，和可以超过一个整体；最后按题意整理表示，再减回核查。

## Join piece counts while retaining piece size

A denominator gives the number of equal parts in a whole; a numerator counts the parts taken. Before like-denominator addition, check that the reference wholes are equal in size. Two eighths plus three eighths make five eighths. Joining does not resize pieces, so the denominator stays eight.

Matching denominators do not replace checking the whole. Half a small cake differs from half a large cake. For measured water, use 1 L as the common whole reference even when the jugs themselves have different capacities.

### Example 1 · Five eighths

1. Join **3/8** and **2/8** of the same whole.
2. Every piece is **1/8**, so no further division is needed.
3. Join three pieces and two: **3+2=5** pieces.
4. **3/8+2/8=5/8**, rather than 5/16.
5. Check by reversing: **5/8−2/8=3/8**. Five shaded tiles still occupy five eighths of a full strip.

### Example 2 · A sum above one whole

1. Two jugs each contain **3/4 L**. Combine them into a sufficiently large container.
2. Each piece represents **1/4 L**. Six pieces give **6/4 L**.
3. Four pieces make **1 L**, with two left: **6/4 L=1 2/4 L**.
4. Simplify to **3/2 L**, or write **1 1/2 L**. No actual water disappears.
5. Subtract one jug: **6/4 L−3/4 L=3/4 L**, recovering the other. A total above one litre is reasonable.

### Quantity equivalence and requested representation

In 1/6+3/6=4/6=2/3, the intermediate 4/6 is the correct amount. If a question requests a simplest fraction, finish with 2/3. The app distinguishes an equivalent value with unfinished form from a wrong quantity. When a denominator is specifically requested, use it; otherwise equivalent fractions do not represent different amounts.

Zero adds no pieces: 0/9+4/9=4/9. Also, 5/8+7/8=12/8=3/2 continues counting eighths beyond one whole. Every complete strip must represent the same-sized whole, including the second strip.

Change numerators and denominators in the workshop. Like-denominator mode uses one denominator for both amounts. Predict first, then inspect equal-size pieces, their joining and the simplest representation. Changing a known quantity clears old results. Inspection supports learning but does not replace independent practice.

**Takeaway:** addition counts equal-size pieces altogether. Retain the whole and piece size; a sum may exceed one. Represent as requested and check by subtracting back.
