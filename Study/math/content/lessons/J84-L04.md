# 一元一次不等式

## 每一步都有依据

本课处理整理后只含一个未知数一次项的不等式，允许未知数出现在两边。展开、合并是在原来每边换写同值表达式，不会反向。两边同加减同一个式子，把未知项集中、常数项集中，也不会反向。最后同除非零系数时，才根据该系数正负决定方向。

分母为已知非零常数时，可以先乘一个公倍数清分母。选正公倍数，方向不变；整个两边的每项都要乘，括号保留到完整分配。若除数含未知字母，其符号和零点另有问题，不能在本课规则下不加条件地处理。

### 例 1：两边有 x 的括号式

解 `3(x−2)≤2x+1`。先展开为 `3x−6≤2x+1`，三必须乘到 x 和负二。两边同减 2x，得 `x−6≤1`；同加六，得 `x≤7`。每步均可逆，没有一次共同乘除负数，故始终不反向。

边界七代回，左为三乘五等十五，右为十四加一等十五，包含七。八代回为十八≤十七，为假；零代回为负六≤一，为真。注意原式含负六并不自动要求反向，它只是一个项。

图窗若只到五，仍不能把解集说成“x≤5”。七及所有符合的更小数由代数决定，画面只负责显示其中一段。

### 例 2：清分母后出现负系数

解 `(x−1)/3>(x+2)/2`。同乘正六，得到 `2(x−1)>3(x+2)`。完整展开得 `2x−2>3x+6`，同减 3x 得 `−x−2>6`，同加二得 `−x>8`。

现在同除负一，必须反向，得 `x<−8`。清分母的六为正，不反向；除未知数系数负一才反向。负九代回，左为 −10/3，右为 −7/2；交叉比较 −20/6>−21/6，成立。负八代回两边均负三，被严格大于排除。不能写 x>−8，也不能把窗外没有标到负八误判为无解。

## 变量消失时停下来判断

`2(x+1)≤2x+3` 展开同减 2x 后得 `2≤3`，对每个实数 x 都真，解集是所有实数。不是“x 等于零”，因为零乘任何实数都为零。工作台也显示完整的真假结论，不把它包装成单一数值。

若条件是 `2(x+1)<2x+1`，剩 `2<1`，是假，故无解。两种情况都不能除以零。严格性也要保留：`0<0` 假，而 `0≤0` 真，虽然常数看起来相同。

工作台逐步展示展开、集中未知项、集中常数、同除或判断常数，并可退一步查看理由。改变数轴视窗不会重置已输入的解集或正确反馈；换原问题才清空旧证据。精确边界如 −7/3 不能用截断小数冒充；完整解集还要保留端点是否包含。

家长先问“这一步对两边做了什么”，再问“哪一步真正出现负数乘除”。如果变量消失，改问剩下的是恒真还是矛盾，不催促孩子继续机械除系数。

# Linear inequalities

## Justify each step

These inequalities reduce to affine expressions in one variable, which may occur on both sides. Expansion and collection rewrite a side with the same value. Common addition or subtraction gathers terms without reversal. Division by the final nonzero coefficient keeps direction if positive and reverses it if negative.

Known nonzero constant denominators can be cleared using a common multiple. A positive multiple preserves direction, and it must multiply every term on both sides. Retain brackets until distribution is complete. Variable denominators would require sign and zero restrictions beyond this unconditional rule.

### Example 1: brackets and x on both sides

Solve 3(x-2)≤2x+1. Expand to 3x-6≤2x+1, multiplying both terms inside the bracket. Subtract 2x from both sides to obtain x-6≤1, then add six to get x≤7. No common negative multiplication or division occurs, so no reversal is needed.

At seven, both original sides equal fifteen. Eight gives 18≤17 and fails; zero gives -6≤1 and passes. A negative term such as minus six does not itself reverse the sign.

If the graph window ends at five, the solution is still x≤7 rather than x≤5. Algebra determines the full set; the screen shows only a portion.

### Example 2: a negative coefficient after clearing fractions

Solve (x-1)/3>(x+2)/2. Multiply both sides by positive six: 2(x-1)>3(x+2). Expand fully to 2x-2>3x+6. Subtract 3x, then add two, giving -x>8.

Now divide by negative one and reverse to x<-8. Clearing denominators used positive six and did not reverse; dividing by minus one does. At -9 the sides are -10/3 and -7/2, with -20/6>-21/6, true. At -8 both sides equal minus three, so the strict comparison excludes it. An off-window boundary does not imply an empty set.

## When the variable disappears

For 2(x+1)≤2x+3, expansion and subtraction leave 2≤3. This holds for every real x, giving all real numbers. It does not mean only x=0: zero times any real is zero.

For 2(x+1)<2x+1 the remaining relation is 2<1, false, so there is no solution. Neither case permits division by zero. Strictness matters too: 0<0 is false, while 0≤0 is true.

The workshop reveals expansion, gathering of variables, gathering of constants and division or constant classification, with reasons and an undo control. Changing the number-line window retains an entered set and feedback; changing the original problem clears old evidence. Keep fractional boundaries such as -7/3 exact and retain endpoint inclusion.

A parent asks what happened to both sides and which step actually used negative scaling. When x disappears, ask whether the constant statement is true or contradictory rather than requesting mechanical division.
