## 第 n 项来自构造，不只是继续猜数字

本课先**定义**图形：第 n 幅有三行圆点，每行 n 个，另放两个固定方块；图号从一开始，只取正整数。n 是图号，也是每行的圆点数；它不是整幅总数。第 1 幅五个、第 2 幅八个，是构造的结果。仅给五、八两个数，并不足以唯一规定后面的每一项。

把结构翻译成式子：第一行 n、第二行 n、第三行 n，再加二，所以总数 **n+n+n+2=3n+2**。这个说明适用于每一个允许图号；比只列几项可靠，因为各部分都能在模型中找到。

### 例 1 · 不画前十九幅也能求第二十幅

1. 题目问第 20 幅，故代入 **n=20**，不是把总数先当二十。
2. 每行二十个，三行贡献 **3×20=60**。
3. 另有两个固定方块，总数 **60+2=62**。
4. 若只写 3n，得到六十，漏掉始终存在的二。
5. 核查第十九幅为五十九，第二十幅六十二，差三；同时三行和固定量都对应正确。

**每次增加三**描述相邻图的变化，**3n+2**描述第 n 幅总数。这是不同的量。第 1 幅已经有五个，不能把“增长三”当作每一幅总量。模型只画一至十幅便于观察，规则仍允许更大的正整数图号；不能把显示范围误当成规则尽头。

### 例 2 · 从总数反找图号

1. 同一构造的一幅共有 **29** 个，问图号。
2. 先去掉两个固定方块，三行一共 **29−2=27** 个。
3. 三行一样多，每行 **27÷3=9** 个。
4. 每行九个对应第九幅，所以 **n=9**，不是 n=27 或 n=29。
5. 代回规则 **3×9+2=29**，总数与条件一致。

若总数给三十，则扣二后为二十八，除三不是整数；在本课正整数图号的构造中，就没有恰好三十个的图。不能为了得到一个图号把结果四舍五入，也不能悄悄允许三分之一幅图。

选择通用式时，用第 1 幅可以排除某些错误式，却不能凭一个数值相同就宣布正确。例如 n+4 在 n=1 也得五，但到 n=2 只有六，并非八。**反例能推翻通用说法；有限次符合不能代替构造说明。**

纸笔任务：画第 1、2、5 幅，用一种符号标三行、另一种标固定两块。不逐个画第 20 幅，仍能说明六十二从哪里来。家长追问“式子的三、n、二各对应哪里”，而不只问下一项。带走一个发现：**图号、总数和增长量分开说，通式要由明确构造支持。**

## Derive the nth term from a construction

Stage n is **defined** to contain three rows of circles, n in each row, plus two fixed squares. Stage numbers start at one and are positive whole numbers. n names the stage and the circle count per row, rather than the overall total. The first totals five and eight follow from this definition. Those two observed numbers alone would not uniquely determine every later term.

Translate each part: n in the first row, n in the second, n in the third, then two. Hence **n+n+n+2=3n+2**. This argument covers every allowed stage because every term corresponds to a defined part, rather than merely matching a short list.

### Example 1 · Find stage twenty without drawing the preceding stages

1. Stage 20 means **n=20**, not an assumed total of twenty.
2. Three rows of twenty contribute **3×20=60**.
3. Add two fixed squares to get **62**.
4. Using only 3n would give sixty and omit the extras.
5. Check that stage nineteen has fifty-nine and stage twenty sixty-two, a growth of three, while also accounting for every constructed part.

“Increase by three each time” describes the change between stages. **3n+2** describes the total at a given stage. They are different quantities: stage one already contains five. The model displays stages one through ten for convenient inspection, but the rule permits larger positive whole-number indices. A screen window does not end the mathematical construction.

### Example 2 · Recover the stage from its total

1. A stage in the same construction contains **29** pieces.
2. Remove two fixed squares: the rows contain **29−2=27**.
3. Divide equally among three rows: **27÷3=9** per row.
4. Nine per row means stage nine, so **n=9**, rather than 27 or 29.
5. Substitute back: **3×9+2=29**.

A proposed total of thirty would leave twenty-eight after removing two. Dividing by three does not give a whole-number stage index, so no stage in this defined construction has exactly thirty pieces. Rounding would silently change the condition; fractional stage indices are outside the stated domain.

One stage may reject a wrong rule, but agreement at one stage does not establish a general rule. For example n+4 also gives five at n=1, yet gives six rather than eight at n=2. A counterexample refutes a general claim; finitely many successful trials do not replace the construction argument. On paper, draw stages 1, 2 and 5 with distinct symbols for rows and fixed extras, then explain the total at stage 20 without drawing every piece. Ask where three, n and two occur in the model. The takeaway separates index, total and growth, using the specified construction as evidence for the nth term.
