## 数字相同，位值可以不同

**数字**是写下的符号；**位值**是这个数字在某个位置代表的数量。千位上的 2 表示 2 × 1,000 = 2,000，个位上的 2 只表示 2。

把每个数位的贡献相加，就是这个数的**展开式**。零的贡献可以不写进加法式，但它在数字写法里的位置必须保留。

### 例 1 · 拆开 62,407

1. 6 在万位：6 × 10,000 = 60,000。
2. 2 在千位：2 × 1,000 = 2,000。
3. 4 在百位：4 × 100 = 400；十位为 0。
4. 7 在个位：7 × 1 = 7。
5. 所以 **62,407 = 60,000 + 2,000 + 400 + 7**。

没有 `+0` 不代表把 7 挤到十位。把展开式合起来，仍要得到原来的数。

### 例 2 · 把展开式拼回去

`40,000 + 600 + 9` 是什么数？

1. 万位为 4，千位为 0。
2. 百位为 6，十位为 0，个位为 9。
3. 写成 **40,609**，不是 469，也不是 46,009。
4. 检查：40,609 − 40,000 = 609 = 600 + 9。

### 相邻位置，单位相差十倍

同一个数字从百位移到千位，贡献会从 600 变成 6,000，增加为原来的 **10 倍**。这里比较的是“一张数字卡的贡献”；若把整串数字都乘 10，需要把每个数字的数位一起处理，不能只移动一个数字就声称整串数乘了 10。

在位值表里选中一列，修改它的数字。观察总数变化：个位加 1，总数增加 1；百位加 1，总数增加 100。**位置决定每一步改变的大小。**

**小检查：** 你能解释 7,073 中两个 7 的值，而不只说“都是 7”吗？

## Same digit, different value

A **digit** is a written symbol. Its **place value** is the amount represented in its position. A 2 in thousands means 2 × 1,000 = 2,000; a 2 in ones means only 2.

Adding each place’s contribution gives the number’s **expanded form**. A zero term can be omitted from a sum, but the place must remain in the written number.

### Example 1 · Expand 62,407

1. The 6 is in ten thousands: 6 × 10,000 = 60,000.
2. The 2 is in thousands: 2 × 1,000 = 2,000.
3. The 4 is in hundreds: 4 × 100 = 400; tens is zero.
4. The 7 is in ones: 7 × 1 = 7.
5. Therefore **62,407 = 60,000 + 2,000 + 400 + 7**.

Leaving out `+0` does not move the 7 into tens. Recombining the terms must still give the original number.

### Example 2 · Rebuild an expanded form

What number is `40,000 + 600 + 9`?

1. Ten thousands is 4 and thousands is 0.
2. Hundreds is 6, tens is 0 and ones is 9.
3. Write **40,609**, not 469 or 46,009.
4. Check: 40,609 − 40,000 = 609 = 600 + 9.

### Neighbouring places differ by ten

Move the same digit from hundreds to thousands and its contribution changes from 600 to 6,000: **ten times as large**. This describes one digit card’s contribution. Multiplying an entire number by ten involves every digit’s place; moving only one digit does not multiply the whole number by ten.

Select a column in the chart and change its digit. Increasing ones by one adds 1 to the total; increasing hundreds by one adds 100. **The place determines the size of the change.**

**Quick check:** can you explain the two values of 7 in 7,073, rather than say only that both digits are 7?
