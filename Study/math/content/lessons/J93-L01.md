# 抛物线与开口方向

## 二次关系不是固定速度的直线

二次函数可整理为 y=ax²+bx+c，其中 a、b、c 是已知实数，a≠0。每个允许的 x 都对应一个确定 y；在没有另给情境限制时，本课先研究所有实数输入。图像叫抛物线。若整理后二次项消失，实际可能是一次函数或常函数，不能因为式子中曾经出现 x² 就继续套抛物线结论。

先看最简单的 y=ax²。x 与−x平方相同，左右对称；x²≥0，乘以正a得到非负输出，乘以负a得到非正输出。由零向两边远离时，平方变大，图像分别向上或向下展开。一般的平移会改变最低或最高位置，但不会改变开口方向，决定方向的仍是非零二次系数。

### 例 1：同一输入、同一标尺

比较 y=x²、y=2x²、y=−2x²。输入x=−2、−1、0、1、2时，第一组输出4、1、0、1、4；第二组8、2、0、2、8；第三组−8、−2、0、−2、−8。固定同一横纵标尺，正系数两曲线都向上，负系数曲线向下；后两者关于横轴互为反射。

在相同离对称轴的距离下，|a|=2的纵向变化是|a|=1的两倍。常说它“更窄”，但这句话必须依赖相同坐标标尺。把截图横向拉长或纵向压缩，会改变肉眼感觉，不会改变函数系数。工作台保留一条虚线参照y=x²，让比较始终有共同基准；窗口改变时应重新看标尺，而不是宣称a变了。

### 例 2：平移不改方向，零会改次数

y=−(1/2)x²+3的a为−1/2，仍向下。常数3让图像整体上移；它不是判断开口的系数。x=0时y=3，x=2或−2时y=1，表现为从中间向两边下降。对y=−2(x+1)²+4，同样由−2判断向下，括号中的移位不改变这个结论。

若a=0，y=0x²+2x+1实际上是y=2x+1，一条直线；若剩常数y=3，是水平直线。二者都不是二次函数。工作台中y=ax²经过a=0时成为y=0，不应该把水平线称为“开口很平的抛物线”，也不能继续给它一个唯一抛物线顶点。

## a不是恒定斜率

一次函数中斜率描述单位横向变化的固定纵向变化。对y=x²，x从0到1输出增加1，从1到2增加3，从2到3增加5，这些变化不同；所以a不是整条曲线的恒定斜率。之后会用顶点式看它怎样控制离轴高度，而不是把直线的语言直接搬过来。

先预测开口，再代入一个非零输入核符号。平方负输入时先加括号：(−3)²=9，2(−3)²=18，不是−18。家长可问：“整理后二次项系数是多少？比较用了相同输入与相同标尺吗？这个结论关于开口，还是关于顶点位置？”一次只改变一个因素，孩子更容易把观察连接到代数依据。

# Parabolas and opening direction

## A quadratic is not a constant-rate line

A quadratic function has collected form y=ax²+bx+c with a≠0. Each allowed input determines one output. Unless a context restricts the domain, this lesson first considers every real input. Its graph is a parabola. If the quadratic term cancels, the function may instead be linear or constant; a former appearance of x² is insufficient.

Begin with y=ax². Opposite inputs have equal squares, producing symmetry. Since x²≥0, positive a gives nonnegative outputs and negative a nonpositive outputs. Moving away from zero makes the square larger, opening the graph upward or downward. Translation changes its location but not the opening determined by a.

### Example 1: common inputs and scales

At x=−2,−1,0,1,2, y=x² gives 4,1,0,1,4. The function y=2x² gives 8,2,0,2,8, while y=−2x² gives −8,−2,0,−2,−8. On identical scales the first two open up and the third down. The last two reflect across the x-axis.

At the same distance from the symmetry axis, |a|=2 doubles the output displacement compared with |a|=1. Calling the graph narrower assumes common horizontal and vertical scales. Stretching a screenshot changes appearance without changing the function. The laboratory retains dashed y=x² as a reference. Read the scales when the window changes; do not infer that a has changed.

### Example 2: translation versus degeneration

For y=−(1/2)x²+3, a=−1/2 makes the graph open down. Constant three translates it upward rather than determining opening. At zero the output is three; at either two or −2 it is one. Likewise y=−2(x+1)²+4 opens down because of −2, independently of its shifts.

At a=0, y=0x²+2x+1 is the line y=2x+1. If only y=3 remains, it is a horizontal line. Neither is quadratic. In the laboratory, y=ax² becomes y=0 as a passes through zero. That is a line, not a very flat parabola with a unique vertex.

## The coefficient is not a constant slope

For y=x², unit input increases from zero to one, one to two and two to three give output increases one, three and five. These are not fixed, so a cannot describe a constant slope of the whole curve. Later vertex form will explain its control of output displacement from the axis.

Predict opening before testing a nonzero input. Parenthesise negative inputs when squaring: 2(−3)²=18 rather than −18. A parent can ask which coefficient survives collecting, whether the comparison uses common inputs and scales, and whether the conclusion concerns opening or position. Changing one factor at a time helps connect the graph to its algebraic cause.
