# 模拟核对理论概率

## 先给模型，再生成记录

理论概率由明确模型计算；模拟则按这个模型的抽取规则生成有限记录。两者必须描述同一事件与一次试验。本课每轮先恢复原袋三红两蓝，按编号等机会抽两次，轮内不放回，关注颜色不同。完整两抽才算一轮，分母不是单个抽球动作数。

轮内首球拿走，使第二球条件变化；轮间恢复原袋，使下一轮又对应原来的同一试验。若一直移走球而不恢复，袋子很快变空，后续已不是同一模型。若改放回或改颜色数，应重新计算理论值并开始新运行，不能把不同规则记录拼接成一个频率。

### 例 1：理论3/5，记录11/20

三红两蓝不放回，RB与BR各3/10，异色理论概率3/5。实际20轮出现11次异色，频率是11/20=0.55。应保留11，而不是按理论改成12。理论说明事件机会，频率说明这一批实际发生的比例，两者含义不同。

零轮零命中时没有频率，因为0/0未定义；图上不画零轮的频率点。第一轮频率只能0或1，不会被人为放在理论线上。工作台虚线标理论，折线由逐轮实际命中数/n计算，所有数值都可以回到编号记录核对。

### 例 2：累计要合并次数

100轮有58次命中，频率58/100=29/50。追加10轮只有3次命中，110轮总命中61，累计频率61/110≈0.5545。不能把58%和30%直接平均成44%，因为两批试验次数不同；应合并命中和总数。

这次追加后，频率离理论0.6反而更远。因此更多试验不保证每一步都靠近。适当独立重复模型的长期稳定趋势不是每一步的单调承诺，也不要求任意有限轮数最后恰好3/5。连续未命中不会在模型中积累“亏欠”，下一轮不被迫补偿。

## 种子与真实证据

本地工具用可重放伪随机数生成编号选择。相同参数、种子与次数得到同一序列；追加十轮会接着原前缀，而不是重新抽一批替换旧记录。换种子表示另一运行，可比较两次结果，但不能只挑更好看的运行汇报。记录参数、事件、种子、轮数与命中，便于家长核验。

模拟是模型产生的数据，不是真实学校调查，也不是对一个真实袋子或装置的测量。一次与理论接近不能证明现实模型正确，一次偏离也不自动推翻；应先核程序规则、条件、样本量和记录，再结合适当证据判断。把理论值画上图不授权修改实际命中。

工作台把理论预测与实际频率输入分开。追加同规则试验应保留理论预测，但频率是新问题，需要根据新记录重算。仅切换最初或最近十条的显示不改变序列。家庭纸上研究保留两种放回规则、两个种子和完整计数，完整理由由家长阅读。

# Testing theoretical probability by simulation

## Define the model before generating records

Theory calculates probability from assumptions; simulation generates finite records under the same rules. Specify both the event and one trial. Restore an original three-red/two-blue bag each trial, choose equally likely numbered IDs, draw twice without replacement within the trial, and record different colours. One complete two-draw experiment counts as one trial, not two.

Removing the first token changes the second conditional distribution. Restoring the bag between trials keeps the next trial on the original model. Continually removing tokens changes the experiment and eventually empties the bag. Changing counts or replacement requires a new model probability and a fresh run, rather than merging unlike records.

### Example 1: theory 3/5, observed 11/20

RB and BR each have probability 3/10, giving different-colour theory 3/5. Eleven hits in twenty trials give frequency 11/20=0.55. Retain eleven instead of replacing it with theoretical twelve. Theory describes model chance; frequency describes realised records.

At zero trials, frequency is undefined because 0/0 is undefined. There is no plotted zero-trial frequency point. After one trial frequency is zero or one, never artificially placed on the theory line. The dashed line represents theory; cumulative points come from actual hits divided by trials and can be audited from the ID records.

### Example 2: combine counts, not unweighted percentages

A hundred trials with 58 hits give 29/50. Add ten trials with three hits: there are now 61 hits in 110, giving 61/110≈0.5545. Averaging 58% and 30% equally gives 44%, incorrectly giving equal weight to batches of unequal size. Add hits and trial counts instead.

Here the appended batch moves frequency farther from theoretical 0.6. More trials do not guarantee every step is closer. Long-run stability under suitable independent repetition is not a monotonic promise or a requirement for exact finite equality. Misses create no probability debt that forces the next trial to compensate.

The local tool uses reproducible pseudorandom selections. Identical parameters, seed and count reproduce the same sequence. Appending trials extends its prefix instead of replacing the old run. A new seed provides another run for comparison, not permission to cherry-pick the prettiest result. Preserve rules, event, seed, count and hits.

Simulated data are model-generated, not real survey responses or measurements of a physical device. One agreement does not prove a real model; one discrepancy does not automatically refute it. Audit rules, conditions, sample size and records, and seek suitable evidence. Drawing a theory line never authorises altering observed hits.

The lab separates theory prediction from observed frequency. Appending trials preserves the former but requires a new frequency calculation. Showing first or latest ten records changes only the view. The paper family study preserves both replacement rules, two seeds and complete counts, with full reasoning reviewed by a parent.
