## 绝对值量到零的距离

数轴上的 −6 与 +6 是不同位置，但各自到零都隔六个单位。这种**一个数所表示的点到零的距离**称为绝对值，写成 **|−6|=6、|6|=6**。竖线表示这道“量到零”的任务，不是另一个负号，也不是随意删除符号的指令。

位置有左右方向，距离本身不带方向负号。绝对值总是**非负**，即可以为零或正数；“一定为正”漏掉了零。相反数则描述关于零对称的位置，是另一种关系。

### 例 1 · 同样远，不是同一个数

1. P 在 **−6**，位于零左六格。
2. Q 在 **+6**，位于零右六格。
3. 两个点到零距离都是六，所以 **|−6|=|6|=6**。
4. P 与 Q 没有重合，仍有 **−6<+6**，不能由绝对值相同推出数值相同。
5. 核查参照：每次都是从零量到一个点，而不是从 P 量到 Q。P 到 Q 要十二格。

工作台先只显示 P，再可显示它的相反数点 Q。两条无方向的量距线从零出发，长度相同；左右位置和字母标签保留，不会为了“绝对值相同”把两个点挪到一处。

### 例 2 · 正数与零帮助区分相反数

1. 取 **+4**。它的相反数在零左四格，值为 **−4**。
2. 但 +4 到零距离为四，故 **|+4|=4**，与相反数 −4 不同。
3. 对 −4 来说，相反数与绝对值碰巧都为四；不能由一个例子的重合宣布两种概念永远相同。
4. 当点为 **0**，零到零没有距离，所以 **|0|=0**。
5. 零的相反数也为零。图中 P 与 Q 此时合并成一个位置，不制造额外一步，也不能说零的绝对值为一。

**参照不能偷换：** 从 −4 走到 +3，相隔四格到零再三格，共七格；而 |−4|=4 只量起点到零，不包含后面的三格。距离都应非负，但起止点不同，计算结果当然可能不同。

**大小与绝对值也不同：** −9 的绝对值九大于 −3 的绝对值三，可是 −9<−3。前一句比较到零距离，后一句比较带符号位置。先说题目问哪一种量，比凭数码大小猜更可靠。

改变 P 的整数后，工作台清空旧结果与对称点；重新观察位置，再量距离，最后核对。试负六、正六和零三个情形，用自己的话说明何处相同、何处不同。零时量距线长度为零，图不会把它画成一个单位。

**家庭纸笔挑战：** 画 −12 到 +12 的整数数轴，标 −6、0、+6，比较位置与到零距离；从 −4 向右七格，分别写终点、带符号变化和距离。家长改变起点或方向，问是否到零、跨零或没有到零。再画三条假想摄氏读数，无需测温或查询真实天气。

**家长陪学：** 请孩子明确“从哪里量到哪里”，并用正数和零反例检验说法。解释不能只有“去掉负号”或“负负得正”；认可对称、数格、量距线与口头推理。**带走一个发现：** 绝对值是到零的非负距离，相同距离不等于相同位置，相反数又是另一个关系。

## Absolute value measures distance to zero

−6 and +6 are different positions, each six unit intervals from zero. The **distance from a number’s position to zero** is its absolute value: **|−6|=6 and |6|=6**. The vertical bars specify that measurement, rather than another minus sign or an instruction to erase signs without interpretation.

Positions carry direction; distance has no left/right sign. Absolute value is **nonnegative**, meaning zero or positive. “Always positive” misses zero. An opposite is a reflected position across zero and describes a different relationship.

### Example 1 · Equally far does not mean the same number

1. P at **−6** lies six intervals left of zero.
2. Q at **+6** lies six right.
3. Both have distance six, giving **|−6|=|6|=6**.
4. The points do not coincide: **−6<+6**. Equal absolute values do not establish equal numbers.
5. Check the reference: measure from zero to each point, not from P to Q. P and Q are twelve intervals apart.

The workshop first shows P, then can display its opposite Q. Two directionless distance segments start at zero with equal lengths, while their letters and signed positions remain distinct.

### Example 2 · Positive values and zero distinguish an opposite

1. The opposite of **+4** is **−4**, four units across zero.
2. Its absolute value is **|+4|=4**, different from opposite −4.
3. For −4, the opposite and absolute value happen to be four. One coinciding example does not make the concepts identical.
4. At **0**, there is no distance to zero, so **|0|=0**.
5. Zero’s opposite is also zero. P and Q merge into one location without adding a unit step; |0| is not one.

Do not change the measurement reference silently. From −4 to +3, travel four intervals to zero and three more, totaling seven. In contrast, |−4|=4 measures only from the start to zero. Both are nonnegative distances, but their different endpoints produce different values.

Order differs from absolute value too. The absolute value nine for −9 exceeds the absolute value three for −3, while signed order gives −9<−3. One statement compares distance to zero and the other compares positions. Identify the requested quantity before using digit size.

Changing P clears old feedback and the reflected point. Observe the new position, measure distance, then check. Compare negative six, positive six and zero, explaining similarities and differences. At zero the distance segment has zero length rather than an invented unit step.

**Family challenge:** draw an integer line from −12 to +12, marking −6, 0 and +6. Compare positions and distances to zero, then move seven right from −4 and separately report endpoint, signed change and distance. A parent changes the start or direction and asks whether the path reaches, crosses or stays on one side of zero. Add three fictional Celsius readings without measuring or looking up weather.

**For a parent:** ask where each distance starts and ends, using positive and zero counterexamples to examine claims. A remove-the-minus slogan alone is insufficient; symmetry, unit intervals, distance segments and spoken reasoning all count. **Takeaway:** absolute value is nonnegative distance to zero. Equal distances need not mean equal positions, and an opposite is another relationship.
