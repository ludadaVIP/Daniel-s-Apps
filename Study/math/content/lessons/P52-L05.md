## 平均分，先明确总量与份数

小数除以正整数，可以表示把总量平均分成几份。每一份必须一样多，**每份量 × 份数 = 总量** 是重要检查。

除数不能为零。本课练习先选可以精确写到千分位的小数；模型也允许探索不能在三位小数结束的分享，明确区分精确分数与近似显示。

### 例 1 · 1.5 ÷ 4

1. 1.5 = 1.500，即 1,500 个千分之一。
2. 把这些相同小单位平均分成 4 份：1,500 ÷ 4 = 375。
3. 每份为 **375 个千分之一 = 0.375**。
4. 检查：0.375×4 = 1.500，精确回到原数。

### 例 2 · 7.2 ÷ 6

1. 7.2 是 72 个十分之一。
2. 72 ÷ 6 = 12 个十分之一。
3. 12 个十分之一 = **1.2**，并不是 12 个一，也不是 12 个百分之一。
4. 检查：1.2×6 = 7.2。总量超过 6 个整体，所以每份可以超过 1。

### 什么情况下三位小数还不够？

1 升平均分 3 杯，精确每杯是 **1/3 升**。若只显示 0.333 升，三份共 0.999 升，比 1 升少 0.001 升。因此要写 **1÷3 ≈ 0.333**，不能用精确等号冒充。

2 升分 3 份，显示到千分位时每份约 0.667 升；三个近似值会合成 2.001 升。差别来自舍入，不代表精确分享破坏了总量。模型各份的精确量仍为 2/3 升，三个精确分数合成 2 升。

0 升分给 4 杯，每杯为 0 升；“没有总量”与“分给零个人”不是同一件事。

探索器先用相同宽度表示相同份额，再显示每份的精确值。三位小数仅在能精确结束时使用等号；其他情况下使用约等号，提示还有更细单位需要分。

**小检查：** 总量固定，份数从 3 变为 6，每份为何减半？用两个除法式或图解释。

## Identify the total and equal-share count

Dividing a decimal by a positive whole number can describe equal sharing. Every share must be equal. **Amount per share × share count = total** is an important check.

The divisor cannot be zero. Initial exercises use values that terminate exactly by thousandths. The model also permits shares that do not end at three decimal places and distinguishes exact fractions from approximate displays.

### Example 1 · 1.5 ÷ 4

1. 1.5 = 1.500, or 1,500 thousandths.
2. Share these matching units equally four ways: 1,500 ÷ 4 = 375.
3. Each share is **375 thousandths = 0.375**.
4. Check: 0.375×4 = 1.500, exactly recovering the original.

### Example 2 · 7.2 ÷ 6

1. 7.2 is 72 tenths.
2. 72 ÷ 6 = 12 tenths.
3. Twelve tenths equal **1.2**, not twelve ones or twelve hundredths.
4. Check: 1.2×6 = 7.2. Since the total exceeds six wholes, each share can exceed one.

### When are three decimal places insufficient?

One litre shared among three cups gives exactly **1/3 litre** each. Displaying 0.333 litre per cup gives a combined 0.999 litre, short by 0.001. Write **1÷3 ≈ 0.333**, rather than claim exact equality.

For two litres shared three ways, each display rounds to about 0.667 litre. Three rounded displays total 2.001 litres. Rounding creates this discrepancy; the exact sharing still preserves the total. The model’s exact shares remain 2/3 litre, which total two litres.

Zero litres shared among four cups gives zero in each. A zero total is not the same as sharing among zero recipients.

The explorer first represents equal shares with equal widths, then shows their exact values. It uses equality for decimals that end exactly by thousandths and an approximate sign otherwise, indicating that finer units remain to be shared.
