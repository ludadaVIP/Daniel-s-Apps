# 圆周角与圆心角关系

## 先选不含顶点的弧

A、B、P在同一圆上且互不重合，∠APB的顶点在圆周，两边经过A、B，这样的角叫圆周角。它所截的弧，是A、B之间不含P的那条弧。圆周角等于这条弧度数的一半，也就是对应圆心扫角的一半；若弧是大弧，对应扫角可能超过180°。不能不看P的位置，就把任意较小圆心角除以二。

同一个弦AB把圆分成两条弧。位于弦同侧的圆上顶点，截到同一条不含它们的弧，所以圆周角相等。顶点换到弦另一侧，截到的是另一条弧，两弧合计360°，对应的圆周角合计180°，一般互补而非相等。旋转图纸不会改变同侧或异侧关系。

### 例 1：80°还是280°

选定小弧AB为80°。P在另一条大弧上时，不含P的弧是80°，所以∠APB=40°。把P移到80°小弧内部，不含P的弧改为360−80=280°，所以∠APB=140°。答案从40变140，并不是定理失效，而是所截弧变了。两个角的和正好180°。

可以在A=0°、B=80°、P=220°的具体布局中连OP理解40°。小圆心角AOP与BOP均为140°，两组三角形各有两条等半径，故P处底角均(180−140)/2=20°。此图OP位于∠APB内部，两角相加为40°。若O落在别的位置，证明可能要用两个角相减；一般定理始终以不含顶点的弧表达，避免依赖某一种画法。

### 例 2：直径角与圆内接四边形

若AB为直径，任取不同于A、B的圆上P，不含P的弧都是半圆180°，所以∠APB=90°。这就是直径所对圆周角为直角。P在圆上不可省略；若P在圆内或圆外，仅给AB是一条直径并不能保证∠APB=90°。

A、B、C、D依次位于同一圆上时，对角A与C分别截BD的两条互补弧。两弧合计360°，各取一半得∠A+∠C=180°。已知A=70°，则C=110°。不是所有四边形的对角都互补；普通四边形只知道四角总和360°，还需要共圆依据才能套这个定理。

## 看清顶点再计算

工作台允许选定逆时针A→B的扫角，并把P放在这条弧或另一条弧上。彩色始终标出真正不含P的所截弧。先用手沿弧指出“这条弧不经过P”，再求一半。选180°时两边都给90°；选大于180°的扫角时，也按明确的选弧与顶点位置判断，不自动取小角。

家长可追问：“P是否确实在同一圆上？图上是哪一条弧？顶点换边时你换弧了吗？”关于等腰三角形的解释仍需人工核验。数值正确可能来自凑巧折半，必须把前提和弧的身份写明。离堂用80°案例分别解释40°与140°，把计算与空间位置连接起来。

# Inscribed angles and central angles

## Select the arc excluding the vertex

Let distinct A, B and P lie on one circle. The inscribed angle APB has its vertex on the circumference and arms through A and B. Its intercepted arc is the arc AB that excludes P. The angle equals half that arc measure, or half the corresponding central sweep. A major intercepted arc may require a reflex sweep above 180°. Do not automatically halve the smaller angle between OA and OB.

Different on-circle vertices on the same side of chord AB intercept the same arc, giving equal angles. A vertex on the other side intercepts the other arc. Those arcs partition 360°, so the two inscribed angles sum to 180° and are generally supplementary, rather than equal. Turning the diagram preserves these side relationships.

### Example 1: eighty or two hundred eighty

Suppose the selected minor arc AB is 80°. With P on the other, major arc, the arc excluding P is 80°, giving APB=40°. Move P into the selected minor arc: now the intercepted arc is 360−80=280°, giving APB=140°. The theorem is unchanged; the intercepted arc has changed. Forty plus one hundred forty is 180°.

For concrete positions A=0°, B=80° and P=220°, join OP. Both minor central angles AOP and BOP are 140°. Equal radii make their base angles at P each twenty degrees. Here OP lies inside APB, so adding gives forty. Other layouts may require subtracting two angles. Expressing the theorem through the arc excluding the vertex handles all these positions consistently.

### Example 2: a diameter and opposite angles

If AB is a diameter, an on-circle P distinct from its endpoints intercepts a semicircle of 180°, giving APB=90°. The on-circle premise matters: an interior or exterior P need not give a right angle merely because AB is a diameter.

For A, B, C and D in order on one circle, opposite angles A and C intercept the two complementary arcs BD. Halving their total 360° gives A+C=180°. Thus A=70° implies C=110°. An arbitrary quadrilateral only has total interior-angle sum 360°; concyclicity is needed for this opposite-angle theorem.

The lab lets you choose a counterclockwise sweep A→B and place P on that selected arc or the other arc. The coloured intercepted arc always excludes P. Point along it before calculating its half. A diameter gives ninety degrees on either side, while major sweeps require the same careful arc selection.
