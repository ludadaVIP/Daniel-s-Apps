# 点、线、射线、角

## 先看对象怎样延伸

点表示位置，没有长度、宽度或大小。屏幕上画一个小圆点，是为了让眼睛看见位置，不是说数学点真的占着圆形面积。字母 A、B、O 给点命名，同一个字母在同一图中应指同一位置。

线段有两个端点，端点之间的部分有限，能量长度。射线从一个端点出发，沿一个方向无限延伸；直线则沿两个方向无限延伸，没有端点。有限屏幕不可能画出无限，因此用箭头说明继续方向。纸边或屏幕裁切处不是几何端点，线上标记的两点也未必是端点。

### 例 1：AB 的字母顺序何时重要

从 A 出发经过 B 并继续，叫射线 AB，起点是 A。射线 BA 从 B 出发经过 A，起点与方向都不同，不能随意把它们当同一个对象。

线段 AB 与线段 BA 则表示相同的有限部分；经过 A、B 的直线 AB 和 BA 也相同。若 A、B 是不同两点，线段有两个端点、射线一个、直线零个。零端点不等于直线不存在，而是在两方向都没有终止处。

在工作台切换对象，先根据箭头和端点说出定义，再填写有限端点数。不要因为画了 A、B 两个小点就每次都答二：它们也可以只是直线上的位置标记。

### 例 2：角由共同端点和方向确定

射线 OA、OB 的共同端点 O 是角的顶点。用三个字母命名为 ∠AOB 时，顶点必须在中间。这里讨论指定的 0°–180° 转角，不让同一对射线的另一侧优角混入问题。

若射线 OC 位于 ∠AOB 内部，∠AOC=35°、∠COB=25°，则原角被分成两个不重叠的相邻部分，∠AOB=35°+25°=60°。若 OC 在外部，就不能不检查位置直接把两个数相加。

将 OA、OB 画出的部分加长，两方向之间仍转过六十度；把两条射线一起旋转，整体朝向变了，夹角仍六十度。角的大小不是围出的“开口长度”，也不靠两个端点距离单独决定。工作台可固定转角，分别改变长度和整体朝向，检查这个不变量。

## 定义先于图形印象

锐角小于 90°，直角为 90°，钝角在 90° 与 180° 之间，平角为 180°。本工作台的活动角限制在 10°–170°，以免标签在重合射线上遮挡；这不表示数学上不存在零角或平角。直角可以任意转向，不必是一条水平边配一条竖直边才算直角。

画一条看似很长的射线，仍只有一个端点；画一个边很短的钝角，也仍可比边很长的锐角大。家长请孩子指出共同端点、每条射线方向，以及本次改变究竟是画法还是夹角。定义和不变量比“我觉得这边更长所以角更大”可靠。

# Points, lines, rays and angles

## Identify how an object continues

A point specifies a position without length, width or size. A visible dot is a drawing convention, not a claim that the mathematical point occupies a circular area. Letters name positions and should refer consistently to the same point within a diagram.

A segment has two endpoints and finite measurable length. A ray starts at one endpoint and continues indefinitely in one direction. A line continues indefinitely in both directions and has no endpoints. Arrows indicate extension because a finite screen cannot display infinity. Paper or screen edges are not geometric ends, and marked points need not be endpoints.

### Example 1: when does the order AB matter?

Ray AB starts at A, passes through B and continues. Ray BA starts at B and passes through A in the opposite direction. They are different rays. Segment AB and segment BA, however, name the same finite part, and line AB and line BA name the same line through distinct A and B.

A segment has two endpoints, a ray one and a line none. No endpoints does not mean the line is absent; it has no terminating position in either direction. Switch objects in the workshop and inspect the arrows before entering the count. Two labelled dots may simply mark positions on a line.

### Example 2: a vertex and two directions define an angle

Rays OA and OB share endpoint O, the vertex. In the three-letter name ∠AOB, O must be in the middle. Here the specified turn is between zero and 180 degrees; do not silently replace it with the reflex angle on the other side.

If ray OC lies inside ∠AOB and the adjacent parts measure 35° and 25°, they partition the original angle into non-overlapping parts. Thus ∠AOB=60°. If OC lies outside, the same addition cannot be assumed without checking positions.

Lengthening the drawn arms does not change their relative directions. Rotating both rays together changes their orientation but preserves the angle. The size of an angle is not the gap length between two drawn endpoints. Fix the turn in the workshop, then change length and whole-figure rotation separately to investigate this invariant.

## Definitions are stronger than impressions

An acute angle is below 90°, a right angle equals 90°, an obtuse angle lies between 90° and 180°, and a straight angle is 180°. The activity limits its moving angle to 10°–170° to keep labels readable; this is a display choice rather than a restriction on mathematical angles. A right angle remains right when rotated.

A long-looking ray still has one endpoint. A short-armed obtuse angle can exceed a long-armed acute angle. A parent asks for the common endpoint, the ray directions and which change affected the drawing rather than the angle. Use definitions and invariants instead of judging size from arm length.
