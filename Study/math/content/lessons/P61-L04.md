## 原来的每小份，还可以继续分

把 3/4 L 水平均分给两人，不需要把“三个四分之一”硬分成整数个四分之一。每个四分之一都能再平均切两份，变成八分之一升。分配以后总水量不能增加，也不能因为每人量较小就把一升标尺改掉。

分数除以正整数表示把同一个分数量平均分成这些份。若除数为一，保留全部；大于一时正水量的每份会变小；原总量为零而人数为正，每人零升也合法。人数不能为零，这与零作为总量是不同情形。

### 例 1 · 四分之三升给两人

1. 总水量 **3/4 L**，正整数份数为 **2**。
2. 每个 **1/4 L** 再平均分二，成为 **1/8 L**；原三个四分之一一共六个八分之一。
3. 六个小份轮流分给两人，每人三份。
4. 每人 **3/8 L**，即 **3/4÷2=3/(4×2)**。
5. 核回：**2×3/8=6/8=3/4 L**，恰好恢复总水量。

工作台分三步：先细分，再给相同人数分颜色，最后核对每人量。每种颜色得到相同的小份数，粗框始终一升。换总量分数或人数会回到原图，避免把旧答案带进新情境。

### 例 2 · 三分之二升分四份

1. 总水量 **2/3 L**，平均分给 **4 份**。
2. 每个三分之一再分四份，小单位变为 **1/12 L**。
3. 两个三分之一共有八小份，四份各分两小份。
4. 每份 **2/12=1/6 L**，也就是 **2/3÷4=2/(3×4)**。
5. 核回 **4×1/6=4/6=2/3 L**，既没有增加也没有丢掉水。

由此解释一般关系 **a/b÷k=a/(bk)**，其中 k 是正整数。不一定要先把分子除以 k 才能算；三不能整除二并不意味着 3/4 不能分两人。可以先细分原小份，保持原整体不变，再分配每一份。

分子分母同时乘二会把 1/2 改写为 2/4，仍是原来半升；它不是半升给两人后的每人量。若每人都得到 2/4，两人合起来一升，比原半升多。正确每人 1/4 L，两人合半升。用乘回去核查，能辨认“等值改写”和“数量平均分”的区别。

总量超过一也适用。5/4 m 彩带平均五段，每段 **5/20=1/4 m**；五段恢复 5/4 m。分数结果尽量用精确形式，别把一段长度四舍五入得过短，再期待所有段能精确拼回原长度。

**带走一个发现：** 分母乘人数不是口诀中的偶然变化，而是每个旧小份继续等分。家长可让孩子指出同量颜色，并问所有人重合起来有多少。出口题为 5/6 L 分两份，要同时给每份量与乘回核查。

## Existing parts can be divided again

Sharing 3/4 L between two does not require an integer number of quarter-pieces per person. Divide every quarter in half to make eighth-litres. Sharing must preserve total water and the one-litre reference, rather than changing the meaning of the whole.

Dividing a fraction by a positive whole number shares that fractional amount equally. A divisor of one retains all; a larger count makes each share of positive water smaller. Zero total water with positive people gives zero each. Zero people is a different, invalid sharing count.

### Example 1 · Three quarters of a litre between two

1. Total water is **3/4 L**, shared into **2** equal portions.
2. Split every **1/4 L** into two **1/8 L** pieces, making six eighths altogether.
3. Distribute six pieces cyclically between two people; each gets three.
4. Each amount is **3/8 L**, so **3/4÷2=3/(4×2)**.
5. Check **2×3/8=6/8=3/4 L**, restoring all the water.

The workshop first subdivides, then allocates equal coloured pieces, then checks. Thick frames remain one litre. Changing the fraction or number of people resets the construction and checked result.

### Example 2 · Two thirds of a litre into four

1. Share **2/3 L** into **4** equal portions.
2. Every third divides into four, making **1/12 L** pieces.
3. Eight small pieces are shared among four portions, two each.
4. Each receives **2/12=1/6 L**, giving **2/3÷4=2/(3×4)**.
5. Check **4×1/6=4/6=2/3 L**, with nothing added or lost.

Thus **a/b÷k=a/(bk)** for a positive whole number k. The numerator need not be divisible by k first. Three failing to divide into two whole quarter-pieces does not make the water impossible to share: refine the original parts and distribute them.

Multiplying numerator and denominator of 1/2 by two gives 2/4, the same original half-litre. It is not the amount each person receives. Two people each receiving 2/4 L would need a whole litre. Correct shares of 1/4 L restore half a litre. Multiplication back distinguishes equivalent rewriting from actually dividing a quantity.

Amounts above one work too. A 5/4 m ribbon shared into five equal lengths gives **5/20=1/4 m** each; five restore 5/4 m. Retain exact lengths rather than rounding every piece and expecting the rounded lengths to reconstruct the original exactly.

**Takeaway:** multiplying the denominator by the people count reflects subdivision of every original part. A parent can identify equal coloured allocations and ask what they make together. Find each share of 5/6 L divided by two in the exit ticket, then multiply back.
