# 指数函数的增长

纸上两种规则都从3开始：每天增加3，或者每天变为2倍。前两项一样，不意味着往后一样。先预测第四天，再列数表比较。

## 一条核心想法

**指数模型在相等输入步长上保持相同倍率。** 取y=C aˣ，C>0、a>0且a≠1。每增加一个单位输入，输出乘a。C是x=0时的初量；a是倍率，不能把百分比增长率直接填进去。若把a=1纳入模型会得到常函数，通常不称为本课指数函数。

a>1时增长，0<a<1时衰减。实数输入域下，正底数的幂始终大于0，值域为(0,∞)。本课实验另给有限输入范围，必须按题目范围研究，不能将图像窗口当作定义域。

### 例1：相同倍率与相同差

纸上模型N(n)=3×2ⁿ，仅允许非负整数天。n=0、1、2、3、4时输出3、6、12、24、48。相邻项比值始终2，差依次3、6、12、24，并不恒定。

相比L(n)=3+3n，输出3、6、9、12、15。两个模型通过最初两点，却在n=2分开。只有题目明确恒定倍率模型，才能从相应观测恢复指数规则；有限资料本身不能证明真实过程永久服从此模型。

### 例2：衰减保留的是多少

从100单位开始，每年减少20%，每年保留80%，所以倍率0.8。两年后100×0.8²=64单位，不是减两次20得到60，也不是乘0.2两次得到4。百分比每次作用于当时的量。

80单位每步减半，数表80、40、20、10、5……。第3步首次不超过11，因为第2步仍为20。任何有限步数都不等于0；“越来越接近”与“达到”不同。对连续y=(1/2)ˣ，向右趋近y=0，y=0叫水平渐近线，而非某个被忽略的零点。

## 图表实验

先选择整数步域或连续域，再预测输出。半步在整数域不允许查询；切换连续模型后才有3√2等值。整数域只画散点，连续域才画曲线。图上看不到超窗的点时，用精确数表读取它，不把点压到图框上沿。改变图窗或显示图表保留预测。

## 迁移与家长核对

解释“每步减少20%”与“每步减少20单位”差在哪。家长让孩子指出初量、倍率、一步的时间单位及允许输入，再用另一种表示核对。出口首次阈值须同时给前一步和本步，不能只列一个满足的值。后续对数课将反过来求未知步数。

# Exponential growth

Two paper rules begin at three: add three every day, or double every day. Matching the first two entries does not make the later values equal. Predict day four before building a comparison table.

## One central idea

**An exponential model preserves a constant multiplier over equal input steps.** In y=C aˣ take C>0, a>0 and a≠1. Each unit increase in input multiplies the output by a. C is the initial quantity at x=0. The multiplier a differs from the percentage growth rate. Allowing a=1 would produce a constant model, usually excluded from the exponential functions studied here.

A base above one gives growth; a base between zero and one gives decay. On the real input domain, positive-base powers stay positive and have range (0,∞). The experiment stipulates a finite input domain instead. Study that stated range of inputs; a graph window never defines the mathematical domain.

### Example 1: Constant ratios versus constant differences

The paper model N(n)=3×2ⁿ allows only nonnegative whole days. At n=0,1,2,3,4 its outputs are 3,6,12,24,48. Successive ratios are always two, while successive differences 3,6,12,24 are not constant.

Compare L(n)=3+3n, whose outputs are 3,6,9,12,15. Both models pass through the first two observations but disagree at n=2. A constant-multiplier premise must be given before the observations determine an exponential rule. A finite record cannot prove that a real process will follow the model indefinitely.

### Example 2: Identify the retained proportion

Starting from 100 units and reducing by 20% annually retains 80% each year, so the multiplier is 0.8. After two years, 100×0.8²=64 units. Subtracting 20 twice gives the incorrect 60, while multiplying by 0.2 twice incorrectly gives four. Each percentage applies to the quantity at that time.

Halving 80 units per step gives 80,40,20,10,5,… . Step three is the first at most 11 because step two still gives 20. No finite step reaches zero: approaching and reaching are different claims. For continuous y=(1/2)ˣ, the graph approaches y=0 to the right. That line is a horizontal asymptote, not a hidden zero of the function.

## Graph and table experiment

Choose whole-step or continuous time before predicting. Half steps are excluded from a whole-step domain; values such as 3√2 become available only after a continuous model is stipulated. Plot whole-step values as unjoined points and continuous values as a curve. If a point leaves the displayed window, read its exact value from the table rather than clamping it to the frame. Changing the window or revealing the graph preserves the mathematical prediction.

## Transfer and parent check

Explain the difference between losing 20% and losing 20 units each step. A parent can ask for the initial quantity, multiplier, time unit per step and admissible inputs, then audit using another representation. A first-threshold exit answer needs both the previous and current step, not merely one successful value. The logarithm lesson will reverse this process to find an unknown number of steps.
