# 二次方程与零点

## 先整理，再命名

一元二次方程能整理为 ax²+bx+c=0，其中 a、b、c 是已知实数，a≠0，未知数只有一个。b、c 可以为零，a 却不能为零，因为它决定整理后的最高次数。看见 x² 只是线索，不能代替合并同类项。这里研究实数解：让原等式成立的实数 x 才是解，未知数的名字或所在位置不影响这个定义。

把原式左边减右边，记为 f(x)。对同一个输入，原左=原右，当且仅当 f(x)=0。因此解方程与找这个差函数的零点是同一问题。零点指横坐标 x；图上对应点是 (x,0)，纵坐标零不是每道题的根值。工作台的曲线始终画“原左减原右”，即使原右边并非零，也按这个差来解释。

### 例 1：两个根、重根和没有实根

x²−5x+6=0 可写为 (x−2)(x−3)=0，根为2和3。代入2得4−10+6=0，代入3得9−15+6=0。两个候选各自正确；由每个因子为零的完整分支，还能说明没有遗漏。答案集合 {2,3} 与 {3,2} 相同，只写2则不完整。

对 (x−2)²=0，两个相同因子都给 x=2，这是二重根，但只有一个不同实数值。图上只有一个接触位置，不能人为画出两个分开的交点。对 x²+1=0，需要 x²=−1，而实数平方非负，因此没有实根。“没有根”与“根是0”不同：0代入原左边为1，并不成立。

### 例 2：含 x² 却不是二次方程

x²+x=x²+3 两边同时减 x²，得到 x=3，是一次方程。x²=x² 则得到0=0，是恒等式，所有实数都满足，不能只答 x=0。x²+1=x² 得到1=0，是矛盾等式，没有实数满足。三种情况都曾出现 x²，整理后的结论却不同。

还要留意更高次数：x³+x²=0 的最高次是三，不属于本课的一元二次方程。判断要看完整整理式，不能只抽取自己熟悉的三项。若只因 a=0 就宣布“无解”，也会错过一次方程的唯一解和恒等式的所有解。

## 用图预测，用原式核验

先猜有几个根，再显示零点核验。横轴窗口只展示局部；把窗口放宽会改变可见的点数，不会改变方程本身。读图所得小数位置可用来估计，最终精确根要用代数或代回检查。代回一个候选只能证明它正确，不能自动证明根集完整；完整性还来自因子分支、平方分支或后续的求根公式。

家长可让孩子依次说：“整理后最高次项是什么？为什么系数不为零？这个输入使原左右两边各等于多少？是否列完所有不同值？”若难点在移项或平方根两分支，先回到相应桥接课，不必加快速度。

# Quadratic equations and zeros

## Collect before classifying

A quadratic equation in one variable can be written ax²+bx+c=0 with known real coefficients and a≠0. The linear and constant coefficients may be zero. The quadratic coefficient cannot vanish because it establishes the highest surviving degree. Seeing x² somewhere is a clue, not a classification rule. A real solution is an input that makes the original equality true.

Define f(x) as original left minus original right. For each input, left=right exactly when f(x)=0. Solving the equation therefore means finding zeros of this difference. A zero is an x-value; its plotted point is (x,0). The ordinate zero is not automatically the answer. The laboratory always graphs the difference, including when the original right side is nonzero.

### Example 1: two roots, a repeated root and none

Factor x²−5x+6=0 as (x−2)(x−3)=0. The roots are two and three. Substitution gives 4−10+6=0 and 9−15+6=0. Each check establishes that a candidate works; exhaustive factor branches establish that the list is complete. The sets {2,3} and {3,2} are equal, but two alone is incomplete.

For (x−2)²=0, both identical factors give x=2. This is a double root with one distinct real value and one contact position on the graph. For x²+1=0, the required square would be −1, impossible over the reals. No roots is different from a root of zero: substituting zero gives one on the left.

### Example 2: cancellation changes the degree

Subtract x² from both sides of x²+x=x²+3 to obtain x=3, a linear equation. The equation x²=x² reduces to 0=0, an identity satisfied by every real number. In contrast, x²+1=x² reduces to contradiction 1=0, satisfied by none. Their surface appearances all include x², but their collected meanings differ.

An equation such as x³+x²=0 has highest degree three and is outside this quadratic lesson. Inspect the entire collected expression rather than selecting three familiar terms. Nor does a=0 automatically mean no solutions: a linear equation may have one, and an identity all reals.

## Predict, then audit

Predict the number of roots before revealing markers. The plotting window is local; widening it may reveal more points without changing the equation. Pixel positions provide approximations, while final roots require exact algebra and substitution. One successful substitution does not establish completeness; factor branches, square-root branches or the later formula provide that argument. A parent can ask which highest-degree term survives, why its coefficient is nonzero, what both original sides equal, and whether every distinct root has been listed.
