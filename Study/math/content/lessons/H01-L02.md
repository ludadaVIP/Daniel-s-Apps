# 交集、并集和补集

音乐组A和运动组B可能有共同成员。若活动邀请“至少参加一个组”的人，同时参加两个组的人当然也被邀请。“或”在这里包含“两者都”，不是“只能选一个”。先分清语言，再写符号。

## 一条核心想法

**集合运算是按每个元素的身份筛选。** 设全集U={1,2,3,4,5,6}，A={1,2,4}，B={2,3,4}，A与B都包含于U。

- A∩B：既在A又在B，对应“且”。
- A∪B：在A或在B，至少在一个，对应包含两者同时的“或”。
- U内A的补集Aᶜ：在U中但不在A，先说明U才能确定结果。
- A∖B：在A但不在B；它不必等于B∖A。

图中圆只是表示集合关系，不用面积大小暗示人数。四区依次是仅A、交叠、仅B、两者之外；它们不重叠且合起来覆盖U。空区标∅，表示没有元素，不是往该区放进一个名叫∅的数字。

### 例1：同一资料，四种问题

共同成员2、4同时通过A与B的检查，所以A∩B={2,4}。至少通过一个检查的有1、2、3、4，所以A∪B={1,2,3,4}。交叠里的2、4既不删除也不重复列。

在U中删去A的1、2、4，得到Aᶜ={3,5,6}。只从A出发删去同时在B的2、4，得到A∖B={1}；反向B∖A={3}。补集从全集出发，差集从左边集合出发，不要混淆起点。

### 例2：集合不变，全集改变

仍令A={1,2,4}，把U改为{1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8}。A没有变化，补集却变成{3,5,6,7,8}。这不是矛盾：补集问题里的参照范围改变了。7、8原来不在U，不应提前列入原来的补集。

同一U下，A∩Aᶜ=∅，因为一个元素不能既在A又不在A；A∪Aᶜ=U，因为U中每个元素必在A或不在A。换成另一个全集后，要同时更新补集与右侧的U。

## 工作台：先筛，再画

先在A、B成员框里调整编号，写出交集预测再显示分区。把运算改为并集，特别检查交叠编号是否保留。再改补集并扩大U，解释新增的7、8为什么默认落在两圆外；如果手动把它们加入A，它们就应离开A的补集。缩小U时，域外编号同时从A、B移除，保持两者是U子集。

若A={1,3}、B={2,4}，交集应写∅而不是{0}。若A=B，交集与并集都等于A，但差集为空。运算名称相似不代表结果必不同，关键是定义。

## 自己说清与迁移

给一张纸上的六个虚构角色贴“戴帽子”“拿书”标签，画四区并说明“或”的含义。不要采集同学个人资料。家长可故意把交叠删去或把U外编号加入补集，让孩子逐元素纠正。最后用“在U中且不在A”解释补集，再用“在A中且不在B”解释差集，而不是只记符号外形。

# Intersection, union and complement

Music group A and sport group B can share members. An invitation to anyone in at least one group includes those in both. Here “or” is inclusive: it does not mean “exactly one”. Translate the language before manipulating symbols.

## One central idea

**A set operation filters elements according to membership.** Let U={1,2,3,4,5,6}, A={1,2,4}, B={2,3,4}. Both A and B are subsets of the stated universe U.

- A∩B contains elements in both A and B: and.
- A∪B contains elements in at least one of A and B: inclusive or.
- The complement Aᶜ in U contains elements of U outside A.
- A∖B contains elements of A outside B; reversing the order can change the result.

The circles encode membership, not cardinality by area. The four regions are A only, overlap, B only and outside both. They are disjoint and together cover U. A region labelled ∅ contains no elements; the symbol is not an extra numerical member.

### Example 1: Four questions about the same data

Two and four pass both membership tests, so A∩B={2,4}. One, two, three and four pass at least one test, so A∪B={1,2,3,4}. Keep the overlap once: neither delete it nor count it twice in a set listing.

Remove A’s 1,2,4 from U to get Aᶜ={3,5,6}. Start from A and remove its members also in B to obtain A∖B={1}. Conversely B∖A={3}. A complement starts from the universe; a difference starts from the left-hand set. These starting collections need not be equal.

### Example 2: Same A, different universe

Keep A={1,2,4} and change U to {1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8}. The complement becomes {3,5,6,7,8}. Nothing about A changed; the reference universe changed. Seven and eight did not belong in the original complement because they were not in the original U.

For a fixed universe, A∩Aᶜ=∅: no element is both inside and outside A. Also A∪Aᶜ=U: every element of U is either in A or outside A. A new universe requires updating both the complement and the U on the right.

## Workbench: filter before revealing

Change A and B membership checkboxes. Predict the intersection before showing regions. Switch to union and check that overlap remains. Next select complement and expand U. Newly available seven and eight start outside both circles; adding either to A removes it from A’s complement. Shrinking U removes out-of-domain members from A and B too, preserving their status as subsets of U.

If A={1,3} and B={2,4}, their intersection is ∅, not {0}. If A=B, their intersection and union both equal A, while their difference is empty. Different operation names do not guarantee different results: the definitions decide.

## Transfer and parent review

Use six fictional characters on paper with two labels such as “wears a hat” and “holds a book”. Place them in four regions and explain inclusive or. A parent can deliberately delete overlap or insert an element outside U into a complement, asking the learner to repair the result element by element. Conclude with words: complement means in U and not in A; difference means in A and not in B. This checks meaning rather than recognition of symbols.
