# 割线逼近切线

割线通过曲线上横坐标不同的两个点。固定一个点A(a,f(a))，把另一个点写成B(a+h,f(a+h))，可以明确“第二点靠近第一点”究竟改变什么。非零的h是横向增量，不是函数高度，也不是画面里的像素距离。

## 固定a，让非零h趋近0

当前割线斜率为[f(a+h)−f(a)]/h，h≠0。h为正时第二点在右，为负时在左。两侧都要观察；只取正的几个小数，尚不能断言两侧有共同极限。

如果差商在h从两侧趋近0时趋同一个有限数L，就以该有限斜率定义这里的切线。当前非零h的斜率与极限L是不同对象。h很小不意味着它严格等于0，也不意味着当前斜率已严格等于L。

数表帮助发现规律，代数则能说明所有足够近的非零h。原差商在0处仍没有值；将一个表达式在穿孔邻域内化简，不会补上原式的除零点。

### 例1：平方函数的精确误差

f(x)=x²，展开(a+h)²−a²=2ah+h²。因为h≠0，可以约去h，差商等于2a+h。h→0时，额外的一项h趋0，所以两侧共同极限为2a。

固定a=1，h=1/2时斜率5/2；h=1/10时为21/10；h=−1/10时为19/10。正负两侧分别在2的上下。h=1/100时2.01距离2仅0.01，但仍不等于2。差商与2a的精确误差恰为h，这比“屏幕看起来重合”更有解释力。

### 例2：三次函数不是同一个差商公式

g(x)=x³，展开(a+h)³−a³=3a²h+3ah²+h³，除非零h后为3a²+3ah+h²。固定a时，后两项从两侧趋0，故切线斜率3a²。

在a=1、h=1/10时，当前斜率3+3/10+1/100=331/100，而极限为3。在a=0时，差商h²趋0；绿色切线水平，但曲线并不变成常数函数。以后还会利用这个例子反驳“导数0必是极值”的猜想。

## 工作台怎样保留边界

选择平方或三次函数，固定a，再从正负增量中改变h。金线是当前割线，绿线是由代数求出的切线。h=0时差商显示undefined，金线消失；绿色切线并不因此消失，因为它来自极限，不来自0/0。

改变h时，未改变的导数预测保留，当前差商改变则要重新计算。改变视窗或核验显示不改变数学答案；竖直平移原函数也不改变两种斜率。精确表同时保留正负h和0，防止把“趋近”偷换成“取到”。

出口需推导对任意a成立的平方函数差商。家长可以追问：“你在约去h时用了哪个条件？”“为什么只看三个小数还不够？”答得出条件，才能进入对极限的单独研究。

# Secants approaching tangents

A secant passes through curve points with different inputs. Fix A(a,f(a)) and write the moving point as B(a+h,f(a+h)). This makes the change precise: h is a nonzero input increment, not the output height or a pixel distance on the screen.

## Fix a and let nonzero h approach zero

The current secant slope is [f(a+h)−f(a)]/h for h≠0. Positive h places B to the right and negative h to the left. Both sides matter; a few positive decimals do not establish two-sided agreement.

If the quotients tend to one finite number L from both sides, that finite slope defines the tangent used here. A particular nonzero-h slope and its limit are distinct objects. Small h is not literally zero, nor does it make the current slope exactly equal to L.

Tables suggest patterns, while algebra can explain every nearby nonzero increment. Simplifying an expression on a punctured neighbourhood does not fill its excluded zero denominator.

### Example 1: Exact error for the square function

For x², expand (a+h)²−a²=2ah+h². Since h is nonzero, cancellation gives quotient 2a+h. As h approaches zero from either side, the additional h term vanishes, leaving limit 2a.

At a=1, h=1/2 gives 5/2, h=1/10 gives 21/10, and h=−1/10 gives 19/10. The slopes approach two from opposite sides. At h=1/100 the slope is 2.01, close but not equal to two. Its exact error from 2a is h, an explanation stronger than visual overlap.

### Example 2: The cubic has a different quotient

For x³, expansion and cancellation for nonzero h give 3a²+3ah+h². With a fixed, the last two terms tend to zero from both sides, leaving tangent slope 3a².

At a=1 and h=1/10, the current quotient is 331/100 whereas the limit is three. At a=0 the quotient h² tends to zero. The tangent is horizontal without making the function constant. Later this example also challenges the claim that every zero derivative creates an extremum.

## Explore the boundary honestly

Choose square or cubic, fix a and change h on either side. Gold is the current secant and green is the algebraically determined tangent. At h=0 the quotient is undefined and the gold secant disappears. The green tangent can remain because it comes from a limit rather than from 0/0.

Changing h preserves a derivative prediction whose answer stays the same, while a changed quotient requires a new prediction. Display windows and audit visibility preserve mathematical answers. A constant vertical shift preserves both slopes. The exact table includes negative, positive and zero h to distinguish approach from substitution.

For the exit, derive the square-function quotient for arbitrary a. A parent can ask which condition permits cancellation and why three decimal samples do not prove the result. Stating that condition prepares the learner to study limits themselves.
