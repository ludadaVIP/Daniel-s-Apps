# 错误答案的合理性判断

## 合理只是检查的开头

一个结果看起来不太离谱，仍可能算错、漏解、单位错误或依据不足。可以先用数量级、符号、范围和单位快速排除明显问题，再代回原关系检查，最后核是否满足题目全部要求。只通过其中一个检查，不代表整个任务已经完成。

本课分三类：已有反例推翻的错误invalid；现有资料还不能建立的insufficient；已按给定条件完成核验的valid。证据不足不等于结论必假。课程不根据孩子写了几个“正确”“所以”关键词判证明，完整解释由家长阅读。

### 例 1：能代回却不完整

方程x²=9，交“全部实根只有3”。3确实能代回，但−3也能，因为(−3)²=9。因此“全部只有3”有明确反例，分类错误，完整不同实根为−3、3。若题目改问非负几何长度，正根3又可能是正确目标；必须先读题目对象。

给定直角及直角边3、4，斜边5可以由25=9+16、取正长度核验。这不是凭图“像3–4–5”就证明，也不是说任何两条边3、4都推出5。成立的结论要连同前提保存，条件改变后不能照抄。

### 例 2：数值、单位与近似要求

5 m×3 m长方形面积数值15，但写成15 m的单位错，应为15 m²。竖升3、水平4的坡度为75%，却不能添上°变成75°；真正坡角是tan⁻¹(3/4)≈36.9°。数值大小近似或形式相似不表示量的含义相同。

√2≈1.41是按百分位舍入的合法近似，若题目要求精确值，就不能交1.41。直接平方得到1.9881≠2，可核它不是精确根。反过来，近似题如果明确只要两位小数，也不应因为它不等于精确根而宣告近似任务失败。先核任务要求，再核答案。

## 缺少证据与有反例不同

只观测f(0)=0、f(1)=1，有人预测f(2)=2。f=x满足这个预测，f=x²也吻合给定观测却给4。因此现有资料不能唯一核验预测。预测2可能为真，这与“−3也是漏掉的方程根”那种直接推翻完整集合主张的反例不同。

一次代入相等也不能证明一般恒等。x²+2x与5x在3处同为15，在1处分离；反例足以推翻“对所有x相同”。如果要证明正确的一般式，需完整展开或结构依据。必要的合理性检查与充分的证明根据，不要混为一句“看起来对”。

家长可按清单阅读：所求对象→单位→符号与范围→全部解→代回原式→几何或模型前提→证据范围。孩子指出某项错误后，还应写修正量与理由；如果条件不足，说明需补什么，而不是随便选一个看似顺眼的数。改变显示不能改变证据分类，改变实际主张则重新核验。

# Judging whether answers make sense

## Plausibility begins the audit

An answer can look reasonable yet have an arithmetic error, missing solution, wrong unit or insufficient support. Screen magnitude, sign, range and dimensions, then substitute into original relationships and check every requirement. Passing one check does not complete them all.

Distinguish invalid, refuted by evidence; insufficient, not established from the available information; and valid, verified under the stated premises. Insufficient evidence is not proof of falsity. A parent reviews complete arguments instead of scoring reassuring keywords.

### Example 1: substitution without completeness

The claim that the complete real root set of x²=9 contains only three is false even though three passes substitution. Minus three also squares to nine, providing a direct counterexample to completeness. The full set is −3,3. A different question asking for a nonnegative geometric length can correctly retain only three; read the target first.

A given right angle and legs three/four establish positive hypotenuse five through 25=9+16. This uses premises and a theorem, not the appearance of a familiar drawing. Any two arbitrary sides of lengths three and four do not establish five without additional conditions.

### Example 2: dimensions and approximation

A 5 m by 3 m rectangle has numerical area fifteen, but writing fifteen metres gives the wrong dimension; use m². Rise three over run four has percentage grade 75%, not an angle of 75°. The angle is approximately 36.9° by inverse tangent. Similar-looking numbers do not describe interchangeable quantities.

1.41 is a legitimate nearest-hundredth approximation to √2, but cannot replace an exact root. Squaring gives 1.9881 rather than two. Conversely, a task explicitly requesting two decimal places should not be rejected merely because a rounded value differs from the exact root. Establish the requested form before auditing the answer.

With only f(0)=0,f(1)=1 observed, a prediction f(2)=2 is not uniquely verified. It fits f=x, while f=x² fits the observations and gives four. The prediction might still be true; its evidence is insufficient. This differs from directly refuting the claim that a complete root set omits minus three.

One matching substitution is likewise not a general identity proof. x²+2x and 5x agree at three but disagree at one. A counterexample refutes equality for every input; a true identity needs a structural or complete algebraic argument. Necessary screening and sufficient justification are different roles.

A parent can audit target → units → sign/range → completeness → original substitution → premises → scope. After finding an error, supply a corrected quantity and reason. With missing conditions, name the information needed rather than guessing a plausible number. Display changes preserve evidence; a changed mathematical claim requires a new audit.
