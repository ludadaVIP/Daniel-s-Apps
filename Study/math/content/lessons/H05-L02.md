# 向量的加减

先向东3步，再向西5步。路程共8步，最后却在起点西边2步。向量加法追踪的是相继移动后的净变化，而不是把每段长度加成一个方向不明的数。

## 一条核心想法

**和用首尾相接，差用加上反向量。** 将第二向量的尾平移到第一向量的头，再由最初尾指向最后头，得到u+v。平移第二支箭头不改变它的向量值。

u−v=u+(−v)。若u、v从同一点出发，u−v从v的头指向u的头；反过来的箭头是v−u。相加可以交换次序，相减一般不能。零向量是加法中不改变结果的向量，u+(−u)=0。

### 例1：和与差的分量核验

u=(3,2)、v=(−1,4)。先u后v，横向总变化3−1=2，纵向2+4=6，所以u+v=(2,6)。先v后u也到同一个终点，给交换律一个图形解释。

u−v=u+(1,−4)=(4,−2)。同尾图中，从v的头(−1,4)到u的头(3,2)，变化恰是(4,−2)。v−u=(−4,2)反向，两者一般不相等。先圈所求次序，再画箭头，可减少减反的问题。

### 例2：向量结果与最后位置

从A=(1,2)出发，先u=(2,−1)，再v=(−3,4)。净位移为(−1,3)，最终位置A+(u+v)=(0,5)。直接把净位移写成终点，是把向量与点混同。

如果v=−u，首尾路线回到起点，和为0。图中两段非零箭头仍然存在，不能从零合向量推断所走路程为0。共线、反向与零向量也可用同样的加法规则，不必强行画出有面积的平行四边形。

## 路线工作台

先预测结果分量。加法模式还要预测当前起点A对应的最终位置；换首尾次序保留两者答案，因为和及终点不变。换起点只重算位置，和向量的预测保留。

差模式直接画同尾u、v以及从V到U的差箭头，不把它冒充从原点出发的第三段路线。可随后展开精确表，核对每支箭头自己的起点、分量和终点。显示图窗、顺序或核验表时，先说明改变的是数学任务还是表示方式。

## 家长核对与迁移

出口要求解释交换律与差的方向。家长可以问：“这支箭头加在v后，是否真的到u？”分量上，u+v=v+u来自两个实数加法交换律；u−v与v−u互为相反向量，只有u=v时二者都为0才相等。下一课把重复相加、减半和反向统一成数乘。

# Vector addition and subtraction

Three steps east followed by five west travels eight steps but ends two steps west of the start. Vector addition tracks net directed change rather than merely adding route lengths without a direction.

## One central idea

**Add head to tail; subtract by adding an additive inverse.** Translate the second vector so its tail meets the first head, then draw from the original tail to the final head. That is u+v. Translating the second arrow preserves its vector value.

u−v=u+(−v). If u and v have common tails, u−v runs from the head of v to the head of u; reversing that arrow gives v−u. Addition commutes, while subtraction generally does not. The zero vector leaves a sum unchanged, and u+(−u)=0.

### Example 1: Audit sums and differences by components

Let u=(3,2) and v=(−1,4). Following u then v gives horizontal change 3−1=2 and vertical change 2+4=6, hence u+v=(2,6). Following v then u reaches the same endpoint, illustrating commutativity.

For subtraction, u−v=u+(1,−4)=(4,−2). In a common-tail picture, going from v’s head (−1,4) to u’s head (3,2) changes position by exactly (4,−2). Reversing gives v−u=(−4,2), generally a different vector. Identify the requested order before drawing an arrow.

### Example 2: Vector result versus final position

Start at A=(1,2), follow u=(2,−1), then v=(−3,4). Net displacement is (−1,3), while final position is A+(u+v)=(0,5). Reporting the displacement as the endpoint confuses a vector with a point.

If v=−u, the head-to-tail route returns to its start and the sum is zero. Both nonzero journey segments still exist; zero resultant displacement does not imply zero distance travelled. Collinear, opposite and zero vectors use the same addition rule without requiring a positive-area parallelogram picture.

## Route workbench

Predict the resultant components first. Addition mode also asks for the final position from current start A. Swapping route order preserves both answers because neither the sum nor endpoint changes. Moving the start resets only the endpoint task and preserves the free-vector prediction.

Subtraction mode draws common-tail u and v with a difference arrow from V to U. It does not pretend that arrow is a third journey beginning at the origin. Reveal the exact table to audit each arrow’s own tail, components and head. Explain whether a changed window, order or audit table changes the mathematical task or only its representation.

## Parent check and transfer

The exit explains commutativity and subtraction direction. Ask whether appending the proposed difference to v really reaches u. Componentwise, u+v=v+u follows from real-number addition. Differences u−v and v−u are negatives; only when u=v do both vanish and agree. The next lesson unifies repeated addition, halving and reversal through scalar multiplication.
