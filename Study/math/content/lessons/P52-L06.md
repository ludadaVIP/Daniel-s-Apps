## 先解释数量关系，再选运算

应用题不是把所有数字随意组合。先找**总量、每份量、份数、单位**，再判断它们的关系。

- 每份量 × 份数 = 总量。
- 总量 ÷ 份数 = 每份量。
- 两个同单位数量可相加；已经用掉的量从总量中减去。
- 商品总价与付款是不同角色：**付款 − 总价 = 找零**。

### 例 1 · 采购与找零

牛奶每瓶 NZ$1.25，买 3 瓶，带 NZ$5。

1. 1.25 是每瓶的元数，3 是瓶数，5 是付款的元数。
2. 总价：1.25×3 = **NZ$3.75**。
3. 找零：5.00−3.75 = **NZ$1.25**。
4. 检查：3.75+1.25 = 5.00，付款被分成了商品成本与找零。

估算每瓶约 NZ$1.30，3 瓶约 NZ$3.90，说明 NZ$5 应该足够。估算不会代替实际总价，也不需要真的去商店购买。

### 例 2 · 剪布的两步问题

有 4 米布，剪出 3 条各 0.85 米的布条。

1. 已用长度：0.85×3 = **2.55 米**。
2. 剩下长度：4.00−2.55 = **1.45 米**。
3. 检查：2.55+1.45 = 4.00 米；剩余量小于原长度，且非负。
4. 若有人算 4−0.85，只减了一条，不对应已经剪了三条的情境。

### 让单位帮助检查

0.5 升加 3 瓶不能直接合成 3.5 升。若每瓶 0.5 升，三瓶的总容量是 0.5×3 = **1.5 升**。同样，1.525 km 与 1.525 m 数字相同，长度却不同。

模型使用整数分计算金额，保留精确的元与分关系；预算不足时显示“还差多少”，不显示令人困惑的负找零。它不模拟商店现金舍入或任何真实交易。

**家庭挑战：** 使用三个家里已有物品的价格或自拟价格，在假想 NZ$10 预算内计划数量。写下估算、精算、余额与检查；再用一根绳子画出平均分享的长度关系。不计分，不需要付款。

## Explain the relationship before choosing an operation

A word problem is not an invitation to combine every number arbitrarily. Identify the **total, amount per item, count and units**, then decide how they relate.

- Amount per item × item count = total.
- Total ÷ count = amount per share.
- Add quantities with matching units; subtract a used amount from its total.
- Total price and payment have different roles: **payment − total price = change**.

### Example 1 · Purchase and change

Milk costs NZ$1.25 per bottle. Buy three bottles with NZ$5.

1. 1.25 is dollars per bottle, 3 is bottle count, and 5 is dollars paid.
2. Total price: 1.25×3 = **NZ$3.75**.
3. Change: 5.00−3.75 = **NZ$1.25**.
4. Check: 3.75+1.25 = 5.00. Payment splits into item cost and change.

Estimating each bottle at about NZ$1.30 gives about NZ$3.90 for three, so NZ$5 should cover them. The estimate does not replace the exact price, and no real purchase is needed.

### Example 2 · Two steps for cutting fabric

Start with four metres and cut three strips of 0.85 metre.

1. Used length: 0.85×3 = **2.55 metres**.
2. Remaining length: 4.00−2.55 = **1.45 metres**.
3. Check: 2.55+1.45 = 4.00 metres. The remainder is smaller than the original and non-negative.
4. Calculating 4−0.85 removes only one strip, not the three described.

### Let units support the check

Adding 0.5 litre and three bottles does not give 3.5 litres. If each bottle holds 0.5 litre, three contain 0.5×3 = **1.5 litres**. Likewise, 1.525 km and 1.525 m have the same digits but different lengths.

The model calculates money in integer cents to preserve exact dollars-and-cents relationships. An insufficient budget shows a positive shortfall instead of confusing negative change. It does not model shop cash rounding or actual transactions.

**Family challenge:** use prices of three things at home or invent prices, and plan quantities within an imaginary NZ$10 budget. Record estimates, exact totals, balance and a check. Also use a string to model equal-sharing lengths. No score or payment is needed.
