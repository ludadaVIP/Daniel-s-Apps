# 代数与几何互相表达

## 图形结构先于代入计算

一个长方形的相邻边是x与x+2，长度使用同一单位且x>0。我们可以问面积的一般关系，也可以问某个x下的面积数值，还可以问外边周长。这三个任务共用同一幅图，却不是同一个答案。先说所求量，再选择相乘、分块或数边，不能看到两个数字就随意相加。

沿宽度x把长方形分成一个x×x的正方形和一个x×2的长条。两块不重叠，也没有漏掉整幅图的部分。整块长×宽给x(x+2)，分块相加给x²+2x，所以两式恒等。图形帮助解释分配律，不只是算完以后补的装饰。

### 例 1：一般式与当前实例

令x=3 m，另一边5 m。整块面积3×5=15 m²，分块为9+6=15 m²。一般式x²+2x描述所有正x，15只是其中一个实例。若题目要展开合并的表达式，x(x+2)虽然等价，仍需完成指定形式；若题目只问面积值，继续交含x的一般式也未回答当前问题。

周长则是两条x与两条x+2之和，P=4x+4。x=3时P=16 m。面积15与周长16不仅数字不同，单位也分别为m²、m。只改变单位字样不能把一个量变成另一个量。工作台将一般式与当前值分开输入，移动x后前者保留、后者重算。

### 例 2：从面积反求长度与周长

如果面积给48 m²，就建立x(x+2)=48，而不是把48直接当作边或周长。展开移项得x²+2x−48=0，因式分解为(x−6)(x+8)=0。数学根6与−8都满足方程；几何条件x>0排除−8，实际两边6与8 m，周长2(6+8)=28 m。

解出6仍只是中间量。原题若问周长，必须继续求另一边并回到外边和；若问x，则6才是最终目标。同一方法链的终点由题目决定。代回面积6×8=48和核周长单位，可以用另一种表示检查结果。

## 改变大小时，哪些关系保持

固定长出的量d时，长方形一般面积x²+dx、周长4x+2d保持。移动当前x只换一个实例，不能把当前面积数冒充恒等式。猜式5x在x=3时也给15，但x=1时给5，而真实面积为3；一个数值吻合不足以证明一般关系。

所有长度统一乘k，周长乘k，面积乘k²。只给宽度加2与把所有边乘2是不同变化，不能互换。几何图要求正长度；代数恒等x(x+2)=x²+2x在更广数域仍成立，不代表负数能直接当这个图里的长度。

家长请孩子用整块、分块和外边三种语言解释公式，并说清一般式、实例、方程条件与最终所求。显示缩放只改画面，不改真实单位；改变d或任务则需重新建立关系。完整解释由家长阅读，某个数值判对不代替结构依据。

# Linking algebra and geometry

## Structure before substitution

A rectangle has adjacent sides x and x+2 in a common length unit, with x>0. General area, area at one x and outer perimeter are different questions about the same picture. State the target before multiplying, partitioning or counting edges.

Split the shape into an x-by-x square and an x-by-two strip. They neither overlap nor leave a gap. Whole length times width gives x(x+2); summing parts gives x²+2x. The picture explains distributivity instead of merely decorating a calculation.

### Example 1: expression and instance

At x=3 m the other side is five, so area is 15 m², also 9+6 from the parts. The general expression describes every positive x; fifteen is one instance. If expansion and collection are requested, the equivalent product still needs the requested form. A question about the current value is not answered by stopping at a general expression.

Perimeter counts two x sides and two x+2 sides: P=4x+4. At three it is sixteen metres. Area and perimeter differ in dimensions as well as value. Changing a unit label does not convert one quantity into another. The lab separates general expression from instance value: moving x preserves the former and requires recalculating the latter.

### Example 2: reverse from area

Given area 48 m², build x(x+2)=48. Expanding and factoring gives (x−6)(x+8)=0. Both six and minus eight satisfy the mathematical equation; positive geometric length excludes minus eight. The sides are six and eight metres, giving perimeter 28 m.

Six is an intermediate quantity when perimeter is asked. Return to the original target, find the other side and add outer edges. Check 6×8=48 in the original area relation and retain the perimeter unit. Different representations audit each other.

For fixed extra length d, general area x²+dx and perimeter 4x+2d stay valid as x moves. One matching substitution is not an identity proof: guessed 5x matches area at x=3 but fails at one, giving five instead of three.

Uniform scale k multiplies perimeter by k and area by k². Adding two to one dimension is a different operation from multiplying every length by two. Geometric lengths are positive; the algebraic identity has a wider domain without turning negative numbers into lengths in this diagram.

A parent reviews whole-shape, partition and boundary reasoning, the distinction between general and current values, equation conditions and the final target. Display zoom changes no real quantity; a changed d or target needs a new relationship. A checked value does not replace the structural explanation.
