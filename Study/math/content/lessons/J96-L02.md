# 余弦与正切

## 邻边指邻直角边

对同一个参照锐角，余弦cosθ=邻直角边/斜边，正切tanθ=对边/邻直角边。英文adjacent在这里省略了leg，不能把同样接触这个角的斜边也算作邻直角边。先找给定直角对面的斜边H，再在两条直角边中匹配O与A。

三种比可以并排检查：sin=O/H，cos=A/H，tan=O/A。比值都没有长度单位，正切不是一种角度单位。只有sin、cos的分母是最长斜边；正切的分母是邻直角边，因此正切不必小于一。

### 例 1：3–4–5的三种比

∠C=90°，AC=4、BC=3、AB=5。以A为参照，O=BC=3，A=AC=4，H=AB=5，所以sinA=3/5、cosA=4/5、tanA=3/4。三个比使用同一参照角，不能在中间偷偷换成B。

改以B为参照，对边AC=4、邻直角边BC=3，斜边仍5，因此sinB=4/5、cosB=3/5、tanB=4/3。可见sinA=cosB，因为A、B互余且交换了两条直角边的身份。这是从定义读出的联系，不需要凭空背另一张表。tanB=4/3也直接给出了正切可以大于一的例子。

### 例 2：已知比值求边，未必总乘

一个已知直角三角形满足cosA=4/5，斜边AB=10 cm。把边名代回定义：AC/10=4/5，故AC=10×4/5=8 cm。邻直角边小于斜边，8<10与几何关系一致。

若改为tanA=3/4、对边BC=6 cm，所求邻直角边AC在分母：6/AC=3/4。解得AC=6÷(3/4)=8 cm。直接算6×3/4=4.5，是把已知对边误当作正向输入的邻边。代回6/8=3/4，可以核验方向；解完后仍要检查单位和边的身份。

## 从结构选函数

已知对边与斜边，直接对应sin；邻直角边与斜边对应cos；对边与邻直角边对应tan。这里说“直接”是为了减少无用步骤，不意味着其他方法都错。若先用勾股求第三边，再建立另一个比，也需要写清新增边长的依据，并保持参照角不变。

工作台先计算不同三角形、不同参照角的精确cos与tan，再用两个求边案例比较乘法和除法。求边图只把已给尺寸写出，未知边标x，不从像素长度代替比例。旋转反射不改字母身份；真正换参照角或所求函数后，要重新判断。

家长可先让孩子只写O、A、H对应的边名，不急着算数字。若这一步正确再问：“哪两种量已知？未知量在原比例的分子还是分母？”正弦、余弦、正切的短记号只有接上这种结构才有意义。出口换成B角求cos，检查孩子是在理解对应，还是沿用上一题的4/5。

# Cosine and tangent

## Adjacent means the adjacent leg

For one acute reference angle, cosθ=adjacent leg/hypotenuse and tanθ=opposite/adjacent leg. Adjacent is shorthand for the adjacent leg; it excludes the hypotenuse even though that side also touches the angle. Locate H opposite the given right angle, then identify O and A among the two legs.

Compare the three definitions: sin=O/H, cos=A/H and tan=O/A. All are dimensionless ratios. Only sine and cosine divide by the longest side. Tangent divides by a leg, so it need not be below one.

### Example 1: three ratios in a 3–4–5 triangle

For C=90°, AC=4, BC=3 and AB=5, reference A gives O=3, A=4 and H=5. Hence sinA=3/5, cosA=4/5 and tanA=3/4. Keep the same reference throughout.

Using B instead gives opposite AC=4, adjacent BC=3 and hypotenuse five. The ratios become 4/5, 3/5 and 4/3. Thus sinA=cosB because complementary angles swap the leg roles. This follows from the definitions. Tangent 4/3 is also a direct example above one.

### Example 2: recovering a side

Given cosA=4/5 and hypotenuse AB=10 cm, write AC/10=4/5. Therefore AC=8 cm, correctly shorter than the hypotenuse.

Given tanA=3/4 and opposite BC=6 cm instead, write 6/AC=3/4. The unknown is a denominator, so AC=6÷(3/4)=8 cm. Multiplying six by 3/4 treats the known opposite side as though it were the adjacent input. Substitution checks the direction: 6/8=3/4.

Select a function from the known side roles. Opposite and hypotenuse directly suggest sine, adjacent leg and hypotenuse cosine, and opposite with adjacent leg tangent. Another valid method may first derive a third side by Pythagoras, but must justify that new length and preserve the reference.

The lab compares exact ratios across triangles and references, then contrasts multiplication with division in the two side-finding cases. Only supplied lengths are labelled in those cases, with x for the unknown. Screen pixels are not a substitute for a proportion. Display transformations preserve labels; a new reference or requested function needs a fresh decision.

A parent can first ask for O/A/H side letters, then for the two known quantities and the unknown's position in the original ratio. The exit question changes to B to check that the learner rematches the sides instead of repeating a previous answer.
