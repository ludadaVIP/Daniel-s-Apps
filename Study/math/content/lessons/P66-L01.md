## 用圆心和端点判断，不凭方向猜

圆心 O 是确定圆的位置参照。在一个圆上，所有圆周点到 O 的距离相同，这个距离是**半径 r**。从圆心到圆周的线段也叫半径。**直径 d** 的两端都在圆周上，并且经过圆心；它由两个相反方向的半径组成，因此同一个圆里 **d=2r**。

两端在圆周上的线段叫**弦**。直径是经过圆心的特殊弦；普通弦未必经过圆心。不能把图中很长或水平的弦直接认作直径，更不能把任意一条弦折半就称作半径。先检查端点和是否经过圆心，才谈线段角色。

### 例 1 · 旋转后仍由两个半径组成

1. 一个圆的半径 **3 cm**，OA 从 O 连到圆周点 A。
2. 沿同一直线向相反方向到圆周点 B，OB 也长三厘米。
3. AB 经过 O，且两端在圆周，所以是直径。
4. **AB=OA+OB=3+3=6 cm**。
5. 将图旋转三十度，长度、端点和经过 O 的关系都保留，仍是六厘米直径。

工作台用不同线型和字母标出 OA、AB、CD，不能只靠颜色分辨。改变弦 CD 的偏移可使它靠近或离开圆心；当偏移为零，CD 与 AB 重合，标签合并成 A=D、B=C，此时它既是弦又是直径。不是“成为直径以后就不叫弦了”。

### 例 2 · 已知直径要折半

1. 直径 **10 cm**，圆心将它分为两个相等的半径。
2. 所以 **r=10÷2=5 cm**，不是再乘二得二十。
3. 若直径改为 **3 cm**，半径为 **1.5 cm**。
4. 半径不必是整数，不能为了方便把 1.5 改成一或二。
5. 核查：两份五回到十，两份一点五回到三，量名和单位保持一致。

本课的圆半径为正。一个点不能拿来做有非零直径的圆周率实验，因为后面要算周长除以直径；不能把“圆小到像点”的屏幕感觉当成直径真的为零。图上厘米按同一显示比例绘制，但屏幕像素不是可以拿尺直接当真实厘米的打印测量。

**同圆条件很重要。** d=2r 连接同一个圆的两个长度。一个大圆的半径可能超过另一个小圆的直径，不能拿不同圆随意套“直径一定比半径大”。

纸笔挑战：画三个旋转方向不同的圆，各标 O，再画一条半径、一条直径和一条不经过圆心的弦。家长遮住文字，让孩子根据端点重认；不按线段朝向或颜色给答案。带走一个发现：**圆心、端点与同圆关系决定角色，直径是两条相反方向半径的和。**

## Classify segments by centre and endpoints

Every boundary point of a circle has the same distance from its centre O. That distance is the **radius r**, also the name of a segment joining centre to boundary. A **diameter d** joins two boundary points through the centre. It comprises two opposite radii, so **d=2r** within the same circle.

A segment with both endpoints on the circle is a **chord**. A diameter is a special chord through the centre; other chords may miss the centre. Being long or horizontal is insufficient. Nor does halving an arbitrary chord generally produce the radius. Inspect endpoints and centre incidence before classifying the segment.

### Example 1 · Rotation preserves the two-radius structure

1. A circle has radius **3 cm**, with OA joining centre to boundary.
2. Extend the same straight line in the opposite direction to boundary point B. OB is also three centimetres.
3. AB has boundary endpoints and passes through O, making it a diameter.
4. **AB=OA+OB=3+3=6 cm**.
5. Rotate the drawing thirty degrees: endpoints, centre incidence and lengths remain unchanged, so AB is still a six-centimetre diameter.

The workshop uses line styles and letters as well as colour. Moving chord CD toward the centre eventually makes it coincide with AB. At zero offset, labels merge into A=D and B=C. It is then both a chord and a diameter; becoming a diameter does not stop it being a chord.

### Example 2 · Halve a given diameter

1. A **10 cm** diameter is divided by the centre into two equal radii.
2. Hence **r=10÷2=5 cm**, rather than twenty.
3. With diameter **3 cm**, the radius is **1.5 cm**.
4. A radius need not be a whole number; do not round the given relationship for convenience.
5. Check that two fives recover ten and two one-and-a-halves recover three, retaining quantity names and units.

These models use positive radii. A point cannot serve as a circle with a nonzero diameter for the later circumference-to-diameter division. A small circle looking like a point on screen does not make its mathematical diameter zero. Screen geometry uses a consistent scale, but screen pixels are not physical centimetres for a ruler measurement.

The same-circle condition matters: d=2r connects two lengths of one circle. A large circle’s radius may exceed another circle’s diameter, so do not apply the comparison across unrelated circles. For a paper task, draw circles in three orientations, mark O and add a radius, a diameter and a chord missing O. Hide labels and explain classifications using endpoints rather than appearance. The takeaway is centre, endpoints and the same-circle relationship, with a diameter equal to two opposite radii.
