# 残差与模型检验

拟合完成后，残差不是应该藏起来的坏消息，而是检查模型遗漏了什么的资料。本课把有符号计算和图形诊断连接起来，同时保留“小图不能证明全部假设”的边界。

## 从散点到残差图

给定预测ŷ，残差e=y−ŷ。残差图保留原x作横坐标，把每个观测y换成对应残差。负值表示观测在线下方，正值表示在上方；纵坐标0是预测位置，不是原来y=0。图中只换表示，原对象ID和配对不变。

若y以厘米计，残差也以厘米计，平方残差以平方厘米计；SSE不是平均距离，不随便开平方后当同一种指标。含截距OLS的残差代数和0（精确算术中），但正负误差可以同时很大。这个代数性质在一条不适合形状的最优直线上也成立。

### 例1：负残差与零和并存

数据(1,1)、(2,3)、(3,2)，拟合ŷ=1+x/2。在x=1，预测3/2，残差1−3/2=−1/2，表示观测低1/2。另两点残差1与−1/2。

残差和−1/2+1−1/2=0，平方和却为3/2；“平均残差零”不是“各点都预测准确”。残差图三点为(1,−1/2)、(2,1)、(3,−1/2)，横坐标不变。三个点太少，不能仅凭它们宣布误差独立、正态或恒方差。

### 例2：r=0仍能漏掉系统曲线

U形数据(1,4)、(2,1)、(3,0)、(4,1)、(5,4)，均值(3,2)。Sxy=0使OLS线为ŷ=2，r=0。残差依次2、−1、−2、−1、2，和0，SSE=14。

两端正、中间负不是随机散布的充分解释，而是提醒直线系统地低估两端、高估中间。可以考虑符合背景的曲线模型，并用独立资料检查；不能仅为训练误差更小就无条件选更复杂模型。

工作台另有扇形案例：在x=1、2、3处分别观察围绕2x的成对值，残差为±1/2、±2、±3。OLS线仍为2x，但散布随着x扩大。这提醒检查不同误差变异、分组、量纲或遗漏因素；它不是自动认定某一种误差分布的统计检验。

## 诊断不等于证明

应结合散点、残差、时间/采集顺序、样本设计与来源检查。残差若随顺序有关联，单看e对x图可能漏掉依赖；极端x处的点也可能强烈影响整条线。不要因点影响大就自动删掉，先核来源并做保留/排除的敏感性比较。

没有明显图案也不能证明所有误差条件成立。高r、低训练SSE和残差均值0回答不同问题，都不直接给因果结论、范围外保证或总体代表性。工作台切换原散点/残差图时保留预测，以强调数学资料相同。

出口要求把U形的零相关、零残差和与系统形状同时解释清楚。家长可让孩子逐个指认“这个点的预测在哪里、残差怎样移动到0线”，再问“图形提出了什么线索，还没有证明什么”。自由论证由家长核验，不按是否出现术语自动通过。

# Residuals and model checking

Residuals are evidence about what a model misses, rather than bad news to hide after fitting. Connect signed arithmetic with graphical diagnosis while recognising what a small plot cannot establish.

## Transform the graph without breaking identities

For prediction ŷ, residual e=y−ŷ. A residual plot keeps original x and replaces the vertical coordinate by its residual. Negative means below prediction; positive means above. Its zero line represents the prediction, not original y=0. Unit identities and pairs remain unchanged.

If y is in centimetres, residuals are in centimetres and squared errors in square centimetres. SSE is not a mean distance, and taking a square root does not make every error summary interchangeable. In exact arithmetic, intercept OLS residuals sum to zero. Large positive and negative errors can nevertheless remain, even when the fitted linear shape is inappropriate.

### Example 1: Negative residuals can sum to zero

For (1,1), (2,3), (3,2), use ŷ=1+x/2. At x=1, prediction 3/2 gives residual 1−3/2=−1/2: the observation is one half below prediction. The other residuals are one and −1/2.

Their sum is zero but SSE is 3/2. Zero average residual is not perfect prediction at every point. Residual-plot coordinates become (1,−1/2), (2,1), (3,−1/2), retaining the original x values. Three points cannot establish independent, normal or constant-variance errors.

### Example 2: Zero correlation can conceal systematic curvature

The U-shaped pairs (1,4), (2,1), (3,0), (4,1), (5,4) have means (3,2). Their Sxy is zero, producing fitted ŷ=2 and r=0. Residuals 2,−1,−2,−1,2 sum to zero but have SSE fourteen.

Positive ends and a negative middle show that the line systematically underpredicts the ends and overpredicts the middle. Consider contextually justified curved models and assess them using independent data. Smaller training error alone does not justify unlimited complexity.

A second workbench example has pairs around 2x at x=1, two and three. Its residuals are ±1/2, ±2 and ±3. The fitted line remains 2x while spread grows with x. This fan prompts checks of changing variation, subgroups, measurement scale or omitted factors. It is a diagnostic clue rather than an automatic test proving a particular error distribution.

## Diagnosis has limits

Inspect the scatterplot, residuals, collection order, sampling design and data sources together. Dependence over collection order can be missed in a residual-versus-x plot. Observations at extreme x may strongly influence the fitted line; influence alone is not a reason to delete them. Check provenance and compare fits with and without a record when appropriate.

Absence of an obvious pattern does not establish every error assumption. High r, low training SSE and zero mean residual answer different questions. None directly supplies causation, extrapolation reliability or population representativeness. Switching between scatter and residual displays preserves answers because the underlying evidence is unchanged.

For the exit, reconcile zero correlation, zero residual sum and missed U-shaped structure. A parent can ask where a point’s prediction lies and how its residual is positioned relative to zero. Then ask what the graph suggests and what it has not proved. Human review evaluates the argument rather than passing it for including terminology.
