## 一个数字，需要一个位置

读数的目标不是记住长长的名称，而是知道**每个数字住在哪一位**。从右向左是个、十、百、千、万、十万、百万。每往左一位，单位变成原来的 10 倍。

写数字时，一个位置没有数量，就写 **0 占位**。读法里只说一次“零”，数字里却可能要有几个 0。先排位值表，再写数字，通常比数口头的“零”更可靠。

### 例 1 · 一万零五 / 10,005

1. “一万”把 1 放在万位。“五”把 5 放在个位。
2. 千、百、十都没有，所以各填一个 0。
3. 从万到个依次是 **1｜0｜0｜0｜5**，写成 **10,005**。
4. 检查：10,000 + 5 = 10,005。1,005 只有一千多，不能表示这个数量。

### 例 2 · 三十四万零六百零一 / 340,601

1. 三十四万 = 340,000；六百 = 600；一 = 1。
2. 合起来：340,000 + 600 + 1 = **340,601**。
3. English 读作 **three hundred and forty thousand six hundred and one**。中文常按“万”组织，英文常按 thousand / million 组织，两种读法说的是同一个数。

### 逗号与零，作用不同

`340601` 与 `340,601` 是同一个数，逗号只是每三位的阅读分组。`34061` 却不同：少一个占位零，右边或左边数字的位置会变。**可以省略阅读逗号，不能随意省略整数内部的 0。**

在探索器里选择 10,005、207,040、340,601。先自己读，再看中英文读法，最后指出每个非零数字所在的列。英文不熟悉时，可请家长读题；不用英语发音流利来证明你理解数学。

## A digit needs a place

Reading a number means understanding **the place of each digit**, rather than memorising a long name. From the right come ones, tens, hundreds, thousands, ten thousands, hundred thousands and millions. Each place to the left has a unit ten times as large.

Use **zero as a placeholder** when a place has no amount. The spoken name may contain one zero word even when the written number needs several zeros. A place-value chart is more reliable than counting spoken zero words.

### Example 1 · Ten thousand and five / 10,005

1. Ten thousand puts 1 in ten thousands. Five puts 5 in ones.
2. Thousands, hundreds and tens are empty, so each needs a zero.
3. The digits are **1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 5**, giving **10,005**.
4. Check: 10,000 + 5 = 10,005. The number 1,005 is only a little over one thousand.

### Example 2 · Three hundred and forty thousand six hundred and one / 340,601

1. Three hundred and forty thousand is 340,000; six hundred is 600; one is 1.
2. Combine them: 340,000 + 600 + 1 = **340,601**.
3. Chinese names often group around 万 (ten thousand); English names group around thousand and million. Both systems describe the same number.

### Commas and zeros do different jobs

`340601` and `340,601` are the same number: commas separate groups of three digits for reading. But `34061` is different. Removing a placeholder changes digit positions. **Reading commas are optional; internal zeros in whole numbers cannot be removed freely.**
