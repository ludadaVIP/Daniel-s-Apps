# 圆中的综合推理

## 每一步都要有来源

综合题往往不缺公式，而是容易把“想用的条件”误当“已经证明的事实”。先列明确已知，再标出能推出的角关系，之后才用全等或相似，最后列数量关系。数值碰巧正确与推理成立是不同问题。图上看起来共圆、垂直或相切，不能代替前提。

本课工作台把数值预测与证明卡片分开。添加卡片时，必须先建立该步骤所需的依据；例如没有相似就不能调用对应边比例，没有四点共圆就不能使用圆内接四边形对角互补。卡片检查帮助暴露依赖关系，完整书面证明仍由家长阅读，不把合法排序自动等同于孩子已经解释清楚。

### 例 1：共圆对角和普通四边形

A、B、C、D依次位于同一圆上，∠DAB=70°。A角与C角分别截BD之间不含各自顶点的弧，这两弧合计360°。各取一半，所以A+C=180°，C=110°。证明链为“依次共圆→两弧合成整圆→对角互补→代入70°”。

删去四点共圆，只保留A=70°，就不能保证C=110°。固定A、B、D并移动C，可以保持A角不变，却改变C角；普通四边形只给四内角总和360°。工作台的缺前提案例会拒绝调用共圆定理，而不是为了得到想要的110°自行添加共圆条件。

### 例 2：直径、垂高与相似边长

AC是直径，B在圆上，BD⊥AC且D位于线段AC内部，AC=25、AD=9 cm。由直径角定理得∠ABC=90°。由垂高得∠ADB=90°；D在AC上，所以∠DAB=∠BAC。两个对应角相等，AA给△ADB∼△ABC。

按顺序A↔A、D↔B、B↔C，所以AD对应AB，AB对应AC，得到AD/AB=AB/AC。交叉相乘得AB²=AD·AC=9×25=225。弦长为正，AB=15 cm。不能把△ADB错配为△ACB，也不能在AA之前先拿这个比例来证明AA，形成循环。

进一步DC=25−9=16 cm。两小直角三角形的正确对应还可证明BD²=AD·DC，于是BD²=9×16=144，BD=12 cm。也可用已求出的AB=15与AD=9在△ADB中勾股核验，15²−9²=144。两条独立路径吻合，有助于发现整边与剩余段混淆，但核验路径不能倒过来替代原证明的前提。

## 写出短而完整的证明

每步至少说明对象和理由。例如“因为AC为直径且B在圆上，故∠ABC=90°”，比只写“直角”更清楚；“由AA，△ADB∼△ABC”比只写“相似”更能防止边配错。最后分清角单位、长度单位和平方量，说明为什么长度取正值。

旋转、缩放显示应保留已经写下的预测与证明草稿；换真实案例则重新建链。用撤回按钮检查某结论移除后，后续还依赖什么。家长核对所引定理的前提是否全部出现、对应顺序是否正确、有没有循环论证。出口需要孩子用自己的话写完整链，再由家长确认。

家庭挑战可在纸上设计虚构圆形滑板场，连接扇形份额、精确π量、切点垂直和相似证明。这里只是数学模型，不是施工图。记录所选弧、前提、单位及一个条件不足反例，比画一张外观漂亮却无法解释的图更有价值。

# Combined circle reasoning

## Give every step a source

In a combined problem the difficulty is often deciding which facts have been established. List givens, derive angle relationships, establish congruence or similarity, and only then write quantitative relationships. A correct numerical guess does not validate a proof. Apparent concyclicity, perpendicularity or tangency supplies no missing premise.

The lab separates numerical prediction from proof cards. A card requires previously established dependencies: corresponding-side proportions need similarity, and cyclic opposite-angle supplementation needs concyclicity. Legal card order is useful evidence about structure, but a parent still reviews the complete written argument and its geometric explanations.

### Example 1: cyclic and general quadrilaterals

A, B, C and D lie in order on one circle, with DAB=70°. Opposite angles A and C intercept the two arcs BD excluding their respective vertices. Those arcs partition 360°, so their half-angles sum to 180°. Thus C=110°. The dependency chain is concyclicity, complementary intercepted arcs, opposite-angle supplementation, then substitution.

Remove concyclicity while retaining A=70°. Keeping A, B and D fixed and moving C preserves A but can change C. A general quadrilateral only has total angle sum 360°. The missing-premise case therefore rejects the cyclic theorem rather than inventing a convenient common-circle condition.

### Example 2: diameter, altitude and similarity

Given diameter AC, B on the circle and BD⊥AC with D inside AC, let AC=25 and AD=9 cm. The diameter-angle theorem gives ABC=90°. The altitude gives ADB=90°, and D on AC gives DAB=BAC. Hence △ADB∼△ABC by AA.

The order gives A↔A, D↔B and B↔C. Consequently AD/AB=AB/AC and AB²=AD·AC=225. Taking the positive length gives AB=15 cm. Matching ADB with ACB would pair the wrong sides. Invoking the proportion before establishing similarity would use an unproved result as its own justification.

Next DC=16 cm. Similarity of the two smaller right triangles can establish BD²=AD·DC=144, giving BD=12 cm. Alternatively use the established AB=15 and AD=9 in triangle ADB: 15²−9²=144. Independent checks help reveal whole-side versus leftover-segment errors, but do not replace the original premises.

Write short, complete statements naming objects and reasons. “AC is a diameter and B lies on the circle, therefore ABC=90°” is stronger than merely writing “right angle”. State the similarity order before the proportion and retain angle, length or squared units correctly. Explain why a length uses a positive root.

Display rotation and zoom preserve your prediction and sketch; a new case resets the chain. Undo steps to inspect dependencies. A parent checks complete premises, matching and circular reasoning. The paper family challenge connects fictional circular-space sectors, exact pi quantities, tangent contacts and a similarity proof as a maths model rather than construction guidance.
