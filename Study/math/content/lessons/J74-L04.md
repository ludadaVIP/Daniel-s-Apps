# 三角形内角和

## 观察支持猜想，理由支持一般结论

本课研究平面欧氏几何中的非退化三角形。三个顶点不共线、三边围成内部区域，每个内角都大于零，三个内角之和为 180°。弯曲表面上的其他几何不在这条平面定理的条件中，不能不加说明地混用。

量角器、剪纸拼角能帮助猜想，但测量有误差，少数图形的读数接近一百八十并不能单独解释全部三角形。我们用过顶点的平行线，将三个角放入同一个平角关系，得到不依赖屏幕精度的理由。

### 例 1：辅助线解释与求第三角

三角形 ABC，过 C 作 ℓ∥AB。由两组内错角相等，C 两侧由辅助线与 CA、CB 构成的角分别等于 A、B。它们与 C 的内角相邻，合起来正好沿辅助线形成平角。因此 `A+B+C=180°`。

若 A=48°、B=67°，则 `C=180°−48°−67°=65°`。这里减的是另外两个内角，不是三条边，也不是一个外角。工作台会保留三角形，在 C 画平行辅助线，另列三个角的旋转副本组成平角；副本帮助看结构，内错角条件才是角度转移的理由。

### 例 2：等腰、直角与外角

直角三角形已有 90° 与 32°，第三角为 `180°−90°−32°=58°`。等腰三角形顶角 38°，两个底角相等，共一百四十二度，各为 71°，不能把一百四十二全给一个底角。

将一边延长，外角 E 与邻接内角 C 构成邻补，`E+C=180°`。再与内角和比较，同减 C 得 `E=A+B`：外角等于两个不相邻内角之和。若外角 124°、其中一个不相邻内角 49°，另一个为 75°；邻接内角 C 则是 56°，这两个未知对象不能混淆。

三个内角为 x、2x、3x 时，列 `6x=180` 得 x=30；实际三角为三十、六十、九十。先求 x，再回到三个内角检查正数条件与总和。52.5°、67° 的第三角为 60.5°，不必为了整齐把精确读数舍入。

## 改形状，保留条件

工作台通过 A、B 构造真实角度的三角形，统一比例缩放保证图不会压扁而改角。为保持标签可读，每角至少二十度；当 A 增大使 B 不再合范围，会显示调整后的 B 作为新条件。这个显示范围不是三角形定理只对二十度以上才成立。

每次改数据先预测 C，再展开辅助线。能把“同位或内错角为什么相等”连接到“这三个角组成哪个平角”，才算解释了内角和。家长不要只问最终数字，也要孩子指出哪条是辅助线、平行来自哪里、外角为什么不算进原三个内角。

# Triangle angle sum

## Observation suggests; relationships justify

This lesson concerns nondegenerate triangles in Euclidean plane geometry. The vertices are not collinear, the sides enclose a region, and each interior angle is positive. Their sum is 180°. Do not silently transfer this plane theorem to other geometries on curved surfaces.

Measuring or cutting corners supports a conjecture, but readings have error and a few examples do not explain the general result. A parallel through a vertex places the three angle sizes into one straight-angle relationship, independently of drawing precision.

### Example 1: an auxiliary parallel and a missing angle

Through C in triangle ABC, construct ℓ parallel to AB. Two alternate-interior equalities place copies of A and B on either side of interior C. The three adjacent angles form a straight angle along ℓ, so A+B+C=180°.

With A=48° and B=67°, subtract both from 180° to obtain C=65°. These are interior angles, not sides or exterior angles. The workshop retains the original triangle, draws the auxiliary parallel through C and shows rotated angle copies in a straight-angle row. Copies support observation; the parallel-angle relationships justify their equality.

### Example 2: right, isosceles and exterior cases

A right triangle with another angle 32° has third angle 58°. An isosceles triangle with vertex angle 38° has two equal base angles totalling 142°, so each is 71°. Do not assign the entire remaining sum to one base.

Extending a side forms exterior E adjacent supplementary to interior C. Compare E+C=180° with A+B+C=180° and subtract C: E=A+B, the two non-adjacent interiors. If E=124° and one remote interior is 49°, the other is 75°. The adjacent interior C is separately 56°; identify the requested quantity.

Angles x, 2x and 3x give 6x=180, hence x=30 and actual angles thirty, sixty and ninety. Substitute back to check positivity and total. With known angles 52.5° and 67°, the third is exactly 60.5°, without rounding.

## Change shape while preserving the premises

The model constructs actual angle geometry and uses one scale for both directions. To keep labels readable, controls retain angles of at least twenty degrees. Increasing A may reduce B, with the new displayed values becoming the givens. This display range does not restrict the theorem itself to angles above twenty.

Predict C before revealing the auxiliary parallel. Explain why the transferred angles equal the originals and which straight angle the three occupy. A parent asks where the auxiliary line came from, which condition makes it parallel and why an exterior angle is not included among the original three interiors.
