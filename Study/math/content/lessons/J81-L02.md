# 单项式乘多项式

## 一个外因子必须到达每一项

分配律 `k(A+B)=kA+kB` 把同一个外因子作用到括号内每个完整项。把 A、B 换成含字母的项，规则仍成立。外因子也是单项式时，它的系数、字母和符号都必须参与每一对乘法，不能只处理第一项。

先把括号内项连同符号分开，再逐对求乘积，最后合并同类项。三项需要三对，不能因为第一对出现了平方，就把剩余项当作普通常数抄下来。分配不是把括号“擦掉”，而是把乘法关系完整写开。

### 例 1：代数配对与正长度面积

展开 `2x(x+3)`。第一对 `2x·x=2x²`，第二对 `2x·3=6x`，所以 `2x²+6x`。两项次数不同，不能再合成八 x³，也不能漏掉第二项中的 x。

若 x>0，可画高 2x、宽 x+3 的长方形。按宽分成 x 与三，两块面积分别 2x² 和 6x。取 x=3 时，外框为六乘六，两个分块各十八，合计三十六平方单位。尺寸与面积都按同一比例画，不能把文字写成 x 就画任意大小。这个图说明正长度情境，代数分配本身适用于更广的实数取值。

### 例 2：负号与分数不能只作用一次

展开 `−x(2x+5)`：`(−x)·2x=−2x²`，`(−x)·5=−5x`，结果 `−2x²−5x`。负号连同 x 一起乘到两项，不是只让第一个乘积变负。

再算 `(1/2)x(4x−6)`。两对为 `2x²` 与 `−3x`，所以 `2x²−3x`。若括号有三项，如 `−2y(x−y+3)`，三对分别为 −2xy、+2y²、−6y，结果 `−2xy+2y²−6y`。第二对为正，是负系数乘负项，不是任意改号。

## 零、等值中间式与图形边界

外因子为零时，`0(x²−x+2)=0`，连常数二也乘零。写零比保留若干零项更清楚。外因子为分数时逐项精确乘，不用小数近似掩盖系数。

本课要“展开并合并”。原式 `2x(x+3)` 或中间式 `2x·x+2x·3` 关系等值，但尚未完成任务。完成后各字母部分只保留一个非零项，系数写前。这个要求检查表达形式，不能把“未完成”说成“代数关系错误”。

乘积表的格子记录有符号的代数配对，不是面积砖块。负偏移或负系数可以在表中处理，却不能直接当成负宽度画实物长方形。面积图只支持所注明的正长度实例；一般恒等关系应由分配律说明，不靠几次试数或不存在的负面积。

工作台每格先显示问号，孩子预测后逐格核对。换实验会清空旧结果与揭示状态。家长请孩子沿每条配对说出系数、字母、符号，再检查原式与结果的关系；不把点完格子视为已经理解。

# Monomial times polynomial

## The outside factor must reach every signed term

The distributive law k(A+B)=kA+kB applies the same factor to each inside term. It still works when the factor and the terms contain letters. Every product must retain the coefficient, letters and sign of the outside monomial.

Identify the signed inside terms, compute each pair and then collect like terms. Three terms require three products. Distribution does not simply erase brackets or allow the last constant to be copied unchanged.

### Example 1: term pairs and a positive-length area

For 2x(x+3), the products are 2x·x=2x² and 2x·3=6x. The result is 2x²+6x. These terms have different variable parts and cannot be combined further. The x in the second product must remain.

For x>0, draw a rectangle of height 2x and width x+3. Split the width into x and three to obtain areas 2x² and 6x. At x=3 the outer rectangle is six by six and the two areas are eighteen each. Keep dimensions and areas proportional. This picture explains a positive-length instance; algebraic distribution has a broader real-number domain.

### Example 2: negative and fractional factors

For −x(2x+5), compute (−x)·2x=−2x² and (−x)·5=−5x, giving −2x²−5x. The negative sign and x travel together into both products, not just the first.

For (1/2)x(4x−6), the two products are 2x² and −3x, giving 2x²−3x. With three inside terms, −2y(x−y+3) yields −2xy,+2y²,−6y. The middle product is positive because its two signed coefficients are negative. It is not an arbitrary sign change.

## Zero, intermediate forms and geometric limits

An outside zero multiplies every term, including the constant: 0(x²−x+2)=0. Write a single zero rather than redundant zero terms. Fractional factors also distribute exactly without rounding.

This task requests expansion and collection. The original 2x(x+3), or 2x·x+2x·3, is equivalent but unfinished. Complete the products, put coefficients first and retain one nonzero term per variable part. Distinguish an unfinished form from a false algebraic relationship.

The product-table cells represent signed algebraic pairs, not literal area tiles. Negative factors or offsets do not create negative physical widths. The area picture uses its stated positive lengths; distribution explains the general identity without fictitious negative areas or a proof by a few numerical trials.
