## 一个整体，大小相同的份

把一整块巧克力当作 **1 个整体**。分成 8 个大小相同的小块，每块是整体的 **1/8**。拿出 3 块，就是 **3/8**。

- **分母 8**：整体有 8 个等份。
- **分子 3**：选中了 3 个等份。

### 例 1 · 找到整体

一条纸带分成 4 等份，涂色 1 份。

1. 整体是一整条纸带。
2. 分母是 4，分子是 1。
3. 涂色部分是 **1/4**。

### 例 2 · 没有等份，就不能这样写

一个蛋糕切成四块，两块大、两块小。虽然有四块，但不能说每块都是 1/4；只有每块大小相同，才能叫四等份。

**记住：先说整体是什么，再检查是否等份，最后数选中了几份。**

## One whole, equal-sized parts

Call one entire chocolate bar **one whole**. Divide it into 8 equal pieces. Each piece is **1/8** of the whole. Select 3 pieces: that is **3/8**.

- **Denominator 8**: the whole has 8 equal parts.
- **Numerator 3**: 3 equal parts are selected.

### Example 1 · Identify the whole

A strip is divided into 4 equal parts; 1 is shaded.

1. The whole is the entire strip.
2. The denominator is 4; the numerator is 1.
3. The shaded part is **1/4**.

### Example 2 · The parts must be equal

A cake is cut into four pieces: two large and two small. There are four pieces, but each is not 1/4. Four equal parts must all have the same size.

**Identify the whole, check equal parts, then count the selected parts.**
