# 最小二乘直线的直觉

“看起来贴近”不足以唯一确定一条线。先声明怎样衡量误差，再决定什么叫最好。本课限定含截距模型ŷ=a+bx，以同一x处竖直残差平方和SSE=Σ(y−ŷ)²作目标。

## 为什么保留平方

残差e=y−ŷ有正负，分别表示点在线上方或下方。直接相加会抵消；平方后再相加保留误差大小，并对大误差赋予更大的权重。目标不是到直线的最短距离，也不是平方残差代数和(Σe)²。

若Sxx>0，最优斜率b*=Sxy/Sxx，截距a*=ȳ−b*x̄，因此最优线经过均值点(x̄,ȳ)。公式针对预测y的竖直误差；交换x、y后重新拟合，一般不是原线的简单反解。斜率、截距与误差都有各自单位，改变单位也会改变SSE数值，比较候选时应固定同一资料和单位。

### 例1：两个候选都输给第三条

数据(1,1)、(2,3)、(3,2)，均值(2,2)、Sxx=2、Sxy=1，得到b*=1/2、a*=1。预测依次3/2、2、5/2，残差−1/2、1、−1/2，SSE=1/4+1+1/4=3/2。

候选ŷ=x给残差0、1、−1，SSE=2。候选ŷ=2给残差−1、1、0，SSE也2。后者经过均值点且残差和0，仍不是最佳。最优线的残差和为0是一条必要的拟合性质，却不是单独充分条件；还可核Σxe=0。

### 例2：菜单之外会不会还有更好的线

只比较三条、十条甚至一千条候选，仍没检查所有实数a、b。对这组三对资料，精确恒等式为SSE(a,b)=3/2+3(a+2b−2)²+2(b−1/2)²。

后两项非负，故任意候选都不低于3/2；两项同时0要求b=1/2，再得a=1，所以这是唯一全局最优。这条恒等式可由展开平方并利用中心化交积求得，一般形式是SSEmin+n(a+bx̄−ȳ)²+Sxx(b−b*)²。它比有限网格“暂时最小”更强，也说明菜单探索如何连接数学理由。

若所有x都等于2，而y为1、3、2，只识别到预测值a+2b=2。所有满足此关系的线SSE同样最小；不能武断地把斜率宣布为0。x有变异而y恒定则不同：唯一最优斜率0，相关系数却因y零方差未定义。

## 工作台与边界

改变候选截距或斜率，逐点橙色线段与平方表同步改变，最优斜率预测保持。显示残差开关只改表示，不改输入；切换资料时按新答案核对。图中距离按竖直方向计算，精确表保留分数，不靠像素读取答案。

“在此训练样本SSE最小”不等于“模型适合总体”，更不等于误差为0、因果成立或外推可靠。下一课要看残差是否还有系统形状。出口需同时给三个SSE和非负平方理由；家长可问“你怎样排除没试过的线”，让孩子从尝试走向覆盖全部候选的解释。

# Least-squares regression intuition

Looking close to the points does not uniquely define a best line. Specify a loss first. Here the model includes an intercept, ŷ=a+bx, and minimises SSE=Σ(y−ŷ)² using vertical differences at the same observed x.

## Why square residuals?

Residual e=y−ŷ is signed: positive above the prediction and negative below. Adding signs allows cancellation. Squaring before adding preserves magnitudes and gives larger errors greater weight. The objective is neither shortest distance to the line nor (Σe)².

When Sxx>0, the optimal slope is b*=Sxy/Sxx and intercept a*=ȳ−b*x̄. The fitted line passes through the mean point (x̄,ȳ). This fits y using vertical errors; reversing x and y and fitting again generally does not simply invert the original line. Slope, intercept and error have units. Unit changes alter SSE numerically, so compare candidates on the same records and scale.

### Example 1: Two candidates lose to a third

For (1,1), (2,3), (3,2), the mean point is (2,2), Sxx=2 and Sxy=1. Hence b*=1/2 and a*=1. Predictions are 3/2, two and 5/2; residuals are −1/2, one and −1/2. Their squared sum is 3/2.

Candidate ŷ=x gives residuals zero, one and −1, with SSE two. Candidate ŷ=2 gives −1, one and zero, also SSE two. The latter passes through the mean point and has residual sum zero, yet is not optimal. Zero residual sum is a property of an intercept fit, not a sufficient optimality test by itself. The fitted residuals also satisfy Σxe=0.

### Example 2: Rule out every untested candidate

Comparing three, ten or a thousand lines still leaves untested real parameters. For these data the exact identity is SSE(a,b)=3/2+3(a+2b−2)²+2(b−1/2)².

The extra terms are nonnegative, so no line has SSE below 3/2. Both vanish only when b=1/2 and a=1, proving a unique global minimum. Expanding squares and using centred sums gives the general identity SSEmin+n(a+bx̄−ȳ)²+Sxx(b−b*)². It connects menu exploration to an argument covering the entire parameter space.

If every x equals two and y values are one, three and two, only a+2b=2 is identified. Every line satisfying it has the same minimum SSE; arbitrarily imposing slope zero confuses a convenient representative with identification. Constant y with varying x is different: it gives a unique slope zero even though correlation is undefined.

## Investigate with the right limits

Changing candidate parameters updates vertical segments and squared-error tables while preserving an unchanged optimal-slope prediction. Toggling residual visibility changes representation rather than answers. Exact fractional tables support verification without reading pixels.

Minimum training SSE does not prove a suitable population model, zero errors, causation or reliable extrapolation. The next lesson inspects residual structure. For the exit, compare all three SSE values and explain the nonnegative-square argument. A parent can ask how an untested line was ruled out, moving the learner from trying candidates to justifying a statement about every candidate.
