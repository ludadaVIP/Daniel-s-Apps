## 周长是沿边界走完一圈

周长计的是封闭图形边界的总长度。把手指想成一个沿边走的人，从起点出发，走遍每段边界一次并回到起点，才是一圈。内部能铺多少格，是面积的另一个问题。

周长用长度单位，例如厘米或米。它不是方格数，不用平方厘米作单位。长方形有两组相等的对边，但周长的基本理由仍是“四条边各计一次”，不是一个需要死记的符号。

### 例 1 · 八厘米与五厘米长方形

1. 上边 8 cm，右边 5 cm，下边 8 cm，左边 5 cm。
2. 绕一圈合计 **8+5+8+5=26 cm**。
3. 也可把一长一短相加，再取两份：2×(8+5)=26 cm。
4. 8+5=13 cm 只走了半圈，没有回到起点。
5. 8×5=40 cm² 计内部方格，不是围栏长度。

### 例 2 · 已知周长找缺少一边

1. 长方形周长 30 cm，宽 9 cm，高未知。
2. 一条宽与一条高合计是半圈，30÷2=15 cm。
3. 去掉已知宽，**高=15−9=6 cm**。
4. 检查 9+6+9+6=30 cm。直接做 30−9=21 会漏掉另一条宽与两条高的关系。

### 哪些图形可以用这个公式

对长方形，2×(宽+高) 来自两组相等对边。三角形的三条直边都要相加，不能机械套长方形公式。三边 5、6、7 cm 的三角形，周长 5+6+7=18 cm；每条边只计一次。

正方形四边都相等。每边六厘米，周长 6+6+6+6=24 cm。六乘六是内部面积，虽然都用到了边长，却回答不同问题。

周长相同不保证内部面积相同。8×5 与 7×6 两个长方形的周长都是 26 cm，但若长度单位为厘米，面积分别为 40 cm² 与 42 cm²。围栏用量相同，覆盖的土地可以不同，不是图画看起来一样大才成立。

工作台把每条边分成 1 cm 段，一次走一段，或预测后走完。金色只标已走的边界，内部没有被这些路程铺满。改变宽或高会重新从起点计数，避免把上一幅图已经走的段数接到新图上。

**小检查：** 9 cm×4 cm 的长方形周长是多少？解释它为什么和 8 cm×5 cm 的周长相同，却不保证相同面积。

## Perimeter completes one circuit along the boundary

Perimeter is the total length of a closed figure's boundary. Imagine walking with your finger: begin at a point, visit each boundary segment once and return to the start. Covering the inside with squares asks a different question about area.

Use length units such as centimetres or metres, not square centimetres. A rectangle has two matching pairs of opposite sides, but the reason for its perimeter expression remains counting each of four sides once.

### Example 1 · An eight-by-five-centimetre rectangle

1. Top is 8 cm, right 5 cm, bottom 8 cm and left 5 cm.
2. One circuit totals **8+5+8+5=26 cm**.
3. Equivalently take two copies of one long and one short side: 2×(8+5)=26 cm.
4. Adding 8+5=13 cm completes only half a circuit.
5. Multiplying 8×5=40 cm² counts interior squares rather than fence length.

### Example 2 · Find a side from perimeter

1. A rectangle has perimeter 30 cm, width 9 cm and unknown height.
2. One width plus one height is half the circuit: 30÷2=15 cm.
3. Remove known width: **height=15−9=6 cm**.
4. Check 9+6+9+6=30 cm. Subtracting only 30−9=21 overlooks the remaining sides.

### Which figures fit the expression?

For rectangles, 2×(width+height) comes from matching opposite sides. A triangle requires adding its actual three sides rather than applying the rectangle expression. Sides 5, 6 and 7 cm give 5+6+7=18 cm, counting each once.

A square has four equal sides. Six-centimetre sides give perimeter 6+6+6+6=24 cm. Multiplying six by six instead measures interior area. Both calculations use side lengths but answer different questions.

Equal perimeter does not ensure equal area. Rectangles 8×5 and 7×6 each have perimeter 26 cm, but areas 40 cm² and 42 cm². Identical fence lengths can enclose different amounts of land. This follows from the calculations, not from pictures looking alike.

The workshop divides each side into 1 cm segments. Walk one at a time or predict before completing the circuit. Gold marks visited boundary segments, not filled interior squares. Changing a side restarts the path so visits to an earlier figure do not count towards the new one.

**Quick check:** find the perimeter of a 9 cm by 4 cm rectangle. Explain why its perimeter matches 8 cm by 5 cm while its area need not match.
