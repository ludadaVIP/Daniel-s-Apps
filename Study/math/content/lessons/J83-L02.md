# 代入消元法

## 等值表达式要整个替换

若一式给出 y=7−x，那么 y 在另一式中可被整个 7−x 替换。等量代换不只代入其中一个数，也不改变原符号。若 y 外面还有系数，应写括号，让系数作用到整个表达式。

这样把两个未知数的共同问题转为一个未知数，再回到原关系求另一个。只求出 x 不等于完成方程组；得到数对后，还应同时代回两条原式。每一步都要能解释为何保留原共同解。

### 例 1：先表示、再代入、最后回代

解 x+y=7、2x+y=11。第一式两边减 x，得到 y=7−x。第二式变成 `2x+(7−x)=11`，合并为 x+7=11，所以 x=4。回代 y=7−4=3，得到数对 (4,3)。

回查和为七、加权值为十一，两式成立。若第二式中出现的是 3y，就必须替换成 `3(7−x)`，不能写成 21−x，也不能改变为 21+x。括号不是装饰，它保留了原来的完整 y 对象和乘法范围。

### 例 2：精确分数也可以是解

解 2x+y=1、x−y=0。第二式给 x=y；代入第一式为三 x 等一，所以 x=1/3，回代 y=1/3。精确数对 `(1/3,1/3)` 使两式左边分别一和零。

写成 (0.33,0.33) 则第一式为 0.99，不是精确一。本课没有要求近似时，分数不能偷偷截断。也可从第一式写 y=1−2x，再代第二式，同样得到三 x 等一；方法不同不改变原解。

## 消元得到零时，先读含义

x+y=4、2x+2y=8 中，用 y=4−x 代入第二式会得到八等于八，或整理成 0=0。这说明第二条没有增加独立限制，不是说所有 x、y 随便取都符合。原关系 x+y=4 仍保留，共同解沿这条线有无穷多。

若第二式为 2x+2y=9，代入却要求八等于九，或 0=1，便没有共同数对。不能把 0=0 或 0=1 当成普通的一次方程再除零。先判断恒等还是矛盾，才决定结论。

工作台逐步显示孤立变量、完整代入式、合并后的一元关系及回代。可以退回一步检查。更换孤立哪个字母只改变方法，不改变原问题，已有数对作答保留；更换方程组才清空。家长请孩子指明被替换的整个表达式、每个括号和最后两次原式核验。

# Substitution method

## Replace the entire equal expression

If one equation gives y=7−x, replace all of y in the other equation with 7−x. Equal substitution neither replaces only part nor changes signs. When y has an outside coefficient, keep brackets so that coefficient multiplies the whole expression.

This reduces the shared problem to one variable, after which back-substitution finds the other. Finding x alone does not complete the system. Verify the resulting ordered pair in both originals and explain why each step preserves the shared solutions.

### Example 1: isolate, substitute and back-substitute

For x+y=7 and 2x+y=11, the first equation gives y=7−x. Substitute into the second: 2x+(7−x)=11, hence x+7=11 and x=4. Back-substitute y=3, obtaining (4,3).

The pair has sum seven and weighted value eleven. If another equation instead contained 3y, replace it by 3(7−x), not 21−x or 21+x. The brackets preserve the complete y object and the range of its multiplier.

### Example 2: retain exact fractions

For 2x+y=1 and x−y=0, the second equation gives x=y. Substitution yields 3x=1, so both variables equal one third. The exact pair (1/3,1/3) gives one and zero in the originals.

The rounded pair (0.33,0.33) instead produces 0.99 in the first equation. Without an approximation request, retain exact fractions. Isolating y=1−2x from the first equation and substituting into the second reaches the same result: a changed strategy does not change the solution.

## Interpret a zero row before dividing

For x+y=4 and 2x+2y=8, substitution yields eight equals eight, or 0=0. The second equation adds no independent restriction, but the original relation remains. Infinitely many pairs lie on x+y=4; arbitrary pairs do not all qualify.

Changing the second constant to nine yields a contradiction such as 0=1, so no shared pair exists. Neither 0=0 nor 0=1 should be handled by dividing by zero. Distinguish identity from contradiction first.

The workshop reveals isolation, whole-expression substitution, the simplified one-variable equation and back-substitution in stages, with an undo step.
