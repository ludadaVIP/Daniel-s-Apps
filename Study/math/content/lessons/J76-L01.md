# 样本与总体初识

## 先说明研究谁，再说抽了谁

总体是本次研究想了解的全部对象或记录，样本是实际取得的那部分观测。还要说明观测单位：研究每本书的厚度时，一本书对应一条记录，不能忽然把书架数量当作书本条数。总体不是抽完后剩下的部分，样本也不与总体相加形成“新的总数”。

抽样框指能够进入选择机制的对象范围。若研究全部二十四条记录，却只允许 A 组八条被抽，B、C 组没有机会，这个覆盖缺口不会因为 A 组抽得更多就消失。方法、覆盖与未回答情况，往往比只看样本条数更关键。

### 例 1：总体、样本与回答

虚构通勤研究的总体有二十四条记录，本次取六条不同编号，样本量是六，占总体四分之一。二十四仍是总体，不是十八；六与二十四相加的三十也不是研究的记录总数。

若邀请八个不同编号回答，只有五个回应，回答率为 `5/8`。三个未答不能补成三个零分钟，否则创造了没有观察到的数据。求回答者均值时用实际完整回答的记录；要推断全部目标，还要考虑未答者是否可能不同，而不是只把分母改好就声称无偏。

### 例 2：算得出样本均值，不等于知道总体

完整样本通勤时间为六、八、十、十二分钟，总和三十六除四，样本均值九分钟。这个九是样本计算的确数，但不自动是总体均值。若抽样框只含近处一组，它可能系统漏掉另一类记录。

在抽样设计适合估计比例的假设下，十个样本中两个有某特征，对一百个总体成员可粗估二十个。二十是估计，不是逐个观察得到的确数；抽样误差与潜在偏差仍需报告。把它写得很精确，不能弥补抽样方法缺陷。

## 随机不等于每次都一样

工作台构造二十四条虚构记录，三个编号组各八条。便利模式固定只取 A 组前几条；随机模式让全部编号有资格，用固定种子的伪随机次序演示不放回抽取，方便重复检查。这是教学模拟，不是真实调查，也不是用程序结果证明某种抽样方法每次都最准确。

随机样本可能少取或没取某组，均值也会波动。抽样框覆盖全部目标与本次样本恰好包含哪些组，是两个问题。随机设计改善机会机制，并不保证某一次样本更接近总体；一次巧合准确也不能证明便利方法可靠。

先预测样本均值，再展开虚构总体对照。家长可问“谁有资格进入抽取？”“这次实际取到谁？”“这个结论只描述样本，还是扩大到总体？”能分清目标、机制和推断范围，比只报平均数更接近数据推理。

# Population and sample basics

## Define the target before the selection

A population is the full set of objects or records targeted by a study. A sample contains the observations actually obtained. State the observation unit: a study of book thickness records one observation per book, not per shelf. The population is not merely the unselected remainder, and sample and population counts are not added together.

The sampling frame is the set eligible for selection. Targeting twenty-four records while allowing only eight in group A excludes B and C. Taking more from A does not repair that coverage gap. Selection, coverage and nonresponse matter beyond sample size alone.

### Example 1: target, sample and response

A fictional commute study targets twenty-four records and selects six distinct IDs. Sample size is six and the sampling fraction is one quarter. The population remains twenty-four, not eighteen or thirty.

If eight distinct IDs are invited and five respond, the response fraction is 5/8. Three missing replies are not three zero-minute observations. Calculate a respondent mean from complete observed records, while considering whether nonrespondents may differ before generalising to the target. A correct denominator alone does not remove nonresponse bias.

### Example 2: a known sample mean is not a known population mean

Sample times six, eight, ten and twelve minutes total thirty-six, giving an exact sample mean of nine. This does not automatically identify the population mean, particularly if the frame covers only one convenient group.

Under a sampling design suitable for a rough proportion estimate, two of ten sampled members with a feature suggests about twenty in a target of one hundred. Twenty is an estimate rather than a census count. Report uncertainty and possible bias; extra numerical precision cannot repair a flawed selection method.

## Random does not mean identical or guaranteed accurate

The workshop constructs twenty-four fictional records in three groups of eight. Convenience mode uses the first few A records. Random mode makes all IDs eligible and uses a fixed-seed pseudorandom ordering to illustrate sampling without replacement reproducibly. It is a demonstration, not a real survey or proof of guaranteed accuracy.

A random sample can miss a group and its mean can vary. Frame coverage and realised composition are different questions. A random design improves selection opportunities without guaranteeing that each particular sample is closer to the population. One coincidentally accurate convenience sample does not validate its method.

Predict the sample mean, then reveal the fictional population. A parent asks who was eligible, who was actually selected and whether a conclusion describes only the sample or generalises to the target. Link scope, mechanism and evidence rather than reporting a mean alone.
