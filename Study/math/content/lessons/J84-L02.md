# 一步不等式

## 保留同一批解

解不等式是把原条件变成容易读的完整范围。两个条件如果对每个允许的 x 都同时真或同时假，就有相同解集，叫等价。只看一个候选碰巧通过，不能证明变形对所有数都等价。

两边同加或同减一个确定的数，大小顺序保持；其实两边同加减同一个有定义的式子也一样。两边同乘或同除正数，顺序保持。两边同乘或同除非零负数，顺序反转，下一课会用数轴解释。乘零不能还原原信息，除零没有定义；因子的符号未知时，不能直接套正数或负数规则。

变形保留的是解集，不是每边原来的数值。我们改变两边，但必须有能逆向恢复的依据。移去一个常数是同步加减的简写，不是“搬到另一边就随意改不等号”。

### 例 1：x+3≤7

第一步，两边同减三，得 `x+3−3≤7−3`。第二步算得 `x≤4`。减三不会改变数轴上的左右顺序，所以不等号不反向；原来允许等号，结果仍允许等号。

核边界：四代回为七≤七，成立。核一内一外：三代回为六≤七，成立；五代回为八≤七，不成立。这些核验帮助发现方向错误，完整等价的依据仍是两边同减三并可逆地同加三。

若只把左边减三，会得到 x≤7。例如六符合这个新条件，却使原式九≤七为假，说明解集变宽了。不能用“我已经把三移走了”来代替同步操作的理由。

### 例 2：正除数与负除数

解 `3x>−6`。两边同除正三，得 `x>−2`，方向保持。虽然右边是负数，也不会因此反向；决定规则的是本步的除数正三。零代回为零>负六，成立；负三代回为负九>负六，为假。负二使等号成立，但原条件严格大于，故排除。

对照 `−2x<6`：这次同除负二，必须反向，得到 `x>−3`。零通过原式，负四不通过；若误写 x<−3，就会刚好允许错误的一侧。包含性和方向分开处理：若原条件改成 −2x≤6，结果为 x≥−3，等号并未消失。

## 文字条件与操作记录

例如虚构温度计显示 x+1.5°C，要求显示至多 6°C，写 x+1.5≤6。同减 1.5 得 x≤4.5。x 是温度，题目没有要求非负，不能额外添加 x≥0。相同代数若描述数量，才需根据题意加数量域。

工作台允许选择操作、操作量、两边或仅左边，以及是否反向。只有符合通用可逆规则的步骤才进入记录；错误试写仍可查看其范围是否改变。某次错误操作碰巧保留解集，也不能据此推出对所有不等式都成立的规则。撤回一步可比较依据，而不是反复猜最终数字。

家长让孩子先说“我对两边做什么、为什么不丢解”，再检查边界。不要把每一步都机械读成“移项改号”：常数项的正负与不等号方向是两件不同的事。

# One-step inequalities

## Preserve the same values

Solving rewrites a condition into a readable complete range. Equivalent conditions are true for exactly the same allowed values of x. One successful test value cannot establish equivalence for the whole set.

Adding or subtracting the same number on both sides preserves order, as does adding the same defined expression. Multiplication or division by a positive number preserves direction. A nonzero negative factor or divisor reverses it; the next lesson explains why. Multiplication by zero loses information, division by zero is undefined, and an unknown sign cannot be treated as definitely positive or negative.

The side values may change while the solution set remains. A justified operation can be undone. “Moving a term” abbreviates common addition or subtraction; it does not permit arbitrary changes to the comparison.

### Example 1: x+3≤7

Subtract three from both sides: x+3-3≤7-3, giving x≤4. Common subtraction retains left-to-right order, so there is no reversal. Equality inclusion is retained too.

Four gives 7≤7 and passes. Three gives 6≤7 and passes, while five gives 8≤7 and fails. These checks can expose mistakes, but equivalence follows from the reversible common subtraction, not from a finite sample.

Subtracting three only on the left gives x≤7. Six would then pass although the original comparison is 9≤7, false. The new set is wider, so the one-sided step is not equivalent.

### Example 2: positive and negative divisors

For 3x>-6, divide both sides by positive three to obtain x>-2. A negative right side does not require reversal: the divisor is positive. Zero passes the original; minus three fails. Minus two gives equality and is excluded by strict >.

Compare -2x<6. Dividing by negative two reverses the comparison, giving x>-3. Zero passes and minus four fails. Writing x<-3 would admit the wrong side. If the original were -2x≤6, the result would be x≥-3: reversal retains equality inclusion.

## Words and operation records

A fictional thermometer reading x+1.5°C must be at most 6°C. Write x+1.5≤6, then subtract 1.5 to obtain x≤4.5. The unknown is a temperature; no nonnegative restriction was given. Quantity domains must come from context rather than being added automatically.

The workshop lets you choose an operation, amount, sides and whether to reverse. Only steps justified by the general reversible rule enter the history. An unadopted trial can still show whether its set changed. A coincidental match in one case does not prove a universal rule. Undo steps to compare their reasoning.

A parent asks what happens to both sides and why it preserves every solution. Separate the signs of terms from the direction of the inequality, rather than using an unexplained “move and change signs” routine.
