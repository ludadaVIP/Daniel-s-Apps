# 生活中的线性模型

## 数量、单位与适用域一起定义

建立模型前说明输入 x、输出 y 代表什么、用什么单位、哪些值允许。y=mx+b 中 m 的单位为“输出单位/输入单位”，b 与 y 同单位。若把 x 单位从分钟改成小时，斜率数值也要相应转换，不能只换轴标签而保留旧系数。

本课全部为明确给定假设的虚构模型，不是实际商家价格、实测水速或天气数据。恒定单位变化在限定范围内成立，不能从少数点就保证任意外推。数学表达式能计算，与实际情境允许，是两层不同的检查。

### 例 1：固定费加每公里费用

虚构已启动的接送服务，启动费 NZ$5、每 km 追加 NZ$2，只讨论 0≤x≤8 km 的行程，y 为 NZD 费用。m=2 NZD/km、b=5 NZD，故 `y=2x+5`。2x 的单位是 NZD，与固定费五同单位才能相加。

三公里费用十一。零公里时模型仍给五，因为题目说服务已启动，并非说明所有真实服务都如此收费。若把一般式误写为 5x+2，既交换固定费与单位费的角色，也改变模型。x=10 虽能代算二十五，却超出题设零至八的适用域，不能声称已得到该情境允许的报价。

已知线性服务在一小时收七、三小时收十三，则单位变化率为六除二等三 NZD/h，零输入固定项为七减三等四，得 y=3x+4。这里“已知线性模型”是前提，两个记录本身不证明服务在所有时长都保持线性。

### 例 2：排空停止与整数整包

虚构容器初有 12 L，每分钟恒定排出 2 L，到排空停止。用 x 分钟、y 余水升数，原规则 `y=12−2x`，m=−2 L/min、b=12 L。两分钟余八升，六分钟余零；允许输入为 0≤x≤6。

七分钟代数给负二升，但不是允许的余水。排空以后如果继续观察，需另外给 y=0 的水平规则，整体成为不同阶段的模型；不能继续原斜率、把负数取绝对值，或把原线无限画下去。实验只绘原规则的有效段。

另有虚构卡片：原来两张，每包三张，最多打开四整包，故 y=3x+2，x 仅可为零、一、二、三、四。四包共十四张。输入半包会代算三点五张，关系计算有值却不在题设整数域；图应只画允许的五个点，不能用连接线暗示每个中间实数输入都可以。

## 家庭挑战

在纸上设计一个连续量费用模型和一个计数模型，注明 m/b 数值、单位、输入域与假设。列三行、画图，从两个不同 x 的点回求参数，再核第三点。交换点序或改变两轴刻度时，解释斜率为何不变；模型换单位时则需转换数值，不能混为一谈。

再设计一条会到达停止边界的规则，例如排空，写出原直线在哪结束以及后续是否另建规则。家长检查单位是否一致、候选是否在域内、离散点是否误连；不把拟合一条线当作模型已获现实验证。全活动只需纸笔，无需购买、实测或个人信息。

# Linear models in real contexts

## Define units and domains with the rule

Define input x, output y, their units and permitted values. In y=mx+b, m has output units per input unit and b has the output unit. Changing minutes to hours requires converting the slope value, not merely relabelling the axis.

All models here are fictional and supplied with assumptions, not actual fares, measured flow or weather observations. A constant rate applies only within its specified scope. Being able to evaluate an expression is different from allowing that input contextually.

### Example 1: a starting fee and per-kilometre cost

A fictional already-started transport service charges NZ$5 plus NZ$2 per km for 0≤x≤8 km. With output cost y NZD, m=2 NZD/km and b=5 NZD, so y=2x+5. The product 2x and fixed five have compatible dollar units.

Three kilometres cost eleven. At zero the model still charges five because the service is started by assumption; this is not a claim about all real services. Swapping the coefficients to 5x+2 changes both roles and the model. Ten kilometres gives an algebraic output twenty-five outside the stated domain, not an established allowed fare.

For a supplied linear hourly model charging seven at one hour and thirteen at three hours, the rate is 6/2=3 NZD/h and b=7-3=4 NZD. Thus y=3x+4. Linearity is a premise; two records alone do not prove unlimited linear behaviour.

### Example 2: stopping at empty and whole packs

A fictional 12-litre tank loses two litres per minute until empty. With time x minutes and remaining water y litres, y=12-2x has m=-2 L/min,b=12 L. Two minutes leaves eight litres and six minutes leaves zero, giving domain 0≤x≤6.

At seven minutes the expression yields minus two litres, outside the original model. Later observation requires a separate y=0 rule after emptying, changing the overall rate structure. Do not extend the decreasing line or take an absolute value to repair impossible water. The experiment draws only the valid segment.

For fictional cards, two initially plus three per pack gives y=3x+2, with whole-pack input x=0,1,2,3,4. Four packs gives fourteen cards. Half a pack algebraically gives 3.5 cards but is not an allowed input. Plot five discrete points without a joining line implying fractional packs.

## Family investigation

Design a continuous-quantity cost model and a count model on paper. State m/b, units, domains and assumptions. Table three inputs, graph, recover parameters from two distinct x values and check the third. Explain why reversing points or changing display scales preserves slope, while changing measurement units requires conversion.

Add a stopping boundary and decide whether later behaviour needs another rule. A parent checks units, allowed candidates and discrete plotting rather than treating a fitted line as real-world validation. No purchases, real measurements or personal information are needed.
