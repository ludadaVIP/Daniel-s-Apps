## 理论概率和本批实验频率各有来源

理论概率来自明确的理想模型；实验频率来自**这批记录中符合事件的次数÷这批总次数**。两者可能接近，却不是同一个量。模拟是用程序生成的演示记录，不是现实中已经做过的测量或实物实验，必须清楚标明。

本工作台使用可重复的伪随机序列：相同种子和转盘结构可以重放同一批，方便核对。理论值仍依据理想均匀角度；程序序列一旦确定可以重放，并不能因此证明所有随机序列的性质。改变转盘结构或种子会开始新批次，避免混合不同模型。

### 例 1 · 十次六个 A，频率不能改成一半

1. 理想转盘 P(A)=1/2，给定十次模拟记录中有 **6 次 A**。
2. 本批实验频率为 **6/10=3/5=0.6**。
3. 理论值一半与记录比例零点六不同，各自如实保留。
4. 不能把六次涂改成五次来“符合理论”，也不能由这批零点六宣布理想概率改变。
5. 若只有五次而 A 零次，频率为 0/5=0，但不证明模型中 A 不可能。

**零次试验**时分母为零，频率未定义，不先初始化为零。还没有记录与已经观察五次都未符合，是不同证据状态。图表在零次时不画一个假的频率零点，也不从零强行连到第一条记录。

### 例 2 · 再多一次可能更远

1. 从十次六个 A 的记录继续，当前频率 0.6，理论值 0.5。
2. 如果下一次还是 A，新频率为 **7/11**，约 0.636，反而离一半更远。
3. 若下一次不是 A，新频率为 **6/11**，约 0.545，更接近一半。
4. 两种后续在理想模型中都有机会，前十次不强迫“补偿”。
5. 因此不能说每增加一次都会更接近，也不保证任意有限批次恰好达到理论比例。

在独立且机制不变的理想重复模型中，过去结果不改变下一次事件概率。连续出现很多 A，不意味着下一次“欠”一个 B。频率长期可能表现得更稳定，仍不能拿这句话保证特定下一次或每批固定配额；本课用明确反例检查这些过度说法，不要求背诵大数定律。

工作台记录累计次数、有利次数和精确频率，同时画理论虚线与频率轨迹。横轴按累计次数比例，纵轴固定零到一；更改观察字母可重新统计同批记录，但不重新抽取。清空重放时归零本批记录，并恢复原种子序列；每批上限一千次，图示最近八次但累计计数保留全部。

家庭挑战：先写理论条件，再生成二十次模拟，如实记录并标来源。两批结果不必相同，比较时写各自次数，不能挑好看的结果留下。家长核对次数、比例、理想条件和下一次说法。带走一个发现：**记录频率诚实计算，理论机会按模型说明；短期不需补偿，零次频率未定义。**

## Theory and observed batch frequency have different sources

Theoretical probability comes from an explicit ideal model. Experimental frequency is **this batch’s event hits divided by its trial count**. They may be close but are not identical quantities. Computer simulation produces demonstration records, not measurements or physical experiments already performed in the world.

The workshop uses a reproducible pseudorandom sequence: the same seed and spinner structure replay the batch for checking. Theory still assumes ideal uniform angles. A fixed program sequence can be reproduced, not used to prove universal properties of random sequences. Changing structure or seed starts a separate batch to avoid mixing models.

### Example 1 · Six A outcomes in ten trials remain frequency 0.6

1. Ideal P(A)=1/2, while ten supplied simulated trials contain **six A outcomes**.
2. Batch frequency is **6/10=3/5=0.6**.
3. Retain both the theoretical one half and recorded proportion 0.6.
4. Do not edit six hits to five to fit theory, or infer that the ideal probability changed.
5. Five trials with no A have frequency 0/5=0, without proving A impossible in the model.

With **no trials**, the denominator is zero and frequency is undefined, not initialized to zero. No observations and five observed failures are different evidence states. The graph therefore shows no fake zero-frequency observation or segment from zero before the first record.

### Example 2 · An additional trial can move farther away

1. Start from six A outcomes in ten trials: frequency 0.6 versus theoretical 0.5.
2. Another A gives **7/11**, about 0.636, farther from one half.
3. A non-A gives **6/11**, about 0.545, closer.
4. Both continuations are possible under the ideal model; history does not require compensation.
5. More trials do not guarantee improvement at every step or exact agreement in a finite batch.

Under unchanged ideal independent repetition, the past does not change the next event’s probability. A long run of A does not make the mechanism owe a B. Greater long-run stability does not guarantee a particular next result or a fixed short-run quota. Explicit counterexamples matter more here than memorizing the name of a theorem.

The workshop records total trials, hits and exact frequency, plotting a theoretical dashed line and cumulative frequencies. The horizontal axis scales by cumulative trial count, while the vertical scale stays zero to one. Changing the counted letter recounts the existing batch without new draws. Clearing restores the seed for replay. Each batch permits up to one thousand trials; the latest-eight list is a display window while full cumulative counts remain. For a family task, state the theoretical assumptions, generate twenty simulated trials and label their source. Compare batches with their own denominators without selecting only attractive results. The takeaway preserves records, derives theory from assumptions, rejects short-run compensation and leaves no-trial frequency undefined.
