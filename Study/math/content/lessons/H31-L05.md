# 约束条件下的优化

优化不是单独解A′=0，而是在可执行的输入中比较目标。本课用三边围栏练习把约束、目标与定义域一起消元，并检查边界和整数并列。

## 从图形到可行域

矩形靠一条直墙，只有两条深度x与一条宽度y需要围栏，总长L米，故2x+y=L。假设围栏可按所需长度分配、无接缝损耗，墙足够长，场地约束为深度不超过c米。

要求正边长x>0、y>0。消元y=L−2x，得到0<x<L/2且x≤c。面积A=x(L−2x)，单位平方米。若另要求整米，x与y还须为整数；L为整数时，只要x是整数，y也为整数。

### 例1：最优可能在导数非零的约束端点

L=24，c=4，连续长度。A=24x−2x²，导数24−4x给未受限驻点6，但6不在0<x≤4内。

整个可行域上A′≥8>0，所以面积递增，最大在x=4取到。此时y=16、A=64。正好用2×4+16=24米，满足正边长与深度上限。若忽略上限得到x6、y12、面积72，那是另一个问题的答案。

改c=9，驻点6进入可行域。配方A=72−2(x−6)²≤72给出全域证明，等号仅x=6，y=12。图上最高的点需和约束交集核对，不能只靠曲线形状或一个导数方程。

### 例2：整数最优不是一律四舍五入

L=22，c=9，整米边长。连续中心L/4=5.5，面积式为60.5−2(x−5.5)²。允许整数中5和6离中心同为0.5，其余可行整数更远。

x5给y12、面积60；x6给y10、面积60。故完整最优深度集合{5,6}，并有两组尺寸。只写“四舍五入到6”遗漏并列，写5.5则违反整数约束。两组均需核2x+y22、正边长和x≤9。

若c=0，约束要求x≤0同时x>0，没有可行矩形。不能画退化边长0然后称最大面积0；域空与非空域里最值未取到也不同。

## 覆盖所有候选才是优化证明

连续可行域里，最靠近抛物线中心L/4的输入最优；若上限在中心左边，取上限，否则取中心。由于中心为正且小于L/2，只要c>0就能取到连续最优。整米模式可以列出全部允许整数，也可用等距平方式证明只需比较中心邻点及可行边界，保留所有并列。

工作台面积图以深度米和面积平方米分别为轴；实线与离散点区分连续/整数可行域，虚线显示不可行比较。尺寸图上下共用一个长度比例，上边标直墙不计围栏；并列时画第一组，核验表保留全部尺寸。连续表列推导的最优候选，不能把一行表误当搜索全部实数的证明。

报告应同时给目标、约束、定义域、所有最优尺寸、单位、证明与无损耗假设。家长复核消元是否丢条件、边界是否可取及整数并列是否完整。此为虚构几何模型，实际材料接缝和场地条件改变时要重新建模。

# Optimisation under constraints

Optimisation compares an objective over executable inputs; solving A′=0 is only part of it. Use a three-sided fence to eliminate variables while preserving constraints, boundaries and integer ties.

## From geometry to the feasible domain

A rectangle lies against a straight wall. Fence two depths x and one width y, so2x+y=L metres. Assume divisible lengths, no joining losses, a sufficiently long wall and a depth cap c metres.

Positive sides require x>0,y>0. Eliminate y=L−2x to obtain0<x<L/2 and x≤c. Area is A=x(L−2x), measured in square metres. Whole-metre mode also requires integer dimensions; integer L and x automatically make y integer.

### Example 1: A constraint endpoint can optimise with nonzero derivative

Take L24,c4 and continuous lengths. A=24x−2x² has derivative24−4x and unconstrained stationary point six, outside0<x≤4.

The derivative is at least eight and positive throughout the feasible domain, so area increases to x4. Width is sixteen and area64. The dimensions use2×4+16=24 metres and satisfy positivity and cap four. Depth six, width twelve and area72 solve a different problem that omitted the cap.

With cap nine, stationary six becomes feasible. Completing the square gives A=72−2(x−6)²≤72, with equality only at six and width twelve. The highest curve point must be checked against the feasible set; a shape or derivative equation alone is insufficient.

### Example 2: Whole-number optima can tie

For L22,c9 and whole-metre sides, the continuous centre is5.5. Write A=60.5−2(x−5.5)². Allowed integers five and six are equally close to the centre; every other feasible integer is farther away.

Depth five gives width twelve and area60; depth six gives width ten and the same area. The complete optimal-depth set is{5,6}, with two dimension pairs. Rounding to six discards a tie, while reporting5.5 violates the integer requirement. Check length22, positive sides and cap nine for both pairs.

If the cap is zero, x≤0 contradicts x>0. The feasible domain is empty. A degenerate zero-depth drawing is not a feasible zero-area optimum, and an empty domain differs from an unattained optimum on a nonempty domain.

## Prove coverage, not just one candidate

The continuous optimum is the feasible input closest to parabola centre L/4. Use the cap when it lies left of the centre, otherwise the centre itself. Since the centre is positive and below L/2, every positive cap allows attainment. For integers, enumerate every feasible input or use the squared-distance identity to justify neighbouring integers and boundaries, retaining all ties.

The area plot labels depth in metres and area in square metres. Solid curves or discrete dots distinguish continuous and integer feasibility; dashed portions show excluded comparisons. The dimensions diagram uses one length scale and leaves the wall unfenced. It draws the first tied optimum while the table retains all dimensions. The continuous table records a derived optimum; one table row is not an exhaustive search over reals.
