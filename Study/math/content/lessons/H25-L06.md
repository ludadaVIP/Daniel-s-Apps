# 切线方程

有限导数提供切线斜率，但一条直线还需要位置。切线必须同时满足：经过正确接触点，并具有该处导数给出的斜率。只有斜率或只有过点，都不能唯一确定所求切线。

## 点与斜率共同决定

在a处先求A(a,f(a))，再求m=f′(a)。有限导数存在时，切线为y−f(a)=m(x−a)。它也可展开为y=mx+[f(a)−ma]。接触点的横坐标a不能当成纵坐标，f′(a)也不能替代f(a)。

求出方程后做两项回查：代x=a是否得到f(a)，直线斜率是否等于f′(a)。零斜率给水平线y=f(a)，不等于竖直线x=a。对于本课未定义有限导数的尖点，不靠猜画一条绿线。

一般函数的切线由局部变化率定义。圆的切线只有一个公共点，是圆这个特定对象的性质，不能挪作所有曲线的通用定义。切线可以穿过曲线，也可能在别处再次相交。

### 例1：平方函数与两种错线

f(x)=x²在a=2处，接触点(2,4)，导数f′(x)=2x给斜率4。点斜式y−4=4(x−2)，展开为y=4x−4。代x=2得4，斜率也为4，两项均满足。

y=4x斜率正确，但代2得8，不经过接触点。y=2x经过(2,4)，但斜率2不是4。两种错误分别漏了位置与变化率。等价方程2y−8x=−8与原切线相同，非零倍数不会改变直线；y=x²则是原曲线，不是完整直线方程。

### 例2：穿越与另一个交点

g(x)=x³在0处点值0、导数0，切线y=0。负x时曲线在此线下，正x时在其上，因此确实穿过切线。这没有违背导数定义。

再取h(x)=x³−3x，在a=1处h(1)=−2、h′(1)=3−3=0，所以切线y=−2。解h(x)=−2等价于x³−3x+2=(x−1)²(x+2)=0，除接触点x=1外，还有x=−2。切线在别处再交，依然是1处的切线；它不因此成为−2处的切线，那里导数为9。

## 工作台与出口

选择曲线与a，先预测接触高度，再预测完整切线。绿色线用精确导数和接触点构造；缩放视窗只裁剪显示，不改变方程或曲线定义域。常数曲线与其水平切线可以重合，这也是有效情形，不能因为交点无限多就拒绝它。

方程接受点斜式、展开式、移项与非零常数倍。输入需要一个等号；只写斜率4并没有指定直线位置。核验表保留a、f(a)、f′(a)和完整方程，避免凭图看着贴合就判定。

出口推导平方切线，并给两个反例说明一般切线的边界。家长可把“代点”和“核斜率”写成两张检查卡，让孩子分别使用；若只完成其中一张，还需回看点斜式与导数定义。

# Equation of a tangent line

A finite derivative supplies a tangent slope, but a line also needs a location. It must pass through the correct contact point and have the derivative’s slope there. Either condition alone is insufficient.

## A point and a slope together

At input a, first find A(a,f(a)), then m=f′(a). With a finite derivative, the tangent is y−f(a)=m(x−a), or y=mx+[f(a)−ma]. Input a is not the contact height, and derivative value f′(a) is not interchangeable with function value f(a).

Check both requirements afterward: substituting x=a must give f(a), and the line’s slope must equal f′(a). Zero slope gives horizontal y=f(a), not vertical x=a. A corner without a finite derivative does not receive an invented line by visual guesswork.

General tangency is defined through local rate. The single-intersection property of a circle tangent belongs to that specific curve, not to every function. A tangent may cross its curve or meet it again elsewhere.

### Example 1: A square curve and two wrong lines

For x² at a=2, the contact is (2,4) and derivative 2x gives slope four. Thus y−4=4(x−2), or y=4x−4. Substituting two gives four, and the slope is four: both checks pass.

The line y=4x has the right slope but gives eight at x=2, missing the point. The line y=2x passes through the point but has the wrong slope. The errors omit location and rate respectively. The equation 2y−8x=−8 represents the same tangent through a nonzero scaling; y=x² is the original curve rather than a line equation.

### Example 2: Crossing and another intersection

For x³ at zero, value and derivative are zero, so the tangent is y=0. Negative inputs give values below the line and positive inputs above it: the curve crosses the tangent without contradicting its derivative.

For h(x)=x³−3x at a=1, h(1)=−2 and h′(1)=0, giving y=−2. Intersections satisfy x³−3x+2=(x−1)²(x+2)=0. Besides the contact at one, there is an intersection at negative two. This remains the tangent at one, but is not the tangent at negative two, where the derivative is nine.

## Explore and explain

Choose a curve and contact input, predict the contact height, then give the entire tangent equation. The green line uses exact point and derivative data. A display-window change clips the drawing without changing equations or domains. A constant curve can coincide with its horizontal tangent; infinitely many intersections do not invalidate that case.

Accepted equations include point-slope form, expansion, rearrangement and nonzero constant multiples. Enter one equals sign: slope four alone leaves the line’s location unspecified. The audit retains a, f(a), f′(a) and the full equation, preventing apparent visual contact from replacing mathematical checks.

For the exit, derive the square tangent and supply the counterexamples. A parent can use separate “substitute the point” and “check the slope” cards. Completing only one means the learner still needs the point-slope relationship or derivative definition.
