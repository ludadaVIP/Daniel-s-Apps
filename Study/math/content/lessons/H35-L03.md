# 三角恒等变形

恒等式声明在规定定义域内，对每一个允许的角都成立。它不同于只寻找少数解的方程，也不同于某张图上看起来相同。三角化简的重要步骤是选择合适表示，把分母条件随每一步保存。

## 单位圆关系与合法约分

令s=sinθ、c=cosθ，则s²+c²=1。这个关系可把s²替成1−c²，或把c²替成1−s²。选择取决于目标形式，不能因为两个表达式在单位圆上相等，就忽略题目要求只用哪一个字母。

约分约掉的是乘法中的公共非零因子，不是加减项。若分母出现1−c，先列1−c≠0；若有s或c，则分别列s≠0或c≠0。化简后这些分母可能不见了，但原式在被排除的点仍没有值。

### 例1：化简一个有孔的商

在1−c≠0时，s²/(1−c)=(1−c²)/(1−c)=[(1−c)(1+c)]/(1−c)=1+c。第一步用单位圆恒等式，第二步因式分解，第三步仅在公共因子非零时约分。

cosθ=1恰在θ=2kπ，k为整数。因此原式定义域排除全部这些角。在闭区间[0,2π]中应排除0与2π两个端点；如果区间改成[0,2π)，才只有0这个排除点，端点条件必须读清。

在θ=0处，化简式1+cosθ等于2；原式却是0/0，无定义。把2填回孔里会得到一个延拓函数，不是原式突然获得了值。两者在原式定义域内相等，这才是此恒等变形的准确含义。

### 例2：目标形式与原式条件分开检查

把(s+c)²−2sc化为常数。展开得到s²+2sc+c²−2sc=s²+c²=1。这里没有分母，所有实θ都可用，不需要凭空增加排除点。

再看(1−c²)/s=s²/s=s，但原式要求s≠0，即θ≠kπ。类似地tanθ cosθ=(s/c)c=s，仍要求c≠0，即θ≠π/2+kπ。右侧sinθ可在所有实角有值，但不能替原左侧补上定义。

若题目把s²−c²改写为仅含c的多项式，结果为1−2c²。原样保留s²−c²虽然等价，却没有完成指定表示。这里同时检查数学相等和目标形式，完整符号证明仍由学生书写。

## 让图上的孔看得见

工作台横轴用θ/π，所以0.5代表π/2；不是0.5弧度。橙实线绘制化简式的延拓，紫虚线绘制原式轨迹，白圈表示原式缺失的点，中心橙点只属于延拓。线型也区分两条曲线，避免只靠颜色。

可切换三个不同分母，并探查特殊角。先预测只含指定字母的结果，再写闭区间[0,2π]内全部排除角。选择孔处时，原式须显示无定义，即使化简式仍有精确值。数值轨迹只是核查工具；有限个角不能证明对所有允许角成立。

## 出口如何论证

按“原域—恒等关系—分解—非零约分—保留条件”解释。家长可以追问被约掉的是因子还是项，以及θ=0的2属于哪个函数。若学生把无定义说成0，先回到分母为0的事实；若学生只报化简式，则要求补完整原域。

本课自动部分只核有限支持语法中的多项式结果、特殊角集合与原域资格。证明过程、为何未填孔及对全部角成立的理由要人工审核，不用几个关键词判定理解。

# Trigonometric identities

An identity holds for every allowed angle on a stated domain. It differs from an equation that asks for particular roots, and from expressions that merely agree at several sampled points. Preserve the original domain while choosing a useful symbolic representation.

## Unit-circle algebra and legal cancellation

Write s=sinθ and c=cosθ, so s²+c²=1. Replace s² by 1−c² or c² by 1−s² according to the requested form. An equivalent expression that still uses a forbidden letter may not complete a representation task.

Cancel common nonzero factors, never separate terms in a sum. A denominator 1−c requires 1−c≠0; denominators s and c require their own nonzero conditions. When cancellation removes a written denominator, its original restrictions do not disappear.

### Example 1: Simplify a quotient with holes

On 1−c≠0, s²/(1−c)=(1−c²)/(1−c)=[(1−c)(1+c)]/(1−c)=1+c. The steps are the unit-circle identity, factorisation and cancellation of a nonzero common factor.

The excluded angles satisfy cosθ=1, namely θ=2kπ for integer k. In the closed interval [0,2π], both zero and 2π must be excluded. In a half-open interval [0,2π), only zero remains in that list. Read endpoint conventions before reporting a finite angle set.

At θ=0 the simplified expression equals two, but the original quotient is 0/0 and undefined. Filling the missing value defines an extension. It does not make the original expression defined there. Equality on the original domain is the correct claim.

### Example 2: Separate target form from domain

Expand (s+c)²−2sc as s²+2sc+c²−2sc=s²+c²=1. There is no denominator, so this identity needs no extra exclusions.

By contrast, (1−c²)/s=s²/s=s requires s≠0, excluding θ=kπ. Likewise tanθ cosθ=(s/c)c=s requires c≠0, excluding θ=π/2+kπ. The simplified sine is defined at more angles; it cannot supply values to the original left side at its holes.

To express s²−c² using only c, substitute s²=1−c² and obtain 1−2c². Repeating s²−c² is mathematically equivalent on the circle but does not meet the requested form. A complete argument checks equivalence, representation and original conditions separately.

## Make missing inputs visible

The workbench’s horizontal coordinate is θ/π: 0.5 represents π/2, not half a radian. Orange solid shows the simplified extension, while purple dashed shows the original trace. White circles mark missing original inputs; orange centres belong only to the extension. Line styles support the distinction without relying solely on colour.

Switch denominators and probe special angles. Predict the polynomial in the permitted letter and list all excluded angles in the closed interval [0,2π]. At a hole, the original must remain undefined even when the extension has an exact value. A sampled graph is an inspection aid, not proof for infinitely many allowed angles.
