# 三角形分类与边的关系

## 先问存在，再问属于哪一类

给出三条正长度，不保证一定能围成三角形。非退化三角形要求任意两边之和严格大于第三边。先找最长边，若另两边之和不超过它，便不能围出区域。等号对应拉成直线的退化边界，不算本课的三角形。

存在性通过后，分类有两条轴。按边可以问等边、等腰、不等边；按角可以问锐角、直角、钝角。同一图形可以同时具有边类别和角类别，不是所有名称彼此互斥。本课等腰采用至少两边相等的定义，因此等边是它的特殊情形。

### 例 1：三条边何时能关上

长度 2、3、5 cm 中，短两边和为五，恰等最长边，只能拉直，不能围成非退化三角形。3、4、5 cm 则满足 `3+4>5`、`3+5>4`、`4+5>3`，可以成形，三边不等，按边最具体名称是不等边。

固定两边三与五厘米，第三边 c 要同时满足 `3+c>5` 与 `3+5>c`，所以 `2<c<8`。若 c 限正整数厘米，可取三、四、五、六、七；最小三、最大七，两个端点二和八都不能取。这个范围来自严格不等式，不是“差不多能关上”就行。

工作台给边长时先检成形；失败便只画三段长度条，不画一个角度看似正常的假三角形。长度条保持统一比例，可以比较哪两段之和不够。改变任一边后，必须重新核范围与分类。

### 例 2：两条分类轴与包含关系

三边 5、5、8 cm 可以成形，恰两边相等，为非等边的等腰三角形。5、5、5 的最具体名称是等边；它也满足至少两边相等，所以同样属于广义等腰。题目要求最具体名称时写等边，不是在宣称等腰这个更宽的说法数学错误。

按角则看最大角：三个都小于 90° 是锐角三角形；一个等于 90° 是直角三角形；一个大于 90° 是钝角三角形。35°、55°、90° 是直角，不能因有两个锐角就叫锐角。30°、40°、110° 是钝角。45°、45°、90° 同时是等腰和直角，说明两个分类方向可以并存。

## 条件与外观不能替换

一条边看起来水平或两个角看起来相等，都不构成精确的边长或角度条件。按边题用给定长度，按角题用给定角与内角和，不能把一张缩小图上的像素长度当作题目的厘米。示意图若省略具体长度，也不能只凭外观断言等边。

家长可给出“2、2、5”追问：虽然有两条相等，为什么还不能叫一个等腰三角形？答案应先指出无法成形。再让孩子举一个等腰锐角、等腰直角或等腰钝角的角组，逐个核对总和一百八十与相等底角，理解分类名的适用条件。

# Classifying triangles and side relationships

## Establish existence before classification

Three positive lengths need not close a triangle. A nondegenerate triangle requires every pair sum to exceed the remaining side strictly. If the shorter pair does not exceed the longest, no region closes. Equality gives a collinear degenerate boundary rather than a triangle in this lesson.

Once existence is established, classify on two axes: by sides as equilateral, isosceles or scalene, and by angles as acute, right or obtuse. A triangle can belong to a side category and an angle category together. Here isosceles means at least two equal sides, so equilateral is a special case.

### Example 1: when can the sides close?

Lengths 2,3,5 cm fail because 2+3=5. Lengths 3,4,5 pass all three strict pair inequalities and form a scalene triangle. For fixed sides three and five, a third side c must satisfy 3+c>5 and 3+5>c, giving 2<c<8.

If c is a positive integer in centimetres, the possible values are three through seven. Neither endpoint is allowed. The strict range follows from geometry, not a judgement that rods almost close.

The workshop first tests its side lengths. When they fail, it shows three proportionally drawn rods rather than inventing a plausible-looking triangle. Changing a side requires a fresh existence check and classification.

### Example 2: classification axes and inclusion

Sides 5,5,8 cm form an isosceles but non-equilateral triangle. Sides 5,5,5 have the most specific name equilateral and also satisfy the inclusive isosceles definition. Asking for the most specific name does not make the broader membership mathematically false.

By angles, inspect the largest: all below 90° is acute, one equal to 90° is right, and one above 90° is obtuse. Angles 35°,55°,90° make a right triangle even though two angles are acute. Angles 30°,40°,110° make an obtuse triangle. The set 45°,45°,90° is both isosceles and right, illustrating the independent axes.

## Use givens rather than appearance

A horizontal-looking side or equal-looking angles do not supply exact length or angle conditions. Use the stated lengths for side classification and the stated angles plus the sum for angle classification. Pixels in a scaled picture are not the supplied centimetre units. An unmarked sketch alone does not establish an equilateral triangle.

A parent can offer 2,2,5 and ask why the two equal lengths do not make an isosceles triangle: existence fails first. Then create angle sets for isosceles acute, right and obtuse triangles, checking equal bases and the 180° sum. Each name has conditions, and checking those conditions comes before applying the label.
