## 先找邻居，再找中点

四舍五入是在指定精度下，用一个方便的数描述原数。**必须先说清楚到最近的十、百还是千。** 原数不会消失，近似数也不是精确数。

本课只讨论非负整数，使用通常的“四舍五入”约定：到两个相邻整数倍的距离相同，也就是正好在中点时，取较大的那个。其他场景可能使用不同舍入约定，要按题目的规则做。

### 例 1 · 3,465 到最近的百

1. 两个相邻整百是 **3,400 与 3,500**。
2. 中点为 (3,400 + 3,500) ÷ 2 = **3,450**。
3. 3,465 离 3,400 是 65，离 3,500 是 35。
4. 因为更接近上边，所以 **3,465 ≈ 3,500（到最近的百）**。

同一个 3,465 到最近的千却是 3,000，因为整千中点是 3,500。两种说法的精度不同，不能只看结果不同就说有人算错。

### 例 2 · 中点与进位 / 9,995

1. 到最近的十，整十邻居为 **9,990 与 10,000**。
2. 两边距离都是 5，正好一半。
3. 按本课约定向上取 **10,000**。进位会连续穿过几个 9，不能写成 9,910。

### 估计帮助检查，不代替精确计算

1,980 + 3,040，先各自到最近的百，得到 2,000 + 3,000 = **约 5,000**。精确合计为 5,020，接近估计；如果笔算得到 50,200，就应该回头检查数位。

不过，先到百、再到千可能错过原来的位置。例如 4,499 直接到最近的千是 **4,000**；先变成 4,500，再舍入会得到 5,000。**直接从原数到题目要求的精度，避免双重舍入。**

**小检查：** 不背口诀，用数轴的邻居、距离和中点解释一次四舍五入。

## Find the neighbours, then the midpoint

Rounding describes a number conveniently at a chosen precision. **State whether the requested unit is tens, hundreds or thousands.** Keep the original value: a rounded number is an approximation, not the exact value.

This lesson considers non-negative whole numbers and uses a half-up convention. If distances to neighbouring multiples are equal, choose the larger multiple. Other contexts may specify another convention; follow the question’s rule.

### Example 1 · 3,465 to the nearest hundred

1. The neighbouring hundreds are **3,400 and 3,500**.
2. Their midpoint is (3,400 + 3,500) ÷ 2 = **3,450**.
3. The distances are 65 to 3,400 and 35 to 3,500.
4. The upper hundred is closer, so **3,465 ≈ 3,500 (nearest hundred)**.

The same 3,465 rounds to 3,000 at the nearest thousand, whose midpoint is 3,500. The precisions differ; different answers alone do not show an error.

### Example 2 · Halfway and carrying / 9,995

1. For the nearest ten, the neighbours are **9,990 and 10,000**.
2. Both distances are five: exactly halfway.
3. This lesson’s convention gives **10,000**. Carrying crosses several 9s; 9,910 is not the result.

### Estimates check exact work

For 1,980 + 3,040, round each to hundreds: 2,000 + 3,000 gives **about 5,000**. The exact sum is 5,020, close to the estimate. An answer of 50,200 should prompt a place-value check.

But rounding to hundreds and then thousands can change the result. Directly round 4,499 to thousands and get **4,000**. First changing it to 4,500 and then rounding gives 5,000 instead. **Round directly from the original value to the requested unit to avoid double rounding.**
