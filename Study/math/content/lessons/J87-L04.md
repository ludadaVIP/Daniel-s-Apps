# 勾股定理应用

## 先认直角与斜边

先从题设找到直角：矩形的邻边垂直、明确垂直的墙地模型，或直接给定的直角三角形。图上看起来垂直不算新条件。找直角对面的斜边 c 后，再列 a²+b²=c²；求斜边平方相加，求一条腿则用斜边平方减另一腿平方。

长度单位必须先统一，例如一米与六十厘米应先都化为米或厘米，再平方。平方结果对应面积单位，开方才回到长度。√r 表示平方等于非负数 r 的非负数；这节只用它记精确长度，不要求把所有根式学完。整数或分数若恰好平方匹配，也可写精确数值。

### 例 1：矩形对角线的精确值

虚构矩形两边三米、四米，对角线把它分成直角三角形。d²=3²+4²=25，因此 d=5 m。七米是边长相加，不是对角线；二十五是平方值，不是长度。

改为二米与三米，d²=4+9=13，所以 d=√13 m。没有整数的平方等于十三，仍能用根式精确表达；约 3.6055 m，若题目要精确值，不能用 3.61 冒充。工作台按精确平方核验，而不把接近的值都判相等。

若矩形边为二与四，d²=20，√20 与 2√5 表示同一个非负长度，因为它们的平方都为二十。本单元不强制某一种根式简化形式；可以先保留 √20。单个根式的有理数倍、根式除以常数及精确小数/分数在表示相同长度时都可接受，输入格式会明确提示。

### 例 2：斜边已知要减平方

已知直角三角形斜边十三厘米、腿五厘米，另一腿 b²=13²−5²=169−25=144，故 b=12 cm。若误把两平方相加，会算出比斜边还长的“腿”，不符合角色；直接十三减五得八，也不是正确的平方关系。

分数同样可以精确处理：两腿 3/4 cm 与 1 cm，斜边平方为 9/16+16/16=25/16，正根为 5/4 cm，也就是精确小数 1.25。不要把平方分母十六误当长度分母十六，更不能把负的 −5/4 当距离。

虚构垂直墙地模型中，梯长五米、触墙高四米，梯是斜边，水平距离平方二十五减十六等九，距离三米。这里仅作数学模型，不安排真实梯子操作。若没有给墙地垂直，就不能额外从插图补成直角。

## 坐标中的同一结构

在互相垂直、同长度单位的坐标轴上，两点横差与纵差组成直角边。例如 (1,2) 与 (4,7) 横差三、纵差五，距离为 √34 个长度单位。若屏幕轴比例不同，像素斜线不代表真实单位距离；应回到坐标数值计算。

家长检查四件事：直角从哪里来、哪条是斜边、所求是长度还是平方、单位是否统一。工作台揭示原式后仍要求解释，不把一个正确数字当作所有情境都能套用定理的证据。

# Using Pythagoras

## Identify the right angle and hypotenuse

Use a supplied right angle: rectangle sides, explicitly perpendicular model surfaces or a stated right triangle. Appearance cannot add that premise. The hypotenuse faces the right angle. Add leg squares to find its square; subtract from a known hypotenuse square to find a missing leg.

Unify length units before squaring. Square values use area units; taking a root returns length. √r denotes the nonnegative number squaring to r. Here it records exact lengths without requiring a complete course on radicals.

### Example 1: exact rectangle diagonals

A fictional three-by-four-metre rectangle has d²=9+16=25, so d=5 m. Seven adds sides and twenty-five is a square value, neither the diagonal length.

For two by three metres, d²=13 and d=√13 m. Its approximate value 3.6055 does not make 3.61 exact. The checker compares exact squares rather than admitting every nearby decimal.

For two by four, d²=20. Both √20 and 2√5 are the same nonnegative length because their squares equal twenty. Simplifying is optional here; supported single-root rational multiples and exact numerical equivalents are explained beside the input.

### Example 2: subtract for a missing leg

With hypotenuse thirteen centimetres and leg five, b²=169-25=144, hence b=12 cm. Adding squares would make a supposed leg longer than the hypotenuse; subtracting lengths directly gives eight, also incorrect.

For legs 3/4 cm and 1 cm, the square is 9/16+16/16=25/16. The nonnegative length is 5/4 cm, exactly 1.25. Do not retain the squared denominator as a length or admit its negative root as distance.

In a fictional perpendicular wall/ground model, a five-metre ladder reaching four metres high has horizontal distance three. This is only a mathematical model, without actual ladder use. If perpendicularity is not given, do not infer it from a sketch.

## Coordinates use the same structure

On perpendicular equal-unit axes, coordinate differences are legs. Points (1,2),(4,7) differ by three and five, giving exact distance √34. Unequal screen display scales do not change the coordinate calculation.

A parent checks the source of rightness, hypotenuse identity, length versus square and consistent units. A correct number alone does not establish that Pythagoras applies to every context.
