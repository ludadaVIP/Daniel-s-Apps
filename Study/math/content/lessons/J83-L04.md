# 坐标图解方程组

## 两条线共同的点，就是共同数对

一条二元一次方程的全部实数解，可以在坐标平面上表示为一条直线。横坐标取 x、纵坐标取 y，同一点必须同时在两条线上，才是方程组的解。图像把“同时满足”变成两集合的共同点，而不是只观察哪条线画得更高。

不同的非平行直线相交于一个点，对应唯一解；不同的平行线没有共同点，对应无解；两线重合时，共同点有无穷多，但只沿那条线，不是整个平面。最终判断仍应结合原方程，不能只凭像素、颜色或视觉斜角。

### 例 1：读交点再核精确值

x+y=5 与 x−y=1 的交点是 (3,2)。代回第一式三加二为五，第二式三减二为一，都符合。交换坐标会让第二式变成负一，所以即使和保持，数对顺序也不能掉。

对于 x+y=1、x−y=0，交点在半与半。图上可能只读到约 0.5，但原式可确认精确值各为 1/2。近似读数如 0.49 不能当成精确解。坐标轴每格代表多少，横纵是否同单位步长，都要分别读，不能靠屏幕上的角度替代数值关系。

### 例 2：竖直、重合与视窗外

x=2 是竖直线，y=3 是水平线，交点 (2,3)。不能因为竖直线无法写成 y=mx+b，就说它不是方程图像。这里按一般关系 ax+by=c 绘制，不强迫每条都先解成 y 的函数。

x+y=4 与 2x+2y=8 重合，(0,4)、(2,2) 都符合，但 (0,0) 不符合。相反，x+y=4 与 x+y=6 是平行不同线，没有同一点能同时把和变成四和六。图能展示这些区别，原关系则给出精确依据。

## 看不见，不等于不存在

若两轴当前只显示 −5 至 5，方程 x+y=12、x−y=2 的共同点 (7,5) 不在完整视窗内。相加得到二 x 等十四，再回代就能确认它存在。只因屏幕上没看到交点就说无解，会把显示边界错当数学条件。

拓宽视窗到 −10 至 10，交点可见；改变横纵显示尺度也可能改变线的外观，但原解仍为 (7,5)。工作台改变视窗时保留方程、已写答案和结论；换一组方程才重置任务。精确代数结论可主动展开，点只有在当前窗内才画出。

两条线使用实线与虚线及文字编号区分，重合时能看到覆盖，不只靠颜色。家长问“无交点，还是窗里看不到？”“这个点代回两式都成立吗？”“重合还保留了哪条条件？”用图提出判断，再用关系控制判断范围。

# Solving systems graphically

## Shared points represent shared ordered pairs

The real solution pairs of a linear equation form a line. Use horizontal x and vertical y. A point solves the system only when it lies on both lines. Graphs show simultaneous satisfaction as shared points, not as whichever line appears higher.

Distinct nonparallel lines meet once, distinct parallels never meet, and coincident lines share infinitely many points restricted to that line. Pixels, colours or apparent angles alone do not establish exact mathematical classifications.

### Example 1: read and verify an exact intersection

The lines x+y=5 and x−y=1 meet at (3,2). Substitution gives five and one. Swapping coordinates preserves the sum but changes the difference to −1, so ordered meaning remains essential.

For x+y=1 and x−y=0, the intersection is exactly (1/2,1/2). A graph may initially suggest approximately 0.5, while substitution confirms the precise fractions. A reading such as 0.49 is not the exact solution. Read the two axis scales separately; a visual angle does not replace the numerical relationship.

### Example 2: vertical and coincident lines

x=2 is vertical and y=3 horizontal, meeting at (2,3). A vertical relation is valid even though it cannot be written y=mx+b. General equations ax+by=c do not require every line to be solved as a function of x.

The equations x+y=4 and 2x+2y=8 coincide. Both (0,4) and (2,2) qualify, but (0,0) does not. In contrast, x+y=4 and x+y=6 are distinct parallels: a shared pair cannot have both sums. The picture illustrates these cases and the original relations justify them exactly.

## Unseen does not mean nonexistent

In a window spanning −5 to 5 on each axis, the intersection (7,5) of x+y=12 and x−y=2 is outside the displayed range. Adding equations gives x=7 and substitution y=5. The display boundary is not a mathematical restriction on the system.

Expanding the window to −10…10 reveals it. Changing scales can alter appearance without changing the pair. The workshop preserves equations, entered answers and conclusions when only the window changes; a new system resets the task. An algebraic result can be revealed explicitly and a point is plotted only inside the current window.

Solid and dashed lines with numbered text distinguish curves, including overlaps, without relying solely on colour. A parent asks whether an intersection is absent or merely outside the view, whether the exact pair satisfies both equations, and which relation coincidence still retains.
