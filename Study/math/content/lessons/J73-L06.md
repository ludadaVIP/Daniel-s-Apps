# 不合理答案的识别

## 原方程与情境条件是两道关

解出一个代数值后，不能立即宣布问题完成。第一关把候选代回原方程，两边精确相等才通过；第二关看它是否属于题目定义的允许范围。原式不成立是计算或推导有误；原式成立却违反范围，说明给定条件合起来没有这个允许解。两个原因应分开报告。

负数、分数和零没有统一的“好坏”。人数、完整袋数须非负整数；长度可以是正分数；精确年龄可以是非负分数；本课假想摄氏读数允许负有理数。范围由数量意义和明确条件决定，不由答案看起来整不整齐决定。

### 例 1：等式通过，完整组数不通过

恰好八人，每组三人且每组完整，设 x 为组数，则 `3x=8` 且 x 为非负整数。代数上同除三得 `x=8/3`，代回 `3×8/3=8`，原等式通过。但 `8/3` 不是整数完整组数，所以本情境没有符合全部条件的解。

不能四舍五入为三组：三组三人需要九人，代回原式左九右八；也不能向下取二组再忽略剩下两人，因为题目要求恰好全体形成完整组。若另一个任务允许余人，应重新明确新的条件，而不是悄悄改变本题。固定每袋两支、共五支的完整袋数同样得到 `5/2`，也不能靠舍入过关。

再看虚构单价 x≥0，`2x+5=3`。代数解负一让等式成立，但违反题设非负单价。错误不是“任何负解都错”，而是这个变量在这道题不允许负值。

### 例 2：分数长度、负温度与零数量

一厘米线段分成三段等长，设每段 x cm，则 `3x=1` 且 x>0。解 `x=1/3 cm`，正分数长度有效，三段合一厘米。写 `0.33 cm` 会使三段总长变成 `0.99 cm`，不是精确原条件。

假想摄氏读数 x，升高五度后为二度，`x+5=2`，解 `x=−3°C`。负读数在本题允许，代回 `−3+5=2`，两关通过。四个相同袋每袋 x 支总共零支，则 `4x=0`，x 为非负整数。唯一解零既满足等式又满足范围；零并不代表没有解。

## 分类比笼统的“答案不合理”更有用

工作台为每个候选同时显示原式两边数值、等式是否成立、情境范围是否通过。先在完整组数案例输入 `8/3`，看等式通过而范围失败；再输入三，看范围通过而等式失败。换到长度输入 `1/3`、温度输入负三、零数量输入零，比较通过原因。

“无解”在情境题中表示无符合全部条件的解，不否认放宽范围后方程仍可能有有理解。若只问代数解，应写精确代数值；若问情境允许解，就要连同整数、正数或非负限制一起判断。本单元的无解输入是一个明确分类，不是自动理解一段文字解释。

家庭挑战可以纸上设计两题：允许分数的年龄或长度，以及必须整数的完整袋数。故意给一组不可能条件，让孩子指出失败的是哪一关，再用原式证明舍入为何不能修复。家长复核的是变量、条件和证据；自动判对数值或分类不能替代这段解释。

# Spotting impossible solutions

## The original equation and the domain are two gates

An algebraic value is not the end of a word problem. First substitute the candidate into both original sides and compare exactly. Then check the allowed domain defined by the problem. A failed equality points to an incorrect candidate or derivation. A passing equality with a failed domain means the value does not satisfy the combined contextual conditions. Report these reasons separately.

Negatives, fractions and zero are not universally valid or invalid. Whole groups and bags require non-negative integers. Lengths may be positive fractions; exact ages may be non-negative fractions. Fictional Celsius readings in this lesson may be negative rationals. Meaning and stated conditions determine the range, not whether the answer looks tidy.

### Example 1: equality passes, whole groups fail

Exactly eight people must form complete groups of three. Let x be the non-negative integer group count. The equation is `3x=8`, with algebraic root x=8/3. Substitution gives `3×8/3=8`, so equality passes. However, 8/3 is not an integer count of complete groups. No allowed solution satisfies all conditions.

Rounding to three groups needs nine people and fails the original equality. Rounding down to two ignores two remaining people, contrary to the requirement that everyone forms complete groups. A different task allowing leftovers would need different explicit conditions. Similarly, whole bags of two containing exactly five pencils cannot be obtained by rounding 5/2.

A fictional non-negative unit price x satisfying `2x+5=3` has the algebraic root −1. It passes equality but violates this stated price domain. The reason is not that all negative roots are wrong; this particular variable is not allowed to be negative.

### Example 2: fractional length, negative temperature and zero count

A one-centimetre segment has three equal parts of length x cm. Here `3x=1` and x>0, giving the valid exact length 1/3 cm. Three such parts total one centimetre. Using 0.33 cm instead totals 0.99 cm and fails the exact condition.

A fictional Celsius reading x rises five degrees to two: `x+5=2`. The root −3°C passes both the equation and the permitted domain. Four identical bags with x pencils each and total zero satisfy `4x=0`, with non-negative integer x. Zero passes both gates and is a solution, rather than no solution.

## Name the failed condition

In the whole-group workshop, try 8/3 and observe that equality passes while integrality fails. Try three and observe the reverse: an allowed integer fails the equation. Switch to length 1/3, temperature negative three and zero pencil count and explain why each passes.

“No solution” in a context means no value satisfies all conditions. It does not deny that the equation might have a rational root in a wider domain. If asked only for the algebraic root, give it exactly. If asked for the allowed contextual solution, include the domain judgement. Automatic classification checking is not automatic understanding of a written explanation.

For a paper family challenge, create one fractional-age or length context and one whole-bag context. Deliberately make one impossible, identify the failed gate and use the original equation to show why rounding does not repair it. A parent reviews the variable meaning, conditions and evidence; an automatically accepted number or classification does not replace that reasoning.
