# 构造反例

全称命题说“每一个符合条件的输入都成立”。反驳它不必列尽输入，只须构造一个在域内、满足前提却使结论为假的例子。主动选择这个例子，比等待软件随机碰出失败更能显示理解。

## 一个反例有三项检查

先确认输入域，再核前提，最后算结论。若前提不成立，该输入对条件命题不适用；若结论也成立，这一例通过，不能称反例。一个通过例通常不能证明无限域的全称断言，一个有效失败却足以否定它。

工作台接受有理数：每个分量绝对值不超过20，约分后分母不超过100，支持分数和有限小数。这个界限是实验输入范围；练习中的命题仍针对全部有理数。找到界内反例已经足以推翻更大的全称命题，找不到则不能声称界外也没有。数对用(a,b)，单数题只填x的数值。

### 例1：选择能保留交叉项的输入

命题：“对所有有理数a,b，(a+b)²=a²+b²。”取a=b=1，左边4，右边2。两个数在域内，前提满足而结论假，所以这是反例。

也可以取a=1/2、b=2：左25/4、右17/4，相差2。展开两边差为2ab，故任意非零有理数a,b都可构造反例。若取a=0，交叉项消失，该输入恰好通过，不能反驳。判题接受所有有效的界内反例，不要求和例题数值相同。

再看“若x²=x，则x=1”。x=0时0²=0前提真，但0≠1结论假，构成反例。x=2的结论也假，却因4≠2不满足前提，不是反例。把x²=x两边除以x会丢失x=0；改成“若x²=x且x≠0，则x=1”才排除了这个失败点，修正后的命题另需证明。

### 例2：同周长与同面积

矩形边长(a,6−a)和(b,6−b)，规定0<a,b<6。两者周长都2×6=12，面积为a(6−a)、b(6−b)。取(a,b)=(1,2)，得到1×5与2×4，面积5与8不同，所以“同周长面积必同”被反驳。

取(1,5)只是1×5和5×1，面积同为5；它满足结论，不是反例。取(0,2)使第一矩形退化，不符合严格正边长前提，也不是有效反例。图采用同一长度刻度，每单位40像素，面积差来自真实边长，不靠任意放大画面暗示。

## 有限表格怎样启发反例

f(x)=x+x(x−1)(x−2)在0、1、2都等于x，因为乘积有一因子0。三个通过行不构成恒等证明。取x=3，乘积6，f(3)=9≠3。结构告诉我们选一个不等于三个根的有理数，比盲试更直接。复习中三个根会移动，仍要针对当前式子构造输入，不能背一个固定数字。

软件精确核有理数、前提及结论两边，并分别反馈格式失败、前提失败、符合结论或有效反例；它不会凭一句“我找到了反例”自动认定论证完成。出口要说明命题的“所有”范围、输入为何合规、前提为什么真、结论为什么假。家长审核完整解释，也可追问怎样修改原命题才能避开已找到的失败点。

# Constructing counterexamples

A universal claim says that every admissible input meeting its conditions works. To refute it, construct one input in the domain with a true premise and false conclusion. Deliberately selecting that case shows more understanding than waiting for random trials to fail.

## Three checks for a counterexample

Check domain first, premise second and conclusion last. A failed premise makes an input inapplicable to the conditional claim. An agreeing conclusion makes that case pass, not a refutation. One passing example normally cannot prove an infinite-domain universal statement, whereas one valid failure refutes it.

The workbench accepts rational components of magnitude at most twenty and reduced denominator at most one hundred, including fractions and finite decimals. These bounds limit the experimental interface; the claims still concern all rational inputs. A bounded witness already refutes the larger universal claim. Failure to find one does not establish anything outside the search range. Enter pairs as (a,b); scalar tasks ask only for the value of x.

### Example 1: Preserve the missing cross term

Consider “(a+b)²=a²+b² for all rational a,b”. With a=b=1, the left is four and the right two. Inputs are admissible, the premise holds and the conclusion fails.

Alternatively a=1/2,b=2 gives 25/4 versus 17/4, differing by two. Expanding shows difference 2ab, so any nonzero rational a,b supply a witness. Choosing a=0 removes the cross term and passes that case instead. Grading accepts other valid bounded witnesses rather than matching only the demonstration values.

For “if x²=x then x=1”, choose zero: 0²=0 holds but zero is not one. Choosing two also makes the conclusion false, but its premise fails because four is not two. Dividing x²=x by x loses zero. The revised claim “if x²=x and x≠0, then x=1” excludes this failure; it is a new claim that requires its own proof.

### Example 2: Equal perimeter does not force equal area

Rectangles have sides (a,6−a) and (b,6−b), with 0<a,b<6. Both perimeters are twelve, while their areas are a(6−a) and b(6−b). Choose (1,2) to obtain one by five and two by four, areas five and eight. The equal-area assertion fails under a true equal-perimeter premise.

Choosing (1,5) merely gives one by five and five by one, both area five; it agrees rather than refutes. Choosing (0,2) degenerates the first rectangle and fails the strictly positive-side premise. Both drawings use the same forty-pixel length unit, so the area difference follows from actual sides rather than arbitrary rescaling.

## Let structure guide an example beyond a finite table

For f(x)=x+x(x−1)(x−2), inputs zero, one and two all give f(x)=x because one factor vanishes. Three agreeing rows do not prove identity. At x=3 the product is six, giving f(3)=9 rather than three. Structure suggests any rational other than the three roots. Review tasks move the roots, requiring a witness for the current expression rather than a memorised number.

Software checks exact rational arithmetic, premise and both conclusion sides. It distinguishes format failure, failed premise, agreement and valid refutation. It does not approve an argument simply because a learner writes “counterexample”. For the exit, identify the universal domain, justify admissibility and the premise, and demonstrate the false conclusion. A parent reviews the whole explanation and can ask how the claim might be revised to exclude the discovered failure.
