# 多项式乘多项式

## 每一项都要与另一边每一项配对

两个括号相乘，可以先把第一个括号看成整体，再分配；接着将每个得到的乘法继续分配。两项乘两项应有四对，三项乘两项应有六对。画行列表能保存每一对的来源，比只记“首尾相乘”可靠。

先列齐乘积，再合并同类项。两个交叉项不是同一个东西数两次，而是来自两个不同配对，可能同号相加，也可能异号相消。不能还没计算就删掉它们。

### 例 1：四块面积与两个交叉项

展开 `(x+2)(x+3)`。按行记录 `x·x=x²`、`x·3=3x`、`2·x=2x`、`2·3=6`，再把两个一次项合为 5x，得 `x²+5x+6`。

取 x=4 作正长度图：六乘七的外框按四与二、四与三分成四块，面积为十六、十二、八、六，合计四十二。这些面积与各配对对应，不能只算对角两块十六和六。图中 x 是同一个四，不能同一字母在不同边取不同值。

### 例 2：有符号配对与两个字母

`(2x+1)(x−3)` 的四对为 `2x²、−6x、+x、−3`。两个一次项合为 −5x，结果 `2x²−5x−3`。漏掉 `1·x` 就会错误留下 −6x；把两括号直接相加则完全改变问题。

对 `(x+y)(x+2y)`，四对为 `x²、2xy、yx、2y²`。交换律使 yx 与 xy 的字母部分相同，可合并成 `x²+3xy+2y²`。最后的 y² 来自 y 乘 2y，不能只把系数乘了却漏掉一个 y。

## 一般式、面积与取值条件

对任意实数 x、a、b，分配得到 `x²+bx+ax+ab=x²+(a+b)x+ab`。中间系数是 a+b，来自两对各贡献一个 x。若 a=−b，中间系数为零，要省掉零项；若偏移同号，两项就同向合并。

用 x、a、b 作分块长度时，它们必须非负且外边长为正。负偏移仍可进行代数分配，但不直接画成负宽度。图的适用条件比代数恒等式窄，不应为了保留图片而偷偷限制公式的一般性。

虚构花园长 n+2 米、宽 n+3 米，n>0，面积为 `n²+5n+6` 平方米；周长则是 `4n+10` 米。两种问题目标与单位都不同，某个 n 的数值面积也不能替代一般式。输入题要求省略单位时，仍应在解释中说清量纲。

工作台改变偏移会清空乘积揭示与旧结果；若只做某个数值检查，也不能证明对所有字母值都成立。家长可以先问“几行几列，几对乘积？”再问“哪些同类、为什么能合并？”顺序完整，符号才不容易丢失。

# Multiplying polynomials

## Every term meets every term of the other factor

Apply distribution first to a whole bracket and then to each resulting product. Two terms by two terms produce four pairs; three by two produce six. A row-and-column table preserves each product’s source more reliably than a rule about only first and last terms.

List all products before collecting. Cross terms arise from distinct pairs, not from counting one piece twice. Their coefficients may add or cancel; do not discard them in advance.

### Example 1: four areas and two cross products

For (x+2)(x+3), record x·x=x², x·3=3x, 2·x=2x and 2·3=6. Collect 3x+2x to obtain x²+5x+6.

At x=4, a six-by-seven rectangle splits along four/two and four/three. Its pieces have areas sixteen, twelve, eight and six, totalling forty-two. Using only diagonal pieces would lose two real regions. Every x represents the same four; it cannot change between sides.

### Example 2: signed pairs and two letters

For (2x+1)(x−3), the four products are 2x²,−6x,+x,−3. The linear terms collect to −5x, yielding 2x²−5x−3. Missing 1·x would incorrectly leave −6x. Adding the brackets instead is a different operation.

For (x+y)(x+2y), the pairs give x²,2xy,yx,2y². Commutativity makes yx and xy like variable parts, giving x²+3xy+2y². The last y² retains both y factors rather than only multiplying numerical coefficients.

## General expressions, areas and domains

For all real x,a,b, distribution gives x²+bx+ax+ab=x²+(a+b)x+ab. Two distinct pairs supply the coefficient a+b. If a=−b the cross terms cancel and the zero term is omitted. Same-sign offsets instead combine in the same direction.

Using x,a,b as partition lengths requires nonnegative segments and positive outer lengths. Negative offsets still work algebraically but are not negative physical widths. The picture’s domain is narrower than the identity’s, and must not silently restrict the general formula.

A fictional garden of n+2 metres by n+3 metres, n>0, has area n²+5n+6 square metres. Its perimeter is 4n+10 metres. Targets and dimensions differ; a numerical area at one n cannot replace the general expression. Even when an answer field omits units, explain them in the reasoning.

Changing an offset resets workshop reveals and answers. A numerical check may detect an error but does not establish every real input. A parent asks how many rows, columns and pairs are needed, then which products are like terms and why. A complete sequence helps prevent lost signs.
