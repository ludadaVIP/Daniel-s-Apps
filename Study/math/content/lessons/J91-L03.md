# 根式的化简

## 值相同，还要看形式是否完成

最简数值二次根式应没有可从根号内提出的非平凡平方因子，根号内没有分母，分母没有根式；同类项已合并，数值系数已算出并省掉零项。一个未化简式可能数值完全正确，却还没有完成题目要求的整理。工作台分别提示这两种情况，不把“等价但未完成”说成数值不相等。

化简依据是非负数的根式乘法与主根约定：b≥0、a为实数时，√(a²b)=|a|√b。取平方因子时开出来的是其非负平方根，不是原平方因子。若外面本来有负号，它仍保留。数值题支持有符号有理系数、√ 或 sqrt(...)，不靠近似小数替代精确根式；这里的受限输入不处理字母式。

### 例 1：平方因子与面积拼块

十二=四乘三。四块面积三的相同小正方形排成 2×2 阵列，每块边长 √3，外框面积十二，外边长是两段 √3，即 2√3。这说明 √12=2√3，而不是4√3或十二。块数四与每个方向两块不能混用，面积单位与长度单位也不能交换。

七十二=三十六乘二，所以 √72=6√2。也可以先提出九，得到3√8，但八仍含平方因子四，继续得3×2√2=6√2；停在3√8虽等价，仍未完成。

对 √98，找四十九乘二，得到7√2；对 −√45，找九乘五，主根为3√5，外部负号使结果为−3√5。被开方数为零时主根零，不画有正面积的方形；为一时 √1=1，最终不保留多余根号。图按各案例外框缩放，跨图像素大小不能直接比较真实边长。

### 例 2：分数被开方数与有理化

√(8/9)=√8/√9=(2√2)/3。分子提出四，分母九的主根是三，不是九；根号内的分数可以分开，是因为分子非负、分母严格正。

√(3/8)=√3/(2√2)。这已经提出分母的平方因子，但分母还含 √2。上下同乘 √2，等于乘一个值为一的非零因子比，得到 √6/4。六没有非平凡平方因子，分母四有理，因此完成。只写 √3/(2√2) 或原式数值等价，但尚未满足最简要求。

检查大小与平方：√6/4 为正，它的平方为6/16=3/8，与原主根相符。若写成 √6/2，平方会是3/2，代回便发现分母缺了一倍。只比较平方还必须核符号，否则负值也可能平方相同。

## 绝对值与外部负号

√((−3)²)=3，是主根的非负性；−√9=−3，是另取相反数。用“根号非负”把所有外部负号删掉，或用“平方开方相消”保留负的主根，都会出错。字母规则里的 |a| 是条件表达，不是装饰；若另已知 a≥0 才能写成 a。

最后同时核数值与形式：平方因子是否尽提、根号内是否整数、分母是否无根式、同类项是否合并、符号是否正确。本课不要求某个固定项序，接受等价的有理分数系数；自由解释与面积证明仍由家长阅读，不由形式检查替代。

# Simplifying radicals

## Equivalent value versus finished form

A finished numerical square-root expression has no remaining nontrivial square factor inside, no fractional radicand or radical denominator, and collected terms with evaluated coefficients and no zero terms. An equivalent unfinished expression is not a different value; it simply has not completed the requested form.

For b≥0 and real a, √(a²b)=|a|√b. Extract the nonnegative root of a square factor, not the factor unchanged. Retain an existing outside minus. Numerical input uses rational coefficients and √ or sqrt(...), not approximate decimals or variable expressions.

### Example 1: square factors and area tiles

Twelve is 4×3. Four equal area-three squares form a 2×2 array. Each side is √3, so the outer area is twelve and side 2√3, not 4√3 or twelve. Four pieces are not four pieces in each direction; area and length differ.

Since 72=36×2, √72=6√2. Extracting nine first gives 3√8, an equivalent but unfinished result because eight still contains square factor four. Continue to 6√2.

Likewise √98=7√2. For -√45, extract nine to get -3√5, retaining the outside minus. Zero area has zero side, while √1 finishes as one without a redundant radical. Each diagram fits its own outer square; pixel sizes across cases do not compare actual lengths.

### Example 2: fractional radicands

√(8/9)=√8/√9=(2√2)/3. The numerator contributes factor two and √9=3, not nine. Splitting requires a nonnegative numerator and positive denominator.

For √(3/8), extraction gives √3/(2√2), still with a radical denominator. Multiply numerator and denominator by nonzero √2 to obtain √6/4. Six is square-free and the denominator rational, so the result is finished.

Its positive value squares to 6/16=3/8. The incorrect √6/2 squares to 3/2, revealing the lost factor. Squared equality must also be accompanied by a sign check because opposite values share a square.

## Signs and the final audit

√((-3)²)=3 uses the principal-root convention; -√9=-3 applies a separate opposite operation. Neither deleting every outside minus nor blindly cancelling square and root is valid. The absolute value in |a| carries a condition; replace it by a only when a≥0 is known.

Audit both value and form: all square factors extracted, integer radicands, rational denominators, collected like terms and correct signs. Term order and equivalent rational coefficients may vary. A parent still reads the explanation or area argument; structural checking cannot replace reasoning.
