## 包住外面，需要多少面片？

一个理想盒长 4 cm、宽 3 cm、高 2 cm。内部能放二十四颗单位立方体，并不表示只需二十四平方厘米的纸包住外面。先在展开图上找全每个面，再预测面积。今天只算指定面片；实际纸张还需要知道如何排版和留接缝。

展开图把立体的面沿棱展开在平面上，面与面连接处可以折回。A、B、C、D 是一圈侧面，E 是顶面，F 是底面。图里看不见的后面和底面也需要计入。每面用两个相邻边长求面积，单位为 cm²；体积的 cm³ 不能拿来标纸面。

### 例 1 · 六个面，一面不漏

1. 前、后面各 **4×2=8 cm²**，两面共十六。
2. 左、右面各 **3×2=6 cm²**，两面共十二。
3. 顶、底面各 **4×3=12 cm²**，两面共二十四。
4. 六面总表面积 **16+12+24=52 cm²**，也可写 **2×(4×3+4×2+3×2)**。
5. 回到网格逐面数：两个十二、两个八、两个六，共五十二。体积仍是 **4×3×2=24 cm³**，测量对象不同。

选择某个面时，先读两条边，自己算，再核对这一面。合计前可在纸上勾记已算的面。工作台改变任一尺寸会清空旧面积结果，避免把上一只盒的答案带到新盒上。棱长 3 cm 的正方体有六个相同正方形面，表面积 **6×3×3=54 cm²**，体积则为二十七立方厘米。

### 例 2 · 无盖只少一个面

1. 保持 **4×3×2 cm** 的尺寸，去掉顶盖，底面保留。
2. 顶面面积是 **4×3=12 cm²**。
3. 五面总面积 **52−12=40 cm²**。
4. 也可直接相加：底十二、前后十六、左右十二，共四十。
5. 若误减掉顶和底，会得二十八；那是两头都开口的另一种结构，不符合无顶盖盒。

现在打开“纸张外接框”。本展开图的外接长方形宽 **2×(4+3)=14 cm**，高 **3+2+3=8 cm**，面积 **112 cm²**。框里有许多空白裁切区，不能把框面积当成盒表面积。反过来，知道面片五十二平方厘米，也不能保证任意同面积的纸都裁得下：一张很窄的长条可能放不下展开图。

一百一十二只属于当前这张网的矩形外接范围，不是所有包装方案的最小用纸量。换另一种展开图、改变排版或把面片分开裁，可能改变纸张要求。接缝、重叠与裁切余量也需额外资料。题目若明确“面片预算五十二，接缝另留六平方厘米”，总面积预算 **52+6=58 cm²**；这仍没有给任意纸张形状保证。

**家庭挑战：** 不需买材料或剪刀。画一只理想盒，标出六面；比较有盖五十二、无盖四十平方厘米和体积二十四立方厘米。再指出外接框中的空白，解释为什么还不能只凭总面积购买某尺寸纸张。家长按面数、单位、排版条件复核，不要求孩子说固定句子。

**带走一个发现：** 表面积数外面平方单位，体积数内部立方单位；实际用纸还需要形状、尺寸和排版资料。出口题改用米，先独立逐面计算，再说单位为什么是 m²。

## How many panels cover the outside?

An ideal box is 4 cm by 3 cm by 2 cm. Its twenty-four unit cubes do not imply twenty-four square centimetres of wrapping. Locate every face on a net before predicting area. We calculate specified panels; real sheet requirements also depend on layout and seams.

A net unfolds connected faces into a plane so they can fold back along shared edges. A, B, C and D form the side strip, E is the top and F the bottom. Include the back and bottom even when a solid drawing hides them. Multiply two adjacent edges for each face area in cm². Cubic centimetres measure a different quantity.

### Example 1 · Include all six faces

1. Front and back each have **4×2=8 cm²**, totalling sixteen.
2. Left and right each have **3×2=6 cm²**, totalling twelve.
3. Top and bottom each have **4×3=12 cm²**, totalling twenty-four.
4. Total surface area is **16+12+24=52 cm²**, also **2×(4×3+4×2+3×2)**.
5. Check the net: two twelves, two eights and two sixes give fifty-two. Volume remains **4×3×2=24 cm³**; it measures something different.

Select a face, read its two edge lengths and calculate before checking. Mark counted faces on paper to avoid omission. Changing a workshop dimension clears previous checked areas. A cube with 3 cm edges has six 9 cm² faces and surface area **6×3×3=54 cm²**, while its volume is twenty-seven cubic centimetres.

### Example 2 · Removing a lid removes one face

1. Keep dimensions **4×3×2 cm** and remove the lid while retaining the bottom.
2. The top has area **4×3=12 cm²**.
3. Five-face area is **52−12=40 cm²**.
4. Check directly: bottom twelve, front/back sixteen and sides twelve total forty.
5. Subtracting top and bottom gives twenty-eight, describing a different structure open at both ends.

Enable the bounding-sheet frame. This net spans **2×(4+3)=14 cm** by **3+2+3=8 cm**, a rectangle of **112 cm²**. Empty cutting regions inside the frame are not box surface. Conversely, a panel sum of fifty-two does not guarantee any equal-area sheet fits: a very narrow strip may be unsuitable.

The 112 cm² frame belongs to this particular net. It is not a universal minimum for every packaging layout. A different net, arrangement or separately cut panels may change sheet requirements. Seams, overlap and cutting allowances need further information. If a question explicitly budgets fifty-two for panels plus six for seams, total area budget is **52+6=58 cm²**, still without guaranteeing every sheet shape works.

**Family challenge:** draw and label all six faces without buying materials or using scissors. Compare closed surface area fifty-two, open-top area forty square centimetres and volume twenty-four cubic centimetres. Point out empty cutting regions and explain what sheet dimensions or layout information is still needed. A parent reviews faces, units and assumptions rather than exact wording.

**Takeaway:** surface area counts outside square units, volume counts cubic units, and actual paper needs shape and layout information. The exit ticket changes dimensions to metres; calculate faces independently and explain why the answer uses m².
