## 估算能筛查，不能代替证明

先估算再精算，有助于发现漏掉十位、单位错写或数量级不合理。估算必须说清楚方法，例如“两个数分别四舍五入到十，再相加”。不同方法可能给出不同合理估计，不能要求所有估计都与精确值相等。

**≈** 表示近似关系，**=** 表示精确相等。一个结果接近估计值，只说明它没有立刻暴露量级问题；还需要精算、反向关系或另一个可靠方法检查。

### 例 1 · 298+403 的估计与精确值

1. 298 四舍五入到十为 300，403 到十为 400。
2. 估计总量 **300+400=700**，写 298+403≈700。
3. 精确计算为 **701**，不能写原和等于 700。
4. 原数在 290–300 与 400–410 之间，所以原和一定在 **690–710** 之间。
5. 7010 超出这个可靠范围，可排除。700 与 701 都在里面，范围本身不能证明哪一个正确。

### 例 2 · 23×19，两个接近的候选

1. 到十的四舍五入给 20 与 20，估计 **20×20=400**。
2. 原数分别在 20–30 与 10–20 之间，且都非负。因此原积在 **20×10=200** 与 **30×20=600** 之间。
3. 候选 43 在范围外，可以排除；437 与 438 都在范围内，仍需精算。
4. 用补偿：**23×19=23×20−23=460−23=437**。
5. 因此 438 不正确，是靠精算发现的，不能说它“估算看起来不合理”。

### 减法的界限要交叉取端点

802 在 800–900 之间，397 在 300–400 之间。要给差的下界，用较小的被减数减较大的减数：800−400=400。上界用较大的被减数减较小的减数：900−300=600。因此精确差 405 在 400–600 内。

分别到百四舍五入，会得到估计 800−400=400。它和可靠下界在这个例子里巧合相同，但“最近舍入”与“下界”是不同任务，不能混为一谈。范围宽也有价值：能拒绝明显离谱的 45，却不会冒充精确答案。

199 m 与 302 m 两段步道分别到百米为 200 m 与 300 m，估计合计 500 m，精确合计 501 m。单位仍为米；数量接近但写成千米，也不能通过单位检查。

工作台先选候选，再展开估计与上下界。在范围内时不会显示“正确”，只有精确核对后才说明是否一致。实际问题若有预算，先估是否可能够，再精算决定，不能把估计余额当成已经确认的钱数。

**家庭挑战：** 纸上设计一次假想 NZ$50 采购，自定整数价格与数量，先估总成本，再拆分精算。超出预算就调整方案。保留每一步单位，比较近似余额与精确余额，不需要购买或计分。

## Estimation screens; it does not replace proof

Estimating before exact calculation helps catch a missing tens place, wrong unit or implausible magnitude. State the method, such as rounding each operand separately to the nearest ten before adding. Different methods may produce different useful estimates; they need not equal the exact answer.

**≈** means approximate equality; **=** means exact equality. Being near an estimate merely avoids an immediate magnitude problem. Exact calculation, an inverse relationship or another reliable check is still needed.

### Example 1 · Estimate and exactly calculate 298+403

1. Round 298 to 300 and 403 to 400, each to the nearest ten.
2. Estimate **300+400=700**, writing 298+403≈700.
3. Exact calculation gives **701**; the original sum does not equal 700.
4. The operands lie in 290–300 and 400–410, so their sum lies in **690–710**.
5. Candidate 7010 lies outside these reliable bounds and can be rejected. Both 700 and 701 lie inside; the interval alone cannot prove which is correct.

### Example 2 · Nearby candidates for 23×19

1. Nearest-ten rounding gives 20 and 20, estimating **20×20=400**.
2. The non-negative operands lie in 20–30 and 10–20. Their product therefore lies between **20×10=200** and **30×20=600**.
3. Candidate 43 is outside and can be rejected. Both 437 and 438 are inside and require exact work.
4. Compensate: **23×19=23×20−23=460−23=437**.
5. Exact calculation rejects 438. Do not claim it looked unreasonable merely from the estimate.

### Cross endpoints to bound a difference

802 lies in 800–900 and 397 in 300–400. For a lower difference, subtract the larger subtrahend from the smaller minuend: 800−400=400. For an upper difference, use the larger minuend and smaller subtrahend: 900−300=600. The exact difference 405 is inside 400–600.

Rounding each operand to the nearest hundred estimates 800−400=400. This happens to match the lower bound in this example, but nearest rounding and finding a lower bound are different tasks. Even a wide interval rejects an implausible 45 without pretending to give the exact answer.

Two track sections of 199 m and 302 m round to 200 m and 300 m, estimating 500 m compared with the exact 501 m. The unit remains metres; a nearby number labelled kilometres still fails a unit check.

The workshop first asks for a candidate, then reveals an estimate and bounds. It does not mark an inside candidate correct. Only exact checking establishes agreement. For a budget, estimate possible affordability and then calculate exactly before deciding; an estimated balance is not a confirmed money amount.
