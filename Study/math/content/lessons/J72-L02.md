# 合并同类项

## 先识别项的类型，再合并数量

一个单项式可以看成数值系数乘一个字母部分。例如 `−3x²y` 的系数是负三，字母部分为 x²y；常数也作为一种项处理。若若干单项式相加，得到多项式；单项式与多项式统称整式。分数系数如 `(1/2)x` 仍可属于整式，但字母出现在分母的 `1/x` 不属于本课的整式范围。

同类项要求字母完全相同，并且每个对应字母的指数相同。x² 与 x 不是同类，x² 与 y² 也不是；虽然总次数都可能是二，字母部分仍不同。xy 与 yx 可先按乘法交换律整理成同一字母顺序，所以是同类项。常数项相互同类，与含字母的项分开。

合并是在数同一种字母部分有多少份，因此只加系数，不改变字母与指数。原来七份 x²，不会因为合并动作而变成 x⁴。别把加法中的合并规则与乘法中的指数变化混在一起。

### 例 1：两类分别登记

整理 `3x²−2x+4x²+5x`。

1. 平方类型有 `3x²` 与 `4x²`，系数 `3+4=7`，得 `7x²`。
2. 一次类型有 `−2x` 与 `5x`，系数 `−2+5=3`，得 `3x`。
3. 结果 `7x²+3x`。两种字母部分不同，不能再合成一项。

卡片工作台保留每张原卡的身份，再按字母部分分组；卡片面积不代表代入后的大小，只登记符号和系数。这样可以检查每个原项有没有被遗漏或重复算。把某组系数改成相反数时，该组抵消为零，结果可省去这项，但原始卡仍可回看。

### 例 2：多字母与分数系数

`5xy−2yx+3y` 中，xy 与 yx 表示同一乘积，前两项系数五减二得三，结果 `3xy+3y`。单独的 y 没有 x 因子，不能并入 xy。

再看 `(1/2)x+(1/3)x−2`。同为 x 的系数先按分数加法计算，`1/2+1/3=5/6`，结果 `(5/6)x−2`。常数负二另保留。写成 `(2/5)x−2` 不仅系数算错，也忘了相同分数单位的要求。

## 零项与形式要求

`4x²−4x²+x−x=0`，所有类型都抵消，答案应明确写零，不是留空。`x−x` 虽与零等值，仍未完成这道“合并并省去零项”的要求；同样，`3x²+4x²+3x` 等值于首例结果，却还留着重复类型。

本课接受项的不同书写顺序和精确等值系数，但要求每种字母部分只保留一个项，不保留尚待分配的括号。文字建模课会允许不同因式形式，因为任务目标不同；这里的目标是把原来分散的同类项真正整理完。

## 不用一次代入决定类型

x² 与 x 在 x=1 时都为一，并不使它们成为同类项；x=2 时已分别为四与二。类型来自字母和指数的结构，不来自碰巧相等的值。家长可让孩子先读出每一项的完整字母部分，再说明系数怎样相加，最后用一个值检错，但不要把检错当通用证明。

# Combining like terms

## Identify the type before combining its amount

A monomial has a numerical coefficient and a variable part. In `−3x²y`, the coefficient is negative three and the variable part is x²y. Constants also count as terms. A sum of monomials is a polynomial; this lesson includes rational coefficients such as `(1/2)x`, while a variable denominator such as `1/x` lies outside its polynomial scope.

Like terms require the same letters with matching corresponding exponents. x² differs from x and from y². Equal total degree does not guarantee the same variable part. By contrast, xy and yx match after commuting their factors. Constants form a separate type.

Collection counts copies of one variable part, so add coefficients without changing letters or powers. Seven copies of x² do not become x⁴. Do not confuse addition of like terms with multiplication of factors.

### Example 1: keep two types separate

Collect `3x²−2x+4x²+5x`.

1. Square terms have coefficients `3+4=7`, giving `7x²`.
2. Linear terms have coefficients `−2+5=3`, giving `3x`.
3. The result is `7x²+3x`. These variable parts differ, so they remain separate terms.

Workshop cards retain original identities when grouped by variable part. Their size does not represent an evaluated area or magnitude; they record signed coefficients. This makes omissions and duplication checkable. Opposite coefficients cancel within one group, which may disappear from the final result while its original cards remain inspectable.

### Example 2: multiple letters and fractional coefficients

For `5xy−2yx+3y`, commute yx to xy and combine five minus two copies. The result is `3xy+3y`. A lone y has no x factor and cannot join the xy group.

For `(1/2)x+(1/3)x−2`, add rational coefficients: `1/2+1/3=5/6`. The result is `(5/6)x−2`, retaining the constant. A coefficient of 2/5 would misuse fraction addition.

## Finish the requested form

Every type cancels in `4x²−4x²+x−x=0`. Write zero explicitly. The equivalent input `x−x` still leaves collection unfinished when the task asks to collect and omit zero terms. Likewise, `3x²+4x²+3x` retains repeated square terms despite matching the final value.

Term order and equivalent exact coefficients are accepted, but retain one term per variable part and complete any pending distribution. A later modelling task permits factored forms because it has a different goal. Here the goal is to finish organising the terms.

x² and x both equal one at x=1, but that does not make them like; at two they already differ. Classification comes from structure. Read each full variable part, explain the coefficient sum and use a substitution as an error check rather than a universal proof.
