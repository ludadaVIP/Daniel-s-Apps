# 求根公式的来源与使用

## 把一次配方推广到一般系数

求根公式不是突然出现的口诀，而是对一般二次方程进行配方。整理为 ax²+bx+c=0 后，先确认 a≠0。两边除以 a，移常数，得到 x²+(b/a)x=−c/a。一次项系数的一半是 b/(2a)，因此两边同加它的平方：

(x+b/(2a))² = −c/a+b²/(4a²) = (b²−4ac)/(4a²)。

记 Δ=b²−4ac。如果 Δ<0，右边为负，不存在实数平方等于它；若 Δ≥0，取平方分支，得到 x=−b/(2a)±√Δ/(2|a|)。这里 √(4a²)=2|a|，不能直接说它等于2a。a为负时，用2a代替2|a|会交换正负两个分支，但完整集合不变，所以可统一写为 x=(−b±√Δ)/(2a)。这解释了负a为什么也能用公式，而不是省略了绝对值条件。

### 例 1：负 b 与整个分母

x²−3x+1=0 中 a=1、b=−3、c=1。先算 Δ=(−3)²−4×1×1=5；再算 −b=3。代入得到 x=(3±√5)/2，两根必须同时保留。写成3±√5/2会只把根式除以二，中心从3/2变为3，已经不是同一个数集。

检验可用配方：(x−3/2)²=5/4，两候选移去3/2后正好为±√5/2，平方均为5/4。这是精确运算。图像小数位置只是帮助观察，不应用有限小数替代根式，声称原式严格为零。

### 例 2：负 a 与根式化简

−2x²+3x+2=0 给 a=−2、b=3、c=2。Δ=9−4×(−2)×2=25，根为 (−3±5)/(−4)。正号分支给−1/2，负号分支给2；两个结果次序与平常感觉不同，但集合仍为 {−1/2,2}。原式代回−1/2得−1/2−3/2+2=0，代回2得−8+6+2=0。

对2x²−4x−1=0，Δ=16+8=24，x=(4±√24)/4=(4±2√6)/4=1±√6/2。约分要作用于整个分子，不能把4变1而根式仍除原分母后又随意变形，也不能误写成 (1±√6)/2。可以保留等价精确形式；解释仍须交代系数、符号和完整分支。

## 先检查公式是否适用

0x²+2x−4=0 实际为一次方程，解x=2，不能把a=0代入分母2a。若二次、一次项都消失，还要分类恒等式与矛盾等式。Δ=0时两个符号得到同一个值，只列一次；Δ<0时本课实数域无解，不把负被开方数强行开方。

做完后问三件事：系数来自整理后的式子吗？分子与分母的括号完整吗？每个根代回原式成立并列完了吗？家长审阅一般配方的依据及负a的分支解释，不以写出公式名称替代推导。若分数配方尚不牢固，可先回上一课完成数值例子，再看一般字母式。

# Deriving and using the quadratic formula

## General completing

Collect ax²+bx+c=0 and check a≠0. Divide by a and move the constant to obtain x²+(b/a)x=−c/a. Add (b/(2a))² to both sides, giving (x+b/(2a))²=(b²−4ac)/(4a²). Define Δ=b²−4ac.

Negative Δ gives no real roots because the squared left side cannot be negative. For Δ≥0, taking branches gives x=−b/(2a)±√Δ/(2|a|). The denominator comes from √(4a²)=2|a|, not always 2a. When a is negative, replacing |a| by a exchanges the plus and minus branches. The full set is therefore still represented by x=(−b±√Δ)/(2a). This explains negative a without falsely claiming |a|=a.

### Example 1: negative b and full grouping

For x²−3x+1=0, a=1,b=−3,c=1. Calculate Δ=(−3)²−4=5 and −b=3. Both roots are (3±√5)/2. Writing 3±√5/2 divides only the radical by two, changing the centre from 3/2 to three.

The completed-square check is (x−3/2)²=5/4. Each candidate differs from 3/2 by ±√5/2, whose square is exactly 5/4. Approximate pixel positions help observation but cannot replace an exact radical with a short decimal and guarantee zero residual.

### Example 2: negative a and simplification

For −2x²+3x+2=0, Δ=9−4(−2)(2)=25. The formula gives (−3±5)/(−4). Its plus branch is −1/2 and minus branch two. Branch order is unimportant; both belong to the set. Original substitution gives −1/2−3/2+2=0 and −8+6+2=0.

For 2x²−4x−1=0, Δ=16+8=24. Hence x=(4±√24)/4=(4±2√6)/4=1±√6/2. Simplification acts on the entire numerator; (1±√6)/2 is a different answer. Equivalent exact forms are acceptable, while the explanation must still identify coefficients, signs and complete branches.

## Check applicability first

The equation 0x²+2x−4=0 is linear, giving x=2. Substituting a=0 into denominator 2a is invalid. If both variable coefficients vanish, distinguish an identity from a contradiction. For Δ=0 the two signs give one distinct value; for negative Δ there are no real branches in this lesson.

Audit the collected coefficients, numerator and denominator grouping, and every root in the original equation. A parent reviews the general completing steps and negative-a branch explanation, rather than accepting a formula name as a derivation. If fractional completing is still difficult, revisit a numerical example before the general letters.
