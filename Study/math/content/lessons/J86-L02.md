# SSS 与 SAS 判定

## 不是任意三条条件

全等判据给出足以保证全等的一组对应条件。SSS 指三组对应边分别相等；SAS 指两组对应边分别相等，并且它们的**夹角**相等。所有三角形在此都非退化，边长为正，且满足严格三角形不等式；不把共线的三个点当成普通三角形。

“夹角”取决于两条边的交点，不取决于文字里角放在第几项。AB 与 AC 在 A 相交，夹角为 A；AB 与 BC 在 B 相交，夹角为 B。看到“两个边、一个角”仍要核角的位置以及左右三角形的对应顺序。

### 例 1：三条边与夹角结构

已知 AB=DE、BC=EF、CA=FD，则按 ABC↔DEF 有三组对应边，SSS 保证全等。用三根定长杆拼三角形，放到基准边后，第三顶点可能出现在基准线两侧，但两种是镜像，仍然全等，不能把翻转当不同大小。

改成只给 AB=DE、AC=DF、角 A=角 D。A/D 都是各自两边的交点，故为两边与夹角，用 SAS。想象两条定长杆铰接，夹角固定后第三边的位置随之固定；若夹角可自由开合，两边相等本身还不够。

先列充分条件、证全等，再可得对应的 BC=EF 或角 B=角 E。若 BC=EF 正是目标，不能先把它填到 SSS 条件里，又说“所以它们相等”，那会循环论证。

### 例 2：非夹角 SSA 的真实反例

给定 AB=6、BC=4、角 A=30°，其中角 A 不在 AB 与 BC 之间。先从 A 沿 30° 的射线找 C，再要求 C 到 B 的距离为四；以 B 为圆心的半径四圆与该射线可以有两个合法交点。

它们对应 AC 长约 2.5504 与 7.8419，均为正，两种三角形都满足原来的 AB、BC 与角 A 条件，第三边却不同，所以不全等。近似小数只帮助看反例，实验按同一构造公式定位；不能据此说 SSA 永远产生两个三角形，它只是可能不唯一，足以否定“总能保证”的一般判据。

本课没有额外给“都是直角三角形、斜边和直角边对应相等”等特殊前提，因此不将某些特殊判定偷加到一般 SSA。若题目确实给了更强条件，需要另按那些条件分析，而不是看到 SSA 三字母就忽略信息。

## 观察与证明分层

工作台只把列出的对应边角当已知。未标的边可能在图上碰巧相等，也不能未经论证补入。旋转、反射只改变位置和方向，不改变已知或作答；换条件组才清空旧输入。主动显示反例可比较两个合法 C 位置。

家长让孩子指出给定两边的交点，明确说“这个角为什么是夹角”。再问有没有一项条件其实来自待证结论。能复述 SSS/SAS 名称不等于能选准对应，解释仍须看具体边角。

# SSS and SAS congruence

## Not just any three facts

SSS supplies three equal corresponding side pairs. SAS supplies two corresponding side pairs and their **included** angle. Triangles here are nondegenerate with positive sides satisfying strict triangle inequalities.

Inclusion depends on the shared vertex: AB and AC include A, whereas AB and BC include B. The order of words in a sentence does not determine the angle’s geometric role.

### Example 1: three sides or an included angle

Given AB=DE,BC=EF,CA=FD under ABC↔DEF, SSS guarantees congruence. Three fixed rods may place the third vertex on either side of a baseline, but those mirror images are congruent rather than different sizes.

With AB=DE,AC=DF and A=D, the angle joins the given sides, giving SAS. Two fixed rods with a fixed hinge angle determine the remaining connection; without fixing that angle, two lengths alone leave freedom.

Establish congruence before taking corresponding conclusions. If BC=EF is the target, inserting it into SSS as a premise would use the desired conclusion to prove itself.

### Example 2: a genuine SSA counterexample

Take AB=6,BC=4 and A=30°, a non-included angle. C lies on a 30-degree ray from A and a radius-four circle about B. The ray can meet that circle at two valid positions.

The resulting AC lengths are approximately 2.5504 and 7.8419. Both triangles satisfy the same supplied sides and angle but have different third sides, so they are not congruent. Approximate lengths illustrate the construction rather than defining exact proof. SSA does not always yield two triangles; the possibility is enough to disprove a universal guarantee.

No additional right-triangle hypothesis is supplied in this lesson. Special stronger premises would require separate analysis rather than being silently added or ignored.

## Observe and justify

Only the listed corresponding relations count as givens. An unmarked equality suggested by the drawing must not be imported. Rotation/reflection retain the problem and answer; a new condition set resets them. Reveal the counterexample to inspect both C positions.

A parent asks where the two given sides meet and why the angle is included. Check whether any premise came from the target itself. A criterion name needs concrete matching elements and reasons.
