# 统计图中的分布

## 一个平均数装不下全部记录

分布说明数值出现在哪里、各出现多少次。比较两组同单位数据时，先看集中位置、分散程度、空隙、簇与可能异常的点，再选择统计量。两个均值相同，只表明这一项摘要相同，不说明所有记录相同，也不保证中位数、极差和形状相同。

点图每个点代表一条记录；相同数值逐个堆叠，不能把重复值删成一个点。两图使用共同刻度，才能直接比较位置和分散。若每张图单独缩放到刚好占满，外观相似可能只是标尺改变，需先读轴。

### 例 1：相同中心，完全不同的分散

A 组为三、三、三、三，B 组为零、零、六、六。两组总和都十二、条数都四，所以均值都三；中位数也都是三。

但 A 全集中在三，极差 `3−3=0`；B 分在两端，中间有空隙，极差 `6−0=6`。B 的均值三甚至没有实际出现。均值是平摊摘要，不要求是一条原观测。零极差也不是零记录，而是全部数值相同。

### 例 2：看频数与整体平移

B 的四条中两条为六，相对频数 `2/4=1/2`。不能因只出现零和六两个不同值，就把分母写成二；概率或频数的分母要与所数的观测单位对应。

比较另一对六条记录：A 为一、二、二、三、三、四，B 为四、五、五、六、六、七。每条都加三，整体向右移三，集中位置随之改变，但各值之间的距离、重复模式和极差都保持。整体平移与向两端拉开，是不同的分布变化。

若数区间 `2≤x≤4`，两个端点都包含。三、三、三、三中的四条全部符合，不是因重复就只数一条；若题目改为严格区间，要重新核边界。区间计数可以更具体地回答“多数记录在哪里”，而不只说平均几。

## 图的描述范围也要守住

点图能描述本组记录的形状，不能单凭外观证明为什么形成两簇、哪种方法造成差异，或直接推广为所有人的总体分布。还需要抽样过程、背景条件与其他证据。观察到的差异与因果结论，不是同一层次。

工作台让两组始终共用零至十的轴，先自己说出集中、空隙和分散，再展开均值、中位数、极差检查。改变对照案例时旧答案清空，因为新记录需要新计算；相同摘要则应解释为何它没有保留全部信息。

家长可问“一个点是一条还是一种值？”“两图刻度一样吗？”“这个结论在描述记录还是猜原因？”完整分布与适当摘要配合，才不会被一个漂亮平均数误导。

# Distribution in charts

## One mean cannot store all the records

A distribution describes where values occur and how often. For same-unit groups, inspect centre, spread, gaps, clusters and possibly unusual points before choosing summaries. Matching means establish only that one summary matches, not that all records or distribution features match.

Each dot represents one observation; duplicate values stack individually. Use common scales for a direct visual comparison. Separately stretching each plot to fill its panel can hide real location or spread differences.

### Example 1: matching centre, different spread

Group A is 3,3,3,3 and B is 0,0,6,6. Each totals twelve over four records, giving mean three. Both medians are also three.

A is concentrated at three with range zero. B has two end clusters, a central gap and range six. B does not even contain an observed three: a mean is a fair-share summary rather than a required data value. Zero range means equal observations, not no observations.

### Example 2: frequencies and a shift

Two of B's four observations equal six, giving relative frequency one half. The denominator is four records, not the two distinct values zero and six. Always match the denominator to the unit being counted.

Another pair has A=1,2,2,3,3,4 and B=4,5,5,6,6,7. Adding three to every observation shifts centre right by three while preserving distances, repetitions and range. Shifting differs from spreading values towards both ends.

For the inclusive interval 2≤x≤4, all four threes in the first group count. Repetition does not reduce them to one. Strict endpoints would need a fresh boundary check. Interval counts can describe concentration more specifically than a mean alone.

## Keep the conclusion within the evidence

A dot plot describes the supplied records. Its shape alone does not establish why clusters formed, which treatment caused differences or what the whole population looks like. Sampling design, context and further evidence are needed for broader or causal claims.

The workshop keeps both groups on the same zero-to-ten scale. Describe concentration, gaps and spread before revealing summaries. A new case clears preceding answers; equal statistics should prompt an explanation of the information they omit.

A parent asks whether a dot counts one record or one distinct value, whether scales agree and whether a statement describes observations or asserts a cause. Combine full distributions with suitable summaries rather than relying on one attractive mean.
