# 相似的意义与比例

## 形状关系先于屏幕方向

把三角形画在透明纸上，旋转、平移或翻面，顶点的身份随纸一起移动。把所有长度再乘同一个正数，形状仍保持。相似图形的对应角相等，对应边成比例；这里的相似比约定为第二个图形的长度除以第一个图形的对应长度。倍率可以大于一、等于一或在零与一之间，分别表示放大、同大或缩小。零会把图形压成点，不属于本课非退化图形的相似倍率。

对应不是“左边对左边”。写△ABC∼△DEF，已经声明A↔D、B↔E、C↔F。因此AB↔DE、BC↔EF、CA↔FD。先写这张对应表，再列DE/AB=EF/BC=FD/CA=k。三个比都用第二个除以第一个，不能中间偷偷换方向。长度单位必须一致，相同单位约去之后，倍率没有单位。

### 例 1：字母次序比朝向可靠

已知△ABC∼△FDE。按顺序配对得到A↔F、B↔D、C↔E，所以BC对应DE，AB对应FD，CA对应EF。即使像三角形被反射后转过九十度，B依然对应D，不能因为D现在最靠右就把它重新命名。

若原三角形对应边为4、5、3 cm，像的对应边为6、15/2、9/2 cm，第二个/第一个分别为6/4、(15/2)/5、(9/2)/3，都等于3/2。反方向则全部是2/3。两个倍率互为倒数，并不是两个互相矛盾的答案；它们回答的方向不同。只检查第一对边，可以得到一个候选倍率，还不能证明整个图形相似。

### 例 2：边长改变并不总是相似

一个完整3×2矩形变为6×3。宽的倍率是2，高的倍率是3/2，不能用同一个k解释。原长宽比3/2，新长宽比2，形状已经变化；旋转新矩形也不能让这两个比例吻合。它们不相似。若改为6×4，宽高都乘2，对应角仍为直角，才是等比放大。

当k=1时，对应角相等且对应边长度相同，图形既相似也全等。相似允许大小不同，全等是其中倍率为一的特殊情形。不要把“相似”理解为“看起来差不多”：这里要求精确的对应关系。照片的横向拉伸、截图的非等比显示，会影响视觉判断；应优先用给出的尺寸和几何条件。

## 先预测，再揭示核验

工作台允许旋转和反射第二图，共同改变显示缩放。这些操作保留答案，因为它们没有改变原来的数学倍率。改变真实横纵倍率会产生一个新问题，应重新预测。尝试关闭横纵统一，比较4倍横边与3倍竖边所给的不同长度比。

提出一个错误的对应顺序，再查看三对边的平方比。如果不统一，只能说这个提出的对应不成立，不能仅凭错误配对宣布整个图形不相似；还要查正确对应。工作台把完整图形的判断与提出的对应核验分别说明。家长可让孩子先指出三个点的身份，口头说清倍率方向，再给一个统一缩放和一个单向拉伸的例子。完整理由由人核验，输入一个正确词语只完成了结论部分。

# Meaning of similarity and scaling

## Start with correspondence

Imagine a labelled triangle on transparent paper. Turning, translating or reflecting the paper moves each vertex together with its label. A uniform enlargement then multiplies every length by one positive number. Similar figures have equal corresponding angles and proportional corresponding sides. In this lesson the scale direction is second figure divided by first figure. A factor above one enlarges, a factor between zero and one reduces, and a factor of one preserves size. A zero factor collapses the figure and is excluded from these nondegenerate similarities.

Writing △ABC∼△DEF states A↔D, B↔E and C↔F. Consequently AB↔DE, BC↔EF and CA↔FD. Write this matching before writing DE/AB=EF/BC=FD/CA=k. Keep the direction consistent in all three ratios. Convert lengths to a common unit first; the units cancel, leaving a dimensionless factor. Screen positions such as left and right do not define correspondence.

### Example 1: labels survive a turn

Given △ABC∼△FDE, match A with F, B with D and C with E. Thus BC corresponds to DE, AB to FD and CA to EF. Reflecting the image and turning it through ninety degrees changes neither these identities nor the corresponding lengths.

Suppose corresponding source lengths are 4, 5 and 3 cm and image lengths are 6, 15/2 and 9/2 cm. Every image/source ratio is 3/2. Reversing the direction gives 2/3 for every pair. These reciprocal factors answer different questions, so state the direction before calculating. A single side pair provides only a candidate factor; it does not establish that the remaining lengths or angles agree.

### Example 2: a directional stretch

A complete 3×2 rectangle becomes 6×3. Its width factor is two and its height factor is 3/2. The aspect ratios, 3/2 and two, differ, so these rectangles are not similar. Turning the second rectangle does not repair the mismatch. A 6×4 rectangle would instead have common factor two and unchanged right angles.

When k=1, corresponding sides and angles agree, giving congruence as well as similarity. Similarity therefore permits different sizes but does not require them. It is an exact mathematical relationship, rather than a judgement that two pictures look approximately alike.

Use the lab to rotate or reflect the image and change the common viewing zoom. Those changes preserve an existing answer because they do not change the mathematical scale. Changing the actual horizontal or vertical factor creates a new question. Audit a deliberately incorrect vertex order: unequal squared-side ratios reject that proposed order, but do not by themselves reject every possible correspondence. Check the correct matching before judging the complete figures. A parent reviews this reasoning separately from the automatically checked conclusion.
