## 两次选取，看交叠

把一个参考正方形横向分成三列，取两列；再沿另一个方向分成四行，取三行。哪些小格同时被选中？深色交叠才表示 2/3×3/4。单独只选了一次的浅色区域不计入乘积。这里求的是原整体的份额，没有实际米单位，不要随意给答案加平方米。

一个方向的等分数和另一个方向的等分数共同决定每个原整体有多少小格。小格在本图里同面积；不要求每个小格都是正方形。分数乘法的分子相乘、分母相乘，都应能从图的结构解释。

### 例 1 · 三列与四行的交叠

1. 横向选 **2/3**，纵向选 **3/4**，共同参考同一个原整体。
2. 一个原整体有 **3×4=12** 个同面积小格，每格是原面积的 **1/12**。
3. 同时选中的有 **2×3=6** 格。
4. 乘积为 **6/12=1/2**，即原整体的一半。
5. 检查：只取原来 2/3 的 3/4，结果应该比 2/3 少；一半符合，5/7 则来自错误相加。

这不是把两块独立面积合并，因此不能把分子分母分别相加。乘以小于一的正分数是在取原量的一部分；“乘法必然变大”只凭过去整数经验，并不适用于所有数。乘一保留全部，乘零没有交叠，乘大于一可以扩大量。

### 例 2 · 因数超过一，原整体保持不变

1. 求 **5/4×2/3**。横向五个四分之一已经超过一个单位框。
2. 画面继续向右扩展，粗框每个仍是原来的一个整体，不能把整个新大框说成一。
3. 每个原整体依旧有 **4×3=12** 个同面积小格，交叠 **5×2=10** 格。
4. 因此结果是 **10/12=5/6**，不是把十格除以扩大画面全部格数。
5. 乘 2/3 应保留 5/4 的三分之二；结果小于 5/4，又大于零，数量关系合理。

工作台横纵因数都可以从零调到二。超过一就增加相同单位框，原单位的粗边界不会消失；两个因数都为二时，交叠四个原整体。每次先预测，再数原整体格数与交叠格数，最后展开核对。换分母是改变当前分割，不能把旧结果带到新题。

也可以先约分再计算，例如 **3/4×4/5=12/20=3/5**，相同的四在乘积中能够消去，因为最后分子分母同除四不改变值。不能跨越加号随便“划掉相同数字”；本课的依据始终是乘积和等值分数。

**带走一个发现：** 分母乘积说原整体分几格，分子乘积说交叠几格。家长请孩子指出“哪一个框是一”，再听他说结果为何不一定变大。出口题用 3/5×5/6，独立计算并用因数与一的关系做合理性检查。

## Two selections reveal an overlap

Partition a reference square into three columns and select two. In the other direction, partition into four rows and select three. The cells selected in both directions form the dark overlap representing 2/3×3/4. Singly selected pale regions are not part of the product. This measures a fraction of a whole, with no actual metre measurement supplied.

The two subdivision counts determine cells per original whole. Cells have equal area but need not individually be squares. Both numerator multiplication and denominator multiplication should be explained from this structure.

### Example 1 · Three columns and four rows

1. Select **2/3** horizontally and **3/4** vertically, retaining the same reference whole.
2. Each original whole contains **3×4=12** equal-area cells, each worth **1/12** of it.
3. **2×3=6** cells overlap.
4. The product is **6/12=1/2**, half the original whole.
5. Taking only 3/4 of 2/3 should reduce that positive amount. One half fits; 5/7 incorrectly comes from adding.

This is not combining two independent areas, so adding numerators and denominators is unjustified. Multiplication by a positive fraction below one selects only part of an amount. Multiplying by one preserves all, zero selects none and a factor above one can enlarge the amount.

### Example 2 · A factor above one keeps the original whole

1. Find **5/4×2/3**. Five horizontal quarters extend beyond one unit frame.
2. Extend the drawing rightwards while every thick frame stays one original whole. The larger outer drawing is not redefined as one.
3. Each original whole still contains **4×3=12** equal-area cells; **5×2=10** overlap.
4. Hence **10/12=5/6**, rather than ten divided by all cells in the enlarged drawing.
5. The result is positive and below 5/4, consistent with retaining two thirds of that amount.

Workshop factors range from zero to two. Exceeding one adds original unit frames; two times two overlaps four such wholes. Predict, count cells per original whole and count overlap before checking. Changing a denominator changes the partition and clears the previous result.

For **3/4×4/5=12/20=3/5**, common factors may be cancelled because dividing the final numerator and denominator by four preserves value. Do not transfer that cancellation to unrelated sums: its justification concerns products and equivalent fractions.

**Takeaway:** the denominator product counts cells per original whole; the numerator product counts overlap. A parent should ask which frame means one and why the product need not be larger. Independently solve 3/5×5/6 in the exit ticket and check its size against the factors.
