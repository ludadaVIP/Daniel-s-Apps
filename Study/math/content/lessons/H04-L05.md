# 简单三角恒等式

单位圆上的横纵坐标不是互不相关的两个数字：它们平方和等于半径1的平方。这条几何事实可变成对全部实角成立的代数恒等式。

## 一条核心想法

**sin²θ+cos²θ=1来自单位圆方程，而变形必须保留原来的条件。** sin²θ表示(sinθ)²，不是sin(θ²)，也不是sinθ乘2。令s=sinθ、c=cosθ，则s²+c²=1；用这一关系可以在多项式中代数消元。

恒等式要求在共同定义域内对每个允许输入成立。几个角的成功例只能帮助观察，不能覆盖全部实数；一个确切反例却足以推翻一个声称对全部角成立的等式。

### 例1：用平方和完成指定形式

1−c²=s²，因为从s²+c²=1两边减c²。若题目要求“只含s”，原样写1−c²虽等值，却没有完成改写要求。同理1−s²=c²，适用于全部实角。

(s+c)²−2sc展开为s²+2sc+c²−2sc=s²+c²=1。这里既使用完全平方公式，又使用单位圆关系，不能跳成(s+c)²=s²+c²。以θ=π/4代入，s+c=√2，平方2，恰好能检查漏掉2sc的错误。

### 例2：平方丢掉符号，约分不能补域

sinθ=3/5、θ在第二象限，则cos²θ=1−9/25=16/25。主平方根4/5给的是|cosθ|，而第二象限横坐标负，所以cosθ=−4/5。若没给象限或其他符号信息，只能保留两种可能。

(1−cos²θ)/sin²θ在sinθ≠0时可化为1；原式排除θ=kπ，k为整数。θ=0时原式0/0无定义，不能因为化简后的常数1有意义就把它补进原域。类似1+tan²θ=1/cos²θ需要cosθ≠0。

## 恒等式实验

代数框约定s=sinθ、c=cosθ，使用次数不超过4的数值系数多项式。程序按c²=1−s²做确切代数消元，而非随机代入几个角。题目另要求只含s、只含c或常数时，还要完成对应形式。分母含变量的论证保留人工审核，不由多项式框冒充处理。

选择第一或第二象限，保持sinθ=3/5，观察cos的符号变化，再解释图为什么符合单位圆。对任意角，√(1−sin²θ)=|cosθ|；以θ=π，左边为1而cosπ=−1，可否定不带绝对值的全称式。

## 家长核对

出口须给具体反例、正确等式及符号依据。家长检查是否把“平方根主值”和“待求带符号坐标”分开，并追问每一次除法需要什么条件。判题正确的多项式仍应口头解释代数来源；下一课用这些域习惯防止把正切无定义点或变形后的多余输入算作解。

# Basic trigonometric identities

Unit-circle coordinates are connected: the sum of their squares equals the square of radius one. This geometric fact becomes an algebraic identity for every real angle.

## One central idea

**sin²θ+cos²θ=1 follows from the unit-circle equation, and transformations must retain their original conditions.** The notation sin²θ means (sinθ)², not sin(θ²) or twice sinθ. With s=sinθ and c=cosθ, the relation s²+c²=1 permits algebraic elimination in polynomials.

An identity must hold for every admissible input in its common domain. Several successful angle substitutions support exploration but do not cover every real input. One exact counterexample does refute an equality claimed for all angles.

### Example 1: Finish the requested form using the sum of squares

1−c²=s² follows by subtracting c² from s²+c²=1. If the task asks for an expression using only s, copying 1−c² is equivalent but unfinished. Likewise 1−s²=c² for every real angle.

Expand (s+c)²−2sc to obtain s²+2sc+c²−2sc=s²+c²=1. This uses both the perfect-square formula and the unit-circle relation. It does not permit (s+c)²=s²+c². At θ=π/4, s+c=√2 and its square is two, providing a useful check against omission of the cross term 2sc.

### Example 2: Squaring loses sign; cancellation cannot add inputs

If sinθ=3/5 in quadrant II, then cos²θ=1−9/25=16/25. The principal root 4/5 gives |cosθ|. Since horizontal position is negative in quadrant II, cosθ=−4/5. Without quadrant or other sign information, both signs would remain possible.

(1−cos²θ)/sin²θ simplifies to one only where sinθ≠0. Its original domain excludes θ=kπ for integers k. At θ=0 the original expression is undefined 0/0; the meaningful simplified constant cannot add that input back. Similarly, 1+tan²θ=1/cos²θ requires cosθ≠0.

## Identity experiment

The algebra field uses s=sinθ and c=cosθ with numerical-coefficient polynomials of degree at most four. It performs exact elimination using c²=1−s² rather than randomly sampling angles. A requested s-only, c-only or constant form must also be completed. Arguments involving variable denominators remain human-reviewed instead of being misrepresented as polynomial handling.

Keep sinθ=3/5 while selecting quadrant I or II. Predict the sign of cosine and explain how the diagram remains on the unit circle. For every real angle, √(1−sin²θ)=|cosθ|. At θ=π this principal root is one while cosπ=−1, refuting the universal formula without an absolute value.
