# 棱柱、棱锥与球

长方体、三棱柱、四棱锥和球可以都摆在桌上，却不能共用同一种“数面”方式。分类要看结构，体积还要看底面积和真正的垂直高。

## 一条核心想法

**先按面与顶点的结构识别立体，再选与该结构匹配的量。** n棱柱有两个全等平行的n边形底面，侧面为平行四边形；n棱锥有一个n边形底面，其余三角形面汇于同一个顶点。侧棱不必竖直。

球的边界是一个曲面，不是一个平面多边形面。表面画的赤道或经线是定位辅助线，不是棱。本文V在“V−E+F”中表示顶点数，在体积公式中另用文字“体积”，读式时先辨认上下文。

### 例1：按组计数，不只数看见的部分

六棱柱的两个底面各有6个顶点，所以顶点数12；上下底棱各6条，加6条侧棱，棱数18；六个侧面加两个底面，平面面数8。核对12−18+8=2。

四棱锥则有底面4个顶点加一个锥顶，共5点；4条底棱加4条侧棱，共8棱；一个底面加4个三角形，共5面。核对5−8+5=2。图中被挡住的顶点、棱和底面仍要计数。

一般n棱柱为(顶点,棱,面)=(2n,3n,n+2)，n棱锥为(n+1,2n,n+1)。这是结构计数，不随观察方向或垂直高改变。球没有这些平面面和棱，不能直接把它的整个曲面填进此处的多面体计数公式。

### 例2：同底等高的体积，与球的半径

底面积12 cm²、垂直高6 cm的棱柱，体积12×6=72 cm³；同底等高棱锥体积12×6/3=24 cm³。棱锥的三分之一来自立体体积关系，不是“有三角形就把任意量除3”。

把上底或锥顶水平侧移，保持底面积与垂直高，体积仍分别72、24。斜移可以改变侧棱和侧面的面积，所以不能据此声称表面积也相同。垂直高是顶点或平行底面之间的垂直距离，不能换成一条更长的斜棱。

半径3 cm的球体积为4π×3³/3=36π cm³，球表面积为4π×3²=36π cm²。此例两个数值系数恰好相同，单位和所测对象仍不同。若给的是直径6 cm，须先得半径3；不能把6直接代入r。

## 结构与高度工作台

选择棱柱、棱锥或球，先预测面数、棱数与体积，再展开核验。多边形底面积固定12 cm²，底边数改变时图形调整底面尺寸，不假设每条底边都同长1。

上端错移只改变形状表示及侧棱，不清除面、棱和体积预测。改变底边数时重做结构任务；仅改高度时保留结构计数。球的半径控制体积，图上辅助圆不会因为更显眼就变成“棱”。

## 家长核对与迁移

出口比较直、斜两个同底等高棱柱。完整解释应说体积都72，并保留“不能保证侧棱和表面积相同”。家长可让孩子指出图中垂直高的垂足，而不是找一条看着竖直的屏幕线。截面与展开图接着追踪面的身份，组合体再分别核算占有空间和外露面积。

# Prisms, pyramids and spheres

A cuboid, triangular prism, square-based pyramid and sphere can all sit on a table, but they do not share one way of counting faces. Classification uses structure; volume also needs base area and true perpendicular height.

## One central idea

**Identify face and vertex structure before choosing the matching measurement.** An n-gonal prism has two congruent parallel n-gonal bases and parallelogram lateral faces. An n-gonal pyramid has one n-gonal base and triangular faces meeting at one apex. Lateral edges need not be vertical.

A sphere has one curved boundary, not a flat polygonal face. Equator or meridian circles are guides, not edges. In the expression V−E+F, V denotes vertex count; the word volume denotes the separate spatial measurement. Identify the context before interpreting a symbol.

### Example 1: Count structural groups, including hidden parts

A hexagonal prism has six vertices in each base, hence12 vertices. Two sets of six base edges plus six lateral edges give18 edges. Six lateral faces plus two bases give8 flat faces. Check12−18+8=2.

A square-based pyramid has four base vertices and one apex, giving5 vertices; four base edges plus four lateral edges give8 edges; one base and four triangles give5 faces. Check5−8+5=2. Hidden vertices, edges and bases still count.

In general, prism counts are (2n,3n,n+2), while pyramid counts are (n+1,2n,n+1). These depend on structure, not camera direction or height. A sphere has no corresponding polygonal faces or edges; its entire curved surface cannot simply be inserted into this elementary polyhedron count.

### Example 2: Equal base and height, and the sphere radius

A prism of base area12 cm² and perpendicular height6 cm has volume12×6=72 cm³. A pyramid with the same base area and height has volume12×6/3=24 cm³. The factor one third is a spatial volume relationship, not permission to divide any measurement by three whenever a triangle is visible.

Shearing the top base or apex sideways while preserving base area and perpendicular height leaves these volumes72 and24. It can alter lateral-edge lengths and side areas, so surface area need not remain equal. Perpendicular height is a distance to the base plane, not an arbitrary longer sloping edge.

A sphere of radius3 cm has volume4π×3³/3=36π cm³ and surface area4π×3²=36π cm². Their numerical coefficients happen to agree here, but units and measured quantities differ. If a diameter6 cm is supplied, first find radius3 rather than substituting six for r.

## Structure and height workbench

Choose prism, pyramid or sphere and predict flat face count, edge count and volume before revealing the audit. Polygonal bases retain area12 cm²; changing the side count adjusts the drawn base dimensions rather than assuming unit sides.

Top shear preserves structure and volume predictions. Changing side count resets structural tasks, while changing only height preserves face and edge counts. A sphere’s radius controls volume, and its guide circles remain guides regardless of how visible they appear.

## Parent check and transfer

The exit compares straight and sheared prisms with the same base and height. A complete response states that both volumes are72 but does not assert equal lateral edges or surface areas. Ask the learner to identify the perpendicular foot rather than a line that looks vertical on screen. Sections and nets next track face identity; composite solids later separate occupied space from exposed area.
