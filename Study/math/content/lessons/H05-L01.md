# 向量有大小和方向

两个人从公园不同入口出发，都向东3格、向北4格。图上的两支箭头不在同一位置，却记录同样的有向位移。向量让我们把“移动多少、朝哪边”与“从哪里开始”分开。

## 一条核心想法

**向量记录大小和方向，自由向量不依赖箭头的位置。** 有向线段是它的一种画法，尾表示起点、头表示终点。保持长度和方向平移一支箭头，它仍表示原来的向量；转动或缩放通常会改变向量。

非零向量相等，需要模长相同且方向相同。只有同长不够，只有同向也不够。零向量的起终点重合，模长0；本课不为它指定方向。两个零向量相等，即使在不同位置用圆点表示。

### 例1：位移大小不是走过的路程

先向东3格、再向北4格，净位移用箭头从最初起点指向最后终点。按勾股定理，模长√(3²+4²)=5格。实际折线路程是3+4=7格。这两个数描述不同对象，没有冲突。

另一个人从别处做同样的有向移动，位移向量仍相等；若只向东走5格，模长也为5，却因为方向不同而不是同一向量。把位移写为(3,4)只是记录东向和北向分量，不是说起点必须在原点。

### 例2：同向、反向与零

u=(2,1)、v=(4,2)，v是u的正两倍，两者同向，但v模长是两倍，所以不相等。u=(3,4)与w=(−3,−4)模长都为5，却反向，也不相等。

如果走一圈回到起点，净位移为零向量，不代表完全没走路。零向量不能根据图上画了一个小圆点，就被认作“向北”或“向右”。圆点仅让它可见，不编码某个箭头方向。

## 先预测，再平移箭头

工作台分别询问相等性、方向关系和u的模长。只移动v的起点，三个预测都保留；改成两倍、反向或不同方向后，相应任务重新判断。精确表可以随后展开，列起点、分量、终点，避免从图形放在哪里直接下结论。

重合箭头保持真实重合，不为好看而错开；重合点合并字母标签。图窗只是显示范围，不能把超窗箭头裁剩的部分当作完整向量。

## 家长核对与迁移

出口比较折线走7格与直线走5格：起终点相同，位移向量相同，路程不同。家长先让孩子说“我在描述哪一种量”，再检查计算。可用同长不同向、同向不同长各举一例，下次在向量加减和数乘中复现。方向比较只对非零向量定义；这不妨碍零向量参与加法。

# Vectors have magnitude and direction

Two people start at different park entrances and each moves three squares east and four north. Their arrows occupy different places but record the same directed displacement. Vectors separate how far and in which direction from where a trip begins.

## One central idea

**A vector records magnitude and direction; a free vector does not depend on arrow location.** A directed segment is one representation, with a tail at the start and head at the end. Translating it without changing length or direction preserves its vector. Rotating or resizing it usually changes the vector.

Equal nonzero vectors require both equal magnitude and equal direction. Equal length alone, or equal direction alone, is insufficient. The zero vector has coincident tail and head and magnitude zero, with no assigned direction in this unit. Two zero vectors are equal even when drawn as dots at different positions.

### Example 1: Displacement magnitude is not route distance

Travel three squares east and then four north. The net-displacement arrow runs from the original start to the final endpoint. By Pythagoras its magnitude is √(3²+4²)=5 grid units. The route distance is 3+4=7. These numbers describe different quantities and do not contradict each other.

Someone making the same directed move elsewhere has an equal displacement vector. Someone travelling only five east also has magnitude five, but a different direction and therefore a different vector. Writing displacement (3,4) records eastward and northward components; it does not require the start to be the origin.

### Example 2: Same direction, reverse direction and zero

For u=(2,1) and v=(4,2), v is twice u with a positive scale. They have the same direction but different magnitudes, so are unequal. Vectors (3,4) and (−3,−4) both have magnitude five but reversed directions and are also unequal.

A round trip has zero net displacement even when distance travelled is substantial. A small dot drawn to represent the zero vector does not assign it a northward or rightward direction. The dot makes the representation visible without encoding an arrow heading.

## Predict, then translate

The workbench asks separately about equality, direction and the magnitude of u. Moving only v’s tail preserves predictions. Selecting doubled, reversed or differently directed values changes the relevant mathematical tasks. Reveal the exact table afterwards to compare tail, components and head instead of judging by where the arrow sits.

Coincident arrows remain coincident rather than being shifted artificially; coincident point labels are combined. A window is only a display range. The visible portion of a clipped arrow must not be mistaken for its complete vector.

## Parent check and transfer

The exit compares a seven-unit bent route with a five-unit straight route. Common endpoints give equal displacement vectors but different distances. Ask which quantity is being described before checking arithmetic. Retrieve equal-length/different-direction and same-direction/different-length examples in vector addition and scaling. Direction comparison requires nonzero vectors, but zero vectors still participate in addition.
