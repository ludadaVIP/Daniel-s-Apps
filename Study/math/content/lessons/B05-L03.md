## 面积计实际覆盖的单位方格

面积表示平面覆盖量。选同样大小的小方格，铺放时没有重叠、没有未计的空缺，每格只计一次。边长一厘米的方格覆盖一个平方厘米，写 **1 cm²**；“平方”表示计方格，不是把长度单位换个好看的写法。

完整长方形可以按“每行方格数×行数”计数。如果图中有空格，只求实际覆盖量时必须去掉空格。外框的长乘宽会包含这些洞，不能把它当成涂色面积。

### 例 1 · 六列四行铺满

1. 每格边长 1 cm，每格面积 1 cm²。
2. 四行每行六格，总数 6+6+6+6=24。
3. **面积=6×4=24 cm²**。
4. 沿外边一圈是 6+4+6+4=20 cm，计的是边界长度，单位不同。
5. 转成四列六行，仍有同样的二十四格，面积保持。

### 例 2 · 三乘三外框里有两个空格

1. 整个外框有九个单位方格位置。
2. 其中七格实际覆盖，两格空着。
3. 覆盖量是 **9−2=7 cm²**，不是九平方厘米。
4. 也可逐行计三、三、一格，3+3+1=7。
5. 两种计数都排除了空格，没有把格线当成额外面积。

### 转动、重排与相同单位

把同一批二十四个单位方格从六列四行排成八列三行，不增加、不删去、不重叠，覆盖面积仍为 24 cm²。周长从 20 cm 变为 22 cm：同面积可以有不同边界长度。

这条守恒只在单位方格大小保持时成立。如果把方格边长也变大，不能还用原来的每格一平方厘米；只说“格数相同”不足以证明实际覆盖量相同。工作台每格明确为 1 cm×1 cm，旋转只改变排列与位置。

四米乘三米的地面可计十二个一平方米方格，面积 12 m²。它不是 12 m，也不是四米与三米相加的七米。比较两块面积前还要确认平方单位相同，不能只比裸数字。

工作台点一格会加入或移除这一个单位的覆盖。空格有独立边框和未选状态，不只依靠颜色辨认；转动按钮把全部已覆盖格一起旋转，洞的位置也一起改变，覆盖总数保持。清除覆盖得到零面积，外框仍存在并不意味着它已经被铺满。

**小检查：** 五列三行铺满后移除两格，面积怎样变？再转动整图，哪一种操作改变了面积，哪一种保留了面积？

## Area counts actual coverage by equal unit squares

Area measures planar coverage. Choose equal squares and count each once without overlaps or uncounted gaps. A one-centimetre-sided square covers one square centimetre, written **1 cm²**. The square unit identifies covered squares rather than merely decorating a length label.

A complete rectangle can be counted as cells per row times row count. If a drawing contains gaps and asks for actual coverage, exclude them. Bounding width times height includes holes and therefore may not equal covered area.

### Example 1 · Fill six columns and four rows

1. Each cell has side 1 cm and area 1 cm².
2. Four rows of six cells total 6+6+6+6=24.
3. **Area=6×4=24 cm²**.
4. The outer circuit instead measures 6+4+6+4=20 cm, a boundary length with a different unit.
5. Rotating into four columns and six rows retains the same twenty-four cells and area.

### Example 2 · Two gaps in three-by-three bounds

1. The bounding rectangle contains nine possible unit-cell positions.
2. Seven are covered and two are empty.
3. Covered area is **9−2=7 cm²**, not nine square centimetres.
4. Alternatively count rows of three, three and one: 3+3+1=7.
5. Both methods exclude gaps and do not count grid lines as extra squares.

### Rotation, rearrangement and matching units

Rearrange the same twenty-four unit squares from six columns by four rows into eight by three without additions, removals or overlaps. Area remains 24 cm² while perimeter changes from 20 cm to 22 cm. Equal coverage can have different boundary lengths.

Conservation assumes square size remains fixed. Enlarging the squares invalidates a claim that each still covers one square centimetre. Equal cell counts alone do not prove equal physical area. Each workshop cell is explicitly 1 cm by 1 cm; rotation changes placement rather than unit size.

A four-metre by three-metre floor covers twelve one-square-metre squares, giving 12 m². This is neither 12 m nor the seven metres from adding side lengths. Comparing areas also requires matching square units, not only bare numbers.

Selecting a workshop cell adds or removes one unit of coverage. Gaps have separate outlines and unselected states, providing more than colour. Rotation moves every covered cell and every hole together while retaining covered count. Clearing coverage gives zero area; the remaining frame does not mean the interior is filled.

**Quick check:** remove two cells from a filled five-by-three grid, then rotate the whole drawing. Which action changed area and which preserved it?
