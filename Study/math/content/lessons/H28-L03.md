# 验证极端情况

一个方法在常见输入算对，仍可能在零值或临界点失败。边界检查主动寻找最容易暴露漏项的输入。本课练习设计测试，并说清测试结果能够支持多大的结论。

## 输入域与可行约束不同

纸模活动人数n的数学输入域是非负整数；每人3张、备用5张、库存4张、每包8张。正活动A费用7+6k，n=0取消、需求和费用0；容量20人，默认预算50筹码。

n=21仍是合法整数输入，可以用来检查“是否可行”。它违反容量，得到不可行结果。n=1.5不在人数输入域，不能拿它作为此规则的有效反例。域决定哪些输入适用，约束决定域内哪些计划能执行。

有目的的测试包括：零人；缺口刚好一整包及超过一张；费用刚好等于预算及比预算多一；人数刚好容量及多一。具体取哪几个输入须看公式的跳点，不能机械照抄所谓极端数字。

### 例1：向下取整为何有时蒙对

4人需求3×4+5=17张，库存4，缺口13张。向下取整⌊13/8⌋=1包只有8张新料，仍少5张；正确向上为2包，费用19筹码。

5人需求20张，缺口16张，此时向下与向上都给2包。5人通过，不能救回向下规则；4人已经满足题设、却使候选输出错误，构成有效反例。正确规则来自最小整数k满足8k≥缺口，而不是“看起来通常够”。

另看2人与3人的跳点：2人缺7张，1包，费用13；3人缺10张，2包，费用19。费用跳6筹码，不能用平均每人费用随意插值。测试跨跳点的相邻人数比重复测试同一台阶更有信息。

### 例2：检查取消与独立约束

候选规则“无论人数多少都收7+6k”在正活动可能正确，但n=0应取消，费用0；候选仍收7，失败。这检验的是题设取消分支。若真实活动约定取消仍收费，模型须先改规则，不能沿用本题结论。

另一个候选只检查费用。取n=21、预算200，需求68、缺64、8包、A费用55。钱够，却超过容量20，故可行性应为假。再看1人、预算12：1人仍要1包，费用13，即使容量通过，预算也失败。两项约束缺一不可；刚好费用13且容量允许才通过。

## 把测试与证明各放在合适位置

一个域内反例足以推翻“所有输入都正确”的候选规则。若只是几个输入通过，只能报告这些输入通过。若声称范围是有限集合0…20，且确实全部穷尽检查，则可证明该明确有限域；这仍不自动覆盖所有非负整数。

工作台候选一比较包数，候选二和四比较费用，候选三比较可行性；不要比较不同单位的输出。valid只表示当前输入与完整规则相符。候选四有公式推导作支撑，但单次绿色结果本身仍不是全域证明。

出口需同时写出候选、域内输入、正确输出、候选输出及为何失败。家长应追问“你试中了什么边界”和“其余输入为何也能成立”，而不是把显示通过当作论证结束。开放解释由人复核，数值测试记录也不代表孩子已经掌握边界推理。

# Checking extreme cases

A method can work for ordinary inputs and fail at zero or a threshold. Boundary testing deliberately looks where omitted conditions are exposed. The goal is to design informative tests and state exactly what they establish.

## Separate input domain from feasibility constraints

Workshop attendance n is a nonnegative integer. Each person uses three cards, with five spares and four cards in stock; whole packets contain eight. Positive events cost A=7+6k. At zero, cancellation sets demand and cost to zero. Capacity is twenty and default budget fifty toy credits.

Twenty-one is an admissible integer input for asking whether a plan is feasible. It violates capacity and should return infeasible. Attendance 1.5 is outside the domain and cannot refute a rule about whole people. The domain says where a rule applies; constraints identify executable plans within that domain.

Purposeful tests include zero, an exact packet shortage and one card beyond it, cost equal to budget and one credit above it, capacity and one person beyond. Choose specific tests from the actual thresholds rather than copying an arbitrary list of “extreme” numbers.

### Example 1: Flooring can occasionally agree

Four people demand 3×4+5=17 cards. Stock leaves shortage thirteen. Flooring 13/8 gives one packet of eight new cards, leaving five missing. The correct ceiling gives two packets and cost nineteen.

Five people demand twenty, shortage sixteen. Both floor and ceiling give two packets. This passing case does not rescue the floor rule: four is already an admissible failure. The correct rule comes from the least integer k satisfying 8k≥shortage, not a pattern that happens to look sufficient.

Another threshold occurs between two and three people. Two leave shortage seven, needing one packet at cost thirteen. Three leave shortage ten, needing two packets at cost nineteen. Cost jumps by six credits; interpolating an average cost per person would miss the packet rule. Adjacent inputs crossing a threshold are more informative than repeated inputs on one plateau.

### Example 2: Cancellation and independent constraints

The candidate “charge 7+6k at every attendance” may work at positive attendance but fails at zero. Cancellation requires cost zero; retaining the fee gives seven. This probes the stated cancellation branch. If a real contract charged a cancellation fee, its rules would need changing before applying these conclusions.

A second candidate checks only affordability. At n=21, budget 200, demand is 68, shortage 64, packets eight and cost 55. Money suffices, but capacity twenty fails. Conversely, one person with budget twelve still needs one packet at cost thirteen: capacity passes and budget fails. Both constraints must hold. Budget thirteen passes that one-person cost boundary exactly.

## Use tests and proof for their respective purposes

One admissible failure refutes a claim of correctness for all inputs. Several passes establish only those inputs. If a claim explicitly covers the finite set zero through twenty and every case is checked, that exhaustive check establishes this finite domain, not every nonnegative integer.

The workbench’s first candidate compares packets, the second and fourth compare costs, and the third compares feasibility. Do not compare quantities with different units. A valid result means agreement for the current input. The fourth candidate has a derivation behind it, but one green feedback result is still not that derivation.

An exit explanation should state candidate, admissible input, correct output, candidate output and reason for failure. A parent can ask which boundary was tested and what argument covers other inputs. Human review assesses the open reasoning. Numerical test records alone do not establish a child’s understanding of boundary arguments.
