# 混合题的策略选择

## 从目标反查需要什么

混合题不是看到熟悉数字就挑一个公式。先写原题真正求什么，再问为了求它需要哪些中间量、这些量由什么关系决定。一个方案可以有不同合法顺序，但不能在依赖还未建立时先引用最后结论。工作台的卡片核这种依赖，完整理由仍要自己说清。

可以在纸上画小链：条件→关系→方法→数学结果→情境筛选→目标。链的某些支线可以并行准备，例如总路程与分段时间；有些步骤则必须等待，如平均速度需要总路程和总时间。按卡片机械点完不是所有数学论证的替代品。

### 例 1：面积给定却问周长

长方形边x与x+2 m，面积48 m²，问周长。先写x(x+2)=48，再因式分解得x=6或−8。数学根都应保留到这里，随后依据长度正值排除−8。另一边8，最后周长2(6+8)=28 m。

交6回答的是中间x，交48附m只是把面积数改了单位字样，都没求周长。可代回6×8=48核关系，再从图数四条外边核28。若面积换80，方程根8与−10，实际边8与10，周长36 m；方法结构保持，具体数据重算。

### 例 2：去返平均速度

去返各3 km，速度分别4与6 km/h，无停留。去程时间3/4 h，返程3/6=1/2 h，总时间5/4 h；总路程6 km。因此全程平均速度6÷(5/4)=24/5=4.8 km/h，不是(4+6)/2=5。

等距离不等时间，慢段占用更久，所以不能给两个速度相同时间权重。另一种表示是用共同分母计算总时间，或先换分钟45与30、合75分钟再换小时；两种办法都必须保单位并回到总路程/总时间。可先求总路程，再求时间，或反过来，依赖本身允许这种换序。

## 方法还需要前提

已知直角及两直角边3与4 m，斜边可由c²=3²+4²=25求正根5 m。先核直角与边身份，再用勾股，不能把两边相加成7，也不能因方程平方根有±5就把负数当长度。若缺直角前提，两边3与4不能唯一决定第三边为5。

这三个题分别用二次方程、时间加和与勾股，选择依据是原数量关系而非表面数字。也可以用另一条合法方法核验，但不得先假定待证结果。若方案里一张卡依赖尚缺，先补关系；若发现数据改变，撤回受影响步骤并重新核验。

工作台保留临时文字草稿，明确离开后不保存；需要长期记录可写到课程的发现便签。改变题型重新建立方案，撤回一步保留自己的草稿。家长核“为什么这一步可用、是否所有根都看过、有没有回到目标”，不以点卡顺序自动宣布整课完成。

# Choosing strategies for mixed problems

## Work backward from the actual target

Do not select a formula merely because its numbers look familiar. State the original target, identify intermediate quantities, and establish the relationships determining them. Different legal orders can exist, but a conclusion cannot be used before its dependencies are known. Cards audit dependencies; your written reasoning still matters.

Sketch conditions → relationship → method → mathematical result → context filter → target. Some preparations, such as total distance and segment times, can proceed independently. Average speed must wait for both total distance and time. Clicking a complete sequence is not a substitute for every part of an argument.

### Example 1: area is given, perimeter asked

Rectangle sides x and x+2 m with area 48 give x(x+2)=48. Factor to obtain six and minus eight. Retain both mathematical roots before using the positive-length condition. The other side is eight; perimeter is 28 m.

Six answers the intermediate x question, while attaching m to 48 changes no quantity. Check 6×8=48 in the original relation, then count four outer edges. With area 80, roots eight and minus ten yield sides eight/ten and perimeter 36. The strategy persists while the data change.

### Example 2: overall return-journey speed

Each direction covers three kilometres at four and six km/h, with no stops. Times are 3/4 and 1/2 hours, totalling 5/4. Total distance is six, so overall average speed is 6÷(5/4)=24/5=4.8 km/h rather than five.

Equal distances do not imply equal time weights: the slower segment lasts longer. A common-denominator calculation or converting times to 45 and 30 minutes both work, provided units and the distance/time definition remain intact. Total distance may be computed before or after time preparations; the dependency structure permits both orders.

Given a right angle with legs three and four metres, Pythagoras gives c²=25 and positive length five. Check the right-angle premise and side identities first. Adding legs does not find the hypotenuse, and the negative square root is not a length. Without the right-angle premise, two given side lengths do not uniquely establish a third length of five.

Quadratic equations, time addition and Pythagoras apply because of relationships rather than surface number patterns. Another justified method can audit the result, but cannot assume its own target. Add missing relationships before a dependent step; changed data require reconsidering affected calculations.

The lab labels its plan text temporary. Use the lesson discovery note for a longer-lived record. A changed problem needs a fresh plan, while undoing a card preserves your draft. A parent checks why each step applies, whether all mathematical roots were considered and whether the final answer returns to the original target. Cards do not automatically complete the course.
