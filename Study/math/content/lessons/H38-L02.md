# 用图表检验假设

图表可以暴露模型与观测的不一致，但不能因为点“看着贴线”就证明模型永远正确。本课主要目标是区分模型开发与验证，并用事先声明的误差规则检验预测。以下数据均为虚构，量的是雨水到达水箱之前的收集量。

## 先声明模型与检验标准

校准数据为(r mm,V L)：(0,0)、(2,30)、(4,60)、(6,90)。它们支持候选比例模型V=15r，系数单位L/mm。先用这些资料建立候选，再查看留出的(8,105)、(10,100)。规定每个留出点都须满足|实测−预测|≤5L；这个容差在看验证误差之前确定。

有符号残差e=实测−预测。负值表示模型高估，正值表示模型低估。容差用绝对值，不是“负误差都小于5所以合格”。同一图的横轴是毫米，纵轴是升，不要求两轴相同的数值单位长度；必须明确各轴标尺。

### 例1：校准完全吻合，验证仍失败

V=15r在8mm预测120L，实际105L，残差105−120=−15L，绝对值15大于5。10mm预测150L，实际100L，残差−50L，也超容差。四个校准点即使都在直线上，仍不能保证新的雨量范围。图上橙色留出点低于紫色模型，残差表把视觉差距变成带单位的数。

不能在看到这两个偏差后把容差放大到50L，再宣称原检验成功。若测量有不确定性或业务需求改变，应交代新标准及原因，重新组织检验，而不是悄悄移动目标。误差模式也不直接证明堵塞、传感器错误或某个物理原因，需要额外调查。

### 例2：改模型以后，数据角色也改变

试候选V=min(15r,105)。原校准点仍符合，8mm预测105、残差0；10mm也预测105、残差−5，按≤5L规则，这两点在容差内，边界−5应算通过。但如果正是观察这两点后想到加105上限，它们已经参与开发，不能继续冒充全新的独立验证。应保留修模过程，并收集新资料检验未来预测。

另一个候选V=15r+10在零雨量时预测10L，与本模型无其他水源、量入箱前收集量的前提冲突。即使某段数据能拟合，物理定义和边界仍要核。这里观察在水箱之前，不能随意把“水箱满了”当成收集量上限的已证原因。

## 观察、反驳与暂时支持

工作台先显示校准点与候选曲线，可先预测8mm的量，再显示留出数据和残差任务。紫实心与橙空心区分用途，颜色不是唯一标记。观测点不连成线，连续曲线只代表明确声明的候选函数。核验表按用途列出雨量、实测、预测与有符号残差。

一次失败可以反驳“这些验证点全部符合当前容差”的命题；一次通过只支持当前样本、范围和标准下的适用性，不能证明所有雨量、所有屋顶或因果关系。还要询问样本覆盖、重复测量、量具误差，以及新数据是否真正独立于选模型过程。

出口比较原直线与修订上限模型，并说明后续验证需求，由人核查论证。家长可问“这个点参与过选模型吗”和“负残差是高估还是低估”；若反号或只看曲线，回到残差课程，先逐点列预测与实测再判断。

# Testing assumptions with graphs

A plot can reveal disagreement between a model and observations, but points looking close to a line do not prove universal correctness. Separate model development from validation, and test predictions against an error rule declared in advance. All data here are fictional and describe water collected before it reaches tank storage.

## Declare the model and test rule first

Calibration observations (r mm,V L) are (0,0), (2,30), (4,60), (6,90). They support candidate V=15r, with coefficient in L/mm. Develop a candidate with these observations before inspecting held-out (8,105) and (10,100). The validation rule requires absolute error at every held-out point to be at most five litres, fixed before viewing those errors.

Signed residual is observed minus predicted. A negative residual means overprediction; a positive one means underprediction. Use its absolute value for tolerance: negative errors do not automatically pass. Horizontal rainfall and vertical volume have different units, so state both scales rather than implying equal numerical units on the axes.

### Example 1: Perfect calibration, failed validation

The line predicts 120 L at eight millimetres, against observed 105 L. Residual is −15 L, whose magnitude exceeds five. At ten millimetres the prediction is 150 L against observed 100 L, giving −50 L, also outside tolerance. Four exact calibration matches did not guarantee predictions at new rainfall values. The residual table quantifies the gap seen between orange validation points and the purple model.

Widening tolerance to fifty litres after seeing these deviations would not make the original test successful. If uncertainty or practical requirements justify a revised standard, document that change and perform a fresh evaluation. The error pattern alone does not identify blockage, a sensor fault or any particular physical cause.

### Example 2: Revision changes the role of data

Candidate V=min(15r,105) still fits the calibration points. It predicts 105 at both held-out rainfall values, giving residuals zero and −5. Both meet the stated ≤5 L rule, including equality at the boundary. However, if these observations inspired the 105 L cap, they have participated in development. They are no longer fresh independent validation. Document the revision and obtain new observations for a further test.

Candidate V=15r+10 instead predicts ten litres at zero rainfall. This conflicts with the no-extra-source assumption and the definition of collected inflow. A local fit does not excuse an inconsistent boundary. Measurements are before storage, so tank saturation cannot simply be asserted as the cause of a collection cap.

## Observation, rejection and provisional support

The workbench initially shows calibration points and a declared model. Predict volume at eight millimetres before revealing held-out data and residual questions. Filled purple and open orange symbols distinguish roles without relying only on colour. Observations are not joined into an implied continuous fact; the continuous curve represents the stated candidate function. The table retains data role, units, observations, predictions and signed residuals.

A failed point refutes the claim that every displayed validation error meets the current tolerance. Passing supports only the sampled range and stated criterion, not every rainfall value, every roof or a causal explanation. Consider coverage, repeated measurements, instrument uncertainty and whether evaluation data influenced model choice. The exit compares both models and identifies fresh-validation needs for human review. Ask whether a point helped choose the model and whether a negative residual indicates overprediction. Return to the earlier residual lesson if signs or data roles are confused.
