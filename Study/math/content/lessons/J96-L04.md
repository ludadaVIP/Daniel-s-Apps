# 已知边求角

## 从比值回到锐角

sin、cos、tan把角对应到边比。已知适当边比反求角，要使用相应反函数键sin⁻¹、cos⁻¹或tan⁻¹。本课只求锐角，范围0<θ<90°，在各自合法比值下角唯一。这里的上标−1表示反函数，不是把数值取倒数；不要把sin⁻¹(3/5)误算成5/3。

先固定参照锐角，再按已知的两边选函数。对边与斜边用sin，邻直角边与斜边用cos，对边与邻直角边用tan。两条长度先统一单位，得到无单位比值，才能作为反函数输入。不要把“3 m/500 cm”直接看作3/500，也不要在计算器里把单位字符串混进数值。

### 例 1：对边三、斜边五

已知sinθ=3/5，设计算器为DEG度数制，θ=sin⁻¹(0.6)≈36.86989765°。题目要求一位小数，则最后写36.9°。不把中间值先粗略改成37再继续后续计算，也不把比值0.6直接当角度。

若计算器在RAD模式，可能显示约0.643501 rad。这表示同一个角的另一种单位，不能直接在0.643501后加°。转换关系为度数=弧度数×180/π，得到同样的36.8699°；或者切回DEG再算。工作台只改变显示模式时，原先按度作答的预测保持，因为题目所求的单位没有变。

### 例 2：同一个比值，不同函数

若给的是cosθ=3/5，应求cos⁻¹(0.6)≈53.1301°，按一位小数写53.1°。不能沿用上一例的36.9°。在3–4–5三角形中，这两个锐角互余，正好能通过对边与邻边互换来核验。

tanθ=1则给等腰直角三角形的45°，要求一位小数写45.0°。sinθ=1/2给30°，同样写30.0°。这些尾零表达题目的精度要求，不能因为角恰为整数就删掉。另一方面，如果题目要求精确角而非小数格式，可以直接写特殊角；总要先读清作答要求。

## 先查合法范围

对锐角，sin和cos必须严格在0与1之间，tan必须大于零。sinθ=6/5不可能来自这种直角三角形，因为对边不能长于斜边；应查原始数据、边名或模型，而不是强迫计算器返回一个角。对sin或cos，比值等于0或1对应的端点也不在本课普通锐角范围内。tan的合法比值范围不同：tanθ=1给45°，仍是合法锐角；tanθ=0才落在被排除的0°端点，不能把sin/cos的上界套给tan。

使用反函数时，写清“已知哪种比→选哪种反函数→用什么角度单位→最终保留几位”。不要把计算器当成替你选择几何模型的工具。它会按输入运算，却不知道你把视线误认成水平距离，或把另一锐角当成参照。

家长核对边的身份、比值范围和DEG/RAD，再看舍入。离堂用自己的话解释反函数与倒数，并把0.6435 rad与36.9°连接起来。完整解释由人审阅，自动判对一个小数不代表这些选择都已理解。

# Finding angles from sides

## Return from a ratio to an acute angle

Sine, cosine and tangent map angles to side ratios. Their inverse functions recover angles from appropriate ratios. This course asks for acute angles only, 0<θ<90°, giving a unique angle for each valid ratio. The superscript −1 denotes an inverse function, not a reciprocal: sin⁻¹(3/5) is not 5/3.

Fix the reference, then select sine for opposite/hypotenuse, cosine for adjacent leg/hypotenuse or tangent for opposite/adjacent leg. Unify length units first. Three metres divided by five hundred centimetres is not simply 3/500. The resulting dimensionless ratio is the inverse function's input.

### Example 1: opposite three, hypotenuse five

Given sinθ=3/5, DEG mode gives θ=sin⁻¹(0.6)≈36.86989765°. To one decimal place report 36.9°. Retain extra digits for intermediate calculations and do not submit the ratio 0.6 as an angle.

RAD mode may instead display approximately 0.643501 rad. This is the same angle in another unit; appending a degree symbol does not convert it. Multiply radians by 180/π to recover degrees, or switch to DEG. The lab preserves a degree-answer prediction when only the display mode changes because the requested quantity remains the same.

### Example 2: the same number, another function

For cosθ=3/5, use cos⁻¹(0.6)≈53.1301°, giving 53.1°. Reusing 36.9° applies the wrong inverse. In a 3–4–5 triangle these acute angles are complementary, providing a check through exchanged opposite and adjacent roles.

For tanθ=1 the angle is 45°, written 45.0° when one decimal place is required. For sinθ=1/2 it is 30.0° under that same format. Trailing zeros retain the requested precision. An exact-angle task may instead accept the special angle directly; read the required representation.

Acute sine and cosine ratios lie strictly between zero and one; tangent is positive. A supposed sinθ=6/5 violates the fact that the hypotenuse is longest. Audit data, matching or model rather than forcing a calculator result. For sine or cosine, ratios zero and one are boundary cases outside the ordinary acute domain here. Tangent has a different range: tanθ=1 gives the valid acute angle 45°, whereas tanθ=0 reaches the excluded zero-degree endpoint. Do not apply the sine/cosine upper bound to tangent.

Write the chain from known ratio to inverse choice, angle unit and final rounding. A calculator does not know whether the wrong side or reference was selected. A parent checks those decisions and reviews the inverse-versus-reciprocal and DEG/RAD explanation separately from the accepted decimal.
