# 根据图像建立模型

## 坐标、规则与定义域一起读

读图先问横纵轴分别代表什么量、单位是什么、每格多少，再判断图中连线是给定规则还是只是有人把观测点连起来。两点能够确定一条直线，不代表两点能够确定任意函数。建立模型需要题目给的函数族、物理过程或其他足够条件。

屏幕中线的倾斜角也不是数值斜率。横轴每格1、纵轴每格3与横纵同刻度的显示，可以把同一数据画出不同外观。变化率始终是纵坐标变化除以横坐标变化。扩大坐标视窗不能改变实际点或把域外输入变成合法输入。

### 例 1：给定一次函数

题目明确y是0≤x≤4上的一次函数，过A(0,3)、B(4,11)。斜率(11−3)/(4−0)=2，截距由x=0处读3，因此y=2x+3。在所给范围内查询x=2，得到7；移动查询点并不改变一般式。

若另一个题只观测f(0)=0、f(1)=1，未给函数族，f=x与f=x²都符合资料，却在2处给2与4。预测2可能为真，但现有证据不能唯一建立它。工作台的两条虚线标为“可能延伸”，不是宣布真实规则有两条，更不是把一次观察当作独立证明。

### 例 2：完整分段水量图

给定完整直线段轨迹(0,12)→(3,6)→(6,6)→(9,0)，横轴分钟、纵轴升，模型范围0–9分钟。前3分钟每分钟减2 L，中间3分钟保持6 L，最后3分钟再每分钟减2 L。t=4在暂停段，水量6 L；t=7在最后下降段，水量4 L。

这次插值有依据，因为题目给了完整分段直线规则，而非只有四次观测。不能把全程强套前段的12−2t，令t=4时误报4，也不能把水平段看成“没有记录”。t=9之后不属于给定图域，不能凭屏幕坐标轴还在继续就自行延伸。

## 两种“不能求”不要混淆

虚构卡片费用给C=2n+3，只允许整数0–5张；固定费用3按这个规则计入，连0张的账面输入也如此。n=1.5虽然能代入代数式，仍不属于允许计数域，所以答“不在定义域/outside-domain”。图上只画六个允许点，不用线段暗示半张可以买。

相比之下，只有两个观测点时，对f(2)说“证据不足/insufficient”，不是已知2在定义域外，也不是证明f(2)不能是2。前者已有规则但排除该输入，后者缺少足够规则；两种原因对补资料和改模型的方向不同。

家长核验轴名与单位、完整规则、分段和定义域，再讨论模型。图像、表格、表达式可以互相翻译，但每次翻译要保留条件。只改视窗应保留结论，改变实际任务或查询对象则需重算。完整论证不由“图看着像”自动评分。

# Building models from graphs

## Read coordinates, rule and domain together

Identify axis quantities, units and tick intervals before reading shape. Establish whether joining lines are supplied rules or simply connections someone drew between observations. Two points determine a line, not an arbitrary function. A model needs an explicit family, process rule or sufficient additional conditions.

Screen inclination is not a numerical rate. Different axis scales can draw the same data at different angles. Rate remains vertical numerical change divided by horizontal change. Enlarging a window neither changes coordinates nor authorises an excluded input.

### Example 1: an explicit affine premise

An affine function on 0≤x≤4 passes through A(0,3),B(4,11). Slope is (11−3)/(4−0)=2 and intercept three, so y=2x+3. At x=2 its value is seven; moving the query does not change the general rule.

By contrast, observations f(0)=0,f(1)=1 without a family permit both f=x and f=x². These give two and four at two. A prediction of two may be true, but is not uniquely established by these data. Dashed possible extensions in the lab are examples, not assertions about the true rule.

### Example 2: a full piecewise water trace

The complete straight-segment trace is (0,12)→(3,6)→(6,6)→(9,0), time in minutes and volume in litres, over zero to nine minutes. It falls by two litres per minute, pauses at six, then falls by two again. At four minutes volume is six; at seven it is four.

Interpolation is justified because the full piecewise-linear rule is supplied. Four observed samples alone would not justify it. Extending the initial rule 12−2t through the pause is wrong, and the horizontal segment is not a missing record. An axis continuing beyond nine does not extend the supplied domain.

A fictional card-cost rule C=2n+3 permits integer counts zero through five, including its stipulated fixed charge at zero. Substitution at 1.5 is algebraically possible but outside the permitted count domain. Plot only the six allowed points instead of connecting them to imply half cards may be purchased.

Outside-domain differs from insufficient evidence. In the card model the input is explicitly excluded by a known rule. With only two observations, a later value is not uniquely established, without showing that the input is excluded or that one particular prediction must be false. These problems require different repairs.

A parent reviews axes, units, rule, segments and domain before accepting a model. Translating among graph, table and expression must preserve the conditions. A view change preserves the mathematics; a changed task or queried quantity may require a new answer. Appearance alone does not supply the argument.
