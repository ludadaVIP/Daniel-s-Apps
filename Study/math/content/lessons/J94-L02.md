# 三角形相似判定

## 用充分证据减少检查

相似的定义同时涉及角与边。三角形的结构比较严格，几组适当的条件就能保证整个关系，无需逐角逐边重复验证。本课所有三角形均为非退化三角形，先固定ABC↔DEF；每条等角和每个边比都来自明确已知或有依据的推导。示意图即使画得很像，也不提供额外条件。

AA：两组对应角分别相等。由三角形内角和180°，第三组角自动相等，并保证相似。SAS：两组对应边的比相同，且这两条边之间的夹角相等。SSS：三组对应边的比都等于同一个正数。这些字母提醒我们检查条件结构，不是输入名称就自动完成证明。

### 例 1：哪些条件正好够用

若∠A=∠D=50°，∠B=∠E=60°，则第三角均为70°。两角已经足够，使用AA。这里没有确定大小倍率，底边为4或6都可能；所以AAA不能证明全等，却足够证明相似。第三角在判定中是冗余信息，并非错误信息。

若DE/AB=DF/AC=3/2，且∠D=∠A=60°，使用SAS。角A由AB与AC围成，角D由DE与DF围成，正是这两对已比边的夹角。若三对边分别为4→6、5→15/2、3→9/2，则三个同向比均3/2，可使用SSS；边不需要相等，倍率也不必为整数。

### 例 2：一般SSA为什么不足

给AB=6、BC=4、∠A=30°。角A不夹在AB和BC之间。由B向射线AC作垂线，垂距为3，垂足到A为3√3；以B为圆心、半径4的圆与射线有两个交点，其到垂足距离为√(4²−3²)=√7。因此AC可能为3√3−√7，也可能为3√3+√7，两者都为正。

选第一个三角形作为原图，把第二种三角形再整体乘3/2作为像。它们满足DE/AB=EF/BC=3/2且∠D=∠A=30°，但第三对边不按3/2对应，不能保证相似。这一个反例已经推翻“任意两边成比例加任意对应角总能判相似”。它没有说明所有SSA配置都不相似；若另外明确直角等特殊条件，还需按那些完整前提重新分析。本课一般判定不把非夹角冒充夹角。

## 不足与不相似是两种结论

“只知一组对应角”不能保证相似，可能补成相似，也可能不相似；应回答条件不足。若已知固定对应下三对边比为3/2、3/2、2，那么SSS的统一倍率前提没有满足，不能挑两对成立就省略第三对。要判断整个图形是否还能换一种对应，需要另作完整核验。

在工作台先读“只用这些已知”，再选AA、SAS、SSS或条件不足。旋转反射只改变展示，不能增加证据。揭示核验后，用手指出SAS的两边夹在哪里，或者写出三对同向边比。家长核对角的来源和逻辑链，而非只认三个字母。离堂时用自己的话区分“我看到一个像相似的图”和“这些已知足以保证相似”。

# Triangle similarity tests

## Sufficient evidence

All triangles here are nondegenerate and the correspondence is fixed as ABC↔DEF. A sketch supplies no extra equalities. AA establishes similarity from two corresponding angle equalities. The angle sum then determines the third. SAS needs two proportional corresponding sides and equality of their included angle. SSS needs all three corresponding side ratios to equal one positive factor. A criterion name summarises an argument; it does not replace the evidence supporting it.

### Example 1: match the structure

If A=D=50° and B=E=60°, both remaining angles are 70°. AA already establishes similarity. The size remains free: bases four and six can belong to triangles with these same angles. This explains why AAA establishes similarity while failing to establish congruence. The third equality is redundant, rather than invalid.

If DE/AB=DF/AC=3/2 and D=A=60°, SAS applies. A is the angle between AB and AC, and D is between DE and DF. The included position matters. Alternatively corresponding sides 4→6, 5→15/2 and 3→9/2 give three ratios of 3/2, establishing SSS without any given angle equalities. Proportional does not mean equal in length, and the common factor need not be an integer.

### Example 2: a genuine SSA counterexample

Set AB=6, BC=4 and A=30°. This angle is not between the given sides AB and BC. The perpendicular distance from B to ray AC is three, and the foot lies 3√3 from A. A circle centred at B with radius four intersects the ray at two positive distances from A: AC=3√3−√7 or AC=3√3+√7. The offset √7 follows from 4²−3².

Use the first alternative as the source, and enlarge the second alternative by 3/2 as the target. Both given side ratios equal 3/2 and A=D=30°, yet the third corresponding sides do not share that factor. Thus general SSA does not guarantee similarity. This counterexample rejects a universal claim, not every individual SSA configuration. Additional special premises, such as explicitly given right angles, require their own complete analysis.

Insufficient evidence differs from a proved nonsimilar relationship. One equal angle can be completed in similar or nonsimilar ways. Three fixed corresponding ratios 3/2, 3/2 and two fail the SSS premise; selecting only the convenient pairs cannot establish it. A different proposed matching would need a separate audit.

In the lab read the listed givens before naming a criterion. Turning or reflecting the diagram changes its presentation without adding evidence. After revealing the audit, point to the included angle or write the three consistent ratios. A parent checks where the conditions came from and how they lead to the conclusion, rather than accepting a correctly typed label as a complete proof.
