# 相似三角形的边长计算

## 计算前先有对应依据

相似边长计算的关键不是交叉相乘，而是确认为什么可以列这个比例。题目可能直接给两三角形相似，也可能需要由平行线推出两角相等，再用AA。只有某一对边长，并不能自动把其商当作所有边的统一倍率。每次先写相似次序，标出所求整边对应哪条整边，再计算。

若△ABC∼△DEF，固定第二个/第一个为k，则DE=kAB、EF=kBC、DF=kAC。正向由原边求像边，乘k；反向由像边求原边，除以k。相似倍率为正数，边长也为正。倍率小于一表示缩小，不改变这些公式。精确分数往往比提前写小数更容易看出约分和对应关系。

### 例 1：同一倍率的正向与反向

已知△ABC∼△DEF，AB=4、DE=6、BC=5 cm。因为DE对应AB，k=6/4=3/2。BC对应EF，EF=5×3/2=15/2 cm。新边应比原边长，7.5>5符合放大方向；如果算成10/3，应回查是否把倍率倒置。

同一对应中若只给DF=9/2 cm，求原边CA。DF/CA=3/2，所以CA=(9/2)÷(3/2)=3 cm。不要又乘一次得到27/4，那是把“由像反求原图”当成正向放大。验证时把3乘3/2，确实回到9/2，且所有长度采用相同单位。

### 例 2：平行线内的小三角形

D在线段AB上，E在线段AC上，明确给DE∥BC。A角共用，∠ADE=∠ABC来自平行线对应角，因而△ADE∼△ABC。注意这个顺序给A↔A、D↔B、E↔C，所以AD↔AB、AE↔AC、DE↔BC。

AB=6、AD=4、AC=9、BC=12 cm，则小/大倍率AD/AB=4/6=2/3。于是AE=9×2/3=6 cm，DE=12×2/3=8 cm。DB=AB−AD=2 cm，但AD/DB=2不是小三角形与大三角形的倍率，DB只是剩余段。若题目给AD=4、DB=2，先把AB补成6，再列整边比例。

## 用反例检查是否偷加了相似

另有两个三角形，仅给AB=4、DE=6、BC=5，却没有任何相似关系或角条件。此时不能直接宣布EF=15/2。保持第二图的DE=6，移动F，可使EF出现多个长度，同时仍满足所有已给。答案应为条件不足。这种核验比在一个看起来熟悉的图上照套比例更可靠。

工作台有正向、反向、嵌套和条件不足四种情况。换一种可能图形不改变已知，也不应清掉对证据充分性的判断；切换实际数学案例则重新作答。先输入预测，再揭示对应链，最后把所求值代回比例。家长可逐问：“哪条角或哪句已知提供相似？小图的整边是哪条？倍率方向是否统一？答案的单位和大小合理吗？”完整推理仍需人工核验，数值正确不保证比例依据正确。

# Finding sides in similar triangles

## Establish the proportion first

Before calculating, identify the statement that permits corresponding-side ratios. Similarity may be supplied directly, or follow from parallel lines and AA. One side pair alone does not supply a common factor for every length. Write the triangle order, match the requested whole sides and only then solve the proportion.

For △ABC∼△DEF with second/first factor k, DE=kAB, EF=kBC and DF=kAC. Multiply when moving from source to image; divide when recovering a source side from an image. All lengths and factors are positive. A factor below one represents a reduction and follows the same rules. Exact fractions preserve the relationships without premature rounding.

### Example 1: forward and reverse

Given AB=4, DE=6 and BC=5 cm under △ABC∼△DEF, the corresponding pair DE/AB gives k=3/2. Hence EF=5×3/2=15/2 cm. This is larger than five, agreeing with enlargement. An answer of 10/3 would indicate an inverted factor.

If DF=9/2 cm and original CA is requested instead, use DF/CA=3/2. Therefore CA=(9/2)÷(3/2)=3 cm. Multiplying again would enlarge an image length instead of recovering its source. Verify by multiplying three by 3/2 to recover 9/2, with consistent length units throughout.

### Example 2: nested triangles

D lies on segment AB and E on segment AC, with explicitly given DE∥BC. The A angle is common, and ∠ADE=∠ABC follows from corresponding angles at parallel lines. AA therefore establishes △ADE∼△ABC. Its order gives AD↔AB, AE↔AC and DE↔BC.

Let AB=6, AD=4, AC=9 and BC=12 cm. The small/large factor is AD/AB=2/3. Thus AE=6 cm and DE=8 cm. Although DB=2 cm, AD/DB is not the triangle scale: DB is a leftover segment, rather than the large triangle's corresponding whole side. If AD=4 and DB=2 are given, reconstruct AB=6 before forming the ratio.

Now remove the similarity premise from two separate triangles with only AB=4, DE=6 and BC=5 known. Keeping DE fixed while moving F gives different possible EF values. Consequently EF is not uniquely determined. A familiar-looking diagram cannot supply the missing premise.

Use the lab's forward, reverse, nested and insufficient-evidence cases. Showing another possible triangle preserves the givens, while changing the mathematical case creates a new task. Predict before revealing the ratio chain, then substitute your result back into it. A parent checks the source of similarity, whole-side correspondence, scale direction and units. A correct number alone does not establish that the proportional reasoning was justified.
