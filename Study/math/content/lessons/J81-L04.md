# 完全平方公式

## 平方的是整个括号

`(A+B)²` 表示 `(A+B)(A+B)`，不是 A²+B²。先写四对乘积：A²、AB、BA、B²。乘法交换律使 AB=BA，两个交叉项合为 2AB，因此 `(A+B)²=A²+2AB+B²`。

减法同样从乘法出发：`(A−B)(A−B)` 的四对为 A²、−AB、−BA、B²，所以 `(A−B)²=A²−2AB+B²`。中间项为负，最后一项仍为正，因为两个负 B 相乘为正 B²。两个式子都是完整分配的简写，不是凭图案猜出的口诀。

### 例 1：两块交叉项必须保留

`(x+3)²=x²+3x+3x+9=x²+6x+9`。正长度 x>0 时，将边长 x+3 的正方形沿两方向各分成 x 与三，会得到 x² 大块、两个不同位置的 3x 长方形及九的小角块。两个 3x 面积相同但位置不同，没有重复计同一块。

若首项改成 2x，必须将整个首项平方：`(2x+3)²=(2x)²+2·(2x)·3+3²=4x²+12x+9`。不能只平方 x 而保留系数二；中间项的两倍也不能丢。分数同理，`((1/2)x+2)²=(1/4)x²+2x+4`。

### 例 2：差的平方与重叠角块

`(x−4)²=x²−4x−4x+16=x²−8x+16`。当 x>4 时，可从边长 x 的正方形，减去右边和下边宽四的两条。每条面积 4x，但角上十六的方块同时属于两条，被减了两次；应补回一次，才能正确得到剩余面积 `(x−4)²`。

补角不是凭空添面积，而是修正重减。模型默认 x=5、b=2 时，先二十五，减两条各十，再补四，余九，正好三的平方。图需要 x>b>0，若字母为负或小于 b，应回到代数分配，不能画负宽度来硬套图。

## 试数能检错，不能替代结构证明

有人声称 `(x+b)²=x²+b²`。当 x=0 时两边确实同为 b²，但这只是一个输入吻合。取 b=2、x=1，原式九、错误式五，便足以否证该主张。对减号平方，同样取 b=2、x=1，原式一、错误式五。代数代入可合法使用这些值，并不表示正长度减块图也适用。

即使试了更多值全相同，也不能据此证明一般恒等；证明的依据是四对乘积、交换律与合并。工作台让你先列齐乘积，再比较漏中项的主张在零与一处的表现。一个反例的作用，与若干吻合的作用并不对称。

本课要求展开合并，保留正确中项、整体平方及正的末项。家长问“两个交叉项分别来自哪里？”“角块为什么补一次？”“图与代数各允许哪些取值？”能够回答这些，比背出首平方、尾平方更可靠。

# Perfect-square identities

## The whole bracket is squared

(A+B)² means (A+B)(A+B), not A²+B². Its four products are A²,AB,BA,B². Since AB=BA, collect the cross terms to obtain A²+2AB+B².

Similarly, (A−B)(A−B) gives A²,−AB,−BA,B², hence A²−2AB+B². The middle term is negative, but the final square is positive because two negative B factors multiply to B². These identities abbreviate full distribution rather than a visual guess.

### Example 1: retain both cross terms

(x+3)²=x²+3x+3x+9=x²+6x+9. For x>0, split both sides of a square x+3 into x and three. The four regions are x², two separate rectangles of area 3x and a corner of area nine. Equal cross areas occupy different regions; neither is double-counted.

If the first term is 2x, square the whole term: (2x+3)²=4x²+12x+9. Squaring only x would miss the coefficient square, and the middle term needs twice the full product. Fractional terms work in exactly the same way: ((1/2)x+2)²=(1/4)x²+2x+4.

### Example 2: a squared difference and overlapping strips

(x−4)²=x²−4x−4x+16=x²−8x+16. For x>4, subtract right and bottom strips of width four from a square of side x. Each strip has area 4x, but their sixteen-unit corner lies in both and was subtracted twice. Restore that corner once to obtain (x−4)².

The restoration corrects double subtraction rather than adding a new region. At the model’s x=5,b=2, start with twenty-five, subtract ten twice and restore four to obtain nine, the square of three. The removal picture requires x>b>0. Algebraic distribution covers values outside that geometric domain without inventing negative widths.

## Numerical checks can disprove without proving identity

Consider the false claim (x+b)²=x²+b². At x=0 both expressions equal b², but this is only one agreement. At b=2,x=1, the original square is nine and the false expression five, disproving it. For the minus square at the same values, the original is one and the false expression five. These substitutions are algebraically valid even when a positive-length removal diagram is not.

More matching numerical trials still do not establish every real input. The proof uses all four products, commutativity and collection. The workshop compares the missing-middle claim at zero and one after examining term pairs. A counterexample and several agreements have different evidential force.

Complete the requested expansion with the correct middle term, whole-term squares and positive final square. A parent asks where both cross terms came from, why the corner is restored once, and which domains apply to the picture and the algebra. Those reasons matter beyond a memorised formula.
