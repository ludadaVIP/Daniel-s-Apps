# 简单不等式组

## 同一个数通过每个条件

不等式组的各条条件必须同时成立。“且”表示取解集的**交集**：留下各条都涂色的部分。“或”表示至少一条成立，是另一个问题。不能把两边涂色简单并在一起，更不能从一个条件取一个数、从另一个条件取不同的数，称作共同解。

先分别解每个不等式，再在同一刻度数轴上比较。下界与上界之间可能留下区间、一个点，也可能没有共同部分。同向两个下界取更严格的下界；例如 x>−2 且 x≥1，所有共同解就是 x≥1，因为满足后一条就自动满足前一条。若边界值相同，要再比较空心与实心，严格条件更强。

### 例 1：区间与边界相遇

`x≥−1` 且 `x<4`：第一条包含负一并向右，第二条排除四并向左。共同部分为 `−1≤x<4`。负一通过两条，四不通过第二条；零与 3.5 通过。只写 x≥−1 会错误允许五，只写 x<4 会错误允许负三。

对照 `x≥2` 且 `x≤2`，两端相遇于二，且双方都包含，所以仅剩 `x=2`。若改成 `x>2` 且 `x≤2`，二被第一条排除，别的数也无法同时通过，因此无解。相遇不一定为空，也不一定留点，必须分别核边界。

`x>3` 且 `x≤1` 则完全没有交叠。虽然每条各有无穷多个解，合在一起为空集。这与“在当前图窗没显示涂色”不同；后者还可能只是解在窗外，例如 7≤x<10 在负五到五的窗内看不到。

### 例 2：连续升数与整袋数

虚构液体 x 升，连续且非负，费用为 2x+1 元，至多八元。金额条件 `2x+1≤8` 给出 x≤7/2，数量域还要求 x≥0。共同允许范围是 `0≤x≤7/2`。三点五升允许，负一升虽然满足金额不等式，却不符合数量域。不能只交金额条件的半条范围。

换成虚构材料每袋 NZ$3，另收固定 NZ$2，总预算至多 NZ$18，袋数是非负整数。`3x+2≤18` 得 x≤16/3。结合数量域，允许袋数为零、一、二、三、四、五；问“最多几袋”时回答五，不回答分数袋数三分之十六，也不能四舍五入为六。五袋费用十七不超预算；六袋费用二十，超过十八。

这里实数解集与实际计数解分开：如果把 x 当任意实数，代数给一条连续范围；题目明确要求整袋，才从中筛出整数。根据预算上界取最大允许整数，需要原条件复核，不能背“凡分数都向下取整”而忽略下界或负数情况。

## 家庭挑战与复盘

用纸笔设计虚构社区活动的两条限制，写清 x 是升数还是袋数、单位与允许域。画每条数轴，再找共同部分。分别测试两个边界、一个允许值、一个不允许值，并说明具体在哪条失败。设计一组双方包含的相遇边界和一组有一侧不包含的边界，比较单点与空集。

工作台保留原两条，候选同一个数逐条代入；独立填写共同解集后可显示交集。改变视窗只改变显示，保留作答；更换条件才重置。家长核孩子是否真正使用“同时”，解释仍由人检查，不把一句“取交集”当作已理解的证据。活动无需购物、真实测量或个人信息。

# Simple systems of inequalities

## The same value must pass every condition

A system requires its conditions simultaneously. AND means the **intersection**: retain values shaded by every condition. OR would mean passing at least one and is a different task. Do not simply unite shading or choose different values for different conditions.

Solve each condition, then compare on a common scale. Lower and upper bounds may leave an interval, one point or nothing. For two lower bounds, retain the stronger: x>-2 AND x≥1 gives x≥1. If boundary values coincide, inspect inclusion as well; a strict condition is stronger than an inclusive one at the same boundary.

### Example 1: intervals and touching bounds

For x≥-1 AND x<4, the shared part is -1≤x<4. Minus one passes both; four fails the second. Zero and 3.5 pass. The first condition alone wrongly admits five, while the second alone wrongly admits minus three.

For x≥2 AND x≤2, both bounds include two, leaving the singleton x=2. Changing the first to x>2 removes that point; no other value passes both, so the set is empty. Touching does not always mean empty or always mean a point: test both endpoint conditions.

For x>3 AND x≤1 there is no overlap, despite infinitely many solutions to each separate condition. Distinguish an empty set from a set outside the window: 7≤x<10 is nonempty even if the visible axis runs only from minus five to five.

### Example 2: continuous litres and whole bags

A fictional continuous liquid amount x litres is nonnegative and costs 2x+1 dollars, at most eight. The cost gives x≤7/2; the quantity domain gives x≥0. Their intersection is 0≤x≤7/2. Three and a half litres is allowed. Minus one satisfies the cost comparison but fails the quantity domain.

Now fictional bags cost NZ$3 each plus a fixed NZ$2 fee, with a budget at most NZ$18. The nonnegative integer count satisfies 3x+2≤18, hence x≤16/3. Allowed counts are zero through five. Asked for the maximum, answer five rather than a fractional bag count or six by rounding. Five costs seventeen; six costs twenty and exceeds the budget.

Separate the real algebraic range from the contextual count set. Integers are filtered only when required. Check a maximum against the original budget rather than adopting a blanket rounding rule that ignores other bounds or signs.

## Family investigation

On paper, invent two restrictions for fictional community supplies. Define x, units and whether it counts bags or measures litres. Graph each condition and the overlap. Test both boundaries, an allowed value and a rejected value, identifying the condition that fails. Contrast touching bounds that both include their endpoint with a case that excludes it.

The workshop retains both originals and checks the same candidate in each. Enter the shared set independently before revealing the intersection. Changing the window retains the answer; changing conditions resets it. A parent checks concrete simultaneous reasoning, not the phrase “take the intersection” alone. No purchases, real measurements or personal information are required.
