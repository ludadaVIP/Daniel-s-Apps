# 曲线下方面积应用

同一“率乘时长”的结构，可以描述位移或流入体积；两条几何曲线的高度差乘横向宽度则描述区域面积。先写清题目要哪一种量，再选择积分、绝对值、初始量及单位。图形只帮助发现结构，精确积分核验结论。

## 一张量纲检查表

速度米/秒乘秒得位移米；速率|v|积分得路程米。流入率升/分钟乘分钟得新增升数，末体积还要加初始量并考虑流出。几何图若两轴都是米，高度差米乘宽米得平方米。屏幕可以分别缩放横纵轴，像素数量不是这些量的测量单位。

计算前列出时间或空间区间、函数定义、初始状态与模型假设。找出所有可能使符号或上/下顺序改变的零点和交点，逐段核符号。把最后的数放回问题，不要只报告一个没有单位的积分值。

### 例1：速度给位移，速率给路程

物体速度v(t)=t−2米/秒，0≤t≤5秒，初始位置s(0)=10米。速度在2秒由负转正。原函数t²/2−2t给0到5的净位移25/2−10=5/2米，因此末位置10+5/2=25/2米。

0到2位移−2米，2到5位移9/2米。路程须保留两个方向的长度，得2+9/2=13/2米，不是|5/2|。初始位置10不属于走过的长度，平移坐标原点也不会改变路程。

平均速度为(5/2)/5=1/2米/秒，平均速率为(13/2)/5=13/10米/秒。若只观察0到4秒，净位移0、路程4米；回到出发位置不等于从未移动。运动公式假设速度按给定函数变化，不另外添加未给出的停留段。

### 例2：流入体积与两曲线面积

水箱q(t)=2t升/分钟，0≤t≤3分钟，初始5升，无流出且不受容量限制。新增量∫₀³2t dt=[t²]₀³=9升，末体积14升。若另有流出，必须给流出率并积分净率，不能继续直接用14。

另一个几何问题中，两轴以米计，求y=2x与y=x²在[0,2]之间的面积。交点由2x=x²得0、2，中间2x−x²≥0。面积∫₀²(2x−x²)dx=[x²−x³/3]₀²=4/3平方米。

若区间扩大为[−1,3]，高度差在0与2变号。三段[−1,0]、[0,2]、[2,3]的有符号贡献依次−4/3、4/3、−4/3；每段几何面积均4/3，总面积4平方米。始终使用2x−x²并直接求净积分只得−4/3，负号揭示顺序发生变化，却不能代表几何总面积。

## 把答案写成可复核报告

工作台在速度、流入和两曲线间切换。改变初始状态只改变末状态，不改变净积分、路程或新增流入。几何视图可展示原来两条曲线，也可展示高度差到横轴；同一分段对应相同区域量，不能把高度差曲线误称物体轨迹。

报告至少说明所求量、区间、分段依据、算式、单位、初始量与假设。家长可以要求学生对照位移与路程，或指出两曲线谁在上。出口不靠关键词自动通过，需要完整解释为何在交点处分段、为何初始量只加入末状态。自动精确判题只核数字和支持的表达式，实际理解仍需听学生陈述。

# Areas under curves in applications

Rate times duration can describe displacement or added volume. Geometric height difference times horizontal width describes area between curves. Identify the requested quantity before choosing a signed integral, absolute values or an initial state. The picture locates structure; exact calculations verify the result.

## Check units and assumptions first

Velocity in m/s times seconds gives displacement in metres. Integrating speed|v| gives distance, also in metres. Inflow in L/min times minutes gives added litres; final volume also needs initial water and any outflow. If both geometric axes measure metres, height difference times width gives m². Independent screen-axis scaling does not turn pixel counts into these physical quantities.

State the interval, functions, initial state and assumptions. Locate all zeros or intersections that can change a sign or upper/lower ordering. After calculating, report a quantity with units rather than an unexplained number.

### Example 1: Displacement, distance and final position

Let v(t)=t−2 m/s for0≤t≤5 seconds, with initial position ten metres. Velocity changes from negative to positive at two. The primitive t²/2−2t gives net displacement25/2−10=5/2 m. Final position is10+5/2=25/2 m.

From zero to two the signed displacement is−2 m; from two to five it is9/2 m. Distance adds their magnitudes:2+9/2=13/2 m. Absolute net displacement|5/2| loses the travel that cancels. Initial position ten is not travelled distance; translating the coordinate origin changes neither travel nor net displacement.

Average velocity is(5/2)/5=1/2 m/s and average speed(13/2)/5=13/10 m/s. Over zero to four, displacement is zero while distance is four metres. Returning to the starting position does not mean the object never moved. Use the stated velocity model without inventing additional stopping intervals.

### Example 2: Inflow and area between curves

For inflow q(t)=2t L/min over zero to three minutes, initial volume five litres, no outflow and no capacity constraint, added volume is∫₀³2t dt=[t²]₀³=9 L. Final volume is fourteen litres. If there is outflow, specify its rate and integrate the net rate; the same fourteen-litre conclusion cannot simply be retained.

For a separate geometric problem, both axes are metres. The curves y=2x and y=x² intersect at zero and two. Between those points,2x−x²≥0, so area is∫₀²(2x−x²)dx=[x²−x³/3]₀²=4/3 m².

Extend the interval to[−1,3]. The height difference changes sign at zero and two. Signed contributions on the three pieces are−4/3,4/3,−4/3. Each geometric magnitude is4/3, giving total area four m². Integrating2x−x² without switching order gives net−4/3, which is not total geometric area. The negative result signals the importance of upper/lower ordering rather than a negative physical area.

## Write a report another person can check

The workbench compares motion, inflow and geometry. Changing initial state affects the final state while preserving signed change and total travel or inflow. For geometry, switch between the actual two curves and their difference from the axis. The associated piecewise quantities agree; the difference curve is not a physical trajectory.

A complete report names the requested quantity, interval, split points, calculation, units and assumptions. Explain why crossings require a new height order and why initial values enter only final states. Parent review assesses that reasoning; exact automatic number checks alone do not establish understanding.
