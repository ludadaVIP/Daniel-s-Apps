# 分布的形状和偏态

均值回答一个位置问题，不能告诉你整组数据长什么样。本课用**原始点、镜像与尾部**描述分布，并检验直方图分组会隐藏什么。

## 一条核心想法

按从小到大排列数轴。右偏（right-skew）的长尾向较大值延伸，左偏（left-skew）向较小值延伸；多数点所在处不决定偏态名称。对称要求围绕一个中心的镜像结构，既可能单峰，也可能双峰；对称不等于正态。

这是一课定性形状判断，不要求计算偏度系数，也不把“均值大于中位数”当所有数据必成立的定义。应说明尾部、峰数、空隙和所观察的有限数据；不能由少量点直接证明总体分布。

### 例1：右偏和镜像后的左偏

原始数据0、1、1、1、2、3、13，7个点的总和21，均值3；排序第4项为1，所以中位数1。多数点在0至3，但单个13让右尾伸得很远。这组叫右偏，而不是左偏。

把每个x变成14−x，排序后得到1、11、12、13、13、13、14。变换翻转左右，长尾向1延伸，因此左偏。均值变成14−3=11，中位数14−1=13。数据仍有7个，三个重复值仍需保留，不因重合点而少算。

工作台固定数轴0至16，以组距1、2或4分组。每组左闭右开，最后一组包含右端16；例如组距4时[0,4)包含0、1、1、1、2、3，[4,8)与[8,12)为空，[12,16]含13。频数为6、0、0、1，共7，组宽相同，柱高才可直接比较频数。

### 例2：相同摘要不保证相同形状

对0、0、2、3、5，总和10，均值2；第3项也是2，中位数2。然而若关于2对称，0应对应4，5应对应−1，原数据都没有。因此它不对称，推翻“均值等于中位数就对称”的断言。

再看0、0、0、4、4、4，关于2镜像对应，均值与中位数都为2，确实对称，却有两个峰，非正态钟形。不同数据可以共享均值和中位数，仍有不同的峰、空隙和尾部。

## 分组工作台

先看逐点图再看直方图；每个点代表一条观测，重复值向上堆叠。改组距只改表示，不改样本数、均值和中位数；原预测保留。换数据组才重新作答。展开核验后，把均值的平衡位置与中位数的排序位置联系起来。

柱形粗看对称，不足以证明细数据镜像；宽组可能把不对称藏在同一柱里。换组距应帮助检查稳健结论，而不是挑一张最支持先入想法的图。不同宽度的组若要比较面积频数，须另学密度；本工作台只用等宽组。

## 家长核对与迁移

要求孩子指出长尾的具体记录，而不只说“右偏”。出口用0、0、2、3、5给镜像反例；要读完整理由，不能靠出现“均值、中位数”就判对。下一课把离均差平方，研究尾部为何会影响标准差，但不会因此自动删掉极端值。

# Shape and skewness

A mean summarizes location, not the entire appearance of a dataset. This lesson describes distributions using **individual observations, reflection and tails**, then checks what histogram grouping can conceal.

## One central idea

Read the horizontal scale from smaller to larger values. A right-skewed distribution has an extended upper-value tail; a left-skewed one has an extended lower-value tail. The crowded side does not name the skew. Symmetry concerns mirror structure about a centre and can coexist with one or two peaks. It does not establish normality.

This is a qualitative shape lesson, not a requirement to compute a skewness coefficient. Mean greater than median is not used as a universal definition of right skew. Describe tails, peaks and gaps in the finite observations actually given; a small dataset does not prove a population model.

### Example 1: A right tail and its reflected left tail

For 0,1,1,1,2,3,13, seven observations total 21, giving mean three. The fourth ordered observation gives median one. Most values lie between zero and three, but the observation at thirteen extends the right tail. This is right-skewed despite the crowd of points on the left.

Transform every value using 14−x. The ordered result is 1,11,12,13,13,13,14. Reflection reverses the tail: it now extends toward one, giving left skew. The mean becomes 14−3=11 and median 14−1=13. There are still seven observations; repeated values do not cease to count because their positions coincide.

The workbench uses a fixed zero-to-sixteen scale and equal bin widths one, two or four. Bins include the left endpoint and exclude the right, except that the last bin includes sixteen. At width four, [0,4) contains six observations, the next two bins are empty, and [12,16] contains thirteen. Frequencies 6,0,0,1 total seven. Equal widths make bar heights directly comparable as frequencies.

### Example 2: Equal summaries do not determine shape

The data 0,0,2,3,5 total ten, giving mean two. Their median is also two. Yet reflection about two would pair zero with four and five with −1; neither counterpart exists. The observations are asymmetric. This is a direct counterexample to the claim that equal mean and median prove symmetry.

By contrast, 0,0,0,4,4,4 mirror exactly about two and also have mean and median two. They have two peaks, not a normal bell shape. Identical location summaries can accompany different tails, gaps and modal structures.

## Grouping workbench

Inspect the stacked individual observations before the histogram. One dot represents one record; repeated values stack vertically. Changing width changes the representation while preserving observations, mean, median and predictions. Switching datasets starts a new prediction. The audit connects the mean's balance position with the median's ordered position.

A coarse histogram can hide asymmetry within a single bin. Compare binning choices to check the stability of a description, not to select the one that confirms an initial belief. Unequal-width bins would require a density discussion to relate bar area to frequency; this workbench deliberately uses only equal widths.

## Parent review and transfer

Ask the learner to point to records forming the tail, rather than only naming a skew. The exit uses 0,0,2,3,5 to give a reflected-value counterexample. Read the argument instead of approving a list of summary keywords. The next lesson uses squared deviations to explain why distant observations influence standard deviation without treating that influence as a reason to delete them.
