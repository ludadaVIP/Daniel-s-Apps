Fiscal Fourth Quarter 2026 — Financial Highlights
GAAP net income in the fiscal fourth quarter was $6.0 billion or $3.19 per share, an increase of 18% and 22%, respectively, over prior year’s results. Current year's results included special items of $469 million for a litigation provision associated with the interchange multidistrict litigation ("MDL") case, $282 million related to the donation of investments to the Visa Foundation, and $37 million for a deferred tax expense recognized due to a change in the U.S. taxation of certain foreign earnings. Current year's results also included $155 million of net gains from equity investments and $75 million from the amortization of acquired intangible assets and acquisition-related costs. Prior year's results included a special item of $899 million for a litigation provision associated with the MDL case and other legal matters, $46 million of net gains from equity investments and $66 million from the amortization of acquired intangible assets and acquisition-related costs. Excluding these items and related tax impacts, non-GAAP net income for the quarter was $6.6 billion or $3.48 per share, an increase of 13% and 17%, respectively, over prior year’s results (refer to Non-GAAP Financial Measures for further details). GAAP earnings per share growth was approximately 22% on a constant-dollar basis, which excludes the impact of foreign currency fluctuations against the U.S. dollar. Non-GAAP earnings per share growth was approximately 17% on a constant-dollar basis. All references to earnings per share assume fully diluted class A share count.
Net revenue in the fiscal fourth quarter was $12.1 billion, an increase of 13%, driven by the year-over-year growth in payments volume, cross-border volume and processed transactions. Net revenue increased 13% on a constant-dollar basis.
Payments volume for the three months ended June 30, 2026, on which fiscal fourth quarter service revenue is recognized, increased 10% over the prior year on a constant-dollar basis.
Payments volume for the three months ended September 30, 2026 increased 9% over the prior year on a constant-dollar basis.
Cross-border volume excluding transactions within Europe, which drives our international transaction revenue, for the three months ended September 30, 2026, increased 14% on a constant-dollar basis over the prior year. Total cross-border volume on a constant-dollar basis increased 14% over the prior year.
Total processed transactions, which represent transactions processed by Visa, for the three months ended September 30, 2026, were 74.2 billion, a 10% increase over the prior year.
Fiscal fourth quarter service revenue was $5.3 billion, an increase of 14% over the prior year, and is recognized based on payments volume in the prior quarter. All other revenue categories are recognized based on current quarter activity. Data processing revenue rose 16% over the prior year to $6.2 billion. International transaction revenue grew 8% over the prior year to $4.1 billion. Other revenue of $1.6 billion rose 40% over the prior year. Client incentives were $5.1 billion, up 21% over the prior year.
GAAP operating expenses were $4.9 billion for the fiscal fourth quarter, a 7% increase over the prior year's results, primarily driven by an increase in general and administrative expenses. GAAP operating expenses included the special items as well as the amortization of acquired intangible assets and acquisition-related costs in the current and prior year. Excluding these items, non-GAAP operating expenses increased 12% over the prior year, primarily driven by increases in marketing and personnel expenses.
GAAP non-operating income was $98 million for the fiscal fourth quarter, including $155 million of net equity investment gains. Excluding this item, non-GAAP non-operating expense was $57 million.
GAAP effective income tax rate was 18.5% for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Excluding the special item related to the deferred tax expense and the related tax impacts from the non-GAAP items, the non-GAAP effective income tax rate was 18.4% for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Cash, cash equivalents and investment securities were $14.2 billion at September 30, 2026.
The weighted-average number of diluted shares of class A common stock outstanding was 1.88 billion for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Fiscal Full-Year 2026 — Financial Highlights
GAAP net income in the fiscal full-year 2026 was $23.5 billion or $12.32 per share, an increase of 17% and 21%, respectively, over prior year’s results. Current year’s results included special items of $1.7 billion for a litigation provision associated with the MDL case and other legal matters, $563 million for severance costs, $314 million for a deferred tax benefit recognized due to a change in the U.S. taxation of certain foreign earnings, and $282 million related to the donation of investments to the Visa Foundation. Current year's results also included $124 million of net gains from equity investments and $305 million from the amortization of acquired intangible assets and acquisition-related costs. Prior year’s results included special items of $2.5 billion for a litigation provision associated with the MDL case and other legal matters, $213 million for severance costs, and $39 million for lease consolidation costs. Prior year's results also included $87 million of net losses from equity investments and $315 million from the amortization of acquired intangible assets and acquisition-related costs. Excluding these items and related tax impacts, non-GAAP net income for the year was $25.3 billion or $13.27 per share, an increase of 12% and 16%, respectively, over prior year’s results (refer to Non-GAAP Financial Measures for further details). GAAP earnings per share growth was approximately 20% on a constant-dollar basis, which excludes the impact of foreign currency fluctuations against the U.S. dollar. Non-GAAP earnings per share growth was approximately 15% on a constant-dollar basis. All references to earnings per share assume fully diluted class A share count.
Net revenue in the fiscal full-year 2026 was $45.9 billion, an increase of 15%, driven by the year-over-year growth in payments volume, cross-border volume and processed transactions. Net revenue increased 14% on a constant-dollar basis.
Payments volume for the twelve months ended September 30, 2026 increased 9% over the prior year on a constant-dollar basis.
Cross-border volume excluding transactions within Europe, which drives our international transaction revenue, for the twelve months ended September 30, 2026, increased 12% on a constant-dollar basis over the prior year. Total cross-border volume on a constant-dollar basis increased 13% over the prior year.
Total processed transactions, which represent transactions processed by Visa, for the twelve months ended September 30, 2026, were 281.3 billion, a 9% increase over the prior year.
Fiscal full-year 2026 service revenue was $19.9 billion, an increase of 14% over the prior year. Data processing revenue rose 17% over the prior year to $23.4 billion. International transaction revenue grew 8% over the prior year to $15.3 billion. Other revenue of $5.7 billion rose 40% over the prior year. Client incentives were $18.3 billion, up 16% over the prior year.
GAAP operating expenses were $17.8 billion for the fiscal full-year 2026, an 11% increase over the prior year's results, primarily driven by increases in personnel and marketing expenses. GAAP operating expenses included the special items as well as the amortization of acquired intangible assets and acquisition-related costs in the current and prior year. Excluding these items, non-GAAP operating expenses increased 16% over the prior year, primarily driven by increases in personnel and marketing expenses.
GAAP non-operating expense was $17 million for the fiscal full-year 2026, including $124 million of net equity investment gains. Excluding this item, non-GAAP non-operating expense was $141 million.
GAAP effective income tax rate was 16.3% for the fiscal full-year 2026. Excluding the special item related to the deferred tax benefit and the related tax impacts from the non-GAAP items, the non-GAAP effective income tax rate was 17.9% for the fiscal full-year ended September 30, 2026.
The weighted-average number of diluted shares of class A common stock outstanding was 1.91 billion for the fiscal full-year ended September 30, 2026.

Other Notable Items
On August 3, 2026, Visa announced it signed a definitive agreement to acquire BioCatch, a leading provider of behavioral-first, multi-signal fraud intelligence, for $2.4 billion in cash. The transaction is subject to customary closing conditions, including applicable regulatory approvals, and is expected to close by the end of the fiscal second quarter of 2027.
On September 18, 2026, Visa deposited $405 million into its litigation escrow account, which was previously established under the Company’s U.S. retrospective responsibility plan to insulate the Company and class A common stockholders from financial liability for certain litigation cases. This deposit has the same economic effect on earnings per share as repurchasing the Company's class A common stock as it reduced each of the as-converted class B-1 common stock, class B-2 common stock and class B-3 common stock share counts at a volume-weighted average price of $366.81.
During the three months ended September 30, 2026, Visa repurchased approximately 11 million shares of class A common stock at an average cost of $369.12 per share for $4.0 billion. In the twelve months ended September 30, 2026, Visa repurchased a total of approximately 61 million shares of class A common stock at an average cost of $335.49 per share for $20.5 billion. The Company had $24.4 billion of remaining authorized funds for share repurchases as of September 30, 2026.
On October 27, 2026, the board of directors declared an increase to Visa's quarterly cash dividend to $0.780 per share of class A common stock (determined in the case of all other outstanding common and preferred stock on an as-converted basis) payable on December 1, 2026, to all holders of record as of November 10, 2026.

Visa Non-GAAP Financial Measures (unaudited)
We use non-GAAP financial measures of our performance which exclude certain items which we believe are not representative of our continuing operations, as they may be non-recurring or have no cash impact, and may distort our longer-term operating trends. Constant-dollar financial measures are calculated by using a fixed current year U.S. dollar/foreign currency exchange rate for each local currency for the current and prior year periods, which eliminates the impact of foreign currency transactions in measuring financial performance. We consider non-GAAP measures useful to investors because they provide greater transparency into management’s view and assessment of our ongoing operating performance. Non-GAAP financial measures should not be relied upon as substitutes for, or considered in isolation from, measures calculated in accordance with GAAP.
We exclude the following from our GAAP financial results to arrive at our non-GAAP financial results:
•Gains and losses on equity investments. Gains and losses on equity investments include periodic non-cash fair value adjustments and gains and losses upon sale of an investment. These long-term investments are strategic in nature and are primarily private company investments. Gains and losses associated with these investments are tied to the performance of the companies that we invest in and therefore do not correlate to the underlying performance of our business.
•Amortization of acquired intangible assets. Amortization of acquired intangible assets consists of amortization of intangible assets such as technology and customer relationships acquired in connection with business combinations executed beginning in fiscal 2019. Amortization charges for our acquired intangible assets are non-cash and are significantly affected by the timing, frequency and size of our acquisitions, rather than our core operations. As such, we have excluded this amount to facilitate an evaluation of our current operating performance and comparison to our past operating performance.
•Acquisition-related costs. Acquisition-related costs consist primarily of one-time transaction and integration costs associated with our business combinations. These costs include professional fees, technology integration fees, restructuring activities and other direct costs related to the purchase and integration of acquired entities. These costs also include retention equity and deferred compensation when they are agreed upon as part of the purchase price of the transaction but are required to be recognized as expense post-combination. We have excluded these amounts as the expenses are recognized for a limited duration and do not reflect the underlying performance of our business.
•Litigation provision. Litigation provision includes significant accruals related to certain legal matters that are not covered by the U.S. retrospective responsibility plan or the Europe retrospective responsibility plan (uncovered legal matters) and additional accruals associated with the interchange multidistrict litigation which are covered by the U.S. retrospective responsibility plan (U.S. covered litigation). Litigation provision associated with these matters can vary significantly based on the facts and circumstances related to each matter and do not correlate to the underlying performance of our business. For the three and twelve months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, we have excluded these amounts to facilitate a comparison to our past operating performance.

For the three and twelve months ended September 30, 2026, we recorded a deferred tax adjustment within income tax provision due to a change in the U.S. taxation of certain foreign earnings. We have excluded this one-time non-cash amount as it is not representative of our ongoing operations.
•Severance costs. For the twelve months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, we recorded severance costs within personnel expense resulting from actions taken to drive operational efficiencies and reinvest in high-growth opportunities. These amounts have been excluded as they are not representative of our ongoing operations.
•Charitable contributions. For the three and twelve months ended September 30, 2026, we donated investments to the Visa Foundation and recognized non-cash general and administrative expenses. We have excluded these amounts as they do not reflect the underlying performance of our business.
•Lease consolidation costs. For the twelve months ended September 30, 2025, we recorded a charge within general and administrative expense associated with the consolidation of certain leased office spaces. We have excluded this amount as it does not reflect the underlying performance of our business.
