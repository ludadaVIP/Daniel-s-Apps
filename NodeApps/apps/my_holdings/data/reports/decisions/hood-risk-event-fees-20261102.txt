You buy Yes if you think the outcome will occur, or No if you think it won't. Robinhood offers event contracts through Robinhood Derivatives and its membership with the partnered exchanges regulated by the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC). Robinhood event contracts allow for speculation in prediction markets—these are not bets. What is an event contract? What is an event contract? An event contract is a financial derivative based on a binary Yes/No outcome. You buy either a Yes contract or a No contract on whether a specific event will happen. If your prediction is correct, the contract settles at $1. If your prediction is wrong, it settles at $0. Contract prices range from $0.01 to $0.99 and reflect the market's implied probability of the outcome. For example, a contract priced at $0.65 means the market implies roughly a 65% chance the outcome occurs. You can buy and sell contracts before expiration at the current market price. Event contracts are available across several categories, including sports, politics, weather, commodities, and entertainment. Fees and commissions Fees and commissions As of June 1, 2026, Robinhood is moving from a flat-rate to a probability-weighted calculation. Your tier-based constant is either 10% without a Robinhood Gold subscription ($5 monthly subscription) or 5% with a Gold subscription. Commission = (k * p * (1 − p) * c) Where: k = tier-based constant (5% with Gold or 10% without Gold) p = contract price, expressed as a decimal between 0 and 1 c = number of contracts traded Note that commissions are lowest at high-conviction prices (near $0 or $1) and highest near $0.50. We round up to the nearest $0.01 on all trades with a maximum commission rate of $0.01 per contract. Exchanges typically charge an additional fee, which varies by exchange and can be up to $0.01 per contract. These fees apply to both opening and closing trades and are reflected in your annual statement under Total fees and commissions . For more details about Robinhood and exchange fees, check out our Robinhood Derivatives Fee Schedule . Example Let’s say you buy 100 Yes contracts at $0.90 each and the exchange fee is $0.01 per contract. With a Gold subscription, the Robinhood commission will be $0.45, so the total cost will be $91.45. The commission formula is (5% * $0.90 * (1 − $0.90) * 100). Without a Gold subscription, the Robinhood commission will be $0.90, so the total cost will be $91.90 . The commission formula is  (10% * $0.90 * (1 − $0.90) * 100). Key terms Key terms Yes contract : A contract that pays $1 if the stated outcome occurs No contract : A contract that pays $1 if the stated outcome does not occur Settlement : The determination of the final outcome and conversion of the contract to cash ($1 or $0 per contract) PCO (position closing only) : A restriction that allows you to close existing positions but not open new ones Threshold : The specific value an outcome must reach (or not reach) for the Yes contract to pay $1 Instant deposits : Uninvested cash from ACH deposits made available before the transfer clears. Instant deposits aren't available for event contracts.
