Our Ethos An Ethos of Responsibility We believe the standards set today will form the foundation for the future of event contract markets. Maintain Market Integrity Promote ecosystem quality through strong protections against fraud and manipulation. Put Compliance First List only event contracts that comply with the Commodity Exchange Act (CEA) and Core Principles. Preserve Reputation Evaluate listings with an eye toward market integrity and the reputations of Rothera and the firms on our venue. Focus on Longevity Operate under U.S. legal and federal regulatory jurisdiction, backed by leading capital markets firms committed to the long-term development of the market. Our Approach Why Rothera? Our approach is designed to meet the expectations of established financial institutions. Alignment We don’t compete with FCMs for end-customer relationships or operate an in-house market maker, minimizing channel and trading conflicts. Collaboration We develop products and services alongside FCMs and market makers, within a market structure designed to support ecosystem-wide quality. Discipline We take a selective approach to listings, applying sound and well-informed financial markets judgment to a rapidly evolving category. Experience & Innovation Our team brings extensive experience designing listed products and electronic trading solutions at exchanges, clearinghouses, prime brokers, and beyond. Reputation & Relationships Decades of work across regulated capital markets have built longstanding relationships with ISVs, infrastructure providers and buy-side and sell-side firms.
